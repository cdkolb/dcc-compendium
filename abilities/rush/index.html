    Rush (and Crowd Blast)¶

    Quick facts

    • Owner: Katia
    • Type: Physical active skill (no mana cost)
    • Cooldown: Once daily (base Rush) → once every 5 minutes (upgraded Crowd Blast, Book 3)
    • Upgrade source: Enchanted Shade Gnoll Riot Forces Crowd Control Shield

    Effect¶

    Rush propels Katia's body forward like a battering ram, dealing heavy impact damage to whatever she hits. Both its distance and aim are canonically unreliable — it's been observed to travel anywhere from roughly 5 to 20 feet and doesn't always land on the intended target.

    Crowd Blast, its Book 3 upgrade, keeps the forward-charge mechanic but adds a wide damage cone in front of Katia as she moves: everything caught in the cone (not just whatever she directly hits) takes an additional 10–20% of the primary impact's damage.

    Scaling¶

    No stated per-level formula for raw damage. The meaningful scaling shown so far is in cooldown: base Rush is limited to once per day; the Crowd Blast upgrade drops that to once every five minutes, making it viable as a repeatable combat tool rather than an emergency-only option.

    Role in the Story¶

    Rush is first used to punch Katia through a solid wall during the Book 2 Doomsday Scenario crisis. In Book 3, a Rush attack aimed at Eva Sigrid misses and instead slams into Hekla, killing her outright — the single most consequential use of the skill so far, and the reason Katia is visibly reluctant to use it again even after it's upgraded. Once upgraded to Crowd Blast, she uses it deliberately to break through packed hallways of Krakaren monsters and ghouls during the Iron Tangle's floor-wide crisis.

    Sourced from Book 2: Carl's Doomsday Scenario and Book 3: The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook.
