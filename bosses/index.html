    Bosses¶

    The dungeon's boss system has a strict tier ladder — Mordecai lists it as Neighborhood, Borough, City, Province, Country, and Floor bosses, plus a separate, rarer category called Elites. Neighborhood bosses are the most common and guard a single room; each tier up scales in danger, group size required, and reward quality (a Bronze Boss Box for Neighborhood, Silver for Borough, and so on). Killing a boss earns a permanent star next to a crawler's name.

    Full pages exist for every boss defeated by Carl and Donut — including ones like the Goblin War Chieftain, killed remotely without ever entering the boss room or triggering an official boss-card sequence. The one exception is Ringmaster Grimaldi, a City Boss + Elite resolved through negotiation rather than a kill — given a full page anyway because of how central the confrontation is to Book 2's main quest. Bosses only seen secondhand (via other crawlers, on broadcast) are listed in the table below.

    Two floors break the ladder entirely. Floor 8 runs on T'Ghee card combat, where bosses are flagged rather than killed. Floor 9 has no ladder at all — Faction Wars replaces it with enemy warlords and with gods, and neither category can be killed the usual way.

    Full pages¶

    Listed by floor, in the order Carl and Donut actually encounter them.

    Floor 1 (Book 1)¶

    • The Hoarder — Level 7 Neighborhood Boss
    • Goblin War Chieftain — Neighborhood Boss (no official card triggered)
    • The Juicer — Level 9 Neighborhood Boss
    • Ball of Swine — Level 15 Borough Boss (guards the only stairwell down to Floor 2)

    Floor 2 (Book 1)¶

    • Krakaren Clone — Level 10 Neighborhood Boss
    • Ralph — Level 11 Neighborhood Boss (Frenzied Gerbil)

    Floor 3 (Book 2)¶

    • Heather the Bear — Level 19 Neighborhood Boss
    • Miss Quill — Level 30 Neighborhood Boss (no official card triggered)
    • Ringmaster Grimaldi — Level 85 City Boss + Elite (not killed — resolved via negotiation)

    Floor 4 (Book 3)¶

    • Gore-Gore the Mantaur — Level 40 Neighborhood Boss
    • Mimic Rex — Level 90 City Boss, resolved as a Death Challenge event rather than a standard fight — see the page for the Grull the War God confrontation that comes with it. One of only six Station Mimics floor-wide; see The Iron Tangle Railway System for how the type works

    Floor 5 (Book 4)¶

    • Ruckus — Level 55 Borough Boss (Spring-operated Chicken Hawk Sentinel)
    • Denise the Feral Goose Mother — Level 53 Borough Boss, a No-Magic/No-Physical-Damage environmental fight
    • Mrs. Ghazi — Level 52 Borough Boss (Sand Ooze); "defeated" per the quest notification, but not actually killed — see the page
    • Lusca — Level 82 City Boss, resolved as a live-broadcast Special Event fight
    • Quetzalcoatlus — unclear tier; never directly fought, destroyed instead by an environmental lightning trap at the cost of every remaining crawler in her quadrant — full page given anyway per the same exception that covers Ringmaster Grimaldi

    Floor 6 (Book 5)¶

    • Claude Sludgington the Fourth — Level 65 Borough Boss (Eryops Gigantis), killed accidentally mid-siege at Fort Freedom
    • Sierra — Level 65 Neighborhood Boss, a vampire-hunter's own mount turned vampire herself
    • Big Tina — Level 80 City Boss (Allosaurus); not killed — pacified via a staged dance recital, per the same exception that covers Mrs. Ghazi and Quetzalcoatlus

    Floor 8 (Book 6)¶

    Floor 8 runs on T'Ghee card combat, which changes what "boss" means. Several of the floor's bosses are never killed at all — they are flagged into totem cards, negotiated with, or (in the case of the deities) simply invulnerable. Full pages are given anyway, per the same exception that covers Ringmaster Grimaldi and Big Tina.

    City bosses and demons

    • Asojano, Lord of Smallpox — Level 130 City Boss, a non-corporeal Orisha spirit; not killed — flagged into a T'Ghee card by Carl at the last second
    • Shi Maria / The Bedlam Bride — Level 140 City Boss, Reaper Spider Minion and demi-god; the title boss of the book. Flagged by mutual agreement, then rips herself free by tattooing into Carl's chest
    • Madre de Aguas — Level 125 City Boss; charmed by Sister Ines, then killed by her own zombism curse. Her corpse becomes Amayon's shell
    • The Reminiscence Hydra of Malicious Compliance — Level 125 City Boss, nine heads built from Carl's and Donut's dead; the first boss in dungeon history created by the System AI with no input parameters at all
    • Minge — Level 225 Minor Feral Demon, Slit's sister; released from the Nothing through a rigged knife game and killed by the Desperado Club's vorpals
    • Amayon — Level 250 Demon Prince of Sheol; not killed (invulnerable). Freed, buffed to full power and sent home — the event that scours the world and saves the stranded crawlers

    Deities

    • Ogun — Level 250 God of Blacksmithery and Armor, unsponsored; never fought, appears in person to smite Paz Lo and kill Anton Lopez
    • Yemaya — Level 250 Goddess of Rivers, a previously locked deity; re-ascended by Carl's shrine choice
    • Eileithyia — Level 250 Goddess of Childbirth and Female Pain, sponsored by Huanxin Jinx; grants a floor boon and the "Spiders" cover quest
    • Ysalte, the Vinegar Bitch — Level 250 Goddess of Hopelessness and Insanity; killed by her own summoned totem, Paz Lo

    Deckmasters

    • The Visitor — Level 70 Nightgaunt, deckmaster 7 of 10; killed by Jola the Yule Cat after Carl's nightgaunt cloak enraged it past using its own deck
    • Tom — Level 75, "Bereft Shaman of Meleagris Gallopavo Rex," deckmaster 8 of 10
    • Sharp-Elbows — Level 80 ogre necromancer witch, deckmaster 9 of 10; killed by Raul the crab
    • Robert the Human — Level 40; Donut's solo card-combat tutorial fight in the Florida Keys, ended instantly by Mongo

    Mock battle arena

    • Der Schachmeister — Level 25 boy deckmaster; a pure lockdown deck, and Carl and Donut's only practice loss in the book
    • Rude-Dolph the Blood-Nosed Slay-Deer — Level 100 Puppet-Master; intangible until all eight reindeer puppets are dead

    Floor 9 (Book 7)¶

    Floor 9 has no boss ladder at all. Faction Wars is a war, not a dungeon crawl: there are no Neighborhood/Borough/City tiers, no boss boxes and no stars. What fills the role is two entirely separate things —

    • the warlords of the nine other Faction Wars teams, each of whom has to be eliminated for the Princess Posse to win, and
    • the gods, level 250 and invulnerable, who get onto the battlefield through a series of loopholes and then cannot be removed by force.

    The gods are the only Floor 9 entities that get full pages here, because the warlords are people and belong on Characters.

    Deities

    • Khepri — Level 250 God of Rebirth and the Sunrise, with a literal bug for a head. Secretly worshipped by Princess Vinata and summoned into his own temple as a trap; the one real boss-battle sequence of the floor (ch. 53). Not killed — banished by Hellik
    • Hellik — Level 250, brother of Emberus. Appears in person, banishes Khepri, and delivers the book's densest lore dump; names Carl a "friend of the church" despite Carl's standing quest to kill him
    • Meatus — a feral god freed from the Nothing; a sapient stolen penis with a gremlin attached. Killed outright by Donut's War Crime
    • Harpocrates — Level 250 God of Silence, and the owner of the stolen half of Meatus. Conscripted with a hell-kissed level-18 potion and used as a weapon by Li Na; beats Yarilo to death before leaving the realm enraged
    • Yarilo — Level 250 feral god of lust, also freed from the Nothing; conscripted by the War Mage Rebellion and then killed by Harpocrates
    • Eris — Level 250 Goddess of Chaos. Loses her pilot mid-book and runs unsponsored; casts the once-ever Ceremony of Chaos, freezing and piling ~80,000 combatants for Donut's Rolling Battle Formation. A prime contender for the vacant Ascendent Throne
    • Eileithyia — Level 250 Goddess of Childbirth, returning from Floor 8. Loses her vessel Huanxin Jinx when Donut kills her, then grants Katia's boon (ch. 86)
    • Emberus — Level 250, Carl's own god. Arrives at the end of the book and destroys the entire floor on a 66-minute timer

    The looming threat

    • Scolopendra — never appears on the page. Warned as "stirring" twice, then "has awakened" in the book's final lines. Not a stat block; a flag for future books — she finally appears in person, and gets a full stat block, in Book 8 (see below)

    Not in Book 7

    Dodola is a Book 6 deity (a storm goddess, sponsored by Huanxin Jinx). She is not mentioned anywhere in Book 7 and has been left out of the table above rather than invented into it.

    Floor 10-11 (Book 8)¶

    Floor 10 ("Don't Come in Last") is a vehicle-racing gauntlet; Floor 11 ("A Parade of Horribles") is a single-arena finale. Between them they resolve or introduce most of the book's major deity and dungeon-boss threads.

    • Scolopendra — Level 500 Dungeon Boss, the first-ever-named tier above Floor Boss. Summoned by Carl at the Floor 11 arena as the crawlers' joint choice; neither killed nor abandoned, but transformed by an enriched pet biscuit into a sapient Level 1 party member
    • Krakaren Prime — Level 235 Floor Boss; a real, individual entity previously only referenced (see Krakaren Clone for the Book 1 copy fought instead). Added to the arena unilaterally by the AI, not requested by Carl; left badly wounded and carried away by Taranis
    • Taranis — Deity, God of Lightning; previously named but unseen since Book 5. Appears in person for the first time, kills his own brother Emberus, and departs with Krakaren Prime and a stowaway Samantha
    • Mitch — Level 120 Adolescent Shadow Mimic posing as the Desperado Club's new croupier; frozen by Carl (transformed into a dog) opening the Eye of the Bedlam Bride, then killed by Donut
    • Emberus — killed by his own brother Taranis after Carl publicly renounces his faith and confesses to Geyrun's murder; permanence unconfirmed
    • Eris — gives Carl a five-sided fate coin, takes a new monstrous physical form at the climax, and confirms the goddess Apito survives in scattered pieces
    • Khepri — brief reappearance as a decoy accusation, dismissed by Taranis

    Grull — a gap, not an omission

    Grull, the war god of the Mantaurs, reappears substantially in Book 8: ejected from his own body (which conceals an orc host called "the Maestro") by Princess Donut's Laundry Day spell during Floor 10's fifth heat, then driven off — not killed, since gods cannot die on Floor 10 specifically — by Dong Quixote and Corcunda's suicidal, magnet-boosted lance charge. No grull.md page exists yet despite Grull being referenced since Book 1 (worshipped by the Mantaurs; see Gore-Gore the Mantaur). This is a gap for a future pass, not a deliberate exclusion — his Book 8 scene is summarized in the table below in the meantime.
