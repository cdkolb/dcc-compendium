# Locations & Places to Go

A directory of recurring place *types* a crawler encounters on every floor, plus full pages for
specific named venues that are places to go rather than organizations with their own agenda (an
organization that runs a location, like [Rev-Up Moonshine Company](../dungeon-factions/rev-up-moonshine.md),
still gets filed under [Dungeon Factions](../dungeon-factions/index.md) instead — the distinction
is whether the entry is about the place or the people/group running it).

## Clubs

Members-only social venues, gated by a pass/tattoo rather than run by any single named
organization — see [Dungeon Factions](../dungeon-factions/index.md) instead for locations that
are actually run by a group with its own agenda.

- [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md) — Floor 3's cross-instance nightclub/bazaar
- [Club Vanquisher](../locations/club-vanquisher.md) — its upscale, clergy-heavy rival

Both clubs recur on Floor 5 with branches in [Hump Town](../locations/hump-town.md) (Desperado
Club) and the pazuzu town of Pandinus (a Club Vanquisher, temporarily closed for "cleansing" after
Prepotente's hellspawn familiar attacks a group of clerics inside).

On Floor 8 both clubs change permanently. The [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md) is
destroyed and closed after the Astrid assassination goes wrong and Carl releases a holding pen of
[vorpals](index.md#vorpals-book-6) inside it. [Club Vanquisher](../locations/club-vanquisher.md),
meanwhile, is finally shown from the inside — including its
[Emberus High Temple of the Ending Sun](../locations/club-vanquisher.md#the-emberus-high-temple-of-the-ending-sun)
— reached through temple entrances seeded into the floor's own geography rather than through a
pass.

On Floor 9 both clubs are back. The [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md) reopens on
**ring 4** of [Larracos](../locations/larracos.md), with the Midnight Market hidden on its bottom
regular floor, a Gray Market on its middle floor, and a Cosmic Lounge in the basement that lets
non-warlords teleport straight to the 18th.
[Club Vanquisher](../locations/club-vanquisher.md) gets two entrances per floor as usual — one
inside the city, which "*would almost always get trashed*," and one outside the city limits, which
this season is the **Temple of the Dagda**.

On Floor 6, the Desperado Club's Zockau branch introduces a new rule: each dungeon floor a
crawler has visited maps to a corresponding physical floor of the club itself, and — after Carl's
opening-day raid strands a group of hunters inside — crawlers and hunters are barred from mixing
between those floors, reached via ordinary doors on the crawler side and a secret stairwell hidden
in a bathroom toilet tank for anyone who needs to reach a floor they're not supposed to access
directly. See [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md#floor-6-book-5) for detail.

## Recurring Place Types

### Safe Rooms / Safe Zones

Combat cannot occur inside a Safe Zone, and they're the only places loot boxes can be opened.
They always appear on the minimap automatically, even under fog of war. Size and amenities vary
widely — Carl and Donut's first real base is built from an intact Polish fast-food restaurant
with a playground; their first Floor 2 base is a storm shelter. Larger Safe Rooms may include
restrooms, sleeping cubicles, food, and healing-potion fountains. Restrooms are notably the one
place camera drones can't follow a crawler.

### Tutorial Guilds

Seeded on Floors 1–3 specifically, run by a Non-Combatant NPC guide (like
[Mordecai](../characters/mordecai.md)). A Tutorial Guild is where a brand-new crawler first
unlocks inventory/menu access and gets walked through core mechanics — it's a one-time
onboarding experience, distinct from the many ordinary Training Guilds scattered elsewhere.

### Training Guilds

Ordinary guildhalls found scattered across every floor (not just 1–3) — Mordecai's own Tutorial
Guild is itself a Training Guild, just the first one a crawler visits. Once a crawler has been
"trained" (assigned a registered guide), entering *any* other Training Guild on that floor
automatically redirects them back to their own guide's room instead: "if you enter any
additional training guilds, you'll be transported back to this room." They're common enough that
groups scouting a floor track which quadrants still have accessible ones nearby.

### Reward Rooms

A rarer room type offering items not obtainable from ordinary loot boxes. Discovering one
triggers a distinct achievement:

> "You have discovered a reward room! Scattered throughout the dungeon, reward rooms offer
> crawlers items generally not available within loot boxes. Most reward rooms only allow one
> choice."

**Pet reward rooms** are a named subtype — caged, selectable pet-class creatures (this is where
[Mongo](../characters/mongo.md) is found):

> "You have discovered a pet reward room. From caterpillars who secrete vodka to basilisks who
> can turn mobs to stone, a good pet can make the difference between survival and the end of the
> road."

## Book 6 (Floor 8): Cuba

Floor 8's phase-one and phase-three region for Carl and Donut is a replay of Havana and its
surroundings, exact down to the Christmas decorations. See [Level 8](../floors/level-08.md) for the
floor's overall shape.

| Location | What it is |
|---|---|
| [El Capitolio Nacional de Cuba](../locations/el-capitolio.md) | The floor's starting chamber and one of the region's two stairwells. Destroyed in phase three; its doorway's destruction takes every key cut for it. **Full page.** |
| [Colón Cemetery](../locations/colon-cemetery.md) | A corrupted **Medium Ghommid Settlement**, liberated and renamed the **Temple of Yemaya**. Holds a Club Vanquisher entrance and the **Yellow Zone** saferoom pub. **Full page.** |
| [The FOCSA building](../locations/focsa-building.md) | A green-and-tan, Y-shaped, 32-storey residential tower; the site of the book's final battle. **Full page.** |
| **Old Havana** | The downtown zone immediately outside the Capitolio ("Entering Old Havana"), adjoining Miramar. A named starting area rather than a destination. |
| **Cuba Libro** | "A small coffee shop name 'Cuba Libro'," with a mob-filled park a street over — a named saferoom, never described inside. |
| **Iglesia San Hilarion** | A phase-three drop point: the interior of an old, empty Catholic church, moonlight through stained glass, roughly 50 km southwest of the exit stairwell. |
| **Iglesia de Jesús de Miramar** | The region's *other* stairwell, and the only surviving exit once the Capitolio falls. Its grounds hold a temple of **Ibeji** and a temple of the ocean goddess **Kuraokami** — the latter doubling as a Club Vanquisher entrance. Both gods are named in a single sentence and nowhere else. The church is deliberately demolished so a channel can be dug beneath it for the escape plan. |
| **Guanajay** | A small town north-west of Havana, reached past midnight, with a huge pileup of cars and a population of duendes. A waypoint on the way to intercept Quan Ch. |
| **The northern beach** | A rocky, empty stretch of coast where Carl and Donut capture monk-seal totems — and, later, where roughly a hundred **Petty Demon Pleasure Valets** are freed from their zombie shells during the Demon Eviction. |
| **The Bay of Pigs marshlands** | The land-crab migration route and the web-choked swamp realm of the boss **Shi Maria** — webs strung over roads and trees "like a cotton candy machine had exploded," broken crab husks, a moped cocooned in silk. See [Bosses](../bosses/index.md). |

## Book 6 (Floor 8): Phase Two — Florida and Iowa

| Location | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Monroe County, Florida (the Florida Keys)** | Carl and Donut's phase-two region. Arrival is inside a dark Victorian mini-mansion; outside is a narrow beach town on a two-lane road with ocean close on both sides. Donut: "we're in the Miami area, but we're at the bottom of the map. We're really in the Florida Keys!" |
| [Dallas County, Iowa](../locations/dallas-county-iowa.md) | A 700-foot mesa of Iowa farmland dropped bodily onto the Keys by a placement glitch, complete with snow, a commercial farm, a turkey barn, a trailer park, and Carl's father's trailer spliced in from another season. **Full page.** |
| **Homestead, Florida — "The Shepherd's House"** | A homeless shelter just south of Miami, found via a flyer: "*Don't spend Christmas alone… Food, a warm bed, and fellowship. Intake open 24 hours.*" A graffiti-covered building in an industrial fruit-packing district, with a wreath on the door and a memory-ghost desk clerk watching anime. Hidden in the graffiti, in Syndicate Standard, is the real message: "*Look for me at breakfast every morning. I wear the red hat with the flaps. Coffee then speech.*" Inside, rows of cots full of sleeping ghosts. This is where Carl hears the Residual Paulie speak — and where Orren pulls him out before the end. See [The Residuals as a Plot Mechanic](index.md#the-residuals-as-a-plot-mechanic-book-6). |

## Book 6: Off-Floor and Recurring Locations

| Location | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Club Vanquisher](../locations/club-vanquisher.md) and the **High Temple of the Ending Sun** | The rival club, shown from the inside for the first time, along with the Emberus temple that is Carl's de facto church. **Full page.** |
| **The Hunting Grounds Casino** | The Desperado Club's middle-floor casino. Carl has "never actually gone into this casino before" and still doesn't — it is only ever seen from the corridor outside, during a security alarm. |
| **The Mock Battle Arena** | A practice arena attached to every saferoom on Floor 8. The base room is a featureless gray hangar; a menu lets a squad pick an environment and adjust gravity, terrain, and crowd favourability by slider. Confirmed presets: the **Arena of the Eulogist** (a colosseum with a hostile Nebular crowd whose friendliness is the only adjustable slider), **Verdant Hills** (grassy rises that break sightlines), and a full **1980s shopping mall** (arcade, Kay-Bee Toys, Waldenbooks, Orange Julius, a Santa line). See [The T'Ghee Card System](tghee-cards.md#the-mock-battle-arena) for pricing and rules. *No "abandoned arena" preset appears under that name in the text.* |
| **The Mercenary Arcade** | Not an independent venue but a purchased training room inside Carl's own guild space, named by Katia. Larger than expected, staffed by an AI drill sergeant who appears in multiple instances at once and can genuinely kill trainees (who "regenerate a minute later"); mercenaries also spar to the death for practice. Carl is warned he "wouldn't be safe in here," and later locks the Penis Parade dancers inside it as a containment measure. |
| **The underwater production facility** | A complex on the ocean floor beneath a space station parked over the Indian Ocean, reached by transferring through the station. It houses the production trailer where Odette's show is taped (dressed to look like Larracos) and the sponsor meeting room Carl knows from CrawlCon — a round ten-seat table with holographic remote attendees, run by a robot called Urkel under the executive Cascadia. |
| **Larracos** | Still flooded from Book 5's [Gate of the Feral Gods](../items/gate-of-the-feral-gods.md); its restaurants reopen but the attendants' boardroom remains unavailable, and the master pulpmancer who could have freed Shi Maria is presumed drowned. New this book: the goddess **Ysalte**, "The Downward Spiral," is native to Larracos and worshipped by its earliest citizens; and the battlefields outside it hold the **Semeru Dwarves'** castle, built along the roots of the All Tree. In the epilogue vote, Larracos is designated the home base of the new all-NPC tenth Faction Wars team. "Larracos level" is also Desperado Club slang for a punitive club tier guarded by [Armored Sai](../species/sai.md) rhinoceros samurai. |
| **Sheol, the 15th floor** | Named and described but never visited — "There's no tourist activity at all on that floor… we never get to see Sheol itself." The closest the book gets is a remote viewing of a lava-rock hellscape crawling with tiny flaming centipedes. Portals to it read "Entrance to Sheol, Floor 15" and pull like a black hole. See [Sheol and the Fifteenth Floor](index.md#sheol-and-the-fifteenth-floor-book-6). |
| **Wenatchee, Washington — the Meadowlark Elder Care facility** | An epilogue location, and the same facility the Meadowlark crawler group is named for. Agatha spent almost ten Earth years disguised as a homeless woman in the alley behind it, sitting by a barrel near a lit kitchen window, shown small kindnesses by two staff brothers named Brandon and Chris. She was there on the night of the collapse. |
| **The Kinder Facility, Mumbai** | Where disqualified crawlers are exported: "You will be disqualified and exported to the Kinder Facility located in the former city of Mumbai, India on the surface of the planet." Glimpsed once more in the epilogue, with humans "constantly battling the security forces, attempting to break into the kinder facility." No interior is ever shown. |
| **The Scolopendra Club** | Mentioned exactly once, and only as leverage: "do you know who is currently trapped in the Scolopendra Club? Over half of the entire galaxy's wealth is already gathered, and that includes my own children." It is the reason the Syndicate Council will not pull [the failsafe](index.md#the-failsafe-book-6). No physical description exists. |
| **The Penis Parade and the Guild of Suffering lair** | The Desperado Club's first-floor strip club — dark, bass-heavy, no pole, with a tusked mohawked troll dancer on the main stage and a young crocodilian "Bucket Boy" working tips — and a front for the [Guild of Suffering](../dungeon-factions/guild-of-suffering.md), whose entrance sits inside it and requires an elbow tattoo. The Night Wyrm's own lair is "hidden up there on the top floor. The vorpals won't go up there," and is never toured. See [Dungeon Factions](../dungeon-factions/index.md) for the organization. |

## Book 7 (Floor 9): Larracos and the Plains

Floor 9's geography is unusually simple to state and unusually consequential: one sunken funnel
city, one new ring of slums around its lip, and nine pie-slices of open plain radiating outward,
one per attacking team. See [Level 9](../floors/level-09.md) for the floor overview.

| Location | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Larracos, the City of Dreams](../locations/larracos.md) | A funnel-shaped city sunk into a volcanic pit, built as **66 rings numbered from the bottom up**. Held by the all-NPC Team Retribution; still flooded to ring 3 from Book 4. Transferred bodily to the 12th floor at the end of the book, leaving an empty pit. **Full page.** |
| [Shanty Town](../locations/shanty-town.md) | A settlement that generated itself around the top lip after the flooding. Carl buys the mayorship from Ferdinand for a bowler hat. Has **no throne room** — it can only be taken by killing the mayor. **Full page.** |
| [The Plains of Larracos](../locations/plains-of-larracos.md) | Nine numbered territories plus the city. Mostly flat except the rocky, lava-prone north. Border crossings are restricted to fixed one-kilometre corridors during the Ramp-Up. **Full page.** |
| [The Nest](../locations/the-nest.md) | The Princess Posse's master-quality granite **synergy tower**, keyed to Atrocity-class spells and built at the edge of the hole into Larracos. Donut's perch. **Full page.** |
| [Area 52](../locations/area-52.md) | The Posse's forest airbase, missile battery, and rear saferoom. **Full page.** |
| **The FUPA** | The Princess Posse's main castle in spot 3 — the *Fortress of Unyielding Power and Authority*, named for the acronym. Site of Baroness Victory's one kill, a Gondii brain-worm infiltration, and a Reaver paratrooper drop. See [The Plains of Larracos](../locations/plains-of-larracos.md). |
| **King's Point** | The orcs' pre-built mountain castle in spot 1 — the only territory that came with a castle. Destroyed by a deliberately engineered volcanic eruption, "*leaving nothing but a throne room just sitting there in open air.*" |
| **Architect Houston's operating theatre** | The Madness warlord's lair, beside his throne room, which nobody had ever breached. Carl does, kills him there, and finds it rigged to a dead-man's switch. |
| **The Reaver honeycomb** | The Reavers' castle: dozens of honeycomb cells stacked three or four storeys high that constantly and randomly re-arrange themselves, with the throne room behind the centre cell. |
| **The Sugar Cube** | The Floor 6 [High Elf Castle](../locations/high-elf-castle.md), *[Zerzura](../spells/zerzura.md)*'d down to Floor 9 at the end of Book 5. It lands in Lemig Sortion territory and becomes their HQ, and is thereafter referred to as the Dream castle in spot 6 — a chain of custody the book never reconciles. Demolished by fighting gods. |
| **The Temple of the Dagda** | The season's out-of-city Club Vanquisher entrance temple. Dagda is "*a weather and 'masculinity' god*" whose only membership requirement is a beard. |
| **The Temple of Khepri** | An endless buffet: "*one long table covered with a never-ending feast. And that feast is covered with 433 different kinds of bugs.*" Reached by a ritual in which female adherents swallow the god's beetles. |
| **The Midnight Market** | The hidden bazaar on the Desperado Club's bottom regular floor — "*Access to the Midnight Market. Safety or Sanity not guaranteed.*" Run by the merchant Paca, an agent for Krakaren Prime, and the source of the hell-kissed Conscription potions. |
| **The calligraphy and cartography guilds** | Two adjoining hidden guildhalls with the same guildmaster, [Milk](../characters/milk.md), who hasn't had a floor-supervisor meeting in over fifty seasons. The calligraphy guild teaches [Words of Power](index.md#the-tattoo-transfer-system-and-words-of-power-book-7). **Their physical location is never stated** — only that both doors are hidden. |

|---|---|
| **The Outreach Guild** | Where a departing crawler negotiates their exit deal, with an assigned attorney. Physically a bare, doorless "zero zone" cubicle. Carl's lawyer is [Quasar](../characters/quasar.md); Carl refuses every offer — "*My official answer is, fuck these options. I am not taking a deal.*" See [Post-Crawl Economics](index.md#post-crawl-economics-book-7). |

| **The Squirties waystation** | "*Squirties waystation and refueling platform. Located just outside the main tunnel access route entrance to the Earth system.*" The setting of the book's closing scene: its saccathian owner Zander is going bankrupt under the Earth-system embargo and facing indenture, and then Eris walks in. |
| **The kinder facility (Earth's surface)** | Where children and pregnant women — ineligible to crawl — are held. Zev puts the count at "*About 120,000. | **The Aryl system** | A previous season's crawl — a battle-royale rather than a dungeon — revealed to have gone completely dark at the exact instant its own crawlers triggered their failsafe: "*They tried triggering it, and nothing happened here. See [The Containment Interface and the Star-Detonation Failsafe](index.md#the-containment-interface-and-the-star-detonation-failsafe-book-7). |
| **The 16th floor — "Agony of Mirrors"** | Revealed, in a deliberately anticlimactic aside, to be nothing at all: "*the 16th Floor, supposedly entitled 'Agony of Mirrors' is actually a giant, empty room with a single mirror in it: a non-magical mirror plucked from an abandoned Motel 6 location in Boise, Idaho.*" |
| **The 17th floor — "The Backstage Death Maze"** | Confirmed fully built and already running, in the same breath: "*Unfortunately, the 17th floor, called 'The Backstage Death Maze' is currently built and running. You will have to proceed through this level should you want to get to the 16th.*" |
| **The Scolopendra Club (18th floor)** | Finally described rather than merely cited: a members-only VIP viewing facility holding over half the galaxy's wealth, with food menus, an activity schedule, personal-trainer feeds and gossip-trained massage NPCs — all "*programmed from orbit*" and therefore usable as a covert channel into the dungeon. Also used as a sentence ("*Death. Scolopendra Club. The Cabaret backstage. Or Earth's surface. Pick.*"), and evacuated at the end of the book when Scolopendra wakes. |

## Book 8 (Floor 10-11): Don't Come in Last / A Parade of Horribles

vehicle race, funneled through a single hub town and a rotating set of themed tracks; Floor 11 is a
single mandatory float parade ending in an arena. See [Level 10](../floors/index.md) and
[Level 11](../floors/index.md) for the floor overviews once those pages exist.

| Location | What it is |
|---|---|

| [Chicken and Waffles and Upgrades (C&W&U)](../locations/chicken-and-waffles-and-upgrades.md) | A hidden upgrade shop behind an ordinary diner front, tucked into Hungry Eyes Village's vendor strip — its jukebox is the real door, and its back room is the war mage Akuma's hideout. **Full page.** |
