# Ratings & Viewership System

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Core stats:** Views → Followers → Favorites → Patrons (each stage funnels into the next)
    - **Active from:** Floor 2 onward — Floor 1 is off-limits to live viewers entirely
    - **Why it matters:** Patrons are, per Mordecai, the single most reliable path to survival-grade gear

## Why This Menu Exists

Mordecai calls Ratings "the single most important menu in the entire game. Your life depends on
these numbers." Floor 1 activity isn't visible to live viewers at all — Borant only releases an
edited highlights reel afterward — so a crawler's public numbers only start moving once they
reach Floor 2.

## The Funnel

- **Views** — accumulated automatically; roughly 8 seconds of someone watching counts as one.
  Doesn't directly help or hurt, but signals how "interesting" a crawler is.
- **Followers** — a viewer has bookmarked the crawler's ID and can check in whenever they want.
- **Favorites** — a stronger commitment; favoriting gets a viewer live updates and combat
  notifications for that crawler. Viewers only get a limited number of favorite slots, so per
  Mordecai it should be "considered an honor."
- **Patrons** — almost always organizations rather than individuals, sponsoring a crawler
  (usually after seeing a strong favorites count) by purchasing them loot boxes as a form of
  advertising. Patron-sponsored boxes can outclass anything else in the dungeon (see the
  [Box Tier System](box-tier-system.md)'s Benefactor Box entry).

## Playing to the Camera

Mordecai's blunt advice: survival isn't just about combat competence — "You need to stand out...
Maybe you can come up with a catchphrase." He credits his own 30-million-follower, four-patron
run as a crawler as the reason he survived at all. This incentive structure is the explicit
in-universe justification for crawlers' larger-than-life, showboating behavior throughout the
series.
