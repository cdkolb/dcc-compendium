# Loot Box Tier System

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tiers (ascending):** Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Legendary → Celestial
    - **Special tier:** Benefactor Boxes — patron-only, can contain off-world tech unavailable anywhere else in the dungeon
    - **Restriction:** Boxes can only be opened inside a Safe Zone

## How Boxes Are Earned

Loot boxes are awarded almost entirely through achievements — comedic, often crude system
notifications that trigger for everything from killing a mob ("You're a murderer! He probably
had a family!") to entering a dungeon with a pet, being one of the first crawlers to enter a new
World Dungeon, or even just wearing gear for the first time. Each achievement names its own
reward box and tier inline.

## Tiers & Sourcing

Per Mordecai, most patrons can only afford Silver or Gold boxes; Bronze boxes "tend to be crap."
Richer patrons may send Platinum boxes. Two tiers are special:

- **Legendary/Celestial** boxes are rare enough that receiving one unprompted (as Carl does for
  his Legendary Pet Box, just for entering with a cat) visibly stuns even a veteran NPC like
  Mordecai.
- **Benefactor Boxes** are patron-exclusive and can contain technology from the patron's home
  world that never naturally spawns in the dungeon — Mordecai's example: "You will never find a
  pulse rifle or automated power armor in any sort of box in the world dungeon, but it's possible
  to get one from a patron."

## Rules

- Boxes can only be opened while inside a **Safe Zone**.
- Once you choose to open your boxes, it's all-or-nothing — you can't selectively open specific
  boxes out of a batch, and unopened boxes can't be sold or transferred, so there's no tactical
  reason to hoard them.
- Boxes will never contain outright cursed loot, but "unstable or damaged" loot is possible — per
  Mordecai's blanket warning: "Always read the descriptions before you activate or wear
  something. Always, always. No exceptions."
- Boxes are somewhat tailored to the recipient's race (not class) — a human barbarian might still
  receive an item intended for a caster.
