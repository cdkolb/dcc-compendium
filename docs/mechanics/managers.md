# Managers

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Unlocked by:** A hidden "Manager Benefit" attached to certain classes (e.g. Former Child Actor)
    - **Effect:** Converts a stationary guildmaster/guide into a mobile, always-present companion
    - **Trade-off:** The guide loses their guildhall, television access, the information codex,
      and eventually their own life once combat protections kick in on Floor 4
    - **Notable Manager:** [Mordecai](../characters/mordecai.md), from Book 2 onward

## What It Does

A Manager can physically teleport to a crawler's location any time they're in a safe room, and
can accompany the party outdoors on urban ("Over City") floors — something a standard guide
cannot do at all. In exchange, the guide permanently loses their guildhall room, access to the
information codex (everything they tell a crawler from that point on comes from memory alone),
and their in-universe television/feed access, which also means they lose all advance warning of
what's about to happen to their crawler.

A Manager is explicitly barred from one thing: direct combat. Per Mordecai, "we can't engage in
direct battle with mobs. Everything else is fair game" — which leaves room for a Manager to steal
supplies, forge potions, gather intelligence, or otherwise support a crawler through any means
short of fighting alongside them.

## Protection & Mortality

Managers (like tourists and staff generally) receive no combat protection on Floors 1–3 — a guide
who wanders into danger during this window can die for real. Starting on Floor 4, Managers
receive the same "kicked from the game, not truly killed" protection tourists get during Floor 9's
Faction Wars: they can still be hurt and can still feel pain, but a fatal injury only ends their
season rather than their life.

A Manager's obligation (and by extension a guide's indentureship) is typically tied to reaching a
specific floor with their crawler — for Mordecai, Floor 4. Once that's reached, he is free to
leave crawler service, collect his stipend, and depart, regardless of what happens afterward. His
final payout scales with how well his crawler continues to perform for the rest of the season,
which is the stated reason a freed Manager might choose to keep helping anyway.

## Why a Crawler Might Hide the Choice

Per advice from Odette relayed to Carl, deliberately picking a class for its Manager benefit
should be disguised as an accident. A guide who knows their ward chose the benefit on purpose —
trading away the guide's own comfort and safety for the crawler's benefit — may hold a lasting
grudge; one who believes it was a naive mistake is far more likely to forgive it.
