# Race & Class Selection

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **When it happens:** Upon reaching Floor 3, in a private session with a crawler's guide
    - **Choices:** Hundreds of races (Carl: 398, Donut: 320) and classes (Carl: 650+, Donut: 34)
    - **AI recommendations:** The system always offers exactly three suggested choices
    - **Stat cost:** Most non-Human races cost stat points relative to a flat +2-to-everything
      Human baseline; exclusive/rare races and classes carry unlock requirements instead

## The Process

Crawlers choose a permanent race and class during a private session inside their guild hall on
Floor 3 — the training-wheels period ends the moment this happens ("the training levels have
concluded. Now the games may truly begin," per Mordecai). Race is chosen first, then class; both
present a scrollable list (alphabetical for race) with exactly three AI-picked recommendations
highlighted at the top, tailored to the crawler's stats, achievements, and skills. The final
choice is irreversible and must be manually confirmed.

**Race** grants a flat set of stat modifiers and species-specific benefits/skills. Some races are
**limited/exclusive**, gated behind an achievement or a skill threshold (e.g. Hobgoblin requires
Level 5 Explosives Handling; Sasquatch requires Level 5 Smush). A handful of races — like
[Primal](../species/primal.md) — are technically available to everyone but carry an unusual stat
penalty in exchange for unlocking normally-capped skill training.

**Class** determines a crawler's build far more heavily than race. Many classes are gated behind
skill thresholds, view-count minimums, or specific achievements. Classes flagged as **Earth
Classes** are an incentive category exclusive to human-world crawlers: choosing one grants an
automatic Silver Earth Box and, per the game's own rules, comes with a mandatory **Earth Hobby
Potion** — a legally-required (per "the Indigenous Species Protection Act") random Level 3 skill
in an Earth-based hobby, which can be anything from a martial art to jai-alai to competitive
stamp collecting.

## Class-Linked Benefits Worth Knowing

- **The Manager Benefit** — a hidden perk on certain classes (see [Managers](managers.md)) that
  converts a crawler's guide from a stationary guildmaster into a mobile, always-present
  companion — at the cost of the guide's own comfort, access, and information.
- **The Character Actor Skill** — found on classes like Former Child Actor, it lets a crawler
  re-pick an entirely new "specialty" class each floor, themed to that floor, with the specific
  skills/spells granted determined semi-randomly by the system AI. Existing permanent
  spells/skills are untouched, but a poor specialty roll can temporarily saddle a crawler with
  mana-cost penalties on their real kit for the length of the floor.
