# Quests

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **First appears:** Floor 3, via a dedicated Quests tab
    - **Reward tiers:** Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Celestial Quest Box
    - **Upgrade achievements:** Hadji, then Bandit — each bumps a quest's reward box up one tier
    - **Elite-linked quests:** Treated as scripted "drama," not random content — see [Elites](boss-tier-ladder.md#elites)

## How Quests Work

Quests can be triggered by walking into a scripted location (Grimaldi's circus), by an NPC
directly asking for help (GumGum's request about the dead sex workers), or by other means. Each
quest's completion criteria can be narrower than they first appear — the text of a quest is the
actual contract, and satisfying it literally (rather than doing what seems intuitively "right")
is often both possible and rewarded. Mordecai's blanket advice on Floor 3 is to avoid quests
entirely unless a crawler is comfortably ahead of the difficulty curve, since quests consume time
against a floor's shortened collapse timer without guaranteed proportional reward.

## Reward Tiers & Upgrade Achievements

A completed quest normally grants a single loot box scaled to its difficulty (Silver for a
relatively simple mystery, Gold for one involving multiple Elites and a City Boss). Two special,
repeatable achievements can upgrade that box after the fact:

- **Hadji** — awarded for completing a quest in an unusually creative or unconventional way; upgrades the box one tier
- **Bandit** — a second, even rarer upgrade tier, awarded (per its own flavor text) even without the crawler doing anything special to earn it

Both upgrades stack, so a quest can jump multiple reward tiers in one completion. Borant has
patched out both achievements at least once after a costly incident (see below), suggesting they
can be disabled mid-season if they threaten the season's finances.

## Event Quests

A distinct, higher-stakes category: **group event quests**, automatically assigned to every
crawler within a defined blast/danger radius when something catastrophic is triggered, with no
option to decline except reaching a safe room within a short window (60 seconds, in the one
seen) — after which all safe rooms in the quest zone lock down until the event concludes. The
crawler(s) responsible for triggering the event are locked in as the "Host" and cannot opt out
under any circumstances, even if they have no way to reach a safe room themselves. Objectives can
be as blunt as "don't die," with a flat reward (a Platinum Quest Box) for every survivor.

## Case Study: The Financial Near-Catastrophe

The event quest **"The Fools Who Broke the Glass"** (see [Level 3: Quests](../floors/level-03.md#quests)
for the full text and context) triggers when Remex's death destabilizes a generations-old spell,
threatening a 45-square-kilometer blast radius. Carl, Donut, and Katia are locked in as Hosts —
unable to opt out even though Katia, lacking Desperado Club access, has nowhere to actually flee.

The twist: it isn't the Hosts who get the big reward. A random *other* survivor in the blast
zone, a level-15 crawler named **Quan Ch**, receives the rare **Bandit** achievement — an
upgrade so powerful its own flavor text admits "you didn't actually do anything to deserve this
prize" — jumping his promised Platinum Quest Box straight to **Celestial** tier. The same
achievement fires for all 83 survivors in the blast radius simultaneously, a number large enough
to threaten bankrupting [Borant Corporation](../factions/borant-corporation.md), which pays
Syndicate tax on every non-sponsored Celestial box given out. Borant invokes a once-per-season
veto to void every prize from the quest — including Carl, Donut, and Katia's own promised
Platinum boxes, despite them having done the actual work — but Quan Ch has already opened his,
so that one box stands. Borant immediately patches out both the Hadji and Bandit achievements to
prevent a repeat.
