# Powerful Strike

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **First seen:** Book 1, via the [Enchanted Toe Ring of the Splatter Skunk](../items/index.md) (+3 to the skill)
    - **Effect:** Multiplies unarmed damage per skill level
    - **Granted by:** Also boosted +1 skill level by the [Enchanted War Gauntlet of the Exalted
      Grull](../items/war-gauntlet-of-the-exalted-grull.md)

## Overview

Per Mordecai, Powerful Strike is one of the most mechanically significant skills in Carl's
build: "Each level multiplies your damage when you're not wielding a weapon. So at level three,
each kick or punch does three times the damage it normally would." Combined with
[Unarmed Combat](unarmed-combat.md) and [Pugilism](pugilism.md), Mordecai notes it's already
making Carl's fists hit harder than either of his starting melee weapons — and suggests a monk
class might enhance it further.

## Scaling

This is one of the few skills in Carl's kit with an explicit, stated formula: **each skill level
directly multiplies unarmed damage** (level 3 = 3x normal damage). It was first boosted +3 by the
[Enchanted Toe Ring of the Splatter Skunk](../items/index.md), and later gains a further +1 skill
level from the [War Gauntlet of the Exalted Grull](../items/war-gauntlet-of-the-exalted-grull.md)
— a direct, permanent boost to the multiplier on top of whatever level he trains up naturally.
