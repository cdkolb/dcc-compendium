# Monologue

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** Princess Donut
    - **Type:** **Natural benefit** — one of several unlocked when a boosted stat tops **300**
    - **Stat:** Charisma
    - **Effect:** Terrifies enemies by monologuing at them. **The more terrified they are, the more
      experience she receives**
    - **Not a spell.** It is a Charisma-derived benefit, tracked with her skills

The single most on-brand ability in the series. Donut has spent seven floors talking at people who
would rather she stopped, and the dungeon has now decided to pay her for it.

!!! note "Exact-verification note"
    **No system description block exists.** The narration below is the whole of it. The threshold is
    given as boosted stats topping **300** in this passage; [Wholesale Discount](wholesale-discount.md)
    is separately stated to unlock at Charisma topping **200**, so the natural-benefit system evidently
    has tiers.

## The Description

Chapter 86, during the confrontation with Huanxin Jinx:

> Donut's movements were mesmerizing. **She was weaving a new charisma-based skill that terrified the
> enemy. The more terrified they were, the more experience points she received. It was called
> Monologue. It was something called a natural benefit. One of several benefits crawlers received when
> their boosted stats topped 300.**

## Use

Its one shown use is the last confrontation of the book, and it is not incidental — it is the reason
the scene takes the shape it does.

Huanxin Jinx is already beaten by the time Donut starts talking. She has been ripped out of the
goddess Eileithyia's soul armor by *[Laundry Day](../spells/laundry-day.md)*, dumped into an
anti-teleport circle of words of power, and had every tech shield shredded off her by
[Claw](claw.md). The crossbow is already mounted on Donut's back.

Donut then stalks in slow circles around her, monologuing, farming experience off the CEO's terror,
before delivering the actual point:

> "Again," Donut said, continuing her slow, panther-like stalk in circles around Huanxin. "**We don't
> really need to go through all this. But we want to. We want to show those of you on the 12th floor
> what's coming.** You're strong, yes. But we are getting stronger every day. And you know what? We
> don't need to kill the gods and goddesses to win. No, honey..."
>
> The crossbow on her back fired…
>
> "...**We just need to kill you.**"

"*We don't really need to go through all this. But we want to*" is a character statement and a
mechanical one at the same time. The monologue is the attack.

See [Crown of the Sepsis Whore](../items/crown-of-the-sepsis-whore.md) for the full sequence.

## Spell or Skill?

Tracked here rather than under [Spells](../spells/index.md) because the text is explicit that it is a
**natural benefit** — an unlock earned by crossing a stat threshold, like
[Love Vampire](love-vampire.md) at 100 Charisma or [Muscle Mommy](muscle-mommy.md) at 100 Strength —
rather than something learned from a tome or costing mana.

## Scaling

No level and no numbers. The only stated variable is the **target's level of terror**, which governs
the experience payout.
