# Targeting

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** Princess Donut
    - **Original source:** The **boonie hat** that ships with her **Deathbed Hellcat** class
      accessory kit (chapter 1)
    - **Permanent source:** Absorbed into the
      [Tiara of a Thousand Lights](../items/tiara-of-a-thousand-lights.md) in chapter 3; confirmed
      active in the tiara by chapter 32
    - **Effect:** Zoom in on and examine mobs at a distance, **plus a range increase to all her
      spells**
    - **This is the skill the Book 6 "advanced targeting" flag was pointing at**

!!! note "Exact-verification note"
    **No system description block exists for this skill.** The narrated paragraph below is the entire
    description Book 7 gives.

## The Description

> The floppy hat added a **Targeting** skill, which allowed her to **zoom in and examine mobs from
> afar**. It was similar to the **gnomish farseerer**, but a lot less awkward. **It also increased the
> range of all her spells, even more than they were already buffed.**

"*Even more than they were already buffed*" refers to the **Deathbed Hellcat** class, which by itself
quadruples the range of every spell Donut owns. Targeting stacks on top of that.

The hat is beige and, per Donut, "*extra floppy, and that's always a nice bonus. I do like floppy.
Though it completely clashes with my fit.*" Carl identifies it: "*We call those boonie hats. It's not
the same thing as a bucket hat. **It's what snipers wear.**"

## The Set Bonus

Worn together with the matching **ghillie suit** from the same accessory kit:

> If she wore both the suit and hat at the same time, all the benefits were buffed even further.
> **She'd be able to snipe line-of-sight enemies from well over a mile away.**

See [Blend into the Background and Cone of Silence](blend-into-the-background.md) for the suit.

## Into the Tiara

Mordecai's very first instruction on receiving the
[Tiara of a Thousand Lights](../items/tiara-of-a-thousand-lights.md) charge pillow is to drain this
specific hat before anything else:

> "**The first thing you'll want to put on that pillow is that hat you just got with the targeting
> ability.** And set a timer in your own interface… You can't see if it worked until you pull the
> drained item off, so you have to be extra, extra careful."

The transfer is confirmed in chapter 32: "*Donut was examining the orc prince via her new Targeting
skill which had just been added to her crown.*" (She uses it to correctly identify **King Rust**
rather than Prince Stalwart, which is the first time the skill earns its keep.)

The point of moving it into the tiara is that Donut no longer has to wear a beige hat that clashes
with her fit in order to have it.

## The Payoff — Chapter 61

This is the line the Book 6 flags on
[Magic Missile](../spells/magic-missile.md) and
[Tiara of Mana Genita](../items/tiara-of-mana-genita.md) were reaching for:

> Crack! That was a new sound, and it took a moment for me to realize what it was. It was Donut
> casting *Magic Missile*. **With her tiara's advanced targeting skills and Donut's elevated position,
> along with her Deathbed Hellcat class, she could hit anything she could see with any of her
> long-range spells.** It was louder than expected.

## Why It Reshapes the Endgame

The Nest tower was designed to give Donut exactly this capability:

> Originally, we planned on making it a straight-up sniper tower, which would greatly enhance Donut's
> ability to zero in on a target across the battlefield. **But it turned out with her tiara, that was
> unnecessary. All Donut needed was line of sight to apply her sniper skills.**
>
> Which, in turn, allowed us to assign a different synergy to the tower.
>
> **The Nest was instead built to have synergy with something called Atrocity-class spells.**

So a floppy beige hat, drained into a tiara, is the reason
*[War Crime](../spells/war-crime.md)* can be cast over an entire battlefield instead of a narrow cone.

## Scaling

No level is ever given for *Targeting*, and no per-level behaviour is described. The two stated
multipliers are the **Deathbed Hellcat** class's 4× spell range and the **ghillie suit set bonus**.
