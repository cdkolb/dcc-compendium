## Overview

Iron Punch is first reported at Level 3, with a system description leaning into an old-timey
"street tough" theme: "With a skill like this, you can always find work as a 1920s street tough
or a collection agent for a bookie." It's a blunt-force punching skill distinct from the more
academic-sounding [Pugilism](pugilism.md), though both stack together as part of Carl's overall
unarmed-damage output.

## Scaling

No explicit per-level formula is stated. It's tracked at Level 3 early on, then reported at
**Level 5** shortly after [The Juicer](../bosses/the-juicer.md) fight. The
[War Gauntlet of the Exalted Grull](../items/war-gauntlet-of-the-exalted-grull.md), obtained
around that same point, grants a flat **+2 skill levels to Iron Punch** on top of whatever Carl
trains up naturally through combat.
