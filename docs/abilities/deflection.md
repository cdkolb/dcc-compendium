# Deflection

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Holder:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** Book 4 — automatically unlocked on reaching Dodge skill level 10
    - **Type:** Passive combat buff, no mana cost

## Effect

A permanent passive that halves the accuracy of both magical and physical ranged attacks (missiles
and arrows) aimed at Donut. Per Mordecai, it isn't complete protection — it doesn't stop an attack
outright, just makes it meaningfully more likely to miss.

## Scaling

Unlocked at a specific skill threshold (Dodge 10) rather than scaling further with level; the book
doesn't describe additional tiers.

## Side Effect

Because Deflection only reduces the *chance* an attack hits Donut, redirected shots have to land
somewhere — Mordecai notes this makes it statistically more likely that a missed attack meant for
Donut instead strikes Carl or Mongo if either is standing nearby.

## Role in the Story

Donut's Dodge skill had been stuck at level 9 for a long stretch before a Book 4 loot-box skill
potion finally pushes it to 10, immediately granting Deflection. It isn't shown saving her from a
specific hit on-page before Floor 5 ends, but Carl and Katia both treat it as a meaningful
survivability upgrade for a character with chronically low Constitution.
