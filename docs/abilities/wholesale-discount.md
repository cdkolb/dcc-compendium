# Wholesale Discount

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** Princess Donut
    - **Type:** **Natural benefit**, selected when her **Charisma topped 200** (chapter 77)
    - **Effect:** **Everything at cost at the store**
    - **That is the entire documented effect**

!!! warning "This page is short because the text is"
    **No system description block exists, and the book gives exactly two lines about this benefit.**
    Both are quoted in full below. Nothing else is stated about what it does, whether it applies to
    all merchants or only the Desperado Club store, or whether it stacks with other discounts. Do not
    fill in the gaps.

## The Two Lines

The trigger threshold:

> **I ALSO HAVE ONE TO PICK FOR MY CHARISMA TOPPING 200. I'M GOING TO PICK ONE CALLED WHOLESALE
> DISCOUNT.**

The effect:

> **Donut: PLUS I GET EVERYTHING AT COST AT THE STORE. IT'S FANTASTIC!**

## The Threshold, and What It Implies

This is the useful structural detail. *Wholesale Discount* unlocks at **Charisma topping 200**, while
[Monologue](monologue.md) is described as one of the benefits crawlers receive "*when their boosted
stats topped 300.*"

So the natural-benefit system has **tiers at 100, 200 and 300**, with a fresh selection offered at
each:

| Threshold | Donut's pick |
|---|---|
| Charisma 100 | [Love Vampire](love-vampire.md) (Book 3) |
| Charisma 200 | **Wholesale Discount** |
| Charisma 300 | [Monologue](monologue.md) |
| Strength 100 | [Muscle Mommy](muscle-mommy.md) |
| Intelligence 100 | [Brain Trust](brain-trust.md) |

Donut, alone among the party, is high enough in a single stat to be collecting the 300-tier benefits.

## Value

Donut gets it in chapter 77, with ten chapters of the book remaining, most of them spent in an active
war rather than shopping. It never visibly changes anything on-page. Its real value is presumably
banked for later floors, where the party's spending runs into the millions of gold.
