# Claw

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** Princess Donut
    - **Type:** **Natural cat-race skill.** Every cat has it
    - **Level:** **4** for most of the series; "*approaching 15*" by chapter 86 (Book 7); broken to
      **level 17** in Book 8 via a celestial boon
    - **Boosted by:** The
      [Cloak of the Benevolent Champion](../items/cloak-of-the-benevolent-champion.md), which Donut
      calls her shawl; permanently upgraded in Book 8 by a **celestial godslayer box** (glowing
      blue, functionally indestructible claws)
    - **Notable use:** Stripping every tech shield off **Huanxin Jinx** in a single pass (Book 7);
      decapitating the racer Dwight the unicorn in a single swipe (Book 8)

Donut has had this skill since the first floor and has spent eight books not using it, on the grounds
that melee is inelegant. It turns out to be the thing that opens up the final kill of Book 7.

!!! note "Exact-verification note"
    **No system description block exists.** The quotes below are the whole of it.

## The Reveal

Donut brings it up herself, mid-monologue, in front of a CEO who is about to die:

> "I also recently **upgraded a skill because of my lovely shawl. It's not quite at 15 yet, but it's
> getting there.** Wanna know what it is?" … "**All cats have it, naturally. It's called Claw. I was
> at level 4 before, but now I'm approaching 15.**"

The [Cloak of the Benevolent Champion](../items/cloak-of-the-benevolent-champion.md) promised "*One
random skill from your list will increase to level 15 over the course of the next 504 hours*" — three
weeks, longer than the floor. *Claw* is the obvious candidate for that roll, and Donut credits the
shawl explicitly.

!!! note "Not conclusively confirmed"
    The book never states outright that *Claw* is the cloak's randomly-selected skill. Donut says the
    shawl upgraded it and that it is "not quite at 15 yet," which fits the cloak's 504-hour timer, but
    the connection is inferred rather than stated.

## Chapter 86

The stacking is absurd and Donut has worked it out in advance. Standing in the same room as the
goddess **Eileithyia**, whose proximity buff gives female party members **500%** melee power, and
partied with Carl, whose own buff adds **another 500%**, while wearing the
[Spiked Knee Pads of Kina](../items/enchanted-spiked-knee-pads-of-kina.md):

> "And do you see my new knee pads? Aren't they lovely? … the Eileithyia boon is a 500% bonus against
> enemies. And because I'm partied with Carl, his bonus gives me another 500%. **I don't have the same
> debuff he has because I'm a girl.**"

Then:

> **Donut moved like lightning, so fast I couldn't see. One moment she was on one side of the circle,
> and the next, she was on the other. Sparks flew in the air.**
>
> Huanxin, who'd pulled herself to her feet, was again on the ground.
>
> … **The shields around Huanxin were now just simply gone.**
>
> "But again," Donut continued, "**That wasn't necessary. I'm just showing off a little.**"

Huanxin's shields are **technological**, not magical, which is why the anti-teleport circle of words
of power does not touch them and why *[Laundry Day](../spells/laundry-day.md)* could not strip them.
They have to be physically torn off, and the only member of the party positioned to do it is the cat
who never uses melee.

See [Crown of the Sepsis Whore](../items/crown-of-the-sepsis-whore.md) for the full kill chain.

## Scaling

No per-level description. The observable facts: **level 4** for essentially the whole series, and
"*approaching 15*" by chapter 86, with the increase attributed to an item rather than to training.

## Book 8: Broken Past 15 by a Celestial Boon

!!! success "A direct parallel to Mind Balance breaking to 16"
    Just as Carl's [Mind Balance](mind-balance.md) trained past its level-15 cap under battlefield
    conditions in Book 7, Donut's *Claw* is pushed past 15 in Book 8 — but by a one-time divine
    reward rather than by combat training.

Killing the god **Meatus** earns Donut a **celestial godslayer box**. Unlike every other reward in
the book, it leaves nothing physical behind — just a cloud of smoke shaped like Meatus, crushed by a
smoky paw — and its actual payload manifests directly on Donut's body:

> "Carl," Donut said, staring down at her forward right paw... She stretched her paw out, and she
> flexed so her claws appeared.
>
> The kitchen table ripped in half.

> When Donut extended her claws, the four front claws — but not her fifth or dewclaw — glowed
> brilliantly blue, like obnoxiously bright LED lights. When she retracted the claws, her paw
> continued to give off a faint glow.

Mordecai's explanation:

> "Her prize is a type of celestial boon... It's permanent, and it affects a part of her body. In
> this case, it's her claws. Just on that paw. It's not a spell or a skill. Not in the way you're
> used to. Donut, your claws are more powerful now. **You've been dipped in radiant light.**"

The in-game description tells Donut the claws "are so sharp, they can cut through anything in the
mortal realm," and that she can use them to cut ghosts as well as physical matter — an ability
otherwise restricted to specialized anti-incorporeal effects. Rosetta supplies claw caps (Donut
picks purple, gets translucent red instead) so the permanently-glowing claws don't shred furniture,
saddles, or Carl's shoulder by accident.

The skill itself jumps straight past its stated ceiling:

> "My goodness," Donut said a moment later. "It's at level 17! 17! I thought it topped out at 15!"
>
> Mordecai nodded... "It's from the gods, and when it comes to gods, all bets are off."

**Level 17** — two full levels past the 15-level training cap that had held *Claw* (and every other
trainable skill) all series, achieved instantly by divine fiat rather than by use. Mordecai frames
the jump plainly as a *combat* upgrade: "With your buffed constitution, you've gone from a ranged
powerhouse to one of the most powerful melee fighters in the game's history."

This same trip to level 17 is also the direct enabler of Donut crushing the Cadillac with
*[Astral Paw](../spells/astral-paw.md)* and single-swiping the racer Dwight's head off later in the
book — see that page's note on which feat belongs to which ability.
