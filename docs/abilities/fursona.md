# Fursona ("Fursuiter")

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** Carl
    - **Type:** New temporary-class mechanic — wearing a killed mob's skin as a costume grants
      temporary class benefits tied to that skin's type
    - **Unlocking achievement:** **"Weaseler"**
    - **Granted class while active:** **Fursuiter**
    - **Revealed by:** The kangaroo-costume infiltration of the boss
      [Prison Pocket](../bosses/index.md#floor-10-11-book-8)

*Fursona* is not flavor text — it's a genuine new game mechanic, distinct from any spell or ordinary
trained skill, that the AI unlocks retroactively based on something Carl was already doing out of
desperation.

## How It Starts

Racing to reach a two-space Nothing Spot before an engineer NPC could be claimed by a rival team,
Carl skins a dead joey off the boss Prison Pocket's own trail of corpses and wears it as a costume
to disguise himself as one of the boss's "babies":

> **Enchanted Juvenile Macropus Dominus Skin.**
>
> **This item fills a costume slot. This can be worn as an outer garment.**
>
> Equipping this costume imbues the following effects:
>
> **Protection from Seeping Acid.**
>
> **A level 10 Jump skill.**
>
> **+5 to the Lotion Application skill.**
>
> **Warning: This is a 12-hour timed item.**

He hops (unable to walk normally in the suit — "Then I guess you gotta act like one") into Prison
Pocket's pouch and detonates two hidden satchels from inside, killing the boss from within — see
[Prison Pocket](../bosses/index.md#floor-10-11-book-8) for the full kill.

## The Skill Itself

Wearing the costume triggers a new personal skill, separate from the item's own listed benefits:

> Or, at least, it worked once I hit level 5 in my new "Fursona" skill.
>
> With each new skill level, the sensation that the suit was my actual body increased, which was
> terrifying.

> **Your Fursona skill is now level 8!**
>
> **Yiffing is unlocked!**

Carl declines to investigate what "Yiffing" does. The skill scales the *immersion* — how much the
worn skin feels like the wearer's own body — rather than granting a stated combat benefit on its
own.

## The Achievement: Weaseler

Killing Prison Pocket while wearing the kangaroo skin, from inside its own pouch, triggers a new
achievement that formalizes the mechanic for the whole party going forward:

> **New Achievement! Weaseler!**
>
> **You've donned the corpse of an enemy in an attempt to fool another creature! And then you
> ENTERED that creature.**
>
> ***Reward:*** **Temporary classes while wearing corpse suits have been unlocked! You will now
> receive benefits and buffs associated with the suit you are wearing. These skills will go away
> once the suit is removed.**
>
> **Current temporary class: Fursuiter.**

This is the actual mechanic: prior to "Weaseler," wearing a corpse-skin costume like the Macropus
Dominus skin presumably granted only its listed item benefits (Jump, Lotion Application, acid
protection). After the achievement unlocks, wearing *any* corpse-suit costume also grants a
**temporary class** — "Fursuiter" in Carl's case — with its own associated benefits and buffs, which
disappear the moment the suit comes off.

## What Fursuiter Grants

Not fully itemized on the page. What's shown: continued access to the suit's own listed benefits
(Jump, Lotion Application, acid protection) plus whatever additional class-level buffs "Fursuiter"
carries, layered on top of the escalating Fursona-skill immersion effect. The book doesn't give a
complete Fursuiter class kit, and this page should not invent one.
