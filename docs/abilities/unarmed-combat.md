# Unarmed Combat

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Starting level:** 3 (from his real-world Coast Guard hand-to-hand training)
    - **Type:** Foundational physical combat skill, not a spell

## Overview

Unarmed Combat is the base hand-to-hand skill already present on Carl's character sheet the
moment he enters the dungeon, a holdover from his real-world background as a Coast Guard MK
(machinery technician) with above-average hand-to-hand training. It's the foundation his entire
bare-knuckle fighting style is built on top of — every other combat skill he picks up
([Pugilism](pugilism.md), [Foot Soldier](foot-soldier.md), [Iron Punch](iron-punch.md),
[Powerful Strike](powerful-strike.md), [Smush](smush.md)) stacks on top of this base rather than
replacing it.

Entering the dungeon with no weapon at all also triggers a dedicated system achievement:

> "So. You just gonna waltz right into something called a 'World Dungeon' and you're not even
> going to bring a weapon? You're either braver than you look, or you're just an idiot. Good
> luck with that, Van Damme."
>
> *Reward: You've received a Bronze Weapon Box!*

## Scaling

The book doesn't give an explicit per-level formula for Unarmed Combat specifically (unlike
[Powerful Strike](powerful-strike.md), which has a stated multiplier). It's described as rising
alongside Carl's other bare-knuckle skills through repeated use — by late Book 1 it's one of
several stacked skills that, combined with gear like the
[War Gauntlet](../items/war-gauntlet-of-the-exalted-grull.md), let his fists hit as hard as an
enchanted weapon.
