# Talon Strike

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Granted by:** The [Enchanted Necklace of the Haute Bourgeoisie](../items/necklace-of-the-haute-bourgeoisie.md)
      (a Poor Sapphire gem tied to the Medium Skyfowl Settlement), not trained independently — Book 3
    - **Type:** Physical melee skill, not a spell
    - **Effect:** Turns a bare-foot kick into a slashing weapon, with a chance to instakill

## Overview

Talon Strike arrives as a +5-level bonus attached to a gem on a leadership necklace Carl loots
from a Platinum Tyrant's Box — a reward tied to his accidental, brief rule over the Medium
Skyfowl Settlement on Floor 4. Its official description leans into the same over-the-top violence
theme as [Smush](smush.md):

> "Birds have ugly feet. Disgusting feet, really. Still, there's something sensual about the
> sight of a hawk swooping down upon its prey, talons out, and slashing... Turns the side of your
> bare foot into a fast-moving slashing weapon, increasing the damage against opponents by up to
> (Level of skill) times your regular kicking damage for (Level of skill) seconds. Each kick
> using this skill has a 2% chance to cast the instakill spell Eviscerate. This melee skill has a
> cooldown of five minutes."

Carl uses it in combat almost immediately, smashing open two downed monsters' heads with quick
foot jabs, and later during a drop-kick against the mob "Gore-Gore" and in the climactic Iron
Tangle train battle alongside [Bang Bro](../spells/bang-bro.md).

## Scaling

Explicitly stated: damage multiplier and effect duration both scale directly with skill level
(level N = N-times kicking damage for N seconds), and the 2% Eviscerate proc chance increases at
higher levels too, per the same description. **At level 15, Talon Strike becomes permanently
active** rather than needing to be triggered — the highest confirmed cap for a Carl skill so far.
