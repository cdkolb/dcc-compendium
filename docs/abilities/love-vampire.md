# Love Vampire

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut
    - **Type:** Passive Charisma-based ability
    - **Unlock condition:** 100 Charisma, reached in Book 3

## Effect

Reflects damage back onto an attacking mob, provided that mob is a lower level than Donut herself.

## Scaling

Not described in detail — the ability is newly unlocked and untriggered as of its introduction, so
no scaling curve has been shown yet.

## Role in the Story

Donut reaches 100 Charisma and unlocks Love Vampire partway through Floor 4, on Carl's earlier
advice to prioritize the stat. She hasn't gotten to use it in practice by the time it's introduced,
since the party has been deliberately keeping her out of situations where she'd take a hit from a
lower-level mob.

## Book 5: Working "At Scale"

During the Butcher's Masquerade's climactic battle, with her Charisma temporarily boosted well
past 300 by a benefactor tiara and other buffs, Donut announces mid-fight that "my love vampire
skill works really good when it's this high" — at that moment, multiple elves in the crowd around
her are shown fighting with little heart icons over their heads, apparently smitten with her
("It doesn't work on the bugs.").
