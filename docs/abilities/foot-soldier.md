# Foot Soldier

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Gained:** Level 3, Book 1
    - **Effect:** Increases damage dealt while kicking

!!! quote "AI Commentary (in-voice, written for this wiki — not a book excerpt)"
    "Foot Soldier: the sensible half of Carl's leg-based murder portfolio. Don't worry, the
    other half gets much, much weirder. See: Smush."

## Overview

Foot Soldier is gained at Level 3, in the same notification wave as [Smush](smush.md), and is
the comparatively normal one of the pair — it simply increases the damage Carl deals with kicks,
without any of Smush's unsettling in-voice flavor text. It rounds out Carl's leg-based offense
alongside [Smush](smush.md)'s bare-foot finishing moves.

## Scaling

The book doesn't give an explicit per-level percentage for Foot Soldier (unlike Smush's stated
10%-per-level pressure increase). It's tracked and leveled alongside Carl's other bare-knuckle
skills — reported at Level 5 by the point he and Donut clear the Scatterer nest near the Hoarder
fight, and Level 6 shortly after [The Juicer](../bosses/the-juicer.md) fight.
