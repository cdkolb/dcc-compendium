# Mind Balance

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** Carl
    - **Source:** The
      [Enchanted Toe Ring of the Well-Balanced](../items/enchanted-toe-ring-of-the-well-balanced.md),
      chapter 4 — left pinky toe
    - **Granted at:** Level 5
    - **Stated effect:** "*Allows protection from Enslavement and Conscription*"
    - **Actual effect:** Also blocks every attempt **Shi Maria** makes to seize Carl's body from
      inside him
    - **Reaches level 16** — **the first skill in the series trained past 15 by battlefield
      conditions rather than potions**

This is the most important defensive skill Carl acquires in Book 7, and for the first ten chapters he
has no idea it is doing anything at all.

## The Grant

> **Mind Balance, Level 5. Allows protection from Enslavement and Conscription.**

Followed immediately, on equipping the ring:

> **Mind Balance is currently triggered.**

"*It didn't give any more information than that.*" Carl reads it, does not understand it, and moves
on. It was triggered because it had already started working.

The stated purpose is Floor 9's defining threat: *[Conscript](../spells/conscript.md)* is how factions
enslave unaffiliated crawlers, and the whole reason Carl bought a Faction Wars team in Book 6 was to
avoid it. The AI's framing on the ring's description — "*this has been given to you to save your ass
because you're too dumb to realize how much danger you're in*" — is about something else entirely.

## The Spam Folder

Chapter 14. Carl digs into the notifications he has been suppressing and finds **hundreds** of entries:

> **Gloom Wraith Phase failed to activate. Mind Balance prevented activation.**
>
> **Your attempt to disable Mind Balance was prevented by Mind Balance.**
>
> **Your Mind Balance has risen to level nine.**

> There were hundreds of these entries, over and over again. **There were pages of me attempting to
> cast something, and each time, it got stopped.**

**Shi Maria** — sealed in the
[Eye of the Bedlam Bride](../items/the-eye-of-the-bedlam-bride.md) tattoo on his chest — has been
trying to use Carl's own spells and skills, continuously, for weeks. Each attempt is logged as *Carl*
attempting to cast, because from the system's point of view it is his body. And each one has been
stopped.

The second line is the one that matters most: **"Your attempt to disable Mind Balance was prevented by
Mind Balance."** She tried to turn the lock off, and the lock stopped her turning it off.

The specific *Gloom Wraith Phase* attempt logged there would have killed the ogre Carl was fighting
and detonated his non-magical bombs in the process, which would have killed Donut.

### The arms race

> The Mind Balance skill came from my new toe ring, though **now it was trained up so much, I'd still
> have the skill, minus five levels, even if I took the ring off. I didn't dare.**

> The spider had managed to speak in my mind, **despite the Mind Balance skill. She was getting
> stronger with each attempt to control me. But so was I. It was a race**... She was constantly
> fighting against the Mind Balance skill.

That is the whole shape of it. Every attempt she makes trains the thing stopping her. She is levelling
his defence by attacking it, and neither of them can stop.

The skill also **outgrows its item**. Once trained above the ring's level-5 grant, Carl retains it at
minus five levels without the ring. He keeps wearing the ring.

## Level 16 — Breaking the Cap

Chapter 65, at the All Tree, under saturation psionic bombardment:

> **Warning: Your Mind Balance is currently overwhelmed as you are getting absolutely hammered with
> psionic debuffs.**
>
> **You've been infected with ten more instances of Endless Thread of Distraction.**
>
> **You've been infected with Self Harm.**
>
> **You've been infected with Intrusive Thoughts.**

Carl is reduced to a babbling idiot with his bare feet buried in an All Tree root. A voice — not Shi
Maria, not Hapanzi, never identified — speaks to him: "*You're family now, after what you did… Good,
Carl. Good. **She would be proud of you.**" Then:

> **Your Mind Balance is now Level 16.**
>
> **New Achievement! You now tread in the realm of gods!**
>
> **You've trained a skill past 15 using battlefield conditions, as opposed to just taking a bunch of
> potions to do it. That's quite the accomplishment. I'd ramble on and on about this, but you're
> currently a babbling idiot with your bare feet buried in an All Tree root. So let's speed up this
> Empire Strikes Back cave scene bullshit and get back to the action.**
>
> **Reward: The skill is the reward. The good news is this level-16 Mind Balance will now wake your
> ass up.**

The All Tree root dies and crumbles when he pulls free.

### What level 16 adds

> **I could now see them. The ones casting mind magic.** That had come with my skill hitting 16.

He immediately starts painting the enemy mind mages for missile strikes. And in the same chapter:

> **Your Mind Balance has negated Bear Witness.**

A level-16 crawler skill negating *[Bear Witness](../spells/bear-witness.md)* — a **level-20 divine
ceremony** by the goddess Eris that freezes every mortal outdoors on the floor — is the clearest
statement of what breaking the cap is worth. Carl is one of a tiny handful of people on Floor 9 who
does not get scooped up.

## Scaling

| Level | Event |
|---|---|
| **5** | Granted by the toe ring. Protection from Enslavement and Conscription |
| **9** | By chapter 14, purely from blocking Shi Maria's attempts |
| **16** | Chapter 65, from battlefield conditions. Grants **visibility of mind-magic casters**; negates a level-20 divine cast |

Above level 5, the skill is retained without the ring at **minus five levels**.

!!! note "Exact-verification note on 'first past 15'"
    The achievement frames training past 15 through combat as an extraordinary feat, and it is the
    first time the *series* shows it. Two other level-16 effects appear later in Book 7, but both come
    from Donut's [Brain Trust](brain-trust.md) natural benefit — a flat +1 to effective spell level —
    rather than from training. Her *Magic Missile* fires at 16 and her *Laundry Day* is described as
    "*level-15, now enhanced to level-16*." Those are a different mechanism.

## Book 8: Still Holding, and a Sibling Break

*Mind Balance* keeps doing its job in Book 8. During a mind-magic barrage, a single line confirms it
is still silently blocking hostile psionic effects exactly as it did in Book 7:

> **Soul Suck has been negated by your Mind Balance skill.**

Separately, Book 8 gives the series its second example of a skill broken past the level-15 training
cap — this time Donut's [Claw](claw.md), pushed to **level 17** by a one-time celestial boon rather
than by battlefield conditions. See that page's "Broken Past 15 by a Celestial Boon" section for the
full scene; the two breaks make an interesting contrast in mechanism (Carl's came from sustained
combat pressure, Donut's from a single divine reward) even though both blow past the same wall.
