# Smush

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Gained:** Level 3, Book 1 (alongside [Foot Soldier](foot-soldier.md))
    - **Effect:** +10% bare-foot crushing pressure per skill level
    - **Notable:** The origin of the System AI's running "foot fetish" joke about Carl

!!! quote "AI Commentary (in-voice, written for this wiki — not a book excerpt)"
    "Look, I don't make the rules. I just really, really enjoy reading them out loud. Especially
    this one. Especially to him."

## Overview

Smush is delivered with noticeably different narration than any other skill Carl receives — the
system's voice turns "deeper, more throaty than usual," described as sounding like someone
"beating himself off while he said it." The official description reads:

> **Smush: Skill Level 3**
>
> "Killing with your feet. Your bare, beautiful feet."
>
> "Taking your bare foot, placing it on top of a living, conscious life, and then pressing
> lovingly down until that life ceases to be. Is there a more noble way to kill?"
>
> "The amount of pressure you can bring to bear upon an enemy with your unshod foot is increased
> by 10% with each level of this skill."

Carl concludes the AI (or whatever controls the game's descriptions) "really did have some sort
of foot fetish." This isn't a one-off joke — it recurs throughout Book 1 in the form of
foot-focused achievements, item flavor text, and narration bits, cementing it as one of the
series' most persistent running gags.

## Scaling

Explicitly stated: **+10% bare-foot crushing pressure per skill level**. It levels alongside
Carl's other bare-knuckle skills through use — reaching Level 5 by the point he clears the
Scatterer nest near the Hoarder fight, and Level 6 shortly after [The
Juicer](../bosses/the-juicer.md) fight. The
[Enchanted Pedicure Kit of the Sylph](../items/index.md) also grants temporary bare-foot combat
buffs that complement it, including an "unbreakable" feet effect that saves Carl's life during
an explosion later in Book 1.
