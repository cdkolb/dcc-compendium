# Hide in Shadows

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Gained:** Book 2, +2 skill level granted by his **Compensated Anarchist** class
    - **Effect:** Stealth/concealment — self-explanatory per Carl, though he immediately notes
      its limits

## Overview

Hide in Shadows is one of several skills bundled into Carl's **Compensated Anarchist** class
card, picked on Floor 3 in Book 2. Carl calls it "self-explanatory" the moment he sees it, but
immediately undercuts its usefulness for his own build: "with Donut and Mongo in tow, I imagined
it wouldn't be too useful" — a stealth-class NPC-partner mismatch the book plays for a quick
laugh rather than a real mechanical problem.

## Scaling

The class card grants a flat **+2 to the Hide in Shadows Skill** on pick. No further per-level
formula is given in the text.
