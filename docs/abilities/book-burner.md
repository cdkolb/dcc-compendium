# Book Burner

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** Carl
    - **Type:** Anti-magic skill — Carl notes that despite being a patch benefit, "*it was really an
      anti-magic skill*"
    - **Source:** The **burning-book upgrade patch** (medium), sewn onto the
      [Enchanted Anarchist's Battle Rattle](../items/enchanted-anarchists-battle-rattle.md). The
      patch also grants **+15% Intelligence**
    - **Uses:** Once per day

## Effect

> **Once per day, you may cause an opponent your level or lower to forget a spell for a period of
> 30 hours. For every opponent player level above your own, this spell has a 5% chance not to work.
> You must know the exact name of the spell or you may choose to target the last spell that was
> cast.**

Two ways to aim it: name the spell exactly, or simply strip whatever the target most recently cast.
The 30-hour duration is deliberately longer than a day, meaning a spell taken away stays away past
the next reset cycle.

Carl's reaction on reading it: "*Whoa... That was pretty cool. Too bad it was only once a day.*"

## Scaling

The scaling is entirely **relative to the opponent's level**, not to any skill level of Carl's:

| Opponent's level | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| At or below Carl's level | Works |
| Above Carl's level | **−5% success chance per level of difference** |

So the ability is reliable against peers and becomes a coin-flip around 10 levels up, worthless
around 20. There is no training path — the patch grants a fixed effect, and the only way to improve
the odds is for Carl to out-level his targets.

## Patch Context

The burning-book patch arrives alongside the [Wand of Nighty-Night](../items/index.md) and a Minor
Find Crawler skill potion in the same haul. The description declines to explain itself:

> **I think this might be a metaphor for knowledge being dangerous. Or maybe it's a metaphor saying
> you should burn books that suck. I don't know. Just because I recently took everything over
> doesn't mean I know what everything means. Most metaphors are bullshit.**

Like all upgrade patches it is **fleeting** — it can be removed, but is destroyed in the process —
and it benefits from the doubling effect of the
[Scavenger's Daughter](../items/the-scavengers-daughter.md) back patch on its stat contribution.
