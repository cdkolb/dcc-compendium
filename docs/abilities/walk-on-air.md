# Walk on Air

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** Carl — **stolen from [Li Jun](../characters/li-jun.md)**
    - **Acquired:** Book 6, chapter 71, when Shi Maria forced Carl to gouge out Li Jun's eye with a
      tattoo stick and eat it. **Carl did not know he had it until Book 7, chapter 4**
    - **Effect:** Literally walk on air, for very short periods
    - **Cooldown:** One minute — though a follow-up jump can be made without it
    - **Li Jun lost it permanently.** His brother had to buy a book to relearn it

This is the first documented transfer in what becomes
[the toraline ink tattoo system](../items/toraline-tattoo-transfer.md), and the party works the entire
mechanic out backward from it.

## "(Already Obtained)"

Carl discovers it by accident, reading the description of his new
[Enchanted Toe Ring of the Well-Balanced](../items/enchanted-toe-ring-of-the-well-balanced.md):

> **Physical Balance, Level 5. At level five, this skill imbues both the Extra Steady Balance skill
> and the Walk on Air Skill (already obtained.)**
>
> "Already obtained?" I asked out loud. That didn't make sense. I moved into my menu. I knew what
> *Walk on Air* did, but I didn't have that skill. **It was what Li Jun…**
>
> I swallowed, suddenly feeling very, very dirty.
>
> **It was what Li Jun used when he fought.**

> The memory of the taste of Li Jun's eye flooded my mouth. I had the sudden urge to vomit. **I
> remembered what Shi Maria had said after she'd plucked the eye out of him. That I might gain some
> of his skills.**

Carl messages Li Jun to apologise and gets no answer.

## What It Does

The only description in the text is Carl's own, in the same chapter:

> The skill worked exactly how it sounded. **It allowed one to literally walk on air for very short
> periods of time, which was especially useful in hand-to-hand combat. It was kung fu movie stuff, and
> it required a lot of practice to get right.**

Which is generous. In use it is considerably less elegant, and Carl says so in chapter 68:

> **The skill was not a flying skill, and it didn't work all that great. It was more of a glorified
> long jump.**
>
> **The Walk on Air had a one minute cooldown, but I could make the next jump without it.**

That last clause is the useful bit of mechanical detail: a *second* jump can be chained immediately,
and only then does the minute run.

## The Rule It Establishes

Rosetta, chapter 47, stating the tattoo system's central rule and citing this exact case as her
evidence:

> **Spells and skills taken against the owner's will were much more powerful and resulted in the owner
> losing them permanently. My Walk on Air skill, stolen from Li Jun, was proof of that.**

The cost fell entirely on Li Jun. He lost the skill permanently, and the eye is unhealable. Chapter
40 notes that "*her brother got Walk on Air back, but they had to buy a book*" — a replacement
purchase, not a recovery.

Compare the consensual version: Li Na donates blood freely for
[Profane Iron](../spells/profane-iron.md), temporarily loses five levels, keeps the ability, and
recovers over four days. Same mechanic, opposite ethics, radically different bill.

## Use

- **Chapter 15** — chained with *[Gloom Wraith Phase](../spells/gloom-wraith-phase.md)*: "*I activated
  Walk on Air and rushed upward, like I was running up a set of stairs*"
- **Chapter 68** — repeated jumps across a gap, which is where its limitations are spelled out
- **Chapter 71** — after the mass bomb drop on Larracos, it is not enough: "*my Walk on Air didn't have
  enough juice to get me to the edge of Shanty Town. I rushed forward through the air, making it about
  ¾'s of the way before I dropped like a bucket of concrete.*" He finishes the trip with
  [Climb](index.md#book-7-skill-catalog)

## Scaling

No level is ever given for Carl's copy. The toe ring's *Physical Balance* would grant it at level 5 if
he did not already have it, which suggests level 5 is a baseline rather than a ceiling, but nothing
confirms what level Carl's stolen version sits at.
