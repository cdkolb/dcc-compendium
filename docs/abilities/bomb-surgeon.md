# Bomb Surgeon

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Gained:** Book 2, +1 skill level granted by his **Compensated Anarchist** class
    - **Effect:** Lets Carl safely disassemble and resize explosives — cut dynamite into smaller,
      less potent charges, or strip smoke bombs down into components for trap-making

!!! quote "AI Commentary (in-voice, written for this wiki — not a book excerpt)"
    "Bomb Surgeon: because sometimes you don't want the *whole* building gone, just the one guy
    inside it who owes you money. Precision. Restraint. Very unlike this man's usual approach."

## Overview

Bomb Surgeon is one of several trap/bomb-making skills bundled into Carl's **Compensated
Anarchist** class card (alongside [Trap Engineer](trap-engineer.md), [Hide in
Shadows](hide-in-shadows.md), [Backfire](backfire.md), and [Escape Plan](escape-plan.md)), which
Carl picks on Floor 3 in Book 2. Even at level one, Carl describes an intuitive, almost tactile
sense for explosives it grants him: "I could feel the depth of the blasting cap in my dynamite
sticks. I could feel how close a piece of dynamite was to prematurely going off without having to
consult the description."

## Scaling

The class card grants a flat **+1 to the Bomb Surgeon Skill** on pick, and the Compensated
Anarchist class separately grants **+5% skill progression speed in all trap-making and
bomb-making skills**, which applies to Bomb Surgeon along with its sibling skills. No further
explicit per-level formula is given in the text beyond the improved sense of an explosive's
internal state described above.
