# Wrecking Ball

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** **Rend**, Carl's Tummy Acher pet
    - **Type:** Innate species special ability. "*The only skill he had*"
    - **Effect:** Increases the user's **mass and speed** during a charge
    - **Species reputation:** "*considered a terror on any battlefield*"
    - **Pairs with:** The
      [Enchanted Tooth Cap of Hi-Yo Silver](../items/enchanted-tooth-cap-of-hi-yo-silver.md), which
      supplies the speed for free
    - **Kill:** **Gustavo 3**, the second of Lucia Mar's two rottweilers

!!! note "Exact-verification note"
    **No system description block exists.** The species entry and the one narrated use below are the
    whole of it.

## The Species Entry

Foreshadowed in chapter 5, in Rend's species description:

> Tummy Achers, also known as Belly Achers, also known as Colon Worms, are named such because they
> were originally parasites, living in the intestinal tracts of Jotan-class Titans. They are generally
> good-natured, though infants can be a little... difficult to control. They are voracious eaters, and
> when they reach full size can swallow just about anything. They have multiple tank-based special
> abilities and are generally resistant to most types of damage. They can survive in almost any
> environment. In addition, **while not necessarily known for their offensive skills, their Wrecking
> Ball attack is considered a terror on any battlefield.**

That is the shape of the creature: a resilient, good-natured, indestructible eating machine with
exactly one offensive option, and the one option is extremely good.

## The Combination

*Wrecking Ball* scales with **mass and speed**, which means its output is limited by how fast the pet
can get moving before it arrives. The
[Enchanted Tooth Cap of Hi-Yo Silver](../items/enchanted-tooth-cap-of-hi-yo-silver.md) solves that: it
yanks Rend toward Carl's position from up to five kilometres away, "*inexorably… at great speed*,"
smashing through any wall in the way.

Carl notes the synergy when Rend receives the cap in chapter 65: "*for Rend, it would work well,
**especially when combined with his new Wrecking Ball special ability**.*"

The targeting method is the clever part. The tooth cap always summons the pet **to the owner**. So to
fire Rend at something, Carl puts himself on the far side of it.

## The Kill — Chapter 72

Carl is being blown off his feet by gale magic, at close range, with Lucia Mar's second rottweiler on
him:

> "**Wrecking Ball!**" I wheezed as I cast *Hi-Yo Silver* a second time. Another gale hit me, and I
> spun head over heels landing on my stomach.
>
> I watched, as if in slow motion, as Rend started rolling toward me. And as he was magically pulled
> in my direction, **he activated the only skill he had. Wrecking Ball. The special attack increased
> his mass and speed, and it did exactly what the name implied. The back half of Gustavo 3, the second
> of Lucia Mar's two rottweilers, terror of the dungeon, exploded in a red mist as Rend rushed right
> up to me, stopping inches from my face.**

Rend stops inches from Carl's face. The dog does not.

## Scaling

No level. The two stated variables are **mass** and **speed**, both increased by the ability on
activation and the second of which can be supplied externally by the tooth cap.
