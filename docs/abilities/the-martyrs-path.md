# The Martyr's Path

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** Carl
    - **Type:** **Floor boon** — granted by Emberus, valid for Floor 8 only, and revocable by the
      god at any time
    - **Granted:** By high priest **Pater Coal**, who washes Carl's feet "in the fires of the dying
      sun" in the Emberus temple at Club Vanquisher: "*Now every step you take will be in Emberus's
      path*"
    - **Requires:** Remaining in Emberus's grace (see
      [Deity worship](../mechanics/index.md#deity-worship-feral-gods-and-the-nothing-book-4))
    - **Bundled with:** +10% Strength

## The Boon

> **The fire of righteousness fills you with power. You have been granted a floor boon. This boon
> will remain in place for the remainder of this floor as long as you remain in Emberus' grace. He
> may revoke this boon at any time.**
>
> **The Martyr's Path. Every step you take may be your last, so make them count. Every step you
> make while you are outside a structure will be marked on the map. If your health reaches 5%, you
> have the option to release a gout of flame from each spot upon which you stood. Steps reset each
> time this is activated. This will activate automatically upon your death.**

The mechanic is a slow-burn trap laid by walking. Every outdoor footstep Carl takes is recorded on
an internal map. At **5% health or below** he may detonate all of them simultaneously, sending a
gout of flame up from every marked spot at once. The step map resets on each activation, and it
fires **automatically if he dies** — which is what makes it a *martyr's* path rather than a
tactical option.

Two constraints matter in play: **outdoors only** (indoor movement is not recorded), and the 5%
health gate means the ability can only ever be used from the edge of death.

## Scaling

No level and no numbers. The ability's power is a function of **distance walked outdoors since the
last activation**, which makes it unusual among Carl's abilities — it scales with patience and
routing rather than with training. Across the back half of Floor 8 Carl covers an enormous amount
of Havana on foot and by motorcycle, so by the climax the marked map is effectively the whole city.

## The Climax (Chapter 70)

Carl uses it once, deliberately, at the worst possible moment by design. His reasoning, laid out in
advance:

> If I wanted my eighth-floor-only Martyr's Path benefit to activate, I would have to be under 5%
> health. It would release flames over all of my outside-of-buildings footsteps. Since my fire
> attacks were currently enhanced with Sheol fire, and since I'd run underneath the demon, it would
> bathe the demon in Sheol fire. It would enhance his power. Hopefully enough.

That is the whole plan stacked in one sentence. The
[Scavenger's Daughter](../items/the-scavengers-daughter.md) patch, reacting to the presence of a
prince of Sheol, had upgraded all of Carl's fire attacks to **Sheol Fire**. So the flame released
from thousands of footprints across Havana is not fire — it is hellfire. And Carl had been careful
to route himself directly beneath **Amayon**.

> **The Martyr's Path has engulfed your steps.**

The result bathes a city and a level-250 demon prince in Sheol Fire simultaneously — which, per the
quest terms, is exactly what Amayon needed to gather the strength to cast his way home.

## Status

The boon is explicitly **floor-limited**. It does not carry past Floor 8.
