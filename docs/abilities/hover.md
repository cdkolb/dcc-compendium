# Hover

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** Princess Donut
    - **Type:** Item-granted movement skill
    - **Source:** The
      [Enchanted Tiara of the Inebriated Dragonfly](../items/enchanted-tiara-of-the-inebriated-dragonfly.md)
    - **Level:** 10 (fixed by the item)
    - **Uses:** 5 per day
    - **Duration:** ~90 seconds
    - **Height:** 2–3 feet
    - **Cooldown:** 5 minutes

## Effect

Donut hovers two to three feet off the ground and can move freely while doing so — Carl's
description of her is "*literally walking on air, tail swishing.*"

Two properties separate it from the *Levitate* spell:

1. **It is not negated by taking damage.** Donut is struck hard enough by a flying bicycle to lose
   health and simply stays airborne.
2. **It arrests a fall.** Activated mid-drop, it sets the user gently on the ground — which, as Carl
   notes, means both he and Donut now have fall protection.

Carl's honest assessment: "*It was like a dollar store version of what Elle had.*"

## Scaling

The skill is locked at **level 10** by the tiara and has no training path — losing the tiara loses
the skill (and 10 Intelligence with it), which is the main argument against Donut wearing any of her
other several hundred hats.

The real constraint is the **five-a-day** limit, which becomes a running gag and a recurring
logistical problem. Donut burns hovers on practice ("*I'm practicing, Carl. If I don't practice, how
will I know how to use it?*"), on showing off to other crawlers mid-mission, and on not walking.
Carl spends much of Floor 8 telling her to stop wasting them, and at one point has to physically
restrain himself from chiding her for burning one purely to demonstrate the skill to Tran's squad.

## Role in the Story

Her reaction on first equipping the tiara sets the tone for the rest of the book:

> "I can fly now. Carl, I can fly! I'm like Wonder Woman!"
>
> "Wonder Woman can't fly," I said.

Beyond the comedy, it gives Donut independent vertical mobility for the first time — she considers
using it to scale a climb rather than ride Carl's shoulder, and it keeps her airborne and out of
reach during the FOCSA street fight, where a bicycle passes straight through the space she'd have
been standing in.
