# Brain Trust

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** Princess Donut
    - **Type:** **Natural benefit**, selected when her **Intelligence topped 100** (chapter 77)
    - **Effect:** **All her spells operate one full level higher than their trained level**
    - **Consequence:** Her level-15 *Magic Missile* fires at **effective level 16** — and vaporizes
      its targets
    - **Chosen for exactly that reason**

The quietest of Donut's four natural benefits and comfortably the most consequential, because it
applies to everything she owns at once and pushes her flagship spell past a number the series had
treated as a hard ceiling.

!!! note "Exact-verification note"
    **No system description block exists.** The two quotes below are the whole of it.

## The Choice

Donut is unambiguous about why she picks it:

> **FOR INTELLIGENCE I PICK BRAIN TRUST SO I CAN MAKE MAGIC MISSILE EVEN MORE POWERFUL.**

## The Effect

Chapter 80, as a Reaver sentry dog ceases to exist:

> This was the effect of Donut's **Brain Trust** upgrade. **It made all spells operate at one full
> level higher than before. Not a huge deal, except her Magic Missile was now 15, meaning she was
> firing these things at level 16. Apparently that evaporated the targets completely.**

The target "*literally evaporated, having been turned into a rapidly expanding group of atoms.*"

"*Not a huge deal*" is Carl underselling it. A +1 to every spell is marginal at low levels and
transformative at the top of the range, because 15 has been the practical maximum for the entire
series. *Brain Trust* is the only thing that lets a crawler cast past it as a matter of routine.

The other confirmed beneficiary is *[Laundry Day](../spells/laundry-day.md)*, described in chapter 86
as "*level-15, now enhanced to level-16*" — which is the cast that rips Huanxin Jinx out of a
goddess's soul armor.

## Level 16 and the Cap

Book 7 contains two distinct routes past level 15, and they are worth keeping apart:

| Route | Who | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **Training** | Carl's [Mind Balance](mind-balance.md) | Genuinely trained from 15 to 16 by battlefield conditions. Earns the achievement "*You now tread in the realm of gods!*" |
| **Brain Trust** | Donut | A flat **+1 effective level** applied on top of the trained level. The underlying spell is still 15 |

Carl's skill *is* 16. Donut's spells *cast as if* 16. The difference matters for anything that reads
the trained value — most obviously *[War Crime](../spells/war-crime.md)*, whose components must be at
least level 8, and which explicitly ignores anything above that.

## Scaling

No level of its own. It is a flat, permanent, universal **+1 effective spell level**, applying to
Donut's entire arsenal — item-granted spells included, on the evidence of *Laundry Day*.
