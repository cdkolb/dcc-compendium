# Pied Piper

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** Carl
    - **Granted by:** The god **Harpocrates**, chapter 70, via the
      [Scavenger's Daughter](../items/the-scavengers-daughter.md)'s deity-proximity mechanic
    - **Level:** **15**
    - **Made permanent:** Chapter 76, by a **coin flip** from the goddess **Eris**
    - **What it actually does:** Lets you **play musical instruments**
    - **"It's not like the fairy tale and has nothing to do with rodents"**

The most anticlimactic permanent ability in the series, acquired by the most dramatic means available.

## The Grant

Chapter 70. Carl's back patch opens her eyes near a god:

> **You are in the presence of a deity. The Scavenger's Daughter has opened her eyes.**
>
> **You are now within range of the god Harpocrates.**
>
> **Temporary effect from Harpocrates:**
>
> **Level 15 Pied Piper skill.**

Standard Scavenger's Daughter behaviour: a random, temporary, god-specific effect that fades when the
deity leaves range.

## The Coin Flip

Chapter 76. Eris — goddess of chaos, currently rolled up inside a screaming ball of tens of thousands
of people thanks to Donut's
*[Rolling Battle Formation](../spells/rolling-battle-formation.md)* — comes into range:

> **You are in range of Eris.**
>
> **Random Effect! As Eris is the goddess of chaos, this effect will change each time you are in her
> presence.**
>
> **Temporary effect from Eris:**
>
> **One current god effect will be permanent!**

Carl has exactly two active god effects at that moment. A spinner appears in front of him with two
entries on it:

> **Dick will fall off!**
>
> **Level-15 Pied Piper!**

The first is from the feral god **Meatus**, whose chapter-76 contribution was "*Your dick will fall
off if you get an erection.*"

> It stopped on **Dick will fall off!** hovered for a second, then ticked over to **Level-15 Pied
> Piper!**
>
> **Your benefit from Harpocrates is permanent!**

A 50/50 spin, landing on the wrong side first, then moving. Carl permanently acquires the ability to
play musical instruments instead of permanently acquiring a genital curse.

!!! note "He gets the other one anyway"
    The Meatus effect is separately made permanent in chapter 77, when the god "*hasn't exactly left
    the realm. He's now slap-chopped all over the realm*" and the system announces "*the benefit
    fades, **though you now have it permanently**, which, I gotta tell you, ain't all that exciting.*"
    Carl does not win the coin flip so much as defer one half of it.

## What It Does

Chapter 79, Carl explaining to Donut, who has ideas:

> "I didn't get to keep *Pied Piper* until after that had happened, and **the skill allows me to play
> musical instruments. It's not like the fairy tale and has nothing to do with rodents.**"

Donut had suggested combining it with her Oak Fell spell *Gather Ye, Vermin*, which draws all small
mammals within ten kilometres to her location. The combination would, in fairness, be a fairly
accurate reconstruction of the fairy tale. Carl declines. His bagpipes remain in the
"probably-never-going-to-need-this-stuff" folder.

At **level 15** — the practical maximum — Carl is now among the best instrumentalists in the dungeon,
and has no use for it whatsoever.

## Scaling

Fixed at **level 15** by the grant. No progression described.
