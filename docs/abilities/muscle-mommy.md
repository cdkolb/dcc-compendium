# Muscle Mommy

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** Princess Donut
    - **Type:** **Natural benefit**, selected when her **Strength topped 100** (chapter 77)
    - **Effect:** Boosts **Mongo's** Strength by **25% of Donut's own**
    - **Chosen over:** The "Swole" benefit, on explicitly aesthetic grounds
    - **Mordecai has reservations about the name**

Donut reaches 100 Strength and 100 Intelligence in the same chapter and is offered a natural-benefit
choice for each. She picks [Brain Trust](brain-trust.md) for Intelligence and this for Strength.

!!! note "Exact-verification note"
    **No system description block exists.** Everything below is party chat and narration.

## The Choice

Mordecai sets it up: "*You'll have a few choices you need to make for both your intelligence and
strength now that they're both over 100.*"

Donut's reasoning, in her usual register:

> **Donut: GOODNESS, I'M NOT GOING TO PICK THE SWOLE BENEFIT. DO YOU REMEMBER WHAT IT DID TO CARL?
> CAN YOU IMAGINE? I'M GOING TO PICK MUSCLE MOMMY FOR STRENGTH SO MONGO IS STRONGER...**
>
> **Mordecai: Uh, are you sure about that Muscle Mommy benefit?**

Mordecai's hesitation is left hanging and never explained. He does not object to the effect; he
objects, apparently, to the name — and Donut does not notice.

The decision itself is entirely in character: offered a personal Strength benefit, she immediately
converts it into a Mongo buff.

## The Effect

Donut's own statement:

> "with my new **Muscle Mommy** benefit, **his strength is boosted by 25% of my own strength!**"

So the benefit is a **transfer**, not a share: a quarter of Donut's Strength is added on top of
Mongo's. As she trains Strength further, Mongo scales with her automatically.

Confirmed visually two chapters later: "*Mongo was decidedly more buff with the Muscle Mommy
benefit.*"

## Scaling

No level. It is a flat **25% of the owner's Strength**, applied to the pet, and therefore scales for
as long as Donut's own Strength keeps climbing.
