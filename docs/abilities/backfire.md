# Backfire

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Gained:** Book 2, +5 skill level granted by his **Compensated Anarchist** class
    - **Effect:** Lets Carl pick up an existing trap and deconstruct it, recovering its
      materials — including valuable triggering mechanisms — for his own use
    - **Notable:** Called out by Mordecai as a genuinely rare skill

!!! quote "AI Commentary (in-voice, written for this wiki — not a book excerpt)"
    "Backfire: turning other people's murder machines into your own personal hardware store.
    One crawler's fatal trap is another crawler's Tuesday side hustle."

## Overview

Backfire is one of several skills bundled into Carl's **Compensated Anarchist** class card,
picked on Floor 3 in Book 2. Mordecai singles it out as unusually valuable: "It allows you to
pick up a trap and deconstruct it. You'll be able to use the materials for your own purpose. The
triggering mechanisms alone are quite valuable. You could make a living just by farming traps."

## Scaling

The class card grants a flat **+5 to the Backfire Skill** on pick — the largest single starting
bonus among Carl's new Compensated Anarchist skills. The class also grants **+5% skill
progression speed in all trap-making and bomb-making skills**, applying here. No further
per-level formula is given in the text.
