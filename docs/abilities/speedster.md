# Speedster

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** Carl
    - **Source:** The
      [Enchanted Hairbrush of the Beefmaster](../items/enchanted-hairbrush-of-the-beefmaster.md),
      chapter 56 — active for **30 hours** per use
    - **Effect:** Move **short distances** extremely quickly
    - **Best feature:** Multiplies the distance he can cover while phased with
      *[Gloom Wraith Phase](../spells/gloom-wraith-phase.md)*
    - **Rarity:** Mordecai had **never seen it awarded by an item.** It is normally a rare natural
      unlock at Dexterity 100

!!! note "Exact-verification note"
    **No system description block exists for the benefit itself** — only the hairbrush's item block
    listing it. The explanation below is Carl's narration.

## What It Does

> The benefit was **similar to Donut's *Twinkle Toes* spell, though instead of casting it on Mongo, it
> worked on myself**. I could already run super-fast thanks to my strength and dexterity, but
> **Speedster basically allowed me to move short distances very, very quickly**. I wouldn't be quite
> like the Flash superhero, but I was still impressively quick. **And while I couldn't do it long
> distance, I could flash across the battlefield in seconds. And best of all, I could combine it with
> my Gloom Wraith Phase, allowing the distance I could travel while phased to be much further.**

The constraint is important and Carl states it twice: **short distances only.** This is not a travel
skill. It is a repositioning skill, for crossing a battlefield in bursts.

## The Combination

The real value is stacking it with *[Gloom Wraith Phase](../spells/gloom-wraith-phase.md)*, Carl's
Celestial non-corporeal lunge. That skill's range is a function of how far he can move during the
phased window, and *Speedster* extends exactly that.

At level 10, *Gloom Wraith Phase* lets him hold the phased state for **five seconds**. Five seconds
of *Speedster* movement, passing through unenchanted walls and flesh, is a considerably longer
distance than five seconds of running.

## Rarity

Mordecai's reaction is the tell:

> Mordecai said he'd **never seen the Speedster benefit awarded like this before**. It was usually
> something someone could sometimes get when their **dexterity naturally reached 100**, though even
> then, **it was rare**. The crawler **Osvaldo**, who was not a member of the Princess Posse,
> supposedly had the benefit.

So *Speedster* is normally a natural benefit — the same category as Donut's
[Muscle Mommy](muscle-mommy.md) and [Brain Trust](brain-trust.md) — and not a guaranteed one even at
the threshold. Getting it from a hairbrush is anomalous.

## The Catch

It is not permanent. Every use of the hairbrush buys **30 hours**, and the hairbrush can only be used
**once per day**, on **one person**, for **five continuous minutes**, and only by someone with at
least **25 cm of hair**.

Which means *Speedster* can in practice be kept running indefinitely — thirty hours of benefit on a
twenty-four-hour cooldown leaves six hours of slack — provided Carl performs a five-minute screaming
hair-brushing ritual every day of a war and never cuts his hair.
