# Pugilism

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **First seen:** Level 4, early Book 1
    - **Official description:** "The art of beating the shit out of your opponents with closed fists."

!!! quote "AI Commentary (in-voice, written for this wiki — not a book excerpt)"
    "Pugilism: for when 'punching' needs a fancier word so it sounds like a skill and not just
    a bar fight. Same result either way — somebody's teeth end up on the floor."

## Overview

Pugilism is one of the earliest named combat skills Carl gains, first reported at Level 4 via a
system notification during his Level 1 fights. It's the closed-fist counterpart to skills like
[Foot Soldier](foot-soldier.md) (kicking) and [Smush](smush.md) (bare-foot finishers) — together
these make up the core of Carl's refusal to carry a real weapon.

## Scaling

No explicit per-level formula is given in the text (unlike [Powerful
Strike](powerful-strike.md)'s stated damage multiplier). It's tracked alongside Carl's other
bare-knuckle skills and rises through repeated use — reaching **Level 10** by the back half of
Book 1, at which point the system explicitly calls out the milestone: "Your Pugilism Skill has
been increased by 3! Your Pugilism Skill is now level 10." Combined with
[Iron Punch](iron-punch.md), [Powerful Strike](powerful-strike.md), and his base
[Unarmed Combat](unarmed-combat.md), it's cited as the reason his fists eventually pack "just as
much damage" as Brandon's lightning maul.
