# Find Weakened

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** Princess Donut
    - **Source:** The
      [Cloak of the Benevolent Champion](../items/cloak-of-the-benevolent-champion.md), Book 7
      chapter 3
    - **Granted at:** Level 5
    - **Stated effect:** "*So you'll always know who to protect first*"
    - **Actual use, per Carl's grim aside:** Quan Ch, the item's previous owner, used it "*for the
      exact opposite of its intended purpose. He'd used it to find and pick off the weakest
      crawlers in a group.*"

## Effect

Among three or more creatures of a similar type, Find Weakened flags whichever one is closest to
death. Framed by the AI as a healer's tool — triage at a glance — so a caster always knows who
needs help first.

## Scaling

No stated per-level progression; granted flatly at level 5 by the cloak, and not separately trained
in Book 7.
