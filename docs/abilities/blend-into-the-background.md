# Blend into the Background and Cone of Silence

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** Princess Donut
    - **Source:** The cat-sized **ghillie suit** from her **Deathbed Hellcat** class accessory kit
      (chapter 1)
    - **Slot:** The suit occupies a **jacket** slot despite magically covering her entire body
    - **Set partner:** The boonie hat that grants [Targeting](targeting.md)
    - **Donut calls it a "ghoulie suit"** for the entire book and is not corrected successfully

!!! warning "Both skills are named and neither is described"
    This is the honest state of the text. Carl explicitly says he has not had time to examine *Blend
    into the Background*, and never comes back to it. *Cone of Silence* is named once and never
    mentioned again. **Do not infer mechanics for either from the names.**

## The Only Description

> I returned my attention to the small, cat-sized ghillie suit. It came with a few interesting
> benefits, though **she would indeed look absolutely ridiculous in it**. The thing looked to be made
> of **long, shredded bits of color-changing fabric that blended into the background**. It took up a
> "jacket" slot on in her inventory even though it magically covered her entire body once she wore
> it. **The suit added a skill called Blend into the Background, which I hadn't yet had the time to
> examine, and a Cone of Silence benefit.** Combined with invisibility, it would be great.

"*Combined with invisibility*" refers to the Deathbed Hellcat class's at-will invisibility. The suit
is meant to stack with it, not replace it.

## The Set Bonus

Worn with the matching boonie hat:

> If she wore both the suit and hat at the same time, all the benefits were buffed even further. She'd
> be able to snipe line-of-sight enemies from well over a mile away.

## Slot Compatibility

A minor but genuinely useful ruling from chapter 1, given Donut's expanding wardrobe:

> "Your crupper is a **waist** accessory, the cloak is a **shoulder** accessory, and the ghillie suit
> is a **jacket**. **You can wear all three at the same time.**"

So the suit,
[Fae Scale Crupper](../items/fae-scale-crupper-of-the-fleet.md) and the
[Cloak of the Benevolent Champion](../items/cloak-of-the-benevolent-champion.md) coexist.

## Use

Donut wears the suit for the sniping work: during the Harpocrates engagement (chapter 70) and in the
Nest tower (chapter 73). It comes off in chapter 74 so Edgar can shave her flank for the
[Bijanbi](../spells/bijanbi.md) tattoo.

The book's best joke at the suit's expense is the chapter-73 image of Donut in the top level of the
tower, wearing the most thoroughly camouflaged garment she owns, with the
[Tiara of a Thousand Lights](../items/tiara-of-a-thousand-lights.md) glowing "*like a lighthouse
beacon*" on her head:

> I finally rushed into the uppermost level to see Donut atop her seat, dressed in her ghillie suit
> leaning against the parapet, her face pressed against the invisible shield like it was glass. **The
> camouflage suit was a strange contrast to her tiara which glowed like a lighthouse beacon.**
