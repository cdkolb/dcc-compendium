# Abilities & Skills

Named physical combat skills tracked on a crawler's skill sheet — distinct from
[Spells](../spells/index.md), which are magic learned from tomes/scrolls and cost mana. Full
pages exist for skills with real plot weight or scaling detail; everything else is covered on
the [Game Mechanics: Skills](../mechanics/index.md#skills) overview.

Any skill named in a full-page character's Quick Facts box must itself have a full page here,
not just a mention in prose, matching the standard already established for
[Spells](../spells/index.md) and [Items & Tattoos](../items/index.md).

## Book 8 skill catalog

Floors 10–11, "Don't Come in Last" and "A Parade of Horribles." Skills with full pages are listed
above.

| Name | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cockroach | Princess Donut | Revealed this book to be a skill Donut personally possesses ("*he doesn't trust my Cockroach skill*"), not just Shi Maria's (see the Book 6 catalog row below). The book never shows how or when Donut acquired it — it's referenced as an established fact, not narrated as a new acquisition — though the existing [Toraline Ink Tattoo System](../items/toraline-tattoo-transfer.md) is the obvious candidate mechanism. A later class option (Elite Gurkha Warrior, not confirmed taken) would have boosted it to level 15 |
| Razzmatazz | Carl | A performance skill granted by **Porky** (the left half of the split mantaur Corcunda, running the Floor 10 stage-performance guild) as a reward for freeing his suit's stuck zipper: "*I will throw a few free skill levels onto your Razzmatazz skill.*" See [Corcunda](../characters/corcunda.md) for Porky's full story |
| Sticky Feet | Carl | Reused twice this book — bracing against a moving vehicle roof while throwing Biscuit Packs at flying insects, and again to keep footing during a floor-collapse tremor. No new mechanics shown; see the Book 6 catalog row for the original grant |

## Book 6 skill catalog

Floor 8, "The Ghosts of Earth." Skills with full pages are listed above.

| Name | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Climb | Carl | Granted by the [Scavenger's Daughter](../items/the-scavengers-daughter.md). The system's own review: "*You can climb stuff better. Ya-hoo. What an **exciting** benefit. I guess that makes you a monkey.*" |
| Find Crawler | Carl, **level 5** | From a "Minor Find Crawler Skill" potion in a Gold Scab box. The potion refuses to work on anyone who already has the skill at level 5 or above. Its blind spots matter: it cannot locate Sister Ines, which is what makes the *Eye in the Sky* scrolls valuable |
| Wand casting | Carl, **level 5** | Raised to 5 as a quest reward, which also came with a Gold Wand Box |
| Find Traps | Carl, **→ 13** | A +3 upgrade potion pushes it to 13 — enough to see "pretty much any trap in the dungeon unless it was placed and deliberately hidden by a trapmaster" |
| [Smush](smush.md) | Carl, **base 12 / 14 effective** | Base level reaches 12. The Enchanted Pedicure Kit of the Sylph supposedly adds three more, but it still displays as 14 — "*Like always, the skills got wonky when items buffs attempted to push them toward 15*" |
| Motorcycle riding | Carl, **level 9** | Accumulated from the Book 1 goblin copper chopper and then pushed to 9 during his long solo ride across Florida on his father's Harley |
| Automaton Pilot | Donut, **level 5** | Earned during the Floor 6 hunter battles. Also lets her affix a limited set of traps to the backs of the small automatons |
| Trap skill | Donut, **level 3** | Too low for Carl's two-part traps. She can program a **Loop-De-Loop** (position swap) but only to trigger on fellow crawlers — she narrows it further to non-party crawlers — and because the skill is so low, a mob, NPC, or party member touching the plate makes it **misfire** immediately |
| Sticky Feet | Carl | Plants a foot immovably against a surface; used to haul Mongo back up a collapsing, tilting barn floor |
| Turtle | **Paz Lo** | An *ability*, not a spell: a temporary, powerful shield while the caster draws aggro. It also **roots him in place and prevents him attacking or moving**, and once cast it cannot be stopped until the timer runs out. If the shield breaks early he's freed, but still eats the excess damage of the attack that broke it |
| Cosmic Sense | **Astrid** | Passive. Like Carl's deity-presence warning, but it detects **both gods and demons** — useful for the head of security at a giant nightclub, and the reason she survives the first phase of Carl's ambush |
| Summon Guard | **Astrid** | Five times a day, instantaneously teleports "between one to three guards" from the club to her location. **No cooldown**, so she can fill a hallway with up to 15 guards at the snap of a finger |
| Cockroach | **Shi Maria**, level 15 | A full heal on what would have been a fatal blow. As a T'Ghee card it works differently: when she "dies," the card returns to the **deck** rather than the discard pile |
| Splitter | **Skylar Spinach** (an enraged ghommid) | Each split iteration is stronger than the last. Splitting a *combined* card returns upgraded "**Gold Edition**" halves |
