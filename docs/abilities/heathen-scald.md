# Heathen Scald

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** Carl
    - **Type:** **Celestial Boon** — a passive damage add-on, not a cast spell (no mana cost, no
      cast action; it simply applies)
    - **Granted:** Chapter 51, as the Reward for completing the church quest **Kill the Blasphemer**
      during the assault on Club Vanquisher — the AI frames it explicitly as "**a Celestial Boon**"
      from Emberus
    - **Level:** **15**, flat, on receipt — not trained up to it
    - **Duration:** **Floor-limited** — "for the remainder of this floor"
    - **Also referred to as:** The "**Heathen Burn**" benefit (chapter 57) — the visible symptom is
      Carl's hands glowing

## The Grant

Immediately after Mork the ram-cultist's death completes the quest, and immediately after ten
seconds of Emberus's own unhinged laughter over the comms:

> **Reward: You have received a Celestial Boon!**
>
> **You have been blessed by your deity, Emberus. You have received a Celestial Boon!**
>
> **Level 15 Heathen Scald Received.**
>
> **For the remainder of this floor, any punch attack will add the Heathen Scald skill damage.**

Carl's own reaction is a non-reaction: "*I didn't have time to look that one up. All around, new
things were happening*" — the grant lands in the middle of the room full of trophy heads coming to
life, and he never circles back to examine it properly on-page.

## Why This Is an Ability, Not a Spell

The wiki splits Carl's Quick Facts spell list by the standing convention: mana-cost, cast-and-
targeted effects go under [Spells](../spells/index.md); passive or automatically-triggered combat
effects with no mana cost go here. *Heathen Scald* fits the ability side cleanly — it is never
"cast," has no mana cost, and simply adds bonus damage to an existing physical action (a punch)
automatically for the rest of the floor, the same shape as Carl's other passive strike-boosting
skills like [Powerful Strike](powerful-strike.md) and [Iron Punch](iron-punch.md).

## What It Does

The system gives no further breakdown of the "skill damage" figure it adds — no number, no
percentage, no combat log ever quotes a bonus-damage value directly attributable to it. The only
other confirmation in the text is indirect: a chapter-57 aside, while Li Jun needles Carl about his
haul from the raid, that his hands are visibly glowing —

> "*I shrugged, suddenly uncomfortable. The glow came from the Heathen Burn benefit I had for the
> rest of the floor.*"

— which is the same boon under an informal name, still active six chapters later, still tied to
"the rest of the floor" as originally stated.

## Scaling

There is effectively nothing to scale: *Heathen Scald* is granted directly at a flat **level 15**
as a one-time Celestial reward rather than trained up through use, the way Carl's other skills are.
No lower-level version is ever shown, no formula or per-level effect is given, and the text never
revisits it with a specific damage number. It is not an example of training past the level-15 cap
(contrast [Mind Balance](mind-balance.md), which the wiki documents as actually broken past that
cap by sustained battlefield conditions) — this is simply a boon issued at the cap and left there.

## Status

Floor-limited by its own grant text ("for the remainder of this floor"). It is never mentioned
again after chapter 57, and does not appear anywhere in Book 8 — consistent with expiring at the
Floor 8-to-9 transition along with Carl's other Club Vanquisher floor boon,
[The Martyr's Path](the-martyrs-path.md).
