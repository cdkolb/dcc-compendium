# Drek

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Level:** 6
    - **Floor:** 4 ("The Iron Tangle")
    - **Appearance:** Fat, wrinkly, grey-skinned, knee-height, demonic-baby-like creatures
    - **Behavior:** Travel in packs of 50+; can climb walls/pillars like spiders

## Official Description

Per the in-universe monster card:

> "Everybody loves babies, right? What kind of asshole doesn't love babies? How about demonic,
> ravenous, berserking babies who travel in packs of at least 50? It's rumored these lil'
> rascals can devour a full-sized elephant down to the bone in less than five seconds. And
> you're a lot smaller than an elephant."

## Behavior

Dreks are the Iron Tangle's most immediate, entry-level threat — a swarming horde mob that packs
train station platforms and surges as one toward anything visible through a train's windows.
Individually weak (Level 6, easily one-shot), their danger is entirely in numbers and their
willingness to climb walls, pillars, and ceilings like spiders to reach prey. One latches onto
Carl's bare foot during his first encounter with them, biting down hard enough to cause real
pain before he stomps it — exploding "like a tomato soup-filled balloon."

## Role in the Story

Dreks are the first monster Carl's party encounters upon arriving on Floor 4, immediately
establishing the danger of the Iron Tangle's train-station platforms and forcing the party into
an improvised defensive retreat through the train's cars.
