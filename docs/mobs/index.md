# Mobs

Regular (non-boss) monster types encountered across the series. Full pages exist for mobs with
real recurrence or plot weight in Book 1; everything else is listed in the table below.

## Full pages

- [Brindle Grub](brindle-grub.md) — Floor 2's corpse-eating "janitor" mob, matures into Brindled Vespas
- [Tuskling](tuskling.md) — Orcish Supremacy ruling class; forms the Ball of Swine
- [Rage Elemental](rage-elemental.md) — Level 93 elite, bathroom-violation punishment mob
- [Street Urchin](street-urchin.md) — Floor 3's corpse-cleanup "janitor" mob
- [Krasue](krasue.md) — undead vampire-women manufactured by Miss Quill
- [Shambling Berserker](shambling-berserker.md) — undead body-part pile, quadrupled power in darkness
- [Remex](remex.md) — Miss Quill's undead husband, a Soul Leech Capacitor
- [Drek](drek.md) — Floor 4's swarming, demonic-baby horde mob
- [Jikininki](jikininki.md) — Floor 4's ghoul "janitor" mob, first tier of the Iron Tangle's corpse-disposal system
- [Frenzied Gerbil](frenzied-gerbil.md) — Level 11 swarm mob; the System AI's recurring in-joke, from [Ralph](../bosses/ralph.md) through Floor 5's gnomish "Live Ammo Balls"
- [Funeral Bell](funeral-bell.md) — Floor 6's mushroom-headed town guard, trap-deactivating and faction-agnostic
- [Night Weasel](night-weasel.md) — Floor 6's invisible, paralysis-biting pack hunter
- [Flesher](flesher.md) — animated corpse-skin, stage one of the war mage cycle; weaponised at scale
  on Floor 9
- [War Mage](war-mage.md) — Level 100, a **true dungeon-born entity** nobody designed; the Floor 9
  wild card
- [Zarogoth](zarogoth.md) — Level 50 soul-generated "sludge ghoul," the swarm mob of the Peeling
  Phase
- [Hunter Wraith](hunter-wraith.md) — Floor 10–11 possession monster carrying **the Inevitable
  Ruin** curse, the book's title
- [Bijou](bijou.md) — tiny fairies that physically carry worship-tendrils between gods and their
  adherents
- [The Horribles](the-horribles.md) — Floor 11's masked, silent parade avatars; revealed as literal
  fragments of the system AI's own lost memory

## Book 6 mob catalog

Floor 8's mobs are grouped by **region** rather than by area, because the floor is a patchwork of
recreated Earth locations and crawlers are scattered across all of them. Most of the entries below
also double as **T'Ghee totem cards** — Floor 8 runs on card combat, so a mob's real significance is
often what it does once it has been flagged rather than how it fights.

hardware** rather than wandering dungeon creatures. The five with real plot weight —

[Hunter Wraith](hunter-wraith.md) and [Bijou](bijou.md) — have their own pages above.
