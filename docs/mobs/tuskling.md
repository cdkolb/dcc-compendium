## Official Description

Per the in-universe monster card for a Tuskling Knight:

> "When it comes to the Tuskling, their titles are about as accurate as their dating website
> photos. The once-mighty warriors conquered the Orcish Supremacy hundreds of years ago,
> defeating all the other clans. They are now shells of the mighty warriors they once were...
> Knights and their slightly-more dangerous female counterparts, Tuskling Courtesans, are only
> worthy adversaries when they come together to form an ancient, Tuskling battle formation known
> as a 'Ball of Swine.'"

## Appearance & Behavior

Short (roughly 4.5 feet), wide-bodied, warthog-headed humanoids, pink-skinned, with four tusks
per individual. Individually weak and largely comedic — drunk, overdressed in formal
tuxedos/gowns, and generally more dangerous to themselves than to crawlers — but their mass
battle formation, the Ball of Swine, is a genuine borough-tier threat. Variants include
**Tuskling Courtesans** (female, "slightly more dangerous") and at least one higher-level
**Tuskling Dominatrix** (Level 8). Mordecai confirms Tusklings, like the Ball of Swine
formation itself, are one of the "real" alien species used by the dungeon rather than a
human-actor-driven caricature.
