# Shambling Berserker

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Level:** 12
    - **Floor:** 3 (Over City ruins), but also confirmed to exist on Floor 1
    - **Aliases:** "Mini Grinder," "the Shrilling"
    - **Danger:** Individually weak in daylight; power quadruples in darkness

## Official Description

Per the in-universe monster card:

> "If you weren't fortunate enough to face one of these neighborhood boss monsters on the first
> level of the dungeon, fret not! Now's your chance to get in on the fun!... What you see here is
> a Shambling Berserker, the smallest iteration of this creature. Also known as the Mini Grinder
> or the Shrilling, this creature consists of extra parts we found after creating the World
> Dungeon. Waste not, want not... This undead abomination is oftentimes found in groups, summoned
> as a slow, but very tenacious assassin. The good news is, they're mostly harmless. Unless, of
> course, you face one in the dark. Their power is quadrupled in the dark. And once they go
> berserk, there's no putting them down."

## Appearance & Behavior

A shambling, thirteen-foot pile of mismatched human body parts — arms, legs, torsos, and
multiple groaning heads — fused together into a single slow-moving, slug-shaped mass, with
tentacle-like limbs waving above it. In daylight (or any bright light source) they're slow and
fragile, falling apart after a single solid hit. In darkness they become vastly more dangerous
and relentless.

## Notable Appearance

A group of Shambling Berserkers ambush Carl and Donut in full darkness outside an abandoned
Over City pool, summoned specifically by [Miss Quill](../bosses/miss-quill.md) as a stalling
tactic. Carl, unable to see them, badly misjudges a stick of dynamite that bounces back toward
him; he ends up falling into and detonating the rotting corpse of a dead boss monster (**The
Divider**) in the resulting chaos, which does more damage to him than the berserkers themselves.
