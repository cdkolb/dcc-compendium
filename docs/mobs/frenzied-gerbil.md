# Frenzied Gerbil

Level 11, and one of the System AI's recurring private jokes. [Ralph](../bosses/ralph.md), the
Floor 2 boss Carl and Donut kill by stomping him underfoot in a kobold fighting pit, is a Frenzied
Gerbil — and the AI has never gotten over how much it enjoyed watching Carl squish him.

The joke resurfaces on Floor 5: dirigible gnome snipers carry bandoliers of **Live Ammo Balls**,
clamshell containers that store a live creature in stasis until impact, fired as a ranged weapon
or point-defense munition. After the *Wasteland* is shot down, Carl and his party loot several
intact balls off gnome corpses — and every single one turns out to contain the exact same thing: a
Frenzied Gerbil. Carl's refusal to squish one on principle (correctly identifying the pattern as
the AI angling for another crowd-pleasing kill) triggers an actual in-game punishment: thousands
of gerbils scattered by the *Wasteland*'s wreckage regroup and swarm Hump Town in a mass attack,
forcing Carl to kill dozens of them by hand — including, eventually, by foot — before the assault
stops on its own. He receives a **You're the reason why daddy drinks!** achievement (and a Gold
box) for "correcting the issue," alongside a warning that the dungeon's underlying "acceleration"
punishment system had been quietly threatening to escalate the whole time.
