# Krasue

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Level:** 16
    - **Floor:** 3 (Medium Skyfowl Settlement)
    - **Classification:** Ghost-class mob — only harmed by magic or magical items
    - **Danger:** Requires finding and destroying the physical corpse to kill permanently, not
      just the flying "head"

## Official Description

Per the in-universe monster card:

> "Said to be the undead shell of a woman who lived a life of sin, Krasue roam the dark places,
> hiding their hideous true form. They are ravenous, and they devour the blood of the
> unsuspecting. In other words, these things are flying heads who are also vampires. And, they
> bring their organs along because... nobody knows why. Probably just because it's really gross.
> You may kill the head and trailing organs to fend her off, but this mob doesn't truly die
> until you find and destroy the rest of her corpse. You will not earn experience for killing
> this mob until it is dead-dead. Not just dead."

## What They Are

Krasue in Book 2 aren't a random spawn — they're deliberately manufactured by [Miss
Quill](../bosses/miss-quill.md) from Desperado Club sex workers lured to her settlement with false
promises of safe passage to lower floors. By day, the transformation's "successes" appear as
ordinary human women working in Quill's administrative building; by night, their heads detach
and fly free, trailing their own organs on wet ropes of tissue, to feed and serve as Quill's
army. Killing the daytime body (or "corpse," as the game labels it even while it's technically
alive) is what actually ends a krasue permanently — decapitating the floating head alone only
drives it off temporarily.

## Notable Appearance

A group of krasue kill the orc NPC GumGum after she starts investigating the deaths of
Desperado Club workers, and later ambush Carl and Donut directly. One transformed krasue,
**Burgundy** — a magistrate's-office assistant Carl and Donut had spoken to earlier — survives
the destruction of Quill's nest long enough to explain the krasue-creation scheme in detail
before begging to be killed while still human, rather than transforming again at nightfall.
