# Funeral Bell

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Level:** ~90–95 (varies by settlement)
    - **Floor:** 6 ("The Hunting Grounds")
    - **Type:** Mushroom-humanoid town guard
    - **Role:** Standard-issue city/village guard NPC across the floor's non-elf settlements

## Overview

Funeral Bells are the mushroom-headed guard race stationed in nearly every town on Floor 6 outside
of the High Elf territory — flat, white, emotionless faces, tall bell-shaped caps, and (per Carl,
who never once hears one speak to a crawler) an apparent ability to communicate with each other
that they simply don't extend to outsiders. They're consistently high-level (90+) even in small
settlements, making them far too dangerous for a crawler to fight directly this early in the
floor.

Their most useful trait is passive: standing guard automatically **deactivates nearby traps** set
for crawlers, which is why the roads immediately around Funeral-Bell-guarded towns and the
caravans that travel between them are safe to walk, while the surrounding wilderness is not.

## Settlement Guards vs. Conquered Guards

Funeral Bells guard towns regardless of who currently controls them. Once Donut or Carl takes over
a settlement (by killing its mayor), the town's existing Funeral Bell garrison switches allegiance
automatically and can be issued orders, armed with missile launchers, and assigned to specific
entrances — this is how [Point Mongo](../locations/point-mongo.md)'s defenses are built up against
the incoming hunters. Individual guards can be renamed by whoever controls the town; Donut renames
several of Point Mongo's garrison (Doctor El, Dinallo, Miss Nance, Ice Man) over Mordecai's
objections.

Funeral Bells are not involved in the personal-space "three strikes" ejection rule — that's a
separate saferoom mechanic: attacking a crawler's own party member/NPC three times inside their
personal space forcibly ejects the attacker, stripped of gear, to the nearest monster nest. This is
the fate of Edict, a Dark Hive mantis merchant who deliberately provokes his own ejection this way
(by attacking Bomo, then Carl, inside Carl's personal space in Zockau — a town with no Funeral Bell
garrison at all) for reasons of mantis honor culture (see [The Dark Hive](../factions/dark-hive.md)).
