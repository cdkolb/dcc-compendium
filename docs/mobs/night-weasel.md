# Night Weasel

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Level:** Unspecified (pack mob)
    - **Floor:** 6 ("The Hunting Grounds")
    - **Type:** Invisible pack-hunting weasel/mustelid mob
    - **Notable trait:** Hunts entirely by sound and smell; inflicts paralysis on contact

## Overview

Night Weasels are naturally invisible, pack-hunting predators found across the Floor 6 jungle,
both wild and occasionally under the control of a hunter (their original owner, one **Hunter
Zabit**, loses his entire pack to Carl and Donut in the floor's opening hours) or of the High
Elves. They hunt in coordinated groups of at least a dozen, attacking from multiple directions at
once, and their bite inflicts a paralysis effect strong enough to freeze a crawler in place for a
full **"Deer in the Headlight-ed"** debuff window.

Since they can't be seen directly, the only reliable way to fight them is to track incoming damage
and sound — Carl and Donut's usual method is a smoke curtain plus *Torch* on infrared, or simply
letting an immune ally (the were-castors [Clint and Holger](../species/were-castor.md) are immune
to the paralysis effect and can smell/hear the weasels perfectly well) tank the fight while everyone
else evacuates.

## Role in the Story

Night Weasels are the first mob Carl and Donut encounter on Floor 6, ambushing the party at a
village gate within minutes of the floor opening — an early demonstration of the *Ping* spell's
value for exposing invisible threats. They recur throughout the book as a background hazard for
anyone traveling off the main caravan roads.
