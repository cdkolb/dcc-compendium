# Jikininki

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Level:** 17
    - **Floor:** 4 ("The Iron Tangle") — the "Janitor's Lair" car type, station 12 and similar stops
    - **Role:** Corpse-cleanup "janitor" mob — the Iron Tangle's version of a recurring series pattern
    - **Appearance:** Seven-foot, emaciated, white-skinned ghouls in tattered conductor-style suits and hats

## Official Description

Per the in-universe monster card:

> "Of all the types of ghouls one may find in the Iron Tangle Rail System, the Jikininki is the
> most common, the most well-behaved, and the most insatiable. Their voracious appetite for
> flesh makes them the perfect janitors. They'll generally leave you alone as long as you're not
> bleeding, as long as you don't litter, and as long as you don't trespass into their personal
> space. It's rude."

## Behavior

Jikininki wear tattered double-breasted suits with golden "Janitor" lettering on their
conductor-style hats — one has a pinback button reading "How may I hurt you?" — and their mouths
chatter up and down "like an industrial shredder." They're found in dedicated "Janitor's Lair"
train cars, marked on the map with a question mark until first entered. Despite the threatening
description, they're not aggressive by default — a pattern consistent with the Iron Tangle's
broader corpse-disposal "ghoul system," which scales up in danger the more bodies accumulate
(see [Wrath Ghouls and Blister Ghouls](index.md), the more dangerous forms this system produces
further down the line).

## Role in the Story

Two Jikininki are the first real fight Carl's party has on Floor 4, killed via a coordinated
double [Magic Missile](../spells/magic-missile.md) headshot followed by melee finishers, while
fleeing a Drek swarm into their car by mistake.
