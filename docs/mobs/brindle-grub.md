# Brindle Grub

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Base level:** 2 (juvenile) → 3 (Cow-Tailed stage) → adult "Brindled Vespa" (hornet form)
    - **Floor:** 2
    - **Role:** Dungeon "janitor" mob — spawns automatically on corpses
    - **Danger:** Individually trivial; dangerous in swarms and at full maturity

## Official Description

Per the in-universe monster card:

> "Have you ever found a dead, bloated, and decomposing body? Have ya poked it with a stick just
> to see what would wiggle out?... Here on the second floor, rats are yesterday's news. Brindle
> Grubs are now all the rage, and janitor duty falls unto them. The more monsters you kill in an
> area, the more the grubs eat. The more the grubs eat, the bigger they get. Once you start
> finding them in the pupa stage, you best move on. Grubs are easy to kill. Their older siblings
> are not."

## Lifecycle

Brindle Grubs are a corpse-cleanup mechanic as much as a monster: every corpse left on Floor 2
spawns more of them, and grubs that eat enough corpses mature — first into **Cow-Tailed Brindle
Grubs** (Level 3, per their card: "finally able to defend itself, kind of like the way a toddler
holding a plastic baseball bat is able to defend himself"), then into pupae, and finally into
**Brindled Vespas**: huge hornet-bodied creatures with clawed, grasping arms and an acid-spit
attack. This escalation is directly responsible for Carl adopting a strict "always destroy the
corpse" policy after learning how the chain works — leaving bodies behind is what turns a
nuisance into a real threat later in the same area.

The System AI patches Brindled Vespa numbers down mid-book after their spitting-acid attack
proves too aggressive for the current floor's difficulty curve.
