# Bijou

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Tiny, non-corporeal fairy mob
    - **Function:** Physically carries the **worship-tendrils** that connect gods, their temples and
      their worshipers
    - **Where:** Everywhere in the dungeon, zipping between floors; invisible to almost everyone
    - **Can they be killed?** "Nobody has ever killed or caught or stopped the Bijou," though
      [Mordecai](../characters/mordecai.md) believes a certain type of trap should in theory work
    - **Plot weight:** The mechanism behind the Sepsis Whore's disappearing act, and the mechanism
      behind Justice Light's climactic trap

## What They Do

Worship in Dungeon Crawler World is not abstract. It is a physical, whisker-thin thread of invisible
light that can penetrate almost any substance or spell — and something has to carry it.

!!! quote "Book 7, ch. 49"
    "When one worships a god, a link is formed between the adherent and the immortal. This link comes
    in the form of an actual, physical, whisker-thin connection made of invisible light. Light that
    can penetrate almost any substance or spell. After a new adherent pledges themselves, a tiny,
    non-corporeal fairy mob called a **Bijou** grabs a tendril of light from the god and drags it to
    the new worshiper and attaches it to the adherent's heart.
    When a person moves around, other tendrils also grow, connecting the adherent to other temples
    and in some cases, other worshipers. These connections, too, are facilitated by the Bijou
    fairies."

[Rosetta Thagra](../characters/rosetta-thagra.md) reads them as a workaround rather than a design
feature — a kludge the original designers used to bolt the deity system onto the game — and the quirk
"usually has no impact on anything."

Certain skills can perceive them. The NPC **Edgar** has a level-15 skill called *Sense Movement* that
catches their motion; upward momentum from the ground means someone nearby has just worshiped a god
for the first time. That is the detection method the Princess Posse uses to test Rosetta's theory
about the naga escape trick.

## The Sepsis Whore's Disappearing Act

The bijou tendril is the road. Of the fifteen "light" deities with temples inside
[Club Vanquisher](../locations/club-vanquisher.md), six carry [**Travel the Path**](../spells/travel-the-path.md), a spell that turns
the caster into light and lets them follow a path at light speed — and which the system does not count
as teleportation. Four of those six also carry the once-ever skill **Welcome Home**.

!!! quote "Book 7, ch. 49"
    "This skill, combined with Travel the Path, would allow the adherent to follow the bijou fairy's
    light tendril path directly to the strongest connected temple, which was invariably inside of
    Club Vanquisher."

That combination is how **[Princess Vinata, the Sepsis Whore](../characters/princess-vinata.md)**, has vanished instantly at the end of
every Faction Wars for centuries without ever being killed. Cracking it is what sends Carl into the
Temple of [Khepri](../bosses/khepri.md) on **Operation: Snake Pit**.

## Justice Light's Trap

The skyfowl trap master **[Justice Light](../characters/justice-light.md)** builds the endgame around them. His trap combines a **bijou
fairy trap** with a demon trap, and it uses the fairies' own unbreakable connections as structural
cable.

!!! quote "Book 7, epilogue"
    "The items that fell were a bijou fairy trap, along with the thousands of threads that connected
    the fairies to the 12th floor, and a demon trap, along with the thousands of evicted demons with
    the power to open a portal back to Sheol, if only they could find something to latch onto...
    Four separate planes—all jumbled and confused—folded and moved together as the unbreakable lines
    of the bijou fairies tightened, creating a flower pattern before the darkness in the center of it
    all broke apart.
    *System Message: The Bijou Trap has deactivated. All captured Bijou have been freed.*"

The consequence of that folding is the last thing the book reports: the Nothing draining into
Scolopendra's Lair, and [Scolopendra](../bosses/scolopendra.md) awakening.

!!! note "Scope"
    Princess Vinata and Justice Light are covered on their own character pages; this page covers the bijou as a
    creature type and the mechanic they exist to serve.
