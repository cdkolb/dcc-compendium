# Street Urchin

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Level:** 8
    - **Floor:** 3 (Over City ruins)
    - **Role:** Floor 3's "janitor" mob — the Over City's equivalent of Floor 2's Brindle Grubs
    - **Danger:** Individually low; aggressive toward anything blocking their cleanup work

## Official Description

Per the in-universe monster card:

> "These pokey little puppies only come out at night. Long, long ago, some of the richest
> citizens of the Over City kept these things as pets... When the cataclysm came, the Street
> Urchins were transformed into what you see today. Larger, deadlier, and a lot spikier. And
> after they cleaned the corpses of their former owners, they moved into the ruins and resumed
> their duties as the janitor mobs of the Over City... The ruin's version of a Roomba, these
> things only have one purpose: to remove dead bodies and any other refuse left behind by
> inconsiderate crawlers. They won't bother you if you don't bother them."

## Behavior

Squat, knee-high, black, spike-covered creatures that move like rolling tumbleweeds. They're
mindless scavengers, not natural predators — they attack only when something (a corpse, debris,
an improvised barricade) is blocking access to whatever they're trying to clean up. Their long,
piercing spikes can seriously wound anything caught in the middle of that cleanup, as Mongo and a
sedated Princess Donut discover when a swarm mistakes Carl's makeshift defensive pile for refuse.

## Notable Appearance

A swarm nearly kills Mongo while he defends an unconscious Donut (held under Tsarina Signet's
*Water Lily* curse) — the urchins are drawn by the debris Carl piled up as a barricade, not by
malice. Removing the debris (and the resulting scattered spikes) causes the swarm to lose
interest immediately.
