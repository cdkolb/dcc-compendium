# Remex

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Level:** 1
    - **Classification:** Soul Leech Capacitor — a "Bereft Minion" (a minion still animate after
      its controller's death)
    - **Floor:** 3 (Medium Skyfowl Settlement)
    - **True identity:** [Miss Quill's](../bosses/miss-quill.md) undead husband and former
      magistrate; once a famous crawler himself

## Official Description

Per the in-universe monster card:

> "Have you ever played with a Ouija board and realized that speaking with a lost loved one just
> wasn't doing it for you anymore? Perhaps you wanted to kick it up a notch? Maybe bring them
> back to life?... A Soul Leech Capacitor can do that for you. These fragile, but physically
> strong undead creatures can only be created by a Necromancer or a Dark Cleric. The spell
> latches onto the most-loved soul of the spell's target and yanks that creature back into
> existence... A simple scratch from this beast will rip your soul straight from your body. That
> soul power is stored in the capacitor, allowing the Leech's owner to access huge reserves of
> mana points."

## What He Is

Remex was once a real crawler — a "tenner" who reached at least the tenth floor and became
famous enough to be recognized by the achievement/views system years later. After his original
death (killed by an orc, "years ago," per his own rambling account), his wife Miss Quill
resurrected him as a Soul Leech Capacitor, deliberately positioning the ritual next to the
town's Swordsman-guard Soul Crystal amplifier so the resulting feedback would also kill and
transform the sitting magistrate, Featherfall — all part of Quill's long-term plan.

As a capacitor, Remex channels and stores the enormous soul power generated whenever something
dies near the amplifier, including every krasue Quill creates. He exists in a permanent state of
grief and confusion, unable to stay on topic, occasionally addressing a dead son who isn't there.

## Notable Appearance

After Quill's death, Carl and Donut find Remex cowering, harmless and non-hostile, in the
Swordsman depot's upper floor. He explains the entire krasue/soul-crystal scheme (see [Miss
Quill](../bosses/miss-quill.md)) in a long, rambling confession before the still-building soul
energy inside him triggers a massive, unstoppable magical explosion — the climax of the "Sex
Workers Who Fell from the Heavens" quest chain. Carl manages to contain and defuse the worst of
it using an item that becomes [Carl's Doomsday Scenario](../items/carls-doomsday-scenario.md),
though Remex himself is destroyed in the blast, finally free of his half-life.
