# Zarogoth

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Level:** 50
    - **Floor:** [9 ("Faction Wars")](../floors/level-09.md), [Larracos](../locations/larracos.md), during the **Peeling Phase**
    - **Also known as:** Sludge Ghoul, Sausage Golem — the System AI renamed them
    - **Type:** Generated mob. Not undead, not actually ghouls, not actually golems
    - **Faction:** Built and fielded by the **War Mage Rebellion**, from a generator hidden in the
      Piccadilly theater
    - **Weakness:** Pierce the casing low on the body and drain it. They do not die until they have
      lost **51% of their internal fluid**

## What They Are

A zarogoth is a translucent, humanoid-shaped sausage casing full of black oil-soup. The soup is made
from captured souls.

!!! quote "Monster description (Book 7, ch. 70)"
    "These leaky edgelords can also be called either Sludge Ghouls or Sausage Golems. I decided to
    rename them Zarogoths because the name is much more appropriate. It's the sort of name a weird,
    socially stunted kid—whose real name is Stan Jr. or some bullshit—would bestow upon himself...
    He's banned on Roblox. You get the idea.
    He's pretty easy to drop, but holy crap is he annoying.
    Anyway, a zarogoth is a generated mob. They are created by capturing rising or falling souls via
    a soul crystal, rerouting the energy through a corruption spell, and then mixing it all together,
    which turns it all into an oil-like soup. They shove all the goo into what's basically a
    translucent, humanoid-shaped sausage casing made of a bunch of gross shit I won't relate here.
    The resulting creatures are not considered undead. They aren't technically ghouls. Nor are they
    precisely golems, either. While souls are used to make them, they do not retain any sort of
    personality of the fallen... They are disposable ground troops with a sadistic streak. They're
    easy to kill once you figure out how they work, but don't underestimate these guys. They can be
    made quickly, attack in swarms, and their **pain ray** attack is no joke. Some even have the
    ability to reshape themselves and can cast pain-inducing dreads. Piercing their skin causes them
    to start to leak, but they won't die until they've lost 51% of their liquid.
    Oh, and they smell really bad, too."

Donut's first sighting is the shorter version: "THEY'RE LIKE FAT, MOVING SHADOWS LEAKING OIL ALL OVER
THE PLACE."

## Role in Faction Wars

Zarogoths are the primary swarm mob of the endgame. The **[War Mage Rebellion](../factions/war-mage-rebellion.md)** — which has no army of
its own, having come into existence as a "non-team entity" partway through the floor — builds a
soul-crystal generator and floods Larracos with them, all flagged onto the war mage team. They are
what occupies the [Semeru Dwarves](../species/semeru-dwarves.md) and the Larracos College of Magic
defenders while the war mages hold Team Retribution's throne room, and their real purpose is delay
rather than kills: "Their purpose was more to delay us, which was working."

The kill trick — slash low and let them drain — spreads through the NPC defenders within minutes of
first contact. A Semeru dwarf hit by the orange pain ray is left writhing on the ground; another
opens a zarogoth's stomach and it deflates and dies, drenching him in oil.

[Carl](../characters/carl.md) ends the problem by drinking his last Levitation potion, shooting up
out of Larracos, and dumping the entire contents of an inventory folder labelled *Shit Just Hit* on
the Piccadilly — about twenty waffle-maker bombs, two **No Disassembles**, a pair of anti-personnel
cheese cutters, some five hundred impact hob-lobbers and uncountable dynamite — onto the theater
housing the generator.

[Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md)'s reaction to the swarm is to want to try
[*Minion Army*](../spells/minion-army.md) on them, which she never gets around to.
