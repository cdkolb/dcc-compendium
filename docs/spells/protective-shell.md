# Protective Shell

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Carl
    - **Granted by:** Enchanted BigBoi Boxers (item-based spell — see [item page](../items/index.md))
    - **Mana cost:** None — item-based, but has a 30-hour cooldown once the underlying item is unequipped or used
    - **Effect radius:** 3-meter sphere centered on the caster's right hand, +50cm per point of Intelligence (4.5m for Carl at the time he first casts it)
    - **Duration:** 5 seconds + 1 second per spell level (20 seconds at first cast)

## Effect

*Protective Shell* creates a static, glowing dome that blocks physical mob attacks and presence
for its duration. Critically, the dome does **not** move with the caster once cast — it remains
fixed in space, filling the area it was cast in "floor to ceiling" regardless of what happens
around it. It offers no protection against magic or non-corporeal attacks.

## Scaling

The Enchanted BigBoi Boxers grant a fixed **level-15** cast of this spell — Carl doesn't level it
up independently, since it's tied to the item rather than his own spell list. At that fixed level:

- **Duration:** 5 seconds base + 1 second per spell level = 20 seconds (matches the level-15 grant exactly)
- **Radius:** 3-meter base sphere + 50cm per point of Intelligence — 4.5m for Carl at the time he first casts it

Since the level is locked to the item, any future growth in this spell's effect would only come
from Carl's Intelligence stat increasing the radius, not from the duration or a higher cast level.

## The Rage Elemental Save

This spell is the reason Carl survives the level-93 Rage Elemental's first charge in Book 1. Cast
in a corridor at the exact moment the elemental attacks, the dome physically repels the creature —
its claws bounce off even though its head can partially pass through. Mordecai notes he had the
same spell during his own crawl and that it "saved him more than once," specifically because its
static nature means a caster can use it to block a hallway or doorway rather than just personally
shield themselves.

## Limitations

- The long 30-hour cooldown makes it a true last-resort, one-per-crisis tool, not a repeatable
  defense — this cooldown directly forces Carl to improvise an entirely different (and much
  riskier) plan to permanently deal with the Rage Elemental rather than simply waiting to recast it
- Because the dome is static rather than caster-following, timing and positioning are everything —
  cast too early or in the wrong spot, and it does nothing

## Future Enhancements

Book 5, ch. 32 confirms the mechanic that makes future growth possible after all: the Nest's
tier-four combined training room lets Carl train **item-granted spells directly**, and doing so
upgrades the **underlying item itself** rather than a separate spell-list entry:

> "*My regular spells were listed as trainable along with spells associated with equipment, like
> my Protective Shell. Apparently, if I upgraded the spell in the training room, the associated
> armor — my boxer shorts in the case of Protective Shell — the equipment itself would actually
> be what got upgraded.*"

This directly supersedes the Book 1-era assumption that only Intelligence could grow the spell's
radius. That said, **Carl never actually spends training time on it** — the one scene that reveals
the mechanic has him pick trap-table and explosives training instead, and every later confirmation
of the spell's level (see Scaling above) still shows a flat **15**. Donut's Book 7 Size-Up scan of
King Rust notes, in the same breath as spotting his *Tripper*, that "*he has that same Protective
Shell as you, but it's level 15!*" — the same number Carl has had since Book 1, so the mechanic is
confirmed to exist but is never shown being exercised on this particular spell.
