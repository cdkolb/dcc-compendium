# Legionnaires of the Damned

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** A Legendary De-Sleeving Box (awarded for what the party did to the goddess
      Diwata at the end of Floor 6), opened in the Floor 8 saferoom
    - **Type:** Summoning spell
    - **Mana cost:** 25
    - **Duration:** (spell level × 2 + Intelligence) seconds
    - **Cooldown:** One minute; only one active instance at a time
    - **Target:** Recently-*unequipped* armor or weapons only; total number of targets "varies
      based on multiple factors"

## Overview

The tome arrives chained shut, "squishy and warm," wriggling like a puppy and hopping on the
table — Mordecai notes that "happens sometimes with the more powerful tomes. Especially summoning
ones." It screams when Donut reads it.

The spell reanimates physical weapons and armor pieces with "an undead, wailing spirit plucked
straight from the river of falling souls," turning each piece into a temporary minion of the
caster. The system's own description spirals off into an unnerving aside before catching itself:

> **There are a lot of angry souls out there. Like, a lot. It's said if one has the ability to
> actually see all the wailing souls that filter through the edges of our world, one would go
> quite insane.**
>
> *(The AI gave an unsettling giggle.)*
>
> **Quite, quite insane, actually. All that death, spiraling down, down, down into the drain.
> Where do they go? Why are they always screaming?**

In practice a purple rent tears open in midair and dozens of shrieking white ghosts pour out,
flowing down into whatever loose metal is lying on the floor.

## The Catch

Two hard limits shape every use:

1. **The gear must have been *recently equipped*** on a combatant or a corpse — friend or foe
   doesn't matter, but a pile of loose inventory items won't work. Carl's original plan to simply
   dump a mountain of scrap on the ground and animate it is killed by this rule.
2. **The gear is usually destroyed.** "In most cases, armor and weapons utilized with this spell
   will disintegrate upon the completion of this spell." Cursed and enchanted items "may react in
   an unpredictable manner" instead.

## "Phantasm" — the Laundry Day Combo

Mordecai spots the combination the moment Donut reads the tome. With
[Laundry Day](laundry-day.md) now at level 15, she can strip the armor off a whole group of mobs
and then animate that same armor against them: *"they will tear through the unarmored monsters
like wildfire."* Carl names the two-spell move **Phantasm**; Donut objects to the name every
single time he calls it.

Carl then builds a delivery system for it. Rather than relying on enemies to be wearing armor, he
sews dozens of individual metal chunks and trash-tier daggers onto a fabric undershirt at his
armorer's table — the game registers the result as a **"Scrapheap Breastplate"** — and repeats
the process for arms, legs, and the rest of the outfit. He puts the whole getup on **Bomo**, the
cretin mercenary bodyguard, who is big enough to carry it.

Against the orisha boss **Asojano** in the ruined temple, Donut casts *Laundry Day* on Bomo; the
armor detonates off him "like a fragmenting grenade," filling the room with dozens of separate,
legally-recently-equipped metal pieces (one of which lodges in Carl's arm). *Legionnaires of the
Damned* then animates all of them at once. Carl calls it an exploit and isn't sure the showrunners
will let it stand, but it works — and because the animating spirits are non-corporeal, the swarm
hurts non-corporeal monsters even though none of the individual pieces are enchanted. The
reanimated daggers also do the fight's decisive non-combat work, cooperating to carry a floating
skull gem across the temple to Yemaya's shrine.

## Scaling

Duration is explicitly level-scaled: **(spell level × 2 + Intelligence) seconds**, so the spell
gets meaningfully longer both as Donut trains it and as her Intelligence climbs. The strength of
the summoned "symbiotes" and the number of targets allowed both "vary based on multiple factors"
that the book never spells out. Mordecai's framing is that the spell is only as good as the
caster's ability to produce loose, recently-worn gear — which is what makes the Laundry Day
pairing the real unlock.
