# Graupel

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** [Elle McGibbons](../characters/elle-mcgibbons.md)
    - **Obtained:** A loot box at the end of Floor 5, after the party's first fight with the
      Maestro in Grull form
    - **Type:** Outdoor-only war spell — a devastating hailstorm
    - **Spell level:** 5
    - **Cooldown:** One week
    - **Casts before Book 6:** One, used to defeat the air quadrant in their bubble on Floor 5

## Effect

*Graupel* (the meteorological term for soft hail) pulls a sheet of fist-sized ice down out of the
sky over a wide area in front of the caster. Mordecai had warned that it was a war spell, meant
only to be cast outside, and the single Book 6 casting demonstrates why: the thunderclap knocks
the whole party off their feet, steam and smoke swallow the street, black asphalt explodes under
each impact, and the ground rumbles like an earthquake.

## The FOCSA Tower Cast (Chapter 65)

Elle uses it as a last resort against the demon horde massed outside the FOCSA building —
grumbling, "*This spell has a one week cooldown, but I guess I gotta use it now. Don't know if
this is going to make things better or worse*" — while Donut is still rooted four minutes deep into
her own *[Minion Army](minion-army.md)* cast.

It goes wrong immediately, in the party's favour. The blue glow around Elle keeps intensifying and
she shouts a warning mid-cast: "*Guys, it says it's casting more powerful than usual. Holy shit,
head's up!*" When the steam clears, the street is a field of mangled demon corpses as far as Carl
can see, almost every red dot on his map has turned to an X, and Elle has gained **six character
levels in one go**, settling at level 72.

Her own reaction is confusion, not triumph:

> **"What the hell? That spell is strong, but it's not usually *that* strong. It's only level five.
> It said it cast at level 16. I just got a bunch of achievements."**

## Scaling

Level 5 is the spell's actual trained level; **16** is the effective level it inexplicably cast at.
No per-level numbers are given, but the jump from "strong" to "wipes a street full of thousands of
demons and awards six levels" gives a rough sense of the curve between the two.

!!! warning "Unexplained in the text"
    **The book never explains the amplification.** Samantha — herself glowing with the same
    bluish-white light — is asked directly and does not know, spinning in circles and talking to
    herself: *"Elle is part earth, part water mage. She's on the four seasons path. I... but how
    did that happen? I am stronger too. I didn't do that. It's the tree, I think. Or maybe. It's
    all there. He's opening a path through the river, the water, and the tree, the earth. It's good
    she's not fire yet, but maybe the prince's spell enhanced her."* She then snaps out of it and
    has no memory of having said any of it. Nothing in Book 6 confirms or resolves any of these
    guesses; Amayon's presence, the Havana setting, and Elle's elemental path are all raised and
    none is settled. Do not treat Samantha's mumbling as an answer.
