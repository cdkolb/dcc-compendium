# Twinkle Toes

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** Book 4 — a platinum quest box tome
    - **Type:** Buff, movement speed

## Effect

*Twinkle Toes* dramatically increases the movement speed of Mongo — or any other bonded minion —
for a duration scaling with Donut's Intelligence. Donut is visibly delighted by the spell,
describing it as one of her favorite acquisitions of the floor.

## Scaling

The book doesn't give an exact formula, but the duration is explicitly tied to Donut's
Intelligence stat, consistent with how her other minion/pet-support spells (like
[Heal Critter](heal-critter.md)) scale.

## Role in the Story

Donut receives the tome late in Book 4 and doesn't get a dedicated combat showcase for it before
the floor ends, but immediately adds it to her active repertoire alongside
[Clockwork Triplicate](clockwork-triplicate.md) as another way to support Mongo in a fight.
