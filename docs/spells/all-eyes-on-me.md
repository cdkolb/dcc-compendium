# All Eyes on Me

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** Book 5, Chapter 1 — granted automatically as part of her starting kit the
      moment she picks **Legendary Diva** as her Character Actor floor specialty in the Floor 6
      class-selection room (see [Standing Ovation](standing-ovation.md) for the acquisition scene);
      the "All Eyes on Me" name is only attached to it later, when she performs it at the Masquerade
    - **Class requirement:** Legendary Diva (Bard floor specialty, Floor 6)
    - **Type:** Mass charm / attention-focusing song

## Effect

A sung charm spell that focuses everyone within earshot's attention onto a subject of the caster's
choosing. The charm itself is minor and doesn't compel action, but its effective range scales
directly with the caster's Charisma — a sufficiently high-Charisma cast can affect an entire room,
including crawlers, hunters, elves, and even attending deities, all at once.

## Scaling

Not given as an explicit numeric curve, but the spell's area of effect is described as
Charisma-dependent; with her benefactor tiara doubling her Charisma to 276+, Donut's cast affects
literally everyone in the Butcher's Masquerade's ballroom simultaneously, Queen Imogen included.

## Role in the Story — Book 5

Donut headlines the Butcher's Masquerade's talent competition by singing "All Eyes on Me" — the
same nonsensical lullaby her original owner, Beatrice, used to sing to her. The number was already
planned as part of the group's escape choreography before [Tsarina Signet](../characters/tsarina-signet.md)
decides, only moments earlier, to sacrifice herself instead of following through on her own riskier
plan; Carl times her cue to the song regardless. The spell locks the entire ballroom's attention
onto the stage (and, immediately after, onto a dancing Big Tina), giving Signet the unnoticed
opening she needs to perform her *Ink Marauder* ritual at the edge of the room.

## Book 8: Cast by Spoken Word Alone

At the Floor 10 stage-performance guild, Donut casts *All Eyes on Me* without singing at all:

> Donut was casting the spell *All Eyes on Me*, which under normal circumstances required a song.
> But because her charisma was now so high, she could now cast it with spoken words. After just a
> few seconds of her talking, they wouldn't be able to keep their eyes off of her.

No specific Charisma threshold is given for when the song requirement drops away — the text credits
it generally to her Charisma being "now so high," consistent with the score already in the
276+ range by Book 5. Treat the exact cutoff as unconfirmed.
