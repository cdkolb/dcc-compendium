# Heal Critter

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** Starting Bronze Pet Box, Book 1
    - **Type:** Single-target heal (pet-class targets only)

## Effect

Heals a pet-class creature. Wastes itself with an on-screen error if cast with no valid pet
target in range — a limitation discovered by Carl and Donut before Donut had actually bonded a
pet of her own.

## Scaling

No skill-level scaling described.

## Role in the Story

Becomes genuinely useful once Mongo joins the party — Donut uses it to stabilize him mid-fight
after he's caught in the Over City's opening magical-mortar barrage on Floor 3.
