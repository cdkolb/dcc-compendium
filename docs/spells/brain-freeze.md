# Brain Freeze

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** Granted automatically with her Floor 8 Character Actor class,
      **Master Telephone Psychic**
    - **Starting level:** 8 — unusually high for a newly-granted spell
    - **Type:** Single-target debuff + damage-over-time
    - **Effect:** Drains Intelligence, lowers resistances, and drains health

## Effect

*Brain Freeze* is the single-target sibling of *[Bad Attitude](bad-attitude.md)*. Mordecai explains
both in the same breath, immediately after Donut lists her new class spells in chat:

> **"*Bad Attitude* is similar to Carl's *[Fear](fear.md)* spell, but it'll be a lot more potent in
> your hands. If you work it together, it'll be a great combo. *Fear* tends to affect dexterity
> while *Bad Attitude* drains intelligence and lowers resistances. *Brain Freeze* does the same
> thing, but it targets only one mob and drains health. It also makes them temporarily blind if you
> can get it up to 10."**

So: the same Intelligence drain and resistance reduction as *Bad Attitude*, narrowed to one target,
with **health drain** added on top.

## Scaling

| Level | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| 8 (starting level) | Intelligence drain, resistance reduction, health drain on a single target |
| **10** | Additionally inflicts **temporary blindness** |

Donut reports that it "started at level 8," and Mordecai's response is immediate: "*Good. You'll
want to train that one.*" The two-level gap to the blindness threshold makes this the class spell
he wants prioritised.

## Notable Use

Against **Raul**, the 150-Intelligence ninja crab, during the ambush on the moving truck: Carl drops
a Fear-inducing smoke curtain into the cargo hold while Donut — still dangling from a rope — casts
"her health-draining, resistance-lowering *Brain Freeze*" on him. The crab starts whimpering and
scrambling backwards with his health already at half. Raul is subsequently recruited as one of
Donut's totems.
