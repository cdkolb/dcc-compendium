# Mute

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** The floor-8 spellbook delivery, waiting in the guild mailbox at the start of
      the floor (Donut is furious that only one spellbook arrived — the Floor 7 delivery was
      forfeited when the party skipped that floor)
    - **Type:** Anti-caster interrupt
    - **Effect:** Stops a target from casting spells

## Overview

Donut's first reaction to the spellbook is "*That sounds boring.*" Mordecai's is immediate and
flat: **"Good. That's on our list. You can stop monsters from casting spells. Learn it and
practice it."** He is right and she is wrong — *Mute* becomes one of the two or three most-used
spells in the book.

Mechanically it is a slow-moving yellow bolt that has to physically travel to the target and
disappear into them, which means it can be dodged, beaten by a faster cast, or simply arrive too
late. The effect lasts roughly a minute against ordinary mobs, but Carl notes it "would only last
a handful of seconds" against a boss-tier monster.

## Notable Uses

- **Practice on Floor 8's ghommids** — the very first outing, alongside
  *Why Are You Hitting Yourself?*, while the party is still learning what Donut's new class gave
  her
- **The Asojano fight** — the whole plan depends on Donut holding *Mute* in reserve so the orisha
  can't inflict the party with disease. It half-works: Asojano gets a shield up before she can
  stop him the first time, but her second cast lands and kills a spell mid-formation
- **Raul the crab** — *Hole* drops him into a truck's cargo hold and *Mute* lands as Donut jumps,
  shutting down a 150-Intelligence caster before he can retaliate. Carl's chat plan for that whole
  sequence is simply: "**Here we go. Mute first, then Magic Missile.**"
- **As the thing Carl misses most** — planning the Astrid assassination inside Club Vanquisher
  without Donut, Carl's chat message lists everything that makes the target dangerous and then
  ends: "*It's too bad we can't get Donut to help with the fight. Her new Mute spell would really
  come in handy.*"

The spell is also shown in enemy hands: during a card battle the crawler Zeynep casts *Mute* on
her **own** teammate to stop him attacking Carl with a spell.

## Scaling

No numeric formula is given on-page. What the book does establish is that duration scales
inversely with target power — roughly a minute on ordinary mobs, "a handful of seconds" on a boss
— so the spell stays useful at high level only as a timed interrupt, not as a lockout.
