# Entourage

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** Book 5, Chapter 1 — granted automatically as part of her starting kit the
      moment she picks **Legendary Diva** as her Character Actor floor specialty in the Floor 6
      class-selection room (see [Standing Ovation](standing-ovation.md) for the acquisition scene)
    - **Class requirement:** Legendary Diva (Bard floor specialty, Floor 6)
    - **Type:** Illusion / decoy

## Effect

Creates multiple illusionary duplicates of a target — Donut initially only ever manages a single,
semi-transparent duplicate of Carl when singing off-key, but a full, in-key cast produces several
solid-seeming copies at once.

## Scaling

Casting quality is tied directly to whether Donut holds pitch: an out-of-tune, low-monotone cast
produces one weak, visibly fake duplicate; a properly sung cast produces multiple convincing ones.

## Role in the Story — Book 5

Donut's first successful full-power cast comes mid-battle against Lucia Mar: she overlays herself
onto the middle of five simultaneous illusionary copies lining a burning treehouse wall, then
stacks an invisibility potion and a *Hole* spell cast under her own feet on top of it in the same
instant — so when Cici the rottweiler lunges and bites down on the "middle" illusion, her jaws
close on nothing, and the real Donut has already dropped straight through the floor to the ground
below, unseen. (It's the same single *Hole*, not a second cast, that finishes the job moments
later: when Cici sticks her head down through that hole hunting for Donut, Donut simply snaps the
still-active spell shut from below, decapitating the dog.)
