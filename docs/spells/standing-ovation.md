# Standing Ovation

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** Book 5, Chapter 1 — granted automatically as part of her starting kit the
      moment she picks **Legendary Diva** as her Character Actor floor specialty in the Floor 6
      class-selection room; she announces the pick, and the songs it came with, over chat
      immediately afterward ("I SING SONGS AND THEY CAST SPELLS. I GOT A BUNCH OF THEM.")
    - **Class requirement:** Legendary Diva (Bard floor specialty, Floor 6)
    - **Type:** Buff song

## Effect

A sung buff spell that grants nearby allies a Dexterity boost and makes the party's own regular
spells more powerful. Like all of Donut's Legendary Diva songs, it requires the caster to sing "in
key" to reliably take effect — failed or off-key casts simply don't work.

## Scaling

Not described in detail; the were-castor Holger identifies it as a good beginner song to practice
on.

## Role in the Story — Book 5

Donut attempts to cast *Standing Ovation* mid-combat against a pack of invisible night weasels,
hoping to buff her were-castor allies Clint and Holger, but fails to hold pitch amid the chaos of
battle — Holger gently tells her afterward that she needs vocal lessons or magical assistance
before trying it again in combat. She later trains the spell using a hired vocal coach installed
in the party's personal space.
