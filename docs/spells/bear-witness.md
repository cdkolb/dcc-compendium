# Bear Witness and Bring Forth the Witnesses

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** **Eris**, goddess of chaos
    - **Type:** The **Ceremony of Chaos** — a divine ritual, not a crawler spell
    - **Uses:** **Once. Ever.** Per Mordecai, this is a hard limit on the goddess herself
    - ***Bear Witness*:** Freezes every mortal **outdoors** on the floor, marks them **Witness**, and
      makes them invulnerable
    - ***Bring Forth the Witnesses*:** Teleports all of them into a single enormous pile at the
      goddess's location
    - **Side effect:** The level-20 cast causes a floor-wide magical backlash — **no long-range
      teleport spells for an hour**

The ceremony is normally a 12th-floor event. Eris casts it on Floor 9, out of what Mordecai reads as
panic, and in doing so hands Donut the setup for
*[Rolling Battle Formation](rolling-battle-formation.md)*.

!!! note "Exact-verification note"
    **No tome or system description block exists.** The quoted material below is system *warnings*
    plus Mordecai's explanation.

## The Warnings

> **You are invulnerable until the completion of the Chaos Ritual.**

> **Warning. The Goddess Eris has cast Bear Witness in a wide area around her current position. This
> will only affect those outside of structures. The area of effect is marked on your map, but it's
> pretty much the whole floor.**
>
> **Your Mind Balance has negated Bear Witness.**

Then the second half:

> **Warning: The Goddess Eris has cast Bring Forth the Witnesses.**
>
> **Warning: The effects of a level-20 Bring Forth the Witnesses has caused a magical backlash over
> the entire floor. No long-range teleport spells will work for the next hour.**

## What It Looks Like

Everyone outdoors freezes as if a boss battle had just started, **eyes gone completely white**, with
the word **Witness** floating over their heads next to a symbol Carl describes as "*two Cs back to
back with a flaming lance through the middle.*"

Then they leave:

> everyone in the army just rocketed up into the air like they'd all been launched out of
> trebuchets… ascended into the air and disappeared like it was the goddamned rapture.

## Who Was Immune

Two categories escaped, and the second one is a surprise.

**Indoors.** The spell explicitly "*will only affect those outside of structures.*" Anyone under a
roof was untouched.

**Otherwise immune:**

| Who | Why |
|---|---|
| **Carl** | **Mind Balance** negated it outright — see [Mind Balance](../abilities/mind-balance.md) |
| Dong Quixote, Bigs the Sluggalette, and the sluggalos | Per Li Na, "*anyone who was once a tattoo… Same with the sluggalos*" |
| Rend, Gonk the yak, Jamal the shark | Pets |
| Samantha | — |

Carl's level-16 **Mind Balance** — trained past the cap by battlefield conditions in the same chapter
— stopping a **level-20 divine ceremony** is the clearest demonstration of what that skill is worth.

## What It Is For

Mordecai's explanation in chapter 66 is the only account of the mechanic's purpose:

> **This is exclusive to Eris, and she usually does this on the 12th floor. I'm not sure why she did
> it now, but it sounds like she's panic casting things and doesn't know what she's doing. Some gods
> can only claim the Ascendent throne if there are no mortals present, so Eris sometimes causes chaos
> by summoning mortal witnesses to the ceremony. It's called the Ceremony of Chaos. The witnesses are
> protected from the gods during this, hence the invulnerability, and she can keep them in the Witness
> state as long as she wants. She can also turn the invulnerability off early if she wants. They're
> brought to her in a giant pile. It's... it's a thing. They're usually NPCs since most crawlers never
> make it to the 12th floor. She can only cast this once. Ever.**

So it is a **spoiler mechanic**: certain gods cannot take the Ascendent throne with mortals watching,
and Eris's counter is to teleport in an audience. It is meant to be used at the throne, on the 12th
floor, against rivals. Using it on Floor 9 wastes it.

## The Consequence Nobody Planned

The giant pile of frozen mortals is sitting in the middle of a god fight. Donut, intending only to
move them somewhere safer, casts
*[Rolling Battle Formation](rolling-battle-formation.md)* on the entire pile — and accidentally
scoops up both Eris and the feral god Meatus along with tens of thousands of people.

That turns out to be the thing that saves them. Eris later **turns her Witness spell off**, removing
the invulnerability she had granted and leaving everyone exposed on an active battlefield. Rosetta,
chapter 68:

> "She turned off her Witness spell and just removed everyone's invulnerability, **but they're still
> invulnerable anyway. The Rolling Battle Formation spell keeps them immobile and invulnerable.** But
> they'll be exposed the moment the ball breaks!"

Carl's assessment to Donut in chapter 75: "*If you hadn't done it, half of everyone would be dead by
now.*"

The teleport ban is its own minor disaster — an hour with no long-range teleportation, in the middle
of a war, imposed by a goddess who by Mordecai's read did not think it through. Carl separately
complains that his lava-abatement work "*had been interrupted by the Bear Witness spell.*"

## Scaling

*Bring Forth the Witnesses* is cast at **level 20** — a number well outside the crawler scale, and
the reason for the floor-wide backlash. The area of *Bear Witness* is nominally "a wide area around
her current position" and in practice "*pretty much the whole floor.*"
