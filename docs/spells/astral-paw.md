# Astral Paw

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** Book 4, Chapter 2 — a spellbook from Donut's Floor 5 "spellbook-of-the-floor
      club" mailbox prize, read back at the personal space right after the Grull/mantaur fight
      ("Carl, Carl, I got a good one this time!"). The same chapter's box-opening session also
      gives her *[Wall of Fire](wall-of-fire.md)*
    - **Type:** Melee-shaped area spell; manifests as a giant translucent cat's paw
    - **First seen:** Book 4, as a mailbox-delivered utility spell; first shown at battlefield scale
      in Book 7, combined into *[War Crime](war-crime.md)*'s only cast

## Effect

*Astral Paw* manifests as an enormous, glowing, translucent cat's paw that can crush, sweep, or
pin whatever it lands on — scaling from a battlefield-sized effect (combined with *War Crime*) down
to a single, close-range strike.

## Acquisition — Book 4, Chapter 2

Donut grabs her monthly mailbox spellbook the moment the party returns to the personal space and
reads it before Carl can stop her. Mordecai identifies it once she describes it:

> "*Astral Paw*," Mordecai said. "Not as good as *Astral Hand* because there's no thumb to
> manipulate and hold things. But there's more force to it, especially at higher levels, and it can
> be used as a weapon. She can grow claws on it at level five. At level 10, she can make the paw a
> lot bigger. At level 15, her skills and abilities will translate directly to the paw... Anyway,
> she can manipulate items at a distance as if she was physically touching them. Distance grows with
> level-up. At level one, it's about 10 meters."

## Book 7: The War Crime Finisher

Donut's only lifetime cast of *[War Crime](war-crime.md)* combines *Bijanbi* and *Astral Paw*. The
paw appears at massive scale, hovering over the hole of Larracos:

> Then I saw it, hovering above the hole of Larracos, right outside the tower. It was the massive,
> glowing outline of a cat's paw. The paw was absolutely enormous.

It catches thousands of frozen elves, gnolls, and Reaver cyborgs — plus the god Meatus — beneath it,
covered in fleshers. The finisher isn't automatic: Donut has to close the paw herself, manually,
once Li Na's chain lattice fails to do the job:

> "I shouldn't have picked Astral Paw," she said. "I have to do it myself."... She closed her eyes,
> and she swiped downward with three quick baps.

See *[War Crime](war-crime.md#the-one-cast-chapter-76)* for the complete scene; it isn't repeated
here.

## Book 8: Two New Feats

### Crushing a car

At the Floor 11 climax, with Chris's truck and the APV knocking down a fleeing Cadillac's shield,
Donut casts *Astral Paw* at point-blank, close-combat scale rather than the battlefield scale seen
in Book 7:

> In that moment, as the shield was down, Donut cast *Astral Paw*. She smashed the car where it
> stood, crushing it like a tin can. The Cadillac simply ceased to exist.

The whole exchange — Donut's shout to the car's destruction — takes less than a second, and costs
her three new player-killer skulls (the vehicle's occupants were fellow crawlers).

### Decapitating a unicorn — not this spell

!!! note "Exact-verification note"
    Book 8 also shows Donut removing the racer Dwight the unicorn's head "in a single swipe of
    Donut's claw" during the Heat 7 starting-line massacre. The text attributes this explicitly to
    her **claw**, not to *Astral Paw* — no spell name is given for that strike, and it reads as the
    newly celestial-boosted [Claw](../abilities/claw.md) skill (glowing, functionally indestructible
    claws, broken past its training cap to level 17 this same book) rather than as an *Astral Paw*
    cast. Treat the car-crushing feat above as the confirmed *Astral Paw* moment, and the
    decapitation as a **Claw** feat instead — see that page for the full context.

## Scaling

Per Mordecai's original Book 4 explanation: manipulation range starts at roughly 10 meters at
level 1 and grows with level-up; the paw grows claws at level 5; the paw itself gets noticeably
bigger at level 10; and at level 15, Donut's own skills and abilities translate directly onto the
paw. No further per-level formula is given past that. What's observable in play: at Nest-tower/War
Crime scale it's large enough to sweep up thousands of combatants and a god; at ordinary combat
scale (unassisted, Book 8) it's still powerful enough to crush a car "like a tin can" in under a
second.
