# Spells

Catalog of spells encountered across the series — how they're learned (tome vs. scroll),
their effects, and notable uses. Full pages exist for spells with real plot weight in Book 1;
everything else is listed in the table below. Any full page should include a **Scaling** section
whenever the book describes how the spell's effect changes with skill level. Any spell named in
a full-page character's Quick Facts box must itself have a full page here, not just a table row.

## Book 8 spell/scroll catalog

Floors 10–11, "Don't Come in Last" and "A Parade of Horribles." Spells with full pages are listed
above; everything else Book 8 names is catalogued here.

| Name | Caster | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Temporary Macaroni | Princess Donut, from her *Banquet Fit for a Princess* book | Casts a giant, solid tube of pasta (15 ft tall, 20 ft wide) as an instant barrier; at level 15 (base 14 + Brain Trust) the caster can choose the size, shape, and pasta type, and chain casts into a continuous tunnel. Only 10 mana per cast. Used to shield the race truck from a hail of exploding sugar-hermit skulls |
| Gutter Bumper | Carl, via a page eaten from *Carl's Book of Boom* | Focuses the area of effect of another spell along a chosen path ("gutter"), rather than the spell's normal shape. | Big-Ass Magnet | Princess Donut, from her *Feast* book | Cast on a chosen object; pulls every loose piece of a matching material (screws, in practice) within 200 meters toward that object, intensifying for about 15 seconds after casting. Used both as a planned car-snatching tool and, in the event, to arm Dong Quixote's lance with a wave of screws for his final charge against the god Grull — see [Corcunda](../characters/corcunda.md) for that scene in full |
| Fresh into Salt | Princess Donut, from her Champion of Nekhebit title | Turns a body of water into salt; used on an entire river to wipe out a swarm of level-50 flying piranhas in one cast |
| Summon Ye, Vermin | Princess Donut | Instantly draws every rodent in the area to the caster; used as a finisher to send a wave of drilling "zipper shrews" through a river's worth of frozen enemies, and to kill the boss Maurice |

## Book 6 spell/scroll catalog

Floor 8, "The Ghosts of Earth." Spells with full pages are listed above; everything else Book 6
names is catalogued here.

### Princess Donut

| Name | Type | Obtained by | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Venom Spitter | Skill (not a spell) | Kept from the few minutes Donut spent as a **Viper Queen** on the skipped Floor 7 | Spits a glob of green poison; the target's skin bubbles and it dies. **Level 3** when she arrives on Floor 8, **level 5** by the time it's shown in action. ~15-second cooldown; very effective on low-level mobs |
| Rigorous | Team buff | A spellbook from Chaco's prize booth on Floor 6 | Damage-reflect, but **only for debuffs and afflictions** — it reflects the *effect*, not the ailment itself, a distinction Mordecai calls important. Similar to Carl's *Super Spreader*. Donut is unimpressed and reads it only on Mordecai's insistence |
| Skedaddle | Bard sheet music | Held from Floor 6 | Grants a **double jump** — "*With Skedaddle, we can jump twice. As long as you sing it in key*" (which is what the [Bard's Golden Throat](../items/the-bards-golden-throat.md) fixes). Used as an escape/reposition tool alongside *[Puddle Jumper](puddle-jumper.md)* |

### Consumables

| Name | Type | Obtained by | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase Through Wall | Potion | Bronze/gold adventurer boxes (many) | Pass through wood, stone or metal walls for **9 seconds**. Duration-plus-one-second cooldown, so it cannot be chained. Mordecai insists it's for extreme emergencies only: the effects are counterintuitive, dangerous against anyone with a counter, and "*if they snapped off prematurely, you could end up cut in half*" |
| Eye in the Sky | Rare scroll (**levels 5, 10 and 15**) | Five looted from Tserendolgor's bequest | "*Like a mix between Find Crawler and Ping, but it covers the whole area*" — pinpoints a named crawler across an entire region unless they're in a saferoom or using advanced hiding. **Level 5** gives the target information about the caster; **level 10** notifies the target they've been found; **level 15** is undetectable |
| Liquid Therapy | Potion | Brewed by Mordecai | Protection against Shi Maria's *Unending Insanity* and *Lights Out*. Must be taken once a day; the protection lasts until you sleep |
