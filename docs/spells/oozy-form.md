# Oozy Form

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Carl
    - **Obtained:** Chapter 7, from a spellbook delivered via the safe room mailbox — a "special
      edition" that Mordecai says trains faster than normal
    - **Type:** Slime/ooze transformation — travel, escape, and camouflage
    - **Comparable to:** *Phase*, and to Donut's *Smoke Form* (her own Assassin of Sekhmet-class
      equivalent)

## Acquisition

The spellbook arrives already animated, oozing and scurrying like an escaped rat before the safe
room's cleaner bot catches it and drops it in Carl's arms:

> "What the hell?" I said, holding it out. I examined it. "It's a spell book called *Oozy Form*."

Mordecai's summary on the spot:

> "It's a great spell... It's a special edition, so it'll train faster, too. You can do all sorts of
> things with it. You can slip between cracks. Turn into a puddle on the floor. It's great as a
> defensive and camouflage spell. But it's kinda like *Phase*, too. Just make sure you're someplace
> safe when it runs out. **At level 15, you can stay in the form indefinitely, but before then, be
> careful.**"

Donut is offered the same spell in the same delivery and declines it outright ("*That is absolutely
disgusting, and I am never going to use it*"), instead relying on her own class-granted *Smoke Form*
for the same travel/camouflage niche.

## Effect

Transforms the caster into a mobile slime/ooze form capable of squeezing through gaps and cracks
too small for a normal body, and of lying flat and unnoticed as a puddle. It functions as a
*Phase*-style escape and infiltration tool rather than an attack spell.

## Scaling

| Level | Effect |
|---|---|
| Below 15 | Time-limited; the caster must return to a safe location before the form expires, or risk being caught mid-transformation |
| **15** | Duration becomes indefinite — the caster can remain in Oozy Form for as long as they like |

The book doesn't give Carl's actual skill level at any point it's used, so it isn't established
whether he's reached the level-15 threshold by the time of his Book 8 uses below. Mordecai's
"special edition, so it'll train faster" line is the only stated modifier to its leveling speed,
and no number is ever attached to how much faster — no formula, just the claim. There's no mention
anywhere of *Oozy Form* being trained past 15; level 15 here is a stated cap on the spell's own
downside (indefinite duration), not the wiki's separate skill-training cap discussed elsewhere.

## Notable Uses — Book 8

**Chapter 10 — sharing the effect.** Discussing a baby-backpack item for carrying the pig Penelope,
Carl notes the harness lets a passenger share his phase effects: "*If I wear it with you buckled
in, I can use my Phase or Oozy spell, and you'll Phase, too.*" This is confirmed in practice at the
Floor 11 climax below, where Penelope rides through Scolopendra's body with him.

**First use — planting the bomb (ch. ~76).** Carl uses *Oozy Form* for the first time to slip,
unnoticed, into Jasha and Radoslav's cul-de-sac while Samantha distracts the two bugbears, so he can
swap one of their beer cans for a duplicate hiding a fail-safe explosive. The bomb is what later
kills them both, deliberately, to save Prepotente — see
[Jasha and Radoslav](../characters/jasha-and-radoslav.md) for that outcome.

> We'd had Samantha roll into the cul-de-sac earlier and distract the two bugbears while I used my
> *Oozy Form* spell for the first time.

**Entering Scolopendra's own body (Floor 11 climax).** With Emberus and Taranis both drawn into the
arena and Club Scolopendra sitting inside the Dungeon Boss's own physical form, Carl casts *Oozy
Form* to slime his way through Scolopendra's interior — organs, tissue, and all — toward the club
entrance, carrying the pig Penelope along as a passenger via her oxygen-ring "carrier":

> "Hang on," I called as I cast *Oozy Form*.
>
> *Splatch!*
>
> I'd only done this once, and the sensation wasn't like anything I'd ever experienced. I lost my
> eyes and my ears, and my vision was suddenly all around, and I could see perfectly in the dark...
> I aimed for the building wall and slimed my way toward it, pushing through and around and over
> strange, giant, pulsing organs.

This is the clearest demonstration of the spell's range: full darkvision while transformed, and
enough structural integrity to travel through a living dungeon boss's tissue under a hard countdown
(Scolopendra's third nine-tier attack) without being digested or dissolved.
