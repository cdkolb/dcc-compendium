# Clockwork Triplicate

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** From the mailbox spellbook-of-the-month club, on Floor 3
    - **Mana cost:** 26 points per cast
    - **Type:** Illusion / decoy summon (pets and minions only)

## Effect

Creates two duplicate copies of a bonded pet or minion (Donut first tests it on Mongo). The
duplicates are physically real for the duration — they can move independently, have their own
apparent "minds," and can absorb hits — but are revealed as clockwork constructs (gears, servos,
electric parts) when the spell ends, disintegrating in a puff of smoke.

## Scaling

At level 1 (its level when learned), the duplicates last about one minute. No further scaling
data given yet in Book 2.

## Limitations

- Can only be cast on pets/minions, not on Donut or Carl themselves ("no clockwork Carl")
- Expensive relative to Donut's early mana pool (26 of a ~29-point pool)
- Doesn't appear to gain experience/level up while cast inside a Safe Room

## Role in the Story

First used for training/entertainment with Mongo, then used tactically in the fight against
Signet's mold lions — Donut reanimates two lions with **Second Chance** and casts Clockwork
Triplicate on one of the undead minions to multiply the party's numbers mid-fight.

## Notable Use — Book 3

Reused on [Hekla's](../characters/hekla.md) own reanimated corpse immediately after her death,
tripling the body into three to stall the rest of Brynhild's Daughters while Carl and Katia loot
the remains — see [Second Chance](second-chance.md#notable-use-book-3).

## Notable Use — Book 5

Donut casts *Clockwork Triplicate* on the mercenary cretin Sledge to create decoy duplicates
during a saferoom teleport-trap ambush, successfully fooling both Lucia Mar and the hunter Vrah's
allies into attacking the wrong target — one clockwork double is teleported directly into the
midst of the hunters and detonates, the other draws Lucia's attack away from the real party. She
also magically charms and re-classifies Queen Imogen's cat familiar, Ferdinand, as her own
minion, specifically to make him eligible for this spell in the future.

## Notable Uses — Book 8

Reused twice during the Floor 10 vehicle race, both times to extend a
*[Second Chance](second-chance.md#book-8-comedic-reuse-reanimated-race-car-drivers)* reanimation
past its timer rather than for combat decoys:

- On the reanimated toad corpse **Olga**, mid-race, to buy roughly twenty more minutes of a driver
  before the original timed out: "I'm going to cast *Clockwork Triplicate* before it times out."
- On **Mongo**, mid-fight against the ambushing Shadow Mimics at the Desperado Club, creating two
  duplicates as Donut flips clear of a grab.

The Olga use directly echoes the Book 3 Second-Chance-into-Clockwork-Triplicate combo on Hekla's own
corpse — the same pairing of spells, a different corpse, and a race-logistics problem standing in
for a combat stall.
