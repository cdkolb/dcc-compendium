# Torch

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** Bronze Boss Box (reward from [The Hoarder](../bosses/the-hoarder.md))
    - **Type:** Utility — light source

## Effect

Per Mordecai's description: **"It makes a curl of light follow you around."** Donut applies it to
herself automatically without reading the description first, and is initially furious to have
gotten "a stupid spell" instead of something flashier.

## Scaling

Per Mordecai: **"The more she uses it, the more powerful it'll become"** — the book doesn't give
an exact curve, but the spell is explicitly usage-based rather than tied to character level.

## Role in the Story

Despite Donut's initial disdain, Torch proves genuinely useful — it lights the party's way
through at least one pitch-black corridor without needing to carry a physical light source.

## Book 5: A Weapon

*Torch* reaches skill level 13–14 by Floor 6, strong enough at full brightness to function as a
genuine offensive tool: turned all the way up, it stuns vampire-infected creatures at full
strength and sets their heads on fire outright, killing a vampiric pterodactyl-like Northropi boss
alongside a fistfight from Carl. Donut also uses it at low, controlled settings to safely
illuminate dark areas without alerting nearby vampiric wildlife.
