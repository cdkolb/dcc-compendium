# Run, Little Günter, Run

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Carl
    - **Obtained:** Chapter 40, from the Legendary **1914 box** — the same box that gave Donut
      *[War Crime](war-crime.md)*
    - **Type:** **Special Edition** spellbook — learned at **level 5**, trains faster than normal
    - **Cost:** Variable, minimum **20 mana**
    - **Cooldown:** Once every **ten hours**
    - **Effect:** Summons a one-hit-point child-like creature who stores an attack and carries it to
      a target

The spell is a delivery system. Carl's own framing is that it is "*like sending explosive robot
spiders after Samantha on floor 6*," except the spider is a small crying boy in lederhosen who runs
away from you as fast as he can.

Like *War Crime*'s tome, this book has the odd property of not registering on the map at all.

>
> **If you do not designate a target, Günter will try to flee from you in a random direction.**

> attack kills Little Günter before he reaches his target or times out, he will immediately trigger
> the stored attack.**

## How It Actually Works

The sequence is: **summon → hit him → he runs → the hit lands on someone else.**

1. Cast the spell. Günter appears directly in front of you and stands there.
2. You have **30 seconds** to activate him, or he dissipates.
3. **You activate him by attacking him.** A spell, a skill, or a punch — whatever you hit him with
   is absorbed and held.
4. Designate a target and he sprints toward it. Don't, and he flees in a random direction, which is
   a considerably worse outcome for everyone nearby.
5. The stored attack fires when he **reaches the target** or when he **times out**, whichever comes
   first.

The warning clause is the tactical hinge in both directions. Günter starts with **one hit point**,
which makes him trivially killable — but the moment he is activated his **Constitution rises to
match the caster's**, so he becomes as durable as Carl. And if someone does manage to kill him
early, the stored attack **detonates immediately, wherever he happens to be standing.**

## Scaling

| Level | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **5** | Carl's starting level (Special Edition). One Günter |
| 5+ | Each level decreases cooldown, increases Günter's speed, and lowers mana cost per second |
| 10 | Günter can **split into a second** Günter after activation, targeting separately |
| 15 | Günter can **split into five** |

Duration is bought with mana: 20 seconds base, plus one second per mana point above the 20-point
minimum.

## Use

Carl immediately starts thinking about loading Günter with the
[Daughter's Kiss](../abilities/daughters-kiss.md) — a charged melee finisher delivered by a courier
who cannot be reasoned with. Katia asks in chapter 40 whether Donut could store a trigger spell on
him instead, which the description permits but the book never shows.

The spell is cast in **chapter 54**, as part of the Khepri temple assault at Club Vanquisher: "*At
the same moment, I cast Run, Little Günter, Run.*"
