# I Need My Personal Space

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Katia
    - **Obtained:** Granted by an Apothecary-sponsored knit beanie, given to Katia on Floor 6
    - **Type:** Area knockback

## Effect

A violent, area-of-effect blow that knocks back everyone caught in it — allies and enemies alike,
with no exceptions for party members.

## Scaling

Not described.

## Role in the Story — Book 5

Katia never casts the spell herself in the text. She gives the beanie granting it away to Carl
mid-battle at the Butcher's Masquerade so he can use its knockback to clear a path through the
nymphs to Donut — leaving her own head bare. That turns out to matter far more than the spell
itself: with no headgear occupying the slot, there's nothing to stop the reforming
[Crown of the Sepsis Whore](../items/crown-of-the-sepsis-whore.md) from landing on Katia's head
later in the same battle, after she kills Eva Sigrid. Carl uses the spell itself only once,
clearing a knot of mantis nymphs off himself while fighting toward the goddess Diwata.
