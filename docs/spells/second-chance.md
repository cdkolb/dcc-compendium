# Second Chance

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** Mailed automatically via the "Dungeon Book of the Floor Club" (a perk from a Boss Box) upon reaching Floor 2
    - **Mana cost:** 10
    - **School:** Necromancy

## Effect

*Second Chance* raises a dead monster to fight temporarily on the party's behalf.

## Scaling

| Spell skill level | Duration | Reanimation level cap |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 minute | Target must be lower level than caster |
| 3 | 3 minutes | Target must be lower level than caster |
| 5+ | 10 minutes | Can raise monsters up to 5 levels **higher** than caster |

Reanimated mobs are tagged **Undead Minion of Crawler [caster name]** and appear as a distinct,
lower-level version of the original creature. They follow simple verbal commands from the caster
(e.g., "rip up the dead bodies of your friends" — used as a demonstration/intimidation tactic
against a group of clurichauns).

## Donut's Reaction

Donut finds the spell genuinely disturbing to use — despite loving showmanship, she's visibly
repulsed by controlling a moaning, decaying corpse, and needs to be talked into casting it more
than once. This is one of the few spells in her kit she doesn't enjoy showing off.

## Tactical Uses in Book 1

- Distraction/decoy: sending a reanimated clurichaun corpse to walk into a boss chamber holding a
  lit incendiary jug, drawing fire away from Carl and Donut
- Free combat practice: Carl uses reanimated clurichauns as target practice for his slingshot
  training, since they pose minimal threat at level 1
- Skill training synergy: raising Second Chance's level (to 5 by late Book 1) both extends
  minion duration and raises the level cap of what can be reanimated

## Notable Use — Book 3

During the mid-battle death of [Hekla](../characters/hekla.md), Donut casts *Second Chance* on
Hekla's own corpse and immediately follows it with [Clockwork Triplicate](clockwork-triplicate.md),
tripling the reanimated body into three to hold off the rest of Brynhild's Daughters while Carl
and Katia loot the remains — one of the spell's more tactically ruthless uses to date. She reuses
the same Second Chance → Clockwork Triplicate combo later in the floor against exploded Krakaren
clone corpses.

## Book 5 Notable Use

Donut casts multiple *Second Chance* spells on the severed heads mounted on hunter Vrah's own
carapace — trophies of fallen crawlers, including the archer Langley — reanimating them just long
enough for them to bite down on Vrah's exposed neck in a coordinated distraction that lets Carl
land a killing blow with a poisoned arrow.

## Future Enhancements

!!! note "Confirmed — Book 4"
    In Book 4 (*The Gate of the Feral Gods*), the spell's normal level-10 caps (reanimate up to 10
    levels above caster, for up to 15 minutes) are explicitly stated — and then her **Glass
    Cannon** class pushes them further: up to **20 levels above her own**, for up to **30
    minutes**. Full detail to be filled in once Book 4 is processed.

## Book 8: Comedic Reuse — Reanimated Race Car Drivers

Donut spends much of the Floor 10 vehicle race keeping a rotating cast of corpses reanimated as
temporary drivers, since neither she nor Carl can safely drive and steer at the same time during
combat. At level 11 (level 12 with [Brain Trust](../abilities/brain-trust.md)), each reanimation
lasts up to fifteen minutes:

> Donut's *Second Chance* spell was currently at level 11, which brought it up to level 12 with her
> Brain Trust skill. The spell brought the dead corpse back to life for a maximum of fifteen
> minutes.

Two corpses are used as drivers this way in the same race: **Olga** (a toad, head caved in, driven
by shouted directions and eventually multiplied with *[Clockwork Triplicate](clockwork-triplicate.md)*
to buy extra time), and **Joel**, a dead Bactrian camel too large for the driver's seat, wrangled
into position by Carl physically pushing the seat back through the corpse's own chest cavity. Both
are steered entirely by voice command while Carl and Donut fight off the track's mobs, continuing
the same undead-chauffeur gag the spell has been good for since its first appearances.
