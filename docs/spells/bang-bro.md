# Bang Bro

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Obtained:** A Boss Box tome, Book 3 (Floor 4)
    - **Cost:** 5 mana
    - **Duration:** 5 minutes + 1 minute per spell level; 5-minute cooldown

## Overview

A tome-taught spell that temporarily enchants an equipped item with combined fire and electrical
damage:

> "Adds both fire and electrical damage to any currently-equipped item. May not be cast on flesh.
> Well, actually it can be cast on (your own) flesh, but I really wouldn't recommend it. May
> inflict Burn and Shock on targets. Can also be used to cook hotdogs and other items."

Carl casts it on his [War Gauntlet](../items/war-gauntlet-of-the-exalted-grull.md) — Mordecai
confirms it works well there, but warns against using it on the [Xistera](../items/xistera.md)
given how Carl loads it, and cautions (unnecessarily, since Carl declines) against casting it on
his own foot even with an invulnerability buff active.

## Scaling

Duration scales directly with spell level: base 5 minutes, +1 minute per level (per the tome's own
text: "*Duration: 5 minutes + 1 minute per level of spell. Requires 5 minute cooldown.*"). No
explicit damage-scaling formula is given anywhere in the text, and no book ever states Carl's
actual *Bang Bro* skill level. The spell stays in continuous, unchanged use through Book 8 — most
notably electrifying his gauntlet to kill a parasite that had latched onto it after a skull
exploded in the FUPA's cab — with no new numbers, effects, or level references appearing at any
point after the Book 3 grant. This is a genuinely thin scaling picture; the text simply doesn't
give more to work with.
