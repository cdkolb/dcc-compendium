# Puddle Jumper

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** Bronze Boss Box (reward from [The Juicer](../bosses/the-juicer.md))
    - **Mana cost:** 20
    - **Cast delay / cooldown:** 10-second delay, 5-hour cooldown between casts

## Effect

Teleports Donut and up to three others to any location within her line of sight — per Mordecai,
"especially useful if you come across rivers of lava or other impassable locations." Not viable
as a combat spell given its 10-second delay, but valuable for utility and escape.

## Scaling

Per Mordecai: **"Higher levels of the spell allow her to go further. If she manages to hit level
15 with the spell, it works almost as well as *Teleport*"** — one of the most powerful spells in
the dungeon. The book doesn't give a level-by-distance formula beyond this benchmark.

## Limitations

The two biggest practical problems, per the text: the 5-hour cooldown (once cast, no second use
for the rest of most encounters) and the 20-mana cost against Donut's small early mana pool,
compounded by a punishing 2-minute cooldown between mana potions.

## Role in the Story

Used both tactically (infiltrating the Rev-Up filling room via Plan Pied Piper) and as an
emergency escape tool later in the book.

## Book 8: Level 17, Almost Any Distance

The Book 7 [Cloak of the Benevolent Champion](../items/cloak-of-the-benevolent-champion.md) grants
Donut a one-time bonus: one spell already raised to level 15 by the Cloak may be pushed to level 16.
Facing a shortlist that includes *Wall of Fire* and *Heal Critter*, she picks *Puddle Jumper*:

> "They already all activate at level 16 because of your Brain Trust skill," Mordecai said,
> sounding awed. "...*Puddle Jumper* at level 17... I don't know, Donut. I don't even know what
> level 16 of that spell can do."
>
> "That's what I picked," she said... "*Puddle Jumper.* It's now officially level 16."

With [Brain Trust](../abilities/brain-trust.md) adding its usual +1 to effective spell level, the
true level-16 spell casts at an effective **level 17** — one level past Mordecai's own original
Book 1 benchmark ("if she manages to hit level 15... it works almost as well as *Teleport*").

Used twice at this level in Book 8: once mid-race to recover Donut and a knocked-out mantaur
half-brother back to the truck, and once at the Floor 11 climax to escape Emberus's arena
immediately after Carl renounces his god:

> "Toodles," Donut said as she jumped to my shoulder and activated *Puddle Jumper*.
>
> At level 17, the spell had the ability to **return her to any place she'd recently visited at
> almost any distance.** In this case, she took us outside to the entrance of the arena.

## Scaling — Book 8 Note

At effective level 17, *Puddle Jumper* can return the caster (plus passengers) to **any
recently-visited location, at almost any distance** — no longer limited to line-of-sight, and well
past the level-15 "works almost as well as *Teleport*" benchmark Mordecai gave back in Book 1.

## Future Enhancements

!!! success "Confirmed — Book 8"
    See above. The spell's Book 1 level-15 benchmark ("works almost as well as *Teleport*") is
    exceeded at the effective level 17 reached in Book 8.
