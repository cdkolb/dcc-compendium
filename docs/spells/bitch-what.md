# Bitch, What? (Fire Berserker)

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** Item-granted by the
      [Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Superior Fire Demon's Hand Maiden](../items/enchanted-nipple-ring-of-the-superior-fire-demons-hand-maiden.md)
      — "**User may cast *Bitch, What?* Once a day**"
    - **Type:** Berserking spell + partial invulnerability
    - **Target:** Self only
    - **Cost:** Item-based (no mana)
    - **Duration:** 20 seconds
    - **Cooldown:** Once every 30 hours
    - **System's internal name:** ***Fire Berserker***

## Description

> ***Bitch, What?***
>
> **Type: Berserking Spell + partial invulnerability.**
>
> **Target: Self only. Cost: Item based.**
>
> **Duration: twenty seconds. Cooldown: Once every 30 hours.**
>
> **Effect: You ever seen an angry, on-fire weasel tossed into a room full of chickens?**

The nipple ring is unambiguously a good item and Mordecai says so, but the granted spell is the one
thing he stops Carl to talk about before Donut equips it. He and Carl have both seen the state
before without realising it:

> **"I have, and so have you. Remember that rage elemental? They're perpetually in that state. It's
> actually called *Fire Berserker*. They've been changing the names a lot more than usual."**

A [Rage Elemental](../mobs/rage-elemental.md) is, mechanically, a creature permanently locked in
this spell. That is the reference point for what twenty seconds of it looks like.

His one instruction is the important one: **"Donut, only cast it when you're by yourself."** The
combination of berserk state and *partial* invulnerability means she will attack whatever is
nearest and cannot reliably be stopped, while being hard to stop safely.

## Scaling

No level. The three numbers — **20-second duration**, **30-hour cooldown**, **once a day** — are
fixed by the item, and the spell has no training path. The 30-hour cooldown is deliberately longer
than a day, so it cannot be reliably banked against a daily schedule.

## Comparison

Carl uses this spell as his own benchmark for danger. On receiving
*[Self-Immolation](self-immolation.md)* from Emberus, his immediate assessment is: "*Yikes. That was
just like Donut's Bitch, What? berserking spell. It was something I wouldn't be able to cast with
anybody nearby.*" Both are extremely powerful, once-per-cycle, self-targeted fire effects that are
functionally unusable in a party formation.
