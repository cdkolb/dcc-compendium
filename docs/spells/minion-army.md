# Minion Army

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tome owner:** Princess Donut — obtained Book 1, first successfully cast in Book 6
    - **Obtained:** Silver Beguiler Box (reward for charming the goblins during the meth-war setup)
    - **Mana cost:** 50
    - **Type:** Battlefield sabotage — turns a percentage of an enemy force against its own side
    - **Casting time:** Five minutes base (four with Donut's Charisma boosts); caster is
      **Rooted in Place** and visibly glowing the entire time

## Effect

Per the Book 1 text: **"caused hostile enemies to fight for you instead of against you."** Doctor
Bones, the skeleton bard who casts the related spell *[Conscript](conscript.md)*, gives the
sharper Book 6 definition: ***"Minion Army* is a battlefield sabotage spell, and it's quite
effective, but it has a long casting time, and it is temporary."**

It only works on **intelligent** mobs. It does not recruit an army so much as poison one: a
percentage of the enemies inside the casting area simply turn on their comrades.

## Book 1: Out of Reach

Donut's mana pool never reaches 50 during Book 1 (she peaks in the 20s–30s), so the tome sits
unread for most of the book. The attempt reveals a rule that holds across the series: **a crawler
cannot self-teach a spell from a tome unless they already have enough mana to cast it.** Donut
tries anyway with 24 mana and the system simply refuses. Carl reassures her they'll either trade
it for something more immediately useful or hold onto it until she can actually cast it.

## Books 1–5: Learned, Never Cast

Donut does eventually learn the spell, but it goes uncast for five books — not for lack of mana
but because of what casting costs. The spell is "designed for war," and the caster is frozen,
glowing, and completely defenseless for four straight minutes. There is almost never a moment in
a dungeon crawl where that trade is survivable. As late as Book 6's midpoint Carl still describes
it to Doctor Bones as the spell "she hasn't tried yet."

## Book 6: The First Cast (Chapter 65)

The FOCSA tower assault in Havana is the first situation that actually fits the spell. Thousands
of demon zombies are massed in the street between the party and the building, and most of them are
standing frozen and unaware.

Donut positions herself at the edge of an alley inside the basket of a taxi bike, low enough that
only her eyes show — invisibility is useless because the spell makes her glow regardless. Elle
raises the obvious objection ("*You said it only works on intelligent mobs, and zombies ain't too
smart*") and Carl answers: "*It's demon zombies. They're intelligent.*"

The numbers are stated plainly. The spell is **only level one**. At that level it turns **two
percent** of the enemies in the casting area against their comrades. With both **Imani** and
**Samantha** enhancing it, that two percent should rise to about **five percent**. Carl thinks
that sounds far too low; Mordecai and Doctor Bones both insist it will create utter chaos. Donut's
Charisma is high enough that she can select the entire mass outside — "*Even some of the birds!*"
— and it trims the cast from five minutes to four.

The result is muddied by luck rather than by the spell: while Donut is still rooted, Elle's
*[Graupel](graupel.md)* misfires upward into level 16 and annihilates most of the ground force
before *Minion Army* even lands. Donut's immediate reaction is outrage ("*Wait, so I cast that
giant spell for nothing? Elle, you stole my experience!*"). It isn't wasted — her casting area had
included the lower edge of the demon-bird tornado circling the tower, which the hail never
reached, and those birds and bats spend the next stretch of the fight tearing each other apart and
raining corpses into the street while Donut collects the experience for every one that dies.

## Scaling

The only hard datapoint is the level-1 baseline: **2% of enemies in the area**, roughly **5%**
with two spell-enhancers stacked on the caster. No per-level table is given. Area size is governed
by the caster's Charisma — Donut's is high enough at level 57 to blanket a street full of
thousands of demons plus part of an aerial formation, and she specifically laments not still
having her Charisma-doubling tiara from the previous floor. Casting time also shortens with
Charisma (five minutes → four).
