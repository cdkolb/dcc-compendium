# Bad Attitude

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** Granted automatically with her Floor 8 Character Actor class,
      **Master Telephone Psychic**
    - **Type:** Mass debuff
    - **Effect:** Drains Intelligence and lowers resistances across a group
    - **Designed to combo with:** Carl's *[Fear](fear.md)*

## Effect

Mordecai's introduction of the spell is also the instruction manual for how the party is meant to
use it:

> **"*Bad Attitude* is similar to Carl's *Fear* spell, but it'll be a lot more potent in your hands.
> If you work it together, it'll be a great combo. *Fear* tends to affect dexterity while
> *Bad Attitude* drains intelligence and lowers resistances."**

The two spells are explicitly complementary rather than redundant. *Fear* wrecks a group's
Dexterity; *Bad Attitude* wrecks its Intelligence and strips its resistances, which opens the same
targets up to everything else Donut can throw — including her
[nipple-ring-amplified](../items/enchanted-nipple-ring-of-the-superior-fire-demons-hand-maiden.md)
fire spells, whose damage the reduced resistances directly multiply.

"A lot more potent in your hands" is a Charisma note: Donut's Charisma is far above Carl's, and
charm- and morale-based effects scale with it.

## Scaling

No numeric formula is given, and unlike its single-target sibling
*[Brain Freeze](brain-freeze.md)* — which gains blindness at level 10 — *Bad Attitude* has no
stated level breakpoint. Donut does not report a starting level for it. What the text does
establish is that its area and potency ride on the caster's Charisma, which is the stat Donut has
in surplus.
