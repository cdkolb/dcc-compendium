# Fireball

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** Granted by the [Enchanted Obsidian Bracelet of the Raggle Rouser](../items/obsidian-bracelet-of-the-raggle-rouser.md), looted on Floor 6
    - **Cooldown:** Once every twenty minutes (an item-granted limitation, not an innate spell cooldown)
    - **Type:** Area fire damage

## Effect

Launches a beachball-sized, slow-moving orb of fire that explodes on impact, igniting the target
and anything flammable nearby. Explicitly an outdoor spell — Mordecai warns against casting it in
enclosed spaces.

## Scaling

Granted at a flat level 10 by the bracelet; no independent training or leveling shown.

## Role in the Story — Book 5

Donut's first cast finishes off the rooted-in-place Eryops boss Claude Sludgington the Fourth,
punching a hole clean through its stomach and setting the wound alight — an accidental
demonstration of the spell's power that also nearly gets Carl killed moments later, when the
*Protective Shell* holding the flaming, bent-back trees in place finally collapses and springs the
dying boss's body back down at him. She casts it again indoors, cornered inside Lucia Mar's
treehouse pub, blasting Lucia into the back wall and setting the whole pub ablaze — almost
certainly the source of a "Did-You-Really-Just-Cast-Fireball-in-a-Room-This-Small" achievement box
Donut mentions receiving, prompting Mordecai's irritated aside ("We still need to have a
discussion about that"); Carl and Donut go on to watch this exact fight play out on a recap
broadcast shortly afterward, which is also where Mordecai separately warns that Fireball is "an
outdoor spell" — well after the fact, since Donut had already cast it indoors twice by then.

She separately kills a dryad mayor with a Fireball to the back while conquering a
village, accidentally setting the pub behind him ablaze and burning half the town. She is not shown
casting it again at the Butcher's Masquerade — Katia's own scouting report on Queen Imogen
specifically notes nobody hits her with a fireball, since Imogen can reflect magical fire back at
the caster.
