# Magic Missile

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** Self-taught from a Tome of Magic Missile in her starting Silver Adventurer Box
    - **Mana cost:** Flat 5 mana per cast early on; from skill level 5 onward, scalable 3–6 points per cast depending on chosen "power"
    - **Type:** Ranged single-target damage

## Effect

*Magic Missile* fires as a visible bolt ("laser blasts," in Carl's words) from Donut's eyes.

## Scaling

| Spell skill level | Behavior |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Flat 5-mana cast, fixed damage, no choice in power |
| 5+ | Caster can choose a "power" from 3–6 mana per cast, trading efficiency for damage — a 6-power cast can vaporize a mid-tier single target outright (drops a level-2 Troglodyte Pygmy in one hit), while a 3-power cast only handles weaker mobs |
| 8 (reached by end of Book 1) | Combined with Intelligence climbing from 11 to 20+ (which governs mana pool size), lets Donut fire many casts before needing a Mana Potion |

## Progression Through Book 1

- First cast: kills nothing, alerts a Bad Llama on the first floor
- Used as the party's primary ranged damage source throughout — softening bosses (the Juicer,
  Krakaren clones) before Carl closes in for melee

## Limitations

- Mana regenerates slowly (roughly 1 point/minute for Donut early on) and potions have a
  punishing ~2-minute cooldown between uses, so *Magic Missile* runs dry fast in extended
  encounters — a recurring tactical problem the duo has to plan around
- Donut is reluctant to rely on melee (calling it "inelegant"), so running out of mana
  mid-fight is a recurring moment of real danger, not just inconvenience

## Notable Use — Book 3

Donut's [Prism Industries Capacitating and Focusing Goggles](../items/prism-industries-capacitating-and-focusing-goggles.md)
let her fire multiple full-power *Magic Missile* bolts at once — a "Double Shot" headshots two
Jikininki ghouls simultaneously in one encounter — and separately let her store a full-power
charge in the goggles for later release as its own "double shot," used against a (fully awake)
province boss during a cart-lift ambush. Earlier in the book, an accidental *Magic Missile* meant
for a group of ghouls instead hits [Katia](../characters/katia.md) — friendly fire that becomes
tangled up in [Hekla](../characters/hekla.md)'s manipulation of Katia shortly before Hekla's
death.

## Book 4: Glass Cannon

On Floor 5, Mordecai steers Donut toward **Glass Cannon** as her Character Actor "floor specialty"
class specifically because it sharply increases spell training speed and power across the board.
The choice is confirmed accurate in play: her *Magic Missile* is repeatedly described as
noticeably stronger for the rest of the floor, and the same boost supercharges her other spells —
most visibly *Second Chance*, whose range and duration both roughly double for the floor. The one
cost is that Donut never receives Glass Cannon's usual +15 Constitution bonus, leaving her exact
mechanical downside somewhat unclear even after the floor ends.

## Book 5 Notes

Donut continues relying on *Magic Missile* as her core damage spell through Floor 6's hunter war,
training it toward level 12 by the book's end; a full-powered cast is enough to stagger even a
level-70+ hunter elite (Vrah) mid-fight. Carl deliberately holds off giving Donut the Pawna's
Cries potion boost meant for her *Laundry Day* spell instead, planning to let *Magic Missile* top
off naturally once it hits level 12.

## Book 6: Reclassified as a Fire Spell

!!! success "Conflict resolved"
    This page previously carried a note claiming that a new Book 6 class *grants* Donut *Fireball*
    and *Wall of Fire* alongside *Magic Missile*, which conflicted with Book 4 (where she already
    casts [Wall of Fire](wall-of-fire.md) twice with no indication it's new). **That note was
    wrong. No class is involved.**

    The actual text is Mordecai speaking in chapter 2, right after the AI reads out the description
    of Donut's new
    [Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Superior Fire Demon's Hand Maiden](../items/enchanted-nipple-ring-of-the-superior-fire-demons-hand-maiden.md):

    > **"Donut, only cast it when you're by yourself. You have three fire spells now. *Fireball,
    > Magic Missile,* and *Wall of Fire*. All three of them are going to be much more intense. So,
    > be very careful."**

    He is listing spells Donut **already has** and explaining that a new *item* will amplify all
    three. The ring's relevant line is: **"All spells with a fire attack now deal (Intelligence)%
    more damage."** It empowers; it teaches nothing. (The "only cast it when you're by yourself"
    warning attaches to the ring's separate *Bitch, What?* berserking spell.)

The one piece of genuinely new information in the exchange is the **classification**: this is the
first time *Magic Missile* is stated to be a fire spell, which means Donut's whole
Intelligence-stacking build now feeds her core damage spell twice over — once through mana pool and
once through the ring's percentage bonus. She continues to use it as her primary ranged attack
throughout Floor 8, frequently paired with *[Mute](mute.md)* ("**Here we go. Mute first, then Magic
Missile**").

## Book 7: Level 16, and It Now Vaporizes People

The "advanced targeting" comes from a third tiara, the
[Tiara of a Thousand Lights](../items/tiara-of-a-thousand-lights.md) — the targeting itself doesn't
originate with the tiara, though; it's **absorbed** into it from the [Targeting](../abilities/targeting.md)
skill on a boonie hat that ships with Donut's new Deathbed Hellcat class.

The payoff line, chapter 61:

> It was Donut casting *Magic Missile*. **With her tiara's advanced targeting skills and Donut's
> elevated position, along with her Deathbed Hellcat class, she could hit anything she could see
> with any of her long-range spells.**

Floor 9 is where *Magic Missile* stops being a workhorse and becomes an execution tool. Three things
stack in the same book:

1. **Range.** The **Deathbed Hellcat** class quadruples the range of all Donut's existing spells
   before anything else is applied. The boonie hat's *Targeting* skill adds more range on top, and
   also lets her zoom in and examine targets at distance. Absorbed into the tiara, that becomes
   permanent and hat-independent.
2. **Level.** The spell reaches **15** by chapter 74 — "*My Magic Missile is level 15 now… It tripled
   in power!*"
3. **Brain Trust.** Donut takes the [Brain Trust](../abilities/brain-trust.md) natural benefit when
   her Intelligence tops 100, which makes every spell she casts operate one full level above its
   trained level. *Magic Missile* therefore fires at an **effective level 16** — a number the series
   had previously treated as effectively unreachable.

The result, chapter 80:

> This was the effect of Donut's Brain Trust upgrade. It made all spells operate at one full level
> higher than before. Not a huge deal, except her *Magic Missile* was now 15, meaning she was firing
> these things at level 16. **Apparently that evaporated the targets completely.**

A Reaver sentry dog hit with one "*literally evaporated, having been turned into a rapidly expanding
group of atoms.*"

There is also a tactical cost attached to the tiara that carries it: it glitters so brightly that by
chapter 73 it "*glowed like a lighthouse beacon*," advertising the exact position of Donut's head
from the top of a sniper tower. Carl hates it. Donut loves it.
