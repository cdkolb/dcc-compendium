# Rolling Battle Formation

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Type:** **Atrocity**
    - **Obtained:** Absorbed from a defeated **skunk battle-wizard's hat** — "*the stinky wizard
      hat*"
    - **Effect:** Balls thousands of creatures, friend and foe alike, into a single enormous sphere
      that is **immobile, invulnerable, and steerable by the caster**
    - **Duration:** Until the ball sustains enough damage
    - **First cast:** Chapter 66, on the pile of mortals Eris had just teleported into one place

This is the spell that accidentally saves half the floor.

!!! note "Exact-verification note"
    **No system description block exists for this spell.** Everything below comes from a mob entry,
    Donut's all-caps chat explanations, and other characters' observations.

## Origin of the Name

The spell is named after a mob the party fought earlier in the book, in the expanded map area — a
skunk-themed riff on Book 1's Ball of Swine:

> **Rolling Battle Formation Ball. Level 70.**
>
> **You don't want to get in this thing's way. It would really stink.**
>
> **I hope you have some hydrogen peroxide, soap, and baking soda ready.**

Carl notes it is not listed as a boss. Donut later acquires the spell from that creature's hat —
presumably via her
[Tiara of a Thousand Lights](../items/tiara-of-a-thousand-lights.md) charge pillow, though the text
never says so explicitly.

## The Cast — Chapter 66

The setup is not Donut's doing. The goddess **Eris** has just cast
*[Bear Witness / Bring Forth the Witnesses](bear-witness.md)*, freezing every mortal outdoors on
Floor 9 and teleporting the lot of them into a single colossal pile as witnesses to her Ceremony of
Chaos. Donut's intention is modest: move the pile away from a god fight.

> **System Message: Warlord Princess Donut has committed an atrocity.**
>
> **Donut: WELL, I NEVER. THAT WAS VERY RUDE.**
>
> **Carl: What was that?**
>
> **Donut: IT'S A TWO-PART PLAN. FIRST I GOTTA MOVE THE PILE TO WHERE ALL THE DEAD BODIES ARE PILED.
> IT'LL GET THEM AWAY FROM THE GOD FIGHT, TOO.**

What she has actually done takes a moment to register on everyone else:

> **Donut: MEATUS GOT PULLED IN, TOO! AND SINCE HE IS STILL WRAPPED AROUND ERIS, SHE'S ALSO PART OF
> IT! IT SAYS THE GODDESS IS UNCONSCIOUS!**
>
> **Rosetta: Ball?**
>
> **Mordecai: Donut, what the hell did you do?**
>
> **Donut: ROLLING BATTLE FORMATION. I GOT THE SPELL FROM THAT SKUNK'S HAT! YOU KNOW, THE STINKY
> WIZARD HAT? IT'S AN ATROCITY SPELL, AND THE TOWER MAKES IT REALLY POWERFUL. I DIDN'T KNOW IT WAS
> GOING TO BE THAT POWERFUL! BUT I CAN TELL IT WHERE TO ROLL! I CAN DRIVE IT LIKE A REMOTE CONTROL.
> IT'S, UH, REALLY, REALLY, REALLY BIG.**
>
> **Carl: How long does it last?**
>
> **Donut: IT SAYS UNTIL IT SUSTAINS ENOUGH DAMAGE!**
>
> **Mordecai: Wait, wait, wait. Are you telling me you just made a ball of swine, but out of
> everyone? Including two gods? There has to be tens of thousands of people in that thing! And the
> system just let you do it?**
>
> **Donut: IN MY DEFENSE, I DIDN'T KNOW THE DINK WAS GOING TO BE SUCKED INTO THE SPELL, TOO. THE
> BACK END IS JUST SLOPPED OUT THE BACK LIKE THE BALL FORGOT TO ZIP UP.**

Two gods — the feral god **Meatus** and the goddess **Eris** herself, wrapped up with him — are
inside the ball. Eris is registered as unconscious.

## Mechanics, as Established in Play

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Targets** | Everyone in the area, indiscriminately. Friend, foe, NPC, deity |
| **State** | The contents are **immobile and invulnerable** for the spell's duration |
| **Control** | The caster steers the ball remotely — "*I can drive it like a remote control*" |
| **Duration** | Until the ball sustains enough damage to break |
| **Range** | Enormously extended by casting from an Atrocity-synergy tower (the Nest) |
| **Sound** | The ball screams; it is a mass of conscious, trapped people |

The invulnerability clause turns out to matter more than anything else. Eris had granted her
Witnesses invulnerability for the ceremony and then **turned it off**; Rosetta, chapter 68:

> "She turned off her Witness spell and just removed everyone's invulnerability, **but they're still
> invulnerable anyway. The Rolling Battle Formation spell keeps them immobile and invulnerable.**
> But they'll be exposed the moment the ball breaks!"

Donut cast an atrocity to tidy up a pile and, in doing so, overrode a goddess's decision to leave
tens of thousands of people to die. Carl's verdict is delivered to her in chapter 75:

> "**You saved them by casting the Battle Formation spell. If you hadn't done it, half of everyone
> would be dead by now.**"

## Aftermath

The ball is a standing feature of the battlefield for roughly ten chapters, rolled around by Donut
and swarmed on the outside by the fleshers from
*[You're Not Done Yet](youre-not-done-yet.md)*. Carl uses *Ping* to locate Tagg and Fang inside it.
When the ball finally breaks apart, the party has *Bounce House* pre-cast below the hole of Larracos
to catch the falling crawlers. In chapter 82, once "the Battle Formation spell was dispelled," King
Rust's harem women flee into the city.

## Scaling

No level and no stated formula. The two real variables are **how many bodies are in range** and
**whether it is being cast from a synergy tower** — and Donut's own testimony is that she badly
misjudged the second one.
