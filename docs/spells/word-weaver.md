# Word Weaver

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** **Sister Ines Quiteria** — "The Reaper of Havana," a Cat Girl **Poet Laureate**,
      crawler #8,199,462
    - **Type:** Charm, delivered as a recited poem
    - **Level:** Raised to **15** with help from her sponsor and patron, the Nebulars
    - **Signature use:** Charming the level-125 city boss *Madre de Aguas* — and, through it,
      every creature in the region

## Overview

*Word Weaver* is the core of Sister Ines's whole build. She explains the chain of choices herself:
Cat Girl "was the only race that gave me a charisma boost for my *Word Weaver* spell, amongst
others, and our game guide insisted it would allow me to choose the Poet Laureate class, which it
did."

The spell is cast as a spoken poem and is sustained by continuing to recite it, which is how she
spends most of her on-screen time in the back half of the book: perched on the head of an enormous
sea snake, reciting, while the world burns around her.

## The Madre de Aguas Charm

The full sequence is laid out in a Kevin-and-Magnificent-Troy broadcast recap in chapter 55:

> "During the battles between the crabs and seals, crawler Sister Ines Quiteria used her level-15
> *[Book of Rhymes](book-of-rhymes.md)* bard skill, which she just got from her patron, to enhance
> her *Word Weaver* spell to charm this creature, a level-125 city boss. **This boss, in turn, has
> the power to control all creatures from the area.**"

That is the leverage: she does not charm an army, she charms the one thing that already commands
the army. Troy's assessment — "*Charming that boss actually sounds like it was a smart move on the
nun's part*" — is correct, and it makes Ines, for a stretch of the book, arguably the single most
powerful crawler on the floor.

## Level 15: Controlling the Zombies Too

When Carl's zombie-turkey accident seeds a contagious undead outbreak into the global ocean, the
mass zombification starts to disrupt Ines's charm poem. Level 15 is what saves it:

> "...since her sponsor *and* patron, the Nebulars, helped her raise that *Word Weaver* spell to
> level 15, she was able to fully control both the boss *and* the spells the boss could cast,
> meaning she could control the zombies, too. That spell is so powerful, in fact, even the fish
> started to pull themselves out of the ocean to get closer."

Troy's verdict: "*That's a level of charm Princess Donut could only dream of!*" Donut objects.

## Scaling

The only stated breakpoint is the one that matters:

| Level | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Below 15 | Charms the target creature |
| 15 (boosted by *Book of Rhymes*) | Charms the target **and** grants control of the spells that target can cast — which, against a boss whose power is controlling every creature in a region, cascades into control of the entire region, zombies included |

## The Limit

What *Word Weaver* cannot hold is a demon. The zombie outbreak (plus an AI ruling that crab sperm
counts as a mob) triggers the earliest demon eviction in the history of the crawl. When the demon
prince **Amayon** is squeezed into the *Madre de Aguas*'s body, Ines's charm technically still
holds the shell — but as Kevin puts it, "*as powerful as that spell is, Faction Wars fans all know
how difficult it is to control an evicted demon.*" Her grip visibly degrades over the following
chapters until she is destroyed by it.
