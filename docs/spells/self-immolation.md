# Self-Immolation

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Carl
    - **Obtained:** Granted at the **Devotee** worship rank of Emberus, God of Sun and Ash
      (achievement "Baptism by Fire"), alongside upgraded Burn immunity and a tithe increase from
      5% to 10%. Devotee is the highest rank Carl can reach, since he is neither a Cleric nor a
      Paladin
    - **Granted at level 10**
    - **Cost:** Nothing — but requires being in Emberus's good graces to cast
    - **Target:** Self only
    - **Duration:** Instantaneous; the fire burst lasts up to three seconds
    - **Cooldown:** Once a day, resetting with the **obeisance countdown** rather than from the
      moment of casting

## Effect

The spell wraps the caster in a non-static sphere of **Sheol Fire** with a one-foot buffer from
every body part, so Carl himself is untouched. Everything else inside the sphere is not.

> **Casts a non-static sphere of *Sheol Fire* around the caster with a 1-foot buffer from all body
> parts of caster. Fire expands in a distance of .15 meters per level of spell until it reaches
> level 15, at which time the distance increases exponentially and may be controlled.**

The system's explanation of Sheol Fire is one of the book's better warnings:

> **You know how when you were a kid, you'd take a lighter and pass your finger over the flame
> really fast, and it wouldn't hurt you? Don't try that with this stuff. It's fire with rabies.
> Wood turns instantly to ash. Rock to lava. Flesh... well. Just be careful. Or don't, if you're
> looking to go out with some char-scented gusto.**

## Scaling

| Spell level | Sphere radius |
|---|---|
| Per level | +0.15 m |
| 10 (as granted) | 1.5 m in every direction — "up, down, left, and right" |
| 15 | The per-level distance "increases exponentially" **and becomes controllable** |

The downward expansion is the part Carl fixates on: at level 10 the sphere reaches 1.5 m below his
feet as well, so casting it on solid ground leaves him standing in a pool of fresh lava. Level 15's
*controllable* radius is what would make the spell safe to use near allies, and Carl never gets
there in Book 6 — and *Self-Immolation* is never mentioned again in Book 7 or Book 8 (unsurprising,
since Carl publicly renounces Emberus at the Floor 11 climax, which is presumably what ends his
access to it). No level-15 cast, and no indication it was ever trained past the cap, appear
anywhere in the series.

## The Cooldown Quirk

Carl flags the reset behaviour as unusual and useful: most of his spells (he compares it to
*[Protective Shell](protective-shell.md)*) don't start their cooldown until they're cast, but
*Self-Immolation* resets on the same daily cycle as the Emberus shrine and his obeisance countdown
— generally around the daily recap episode. Cast it late in one cycle and early in the next and
you effectively get two uses close together. See
[Deity worship](../mechanics/index.md#deity-worship-feral-gods-and-the-nothing-book-4) for the
obligations attached to the rank.

Carl's own summary when he reads it: *"That was just like Donut's Bitch, What? berserking spell.
It was something I wouldn't be able to cast with anybody nearby."*

## The One Cast (Chapter 71)

He's right, and the single on-page use proves it — badly. During the Ysalte/Amayon climax,
**Shi Maria** takes control of Carl's body while summoned as a totem. Needing extreme heat to brew
tattoo ink on the spot, she rifles his magic menu, makes him warn everyone to step back ("*I won't
warn you again. Step back now.*"), and casts *Self-Immolation* with the party standing around him.

He snaps it off within a second. It is still enough to burn a hole clean through the floor and
drop him into the apartment below, leaving the building melting around him. Donut's message
arrives immediately: **"CARL, YOU HURT MONGO AND KATIA!"** Katia is "grievously injured"; Mongo
loses his entire right wing and most of the scales and feathers down his right side. Both survive
— one of the potions Shi Maria upgrades on her way out will regrow the wing — but Carl wakes up
looking at the damage and thinking *Oh no. I did that.*
