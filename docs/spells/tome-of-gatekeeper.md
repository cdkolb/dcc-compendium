# Tome of Gatekeeper

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** Floor 11, from a loot box opened just before *The Parade Begins* countdown
    - **Type:** Advance Reader Copy — installable now, **unusable until the 12th floor**
    - **Cost:** 75 mana
    - **Target:** Any opponent, NPC, deity, or **OI entity**
    - **Duration:** (5 minutes + 1 minute per level of spell) × [caster's Intelligence /
      (target's Constitution + Charisma)]

## The Advance Reader Copy

Donut receives the spellbook as a paperback — unusual in itself, since it doesn't resemble any other
spell book she's owned — with a cover illustration of a gate and an angry human woman standing in
front of it. She hands it to Carl unread, unable to make sense of "the duration formula" in "nerd
math." The book's own description opens with an explicit release-date restriction:

> **Tome of Gatekeeper.**
>
> **This is an Advance Reader Copy. Street date: 12th floor.**
>
> **Cost: 75 Mana.**
>
> **Target: Any opponent or NPC or deity or OI entity.**
>
> **Duration: (5 minutes + 1 minute per level of spell) × [Your Intelligence / (Target's
> Constitution + Charisma)]**

The AI's voice narrates a lengthy, self-aware digression about "gatekeeping" in the internet sense
before getting back to mechanics:

> **Anyway... this is an Advance Reader Copy. That means you can install the spell now, but it won't
> work until a specific floor. In this case, the 12th floor.**

So the spell can be **learned and installed in Book 8**, but is confirmed unusable — not merely
untrained, but flatly inert — until the party reaches Floor 12. This page documents it now, ahead of
that floor, because it's acquired here; expect it to actually see use starting in Book 9's coverage
of Floor 12.

## Effect

> **This spell removes the target's ability to use any skills or abilities associated with their
> class for a short period.**
>
> **If this spell is cast on a deity, they will temporarily lose access to their Worshipper Base
> Associated skill and spell set. You probably don't know what that means yet. It's basically most
> of their spells and skills, but if it's a god made of lava, they'll probably still be able to
> fuck you up with lava. Get it? No? Don't worry, you'll figure it out.**

The duration formula scales directly with the caster's Intelligence relative to the target's
Constitution plus Charisma — meaning it hits weaker-willed, lower-Constitution targets far harder
and longer than tough or charismatic ones, independent of the target's raw level or class.

## Targets: Deities, and "OI Units"

The spell's target line is unusual for a crawler-tier spell: it explicitly names **deities** as a
valid target (stripping their class-based "Worshipper Base" kit, though not any innate elemental
power — the lava-god caveat above), and a third category neither Carl nor Mordecai recognizes:

> **Carl:** Mordecai, what's an OI unit?
>
> **Mordecai:** In what context?
>
> **Carl:** It's a spell target for Donut's *Gatekeeper* spell. It says opponents, deities, and OI
> units.
>
> **Mordecai:** I have no clue.

"OI units" are not explained anywhere in Book 8. What they are is a
[Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md)-pass topic; this page notes only that *Tome of Gatekeeper* works
on them by name, alongside ordinary opponents/NPCs and deities.

## Setup for Floor 12

Between the Advance Reader Copy's explicit 12th-floor lock and the spell's ability to strip a
*deity's* class kit, *Tome of Gatekeeper* reads as direct, deliberate setup for the
**Celestial Ascendency** fight the party is already bound for on Floor 12 (see
[The Celestial Ascendency](../mechanics/index.md#the-celestial-ascendency-book-6) for the general
mechanic). A spell that can neutralize a god's or an OI unit's class-based kit for a duration the
caster can stack Intelligence against is exactly the kind of tool a fight against ascending
divine/OI contestants would call for — and the book is unusually explicit that it can't be used a
single floor early.
