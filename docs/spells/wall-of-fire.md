# Wall of Fire

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** Book 4, Chapter 2 — a 15-mana spellbook from Donut's **Platinum "That Wasn't
      Too Smart, Was It?" Box**, awarded for attacking the Province Boss during the mantaur/Grull
      fight. She reads it back at the personal space in the same box-opening session that also
      gives her *[Astral Paw](astral-paw.md)*, before either of the notable Book 4 uses below
    - **Type:** Area-effect fire damage/barrier

## Effect

*Wall of Fire* conjures a burning barrier capable of igniting anything that passes through it. In
practice this cuts both ways: it reliably kills or damages enemies forced through it, but it can
also ignite the caster's own allies or environment if used carelessly in an enclosed space.

## Acquisition — Book 4, Chapter 2

Back at the personal space after the Grull/mantaur fight, Donut runs through a stack of boss and
achievement boxes — including a Platinum "That Wasn't Too Smart, Was It?" Box for attacking the
Province Boss. Mordecai narrates it as she reads:

> Donut's final, platinum box contained another magic book. It was a 15-mana spell called *Wall of
> Fire*.
>
> "That's an escape spell," Mordecai said. "It's about a 10 meter wide, two meter high wall of fire
> that lasts 15 seconds at level 1. It's a common spell. Powerful later on, but it's also fire.
> Fire tends to spread. When the magic goes away, if it's cast on something flammable, it stays on
> fire... Fire gets out of control very easily."

Donut's own reaction is dismissive — "It's not *Fireball*," she grumbles, "That's what I really
want" — before glowing as she reads the book anyway. This is the same chapter-2 box-opening session
that gives her *[Astral Paw](astral-paw.md)*, and it precedes both notable Book 4 uses below.

## Notable Uses — Book 4

- Cast to trap the sand-ooze boss **Mrs. Ghazi** and its summoner Ghazi inside a burning room
  during the Mad Dune Mage encounter — the fire catches Ghazi's cluttered lab alight and traps him
  on the far side of the flames, forcing him to grab his only prized possession and flee toward
  Carl and Katia instead of hiding
- Cast defensively against a swarm of thousands of exploding **Live Ammo Ball** gerbils outside
  Hump Town — backfires when the leaping gerbils ignite on contact with the flames, turning into
  miniature fireballs that still manage to set several dromedarian defenders on fire before dying

## Scaling

At level 1: roughly 10 meters wide, 2 meters high, lasting 15 seconds, for 15 mana. No further
per-level formula is given beyond Mordecai's level-1 baseline and the Book 8 effective-level-16
datapoint below.

## Book 6: Empowered, Not Granted

!!! success "Conflict resolved"
    This page previously flagged a conflict with the [Magic Missile](magic-missile.md) page, which
    claimed a new Book 6 class *grants* Donut *Fireball* and *Wall of Fire*. **That was a misread,
    and the prediction recorded here was correct: Book 6 empowers an already-known spell, it does
    not teach it.**

    The line in question is Mordecai's, in chapter 2, immediately after the AI finishes reading out
    the description of Donut's new
    [Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Superior Fire Demon's Hand Maiden](../items/enchanted-nipple-ring-of-the-superior-fire-demons-hand-maiden.md):

    > **"Donut, only cast it when you're by yourself. You have three fire spells now. *Fireball,
    > Magic Missile,* and *Wall of Fire*. All three of them are going to be much more intense. So,
    > be very careful."**

    "You have three fire spells **now**" is a stock-take, not an acquisition. No class is involved
    — the item is a nipple ring from a Legendary Beguiler's Box, and the "only cast it when you're
    by yourself" warning refers to the ring's separate *Bitch, What?* berserking spell, not to any
    of the three fire spells. Donut already knew all three going in.

The mechanism is a single line on the ring: **"All spells with a fire attack now deal
(Intelligence)% more damage."** Since Intelligence is Donut's headline stat, the multiplier is
large and grows all book. The effect is confirmed in play the first time she uses this spell on
Floor 8 — against a swarm of ghommids in Havana, her *Wall of Fire* "crackled bigger than ever
thanks to her new nipple ring."

The one genuinely new piece of classification in Mordecai's line is that **Magic Missile is a fire
spell**, which had not been established in any earlier book.

## Book 8: Level 16, Over Ten Thousand Kills, a Mile Long

Donut's Book 7 [Cloak of the Benevolent Champion](../items/cloak-of-the-benevolent-champion.md)
bonus (one Cloak-raised-to-15 spell may go to 16) goes to *Puddle Jumper* instead of *Wall of Fire*
(see [Puddle Jumper](puddle-jumper.md#book-8-level-17-almost-any-distance)), but her
[Brain Trust](../abilities/brain-trust.md) natural benefit still bumps *Wall of Fire*'s effective
cast up one level on its own — from a true level 15 to an effective **level 16**:

> Donut leaned forward and cast her level 15 *Wall of Fire*, which had been powered up to level 16.

Cast during the Floor 10 race against a migrating swarm of sugar hermits blocking the finish line,
the wall stretches far past anything shown for the spell before:

> "Whoa, shit!" I shouted as the wall appeared, running parallel with the side of the truck... The
> flames went straight up, easily a hundred feet into the air. **The line had to be a mile long.**

> "I killed more than **ten thousand** of them!" Donut shouted. "I didn't even go up a level! This
> is an outrage!"

## Scaling — Book 8 Note

A level-16 cast (base 15 + Brain Trust's +1) produces a flame wall roughly **a mile long** and about
**a hundred feet tall**, killing **over ten thousand** level-appropriate mobs in a single cast
without granting Donut any experience for it — confirmation that her level (and the mobs' relative
weakness) caps the spell's XP yield well below its raw destructive scale.
