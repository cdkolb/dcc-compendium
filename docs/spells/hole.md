# Hole

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** Spellbook-of-the-month mailbox subscription, Book 3
    - **Effect:** Creates a temporary hole in a chosen surface
    - **Duration:** ~5 minutes at low levels; can be toggled off early at will

## Overview

Donut receives this spell via her recurring mailbox subscription and is initially unimpressed —
she's outgrown [Magic Missile](magic-missile.md) and wants something flashier, like Fireball.
Mordecai talks her into reading it anyway. Per his explanation: "It makes a temporary hole on a
surface of her choosing. She'll need to level it up before it's too helpful, as a level one isn't
thick enough to get through most doors, but it's a useful spell. Especially if you're creative
with it."

Carl immediately sees the tactical potential, and the spell earns its keep — used to open
shortcuts through non-reinforced doors, and, most memorably, to decapitate a war mage mid-battle
(a clip the broadcast splices misleadingly to make Carl look unstable). It cannot be cast on
Safe Room doors.

## Scaling

Depth scales directly with skill level: a level 1 cast produces roughly a 1-inch-deep dimple; by
level 2, it reaches about 2 inches deep (precisely 6 centimeters, or 3 cm/level — the dungeon
uses metric internally), enough to punch through most non-reinforced doors. It does not work on
mobs directly, but it does work on the ground beneath a mob standing on a surface.

## Book 5 Notable Uses

Donut opens a *Hole* directly under her own feet mid-battle against Lucia Mar's rottweiler Cici,
using it to drop safely out of a burning treehouse pub — then snaps the hole shut the instant
Cici follows her head-first through the gap, decapitating the dog outright. Later, at the Butcher's
Masquerade, she casts a much deeper *Hole* through three stacked floors between the two identical
"goodwill ballrooms" hosting hunters and crawlers, which is what actually causes Mongo to end up in
the same physical (rather than illusory) room as the hunters' side, and which she uses again as a
last-resort collapse plan ("Escape Plan C") to drop the whole party out of a sealed ballroom.
