# Li Na's Dreads

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** **Li Na**
    - **Type:** **Dreads** — hostile auras, activated at will
    - **The four:** *Dark Purpose*, *Defile Soul*, *Bleeding Horror*, *Succulent Decay*
    - **Why they matter:** **Dreads are not classified as offensive magic**, so they remain legal
      during the Ramp-Up phase's magic restrictions (see the Mechanics pages for the Faction Wars
      Ramp-Up rules)
    - **Delivered by:** The clocktower **Extend Aura** synergy tower, plus Mistress Tiatha's wire
      hack
    - **Result:** All four trained to or near level 15 in the course of a single assault

!!! note "Exact-verification note"
    None of the four has a system description block. *Defile Soul* and *Succulent Decay* have **no
    described mechanics at all** — they are named and nothing more. Everything below is prose,
    party chat, and observed effect.

## Auras, Blessings and Dreads

Book 7 formalises a mechanic the series had only gestured at. Carl's own summary:

> The aura mechanic wasn't normally something I needed to worry about. We only had two crawlers in
> the guild who used it. One was **Imani**, who had a wide variety of aura effects imparted by
> touching her wings. Some were persistent, and she couldn't turn them off. The other aura effects,
> like some of her more specialized healing and protection spells, were called **Blessings**, and
> they could be turned on at will. Blessings drained her mana while they were active.
>
> The other crawler who used auras was **Li Na**.
>
> Hers were very, very different.
>
> The ones she could activate at will were called **Dreads**.

The counter exists too — Mordecai brews a potion that produces:

> **You have been Closed Off! You are no longer susceptible to aura effects, both Blessings and
> Dreads. This will not cancel existing effects nor will it negate your own auras.**

That potion is also what finally makes Li Na recruitable: without it, her own party could not stand
near her.

## The Legality Loophole

This is the whole reason the Dreads matter strategically. During the Faction Wars Ramp-Up phase,
offensive magic is banned. Dreads are auras.

> **Best of all, dreads didn't count as offensive magic.**

The delivery mechanism is a second exploit stacked on the first. The clocktower's **Extend Aura**
synergy — "*one of the few synergy towers that could be used during the offensive magic ban*" — is
combined with **Mistress Tiatha's** wire hack: dozens of wires dangling from the clock arms, run into
Club Vanquisher as an improvised antenna. Anyone within about **20 meters of the wires** catches the
aura.

Li Na's Dreads normally have almost no reach — *Dark Purpose* only affects someone **physically
touching her**. The tower and the wires turn a contact-range debuff into building-wide saturation.

## Dark Purpose

The one they chose, and by a wide margin the one the book actually shows.

> When we'd first discussed this next part of the plan, Li Na stated she had several dreads that
> would be effective if they were allowed to fill Club Vanquisher.
>
> We settled on one called **Dark Purpose**.
>
> **Normally, this was a fairly small-area aura, and it would only affect someone if they were
> physically touching Li Na.** Now, with the tower mixed with Tiatha's wire hack, anyone within
> about 20 meters of the wires would get hit with the effect.
>
> Mordecai said **he'd never even heard of this particular dread**. According to Li Na, **it was
> basically the same thing as Donut's [Why Are You Hitting Yourself?](why-are-you-hitting-yourself.md)**
> The feeling normally only lasted a moment, **but it accelerated the longer one was touching Li
> Na.** But because we were using the wires to amplify the signal, it would be almost impossible to
> escape.

Li Na's own comparison undersells it badly. Carl's verdict once it is running: "*This was not the
same thing as Why Are You Hitting Yourself? This wasn't even close.*"

**What it does.** After roughly a minute's delay, everyone in range begins destroying themselves.
Mork the ram pulls out his own eyes and reinserts them backward. A mantaur stabs himself in the
throat. Hundreds of taxidermied trophy heads and the rugs on the walls come alive and scream. Mork
then tries to rip out his own heart.

Then the part that makes it a *dread* rather than a charm: **ethereal white chains rise out of the
floor and drag the ghosts of the dead down through it.**

> **Warning: This area of Club Vanquisher has been defiled.**

The club's own PA system responds with what amounts to a demonic-incursion alarm:

> "**Shelter in place! Shelter in place! Demonic activity has been detected within the club! Security
> to the lobby! All hands! All hands! Implement Aura protections before you venture out!**"

## Training on the Job

The assault doubles as a training montage. Li Na cycles Dreads deliberately, burning each one up the
level track and moving to the next:

> **Li Na: Dark Purpose was level 4 when I cast. It is not meant to be used like this, and it
> leveled even more rapidly than we anticipated. It hit level 15, and I moved to a different dread.
> I am cycling my dreads and rapidly leveling them all. Right now I am using Defile Soul. Next I
> will train Bleeding Horror.**

Level **4 to 15**, in a single engagement, on an aura that normally requires skin contact to work at
all.

## Defile Soul

Named, activated in sequence after *Dark Purpose* hits 15, and **never described**. Contextually it
is what is running during and after the ghost-chain sequence, and the "*This area of Club Vanquisher
has been defiled*" warning may relate to it rather than to *Dark Purpose* — the text does not say
which.

## Bleeding Horror

Li Na's own explanation, chapter 52 — and note she has never made it work before:

> **Li Na: I've never gotten it to work before, but if someone with bloody hands gets near me, and I
> have the dread active, it's supposed to turn their blood against them. And then the blood would
> turn on my enemies. I've never gotten it past level two because they're usually dead within moments
> of coming near me. It takes a lot of mana.**

> **Li Na: … When mantaurs extend their claws, they bleed. That should be enough when the dread
> trains high enough. It is already level 5 and rising. You are marked as an ally. The blood won't
> hurt you, but if the new guards have aura protection, this will be better because the blood already
> under my control will still attack them.**

That last clause is the tactical point: blood already under her control keeps fighting even against
targets who are warded against auras. It routes around the counter.

Observed: a ram's own blood swirls "*like liquid mercury*" and pours into his mouth and nostrils, and
"*what appeared to be a sentient pool of blood rushed across the wall*" — Prepotente jars a sample.

!!! note "Naming inconsistency"
    The text calls this **"Bleeding Horror"** when Li Na announces her training plan and **"Blood
    Horror"** in her later message reporting it at level 15. Both refer to the same dread.

## Succulent Decay

The fourth and last, activated in chapter 52:

> **Li Na: I am ready. My Blood Horror is now level 15. I am turning on the last of my dreads, and I
> do not know how it will react. It is called Succulent Decay.**
>
> **Carl: Jesus. Okay.**

The Khepri temple door opens immediately afterward and the point of view moves inside. **No effect is
ever described.** "*I do not know how it will react*" is the last word the book gives on it.

## Collateral

Dreads do not distinguish carefully between targets in a building. Chapter 57 records one casualty
found afterward: "*The bearded cleric within the temple had apparently boiled himself alive in his
own stew, collateral damage from one of Li Na's dreads during the assault on Club Vanquisher.*"

There is also a downstream consequence for loot. In chapter 58 Li Na is offered the
[Necklace of Indelible Woe](../items/necklace-of-indelible-woe.md) and turns it down "*especially now
that she had several level-15 dreads that she could possibly lose if she used the necklace.*" Having
four maxed Dreads makes any spell-swapping item too dangerous to touch.

## Scaling

No per-level tables. What the book establishes:

- **Dark Purpose** — effect "accelerates the longer one is touching" the caster; range is normally
  contact-only
- **Bleeding Horror** — requires nearby bleeding targets to train at all, which is why it had been
  stuck at level 2 for Li Na's entire career; high mana cost
- All four trained from low levels to 15 (or near it) inside one extended engagement, because the
  tower let them run continuously against a full building
