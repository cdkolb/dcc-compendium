# Gloom Wraith Phase

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **User:** Carl
    - **Type:** Technically a **skill**, but it costs mana — the description says so explicitly
    - **Obtained:** Chapter 4, from a **Hell-Kissed Celestial Skill Potion of the Prince of Woe**
    - **Level:** **13** on acquisition
    - **Cost:** Each burst **halves** the user's current mana. Minimum 2 mana to initiate
    - **Effect:** A non-corporeal lunge that passes through unenchanted matter and "scrambles"
      unarmored flesh
    - **Notable kill:** Architect Houston (chapter 68)

Carl's third Celestial item, per Kevin the announcer, after the
[Scavenger's Daughter](../items/the-scavengers-daughter.md) and ahead of the
[Hairbrush of the Beefmaster](../items/enchanted-hairbrush-of-the-beefmaster.md).

## The Potion

> **Hell-Kissed Celestial Skill Potion of the Prince of Woe.**
>
> **In case you haven't figured this out yet, we're trying to kick things up a notch this year. If
> we're gonna do this, we want all of you fuckers as deadly as possible. Hence, this prize.**
>
> **It's time to bring out your inner demon.**
>
> **Drinking this potion will give you a random level 10 to 15 Celestial-tier skill. The skill will
> be combat based, and it will be something you don't already have.**
>
> **This skill potion is hell-kissed. That means this skill will be any combat-based skill from any
> Sheol-level mob level 200 or higher.**
>
> **Go ahead and drink it. Do it. Do it.**

After a flash-vision of Sheol and a demon-skull button:

> **You have gained a skill.**
>
> **You have gained the level-13 ability, Gloom Wraith Phase.**

## The Description

> **Gloom Wraith Phase.**
>
> **This scary-ass skill allows for non-corporeal, intangible bursts of speed. You and all your
> equipped items become non-corporeal during a lunge attack, allowing you to pass through
> unenchanted weapons and flesh and obstacles with ease. Mirrors that of multiple phase skills and
> spells, effectively combining both Path of Destruction and Rush.**
>
> **Upon activation, you become non-corporeal and rush forward. Any biological entity that is
> unarmored against non-corporeal attacks is... scrambled. Yeah. This is a good, gnarly spell. It's
> really fucking gross, but holy shit.**
>
> **Note: While this is a skill, it still requires mana to utilize. Each burst will halve your mana
> points. You must have at least two mana points to initiate a burst.**
>
> **At level 5, you have the option to designate a weapon or item that can remain corporeal as you
> rush forward.**
>
> **At level 10, you have the option to remain non-corporeal up to five seconds at a time with a
> cooldown of thirty seconds between bursts.**
>
> **At level 15, the cooldown lowers to six seconds between bursts.**

Mordecai's reaction to Carl and Donut's chapter-4 haul, taken together: "*You two are both some
seriously scary bastards now.*"

## Scaling

| Level | Behavior |
|---|---|
| 5 | May designate **one weapon or item to remain corporeal** during the rush |
| 10 | May sustain the phased state for up to **five seconds**, with a **30-second** cooldown |
| **13** | Carl's level on acquisition — he has the level-10 tier, not yet the level-15 cooldown |
| 15 | Cooldown drops to **six seconds** |

The mana cost is unusual in that it is **proportional rather than flat**: each burst halves whatever
mana is currently in the pool. A full bar buys many more bursts than the first one suggests, but the
pool never quite empties and never quite refills between uses in a long fight.

Book 8 uses the skill twice more (chapters covering the Floor 10 race and the Floor 11 climax) with
no new level, cooldown, or effect mentioned — Carl is never shown reaching level 15 with it
on-page, and there's no record of it being trained past the cap the way [Mind
Balance](../abilities/mind-balance.md) was.

## The Shi Maria Problem

The skill's most consequential appearance in Book 7 is a use that **never happens**. Buried in Carl's
suppressed notification spam, discovered in chapter 14:

> **Gloom Wraith Phase failed to activate. Mind Balance prevented activation.**
>
> **Your attempt to disable Mind Balance was prevented by Mind Balance.**
>
> **Your Mind Balance has risen to level nine.**

There are hundreds of these entries. **Shi Maria**, sealed in the tattoo on Carl's chest, has been
trying to cast his own skills without his permission, over and over, and
[Mind Balance](../abilities/mind-balance.md) — from the
[Enchanted Toe Ring of the Well-Balanced](../items/enchanted-toe-ring-of-the-well-balanced.md) — has
been silently stopping her every time. The specific attempt logged here would have killed the ogre
Carl was fighting and set off his non-magical bombs in the process, which would have killed Donut.

## Speedster

Chapter 57 pairs the skill with the **Speedster** benefit from the
[Hairbrush of the Beefmaster](../items/enchanted-hairbrush-of-the-beefmaster.md), and the combination
is better than either part:

> And best of all, I could combine it with my Gloom Wraith Phase, **allowing the distance I could
> travel while phased to be much further.**

## Uses

- **Chapter 14** — escaping a cloud attack
- **Chapter 15** — combined with *Walk on Air*: "*I activated Walk on Air and rushed upward, like I
  was running up a set of stairs*"
- **Chapter 68 — killing Architect Houston.** "*For my part, I activated Gloom Wraith Phase the very
  moment my feet touched the ground.*" The escape is messier than the kill: "*I'd cast Gloom Wraith
  Phase to escape the room, but I'd hit something magical on the way out, which had injured me.*"
  The skill passes through *unenchanted* matter only, and enchanted obstacles hurt. "*Either way,
  Architect Houston was dead. Burcu was dead. I'd barely escaped.*"
- **Chapter 71** — dodging the gale tower on the run to the Nest
