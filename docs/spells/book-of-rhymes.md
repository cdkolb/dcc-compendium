# Book of Rhymes

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** **Sister Ines Quiteria**, Cat Girl Poet Laureate
    - **Type:** Bard skill
    - **Level:** 15
    - **Source:** Her **patron**, the Nebulars — newly granted during Floor 8
    - **Effect:** Enhances her other bard spells

## Overview

*Book of Rhymes* is a force multiplier rather than a spell in its own right. Sister Ines receives
it from her patron partway through the floor, at level 15, and its entire function is to make the
rest of her bard kit hit harder.

Most bards operate on the **patron** system rather than the cleric/deity system — a patron supplies
money, spells, and equipment in exchange for entertainment, and unlike a god, a patron cannot smite
you. Doctor Bones explains the arrangement to Carl in almost exactly those terms. Ines's patrons
are the **Nebulars**, who are also her sponsors, and they invest in her heavily: they fund both
this skill and the push that takes *[Word Weaver](word-weaver.md)* to level 15.

## Why It Matters

The one on-page application is decisive. Per the broadcast recap:

> "...crawler Sister Ines Quiteria used her level-15 *Book of Rhymes* bard skill, which she just
> got from her patron, to enhance her *Word Weaver* spell to charm this creature, a level-125 city
> boss."

Without *Book of Rhymes*, *Word Weaver* does not reach a level-125 city boss. With it, Ines charms
the **Madre de Aguas** and inherits its regional control over every creature in the area — the
single most consequential act by any non-party crawler in the book, and the chain of events that
eventually drags a prince of Sheol onto Floor 8.

## Scaling

No per-level detail is given; the skill is granted at 15, the maximum standard level, and is only
ever seen operating there. What the book does establish is that the enhancement applies to her
bard *spells* generally, not to *Word Weaver* alone — Ines is also a cleric-style support caster,
buffing allies and throwing afflictions, and Carl notes she "mostly fought like Imani did."
