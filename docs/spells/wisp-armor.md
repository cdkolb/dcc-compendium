# Wisp Armor

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tome owner:** Carl — unread by Book 1's end, but read and cast by Carl himself in Book 2
    - **Obtained:** Bronze Boss Box
    - **Mana cost:** 5
    - **Target:** Self only

## Effect

Per its in-game description: **"Surrounds your body with tendrils of light. While ineffective
against physical attacks, this spell negates 75% of incoming damage from magic-based attacks.
Provides temporary immunity to mind-control effects."** Base duration is **5 minutes + 1 minute
per spell level**, with a 5-minute cooldown. It also has a cosmetic effect — described as making
the caster look "wispy and ethereal and druid-like."

## Scaling

The tome's own text gives one explicit formula and one vague promise:

> **Duration: 5 minutes + 1 minute per level of spell. Requires 5 minute cooldown.** ...
> **Higher skill levels increase both effectiveness and duration.**

The duration formula lets on-page durations be read as a level indicator. Three are confirmed
across the series:

| Confirmed on-page duration | Book / ch. | Implied level (5 min + 1 min/level) |
|---|---|---|
| 6 minutes | Book 2, ch. 11 (first self-cast) | Level 1 — *implied by the stated formula, not stated outright* |
| 7 minutes | Book 4, ch. 27 | Level 2 — *implied by the stated formula* |
| 10 minutes | Book 5, ch. 25 | Level 5 — *implied by the stated formula* |

These three are the **only** duration data points the text gives, and they're consistent with
(and only interpretable through) the level-1/2/5 formula — Carl's actual spell level is never
stated in narration at any of these points. The "**effectiveness**" side of the promise (the 75%
magic-damage reduction) is never revised with a new percentage anywhere in the series — Book 3
(ch. 24) shows the shield absorbing "almost a quarter" of a magic missile's damage in one hit
without a percentage attached, and no later book gives a number either. So: duration scaling is
confirmed via formula + three data points; effectiveness scaling is confirmed to exist in principle
but never quantified past the base 75%.

## Role in the Story

In Book 1, Carl can't afford to cast it himself (his mana pool is only 3 against the tome's
5-mana cost), so he considers handing it to Donut instead — it remains unread/untaught by the end
of Book 1.

## Book 2: Carl Reads It Himself

Cornered during the Grimaldi/circus quest and out of better options, Carl reads the tome and
casts it on himself before deliberately eating a parasitic worm infestation offered by the elite
Apollon the Mighty. The spell's mind-control immunity is what lets him keep his own free will for
several minutes while infested — long enough to reason with the possessed Grimaldi and talk him
down — before the infestation is cured with an antidote potion.

## Later Use — Books 3 Through 7

*Wisp Armor* stays in Carl's regular rotation for the rest of the series with no further upgrades,
replacements, or new effects shown on-page — only the duration increases charted in Scaling above.
It remains his go-to answer to charm/mind-control effects (it fails against Li Na's charisma-based
charm in Book 6, since that isn't technically mind control) and his default reflex whenever
incoming fire turns magical, right through the Book 7 psionic bombardment where Imani and
Prepotente both tell him to cast it.
