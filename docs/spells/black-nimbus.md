# Black Nimbus

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Caster:** Carl
    - **Obtained:** Granted by the
      [Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Defiler](../items/enchanted-nipple-ring-of-the-defiler.md)
    - **Type:** Involuntary Abjuration
    - **Cost:** Item-based (no mana)
    - **Cooldown:** Once every hour
    - **Target:** Single target
    - **Duration:** Instantaneous cast; the target suffers the effect for
      **(30 + [Caster's Intelligence + Constitution] − [Target's Intelligence / 2]) seconds**

## Effect

> **Has the same effect as wearing a shirt for the band Cattle Decapitation to church. Instantly
> removes target from the blessing of their deity and all associated buffs and effects. Will make
> it so target is unable to physically enter church or temple while the spell is in effect. Higher
> levels of this spell increase the possibility of forcing a smite upon the target.**
>
> **Warning! You probably don't want to cast this on members of the same religion more than two or
> three times in a row.**

Three distinct effects, then: strip the deity's blessing and every buff riding on it; lock the
target out of temples for the duration; and — at higher spell levels — a growing chance of calling
down a smite on them outright. The closing warning is unexplained, but the implication is that
repeatedly defiling the same faith draws that faith's attention back to the caster.

## Scaling

The duration formula is the only hard number: it scales up with the caster's **Intelligence +
Constitution** and down with half the **target's Intelligence**, meaning it holds far longer on a
brute than on a mage. The smite chance is stated to rise with spell level, but no per-level table
is given, and Carl never trains it.

## Notable Use — Ogun's Judgement (Chapter 17)

Carl's only on-page cast is a rescue attempt that fails in an instructive way. After the party
repairs Yemaya's shrine instead of Ogun's, the level-250 god **Ogun** arrives in person to punish
his two worshippers, **Paz** and **Anton**. Carl reasons that if *Black Nimbus* temporarily removes
all the benefits of worshipping a god, it might also remove Anton from the god's reach — but he can
only cast it on one of the two men, and casting anything at all risks drawing the god's eye. He
describes the odds as "like trying to stop a train by stepping in front of it."

He casts it on Anton anyway, following with two *Heal* scrolls. Ogun notices, and corrects him
without much anger:

> **"Black Nimbus," the god said. He sounded more curious than angry. "That was a good attempt.
> But all you have done is removed this man from my protection. From my mercy. Not from my
> wrath."**

The spell does exactly what it says on the label. It severs the connection to the deity — which
also severs whatever mercy that deity might have extended.
