# Club Vanquisher

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Members-only social club, Over City floors — the [Desperado Club](desperado-club.md)'s rival
    - **Access:** Mutually exclusive with Desperado Club membership — holding one pass generally blocks the other

## Overview

Club Vanquisher is the Desperado Club's upscale counterpart. For four books it exists as a single
line of Mordecai's, drawn as a contrast while he walks Carl and Donut into the Desperado Club
itself; the interior isn't shown until Floor 8 (see [below](#inside-at-last-book-6)):

> "There are two main clubs in the Over City. The Desperado Club and Club Vanquisher. Generally,
> once you have access to one, you're not allowed in the other. This one is easier to get into.
> Club Vanquisher is more like a country club where old clerics sit on leather chairs and smoke
> pipes and occasionally tell racist jokes."

Neither Carl nor Donut visits it in Book 2, and for three books afterward everything known about it
is contained in that single comparison — a genteel, clergy-heavy counterpoint to the Desperado
Club's Vegas-meets-Mardi-Gras
atmosphere. Access to it is also implicitly why the Desperado Pass locks a crawler out of Cleric
and Paladin classes (see [Desperado Club: Overview](desperado-club.md)) — those "goody-two-shoes"
classes appear to be Club Vanquisher's natural clientele instead.

## Inside, at Last (Book 6)

Floor 8 is where the book finally walks through the door. Carl goes in cold — "The cookbook held
precious little about Club Vanquisher… I was going in blind" — having gained access not through a
pass but through his worship of Emberus, via the temple entrance inside the
[Colón Cemetery](colon-cemetery.md). Havana's second entrance is in the Kuraokami temple on the
grounds of the Iglesia de Jesús de Miramar.

The greeting sets the tone:

> **Entering Club Vanquisher. Members Only.**
>
> **Heathens will find no solace here.**

**The vestibule** is small and clean, with a stained-glass door showing a winged angel rising
toward an inverted tree. The Ares-worshipping ram-headed guards insist the figure is Hellik; it is
in fact Taranis, Hellik's brother.

**The lobby** reads, unexpectedly, as "the lobby of a ski lodge": a brick fireplace, leather chairs,
dwarves playing chess and served by an elf waitress, and trophy monster heads on the walls
including a giant black dragon's. "A dozen stairwells led up and down to different places."
Mantaur guards work the room, and security is on heightened alert "especially since the caprid
incident" — Prepotente's hellspawn familiar attacking clerics in the Pandinus branch on Floor 5.

**The upper level** holds a market called Comforting Supplies, a guild hallway, a jeweller
("Blessed Sun Enchantments," one visit per day for members), and the fairy receptionist **Yasmine**,
a level-75 Wisp with the title Secretary of the Club Vanquisher Temple Portal.

### The Emberus High Temple of the Ending Sun

The club's marquee venue, and the closest thing Carl has to a church.

> **Entering the Emberus High Temple of the Ending Sun.**
>
> **Welcome home, Initiate.**
>
> **You are protected in this place.**

"We stood upon the sun." There is no roof and no walls except the one the entry door sits in; the
sky is black and the floor is a burning field of fire that is warm rather than painful. Perhaps two
dozen worshippers lie prostrate toward a distant glow. A marble fountain and a charred tree stump
rise out of the fire for a foot-washing ritual.

Its clergy are **Pater Coal**, a [Hobbledehoy](../species/hobbledehoy.md) High Cleric Supreme of
Emberus (level 101), and the openly hostile Ares cleric Potsy. Carl, admitted only as a
limited-access worshipper, is treated for slugpox here, learns of the murdered god Geyrun, and buys
a ring he would rather nobody knew about. The temple recurs across the book, and Carl's promotion
to **Devotee** — the ceiling rank for a non-Cleric — is an Emberus rank earned in its service. See
[Deity Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md#deity-mechanics-book-6).
