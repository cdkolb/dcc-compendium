# El Capitolio Nacional de Cuba

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Floor:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth") — see [Level 8](../floors/level-08.md)
    - **Region:** Havana, Cuba — Carl and Donut's phase-one and phase-three region
    - **Role:** The floor's starting chamber, and one of the region's two stairwells
    - **Fate:** Its stairwell doorway is destroyed during phase three, sealing the exit
      permanently; the building itself collapses shortly afterward

## Overview

Every crawler on Floor 8 begins inside "a large structure within your starting area." In Cuba, that
structure is the historic capitol building in downtown Havana. The system announces it plainly —
**"Entering El Capitolio Nacional de Cuba"** — and the room Carl and Donut step into is a marble
rotunda deliberately reminiscent of the US Capitol: blue and magenta floor tiles running in both
directions, enormous arched windows at regular intervals, a curved half-pipe ceiling tiled in
relieved bronze squares, and a round divot at the centre opening into an enormous cupola. The light
comes from what look like street lamps — warm and yellow, and entirely wrong for the hour.

Two details establish the floor's rules immediately. The windows show nothing on the minimap,
because the outside world hasn't been explored yet. And the brass lamps are hung with red and green
ribbon: it is December 10th, and the Earth being replayed around them is decorated for a Christmas
that already happened.

## As a Stairwell

The Capitolio holds one of the region's two stairwells — the one Carl and Donut are generated
beside, and therefore the one their phase-two key will be cut for. Several of the building's doors
are locked, and the stairwell itself needs a key that cannot be obtained until phase two begins.

Carl treats the building as a fortification from the first week, ringing the exit with alarm traps
and teleport traps long before there is anything to defend it against. That groundwork is what
makes the phase-three fight survivable.

## The Battle for the Keys

Phase three drops keyless squads directly onto keyholders' doorsteps, which makes the Capitolio a
killing floor by design. The battle that follows runs across several chapters and involves the
rival crawlers Quan Ch and Tserendolgor ("Ren"):

- Quan Ch strips the phase keys from Sister Ines and then from Donut before Carl and Donut ambush
  and kill him with teleport traps smuggled inside [Samantha](../characters/samantha.md),
  recovering both.
- A Mongolian death worm summoned mid-fight cracks the ceiling and tears open the structure.
- The stairwell doorway is destroyed, and the system's notice is final:
  **"The Stairwell Exit at El Capitolio Nacional de Cuba has been rendered inaccessible. All
  available keys through this exit have been destroyed."** See
  [Stairwell Chambers](../mechanics/index.md#stairwell-chambers-book-6) for why the doorway can be
  destroyed when the stairwell itself cannot.
- Tserendolgor, refusing to fight Donut for the last remaining key, hands Carl her gear and her pet
  Garret and detonates a bomb rather than let the space be used — denying the building to everyone.

Carl flees as the Capitolio crumbles behind him. With its exit gone, the region's *other* stairwell
— at the [Iglesia de Jesús de Miramar](../mechanics/locations.md#book-6-floor-8-cuba) — becomes the
only way off the planet, which is what forces the endgame plan.
