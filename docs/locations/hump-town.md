# Hump Town

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Floor:** 5 ("The Bubbles"), bubble #543, Air Quadrant
    - **Run by:** The [Dromedarian](../species/index.md#other-book-4-species) population,
      secretly infiltrated by the [Changeling Principals](../dungeon-factions/the-changeling-principals.md)
    - **Notable features:** "Bang twice to enter" entry policy at the main gate; a toll paid in
      gold, drugs, or "pleasure"

Hump Town is the last surviving camel settlement in Carl and Donut's bubble on Floor 5, home base
for the entire Air Quadrant campaign. It's a Dromedarian brothel town — the name is not
subtle — protected from the desert's twin suns and sandstorms by a magical, balloon-fabric shield
that has to be manually re-stretched by the town's residents after every storm.

## Layout & Notable Areas

- **The Toe** — the town's central tavern and the location of Carl's personal-space pub entrance;
  badly damaged when a chunk of the *Wasteland* crashes into the surrounding block, though the
  entrance to the personal space survives
- **Weird Shit Alley** — the street everyone in town is a little afraid of; home to the only true
  saferoom tavern available to crawlers without their own personal space
- **The Acrotomophilia Oasis ("the A.O.")** — the saferoom tavern in Weird Shit Alley; unpopular
  with most crawlers despite (or because of) its name
- **The Spit and Swallow** — a second tavern, used by Juice Box to discuss changeling business
  away from prying ears
- **City Hall** — houses the basement chamber where the gnome hostage Wynne is kept and drugged;
  burned down and collapsed after Carl's party stages a fake "grulke toad" break-in to rescue him
- **A branch of the [Desperado Club](desperado-club.md)** — present in Hump Town as in every
  transfer-station-sized settlement; features the usual Penis Parade, Silk Road, and casino areas

## The Siege

Hump Town survives two separate near-destructions in Book 4: first the initial bombing threat
after Wynne's collateral status is compromised (averted via Carl's decoy-negotiation ambush on
[the Wasteland](../dungeon-factions/the-wasteland.md)), and later a mass swarm attack by thousands
of regrouped [Frenzied Gerbils](../mobs/frenzied-gerbil.md) released from the wreckage of the
destroyed dreadnaught. Both crises are survived with the help of Carl's personal space, which
briefly shelters dozens of Dromedarian and Changeling children (plus several fellow crawlers)
during each attack.

## Notable Residents

- [Juice Box](../characters/juice-box.md) — a Changeling sex worker who becomes Carl's party's
  main source of information on the town's hidden faction politics
- **Skarn** — an orphaned juvenile Changeling child who runs a small telescope-rental business and
  later helps organize the town's children during the sandstorm evacuations
- **Bonnie** — the young Dirigible Gnome daughter of Commandant Kane, taken in by Juice Box after
  the destruction of the *Wasteland*
