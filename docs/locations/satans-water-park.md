## Overview

Satan's Water Park is exactly what the name promises: a kaiju-scale water park built around, owned
by, and lazily narrated by an invulnerable, comic-relief boss named Satan — who turns out to be a
mountain-sized hedgehog with an exaggerated posh British accent, utterly indifferent to the racers
tearing through his property.

> Satan himself was a country boss, but he was invulnerable, and he wasn't a boss that one was meant
> to fight. He was the owner of the water park, and he lounged near the exit, occasionally offering
> loud opinions on the things happening, occasionally poking at the racers.

Donut correctly predicts the accent before he ever speaks, on the strength of a single childhood
grudge — she once lost a cat show to a hedgehog named Jezebel. Satan does not disappoint:

> "Jolly good show," he said. "Pip-pip!"

His running commentary supplies most of the track's comic relief: "Blood 'ell," he grumbles from
his perch, rolling onto his back and toppling a roller coaster full of screaming riders by accident;
elsewhere he complains about the water "right into my delicate bum," and when he gets bored of
watching, he headbutts an entire loop-de-loop track apart just to see what happens.

## Track Layout

The park's first half runs through slide tubes reminiscent of the Iron Tangle's rail maze — easy to
follow, hard to survive if a racer takes a wrong turn, since the wrong path dumps a vehicle directly
*inside* Satan's own body. Ground level opens onto a toddler splash pad, a wave pool, and the
aforementioned loop-de-loop. A hidden trap beneath the park drops racers into a basement gauntlet
of Sheol demons and baby versions of the hedgehog boss, ending in a **Garden Gnome Sprinkler**
borough boss fight.

### The River of Sloth

Carl and Donut's allies are routed onto the park's slowest, safest option — a lazy river marked
by "a red-eyed sloth taking a rip from a bong":

> Our path was just south of the loop and was called the River of Sloth. This last one was clearly
> the slowest path. It was the water park's version of a lazy river. This was deeper than the water
> in the tubes, and there were supposedly rafts.

The cannibal tribes menacing the jungle roads around [Upano](upano.md) reappear here in the same
role, rebranded as "season pass holders" rather than cannibals.
