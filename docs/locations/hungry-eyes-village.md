# Hungry Eyes Village

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Floor:** [10](../floors/index.md) ("Don't Come in Last," Book 8)
    - **Type:** The race floor's central hub town — garage row, vendor village, mercenary market,
      and finish line, all in one
    - **Aesthetic:** A "1980s neon-infused, cyberpunk fever dream" — deliberately contrasted against
      the "faux-medieval style" of most other dungeon towns
    - **Notable NPCs:** **Hedy**, Carl and Donut's garage-attendant gremlin; the bugbears
      **Jasha and Radoslav**; **Darla-Dean**, the imp hostess at
      [C&W&U](chicken-and-waffles-and-upgrades.md)

## Overview

Hungry Eyes Village is Floor 10's one constant. Every one of the floor's seven [Heats](../mechanics/index.md#floor-10s-heat-ruleset-dont-come-in-last-book-8)
starts and ends here, and it's the only place on the floor where a team can safely repair, shop,
hire mercenaries, or simply catch its breath between races. The dots that first appear on Carl's
GPS as he closes in on the finish line are labeled, simply, **"Hungry Eyes Village and Finish
Line."**

Its look is a deliberate departure from the rest of the series. Most dungeon settlements go for a
"faux-medieval style"; Hungry Eyes instead reads as "straight out of a 1980s neon-infused,
cyberpunk fever dream" — gaudy flashing signage, a skyline of glowing temples, and streets full of
shouting vendors. Donut's own review: "You would think this pink-and-teal-and-purple aesthetic
would be right up my alley, but it's quite garish, don't you think? Like I'm trapped in a
clearance rack at a Claire's."

## The Team Roundabout

Each racing team is assigned its own numbered garage inside a private cul-de-sac called the
**Team Roundabout** — entering it, or the garage itself, is announced as ordinary Safe Zone
territory ("Safe room rules apply"). Carl and Donut's own arrival:

> **Welcome to Hungry Eyes. You are standing in your Team Roundabout.**

The Roundabout itself is an incongruously suburban pocket: "a large asphalt cul-de-sac ringed by
nine garage doors painted with numbers... Above, a blue sky with a red sun blazed, and the air was
a little hot. Tall trees ringed the small neighborhood, and the spaces between driveways were
filled with manicured green grass... The whole place even smelled like recently cut grass." A
garage numbered with a team's most recent finishing position sits open to the circle; a narrow
walking path connects the Roundabout to the neon vendor village beyond, through a shimmering arched
portal too narrow for any vehicle to pass. Eliminated teams' garages are marked with a painted
skull.

Lawn-chair life along the Roundabout is where most of the floor's cross-team socializing happens —
this is where Carl and Donut first meet the bugbear musicians **[Jasha and Radoslav](../characters/jasha-and-radoslav.md)**,
the razor fox mercenary **Chiyome**, and the womantaurs **Genesis** and **Rapture**, all trading
beer and gossip between heats.

## The Vendor Village

Past the portal, the tone flips entirely from suburban to neon-lit strip: "Another street was full
of flashing shops of all kinds with dozens and dozens of NPCs moving about. Shouting street vendors
with food carts and what appeared to be glowing souvenirs littered the sidewalk." Staffing is mostly
gremlin, with a smattering of Draconians. Unlike the Roundabout, the main square is explicitly not a
protected Safe Zone:

> **Entering Hungry Eyes Vendor Village.**
>
> **Safe room rules do NOT apply in the main square.**
>
> **Even in safe room areas, poisoning rules do not apply. Eat at your own risk.**

Food carts sell unlabeled meat-on-a-stick under a blanket system warning — "No food in Hungry Eyes
may be examined. Good luck." — and a cluster of temples forms the skyline, one of them marked with a
hanging black "X": the entrance to [Club Vanquisher](club-vanquisher.md), which Carl remains banned
from.

### The Hairpin and the Lollipop

Two named bars anchor the strip. **The Hairpin** advertises itself with "a Formula 1–style race car
in pink-and-purple neon" and doubles as a [Desperado Club](desperado-club.md) entrance ("Desperado
Club entrance in the back"). **The Lollipop**, marked by a neon gremlin waving an oversized candy,
is an 1980s-only karaoke bar — and secretly conceals the entrance to the **Stage Performance Guild**,
reachable only by singing a marked song to a perfect score the whole way through.

### Mercenary Market

Hungry Eyes is also where every team hires its allotted mercenaries for the heat ahead — "You can
add up to three mercenaries each heat. There's a bar out there in Hungry Eyes where you can hire
'em." Carl's own hires are negotiated through **Rosetta** and **Tipid**, and prices climb every time
a team loses a previous hire in the field.

## Hedy the Garage Attendant

Carl and Donut's own garage manager is decided within their first minute on the floor, in a job
interview that turns violent between two rival gremlin applicants — **Hedy** (a mechanic) and
**Waldrip Chris** (an animal trainer) — brawling over which discipline the team will need:

> **Hedy. Gremlin. Level 30 vehicle mechanic. This is an applicant to be manager of your garage...
> Hedy is an expert in all things mechanical... If you can limp your vehicle back to the garage,
> rest assured it'll be good as new by the time the next heat starts.**
>
> **Warning: Just because Hedy is a non-combatant NPC, it doesn't mean she's invulnerable. If
> something happens to her, your vehicle will not get repaired between heats. Hedy is not allowed
> to enter your safe room.**

Donut picks mechanical (and Hedy) specifically so a mechanical vehicle "won't ever try to eat"
Mongo; Waldrip Chris explodes on the spot the instant he's passed over. Hedy remains Carl's
garage attendant for the rest of the floor — patois-heavy, endlessly harried, and the one constant
NPC presence guiding the team through every upgrade choice. She is confirmed killed during the
Floor 10 evacuation into the arena at the very start of Floor 11, when the Team Roundabout's own
"ice-cream truck" garage is destroyed.
