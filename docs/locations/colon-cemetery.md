# Colón Cemetery / The Temple of Yemaya

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Floor:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth") — see [Level 8](../floors/level-08.md)
    - **Region:** Havana, Cuba
    - **System designation:** **Medium Ghommid Settlement** — corrupted, and unclaimable until the
      corruption is removed
    - **Renamed:** **Club Vanquisher Entrance Temple, The Temple of Yemaya**, after Carl repairs
      Yemaya's shrine
    - **Contains:** A [Club Vanquisher](club-vanquisher.md) entrance, and a saferoom pub called
      **The Yellow Zone**

## Overview

The real Colón Cemetery is, as Paz Lo puts it, "one of the largest cemeteries in Latin America…
it's like a park filled with monuments." The dungeon's version is walled behind a black spiked
fence and a gothic archway, permanently fogged, with a church hidden at its centre — and the system
tags it not as terrain but as a settlement:

> **Entering Medium Ghommid Settlement.**
>
> **Warning! This town has been corrupted. You may not claim or repopulate the town until the rot
> has been eradicated.**

The residents are [ghommids](../species/ghommid.md), creatures that settle "where the veil is
thin. Like cemeteries." Entering triggers the quest **Pueblo de los Olvidados** — the village of
the forgotten.

## The Corruption

The settlement's rot has a cause with a name: the orisha **Asojano**, Lord of Smallpox, squatting
in the cemetery and sickening its population. The ghommids are hostile on arrival because they are
dying, not because they are malicious — a distinction the floor's design does very little to make
obvious.

Notable ghommids include **Kipper**, a level-30 unsettled ghommid, and **Skylar Spinach**, a
donkey-headed "Two-Thirds of an Enraged Ghommid Splitter" who later becomes one of Carl's totem
cards (see [Splitters](../mechanics/index.md#splitters-book-6)).

## The Thorn Room

At the settlement's heart is a
[Celestial Thorn Room](../mechanics/index.md#celestial-thorn-rooms-book-6) holding seven broken
shrines — Ogun, Legba, Shango, Oshun, Asojano, Inle, and Yemaya — of which exactly one may be
repaired. Each god is present as a ghost, whispering at the crawler to choose theirs.

Repairing Asojano's shrine would cure the ghommids and win the quest outright, at the price of
returning a god the pantheon considers "too much of a dangerous asshole to let back into the
Halls." Acting on a private warning from [Katia](../characters/katia.md), Carl repairs **Yemaya's**
instead. The quest is won anyway, and the cost lands on other people: Ogun, passed over, kills Anton
Lopez outright and lays a permanent unhealable smite on Paz Lo.

## The Temple of Yemaya

The liberation sequence rewrites the location wholesale:

> **Medium Ghommid Settlement has been liberated.**
>
> **The surviving ghommids have been cured.**
>
> **Entering Club Vanquisher Entrance Temple, The Temple of Yemaya.**
>
> **Welcome, Friend of Yemaya.**
>
> **System Message. The Goddess Yemaya has re-ascended. She is welcomed into the family by all.**

Afterward the six unrepaired shrines vanish, leaving only Yemaya's. The ground becomes tiled and
threaded with blue tributary fountains, the tombs convert into shops, and the surviving ghommids
turn friendly and settle in as NPC residents. Yemaya herself appears as a level-250 two-tailed
mermaid goddess — see
[Deity Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md#deity-mechanics-book-6) for the temple-appearance rule that
makes a goddess standing in her own temple so dangerous.

## The Yellow Zone

The cemetery's saferoom is "a crumbling, marble, gothic-style crypt with a little neon sign hanging
on it that read 'The Yellow Zone.'" The book never takes the reader inside — the pub exists in the
text as an exterior and a name only.

## Club Vanquisher

The temple at the cemetery's centre doubles as one of Cuba's two
[Club Vanquisher](club-vanquisher.md) entrances, which is how Carl — a worshipper of Emberus
rather than a Desperado Club regular — gets access to the rival club at all. The other Havana
entrance sits in the Kuraokami temple on the grounds of the Iglesia de Jesús de Miramar.
