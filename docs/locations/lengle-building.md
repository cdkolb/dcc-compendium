## The Track

Announced simply as "Track: The L'Engle Building," the race requires travelling from the top floor
to the bottom for Heat 6, then back up for Heat 7:

> **Tasks required to complete: Multiple. This track has nonlinear paths, meaning there is no
> direct route to the finish line. You must get from the top floor to the bottom floor for this
> sixth heat. For Heat 7, you will return.**
>
> **Special Rules: All remaining heats will be competing on the same track. As we have over 300
> heats remaining, this means the racers will be shrunk to accommodate the track, which is the
> interior of a human apartment building.**
>
> **Environment: It's a 7-floor apartment building. You may not exit the building during the race
> except through the front door.**
>
> **Hazards: Black Lung Smog. Different apartments may have additional hazards... assuming this is
> an accurate depiction of a human apartment, there'll be fleas and poorly trained Yorkshire
> terriers and the loudest, most annoying, most selfish people you've ever met...**

## Scale and Atmosphere

With well over three hundred heats needing to share a single seven-story building at once, every
racer is shrunk drastically to fit — from roughly the size of a Lego brick in Heat 6 to about the
size of a computer mouse (three times larger) by Heat 7. Teams start inside the building's ductwork,
sewer pipes, and toilets — "we're inside of a goddamned waterless shitter lined up like turds" — and
glide or fall from there into individual apartments, each one hazed with a fog-machine-like mist.

The building's most disquieting visual is constant and unexplained until the AI spells it out:
every human, animal, or photographed figure inside an apartment is a **Human Shell** or
**Dog/Cat Shell** (Level 99), its eyes sewn shut with black wire in an "X" pattern — except for
whichever figure is central to that apartment's particular what-if scenario. Disturbing a shell
enough to "rupture" its loop spills giant insects (a "Ruptured Feast Hive" of Sacred Feaster
Scarab Beetles) out through its face.

## Layout Notes

Specific apartments visited or referenced on the page include 130 (first floor), 214 and 310 (third
floor), 4th-floor Archie Mu's apartment, 614 and 712 (sixth/seventh floor, both tied to Louis and
Tran respectively), and 728. See
[Mechanics: The What-If Apartment Simulations](../mechanics/index.md#the-what-if-apartment-simulations-ad-infinitum-book-8)
for what each apartment actually shows and who it belongs to — this page documents the building
itself rather than repeating the simulation content apartment by apartment.

A planned floor boss — described in the AI's own aside as "the Sacred Guardian of the Immortal Tem,"
a hundred-foot construct — never finishes assembling correctly due to an in-universe scripting
error tied to the god Khepri, leaving the invulnerable Scarab Beetles roaming loose as a straight
hazard rather than a proper boss encounter.
