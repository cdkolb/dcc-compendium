# Fort Freedom

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Floor:** 6 ("The Hunting Grounds")
    - **Formerly:** The Naiad royal river palace
    - **Occupied by:** The Naiad Confederacy (see [Naiad](../species/naiad.md#the-confederacy)),
      then squatted by [Claude Sludgington the Fourth](../bosses/claude-sludgington.md) in its
      throne room
    - **Liberated by:** [Tsarina Signet](../characters/tsarina-signet.md)'s forces, with Carl and
      Donut's help — Part One of the *Vengeance of the Daughter* quest

## Overview

Fort Freedom is the submerged former seat of Naiad royal power — a decaying castle standing about
a quarter-mile offshore in the river dividing Floor 6's human and High Elf territories, its
ramparts crumbling and its stained-glass windows shot through with holes. The Confederacy that
seized it generations earlier never bothered to maintain the place, leaving huge sections
flooded and abandoned; by the time of Book 5, its own throne room has been quietly overtaken by
an unrelated boss monster (see [Claude Sludgington the Fourth](../bosses/claude-sludgington.md))
that the Confederacy's remaining leadership either couldn't or wouldn't deal with.

## The Assault

Signet's forces — a mixed group of bush elves, [were-castors](../species/were-castor.md), pixies,
a shell-mage tortoise (Edgar), and paper "blood and ink elemental" tattoo-warriors summoned via her
*Ink Marauder* spell — stage a coordinated amphibious assault: Carl distracts the castle's guards
by launching the possessed head [Samantha](../characters/samantha.md) into the water as a
loud, indestructible decoy, drawing the defenders toward the front gate while Signet's real
strike team slips in from the undefended air/water approach. The castle falls with minimal
resistance once its guards are lured away, but the operation costs the lives of Nadine and Clint
(see [Were-castor](../species/were-castor.md)) when Claude Sludgington is accidentally summoned
into the middle of the fight.

## Aftermath

With the Confederacy defeated, Signet declines to rule in her murdered mother's place, citing her
own curse (water will not touch her skin). The quest immediately pivots — see
[Timeline](../timeline/index.md#floor-6-the-hunting-grounds-book-5) — toward Signet's real target,
her half-sister [Queen Imogen](../characters/queen-imogen.md).
