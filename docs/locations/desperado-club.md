# Desperado Club

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Cross-instance nightclub, bazaar, and members-only social venue — not a faction
      or organization; nobody in the text is shown running it or acting on its behalf
    - **Access:** Via a Desperado Pass neck tattoo, awarded from certain loot boxes (or, for at
      least one NPC, promised as payment for a job)
    - **Structure:** Exists as a shared instance across every Over City location — enter through
      any local branch, and you're in the same room as crawlers and NPCs from anywhere on the
      floor. Confirmed (Book 3) to also maintain entrances on non-Over-City floors like the Iron
      Tangle, so it isn't strictly limited to Over City levels
    - **Rival club:** Club Vanquisher — access to one generally excludes the other
    - **Notable staff:** Clarabelle (Crocodilian bouncer), [Quint](../characters/supporting-cast.md) (Half-Djinn pharmacist)

## Overview

First introduced in Book 1 as a mysterious tattoo with no explained purpose, the Desperado Club
turns out to be a massive, cross-instance nightclub/bazaar complex, physically marked by a tower
with a rotating knife sign reading "So fun it hurts" and "Members only section + public pub." Per
Mordecai, it's the wilder of the Over City's two major clubs: "This place is like your Las Vegas
Strip mixed in with Mardi Gras and the discos of the 1970s," in contrast to **Club Vanquisher** —
"more like a country club where old clerics sit on leather chairs and smoke pipes."

The club won't admit "goody-two-shoes" crawlers — holding a Desperado Pass locks a crawler out of
Cleric, Paladin, and several Monk classes entirely during race/class selection. Mordecai
repeatedly warns Carl and Donut never to go without an escort.

## Layout

- **Public pub (first room)** — open to anyone, no pass required, always busy
- **Main room** — members-only; a 200-person dance floor with a retro-futuristic, *Blade
  Runner*-style aesthetic, populated mostly by disposable "bot" NPCs that vanish the instant a
  real crawler or NPC takes their place, plus a handful of roaming Elites
- **Bitches / Penis Parade** — brothels (female- and male-clientele respectively); crawlers are
  discouraged from visiting either, for safety and for the on-air ridicule that follows
- **Jobs** — a quest board, mostly NPC assassination and theft contracts
- **The Silk Road** — a marketplace mirrored on the floor below, selling higher-end magical goods
  than general stores, including a booth run by the hobgoblin explosives dealer
  [Pustule](../characters/supporting-cast.md)
- **Guild-O-Rama** — houses several rogue-themed guild rooms (an explosives guild among them)
- **Playroom** — a familiars/pets area (no pets otherwise allowed inside); safety not guaranteed
- **Casino** — not yet open on Floor 3; features a High Roller's Roulette Table and a Wheel of
  Fortune, playable only with non-transferable comp chips won as loot
- **The Hunting Grounds** — a staircase down, accessible only once a crawler reaches Floor 6
- **Casino** — open by Floor 4, with a working High Roller's Roulette Table and Larracos card
  tables, playable with non-transferable comp chips

## Scale and Access (Book 3)

The club's physical scale varies enormously by branch — the Iron Tangle's Station-131 location is
noticeably smaller and quieter than the Over City flagship, staffed by a skeleton crew including
**Astrid**, a Bloodlust Sprite assistant manager (Level 125). Entrances are tied to specific
station numbers ending in 1 (members-only, guarded) versus 9 (public pub only) along a given
line — a pattern crawlers use to predict which stops are worth a detour. Every branch maintains
its own private security/bodyguard system regardless of scale; hired cretin bodyguards (see
[Species](../species/index.md)) can be purchased outright by a patron to protect a specific person
for the duration of a visit, as Donut's party does at Station 131.

## The Recruitment Pipeline (Miss Quill Connection)

The club has a genuinely dark undercurrent that ties directly into Book 2's main quest. Local
elves recruit women from other settlements with the promise of club jobs — bar and brothel work
— and the rumor that working there is a path toward eventually descending through the Hunting
Grounds to safety. It's a lie: recruits are instead delivered to
[Remex](../mobs/remex.md), Miss Quill's undead husband, who "twists" them. Most die outright or
become the [krasue](../mobs/krasue.md) terrorizing the settlement; a rare few, like the dying
woman **Burgundy**, survive as day-human, night-monster hybrids. This recruitment pipeline is the
actual answer behind [The Sex Workers Who Fell from the Heavens](../floors/level-03.md#quests)
quest — the dead women found in the alleys every morning are club recruits who didn't survive
Remex's transformation.

Separately, a member of the [201st Security Group](../dungeon-factions/index.md) reveals he was
sent to assassinate a target specifically as payment for a promised Desperado Club membership —
club access is valuable enough in-world to be traded as a reward for contract killing.

## Floor 6 (Book 5)

Carl's opening-day raid on Zockau, Floor 6's capital city — blocking off the club's entrance bar
mid-battle — strands a group of hunters inside the Zockau branch's Hunting Grounds level, unable
to leave until the mayor (a mantis, eventually revealed to be Vrah) approves rebuilding the
entrance. Management responds by permanently segregating the two groups: each floor of the
dungeon a crawler has reached maps to a corresponding physical floor inside the club (crawlers
enter and exit on their own floor; hunters are confined to theirs), with no more mixing between
them. A concealed stairwell — reached by removing the tank lid of a toilet in the last stall of
the women's restroom and pulling a hidden latch — connects the floors for anyone willing to sneak
past the rule, which is how Carl and Donut reach the Hunting Grounds level directly to meet with
changeling contacts hiding there. That level includes its own restaurant, casino, "Silk Pavilion"
market, and skill-guild hallway, staffed by assistant manager **Astrid** (see [Bloodlust
Sprite](../species/index.md#other-book-3-species)), the same rank/role Book 3 introduces at the
Iron Tangle's Station-131 branch.

## Notable Incidents

- **Lucia Mar's rampage:** banned from the club after her two rottweilers kill two attendants and
  another patron's pet in the playroom; she then freezes the entire room with an unexpectedly
  powerful spell, robs the bar, and kills an elite gnoll — all in one visit. The playroom closes
  for a week afterward, and the System AI is later implied to be deliberately buffing enemy
  difficulty in response to "high performers" like her getting too strong too fast.
- **The wild magic burst evacuation:** during the climax of [The Fools Who Broke the
  Glass](../floors/level-03.md#quests), the club's back room — the safest nearby evacuation point
  — is sealed off by Borant to insulate the venue from the incoming blast, forcing Carl to
  reroute everyone without club access to a different stairwell entirely.

## Book 8: Shadow Mimic Infestation

By Book 8, the club is under new management — **Hamed**, the Night Wyrm, husband of the late
Astrid, banning all crawlers on taking over (see [Dungeon
Factions](../dungeon-factions/index.md#other-book-7-dungeon-groups)). The casino itself has been
rebuilt more ornately than before, with a new Wheel of Fortune croupier standing in for the previous
one, Tito.

That croupier, "Mitch," turns out to be an **Adolescent Shadow Mimic** — see [Species: Shadow
Mimics](../species/shadow-mimics.md) — running a long-planned ambush with the casino's entire
furniture set (tables, chairs, chandeliers, slot machines) revealed as more mimics the moment the
trap springs. See [Mitch](../bosses/mitch.md) for the fight itself.

The aftermath permanently changes the club's status:

!!! quote "Mordecai, Book 8"
    "If the Desperado Club is infested, you guys can't go back. Those things are almost impossible
    to root out. The whole place will need to be fumigated. They have a special ability to avoid
    most detection spells. They're like one of Carl's level 15 traps. All but invisible. You should
    all devise safe words and quiz each other every day from now on. They can mimic crawlers, and
    the system will label them as such until you discover them."

The club is left **effectively off-limits going forward**: even setting aside Hamed's standing ban
on crawlers, the party itself has no safe way back in until (if ever) the infestation is cleared.
Several of Hamed's own stripper-band employees — Splash Zone, Gluteus Maxx, and Doctor Bones — go
missing inside the club on an errand to see Hamed shortly before the ambush and cannot be reached
afterward; the book does not resolve whether they are casualties of the same infestation or a
separate incident. Whether Hamed's own leadership knew about the mimics in advance is not addressed.
