# The Plains of Larracos

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Floor:** 9 ("Faction Wars")
    - **What it is:** The battlefield territory surrounding the sunken city of
      [Larracos](larracos.md), divided into **nine numbered pie-slice zones**, one per attacking
      team
    - **The tenth team:** Team Retribution, the NPCs, holds no plains territory — they defend the
      city itself
    - **Terrain:** "*mostly flat, except for the northernmost area of the map*"
    - **Movement:** During the Ramp-Up phase, crossing into enemy territory is only legal through
      designated **one-kilometre corridors**

## Layout

Ten teams start Faction Wars — "*Nine attacking teams and one defending team, the NPCs, who have
decided to call themselves Team Retribution.*" The nine attackers each get a wedge of the plains
radiating out from the Larracos funnel; the NPCs get the city.

The land itself is unremarkable apart from the north. The northernmost slice (spot 1) is rocky
mountain country with lava flows, giving way southward to rolling hills (spot 2) and then to open
flats. The orcish north also holds most of the floor's water — the rivers there feed "*the large,
fast-moving river that eventually drained into Larracos itself*," which passes through
[Shanty Town](shanty-town.md) and plunges into the funnel as the waterfall that lands on the
Piccadilly Theater's roof.

Distances are measured in single-digit to low-double-digit miles: the Princess Posse's northern
border with the Operatic Collective is "*a good ten miles away*," and the Blood Sultanate's castle
sits "*just about eight miles*" in from its border with the Posse.

### The Corridors

> "Egress into enemy territory by non-scouts is only allowed through narrow, kilometer-wide
> 'gates.' Each corridor is at the same spot, and it is where the enemies are massing."

Each pair of adjacent territories is joined by one fixed one-kilometre corridor. Scouts are
exempt; everyone else funnels through, which turns every border into a known, pre-registered
killing ground. The Posse's response is to build a no-man's-land of Tripper-proof traps and
trenches on their own side. See
[The Four Phases of Faction Wars](../mechanics/index.md#the-four-phases-of-faction-wars-book-7).

## The Nine Territories

| Spot | Team | Base and notes |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | The Bone Clan / Skull Empire (orcs) | **King's Point** — "*the only of nine pie pieces that came with a pre-built castle, and it sat in a highly defensible position atop a small, rocky mountain that occasionally had lava flow around it.*" Destroyed by an engineered eruption during Operation Ruin: the Semeru dwarves agitate the mountain's lava tubes, and the blast "*blow[s] the top off the small mountain and completely obliterate[s] the castle, leaving nothing but a throne room just sitting there in open air.*" |
| **2** | The Operatic Collective | Rolling hills south of the orcish peaks. Eliminated when Volteeg detonates a tank, killing Warlord Hortense; their assets are awarded to the Bone Clan. |
| **3** | The Princess Posse | **The FUPA** — the *Fortress of Unyielding Power and Authority*. "*'Fortress of Unyielding Power and Authority?' I laughed. 'F U P A. The FUPA.'*" Built from scratch on mostly flat ground with a few hills. Its flag room is the site of Baroness Victory's one and only kill. Survives a Gondii brain-worm infiltration and a Reaver paratrooper drop. |
| **4** | The Blood Sultanate (naga) | The Sultana's castle, deliberately sited on rocky ground "*to stop anyone from tunneling in from below*." Taken in **Operation Snake Pit**; Princess Vinata dies and the team is eliminated. |
| **5** | The Prism Kingdom | Empress D'Nadia's base, the first major castle to fall — taken during the Ramp-Up via the southern trench assault. The "former Prism castle" then changes hands repeatedly between Madness and Dream mercenaries. |
| **6** | The Dream | The site of **the Sugar Cube** — the high-elf castle from Floor 6, *Zerzura*'d down to Floor 9 at the end of Book 5. See the note below. Demolished outright when three gods fight across it. |
| **7–8** | The Madness | Two slices. Contains **Architect Houston's operating theatre**, his lair beside the throne room — "*Nobody has managed to get into Houston's operating theater.*" Carl finally does, and kills him there; the room is rigged to a dead-man's switch. |
| **9** | The Reavers | A castle built as a shifting honeycomb — "*the very front part was a large, featureless room, which in turn led off to dozens of honeycomb-shaped hallways … stacked up to the ceiling, which was about three or four stories high*," with the cells constantly and randomly re-arranging themselves. The throne room sits behind the centre honeycomb. Warlord Fang dies here, hiding inside his own dragon. |

!!! warning "The Sugar Cube's chain of custody is inconsistent in the source"
    The [High Elf Castle](high-elf-castle.md) — nicknamed the Sugar Cube — lands on Floor 9 "*in
    the territory of the Lemig Sortion*," who use it as their headquarters. When Commander
    Stockade dies, the system announces that "*All assets of the Lemig Sortion have been awarded
    to the Madness*" — yet the castle is thereafter consistently referred to as **the Dream
    castle**, in spot 6, and it is the Dream that is eliminated when it falls. The book never
    reconciles how the property passed from Lemig to the Madness to the Dream. The wiki records
    both statements rather than picking one.

    The text also uses "**Democratic Sortion**" and "**Lemig Sortion**" interchangeably for the
    same team.

## Territory Changes

Territory is not fixed. As teams are eliminated their assets are formally awarded to another team
by system message, and the Peeling of Larracos only triggers once **four teams remain**. One
unplanned faction is created mid-floor rather than eliminated: the **War Mage Rebellion**, which
kills King Stalwart, seizes the [Gate of the Feral Gods](../items/gate-of-the-feral-gods.md), and
takes over Bone Clan territory as a new team in its own right. See
[The Faction Wars Teams](../factions/faction-wars-teams.md).

## Other Named Places on the Plains

- **[Area 52](area-52.md)** — the Princess Posse's forest airbase and saferoom, and the launch
  point for their missile batteries. **Full page.**
- **The Temple of the Dagda** — the season's out-of-city
  [Club Vanquisher](club-vanquisher.md) entrance, which is the one that doesn't get trashed. See
  [Larracos](larracos.md#temples-and-other-venues).
- **The southern trench network** — the Posse's engineered approach to the Prism Kingdom, built
  with Dazzler trap modules, an exploit of the *Build Trench* spell, and an acid-filled trench
  trap.
- **The volcano** — the mountain under King's Point, deliberately erupted by the Semeru to open
  Operation Ruin. Its lava flow clogs the main river, reaches Shanty Town, and pours into the top
  rings of Larracos.
