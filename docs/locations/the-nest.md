# The Nest

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Floor:** 9 ("Faction Wars")
    - **Where:** [Shanty Town](shanty-town.md), right at the edge of the hole leading down into
      [Larracos](larracos.md)
    - **What it is:** A master-quality granite **synergy tower**, keyed to **Atrocity-class**
      spells
    - **Built by:** Li Na's builder squad, led by [Toyotomi](../characters/toyotomi.md) the
      rhinoceros
    - **Manned by:** [Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md) — her sniper perch and
      command post for the back half of the book

## What It Is

The Shanty Town settlement menu allows any number of automated defence towers but only "*a
handful*" of **manned** towers, called **synergy towers**. A synergy tower does nothing unless it
is occupied by someone whose own abilities match the synergy type it was built for — see
[Base and Tower Construction](../mechanics/index.md#base-and-tower-construction-book-7).

The Nest is the Posse's expensive one:

> "Li Na's builder squad led by Toyotomi would get to work on a very tall, very powerful, very
> specialized, very expensive synergy tower. It would be placed right at the edge of the hole
> leading down into Larracos … this particular tower had a very simple name … It was called The
> Nest."

It took almost two full days to build, and it is "*by far, the tallest structure outside of the
sunken funnel of Larracos*" — tall enough to give Donut line of sight over the entire battlefield
from the top.

## The Synergy Choice

The original plan was mundane. A sniper-synergy tower would have sharpened Donut's targeting
enormously — but her [Tiara of a Thousand Lights](../items/tiara-of-a-thousand-lights.md) already
solved that problem, since "*All Donut needed was line of sight to apply her sniper skills.*" That
freed the slot for something far worse:

> "The Nest was instead built to have synergy with something called Atrocity-class spells."

This single decision is what makes the back half of the book possible. An
[Atrocity-class spell](../mechanics/index.md#atrocity-class-spells-book-7) cast from the Nest is
amplified in both power and area — and area is exactly the limiting factor on
*[War Crime](../spells/war-crime.md)*:

> "Donut's War Crime spell was both simple and horrific in both usage and scope. And while the
> area of effect was relatively low, the synergy tower's ability to upgrade Atrocity spells and
> allow them to be cast over a wide area made War Crime a true spell of mass destruction."

The same amplification is what lets *[Rolling Battle Formation](../spells/war-crime.md)* ball up
roughly 80,000 combatants at once, and what makes *[You're Not Done
Yet](../spells/youre-not-done-yet.md)* a battlefield-scale flesher factory rather than a corpse
trick.

## Construction and Stats

Examined mid-build:

> Master Quality. This fortification is at 150% Strength. This edifice is currently under
> construction. 1/15 Enchantments active. Current Enchantments: Increased Strength.

Toyotomi's rules of thumb: each block takes a maximum of 15 protective enchantments, which bleed
outward to nearby blocks in a net pattern; completing the build as master quality lifts the
structure to 200% strength and makes it glow gold; and his own *Edifice Shield* spell doubles that
again, to 400%. The tower is left unpainted granite specifically so the mages have time to coat it
in protection spells before the fighting starts.

### Donkey Kong

An expensive last-ditch interior defence. Like castles, manned synergy towers must by rule have a
path to the main room — but nothing says the path has to be survivable. When Donut hits the
switch, the stairs collapse into a ramp and barrels of assorted unpleasantness start rolling down
it.

## In the Book

The Nest is where most of the Peeling phase is fought from, and it takes a brutal beating. Its
integrity is tracked in system readouts as the battle wears on — 45%, then 25%, then 10%, then 8%.
**Lucia Mar** storms it personally, killing the NPCs **Bomo** and **Toyotomi** on the way up
before Rend kills her last rottweiler and Carl forcibly conscripts her.

The tower survives, badly damaged, and Ferdinand "directs" its repair in the lull before the
final act. Donut's three Atrocities — the casts that earn her the second class **The Oak Fell**
and the title **Champion of Nekhebit** — are all cast from the top of it.
