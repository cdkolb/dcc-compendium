# The FOCSA Building

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Floor:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth") — see [Level 8](../floors/level-08.md)
    - **Region:** Havana, Cuba
    - **Shape:** Green and tan, **Y-shaped**, 32 storeys of regular residences plus penthouses and
      a roof
    - **Role:** Site of Book 6's final battle, and of the deaths of Sister Ines and the goddess
      Ysalte
    - **Fate:** Destroyed floor by floor during the fight

## Overview

Havana's FOCSA tower becomes the endgame's fixed point for a simple reason: during the planet-wide
[Demon Eviction](../mechanics/index.md#demon-eviction-book-6), the demons converge on it "as if
they knew their best chance at getting home was there." What roosts on top of it is the demon
prince **Amayon**, riding the city boss **Madre de Aguas** — a snake reared vertical like a spire,
pouring fire into the sky at the centre of a tornado of bird and bat demons, visible from across
the city.

## Layout

The tower is a genuine mixed-use building, replayed intact from the Earth memory:

- **Ground floor** — an open-air shopping mall wrapping the perimeter: grocery stores, electronics
  shops, bakeries, all of it populated by memory ghosts going about a December day they have no
  idea is being watched.
- **Residential lobby** — elevators and a stairwell leading up.
- **Floors up to 32** — ordinary apartments. The 32nd is "the top floor of the regular residences.
  Everything above here was through a separate stairwell and consisted of expensive, penthouse
  apartments."
- **Penthouses and roof** — reached by a separate service stairwell.

The Y-shaped floor plan matters in the fight: three wings radiating from a core means no single
sightline covers the building, and collapsing one wing does not immediately drop the others.

## The Final Battle

The battle spans the roof and works its way down through collapsing floors.

- Louis kills **Sister Ines**, which summons her divine patron **Ysalte**, a goddess native to
  Larracos and known as "The Downward Spiral."
- **Amayon** is freed from the Madre de Aguas shell but remains enslaved to Ysalte, who must hold
  control against a ten-minute timer or lose him.
- In the last T'Ghee card battle of the book, **Paz Lo** — Ysalte's own stolen totem — kills the
  goddess with Katia's reused [Bolt of Ophiotaurus](../items/bolt-of-ophiotaurus.md) and his own
  self-destruct. See [Deity Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md#deity-mechanics-book-6): gods are
  invulnerable on this floor *except* to their own summoned totems.
- Carl teleports himself inside Samantha to drive his health down and trigger **The Martyr's Path**,
  bathing the tower and Amayon in Sheol Fire — which, per the cookbook's own warning, makes a demon
  exponentially stronger. That is the point.
- Donut ends the sequence stranded on "maybe the 25th or 26th floor" amid falling girders as the
  building comes apart around her.
- Escaping the wreckage, a misfired **Golden Combo** fuses Carl with the boss **Shi Maria**, who
  takes control of his body long enough to tattoo the Eye of the Bedlam Bride into his chest.

By the time Amayon casts his homecoming spell, the tower is effectively gone.
