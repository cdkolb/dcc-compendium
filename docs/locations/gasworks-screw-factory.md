# Gasworks Screw Factory, Steel Mill, and Doll Workshop

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Floor:** [10](../floors/index.md) ("Don't Come in Last," Book 8) — Heat 5's track
    - **Full name:** "Gasworks Screw Factory, Steel Mill, and Doll Workshop That Is Totally Not a
      Front for Anything Else"
    - **Type:** A single combined industrial track, run as one connected factory building rather
      than several separate locations
    - **Notable events:** [Dong Quixote](../characters/index.md) and
      [Corcunda](../characters/corcunda.md)'s reunion and double death fighting Grull

## One Building, Not Several

Despite the four-part name, the text is explicit that this is **one track through one dilapidated
multistory building**, not a cluster of separate locations — the full name appears as a single
neon sign, with its own punchline underneath:

> A flickering neon sign hung from the top of the dilapidated multistory building. It read,
> "Gasworks Screw Factory, Steel Mill, and Doll Workshop." And under it, a smaller sign read,
> "There's no cocaine in here." Sparks flew from the sign.

The system's pre-race announcement confirms the combined, single-track nature and its brutal
physical rules:

> **Track: Gasworks Screw Factory, Steel Mill, and Doll Workshop That Is Totally Not a Front for
> Anything Else. This track will require vertical climb, inverted racing, and gap coverage of up to
> 100 meters. There will be multiple paths to the exit.**
>
> **Tasks required to complete: Don't get squished. Don't get melted. Don't get screwed.**
>
> **Environment: Extreme heat. Containment field will protect from heat and molten metal.**
>
> **Hazards: Containment theft is now enabled. If your containment touches another containment for
> more than 5 seconds, you may lose it upon separation.**

Racers pass through a sequence of connected rooms inside the same structure: a receiving bay piled
with metal balls, a conveyor-belt jump room, a molten-vat steel-mill chamber, a doll-packing room,
an inverted ceiling-track section flooded with molten metal, and a final crate room leading to the
exit. All paths are only wide enough for two vehicles at a time, and the track is shared
simultaneously with eleven other heats.

## The Creepy Dolls

The steel mill's own manufacturing accident is the track's other running joke and its real hazard.
The factory's knocker workforce (see [Species](../species/index.md) once a dedicated Knocker page
exists) had been using the mill to cook a recreational drug nicknamed "Toddler Cocaine" ("Daddy's
Little Snow Drops"); casting the elemental-summoning spell needed to generate the track's intended
boss monsters let some of that same drug-infused magic leak into the factory's inventory of
Barbie/Bratz/G.I.-Joe-style dolls sitting nearby:

> **Toot-Toot doll. Level 12 Grape-flavored Cocaine Elemental.**
>
> **So, the dolls were a mistake. In preparing to defend their factory, the knockers got a little
> enthusiastic with the elemental magic. The metal frames of these dolls are infused with their
> special blend, and when they cast the magic spell in the steel mill to create the elemental boss
> monsters you'll probably face in a few minutes, some of that magic accidentally leaked. And now
> these creepy little fuckers are everywhere.**

Named doll elementals include Toot-Toot, Sally Speedball, and Belushi — animate, corrosive,
shield-piercing toys roaming a sealed room that only opens once racers break its containment.

## Dong Quixote and Corcunda's Reunion

The track is also where the floor's longest-running NPC subplot resolves. **Corky**, half of the
mantaur [Corcunda](../characters/corcunda.md) split by a *Split Personality* spell, is thrown into
this same factory when the Lady Dominators' car crashes through a trap floor into the doll room;
his other half, **Porky**, is recovered separately from the hidden Stage Performance Guild inside
[the Lollipop](hungry-eyes-village.md#the-hairpin-and-the-lollipop). The fleshmancer
[Grigori the Placid](../characters/grigori.md) performs the reunion mid-race — both halves unzipped
from their containment gimp suits, heads sealed in plastic wrap, organs floated in place by spell —
while the truck itself races upside-down along the track's inverted ceiling section. Dong Quixote
holds the two halves together bodily throughout, repeating "I have you... I got you" until their
separate health bars merge into one and the name **Corcunda** reappears whole.

Reunification comes with a fatal cost: while split, one half of Corcunda had quietly kept
worshipping [Grull](../bosses/index.md), and the god arrives the instant the reunion completes to
punish the whole for the apostasy of the half that raced for a rival team:

> **System Message: Grull has entered the realm.**

Corcunda accepts the smite willingly rather than let Grull turn on the truck, and Dong Quixote — who
had kept a long-hidden lance since the earliest days of the floor — charges the war god at his
side rather than be parted from him again:

> "It's been a long time since I've tilted a giant."

Both die in the charge, screwed to death by Donut's *Big-Ass Magnet* spell dragging thousands of
loose factory screws through them and into Grull, who is unharmed (gods on this floor cannot be
killed by crawlers) but departs satisfied: "A good death... A good death indeed." Dong Quixote's own
farewell to the party — "Goodbye, Carl. Goodbye, Princess Donut... it was my pleasure to serve with
them" — closes out one of Floor 10's most sincere relationships. See
[Corcunda](../characters/corcunda.md) and [Grigori the Placid](../characters/grigori.md) for both
NPCs' own character arcs in full rather than duplicated here.
