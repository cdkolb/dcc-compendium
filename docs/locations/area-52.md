# Area 52

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Floor:** 9 ("Faction Wars")
    - **Where:** The forested region of the Princess Posse's territory (spot 3), on the
      [Plains of Larracos](plains-of-larracos.md)
    - **What it is:** The Posse's rear-area airbase, missile battery, and saferoom
    - **Named by:** The Princess Posse, in the obvious joke on the Nevada original

## Overview

Area 52 is the Princess Posse's second base — not a fortress like [the FUPA](plains-of-larracos.md)
but a working rear installation tucked into the woods, well back from the corridors. It does three
jobs.

**Saferoom.** It holds one of the handful of true saferooms inside Posse territory: "*There are a
few out in the forested region, including the one at the Area 52 base.*" Late in the book it is
also wired into the party's [doggie-door](../mechanics/index.md#doggie-doors-and-personal-space-fast-travel-book-6)
fast-travel network — an escape route teleports the party "*all the way to just outside the safe
room at Area 52, where we could go in and use the doggie door to go anywhere we wanted.*"

**Missile base.** The Posse's missile batteries are fired from Area 52 rather than from the front,
operated remotely through a pair of controllers the system calls a **Trollish Portable Control
Room** — a repurposed oversized video-game controller. The Sledge is one of the operators.

**Airship dock.** Louis's airship *Party Planner* docks, reloads, and repairs here between
sorties; on one return Louis burns "*a precious Emergency Repair scroll to speed up the repair of
Party Planner*" after unloading evacuated children.
