# Club Scolopendra

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** A luxury resort literally built inside the living body of the dungeon boss
      [Scolopendra](../bosses/scolopendra.md) — not a separate structure
    - **Floor:** Physically wherever Scolopendra herself is. Established on the **18th floor**
      across Books 6–7 (see [Mechanics: Post-Crawl Economics](../mechanics/index.md#the-scolopendra-club));
      reached again at the **Floor 11** arena climax in Book 8, mid-battle, via
      [Oozy Form](../spells/oozy-form.md)
    - **Judgment call — same venue, not two:** This wiki treats Book 8's "Club Scolopendra" as the
      **same facility** as the "Scolopendra Club" named in Books 6–7, rather than a coincidentally
      similarly-named second venue. Book 8 is simply the first time the club's actual mechanism —
      built inside its host boss, and therefore relocating wherever she goes — is shown on the page.
      See [Continuity with the 18th-Floor Club](#continuity-with-the-18th-floor-club) below for the
      reasoning; treat this as an editorial judgment call, not something the text states outright in
      so many words.
    - **Notable residents/threats found inside:** [Emberus](../bosses/emberus.md),
      [Taranis](../bosses/taranis.md), a tortured tourist named Cerni Pinta, and traps set by the
      unexplained **"the Kyryap"** — see [Tracking Tag: The Kyryap](../items/tracking-tag-kyryap.md)

## Overview

Club Scolopendra is one of Book 8's stranger pieces of worldbuilding, and it resolves a question the
series left open since Book 6: what the "Scolopendra Club" — the members-only VIP resort holding
over half the galaxy's wealth, cited since Book 6 as leverage against pulling the failsafe — actually
*is*. Book 8 shows it directly for the first time: a functioning, staffed resort built physically
inside the dungeon boss Scolopendra's own body, complete with numbered guest rooms, an automated PA
system, and paying tourists — all of it apparently normal enough to run day to day, even while its
host is actively rampaging through the Floor 11 arena. Carl and the sapient pig
[Penelope](../characters/penelope.md) reach it mid-battle by tunneling directly into Scolopendra's
flesh with [Oozy Form](../spells/oozy-form.md), using her body as a shortcut to a private meeting
with the gods [Emberus](../bosses/emberus.md) and [Taranis](../bosses/taranis.md) — see
[Timeline](../timeline/index.md#floor-11-the-parade-of-horribles) for how that confrontation plays
out.

### Continuity with the 18th-Floor Club

Books 6 and 7 already established a "Scolopendra Club" on the 18th floor — see
[Mechanics: Post-Crawl Economics](../mechanics/index.md#the-scolopendra-club) for that account in
full, not repeated here. Nothing in that earlier material spells out what the club physically is;
it's introduced only as a members-only viewing facility "programmed from orbit." Three details point
toward this page's club being the same facility rather than a second, differently-located one:

- The Book 7 evacuation warning is phrased as tied to the **boss's own location**, not a fixed floor:
  "when the Nothing drains into Scolopendra's Lair, the planetwide message reads 'It is advised all
  current guests at the Scolopendra Club evacuate immediately.'" The club empties out precisely when
  *her* lair is disturbed.
- Book 8 opens with Scolopendra already loose and rampaging across the upper floors, and — in the
  same breath — "the 18th floor's Scolopendra Club has already gone dark, its guests forced through a
  gauntlet to reach safety" (see [Scolopendra](../bosses/scolopendra.md#background-through-book-7)).
  A club that "goes dark" the moment its host boss physically leaves the 18th floor reads as a venue
  whose power/access literally depends on her presence, not an unrelated fixed building she happens
  to share a name with.
- By the Floor 11 climax, a fully staffed, functioning "Club Scolopendra" is again reachable —
  specifically by tunneling into her body — with guests checked into numbered rooms exactly like a
  standing resort operation, not a hastily-built pop-up.

Treat this as this wiki's own inference connecting the dots across three books, not a fact the text
states in a single sentence.

Reaching the club requires physically boring through the boss's own flesh:

> I activated *Oozy Form*... We lost our eyes and our ears, and my vision was suddenly all around,
> and I could see perfectly in the dark... I aimed for the building wall and slimed my way toward
> it, pushing through and around and over strange, giant, pulsing organs.
>
> **Entering Club Scolopendra.**

## A Working Resort, Mid-Rampage

Despite being lodged inside a berserk Level 500 dungeon boss, the club continues operating on
autopilot, complete with an automated, cheerfully oblivious PA announcer smoothing over the
building's own violent shaking:

!!! quote "Automated resort announcement, Book 8"
    "Honored guests, not to worry. That is nothing but Scolopendra settling as she slumbers. Rest
    assured such movements are perfectly natural, and the resort's centrifugal dampeners are more
    than up to the task of keeping you safe."

The interior is a long hallway of numbered guest doors, some broken open onto "opulent,
bigger-on-the-inside rooms," soaked in blaster scoring, spell fire, and a sticky orange webbing
that isn't otherwise explained. Deity auras outside are blocked for anyone standing inside the
club's walls — Carl briefly gains an *Immune to Sheol-based attacks* buff purely from Emberus's
proximity once inside — though the club's protections do **not** extend to Scolopendra's own
nine-tier attacks, which reach guests and intruders alike regardless of location. See
[Mechanics: Scolopendra's Nine-Tier Attack](../mechanics/index.md#scolopendras-nine-tier-attack-book-8)
for that mechanic in general.

## Cerni Pinta and the Tortured Tourists

The club's darker side surfaces immediately: hanging in the hallway webbing is a tortured guest, a
quill-covered alien named **Cerni Pinta**, examined as:

> **Cerni Pinta. Non-Player Tourist. Guest of Club Scolopendra, and she is currently checked into
> room #422 along with her three husbands. Pinta is a type of alien called a bristle. She is the
> vice president of Nova Client Services, LTD., a notorious medical collections agency whose
> primary client is the Viceroy system.**

She begs Carl to kill her outright rather than let whatever tortured her return. He can't bring
himself to, and the choice is taken out of his hands moments later: Scolopendra's second nine-tier
attack (Sin of Consumption) fires while they're still talking, and Pinta rolls red where Carl and
Penny both roll green — worms burst from her body and she is devoured on the spot. See
[Scolopendra](../bosses/scolopendra.md#the-nine-tier-attack) for the attack itself.

## "The Kyryap"

Twice within the club, the party encounters evidence of a named, dreaded presence loose in the
building well before Carl himself is later marked by it: a caprid news panel elsewhere in the book
places "the dread Kyryap" inside Club Scolopendra in the same breath as
[Krakaren Prime](../bosses/krakaren-prime.md), and Carl personally disarms a snare trap here
credited to the same name:

> A level 13 snare trap sat in the middle of the hallway ahead. Something set by "the Kyryap." I
> deactivated the trap and hesitantly moved to the stairs.

Nothing more is learned about the Kyryap here or anywhere else in the book — see
[Tracking Tag: The Kyryap](../items/tracking-tag-kyryap.md) for the full, deliberately unresolved
thread, including Carl's own tracking tag found on his leg after the arena fight. This page should
not be treated as resolving that mystery; it only confirms Club Scolopendra as the place the
Kyryap is first established as active in Book 8.

## Status After the Battle

When Scolopendra is transformed into a Level 1 crawler by the [enriched pet biscuit](../items/enriched-pet-biscuits.md)
mix-up, the club and everyone inside it "blinked out" instantly — Prime Minister Victory's epilogue
account confirms the guests died but then regenerated, exactly as tourists elsewhere in the dungeon
always have, implying the club exists inside some kind of protected dimensional space of its own
rather than the ordinary playing field:

> "The moment that transformation happened, all the non-protected entities within the club had
> blinked out. The guests had also all died, but they had regenerated, just like always. She was
> waiting to hear how that was possible. Likely some sort of dimensional space."

Whether the club (or Scolopendra's body itself) persists in any form after Book 8 is not addressed.
