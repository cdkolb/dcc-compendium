# Dallas County, Iowa (on the Florida Keys)

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Floor:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth"), phase two — see [Level 8](../floors/level-08.md)
    - **What it is:** A slab of Iowa farmland physically transplanted onto Monroe County, Florida
      by a placement glitch
    - **System designation:** **Entering Dallas County, Iowa** — while standing in the Keys
    - **Notable features:** A commercial farm, a trailer park, a turkey barn, and Carl's father's
      trailer, spliced in from a different broadcast season

## The Glitch

Phase two is supposed to offer each squad leader three destinations: "One chosen by us, one by the
AI, and one randomly selected spot." The transfer misfires dungeon-wide. Zev's account:

> "You should have only received three choices for a new location… You received four, and we don't
> know why… Some crawlers had four choices. Some only had one. A few didn't have any at all, and one
> squad leader had the whole globe to choose and dropped herself into the middle of the ocean for
> some reason."

Donut, given four options, picks Miami — and the party lands in a dark Victorian mini-mansion in
the **Florida Keys**, tagged by the system as Monroe County, Florida. Then the system pauses
("Please wait" / "Thank you for your patience"), a small earthquake moves the key marker, and a
chunk of Iowa terrain is dropped bodily onto the map. Katia's squad suffers the same class of
error, choosing Ukraine and arriving in a Ukraine/Vietnam hybrid with its key in Da Nang.

## The Mound

What lands is a mesa of Iowa farmland about 700 feet high, surrounded by ocean and already eroding
into it. It has to be climbed: Carl scales the cliff face and picks up an achievement
("Cliffhanger") for it. Cross the ridge and the system announces **Entering Dallas County, Iowa**,
and it is abruptly winter — snow, Christmas, and Midwest farmland, sitting on top of a subtropical
island chain.

The terrain on top:

> "a street dotted with farm-style houses followed by a sparse trailer park. Immediately to our
> left was half of a commercial farm. A few large buildings dotted the property along with a
> corrugated silo that stood precariously close to the edge."

### The turkey barn

"A large, corrugated building… looked like an airplane hangar, but much longer," packed wall to
wall with level-5 turkeys. They turn hostile and then undead — the "zombie turkey barn" — and the
infection spreads outward into the rest of the farm's animals. Carl's
[extinction sigil](../mechanics/index.md#extinction-sigils-book-6) charm normally pacifies turkeys
(they are the natural predators of a lizard species he wiped out on Floor 4), but the effect is
negated the moment a boss-tier deckmaster is involved. The sequence's reward is the turkey-shaped
**Gold Gobble box**.

### The trailer park

Site of a deckmaster fight against a hydra that summons the giant Christmas cat **Jola**. It is
also where Carl starts recognizing the dead: real people from his own life, fed into the dungeon's
monster roster — his landlord, a friend, possibly his own former neighbours.

### Carl's father's trailer

A roughly twenty-foot rectangle of *summer* sitting inside the snow — dry grass, green leaves,
untouched by the Christmas weather around it. Carl works out what he is looking at immediately:

> "This yard and trailer is not from Christmas. They *are* cheating. This little spot is from a
> different time… It's spliced in."

The system confirms it with three stacked warnings: a forced 42-minute-30-second delay before the
fight commences, "This area exists in a different time instance than the remainder of this floor,"
and "Physical interactions in this area act differently than other locations on this floor." The
seam is visible — a bicycle, a fence, and a minivan are all cut cleanly at the boundary.

Inside the pocket: Carl's father's 1965 Harley-Davidson FLH Electra Glide Panhead (which still
runs, and which Carl later rides to the Shepherd's House), a Ford truck, a shed, and a flower garden
planted in unfamiliar, feminine handwriting — evidence of a woman who lived there after Carl left.
Vehicles looted here file into a dedicated **"Shitty Transportation Items"** inventory category.

This is where Carl watches his father die of a morphine overdose administered by his stepmother
Tami-Lynn, and where he learns he has a six-year-old half-brother, **Asher**. The book never names
his father.

## Nine Deckmasters

The mound is a gauntlet: Carl and Donut beat nine deckmasters in sequence across it, ending with
the **Reminiscence Hydra of Malicious Compliance** — built from the faces of everyone they have
known, Asher's included, and the first boss the system AI ever built from nothing. Killing it wins
the phase key and raises Carl to **Devotee**.
