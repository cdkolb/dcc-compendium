# Upano

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Floor:** [10](../floors/index.md) ("Don't Come in Last," Book 8)
    - **Type:** Mandatory jungle pit stop, shared by four parallel Heat 4 tracks at once
    - **Full announcement:** "There will be a single mandatory refueling pit stop along this route.
      Pit stop will be within the lost jungle city of Upano."
    - **Entry message:** "Entering the Lost City of Upano. Mind the locals."

## Overview

Upano is the one fixed point shared by Heat 4's otherwise-divergent tracks. Where every other heat
sends each team down a single, private route, Heat 4 splits into several simultaneous
"worlds" that all happen to converge on the same jungle way station partway through — Carl and
Donut's own team runs **the You're Gonna Die If You Go In Here Jungle Trail of Tremendous
Cannibalistic Slaughter**, while [Prepotente](../characters/prepotente.md)'s and Louis's teams are
routed instead through [Satan's Water Park](satans-water-park.md), and still other teams end up on
"the devil's cliffs" or a track described only as "a rip-off of the third floor called 'the eternal
slum.'" All of them arrive at Upano.

## Geography

The city itself is small — barely more than a single long street of rearranged garage-houses
transplanted wholesale from every heat sharing the stop — but the land around it is deliberately
disorienting:

> Beyond the small city was a strange sight. Where I was expecting more jungle was something
> completely incongruous with the setting. To the east was what appeared to be a tall group of
> black tubes. It just went up and up and up, and opposite that was a group of cliffs. Opposite us,
> I was expecting more jungle, but it appeared like Upano just went on and on and on.

Neither the black tubes nor the cliffs are explained or explored on the page — they read as scenery
marking the boundary between Upano and the water-park and cliffside tracks converging on the same
stop, rather than a location in their own right.

## Rules of the Pit Stop

Upano runs on a hard timer rather than ordinary Safe Zone rules:

> A two-hour timer starts the moment you park in your garage... Garage doors may not be closed
> during pit stops. Safe room access is unavailable during pit stops.

The open-garage rule turns lethal almost immediately: with doors forced open and no Safe Zone
protection, three of Carl's hired mercenaries — **Finley**, **Olga**, and **Quemada** — are run down
and killed right outside their own garage by team One Fine Pig's APV, moments after Finley's own
speech about wanting to protect his family. Carl's retaliation — setting silent, Tigran-only
disintegration traps around the enemy garage — kills both Tigran racers, **Nico** and **Dario**,
eliminating One Fine Pig by the end of the heat.

## The Cannibal Tribes

The "mind the locals" warning belongs to the jungle roads leading *into* Upano rather than the
city itself: a southern cannibal village and a rival northern tribe (led by the chieftain
**Maurice**) trigger a mandatory quest to kill Maurice once his southern counterpart, Great Rusty,
is already dead — failing to do so blocks the race outright. The same cannibal population resurfaces,
reskinned as "season pass holders," inside the parallel [Satan's Water Park](satans-water-park.md)
track.
