# Necropolis of Anser

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Floor:** 5 ("The Bubbles"), bubble #543, Subterranean Quadrant
    - **Scale:** A volcano-shaped tomb roughly the height of Mount Everest — Carl measures it at
      just over nine kilometers above the Water Quadrant's sea level
    - **Sealed by:** Emperor Anser, who entombed his own court (and his wife) alive with him

The Necropolis of Anser is the ancient, tomb-shaped structure at the heart of bubble #543,
predating every faction currently fighting over the bubble. Emperor Anser subjugated the
[Dromedarians](../species/index.md#other-book-4-species), [Bactrians](../species/index.md#other-book-4-species),
and [Nude Glabers](../mobs/index.md#book-4-mob-catalog) to build it, then had his entire court —
including his wife, later the ghost [Quetzalcoatlus](../bosses/quetzalcoatlus.md) — sealed inside
alive upon his death.

## Layout

- The upper settlements, populated by undead **Nude Glabers**, sit near the top of the structure
  and are reachable without diving; below them, the tomb is a "massive, fuck-you-you're-going-to-die
  maze" of dead-end tunnels and traps, mapped in fragments by a crawler "tomb raider" team
  (Morris Sp, Bobby, Low Thi) sent in specifically to find and lose two competing maps to the
  place
- Deeper still, the tomb connects directly to the wrecked submarine *Akula*, docked against an
  underwater entrance by the bugbear treasure hunter Shamus Chaindrive decades earlier while
  searching for the [Gate of the Feral Gods](../items/gate-of-the-feral-gods.md)
- Twin lightning-rod towers stand outside the main sandcastle entrance, originally built for an
  unrelated purpose but repurposed by Carl's party as the mechanism that finally destroys
  Quetzalcoatlus
- At the very bottom sits Anser's own sarcophagus — just the golden-crowned skeleton of a
  goose — recovered only after the tomb is fully drained

## Notable Events

- The entire tomb floods once the Water Quadrant's castle (the *Akula*) is disturbed, with no
  drainage possible until the Land Quadrant's hidden dam valve is found and used
- Every crawler and Changeling Principal who enters the tomb dies there over the course of the
  book — see [Quetzalcoatlus](../bosses/quetzalcoatlus.md) and [The Changeling
  Principals](../dungeon-factions/the-changeling-principals.md)
