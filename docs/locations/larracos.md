# Larracos, the City of Dreams

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Floor:** 9 ("Faction Wars")
    - **Shape:** A funnel-shaped city sunk into a pit in a volcanic plain, built as concentric
      **rings** terraced down the inside of the hole
    - **Rings:** 66, numbered **from the bottom up** — "*We say there are 66 levels, but that's not
      true. Not all rings completely circle the city … We number from the bottom up.*"
    - **Held by:** **Team Retribution**, the all-NPC tenth Faction Wars team — the only team that
      defends the city instead of holding a slice of the
      [Plains of Larracos](plains-of-larracos.md)
    - **Status at book's end:** Transferred bodily to the **12th floor** by a second casting of
      *[Zerzura](../spells/zerzura.md)*, leaving nothing on Floor 9 but an empty pit

## Overview

Larracos is the ninth floor's capital and the prize the whole of Faction Wars is fought over. It
is not a city on a plain but a city **in a hole** — a vast funnel sunk into the volcanic ground,
with its streets, districts, and temples built as rings stacked down the inside wall. Carl's first
proper look at it from ring 49 puts the top lip "*maybe 3-400 feet up*," and the text is blunt
about what the place actually is: "*This funnel was nothing more than a hole in the ground.*"

The counting convention is the thing to keep straight: **ring 1 is the bottom and ring 66 is the
top lip**. The higher the number, the closer to the surface. Ring 66 is the outermost defensive
ring, "*normally occupied by barracks and soldiers*"; the Semeru dwarves' castle sits at the very
bottom.

Larracos has appeared by name since Book 4, when Carl dumped a feral god through a portal into its
marketplace and flooded the city. The damage is still visible throughout Book 7 — the adventuring
shops "*got ruined when you guys flooded the city*," and the floodwater, which once stood at ring
39, has drained to ring 3, "*just enough to cover the highest tower of the castle*." Several NPCs
the party wanted are simply gone, drowned or vanished in that flood.

For the floor as a whole, see [Level 9](../floors/level-09.md). For the territory outside the
funnel, see [The Plains of Larracos](plains-of-larracos.md).

## The Rings

Only a handful of the 66 rings are described in any detail. Those that are:

| Ring | What's there |
|---|---|
| **66** | The top lip and outermost defensive ring — barracks and soldiers. The new [Shanty Town](shanty-town.md) settlement grows in a band immediately outside it. |
| **50** | The uppermost of Larracos's **three cultural-district rings**. The [Larracos College of Magic](#the-larracos-college-of-magic) takes up a third of the ring (elsewhere described as a quarter); the rest is the high cultural district and the museums. The **arts district** — theatres, restaurants, shops — sits just outside the college. |
| **49** | The ring immediately below, and the party's entry point on their first tour of the city. |
| **9** | The **Piccadilly Theater** and its surrounding bars, several of them true saferooms. |
| **4** | The [Desperado Club](desperado-club.md). |
| **3** | The current waterline of the Book 4 flood. |
| **Bottom** | The **Semeru dwarves' castle**, built along the roots of the All Tree. |

The other two cultural-district rings are never numbered or named.

### The Larracos College of Magic

"*Entering The Larracos College of Magic.*" The college occupies a large fraction of ring 50 —
the text gives it as a third in one place and a quarter in another — and is the city's academic
heart, sitting alongside the high cultural district and its museums. It is used late in the book
as a staging point for the assault on the war-mage-held castle, with Britney coordinating from
inside it.

### The Piccadilly Theater

Larracos's biggest theatre, down on ring 9, and one of the most distinctive pieces of architecture
in the series:

> "The floor contained the massive Piccadilly Theater, surrounded by bars, including a few true
> saferooms. The large theater building sat right underneath the waterfall, and the sculpture-
> covered roof doubled as the step for the water from above to land and cascade off the edge."

The river that crosses the [plains](plains-of-larracos.md) drains through Shanty Town and plunges
into the funnel as a waterfall; the Piccadilly's roof is the landing it breaks on. Ring 9 was
under water after the Book 4 flood — Arief's uncle drained the theatre and found it "*filled with
jellyfish*," and it is "*still a little wet*" when the party arrives.

By the Peeling phase the Piccadilly has become a military target rather than a venue: it houses
the generator spawning the floor's [zarogoth](../mobs/zarogoth.md) mobs, and Donut bombs it from
the air — "*I can start tossing bombs on the Piccadilly Theater!*"

### The Desperado Club (Ring 4)

The ninth floor's [Desperado Club](desperado-club.md) branch, reopened after Book 6's demolition
of the club by the demon Minge — and reorganised in the process, with every sai and cretin guard
fired and replaced (which is how [Toyotomi](../characters/toyotomi.md) ends up on the mercenary
market). Architecturally it is the odd one out:

> The Desperado Club stuck out amongst all the other buildings on the fourth floor … The square,
> concrete building stood on a group of pillars, looking like someone had taken a modernist
> building built in the 1960's and dropped it right into the middle of renaissance France. A Las
> Vegas-style neon sign stood above the large building, but only the red, bottom half of the arrow
> was lit.

Ring 4 is small, and the hole at its centre is still full of water from the rings below. The
club's entrance bar this season is **The Headlamp Colonnade**. Three sub-venues matter:

- **The Cosmic Lounge** — in the basement, and the one place a non-warlord can teleport directly
  to the 18th floor.
- **The Midnight Market** — "*a special bazaar hidden in the bottom regular floor of the Desperado
  Club*," entered under the warning "*Access to the Midnight Market. Safety or Sanity not
  guaranteed.*" Run by the merchant **Paca**, an agent for Krakaren Prime — which is where the
  hell-kissed [Conscription](../spells/conscript.md) potions come from.
- **The Gray Market** — a second hidden market on the middle floor, which Carl never manages to
  get into at all.

Access to the club is precarious in wartime: Ferdinand spends part of the Peeling phase locked out
of it and being chased through the city.

### The Semeru Castle and the All Tree

At the very bottom of the funnel, beneath everything, the [Semeru
dwarves](../species/semeru-dwarves.md) built their castle on a mat of enormous roots:

> "These roots traveled through everything, and they were attached to something they called the
> All Tree. A great tree that is so vast, it connects everything."

The dwarves had dug looking for the gods and found the roots instead. What they built there is
explicitly not a fortification:

> "They built their castle at the very bottom, along the thick roots. But it's more than a castle.
> It's a monument. An apology."

Originally grown from living wood and since "*encased in intricately carved stone*," the castle is
wrecked in the fighting. The Semeru themselves numbered in the millions before Scolopendra's
nine-tier attack; fewer than 10,000 remain.

## Temples and Other Venues

| Venue | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Temple of the Dagda** | The season's [Club Vanquisher](club-vanquisher.md) entrance temple. Each floor gets two Vanquisher entrances — one inside Larracos, which "*would almost always get trashed*," and one outside the city limits, which is usually left alone. This season's is dedicated to Dagda (or *the* Dagda), "*a weather and 'masculinity' god*" whose only membership requirement is growing a beard. The outside-the-limits temple is where the party bases its Vanquisher operations. |
| **Temple of Khepri** | The endless-buffet temple of Khepri, God of Rebirth and the Sunrise (Level 250), reached by a ritual in which female adherents swallow his beetles. "*this 'endless' temple is just a room with a door that leads to one long table covered with a never-ending feast. And that feast is covered with 433 different kinds of bugs. Some of those bugs are quite unpleasant in their own right.*" After Khepri himself is dealt with, "*His flaming, immortal bugs*" remain. |
| **The Midnight Market** | See the [Desperado Club](#the-desperado-club-ring-4) above — the hidden bazaar on the club's bottom regular floor. |
| **The Calligraphy and Cartography guilds** | Two adjoining hidden guildhalls, both run by the same guildmaster, [Milk](../characters/milk.md), who has not had a one-on-one with a floor supervisor in over fifty seasons. The calligraphy guild is where the [Words of Power](../mechanics/index.md#the-tattoo-transfer-system-and-words-of-power-book-7) system is taught. **The text never states which ring or building either guild occupies** — only that both doors are hidden and that nobody ever enters. |

## The Peeling of Larracos

Once only four teams remain, the floor's fourth phase triggers automatically and the city
physically opens up, exposing the throne room to whoever can reach it. Holding it for six
continuous hours wins Faction Wars; holding it for a full week makes the win stick. See
[The Four Phases of Faction Wars](../mechanics/index.md#the-four-phases-of-faction-wars-book-7).

## Fate: The Second Zerzura

The Princess Posse never storms the throne room. Instead, with the floor under a doomsday
countdown, they cede Shanty Town back to the NPCs so that the two settlements register as one
town, and **the Sledge** casts *[Zerzura](../spells/zerzura.md)* for the second time in the
series — the first casting having dropped the High Elf Castle from Floor 6 to Floor 9 back in
Book 5.

> Rosetta: It worked. Shanty Town is included in the glow. It successfully combined the two
> towns …

The entire funnel city, plus the Shanty Town ring, transfers to the **12th floor** — the Court of
the Ascendency. Because Team Retribution's city is no longer on Floor 9 to be taken, the Princess
Posse wins Faction Wars by default. Hired mercenaries do not transfer; thousands of them are
simply removed from the dungeon the moment the spell fires.

What's left behind:

> "Larracos was gone. All that was left now was a massive pit. A few saferooms remained, just
> doorways against the sloped pit of Larracos, including one, thankfully, at the very bottom."

Tree roots cover a giant hole at the lowest point of the empty pit.

## Unverified / Not Stated

- **No population or diameter figure** for Larracos appears anywhere in the text. The only
  dimension given is the ~300–400-foot distance from ring 49 up to the lip.
- **The two remaining cultural-district rings** are never identified.
- **The calligraphy and cartography guilds' physical location** inside the city is never stated.
