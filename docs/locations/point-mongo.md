# Point Mongo

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Floor:** 6 ("The Hunting Grounds")
    - **Formerly:** An unnamed Large [Ursine](../species/index.md#other-book-5-species) Settlement
    - **Conquered by:** Carl and Donut, after accidentally killing mayor Elmer during a bribery
      stunt
    - **Run by:** Princess Donut, via the settlement-conquest system — see
      [Game Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md#settlement-conquest-the-collar-charm-book-5)

## Overview

Point Mongo is Carl and Donut's main base of operations for the back half of Floor 6's early
chapters — a formerly nameless Ursine settlement on the near side of the river dividing the
Hunting Grounds' human and elf territories, renamed by Donut the moment she gains control of it.
It's a strict, puritanical theocracy under its original leadership (the town's rulebook bans
dancing, singing outside a temple, gambling, and — specifically, in a rule aimed squarely at
Mongo — dinosaurs "of any kind, no exceptions"), rules Donut immediately guts once she takes over.

## Conquest

Carl and Donut trigger the town's takeover almost by accident: Donut, offended at Mongo being
banned by name, has Carl offer the mayor, **Elmer**, a bribe to have the guards "get rid of" Mongo
and Donut as a joke — and when Elmer steps forward to take the money, Donut shoves him off the
town's edge with *Astral Paw*. The fall alone kills the surprisingly fragile Level 40 mayor before
a passing Naiad drags the body into the river. Ownership of the settlement transfers to Donut
automatically the moment the mayor dies.

## Defense

Once in charge, Carl and Donut spend several chapters fortifying Point Mongo against the incoming
wave of Floor 6 hunters: distributing missile launchers to the town's [Funeral
Bell](../mobs/funeral-bell.md) garrison, ringing the town with trap networks (each keyed to a
different song), and hosting a rotating cast of allies, including the were-castors
[Clint and Holger](../species/were-castor.md), who help build up the town's picket defenses. The
town survives multiple hunter raids and a High Elf artillery bombardment (a small trebuchet team
firing poison-gas rocks from beyond the walls) before eventually being abandoned as the
[Gehenna Bramble](../mechanics/index.md#the-creeping-apocalypse-odious-creepers-book-5) event
sweeps across the map.

## Notable Residents

- **Elmer** — the Ursine mayor killed to trigger the conquest
- **Tam** — a Light Cleric who unsuccessfully lobbies Donut to reinstate the anti-dinosaur/anti-naiad rules
- **Prudence** — an Ursine barmaid and mother of two cubs, later killed defending them during the
  climax; her account of [Big Tina](../bosses/big-tina.md)'s backstory is the key to solving
  "The Recital" quest
