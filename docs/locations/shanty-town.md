# Shanty Town

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Floor:** 9 ("Faction Wars")
    - **What it is:** A brand-new settlement ring of hovels and markets encircling the top lip of
      the [Larracos](larracos.md) funnel — effectively a 67th ring, outside the city proper
    - **Mayor:** Carl, who buys the office from [Sir Ferdinand](../characters/ferdinand.md) for a
      bowler hat
    - **Special rule:** It has **no throne room**. The only way to take it is to kill the mayor.
    - **Fate:** Flattened during the Peeling phase, patched up, then transferred to the 12th floor
      along with Larracos by the second casting of *[Zerzura](../spells/zerzura.md)*

## Origin

Shanty Town is new — it did not exist in any previous book, and nobody built it:

> "It turned out the existence of the shanty town, a ring of hastily-built buildings and markets
> surrounding the entrance to Larracos, was a new thing … It'd appeared soon after the flooding,
> ostensibly built by the displaced NPCs. Nobody had actually built it. The hovels just sprung up
> out of nowhere. Since the slums completely encircled the top entrance to Larracos, they formed
> the equivalent of a new, large-sized settlement."

The flooding in question is Carl's own, from Book 4 — he dumped a feral god into the Larracos
marketplace and drove the city's population upward. The system responded by generating a slum
around the hole and registering it as a settlement in its own right, complete with a mayor and a
build menu. What the Princess Posse *does* generate is everything that goes on top of it: walls,
automated defence towers, and the handful of manned
[synergy towers](../mechanics/index.md#base-and-tower-construction-book-7) the settlement menu
allows.

## The Bowler Hat Trade

Ferdinand holds the mayorship when the party arrives. Carl wants it, and pays for it with a
weathered black bowler hat that decapitates an enemy and boomerangs back to its owner:

> "I want to be mayor of that town. Shanty Town. You'll have to transfer your mayorship to us,
> which means that jewel on your necklace will go away. You'll get this hat, and we'll trade it
> back for your turban in a day or so … Before he could accept or deny the invite, I reached over
> and placed the bowler hat on the cat's head."

Ferdinand gets his drained turban back eventually and keeps wearing the bowler hat anyway; Donut
lets him keep it.

## Strategic Value

Two properties make Shanty Town matter far more than a slum should:

- **No throne room.** Every other base on the floor can be taken by capturing its throne room.
  Shanty Town cannot: "*the town's rules were different. There was no throne room. They could only
  take it by killing me.*" That makes the Posse's forward position effectively un-flippable while
  Carl lives.
- **It is inside Larracos's field of fire.** Anyone entering Shanty Town "*would open themselves
  up to getting attacked by Larracos's defense system*" — the NPC city's automated towers cover
  the ring, which turns the Posse's alliance with Team Retribution into a live artillery umbrella.

It also holds a hidden [Club Vanquisher](club-vanquisher.md) entrance somewhere in its sprawl, and
at least one named bar, **Possum Envy**.

| **Li Na's clock tower** | A manned **Extend Aura** synergy tower — "*it could only be manned by a crawler or NPC who had 'synergy' with the tower's attack. ## Destruction

Shanty Town is wrecked during the Peeling phase, and the text is specific about the cause: it was
**trampled by fighting gods**, not by any one spell.

> "I was momentarily taken aback by the sheer destruction wrought over what used to be Shanty
> Town. Most of our own towers, which we really never got to use, were trampled. The nest tower
> along with Li Na's abandoned synergy towers still stood, but both were drawing a lot of fire.
> All the other buildings were just gone, flattened by the passage of the gods."

Separately, the engineered volcanic eruption sends a lava flow across the plains and into the
ring — "*A slow-moving glob of molten rock was just about to enter Shanty Town, headed straight
for the giant hole in the ground*" — and the days after the god battle are spent "*clear[ing] out
the damage caused by the lava.*"

!!! note "On the War Crime spell"
    Donut casts *[War Crime](../spells/war-crime.md)* from the Nest, over the same ground, but the
    text never credits that spell with destroying Shanty Town itself — the buildings are already
    described as flattened before the casting, by the gods' passage. Treat "War Crime destroyed
    Shanty Town" as an oversimplification.

## Fate

To make the second *Zerzura* work, the settlement has to read as part of Larracos rather than as a
separate town, which means Carl ceding the territory back to the NPCs first. It works —
"*Shanty Town is included in the glow. It successfully combined the two towns*" — and the ring
goes to the 12th floor with the city it surrounds.
