**Chapter 1 — The Harvest**{: #chapter-1 .chapter-title }

At 2:23 AM PST on January 3rd, Carl (27, ex-Coast Guard MK/marine tech, Seattle) and his ex-girlfriend Beatrice's cat Donut survive only because they're outside retrieving Donut from a tree when every roofed structure on Earth is instantly crushed. A neighbor, Mrs. Parsons, dies in front of Carl. The Syndicate/Borant Corporation announcement plays: Earth defaulted on a resource-rights appeal, and Borant has chosen "Option 3, the 18-Level World Dungeon" as the reclamation method. 150,000 Level-1 entrances open for exactly one hour, halving each level down to two entrances (one exit) on Level 18. Carl runs toward a nearby glowing entrance, Donut in his arms.

**Chapter 2 — Into the Dungeon**{: #chapter-2 .chapter-title }

Carl descends an ornate staircase into Floor 1 (five-day collapse timer). Achievements fire rapid-fire as the system registers him and Donut as crawlers — Donut as Crawler #4,119 (Level 1, Race Cat, Class Not yet assigned), Carl as Crawler #4,122. A Goblin Murder Dozer ambushes them in the tunnel. Carl flees, finds a hidden Tutorial Guild door, but the guildmaster Mordecai refuses entry while mobs are still nearby. Carl bare-handedly kills one goblin against the guild door while Donut sneaks inside through the cracked door.

**Chapter 3 — Mordecai's Guild**{: #chapter-3 .chapter-title }

Mordecai (Rat Hooligan, Level 50, Non-Combatant NPC) scolds Carl for the mess Donut made. Carl goes back out and kills the remaining goblins, narrowly escaping the Murder Dozer's boiler explosion. Mordecai begins explaining who he is: a displaced alien ("skyfowl") who reached Floor 11 generations ago in his own crawl, on his last posting before retirement. Only one crawler in history — another human, from a different world — has ever reached Floor 13, and died within 30 minutes.

**Chapter 4 — The Rules of the Game**{: #chapter-4 .chapter-title }

Mordecai lays out the setup: the Syndicate seeds worlds with six starter species, waits millennia, then returns; Earth defaulted on its 50-year appeal window. Borant profits from mining and from broadcasting the dungeon as an interstellar reality show to quintillions of viewers. He explains the Ratings system (Views → Followers → Favorites → Patrons), loot box tiers (Bronze through Celestial, plus rare Benefactor Boxes), Party mechanics, permanent death ("Dead is dead"), and staircase/collapse rules — and namedrops the rival Squim Conglomerate's brutal battle-royale format.

**Chapter 5 — Opening the Boxes**{: #chapter-5 .chapter-title }

Mordecai activates Carl's inventory (unlimited dimensional storage) and hotlist. Carl opens his 10 starting loot boxes, receiving a Toad Cudgel, a Poker, the **Goblin Pass** tattoo (branded onto his forearm), the **Enchanted Nightgaunt Cloak of Stoutness** and **Enchanted Trollskin Shirt of Pummeling**, the **Enchanted Toe Ring of the Splatter Skunk**, and finally an Enhanced Pet Biscuit — which Donut impulsively eats herself, dissolving into a quivering blob with a "Waiting..." status.

**Chapter 6 — Suiting Up**{: #chapter-6 .chapter-title }

While waiting on Donut, Carl equips his new gear and discovers an old arcade *Frogger* high score buried in his exhaustive skills list. Donut then wakes from her blob state — talking.

**Chapter 7 — Donut Wakes Up Talking**{: #chapter-7 .chapter-title }

Donut demands to be addressed by her full title, "GC, BWR, NW Princess Donut the Queen Anne Chonk." Her transformed stats come with a permanent buff granting bonus Strength/Intelligence/Charisma every level with no manual allocation. She receives a self-taught Tome of Magic Missile and, from a Legendary box, the **Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore** — a Fleeting/Unique item that irrevocably enrolls her in the royal line of succession for the Blood Sultanate on Floor 9. She puts it on anyway because it's purple. She renames the party "The Royal Court of Princess Donut," crowning herself "Grand Champion Best in Dungeon" and dubbing Carl her "Royal Bodyguard."

**Chapter 8 — First Blood**{: #chapter-8 .chapter-title }

Leaving the guild, Mordecai gives Carl a cryptic parting warning about Floor 12. Wandering the tunnels, Donut admits she remembers her whole cat life and noticed Carl considering "catnapping" her instead of returning her to Beatrice's family. They kill a lava-spitting **Bad Llama**, then fight through rats and Scatterer cockroaches — Carl levels up, gaining the Foot Soldier and **Smush** skills (the game's recurring foot-fetish joke). They discover every dungeon "bathroom" leads to the same identical stall.

**Chapter 9 — The Safe Room and Tally**{: #chapter-9 .chapter-title }

They find a Safe Room built from an intact Polish fast-food restaurant, complete with playground. Screens show the collapse countdown and a remaining-crawler count already cratering from 10 million to ~4.3 million. They meet **Tally**, a Bopca Protector who cooks Carl an incredible meal and calls Donut "Your Highness." They decide to base out of this room and clear a nearby Scatterer nest for experience.

**Chapter 10 — Grinding Scatterers**{: #chapter-10 .chapter-title }

After sleeping and showering, they clear a Scatterer Brood Guardian and roughly two dozen more Scatterers, hitting Level 5, before reaching a locked, garbage-smelling door marked with a health-department warning sign.

**Chapter 11 — Dynamite in the Dark**{: #chapter-11 .chapter-title }

Carl tosses dynamite into the dark room beyond the door, badly injuring himself — and triggering a boss encounter. **The Hoarder** (Level 7 Neighborhood Boss) emerges: a 15-foot-tall transformed woman, pleading in Spanish, vomiting up Scatterers as an attack. Carl chokes her with a found chain while Donut fires Magic Missiles; he finishes her off bare-handed.

**Chapter 12 — The Boss Tier Lesson**{: #chapter-12 .chapter-title }

At a nearby guild, a hungover Mordecai explains the boss tier ladder (Neighborhood, Borough, City, Province, Country, Floor, plus rarer "Elites") and that boss kills grant a permanent star and a tier-matched Boss Box. Carl receives the **Tome of Wisp Armor** (handed to Donut, since his mana pool can't cover its cost) and his first leg armor, the **Enchanted Spiked Kneepads of the Shade Gnoll Riot Forces**.

**Chapter 13 — Rebecca W.**{: #chapter-13 .chapter-title }

Investigating a marked corpse, they find fellow crawler Rebecca W., shot dead and looted down to a single apple core — "Killed by Crawler Frank Q." Realizing another crawler is murdering and robbing people, Carl decides they should hunt him down.

**Chapter 14 — The Premiere**{: #chapter-14 .chapter-title }

*Dungeon Crawler World: Earth* airs its premiere via immersive alien broadcast. A recap of the rival Squim Conglomerate's brutal format plays; Earth's own premiere frames humanity at its worst to justify the "rescue" narrative. Highlighted crawlers include the first team to kill a Borough Boss and breakout star Lucia Mar. Despite killing the Hoarder, Carl and Donut get zero screen time.

**Chapter 15 — Slimes and Charm**{: #chapter-15 .chapter-title }

A green slime nearly dissolves Carl's arm off — a lesson that slimes are a no-go for now. Using Carl's Goblin Pass tattoo for free passage into goblin territory, Donut discovers her absurd Charisma lets her charm and command almost any non-hostile NPC. They're led to a workshop guarded by Goblin Shamankas, including the heavily pierced **Rory**, whom Donut emotionally manipulates into full cooperation.

**Chapter 16 — The Goblin Chopper**{: #chapter-16 .chapter-title }

Donut talks the shamankas into building Carl a custom vehicle — the Goblin Copper Chopper — in trade for looted drugs. She then deliberately tips the goblins off that a rival group (the llamas) is holding drugs, triggering a "meth war" raid that empties the workshop, clearing the way to loot everything and go after the Goblin War Chieftain.

**Chapter 17 — Building the Cart Bomb**{: #chapter-17 .chapter-title }

They strip the abandoned workshop and build a cart bomb using a rare explosive and single-use magic detonators. They shove the loaded cart into the chieftain's sealed room, retreat, and detonate it remotely — Donut mentions, only after the trigger's pulled, that she saw goblin infants inside.

**Chapter 18 — Collateral Damage**{: #chapter-18 .chapter-title }

The blast kills the **Goblin War Chieftain** (a bronze star, Level 8, no boss card ever triggered) — but Carl is horrified to learn he's killed goblin children along with him. The workshop burns, and they flee before the raiding party returns.

**Chapter 19 — You Monster**{: #chapter-19 .chapter-title }

Dark-comedy achievement notifications needle Carl over the infant deaths. They fight through a Rot Sticker neighborhood to a safe room with a quirky daily "experience cookie." Loot includes an unexplained **Desperado Pass** tattoo for both of them and Donut's **Enchanted Fae Scale Quadruped Crupper of the Fleet**. Carl settles on a private mantra: "You're not going to break me."

**Chapter 20 — Frank Q**{: #chapter-20 .chapter-title }

During the Day 2 broadcast, Donut spots **Frank Q** — the crawler who killed Rebecca W. — in this same safe room. Frank claims self-defense: his ICE-agent partner Maggie was killed by human traffickers he was investigating. He stalls Carl with an offer of ill-fitting looted pants while his actual partner, **Maggie My**, sneaks up to ambush them both. Carl, forewarned by Mordecai's tip about safe-zone violence penalties, has already told Donut over party chat not to react. Both attackers freeze mid-attack, penalized by the safe zone's rules. Carl rigs a corpse with live explosives as a parting gift, and they flee toward the Chopper just as a daily update announces patch notes and the removal of an overtuned mob that had killed 15,000 crawlers in a day.

**Chapter 21 — The Trap and the Getaway**{: #chapter-21 .chapter-title }

They ride the Chopper east for hours, fighting off raccoon-headed Scat Thugs during a bathroom stop, before finally finding a "Stairs + People" sign spray-painted by another group.

**Chapter 22 — Team Meadow Lark**{: #chapter-22 .chapter-title }

They meet an encampment of eldercare workers — **Brandon An**, his near-silent brother **Chris Andrews 2**, CNA **Yolanda Martinez 13**, and swordswoman **Imani C** — plus the strange homeless woman **Agatha**. Evacuated from a Wenatchee facility when a fire coincided with the dungeon's opening, only 38 of ~250 residents remain, mostly elderly and Level 1. The only way down is through a guarded spiral maze holding an unknown, dangerous creature. Carl proposes they train up and attack it together.

**Chapter 23 — Covering the Signs**{: #chapter-23 .chapter-title }

Worried other crawlers might exploit the vulnerable group, Carl quietly defaces the encampment's own directional signs. He and Donut grind through a neighborhood of Troglodytes before finding the area's boss room — a gym.

**Chapter 24 — The Juicer**{: #chapter-24 .chapter-title }

**The Juicer** (Level 9 Neighborhood Boss), a steroid-caricature bodybuilder, hurls flaming weight plates in a chaotic gym fight. Carl beats him down with a barbell; the boss nearly strangles Carl to death before Donut saves him by biting through his neck vein, taking a near-fatal hit herself in the process.

**Chapter 25 — Debrief and the War Gauntlet**{: #chapter-25 .chapter-title }

They loot the gym and hit Level 9. Mordecai debriefs the fight, confirming Carl's bare-knuckle damage now equals an unenchanted weapon, and reinforces the "find the weak point" lesson. Loot includes the **Enchanted Shuriken of Bloodlust** and Carl's signature weapon, the **Enchanted War Gauntlet of the Exalted Grull**.

**Chapter 26 — The Spiral Maze**{: #chapter-26 .chapter-title }

With the floor's timer running out, the group enters the spiral maze together. The escalated "B-B-B-Boss Battle!" announcement reveals **The Ball of Swine** (Level 15 Borough Boss) — dozens of Tuskling nobility fused into a rolling battle-ball. Using spike strips and terrain tricks to dodge its charges, they build a modular "redoubt" fortress designed to wedge the ball against the tunnel ceiling. The redoubt traps the ball; the party stabs it open, scattering dozens of disoriented, unarmed Tusklings who are slaughtered in the chaos.

**Chapter 27 — Down the Ramp**{: #chapter-27 .chapter-title }

With the last Tuskling dead, they win, level up across the board, and Carl agrees to scout Floor 2 alone with Donut first. At the bottom of the transformed stairwell-ramp, instead of stepping onto Floor 2, they're unexpectedly pulled into a production trailer — plucked for a televised interview.

**Chapter 28 — Plucked for TV**{: #chapter-28 .chapter-title }

Associate producer **Lexis** explains the rules: this is a private program, *Dungeon Crawler After Hours with Odette*, and Carl and Donut are the only real crawlers in an otherwise holographic set. Donut preps for the interview with practiced showbiz instinct while Carl warns her "these people aren't our friends." They meet **Odette**, one of the galaxy's most popular talk-show hosts — a bizarre crab-bodied, insect-headed alien with an exaggeratedly human torso.

**Chapter 29 — Backstage with Odette**{: #chapter-29 .chapter-title }

Backstage, Odette removes her elaborate costume to reveal a legless older human woman — gear from her own crawl, worn when she reached the stairwell to Floor 13 (she never descended, having struck an unspecified "deal"). She reveals she trained Mordecai generations ago, warns Carl about a slur to avoid, tells Donut never to change from her cute cat race, and admits the loot system is deliberately rigged to keep profitable crawlers alive and in danger for ratings.

**Chapter 30 — Floor Two**{: #chapter-30 .chapter-title }

Carl and Donut arrive on Floor 2 — structurally similar to Floor 1, but reinforced and cracked. They fight Brindle Grubs and Danger Dingoes, and Carl adopts a strict "destroy every corpse" policy once he learns how the grub-escalation mechanic works. They find a storm-shelter safe room with a mailbox that enrolls Donut in a spellbook-of-the-month club, netting her the necromancy spell **Second Chance**.

**Chapter 31 — The First Collapse**{: #chapter-31 .chapter-title }

Floor 1 officially collapses as episode two airs; the crawler count drops by roughly 700,000 in a single tick. More Waffle House refugees join the group; Agatha goes missing and is strangely edited out of the broadcast recap despite witnesses insisting she was present. A harsh new rule debuts: public urination now summons a punishment "Rage Elemental" — foreshadowing disaster. Donut gets her first follower and starts craving the attention.

**Chapter 32 — Rev-Up Consultants**{: #chapter-32 .chapter-title }

With their social numbers exploded after Odette's show aired, Carl and Donut scout toward Floor 2's stairwell and encounter gambling **Clurichaun Rev-Up Consultants** — hillbilly-goblin creatures who turn out to be the low rung of a moonshine-company pyramid scheme.

**Chapter 33 — Plan Pied Piper**{: #chapter-33 .chapter-title }

Using Puddle Jumper, they infiltrate the Rev-Up filling room. Unable to charm the rank-and-file workers, they lure the whole crowd (plus their two fairy managers) into an ambush hallway and firebomb them. They loot moonshine, an alchemy workstation, and a recipe for a "Rev-Up Immunity Smoothie." A "Live, Laugh, Love" door suggests the boss room may hold children — but the boss bursts straight through the walls into their room instead.

**Chapter 34 — The Krakaren Clone**{: #chapter-34 .chapter-title }

The **Krakaren Clone** (Level 10 Neighborhood Boss — an explicit clone; killing it just spawns two more) attacks with dozens of screaming tentacles and waves of reinforcements. Carl blends an Immunity Smoothie mid-fight (both he and Donut get visibly drunk from it) and sends a reanimated zombie in as an unwitting suicide bomber.

**Chapter 35 — Six Percent Health**{: #chapter-35 .chapter-title }

Carl survives the blast catastrophically injured — saved in part by a buff that redirected what should have been a fatal hit. They discover their looted crafting tables are seemingly indestructible (a bug Carl quietly decides to exploit) and find the Krakaren's actual corpse, confirming it died from the explosion, not direct combat. Mordecai, now in a new form, furiously refuses Odette's request to reconcile. Loot includes the **Enchanted BigBoi Boxers**, granting the **Protective Shell** spell.

**Chapter 36 — Zev**{: #chapter-36 .chapter-title }

A kua-tin Admin, **Zev**, becomes Carl and Donut's official PR agent. Carl explodes at her about being forced to perform for cameras; she surprisingly agrees, revealing she's just as trapped within the system as he is. Mordecai privately warns that the kua-tin's ruling party is pressuring Borant to end the season fast, meaning the dungeon is being made deliberately more dangerous.

**Chapter 37 — The Grub Swarm**{: #chapter-37 .chapter-title }

Mordecai explains the dungeon's "waste disposal" system: grub swarms scale directly with nearby corpse counts, capped per quadrant. They push through a massive swarm to link up with Team Meadow Lark, who've built an improvised "parade float" train to move all the residents at once. Mid-conversation, Zev breaks in with an urgent warning: an elderly resident has started urinating on a hallway wall.

**Chapter 38 — The Rage Elemental**{: #chapter-38 .chapter-title }

The bathroom-violation punishment triggers exactly as threatened: a **Rage Elemental** (Level 93) is summoned. It kills the offender, another elderly resident, and then **Yolanda Martinez** herself as she tries to hold it off. Carl's Protective Shell spell saves the rest of the group, buying time to reach a safe room. They seal the door with the elemental clawing outside — its only real counter now on a long cooldown.

**Chapter 39 — Views and Consequences**{: #chapter-39 .chapter-title }

The recap broadcasts the disaster to a massive new audience, treating one death as a crude joke and Yolanda's with real gravity. Loot includes risky scratch-off lottery items for both Carl and Donut. A system update announces safe rooms will now forcibly eject everyone one hour before every floor collapse, closing off their usual strategy.

**Chapter 40 — Bomb Chicken**{: #chapter-40 .chapter-title }

Rather than wait out his spell's cooldown, Carl devises a plan to kill the elemental outright, recalling that anything shoved down a stairwell without properly descending "dissolves." The group builds the "MOAB" — nicknamed "Bomb Chicken" — a bike-trailer bomb launcher, and lures the elemental on a chase down the hallway, tangling its legs with a magic chain and using an oil slick to make it slip. At the stairwell chamber, they abandon their burning chopper as a decoy and teleport to safety just as a final bomb shoves the wounded elemental into the hole, dissolving it — netting no combat experience since neither is credited with the kill.

**Chapter 41 — Goodbye, Meadow Lark**{: #chapter-41 .chapter-title }

Team Meadow Lark descends to Floor 3; Carl has a poignant goodbye with dementia-affected Elle McGibbons, who mistakes him for her late husband. Their friendly admin Zev is abruptly replaced by a hostile one, **Mukta**, who forces them onto a different show against their wishes.

**Chapter 42 — Death Watch Extreme**{: #chapter-42 .chapter-title }

They're transported to a sinister set for *Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem*, hosted by **the Maestro** — a cruel young orc prince — before an audience of jeering pre-teens. The show's format: two freshly-plucked crawlers must gamble on whether people in mortal danger live or die to earn rescue points. Disgusted, Carl physically breaks the illusion to refuse participation, then negotiates a compromise to keep speaking his mind without being killed for it.

**Chapter 43 — Yvette**{: #chapter-43 .chapter-title }

Carl talks the two trapped crawlers into using their earned points to save everyone rather than choosing one person, hijacking the Maestro's cruel narrative. Furious, the Maestro reveals he's a real prince of the powerful Skull Empire. The show then airs the true, horrifying backstory of Frank Q and Maggie My: their "daughter" Yvette was a captured teenager, and Maggie strangles the mortally wounded girl live rather than let her be saved. Maggie attacks Carl and only hits the trailer's invisible wall, vowing revenge.

**Chapter 44 — The Skull Empire**{: #chapter-44 .chapter-title }

Donut reveals she overheard Mordecai's private warning that Carl should abandon her before Floor 9's Blood Sultanate gauntlet. A patch closes their stairwell-dissolve exploit. Zev returns, cleared of a minor disciplinary mark, and explains the Maestro's family is a major galactic power. She reveals a mysteriously high-quality, anonymous "Pork Boy" deepfake of Carl and the Maestro has gone massively viral, humiliating the Skull Empire.

**Chapter 45 — Ralph**{: #chapter-45 .chapter-title }

Hunting the Kobold quadrant's boss, Carl and Donut find caged, abused Danger Dingoes used in an underground fighting pit and flip them neutral with pet biscuits. The boss fight begins against **Ralph**, a Level 11 Frenzied Gerbil — see [Ralph's boss page](../bosses/ralph.md) for the full account, including the twist where a freed dingo swallows him mid-fight before he bursts back out for a final ambush.

**Chapter 46 — Mongo**{: #chapter-46 .chapter-title }

With Ralph dead, Carl loots a Master Pen Key that unlocks a pet reward room. Over Carl's objection, Donut insists on a strange, red-tagged chick-like baby dinosaur she names **Mongo** — who, unlike the tamed dingoes, stays hostile even after pet biscuits, kicking off a bonding-process arc.

**Chapter 47 — Bonding**{: #chapter-47 .chapter-title }

Carl and Donut spend the rest of Floor 2 training Mongo, eventually bonding him (title "Royal Steed") after Donut saves him from a Brindled Vespa. Both hit Level 13. In a strange twist, Carl finds a "History" tab listing three items he's never owned — Valtay Corporation tech, clearly tied to Agatha's mysterious shopping cart. Someone is approaching on the map as the chapter ends.

**Epilogue — One Last Piece of Advice**{: #epilogue .chapter-title }

Back on Odette's show, Carl and Donut discuss the Rage Elemental kill and introduce Mongo. *Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem* has gone on hiatus "to restructure," and the Skull Empire's request to stop calling the prince "Carl's Naughty Little Piggie" only makes it stick harder. Backstage, Odette warns Carl about the **Valtay Corporation** — a major rival power with a fleet parked outside the Borant system — all but confirming where the found tech (and possibly Agatha) came from. She leaves them with one last piece of advice as the book ends: during the upcoming race/class selection, look for a hidden, high-charisma-only option that would greatly help their survival — but picking it will make someone very angry.
