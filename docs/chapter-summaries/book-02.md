**Chapter 1 — Donut's Choice**{: #chapter-1 .chapter-title }

Carl, Donut, and Mongo arrive on Floor 3 (the Over City) and meet Mordecai in his new Incubus form — his final shape before his guildhall permanently relocates. Donut keeps her Cat race outright. For class, Mordecai presents three AI-recommended options; Donut nearly picks the flashy **NecroBard** before Carl steers her toward **Artist Alley Mogul** — only for Donut to "accidentally" pick **Former Child Actor** instead, secretly following Odette's advice from Book 1 to trigger the hidden **Manager Benefit** without Mordecai suspecting it was planned. Mordecai is furious, realizing too late he's now bound to travel everywhere with them, losing his guildhall, television access, and privacy.

**Chapter 2 — Primal**{: #chapter-2 .chapter-title }

Carl works through his own race and class choices with Mordecai. He rejects the AI-recommended Hobgoblin and Sasquatch (too monstrous, would cost viewers) and instead picks the rare **Primal** race — a "blank slate" race tied to a mysterious, vanished precursor civilization, at the cost of -1 to all stats but with all higher-tier skills unlockable to level 20. For class, he picks **Compensated Anarchist**, an exclusive Monk/Rogue trapmaking-and-bomb-making class requiring 500 billion views and Explosives Handling 5, which comes with the **Fear** spell and access to the Desperado Club. Floor 2 collapses mid-selection; around 700,000 crawlers remain of the original 13 million.

**Chapter 3 — The Over City**{: #chapter-3 .chapter-title }

Mordecai formally presents Carl and Donut to the Over City, a themed urban floor built as a "stage set" around a fictional volcano-kingdom backstory. He explains that most NPCs are biologically engineered by Borant, memory-wiped and reused between seasons, and that city guards will kill on sight if provoked — but won't pursue into the ruins. Village signs act as a rough Pathfinder substitute, marking distances to settlements, stairwells, and saferooms. Mordecai warns Carl specifically to avoid "girls with horns on their heads" and to lean on his new Fear spell, then sends the party toward a larger settlement half a day's walk east.

**Chapter 4 — Grimaldi's Traveling Circus**{: #chapter-4 .chapter-title }

En route, the party is ambushed by knife-throwing **Former Circus Lemurs** and a stilt-legged **Terror the Clown**, remnants of a cataclysm-warped circus. Fighting through to a saferoom, they trigger their first-ever quest: **The Show Must Go On**, tasking them with investigating why the circus still terrorizes the ruins centuries later. Mordecai, meanwhile, gets catastrophically drunk on his first night out as manager (an Incubus weakness to alcohol he didn't know about) and passes out mid-sentence, leaving Carl and Donut to handle their first quest and boss-tier threat completely alone.

**Chapter 5 — Clockwork Triplicate**{: #chapter-5 .chapter-title }

While Mordecai sleeps it off, Carl and Donut open their Earth boxes: Donut gets a cat tree, a candle, and a mandatory **Earth Hobby Potion** that grants a random skill (hers is "Scutelliphily" — turtle-shell collecting); Carl's gives him "Cesta Punta," which turns out to be jai alai. Donut learns the new spell **Clockwork Triplicate**, which spawns two temporary illusory duplicates of a pet. That night the pair scout the circus perimeter, quietly killing sentry lemurs and getting their first look at Grimaldi's fortified encampment — including a defaced "Wanted: Traitor" poster of a horned elite named Tsarina Signet.

**Chapter 6 — Tsarina Signet**{: #chapter-6 .chapter-title }

Fleeing a mortar barrage from the circus's magical artillery, Carl and Donut fall through a collapsing floor into a hidden basement, only to be confronted outside by the poster's subject herself: **Tsarina Signet**, a half-Naiad, half-High-Elf Elite who tries to charm Carl before "helpfully" surrounding the party with six **Mold Lions** as unwitting blood sacrifices for her summoning magic. Carl breaks her charm effect by snapping his own finger (Mordecai's trick from chat) and uses Protective Shell to pin and kill the lions before her ritual can claim him, denying her the sacrifice she wanted.

**Chapter 7 — Elites**{: #chapter-7 .chapter-title }

With her sacrifice spell fizzled, Signet reveals her spells require a sacrificial death she can't personally cause, and admits she'd hoped Carl would die defending her. She invites the party back the next night to hear her and Grimaldi's backstory. Back at the saferoom, Mordecai — now recovered — explains what Elites really are: unique, AI-imprinted NPCs starring in scripted, producer-driven serial dramas that span the urban floors, protected by heavy "plot armor" and best avoided entirely. The recap show reveals Hekla's Amazonian/Shieldmaiden transformation and Lucia Mar's extreme Lajabless race and Black Inquisitor General class.

**Chapter 8 — Water Lily**{: #chapter-8 .chapter-title }

The next morning, Carl and Donut are ambushed and knocked unconscious for fifteen hours by an unseen, overwhelming spell. Carl wakes alone on a couch — Signet's captive — to learn Donut has been placed under her mother's heirloom **Water Lily** curse: if Signet dies, Donut dies with her, and moving her body drains her health. Signet reveals her true bargain: Carl must personally kill **Heather**, a captured, tortured circus bear, so Signet can use her blood for the sacrifice spell she couldn't cast on Carl the night before, since Signet herself is barred from directly influencing the fight.

**Chapter 9 — Heather the Bear**{: #chapter-9 .chapter-title }

Carl solo-fights **Heather the Bear** (Level 19 Neighborhood Boss), a parasite-infested former circus performer whose worm-covered claws speak directly into his mind. After a chaotic fight involving smoke bombs, a lottery-ticket custard blast that accidentally heals the worms off her, and a mercy kill, Carl frees the real Heather's spirit from the parasites just before finishing her. Her blood powers Signet's strongest **Ink Marauder** summoning yet — a towering army of animated, paper-thin "tattoo" monsters — and the two march on the circus together for the night's assault.

**Chapter 10 — The Big Top**{: #chapter-10 .chapter-title }

Signet's paper army tears through the circus's outer defenses in minutes, but an ancient protective ward stops them at the big top's canvas. Carl alone must enter with a stolen ticket, break the tent's three support poles, and confront the vine-thing at its center. Inside, he discovers the true final boss: **Ringmaster Grimaldi**, revealed as a Level 85 City Boss *and* Elite — a sentient **Pestiferous Vine** that reanimates his transformed "family" of circus monsters nightly. Using Mordecai's lore on poisoning vine-type mobs, Carl secretly poisons himself and bleeds onto Grimaldi's roots to try to kill him from within.

**Chapter 11 — Papa Grimaldi**{: #chapter-11 .chapter-title }

Realizing the producers of *Vengeance of the Daughter* are watching, Carl brazenly negotiates through Zev — threatening to spoil the drama's climax unless they let him resolve it his way, in exchange for future guest appearances. He then eats a parasite-laced "ice cream" to link minds with Grimaldi directly, using his temporary **Wisp Armor** spell to resist full mind control while begging Grimaldi to listen to Signet. Convinced, Grimaldi un-banishes her, ending their centuries-long war without a kill. The quest completes as **The Show Must Go On** — but Mordecai warns that Donut's health has dropped critically back at Signet's hideout.

**Chapter 12 — Street Urchins**{: #chapter-12 .chapter-title }

Carl races back to find Mongo, grown and battered, single-handedly holding off a swarm of spike-covered **Street Urchins** (Floor 3's "janitor" mob) to protect the still-cursed Donut. A jury-rigged potion recipe from Mordecai saves the badly wounded Mongo. Donut wakes at dawn as promised, having gained a huge share of Carl's quest experience. Passing the now-empty circus ruins, they find a new stairwell down to Floor 4 revealed at the site. Carl reflects on how much Donut has matured, and Zev reveals the mysterious production company behind Signet's drama: **Sensation Entertainment, Incorporated**.

**Chapter 13 — The Freeballing Benefit**{: #chapter-13 .chapter-title }

Back with Mordecai, the party opens a haul of quest rewards, including two "Hadji"-upgraded boxes. Donut receives **Fang Caps** (later given to Mongo) and a skill potion; Carl uses a rare **Scroll of Upgrade** on his Enchanted BigBoi Boxers, which comically grants +5 Dexterity, +5 Intelligence, +3 more Constitution, and the **Freeballing Benefit** — locking him out of ever wearing pants again. That night's recap show reveals a fellow crawler's pet goat transformed into a monstrous **Hellspawn**, and introduces a grieving orc NPC, **GumGum**, whom Mordecai flatly refuses to let the party help.

**Chapter 14 — GumGum**{: #chapter-14 .chapter-title }

Despite Mordecai's warning that "you can't save them all," Carl sneaks out that night to find GumGum and ask about her quest, but she's already gone. The next day, after a long shopping run through the settlement's general stores (with Donut's Charisma driving hard bargains), the party discovers GumGum again outside the town gate, piling up a mountain of murdered prostitutes' corpses — women who keep turning up dead and drained of blood in the alleys with no explanation. She begs for help, and the party accepts **The Sex Workers Who Fell from the Heavens** quest, against Mordecai's explicit advice.

**Chapter 15 — The Desperado Club**{: #chapter-15 .chapter-title }

Mordecai escorts Carl and Donut into the **Desperado Club** for the first time, explaining its layout — public bar, members-only main floor, and specialty rooms including a casino, a brothel, and a black market. They meet **Quint**, a floating Half-Djinn drug dealer and old friend of Mordecai's, and trade moonshine for a rare alchemy starter kit. Quint recounts a wild story from the night before: a crawler with one goat leg (clearly Lucia Mar) trashed the club's pet playroom and stole liquor after her rottweilers went berserk — the first hint of her escalating notoriety.

**Chapter 16 — Pustule**{: #chapter-16 .chapter-title }

At the Silk Road market, the party meets **Pustule**, an unnervingly cheerful, pus-covered hobgoblin explosives dealer who becomes a recurring supplier, and gets an appraisal on Carl's Jug O' Boom (worth 7,500 gold, mostly for the rare moonshine). Walking back to the inn that night, Mongo bolts into a dark alley and the party discovers GumGum's mutilated corpse alongside three horrifying flying-head **Krasue** and a deranged, self-proclaimed "final boss" city elf named **Vicente**, who dies instantly to a kick and a magic missile — but not before revealing he somehow already knew Carl and Donut's names.

**Chapter 17 — The Xistera**{: #chapter-17 .chapter-title }

Looting Vicente reveals a **201st Security Group** military uniform; Mordecai explains city elves as a banished, doomsday-cult offshoot who worship the skyfowl as literal angels. GumGum's body yields a blood-soaked **Mysterious Letter** (written in necromancer's script) and a magistrate's **Gate Pass**. That night, fan boxes bring Donut a framed photo of Beatrice (which Carl finds no longer bothers him) and Carl a **xistera** — a jai alai scoop that instantly becomes his new primary ranged weapon, retiring his slingshot for good. A second dead prostitute is found the next morning with "No, you won't" carved into her back.

**Chapter 18 — Miss Quill**{: #chapter-18 .chapter-title }

Investigating the town hall, the party meets **Miss Quill**, the elderly, Beanie-Baby-obsessed receptionist who refuses them an audience with the reclusive Magistrate Featherfall (who, they're told, "only comes out at night now"). Carl dumps a corpse on her desk to force the point, to no avail. Later that day, hunting near a dead city boss's corpse, the party is ambushed in an unnatural, magic-resistant darkness by a group of towering, undead **Shambling Berserkers**. Carl's dynamite triggers the rotting boss corpse to detonate catastrophically, launching him unconscious — Donut and Mongo finish the fight alone.

**Chapter 19 — Danger Zone**{: #chapter-19 .chapter-title }

Carl and Donut appear on the round-table talk show *Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton*, meeting Hekla in person for the first time alongside Princess D'nadia, the Naga singer Manasa, and an obnoxious comedian named Tucker. Pressed on Skull Empire politics, Carl gives an impassioned "boil the frog" speech calling for the citizens to rise up. Minutes later, Prince Stalwart orders a assassination pulse-strike meant for Carl and Donut's trailer — but it hits Manasa's instead, killing her. The Valtay Corporation retaliates by destroying a Skull Empire royal yacht, killing Queen Consort Ugloo and the disgraced former Prince Maestro.

**Chapter 20 — The Apito Education Center**{: #chapter-20 .chapter-title }

Suspecting the city elves' "Apito Education Center" temple is tied to Featherfall's undead operation, Carl and Donut provoke a confrontation with nine armed cultist elves — only to accidentally aggro the town's guards in the chaos. Using their "Mic Drop" play (a mid-air boom jug), they incinerate the temple and most of the elves, but not before a pregnant dwarf named **Eunice** and her son **Ricky Joe** get caught in the crossfire — Mongo bites off the boy's arm mid-brawl. The elves reveal, under duress, that they tortured and killed GumGum for asking too many questions.

**Chapter 21 — Katia**{: #chapter-21 .chapter-title }

Hiding from the aggro'd guards, Carl questions the traumatized Ricky Joe about the temple before the party slips away. Back at the inn, they meet **Katia Grim**, a shy, poorly-sculpted Doppelganger sent by Hekla to level up with the party as a personal favor — she's a Level 9 **Monster Truck Driver** with absurdly high Constitution but far below Carl and Donut's level. The recap show airs a manufactured "feud" between Donut and Lucia Mar's rottweilers, with Lucia Mar coldly promising to hunt Donut and Carl down in the dungeon once given the chance.

**Chapter 22 — The Krasue Nest**{: #chapter-22 .chapter-title }

Using the bloodstained Mysterious Letter as bait — Mordecai deduces it's a scrying spell that lets the caster hear wherever it's removed from inventory — Carl and Donut lure their unseen enemy by planting explosives in Miss Quill's office and detonating remotely. The blast kills both Featherfall (already a centuries-old, mummified husk used as a magical prop) and, they hope, Miss Quill. Breaching the ruins, they find a nest of sleeping **Krasue** and, dying among them, a former receptionist named **Burgundy**, who reveals the truth: Miss Quill has secretly run the town for ages and was preparing an heirloom genocide spell to wipe out every non-flying resident.

**Chapter 23 — Daniel Bautista**{: #chapter-23 .chapter-title }

Leaving the collapsing building, Carl and Donut meet **Daniel Bautista 2**, a Tigran Swashbuckler whose entire family — the crawlers whose corpses yielded Donut's Fallen Oak anklet — was slaughtered by the circus lemurs back in Chapter 4. He thanks them for avenging his family and offers a favor in return before departing. Reflecting on how broken and lonely most of the crawlers and NPCs around them seem, Carl and Donut decide to finish the quest that night rather than wait, and head to the Swordsmen depot warehouse to confront the entity they now believe is a lich hiding above it.

**Chapter 24 — Remex's Story**{: #chapter-24 .chapter-title }

Braced for a lich, Carl instead finds **Remex**, a pitiful "Soul Leech Capacitor" — the resurrected husk of Miss Quill's dead husband, forcibly bound to power her spells. His rambling, digression-filled confession reveals the full plot: Quill, a disowned skyfowl royal, has spent generations building toward a three-part heirloom spell that would exterminate every flightless resident of the Over City and restore skyfowl rule. The quest **The Sex Workers Who Fell from the Heavens** completes — but instantly, a new **event quest** announces an uncontrollable magical explosion at Remex's location, giving everyone in a 45-kilometer radius seven minutes to escape or die. Unable to outrun it in time, Carl and Donut choose to stay and gamble on containing the blast, trapping the swelling soul crystal inside a rare **Sheol Glass Reaper Case** moments before detonation.

**Chapter 25 — Carl's Doomsday Scenario**{: #chapter-25 .chapter-title }

The case holds — barely — caging the silent explosion, and Carl manages to pull the now-transformed, unstable, un-inventoriable superweapon into his inventory anyway, naming it **Carl's Doomsday Scenario**. A series of magical "precursor bursts" strip the fleeing crowd of crawlers of their gear and stats before the real explosion; Carl strips down to nothing, Donut sacrifices her Sepsis Crown, and the party (now including Katia) narrowly escapes down a stairwell to Floor 4 alongside dozens of other survivors, ending Book 2's main floor storyline.

**Epilogue — The Leaderboard**{: #epilogue .chapter-title }

On Odette's show, Carl (still stuck in a toilet-paper loincloth) learns Borant used its one seasonal veto to strip the party of the Celestial-tier reward they'd earned — while a rival crawler exploited a loophole to claim one anyway, threatening Borant with a season-ending financial penalty. Despite losing the prize, the party's raw combat experience still rockets them up: Carl to Level 27, Donut to 26, Katia from 9 to 21. The current Floor 3 leaderboard reveals Carl and Donut sitting at 6th and 7th, behind Lucia Mar (1st) and Hekla (2nd) — and, astonishingly, Mrs. Elle McGibbons in 10th. Privately, Odette warns Carl that Hekla is quietly maneuvering to recruit Donut (and, through her, Mordecai) away from him, and that the Skull Empire may have purchased a deity sponsorship — likely Grull — aimed squarely at Carl heading into Floor 4.
