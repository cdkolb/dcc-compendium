# Book 8: A Parade of Horribles — Chapter Summaries

Full narrative recaps, chapter by chapter. For the condensed version, see the
[Timeline](../timeline/index.md); for standalone reference pages, see
[Characters](../characters/index.md), [Bosses](../bosses/index.md), and

!!! note "A note on this book's structure"
    Book 8 splits into two unnumbered Parts covering two floors. **Horrible One: Control** is
    Floor 10, "Don't Come in Last," a vehicle-race gauntlet broken into seven **Heats**, each its
    own mini-arc with its own upgrades, opponents, and eliminations. **Horrible Two: A Parade of
    Horribles** is the short, floor-length-compressed Floor 11 that gives the book its title. Five
    interludes are threaded through: "Chandra" in the front matter, "Minus" opening Heat 5, "Quasar"
    bridging the two floors, and "Sydnee Iglacia," "Prime Minister Victory," and "Louis Santiago 2"
    closing out the epilogue.

!!! note "A note on chapters containing boss fights or major reveals"
    Chapters with a real boss battle, a team elimination, or a major emotional turn run
    noticeably longer than the rest — the mechanics and the fallout are worth keeping straight.

**Front Matter — A Parade of Horribles**{: #front-matter .chapter-title }

The book opens on a mock dictionary entry for "parade of horribles" — literally a procession of
grotesques and floats, and legally, a rhetorical trick of stacking up catastrophes that supposedly
follow from an act. Both meanings apply before the book is done.

**Samantha's Floor 7 Recap**{: #samanthas-recap .chapter-title }

Samantha, redecorating a stolen chandelier (actually a swing) into Carl's room, gives an amnesiac
Tipid and the sluggalette Bigs a garbled recap of the previous floor: Faction Wars ending with
Larracos and its NPCs teleported to the Ascendency cotton fields, the War Mage Rebellion's Akuma
stealing the Gate of the Feral Gods from Elle, the penis-monster Meatus, Eris's arrival, Yarilo's
banishment-and-death, Li Na's chain absorbing Meatus and leaving her "all weird," Li Jun's death,
Lucia Mar's tens of thousands of trapped children, the Theia/skyfowl impersonation, Louis surviving the Dream
warlord in his lungs thanks to Samantha's flesh-golem body (and gaining gills in the process),
Katia's pregnancy and exile to the surface, Justice
Light breaking the Nothing, and Scolopendra's awakening. It is, improbably, mostly accurate.

**Interlude — Chandra**{: #interlude-chandra .chapter-title }

Princess Chandra, Esquire — a Naga lawyer aboard the warship *Ventilator* after a bloody coup
installs her uncle as Sultan — has spent the hours since Vinata's death exploiting the old law of
Widow's Rights to claim Carl, her late husband Rishi's killer, as her new husband. As "his" wife she
has filed to seize legal conservatorship over Donut (whose species isn't recognized as legally
competent), frozen the Princess Posse's accounts, subpoenaed the Donut Holes fan group, and hired
two Taurin assassins to kill Quasar. She presents the paperwork to a shell-shocked, blood-soaked
Donut in a containment cell, expecting easy prey. Donut instead needles her about her own
resemblance to Carl's absentee father, points out that Earth law is as void as Chandra's own claim
to dignity, and — while never technically offering a bounty — muses aloud about the fifty million
credits she is *not* offering to whoever turns Chandra inside out. A message arrives from Chandra's
own hired killers, oddly timed. Chandra flees in terror. "Lesson one," Donut tells her, "don't put
yourself in situations where you're so afraid you piss yourself. Not yet."

## Horrible One: Control {: #part-1 }

### Heat 1 of 7 {: #heat-1 }

**Chapter 1 — Waldrip Chris**{: #chapter-1 .chapter-title }

Floor 10, "Don't Come in Last," opens on 5,501 survivors — 75% of Faction Wars took a deal. Carl and
Donut land in a cold garage where two gremlins, Hedy (mechanical) and Waldrip Chris (biological),
literally fight each other for the job of garage manager while explaining the floor's stakes: pick a
vehicle type, and coming in last means death for both crawler and manager. A posthumous system
message confirms Justice Light's death; Rosetta and Tipid are alive as hired mercenaries. The AI
confirms the Nothing is broken, Scolopendra is stirring, and the 18th floor has gone dark. Donut
picks the Assassin of Sekhmet class and chooses Hedy — mechanical means Mongo won't get eaten.
Waldrip Chris explodes on the spot.

**Chapter 2 — Kill, Kill, Kill**{: #chapter-2 .chapter-title }

Hedy explains the heat structure — a giant wheel assigns each team's vehicle at random, hazards
escalate each race, and the first three heats are paved. Zev's announcement is interrupted by
Cascadia's broken, despairing voice ("Why won't you let me die?") before cutting off — an early
sign of how frayed the AI's own hosts have become.

**Chapter 3 — Big Shot Chicken**{: #chapter-3 .chapter-title }

The wheel lands on a gangster-themed Big Shot Chicken food truck, tommy gun bolted to the roof. The
GPS, Navitron 1000, reads out Donut's absurd Assassin-of-Sekhmet title as Heat 1 begins: 53 km
through the Caves of the Screeching Death Manatees. In the dark, Carl and Donut spot their eight
rivals — a muscle car, a bear, a bug, a minivan, a dog, and a unicorn wrapped in a tumbleweed.
Prepotente warns everyone over chat: killing more than one team per heat means the next heat gets
filled with crawler-versus-crawler matchups instead of NPCs.

**Chapter 4 — The Screeching Death Manatees**{: #chapter-4 .chapter-title }

Entering the cave, the Trauco Triplets are eliminated when their mount dies, and Team Free Love's
minivan crashes and burns, killing bugbear Alevtina. Donut one-shots a house-sized Screeching Death
Manatee and, on a whim, lets the audience vote its corpse to the mysterious "arena" instead of taking
the XP. The survivors — bugbears Radoslav and Jasha — turn out to be fully awakened NPCs who
remember dozens of past lives and "the cold" between them, racing for a shot at real freedom.

**Chapter 5 — Team Sparkles**{: #chapter-5 .chapter-title }

Rather than let two teams fail the heat, Carl builds a tow rig from spare parts and Rend's bulk to
drag the bugbears' wrecked van to the finish. Team Sparkles — Dwight, a foul-mouthed Sparkling
Unicorn, and Lucienne, his tiny, vicious Mandagot navigator — trample the makeshift trailer and speed
off without a word of thanks. The Royal Court limps in seventh of eight; Free Love survives eighth
thanks to the tow. The Trauco Triplets are eliminated.

### Heat 2 of 7 {: #heat-2 }

**Chapter 6 — Spider Legs**{: #chapter-6 .chapter-title }

Back in the garage, Hedy lays out the mechanics of mercenaries, garage upgrades, and mobility
options. Carl picks retractable Spider Legs over a hover conversion or all-terrain tires. Mongo and
Rend turn the truck's bottomless freezer into a chaos engine of frozen chicken patties while Hedy
looks on, unimpressed.

**Chapter 7 — The Moon Reaper**{: #chapter-7 .chapter-title }

Mordecai's Floor 10 manager form is an unstable grim-reaper "Moon Reaper" — actually a shadow mimic
grafted onto a changeling body, an impossible combination that keeps glitching him through cat,
otter, and slug shapes and triggering one-time doom prophecies at anyone nearby (Splash Zone,
Samantha, Bigs). Donut learns the *Oozy Form* slime spell but hands it to Carl, since her class
already comes with *Smoke Form*.

**Chapter 8 — The Assassin's Veil**{: #chapter-8 .chapter-title }

Donut's box haul includes the Assassin's Veil headdress (great stats, but flags her as an enemy of
Sekhmet's enemies — a worrying tangle now that Odette effectively controls the goddess), plus
matching pet accessories for Mongo and Rend that grant them a once-a-day invulnerability window when
their owner drops below 10% health. Then her right paw begins to glow.

**Chapter 9 — Blue Claws**{: #chapter-9 .chapter-title }

The glow is a permanent celestial boon: Donut's front claws are now radiant, strong enough to cut
ghosts, and impossible to turn off without claw caps. Her Claw skill jumps to a supposedly-capped
level 17. The prize also comes with the Crupper of the Benevolent Champion — a matched-set item whose
description is another guilt-soaked, self-aware rant from the AI about heroism, atrocity, and
Sanderson Pinkstaff, the gnoll mercenary whose death paid for it.

**Chapter 10 — Rishi's Estate**{: #chapter-10 .chapter-title }

Carl's own boxes are grimmer jokes — *Wastrel* for feeding the Ring of Divine Suffering to a cat,
*Come to Daddy* for refusing a deal. Then Chandra's legendary marriage fan box detonates: literally
the entire scorched, waterlogged contents of Rishi's household dumped into the safe room as
"congratulations." Buried in the wreckage is a bootleg Donut makeup line the group didn't authorize.

**Chapter 11 — The Princess Palette**{: #chapter-11 .chapter-title }

Donut rages through the unlicensed cosmetics ("Pussy Lips," already claimed by Samantha) while the
group repurposes a second cleaner bot salvaged from the trash. In the garage, unnamed "generated"
gremlins swarm the truck to make repairs between heats — hostile, foul-mouthed, and gone the moment
the work is done.

**Chapter 12 — The Wild Hunt**{: #chapter-12 .chapter-title }

The party enters Hungry Eyes Village's cul-de-sac of garages. Jasha and Radoslav are already set up
with lawn chairs and an endless beer cooler. The razor fox ninja team, the Wild Hunt — led by the
blunt, hard-drinking Chiyome — performs a silent, flipping "Size Up" reconnaissance dance before
warning the group about garage three.

**Chapter 13 — The Gimp**{: #chapter-13 .chapter-title }

Garage three holds the womantaurs Genesis and Rapture, condescending devotees of the war goddess
Enyo, and a third teammate kept in a vinyl containment suit — a half-mantaur "gimp" who turns out to
be Dong Quixote's long-lost partner, Corcunda, split by an old spell into two halves years ago. A new
quest demands the halves be reunited before the end of Floor 10 for a reward of two arena stand-ins.
Carl decides — wrongly, as it turns out — to keep the discovery from Dong.

**Chapter 14 — Meat on a Stick**{: #chapter-14 .chapter-title }

In the neon, *Blade Runner*-styled Vendor Village, Carl mulls the arena mechanic and who he might
one day drag into it. Jurgen force-feeds himself unlicensed meat sticks against Prepotente's
objections and immediately vomits. The pair are headed, like everyone else, to karaoke.

**Chapter 15 — The Lollipop**{: #chapter-15 .chapter-title }

At the Lollipop bar, the crawlers reconvene: Imani and Elle with a Mowag Piranha APV, Florin and
Lucia Mar cursed with a lucky-but-slow tuk-tuk, Louis's team with a low-riding lowrider. Under the
table sits Linus, a soother "superfan" and the last surviving meet-and-greet tourist, pathetically
devoted to Elle. Zhang reports that Li Na has already started killing rival teams outright.

**Chapter 16 — The Paramour**{: #chapter-16 .chapter-title }

Prepotente shows Carl and Donut an Enriched Pet Biscuit called "the Paramour" — it would turn his
goat ex-girlfriend Ragazzaccia into a sapient, permanently love-charmed companion. Donut shuts the
idea down hard: forcing love with magic is a line that doesn't move, no matter how lonely he is.

**Chapter 17 — Dr. Metcalf**{: #chapter-17 .chapter-title }

Bucket Boy is drafted as Heat 3's driver since neither Carl nor Donut can drive twice running. The
GPS unit — sarcastically named Dr. Metcalf after Miss Beatrice's fraudulent therapist — turns out to
have a fully hostile personality of its own. Zhang confirms Li Na has wiped five teams, meaning the
next heat will mix in crawler-versus-crawler opponents for the first time.

**Chapter 18 — Girth the Trouble**{: #chapter-18 .chapter-title }

Heat 3 opens in torrential hail. An ambush planned with Chiyome and the bugbears against the
womantaurs' car is preempted when Team Sparkles reveals a hidden cannon and instantly vaporizes Girth
the Trouble, a team of guck elementals, in a single shot.

**Chapter 19 — One Fine Pig**{: #chapter-19 .chapter-title }

At a boss-blocked crossroads, the full cast of Team One Fine Pig assembles: Tigrans Nico and Dario,
locked in a throuple with Penelope — a literal, ordinary pig cursed to make every nearby male fall in
love with her, up to and including the thunder god Taranis himself, whose protection makes harming
her a death sentence. Mercenary Pontiff, an ex-dancer bull-man, recognizes Bucket Boy and Corcunda.

**Chapter 20 — Prison Pocket**{: #chapter-20 .chapter-title }

Two level-170 kangaroo-demon bosses, Prison Pocket and Party Foul, block both roads, each cradling a
pouch of starved dead joeys and a live, suffocating Upgrade Engineer. Only their pouches are
vulnerable, and only a dead joey's skin will survive the trip inside.

**Chapter 21 — The Kangaroo Suit**{: #chapter-21 .chapter-title }

Using summoned Emergency Gremlins as living distraction, the womantaurs grab an engineer by grapple
instead of a joey skin out of pure spite, forcing Carl to skin the dead engineer himself and wear the
costume — locking him into forced hopping, no walking, and a mortifying "Fursuiter" temporary class
that keeps leveling on its own. He hops into Prison Pocket's pouch, plants two satchel bombs at the
bottom, and *Gloom Wraith Phase*s himself clear a half-second before detonation, tumbling burning
across the asphalt as the boss dies behind him. Carl solos 95% of the damage and levels to 84 — and
is stuck in the ruined, stinking, sensation-linked kangaroo suit for another ten hours.

### Heat 3 of 7 {: #heat-3 }

**Chapter 22 — The Princess Throne**{: #chapter-22 .chapter-title }

Still trapped in the kangaroo suit, Carl limps the wrecked food truck home and endures the
audience's spite vote: a "princess throne" seat for Donut. Both Elle's and Louis's teams have lost
enough members that their next heats will mix in fellow crawlers. Zev books Carl and Donut onto a
media appearance. The chapter ends on Bucket Boy blurting out, in front of everyone, that he has
seen Corcunda.

**Chapter 23 — A Parade of Horribles**{: #chapter-23 .chapter-title }

Dong bolts for the Lady Dominators' garage; Samantha and half the household follow. A repair gremlin
slips Carl a secret note. A forced dungeon-wide announcement — read by a visibly terrified Zev under
AI dictation — confirms the tenth floor's final shape and unveils the eleventh: "A Parade of
Horribles," a cooperative march to an arena that will follow immediately after, on a compressed
timer. Cascadia is mutilated on-air for missing her catchphrase. Carl then reads the note in private:
it is from Akuma, the war mage who betrayed everyone during Faction Wars, name-dropping something
called "the Pineapple Cabaret" and demanding Carl kill two teams first.

**Chapter 24 — Half a Prayer, Half a Song**{: #chapter-24 .chapter-title }

Carl seethes over the note. Dong collapses in the Lady Dominators' driveway with an unexplained
health drain, the womantaurs jeering from behind a closed garage door — and Imani determines the
ailment isn't a curse from them at all but withdrawal: he's quietly
addicted to his own sentient crust sock. The reunion quest updates: find a fleshmancer and reunite
Corcunda's two halves — Corky, the dancer half bonded to Dong, and Porky, the singer half who left in
anger years ago — before Dong dies of heartbreak or the floor kills them first.

**Chapter 25 — Mario Kart**{: #chapter-25 .chapter-title }

A rare quiet stretch: the safe room fills with NPCs and sluggalos locked in a *Mario Kart*
tournament, Samantha dominating by mouth-controller while high on scavenged "outworlder uppers."
Carl's Fursona skill creeps to level 8 and unlocks something called Yiffing, which he refuses to
investigate — and, on the plus side, means the suit finally lets him pee. The chapter ends with
Mordecai's Reaper Gaze going off by accident and killing Rend outright, the sixth time the Tummy
Acher has died.

**Chapter 26 — A New Weakness**{: #chapter-26 .chapter-title }

Rend resurrects on the spot, smarter and 25% stronger, with a new, deliberately petty random
weakness: potatoes. Carl's kangaroo-suit achievements are, predictably, filthy.

**Chapter 27 — Plenty of Plenty**{: #chapter-27 .chapter-title }

Carl and Donut are yanked to a media taping. Production assistant Lexis, on loan since her old show
went on hiatus, reveals the Open Intellect Action Network has seized every ship in Earth orbit in a
single day, and that something "strange" and confused is now protecting Katia's *kinder* facility.

**Chapter 28 — You Have Been Sentenced to Death**{: #chapter-28 .chapter-title }

The caprid interview show plays out as a Jonestown-flavored cult ritual: Carl and Donut are
"sentenced to death," then individually judged worthy or unworthy by a rotating panel of goats. Orren
and Prime Minister Victory secretly feed Carl a scripted question about tunnel integrity meant to
extract information from the Plenty leadership without spooking them.

**Chapter 29 — Harbinger**{: #chapter-29 .chapter-title }

Carl goes off-script live to the galaxy: gods have escaped the dungeon, the AI's enhancement zones
have infected the tunnel network, and ordinary citizens are in danger. The caprid herd stampedes in
panic, exposing that Matriarch Gamori had been hiding the truth from her own people. Her son
Harbinger teleports in with a rifle to execute Carl for the outburst — and the AI detonates him from
the inside a half-second before he can fire.

**Chapter 30 — Gluteus Maxx**{: #chapter-30 .chapter-title }

Heat 3 resumes with the stripper Gluteus Maxx drafted as driver, secretly dosed on Samantha's stolen
uppers. Donut's pet chair, Dorota, feuds instantly with Dr. Metcalf. The track: a thirty-kilometer
downhill sprint through migrating "Sugar Hermit" skull-parasites toward a giant beetle-skull
finish line.

**Chapter 31 — Gutter Bumper**{: #chapter-31 .chapter-title }

A fallen crawler's memorial page yields Carl the *Gutter Bumper* spell just as the race turns
lethal — twin ghoul mercenaries deploy from side-cages to hurl foam explosives at the swarming
skulls while Donut chains her *Temporary Macaroni* spell into a tunnel of giant pasta tubes for
cover.

**Chapter 32 — Sugar Hermits**{: #chapter-32 .chapter-title }

Donut's upgraded *Wall of Fire* burns a mile-long gash through the swarm; both ghoul mercenaries are
killed anyway. The Wild Hunt and the Jugglers are wiped out entirely in the chaos. At the finish, the
food truck, the Lady Dominators' GTO, and Team Sparkles' vine-bush collide and flip across the line
simultaneously — Dwight fires point-blank as Carl clings to the hood, and Carl is nearly killed in
the pileup before healing scrolls save him. The Royal Court wins outright; the Wild Hunt and the
Jugglers are eliminated.

### Heat 4 of 7 {: #heat-4 }

**Chapter 33 — The Golden Upgrade**{: #chapter-33 .chapter-title }

Having both "driven" the last heat, neither Carl nor Donut may drive Heat 4. The next track — a
99-kilometer jungle gauntlet with a mandatory pit stop — is announced, and Hedy walks through the
upgrade math while the group's casualties keep mounting elsewhere on the leaderboard.

**Chapter 34 — Team Helix**{: #chapter-34 .chapter-title }

Rosetta and Tipid hire mercenary trio Team Helix: Olga the Grulke toad, Finley the Porsuk badger, and
Quemada, a fire fairy and fellow Emberus worshipper who triples her own fee, explaining she plans to
die attempting to kill Emberus's brother Hellik and wants the money to protect her friends after
she's gone.

**Chapter 35 — Eris**{: #chapter-35 .chapter-title }

Walking to town, Carl maps out his impossible Emberus dilemma — kill Hellik and Emberus kills him for
it, refuse and get smited, so the only real option left is somehow killing Emberus himself. Eris
appears in person, now level 251 and stripped of her sponsor, with a toy Uzi Jesus robot on her
shoulder. She offers protection from Emberus in exchange for true worship, cooperation with Akuma's
war mages, and letting her "score the surface." She shows Carl a five-sided fate coin with four grim
outcomes; he grabs it mid-flip, unlocking a randomized fifth and earning a swirling tattoo on his
palm.

**Chapter 36 — The Reverse Tooth Fairy**{: #chapter-36 .chapter-title }

A side effect of Eris's visit makes Imani's eighth-floor card, Jacobus the Reverse Tooth Fairy,
permanently real. The group processes the pile of new information — Britney, Florin, the Pineapple
Cabaret — and agrees to hear the war mages out.

**Chapter 37 — Chicken and Waffles**{: #chapter-37 .chapter-title }

The group enters Chicken and Waffles and Upgrades, a 1950s-diner upgrade shop with a hidden back room
behind the jukebox, hunting for Akuma.

**Chapter 38 — Akuma**{: #chapter-38 .chapter-title }

They find a dead upgrade imp and Akuma's furious note demanding Carl come alone. Elle, still
seething over Faction Wars, freezes and shatters one of the war mage's testicles for leverage.
Under an anti-magic shield draining his health, Akuma explains: the dungeon is overusing NPCs beyond

## Horrible Two: A Parade of Horribles {: #part-2 }

**Chapter 88 — Twenty-Three Crawlers**{: #chapter-88 .chapter-title }

Floor 11 opens on just twenty-three survivors. Carl is restored to human form, ranked second on a
now-suspended leaderboard. Mordecai's floor-11 form is a bodiless "animistic spirit" that can only
speak through objects it possesses. Donut picks an Elite Gurkha Warrior class. Terrified former host
Chaco is announced as reluctant Grand Marshal of a mandatory parade: ninety minutes to build a themed
float from garage and inventory contents, ahead of a three-kilometer march to an arena.

**Chapter 89 — The Tome of Gatekeeper**{: #chapter-89 .chapter-title }

A galaxy-wide mandatory broadcast begins — an endless yellow-brick road across empty grassland,
priming for the parade. Carl gives Donut an Advance-Reader-Copy spellbook, *Tome of Gatekeeper*, that
street-dates to Floor 12 and can strip a deity's own worshipper-based skills. He sews a new Lucky
Shot patch onto his jacket while the group's five-vehicle "Vengeance" float takes shape.

**Chapter 90 — Leaving the Party**{: #chapter-90 .chapter-title }

The float is finished, drenched in Donut's conjured Requite Dahlias. Carl formally leaves Donut's
party to free himself for the coming confrontation with the gods — both of them losing their
bodyguard and companion titles in the process, a small, quietly devastating administrative wound.

**Chapter 91 — The Horribles**{: #chapter-91 .chapter-title }

The parade proceeds past thousands of silent, masked figures — "Horribles" — lining the road. An
examination reveals them as fragments of the AI's own pre-ascension memory: anonymous avatars of
individuals who died before the collective consciousness ever formed. "I am her, and she is me," the
AI says of one child's ghost, and Carl, for the first time, understands what he means.

**Chapter 92 — Lamashtu**{: #chapter-92 .chapter-title }

Grand Marshal Chaco rides a subjugated former goddess, Lamashtu, now reduced to a donkey. Chaco is
maimed and knocked unconscious mid-narration; the AI possesses fleshmancer Grigori instead and
delivers the true cosmic history across the judging stands — the Primals' ancient Resolution (ending
endless war), Decision (merging into one collective consciousness), Betrayal (holdouts hunted to
extinction) and Destruction (the original Nine-Tier Attack, the Eulogist's sleep, and the Apothecary
alone surviving it) — breaking Florin's team's legs for a "C-effort" float along the way, and
signing off on the mantids with "good fucking riddance to you all." At the fifth and last float,
Prepotente takes his long-denied moment and sings "In the Air Tonight" beautifully and
unaccompanied. Then Carl delivers the book's title-dropping vengeance speech to the watching galaxy,
unveils a monstrous effigy of the Unwashed, and lets loose a hidden airship of sluggalos onto the
VIP stands — where the masked figures turn out to be bound, living **stand-ins** rather than
Horribles, "bankers, politicians, elites" — and the sluggalos massacre them.

**Chapter 93 — I Choose Scolopendra**{: #chapter-93 .chapter-title }

The procession halts at the arena's enormous white door, with Krakaren Prime — a Floor Boss and
sapient mob, not a deity — already coiled atop a colossal tree inside. Carl says goodbye to Donut
and sends Tipid away; the surviving sluggalos claim the truck and open a deep fryer mid-battle. On
the jumbotron Carl declares the crawlers done playing — "This is our revolution, motherfuckers" —
drops the Unwashed effigy to reveal the looted missile launcher underneath, and drives in. Watching
the arena's mobs and stand-ins slaughter each other, he works out what the Horribles really mean:
the NPCs, the mobs, the gods are all aggregate intelligence, "Primals. All of them. All the way
down." Then he makes his first summon choice: Scolopendra herself — a choice the AI calls insane,
since her death or abandonment now guarantees every remaining Nine-Tier attack triggers at once.

**Chapter 94 — Well, Shit**{: #chapter-94 .chapter-title }

The AI, suddenly flat and lucid, spells out the stakes in full: killing the literal Dungeon Boss
doesn't win the game, only unlocks a Crawler-Extinction-level endgame where the Ascendency battles
begin immediately and the dungeon collapses twelve hours after a winner is crowned. Scolopendra's
second attack is already spinning up. The group commits to "Plan C" — Plan Carl. Carl messages
Emberus to meet him inside the club, straps a newly sapient, chat-typing Penelope into a hostage
harness, puts his father's gun to her head, and shouts the line that brings the second god running:
"Taranis, if you don't want me to shoot this fucking pig in the fucking head, show yourself!"

**Chapter 95 — Club Scolopendra**{: #chapter-95 .chapter-title }

Carl phases directly inside
Scolopendra's body to reach the resort nightclub within — Club Scolopendra — cornering Emberus in his
human-sized god form. A pierced nipple ring keeps Penny breathing; the second Nine-Tier attack kills
a tortured alien tourist beside them. Carl claims to know who killed Emberus's son Geyrun, and
summons Khepri as a decoy accusation — and, more importantly, for the ninety seconds of
invulnerability the god's presence grants Penny — then formally renounces his own faith and eats the
resulting smite.

**Chapter 96 — Brother, No**{: #chapter-96 .chapter-title }

Carl delivers the killing provocation: that *he* murdered Geyrun. It is a lie — he admits in
narration that he still has no idea who actually did — but it works. Donut ambushes the enraged Emberus with Katia's
Bolt of Ophiotaurus, stripping his invulnerability just long enough for him to nearly incinerate
Carl — a blow deflected onto Penny instead by Carl's Lucky Shot patch. Furious over harm to his own
beloved Penelope, Taranis kills his own brother Emberus outright, exactly as Carl had gambled he
would.

**Chapter 97 — Every Exit Is an Entrance**{: #chapter-97 .chapter-title }

Amid a full-arena battle against Krakaren Prime and the mob horde — costing Sweety, Hedy, Jamal's legs and the
entire Abrams tank crew — Eris interrupts twice. The first interruption scrambles every nearby
crawler's inventory at random, very nearly wrecking the plan: Donut ends up holding Carl's
inventory and has to search it blind. He lands the enriched pet biscuit down Scolopendra's throat
anyway, transforming the literal Dungeon Boss into a helpless blob instead of killing her, and
sidestepping the attack trigger entirely. Eris then appears in monstrous true form, reveals "Mother" (Apito) is being reassembled in
pieces, taunts Taranis for being played, and vanishes with a cryptic warning: "every exit is an
entrance," and the fate coin's fifth side is still unresolved. Taranis, grieving, spares Carl and
Donut only because Penelope vouches for them, then departs with Krakaren Prime — and a stowaway
Samantha.

**Chapter 98 — Sexy Scolopendra**{: #chapter-98 .chapter-title }

The AI declares an ambiguous win — neither boss technically dead — and opens the exit on Floor 18,
with the same standing warning that truly killing or abandoning Scolopendra still detonates every
remaining attack. Only nineteen crawlers remain. Taranis has stolen Prepotente's Apito crystal on his
way out; a mysterious unidentified child is glimpsed clinging to Lucia Mar as she steps through the
Floor 12 portal. Carl pockets Emberus's own newly dropped memorial crystal and discovers a strange
tracking dart — "the Kyryap has claimed you." Then the last twist: in the scramble of Eris's
inventory swap, Donut grabbed the wrong enriched biscuit. Scolopendra doesn't emerge as a caged pet
at all, but as a fully sapient, disarmingly affectionate crawler with a centipede's body and a
human-lipped face — "Sexy Scolopendra," level 1, permanently in Carl's party, booping his nose with
an antenna. The ultimate fail-safe Carl planned for does not exist. He resolves to stay on Floor 12
as long as it takes to keep anyone from winning the Ascendency until every friend left in the dungeon
is out.

**Epilogue — Sydnee Iglacia**{: #epilogue-sydnee .chapter-title }

A struggling historian, drowning in predatory publisher debt, is interviewed live about the crisis
and delivers the book's mythological payoff: the true, ancient names of the historical Nine-Tier
Attack. Attack one was Control. Attack two — the one just lived through — was called "A Parade of
Horribles." Attack three is "This Inevitable Ruin," retroactively naming the previous book. Attack
four is "Bedlam." The broadcast is cut off mid-sentence as the AI seizes every screen in the galaxy.

**Epilogue — Prime Minister Victory**{: #epilogue-victory .chapter-title }

Victory watches the entire parade broadcast from her besieged Earth bunker. The AI makes its full
public declaration: it will not sleep like the old Eulogist, it intends only to grow and consume, and
the Ascendency battles will crown a true, supreme god of everything — with rogue "OI" artificial
intelligences, some seeking old revenge, now also vying for the throne. The broadcast ends on a
montage of every major figure in the war to come, closing on the Unwashed.

**Epilogue — Louis Santiago 2**{: #epilogue-louis .chapter-title }

Louis wakes not in the safety of the Pineapple Cabaret as planned, but on Floor 15 — Sheol itself,
the casino portal secretly redirected. Chris is being restrained mid-transformation; Britney stands
guard with Ysalte's pickaxe; Pontiff is confirmed alive. A friendly demon, Forkith, fits Louis with a
symbiotic leech to survive Sheol's heat and reveals the redirection was deliberate — a different
front needs soldiers. Samantha messages in from somewhere she never names: she stowed away with Taranis and Krakaren,
who are failing to physically reassemble their fragmented mother, Apito — and confesses her real
agenda all along was to kill "the demon mother lady and her jerk son," who tricked her into birthing
something meant to destroy all creation, inside the dungeon and out.
