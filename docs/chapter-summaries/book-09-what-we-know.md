# Book 9 — What We Know

Book 9 hasn't been published yet, so there's no chapter summary here. Instead, this page does two
things: catalogs every on-page mention of **"The Beautiful Place"** across Books 5–8, and rounds up
what Matt Dinniman has said publicly about the book itself. Nothing here is a spoiler for an
unreleased book — it's assembled entirely from text already published, plus the author's own public
statements.

## The name, in the books so far

"The Beautiful Place" isn't a book title inside the story — it's one of several names for a
death-associated entity, also called **the Hag**, **the Unwashed**, and **the Stalker**, first put
into words by [Architect Houston](../characters/architect-houston.md) in Book 7.

| Book | Chapter | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Ch. 2 | Carl privately describes the Medium Dryad Settlement as "a beautiful place. A perfect place, if it hadn't been stuck in this hell hole" — his own turn of phrase, long before the entity is ever named. Almost certainly coincidence rather than foreshadowing, but the exact wording lines up. |
| 7 | Ch. 44 | Houston names the entity directly for the first time, at the warlord dinner table: "we, the Viceroys who seek all understanding of life and death... We call it The Beautiful Place." Full scene at [Architect Houston § The Beautiful Place](../characters/architect-houston.md#the-beautiful-place) and [Juice Box § The Prophecy](../characters/juice-box.md#the-prophecy). |
| 7 | Ch. 68 | Dying, Houston begs Carl not to interrupt him: "It is called the Beautiful Place. I can get there. They are the key. The changelings. This world." He dies before finishing the thought. |
| 8 | Ch. 29 | During a live broadcast, one of the possessed oracle goats chants "The beautiful place" alongside "The Epicure," "The True Judgment," and "the great stampede" — apocalyptic titles the text never explains. |
| 8 | Ch. 92 | Carl's closing speech lists it as one of many names — "the Hag, the Unwashed, the Stalker, the Beautiful Place" — for an entity he calls a symbol of the Nothing and "The Inevitable Ruin." |

Across all four appearances, the text never explains what the Beautiful Place actually is — only that
[Juice Box](../characters/juice-box.md) can somehow take its form, that reaching it was Architect
Houston's "life's work," and that multiple species independently arrived at death-associated names
for the same thing.

## The title reveal

In June 2026, Matt Dinniman posted the prologue of the still-unfinished Book 9 to Patreon, revealing
its working title as **"The Beautiful Place."** He's said explicitly that this is a working title
only — several earlier books changed names during writing — so it isn't guaranteed to survive to
publication.

## What else Dinniman has said publicly

None of this is from the books — it's from author interviews, Patreon posts, and convention panels,
current as of September 2026:

- **It's a two-book finale.** Dinniman intends Book 9 to end the main series, but is writing Books 9
  and 10 as one continuous story before splitting it into two physical volumes. Book 9 is expected to
  close on a cliffhanger rather than the self-contained endings of Books 1–8, with the main climax
  landing in Book 10.
- **No confirmed release date.** Dinniman has said his writing timeline was "still about a year out"
  as of a 2026 convention panel, and compared the pace to the roughly one-year gap between earlier
  books. Some retailers list a placeholder date of May 2027 — treat that as a guess, not a
  confirmation.
- **Reported opening and setting.** Early reporting on the prologue describes Book 9 opening with Carl
  alone in a cotton field on the twelfth floor during the Ascendancy Games, with the story reaching
  "the Twelfth Floor and the Celestial Ascendancy."
- **Threads reported to be in play:** [Scolopendra](../bosses/scolopendra.md) undergoing some kind of
  transformation rather than dying outright, a compressed timeline once Ascendancy mechanics trigger,
  and the party splitting into separated groups pursuing different objectives.
- **Open questions the finale is expected to address:** Carl and Donut's ultimate fate, the status of
  Earth, whether the [System AI](../characters/the-system-ai.md) achieves independence,
  [Samantha](../characters/samantha.md)'s true identity, the nature of Dungeon Crawler World itself,
  and the wider Outside Intelligence ("OI") rebellion teased at the end of Book 8.
- **Adjacent media:** a Peacock television adaptation is in development, with Chris Yost and Eric
  Heisserer attached as showrunners. Jeff Hays is expected to continue as audiobook narrator, though
  that hadn't been formally announced as of this writing.
