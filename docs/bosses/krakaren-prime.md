# Krakaren Prime

!!! warning "Not the same entity as Krakaren Clone"
    This page is about **Krakaren Prime**, a single named individual. She is **not** the Floor 2
    boss the party actually fought and killed in Book 1 — see [Krakaren Clone](krakaren-clone.md).
    That fight, and the Floor 4 drug-den Krakaren bosses in the [bosses index](index.md#floor-4-book-3),
    are all copies: "Krakaren Prime births two more" for every clone killed. No dedicated
    Krakaren species or faction page exists yet in [Species](../species/index.md) or
    [Factions](../factions/index.md) — when one is written, it should note this same distinction
    rather than treat "the Krakaren" as a single collective mind. Book 8 is simply the first time
    the real, individual Krakaren Prime appears in person.

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Floor Boss
    - **Level:** 235
    - **Floor:** Referenced since Book 7 (banished in the Nothing); physically appears for the first
      time at the Floor 11 arena climax (Book 8)
    - **Pronouns:** She/her
    - **Form:** A massive tentacled monstrosity, seen atop the shattering white tree in the Floor 11
      arena
    - **Brought to the arena by:** The system AI, **not** requested by Carl — added unilaterally
      once the AI "caught wind" of what the crawlers (and "someone else") were planning and decided
      "it would be appropriate"
    - **Resolution:** Not killed. Left in the stands with only two tentacles remaining, health in the
      red, and carried away by [Taranis](taranis.md) at the end of the fight

## Established Lore (Book 7)

Krakaren Prime is not new to this wiki. She has been referenced as a real, extant, individual entity
since Book 7, well before she ever appears on the page:

- The **[Krakaren Clone](krakaren-clone.md)** boss card fought on Floor 2 in Book 1 states outright
  that it is not "*the*" Krakaren — "This is *a* Krakaren... For every one that is killed, Krakaren
  Prime births two more." The Floor 4 drug-den Krakaren bosses (see the
  [bosses index](index.md#floor-4-book-3)) are the same kind of copy.
- The **[Hell-Kissed Potion of Conscription](../items/hell-kissed-potion-of-conscription.md)** —
  level 18, three levels above the prior series ceiling — is brewed "in the dark recesses of the
  Nothing by Krakaren Prime, who is said to yearn for domination, which was why she'd been banished
  there in the first place. She prepares for a war she considers inevitable."
- **[The Midnight Market](../mechanics/locations.md)**, the hidden bazaar under the Desperado Club, is
  run by the merchant **Paca**, "an agent for Krakaren Prime, who is stuck in the Nothing" — the same
  Paca who swallows the **[Gate of the Feral Gods](../items/gate-of-the-feral-gods.md)** in trade with
  the [War Mage Rebellion](../factions/war-mage-rebellion.md) for five of her potions.
- [Eris](eris.md) names her directly in Book 7 as the key to recovering the stolen Gate.

Book 8 is the first time any of this pays off in person — the "real" Krakaren Prime, not a copy,
physically present and fighting.

## The Floor 11 Arena

The arena door opens onto a hundreds-of-feet-tall white tree with something enormous already coiled
at its top, roaring loud enough to shake the world. Carl recognizes her before any system text
confirms it: "Krakaren Prime, I assumed. She'd been banished to the Nothing, and the last I'd heard,
she'd ended up on the eighteenth floor when she'd escaped" — his own assumption, based on secondhand
reports, not a confirmed on-page fact.

Crucially, **Carl never chooses to summon her.** His own request — "For my first choice, I choose
Scolopendra" — brings only [Scolopendra](scolopendra.md). Krakaren Prime is already there, added by
the AI on its own initiative as a second, uninvited boss fight stacked on top of the first:

!!! quote "System Message, then the AI's own aside (Book 8, ch. 94)"
    "You have entered the lair of a Floor Boss. You have entered the lair of the Dungeon Boss...
    Krakaren Prime. Level 235 Floor boss."

    "Miss Krakaren Prime herself is sitting up there right now, *very* pissed off, and not just at
    you. Honestly, I wasn't going to bring her here, but I caught wind of what you and someone else
    are planning, and I thought it would be appropriate."

She spends most of the fight wrapped around the tree, roaring and shedding tentacles as [Scolopendra](scolopendra.md)
coils around the same trunk below her, the two bosses tangled together as the tree itself splinters.
[Taranis](taranis.md), once drawn into the open as bait against [Emberus](emberus.md), tears her
tentacles off one by one bare-handed while defending [Penelope](../characters/penelope.md) — by the
time the dust settles she has only two left, health in the red, "smashed amongst the unmoving
horribles avatars" in the stands. A hexcrafted tank round from Sarah Hayse's Abrams tank barely dents
her further. She is never killed.

## Taken Away

At the very end of the fight, Taranis — declaring "I do not know what my sister meant about there
being four pieces of my mother, but I am taking two of them" — snatches
[Samantha](../characters/samantha.md) out of the air and departs the realm, taking Krakaren Prime with
him at the same instant:

!!! quote "System Message (Book 8, ch. 97)"
    "Taranis has left the realm. Krakaren has left the realm."

## Open Threads

Book 8 deliberately does not resolve what any of this means:

- **Apito.** Carl's own theory, stated but never confirmed: "I think Krakaren has something to do
  with Apito. So does Samantha." Whether Krakaren Prime is connected to the goddess Apito's
  scattered pieces, or simply another casualty Taranis is retrieving alongside her, is unstated.
  See [Taranis](taranis.md#the-crystal-the-rejection-and-leaving-with-samantha) for the parallel
  theft of Prepotente's Apito memorial crystal in the same scene.
- **Samantha.** Her own stated plan involves "the demon mother lady and her jerk son" and a monster
  she was tricked into birthing — entirely her own arc; see
  [Samantha](../characters/samantha.md#leaving-with-taranis) rather than treated as resolved here.
- **The War Mage Rebellion and the Midnight Market.** With Krakaren Prime physically removed from
  the Nothing, the status of Paca, the Midnight Market, and the supply of hell-kissed Conscription
  potions is left an open question for future books.

These are flagged as open threads, not guessed at further.
