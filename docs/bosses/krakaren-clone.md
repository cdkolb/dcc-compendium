## B... B... B... Boss Battle

Per the in-universe boss-battle card:

> "First off, this isn't *the* Krakaren. This is *a* Krakaren. For every one that is killed,
> Krakaren Prime births two more. Part of a collective mind intent upon destroying any semblance
> of scientific progress in the universe, the Krakaren is the only communal brain entity in the
> galaxy who actually gets stupider as time moves on... Even Eris, Goddess of Chaos, doesn't want
> anything to do with these crazy assholes."

## Encounter

An unusual, unsettling fight: this boss breaks through walls and leaves its own lair (earning
Carl the achievement "Wait, Bosses Can Leave Their Rooms?"), tracking them into a Rev-Up
moonshine distillery room staffed by clurichaun and laminak-fairy workers. Mid-fight, a glob of
the creature's dripping mucus splashes onto Carl and inflicts an infection debuff called **the
Taint**, which blocks all healing. Under fire from tentacles and clurichaun workers, Carl
improvises a "Rev-Up Immunity Smoothie" (moonshine blended with a dead laminak fairy's corpse,
per an in-world recipe, mixed using a bicycle-powered blender) for temporary immunity to
debuffs — it doesn't cure the Taint outright, but keeps it from killing him while he survives the
fight. The Krakaren itself is never fought in a conventional melee confrontation: the boss is
ultimately killed by a large explosion (from the distillery's own still equipment) that Carl and
Donut barely escape with third-degree burns and broken legs, healed just in time by Donut's last
Heal scroll. Afterward they find the Krakaren's corpse in the wreckage — a massive, octopus-like
entity, roughly 20 feet tall, half its head burned away.

Mordecai later implies this is a recurring franchise-level threat across the wider series
("it wasn't the last we'd see of that particular boss") and refuses to explain its weakness,
which Carl takes as confirmation he's already figured out something true about it.
