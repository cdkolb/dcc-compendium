# Taranis

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Deity — God of Lightning and Thunder
    - **Level:** 250 (deity tier)
    - **First referenced:** Book 5 ([The Apito Pantheon](../mechanics/index.md#the-apito-pantheon-book-5))
      and Book 6–7 (worshipped by Club Vanquisher's Ogre Basilica Cleric Guards, whose faith grants
      the rare **Untouchable** status); named but never appearing in person until Book 8
    - **Floor:** [Floor 10](index.md) ("Don't Come in Last") and the [Floor 11](index.md) arena
      climax (Book 8)
    - **Family:** Husband of **Apito**, the Blessed Oak Mother — also, per Book 5's Apito Pantheon
      lore and Book 8's own text, her son; half-brother of [Eris](eris.md); brother of
      [Emberus](emberus.md) and [Hellik](hellik.md); father (with Apito) of the banished god Yarilo
    - **Resolution:** Not fought — lured into the open as bait, kills his own brother Emberus, and
      leaves the realm of his own accord
    - **Presence effect:** *All lightning-based attacks are 50% more powerful; you take 100% more
      damage from lightning attacks yourself*

## Who He Is

Taranis is one of the dungeon's oldest-referenced gods and one of its longest-unseen. His worship
grants Club Vanquisher's Ogre Basilica Cleric Guards their **Untouchable** status — gods cannot
attack them, and they can bar a deity from crossing a threshold — which is exactly why, once those
guards abandon their post during Faction Wars, the gods are free to wander onto the Floor 9
battlefield at all (see [Floor 9](../floors/level-09.md) and [Club Vanquisher](../locations/club-vanquisher.md)).
Long before he appears, his image is misidentified: the ram-headed guards at Club Vanquisher's
vestibule insist a stained-glass window depicting a winged angel rising toward an inverted tree
shows Hellik; "it is in fact Taranis, Hellik's brother."

Per the origin myth Queen Imogen relates in Book 5 (see
[The Apito Pantheon](../mechanics/index.md#the-apito-pantheon-book-5)), Taranis and his wife Apito,
the Blessed Oak Mother, once hosted a peace-night party for their "squabbling divine children" in the
Halls of the Ascendency. Their son **Yarilo**, god of lust, broke the peace by assaulting another
god's familiar and was banished into the Nothing by Apito — enraging Taranis and triggering a
slaughter that killed every mortal and demigod guest. That party became the recurring **Butcher's
Masquerade** ritual. Eris also names **Grull** as one of "Apito's brood," making Taranis, Emberus,
Eris, Yarilo and Grull siblings — while Apito is, per the text itself, both Taranis's mother *and*
his wife. Book 8 does not soften or explain this; Eris's own words to Taranis are blunt: "Ever since
you started fucking mom, I knew you'd lost it."

Eris also names him, alongside Emberus and Hellik, as one of the three gods she would require Carl to
kill in exchange for her own patronage (see [Eris](eris.md) and
[Champion Status](../mechanics/index.md#champion-status-book-7)) — a debt still outstanding
when Book 8 opens.

## Falling for Penelope

Before Book 8's climax, Taranis is already entangled with the party through
[Penelope](../characters/penelope.md), then still an ordinary pig belonging to Nico and Dario's
"One Fine Pig" racing team. Penny carries a stray love-curse (the Snowdrop Curse / Touch of Maw-Loo)
that makes certain NPCs and deities — Taranis chief among them — become instantly, helplessly
infatuated with her, with Taranis personally shielding her from harm on pain of death. This is
established well before Carl ever meets the god directly, and it is what makes Penny viable bait
later.

## The Bait

At the Floor 11 arena finale, Carl needs Emberus vulnerable long enough to kill, and he needs a way
to draw a second, far stronger god into the fight to actually land the killing blow. He weaponizes
Taranis's obsession with Penny directly, strapping her into a movement harness, dosing her with
"splooge," and holding a gun to her head on open channel:

!!! quote "Book 8, ch. 94"
    "Taranis, if you don't want me to shoot this fucking pig in the fucking head, show yourself!"

Both gods answer. Taranis's reaction is immediate and genuine — "Penelope! My sweet love! Hold on! I
will protect you as soon as I figure out how to get you out!" — and for the rest of the fight he
remains physically outside [Club Scolopendra](scolopendra.md), pinned to the spot by his need to keep
Penny safe, exactly as Carl's plan requires. See [Penelope](../characters/penelope.md) for the full
mechanics of the bait and her side of what follows.

While Taranis is distracted, Carl publicly renounces his own faith and confesses to killing Emberus's
son Geyrun (see [Carl](../characters/carl.md#renouncing-emberus) and [Emberus](emberus.md#book-8-a-parade-of-horribles)
for that confrontation in full). Emberus, pinned and vulnerable from a pair of **Katia's Bolt of
Ophiotaurus** crossbow bolts, hurls a lethal fire attack — but the party's **Four-Leaf Clover Patch**
redirects it onto Penny instead, momentarily reducing her to "a tiny bubbling pile of plasma."

## Killing Emberus

Taranis sees his own brother's attack land on Penny and reacts without hesitation.

!!! quote "Book 8, ch. 96"
    "Taranis, seeing his little brother rising in the arena and knowing he was the one who had fired
    the attack at his beloved pig, raised his hand. Emberus paused, a moment of lucidity coming over
    the insane deity. 'Brother, no,' he said. Taranis twisted his wrist. ... With one second left on
    his invulnerability, Emberus, god of sun and ash, exploded."

!!! quote "System Message"
    "A deity has fallen. The heavens tremble with rage."

The kill is committed with Emberus's own invulnerability timer at one second remaining — the entire
plan's margin. Taranis then personally revives Penny, cradling and healing her before setting her
gently down beside Carl and Donut. Whether Emberus's death is permanent is not confirmed anywhere in
Book 8; see the caveat on [Emberus's own page](emberus.md#permanence-not-confirmed) — gods in this
series have "died" and returned before (**Diwata**, killed on Floor 6, was active again by Book 7).

## The Crystal, the Rejection, and Leaving With Samantha

In the same chaotic stretch, [Prepotente](../characters/prepotente.md)'s **Memorial Crystal: Apito**
is pulled from around his neck by an unnamed thief — "The deity stole my memorial crystal! The thief
pulled it right off my neck!" The book never names the thief on the page. Carl's own guess points at
Taranis, on the strength of Taranis being the god confirmed to leave the realm carrying off both
[Krakaren Prime](krakaren-prime.md) and [Samantha](../characters/samantha.md) at the same moment: "I
think Krakaren has something to do with Apito. So does Samantha. And he took the memorial crystal
because it contains all her memories and spells." Treat the identity of the thief as a **strong
inference, not a confirmed fact** — see [Prepotente's own page](../characters/prepotente.md) for the
full "which deity" caveat.

Once the fight ends, Penny herself ends things with Taranis — not over the danger he put her in, but
over a smaller, more human betrayal:

!!! quote "Book 8, ch. 97"
    "'HE LIED ABOUT THE SPLOOGE, SO WE BROKE UP. I HAD NO IDEA HE WAS MARRIED.'"

Taranis does **not** leave the realm with Penny, despite how hard he fell for her. Instead:

!!! quote "Book 8, ch. 97"
    "'I do not know what my sister meant about there being four pieces of my mother, but I am taking
    two of them. I am sparing your lives because my Penelope spoke on your behalf. We are not done.'"

He then snatches [Samantha](../characters/samantha.md) — who has been quietly whispering in his ear —
out of the air, and departs with both her and the badly wounded [Krakaren Prime](krakaren-prime.md).
Samantha's own reasons for stowing away are her own arc, not Taranis's; see
[Samantha's page](../characters/samantha.md#leaving-with-taranis) rather than duplicating it here. Per
Eris, the pair are headed to a temple to try to "combine a bunch of things to make whole his mom
wife" (Apito) — an attempt Samantha's own parting message says is "not going to work" because "they're
missing a piece."

## Status at Book's End

Taranis leaves Floor 11 alive, unfought, and unresolved: still married to (and descended from) Apito,
still owed a kill by Eris's old bargain, and now traveling with both Krakaren Prime and a stowaway
Samantha toward an attempt to reassemble his mother/wife's scattered pieces. Nothing about that
attempt, or about Apito's true condition, is resolved in Book 8.
