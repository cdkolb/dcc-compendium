# Rude-Dolph the Blood-Nosed Slay-Deer

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Mock battle arena opponent — **Puppet-Master** type
    - **Level:** 100
    - **Classification:** "Eastern United States Legendary Creature"
    - **Floor:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth"), the mock battle arena (a simulated shopping mall)
    - **Killed by:** [Shi Maria](shi-maria.md), who ripped him in half once his puppets were down
      (ch. 28)

## The Puppet-Master Mechanic

Rude-Dolph is the party's first encounter with the **Puppet-Master** monster type, and the mechanic
is the whole fight:

!!! quote "Monster description (Book 6, ch. 28)"
    "Warning: This is a Puppet-Master. This creature is intangible until all the puppets have been
    killed.
    Eight of Eight Puppets still live.
    Nobody is laughing at him anymore."

He rises as a ninth reindeer behind eight already-summoned ones, glowing yellow to mark his
invulnerability, with the severed head of the mall's Santa hanging from his mouth. Until all eight
puppet reindeer are dead, nothing can touch him — a marker over his head tracks the count.

## Attacks

His nose fires a sustained laser, "like a continuous Magic Missile blast," which sweeps through the
simulated mall: it collapses a ceiling support, shears a leg clean off Shi Maria, and cuts straight
through Skylar Spinach. The eight puppets attack independently, one of them (Comet) casting white
magic bolts.

## The Fight

Donut plays **Skylar Spinach**, then **Greased Lightning** on the injured Shi Maria — which splits
the spider into four identical copies. Shi Maria then opens her **fifth eye**, and the puppet herd
turns on itself: half the surviving reindeer gore each other, frothing, as if hit with an enrage
debuff and *Why Are You Hitting Yourself* simultaneously. All eight puppets are dead within seconds.

The moment the marker over Rude-Dolph blinks out and his yellow glow vanishes, Shi Maria — still
missing a leg — dodges his next nose blast, climbs to the mall's second floor, leaps across the
atrium and physically rips him in two. The two halves hang in the air, his nose sputtering out.
