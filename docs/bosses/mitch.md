# Mitch ("Mitch Fiorelli")

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Not on the standard Neighborhood–Country boss ladder; a named individual mob boss
      (an ambush encounter, not a boss-carded fight)
    - **Level:** 120
    - **Species:** **Adolescent Shadow Mimic** — see [Species](../species/index.md) once a dedicated
      page exists (a Species-pass topic); referenced here only by term
    - **Floor:** [Floor 10](index.md) ("Don't Come in Last"), the remodeled Desperado Club casino
      (Book 8)
    - **Cover identity:** The Desperado Club's new Wheel of Fortune croupier, replacing the
      previous croupier **Tito**
    - **Killed by:** Frozen by [Carl](../characters/carl.md) opening the **Eye of the Bedlam Bride**
      while transformed into a dog, then finished off by Princess Donut's *Magic Missile*

## The Setup

By Book 8, the Desperado Club casino has been rebuilt more ornately than before, and its Wheel of
Fortune game has a new dealer standing in for the previous croupier, Tito. Carl and party come to
this "Mitch" hoping to buy access to the game's two-space **"Nothing" spot** — a wheel result that,
per the war mage [Akuma](../characters/akuma.md), had earlier let their ally Pontiff jump safely
into a stasis "holding area" connected to the **Pineapple Cabaret** (see
[the war mage rebellion](../factions/war-mage-rebellion.md) for the Cabaret's own background).

Examining Mitch returns an unusually blocked-out description:

!!! quote "Book 8"
    "Mitch Fiorelli. Level 120. Desperado Club Wheel of Fortune Croupier. Also, the guy who will
    tea-bag you. This is an Enforcer for the \<Redacted\>. You may not further examine soldiers of
    \<Redacted\> until \<Redacted\>."

Mitch charges steep, escalating fees to turn the wheel manually to the Nothing spot and let crawlers
jump through — two thousand gold a head plus a 20% tip, then five thousand for a single test run,
then another five thousand when Britney jumps through uninvited, then ten thousand a head for
everyone after that. He does so twice,
successfully sending **Chris Andrews, Britney Proskurina, and Louis Santiago** through to what the
party believes is the same stasis holding area — high-stakes gambles the party takes because they
have no other way to save the crawlers from an unwinnable final race heat.

## The Reveal

Once the party has spent enough and pushed enough, Mitch snaps his fingers, and the entire casino —
every table, chair, slot machine, and chandelier — reveals itself as an ambush of mimics that had
been waiting the whole time. Mitch himself elongates, splitting open along the middle to reveal a
serpentine, razor-fanged mouth.

!!! quote "Book 8"
    "Mitch. Level 120. Adolescent Shadow Mimic. This is an Enforcer for the \<Redacted\>."

The system AI uses the fight to lore-dump the true nature of Shadow Mimics and, in the same breath,
its own "Big Six" taxonomy of the dungeon's competing power factions — a
[Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md)-pass topic referenced here only by name; see
[Carl's own page](../characters/carl.md#freezing-mitch-as-a-dog) for the full list as delivered
on-page. The short version: Shadow Mimics are near-undetectable, grow in intelligence and power
without any hard ceiling, and are one part of "Group Four" (the sapient mobs), alongside the Ogre
Imperium, the War Mage Rebellion, and non-deity boss monsters like
[Krakaren Prime](krakaren-prime.md).

The AI also confirms what happened to the real croupier:

!!! quote "Book 8"
    "The real Mitch, incidentally, was someone called a Wall Rat. Those are NPCs who've figured out
    how to extricate themselves from the whole dungeon-wipe-rewrite-dungeon cycle of most NPCs...
    Mitch Prime was eaten by Mimic Mitch here. Shadow Mimics love eating flesh."

## The Fight

The ambush triggers a chaotic melee against roughly fifty mimicked objects and NPCs, ranging from
level 25 to 120, most around level 80. The party — including Chris Andrews (mid-werewolf
transformation), Elle (mid-goblin transformation from Scolopendra's first nine-tier attack), and
Carl himself, currently a transformed dachshund — clears the room quickly once the element of
surprise is gone, since mimics are ambush predators that struggle once spotted.

Mitch is the last one standing, reduced to a legless, regenerating blob and begging for a deal —
claiming the Shadow Mimics wanted crawlers "for the Cabaret," to help settle a three-way struggle
over "the dungeon back door" between the war mages, the Ogre Imperium, and a faction he calls
"Cock-A-Doodle-Do." Before he can finish reshaping himself to attack again, Carl — still transformed
into a dog — reflexively opens the **[Eye of the Bedlam Bride](../items/the-eye-of-the-bedlam-bride.md)**,
tattooed on his chest since Book 6 and relocated to his forehead in canine form, and never
deliberately used before this moment.

!!! quote "Book 8"
    "'No,' Mitch said. His entire form started to sparkle like someone had poured glitter into him.
    'No. Don't do this. It's too cold.' Several mouths started to form on his body, all screaming.
    'It's too cold!'"

Donut finishes him with a full-strength *Magic Missile* while he is frozen. Carl confirms
afterward, privately, that he stayed in control the whole time and sealed the Eye shut again before
Floor 11 — see [Carl's own page](../characters/carl.md#freezing-mitch-as-a-dog) for that side of the
encounter in full rather than duplicated here.

## Aftermath

- The Desperado Club is left infested: "Those things are almost impossible to root out. The whole
  place will need to be fumigated," per Mordecai, who recommends the party adopt safe words to
  detect future mimic impersonation.
- The party never learns whether the crawlers Mitch sent through the Nothing spot (Chris, Britney,
  Louis) actually reached the Pineapple Cabaret or somewhere else entirely — the book's final
  interlude shows Louis and Chris instead surfacing in **Sheol**, the fifteenth floor, met by
  Britney, Pontiff, and a demon calling himself **Forkith**, with no explanation of how or why the
  jump diverted. This is left an open thread for a future book.
- Neither Hamed (the Desperado Club's current leader) nor Clay-Ton and the other stripper-band NPCs
  are shown to have known about the infestation in advance; the text does not resolve whether the
  club's leadership was complicit.
