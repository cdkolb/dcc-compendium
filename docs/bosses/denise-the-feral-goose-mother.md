## An Ordinary-Looking Pet

Commandant Kane originally acquired Denise as a replacement pet for his daughter
[Bonnie](../characters/carl.md#the-gate-of-the-feral-gods) after her original pig, Sausage, was
taken hostage by the Bactrians. Denise looks like an unremarkable white goose in a frilly bonnet
and shawl — until her card reveals she was "plucked directly from the seventh floor" when Emperor
Anser first brought his people to this world through a gate, and that she lingered too long in the
Nothing on the way, driving her mad and granting her an **environmental** damage type: she is
immune to both magic and ordinary physical attacks, but vulnerable to blunt trauma from objects
already present in the room (furniture, dishware, the sink) rather than any weapon a crawler
brings in themselves.

When Carl and Donut arrive to loot Kane's corpse, Denise is out getting food; she returns partway
through the encounter, having already killed Kane off-screen for reasons Bonnie — under a
grief-numbing potion at the time — can't fully explain.

## The Fight

The moment Carl accidentally triggers the boss card, the game locks out both offense types at
once — no spells, no scrolls, no gauntlet formation, and no benefit from unarmed combat skills.
Mongo ignores the warning and engages anyway, taking heavy, near-lethal damage for no effect on
Denise's health. Bonnie, oblivious to the danger, keeps trying to get the party to test her
lemonade recipe throughout the fight — which turns out to be a deliberate misdirection: the
"important-looking" pitcher is just Mountain-Dew-colored lemonade, confirmed by an achievement
mocking Carl for assuming it was a quest item (**Wild Goose Chase**).

The actual clue is environmental: a mug and a serving pitcher both visibly damage Denise's health
when thrown, and Bonnie's earlier gesture toward the kitchen sink turns out to be a hint about
what really killed her father. With the timer at ten seconds, Carl wrestles Denise's head into the
sink drain and has Donut flip the garbage disposal switch, killing her instantly.

## Aftermath

- The fight ends with the standard "throne room" bubble notification — the Air Quadrant is
  liberated the moment Denise dies
- Neither Carl nor Donut gain more than a single level from the kill; Mordecai has no explanation
  for why boxed experience payouts feel arbitrary
- Carl and Donut inherit custody of Bonnie, the orphaned gnome child, who is later left in
  [Juice Box](../characters/juice-box.md)'s care in Hump Town
- The house's soul-crystal-powered balloon and the second piece of the
  [Gate of the Feral Gods](../items/gate-of-the-feral-gods.md) (a second pocket watch, looted from
  Kane's corpse) both pass to Carl afterward
