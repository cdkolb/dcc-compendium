# Claude Sludgington the Fourth

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Boss tier:** Borough Boss
    - **Level:** 65
    - **Floor:** 6 ("The Hunting Grounds"), throne room of [Fort Freedom](../locations/fort-freedom.md)
    - **Full title:** Eryops Gigantis, a.k.a. "The River Squatter" or "The Mud Kato"
    - **Killed by:** Carl (accidental *Protective Shell* launch + a bare-fisted dive through its
      exposed stomach), Donut (*Fireball*), and Mongo (finishing the job from the inside out)
    - **Loot:** A neighborhood field guide showing safe/naiad-occupied river crossings

!!! quote "AI Commentary (in-voice, written for this wiki — not a book excerpt)"
    "Oh boy are you in for a treat. These bottom-dwelling, lightning-tossing water pigs will ruin
    just about anyone's day. It's pretty much what they live for. Also known as the River Squatter
    or the Mud Kato, one of the cardinal rules of living under the water in a river is that you
    never, ever invite an Eryops into your home. Why? Because they never goddamn leave."

## The Uninvited Guest

Claude is an eight-foot-tall, fifteen-foot-long, tailless amphibian — half frog, half squished
alligator — who crashed a fundraising gala the cash-strapped
[Naiad Confederacy](../species/naiad.md#the-confederacy) threw years earlier to cover the upkeep
on their crumbling river palace. The party charged a 50-gold cover; almost nobody showed, because
the starving regular naiad citizenry ate anyone who got too close to the water. Claude walked in
anyway at the last minute, and the guards let him through despite his reputation. He never left,
eventually squatting in the throne room itself and displacing whatever government remained.

Eryops receive a **10x constitution bonus** while rooted in place and can fire branching lightning
bolts from their eyes; per the AI, they're "notoriously difficult to kill. Or even move." As a
lizard-class mob, Claude also deals 20% bonus damage against Carl thanks to his Extinction Sigil.

## The Fight

Claude isn't fought on purpose — he's discovered mid-battle, teleported directly on top of Carl
during [Tsarina Signet](../characters/tsarina-signet.md)'s assault on Fort Freedom, after
[Samantha](../characters/samantha.md) gets herself swallowed by him while scouting the flooded
throne room instead of teleporting back as ordered. The impact scatters the nearby fight (Donut,
Mongo, and the elite [Areson the Wise](../mechanics/boss-tier-ladder.md#elites)) and vomits up an
unconscious Holger the were-castor along with Samantha, both still alive inside the frog's gut.

The monster-card hint — "rooted in place... difficult to kill or even move" — points straight at
the solution: expose the stomach. Carl casts *Protective Shell* too close and at too shallow an
angle, accidentally launching Claude straight up into the night sky instead of flipping him
sideways. As the boss falls back to earth, Donut lands a level-10 *Fireball* directly into his
exposed belly, catching him fully alight and knocking him into the treeline, where he gets stuck
face-down, legs waving, unable to fall either way before the shell's duration runs out.

With seconds left, Carl throws his weakest hob-lobber into the open wound while Donut finishes
with magic missiles. Mongo dives headfirst into the wound and eats his way straight through the
creature — dragging Carl in behind him when the trees finally give way and launch the boss
backward like a spring-loaded mousetrap. Carl is briefly trapped and crushed inside the
still-dying Eryops before Mongo claws his way out the other side, pulling Carl with him. The whole
fight lasts roughly thirty seconds.

## Aftermath

- Carl receives the **"Uprooted"** achievement — for making a Rooted-in-Place boss monster move,
  with an unusually sincere, non-sarcastic wording that stands out from the AI's normal voice
- The fight ends up being a genuine surprise "boss kill before the actual quest trigger," earning
  the **"Premature Questjaculation"** achievement (no reward) since Signet's team hadn't yet fully
  cleared the castle when it happened
- Confirms [Fort Freedom](../locations/fort-freedom.md) as liberated shortly after, completing
  Part One of the *Vengeance of the Daughter* quest
