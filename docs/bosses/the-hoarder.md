## B... B... B... Boss Battle

Per the in-universe boss-battle card:

> "Trapped in her pile of rubbish, abandoned by society, the war inside her head has seeped out
> of her mind and infected both her body and her surroundings. Now nothing more than a garbage
> troll, the Hoarder is a horrific reminder of what can happen to those who fall out of the
> light! Protected by her minions, she'll do anything to keep her precious stockpile safe!"

## Encounter

Carl and Donut clear a garbage-filled neighborhood room with dynamite before realizing it's a
boss lair — the door seals behind them mid-explosion, triggering the "Boss Battle!" sequence.
The Hoarder is revealed as a 15-foot-tall, transformed human woman, badly burned and already
with 3/4 of her health gone (down to roughly a quarter) from the dynamite blast that preceded
the official fight start. She speaks only Spanish, pleading for help and clearly still partially
conscious inside the transformation ("Ayúdame por favor... No se donde estoy. Por favor, tengo
miedo."). Her attack is vomiting up Scatterers — level-2 bugs at first, then level-4 "brood
guardian" Scatterers as the fight escalates. Carl briefly urges mercy, but Donut opens fire with
Magic Missile regardless.

This fight is Carl's first real gut-check about the humanity of the "monsters" the dungeon
generates from transformed people — a theme that recurs across the book.
