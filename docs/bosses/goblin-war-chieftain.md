## Encounter

After trading intel to spark a "meth war" between the local goblins and a rival llama gang (see
[Rory & Lorelai](../characters/supporting-cast.md)), Carl and Donut are left alone in an
abandoned goblin engineering workshop, with the chieftain sealed behind a single door on the far
side of the room. Rather than open the door and trigger a conventional boss fight, they loot the
workshop for black powder and a rare C4-like explosive called "Hobgoblin Pus," load it onto a
cart, shove the cart through the door into the boss room, seal the door, retreat several rooms
away, and detonate it remotely.

Donut catches a brief glimpse inside before the door closes: the chieftain "sitting down, reading
a book" — and, critically, a room full of goblin infants and pregnant goblins she doesn't mention
until after Carl has already pushed the trigger. The explosion kills the chieftain (confirmed by
an achievement wall and a bronze star for both crawlers, jumping them to Level 8), but Carl
realizes afterward that he's just killed goblin children along with it.

This is Book 1's darkest moment for Carl — see [Carl's Personal Rules](../characters/carl.md) for
the "You will not break me" mantra he adopts in its aftermath.
