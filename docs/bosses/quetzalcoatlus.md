## A Ghost with Two Owners

Quetzalcoatlus is the non-corporeal ghost of Emperor Anser's wife, sealed inside the Necropolis of
Anser along with the rest of his court centuries ago. She's a classic **Wailing Shrieker**
type — formed when a grieving widow dies in despair near her lover's corpse on hallowed ground —
defeated only by a high-level exorcism, destruction of her lover's corpse, banishment magic, or
electric shock, per a torn cleric's journal page Carl loots from Henrik's body. What none of the
quadrant's various factions initially realize is that she's also been possessed: her body is the
vessel the banished lesser deity [Psamathe](../characters/samantha.md) hijacked after being duped
by [Ghazi the Mad Dune Mage](mrs-ghazi.md) into thinking she was helping him summon the god
Yarilo.

Three separate groups spend the entire floor trying to reach her for incompatible reasons:

- **The [Changeling Principals](../dungeon-factions/the-changeling-principals.md)**, led by
  Henrik, want to give her a temporary physical body (via the gnome Wynne's Flesh Mechanic magic)
  so they can touch her and learn the plant-based spell they believe will cure their people's
  hereditary Compression Sickness.
- **Ghazi** wants her to *stay* non-corporeal, since letting her leave the tomb risks Psamathe
  fully resurrecting and provoking the actual pantheon — his backup plan, per his own notes, is to
  use the [Gate of the Feral Gods](../items/gate-of-the-feral-gods.md) to erase the entire island
  rather than let that happen.
- **A crawler team of "tomb raiders"** (Morris Sp, Bobby, and later Low Thi) is funneled into the
  Necropolis from the start with a doomed quest to map the tomb and eventually kill her outright.

## The Resolution

Carl's plan never involves a direct fight. After the Water and Land Quadrants are both cleared,
the necropolis is deliberately left half-flooded and its drainage pump switched off, so that when
the Subterranean Quadrant's twin lightning-rod towers are struck during the floor's final,
extended equinox sandstorm, the resulting current runs directly through the tomb's interior
waterways. Reaching the required connector deep inside the flooded tomb costs the lives of nearly
every remaining crawler in the quadrant — Bobby is crushed, Low Thi sacrifices himself as a
distraction, and Morris Sp completes the final connection just as Quetzalcoatlus catches up to
him, dying in the same instant the lightning strike goes off. Every original occupant of the
Subterranean Quadrant dies in the process of winning it.

## Aftermath

- **Bubble Notification:** "The guardian of Anser's tomb has been successfully destroyed. The
  Subterranean Quadrant has been liberated!" — followed immediately by "Oh, wait. They're all
  dead!", closing out all four quadrants of bubble #543 at once
- Psamathe's other half — the crystalized Lika sex-doll head — survives independently in Carl's
  inventory (see [Mrs. Ghazi](mrs-ghazi.md)) and reawakens once the Land Quadrant castle's
  crystallization spell is later broken; see [Samantha](../characters/samantha.md) for what
  happens to her afterward
- Katia's turned-off drain plan later empties the rest of the necropolis, exposing the corpse of
  Emperor Anser himself — just a golden-crowned goose skeleton in a sarcophagus — along with a
  partial map of Floor 6 coordinates
