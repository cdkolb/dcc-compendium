# Ysalte, the Vinegar Bitch

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Deity — Goddess of Hopelessness and Insanity
    - **Level:** 250
    - **Floor:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth"), the FOCSA building, Havana
    - **Also known as:** The Vinegar Bitch, The Downward Spiral; formerly Goddess of the Dirt, then
      of Tears
    - **Sponsor:** Pontifex Shine of the Nebular Balance (not inhabiting her shell during the fight)
    - **Killed by:** **Paz Lo** — her own summoned totem — at the end of the card battle (ch. 69)
    - **Presence effect:** Every potion quaffed or handled outside inventory turns to vinegar; they
      revert to a *more powerful* version on her banishment

## Who She Is

Ysalte is the sole goddess of the **Semeru Dwarves** of the ninth floor, the people who dug Larracos
down toward the roots of the All Tree. Per Carl's reading, she "only has a single temple in the
entire dungeon," she appears near the end of Faction Wars as the final teams approach the castle
gates, and no crawler has ever been known to worship her — Mordecai's advice on hearing her name is
simply: if she shows up, run.

!!! quote "Monster description (Book 6, ch. 66)"
    "The earliest citizens of Larracos always worshipped Ysalte, which is fitting. She hasn't always
    been considered the Goddess of Hopelessness and Insanity. First it was of the Dirt. Then it was
    of Tears. It's a recent thing that they started calling her something else. The Downward Spiral.
    The Vinegar Bitch.
    Her downfall mirrors that of the citizens of Larracos, who have embraced the deity in all her
    forms.
    That's what happens when everything you know is upended, and everything is taken from you.
    First they vilify you. Then they separate you. Then they blame you for what they're doing to
    you. Then they hunt you. It's the step after that which is important.
    The one where they start to fear you."

She takes no worshippers beyond the citizens of Larracos, but she is a **patron of the arts** and
one of the very few deities who will sponsor a bard or performer — "She asks nothing in return,
though occasionally her gifts are more than what they seem, and some may even carry a curse." That
patronage is how she is attached to [Sister Ines Quiteria](../characters/sister-ines-quiteria.md),
her bard, and how she ends up on Floor 8 at all.

**Backstory:** she is half-sister *and* niece to her former lover **Taranis**. Threatened with
**the Nothing** by the god **Apito**, she fled to Sheol — where she took up with
[Amayon](amayon.md), bore his child, and was seized by him when she tried to leave. She has since
returned to the Halls of Ascendency, "where she is feared and reviled by all," and is judged a
dangerous contender for the throne precisely because she does not appear to want it.

## Appearance and Arrival

Louis Santiago kills Sister Ines in her sleep; the corpse dissolves and Ysalte rises out of it.
Because she is summoned in an ordinary hallway rather than a temple, she manifests at ordinary
scale: a young-faced female dwarf with a pickaxe and a helmet topped by a sparking, dynamite-fuse
candle, speaking softly with a French rather than Scottish lilt. Her face flickers like a bad
television, and in the static her mouth is a soundless scream.

Her first words are to Louis: "You killed my bard... She was asleep, and you killed her. Her mind is
finally at ease, which is a blessing, but I was not done with her yet, and for that you must
suffer." Samantha then greets her by name, insults her child, and takes a full god-swung pickaxe to
the face — which bounces harmlessly off the sex doll's head, knocking the goddess backwards and
freezing the scene into the boss battle.

## The Card Battle

Ysalte enters the fight with roughly 17 cards — "a slightly enhanced version of the deck of the late
Sister Ines" — against five crawler squads. She is **invulnerable**, so the win condition is
inverted: the crawlers must *keep her deck from emptying* for ten minutes, because the moment she
runs out of cards she is free to cast directly. She spends the fight actively discarding her own
cards to get there, plays the mystic **Raid Stash** to steal one card from every squad at once, and
at one point dumps four stolen totems onto the field simultaneously.

Her side objective is Amayon: she fights to stop him playing his last card, knowing he is free to
cast his own spells once his hand is empty. Donut breaks that with a **Force Discard**.

## Death

Ysalte is killed by her own summoned totem, the card-bound former crawler **Paz Lo**, with two
seconds left on the ten-minute timer. Carl is unconscious inside Samantha at the moment it happens
and never sees it; what he learns afterward is that
[Katia's **Bolt of Ophiotaurus**](../items/bolt-of-ophiotaurus.md) was the weapon.

!!! note "Unverified detail"
    Carl explicitly says to Katia, "You're the one who should've gotten credit for killing the god.
    You had to use your Bolt of Ophiotaurus to do it," and Katia notes that magical bolts can be
    reused as long as they don't break. The precise blocking of the kill — commonly described as a
    bolt to the eye followed by Paz's own directional self-destruct to the neck — is **not narrated
    on the page** in Book 6; Carl's POV is elsewhere for the whole sequence. Paz's *Self-Destruct*
    is an established, magically-aimable, suicide-on-cast spell of his from early in the book.

The system messages that follow are the reason the kill matters:

!!! quote "System messages (Book 6, ch. 69)"
    "A deity has fallen. The heavens tremble with rage.
    **System Message.** Crawler Paz Lo has slain the goddess, Ysalte. He has been marked for death
    by all deities except sworn enemies of the late goddess.
    **System Message.** A Memorial Crystal for the Goddess Ysalte has been generated. It will be
    available to be looted on a future floor. Great riches abound to who may find it.
    **System Message.** All worshippers of Ysalte have permanently been inflicted with the
    *Despondent* debuff."

## Aftermath

- Paz's card description changes to **"Paz Lo. The God Predator. Level 101 Former Crawler."** Her
  equipment all goes to him rather than dropping as loot, upgrading his armor
- Katia declines to claim credit precisely because of the marked-for-death clause
- The card is ripped at Carl's insistence — "We should never have let the Sister turn him into one
  in the first place" — and Donut does not receive a player killer skull for it
- Her sponsor, Pontifex Shine, is "currently reading the fine print on his contract, and let me tell
  you, he's pretty mad"
- The **Despondent** affliction lands on the entire Semeru Dwarf population of Larracos, ahead of
  Faction Wars on Floor 9
