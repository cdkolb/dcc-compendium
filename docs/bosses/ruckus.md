## The Bereft Minion

Ruckus is a self-replicating, spring-operated automaton — half biological, half steampunk
machine — left behind by the long-dead bugbear treasure hunter **Shamus Chaindrive**, who spent
nearly twenty years searching for the [Gate of the Feral Gods](../items/gate-of-the-feral-gods.md)
before docking his submarine, the *Akula*, against an underwater entrance to the
[Necropolis of Anser](../locations/necropolis-of-anser.md). Chaindrive set Ruckus loose to
harass anyone else competing for his prize — a role it kept performing for years after his death,
roosting near Hump Town every night after the sandstorms.

Physically, Ruckus resembles a house-sized hawk wearing brass, pipe-covered steampunk armor, with
organic wings and a real beak protruding from a helmet that obscures its eyes. Its signature
weapon is a retractable, 25-foot-long, ten-bladed buzzsaw that hangs beneath it on cables — the
same weapon type (scaled down) carried by the more common "chainsaw buzz-ards" that harass
[Gwendolyn Duet](../characters/gwendolyn-duet.md)'s team on the Land Quadrant.

## The Fight

Carl builds a four-missile launcher for his and Katia's [Royal Chariot](../items/enchanted-anarchists-battle-rattle.md)
contraption specifically to field-test a two-stage rocket design (using Mordecai's improved
accelerant) before it's needed against the *Wasteland*'s bomb-carrying "Knock-Knocks." The first
missile detonates prematurely mid-flight, confirming the second-stage propellant isn't reliable
yet — but the moment the party examines Ruckus through a telescope, the world freezes with a
**System Message: Please Wait**, and Ruckus's description silently updates from a passive
"bereft minion" to an active hunter that has regained contact with the *Akula* and now considers
the party enemies of its captain. It wakes up and takes flight seconds later.

With the two-stage design proven unreliable, Carl switches to the original single-stage rockets.
Katia drives the Chariot directly at the charging boss so all three remaining missiles — targeted
at the wing joint, exposed neck, and buzzsaw cable mount — can't miss. All three connect at once;
the explosion severs Ruckus's left wing, and the momentum of its own detached, still-spinning
buzzsaw finishes tearing it apart as it crashes.

## Aftermath

- Neither the two-stage rocket design nor Ruckus's field guide reveal anything about the Gate
  itself, but Chaindrive's boss-card lore is the first confirmation in Book 4 that the artifact is
  physically buried somewhere in the Necropolis, sought by at least three separate parties (the
  bugbear, the [Mad Dune Mage](quetzalcoatlus.md), and a colony of dirigible gnomes)
- The looted buzzsaw weapon is later repurposed by Katia as an improvised melee weapon (nicknamed
  "Leatherface") during the [Lusca](lusca.md) fight
- Carl receives a Silver Boss Box (25 more Surefire targeting upgrades and a Jellyfish Salve
  potion) plus a Silver Fan Box (the Enchanted Roll of Never-ending Duct Tape, later used to
  disguise Katia as Wynne the gnome's "body" during the *Wasteland* infiltration)
