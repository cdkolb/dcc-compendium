# Asojano, Lord of Smallpox

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Boss tier:** City Boss
    - **Level:** 130
    - **Floor:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth"), the Colón Cemetery ghommid settlement outside Havana, Cuba
    - **Full title:** "Asojano – Orisha"; also known as the **Lord of Smallpox**
    - **Type:** Non-corporeal spirit — a faded god reduced to a ghommid
    - **Resolution:** Not killed. [Carl](../characters/carl.md) flagged him into a T'Ghee totem card
      at the instant his health started to blink (ch. 15)
    - **Later fate as a card:** Combined with **Uzi Jesus** into **Lazarus-A-Bang-Bang**; when that
      fused card later died it reverted, returning him as **Lord of Blight – Gold Edition**

## A God Who Outlived His Worshippers

Asojano's own monster description lays out the mechanism that drives most of Floor 8's Orisha
storyline:

!!! quote "Monster description (Book 6, ch. 11)"
    "Also known as the Lord of Smallpox, Asojano is one of the most respected and feared of the
    Orishas, a former god made flesh upon the rise of the Ascendency.
    As the worshippers of these gods grew more and more extinct, their temples crumbled, and the
    power of these former deities waned. Eventually, they faded into nothing, slipping away beyond
    the veil, becoming nothing more than any other ghommid, only able to visit the physical world
    on occasion.
    One day, not so long ago, Asojano found himself here in this small town, visiting the world he
    once helped rule. There was no malice to his actions. He came just so he could look upon what
    he once had and what he had lost.
    Still, power lingers, even in the most forgotten of shells.
    Asojano is disease incarnate. He kills, and he heals. He is life, and he is death. He also
    wears a reed hat that makes it difficult to see where he's going, so he bumps into stuff a lot."

He burned the last remaining power of his shrine to enter the cemetery town and is now unable to
leave. His mere presence is what corrupted the settlement: per the *Pueblo de los Olvidados* quest
text, "The vile power of his presence has altered the minds of the town's residents, turning them
mad. Remove him to free the town." A health bar appears over him on sight and drains on its own,
with nobody attacking — being cut off from the other ghommids drains him further still.

## The Shrine Choice

The boss encounter is not a straight fight. Inside La Iglesia de los Olvidados, five broken Orisha
shrines stand around a floating gem called the **Sun of Reawakening**, and the party may repair
exactly one. Repairing Asojano's shrine wins the quest outright and cures the ghommids; refusing
turns it into a City Boss battle. The other claimants each make their own pitch — Oshun's raw charm
aura, Papa Legba's bribery, the mute Inle, and **Ogun**, whose two worshippers (**Paz Lo** and
**Anton Lopez**, of [Sister Ines Quiteria](../characters/sister-ines-quiteria.md)'s squad) are
threatened with a smite if any other shrine is chosen.

Carl instead chooses **Yemaya**'s shrine, on the strength of a message from
[Katia](../characters/katia.md) — a decision that re-ascends a locked goddess (see
[Yemaya](yemaya.md)) and gets Ogun's two worshippers smited in person (see [Ogun](ogun.md)).

## The Fight and the Flag

Asojano screams "Betrayal!" and breaks the temple's protection, letting the ghommid horde surge the
building. [Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md) does most of the damage with a swarm of
ghost-possessed armor and daggers, backed by *Clockwork Triplicate* on [Mongo](../characters/mongo.md)
and a *Mute* spell that lands just as he tries to re-cast. His shield burns down, and his health
continues to drain on its own once Carl chocks the door shut and severs him from the other ghommids.

With his health blinking, Carl gives up the boss boxes entirely: he sprints across the room, drives
a flag into the top of the orisha's head, and turns him into a card. Underneath the grass skirts is
"nothing more than a pale, albino man, wide-eyed and afraid," half his face covered in boils. Carl
immediately catches **SlugPox** for the trouble — an escalating affliction that pops Pox Slug mobs
out of his skin (see [Mobs](../mobs/index.md#book-6-mob-catalog)).

## As a Card

Asojano ends up one of the party's six starting totems, alongside Geraldo the monk seal, Skylar
Spinach, Uzi Jesus, Raul the crab, and [Shi Maria](shi-maria.md). He is also one of the two worst
behaved. Carl's assessment is blunt — "As expected, Asojano was a complete dick" — his first act in
the mock battle arena being an attempt to debuff Donut, blocked only by arena safeties.

Donut eventually uses a **Combo** card to fuse Asojano (level 130, undead, seven utility slots) with
**Uzi Jesus** (level 140, given to the party by [Florin](../characters/florin.md)) into the level-140
unique card **Lazarus-A-Bang-Bang** — a bazooka-wielding, boil-covered figure in a crown of thorns
introduced by the AI as "the calamitous, rapturous, and ultimately hazardous master of the
life-death boomerang." Lazarus is better behaved than either parent card and is later combined
again, this time with the splitter ghommid Skylar Spinach.

That second combination has a consequence. When Lazarus is killed during the Desperado Club fight
against Quan, he reverts to the two cards he was built from — and because Skylar is a splitter whose
iterations get *stronger*, both halves come back upgraded: **Uzi Jesus – Gold Edition** and
**Lord of Blight – Gold Edition**, Asojano in woven gold with a sugar-skull staff.
