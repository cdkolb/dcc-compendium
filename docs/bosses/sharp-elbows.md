# Sharp-Elbows

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Deckmaster — **Number Nine of Ten**
    - **Level:** 80
    - **Type:** Ogre necromancer witch, "loosely and half-assedly based on the rich and beautiful
      Ioway culture"
    - **Floor:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth"), the same Iowa turkey barn as [Tom](tom.md)
    - **Killed by:** **Raul the crab**, who snapped her necro-staff totem in half and then
      decapitated her (ch. 42)
    - **Notable:** The whispered line she gives Carl while impaling him is one of Book 6's key beats
      in the "are the mobs sentient" thread

## The Witch in the Barn

A seven-foot, hunched ogre woman in rags, green-skinned with an angry, half-melted face and stringy
black hair, carrying a thick magical walking staff. Her defining feature is the spikes: two-foot,
black, venom-dripping spines covering her whole body, which look less like anatomy than like
something growing out of her.

She is the "very interesting ogre necromancer witch" who took Tom's god-bereft turkey congregation
under her protection in exchange for a daily sacrifice, and she is fought simultaneously with him —
a three-way standoff with Donut at one corner, Tom at another and Sharp-Elbows at the third.

The AI, sulking at Carl for complaining about long descriptions, gives her the shortest boss
description in the book — "Blah, blah, blah basically a Necromancer" — and then, when asked nicely,
an actively spiteful one: "this legendary creature has a really interesting backstory, but you don't
want to hear it, do you? Suit yourself. I just hope you haven't killed too many of those turkeys."

## The Fight

Donut opens with Skylar Spinach and immediately steals a card off her with **The Thief**.
Sharp-Elbows answers with **Unk Tehi Temple Guards** (level 85), snares Shi Maria the moment she
appears, and finally summons her signature totem: a gnarled root-tree studded with feathers and
turkey claws, a black beating human heart hanging off it like an apple, and a golden lizard skull
with gems for eyes on top — a necromantic staff she rips out of the ground and wields two-handed.

Once Tom dies, the barn begins sliding off the cliff, and the fight finishes in a collapsing
structure with turkeys, zombie turkeys and gore cascading toward the ocean.

## The Impalement

Sharp-Elbows rolls into Carl at speed and pins him against a pole, driving her spikes through his
arms, stomach, legs, neck and — the detail that earns him the **Pincushioned!** achievement — both
of his eyeballs at once. Blind, held mouth-to-mouth with her, he hears her:

!!! quote "Sharp-Elbows, Book 6, ch. 42"
    "They starve us, you know.
    When we're not here, we are there. And when we're there, they make certain the hunger festers.
    You can stop it. You can satiate us all, Carl."

She is whispering, and Carl notes he "felt them more than I heard them." As she finally peels away,
the last thing he sees when his sight returns is her face directly in front of his: "the ogre face
was right there, staring directly into my eyes, and they were pleading, sad, afraid. *I've seen
those eyes before*."

He spends the next several chapters unable to place where — and the memory of "that look the ogre
had given me at the end of the fight, when she'd transformed" gives him chills every time it comes
back. See [Game Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md#ai-goes-primal-book-6) for the wider
mob-sentience thread this feeds.

## The Kill

**Raul** — the Red Maníseros Land Crab totem, otherwise the party's biggest coward — snaps the necro
staff in half, killing her last totem and freeing the deckmaster to be attacked directly. He
announces himself ("Master Carl, this insignificant cur is here to rescue you!") and then snaps
again, taking her head off while her body is still peeling free of Carl's.

Carl passes out for forty minutes afterwards, riddled with **Woozy**, **Sore as Shit** and
**Bonked**, saved from the worst of it by Pater Coal's disguised debuff-suppressing ring. He has the
presence of mind to pull both deckmaster corpses into his inventory before the barn goes over the
cliff, which is the only reason the key quest doesn't fail.

Donut loots the cards, which include "some of our old favorites" and a new totem for the deck.

!!! note "On Raul"
    Raul is a level-70 **Red Maníseros Land Crab**, "Sun-Kissed Cloud Rank Warrior," flagged in Cuba
    and one of the party's six starting totems. He is catalogued with the other Cuban mobs on the
    [Mobs](../mobs/index.md#book-6-mob-catalog) page.
