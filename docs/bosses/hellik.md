# Hellik

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Deity — brother of **Emberus**, Carl's own god
    - **Level:** 250 (deity tier; never given an explicit level readout on the page — Carl cannot
      read his description at all)
    - **Floor:** [9 ("Faction Wars")](../floors/level-09.md), the entrance vestibule of
      [Club Vanquisher](../locations/club-vanquisher.md) (ch. 54)
    - **Resolution:** Never fought. Appears in person, banishes [Khepri](khepri.md), delivers a
      message, and leaves
    - **Standing quest:** Carl is under an active [Emberus](emberus.md) quest to **kill Hellik**
      before the end of the eleventh floor — unresolved, and made worse by this meeting

## Who He Is

Hellik is the brother of Emberus and the **main suspect in the murder of Geyrun**, Emberus's son —
the crime that underwrites Carl's entire religious storyline since Book 4. He is a widely worshiped
god on Floor 9: the Larracos ram NPCs follow him, and any Hellik worshiper is automatically hostile
to Carl on sight, which Carl exploits for bounty quests and Emberus's favour.

He appears as an ordinary, olive-skinned human man who sits down cross-legged next to a dying Carl
as though joining him at a restaurant table. The only thing marking him as a god is the heat coming
off him, which cooks Carl's skin while they talk.

## The Message

Hellik arrives in the middle of the Operation: Snake Pit disaster, banishes Khepri with a single
line, waves away the burning immortal bugs, and hands Carl the most information-dense speech in the
book. Carl is, at this moment, being beaten to death by **[Princess Vinata](../characters/princess-vinata.md)**; Hellik simply talks
over it.

!!! quote "Hellik's message for Emberus (Book 7, ch. 54)"
    "Tell my brother I did not kill his son. Tell him he cannot kill me, no matter how hard he
    tries. Tell him I love him, despite his madness. Tell him everything he thinks about me is a
    lie. Tell him our mother and sister, Apito, has been corrupted. There is a malevolent presence
    about her, and I believe my nephew learned of it. Tell him the All Tree is beginning to rot.
    Tell him Nekhebit has returned, and I believe she is behind it all. Tell him we need to work
    together if we want to survive and discover what happened to our mother and his son. I know you
    seek an audience with Issitoq to break the truce. I will treat with him on your behalf.
    **Scolopendra stirs.** It is all connected."

That is five separate plot threads handed over in one breath: Apito's corruption, the rot in the All
Tree, **Nekhebit**'s return, Hellik's intercession with **Issitoq** on the Sheol truce, and the
first in-scene use of the phrase **"Scolopendra stirs"** — see [Scolopendra](scolopendra.md).

## Friend of the Church

!!! quote "System message (Book 7, ch. 54)"
    "You have been marked as a friend of the church by Hellik. Worshipers of Hellik will no longer
    be automatically hostile toward you."

Carl is named a friend of Hellik's church **while still carrying an active quest to kill him** — a
contradiction he notes explicitly. When the Club Vanquisher raid settles, his "friend of the church"
list reads: *Apito. Eileithyia. Eris. Grull. Hellik. Issitoq. Nekhebit. Theia. Yemaya.* Carl finds
Hellik's inclusion surprising, Grull's inexplicable, and Issitoq's and Eris's a mystery.

## Consequences

Delivering the message does not defuse anything. It **enrages Emberus**, who responds with a new
quest (*Kill 100 worshipers of Hellik*, before the collapse of the eleventh floor and, as a practical
matter, before the Kill Hellik quest itself) and then a far more alarming private note.

!!! quote "Emberus, after receiving the message (Book 7, ch. 55)"
    "Am I wrong to hate him? He has treated with Issitoq on our behalf, who has in turn allowed the
    peace to be breached. I am now allowed to kill the other Sheol princes in exchange for the
    information Amayon has about Geyrun. And what does he mean about our mother and sister? You, my
    martyr, be vigilant. If you survive killing Hellik, like he surely deserves, I fear I may turn
    my rage and grief on you for daring to hurt my own kin, and for this, I love you, beloved but
    doomed worshiper. **I promise the burning will be exquisite.**"

Later in the book the goddess [Eris](eris.md) offers Carl a competing path, naming the three gods he
would have to kill to take her patronage: **Taranis, Emberus, and Hellik**.

!!! note "Chapter placement"
    Hellik's appearance is in **chapter 54**, immediately after Khepri's banishment; the fallout
    — Emberus's new quest and the "exquisite burning" message — lands in **chapter 55**.
