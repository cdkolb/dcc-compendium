# Eileithyia

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Deity — Goddess of Childbirth and Female Pain
    - **Level:** 250
    - **Sponsor:** **Huanxin Jinx of the Grixist Swarm, CEO of Icon Industries**
    - **Floors:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth"), the Temple of Yemaya, Colón Cemetery (ch. 17); returns
      on Floor 9 ("Faction Wars") in Book 7, ch. 86
    - **Resolution:** Never fought — invulnerable on both floors. She arrives alongside
      [Yemaya](yemaya.md) to hand out boons and open a quest, and returns a book later to collect on
      the bargain
    - **Presence effect:** All damage struck against female opponents is quartered; all female party
      members strike with 500% melee power — "Girl power, bitches!"

## Who She Is

Eileithyia is the old friend who never stopped remembering Yemaya, and Yemaya's re-ascension is
effectively her win. She is not a Greek goddess despite the borrowed name — she appears as a
five-foot, blue-and-black-haired, bipedal alien with six eyes in a V on a bug-like face, four
three-fingered arms, a long thin neck and a heavily distended belly, wearing a blood-red toga and a
laurel crown. Carl notes she is **not** wearing soul armor, meaning this is who she really is rather
than a sponsor's projection.

!!! quote "Monster description (Book 6, ch. 17)"
    "Eileithyia is something of an oddity. She's benevolent. Unlike most of these psychos, she's
    known for her kind heart. She's prayed to by all mothers, both when they're attempting to sow
    the seeds and when the harvest comes. And while she does many things, she's mostly known as She
    Who Eases Pain. Both the physical pain of childbirth and the inevitable darkness that comes to
    those who are unable to complete their most sacred task. When the stillness comes, she is not
    the one who has caused the heartbreak. But she is the one holding the grieving mother's hand.
    She's not known to be someone who seeks the throne, which likely makes her one of the most
    qualified of the deities to claim the title.
    Hopefully her sponsor this season is someone who shares the same grace as her character."

That last line is doing a great deal of work. Her sponsor is the grixist CEO **Huanxin Jinx**, whom
[Odette](../characters/odette.md) describes as "the most evil person I know, and that's saying a
lot" — out purely for revenge, and dangerous precisely because she is smart.

## The Floor Boon

!!! quote "Boon (Book 6, ch. 17)"
    "The light of grace fills you with peace. You have been granted a floor boon. This boon will
    remain in place for the remainder of this floor as long as you remain in Eileithyia's grace. She
    may revoke this boon at any time.
    Protection from Spiritual Possession.
    10% Healing speed.
    Plus 10% Constitution."

## Quest: "Spiders"

!!! quote "New Quest! Spiders."
    "Warning: This quest comes from a deity you do not worship. You are not required to complete
    this quest, but they might not like it if you ignore them.
    Kill five spiders in my name every day until I ask you to stop.
    ***Reward***: You will receive another boon from me."

The quest is a cover, and she says so in a deity message the system is not permitted to monitor: it
exists to give her a private channel to [Carl](../characters/carl.md) and a legal mechanism to grant
his party further boons. Carl has to scratch his left arm to acknowledge, since any typed reply
would be visible.

The real bargain: when [Odette](../characters/odette.md) offers to serve as Faction Wars team
Adjutant, Carl must accept. In exchange, Eileithyia guarantees the votes to turn team protections
**off** on the ninth floor, and promises to clear the outstanding problem with the **Crown of the
Sepsis Whore** that threatens [Katia](../characters/katia.md) and
[Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md). Carl must tell nobody — not Donut, not Mordecai,
not Odette, not aloud and not through the bathroom system — or the deal is void and, in her words,
"I will make certain everyone you've ever known and loved is pounded into dirt."

Her closing pitch is the reason the quest matters well past Floor 8: "I will make certain you
survive until the twelfth floor, where you and Katia and the cat will help me claim the Celestial
Throne once and for all... I help my friends, Carl. I utterly destroy my enemies."

## Floor 9 (Book 7): Losing Her Pilot

Eileithyia returns at the very end of Faction Wars, summoned by
[Katia](../characters/katia.md) consuming the **Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace** — a
60-minute process forced into the floor's final hour by [Emberus](emberus.md)'s doomsday timer.

!!! quote "System messages (Book 7, ch. 86)"
    "The Orchid of Eileithyia has been consumed. The goddess has been summoned.
    ...
    Eileithyia has entered the realm.
    You are in the presence of a deity. The Scavenger's Daughter has opened her eyes. She fills with
    power.
    Temporary effect from Eileithyia: All damage you strike against female opponents is quartered.
    All female members of your party strike with a melee power of 500%. Girl power, bitches!"

Her boss readout is unchanged from Book 6 — Level 250, sponsored by Huanxin Jinx, invulnerable on
this floor, summoned to this location — but this is the first time the party sees her **true form**
rather than the grixist's projection: a large humanoid woman with olive skin, dark curly hair and
flowers in it, white and gold robes, three fingers on each hand and three toes on each foot, and a
distinctly alien lower face.

### The exploit that kills her sponsor

A quirk of the system freezes a sponsored deity in place between her summoning and the worshiper's
choice — Baroness Victory calls it a long-standing exploit that is hard to set up, requiring both a
choice option and a sponsored god. With Eileithyia frozen and unable to intervene,
**Huanxin Jinx** — ejected from the goddess's shell and standing in the tent in person — is trapped
in a circle of words of power and executed by [Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md), with
a crossbow bolt tipped with [Mordecai](../characters/mordecai.md)'s mother's ashes, fired from a
quadruped weapon mount on Donut's back.

!!! quote "Donut, Book 7, ch. 86"
    "You're strong, yes. But we are getting stronger every day. And you know what? We don't need to
    kill the gods and goddesses to win. No, honey... **We just need to kill you.**"

Carl loots the body immediately, "because we didn't want Eileithyia to get too upset." The goddess
then unfreezes as an unsponsored, unpiloted deity and finishes her business as if nothing had
happened.

### Granting Katia's boon

Katia's three options under the Orchid deal are an extreme constitution boost, becoming a **celestial
attendant** in Eileithyia's court, or **pregnancy**. Eileithyia presses her toward the safety of the
court — "as a woman with gift, your challenges will be even greater... I promise you will be kept
safe" — and Katia declines. The goddess makes her human again, restores her body (revealing how much
of it Katia had quietly lost: an arm, a leg, most of the other leg, an ear), and grants the boon.

!!! quote "Book 7, ch. 86"
    "'Then it will be so,' Eileithyia said... She put her hand on Katia's stomach. 'First, you will
    be made human again.'
    ...
    'It is done. It will be a boy.'"

Katia is flagged *Ineligible* and removed from the dungeon; Eileithyia fades out after her. She also
delivers, in passing, a burst of twelfth-floor news: the teleported city of Larracos has splattered a
convent of **Resheph**'s nuns, and [Odette](../characters/odette.md) has taken control of the
resulting zombies "because she's already taken out **Inpewt**."

!!! note "Scope"
    Katia's exit and what the boon means for her are a Characters-pass topic — see
    [Katia](../characters/katia.md). This page records only the mechanics of the goddess granting it.
