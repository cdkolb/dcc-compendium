# Bosses

The dungeon's boss system has a strict tier ladder — Mordecai lists it as **Neighborhood,
Borough, City, Province, Country, and Floor** bosses, plus a separate, rarer category called
**Elites**. Neighborhood bosses are the most common and guard a single room; each tier up scales
in danger, group size required, and reward quality (a Bronze Boss Box for Neighborhood, Silver
for Borough, and so on). Killing a boss earns a permanent star next to a crawler's name.

Full pages exist for every boss defeated by Carl and Donut — including ones like the Goblin War
Chieftain, killed remotely without ever entering the boss room or triggering an official
boss-card sequence. The one exception is [Ringmaster Grimaldi](ringmaster-grimaldi.md), a City
Boss + Elite resolved through negotiation rather than a kill — given a full page anyway because
of how central the confrontation is to Book 2's main quest. Bosses only seen secondhand (via
other crawlers, on broadcast) are listed in the table below.

Two floors break the ladder entirely. **Floor 8** runs on T'Ghee card combat, where bosses are
flagged rather than killed. **[Floor 9](../floors/level-09.md)** has no ladder at all — Faction Wars replaces it with enemy
**warlords** and with **gods**, and neither category can be killed the usual way.

### Floor 9 (Book 7)

Floor 9 has **no boss ladder at all**. Faction Wars is a war, not a dungeon crawl: there are no
Neighborhood/Borough/City tiers, no boss boxes and no stars. What fills the role is two entirely
separate things —

- the **warlords** of the nine other Faction Wars teams, each of whom has to be eliminated for the
  Princess Posse to win, and
- the **gods**, level 250 and invulnerable, who get onto the battlefield through a series of
  loopholes and then cannot be removed by force.

The gods are the only Floor 9 entities that get full pages here, because the warlords are people and
belong on [Characters](../characters/index.md).

**Deities**

- [Khepri](khepri.md) — Level 250 God of Rebirth and the Sunrise, with a literal bug for a head.
  Secretly worshipped by [Princess Vinata](../characters/princess-vinata.md) and summoned into his own temple as a trap; the one real
  boss-battle sequence of the floor (ch. 53). Not killed — **banished by [Hellik](hellik.md)**
- [Hellik](hellik.md) — Level 250, brother of Emberus. Appears in person, banishes Khepri, and
  delivers the book's densest lore dump; names Carl a "friend of the church" despite Carl's standing
  quest to kill him
- [Meatus](meatus.md) — a **feral god** freed from the Nothing; a sapient stolen penis with a gremlin
  attached. Killed outright by Donut's [*War Crime*](../spells/war-crime.md)
- [Harpocrates](harpocrates.md) — Level 250 God of Silence, and the owner of the stolen half of
  Meatus. **Conscripted** with a hell-kissed level-18 potion and used as a weapon by Li Na; beats
  Yarilo to death before leaving the realm enraged
- [Yarilo](yarilo.md) — Level 250 **feral god of lust**, also freed from the Nothing; conscripted by
  the [War Mage Rebellion](../factions/war-mage-rebellion.md) and then **killed** by Harpocrates
- [Eris](eris.md) — Level 250 Goddess of Chaos. Loses her pilot mid-book and runs unsponsored; casts
  the once-ever **Ceremony of Chaos**, freezing and piling ~80,000 combatants for Donut's [Rolling
  Battle Formation](../spells/rolling-battle-formation.md). A prime contender for the vacant Ascendent Throne
- [Eileithyia](eileithyia.md) — Level 250 Goddess of Childbirth, returning from Floor 8. Loses her
  vessel Huanxin Jinx when Donut kills her, then grants Katia's boon (ch. 86)
- [Emberus](emberus.md) — Level 250, Carl's own god. Arrives at the end of the book and **destroys
  the entire floor** on a 66-minute timer

**The looming threat**

- [Scolopendra](scolopendra.md) — never appears on the page. Warned as "stirring" twice, then
  **"has awakened"** in the book's final lines. Not a stat block; a flag for future books — she
  finally appears in person, and gets a full stat block, in Book 8 (see below)

### Floor 10-11 (Book 8)

Floor 10 ("Don't Come in Last") is a vehicle-racing gauntlet; Floor 11 ("A Parade of Horribles") is
a single-arena finale. Between them they resolve or introduce most of the book's major deity and
dungeon-boss threads.

- [Scolopendra](scolopendra.md) — Level 500 **Dungeon Boss**, the first-ever-named tier above Floor
  Boss. Summoned by Carl at the Floor 11 arena as the crawlers' joint choice; neither killed nor
  abandoned, but transformed by an enriched pet biscuit into a sapient Level 1 party member
- [Krakaren Prime](krakaren-prime.md) — Level 235 Floor Boss; a real, individual entity previously
  only referenced (see [Krakaren Clone](krakaren-clone.md) for the Book 1 copy fought instead). Added
  to the arena unilaterally by the AI, not requested by Carl; left badly wounded and carried away by
  Taranis
- [Taranis](taranis.md) — Deity, God of Lightning; previously named but unseen since Book 5. Appears
  in person for the first time, kills his own brother **[Emberus](emberus.md)**, and departs with
  Krakaren Prime and a stowaway [Samantha](../characters/samantha.md)
- [Mitch](mitch.md) — Level 120 Adolescent Shadow Mimic posing as the Desperado Club's new croupier;
  frozen by Carl (transformed into a dog) opening the Eye of the Bedlam Bride, then killed by Donut
- [Emberus](emberus.md#book-8-a-parade-of-horribles) — killed by his own brother Taranis after Carl
  publicly renounces his faith and confesses to Geyrun's murder; permanence unconfirmed
- [Eris](eris.md#book-8-a-parade-of-horribles) — gives Carl a five-sided fate coin, takes a new
  monstrous physical form at the climax, and confirms the goddess Apito survives in scattered pieces
- [Khepri](khepri.md#book-8-a-parade-of-horribles) — brief reappearance as a decoy accusation, dismissed
  by Taranis

!!! warning "Grull — a gap, not an omission"
    **Grull**, the war god of the Mantaurs, reappears substantially in Book 8: ejected from his own
    body (which conceals an orc host called "the Maestro") by Princess Donut's
    [*Laundry Day*](../spells/laundry-day.md) spell during Floor 10's fifth heat, then driven off —
    not killed, since gods cannot die on Floor 10 specifically — by
    [Dong Quixote](../characters/supporting-cast.md) and [Corcunda](../characters/corcunda.md)'s
    suicidal, magnet-boosted lance charge. No `grull.md` page exists yet despite Grull being
    referenced since Book 1 (worshipped by the Mantaurs; see
    [Gore-Gore the Mantaur](gore-gore-the-mantaur.md)). This is a **gap for a future pass**, not a
    deliberate exclusion — his Book 8 scene is summarized in the table below in the meantime.
