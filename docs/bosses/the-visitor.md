# The Visitor

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Deckmaster — **Number Seven of Ten**
    - **Level:** 70
    - **Species:** Nightgaunt
    - **Floor:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth"), a snowbound Iowa trailer park
    - **Killed by:** **Jola the Yule Cat**, [Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md)'s
      mythic Icelandic totem (ch. 41)
    - **Minions:** Six level-30 **Quench Moths**

## An Iowa Urban Legend

The Visitor is one of the rare cryptids in the dungeon's Earth catalog that, per the AI, actually
existed.

!!! quote "Monster description (Book 6, ch. 41)"
    "Nightgaunts exist outside of the dungeon. We use them as demons here in the game because, well,
    look at the fuckers. But in reality, they're part of a semi-intelligent species that normally
    lives in a Nullian world. Real Nightgaunts are peaceful, vegetarian, nocturnal creatures. Think
    of them like bats mixed with Neanderthals mixed with donkeys.
    But that's boring, so this version is much more intense. And deadly...
    Anyway, for some inexplicable reason, way back in 1903, a Nullian brought a Nightgaunt with him
    during an unauthorized site-seeing trip to Earth. It got out. Why did he bring the monster with
    him?... The poor creature ended up shotgunned and splattered all over the Iowa countryside. They
    called it 'The Visitor.' An urban legend was born."

The Nullians ("The Null," the big-headed greys) routinely capture the peaceful bat creatures and use
them as mounts and pack animals — a detail the AI lingers on with some distaste.

## The Cloak Problem

Carl is wearing his
[Nightgaunt Cloak of Stoutness](../items/nightgaunt-cloak-of-stoutness.md) — a cloak made from the
flesh of one of this creature's own kind. Mordecai had warned him the item would aggravate this mob
type, but nobody anticipated the scale of it:

!!! quote "Warning (Book 6, ch. 41)"
    "Warning: Your equipped cloak has aggravated the Nightgaunt."

The Visitor is enraged past all reason. It ignores its own card deck completely, ignores the giant
Christmas cat physically mauling it, and does nothing for the entire fight but try to reach Carl. It
never plays a single card.

This is, in practice, a lucky break. Carl's own post-fight assessment is that a Nightgaunt that
actually played would have been a genuinely dangerous opponent: its strategy was to snare the
crawler's totems while the Quench Moths swarmed, and quench moths gain a speed and constitution
bonus whenever they are touching a light source — which the boss's own giant summoned candle
provides. It is the same tactic the crawlers used to finish Queen Imogen at the end of the Butcher's
Masquerade.

The corresponding achievement later confirms it as a repeatable trick: "You did the same thing, but
with a cloak. You successfully taunted an opponent with a garment."

## The Fight

Donut summons **Jola the Yule Cat**, whose entrance blacks out the sky, drops digital snow and hangs
Christmas lights over the real trailer park. The Visitor's own summon — a half-melted, garbage-can
sized candle that lights on top of its head — is what finally makes the invisible deckmaster
visible.

Because the Visitor has no totems summoned, Jola is allowed to attack the deckmaster directly. He
slams it to the ground, picks it up in his jaws, shakes it to death, tosses it into the air, catches
it and crunches.

## Loot

Thirty cards drop, eighteen of them totems — mostly more Quench Moths, plus one legendary level-110
**Demon Queen** (a fat, upright ant thing in a crown). The real prize is ten consumable snare cards
called **Lamp**, which paralyse a totem for ten seconds and make it glow brightly.
