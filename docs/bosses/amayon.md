# Amayon

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Demon Prince of Sheol — one of the four crown princes
    - **Level:** 250
    - **Floor:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth"), Havana, Cuba
    - **Resolution:** Not killed — invulnerable on this floor. Freed from the
      [Madre de Aguas](madre-de-aguas.md) snake corpse, buffed to full power by
      [Katia](../characters/katia.md) and [Carl](../characters/carl.md), and sent home to Sheol
      (ch. 69–70)
    - **Consequence:** His departure spell scours the entire world and gives every stranded crawler
      a path to Floor 9
    - **Personal:** Youngest of the four princes; expelled by one of his three brothers;
      [Ysalte](ysalte.md)'s former lover and the father of her child

## Arrival

Amayon does not choose to come. He "involuntarily slip[s] into the realm" through the vulnerability
created when the *Madre de Aguas* dies of her zombism curse during an active **Demon Eviction**
event, and lands in her risen snake corpse.

[Samantha](../characters/samantha.md) recognises the name immediately: one of the four crown princes
of Sheol, "the youngest one I think," fond of setting things on fire — "Kinda like Emberus, actually.
They don't like each other."

For most of the floor's endgame he sits atop the tallest building in Havana, ramrod straight, pouring
fire into the sky in a tornado of demon birds and bats, held in the snake shell and under the control
of [Sister Ines Quiteria](../characters/sister-ines-quiteria.md).

## The Quest: The Missing Piece

!!! quote "Quest description (Book 6, ch. 62)"
    "Amayon, one of the four princes of Sheol has been betrayed by one of his brothers. After being
    unceremoniously expelled from the kingdom of Sheol, the demon prince has managed to just barely
    hold on long enough to pull himself onto the equivalent of a very, very small ledge. The level
    250 demon lord now inhabits the body of a lesser deity, a once-worshiped sea serpent who went by
    the name of the *Madre de Aguas*.
    In order to return home, Amayon simply must cast a spell. But nobody, not even a prince, rides
    the river for free. A tithe of souls is required to pass back downward."

The quest offers three options: kill Sister Ines to break his tether, free Amayon from his shell, or
kill him. Carl's party takes option two — with the AI's dry note attached: "We've never had a
full-powered, untethered hell prince unleashed on a lower floor before. I'm sure it'll be fine."

## The Card Battle: "The Big O"

Killing Sister Ines does not free him. Her death instead summons her sponsor-goddess
**[Ysalte, the Vinegar Bitch](ysalte.md)**, who seizes Amayon directly, intending to take him to the
Ascendency as her retainer — which would strand every remaining crawler without a key.

The result is a five-squad hybrid world quest, fought as a card battle on two fronts at once:

- **Offense against Amayon** — kill his six totems as fast as possible, which lets the crawlers talk
  to him
- **Defense against Ysalte** — she cannot be killed, so the squads must keep her deck from running
  dry for ten minutes; the moment she runs out of cards she can "nuke us from orbit"

The mechanic behind his power-up is a piece of the AI's own bureaucratic malice. The Demon Eviction
event only fired because the AI decided to classify crab sperm as mobs; the same ruling means the
soul tithe for Amayon's spell can be paid the same way. Carl summons **Raul the crab** and, in the
book's own phrasing, "jerked off a crab" to flood the world with souls, while Carl's
**Martyr's Path** floor benefit bathes the demon in Sheol fire to buff his strength.

Ysalte is killed by **Paz Lo** with two seconds left on the timer. Amayon casts. Powered by "the
flash presence of billions of souls," the spell's area expands from the point of origin to cover the
entire world — which is what saves the thousands of crawlers stranded across every other region.

## The Geyrun Answer

While the *Why are you Hitting Yourself?* spell is still running on him, Carl cashes in the quest
update Emberus attached to Amayon's arrival:

!!! quote "Amayon, Book 6, ch. 69"
    "That's what he sent you to ask? To ask about his son? You tell him that I know exactly what
    happened to his child. I was witness to the deal being signed. If he assists in helping me kill
    my three brothers, I will tell him."

This is the biggest movement on Carl's standing Emberus quest to find Geyrun's murderer (see
[Carl](../characters/carl.md)) since it was issued on Floor 5 — a named witness, with a price.

Amayon then dismisses any debt between them: "You also helped slay Ysalte, the mother of my child,
so we are even, and I owe you nothing, mortal. The act of speaking to you already delivered the
message to Emberus. Soon, this entire world will be scoured." He tips Carl and Samantha off his hand
toward the fire and goes home to wage war on his brother.
