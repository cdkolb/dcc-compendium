# Meatus

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Feral God — escaped from **the Nothing**
    - **Floor:** [9 ("Faction Wars")](../floors/level-09.md), roaming the open battlefield from ch. 59 onward
    - **What he is:** Two beings under one name — a gremlin-like creature with
      [Harpocrates](harpocrates.md)'s stolen genitalia sewn onto it. The gremlin half stays
      gremlin-sized wherever he is summoned; the stolen half scales to full god size
    - **Sponsor:** None. A free agent all book — never conscripted by any faction
    - **Resolution:** **Killed** by [Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md)'s [*War Crime*](../spells/war-crime.md)
      spell in ch. 76, wrapped in fleshers under her Astral Paw

## Who He Is

Meatus is the punchline of a very old Ascendency story. A normal-sized gremlin stole the god of
silence's genitalia, sewed it onto himself, and thereby became a god. The stolen part is separately
sapient, extremely loud, and refuses to go back because it likes music.

!!! quote "Mordecai on Meatus (Book 7, ch. 59)"
    "Meatus is actually two beings, though they only have one name. Originally he was a normal-sized
    gremlin-like creature who stole Harpocrates's dink, sewed it on himself, and became a god...
    The gremlin part of Meatus stays the same size no matter where or how he's summoned, but the
    dink itself takes on the size that the god would if he was summoned to the same area. That part
    of him is sapient and very loud and doesn't want to go back to Harpocrates because it likes
    music. And Harpocrates isn't deaf. He is the god of silence, and he can hear the heartbeat of a
    fly from half a planet away."

Mordecai expects the standard script: Harpocrates fights and kills Meatus, fails to reattach him,
gets enraged, and spends a while smashing everything noisy until he times out. Floor 9 does not run
the standard script.

## The Fight and the Ball

Meatus's entry — "**The Feral God Meatus has entered the realm**" — sends Mordecai into a panic,
because a feral god loose in the realm means the god who originally banished him is coming too.
Harpocrates duly arrives, and the two fight across the entire map, audible from anywhere.

Meatus's contribution to the chaos is to **wrap himself around the goddess [Eris](eris.md)** and use
her as a battering ram against Harpocrates — which is how he ends up inside the single most absurd
object in the book. When Donut casts her tower-amplified [**Rolling Battle Formation**](../spells/rolling-battle-formation.md) on the Dream
and Reaver armies, Meatus is pulled in with everyone else, with Eris still wrapped inside him. The
result is a mile-wide ball of compressed, invulnerable bodies with the base of a god's penis hanging
out one side and a screaming gremlin on the end of it, which Harpocrates then picks up by the shaft
and swings as a flail.

His running dialogue through all of it is the same plea: "NOT THIS TIME. I AM FREE, AND YOU CAN'T
MAKE ME GO BACK!" and "I YEARN TO SING."

## Presence Effect and Death

Being in Meatus's range inflicts a curse on Carl that Eris later describes as "that nasty curse from
Meatus" — the effect that eventually ends up on her one-current-effect-becomes-permanent spinner as
"Dick will fall off!"

When the ball finally breaks apart over the Nest tower, Meatus floats up with the rest of the
prisoners into Donut's frozen Astral Paw, covered in fleshers, and is crushed along with thousands of
Reaver and Dream soldiers by her [*War Crime*](../spells/war-crime.md)–amplified attack. His last words are to his own stolen
half: "No. Daddy, help me! I just wanted to sing!"

!!! quote "System message (Book 7, ch. 77)"
    "Meatus hasn't exactly left the realm. He's now slap-chopped all over the realm. Holy shit.
    Still, the Scavenger's Daughter looks away because even she doesn't want to look at that.
    Luckily for you, the benefit fades. For the remainder of this floor, your penis is now protected
    from getting chopped off! Isn't that great?"

Harpocrates's reaction to watching his own genitalia destroyed — "My cock! My beloved cock! You will
pay for this, Champion of Nekhebit!" — is the last thing he says before leaving the realm, and marks
Donut for a grudge that the book does not resolve.
