## What He Is

Grimaldi was the dwarf ringleader of a beloved Over City circus before the cataclysm; the same
poison cloud that transformed his performers into monsters turned him into a **Pestiferous
Vine** — a plant/fungal hybrid that infects other creatures with mind-controlling parasitic
worms, binding them into his "family." He keeps his transformed performers (lemurs, clowns,
Heather the bear) alive indefinitely: when killed, the spores return to the vine and they're
reborn the next day with memories intact.

Per Mordecai, Pestiferous Vines are killable by a specific trick: poison yourself, drip the
poisoned blood directly onto the vine, and wait 15–20 minutes for the poison to spread through
its network of symbionts and kill it — but the vine can sense and cure the poisoning if it
notices, using an equal amount of the same blood.

## Encounter

Carl infiltrates the circus's main tent using a looted **Big Top Ticket** (which grants safe
passage through public areas only) after defeating [Heather the Bear](heather-the-bear.md). He
finds Grimaldi's true form — an enormous vine filling the entire center ring — and successfully
poisons him using Mordecai's method, undetected.

Rather than let the poison finish the job, Carl uses the game's own production-negotiation
mechanic: speaking through his PR contact Zev, he directly addresses the (real, off-screen)
producers of the in-universe drama *Vengeance of the Daughter*, correctly guessing that killing
Grimaldi is the intended climax and that the show would rather keep both him and [Tsarina
Signet](../characters/tsarina-signet.md) as recurring elements. He offers to become an exclusive
guest star on future *Vengeance of the Daughter* quests through Floor 6 in exchange for the
producers letting him choose a non-lethal resolution — they agree live, mid-scene.

Carl then deliberately re-infects himself by eating a cursed "ice cream" cone made of the same
parasitic worms (served by fellow Elite **Apollon the Mighty**), briefly sharing a telepathic
link with Grimaldi's fractured mind while his *Wisp Armor* spell keeps the parasites from taking
full control. He talks Grimaldi and Signet — who turn out to be estranged family, not enemies —
into recognizing that they both want the same thing (freeing their transformed family from
endless death and rebirth), then cures himself and leaves before the confrontation's real
resolution. The quest completes without Carl ever learning conclusively whether Grimaldi
survives; a hidden stairwell to Floor 4 appears in the circus ruins afterward, suggesting the
story moved on regardless.

## Role in the Story

This encounter — and the surrounding "Show Must Go On" quest — establishes a recurring mechanic:
Elites are tied to ongoing, producer-driven "serial" storylines that exist independently of the
crawl, and Carl can negotiate directly with those unseen production teams for narrative leverage.
Mordecai's warning is blunt: elites have "thick plot armor," and killing one outright usually
ends badly for the crawler who tries.
