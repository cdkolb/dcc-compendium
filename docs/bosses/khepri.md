# Khepri

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Deity — God of Rebirth and the Sunrise
    - **Level:** 250
    - **Floor:** [9 ("Faction Wars")](../floors/level-09.md), the Temple of Khepri inside
      [Club Vanquisher](../locations/club-vanquisher.md) (ch. 52–54)
    - **Sponsor:** None — "This is a locked deity. He will not be sponsored this season"
    - **Allegiance:** Retainer of **Adrasteia**; formerly a retainer of **Khnum**, who "mysteriously
      dropped dead a few days back"
    - **Resolution:** Not killed — invulnerable. **Banished** by the god [Hellik](hellik.md), whose
      single line — "You. Begone false god" — ends the fight
    - **Presence effect:** *You are immortal for the next 90 seconds*

## Who He Is

Khepri is the god with a bug for a head — literally, the body of an insect sitting on a five-foot
humanoid frame in the gold-fringed robes of the Ascendency. He does not speak; he chitters. He is a
minor, obscure deity nobody sponsors and nobody courts, kept around because the big players
occasionally need something from him.

!!! quote "Boss description (Book 7, ch. 53)"
    "God of Rebirth and the Sunrise. Khepri! Level 250.
    This is a locked deity. He will not be sponsored this season.
    This god is a retainer of Adrasteia.
    Warning: This is a deity. He is invulnerable on this floor.
    This god is within his own temple. Temple appearance rules apply.
    Like many of the gods one may find roaming the halls of the Ascendency, Khepri is mostly ignored
    by the big players and is only acknowledged when they need something from him. They usually just
    leave him alone because he's a goddamned weirdo. His head is the body of a bug, which is creepy,
    even to me. His temple is 'endless.'"

His worship mechanic is the reason he matters to the plot. Of the fifteen "light" deities with
temples inside Club Vanquisher, six carry the spell [**Travel the Path**](../spells/travel-the-path.md) — a light-speed movement
along a worship-tendril that the system does not classify as teleportation — and four of those also
carry the once-ever skill **Welcome Home**, which follows the tendril straight to the caster's
strongest connected temple. Khepri's own recruitment quest turns prospective female adherents into
actual beetles, which the worshiper must swallow whole and later give birth to on the buffet table.

His temple is one long banquet table that never ends, covered in **433 different kinds of bug** —
most of them immortal, and after the fight, most of them on fire. (The temple itself is a
Locations-pass topic; see [Club Vanquisher](../locations/club-vanquisher.md).)

## Operation: Snake Pit

Khepri is the target of Carl's Club Vanquisher raid because **[Princess Vinata, the Sepsis Whore](../characters/princess-vinata.md)**,
secretly worships him. Vinata's centuries-long unbeaten record in Faction Wars rests on vanishing
into a Club Vanquisher temple the moment her position collapses, and
[Rosetta Thagra](../characters/rosetta-thagra.md)'s reconstruction of the trick identifies Khepri as
the likely destination — "In the codex, Khepri is number 453... Khepri only has one [temple] thank
the gods, but his address should be 453 or 453.A. Once you get inside, move fast. Don't let them
run. His temple is an infinite table."

The raid party — [Carl](../characters/carl.md), [Katia](../characters/katia.md),
[Prepotente](../characters/prepotente.md), [Li Jun](../characters/li-jun.md),
[Bautista](../characters/bautista.md), Zhang and Li Na — enters through the temple of **the Dagda**,
crosses the club, and forces the Khepri door. Carl can open it at all only because a "damned"
petitioner may enter any church.

## The Trap

It is a trap, and Vinata set it. She had anticipated that Carl would find the hiding place, so she
worshiped Khepri herself and arranged to **summon the god into his own temple** before the raiders
arrived, knowing that trespassers in a deity's own temple are finished the moment the boss sequence
unfreezes.

!!! quote "Achievement: Charge of the Light Brigade (Book 7, ch. 52)"
    "Okay, so there are traps. There are TRAPS. And then there's what you just walked into.
    Oh man, oh man. It's gonna be glorious, one for the ages, but those of you watching at home, you
    might want to get your crying hankies out. This is about to get really messy.
    ***Reward***: Fuck. Even I'm not a big enough of a prick to give you a reward for this one."

The one thing that saves Carl is the presence effect itself: standing in front of Khepri grants
**90 seconds of Immortality**, and Vinata — already immortal from the same source, with a frozen
countdown at 63 seconds — will run out 27 seconds before he does. The entire fight becomes a clock
problem: survive long enough to outlast the Sepsis Whore's invulnerability.

The System AI runs the whole thing as a manual, old-fashioned boss battle, complete with portrait
slams, a rolling sun and a "2X Jackpot if Zhang Lives!" banner.

## Resolution

Carl bombs Khepri twice, once directly and once by sending the war-goose **Günter** down the endless
hallway to detonate, hoping to push the god far enough back to buy time. Katia and Prepotente eject
themselves with Black Nimbus potions; Carl is thrown out of the temple entirely and lands in front of
the portal, where Vinata — driven insane by the open
[Eye of the Bedlam Bride](../items/the-eye-of-the-bedlam-bride.md) on his chest — pins him and beats
his head against the floor of the club.

Khepri follows them out of the portal, fully enraged, with his burning immortal bugs swarming behind
him. He never gets to land the blow.

!!! quote "Banishment (Book 7, ch. 54)"
    "'You. Begone false god,' a deep voice said.
    Khepri chittered, but there was a crack, followed by the notification: **Khepri has left the
    realm.** The Scavenger's Daughter closes her eyes to his presence. Your boon is timed and will
    run out on its own."

The voice is [Hellik](hellik.md). Khepri's bugs stay behind and keep burning; Vinata is killed a
moment later by the sluggalette **[Kandy "Bigs" Newton](../characters/kandy-bigs-newton.md)**.

## Aftermath

- Carl is **permanently marked for death by Khepri**, and receives no credit or reward for the
  encounter — the achievement for surviving the god's presence goes to Carl, Katia and Prepotente
  alike
- Carl reads Khepri's dead patron **Khnum** as a clue: Khepri and
  [Odette](../characters/odette.md)'s **Nekhebit** belong to the same sub-pantheon, and somebody on
  the twelfth floor is working through that group killing gods. Carl's two suspects are Odette
  herself and Huanxin Jinx. The text does not resolve this
- The raid's other half — getting [Samantha](../characters/samantha.md) into the temple of her own
  mother **Theia** — is what got the door open in the first place

## Book 8: A Parade of Horribles

Khepri makes one brief reappearance at the Floor 11 arena climax, used by Carl purely as a decoy.
Needing to lure [Emberus](emberus.md) into position without tipping off his real plan, Carl falsely
names Khepri as the first of two gods responsible for killing Emberus's son Geyrun: "The first person
to kill your son was the god Khepri." Khepri is duly summoned into the fight — "**Khepri has entered
the realm**" — and then almost immediately dismissed once he's served his purpose, brushed aside by
[Taranis](taranis.md) with a single line:

!!! quote "Book 8, ch. 97"
    "'You!' Taranis roared, pointing with his free left hand at something... 'Go home.' He was
    talking to Khepri, who pointed at me and Donut and chittered angrily. 'I don't care. I have
    business. Kill them later. Begone!'"

Khepri leaves the realm without incident, having never actually threatened anyone — a scapegoat used
and discarded within the same fight, with no confirmation he had anything to do with Geyrun's death
at all.
