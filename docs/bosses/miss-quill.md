## What She Is

Miss Quill poses as the aging, no-nonsense secretary to Magistrate Featherfall, guarding his
office and collecting a shelf of stuffed-animal "beanies." In reality, Featherfall has been dead
for years — his mummified corpse hangs at the center of a krasue nest — and Quill has secretly
run the town under his name ever since, using a forgery spell to maintain the illusion.

She is a powerful blood mage descended from Over City skyfowl royalty, working toward a
multi-generational spell called **The Final War**: a three-night ritual, begun by her
grandfather and continued by her mother, meant to unleash a mass-casualty magical event that
would kill every non-flying resident of the Over City and let the skyfowl "reclaim" it — mirroring
the original cataclysm that destroyed the volcano kingdom generations earlier.

## Encounter

Quill's methods layer several systems Carl and Donut have to unravel over multiple nights:

- **The 201st Security Group** — a cult militia of banished "city elves" who worship the skyfowl
  as living angels, glamoured into serving as her enforcers and corpse-disposal crew
- **Krasue** — undead vampire-women created from Desperado Club sex workers lured to the
  settlement with false promises of descending to safer floors; failed transformations are fed
  to the city elves and dumped in alleys, which is what triggers the original quest
- A **Soul Crystal** amplifier hidden above the town's Swordsman-guard depot, which recharges the
  guards nightly and doubles as a power source for her spell — fed by her undead husband
  **Remex**, a "Soul Leech Capacitor" who channels her magic and stores the resulting mana

Carl and Donut kill her with a dynamite ambush hidden inside her own office (rigged into the
hollow shelving that held her beanie-baby collection), detonated remotely after luring her and
Featherfall's mummified body into the blast radius. The kill happens off-screen, mid-explosion —
neither crawler ever meets her in a direct confrontation. Her death is confirmed only by the
resulting achievements (**Ultimate Extreme Power**, for killing the town's acting magistrate;
**CockBlock**, for accidentally stealing another crawler's assassination-quest reward) and by
Remex's later testimony.

## Aftermath

Her death doesn't end the threat: Remex, still bound to the half-completed spell, becomes the
unstable core of a follow-up **event quest** ("The Fools Who Broke the Glass") that nearly kills
Carl, Donut, and the town's entire crawler population in a wild-magic explosion. See
[Remex](../mobs/remex.md) and [Carl's Doomsday Scenario](../items/carls-doomsday-scenario.md)
(the improvised device Carl creates to survive it) for how that resolves.
