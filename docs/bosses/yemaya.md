# Yemaya

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Deity — Goddess of Rivers
    - **Level:** 250
    - **Sponsor:** None — "This is a locked deity. She will not be sponsored this season."
    - **Status:** A **retainer of [Eileithyia](eileithyia.md)**
    - **Floor:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth"), the Temple of Yemaya, Colón Cemetery
    - **Resolution:** Never fought. Re-ascended when [Carl](../characters/carl.md) chose to repair
      her shrine over Ogun's in the Celestial Thorn Room (ch. 15)
    - **Presence effect:** All healing skills and spells cost no mana and have no cooldown

## Re-Ascension

Yemaya is one of five faded Orishas offering their shrine for repair, and — crucially — the only one
whose restoration actually brings a locked deity back into the game. Carl picks her over
[Ogun](ogun.md) on the strength of a message from [Katia](../characters/katia.md), a choice that gets
Ogun's two worshippers smited on the spot and rewrites the rest of the floor.

!!! quote "System message (Book 6, ch. 15)"
    "The Goddess Yemaya has re-ascended. She is welcomed into the family by all."

!!! quote "Monster description (Book 6, ch. 17)"
    "Yemaya once was the goddess of all oceans, all bodies of water big and small. But she was more
    than that, too. She was the queen of love and the bringer of life. But, eventually, her people
    died off, her temples crumbled, and she was forgotten by all except her dear friend, the goddess
    Eileithyia, who wished every day that her old friend would someday come back from the land of
    the forgotten.
    Now that Yemaya has re-ascended into the Halls, she will have a lot of work to do to make
    certain nobody ever forgets her name again. She's an unlikely candidate to claim the throne, but
    she would work tirelessly for her good friend Eileithyia, should she desire such a burden."

## Appearance

She rises out of the temple fountain: a stunningly beautiful dark-skinned siren with neck gills,
pointed ears and large eyes, splitting at the waist into **two** scaled mermaid tails that arch up
behind her and meet in a heart shape — which prompts Donut's immediate identification, "*She's the
Starbucks logo!*" Water pours from her breasts back into the fountain. Carl notes she carries the
same otherworldly charisma he felt from Signet.

Her demeanour is warm and slightly imperious. She recognises Carl's **SlugPox** instantly and names
its source — her brother, [Asojano](asojano.md) — with no sympathy for him at all: "He did it on
purpose, just so you know. Strand himself here, I mean. He feigns innocence, but he always knew what
he was doing. I'm glad you saw him for what he was."

## Gifts

- **A mythic T'Ghee card to each squad present.** One goes to
  [Sister Ines Quiteria](../characters/sister-ines-quiteria.md), one to
  [Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md). Donut's is ***The Thief***, a non-consumable
  **mystic** card that steals a card from an opponent's hand — completing her set of all five card
  types, and a workhorse of the rest of the floor's card battles
- **She tries to recruit Donut** as a worshipper on the spot ("Perhaps I can entice you into
  worshipping at my altar?"); Donut politely defers
- **She personally teleports the NPC Bomo to Floor 9.** Bomo, the cretin who physically rebuilt her
  shrine, is granted a boon and asks to be reunited with his friend The Sledge. He names Larracos,
  and Yemaya says "It is done" — he blinks out of existence instantly. Carl's reaction is dismay
  rather than gratitude: the party needed Bomo's spell to reach the stairwell

The Bomo teleport also becomes a plot point in its own right. Eileithyia later cites it directly as
proof of what she can do for Carl: "You've just seen what a goddess in her own temple can do, how
she transferred that cretin to the ninth floor. A deity in their own temple has more powers than
even the AI in some cases."

## Aftermath

Repairing Yemaya's shrine also completes **Quest: Pueblo de los Olvidados**, liberating the ghommid
settlement and curing the surviving ghommids, and converts the church into the **Club Vanquisher
Entrance Temple, The Temple of Yemaya**. Carl notes afterwards that other crawler groups across the
floor are getting near-identical Orisha shrine quests — the Floor 12 Ascendency players farming
crawlers to wake up forgotten gods.
