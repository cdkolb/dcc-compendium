# Robert the Human

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Boss battle (the card-combat tutorial fight)
    - **Level:** 40
    - **Floor:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth"), an old house in Monroe County, Florida — the Florida Keys
    - **Fought by:** [Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md), **alone** (ch. 37)
    - **Killed by:** [Mongo](../characters/mongo.md), who bit through the deckmaster's throat while
      the totems were still fighting
    - **His totems on death:** **Robert the Doll** and **Dog**

## Donut's Solo Fight

This is the first proper deck-versus-deck boss battle of the Phase Two transition, and Donut fights
it by herself. All crawlers except squad leaders were put into temporary stasis during a liaison
changeover, which is why [Carl](../characters/carl.md) watches the whole thing on a screen from
orbit, mid-argument with [Odette](../characters/odette.md), rather than standing next to her.

It is also the fight the system uses to teach the card system to the home audience: Robert freezes
the moment he appears, and the orange announcer runs a several-minute explainer on every card type
while Donut stands there, already knowing all of it.

!!! note "Location mix-up"
    Donut picks **Miami** off the phase-two globe and is told there is a problem with the location
    and she is being sent to **Iowa** instead — then arrives in the Florida Keys anyway, with the
    location marker briefly reading Iowa. She is convinced the game is broken.

## The Opponent

A hunched, deranged-looking man of about seventy in a too-small, old-school white sailor's outfit,
twitching, with a nametag reading "Gene" and a fistful of cards held against his chest. He summons
two totems:

| Totem | Description |
|---|---|
| **Robert the Doll** | A tall, faceless doll wearing a matching sailor suit — the Key West legend the encounter is built on |
| **Dog** | A small stuffed dog with rolling bug eyes |

## The Fight

Donut plays **Geraldo the Monk Seal**, who launches at the doll with his usual over-the-top entrance
explosion. Before he lands a hit, Mongo flies across the room and chomps down on Robert the Human's
throat, killing the deckmaster outright and ending the fight instantly. All totems poof away.

Donut's first action afterwards is to loot the dead man's **sailor hat**, which she then wears for
the rest of the chapter. Carl arrives in the house to find Mongo still tearing the corpse apart.

She keeps the non-totem cards; the two totems aren't worth ditching an existing squad member for.
