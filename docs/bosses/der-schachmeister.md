# Der Schachmeister

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Mock battle arena opponent (deckmaster)
    - **Level:** 25
    - **Floor:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth"), the mock battle arena
    - **Deck:** Twenty cards including six totems, with an **eight-second** summoning cooldown
    - **Result:** **Carl and Donut lose.** Their only practice defeat in the book

## The Kid

A human boy of about twelve in a tuxedo and round glasses, who appears at the top of a hill between
two summoned wolves, wipes his nose on his sleeve and flips the party off.

!!! quote "Monster description (Book 6, ch. 29)"
    "Holds a deck consisting of twenty cards, including six totems.
    Has an eight-second card summoning cooldown.
    Focuses on debuffs and disabling the opponent. He's an annoying little prick without any regard
    for his own personal safety. You'll have to deal with him the same way you would if this were a
    middle school playground. You gotta kick his ass before he outsmarts you and gets the adults
    involved."

The eight-second cooldown is the detail that alarms Carl. Donut's own cards take **ten** seconds to
refresh; he had assumed that was a hard rule of the system rather than a stat an opponent could
beat.

## His Totems

- **Der Kornwolf** (level 100) — a giant ethereal wolf
- **Die Kartoffelwölfin** (level 100) — visually identical to the first, different name only

## How He Wins

Der Schachmeister is a pure lockdown/mystic deck and he never once needs to fight.

1. **Summon lock** — a flaming animated padlock over Donut's hand with a 30-second countdown,
   chaining her cards. She cannot summon Shi Maria or anything else
2. **Peekaboo!** — a mystic card that opens a pair of bloodshot eyes over Donut's deck and shows him
   her hand for the remainder of the battle
3. **To Hell With Ya!** — a legendary card that force-discards the Shi Maria card and every other
   copy of it in hand and deck. Donut only has one, so she loses her best totem outright and cannot
   summon her again for the rest of the fight

Raul kills Der Kornwolf with a ranged *Bris*, but the second wolf tackles him and rolls down the
hill with him. Samantha nearly saves the fight with a new, unexplained glowing-blue power, but
fizzles out mid-charge ("God mother damnit. I used it all up"). The surviving wolf reaches Donut.

!!! quote "Book 6, ch. 29"
    "Simulation Failed.
    Congratulations. If this was a real fight, you would all be dead right now."

Donut's verdict: "That guy was a cheater. How are we supposed to win if we can't use our deck?"

## Why It Matters

The loss is the turning point in the party's card strategy. Carl's conclusion is that their deck is
too slow and too dependent on one dominant totem, and that the reliable answer to a mage-type
deckmaster is the same as it always was — *they can't cast their spells when you're punching them in
the face*. From here on he and Mongo practise rushing the deckmaster directly rather than trading
totems, and the party starts deliberately rebuilding the deck for speed.
