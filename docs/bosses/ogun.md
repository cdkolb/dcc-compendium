# Ogun

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Deity — God of Blacksmithery and Armor
    - **Level:** 250
    - **Sponsor:** None. "This god is not sponsored."
    - **Floor:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth"), La Iglesia de los Olvidados / the Temple of Yemaya,
      Colón Cemetery
    - **Resolution:** Never fought — invulnerable on this floor. He appears in person to punish his
      own worshippers and leaves (ch. 16)
    - **Presence effect:** All smash-related attacks +300%

## The Only Orisha Who Ascended

Ogun is the one Orisha in the Celestial Thorn Room who does not need his shrine repaired — he
already ascended to the Halls and has a whole new generation of followers. Per the AI, he requested
the repair anyway purely out of spite: "he's kind of a dick and doesn't want any of his old family
members to get back into the game." Two crawlers in the room, **Paz Lo** and **Anton Lopez**, worship
him, and the quest he issues them is explicit — fix *his* shrine, or take a smite.

!!! quote "Monster description (Book 6, ch. 16)"
    "Ogun is an outsider. He is one of the old gods, originally a member of an obscure pantheon that
    has since dissipated into time. He spends his days at his celestial anvil, angrily creating
    armor, piece after piece, but no one knows why he toils or what becomes of the armor when it's
    completed.
    It's said his creations have a strange shape, all designed for four-legged beasts.
    Ogun is no stranger to conflict. He is—well, he *was* until a minute ago—the last surviving
    member of an ancient pantheon that was known for its bloody conflicts. Like most of the older
    gods, he isn't the same entity he once was. In the great Halls, he is considered a god of
    blacksmiths and armorers. He once was something even greater.
    His power and ambition makes him a strong contender for the celestial throne.
    He's here now because Ogun strongly believes all punishments should be handed out in person."

!!! tip "The four-legged hint"
    A god who makes nothing but armor for four-legged beasts, and nobody knows why or where it goes,
    is not a throwaway line — it is a pointed setup aimed squarely at
    [Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md).

## Appearance

A bolt of lightning through the ceiling, then a human-sized, dark-skinned, heavily muscled man in a
leather apron with no shirt, shorts, and a necklace of large white teeth, carrying a giant war
hammer over one shoulder. His eyes are pure white light and he glows yellow. He speaks with a calm,
even voice and a hint of a Caribbean accent — which, as Carl notes, makes him seem *more* incensed,
not less. Donut's contribution: "For a god that's always making armor, you'd think he would be
better dressed."

## The Smiting

Carl chooses [Yemaya](yemaya.md)'s shrine over Ogun's, and Ogun arrives to collect.

- He waves a hand at [Sister Ines Quiteria](../characters/sister-ines-quiteria.md) when she puts
  herself between him and her party; her trident shatters and she takes a six-minute **Shelved**
  debuff. Carl's read: "She's lucky he didn't turn her to ash"
- He heals **Paz Lo** completely, then revokes his grace — taking away Paz's ability to ever heal the
  affliction he is suffering: "Paz, you are no longer under my grace. For your failure, I am taking
  your ability to heal this condition you now suffer. Go in darkness, and when you pierce the veil,
  you will find yourself alone and without armor"
- Carl casts *Black Nimbus* on **Anton Lopez** to strip him of worshipper status, hoping to spare
  him the smite. Ogun is only curious: "That was a good attempt. But all you have done is removed
  this man from my protection. From my mercy. Not from my wrath." He then steps on Anton's head and
  crushes it, killing him instantly, while keeping his eyes locked on Carl's

His parting words spare Carl only on a technicality: "If I didn't hold Emberus in such high esteem,
I would crush you, too. Now tell my sister I said, 'Welcome Back.'"

## Consequences

- Anton Lopez is dead; Paz Lo is dying and uncurable, which is the road that leads to Sister Ines
  turning him into a T'Ghee card (and, eventually, to Paz killing [Ysalte](ysalte.md))
- Yemaya notes that Ogun shed blood inside her own temple, and Eileithyia promises to take it to
  the council — "Yes, Ogun insulted you by shedding blood in your own temple. We'll make certain the
  council hears about it"
- Paz later confirms the smite was not complete abandonment: "Ogun, he cured me of everything.
  Everything except the Inevitable debuff... He promised he would fix it, and he did. But only at
  the end."
