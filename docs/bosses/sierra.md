## A Hunter's Last Mount

Sierra is a Northropi — the same pterodactyl-class mob type as Floor 5's ghostly
[Quetzalcoatlus](quetzalcoatlus.md), but encountered here in the flesh rather than as a possessed
spirit. She was the trained mount of **Viscount Fog**, an elite vampire hunter (star of an
in-universe program called *Blood Hunter*) who tracked [Miriam Dom](../characters/miriam-dom.md)
to Floor 6 the moment she and Prepotente arrived. When Fog attacked Miriam a second time and lost,
Sierra was injured trying to save him mid-fight and fled, only for the vampirism curse she picked
up in the process to fully take hold within a day — turning her into exactly the kind of creature
her master spent his life exterminating.

## The Fight

Carl and Donut encounter Sierra by accident while flying toward Miriam and Prepotente's location
using a Levitation potion — she slams into Carl from the side mid-flight, triggering a boss battle
freeze-frame in open air. Her attack is a crushing double-talon grip meant to tear a target in
half; Carl survives the initial squeeze with a Holy Gooper potion (blessed water plus a healing
potion and a coagulant, effectively napalm to vampires) smashed directly against one talon.

Donut breaks off the standoff by climbing above the tree canopy and unleashing her maxed *Torch*
spell at full brightness — vampires take severe damage from direct light, and Sierra's head
catches fire on contact. The blast also throws Carl skyward; he falls, fist-first, straight
through Sierra's chest, tearing a hole clean through her torso before he plummets on through to
the forest floor alone — Sierra herself stays impaled between two trees, suspended above him, her
entrails and guts raining down as he falls clear. Still alive despite the wound, Sierra keeps
burning and thrashing in the canopy until Carl finds her exposed, still-beating heart on the ground
below (torn loose in the fall) and stabs it with a broken branch, finally killing her — the
still-suspended body then explodes, showering the area in gore.

## Aftermath

- Confirms Viscount Fog is already dead by this point — killed off-page in a duel against Miriam
  and [Prepotente](../characters/prepotente.md) that leaves Prepotente stunned and Fragile for
  hours, setting up Miriam's own death scene shortly after
- The kill nets Donut a level-up to her *Torch* spell (up to 14) and no other named prizes; Carl
  notes vampirism is highly contagious by touch or by eating a vampire's remains, and keeps Mongo
  well away from the corpse
- Establishes that vampirism is actively spreading through the Floor 6 forest population — see
  [Vampirism as a Floor-Wide Curse](../mechanics/index.md#vampirism-as-a-floor-wide-curse-book-5)
