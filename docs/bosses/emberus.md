# Emberus

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Deity — Carl's own god, patron of martyrs; brother of [Hellik](hellik.md), father of
      the murdered **Geyrun**
    - **Level:** 250 (deity tier)
    - **Floor:** [9 ("Faction Wars")](../floors/level-09.md), entering the realm in ch. 84 and destroying the floor in ch. 87
    - **Resolution (Book 7):** Not fought. Burns the entire ninth floor to the ground on a **66-minute
      doomsday timer**, and leaves an explicit promise to burn Carl "exquisitely" later
    - **Resolution (Book 8):** Stripped of invulnerability by Katia's Bolt of Ophiotaurus and pinned
      by a Danforth bolt, after Carl publicly renounces his
      faith and (falsely) confesses to killing Geyrun; killed by his own brother **[Taranis](taranis.md)** at
      the Floor 11 arena climax — permanence unconfirmed (see below)
    - **Recurring since:** Book 3 — Carl's patron deity, tracked through the
      [Enchanted Pauper's Ring of the Steadfast Emberus](../items/enchanted-paupers-ring-of-the-steadfast-emberus.md)

## Role Through the Floor

For most of Book 7, Emberus is a voice rather than a presence: a burning ring on Carl's finger, a
stream of quests, and an escalating tone. His Floor 9 quest chain runs through the **Kill Hellik**
thread — obtain an audience with **Issitoq** at a Club Vanquisher Temple of the Watch, break the
Sheol truce, and kill his brother before the collapse of the eleventh floor.

Because Carl worships Emberus, every Hellik worshiper on the floor is automatically hostile to him,
which the Larracos rams turn into a 500,000-gold Holy Crusade bounty on Carl's head. Emberus's
response — "This worshiper of Hellik has insulted me. Kill him," with the reward scaling to how
entertaining Carl makes it — produces one of the book's better set pieces and a Celestial Boon.

## After Hellik's Message

When Carl relays [Hellik's message](hellik.md#the-message) — that Hellik did not kill Geyrun, that
their mother and sister **Apito** has been corrupted, that the **All Tree** is rotting, that
**Nekhebit** has returned, and that **Scolopendra stirs** — it does not calm Emberus down. It makes
everything worse.

He issues a new quest (*Kill 100 worshipers of Hellik*, before the collapse of the eleventh floor),
then follows it with a private, contemplative message that is effectively a death sentence with a
delay on it.

!!! quote "Emberus to Carl (Book 7, ch. 55)"
    "Am I wrong to hate him? He has treated with Issitoq on our behalf, who has in turn allowed the
    peace to be breached. I am now allowed to kill the other Sheol princes in exchange for the
    information Amayon has about Geyrun. And what does he mean about our mother and sister? You, my
    martyr, be vigilant. **If you survive killing Hellik, like he surely deserves, I fear I may turn
    my rage and grief on you for daring to hurt my own kin, and for this, I love you, beloved but
    doomed worshiper. I promise the burning will be exquisite.**"

!!! danger "Unresolved threat for future books"
    Carl is now caught between two incompatible obligations from the same god: kill Hellik, and be
    punished for killing Hellik. The promise of an "exquisite" burning is **not** discharged in Book
    7 — Emberus burns the floor for unrelated reasons and Carl escapes. It remains outstanding.

## Entering the Realm

At the very end of the book, [Samantha](../characters/samantha.md) confesses to having taken part in
Geyrun's murder. Emberus comes in person.

!!! quote "System Message (Book 7, ch. 84)"
    "Emberus has entered the realm."

!!! quote "Team quest — no host, no opt-out (Book 7, ch. 84)"
    "A very angry god that many of you are familiar with has just entered the realm. His name is
    Emberus. His son, Geyrun, was murdered... But a demigod named 'Samantha' has just admitted to
    having taken part in the crime. Emberus, being the loving father he is, has come to the realm to
    question this demigod. Regardless of what Samantha says to him, **Emberus is going to turn the
    entirety of the 9th floor into a cremation chamber.**
    This will happen in at least **66 minutes**, but your cremation may be delayed slightly if
    Samantha actually shows up to the interrogation.
    Regardless, everything and everybody on this floor is going to die. This is not something that
    can be stopped. That is, unless you manage to kill Emberus, which as we all know is possible, but
    unlikely.
    So here's the quest part. You need to win faction wars. You gotta do it fast. And once you do
    that, get the hell out of there before the world goes up in flames."

The reward for surviving: one year off any indentureship contract negotiated on the next floor, and
all food boxes upgraded with the "extra toasty" option.

## What the Timer Forces

The 66 minutes is the engine of the entire endgame. Everything the Princess Posse had planned to do
over days has to happen inside an hour:

- [Katia](../characters/katia.md) must eat the **Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace** immediately, because
  it takes exactly 60 minutes to work — collapsing her goodbye into nothing
- The Louis Santiago transplant surgery that removes **Epitome Tagg** from his chest has to be rushed
- The second casting of [Zerzura](../spells/zerzura.md) — the Princess Posse's actual win condition —
  has to be triggered while the clock runs
- Every remaining crawler on the floor has to sprint for the stairwells early

Samantha is sent to stall him, and does: she tells him everything she knows, which only makes him
angrier. The heat rises across the map for the whole hour, with Emberus bellowing for her in the
distance.

!!! quote "Book 7, ch. 87"
    "Warning: Emberus will destroy the 9th floor in three minutes."

He is, at the end, explicitly enraged at **both Carl and Hellik**, and the floor burns: "One was
Emberus, who filled the world with fire."

## Book 8: A Parade of Horribles

### Renounced and Confessed

At the Floor 11 arena climax, Carl deliberately springs a trap on his own god. He pulls off the
**[Enchanted Pauper's Ring of the Steadfast Emberus](../items/enchanted-paupers-ring-of-the-steadfast-emberus.md)**,
publicly renounces his faith, and confesses on the record to the crime his own Emberus church quest
had been circling since Book 6 — that he, not Hellik, killed Emberus's son **Geyrun**:

!!! quote "Book 8, ch. 95"
    "The other guy to kill your worthless smear of a son was me. That's right. I killed him with my
    bare hands, and as he died, I said, 'I'm doing this because your dad is a little bitch.'"

!!! warning "The confession is a lie — the renunciation is not"
    These are two separate acts and only one of them is sincere. Carl really does leave the faith:
    he removes the ring, confirms the menu prompt, eats the smite, and carries the resulting
    **Mark of the Damned** scars for the rest of the book. The *confession*, by contrast, is pure
    provocation — Carl says so in narration one page earlier: "*I actually had no idea for certain
    who killed Geyrun, though I now knew this was something — if I survived this — I would still
    have to figure out.*" The murder of Geyrun therefore remains an **open mystery** at the end of
    Book 8, not a solved one.

Carl first names [Khepri](khepri.md) as the killer — a decoy accusation, and also a pretext for
summoning him, since it is Khepri's deity-presence effect (routed through Penny's Kneepads of Kina)
that grants Penny ninety seconds of invulnerability at the critical moment. Only then does Carl
deliver the "second killer" line about himself. See
[Carl's own page](../characters/carl.md#renouncing-emberus) for the smite's aftermath (the Emberus
tattoos on the backs of Carl's hands are burned into permanent, unexplained scars called the **Mark
of the Damned**).

### Pinned and Killed by His Own Brother

The confession is the second half of a two-part trap. Donut, firing from her *Scorpion's Bite*
weapon mount the instant Emberus rears back to attack, puts **two different bolts** into his two
eye sockets: first **Katia's Bolt of Ophiotaurus**, which strips his invulnerability for roughly
fifteen seconds, then one of her ten **Danforth bolts**, which anchors him in place so he cannot
move while vulnerable. The first half of the trap is [Penelope](../characters/penelope.md), used as bait to draw
both Emberus and his brother [Taranis](taranis.md) into the same place; see
[Taranis's own page](taranis.md#the-bait) for the mechanics of that lure.

Vulnerable and enraged, Emberus hurls a fire attack that would have killed Carl outright — the
party's **Four-Leaf Clover Patch** redirects it onto Penny instead. Taranis, watching his own
brother's attack land on the pig he has become instantly infatuated with, kills Emberus without
hesitation, one second before his invulnerability would have returned:

!!! quote "Book 8, ch. 96"
    "Taranis, seeing his little brother rising in the arena and knowing he was the one who had fired
    the attack at his beloved pig, raised his hand. Emberus paused, a moment of lucidity coming over
    the insane deity. 'Brother, no,' he said. Taranis twisted his wrist... With one second left on his
    invulnerability, Emberus, god of sun and ash, exploded."

!!! quote "System Message"
    "A deity has fallen. The heavens tremble with rage."

See [Taranis](taranis.md#killing-emberus) for the full confrontation from the killer's side.

### Permanence Not Confirmed

Book 8 never states that Emberus is gone for good, and this page does not assert it either. Gods in
this series have "died" and returned before — **Diwata** was killed on Floor 6 and was active again
by Book 7 (see the [Book 7 gods table](index.md#floor-9-book-7)). Whether the same holds for Emberus,
whose Memorial Crystal Carl recovers from the arena dirt moments later, is left an open question for
future books.

### Loose Threads

- The long-running **Kill Hellik** church quest and the promised "exquisite" burning smite (see
  above) are both rendered moot by Carl's renunciation, but neither is explicitly closed out on the
  page.
- Emberus's **Memorial Crystal** is looted from the arena floor by Carl immediately after the kill —
  a second Apito-family memorial crystal alongside Prepotente's stolen one (see
  [Taranis](taranis.md#the-crystal-the-rejection-and-leaving-with-samantha)).
- No full page yet exists documenting Emberus's earlier appearances in Books 5–6; this page still
  begins at Book 7 and should be backfilled by a future pass rather than expanded from scratch here.
