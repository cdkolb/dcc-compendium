# Madre de Aguas

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Boss tier:** City Boss
    - **Level:** 125
    - **Floor:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth"), the Cuban coast west of Havana
    - **Type:** Giant freshwater boa — the "Mother of Waters" of Cuban legend; able to control every
      creature in her area
    - **Resolution:** Charmed by [Sister Ines Quiteria](../characters/sister-ines-quiteria.md), then
      killed by her own **pre-zombism** curse (ch. 58). Nobody lands a killing blow
    - **Aftermath:** Her risen corpse becomes the physical shell and prison of the demon prince
      [Amayon](amayon.md)

## The Mother of Waters

The snake iteration of the Cuban legend: a giant boa who protects the island's fresh water and can
control the local wildlife to defend it. Seventeen or eighteen feet tall in her coil when Carl first
sees her, and by the AI's own framing, this version is firmly the "batshit murder machine"
iteration.

Her defining property is the control: whoever controls the *Madre* controls, by extension, "pretty
much every creature within shouting distance" — the monk seals, the land crabs, the zombified fish
and seagulls, the lot.

## Charmed, Then Uncharmed

Sister Ines Quiteria had hunted the *Madre* days earlier and failed to flag her. Returning with new
abilities, she instead glamoured the snake: her level-15 ***Book of Rhymes*** bard skill, freshly
granted by her patron, boosted her ***Word Weaver*** spell far enough to charm a level-125 City Boss
outright. Through the snake she took command of the entire coastal ecosystem and was assembling an
army to march on Havana.

The plan collapses because the charm breaks early. The AI's description is unsentimental about it:

!!! quote "Monster description (Book 6, ch. 55)"
    "The *Madre* was hunted and almost flagged several days earlier by a nun of all things. That
    crawler was unable to seal the deal. Upon returning to the area, this nun instead decided to
    glamor the creature using some newly-acquired skills. Controlling the snake allowed her to in
    turn control pretty much every creature within shouting distance. It was a brilliant plan until
    the spell got broken prematurely. Now the recently un-charmed *Madre de Aguas* is quite upset,
    and she's about to ruin the day of every motherfucker on the island."

## Pre-Zombism and the Demon

The same description carries two warnings that matter far more than the snake herself. She has been
bitten repeatedly by zombie fish and is carrying **pre-zombism** — still living flesh, but
guaranteed to rise again with new powers once she dies. And the island is in the middle of a
**Demon Eviction** event (see [Game Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md)), which the AI flags as
dangerous without explaining why: "Sometimes it's better to be surprised."

The answer arrives shortly after:

!!! quote "Area Message (Book 6, ch. 58)"
    "The *Madre de Aguas* has succumbed to her zombism curse. She has fallen and risen again. But
    the curse has left her vulnerable, and a major demon has involuntarily slipped into the realm.
    Amayon has arrived. Beware, all. Beware.
    Hell has come to burn the world."

From that point the *Madre*'s body is no longer the boss — it is a containment vessel. Amayon,
youngest of the four princes of Sheol, inhabits the snake's corpse and uses it to pour fire into the
sky over Havana for most of the floor's endgame, while Sister Ines retains control of him through
her hold on the shell. The snake body is finally dropped when Amayon is freed and takes his true
form at the FOCSA building (ch. 66).
