# Yarilo

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Feral God — God of Lust, escaped from **the Nothing**
    - **Level:** 250 (deity tier). Feral, and therefore **not immortal on this floor**
    - **Floor:** [9 ("Faction Wars")](../floors/level-09.md), the open battlefield and Shanty Town (ch. 64–73)
    - **Allegiance:** Conscripted by the **War Mage Rebellion** with a hell-kissed level-18
      Conscription potion
    - **Resolution:** **Killed** — beaten to death by [Harpocrates](harpocrates.md), who was himself
      under **Li Na's** conscription, with the Rolling Battle Formation ball
      used as a club
    - **Family:** "the nephew or cousin or half-brother of [Emberus](emberus.md). Probably all of
      those things"

## Who He Is

Yarilo is the second feral god to break out of the Nothing on Floor 9, after [Meatus](meatus.md). He
was banished there in the first place for ruining one of **Apito**'s Butcher's Masquerade parties —
which matters, because under the usual rule the god who banished a feral escapee is the one who comes
to collect them, and Apito is "both dead and not dead," a mystery Carl reads as another deliberate
clue.

He is huge but much smaller than Harpocrates, humanoid, elf-like, with long blond hair.

He also has history with [Samantha](../characters/samantha.md): he is the god she impersonated to
trick the Mad Dune Mage into letting her escape the Nothing back on the bubble floors, and he holds a
grudge about it.

!!! quote "Samantha, on being chased (Book 7, ch. 64)"
    "YOU DON'T HAVE TO TELL ME TWICE. THIS IS A GOD, NOT A DEMON. IT'S YARILO, AND HE DOESN'T LIKE
    ME! HE'S MAD BECAUSE HE THINKS I HELPED GET HIM TOSSED IN THE NOTHING IN THE FIRST PLACE. IT'S
    NOT MY FAULT HE'S SEXUALLY ATTRACTED TO ANIMALS."

He spends much of his screen time chasing Samantha in circles without actually doing anything, then
giving up — which she takes personally: "GOD OF LUST? I DON'T THINK SO. HE'S THE GOD OF BEING A
PERVERTED LITTLE BITCH."

## Conscription

A War Mage Rebellion mage flies in and lands a hell-kissed Conscription potion on him. It works — and
the caster immediately explodes, which is the expected outcome when the level disparity is that
extreme. Yarilo takes *Under Orders* as a member of the War Mage team and runs off, wrecking
Samantha's plan to use him to rescue [Louis Santiago](../characters/louis-santiago.md).

Being in his range grants two temporary effects to anyone nearby, and he is the first god the
Princess Posse sees weaponised by an enemy faction — the precedent that pushes Li Na to conscript
Harpocrates in response.

## Death

Li Na's first order to the conscripted Harpocrates is to deal with the god of lust. The fight moves
across Shanty Town, and it is not a fight: Harpocrates holds the Rolling Battle Formation ball by
Meatus's shaft with both hands and hammers Yarilo down with it repeatedly while Yarilo throws chunks
of buildings and pleads.

!!! quote "Book 7, ch. 73"
    "'Stop!' Yarilo shrieked as Harpocrates hammered into him. 'Not my face. Anything but my face!'
    ...
    'Please!' came the weak shout of Yarilo, followed by a terrible, wet splatch as Harpocrates
    slammed the ball down, shaking the ground."

!!! quote "System message (Book 7, ch. 73)"
    "A deity has fallen. And while Yarilo was damned to the Nothing, his death has angered the gods.
    The heavens tremble with rage.
    The Scavenger's Daughter has looked away, though her eyes remain open. The benefit from Yarilo
    fades."

Samantha spends the aftermath sobbing in the group chat about it.
