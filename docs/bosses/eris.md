# Eris

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Deity — Goddess of Chaos
    - **Level:** 250
    - **Floor:** [9 ("Faction Wars")](../floors/level-09.md), the Dream's territory and then the whole map (ch. 61–76)
    - **Sponsor:** **Nami**, a model and influencer who outbid the Priory for the slot — killed
      mid-book, after which Eris runs loose and unsponsored: "This deity has lost her sponsor and
      can no longer be sponsored this season"
    - **Resolution:** Never fought. Escapes the Rolling Battle Formation ball, speaks privately with
      [Carl](../characters/carl.md), and leaves the realm of her own accord
    - **Presence effect:** *Random Effect! As Eris is the goddess of chaos, this effect will change
      each time you are in her presence*
    - **Standing:** "**Eris is a prime contender for the Ascendent Throne**"

## Who She Is

Tall, elf-like with a strangely long neck and a few serpentine features that suggest a distant naga
relative; long blonde hair, a forked tongue, a black-and-white striped floor-length jacket over
glowing Valkyrie armor, and over-inflated lips tattooed with a spiral. Her symbol is two back-to-back
Cs with a flaming lance through them.

!!! quote "Deity description (Book 7, ch. 76)"
    "Eris. Goddess of Chaos. Half-sister of Taranis, mother to nobody, lover of parties, drinking,
    chaos—of course, and generally known as the Ascendency's worst, most unapologetic gossip. If she
    were mortal and human, she'd be called a wine aunt. She'd have a rich, dead husband who died
    under mysterious circumstances. She would split her time between the hair salon and spending her
    days on the local community Facebook page where she would be constantly creating utter
    pandemonium with her posts and her 'this is just my opinion, but...' style messages.
    All of her nieces and nephews love her. In fact, everybody loves her, even though they all know
    deep down she's an utter piece of shit...
    One of her abilities is being **All Knowing**. She knows everybody's business, which makes her a
    dangerous foe and an even more dangerous friend. If she gives you information, it's probably
    true, and it's probably something that's going to get you into trouble for knowing. While she's
    able to lie, she rarely does. Why bother when the truth is often so much worse?"

The description notes this is a genuinely unique season: there has never been a Faction Wars where
Eris was not sponsored, so this is the first time her real personality has been allowed to operate.
Her pilot **Nami** had cosmetic surgery to look and sound like the old crawler
[Hekla](../characters/hekla.md), which is why Eris speaks in Hekla's voice — fading once Nami is
dead.

## Losing Her Pilot

Eris is dragged into the Meatus–Harpocrates fight almost immediately, with [Meatus](meatus.md)
wrapping around her and swinging her as a battering ram. She panics and starts casting.

A War Mage Rebellion mage later reaches her inside the ball and strips her **soul armor**, ejecting
Nami — who is spat out of the ball as an ordinary body, immediately skinned by a
[flesher](../mobs/flesher.md) and destroyed by Carl's hob-lobber before she can become a new war
mage. From that point Eris is unsponsored, unpiloted and entirely her own.

## The Ceremony of Chaos

Panicking mid-fight, Eris casts a pair of spells she is normally supposed to save for the twelfth
floor: [**Bear Witness**](../spells/bear-witness.md), followed by **Bring Forth the Witnesses**.

!!! quote "System warning (Book 7, ch. 65)"
    "Warning. The Goddess Eris has cast Bear Witness in a wide area around her current position. This
    will only affect those outside of structures. The area of effect is marked on your map, but it's
    pretty much the whole floor."

!!! quote "Mordecai on the Ceremony of Chaos (Book 7, ch. 66)"
    "This is exclusive to Eris, and she usually does this on the 12th floor... Some gods can only
    claim the Ascendent throne if there are no mortals present, so Eris sometimes causes chaos by
    summoning mortal witnesses to the ceremony. It's called the **Ceremony of Chaos**. The witnesses
    are protected from the gods during this, hence the invulnerability, and she can keep them in the
    Witness state as long as she wants... They're brought to her in a giant pile. It's... it's a
    thing. **She can only cast this once. Ever.**"

The result: roughly **80,000 combatants** frozen, blinded to the chat, made invulnerable and
teleported into one enormous heap beside the ruined Dream castle. The backlash also kills long-range
teleportation floor-wide for an hour and interrupts the lava-abatement plan for Larracos.

That heap is what Donut then wraps in her tower-amplified [**Rolling Battle Formation**](../spells/rolling-battle-formation.md), producing the
mile-wide rolling ball that dominates the rest of the floor. Carl's assessment afterward, relayed to
Donut: "That's on that dead alien who was driving Eris. You saved them by casting the Battle Formation
spell. If you hadn't done it, half of everyone would be dead by now."

Eris wakes inside the ball, switches her Witness spell off — which changes nothing, because Donut's
spell is now doing the immobilising — and is trapped there until it breaks.

## The Conversation with Carl

When the ball finally breaks, Eris freezes the world and appears beside Carl at human scale. She is
explicitly **not** under War Mage control. She spins him a two-item wheel that makes one of his
current god-effects permanent (Meatus's curse, or Harpocrates's Pied Piper skill — it lands on Pied
Piper), bites his ear, snaps his pinkie for laughing at, and recruits.

!!! quote "Eris's pitch (Book 7, ch. 76)"
    "Don't die, Carl... I know you worship grumpy ol' Emberus, but we'll change that soon, especially
    now he has put you in an impossible situation. See how Donut was just named Champion? I can do
    that to you as well. You'll have to kill all three, of course. **Taranis, Emberus, and Hellik.**
    But don't worry. I'll help."

Then she hands over the two pieces of information that drive the rest of the book:

!!! quote "Book 7, ch. 76"
    "The War Mages traded the **Gate of the Feral Gods** with a merchant named **Paca** at the
    Midnight Market. Paca is an agent for Krakaren Prime, who is stuck in the Nothing. Paca has the
    gate in her stomach. Get the gate back and give it to that insane Skyfowl who built the antennae
    underneath your castle, and maybe you can actually win this thing. Also, **Britney picked that
    axe off the ground and is now possessed**."

!!! warning "Unverified: the Britney claim"
    Eris's statement that [Britney](../characters/britney.md) is possessed is **never confirmed or
    denied on the page** in Book 7. Nothing later in the book resolves it, and Carl's own description
    of Eris — that when she gives you information "it's probably true, and it's probably something
    that's going to get you into trouble for knowing" — is the only weight behind it. Treat it as an
    open thread, not an established fact.

Carl relays everything Eris said to Rosetta, Tipid, Donut and most of the officers. Both Rosetta and
Tipid are wary of the idea of fetching the [Gate of the Feral Gods](../items/gate-of-the-feral-gods.md)
for [Justice Light](../characters/justice-light.md), being protective of the skyfowl; Carl advocates for it anyway.

## Afterward

- Eris leaves the realm on her own terms — "Eris has left the Realm. The Scavenger's Daughter looks
  away"
- She had earlier re-enacted the teleport ban that strands crawlers late in the floor
- Carl is on her "friend of the church" list, which he finds a mystery
- At the very end of the book, Carl hears laughter "distinctly like that of Eris" from inside the
  river in his own head — a late, unexplained beat
- She appears once more in the epilogue, in person, in a shop, asking whether anyone is really going
  to ask Eris, goddess of chaos, if she is wearing a costume

## Book 8: A Parade of Horribles

Eris's presence escalates sharply across Book 8, culminating in a new, far more monstrous physical
form at the Floor 11 climax.

### The Five-Sided Coin

Early in Floor 10, Eris appears to Carl in Hungry Eyes Village, casually warns him he's "running out
of time" on his Emberus problem, and hands him a fate coin:

!!! quote "Book 8"
    "She spat into her own hand, and a large coin appeared. 'Ever seen one of these? It's a
    five-sided coin.'"

The coin shows four permanent-effect outcomes on its faces — Donut's death ("RIP"), Carl's feet
severed ("de-feeted"), most of Carl's friends dying, and Carl hanged-but-smiling ("I become what
they accuse me of") — with a fifth, unrevealed side. Before her pet Uzi Jesus can flip it, Carl grabs
the coin out of the air himself, which Eris declares unlocks the random fifth outcome instead of
committing to one of the first four: "You picked the fifth side! Great! Chaos wins again!" A swirling
tattoo appears on his palm as a permanent marker. By the end of the book, the coin's result is still
undetermined — Eris's parting words are "we have until the end of the twelfth to figure out how the
fifth side lands."

In the same scene, Eris confirms her price for patronage explicitly names three gods Carl would have
to kill: [Taranis](taranis.md), [Emberus](emberus.md), and [Hellik](hellik.md) — and drops early hints
about the goddess **Apito**'s "scattered pieces" that pay off at the climax.

### The Arena Form

At the Floor 11 finale, immediately after Emberus's death, Eris appears again — freezing the world,
swapping every nearby crawler's inventory at random as her chaos effect, and revealing a physical
form nothing like her earlier human-scale appearance:

!!! quote "Book 8, ch. 97"
    "Eris, her voice howling with laughter, cracked into existence, standing directly over me, her
    body huge, nothing like the form I was used to. Her serpentine face was the same, but her body
    was that of a Saccathian mixed with the Krakaren boss still in the stands. But instead of
    tentacles, her lower mass was nothing but the tails of snakes. Thousands of them, like a reverse
    Medusa, many with rattles on them."

She taunts [Taranis](taranis.md) for his relationship with their mother/wife **Apito** and confirms
the goddess is not fully gone:

!!! quote "Book 8, ch. 97"
    "Our mom is going to be so pissed when she fully comes back from the dead... Four of her five
    pieces are in here right now watching you try to literally fuck a pig!"

This is the book's clearest confirmation that Apito — introduced across Books 5–7 as "both dead and
not dead" — survives in some scattered, five-piece form, with unfinished business explicitly teed up
for [Floor 12](../floors/index.md) and beyond: "if we let those guys downstairs win the Ascendency,
it's lights out. No more fun."

### Aftermath

- Eris departs on her own terms, as always — "Eris has left the realm. Your inventory has been
  restored" — leaving the fate coin's outcome and Apito's condition both explicitly unresolved.
- She remains, per her own words, unconcerned with worship in the traditional sense: "You are chaos
  incarnate... I don't need you to worship me."
