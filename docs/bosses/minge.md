# Minge

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Minor Feral Demon
    - **Level:** 225
    - **Floor:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth"), the middle floor of the
      [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md)
    - **Released:** Accidentally, through a rigged knife game in the club's casino (ch. 32)
    - **Killed by:** **the vorpals**, unleashed by the club's assistant manager Astrid as a last
      resort (ch. 33)
    - **Family:** Sister of **Slit**, the feral demon who hunted
      [Samantha](../characters/samantha.md) on Floor 5 (see
      [Other Bosses](index.md#other-bosses)). A third sister, **Gash**, is named but never appears

## The Breach

Samantha, brought into the Desperado Club as part of Carl and Katia's plan to kill Astrid, wanders
into the casino with her ex-boyfriend's head and gets chased by guards. At the same moment a patron
loses the **knife game** and is pulled into the Nothing — and the hole tears open on the way. Minge
comes out through the tear.

!!! quote "Samantha, Book 6, ch. 33"
    "I saw the table with the knife game, and the guards were chasing me because I had my
    ex-boyfriend with me, and somebody lost the game at the same moment, and the guy got pulled into
    the Nothing, but when it opened, it tore and Minge came out. She's Slit's little sister. We used
    to be friends. It's sad, really."
    "I'm just glad it wasn't Gash. She's the mean one."

!!! note "Gash never appears"
    Gash is mentioned exactly once, in the line above. She does not appear on the page at any point
    in Book 6 — no description, no encounter, no stat block.

## The Creature

Where Slit was a hundred-foot kaiju, Minge is sized to fit the building — and barely. A naked,
corpulent, sore-covered pink mass crammed into the club's restaurant with her bald head shoved
sideways against the ceiling, "like she'd been oozed into the room from an ice-cream machine and was
just now starting to melt." She has no visible legs but at least twenty slender, multi-jointed arms
ending in kite-sized claws, long enough to snake across the entire room and strong enough to hold
struggling cretins by the neck. Her face wears the same thick clown makeup as her sister's, with
smeared red lipstick, and she stinks of diseased skin.

At level 225 she is only 25 levels short of a god. Carl notes she "wasn't going to be really
injured" by anything he could bring, and in fact her health bar never visibly moves during his
entire attempt.

## Rampage

Minge tears through the club hunting Samantha — whom she screams for by her true name, **Psamathe**
— and takes the middle floor apart in the process. She kills the guards, rips the head off a Sai
rhinoceros samurai, pulls the double doors and a chunk of wall out of the badger bar, reaches
through into the silk road and waves merchants around in her claws, and eventually drops the main
stage away entirely. She also rips the badger bartender in half after he gets an arm stuck in
Samantha's neck hole.

Carl's only meaningful contribution is a **demon snare** trap mounted on a spider automaton, rigged
to detonate and splash her with blood water from Emberus's shrine — the equivalent, as he puts it,
"of using a mouse trap to catch an elephant." It hurts her enough to make her shrink back, which is
all the opening he needs.

## Death

Astrid, unable to kill the demon herself, pulls the trigger on the club's own last resort and
unleashes **the vorpals** from behind a sealed door on the middle floor. Per Mordecai, vorpal is a
condition rather than a species — creatures "deputized by the gods," celestial foot soldiers that
grow stronger and angrier the closer they get to a demon, draw demon aggro, and explode if they
think they are losing. They only dissipate once they have been fed demon blood.

They find Minge. A buzzsaw-like shriek rises through the hole in the floor along with a spray of
yellow fluid, and she dies screaming, "PSAMATHE! YOU FUCKING BITCH!" Her corpse is still being
eviscerated below while Carl evacuates the club's dancers.

## Cost

Minge's rampage destroys the Desperado Club's entire middle floor and kills most of its guards and
staff — cover that Carl and Katia use to complete the Astrid assassination without being identified.
Astrid herself is killed in the middle of the chaos (see
[Mobs](../mobs/index.md#book-5-mob-catalog) for the correction to her old catalog entry).
