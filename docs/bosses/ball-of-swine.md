## Encounter

Carl and Donut find this boss's lair — a circular "spiral maze" reached after a full day of
chopper scouting down a highway corridor — while searching for a way off Floor 1 entirely; the
map marks the location itself as **"Stairwell to Floor Two."** Graffiti at the entrance warns
"DO NOT go deeper into the spiral or you will activate the borough boss." Mordecai's advice is to
skip it and find an unguarded stairwell elsewhere, but Carl, having found only this one exit
after a full day of searching, decides they can't risk not finding another and chooses to fight
it head-on alongside Brandon An's group.

The fight takes place in a fortified position built from scavenged furniture and a
ninja-star-covered tabletop trap. The Ball itself disassembles into dozens of individual
[Tuskling](../mobs/tuskling.md) combatants (Knights, Courtesans, and at least one "Tuskling
Dominatrix") once engaged — Donut fights primarily with Magic Missile, Carl with improvised
melee and his fists, and Brandon's group with a sword and a hammer. Everyone in the mixed party
survives the fight itself.

With the boss dead, the red forcefield around the stairwell drops. Carl and Donut descend
immediately afterward, arriving on Floor 2 within minutes (confirmed when producer Lexis
congratulates them shortly after on "making it down to the second floor"). The broader
Meadow Lark evacuation continues onto Floor 2 as well, and costs the group a member there (see
[Carl](../characters/carl.md) for the Rage Elemental aftermath).
