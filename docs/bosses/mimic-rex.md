# Mimic Rex

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Boss tier:** City Boss
    - **Level:** 90
    - **Floor:** 4 ("The Iron Tangle"), Trainyard E
    - **Full title:** "The current crawler-killer champion of the floor" — over 23,000 attributed deaths
    - **Killed by:** Grull the War God (see below) — Carl survives but does not land the killing blow himself
    - **Format:** A **Death Challenge**, not a standard boss card — see Game Mechanics note below

!!! quote "AI Commentary (in-voice, written for this wiki — not a book excerpt)"

## The Death Challenge

Carl is accidentally teleported directly into the mimic's lair while trying to redirect it into
a portal. Rather than a normal boss-card reveal, the game frames the whole encounter as a live
gambling event:

> "A Death Challenge is not a boss battle. It's a pop-up gambling event where viewers... are
> given the option to bet on a multiple set of outcomes, with each possible outcome being given
> different odds... The system AI initially put your general odds of survival at 50 to 1."

The broadcast introduces both combatants with full announcer pageantry — the Mimic Rex ("the
apex predator of her world, the voracious, the insatiable, the grand impersonator supreme") on
one side, and on the other, the *sponsor* of the war god Grull (already established via the
[War Gauntlet](../items/war-gauntlet-of-the-exalted-grull.md)'s involuntary-summon mechanic):
**Prince Maestro of the Skull Empire**, previously presumed dead, secretly alive and piloting
Grull as his broadcast comeback. Carl himself had guessed wrong on both counts earlier in the
book, assuming Maestro was actually dead and that it was **Crown Prince Stalwart** "walking
around in the skin of the war god Grull" — the Death Challenge reveal corrects him on the spot.

## The Mimic Rex

A city-sized mimic — bigger than Grull himself — that fights with a train-length tongue and
spawns rhinoceros-sized "mouth" minions called **Jabbering Jibber-Jabbers** (see
[Mobs](../mobs/index.md) — a splitting minion type that hates everyone and everything, driven
only by an urge to feed). Per the announcer, she's the floor's single deadliest recorded threat.
She's ultimately killed
mid-fight when Grull cleaves her in half with his axe — collateral damage from the god's rampage
rather than a kill Carl or his party land directly.

## Grull the War God

> "War God Grull. Level 250. Sponsored by Prince Maestro of the Skull Empire. Warning: This is a
> deity. He is invulnerable on this floor. This god has been involuntarily summoned to this
> location. Summoning rules apply."

Grull is a black-skinned, horse-bodied, bull-headed centaur god wielding a smoking double-headed
axe, roughly four stories tall (described as still only a "miniature" version of his true form).
He is explicitly unkillable at this point in the story — Carl's entire strategy revolves around
manipulating the involuntary-summon mechanic to banish him rather than fight him. After Grull
kills the Mimic Rex, Carl's party lures him away with a Katia-disguised decoy, then triggers a
second involuntary summon on a captured, dismembered mantaur vessel and shoves it through a
portal set to the abyss with about one second left on its summon timer — pulling Grull (and
Maestro) out of the arena entirely before he can kill anyone.

## Aftermath

- Neither Carl nor his party personally lands a killing blow on either the Mimic Rex or Grull —
  the "win" is survival plus successfully banishing both threats
- Looting the Mimic Rex's remains yields the **Map of the Stars**, a boss-tier item adding boss
  locations/descriptions to a wide map area
- Carl and Elle later send the *Nightmare* train into the abyss as a follow-up bomb, hoping to
  finish off the god, the relocated Mimic remains, and a nearby [province
  boss](index.md#other-bosses) all at once — results are not fully confirmed in Book 3
- The event permanently humiliates Prince Maestro on-air for a third time (after the "Pork Boy"
  incident and his earlier disowning)
