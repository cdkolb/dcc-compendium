# Scolopendra

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** **Dungeon Boss** — a brand-new tier above Floor Boss, named for the first time in
      Book 8: "Just so we're crystal clear. I just summoned, at your fucking request, the final boss
      of the entire dungeon to the 11th floor."
    - **Level:** 500
    - **Floor:** Referenced since [Floor 9](../floors/level-09.md) (Book 7); physically appears for
      the first and only time at the [Floor 11](../floors/level-11.md) arena climax (Book 8)
    - **Form:** A centipede "the size of a goddamned aircraft carrier but longer," segmented,
      brown-and-red with bright yellow legs — "It wasn't anything special. It looked exactly like a
      centipede from Earth."
    - **Summoned by:** Carl, as the first of the 23 surviving crawlers' joint arena choices — "For my
      first choice, I choose Scolopendra"
    - **Resolution:** Neither killed nor abandoned — **transformed** by an enriched pet biscuit into a
      Level 1 sapient crawler party member, "Sexy Scolopendra"
    - **Status at end of Book 8:** Alive, sapient, in Carl and Donut's party

## Background Through Book 7

Nothing about Scolopendra's nature, form, or motive was ever shown on the page before Book 8. What
existed was pure dread: two system warnings ("Scolopendra Stirs") tying her to the corruption of
[Apito](../characters/samantha.md), the rot in the All Tree, and [Nekhebit](../characters/odette.md)'s
return, and a final Book 7 system message declaring her **awakened**, apparently triggered by
[Justice Light](../characters/justice-light.md)'s climactic trap draining the broken Nothing into
Scolopendra's Lair. See the [Book 7 boss table](index.md#floor-9-book-7) for the full list of prior
warnings and the civilisation-scale catastrophe she was already blamed for (the near-extinction of
the [Semeru Dwarves](../species/semeru-dwarves.md), among others).

Book 8 opens with her already loose and rampaging across the upper floors — Quasar reports she's
"so far ... hadn't yet done anything other than move around a lot," and the 18th floor's Scolopendra
Club has already gone dark, its guests forced through a gauntlet to reach safety.

## The Nine-Tier Attack

Late in Floor 10, provoked purely by the AI's own building rage at the crawlers' refusal to die on
schedule, Scolopendra's clock starts:

!!! quote "System Message (Book 8, Heat 7 start)"
    "Scolopendra has released the first of nine attacks... You think these nine attacks are only
    going to affect the Scolopendra levels? Think again, bitches!... There will be at least 30 hours
    between each assault, unless Scolopendra is confronted directly or further upset. Then the
    remaining attacks may come hard and fast."

Each attack is a wheel-spin — the specific effect is not predetermined even by the AI itself
("What is this first attack? I don't actually know! Let's find out!") — followed by a second,
individual green/red wheel per person determining who is actually hit. The general shape of this
"Nine-Tier Attack" quest mechanic (the wheel, the timing rules, the escalation) is a
[Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md)-pass topic; this page covers only what happens to Scolopendra and
the party specifically.

**Attack 1 — the Beast of Burden Sin (Transformation).** Fires while the teams are still sitting in
the Heat 7 starting blocks, minutes before the final Floor 10 race begins, with a stated 20% chance
of hitting any given target:

!!! quote "Book 8"
    "You have been hit with a level 20 Transformation spell! ... Change selected."

- **Carl** → a long-haired dachshund (with a German accent he can't shake)
- **Elle McGibbons** → a goblin
- **Chris Andrews** → a werewolf, spending most of his time in human form
- **Jacobus**, Imani's permanently-summoned reverse tooth fairy → a starfish
- Donut, Jamal, and Samantha all roll green and are unaffected; game guides are immune; Prepotente
  and Jurgen are shielded by his Apito memorial crystal

The duration clause matters: "*For crawlers, the effects will last until you pass through a
stairwell. For NPCs and everyone else, the effects will last 6 hours.*" Carl and Elle therefore
change back on the stairs down to Floor 11 — but **Chris**, who leaves the dungeon through the
Desperado Club's casino portal rather than a stairwell, never triggers the reset and is still a
werewolf in the epilogue. Imani treats that as the good outcome, not a curse: his Igneous rock form
is what would have killed him outside an enhancement zone.

**Attack 2 — the Sin of Consumption.** Fires dungeon-wide during the Floor 11 arena approach, with a
30% chance per person: worms burst from and devour anyone who fails their roll. Being physically
inside Club Scolopendra does **not** protect Carl ("*The club wasn't protecting me from the
attack!*"), and neither do safe rooms. Carl and Penny both roll green; it kills the tortured tourist
Cerni Pinta on the spot, kills Prepotente's mount Sweety, wounds Gonk, and takes out roughly a third
of the arena's mob population.

**Attack 3 — interrupted.** Scolopendra begins spinning up a third attack mid-arena-battle; this is
the one Carl and Donut stop cold by feeding her the biscuit (below) with roughly ten seconds left on
the countdown.

## The Floor 11 Arena

At the climax, Carl chooses to summon Scolopendra as the crawlers' collective first pick for the
arena's stand-in fight — a plan the AI itself calls "the fucking prize" of "some suicidal gambits
throughout the history of the crawl."

!!! quote "System Message (Book 8, ch. 94)"
    "You have entered the lair of a Floor Boss. You have entered the lair of the Dungeon Boss...
    Krakaren Prime. Level 235 Floor boss. ... Scolopendra. Level 500 Dungeon boss. Well, shit."

The AI lays out the stakes in unusually calm, glitch-free narration — a tonal shift the text notes
explicitly ("which somehow made it seem even more terrifying"):

- Killing her is not the same as winning the dungeon; the only exit remains the 18th floor.
- Killing her **does** stop level-collapse timers dungeon-wide and kicks off the Ascendency battles
  immediately.
- **Abandoning her on a collapsing floor counts as killing her.**
- Either way — kill her outright, *or* leave her behind when the floor collapses — **all of her
  remaining nine-tier attacks trigger at once**, with no way to stop it: "You kill her, the remaining
  attacks trigger. You escape, the remaining attacks trigger. Yeah, good luck with this one,
  dumbass."

This is the trap the entire finale is built around avoiding. Scolopendra herself is not a
combatant with intent — "The beast itself is nothing but a mindless animal reacting to the world
around her... She is not rage. She is not anger. She is not vengeance. She is nature."

### The Biscuit

While Carl is inside Club Scolopendra using [Penelope](../characters/penelope.md) as bait to draw out
[Taranis](taranis.md) and [Emberus](emberus.md), Eris's chaos effect swaps every nearby crawler's
inventory at random. In the scramble to recover before Scolopendra's third attack fires, Carl has
Donut search his scrambled hotlist for a specific combination:

!!! quote "Book 8, ch. 97"
    "Donut, be careful! There are lots of bombs in there. Don't pull anything out unless I tell you
    to! Go to my hotlist. I need you to combine the ball in slot ten with the biscuit in slot
    twelve!"

The result is a banger sphere loaded with what Carl believes is **the plain "Nothing Special"
enriched pet biscuit** — the mindless-pet variety, not the Party Companion one already spent on
[Penelope](../characters/penelope.md) during the float-building phase. Carl throws it directly into
Scolopendra's mouth, and the Level 500 Dungeon Boss instantly collapses into "a gelatinous puddle
the size of a small elephant" — reduced, per the item's normal function, to a Level 1 creature
ready to be claimed as a pet.

!!! quote "System reaction (Book 8, ch. 97)"
    "New Achievement. What the actual fuck, Carl? Okay, so there are power moves. And then there's
    this... Reward: ... Yeah, I got nothing for this."

This is not a kill and not an abandonment — she survives the floor collapse safely tucked away as a
pet, which sidesteps the trap entirely and pauses the remaining nine-tier attacks rather than
triggering them.

### The Mix-Up

The plan was to end up with a mindless Level 1 **pet** — the "ultimate weapon," as Carl frames it,
because keeping her in his inventory means her death (and the attack cascade) is now entirely tied to
his own survival. That is not what happens.

!!! quote "Book 8, ch. 98"
    "'Carl?' Donut said. 'Uh, okay, so we have a problem. Don't get mad.' ... 'So, with the inventory.
    It was happening so fast, I made a mistake. Like, a really, really bad one.'"

Because of the Eris-triggered inventory chaos, Donut had grabbed the **wrong** enriched pet biscuit.
Scolopendra emerges not as a mindless pet but as a new, fully conscious crawler:

!!! quote "Book 8, ch. 98"
    "A new member has joined your party! Sexy Scolopendra. Level 1. Crawler #12,953,456."

She retains her centipede body — "a humanlike face with strangely giant lips... no arms. Twin yellow
antennae twitched on her head. She had a red bow on one of the feelers" — but is now sapient,
soft-spoken, and immediately, cheerfully flirtatious:

!!! quote "Book 8, ch. 98"
    "'Hi, Carl,' Scolopendra said, her voice soft and sultry. She fell to the ground, skittered
    forward, then stood, wrapping herself around me, squeezing tight until we were face-to-face...
    'Boop,' she said."

!!! warning "Which biscuit she actually got is never stated on the page"
    The text never names the biscuit Donut grabbed by mistake — only that she grabbed the wrong
    one. It is worth recording what the result rules out and what it points at, without treating
    either as confirmed:

    - It is **not** the plain **"Nothing Special"** pet biscuit Carl asked for. That grants "no
      sapience whatsoever" and reverts the creature to juvenile form; Scolopendra is a sapient,
      numbered crawler.
    - It is almost certainly **not** the **"Nothing Special Party Companion"** biscuit already
      spent on Penelope, which this wiki previously asserted. That result is explicitly path one
      — the creature "will stay in their existing form... the anatomy won't change at all, meaning
      they won't gain the ability to talk." Scolopendra *does* talk out loud, and she is no longer
      centipede-shaped in the ordinary sense: she is human-sized, upright, with "a humanlike face,"
      and Carl compares her directly to Commander Stockade, a documented **subtree two-B**
      transformation.
    - Those two traits — a subtree two-B physical transformation *plus* speech — match **path
      two**, and the only enriched path-two result named anywhere in the book is
      **["The Paramour"](../items/enriched-pet-biscuits.md)**, whose extra clause is "a permanent,
      incurable charm that will make them fall hopelessly in love with the entity who fed them the
      biscuit." Carl is the one who threw the biscuit, and her first act is to wrap herself around
      him and boop his nose.

    The book does not connect those dots itself, never shows how Prepotente's unique Paramour
    biscuit would have reached Carl's hotlist, and never rules out the existence of enriched
    biscuits beyond the three Prepotente was shown receiving. Treat the identity of the biscuit as
    **unresolved**, with the Paramour as the indicated but unconfirmed candidate.

## Status at Book's End

Scolopendra leaves Floor 11 as a Level 1 member of Carl and Donut's own party rather than a tamed
pet or a corpse — a wildcard nobody, including Carl, appears to have anticipated. Whether the
"abandon her and the remaining attacks trigger" clause still applies to a sapient party member
(rather than a caged pet) is not addressed anywhere in Book 8 and should be treated as an open
question for future books, not resolved here.
