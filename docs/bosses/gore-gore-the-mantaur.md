## Encounter

Gore-Gore bursts out of [Club Vanquisher](../locations/club-vanquisher.md) after a ram-headed
cleric tips him off that Carl's group crashed one of his trains. He's a Mantaur — a
centaur-adjacent, four-armed warrior race devoted to the war god Grull — wielding metallic claw
weapons and immune to Magic Missile damage. The fight takes place on and around a wrecked,
smoking train platform.

The fight has a real complication: Carl's [War Gauntlet](../items/war-gauntlet-of-the-exalted-grull.md)
carries a small, *cumulative* chance to involuntarily summon the god Grull on every successful
punch. Carl doesn't realize the odds stack (1.5%, 3.0%, 4.5%...) until the counter is already at
7.5%, and by the time Gore-Gore is staggered, the summon has been triggered — a ten-second
countdown begins. Carl finishes Gore-Gore off with a flying [Talon
Strike](../abilities/talon-strike.md) drop kick that sends the boss's body onto the subway's
live third rail, electrocuting him — killing the boss and ending the fight (and, crucially, the
summon countdown) in the same instant, mere seconds before Grull would have fully manifested.

Carl is knocked around badly by the fight (a throat-grab into a fall through wreckage, several
electrical burns) but survives; the encounter closes with a live sponsorship notification from
the **Valtay Corporation** — Carl's first Valtay sponsor.
