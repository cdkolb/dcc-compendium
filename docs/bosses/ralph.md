## B... B... B... Boss Battle

Per the in-universe boss-battle card — unusually long, framed as a mock history lesson:

> "When the Black Death swept through 14th century Europe, killing upwards of 200 million people
> and forever altering the course of human history, one of the original culprits of the epidemic
> was said to be the black rat... This is, in fact, not true. The true perpetrator was actually
> the Asian great gerbil... This is only important to know because Ralph, champion pit fighter of
> the kobold training grounds, lives his life in a perpetual state of rage... Part earth rodent,
> part the embodiment of death, Frenzied Gerbils are regular mobs one might encounter on the
> fifth or seventh floors. But Ralph here is special... He is fast, he is angry, and by the time
> you're done reading this, he's already halfway to your jugular. You might want to duck."

## Encounter

Carl and Donut firebomb a kobold fighting-pit arena (about 40 kobolds) with a boom jug, only for
the boss card to reveal Ralph — a small, absurdly cute gerbil boss with an oversized, physics-defying
jaw — mid-explosion. Before the fight properly resumes, a surviving kobold triggers a switch that
opens the arena's animal cages, releasing a pack of chained, abused danger dingoes. Carl and Donut
had earlier shown one of the dingoes a small kindness (feeding it), which breaks its conditioning:
the freed dingoes turn on their kobold captors instead of the crawlers.

One dingo swallows Ralph whole in a single bite, seemingly ending the fight anticlimactically.
But the boss music doesn't stop — moments later, the dingo's head explodes and Ralph bursts back
out, rocketing at Carl's throat. Carl punches it out of the air bare-handed, triggering his War
Gauntlet's rare Stun proc (a 2% chance he'd never seen land before) for the first time in the
book. He finishes Ralph off with a bare-foot stomp, apologizing as he does it ("Sorry, Ralph").

Clearing the fight unlocks the room's reward: kobold loot, a **Master Pen Key**, and — through
the key — a pet reward room, where Donut chooses the caged Mongoliensis that becomes
[Mongo](../characters/mongo.md).
