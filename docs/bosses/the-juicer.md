## Appearance & Encounter

A Troglodyte Basher fused with a bodybuilder physique — scaled, lizard-headed, grotesquely
over-muscled to the point of waddling, wearing only small black shorts. He fights by hurling
massive free weights (which ignite on impact) and grappling. Like the Hoarder, he's implied to
be a transformed human ("somebody from our world mixed up with one of those troglodytes,"
per Carl) whose consciousness is still partially present under an AI-driven caricature
personality.

Carl tanks the support Bashers and a latched-on Virtuoso (a poison-tongued creature that
clamps onto his fist and has to be smashed against equipment to kill) while Donut plinks the
Juicer down with repeated Magic Missiles. Carl also beats the Juicer with an improvised
barbell/weight weapon mid-fight — Mordecai later confirms this hit did no more damage than a bare
fist would have, since Carl's bare-knuckle skill is now on par with an unenchanted weapon. The
real finishing blow comes when the Juicer grabs Carl by the throat and is seconds from killing
him: Donut saves him by biting straight into a vein in the Juicer's neck, and the resulting blood
loss leaves him almost dead. Carl then lands one more bare-handed "weak jab" that finishes him,
followed by a stomp to the head for good measure.
