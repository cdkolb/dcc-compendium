## Encounter

Heather is Tsarina Signet's chosen "sacrifice" for her nightly blood-summoning ritual against
Grimaldi's circus — captured and put under a sleep curse rather than killed outright, since
Signet's *Ink Marauder* spell requires her to personally witness the kill, not perform it. Carl
is manipulated into the fight after Signet puts both him and Princess Donut to sleep with a
*Water Lily* curse, then wakes only Carl and forces him to fight alone as the price of Donut's
safety.

Heather is a former circus attraction — a "roller-skating bear" act — transformed by the same
cataclysm that warped the rest of Grimaldi's troupe. She stands roughly eight feet tall on her
skates, with living, worm-like growths in place of claws that burrow into flesh and speak
directly into a victim's mind ("Strong he is... he will feed our clowns well"). Carl survives her
opening attack (an unnamed spell over level 15) only by luck, severs the worm-claws with his
*Protective Shell*, then improvises: he douses her in flammable moonshine and sets her alight,
then finishes the fight with a lottery scratch-ticket that could have gone either way — a
level-15 fireball, or a healing custard splash. It rolls custard.

The healing "kill" turns out to be the real solution: curing the wounds also kills the parasitic
worms controlling her, restoring the real Heather underneath — an old, gray-muzzled circus bear,
free of the mind control for the first time since the cataclysm, and clearly asking with her eyes
to be put out of her misery. Carl obliges, ending the fight with two punches and a stomp.

## Notable Achievements
