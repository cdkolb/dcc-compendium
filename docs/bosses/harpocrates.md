# Harpocrates

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Deity — God of Silence
    - **Level:** 250
    - **Floor:** [9 ("Faction Wars")](../floors/level-09.md), roaming the open battlefield and Shanty Town (ch. 59–77)
    - **Sponsor:** None — he arrives on his own, chasing the feral god [Meatus](meatus.md)
    - **Resolution:** Not killed. **Conscripted** into the Princess Posse with a hell-kissed
      level-18 Conscription potion, used as a weapon by **Li Na**, and finally leaves the realm
      enraged after Meatus is destroyed
    - **Presence effect:** Grants Carl the **level-15 Pied Piper** skill — which
      [Eris](eris.md)'s chaos spinner then makes permanent

## Who He Is

Harpocrates is the god whose genitalia was stolen, sewn onto a gremlin, and thereby became the
separate god [Meatus](meatus.md). It is, per [Mordecai](../characters/mordecai.md), "an old story and
kind of a dumb, recurring joke" that normally plays out during the Ascendency battles rather than in
the dungeon.

He is not mute and he is not deaf — he is the **god of silence**, and "he can hear the heartbeat of a
fly from half a planet away." Sound enrages him. Mordecai's standing order the entire time he is on
the map is that Donut must not use her PA system.

Seen up close from the Nest tower, he is a kaiju: taller than the tower itself, slim, younger-looking
than expected, and otherwise near-human, in white and gold robes torn and filthy from fighting his
own penis, with the front stained red as a permanent visual reminder of what was taken from him.

Rosetta's read on him is that he is fundamentally a decent sort — "Harpocrates hasn't cast his worst
spells because he's generally a good guy. He doesn't want to level the town if he doesn't have to.
But he's still a god."

## The Chase

Harpocrates enters the realm chasing Meatus and fights him across the whole map. Donut's loud
commentary and music earn her a personal **marked-for-death** flag, and he spends much of the back
half of the floor stalking the [Nest](../locations/the-nest.md) tower. Two obstacles slow him:

- the Larracos **deflector towers**, which he pauses at but will eventually push past
- a pair of [changelings](../species/changeling.md) disguised as **ogre basilica guards**, whose
  *Untouchable* status means a god may not attack them or cross the threshold they hold. They stand
  firm and keep him
out of [Shanty Town](../locations/shanty-town.md)

When Donut's [**Rolling Battle Formation**](../spells/rolling-battle-formation.md) swallows Meatus and Eris into one enormous ball, Harpocrates
picks the ball up by the shaft and uses it as a flail — and casts a protective spell over it to keep
its health from dropping, inadvertently preserving thousands of trapped combatants.

## The Conscription

The War Mage Rebellion is the first faction to try enslaving a god, using hell-kissed Conscription
potions brewed in the Nothing by **Krakaren Prime**; their attempt on [Yarilo](yarilo.md) works, and
the casting war mage promptly explodes from the level disparity. **Li Na** loots the duplicate potion
off the dead war mage, has it copied with her brother's scroll of potion duplication, and uses the
original on Harpocrates.

!!! quote "Warlord message (Book 7, ch. 72)"
    "Harpocrates has been successfully conscripted. Harpocrates, level 250 is now a member of the
    Princess Posse."

!!! quote "Hell-Kissed Potion of Conscription, level 18 (Book 7, ch. 72)"
    "This works like a regular potion of Conscription, but it has been hell-kissed. At level 18, it
    will work against pretty much any non-affiliated entities, regardless of their protections,
    including most deities. Most. It was brewed in the dark recesses of the Nothing by Krakaren
    Prime, who is said to yearn for domination, which was why she'd been banished there in the first
    place."

Under Li Na's control he is rank Legionnaire in the White Dragons, with *Under Orders* over his head.
She rides him like a mount and lets Juice Box's changelings climb aboard as well. The god is visibly
enraged by this and fights the compulsion the whole time — "he's harder to control when I'm not near
him," she reports, and later, "I think Harpocrates is fighting the Conscription, so we need to do this
now."

## Killing Yarilo

Li Na's first order is to set Harpocrates on **[Yarilo](yarilo.md)**, the other freed feral god, who
had been conscripted onto the War Mage team. Harpocrates beats him to death with the Rolling Battle
Formation ball — a weapon larger than Yarilo himself — while Yarilo throws chunks of buildings at him
and begs: "Stop! Not my face. Anything but my face!"

!!! quote "System message (Book 7, ch. 73)"
    "A deity has fallen. And while Yarilo was damned to the Nothing, his death has angered the gods.
    The heavens tremble with rage."

## Departure

Harpocrates's last act under conscription is to toss the ball over the lip of Larracos and into the
funnel, setting up the Nest-tower endgame. When the ball breaks and Donut destroys Meatus with [*War
Crime*](../spells/war-crime.md), he watches his own stolen half get pulverised.

!!! quote "Book 7, ch. 77"
    "'Noooooooooooo!' came a deep scream, shaking the Nest. Harpocrates. The god wasn't mute after
    all. He raised his arms in the air. '**My cock! My beloved cock! You will pay for this, Champion
    of Nekhebit!**' And with that, he too, disappeared.
    *Harpocrates has left the realm.*"

Carl keeps the Pied Piper skill permanently; Donut keeps the grudge.

!!! note "Unresolved"
    The text does not narrate a formal end to the Conscription — Harpocrates simply leaves the realm
    at the moment Meatus is destroyed, threatening Donut on the way out. His threat against the
    "Champion of Nekhebit" is left open.
