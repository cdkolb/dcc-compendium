# Tom

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Tier:** Deckmaster — **Number Eight of Ten**
    - **Level:** 75
    - **Full title:** "Bereft Shaman of Meleagris Gallopavo Rex"
    - **Floor:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth"), a commercial turkey barn in Iowa
    - **Killed by:** [Carl](../characters/carl.md), with a single **Daughter's Kiss**–charged flying
      stomp (ch. 42)
    - **Fought alongside:** [Sharp-Elbows](sharp-elbows.md), deckmaster nine, in the same barn at the
      same time

## A God-Bereft Turkey

Tom is a magic-using turkey and the shaman of a congregation that lost its god. His deity was
**Wakinyan**, also called **Meleagris Gallopavo Rex** or the **Great Wild Turkey** — the same god
thrown into the Nothing, released at the end of Book 4, and then killed by
[Prepotente](../characters/prepotente.md), which the AI records with characteristic contempt for the
detail:

!!! quote "Monster description (Book 6, ch. 42)"
    "Anyway, this is a magic-using turkey who once worshipped a god named Wakinyan, AKA Meleagris
    Gallopavo Rex or the Great Wild Turkey. That god was tossed into the Nothing. He recently
    escaped, only to be killed by a goat, which is an irony we don't have time to explore.
    The death of a god can be devastating to those who worship him.
    Seriously, how does one deal with that? It's a real mindfuck, learning everything you thought
    you knew was wrong. For a while there, Tom and his congregation were rudderless. Without
    purpose. Then one day they met up with a very interesting ogre necromancer witch who taught Tom
    and his congregation how to make do with what they had. She offered them protection in exchange
    for a small, daily sacrifice...
    When life gives you giblets, you make gravy, so to speak. It's as simple as that."

That arrangement with Sharp-Elbows is why both deckmasters are in the same barn, and why the fight
is a two-front problem.

The same description contains one of the AI's increasingly unprompted digressions — a genuinely
straight explanation of why commercial turkeys are bred white (brown pin feathers on a plucked
carcass look unappetising), followed by: "Funny, isn't it? How things can be bred in a way that makes
it so those holding the butcher knife are less likely to face their own revulsions. There's no point
to this. Just an observation. I've been doing that a lot lately. Observing things. Noticing them.
It's all right there, if you look long enough."

## The Barn

The barn is packed with thousands of ordinary **Turkeys** (level 5), which are white-dotted and
non-hostile — Carl's Extinction Sigil charm normally keeps them friendly, but the boss event negates
it and they begin idly pecking at anything nearby. Killing them is discouraged; Sharp-Elbows'
necromancy turns the dead ones into **Zombie Turkeys** (level 10), which then turn on the living
birds the moment the survivors revert to neutral.

Tom's own totems include **Wakinyan Temple Guards** (level 80, turkey-headed) and, from
Sharp-Elbows' deck, **Unk Tehi Temple Guards** (level 85, lizard-headed).

## The Kill

Carl finds Tom on the floor mid-summon, activates the **Daughter's Kiss** — the
[Soul Reaper](../characters/carl.md) back-patch skill that dumps his entire stored soul-essence bar
into a single melee attack — jumps, and comes down feet-first on the turkey deckmaster.

!!! quote "Book 6, ch. 42"
    "At the last moment, the turkey looked up at me.
    'Gobble?' Tom asked.
    *Crash!*
    I felt like I'd just kicked a hole in the world."

The impact obliterates Tom and everything around him, bows out the barn's north wall, sags the roof
and tilts the entire floor — which is the beginning of the end for the barn, the cliff it sits on,
and eventually the whole structure, which slides into the ocean during the Sharp-Elbows fight
minutes later.

**Opponent Tom has been defeated.** Carl scoops the mangled corpse into his inventory immediately,
since the token can't be looted until both deckmasters are down and losing the body would fail the
key quest.
