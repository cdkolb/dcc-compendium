# Shi Maria, the Bedlam Bride

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Boss tier:** City Boss
    - **Level:** 140
    - **Floor:** 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth"), a web-covered swamp in the Cuban interior
    - **Full title:** "The Bedlam Bride. Reaper Spider Minion." — a **demi-god**
    - **Aliases:** The Screamer, The Reviled, Kwaku's Love, The Corrupted Paramour, The Widow
      Endless, the Bedlam Bride; she prefers **MaeMae** among friends
    - **Resolution:** Not defeated in combat. Flagged into a T'Ghee card by mutual agreement
      (ch. 26–27), used as the party's strongest totem for the back half of the book, and ripped by
      [Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md) at the climax (ch. 72) — after she had
      already tattooed herself permanently into Carl's chest
    - **Stated weakness:** Psionic attacks. That is the entire list

## The Title Boss

Shi Maria is the book's namesake. Samantha finds her first — "I FELT HER PRESENCE. SHE'S A DEMI-GOD.
PROBABLY NEVER EVEN GOT AN INVITE TO A PARTY" — and befriends her on the strength of a shared
grievance: both are looking for a missing husband.

!!! quote "Monster description (Book 6, ch. 26)"
    "Reaper Spider minions are regular mobs one may find on the fifteenth floor, working as minions
    of something kinda fucked up we're not ready to talk about yet. They have celestial blood...
    But not all of them are merchants. Some have escaped out into the lower levels and have found
    their own path. Some have grown in power beyond that of a regular Reaper Spider Minion. This
    particular one is special. She has had many names. She currently goes by Shi Maria, and her
    story is long and tragic and can't possibly be fully told here. Once upon a time, she found
    herself married to a god. It didn't go so well. It's said she killed him... The thing is, she
    doesn't remember actually killing him. Honestly, it's not really important. What is important is
    that she was kicked out of the celestial realm and banished to this swamp where she spends her
    days plotting on how to get revenge on everyone and everything who has ever done her wrong.
    That list is very, very long."

The Bahamas book entry adds the part she disputes: "The problem is, legend has it she killed her
husband. That she ate him right up. She was banished for the crime. She insists she is blameless,
but sometimes, during her more lucid moments, she's not so certain of her own innocence. **Beware
her lies.**"

She appears as a giant spider with an almost-human head on a long, putty-like neck that rotates
freely, four bulbous eyes, flaking skin and greasy black hair, with long hair-covered fangs. Legs
long enough to skewer a crawler from a treetop.

## Abilities

- **Permanent Insanity** and **Permanent Blindness** — level-15 *Unending Insanity* and *Lights Out*,
  neither curable without divine intervention. Mordecai's *Liquid Therapy* protection potion, taken
  once a day, is the only reason the party survives the meeting
- **The fifth eye** — an extra eye that opens in the centre of her head, "black and white and yellow
  and red all at once," which blinds and maddens. Katia's people have a proverb about it: *Never
  stare into the blinding eye of the Bedlam Bride* — meaning don't become so obsessed with one thing
  that you go blind to everything else
- **Spinneret** and **Venomous Bite**
- **Cockroach** (level 15) — a full heal on what would be a fatal blow. As a card, it works
  differently: when she "dies" she is returned to Donut's **deck** rather than the discard pile.
  Carl's note is that in practice "she never died"
- **Weakness: psionic attacks.** Donut's Floor 8 class is **Master Telephone Psychic**, a psionic
  class — the book does not spell out the connection, but it is the only listed vulnerability she has

## The Bargain

Rather than fight her, Carl negotiates. She wants to reach Larracos, where she believes a master
pulpmancer can free her from a card prison and help her find her husband; Carl wants her firepower.
Both know the other is probably lying.

!!! quote "Shi Maria, Book 6, ch. 27"
    "That fool Samantha says you keep your promises. I could devour you now, but if I am to leave
    this horrible place, I must trust in you. I must find my husband and prove I didn't kill him. In
    order to do that, I must first get to Larracos. I have no quarrel with you. Once the city is
    reached, I will tell you how to undo the card magic, and you can set me free. In the meantime, I
    will help you."

Carl already knows the pulpmancer's shop is in the flooded lower levels of Larracos and the mage is
almost certainly dead. He does not mention it. **Quest: The Bedlam Bride** is simply "Get her crazy
ass to the city of Larracos. That's it."

## As a Card

Shi Maria is the party's strongest totem by a wide margin — she appears, assesses, and clears the
field, often in seconds. She also demands to see Donut's hand and suggests buffs. She has a sadistic
streak: she keeps opponents alive to eat them in pieces, and she plays with gorgons. She dislikes
Raul the crab (she once used him as a club to beat a group of kappas to death) and is jealous of
Lazarus-A-Bang-Bang's entrance sequence.

She is unusually effective against the [Reminiscence Hydra](reminiscence-hydra.md), ripping heads
off the whole one by one until three Monobrow Sam death rays bring her down — at which point
*Cockroach* returns her to the deck.

## The Tattoo and the Rip

At the climax in the burning FOCSA building, Donut plays a card that **temporarily combines Carl
with the totem** — Carl from the waist up, spider from the waist down, and Shi Maria in full control
of both. She spends the 180-second timer looting his inventory, casting *Self-Immolation* (injuring
Mongo and Katia and dropping him through the floor), stabbing out and eating
[Li Jun](../characters/li-jun.md)'s eye for ink, brewing that eye into a beaker with toraline root,
and stick-and-poke tattooing an eye into the centre of Carl's chest at superhuman speed.

!!! quote "Shi Maria, Book 6, ch. 71"
    "You have so much power in your hands, Carl, and you don't even know what you have. That's okay.
    Today, you live. If you won't bring this card to the next floor, I will have to attach myself to
    something you can't discard so easily...
    But I am with you now, Carl, until you decide to free me. I will no longer be a card you can
    abandon, one you can rip away. I am a part of you, and you will have to decide. Keep me with you
    and gain my power, or set me free. Either way, I win."

A second, male voice argues with her throughout — one she insists isn't real and that she didn't put
there. It claims otherwise: "You did. You killed me, and now I am here." Its last words to Carl are
"Don't trust her, boy. Get her out of you as soon as you can. Don't ever trust her."

The result is the tattoo **The Eye of the Bedlam Bride**, with no description text of any kind. It
is invisible while the eye is closed and feels like a bruise. Only Li Na, Li Jun and Imani see it.

Donut rips the Shi Maria card the moment the battle ends — "She's not going to hurt Mongo ever
again" — which is exactly the outcome Shi Maria planned around.
