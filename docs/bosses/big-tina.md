## A Recital, Interrupted

Long before Scolopendra's nine-tier attack, Tina was an eight-year-old Ursine girl — daughter of a
Floor 6 village's head cleric — training in secret for a forbidden dance recital, since her
father's strict, dancing-and-singing-banned religious order considered ballet sinful. On the night
of the performance, her father and a group of fellow clerics stormed the recital just as Tina was
about to take the stage, whipping her mother nearly to death in front of the audience and shoving
Tina aside hard enough to break the magic sparkle-wand she'd been given for the show. Before the
clerics could punish the rest of the children involved, Scolopendra's attack struck the whole
world at once, transmuting nearly every female Ursine (including Tina and her mother, Kiwi) into
dinosaurs. Tina's mind never recovered from the trauma; she became **Big Tina**, an enormous,
deranged, permanently-enraged Allosaurus who spends her unnaturally long life (per her monster
card, she's the "Immortalized" only child ever turned this way) hunting and slaughtering Ursine
wherever she finds them.

Kiwi and the surviving mothers from that night — now the pack of ordinary Mongoliensis raptors led
by Kiwi herself — have spent centuries quietly following Tina around, trying and mostly failing
to contain her rampages.

## "The Recital" Quest

Carl and Donut first learn Tina's identity from **Prudence**, a captured Ursine barmaid, after
Tina's attacks escalate into full village massacres feeding a separate, unrelated crisis (see
[The Creeping Apocalypse](../mechanics/index.md#the-creeping-apocalypse-odious-creepers-book-5)).
The system's own quest text is blunt about the intended solution: *"Kill her. Or give her what she
wants."* Donut correctly identifies the reference (*Footloose*, not *Rambo*, in her words) — Tina
doesn't want to be killed, she wants to finish the recital her father interrupted, and she wants
her mother back, whom her ruined mind no longer recognizes even though Kiwi has never once left
her side.

Complicating things further, a vampire-hunter elite's Floor 6 storyline (see [Sierra](sierra.md))
briefly infects Tina with vampirism after Miriam Dom fights her; Tina is cured along with every
other victim the moment Miriam sacrifices herself, but the rampage and the reanimated-body-part
crisis it kicks off is already underway by then.

## The Performance

Donut books Tina — under a false pretense that it's just a dinosaur act — into the pet/talent
double bill at the [Butcher's Masquerade](../mechanics/index.md#the-butchers-masquerade-book-5),
using a recharge scroll to restore the last charge in Tina's original sparkle wand. On stage, the
wand casts a genuine charm spell, ***The Recital***, layering real, uncomplicated joy over
everyone watching as Tina finally performs her interrupted "Water Ballad" alongside a chorus line
of thirty-plus raptors and Mongo, all in hand-sewn sea-creature costumes.

Mid-battle during the Masquerade's climactic ambush, Donut has Kiwi swallow a smuggled dose of the
**I Take it All Back** potion (a benefactor-box item explicitly labeled *"Give this to Princess
Donut... Do NOT give to Tina"*), which strips Scolopendra's Transmutation curse — turning Kiwi back
into a very pregnant Ursine woman on the spot. Tina, still in dinosaur form, sees the
transformation and finally recognizes her mother, roaring the word "Mommy" for the first time in
centuries. **Quest Complete: The Recital.** Tina herself is never cured or killed — she remains an
Allosaurus, last seen transported away with the rest of the Butcher's Masquerade battlefield when
the ruined High Elf castle teleports to Floor 9.
