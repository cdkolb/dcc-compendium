## An Unwilling Marriage

Mrs. Ghazi began as an unnamed familiar sent by the banished lesser deity
[Psamathe](../characters/samantha.md) to accompany the human mage **Ghazi**, who had come to the
Necropolis of Anser to summon what he believed was the banished god of lust, Yarilo, in hopes of
bringing his sex-doll companion Lika to life. Psamathe tricked him instead, and the botched
summoning crystalized his entire castle — turning most of it, and Ghazi himself outside the
sandstorm hours, to glass and sand. The only part of the deal that "worked" was that Ghazi was
made to marry the sand familiar during the ritual; ever since, it takes the form of his sand
"wife," burying and consuming him daily as its own twisted expression of affection.

Per Ghazi's own testimony, the ooze can't be permanently destroyed by conventional means — fire,
cold, electricity, or dilution only shrink it, since as long as Psamathe's second half (housed at
the time inside the crystalized Lika doll's decapitated head) remains anywhere in the world, a
single grain of sand is enough for her familiar to regenerate.

## The Fight

When Carl, Donut, and Katia stake out Ghazi's basement lab, the ooze pours in through the door
before they can trigger a formal boss card, forcing an improvised approach: Carl uses his new
[Sticky Feet](../characters/carl.md#book-4-the-gate-of-the-feral-gods) ability to walk the ceiling
upside-down while Katia distracts the ooze from the stairwell with a bass-heavy alarm trap
("Jump Around"), letting Carl reach Ghazi's recliner — which sits directly over the ooze's
yolk-like core — undetected. He douses the chair in Mordecai's homemade incendiary gel and sets it
alight; the ooze reflexively yanks the burning chair straight up into the ceiling, spreading the
fire directly onto its own core and dropping its health bar for the first time.

The improvised plan backfires structurally: the heat from the gel collapses the basement ceiling
moments before the kill lands, burying Carl and Donut and forcing Katia to kill Ghazi himself
(mistakenly assumed necessary to undo the castle's crystallization) while the two dig themselves
free. The castle's own destruction then washes the survivors straight down into the Water
Quadrant through the necropolis's flooded interior.

## Aftermath

- A notification confirms "the boss was defeated" and the Land Quadrant's throne room opens, but
  the text is explicit that the ooze itself "wasn't fully 'defeated'" — no boss box is ever
  awarded, and Mordecai suspects it survived as a single grain of sand somewhere in the ocean,
  unable to properly regenerate
- Ghazi's letter to his former colleague Tish, looted alongside his notes on the
  [Gate of the Feral Gods](../items/gate-of-the-feral-gods.md), confirms Psamathe's full backstory
  and the winding box's mechanics — see [Samantha](../characters/samantha.md) for what happens
  once her other half is later found
- The decapitated, crystalized head of the Lika sex doll — Psamathe's other half — survives the
  collapse in Carl's inventory and reawakens later once Gwen's team breaks the castle's
  crystallization spell for good
