## Special Event Format

Lusca is broadcast as a live "Special Event" boss battle — a beta-tested format Carl and his
party had never encountered before, complete with two announcer hosts (a lizard newscaster named
Kevin and an orc sportscaster, "Magnificent Troy") providing real-time commentary over the fight's
own music, cartoon graphics explaining her mouthbrooding mechanic, and a survey requested from
participants afterward. She's described as a real (non-invented) creature — an "Octo-Shark," not
literally a "Sharktopus" — the size of a small aircraft carrier, and her card leans hard into
"single mother" jokes about needing to feed her enormous brood of juveniles.

## The Fight

Lusca surfaces after a Henrik-led changeling expedition disturbs the *Akula*'s wreck, dragging the
sub — and Carl, Katia, and fellow crawlers Tran and Vadim inside it — down to the sea floor before
swallowing the whole vessel. Her mouthbrooding mechanic means her juveniles (Level 30 **Juvenile
Octo-Sharks**) swarm anyone trapped with the sub rather than the mother herself attacking directly
during the ten-minute digestion window. Carl breaks the stalemate early by planting a
duct-taped-together makeshift bomb loaded with a potion of Bloodlust inside the sub's minifridge —
when it detonates and the potion disperses through the swallowed water, the surviving juveniles go
berserk and slaughter each other, thinning their own numbers to almost nothing before the party
even has to fight their way out.

Escaping through Lusca's mouth toward her gill slits, Carl casts *Protective Shell* at point-blank
range as Lusca swallows, and the static sphere punches clean through her skull and jaw from the
inside — she stops dead in the water, jaw peeled open and shattered. Katia, mid-escape via a
pressurized steam-rocket boost, deploys the massive buzzsaw looted from [Ruckus](ruckus.md) as an
improvised melee weapon (nicknamed "Leatherface"); the trio pinwheel out of control and the
spinning buzzsaw carves straight through Lusca's already-wounded head, finishing the kill.

## Aftermath

- Lusca's death frees the Water Quadrant's remaining survivor, Vadim, to attempt an escape-pod
  launch — he's killed moments later by a Pain Amplifier Jelly that gets sucked into the pod with
  him
- Carl and Katia both receive a Gold Boss Box each, plus an unusually large stack of achievement
  boxes for the depth reached (over 1,000 meters below the surface) and for the special-event
  participation itself
- Katia's newly-acquired backpack upgrade (from her own Gold boss box) becomes her main mass-
  storage tool for the rest of the floor
