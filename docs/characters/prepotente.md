# Prepotente

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Race:** Caprid
    - **Class:** Forsaken Aerialist → **Profane Vitiate** (Book 5, Floor 6 specialization)
    - **Level:** 27 (Book 2) → 34 (Book 3) → 57 → **71** (end of Book 5) → **#1 on the leaderboard**
      (Book 6) → **99 → 100** (Book 7). At level 99 he is "by far—the strongest crawler in the
      dungeon," and stays that way until [Donut](princess-donut.md) passes him late in Book 7
    - **Sapience:** Confirmed in Book 3 — one of only two Earth-origin creatures (alongside [Princess Donut](princess-donut.md)) known to have gained true human-level sapience
    - **Party:** Solo, alongside his familiar [Miriam Dom](miriam-dom.md) — deceased as of Book 5;
      briefly parties with Carl and Donut at the very end of Book 5, for convenience, then leaves
    - **Affiliation (Book 7):** **Colonel in the Princess Posse** — he asks Carl to recruit him in
      chapter 48, ending six books of operating alone
    - **Notable items:** [Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering](../items/enchanted-night-wyrms-ring-of-divine-suffering.md)
      (Book 5, a second copy of Carl's — swallowed whole after Miriam Dom's death);
      **"Memorial Crystal: Apito"** (Book 7, acquired off-page from the crawler Osvaldo); a complete
      memorised interior map of [Club Vanquisher](../locations/club-vanquisher.md)

## Overview

Prepotente is, as of Book 2, a complete mystery — a name and a rank with no attached scene,
backstory, or physical description anywhere in the text. Prepotente appears exactly once: as an
entry on the tentative Floor 3 leaderboard Odette reveals near the end of the book. Even Carl,
reading down the list in real time, has no idea who this is:

> "Who is Prepotente?" I asked, reading down the list. I knew Florin was the crocodile-headed
> dude with the shotgun. Miriam Dom was the goat lady. I didn't know Ifechi.

Nobody answers him. Unlike Florin, Miriam Dom, or Li Jun — all of whom Carl can immediately
place from prior broadcast appearances — Prepotente gets no follow-up identification at all in
Book 2.

## Leaderboard

Ranked 3rd, at Level 27 with a bounty of 400,000 — see

## Book 3: The Mystery Solved

Prepotente turns out to be exactly what his class name suggests: a former goat belonging to
[Miriam Dom](miriam-dom.md), transformed into a sapient, aerialist crawler by the same
Enhanced Pet Biscuit mechanic that turned Donut into a person. Carl meets him directly on Floor 4,
mid-battle, through a portal connecting their two locations — Prepotente is condescending,
theatrical, prone to shrieking mid-sentence for no apparent reason, and openly proud of being one
of only two Earth creatures (himself and Donut) to have achieved true sapience. He was in the
middle of a slow, days-long assassination of a province boss (via cardiac arrest, entranced) when
Carl's group accidentally teleports the target away, souring the encounter considerably. He
threatens to kill several people over the course of the conversation, none of it apparently
sincere; Miriam Dom manages him with a shepherd's crook and casual affection, whacking him with it
when he's rude and kissing him on the head as a reward for good behavior. He expresses a genuine,
long-standing wish to share a drink with Donut and discuss their shared, singular circumstances.

## Book 5: Mother's Death and a Vow of Extinction

Prepotente and Miriam Dom are hunted across Floor 6 by an elite vampire hunter, Viscount Fog, and
his cursed mount. A botched scroll of *Community Pool* — meant to transfer Miriam's new vampirism
curse onto Fog — backfires when Fog's counter-ring bounces the spell back and forth between them,
leaving Prepotente Stunned and then Fragile (one hit point, unhealable) at the exact moment his own
newly-acquired [Ring of Divine Suffering](../items/enchanted-night-wyrms-ring-of-divine-suffering.md)
activates on its own and marks Miriam — the only eligible target in range — leaving Prepotente
himself afflicted with **Left to Fester**, unable to heal until his own mark, his mother, dies.
Fog dies of his own reflected curse at sunset; Prepotente is left paralyzed, aware, and unable to
move or speak while his mother spends the night defending his body from infected wildlife, then
asks Carl and Donut to watch over them both until dawn.

At sunrise, Miriam Dom burns to death by choice — Carl having secretly marked her to break the
curse — and Prepotente, freed the moment she dies, collects every item she'd left behind, then takes his
own Ring of Divine Suffering off, picks it back up off the ground, and **eats it whole** rather
than continuing to wear or loot it normally. He
declares, screaming to the sky, that he intends to kill everyone even remotely responsible for her
death "or perish trying," then mounts his mother's now-flight-capable Hellspawn familiar, Bianca
Del Ciao, and spends the rest of the floor grinding levels almost exclusively by killing the
world-quest "Odious Creeper" undead monsters Miriam's death unleashed — racking up ten solo kills,
gaining ten levels and roughly eighty stat points in a single day, funded by an emergency
benefactor box from his sponsor, **the Plenty**.

### The Butcher's Masquerade

At the masquerade, Prepotente corners Carl and insists on temporarily joining his and Donut's
party — not out of any interest in the changeling-evacuation plot happening around him, but purely
to get his hands on a pair of sponsor-gifted items in Donut's possession, an **Enchanted Hammer of
Fast Forward** and an **Enchanted Stick of Cascadia's Screams** (both benefactor-box items whose
notes specified they be given to Donut). He'd also used his new wealth to purchase a **Seventh
Floor Pole Position** boon at the prize counter, and separately deduces (correctly) that Floor 7 is
built around the fairy tale of a boy trapped in an inescapable, resurrection-looped maze —
intelligence he pieces together from an overheard interview and from the unusually long item
description on his mother's looted boots, which he refuses to fully explain to Carl. At the
battle's climax, he personally delivers the killing blow to Queen Imogen, smashing a potion into
her face moments after Carl's disco-ball attack disorients her — and pursues his own quest for the **Memorial Crystal: Apito**
(its exact nature undisclosed to Carl) — which turns out to double as a lead in Carl's own,
separate, preexisting Emberus quest to find whoever killed a figure named Geyrun. Neither thread
gets resolved: the crystal is looted by a third crawler, Osvaldo, before either Prepotente or Carl
can claim it. He briefly, grudgingly parties with Carl and Donut as Floor 7 begins (strictly for
Donut's help, per him — "Carl is not necessary") just long enough to borrow the hammer and stick
back from her, then leaves the party and uses them, together with the knowledge from his mother's
boots, to shatter the level's opening puzzle chamber outright — ending the floor's intended maze
mechanic for every crawler simultaneously, to the AI's audible fury ("Godsdamnit, Prepotente.
Don't do it. Please. I worked so hard to make this floor happen.").

## Book 6: The Stairwell Theory, and a Grudging Pardon

Prepotente spends Floor 8 alone in the Bahamas with Bianca, sitting at **#1** on the leaderboard,
lonely enough that he keeps asking Carl to read him the region's lore book.

### "A Carl and Pony Theory"

Carl works out that a hell portal could be made to scour the entire planet, and that a crawler
standing directly beneath an enclosed stairwell chamber would be carried up with it and could strike
the stairwell on the way. He then takes the idea to the smartest crawler he knows to have it broken:

> "Actually, you're the smartest guy I know. Let me run something by you really fast. Here's the
> thing. I don't want it to be local. I want it to spread over the whole world. **I need ideas.**"

Carl credits the finished plan jointly — "It's a theory. It's a **Carl and Pony theory**, though, if
that means anything" — and it is Prepotente's endorsement that gets the rest of the dungeon to
actually risk their lives on it:

> "Everyone. As much as it pains me to say this, **I believe Carl is correct.** We have seen gated
> stairwell encasements break before. I once watched Lucia Mar attempt to teleport a stairwell to
> another location, and I witnessed what happened to both the stairwell itself and the surrounding
> structure. This portal is of a particular type, and I believe Carl's theory is sound. I'm certainly
> not going to risk it myself. I am going to make my way down the stairwell using my key in just a
> moment, but **I am reasonably certain there's at least a fifty percent chance this will work. Maybe
> forty percent.**"

He then revises it downward twice more within the hour: "Remember, I amended it to 40 percent, Carl…
So maybe I should have said 35 percent."

He also warns Carl to read the quest's winning conditions carefully, because the crawlers in
Indonesia — the landmass furthest from Havana — will be the ones who die if the demon isn't buffed
enough.

### The Forgiveness

His actual goodbye is warm, by his standards:

> "For what it's worth, Carl. I do hope you and Princess Donut survive this foolish endeavor. You are
> an oafish brute, and it's a miracle you have made it this far, but the dungeon would not be the
> same without you."

And then, as an afterthought, he lets Carl off for [Miriam Dom](miriam-dom.md):

> **Prepotente: I've decided to not kill you for letting my mother hurt herself. I have been
> contemplating it for some time now. I've decided you were too stupid to have done anything about it
> anyway. Your penance will be to assist me in exacting my revenge on the true culprits.**

The running argument is intact to the end: "How many times must I tell you not to call me by that
name?"

## Book 7: This Inevitable Ruin

For most of Book 7 Prepotente is simply the strongest thing on the floor. He enters at **level 99**,
unaffiliated, hiding his position from everyone, riding a **Bianca Del Ciao** who has grown to level
70 and full size with a second set of wings. He announces his intentions in chapter 18 and then goes
and does exactly what he said:

> Prepotente: …Once the festivities begin, I have a very specific score to settle with the lead
> producer of the Blood Hunter program, and after that is complete, only then will I come to your aid
> and join in your military. If you’re still alive.

### He kills the Blood Sultanate's army by himself

The naga — the **Blood Sultanate** — own *Blood Hunter*, the program that sent Viscount Fog after his
mother. The Posse spends the opening of Open Hostilities unable to work out why the naga seem to have
no troops. The answer, found by Elle and Edgar scanning the stronghold, is that the barracks are full
of corpses.

> They were dead. The Blood Sultanate was almost defeated already, and they were trying to hide it.

Elle's report when she runs into him is the first anyone hears of it:

> Elle: …the moment we moved into position that goddamned goat friend of yours appeared out of
> nowhere and went absolutely apeshit on us. He's the one who took out most of their army! I don’t
> know how he did it, especially without magic, but that goddamned thing is level 99 now.

He did it alone, and he did it before **Operation: Snake Pit** — Carl's own plan to kill the naga
line of succession — could reach them. Carl only learns of it after Snake Pit has already launched,
which means the entire operation was aimed at an enemy that no longer had an army. The method is
never explained: "Prepotente did something—the details of which were still unclear—that killed off a
huge chunk of their army."

!!! note "Unresolved"
    Carl asks him directly how he did it and never gets an answer. The book never returns to it.

He did not get to finish. The naga called foul, claiming he was an unofficial agent of the Princess
Posse. He won the hearing, then threatened the entire quorum — including Baroness Victory — and was
put in penalty stasis. His furious message to Carl, delivered in chat, is his best rant in the series:

> Prepotente: Carl, you idiotic, bumbling, feckless imbecile. You cad. You fool. You ruiner… I have
> already defeated their standing army, and just as I was about to finish off my vengeance, I was
> stopped because they claimed I am unofficially part of YOUR silly fighting force… SHE WAS MY
> MOTHER, AND IT IS MY RIGHT TO EXACT VENGEANCE AGAINST THE NAGA… THERE WILL BE NO MORE WARM HUGS
> FROM ME. YOU ARE BANNED! SO HELP ME IF SHE GETS AWAY, I WILL HOLD YOU ACCOUNTABLE. I SPIT! I SPIT
> IN YOUR EYE!

Carl's read is that the naga complaint accidentally saved everyone: Prepotente did not know the
Sultana could vanish at will, and had he pressed on, he would have spooked her into hiding and damned
both Donut and Katia.

### Joining the Princess Posse

Chapter 48 is the status change. Adjudication rules mean Carl cannot so much as coordinate with an
unaffiliated crawler, and Prepotente has worked that out before Carl opens his mouth.

> I held up a hand at Prepotente. “Look. We can’t talk at all unless you’re in the army. I know you
> don’t want...”
>
> “I know, Carl. I am not an imbecile. Recruit me.”
>
> I paused. “Are you sure?”
>
> “Do it now before I change my mind. If this is the only way I may salvage this utter fuckup on your
> end, then make it so.”

Carl had a potion of **Conscription** in his other hand, behind his back, and stows it — a small
private moment about how close he came to doing this the ugly way. He makes Prepotente a **Colonel**
instead of the Legionnaire his first instinct suggested.

What follows is not what anyone in the clearing expected.

> The goat rushed forward and pulled me into a tight hug. He started to uncontrollably sob. “You’re
> my best friend, Carl. I’ve been so lonely. When you didn’t show up at the Bahamas on the last
> floor, I thought...” He didn’t finish the sentence. He was letting out these huge, wet, snot-filled
> sobs. It had come so suddenly and unexpectedly, and he was clutching onto me like I was a life
> raft. Everyone around us just stopped what they were doing to look at us. Even Bianca appeared
> embarrassed.

> He continued to sob, and he spoke between the gasps. “Let us put our differences aside... I’m ready
> for the emptiness to be filled. I have several enhanced pet biscuits ... but after what happened
> last time, I’ve been so afraid... And now I’m afraid it’s too late. Bianca is fine company, but she
> doesn’t speak. You are absolutely right. ... We need to keep communicating. I need to come to terms
> with you being an idiot and be grateful for what I have. ... You can’t help how stupid you are.”

And then, the moment Carl relaxes:

> You’d think I’d be ready for what happened next by now, but it still caught me off guard.
>
> He screamed right in my damn face.

### The Club Vanquisher map

On the walk to the Club Vanquisher raid, he mentions in passing that he has the layout of the entire
place.

> I stopped dead, my hand on the door to the temple. I turned to look at Prepotente, incredulous.
> “You have an interior map of Club Vanquisher?”
>
> “Oh, yes. My mother, Bianca, and I made friends with a delightful guildmaster inside the club, and
> she gave us the map. We used a secret exit, and we came out of a bathroom stall inside of the
> entrance temple.”

> He tapped the side of his head. “It’s all right here. Perfect recall, Carl.”

Carl's reaction is regret rather than relief: "That would’ve been really useful. I was once again
reminded of Donut’s advice long ago that I should’ve tried harder to speak with him."

### Miriam Dom, vampire, and the ban

The **Milk** interlude supplies the other half of that story, and confirms something Book 5 left
implicit. Milk, the xenopus cartography guildmaster, is woken by screaming outside her hidden guild
and examines the two crawlers who burst in:

> The older, female crawler was originally a human, but her race was **Vampire**, which wasn’t a race
> someone could voluntarily choose. That meant it was likely a recent thing, and upon noticing this,
> they had likely just told her that her access to Club Vanquisher was being revoked. Her child, who’d
> apparently taken a caprid race, was upset about this.

"Upset" is Prepotente melting the mantaur guards with Bianca while [Miriam Dom](miriam-dom.md) tries
to drag him out — "Prepotente Lorenzo De La Rosa! Stop that this instant!" Milk hands them the map,
and the secret exits, under the fig leaf of being threatened into it. It is also where Miriam's
kindness to an NPC — healing Milk's burned arm with her own salve, for nothing — starts the chain of
events that eventually brings Milk into Faction Wars.

### The crystal

Carl's standing **Emberus** quest and Prepotente's own private quest have been chasing the same object
since Book 5. In Book 7 it surfaces in the hands of the crawler **Osvaldo**, a holdout hiding in the
woods with a group called Team Flamengo, and it starts glowing hard enough to light up the sky in the
middle of Carl's raid on the Madness castle. Neither Carl nor Prepotente can go for it at the time.

Then it simply changes hands:

> Memorial Crystal: Apito has fallen from Crawler Osvaldo’s hands and is now in possession of Crawler
> Prepotente. Retrieve it from him before he cracks the secrets and gives them to the Epicure.

> I had no idea how the memorial crystal had gotten from Osvaldo to Prepotente without a fight.

!!! note "Unresolved"
    The transfer happens entirely off-page. Carl decides to sit Prepotente down and explain the whole
    Emberus business when things calm down, expecting him to be "an ass about it," and never gets the
    chance inside Book 7. The wider Emberus/Apito material is a Mechanics topic; what matters here is
    that the crystal is now Prepotente's and that it is the live thread in [Carl](carl.md)'s quest.

### Around the floor

- He rides in to shield Carl's whole force against the Madness's mass psionic attack outside their
  castle — "I will be to you in moments, Carl, and I will shield you and the rest of the force" —
  and warns of a third enemy column coming up from under the castle.
- He drinks soda pop before battle — Big Red, sarsaparilla, Diet 7-Up, "I can only drink up to five
  soda pops a day. It’s a hard rule my mother gave me. She says... she said if I drink too many, I
  get hyper." The tense correction is the whole character in one line.
- He plays the prisoner to get past the Club Vanquisher door guard, throwing himself on the floor in
  fuzzy unlocked handcuffs: "I have been caught! My goose has been cooked! Oh woe! Oh woe!"
- Before the temple assault he mutters a litany of names to himself — Miriam, Bianca, several others,
  ending on "Ragazzaccia," which Carl does not recognise.
- He is level **100** by the end, and no longer the strongest crawler in the dungeon: Donut passes him
  after *War Crime*, and Carl notes he is "getting left behind" by Donut, Li Na and Prepotente alike.

## Book 8: A Parade of Horribles

Floor 10 gives Prepotente the book's biggest emotional arc outside of Carl and Donut themselves —
a temptation he talks himself out of, a devastating vision he's talked out of, a surprise musical
triumph, a stolen relic, and a rescue that costs two other people their lives.

### The Paramour Biscuit

Early in Heat 2, Prepotente shows Carl and Donut a celestial-box **Enriched Pet Biscuit** called
**"The Paramour"** and asks for advice — the only time in the series he's shown asking anyone for
help with a personal decision. Feeding it to a pet grants full sapience and transformation *plus* an
incurable charm that makes the pet fall hopelessly in love with whoever fed it to them. He wants to
use it on **Ragazzaccia**, a goat who was his girlfriend before the dungeon began, whom he still has
in a pet carrier:

> "So . . ." Prepotente said. "Her name is Ragazzaccia, and before the dungeon began, she was—"
>
> "Absolutely not," Donut said. "You will not be turning anyone into a sex slave, Prepotente. How is
> this even a conversation? My goodness."

Donut talks him down entirely on her own authority, walking him through what the biscuit would
actually do to Ragazzaccia (make her a level-1 crawler, exposed to real danger, permanently
compelled to love him rather than choosing to) rather than simply forbidding it:

> "Nobody will like you if you use that pet biscuit on *anything*. Can you imagine if that's what
> had happened to me? Do you really think I could be a proper girlfriend for Carl? It's a crime
> against nature. Don't use it. Ever. Ever, ever, ever."

Prepotente accepts it, and admits the loneliness underneath the request: "I don't have anybody to
ask these things. Sometimes I imagine my mother and me having these conversations... but I don't
have that." Donut agrees to talk to him daily going forward. This is the same biscuit family later
used, deliberately, on [Penelope](penelope.md) — and, by accident, on **Scolopendra** herself (see
[Princess Donut](princess-donut.md#book-8-notable-events)).

### The Vision of His Mother

During the Heat 6 apartment-building sequence — a floor-wide, unauthorized AI event called **"Ad
Infinitum,"** in which the unstable system AI generates "what-if" simulations of crawlers' loved
ones — Prepotente freezes inside an apartment showing his mother, [Miriam Dom](miriam-dom.md), as a
professional concert pianist who never stayed home to raise him:

> "This is my mother if she had never stayed to take care of her parents," Prepotente said... "She
> would've become a professional concert pianist. She wouldn't ever have known me... She would've
> had everything she had ever dreamed of."

He reads the vision as proof he doesn't belong anywhere — "Nobody wants me around... I don't fit in
anywhere... I wish I'd never taken the pet biscuit" — and comes close to giving up entirely.
**Carl**, not Donut, is the one who talks him back from it in person, arguing that the vision is a
toxic hypothetical and that the real Miriam had him, not loneliness:

> "This what-if bullshit is insidious. It's like a goddamned toxic virus on your brain... Look,
> buddy. We don't have time. I'm sorry if we didn't get to see you sing. But you are always welcome
> with us... you and I and Donut and everyone else, we are a herd, and—"

Prepotente tackles him into a hug and sobs, then tells him afterward: "You can call me Pony if you
like."

### The Cost of the Rescue

Getting to Prepotente in time costs Carl minutes he doesn't have — and those minutes are why he
detonates a bomb, planned days in advance, that kills **[Jasha and Radoslav](jasha-and-radoslav.md)**
of Team Free Love outright. Prepotente never learns, on the page, that his rescue was the reason.

### The Song He Never Got to Sing

At the Floor 11 finale's parade, Prepotente is supposed to deliver a scripted vengeance line about
the downtrodden rising up. Instead, riding the tapir Sweety, he abandons the script and sings —
"In the Air Tonight" by Phil Collins, the same song he never got to perform back at the Heat 5 bar
before the fight there interrupted him — accompanied by nothing but Sweety stamping out the beat:

> Not only was he singing it, but he was singing it well. I was absolutely floored. It was strong,
> it was steady, and it was goddamned beautiful.

Even Elle is stunned: "Holy shit, guys. How does that voice come out of him?"

### The Stolen Crystal

Prepotente's **Memorial Crystal: Apito** — his standing personal quest object since Book 5, and the
same crystal Carl's own Emberus quest is tangled up in — protects everyone riding with him from
Scolopendra's attacks for most of the Floor 11 finale, charging as the fight goes on. In the chaos of
the boss fight's end it's pulled from around his neck:

> **Prepotente:** I will recover. Tend to Jurgen. I was electrocuted, but I am now fine. The deity
> stole my memorial crystal! The thief pulled it right off my neck! I have filed a formal complaint
> with the Epicure!

!!! note "Which deity — a correction"
    Prepotente never names the thief on the page, and Carl's own follow-up guess points not at Eris
    but at the god who is confirmed to leave the realm carrying off both **Krakaren Prime** and
    **Samantha** at the same moment — implied, though not stated outright, to be **Taranis**: "I
    think Krakaren has something to do with Apito. So does Samantha. And he took the memorial
    crystal because it contains all her memories and spells." Treat the identity of the thief as
    unconfirmed rather than settled either way.

He is left, by the book's end, both without his crystal and without the protection it provided —
and Carl's only promise is "I'll see what we can do."
