# Akuma

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** **War mage** — a dungeon-born symbiote of skeleton, flesher and sapient magic.
      Leader, and eventually sole Warlord, of the **War Mage Rebellion**
    - **Race:** War mage. "This was originally a high-elf cobbler with a human skin, resleeved 12
      times before the ambient magic gave him his final form." He presents as "a long-haired elf,
      old and wizened with an angry sneer on his sagging face"
    - **Class:** **War Mage** — the system prints it as his class line
    - **Subclass:** None stated
    - **Level:** **100**
    - **Role:** Warlord of a non-team entity that is handed official standing by accident, wins
      control of the city defense system, and then walks away from it
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** None named individually. "War mages come with knowledge of
      hundreds of spells," and he is "arguably the most powerful non-god magic caster on this floor."
      His group breaks a *Conscription* lock with "a clever combination of Pull spells." A war
      mage's head detonates as a **Sapient Moment of Destruction** roughly ten minutes after death
    - **Notable items:** The [Gate of the Feral Gods](../items/gate-of-the-feral-gods.md), taken
      from [Elle McGibbons](elle-mcgibbons.md) in Chapter 61
    - **Status:** **Alive** — he voluntarily leaves the realm in Chapter 78, which defeats his own
      team

## Overview

Akuma is the only warlord in Book 7 who wins his war and then declines the prize. He begins the
floor as a conscripted asset of the Skull Empire, ends it holding the throne room that would have
won Faction Wars outright, and leaves the floor with the one thing he actually came for.

Carl first examines him in the clearing where **King Stalwart** dies:

> **Akuma. War Mage. Level 100.**
>
> **Leader of the War Mage Rebellion.**
>
> **This was originally a high-elf cobbler with a human skin, resleeved 12 times before the ambient
> magic gave him his final form. He’s arguably the most powerful non-god magic caster on this floor.
> He’s also a complete prick, as evidenced by the company he keeps.**

The accompanying cookbook entry is a long essay on war mages as a species: they are "one of the few,
true dungeon-born entities," created accidentally by overuse of *You're Not Done Yet*, formed when a
flesher re-sleeves a reanimated skeleton enough times that the magic itself becomes sapient. "And
nobody made them! All of this just happened on its own." The entry closes with a pointed warning to
the showrunners: "maybe you should ask that pissed-off changeling army that's in the middle of
wrecking your shit why you should probably keep an eye on the things you unleash, especially when
you don't fully understand what they are."

## Killing Stalwart and Taking the Gate

In Chapter 61, Carl and Elle have the Gate of the Feral Gods open in orc territory. War mages
teleport in en masse; Elle is knocked unconscious into a tree. Four of Donut's *Magic Missile* kills
are already smoking on the ground when Carl arrives.

> Stalwart was here, on his knees, crying in pain. There were four corpses on the ground. War mages.
> The magical beings were dead and smoking. They’d all been hit and killed with Donut’s amped-up
> Magic Missile before they’d all teleported here. Three more of the war mages surrounded Stalwart,
> shouting. A fourth stood off to the side.

The fourth is Akuma. His colonel's insignia fades away as Carl watches, and the group's dots turn
white and then "a brownish-orange glow" — a color no army on the board uses.

> As I watched in horror, the three war mages surrounding Stalwart each chanted something in unison,
> and the orc’s flesh began to tear.
>
> I met eyes with the terrified orc for just a moment.
>
> “My sister was right,” King Stalwart, leader of the Skull Empire, said in the moment before he
> exploded right in front of me, showering the clearing with his gore.

The four mages do not attack Carl. Then he sees what Akuma is holding.

> For the first time, I saw what was in the hands of Akuma, the leader of this group.
>
> He had taken the Gate of the Feral Gods from Elle.
>
> The war mage was staring at my bare feet.
>
> “Nice shoes, asshole,” he said to me before the four of them teleported away.

Elle's own account: "That war mage prick snatched the gate right out of my hands." She also notices
he pulled a punch — "That strong one, the elf-looking guy. He pulled back at the last moment. It was
almost like he was trying not to kill me."

!!! note "Who gets the credit"
    The system awards the kill to "three members of the War Mage Rebellion," and the text places
    Akuma standing apart from the three who did the chanting. He is named as the group's leader and
    as the one holding the gate, not as one of the three killers.

**Baroness Victory**'s reconstruction: "it appears the War Mages broke their own allegiance against
the Skull Empire, which they shouldn't be able to do. They were under strict Conscription rules, but
they used a clever combination of Pull spells to kill Stalwart anyway. I think Stalwart might've
ejected them from his army on purpose in an attempt to get them to stop hurting him. It didn't
work."

## How a Non-Team Got Standing

The killing creates a category the game does not have. Victory, immediately after: "We just received
notification that there's a new, non-team entity called the War Mage Rebellion. I still don't know
what that even means. 'Non-team entity' is not a thing. At least it wasn't until just now."

Because they are not a team, Stalwart's assets are awarded to whoever is sitting in the Bone Clan's
throne room instead. **Drick**, Team Retribution's own adjutant, then calls a foul against his own
team, alleging illegal interference, and a full quorum is convened. The quorum's finding backfires
spectacularly:

> **Warlord Message: A full quorum adjudication has determined no cheating has occurred by any
> warlords. Any players under an administrative hold have now been released.**
>
> **Warlord Message: After a careful deliberation, it has been determined that the Non-Team Entity
> known as the War Mage Rebellion will not be allowed access to Larracos until the Peeling phase.**
>
> **Warlord Message: Since we are now in the Peeling Phase, this ruling is rendered moot. However,
> the system has determined that the Quorum’s ruling has given the War Mage Rebellion standing.**

A ruling meant to restrict the war mages is the ruling that makes them real. The faction itself is
covered elsewhere; what matters for Akuma is that this is the moment he becomes a warlord with
system access.

## The Agatha Problem

Standing comes with a warlord, and the system does not give it to him. **Agatha** — the residual
Carl was ordered by [Zev](zev.md) never to interact with ("You are not to interact with Agatha. This
is a direct order.") — is named Warlord of the War Mage Rebellion, and
Akuma is attached as co-warlord. Her three emergency action items grant the Rebellion full warlord
access, open the *Conscription* spell to unaffiliated players, and revert the Peeling Phase to the
original Faction Wars rule that every soldier and warlord of a defeated team drops dead where they
stand.

Victory, on the arrangement (Chapter 69): "They clearly did not anticipate Agatha would be named as
a co-warlord, and the other leader, Akuma, does not like her. The system is going all in with them."
And later in the same chapter: "Based on that Akuma guy's reaction when they assigned her as
co-warlord, I don't think they are assisting each other at all."

Agatha then abandons the post she was handed:

> **Warlord Message: Warlord Agatha of the War Mage Rebellion has been deemed to have abandoned her
> post. As such, she has been removed as Warlord from the War Mage Rebellion. Warlord Akuma has been
> given full system access and named sole Warlord.**

## "She Seeks to Kill Us All"

In Chapter 77 the Rebellion abandons Team Retribution's throne room — the six-hour hold that would
have won them the season — and the city defense system goes inert. In Chapter 78, Akuma opens a
conversation in the warlord chat that Carl calls "bewildering."

> **Warlord Akuma: Hey, underwear boy. Do me a favor. Kill that old lady for me, and I’ll forgive
> you and your pet for killing off so many of my brothers.**
>
> **The Champion of Nekhebit: I AM NOT A PET, MR. WAR MAGE. AND WHY ARE YOU MAD AT ELLE? SHE IS
> DELIGHTFUL. ALSO HOW CAN I CHANGE MY NAME BACK TO WARLORD DONUT? IT DOESN’T DO THIS IN THE REGULAR
> CHAT.**
>
> **Warlord Akuma: Is Elle the one on the Four Seasons path? Not her, dipshit. Keep her alive if you
> wish for us to face Scolopendra and survive. Agatha. The other warlord we were forced to accept in
> order to gain system access. She was forced upon us, and she is an enemy to you. She is an enemy
> to us all. We had no hand in those so-called “action items” other than the Conscription one. She
> slipped away before I was able to kill her. Do this for me, and we will be allies.**

Carl catches the word that matters — "Us? If you wish for 'us' to survive?" — and offers to meet.
Akuma's answer is his exit statement:

> **Warlord Akuma: You won’t be seeing me again on this floor. We never wished to keep that
> falling-down castle. We sought to test the potions we gathered. We wished to find the Scavenger’s
> daughter, who was hidden within the castle, and we found her. She is with us now. Once we had her
> in our possession, we left. That was all we ever wanted with this place, and we are no enemies to
> you. But Agatha’s actions continue to place everybody and everything in jeopardy, and she must be
> dealt with. She has a method that allows her to jump to the Waiting Room and reappear outside an
> open stairwell. You won’t likely see her on this floor again. You will have to find her on the
> 10th. Do this for us if you want to survive. She seeks to kill us all. Not just you and not just
> me. Everybody and everything.**

Donut's contribution to the exchange, in full: "DID YOU JUST CALL ME A DIPSHIT? I'M A HIGHER LEVEL
THAN YOU ARE, YOU SAGGY TWAT."

## Leaving the Realm

The message thread is immediately followed by the cleanest exit in the book:

> **Warlord Message: Warlord Akuma has left the realm. As per the current season’s ruleset, the War
> Mage rebellion has been defeated. They have no assets to award.**

No throne room falls, no soldiers die, nothing transfers. Carl forwards the whole conversation to
Victory and Zev; neither answers quickly, which worries him more. Victory's eventual reply
dodges the question entirely: "I don't know where Akuma went. That team is officially defeated and
therefore no longer under our authority."

!!! note "Unresolved"
    Three threads are left open. What the war mages were testing their gathered potions *for* is
    never explained. Who or what "the Scavenger's daughter, who was hidden within the castle" is —
    a person, given Akuma's "she" — is never established; Carl asks **Mordecai** directly ("who is
    the Scavenger? And who is the Scavenger's Daughter? I know it's the name of my patch, but I
    didn't know there was more to it than that") and is brushed off. See
    [The Scavenger's Daughter](../items/the-scavengers-daughter.md). And Akuma's reference to facing
    **Scolopendra** — and to Elle being "the one on the Four Seasons path" — is dropped without
    follow-up.

## Book 8: A Parade of Horribles

Akuma resurfaces on Floor 10, and this time he isn't running from Carl — he needs him.

### The Note

Early in Heat 3, a gremlin delivers Carl a note that Carl recognizes as Akuma's the instant he reads
it:

> "Don't tell anyone about this note. You need to kill two teams next heat. Come visit us when we
> open after the next race, and I'll explain why. Ask for the top-shelf upgrades so you're led to the
> back. We don't have to be enemies, Carl. We want the same thing. I can smuggle friends to the
> Pineapple Cabaret. We can escape this chaos. Come alone. — Akuma."

### Cornered and Tortured

Carl's group ambushes him instead of coming alone, at the Chicken & Waffles upgrade shop in Heat 4.
[Elle McGibbons](elle-mcgibbons.md#book-8-a-parade-of-horribles) — still owed payback for the stolen
Gate of the Feral Gods — freezes one of his testicles solid with an ice bolt the moment Donut mutes
him, and the interrogation proceeds from there. What Akuma gives up, under duress and admittedly
"fuzzy" on some details, is the single biggest lore-dump of the book's first half:

- **Herot**, the 16th-edition cookbook author, is a skink former crawler still alive, secretly
  running the hidden pocket dimension called the **Pineapple Cabaret** from the inside — a discovery
  Akuma made alongside another former crawler, **Menerva**, after they realized NPCs and mobs were
  supposed to be periodically "recycled" for their own sake but were instead being exploited and
  reused indefinitely.
- The Pineapple Cabaret itself was originally built as a cover project — the never-visited
  17th floor — that quietly became a genuine escape route for exploited NPCs, one Akuma admits he
  engineered forcing Carl into a two-team kill to guarantee Carl would need his help getting access
  to.
- Akuma's team is chasing something called **the Scavenger's Daughter** inside the Cabaret, which he
  is deliberately vague about.
- He's the source (though not the only one) of the book's exposition on the **"Big Six"** dungeon
  power factions — the system AI itself delivers a fuller version later, in its own voice, during
  the Heat 7 casino ambush with the shadow mimic "Mitch" (see [Carl](carl.md#book-8-a-parade-of-horribles)).

Akuma's own summary of why any of this matters: "After what just happened with the Nothing being
broken, *everyone* is aware of them now... That is why we must work together. You didn't need to
crush my fucking balls."

### Fate

Akuma tells Carl directly, before the end of Heat 5, that he intends to leave the floor: "I am still
here. I will be leaving soon. Stop messaging me unless it's important." He is not shown captured,
killed, or freed on the page after that — the last confirmation of his status is his own statement
of intent to leave, and he later appears only as one of several named figures (alongside Carl, Donut,
Li Na, Samantha, and others) flashed across the screen during the system AI's galaxy-wide Ascendency
announcement in the epilogue, implying continued relevance rather than closure.

!!! note "Unresolved"
    Book 8 never confirms what became of Akuma after Heat 5, nor what the War Mage Rebellion's
    ultimate relationship to the Pineapple Cabaret and the Scavenger's Daughter turns out to be.
