# Tsarina Signet

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Elite (a scripted, "soap opera" NPC — see below)
    - **Race:** Half Naiad, Half High-Elf Summoner
    - **Level:** 60
    - **Class:** Summoner (Blood Magic)
    - **Also known as:** Signet the Bastard
    - **Status:** **Deceased** — sacrifices herself to break the peace seal at the Butcher's
      Masquerade in Book 5
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** Blood Magic summoning (requires a sacrificial death to animate her "battle squad"); an inherent Charisma-based charm effect ("Puppy Dog"); the level-15+ sleep spell **Water Lily**; **Ink Marauder** (Book 5, her signature tattoo-reanimation spell — normally requires a caster, a sacrifice, and a killer, but can be cast solo by one person filling all three roles)
    - **Notable items:** None named — her body itself is covered in living, animated tattoos of monsters, including memorialized fallen companions such as the were-castor **Miss Nadine** and her murdered mother's spirit

## What She Is

Signet is Carl and Donut's first encounter with an **Elite** — a category of NPC Mordecai
explains as fundamentally different from ordinary mobs. Elites are never "naturals": they're
designed and imprinted by Borant to act out ongoing, scripted storylines ("soap operas") that
continue across the urban floors regardless of whether any crawler gets involved, protected by
writers who will bend rules to keep a popular storyline alive — making them explicitly more
dangerous to interfere with than their raw stats suggest.

Half Naiad and half High-Elf — the illegitimate child of a promiscuous elf king and a member of
a species known for drowning outsiders — Signet was an outcast from birth. She was taken in as a
child by **Grimaldi**, ringmaster of the Over City's traveling circus, who raised her and later
became the love of her life.

## Blood Magic

Signet's summoning requires a "proper sacrifice": she must capture two creatures, pit them
against each other, and cast her spell on whichever one is about to lose, timed to the instant of
a fatal blow. She cannot influence the fight herself, and cannot bleed the sacrifice with her own
hands — only capture, pair, and wait. The quality of her "battle squad" depends entirely on the
quality of the blood spent. She can only cast this once per night.

Her innate Charisma also carries a passive charm effect called **Puppy Dog**, which Mordecai
warns Carl about in advance — the only known counter is to inflict sudden pain on yourself (Carl
breaks his own finger) to snap out of it.

## The Grimaldi Tragedy

Signet was the only member of the circus untouched by "Scolopendra's" cataclysmic poison cloud.
Grimaldi, mid-performance in the center ring, transformed into a mind-controlling vine/parasite
that infected everyone else in the tent — the lemurs, the clowns, the animals — binding them to
his will and letting them regenerate from spores whenever killed. Signet has waged a nightly,
unwinnable war against her own adoptive family and lost love ever since, hoping to free them.

## Encounter with Carl & Donut

Signet approaches Carl under the guise of concern after watching him survive an ambush, using her
charm effect on him while Donut resists it. When Carl declines to help her, she reveals her real
intent had been to use him as her night's blood sacrifice — his survival (via Protective Shell)
denies her the summon. The next morning, she captures both Carl and Donut, placing Donut under
her **Water Lily** sleep spell (which kills the sleeper if the caster dies, and only ends if
Signet chooses) as leverage to force Carl into providing a substitute sacrifice: a transformed
circus bear named **Heather** (a Level 19 Neighborhood Boss — full page pending the Bosses pass
for Book 2). Carl instead cures Heather of her mind control mid-fight by healing away the
parasitic worms controlling her, rather than simply killing her — denying Signet another usable
death.

## Book 5: The Vengeance of the Daughter

Signet reappears on Floor 6 pursuing a two-part quest — **The Vengeance of the Daughter** —
against the naiad Confederacy that murdered her mother and the High Elf regime (ruled by her
half-sister **[Queen Imogen](queen-imogen.md)**) that enabled it. Carl and Donut become entangled
in the storyline early, helping her sack the ruined naiad palace Fort Freedom (killing an
accidental Eryops boss, Claude Sludgington the Fourth, in the process) before Signet formally
declares her true target: assassinating Imogen at the Butcher's Masquerade.

Learning that the masquerade operates under an unbreakable, goddess-enforced peace seal that would
make any premature attack suicidal, Carl talks Signet into holding back and waiting for his
signal — a plan she agrees to, unaware Carl privately intends to use her only as insurance. The
system's own AI (nudged, unprompted, by Apito) instead teleports her directly into the sealed
ballroom as an invited guest, exploiting the same "invite your enemies" mechanic the masquerade
was built on.

When Carl's own escape plan collapses and he privately resolves to kill fellow crawler Eva Sigrid
to break the seal himself, Signet intervenes first. Having just been told, in front of Circe
Took's own hive-mind spies, that "Empress" D'Nadia doubts her tattoo-warriors and murdered
family are even real, Signet chooses to prove otherwise: she performs her Ink Marauder ritual
solo — caster, sacrifice, and killer in one — and slits her own throat with Carl's knife,
summoning her full battle squad of animated tattoos (including a still-living memorial of the
were-castor Miss Nadine) at full, unrestrained strength for the first and last time. Her death
shatters the party's peace seal, triggering the book's climactic battle. She's pulled into "the
Nothing" as she dies, leaving only a hollow, ash-outline echo of her tattoos to fight one final
battle in her name — and asks Carl, as her last request, to tell her family "she did this out of
her own free will."
