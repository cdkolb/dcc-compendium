## Overview

Imogen rules the High Elves of Floor 6's Hunting Grounds from a stark white castle nicknamed "the
Sugar Cube." She usurped her position after her father King Finian's death, purging half-elf
"bastards" (including her half-sister Signet) to secure her claim, and has ruled ever since through
a mix of xenophobic religious doctrine (worship of the goddess Apito, who preaches "cutting off the
rot" to grow "the perfect tree") and calculated hospitality.

## The Butcher's Masquerade

Imogen personally casts the titular ritual — a once-per-lifetime spell that invites a
goddess-selected guest list (deliberately including one's worst enemies) to a mandatory party under
an absolute peace seal, with the promise of an unspecified boon from Apito if the evening concludes
without violence. She meets Carl in person before the party to warn him off any scheme, revealing
the ritual's full mythological backstory and confirming she genuinely cannot harm him without
breaking her own seal — even as she promises retribution the instant anyone else breaks it first.

Her plan collapses when Signet, having been invited into the sealed ballroom by the ritual itself,
sacrifices herself to shatter the peace seal from within — dropping the entire party (crawlers,
hunters, elves, and the god Diwata, who has infiltrated the castle) into the floor's climactic
"Cage Fight Extreme" boss battle the instant Apito withdraws her unseen protection. Imogen fights ferociously — she magically flings one of her own burning, nymph-covered
elk mounts into Dmitri and Maxim Popov, killing the two-headed nodling crawler outright — before
being worn down by coordinated, blunt-force fire from Florin's shotgun and dozens of other
crawlers. Prepotente lands the killing blow, smashing a potion into her face while she's
disoriented from an earlier disco-ball attack.

## Ferdinand

Imogen's familiar is a house cat, **Sir Ferdinand** — a Level 100 Province Boss in his own right —
whom Donut successfully magically charms into betraying her during the party, using a
Charm Animal potion brewed specifically for the purpose. Ferdinand fights on Donut's side during
the final battle and survives, transferring with the rest of the castle to Floor 9 as a listed
minion of Donut's.
