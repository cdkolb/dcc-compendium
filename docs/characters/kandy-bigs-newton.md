# Kandy “Bigs” Newton

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** NPC — a **sluggalette**, the female form of the **sluggalo**; a member of the
      Princess Posse
    - **Race:** Sluggalo. She is the size of a bulldog, slimy, and leaves orange goo behind her
    - **Class:** None stated
    - **Subclass:** None stated
    - **Level:** **29** at her introduction in chapter 54 — “She was only level 29, and she’d killed
      Vinata”
    - **Role:** Carl’s improvised bodyguard out of Club Vanquisher, later a permanent hanger-on of
      the Princess Posse column
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** None shown. She fights entirely with the hatchet, and she is
      immune to the **Witness** spell because the system classes sluggalos as a different kind of NPC
    - **Notable items:** None named — “A single hatchet grew straight up from the center of her head.”
      It is part of her body, not equipment
    - **Status:** Alive as of her last appearance in chapter 82. The text does not say what becomes of
      her at the end of the floor, though most of the surviving sluggalos stay with the Posse

## What a Sluggalette Is

The sluggalos are [Carl](carl.md)’s own disease weaponised. **SlugPox** — “a nasty, old spell,” per
Pater Coal — is what Carl caught on Floor 8 in Book 6 when he flagged the dying boss Asojano
(*“**Warning: You have been infected with SlugPox.**”*). Boils grow on the infected, and out of each
boil comes a fully formed slug person, each one higher level than the last. Carl can pass it on with
his ***Super Spreader*** skill. His own summary, from Book 6:

> **Carl: They’re not punk rock. They’re Sluggalos. And I only infected one of them. Doctor Bones. He
> has the same *Super Spreader* spell as I do, but his is better. I can only do it on one person once
> per hour. He infected the others all at the same time, and then he cast a spell on the slugs so
> they’re not so hostile after they come out.**

Doctor Bones mass-infected the Penis Parade strippers with that improved variant, which is where the
sluggalo army comes from. They carry into Book 7 as Princess Posse troops — loud, obscene, suicidally
enthusiastic, devoted to Imani, and used by Carl’s commanders as shock infantry precisely because they
“swarm the castle face-first, inexpertly killing and getting themselves splattered like a bunch of
idiots.” The system treats them, along with the former tattoos, as a separate category of NPC, which
is why they are unaffected by several floor-wide effects that hit everyone else.

Carl never had his own slugpox cured, and by chapter 54 he is carrying an untreated boil on his back
with the slug level somewhere around twenty or thirty. Bigs is what comes out of it. The mob type
itself is covered separately.

## Killing the Sepsis Whore

The end of chapter 54 is the worst moment of Carl’s floor. He has blown himself up inside Club
Vanquisher, and [Princess Vinata](princess-vinata.md) — the Sepsis Whore, warlord of the Blood
Sultanate — has survived the blast wrapped around him, driven insane by staring into the Eye of the Bedlam Bride on his chest. She
is crushing him, stabbing him, and beating his head against the floor while gods walk past and ignore
them both. Carl is drunk, cannot bend his knees, has screaming worms coming out of his nose, and is
counting down his own immortality timer against hers.

> I was about to drop the next bomb when I felt the sluggalo burst out of my back. He—actually, a
> she—slipped out from under my shirt, whooped loudly, and planted a hatchet right between Vinata’s
> eyes, killing her.
>
> “Yeah, bitch! Right in the nugget!” the sluggalo woman shouted. She looked down at me as Vinata’s
> lifeless body slipped off of me and to the side. “I wish I had feet. I’d make me some snakeskin
> boots!”

Chapter 55 opens on the system messages, which is how Carl learns her name at the same time the rest
of the party does:

> System Message: The Sepsis Whore of the Blood Sultanate has fallen. Credit for the kill has been
> given to Kandy “Bigs” Newton of the Princess Posse.
>
> System Message: The Blood Sultanate has been defeated.
>
> System Message: All assets of the Blood Sultanate, as per prior arrangement, have been split evenly
> with the surviving blood relatives of the Sepsis Whore, Warlord Princess Donut and General Katia
> Grim, both of the Princess Posse. As per prior arrangement, all assets have been forfeited to the
> Princess Posse.

> Katia: Who the hell is Kandy Newton, and where the hell are you, Carl?

That is a level-29 spontaneously generated slug eliminating an entire Faction Wars team, at the exact
moment the operation had collapsed, by hitting the warlord in the head with the axe she was born with.
A full page here rests on that single kill and nothing else needs to be added to justify it.

Carl gets his first proper look at her a moment later:

> “Oh, daddy, you don’t look so good,” the sluggalette said, looking down at me. She was only level 29,
> and she’d killed Vinata. The naga must not have fully healed herself before the immortality expired.
> The slug was the size of a bulldog. She had little wisps of pink-dyed hair, monk-style, circling
> around her round head. Piercings ran down the slide of her slimy head, reminding me of that goblin
> shamanka from so long ago. A single hatchet grew straight up from the center of her head. It dripped
> with blood.

She names herself in the same chapter, while Carl is trying to give her orders:

> “Okay, Kandy is it?” I asked the sluggalette… “Stay with me. Kill anyone who gets in my way. I have
> some friends we’re meeting halfway. Don’t hurt them. But I want you to sink a hatchet into anyone
> else. Things are about to get loud.”
>
> “Call me Bigs,” the sluggalette said. She swung her head hatchet which flecked naga blood everywhere.
> “Is this ogre dude our friend or is he getting kneecapped?”

She also, undeterred by the fact that she is a bulldog-sized slug, repeatedly tries to bury the hatchet
in Baroness Victory, who is not physically present and whose leg it passes straight through.

## After the Club

Bigs never leaves. She turns up mounted on Gonk the swamp yak alongside Dong Quixote in chapter 63,
having got there because a sluggalo told her that if a mount takes a teleport trap, all its riders go
through with it. Her mourning paint has been updated:

> Since I’d last seen her, her face/eye paint had changed. She now had a massive teardrop built into
> the stalk of her right eye alongside the row of piercings. The hatchet protruding from her head
> gleamed. She leapt off the back of Gonk and splotched into the woods with a loud whoop. She waved the
> axe. “Imma get my chop on today. Where the bad guys at?”

She is one of the handful immune to the **Witness** spell in chapter 65 — the mass effect that sweeps
everyone caught outdoors into a single rolling ball of bodies — along with Rend, Gonk, Jamal and Dong,
because the system does not count sluggalos and former tattoos as the same sort of NPC.

On the Madness castle raid she scouts the front door, ties up two conscripted crawlers Carl has just
rescued, and requires supervision about it:

> “Can I kill one of them?” Bigs asked. “That one is already bleeding pretty good.”
>
> “Holy shit, Bigs. She’s a friend. One of us. A good guy. Keep them safe.”
>
> “I’ll wait until he’s gone,” Bigs whispered loudly at the two women. “Then one of you is getting the
> chop.”

Her other recurring notes are a running propositioning of anything that holds still — she offers an
outraged antlered alien ten gold to lick her tail, and gets seduction coaching from Samantha while
riding on her head — and a hard, unexplained animosity toward Li Na:

> “No, she’s a bad guy,” Bigs said. “You know what she did to Sneaky Tyler C?”
>
> “Uh...” I said. I was assuming Tyler C was another sluggalo. “No?”
>
> “She tied a potion to him and then ordered him to run right into the god. He blew up the moment he
> touched the thing. That’s what I heard. It was a bitch-ass move. I don’t like her.”

Sneaky Tyler C was the lead sluggalo on Justice Light’s underground project. It is the only time in
the book that a sluggalo holds a grudge.
