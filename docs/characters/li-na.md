# Li Na

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Human Crawler; sister of **[Li Jun](li-jun.md)**, partied with **Zhang**
    - **Race:** A demon-like race — Carl describes "the woman's demonic face," and her passive aura
      makes her physically untouchable; nobody in the guild can shake her hand without a
      potion. (Book 3 names the race **Changbi**; Book 7 never restates it)
    - **Class:** Not restated in Book 7. (Book 3 gives her class as **Slave Driver**)
    - **Subclass:** None stated
    - **Level:** **84** at the time of the Club Vanquisher broadcast — "number two on the top ten
      list, passing Prepotente" — and **87** by the end of the book
    - **Role:** Commander of the **107th White Dragon Urban Defense** battalion; runs the **Extend
      Aura** synergy tower in Shanty Town; the Princess Posse's builder and jailer
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** Her **Dreads** — at-will auras, not counted as offensive magic —
      including **Dark Purpose**, **Defile Soul**, **Bleeding Horror** and **Succulent Decay**;
      ethereal chains, including a once-a-day multi-chain skill she loans Carl by tattoo; flight and
      new hunting skills after the ball; the **Damned** status, which lets her enter any temple
    - **Notable items:** Carl's second **[Ring of Divine Suffering](../items/enchanted-night-wyrms-ring-of-divine-suffering.md)**,
      looted from King Rust; her dead brother's celestial-tier eye, **The Engine of Tomorrow**
    - **Status:** Alive — walks into the stairwell to Floor 10 alone and silent, permanently unable
      to heal

## Overview

Li Na had been a background figure for four books: her brother's blunt, unsettling sister, the one
who kept a mantaur alive by cutting it down to a torso on the Iron Tangle. Book 7 puts her at the
centre of the floor and then takes her brother away, and what is left is the most powerful crawler
in the dungeon and the one Carl uses, at the very end, as the argument against becoming her.

She starts the floor barely speaking to Carl at all — he ate her brother's eye on Floor 8. Katia
diagnoses it for him:

> "She's not mad at you," Katia said. "She's mad at herself for not protecting her brother. She knows
> you didn't do it on purpose."

She cannot be recruited into the army normally, because nobody can touch her; Mordecai has to brew a
potion that negates aura effects first. The moment she joins, she asks for a job: she and her squad
want to build the defences in and around Shanty Town. Carl agrees, and the **107th White Dragons**
become the Posse's engineers, jailers and urban-defence battalion, with the Prism sergeant
**Toyotomi** running her builder squad.

## The Dreads

Carl's own aura magic comes from Imani. Li Na's is a different mechanic entirely:

> The other crawler who used auras was Li Na.
>
> Hers were very, very different.
>
> The ones she could activate at will were called Dreads.

Dreads normally only affect someone physically touching her, and they do not count as offensive
magic — which matters enormously during the ceasefire's magic ban. For the assault on **Club
Vanquisher**, Elle's manager Mistress Tiatha supplies the exploit: Li Na mans an **Extend Aura**
synergy tower in the Shanty Town clock tower, dozens of capacitor wires are strung from the clock
arms, and Li Jun and Zhang trail those wires into the club. Anyone within about 20 metres of the wire
catches the full effect.

- **Dark Purpose** — the one they planned on. Li Na compares it to Donut's
  *[Why Are You Hitting Yourself?](../spells/why-are-you-hitting-yourself.md)*; Mordecai has never
  heard of it. Amplified, it is a massacre. Guards tear their own throats open; a mantaur is found
  with "his own claws buried up through his chin, eyes open with surprise"; ghostly chains drag
  shrieking apparitions down through the floor. Carl, watching: "Hey, uh, Li Na. That spell isn't
  exactly what I was expecting."
- **Defile Soul** — cycled in next. Its effect is never described on-page.
- **Bleeding Horror** — "if someone with bloody hands gets near me, and I have the dread active,
  it's supposed to turn their blood against them. And then the blood would turn on my enemies." She
  has never previously got it past level 2 because victims die too fast. Carl runs through a
  corridor where a ram's own blood is pouring back into his mouth and nose "like liquid mercury."
- **Succulent Decay** — "I am turning on the last of my dreads, and I do not know how it will react."
  The text never shows what it does.

She was never really casting them for Carl. She was power-levelling:

> Li Na: Dark Purpose was level 4 when I cast. It is not meant to be used like this, and it leveled
> even more rapidly than we anticipated. It hit level 15, and I moved to a different dread. I am
> cycling my dreads and rapidly leveling them all. Right now I am using Defile Soul. Next I will
> train Bleeding Horror.

Carl asks her to switch back to save his own approach and she refuses on the spot — "I will if I have
to, Carl. I need to train, and I can't give up this opportunity" — then gives him a tactically
correct reason why her plan is better. Donut yells at her in chat. It does not work.

> Her simple, matter-of-fact, almost emotionless delivery was chilling. Christ, I thought. It was like
> she was a sociopath. She was always so calm. How was it I'd never noticed this before?

The collateral runs on for days: a cleric in the neighbouring Dagda temple boiled himself alive in
his own stew.

!!! note "Naming"
    The source calls the blood dread **Bleeding Horror** when Li Na announces her training order, and
    **Blood Horror** when she reports it hitting level 15. Both spellings are in the book.

## Rishi's Arms

Li Na's White Dragons hold the prisoners. When Carl finally goes to the brig to see
**Rishi** — the one captive he had explicitly ordered left alone — the cells are otherwise empty, and
Rishi has been mutilated:

> Still, someone—clearly Li Na—thought it would be best to take extra precautions and had his four
> arms removed, which was especially barbaric.

Carl does not confront her about it.

## The Second Ring

Carl loots a second **Ring of Divine Suffering** from King Rust's corpse. Li Na asks for it.

> When Li Na first approached me and asked if she could have my extra Ring of Divine Suffering, my
> initial reaction had been fuck no. She was quite possibly the most powerful crawler in the dungeon
> already, and I'd worried that while it would make her stronger, it'd also drive her to do something
> crazy.

Only Imani objects. Elle is emphatic the other way — "She can handle it better than you certainly
can. And if she can't, at least it'll be the ring that kills her. She's a scary psycho, but at least
she's our scary psycho, Carl" — and it is Donut, out loud and without giving a reason, who settles
it: "Please, Carl. Please give it to her. Do it as soon as you can." Carl works out why the moment he
sees Rishi's cell.

Li Na also asks about the **Necklace of Indelible Woe** and turns it down, because using it might cost
her the level-15 dreads.

## Her Brother

[Li Jun](li-jun.md) dies in Chapter 68, drawing the god **Harpocrates** away from Donut's tower with a
single unmuted speaker on the back of a cart. Li Na's last words to him are "Jun, no. No!" His last
message is to her.

Rosetta's status report an hour later: "I've ordered Li Na to assist the NPCs, but she is currently in
a fit of hysterics over the death of her brother." Then she goes dark — off her synergy tower,
unreachable, not answering Carl's messages.

She resurfaces in the stairwell of the Nest tower, and Carl finally understands what she meant when
she said she was "installing" her brother's eye:

> "Holy shit," I exclaimed upon seeing her face. I'd heard her say she was "installing" Li Jun's fake
> eye into herself, but the meaning hadn't really hit me until that moment.
>
> The front of the woman's demonic face was covered with blood. She'd gouged her own eye out and
> replaced it with Li Jun's celestial-quality eye. The eye would allow her to slow down time and move
> with incredible speed.

She has also looted and duplicated the war mage's **Hell-Kissed Potion of Conscription**, hands Carl
the copy, and states her intentions in a flat monotone before walking out:

> "I used Jun's scroll of potion duplication on it," Li Na said. She spoke with a dead, chilling calm.
> "I am going to use the original now on Harpocrates and then I will save Zhang, Elle, Katia, and the
> others before they, too, fall victim to your chaos."

Carl shouts a warning after her that the level discrepancy could kill her, as it killed the war mage.
She does not wait. She uses it correctly, conscripts a god, and spends the next stretch of the book
riding Harpocrates like a mount, with Juice Box's changelings climbing on to help hold him.

She also blames Donut for the death, to Donut's face. Donut repeats it to Carl and then defends her:
"But she's not wrong, either. I've made a lot of mistakes already today… He was her brother."

## Three Hundred

Donut's **War Crime** spell is the endgame: the entire enemy army, thousands of them, welded into a
rolling ball. Li Na's contribution is the anvil. She builds a lattice of ethereal chains across the
wreckage of Shanty Town — "There were more chains than I thought possible" — anchored to the Nest,
and tells Donut to slam the ball into it:

> Li Na: Donut, this is all I can build. Do it.

> The chains broke one by one. But as they broke, Li Na grew more. With each slam, the survivors who
> remained stuck to the paw were slammed down again. And again. And each time they pushed through
> chains, they were cut to pieces.

She had already marked every one of them with the Ring of Divine Suffering — which is why Carl,
trying to use his own ring, found he could not.

> System message. A crawler has maxed out her base strength. Crawler Li Na now has 300 strength.
>
> System message. A crawler has maxed out her base intelligence. Crawler Li Na now has 300
> intelligence.
>
> System message... yeah, I can keep doing this, but she maxed them all. Even Charisma, which is a
> hard one.
>
> System Message. So, this has never happened before, but a crawler has reached max base stats. She
> can still raise her stats beyond that with equipment and bonuses and spells, but we don't actually
> have a plan to deal with this, so I'm gonna have to come up with something.

Carl's reaction is one line: "I felt a deep, deep chill." Donut's is outrage that Li Na did it at
level 87 while Donut had to reach 135 to be the strongest crawler in the dungeon.

## The Debt

The ring's price comes due immediately. Marks that are not cashed in do not expire quietly.

> "She used the Ring of Divine Suffering," I said. "But she has a problem. I'm pretty sure she'd
> marked both Tagg and Fang, but we didn't kill them. They both got away somehow. More might have
> gotten away, too."
>
> "Uh, oh," Donut said. "Doesn't that mean she can't heal?"
>
> "Yes. And every time she uses her chains, she loses just a little health."

She tears through the Larracos castle looking for them, glowing, not speaking to anyone, and then
takes to the woods. Prepotente reports her flying — "She appears to have some new hunting skills along
with the ability to fly" — and unable to find the ones she marked. Eighteen of them existed; three
survived.

They were **King Rust**'s harem, non-combatants he had negotiated into the dungeon under a private
deal with the AI, and they reached the Cosmic Lounge in the Desperado Club and went back to the 18th
floor.

> "They are gone, and they are forever out of Li Na's reach. She now has a condition that will never
> allow her to heal. Luckily for her, her health is still several times that of all of us combined.
> But she will need to be very careful, despite her massively increased stats."

Donut, unable to resist: "I TOLD YOU THE RING WAS BAD NEWS, CARL."

!!! note "What Book 7 actually says"
    The permanent no-healing condition is a **Ring of Divine Suffering** penalty, not a side effect of
    the transplanted eye — it applies because three crawlers she marked escaped the floor alive. Book
    7 states the condition itself as permanent ("forever out of Li Na's reach," "a condition that will
    never allow her to heal") and says nothing further about it.

## Bigs

Not everyone accepts the grief as an excuse. When Li Na hangs in the air over the column, twenty feet
up, glaring down at Louis — the last person still carrying one of her marks — the sluggalette
**[Kandy "Bigs" Newton](kandy-bigs-newton.md)** growls at her:

> "She's not a bad guy," I said. "She's just going through some shit. Her brother died."
>
> "No, she's a bad guy," Bigs said. "You know what she did to Sneaky Tyler C?"
>
> "Uh..." I said. I was assuming Tyler C was another sluggalo. "No?"
>
> "She tied a potion to him and then ordered him to run right into the god. He blew up the moment he
> touched the thing. That's what I heard. It was a bitch-ass move. I don't like her."

## What Carl Takes From It

In the last chapter, with twenty thousand surrendered mercenaries milling around Larracos and the
Ring of Divine Suffering still in his inventory, Carl works through the same maths Li Na did:

> My initial instinct was to kill them. I could mark them like Li Na had, and I could kill them all,
> raising my stats to 300 across the board just like she had. I knew a lot of people following my
> progress would be enraged at me not doing exactly that. They would call me stupid. They would be
> disappointed in the lack of blood I shed, and they would angrily unfollow my feed because I was not
> the monster they hoped I would be.
>
> Fuck you all, I thought. Fuck you all.
>
> One look at Li Na, and I knew that path, that slope was too steep. Survival had more than one
> meaning.

He holds the ring up to Donut, and she eats it.

## Exit

Li Na goes down to Floor 10 by herself. Zhang, still partied with her, goes in alone too.

> Tran went into the stairwell, as did so many others, including Zhang, who went in alone. I'd asked
> him if he was going to take a deal, and he said he didn't know. Li Na remained partied with him, but
> she hadn't spoken to him at all. She, too, had gone into the stairwell without saying a word.

## Book 8: A Parade of Horribles

Li Na's permanent no-healing condition from Book 7 — the price of marking eighteen crawlers with her
Ring of Divine Suffering and losing three of them off the floor alive — becomes the engine of one of
Book 8's central subplots: an escape attempt built entirely around her.

### The Cleaner-Bot Plan

The party engineers Li Na's escape through a "cleaner-bot exploit" into a hidden pocket dimension
called the **Pineapple Cabaret**, hoping that leaving the dungeon entirely might stop her condition
from worsening, or possibly cure it outright — though the text is explicit that this is only a
theory, never a confirmed fix. Li Na goes first, alone, as the test case: if she signals back that
it's safe, the rest of a much larger group of volunteers will follow the same route.

Mordecai rigs a signal system for exactly this purpose — a potted cactus in the guildhall, tied to
her life and her location, that only he can read:

> **Mordecai:** Okay, guys. A single flower just bloomed on the cactus.

> It had worked. A single flower was the code meaning that Li Na had made it to the Pineapple
> Cabaret and that it was safe to bring the others.

The system's own caution, delivered in the same message where it hands Carl the choice: "Beware
exits that seem too good to be true."

### 227 More

Li Na's confirmed safe arrival opens the floodgates. Using the same method — and a second, larger
cleaner bot recruited from another crawler — the guild sends a mass wave of volunteers after her:

> **Imani:** It went smoothly. Your cleaner bot could only fit forty people, but the one from Bobby
> Montague, the Illusionist guy with the tall hair, managed to fit 105. The total number sent to the
> Pineapple Cabaret is 227, not including Na. Zhang, Tran, Daniel, Archie, Randall 3. All gone.

Mordecai builds a "Garden of Deliverance" — a terrarium of over two hundred individually-tied potted
plants, one per volunteer, so the guild has some way of tracking who lives, dies, or gets out even
though "even the showrunners can't see what's going on in there." Li Na's own cactus remains
distinct from the rest, "the only one with the complicated messaging system," since she was the
proof of concept the whole plan was staked on.

!!! warning "Confirmed: safe arrival, not a cure"
    Book 8 confirms only that Li Na (and, after her, 227 more volunteers) reached the Pineapple
    Cabaret alive. Nothing in the text confirms her permanent no-healing condition is cured, reduced,
    or otherwise changed by leaving the dungeon — that remains an open, unconfirmed hope, not a
    resolved fact.
