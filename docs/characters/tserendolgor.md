# Tserendolgor ("Ren")

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Human Crawler, Mongolian — a pre-collapse celebrity, and the closest thing the
      dungeon has to a genuine one
    - **Race:** Not stated in Book 6. Carl consistently calls her "the dog woman" and "the dog
      soldier"; Donut's needling — "If she was a supermodel, then why did she turn into a dog?" —
      is the only description of her current form the book offers
    - **Class / Subclass:** **Not stated anywhere in Book 6.** Her squad is never statted out; Carl
      admits he can't remember her squadmate Khulan's class either
    - **Level:** Not stated in Book 6
    - **Squad:** Herself, **Chuluuna** (a Mongolian strongman tank who turns his skin to stone) and
      **Khulan** (Chuluuna's wife, who paralyses targets so Ren can burn them). All from the same
      tiny village; her original party of seven or eight was down to three by phase two
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** *Conscript*-style totem play; she summons a **Mongolian Death
      Worm** totem named **Basquiat**
    - **Notable items:** [Velma](../items/velma.md) — an enchanted flamethrower that is "part
      breastplate, part weapon," body-mounted, and works even inside a card battle (Book 7:
      inherited by Carl, modified by Rosetta's crafting team and mounted on the underbelly harness
      of **Jamal** the hammerhead shark flesh golem); the **Knife of Frozen Tears**, a
      sylvan-crystal blade "like a butter knife" that glints red; a heart locket with a dog's paw
      print; a *Predator*-style self-destruct wrist bracer bought at the Desperado Club
    - **Pet:** **Garret**, a **Belly Acher** (also spelled "tummy acher") — an armless, giggling,
      near-immortal blob tank; the pet that beat Mongo at the Butcher's Masquerade. Book 7: re-bonded
      by Carl in chapter 5 and renamed [Sir Rendlegore](sir-rendlegore.md), "Rend"
    - **Status:** **Deceased** — self-detonated in Chapter 60 rather than fight Donut for a key

## Overview

Ren was a Mongolian runway model in Paris, the biggest genuine pre-collapse celebrity in the
dungeon. A jealous ex-boyfriend stabbed her and her dog; she spent a year in hospital, retired, and
had just moved home to Mongolia when the collapse took her. Her whole squad were people she grew up
with in the same village.

Before Book 6 she was a name on a leaderboard and the villain of Donut's longest-running grudge —
the crawler Donut is convinced stole Mongo's pet-show crown at the Butcher's Masquerade. Donut's
fanclub newsletter calls her "an evil, vile, experience hog crawler." Book 6 spends two chapters
turning that into one of the series' best deaths.

She also, quietly, is the person who talks Britney out of her shame about her burned face — "I have
been talking to Tserendolgor, who once had a similar scar upon her face."

## Garret

Garret is a Belly Acher: a damage sponge that "could swallow just about anything whole," bigger than
Chuluuna and constitutionally incapable of not giggling. Ren's explanation of the type is also, as
she says it, the book's thesis about the crawlers themselves:

> "He's a great tank, and he's practically immortal. It's really hard to kill him, and if he dies,
> he regenerates. But every five times he dies, he resets back to level one, and you have to get him
> to bond with you again… Each time he regenerates, he's tougher, but he also has a new weakness, so
> you have to be careful."
>
> "It's just like the rest of us. We look stronger, we have tougher skin, more scars, but there's
> another crack in our…" She couldn't finish.

## Chapter 58–59: Losing

Quan Ch holds both keys and Ren's squad holds the Capitol building stairwell. In the card battle,
Quan plays a card that flips totem control, and Ren's own Mongolian Death Worm tears **Chuluuna**
apart in the seconds Carl isn't looking — killed by his own team's totem, then eaten and vomited
back out by it. Khulan had been teleport-trapped away and arrives too late for the fight.

Carl kills Quan; Donut ends up holding both keys. Ren's realization, in rising panic: "Where're the
keys? I didn't get the keys! "

There is no clean way to give her one. The rules bar simply handing a key over. To transfer one,
Donut would have to be dragged into a card battle and burn a *Flee* card, losing a key of the
system's choosing — most likely stranding Carl, Donut and Imani. Carl's summary: "There were no
scenarios left where it wasn't her or us."

## The Refusal

Carl offers her his key. Then he offers her a fair fight for it. She turns down both.

> "So we can kill someone else for their key?" she asked. "By my count, there's one key for every
> three or four squads. They really fucked us this floor… I'm not going to do that, Carl. I don't
> want to be responsible for hurting someone else."

> "Carl," she said, turning to look at me. She shook her head. "I say this with love. You are an
> idiot. You can't fight us. You can't give your key to Imani. You have to save it for yourself. Do
> you remember when you flew that airplane to save our bubble on the fifth floor? And then when you
> killed all the hunters? That's when I realized who you are. Don't you understand? You're more than
> just a person. If we lose you, we lose *everything*. You're a symbol. You're the embodiment of our
> hope. You and that fucking, annoying cat that everyone seems to like for some reason… If you two
> don't get down to the next floor, we all lose that. You can't sacrifice yourself."

Carl watches his view counter spike as the audience anticipates a fight, then *ease off* once it's
clear there won't be one. "Which made me unreasonably angry. *Fuck you all.*"

## The Confession

Before she goes, she settles Donut's grudge on the record. Note that she confesses to two separate
things — the experience theft *and* the ballot:

> **Ren: Princess Donut. I have a confession to make. Everything you're saying is true. I *was*
> trying to steal your experience the first time we met. I *did* cheat at the pet show. Your Mongo
> is the rightful champion. I'm sorry.**
>
> **Donut: I KNEW IT! I KNEW IT!**
>
> "You didn't have to do that," I said, chuckling.
>
> She grunted with amusement.

And then, privately to Carl: "You know, many of my people are afraid of cats. Some think if you look
into their eyes, they can steal your soul. I hope that's true. I hope she lives up to her hype."

This is the vindication Donut spent an entire book-recap newsletter demanding. #JusticeForMongo,
confessed and closed, four chapters before the fanclub could produce a single frame of evidence.

## Everything She Owns

She kicks Velma across the floor at Carl and empties her inventory: rings, clothing, potions,
scrolls, *Eye in the Sky* scrolls. Khulan does the same — rings, potions, a glowing spear, armour,
unread tomes, a pile of coins. Ren strips her deck down to her totems and hands Carl all her cards,
including two consumables she never got to use, **Glow-Up** and **The Midnight Express**. Both
matter later: Glow-Up permanently fuses two of Donut's best totems, and The Midnight Express is
played in the Ysalte fight.

Then she kills Garret. Deliberately, with the Knife of Frozen Tears, because a Belly Acher that dies
resets to level one and can re-bond with a new owner.

> "I had a dog once, before all this. He died protecting me, and now I'm going to protect you.
> You're going to go live with Carl. He's going to take care of you because I can't do it anymore.
> He will feed you every day, and you're going to be a good boy for me, won't you?"

Garret says "No. No, no" — more a sound than words — and pushes himself against her, unable to get
close enough. She puts him in a pet carrier, kisses him, and says "I'm sorry." Her handover
instructions are entirely practical: feed him a few times to bond, nothing undead, nothing touched
by a hobgoblin, and find out what his new **Reaper Gaze** weakness means as soon as possible.

She also asks Carl for one thing: "If you do figure out some miracle before this floor is done, make
sure that Sister Ines doesn't go down the stairs. I've met Paz a few times, and he's a good man.
Don't let her get away with keeping his card."

## Chapter 60: Denying Them the Fight

Khulan has lost her husband, and lost a little girl who was inside when it started. Ren is clear-eyed
about what's coming:

> "As soon as Khu is done crying, she is going to kill herself. She's lost everything… I don't want
> my friend killing herself to be the last thing I see. It has to be now. I want to go out having
> done something good. **All I have left for this floor is to deny them a fight. I'll take it.**"

She holds up a wrist bracer and asks Carl if he's seen *Predator*.

> "You're going to blow yourself up? Just like that?"
>
> "**They want us to fight. Me and Donut. This is me going out on my terms. You would've wiped the
> floor with us anyway. This is me telling them to go fuck themselves.**"

Her last exchange with Carl:

> "You sure about this?" I asked one last time.
>
> "**Carl. Make them suffer.**"
>
> I put my hand on the dog woman's shoulder. Her whole body trembled.
>
> "I will kill every last one of them. Every one." I leaned in to her ear, and I whispered, "I
> goddamned swear it."

Her actual last words go out to the whole dungeon in global chat:

> **Ren: Goodbye, everyone. At least I won't have to listen to anyone butcher the pronunciation of
> my name anymore. Do me a favor, and fuck them up for me. Make them all pay.**

The dome comes down "like it was a controlled detonation." Donut's frantic **WAIT, WAIT, WHAT ARE
YOU DOING?** arrives too late. "Tserendolgor never saw the message. Her name popped into the deceased
crawlers folder. It was done."

Carl's oath to Ren is the same oath he swears to Asher thirteen chapters later, in almost the same
words, on the last page of the book.

!!! note "Not confirmed in the text"
    Khulan's death is stated as a certainty by Ren but never shown. Only Tserendolgor's name enters
    the deceased-crawlers folder; Khulan is not mentioned again. Similarly, Ren's class, subclass,
    race and level are never given anywhere in Book 6.

## Book 7: This Inevitable Ruin — Legacy

Ren is dead before Book 7 opens, but the book names her repeatedly and her two bequests both turn
into load-bearing parts of the floor.

### Garret becomes Sir Rendlegore

Carl carries out the promise he made in Book 6 in chapter 5. The tummy acher zaps out onto the
saferoom table at level 1, half the size of Carl's fist, with the same pronounced underbite and
single round tooth — and no name, because the system will not let the original be reused. Mongo
immediately tries to eat him (and briefly succeeds). One bowl of chowder bonds him, which surprises
Carl: "Ren said we had to feed him a few times, but it only took one bowl."

[Donut](princess-donut.md) objects to the adoption on principle — she is still convinced Ren cheated at the pet show,
and when [Bautista](bautista.md) suggests naming him after her, she snaps "We can't name him Cheater." Carl's own
reason is simpler: "I promised Ren I would look after him. I feel obligated." [Katia](katia.md) settles the
brief: "It should be something that Ren would've liked."

It is [Louis Santiago](louis-santiago.md) who lands it, holding the meatball up like Simba:

> "Sir Rendlegore. You know, Sir, S-I-R like a knight. And then Rendlegore. You can call him Rend.
> Sir Rendlegore. It fits with the royal court theme, and it still honors the past. Plus, Ren
> would've loved it. Nobody could ever pronounce her name. She pretended it made her mad, but I
> think she kinda liked it."

> Your pet has been named Sir Rendlegore!

Rend grows through the book from fist-sized to roughly three-quarters of his former mass and
beyond, becomes Mongo's inseparable best friend, and at the end of chapter 72 saves Mongo's life by
splattering the rottweiler **Gustavo 3** with a Wrecking Ball charge. See
[Sir Rendlegore](sir-rendlegore.md) and [Mongo](mongo.md). Louis, earlier in the same book: "I feel
bad about her, about Ren. She died, but Carl figured out how to save everyone like an hour later."

### Velma, remounted

[Velma](../items/velma.md) is put back into service. [Rosetta](rosetta-thagra.md)'s crafting team modifies the
flamethrower to fit the harness of **Jamal**, the talkative hammerhead shark flesh golem, mounted on
his underbelly:

> He let out a little burst of flame from the new attachment on his underbelly. It was "Velma,"
> Tserendolgor's flamethrower. Rosetta's team in the crafting room had modified it to fit his
> harness. "Oh. Please excuse Jamal and Velma."

Her weapon type also turns up as a benchmark in the trench fighting, where a soldier with "a
Tserendolgor-like flamethrower" bakes infected Gurglers into a sealed dirt wall.

### Named in the text

She is not just an off-page memory. [Mordecai](mordecai.md) raises "some of the other items you, uh, got from
Tserendolgor" in the first days of the floor; Carl hands out five low-tier spellbooks inherited
from her squadmate **Khulan**; and both Carl and Donut receive a **Platinum Tserendolgor Box** — a
jeweled, life-sized sitting German Shepherd in an army helmet that barks once and detonates into
firecrackers. The achievement that awards it calls Carl a cheater for the card-totem trick used in
her last card battle, and he takes it personally on her behalf:

> Ren was not a cheater, and she'd never been. The AI was just being an asshole.
