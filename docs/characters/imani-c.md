# Imani C

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Crawler
    - **Role:** Nurse and swordswoman (Book 1, Meadow Lark group) → floor-defense organizer (Book 3)
    - **Race:** A winged race with ethereal, colorful butterfly wings that grant a passive Constitution buff on contact
    - **Level:** Not stated — including in Book 7, where she is a senior officer of the Princess
      Posse but never has a number attached to her on-page
    - **Party:** Independent; closely allied with [Carl](carl.md)'s party and Elle McGibbons/Li Jun's group on Floor 4. Princess Posse officer and guild leader of the airship guild (Book 7)
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** Passive Constitution-buffing wing contact (non-stacking, 10-minute refresh); an acid-negation spell/cast used to save Carl, Donut, and Katia from caustic Krakaren blood; flight and healing from Book 5 onward. In Book 7: a **wide variety of aura effects imparted by touching her wings**, some of them permanently on, including **Extreme Focus**; a head-bubble ear-protection spell; a wing-cast area shield; battlefield buffs learned from Mistress Tiatha; and Carl's [*Tripper*](../spells/tripper.md) spell, borrowed via tattoo
    - **Notable items:** Chain-conjuring scrolls, used to rig a crane system for a suspended interdiction cart; **a barbed wire tattoo circling her ankle** (Book 7), permanent, which carries Carl's *Tripper* spell

## From Meadow Lark to Floor Command

Introduced in Book 1 as a nurse and swordswoman among the elderly "Meadow Lark" evacuee group led
by [Brandon An](brandon-an.md), Imani is noted early for her high crawler-kill count —
largely from mercy-killing infected or turned patients rather than combat proper, a grim
continuation of pre-collapse triage instincts.

By Book 3, Imani has become one of the floor's most capable on-the-ground leaders. During the Iron
Tangle's Krakaren-collapse crisis, she takes de facto command of the defense at a besieged
stairwell station, organizing flamethrower crews, chokepoint rotations, and evacuation routes for
hundreds of stranded crawlers — repeatedly the voice of caution against Carl's riskier
improvisations ("Are you sure about this?"), while still fully committing to and executing them
once decided. Her passive Constitution buff (delivered via casual contact from her wings) becomes
a running, appreciated fixture of the group's defense, and her acid-negation casting directly
saves Carl, Donut, and Katia after a caustic Krakaren counterattack.

## Personality

Blunt, protective, and unflappable under pressure — she's the one who tells Prepotente and Carl
alike to "put your dicks away" mid-crisis, and the one everyone defers to for triage-style,
big-picture calls even when she isn't nominally in charge. Her working relationship with
[Elle McGibbons](elle-mcgibbons.md) and [Li Jun](li-jun.md)'s circle over the course of the floor
makes her one of the most consistently present non-party characters in Book 3.

## Book 5 Notes

Imani's wings have taken on a more solid, fire-wisped appearance by Floor 6, and she's gained the
ability to fly. She serves as Katia's second-in-command through the floor's hunter war and the
Butcher's Masquerade planning, repeatedly the voice pushing back on Carl's riskier improvisations
while still helping execute them, and personally rescues/heals multiple crawlers during the
masquerade's final battle.

## Book 6 Notes

### Her Own Totem Kills Her Squad

Floor 8's card-battle mechanic turns on Imani in the worst possible way. In a battle Carl doesn't
witness, an enemy totem possesses two of her four squadmates and forces them to fight each other. A
third — **Randall**, a former Meadowlark resident reclassed into a support-magic lutin — tries to
defuse it by freezing all four of them in place. Then Imani's own **Amhuluk** totem, a mossy
disease-dealing lizard, casts a poison cloud and kills all four in a single attack.

> They died frozen in place, their skin all melting away. The scene ended with Imani screaming while
> the Amhuluk finished off the enemy deckmaster and won the battle.

The system gives her **four player-killer skulls** for it. Carl: "The four player killer skulls was
just a slap to the face. It was the same thing they'd done to Florin when Lucia Mar had killed
Ifechi." She already had twelve.

Afterwards she skips her own final memory battle — her family home in Detroit — and sits staring into
space, shaking, talking to nobody but Elle. Bautista makes her tea she won't drink.

!!! note "Only one of the four is named"
    Randall is the only squadmate the book names. The other three are described only as "former
    Meadowlark residents I didn't know very well."

### Refusing the Key

When Carl offers her his exit key, and floats using his one-shot Mysterious Bone Key on himself
instead:

> "And I'm not taking your key," Imani added. "Donut told me about your dumbass idea to use one of
> your own bones or whatever to get out. **People have bones for a reason, Carl. No. Like Elle said.
> It's all or nothing.**"

### The Sluggalos

She quietly undoes one of Carl's crueller decisions by curing the mercenaries of slug disease — "the
room healer refused to do it. She and that Bucket Boy kid said you told them not to" / "Yes, Carl. I
am a healer. It's what I do."

She then ends up commanding the forty-odd **sluggalos** that come out of the strippers, mostly level
20s and 30s with a few 50s, in the assault on the FOCSA building. She herds them physically — when
they scatter, "Imani yelled something at them, and they all lowered their heads and turned back
toward the church compound. One had a little hatchet attached to the side of its neck, and it
started waving it threateningly at Imani, and she pointed a stern finger at it, and it backed off."
The slugs address her as "313."

She is also one of the three people who ride the stairwell chamber out of Cuba with Carl and Donut at
the very end, and her last line in the book is the one she says with her wings wrapped around both of
them:

> "No matter what happens, none of us are getting out of this unscathed. We gain things, and we lose
> them. That's what happens in war. **Thank you, Carl and Donut. Thank you for not giving up on me.**"

## Book 7: This Inevitable Ruin

### The buffer

Imani spends Floor 9 doing the job she has been doing since Book 3, at army scale. She holds the
field hospital at the Posse's headquarters with Bucket Boy under her, trains on battlefield
enhancement spells in her upgraded magic room with **Mistress Tiatha**, runs the airship guild's
portal permissions, and works the trench line buffing soldiers one at a time:

> “I was buffing people down the line,” Imani said. “And before that, I was helping with the
> trebuchet. I buffed the guy aiming the thing. He’s ready to go.”

She fights in the crater assault with Donut and Mongo, landing with two soldiers slung over each
shoulder, and afterward is tasked with saving as many injured as she can rather than pushing forward. Later, outside the
Madness castle, she is the one who briefs a battalion on what they are walking into, raising her
voice "supernaturally" as she flies the line:

> “Keep an eye on the person to your left and to your right. The Madness is known for their mind
> magic. That and Atrocity magic. If you see someone acting unusual, call it out.”

### Extreme Focus

Her auras have expanded into a whole category — Carl notes that of the two crawlers in the guild who
use aura mechanics at all, "One was Imani, who had a wide variety of aura effects imparted by
touching her wings. Some were persistent, and she couldn’t turn them off."

**Extreme Focus** is the one that gets named and explained, and only because it misfires. During the
defense of the castle, Carl notices that Donut, Tipid and Splash Zone have all become manic — talking
too fast, unable to stay on topic in the middle of a battle. Imani, talking fast herself, diagnoses
it:

> “It’s a side effect of the Extreme Focus buff,” a new voice said, settling next to me. Imani, but
> she, too, was talking faster than she normally did… “Its effectiveness just doubled when I landed
> in the castle. Everyone in this zone is given a buff to their dexterity, and it helps with
> multitasking. But when it’s a little too powerful, it makes chatty people, like Donut, extra
> chatty. It’s like we’ve all been given a dose of speed. It doesn’t seem to be affecting you.
> Probably because of your Mind Focus, but I’m not sure.”

That is the whole of what the book says about it: a zone-wide dexterity and multitasking buff, doubled
by some castle-related condition, with a speed-jitter side effect on anyone already inclined to talk.

### The tattoo

To crack the naga stronghold the Posse needs someone other than Carl casting
[*Tripper*](../spells/tripper.md) — his version
comes from **Drakea's Enchanted Kerchief of Disorder**, and neither Imani nor Elle can wear the item
at all, because "fairy-class, and other magical races, were extremely limited on the sort of magical
gear they could wear."

So Katia tattoos the spell onto her instead, using toraline yam ink mixed with Carl's blood. Carl
feels it fire from across the map:

> Crawler Imani C has used your Tripper spell.
>
> Imani: Okay, I used your spell, Carl. Katia, your tattoo worked. Lots of traps just went off.
>
> …
>
> Carl: Yeah, I noticed. I got a notification when you used it. It kinda hurt.
>
> Imani: Oh wow, really? I’m sorry about that.

> Donut: IT BETTER HAVE WORKED AFTER YOU FORCED IMANI TO PERMANENTLY DISFIGURE HERSELF.

The borrowed spell will fade over roughly four days; the tattoo is permanent.

> Anyway, Imani’s tattoo was a small, barbed wire symbol that circled her ankle. I hadn’t seen it yet,
> but when Donut learned Katia was planning on that style of tattoo, she loudly voiced her objection.
>
> Donut: THIS ISN’T THE 1990’s. I SWEAR TO GOD IF IMANI STARTS USING THE PHRASE “HOME SKILLET,” I’M
> GOING TO LOSE MY MIND.

Carl reads Donut's complaining correctly — it is guilt, not aesthetics. Imani accepting a permanent
mark on her body so that Donut can get off the floor is a cost Donut does not know how to carry.

### What Carl saw

In the *Several Days Earlier* interlude, a dream-sharing potion goes wrong and Carl's mind floods
outward over everyone in the tent. Imani had been the one physically separating him and Katia when it
happened, which is why she was close enough to be caught:

> And Imani. Oh, god. Imani. Most of what I saw with the others was emotion and maybe an inkling at
> what caused it, but with her, and Katia, I saw more. I saw what Imani carried. It was just a
> glimpse, but it was so, so much. She’d fled her home in Detroit, and her older brothers and sisters
> blamed her for leaving the family. But she had to. She had to get away. She’d earned her nursing
> certificate and found a job in what she considered the middle of nowhere. A relatively small,
> beautiful town where drugs and violence and despair were just something one saw on the news. She
> was always chasing peace despite the overwhelming guilt she carried, like a heavy sack she couldn’t
> put down. **She was sick of being looked at as the one in charge, the one who had to make the tough
> decisions. She didn’t want that responsibility, yet it always fell to her, despite her always being
> the youngest one in the room.**
>
> **She was in love with Chris, and I had no idea.** She loved his simplicity. His quiet kindness.

Carl knows immediately that he has done something he cannot undo:

> It felt so private, so dirty for me to have learned about this in such a way. Like I had broken into
> her house and spied her utterly naked, all without her knowing.
>
> She hadn’t invited me into her mind, and I should have looked away. But I couldn’t. I didn’t know
> how. My only solace was that I knew—somehow I knew—I’d forget much of this when I finally woke up.

He never tells her, and neither of them ever raises it. See [Chris Andrews](chris-andrews.md).

!!! note "Unresolved"
    Nothing comes of it in Book 7. The closest the two of them get on-page is a single moment before
    the Madness assault, with both of them expecting to die: "I watched as Imani walked up and gave
    Chris a quick hug using her wings, not her arms. Chris reached up, as if to touch her face, but
    stopped just short."

### Elsewhere on the floor

- She and Elle run the Posse's recruiting for most of the ceasefire.
- She takes the four-cart infiltration team into the naga stronghold with Elle, sweeping traps by hand
  because Prepotente scattered the expendable sluggalos that were supposed to go in first.
- She is the only officer who argues *against* giving Li Na the spare Ring of Divine Suffering.
- She is frozen with everyone else by the **Bear Witness** spell and freed by Donut's *War Crime*.
- In the Reaver castle she throws a wing-cast shield none of the party had seen before, covering Elle,
  Holger and the strippers through a blast, and then wakes Louis from a paralytic.
- She and Rosetta open Princess Lunette to extract the donor heart and lungs for Louis's transplant,
  and Imani is the one who places them in his chest: "Organs incoming!"
- She delivers Kiwi's babies in a tent in the middle of the siege, shouting Bucket Boy out of the room
  for nearly vomiting.
- She is in the tent for Katia's goodbye, and alive at the end of the book.

## Book 8: A Parade of Horribles

### Jacobus, Permanently

Imani's tooth-fairy T'Ghee card **Jacobus** — a Danish-speaking "Reverse Tooth Fairy," level 90,
established in Book 7 — is caught in the chaos goddess Eris's Floor 10 visit. One of Eris's random
effects makes a nearby temporary condition permanent, and it lands on him:

> "I just received a notification that he is now permanently summoned and can no longer be used as a
> card."

From that point on Jacobus simply follows Imani around as a standing companion rather than a card she
can hold in reserve — squirting water in reaction to conversations, delighting openly in Akuma's
torture, and no longer something she can put away.

### A Target

Imani is confirmed as one of the two named targets of the Syndicate assassin **Minus**, posing as
Elle's superfan "Linus" — see [Elle McGibbons](elle-mcgibbons.md#book-8-a-parade-of-horribles) and
[Supporting cast](supporting-cast.md) for the reveal and unmasking. Carl's own retrospective
judgment, once the assassination plot is exposed: "That alien had been correct to target her for
assassination... it'd become clear that Imani and Katia were the true bedrocks of our ability to
stay organized."
