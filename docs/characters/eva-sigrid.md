# Eva Sigrid

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Crawler
    - **Race:** A nagini/orc hybrid — cobra-faced, snake-bodied from the waist down
    - **Class:** Nimblefoot Enforcer
    - **Group:** Formerly [Katia](katia.md)'s closest friend on Earth; later a senior member, then leader, of Brynhild's Daughters — by Book 5, operating alone
    - **Level:** 55 (Book 5)
    - **Status:** **Deceased** — killed by Katia at the end of Book 5, after over 50 player-killer skulls
    - **Party:** [Hekla](hekla.md)'s Brynhild's Daughters, until its collapse
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** Not described in detail; fights with a matched pair of enchanted sabers
    - **Notable items:** **The Left Fang of the Green Sultan** — half of a matched saber set, non-magical without its twin (lost to Katia after the fight that kills Hekla); a poisoned rapier prosthetic replacing her severed top-left hand (Book 3–5); an escape ring worn on her top-right hand (Book 5); the reforming [Crown of the Sepsis Whore](../items/crown-of-the-sepsis-whore.md), carried openly on the back shoulder of her breastplate, disguised as decoration, as of Book 5

## Katia's Oldest Friend

Eva and Katia entered the dungeon together, sharing the same game guide through the early floors.
On reaching Floor 3, the pair should have entered character creation and race/class selection
together — but Eva went first, transformed, and left the saferoom before Katia was ready, later
telling her she'd "accidentally made one of the swordsmen guards angry" and fled town on a
caravan, reconnecting with [Hekla](hekla.md) in a different city entirely. This account is
revealed as at least partly self-serving: a Book 3 recap episode shows Eva abandoning Katia on the
third floor deliberately, a betrayal she later throws in Katia's face outright during their final
confrontation ("This is why I left you behind on the third floor").

## The Plot Against Hekla's Own Party

By Book 3, Eva is co-architect (alongside Hekla) of a scheme to have Carl provoked into attacking
Brynhild's Daughters' healer Silfa, using the resulting justification to kill Carl and pressure
Donut into abandoning her own party for Hekla's. When the plot collapses aboard a moving train
during a shared battle, Eva reveals the entire scheme to Katia's face, seemingly out of pure
cruelty rather than necessity — a psychological attack that appears to be the final catalyst for
the chaotic brawl that follows. See [Hekla](hekla.md) for the full sequence.

## Survival and Disappearance

Unlike Hekla, Eva survives the confrontation — Katia's **Rush** attack, aimed at her, misses and
kills Hekla instead. Eva loses her saber (**The Left Fang of the Green Sultan**, looted by Katia)
but escapes the wreck of the train. In the immediate aftermath, Brynhild's Daughters disbands
entirely; Eva, deeply unpopular with the group's remaining members, nominally inherits leadership
of what's left of it. Carl spends the rest of the floor trying and failing to relocate her through
contacts among the former Daughters — by the time the floor ends, her whereabouts and intentions
remain unknown and unresolved.

## Book 5: Death at the Butcher's Masquerade

Eva resurfaces on Floor 6 with over 50 player-killer skulls and four hunter-killer marks, having
abandoned or lost every remaining member of her old group. At the Butcher's Masquerade, she goads
Katia in front of the assembled hunters, tries to steal potions meant for the whole party, and
appears to be coordinating — loosely, alongside Vrah's hunters — toward breaking the party's peace
seal for her own escape. When Tsarina Signet's sacrifice shatters the seal first, Eva flees the
resulting chaos rather than fight.

Katia and Li Na track her down, half-buried and near death from a dinosaur mauling, in the
battle's aftermath. Li Na casts a spell (*Preserve Injury*) preventing her from healing herself;
Katia cuts off Eva's top-right hand (which bears an escape ring) and the poisoned rapier stub on
her top-left arm, leaving her only her two lower hands, then executes her with **The Left Fang of
the Green Sultan** — the same saber looted from her back in Book 3 — finally closing the loop from
her Book 3 betrayal. As she dies from the blow, Eva uses her one remaining free hand to pull a
piece she'd carried the whole time on the back shoulder of her own armor, disguised as decoration:
the reforming **[Crown of the Sepsis Whore](../items/crown-of-the-sepsis-whore.md)**. Eva is
already dead by the moment the crown actually equips itself, transferring onto Katia's bare
head — a last, deliberate act of spite that leaves Katia newly entangled in the Blood Sultanate
succession curse Donut has carried since losing the crown at the end of the third floor.
