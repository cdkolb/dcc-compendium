# Milk

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** NPC — an indentured former crawler, guildmaster inside
      [Club Vanquisher](../locations/club-vanquisher.md)
    - **Race:** **Xenopus** — a winged clawed-frog race chosen in the dungeon. Birth race:
      **Vesper** (a bat). Carl reads her on sight as “half frog, half bat”
    - **Class:** **Cartographer**
    - **Subclass:** None stated
    - **Level:** Top level **73** during her own crawl. No current level is shown
    - **Role:** Guildmaster of *both* hidden guilds on the club’s second floor — the **Cartography
      (Map-Making) Guild** and the **Calligrapher’s Guild**; author of the **6th edition** of
      [The Dungeon Anarchist’s Cookbook](../items/the-dungeon-anarchists-cookbook.md)
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** **Words of Power** — calligraphy magic, written onto skin or
      objects. She scrawls a power word on her own wrist to survive a demon’s fire, etches one on her
      own throat to magically amplify her voice, and writes them over a tattoo to boost a spell’s
      level. She can glide on her wings, and long ago wrote a scroll that mutes the dungeon’s
      announcements to her
    - **Notable items:** None worn. She gives Carl **825 ml of Stayfast Ink** and an **Adept’s
      Fountain Pen**, and keeps the maps of Club Vanquisher’s hidden entrances and exits
    - **Status:** **Alive** and, by the end of Book 7, **free** — “I fulfilled my contract. I still
      have to live out the season, but I can leave after this.” She stays behind on Floor 9 rather
      than transferring to the 12th

## Overview

Milk is the cookbook author who was in reach the entire time. Her contract, per her status card,
holds her until “her services are used at least 333 times in the dungeon” — and at the point of her
interlude, across an indefinite number of seasons, the counter reads **6**. Nobody comes to the
calligraphy guild. Nobody comes to the cartography guild next door. Both doors are hidden, and she
has not had a one-on-one with a floor supervisor in over fifty seasons.

So she sleeps. Her own interlude opens with her waking up starving in a dusty nest of straw, her
wings sore and atrophied from disuse, unsure what season it is:

> Warning. Your health levels are low due to starvation. You are not allowed to self-terminate, and
> if you expire due to starvation, you will be saved and time will be added to your indentureship
> contract.
>
> “Oh, fuck off,” she muttered.

The self-assessment she cannot get away from:

> You were a crawler. You fought. You were part of something, once. And now, look at you. All you do
> is sleep. You’ve missed entire seasons more times than you can count. You’re a coward, that’s what
> you are. When was the last time you even tried to help someone? Your family would be ashamed.

## The Door Opens

Her interlude is set during Book 4, on Floor 5, and the crawlers who kick her awake are
[Miriam Dom](miriam-dom.md) and [Prepotente](prepotente.md), on the run after Miriam’s vampirism got
her club access revoked. Prepotente’s hellspawn familiar **Bianca** sets fire to Milk’s carpet, her
newsletters and her scroll linen, and tries to incinerate Milk herself — who survives by scribbling
a power word onto her own skin in the half-second she has.

Miriam then does something Milk says she has never seen a crawler do in all her cycles: she stops to
heal an NPC, rubbing salve into the burn on Milk’s arm while apologising for her son.

Milk warns them they will not be allowed out of the club alive — and gets a system warning for it —
then finds the loophole that lets her help anyway:

> “I sure hope you don’t threaten me,” she said to the pair. “If you threaten me, I might be forced
> to give you information.”

Miriam pulls a knife and asks what sort of information.

> Milk grinned. “Information on the many secret entrances and exits from Club Vanquisher, including
> the one in the back of this very room. I have a map, and I hope you don’t steal it from me.”

## “Milk?”

Carl finds her by accident at the end of Chapter 55, half-dead and stiff-legged, fleeing the
Sepsis Whore fight through Club Vanquisher with [Katia](katia.md), Prepotente, **Li Jun**, **Zhang**
and the sluggalette **Bigs**. Prepotente presses a wall panel, and the party falls through into the
Calligrapher’s Guild.

He recognises her from her own pages before he reads her name — she had written extensively about
the difference between Xenopus wings and Vesper wings.

> “Milk?” I asked.
>
> And even though she didn’t know who I was before this, in that moment, she did. I could see it. I
> could feel it. This was my sister, and I was her brother, and we now both knew it.

It breaks him open. This is the chapter immediately after Carl detonated a bomb at his own feet to
kill Vinata, and he has been holding the aftermath of being torn apart and reassembled entirely
inside himself:

> And up until that moment, I’d been holding it all back. Those moments when I wasn’t there and I
> wasn’t here. Those horrific seconds as I’d reformed. It was like all the pain anyone had ever felt,
> all shoved into a single moment. I had borne it because what other choice did I have?…
>
> I didn’t break. No. I wasn’t broken. I will not be broken.
>
> “Milk,” I whispered a second time. This time, it wasn’t a question.
>
> “Child, come here,” the strange creature said, wrapping me in leathery wings. She held on me as I
> pretended like I wasn’t, finally, sobbing.
>
> “It’s not over yet,” I managed to whisper. “Not even close.”
>
> “I know. Don’t you worry. It’s okay,” she said, stroking me with her wings…
>
> “Let it out,” she whispered. “It’s okay. It’s okay.”

The rest of the party watches this “absolutely bewildered.” Katia, Li Jun and Zhang all clearly want
to ask and are all smart enough not to.

## Kidnapped, Technically

She refuses to leave with them, and the system will not let them hire her. Rosetta’s advice is
blunt: kidnap her. Tying up and “torturing” a guildmaster for their information turns out to be not
merely legal but encouraged. So Milk is bound, carried out through the temple bathroom, and held in
the **FUPA** — with everyone involved keeping up the pretence for the rest of the book. By Chapter
74 her wrists are “loosely bound” and she is untying herself to help right a tipped-over tortoise.

Her maps immediately become part of the war effort. Katia, mid-operation: “Thank Milk. Her maps are
insanely accurate.”

## Words of Power

The magic system arrives with her. In Chapter 74, **Edgar** the tortoise tattoos Donut with a
Dinner Bell variant spell that has to be pushed above level eight to work, and Rosetta explains how:
“It’s gotta be a tattoo, and Milk here is going to etch words of power over it to increase the
spell’s level.”

> “Not quite yet, child. We must get it temporarily above level eight. This may sting a little,”
> Milk said. She came forward and produced a pen, dripping with black ink. Her hands were no longer
> bound. She wrote some words atop the tattoo with a flourish as Donut hissed with pain. I didn’t
> recognize the script, but when I examined the characters, the system simply said, Words of Power.

She then dips the pen into her shirt and it comes out purple.

> “It’s a magic style that I haven’t seen used often in the dungeon. The calligraphy guild teaches
> it.”

The effect fades as the ink fades: “As the ink fades, the power will decrease, but if you practice
using the spell at that high of a level, it will assist greatly with training.”

Asked how many magic systems there are, she gives the fullest statement of her craft in the book:

> “There’s more than you can imagine. Even with just ink and calligraphy and maps, there are dozens
> of different methods. It is dangerous, though, attempting to learn too much. There’s a lot the
> right kind of ink can do. But, over the turns, I’ve learned the danger of using recipes I don’t
> quite understand. Inks and letters that shine the brightest burn out the fastest.”

And then the confession underneath it, delivered with a webbed hand on Carl’s cheek:

> “I had a book once, and I wrote several of my recipes in it. But later, I switched to a different
> type of ink. It was an attempt to share my knowledge with a wider group, and it wasn’t until
> recently did I learn all that I wrote after I changed methods faded and disappeared.”… “Some of my
> best recipes were lost, and it wasn’t until they were needed the most did I learn they were gone.
> It is my fault that I didn’t write them properly.”

That is why Carl found nothing in her entries about the fifth-floor yam gift when he looked, three
times, in Chapter 47. It is also why she hands him **Stayfast Ink** — 825 ml of it, “Enough ink for
several chonky books,” ink that nothing will ever make fade — and an **Adept’s Fountain Pen**, with a
pointed nudge toward
[The Filthy Little Crawler’s Book of Voodoo](../items/the-filthy-little-crawlers-book-of-voodoo.md):

> “He also has another book. One that all of you have. The Book of Voodoo, I believe it is called.
> Anything he writes in it will be given to everybody who already has the book. Best of luck to you.”

Her people’s saying, offered as the lesson: “‘No single recipe is a feast in itself.’ Meaning there’s
always another way.”

!!! note "Not the party's tattooist"
    The tattoo work in Book 7 is **Edgar** the tortoise’s. Milk’s contribution is the calligraphy
    written *over* a finished tattoo, and the ink formulation advice that let Edgar work without
    toraline. Katia is present for several of these scenes but is not involved in the tattooing.

## The Last Meal

By Chapter 82 she has stopped hiding entirely — Carl spots her floating above the third ring
of Larracos, lecturing a large crowd of crawlers. In the final chapter it becomes clear what the
lecture was.

She amplifies her voice with a word etched on her own throat and presents the departing crawlers’
gift: every crawler taking a deal has painted three skills or spells onto pages — one for Carl, one
for Donut, one left open in the Book of Voodoo for anyone. The book is updated “with a metric
fuckton of entries” and splits into two volumes, the second titled *Carl’s Book of Boom*.

> “They’re leaving the dungeon, so I taught them how to remove a skill or spell and paint it onto a
> page.”

And of the former crawlers, who cannot transfer anything:

> “They already gave what they could. And some gave everything they had.”

That act is the one that finishes her sentence, 333 services rendered:

> “I did it,” she said. “I fulfilled my contract. I still have to live out the season, but I can
> leave after this.” She grinned at me. “I am glad I was given the chance to cook one final meal.”

She does not transfer to the 12th floor. She goes back to her guild.

> “Goodbye, Carl,” Milk said. “If you can sneak back into Club Vanquisher, come say hello. But if
> not, that’s okay. You have given me something I didn’t think I had anymore. Burn it all to the
> ground.”
>
> I gave my sister a hug.

In the epilogue, **Quasar** reviews Carl’s own exit offers and dismisses one for having “a contingent
exit clause that’s almost as bad as that clause your bizarre friend Milk had.”
