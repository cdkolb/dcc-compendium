# Hekla

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Origin:** Iceland
    - **Group:** Leader of **Brynhild's Daughters**, an "ever-growing crowd of female warriors"
    - **Signature gear:** Snow gear, a Valkyrie-style helmet, and an enchanted repeating crossbow
      that fires firebolts
    - **Notable kill:** A Yeti Borough Boss, alongside three other women
    - **Type/Class/Subclass:** Not shown in Book 1 → **Amazonian** race / **Shieldmaiden** class, revealed Book 2
    - **Level:** 28 (end of Book 2, 2nd on the Floor 3 leaderboard) — **killed in Book 3**
    - **Status:** **Deceased** — killed on Floor 4 by [Katia](katia.md), accidentally, during a botched murder plot of her own making
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** No named spells; combat shown is physical (throwing a bear-sized monster) plus ranged crossbow fire
    - **Notable items:** An enchanted, automatic repeating crossbow that fires firebolts ("like a ranged chainsaw") — looted after death as the [Enchanted Repeating Crossbow of the Scavenger Mother of Mothers](../items/enchanted-repeating-crossbow-of-the-scavenger-mother-of-mothers.md); snow gear; a Valkyrie-style helmet; a later "glittering breastplate"

## Overview

In Book 1, Hekla is a broadcast-only figure — Carl and Donut never meet her, but see her
repeatedly on recap episodes. She's first introduced simply as "a woman dressed in snow gear and
what looked like a Valkyrie helmet," shown plowing through a group of gnolls with her firebolt
crossbow before the show later gives her a name and backstory. In Book 2, this changes — she
appears as a live guest on the talk show *Danger Zone* alongside Carl and Donut, their first
direct interaction.

## Brynhild's Daughters

Hekla leads a growing all-female group of warriors who take the name **Brynhild's Daughters**.
Carl witnesses her casually kill a bear-sized tentacle monster by picking it up and throwing it
into the air before shooting it twice with her crossbow — strong enough that he estimates she's
"likely twice as powerful" as he is at that point in the book. She and three other members of her
group later team up to kill a **Yeti Borough Boss**, one of only a handful of borough-tier kills
shown across the season's recaps.

## The Broadcast's Narrative

The show leans hard into a "Nordic shieldmaiden" persona for Hekla once her name and home country
are revealed, and the in-universe AI is described as deliberately feeding her loot to reinforce
it — including, per Carl's description, "a ridiculous, glittering breastplate that seemed more
apt for the cover of a harem fantasy novel than appropriate dungeon armor." This is one of several
moments in Book 1 where the production is shown actively shaping a crawler's gear and image for
entertainment value rather than pure survival utility.

## Book 2: Direct Contact

Hekla's group is scattered upon entering Floor 3 (they had different game guides) but mostly
regroups, taking out an Owlbear Borough Boss along the way. Her race/class reveal — **Amazonian**
(bodybuilder physique) and **Shieldmaiden** — lands her **2nd on the Floor 3 leaderboard** at
Level 28, one spot behind Lucia Mar. She appears live alongside Carl and Donut on the talk show
*Danger Zone*, and asks them to help a stray member of Brynhild's Daughters who's stranded near
their location — Carl agrees, cautiously.

Odette privately warns Carl afterward that Hekla isn't evil, but is practical and cunning, and
has become "obsessed" with recruiting Donut into her own group specifically to gain permanent
access to a manager like Mordecai — a plot thread that comes to a head, and ends, in Book 3.

## Book 3: The Plot and the Death

On Floor 4, Hekla's ambitions curdle into an outright murder scheme, orchestrated with
[Eva Sigrid](eva-sigrid.md). Per Carl's own read of the situation afterward, Hekla had built her
entire party around herself and her signature crossbow — too many healers and mages, not enough
damage dealers, no real tank — and likely sensed the group was unsustainable long-term. Her plan:
have Carl provoked into attacking Brynhild's Daughters' healer, **Silfa**, as bait, "justifying" a
retaliatory kill on Carl and using the resulting chaos to isolate Donut into joining her group for
permanent manager access.

The plot collapses aboard a moving train during a joint battle against a ghoul horde. Hekla shoots
Katia with two invisible, magically untraceable **Dirty Little Phased Bolts**, designed to kill
slowly via bleed-and-amnesia damage that would leave no evidence of foul play — a shot only
spotted because Donut's new [Prism goggles](../items/prism-industries-capacitating-and-focusing-goggles.md)
reveal it in real time. In the brawl that follows, Donut blasts Hekla with a full-power
[Magic Missile](../spells/magic-missile.md), Eva reveals the entire plot to Katia's face out of
apparent cruelty, and Katia's **Rush** — canonically unreliable in both range and aim — goes off
while she's aiming for Eva, instead slamming into Hekla and killing her outright by force of
impact against the train wall.

Katia becomes the season's first crawler to claim a top-10 bounty, earning the 500,000-gold prize
tied to Hekla's #2 ranking and jumping from Level 24 to 37 in the process. Donut reanimates
Hekla's corpse with [Second Chance](../spells/second-chance.md), then
[Clockwork Triplicate](../spells/clockwork-triplicate.md)s it into three bodies to hold off the
rest of Brynhild's Daughters while Carl and Katia loot the remains — recovering only her legs and
half her torso, plus her signature crossbow and a key.

### Aftermath

Brynhild's Daughters disbands within hours. The captured-then-released healer Silfa confirms the
group has lost everything and that Eva, deeply unpopular, has inherited leadership of a group that
no longer really exists. A recap-show tribute episode fills in Hekla's full origin: she entered
the dungeon fighting with her husband, left him poisoned and dying, returned to find him being
devoured by rats, and killed one with his own exposed rib bone — winning a Legendary Grrl Power
box that's implied to be the actual origin of her crossbow. The same episode reveals that Katia
and Eva shared a game guide entering Floor 3, and that Eva abandoned Katia there deliberately,
later finding and joining Hekla instead — the specific betrayal Eva throws in Katia's face during
the fatal confrontation.

Odette is later revealed to have personally (not through her husband, as Carl briefly assumed)
sponsored Hekla — motive unstated, though her own advice ("don't trust anybody until you know
their motivation") applies as much to her as anyone.
