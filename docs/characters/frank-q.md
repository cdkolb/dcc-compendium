# Frank Q

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type/Class/Subclass (Book 3):** Night Elf / Blood Assassin
    - **Level (Book 3):** 17 — significantly behind Carl and Donut's pace
    - **Notable items:** Gives Carl the [Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine
      Suffering](../items/enchanted-night-wyrms-ring-of-divine-suffering.md)
    - **Status:** Killed on Floor 4, reportedly by [Chris Andrews](chris-andrews.md)

## Book 1: The Fake Fed

Frank Q first appears posing as an ICE agent working with his wife Maggie My (in truth, a
Seattle PD detective) on a fabricated "Customs Enforcement" cover story. He murders and robs
fellow crawler Rebecca W., and later confronts Carl and Donut in a safe room alongside Maggie —
both are stopped by the safe zone's anti-violence rules before real harm is done. He's left
missing a hand by the end of that encounter.

## Book 3: The Bar at the Desperado Club

Carl runs into Frank again on Floor 4, drunk and alone at a Desperado Club bar branch, still
missing his right hand (having picked a race that couldn't regenerate it). His character sheet
now reads Level 17, Night Elf, Blood Assassin — badly behind for the floor, a purple-skinned,
fanged figure still incongruously wearing his old Seahawks beanie.

Frank fills in the gap since Book 1: Maggie left him at the end of Floor 2 after a fight about
Carl and Donut, cutting off contact entirely; he's drifted from group to group since, always
eventually driven out once his player-killer skulls make him impossible to trust. He confirms
their old cover story — Customs Enforcement for him, Seattle PD for Maggie — and reveals he's
been unable to stop thinking about their daughter **Yvette**, whom Maggie choked to death back
on Floor 1 after Carl's dynamite trap went wrong.

Frank's version of "revenge" isn't violence — it's a gift. He hands Carl the
**[Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering](../items/enchanted-night-wyrms-ring-of-divine-suffering.md)**,
the item he says ruined his and Maggie's lives, explaining that owning something this valuable
and this dangerous will do to Carl exactly what it did to them — and that understanding the
consequence, not just hearing about it, is the only revenge he has left to offer for Yvette's
death. Carl accepts it, unable to refuse something this valuable, and the two part on unexpectedly
non-hostile terms: "Goodbye, Frank."

## Death

Shortly after the bar conversation, Frank is killed — reported to Carl secondhand via a bartender
contact ("Sledgie"), who says Frank got into a fight with another crawler who killed him and
walked out. That crawler is identified as [Chris Andrews](chris-andrews.md), leaving Carl
uncertain whether the timing (so soon after Frank shared his history with Carl) was coincidence.

## Update — Book 4

Book 4 confirms it wasn't coincidence, and it wasn't really Chris: **Maggie My** had already fully
possessed Chris's body via an Infiltrator (Scree) parasite by the time of the killing. Maggie
tracked Frank down at the Desperado Club — likely hunting for the [Ring of Divine
Suffering](../items/enchanted-night-wyrms-ring-of-divine-suffering.md) Frank had just given away —
and killed her own husband using Chris as a weapon. See [Chris Andrews](chris-andrews.md) for the
full reveal.

## Update — Book 6: Leveled-Up Frank

Frank comes back as a card.

The Floor 8 boss built out of Carl's own past — the **Reminiscence Hydra of Malicious Compliance**,
nine heads, each one somebody Carl knew — carries a four-card deck, and the fourth totem in it is
Frank Q. Carl finds it unsummoned in the loot spread floating over the spot in the burned-out trailer
where his father died.

> The fourth was **Leveled-Up Frank**. That was what it was called. It was crawler Frank Q. Husband
> to Maggie My and father to Yvette. He'd been long dead, killed by a Maggie-controlled Chris way back
> on the Iron Tangle.
>
> This wasn't really him just like Alpha Carl really wasn't me. He was a **level-90 Blood Assassin
> Night Elf**, and I was glad the hydra never had a chance to summon him because he would've been a
> fast, tough opponent. That, and he would've had a ton of stupid shit to say.

The card is never played. What matters is the precedent it establishes, which Carl grasps
immediately:

> "Jesus Christ," I muttered, examining the totem. **The existence of Frank as a card opened up a
> new, horrific possibility I hadn't even considered. I hadn't seen a single instance of this in the
> cookbook, of them bringing back dead crawlers. There were a lot of people who wouldn't be able to
> handle that.**

Before examining anything else in the pile, he sends a warning to every crawler on the floor:
"Remember, it's not really them. Especially not the cards. Don't let them get under your skin. Don't
let them break you."

The precedent is real, and the book proves it twice more: Sister Ines turns the dying crawler
**[Paz Lo](paz-lo.md)** into a totem at the moment of his death, and Paz then kills a goddess as
somebody else's summon. See [Game Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md) for the mechanic itself.

!!! note "Card name"
    The printed card name is "**Leveled-Up Frank**." The level and class come from Carl's narration,
    not from a card title, and no stat block is shown.
