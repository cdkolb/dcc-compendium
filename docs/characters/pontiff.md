# Pontiff

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Hired mercenary NPC
    - **Race:** Taurin (see [Species: Taurine](../species/index.md))
    - **Class:** Level 83 **Rhythm Rider** — a mage class built around sound-wave magic
    - **Prior job:** Dancer at the [Penis Parade](../dungeon-factions/penis-parade-crew.md), fired by
      **[Damascus Steel](damascus-steel.md)** before the book begins
    - **Role:** Team One Fine Pig's hired muscle, then Carl's crew's dedicated driver from Heat 6
      onward
    - **Status:** Deliberately gives up his mercenary contract to test an escape route; confirmed
      alive in Sheol in the epilogue

## A Complicated Reputation

Pontiff is introduced secondhand, at the mercenary market, through **Bucket Boy**'s recognition:

> "Hey, hey, I know that guy! The other one. The Taurin. His name is Pontiff. He *was* a dancer but
> Damascus sent him back to the mercenary market because he was a dick."

Bucket Boy has personal history with him — Pontiff once slapped him for "getting in the way" during
a stage routine, with no audience even present. He'd been part of the
[Penis Parade](../dungeon-factions/penis-parade-crew.md) roster before [Damascus
Steel](damascus-steel.md) let him go. He's first seen hired onto **Team One Fine Pig** as a level 83
Taurin Rhythm Rider, fighting with a massive glowing mallet. He briefly reunites with
**[Corcunda](corcunda.md)** — a fellow former stripper — with nothing more said than "We used to work
together," before she's carried off, drugged, on a rival team.

## Driving for Carl

Rosetta and Tipid hire him directly ahead of Heat 6, "Ad Infinitum," as the crew's driver. Carl
overhears him mid-apology to Bucket Boy just before the race:

> "Believe me, kid. I would take it all back if I could. But I can't, so all I can do is make amends
> moving forward."

It's the clearest hint that the mercenary-market firing wasn't the whole story, and that he's
actively trying to change. He proves an excellent driver through the "Ad Infinitum" what-if
apartments — rocket boosts, spider-leg wall-climbing, and his own sound magic smoothing at least one
tight maneuver ("The spell makes this very easy," threading a *Hole*-spell shortcut) — and reacts
right alongside the crawlers to the floor's horrors, from the shell-people's sewn-shut eyes to
Samantha's disembodied hologram head popping up over the dash. See
[Game Mechanics: The "What-If" Apartment Simulations](../mechanics/index.md#the-what-if-apartment-simulations-ad-infinitum-book-8)
for the race itself.

Afterward, Carl keeps him on the payroll specifically to investigate a string of missing strippers
tied to the [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md) — on the theory that it's "worth a shot,"
even though he half expects Pontiff to simply disappear like the others.

## The Nothing Portal

The Desperado Club casino's Wheel of Fortune has a two-spot "Nothing" slot, and Pontiff volunteers to
find out what it actually is. He buys a portal-examination spell and works out a test with Rosetta:
if he steps through, survives, *and* is dropped from Carl's employ as a mercenary, that specifically
means he's been pulled somewhere outside the normal playing field — proof the exit is real.

> "I keep thinking back to the type of person I once was. I have made a change. But knowing what I
> know now, I know simply changing myself is not enough. I do not know if this will help you, but no
> matter what happens, you will have information. And this information may help those who I once
> called 'friend.'"

Carl's last words to him are simple thanks, plus one request: relay a message to **Herot**, warning
him that people who still worship gods might be coming through, and that it's fine to hold them in
stasis until the gods start losing power in the Ascendency battles.

The system confirms it worked:

> **Your Mercenary Pontiff has left the playing field and is no longer considered a mercenary. You
> will no longer be charged.**

Carl recognizes the pattern from Splash Zone and the others who'd used the same exploit before — an
NPC entering an off-limits area, not dying. Later, back at the same casino, the new croupier **Mitch**
confirms it directly: "He gave me a thousand gold tip just to turn the wheel to the Nothing spot and
let him jump in." Pontiff's gamble is what convinces Carl the route is real, setting up **Louis
Santiago**'s own trip through the same casino portal at the end of the book.

## Sheol

Pontiff reappears, alive, in the Book 8 epilogue — standing at Britney's side when Louis wakes up on
Floor 15:

> He turned, and he spied Britney looking down at him with concern. She had that pickaxe out on her
> shoulder. Standing next to her was a Taurin, arms crossed. Pontiff. He recognized him from when he
> and Britney had been talking the first time they'd gone to the mercenary market.

The demon **Forkith**, fitting Louis with a breathing leech moments later, name-drops him as part of
the same operation: "I am friends with Pontiff, and I am hoping to be friends with you." Whatever
Pontiff found on the other side of the Nothing portal, it put him in with Britney, Forkith, and
whatever long-planned resistance is quietly assembling in Sheol.

!!! note "Sheol, not the Cabaret"
    Everyone who uses the Nothing portal — Pontiff, then later Chris Andrews, Britney, and Louis —
    believes they're headed for the Pineapple Cabaret's holding area. The book never reconciles this
    with where they actually turn up. Whether the route failed, was intercepted, or was never really
    connected to the Cabaret at all is left open — see [The Pineapple
    Cabaret](../dungeon-factions/pineapple-cabaret.md#open-threads) and
    [Timeline](../timeline/index.md#end-of-book-8) for the full discrepancy.
