# Mongo

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Species:** Mongoliensis — actually a velociraptor (see below)
    - **Sex:** Male
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut (pet-class mob, bonded)
    - **Title:** Royal Steed
    - **Obtained:** Floor 2 pet reward room, from a locked, red-tagged cage
    - **Full-grown danger level:** Level 15 ("especially powerful, fast, and vicious")
    - **Type/Class/Subclass:** Not applicable — pet-class mob, not a crawler
    - **Level:** 6+ by mid-Book 2, rising to 13+ by the end of the Floor 3 quests, then 26 by the end of Book 3 → 33 by the end of Book 4 → **34+** by the end of Book 5 — grows from turkey-sized to pony-sized to full adult size (roughly pony-height, about 13 feet long including tail) over the series so far. **No level is stated for him anywhere in Book 7**, and no evolution or upgrade notification is printed for him in that book
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** No named spells; combat is physical — biting, clawing, pack-hunting instinct (confirmed useful against Brindle Grubs); leaps nearly 10 feet vertically by Book 2, later able to clear a full building story in a single jump; immune to acid-blood splash damage from Book 3's Krakaren monsters; Book 4: destroys at least four defective "Robot Donut" toys on sight, and delivers the killing bite/crunch on the Scree parasite (Maggie My) after it's forced out of Chris Andrews and Carl's own body; Book 5: has a rarely-used **Earthquake** attack that damages and reveals invisible foes over an area (used against a pack of invisible Night Weasels), and is trained by Donut to moonwalk for the Butcher's Masquerade pet showcase; Book 7: Donut's new **Muscle Mommy** benefit boosts his Strength by 25% of her own, and her [Clockwork Triplicate](../spells/clockwork-triplicate.md) spell splits him into two clockwork duplicates plus the original
    - **Notable items:** A magical tracking collar (Book 2, from a Gold Pet Box) that keeps his dot visible on the minimap at all times; [Enchanted Fang Caps of the Expectorating Tizheruk](../items/fang-caps-of-the-expectorating-tizheruk.md) (Book 2, transferred from Donut; Book 4: a second pair adds +15% movement speed and +15% Strength)
    - **Offspring (Book 7):** Twin **Ursensus** daughters by **Kiwi**, born mid-book in Bucket Boy's clinic — *Legendary Creature*, "Part raptor, part bear, part Scolopendra magic"

## Book 2 Growth

Mongo grows visibly with almost every fight on Floor 3, gaining size, adult plumage, and strength
at a pace that surprises even Donut ("They grow up so fast"). He remains prone to breaking
training the moment he gets excited near a kill, but grows steadily more useful — including
helping fend off Grimaldi's circus lemurs and mold lions alongside Carl and Donut. He single-
handedly holds off a room full of spike-throwing Street Urchins to protect an unconscious Donut,
earning him fang caps as a reward for his own inventory, and later helps clear the krasue nest in
Miss Quill's lair, using his enchanted bite to kill the ghost-class monsters outright.

## Origin

Mongo is found caged in a Floor 2 pet reward room alongside other red-tagged (dangerous)
pet-class options. His in-game description reads:

> "The stubborn and hot-headed Mongoliensis is not the type of pet to ever be 'tamed.' The best
> one can hope for is mutual respect. And even then they still might try to eat you if the fancy
> strikes them. While especially powerful, fast, and vicious when they are fully grown at level
> 15, getting them to level 15 is about as likely as a cheerleader from West Virginia reaching
> her 18th birthday as a virgin."

Donut names him on sight, before even confirming his sex: "If it's a boy I'm gonna call him
Mongo... If it's a girl I'm naming her Sissy."

## What He Actually Is

Mongo is described and pictured as a small, feathered, beaked creature — Carl repeatedly calls
him "the little chicken" or "the little dinosaur." Late in the book, talk-show host Odette
reveals the translation gap behind his species name: "Mongoliensis" is the scientific name for
what humans call **velociraptors**. Carl hadn't put it together because of the pink feathers and
beak.

## Pet-Class Mechanics

Per Mordecai, pet-class mobs behave differently from normal mobs:

> "Mobs that start out as a pet class will always be level one regardless of what they usually
> are when you find them in the dungeon. And they'll stay level one until they're bonded. Only
> then will they start to level."

Mongo starts fully hostile (a red status dot) and has to be carried in a gerbil cage to avoid
biting Carl or teleporting away. Bonding happens in stages:

1. **Remove auto-hostility** — achieved by letting Mongo kill (and eat) live Brindle Grubs,
   after Carl learns from a recap broadcast that Mongoliensis are pack hunters. His status dot
   turns from red to white.
2. **Obedience training** — Donut spends a full day training him with pet biscuits until he'll
   hold an attack until she gives the command (which evolves from "attack" to "sic 'em").
3. **Full bond** — triggered when Mongo is nearly killed by a hornet-type monster in the
   stairwell hallway (the same hallway where the Rage Elemental fight happened) and Donut saves
   him. His dot turns from white to orange, and the system announces:

> "Mongo the Mongoliensis Level One (Pet of Grand Champion Best in Dungeon Princess Donut) has
> joined the party."
>
> "Mongo's title has been changed to Royal Steed."

## Timeline Notes (Book 1)

- Found and caged in a Floor 2 pet reward room
- Named by Donut before his sex or species were confirmed
- Bonding process consumes most of the party's remaining time on Floor 2
- Saved by Donut from a hornet-type mob in the stairwell hallway, completing the bond
- Revealed by Odette, during a broadcast interview, to be a velociraptor rather than an
  unfamiliar alien species
- Descends to Floor 3 with Carl and Donut at the end of the book

## Book 3 Notes

Mongo reaches Level 23 mid-floor and finishes at Level 26, having grown into what Carl and Donut
agree is his full adult size — he "won't get any bigger." He plays a recurring, dark-comic role in
the floor's Growler Gary hand-farming plan (see [Carl](carl.md)), efficiently and repeatedly
killing and dismembering the self-resurrecting gnoll on command, and proves immune to the caustic
acid blood of the floor's Krakaren monsters, letting him fight them in melee without the
protective measures the rest of the party needs. He's kept caged for a stretch around the remnants
of Brynhild's Daughters following Hekla's death, for safety on both sides.

## Book 4 Notes

Mongo hits Level 33 on Floor 5, gaining three levels in a single fan-box-fueled battle (the
airship heist) despite not doing anything himself, to Donut's mock outrage. He's temporarily
released from his pet carrier to make room for *Clockwork Triplicate* duplicates during the
Wasteland assault, terrifying him with the height. He develops a habit of instantly destroying
every defective "Robot Donut" merchandising toy Veriluxx sends the party (several detonate
harmlessly after he bites through their heads), and picks up a stuffed pink rabbit from the
gnomish floating house that he carries around for the rest of the floor. At the climax, he lands
the finishing bite on the Scree parasite (Maggie My) after Carl forces it out of both Chris
Andrews's skull and his own neck.

## Book 5 Notes

On Floor 6, Mongo encounters what appears to be a matriarchal pack of wild Mongoliensis led by a
scarred, level-60 female named **Kiwi**, and mates with her almost immediately, to Donut's
horrified running commentary — Carl jokes on the spot that Kiwi is now "most definitely" pregnant
by Mongo, a claim the book bears out shortly afterward: when the High Elf castle is yanked away to
Floor 9 mid-battle, the narration lists everything lost with it, including "Kiwi, who'd turned into
a bear—**a very pregnant bear**—was gone" (the on-stage transformation scene itself, moments
earlier, doesn't mention it). Kiwi is not actually his own species at all — she's later revealed
to be an **Ursine woman transformed into raptor form by one of Scolopendra's curses**, and her
"daughter" **Big Tina** (a deranged allosaurus terrorizing the region) is the same curse's other
victim. Donut tames only **Kiwi** herself as an actual bonded minion (a potion keyed to Mongo's own blood
as the special ingredient); the rest of the pack merely goes non-hostile in step with their
leader and is never personally bonded. Donut later cures Kiwi with a sponsor-sent potion during
the Butcher's Masquerade climax — reverting her, mid-battle, back into her Ursine form — while the
rest of her raptor pack immediately turns hostile again the instant Kiwi's control lapses. Tina,
tragically, is never cured and remains
trapped as a dinosaur, crying out for her mother as the transformation happens. Mongo survives a
lightning bolt meant for him — cast from a concealed litter later revealed to be carrying Queen
Imogen's cat familiar, Ferdinand — only because Donut throws herself in the way; helps Kiwi tear
Vrah apart, then delivers the actual finishing bite alone after Carl sends Kiwi back to the stage
("Kiwi... back onto the stage. Quick. Mongo. Finish her off for me."); and is trained by Donut to
moonwalk on stage during the masquerade's pet showcase, drawing applause even from some of the
watching hunters.

## Book 7: This Inevitable Ruin

Floor 9, the Faction Wars floor, is the biggest book Mongo has had. He is credited with killing two
of the ten enemy warlords, is reunited with **Kiwi**, becomes a father, and very nearly dies. The
book never states his level and never prints an evolution or upgrade notification for him.

### Getting His Wing Back

He enters the book maimed. Carl, controlled by Shi Maria at the end of Book 6, had burned off his
feathers and taken his right wing, and Carl can't look at him without flinching. Donut has already
used a Heal Severe Injury potion on the burns; the wing needs a rare Heal Pet potion, which
[Mordecai](mordecai.md) insists on reverse-engineering on his upgraded crafting table first.

> "These are super rare, and expensive, but with my newest table upgrade, I can figure out the
> recipe without ruining it. So let me look at it first, and we can give it to Mongo after. It'll
> grow his wing back in aboot an hour or so." He patted Mongo on the head. "You'll be as good as
> new. Well, almost. You'll probably still have some scars."

Carl administers it by smashing the vial on Mongo's backside — Mordecai's potion-ball trick, and
"a hell of a lot safer than trying to pour it down his throat."

### Rend

In chapter 5 Carl re-bonds the tummy acher he inherited from [Tserendolgor](tserendolgor.md), who
is renamed [Sir Rendlegore](sir-rendlegore.md) — "Rend." Mongo's first reaction is to try to eat
him; the meatball hops into his open mouth on purpose, and Mongo swallows him, then yaks him back
up on the table. They are inseparable from that point on. Rend rides on Mongo's back (he has no
arms and works out how to climb up unaided), gnaws on him constantly, licks gore off his neck
after fights, and steals his bacon. Donut's verdict on Rend, twice over, is "He's a bad influence
on Mongo" and later "He's a terrible influence on Mongo."

### Killing Empress D'Nadia

In chapter 26 Carl and Donut assault the Prism throne room to kill
[Empress D'Nadia](princess-dnadia.md). Donut casts *Clockwork Triplicate* on Mongo, and it is the
two clockwork copies — not the real one — that do the work of stripping the Empress's three layered
tech shields and tearing her armour and gown off her. The real Mongo is held back the whole time:

> The clockwork Mongos went back to work, tearing at her. The real Mongo held back, hopping from
> one foot to the next, his arm feathers rustling with anticipation.

Carl nearly takes the kill himself, and Donut talks him down from it. Then she settles an older
score — the pet show Tserendolgor admitted cheating at in Book 6:

> "Empress, Darling," Donut said. "Mongo would like to express his utmost disappointment at how
> you judged him during the pet show."

The chapter ends there. Chapter 27 opens with the system's ruling:

> System Message: Warlord D'Nadia of the Prism has fallen. Credit for the kill has been given to
> pet Mongo of the Princess Posse.

Afterwards Mongo and Rend are found licking blue Saccathian gore off the throne room wall, and
Mongo squawks agreement when Donut demands Carl not rename the party now that he's outranked her:
"Is that what you want? Mongo to come from a broken home?"

### Muscle Mommy

Once Donut becomes the strongest crawler in the dungeon again, her new **Muscle Mommy** benefit
boosts Mongo's Strength by 25% of her own. Carl notes he is "decidedly more buff" afterwards.
Combined with Donut's [Twinkle Toes](../spells/twinkle-toes.md) he becomes the party's fastest
ground mount.

### The Nest, and Nearly Dying

Late in the siege of Larracos (end of chapter 72) Mongo and his clockwork duplicates are knocked
down a chute out of the Nest tower, and Donut is locked in the room above, screaming for him.
Outside, they land in front of **Gustavo 3**, the last of [Lucia Mar](lucia-mar.md)'s rottweilers.
Gustavo's lightning destroys both clockworks; when the real Mongo bites down on him, the dog's
**Porcupine Defense** erupts steel quills up through Mongo's mouth from the inside out, then
electrifies them. Gustavo takes the unconscious dinosaur by the throat and starts shaking.

Mongo is saved by Rend. Carl casts Hi-Yo Silver to summon him, and Rend's **Wrecking Ball** does
the rest:

> The back half of Gustavo 3, the second of Lucia Mar's two rottweilers, terror of the dungeon,
> exploded in a red mist as Rend rushed right up to me, stopping inches from my face.

Lucia Mar herself, sobbing, pours a pet healing potion on Mongo, whose health is down to "the
barest sliver of red," his throat ripped open. He survives, blood-soaked around the collar, missing
neck feathers.

### Kiwi

Mongo is reunited with **Kiwi** in chapter 13, on Carl and Donut's first walk around their new
headquarters base (shortly before it is christened the FUPA), and the scene plays out almost
entirely without dialogue. [Big Tina](../bosses/big-tina.md) — the allosaurus, Kiwi's daughter, now able to
talk — greets him as "Pa-pa," to Donut's outrage. Then Kiwi comes out of the house in an apron,
one-eyed and heavily pregnant. She is now an Ursine again, cured at the Butcher's Masquerade;
Carl's narration is blunt about the arithmetic: "before she'd turned, she'd had... relations with
Mongo when they were both dinosaurs."

> "Mongo?" Kiwi asked.
>
> Mongo gave a single, quiet roar. He pushed past Tina and moved, head low to the ground as
> everybody watched, all of us holding our breath, all of us wondering what was going to happen
> next. Mongo sniffed at Kiwi and gave an uncertain peep.

> Kiwi stepped forward, wrapped her arms around Mongo's neck, and she hugged him. Mongo just stood
> there. They hugged for a few moments, and then Kiwi turned away.

She goes back inside to make dinner. Mongo is left standing there "a confused look upon the
dinosaur's face, like he didn't know what had just happened," and Carl tells him, "Sometimes people
change, and that's all there is to it." Her personality has changed completely — from the scarred,
murderous pack leader of Book 5 to a soft-spoken woman who cooks for the troops. Mongo keeps at it
anyway; [Samantha](samantha.md) reports that while Carl and Donut are in the planning room, "Mongo goes in the
kitchens and sniffs Kiwi's butt so much she has to swat him away."

### The Twins

Kiwi goes into labour mid-book while Carl's group is away; [Florin](florin.md) messages that she is in
Bucket Boy's new clinic "in active labor and is asking for Mongo." Mongo's response to Donut
telling him he's about to be a father is to cock his head, then start backing up and screeching
"like he did when he didn't want to get zapped away into his cage."

He goes in anyway. Kiwi puts a hand on his side through the delivery; he squeals in concern each
time she pushes. The birth runs through the end of chapter 82, intercut with Carl's conversation
with [Juice Box](juice-box.md) about the Inevitable Ruin:

> A second cry filled the tent as Mongo squawked in confusion and fear and perhaps a tiny bit of
> joy.
>
> "Oh my god, oh my god," Donut called from my shoulder. "Mongo... Mongo... you're a daddy!"

Twin girls. Carl's description is unsparing — "They weren't bears. They weren't raptors. They were
something in-between," four-legged, stub-winged, covered in wet brown fur, with distinctly
Mongo-like heads but no feathers, beaks, mammal-like cries, and badly mismatched, unfocused pug
eyes. He privately calls them "goddamn abominations." Then the system card lands:

> Newborn Ursensus. Female. Level 0.
>
> Legendary Creature.
>
> I just love it when I'm allowed to create new monsters within the confines of the game rules.
> Part raptor, part bear, part Scolopendra magic. All adorable. These fuzzy little meat-eaters are
> going to be quite the handful when they grow up.
>
> Because of this mob's intelligence level they cannot be used as pets.

**Ursensus** is a brand-new mob type, created by the AI on the spot. Donut: "Mongo, you made a
legendary creature!" Carl's own contribution is to pat him on the back — "If I had a cigar, I'd
give it to you, buddy."

### Killing Epitome Tagg

Mongo's second warlord kill, in chapter 84, is an accident. [Epitome Tagg](epitome-tagg.md) of the
Dream has hidden himself inside the biomechanical heart and lungs surgically installed in
[Louis Santiago](louis-santiago.md)'s chest. The team's fix is to cut the organs out and replace
them with a flesh golem's, on a rapid-healing clock. Bucket Boy yanks the heart and lungs free and
throws them over his shoulder onto the tent floor — where, unnoticed, Mongo eats them.

> Mongo popped up and let out an angry screech. He had blood on his beak, and he spit out the
> chewed-to-hell plastic heart and lungs onto the ground of the tent with disgust.
>
> System Message: Warlord Epitome Tagg of the Dream has fallen. Credit for the kill has been
> given... Well, this one is just as complicated as the last one, but imma give it to pet Mongo of
> the Princess Posse. I almost called it a suicide, and I almost gave it to that Bucket Boy kid,
> but it's going to Mongo. Consider it a present for the baby shower.

The Dream is eliminated and all its assets go to the Princess Posse, leaving two enemy teams.

### End of the Floor

At the going-away party Mongo sniffs at the babies while Rend tries to catch streamers in his
mouth. Kiwi, Tina and the twins go down to the 12th floor with the other children; Mongo and Rend
stay with Carl and Donut. Mongo "freaked out when Tina and Kiwi and the new babies had
disappeared," and has to be coaxed into his carrier.
