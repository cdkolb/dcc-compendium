## Background

A roughly 45-year-old Canadian woman of First Nations origin, built on a lifetime of hard physical
labor. She has an old, weathered double-V tribal forehead tattoo and simple line tattoos on the
backs of her hands and fingers, predating the dungeon. Carl immediately recognizes the type from
his years working shipyards — someone whose days were physical work and whose nights were often
bar fights.

## Role in the Story — Book 4

Gwen leads the crawler team assigned to Floor 5's land quadrant, methodically breaching all four
walls of a sandcastle guarding the Necropolis of Anser despite constant harassment from beach
mobs and a borough-boss chainsaw-buzzard. She's blunt to the point of bullying — Katia compares
her disposition to [Hekla](hekla.md)'s, minus the pretense of warmth — and the two clash
repeatedly over tactics while Carl and Donut are unreachable for five days following the death of
Admin Loita. Katia specifically overrides Gwen's preferred "easy" plan (draining the castle with
water to destroy it outright) in favor of a riskier lightning-door assault that preserves a chance
at recovering the Mad Dune Mage's winding box — a decision Gwen visibly resents but ultimately
respects once it pays off.

Gwen personally helps clear the castle, fights alongside Carl and Katia during the climactic
lightning-storm sequence that frees Orthrus's giant puppy-god and finishes the subterranean
quadrant, and survives the floor at Level 34. She and her teammate Tran are among the last to
leave Floor 5, later helping unearth Anser's actual tomb (a golden, unenchanted crown on a goose
skeleton) once the necropolis fully drains.

## Relationships

- **Katia** — a contentious but ultimately respectful working relationship; the two argue often
  but Gwen agrees to follow Katia's harder, longer-odds plan when it matters
- **Carl** — professional courtesy and dry humor ("hello, bomber guy"); no deep bond, but mutual
  respect earned through the floor's fighting
- Joins **Katia's new team** (alongside Tran, Louis and Firas, Bautista, and Florin) at the end of
  Book 4 to help protect Eva Sigrid's scattered former "daughters"

## Book 5: Death at the Masquerade

Gwen continues fighting alongside Katia's splinter team through Floor 6's hunter war — during one
coordinated raid she takes a pulse-rifle shot to the gut that punches a grapefruit-sized hole
through her midsection, which the system doesn't even treat as a wound requiring amputation; she's
healed and back to her usual grumpy self within minutes. She's among the crawlers who make it into
the Butcher's Masquerade, and dies during the climactic "Cage Fight Extreme" battle against Queen
Imogen and Diwata — her body found afterward alongside Cleiton, the crawler who'd told a nervous
knock-knock joke during the masquerade's talent show only hours earlier.
