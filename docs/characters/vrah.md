# Vrah

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Elite hunter
    - **Race:** Mantis (Dark Hive)
    - **Level:** 70 → 74 (Book 5)
    - **Faction:** The Dark Hive — a private, for-profit mantis amusement-park corporation, distinct
      from the mantis royal government (the Burrowers)
    - **Family:** Daughter of hive matriarch **[Circe Took](circe-took.md)**; sister of Xindy
      (killed by Carl early in Book 5) and six other warrior sisters (seven warriors total,
      including Vrah, survive Xindy's death), plus one brother — a trade representative based in
      Zockau
    - **Status:** **Deceased** — killed by Mongo during the Butcher's Masquerade's final battle
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** Flight; a crossbow used at range; collects the severed heads of
      her kills and mounts them on her own carapace, including two reanimated by Donut's
      *Second Chance* that briefly turn on her mid-battle
    - **Notable items:** A crossbow; none named beyond her natural chitin armor

## Overview

Vrah is the top hunter on Floor 6, next in line to become a Dark Hive queen, and the central
antagonist of Carl's personal storyline for the floor. She's introduced hunting the top crawlers
personally, decapitating victims and wearing their heads as trophies, and quickly targets Carl and
Donut directly after Carl's opening raid on the hunters' home city, Zockau, kills her sister Xindy.

## The Gonorrhea Arrow

Vrah tracks Carl down at the ruined village of Alucarda, landing to confront him with the mounted
heads of past kills (including fellow crawlers Langley and Bogdon Ro) still fixed to her carapace.
Carl distracts her by having Donut's *Second Chance*-reanimated Langley and Bogdon Ro heads bite
into her exposed neck, then rams a looted
**[Arrow of Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea](../items/arrow-of-enthusiastic-double-gonorrhea.md)**
directly into the wound before she can recover and flee. As the heir to a hive queen, Vrah cannot
geld herself (the disease's only self-cure) without disqualifying herself from succession, and
mantis mating customs make finding a willing, consenting partner to pass the infection to legally
and socially fraught. The infection cripples her for most of the rest of the book; her mother,
Circe Took, ends up sponsoring the shapeshifting deity **Diwata** into the realm specifically to
cure her — Diwata mates with Vrah as a male mantis to lift the infection, then turns female and
uses Circe's own body as a vessel to keep giving birth indefinitely, with catastrophic
consequences for everyone at the Butcher's Masquerade.

## Death

Vrah is finally re-marked and killed during the Butcher's Masquerade's climactic battle. Reduced
to a crawling, dismembered wreck by Mongo and Kiwi, she's finished off by Mongo on Carl's direct
order. Samantha bites into her dying body as well, shrieking "Remember me, bitch!" at Vrah — not
at Circe Took, who dies separately later in the same battle.
