# Beatrice

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Role:** Carl's ex-girlfriend; Princess Donut's original owner
    - **Type:** Human survivor, never a crawler — remained on Earth's surface, then in Syndicate
      custody
    - **Status:** Alive as of Book 5, confirmed for the first time since the Book 1 collapse
    - **Notable items/relationships:** Donut's owner on the pre-collapse Persian-cat show circuit
      (Bea's family raised and showed the cats); the poorly-drawn tattoo of Donut's grandmother,
      Princess Chonkalot, on her lower back; her ever-present Aries anklet

## Background

Before the collapse, Beatrice ("Bea") was Carl's girlfriend, living with him in Seattle. Days
before the world was harvested into the dungeon, Carl learned she'd cheated on him with a man named
Brad while on a Bahamas vacation, and their relationship had ended. Bea was traveling at the moment
the dungeon opened, leaving Carl looking after her champion show cat, Donut — which is why Carl,
not Bea, ended up inside the dungeon holding her.

Book 4's epilogue first revealed Bea survived the collapse on Earth's surface, living among a
native human settlement with Brad for months, before being captured (unharmed) by an operative
working for the talk-show host [Odette](odette.md) — see [Supporting Cast](supporting-cast.md) for
that book's account.

## Book 5: Alive, and Cast Aside

Odette reveals to Carl and Donut, moments before a live broadcast, that Bea is not only alive but
was being hunted by bounty hunters intending to illegally sell her to Borant so she could be
forcibly transformed into a country boss — a fate Odette says she extracted Bea off-world to
prevent, framing it as protecting her own investment in Carl and Donut — and to keep a rival
production from using Bea's survival as a hostile ambush story. Bea appears on Odette's talk
show as a surprise guest, broadcast in remotely from off-planet: thin, aged, drugged into a hollow,
unresponsive state, and dressed for the broadcast in a revealing gown that exposes Donut's
grandmother's tattoo on her back. She spots Carl first and rushes to hug him, her arms passing
harmlessly through him since she isn't physically present in the room; she then reaches for Donut
and passes through her the same way.

Donut, who has spent the whole series privately grieving Bea's presumed death, delivers a raw,
unscripted rejection live on air — accusing Bea of planning to abandon her, of treating her as
little more than a way to win more ribbons than Bea's own mother, and declaring that Carl is her
person now, and always has been. Carl backs her up, coldly wishing Bea well and asking Odette to
end the segment. The confrontation closes the loop on a plot thread the series has carried since
Book 1 without offering Bea any further redemption or explanation.

The interview is filmed the same day Borant announces, in a real-time system message (not a
broadcast), that the Valtay Corporation has acquired 51% ownership of the Borant Corporation — but
Odette insists the Bea segment itself won't actually air until two recap episodes later. Odette
does use Bea's rescue as leverage for a higher revenue-share contract with Borant — but that is a
separate deal from the Valtay takeover itself, which Donut cryptically takes credit for moments
later ("Viva la revolución, Carl"), tied to her own undisclosed dealings with Zev and the Borant
Emancipation Front.
