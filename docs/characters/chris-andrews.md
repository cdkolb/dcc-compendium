# Chris Andrews (Andrews 2)

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Role:** Brandon An's younger brother, part of the "Meadow Lark" evacuee group (Book 1)
    - **Race change (Book 3):** **Igneous** — a rock-monster race made of lava-textured stone,
      covered in small glowing holes
    - **Status:** Alive; freed from possession and rejoins Carl's party at the end of Book 4. Enters
      the Floor 10 stairwell at the end of Book 7 alongside [Britney](britney.md)
    - **Level (Book 4):** 35, Igneous, **Zulu Warrior** class; no level given in Book 7
    - **Notable act:** Killed [Frank Q](frank-q.md) — revealed in Book 4 to have been done by the
      Infiltrator parasite controlling his body, not by Chris himself

## Book 1: The Quiet Brother

Introduced alongside his brother [Brandon An](brandon-an.md) in the Meadow Lark evacuee group —
speaks rarely but decisively, and is the one who builds the group's transport rigs. Their mother
apparently worried something was "wrong" with him, but per Brandon's own later assessment, "he
ain't slow" — just quiet.

## Book 3: The Falling-Out

On Floor 3, Chris picks the **Igneous** race, transforming into a hulking, lava-rock-textured
creature — a change multiple secondhand accounts (Elle McGibbons, Imani C) describe as altering
his personality along with his body. He becomes obsessed with hunting and fighting, wants to
leave the group's defensive position near the stairwell to go reconnect with Carl and Donut
("he respected that you're aggressive"), and clashes repeatedly with Brandon, who wants to stay
put and train safely. The argument ends with Chris leaving the party, taking several other men
with him — all of whom are later confirmed dead, except Chris.

Chris goes fully unreachable after that: still visibly alive on Brandon's (and later Carl's)
chat, but unable or unwilling to accept messages. Brandon's dying wish, in the message that
reaches Carl only after Floor 3 has already collapsed, is that Carl find a way to tell Chris he
loved him — a message Chris never receives on-page.

## The Frank Q Killing

Later on Floor 4, Chris is reported — via an eyewitness account relayed through a bartender
contact ("Sledgie") at the Desperado Club — to have gotten into a fight with
[Frank Q](frank-q.md) and killed him, then walked out and been banned from the club. Carl
learns of it secondhand and reacts with genuine disbelief ("That didn't seem like the Chris I'd
known"), unable to reconcile it with the quiet brother he remembers, though Imani and Elle both
confirm Chris has changed significantly since his race change. Carl has no way to confirm
Chris's motive, though the timing — shortly after Frank recounted his and Maggie's history with
Yvette's death to Carl at the same bar — leaves it an open question whether Chris somehow knew
Frank's history with Carl's own past.

As of the end of Book 3, Chris remains at large, unreachable, and — per a cryptic, cut-off remark
from Odette near the book's end ("It's not like I warned them that Chris...") — apparently tied
to some larger unresolved thread Odette knows more about than she's said.

## Book 4: The Infiltrator Revealed

Chris resurfaces on Floor 5, having survived the destruction of the underwater submarine *Akula*
(a borough boss chamber he single-handedly cleared while invisible to its defenses) and washing
up near Carl's bubble shortly after the *Wasteland* falls. He behaves like a stranger — cold,
fatalistic, unable to use chat or inventory without taking damage — and is on the verge of
attacking Carl (at the direction of a disguised **Juice Box**) when a Katia crossbow bolt reveals
the truth: Chris has been infected since the end of Floor 3 by **Maggie My**, who took an
**Infiltrator** (a member of the **Scree** race) — a full-body brain parasite that, once fully
annexed, locks the host out of their own body entirely while leaving them conscious and aware.
Chris has been trapped as a helpless passenger the whole time, including during the killing of
[Frank Q](frank-q.md), which was Maggie's act, not his own.

Unable to safely separate the two on the spot, Donut portals the possessed Chris into a sealed,
flooding chamber below Hump Town rather than let Carl kill him outright. He remains trapped there
— alive, camouflaged in sand, occasionally speaking through Maggie's mimicry of his voice — for
most of the rest of the floor, while Mordecai works out an antiparasitic cure. At the very end of
the book, using a Phase Lava Rock potion, Carl reaches directly into Chris's skull mid-fight and
extracts the worm; it briefly re-infests Carl's own arm before a double-healing potion forces it
back out through his neck, and Mongo finishes it off. Chris, freed, joins Carl's party for the
descent to Floor 6, grieving his brother Brandon anew now that he's able to process the loss.

## Book 6 Notes

Chris spends Floor 8 based in Beijing with Li Na and Li Jun's team rather than with the Meadowlark
crew, and is quietly useful throughout rather than prominent.

- He scouts ahead to the region's final boss and brings back the intel that defines the whole key
  phase for the guild — a giant vending machine blocking the entrance, requiring nine tokens.
- His iPod supplies the date for Louis's Christmas party ("that iPod is actually Chris's. He brought
  it with him, and it says it's March 20th").
- He comes to Cuba with the rescue party and ends up as the guild's demolition engineer: he says "I
  feel bad" about blowing up the church, then leads the controlled demolition and the digging of the
  escape channel beneath it with Zhang and the strippers, and checks that nothing is left standing
  directly above their heads.
- He paints the **neon X** on the tunnel ceiling directly beneath the stairwell — the mark everyone
  has to stand on to survive the floor's collapse.
- A level-120 rock monster wearing Chris's face attacks Louis's group during the Ysalte fight.

The epilogue circles back to him on the other side of the story: in the Meadowlark flashback, the
Residual agent Agatha notes that "The other, Chris, had peeked outside earlier in the day, looking
for her. He'd called out her name" — making sure the homeless woman had been picked up before the
cold.

## Book 7: This Inevitable Ruin

Chris fights Floor 9 mostly out of Carl's sight, on the northern front under
[Florin](florin.md), and says almost nothing all book. Two things happen to him anyway.

### Clay-Ton

He is inseparable all floor from **Clay-Ton the cretin**, the fourth of the Desperado Club cretins,
who was assigned as his bodyguard back on the Hunting Grounds:

> The cretin and the lava rock crawler had formed a strange friendship since we'd assigned Clay-Ton to
> be Chris's bodyguard on the Hunting Grounds. The two would just sit next to each other and
> occasionally grunt at one another. And now they were with Florin in the northern trenches.

When Carl teleports in his hand-picked team for the assault on the Madness castle, the two arrive
together: "Also present were Chris and Clay-Ton the cretin. Those last two had been inseparable this
entire floor."

Afterwards, when Lucia Mar kills the Sledge's best friend Bomo, Chris, Clay-Ton and Very Sullen are
the three sitting in a ring around the grieving rock monster, not saying anything.

### Underground

Early in the ceasefire he is part of Carl's hunting party into the newly-tacked-on wilderness, where
his usefulness is entirely subterranean. He buries himself in the ground and blends with it so
thoroughly that Carl ends up standing on top of him without knowing — "I felt strange about standing
directly atop him, but he insisted it didn't bother him" — and calls the timing of an incoming boss
from below: "It's different. It's slower. It's still fast, but it's slower." He drags three skunks
under the earth by their legs, then rides out the mob's stink attack buried:

> Chris: I am buried. Tell me when it's safe to come up. I can smell it even down here.

He is one of only a handful of the party who avoid the debuff entirely. Bodi, the Land War veteran,
is the only person in the book quiet enough to make Chris "seem downright chatty."

### Imani

The book's biggest revelation about Chris is not about Chris at all, and he never learns it. Carl and
Katia take dream potions together and Carl's mind briefly slips its bindings, flooding into everyone
in the room. He catches a fragment of each of them — Mordecai's bottomless sadness, Rosetta's tired
rage, Edgar's fear and wonder — and then he lands on **[Imani C](imani-c.md)**, and gets far more
than a fragment:

> She was in love with Chris, and I had no idea. She loved his simplicity. His quiet kindness.
>
> It felt so private, so dirty for me to have learned about this in such a way. Like I had broken into
> her house and spied her utterly naked, all without her knowing.

Carl never tells either of them. See [Imani C](imani-c.md) for the scene from her side.

The only thing in the book that touches it is a moment Carl watches from the outside, on the field,
just before the Madness castle raid:

> I watched as Imani walked up and gave Chris a quick hug using her wings, not her arms. Chris reached
> up, as if to touch her face, but stopped just short.

### The Ball

Chris is among the thousands swept up by the war mages' **Witness** spell and then rolled into Donut's
**Rolling Battle Formation** ball. His one line to her afterwards, while she is apologising to
everyone in chat, is the most forceful thing he says all book:

> Chris: The Witness spell was just as bad as being controlled by Maggie. You saved us.

### Exit

At the stairwell down to Floor 10 he leaves with Britney, who "said she was contemplating a deal, but
was afraid and would probably be back. She went in with Chris." The book does not say what Chris
himself decided.
