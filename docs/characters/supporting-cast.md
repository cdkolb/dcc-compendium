# Supporting Cast

Characters who appear with real substance but haven't yet earned a full page. See the
[Characters overview](index.md) for the promotion rule that moves someone from here to a full
page.

| Name | Role | Notes |

| **Admiral Corinne Whimpleshin** | Dromedarian camel; adjutant for the **Blood Sultanate** (Book 7) | A single-humped camel in a sundress holding "an honest-to-goodness martini," and a little heavyset for a camel. | **Agatha** | Homeless woman; revealed in Book 6 to be **Residual Agent number 22** | Survives events she shouldn't; carries unexplained high-tech items (Valtay Corporation weapon/shield/comms) later found in her cart. Still alive and pushing her cart on Floor 4 (Book 3); briefly glimpsed on Floor 5 (Book 4), now Level 12. Book 6's epilogue is her POV and explains everything: she is a member of a rare, ancient species that "peeled away from the collective" ten years before the collapse, wearing a human shell she took by hunting down and killing one of the Apothecary's own residuals — which is how she has access to both feeds. She spent those ten years in Wenatchee, Washington, and liked it. At the emergency collapse order she is in the alley behind the **Meadowlark Elder Care facility**, planning to light a barrel fire at exactly the right moment to draw staff and police outside so a dungeon entrance will generate near them — i.e. she is why the Meadowlark crawlers got in at all. She was fond of Brandon, who shared his lunch with her. Her faction's goal is recruiting the system AI; her private view is that "this entire universe would be swept clean of biological life". **Book 7:** Zev relays a direct order from Prime Minister Glory that Carl is not to interact with her. She is then named **co-warlord of the War Mage Rebellion** alongside [Akuma](akuma.md), who plainly does not want her there. Fopsy the Dream's adjutant theorises her aim is not to win Faction Wars at all but to end the Celestial Ascendency early — because once the ascendency is finished with no crawlers left, the system's hard-coded shutdown-and-hibernation sequence triggers, an alternative to the failsafe — and that she now believes the local AI is "a threat to the Eulogist." Akuma's own warning to Carl is that she "seeks to kill us all" |
| **Allister** | Cookbook author, 13th edition; T'Ghee practitioner (Books 3, 5–7) | Contributes recurring marginal notes to *[The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook](../items/the-dungeon-anarchists-cookbook.md#known-authors-their-editions)* on explosives stability, vampire lore, and protection spells, all filtered through his home culture's T'Ghee card-and-meditation tradition — his own T'Ghee deck holds cards like the Plague Bearer and the Open Wound. Confirms Batbilge's death was likely his final entry |

One of Donut's two favourite dancers. Found weeping beside Damascus when word of their mother's assassination reaches the club, and insists on going with him to confront their father: "If you're going to fight him, I'm going, too… **This is family business.**" Does not join Carl's mercenaries; neither brother is seen again in Book 6 |
| **Anton Lopez** | Level 47 human crawler, class **Fugitive** (Book 6) | Cousin of **[Paz Lo](paz-lo.md)**, fellow Havana prison guard, and the third member of Sister Ines's Cuban squad. Worships Ogun. Charmed by the goddess Oshun into throwing the Ogun shrine piece out the door, which triggers the smiting of both men; when Carl casts *Black Nimbus* to pull him out of the god's targeting, Ogun simply steps backward onto his head and crushes it — "Just like that, as if he was nothing" |
| **Areson the Wise** | Ogre strongman, one of the three brothers from Grimaldi's circus (Book 6–7) | Brother of **Herman the Fleet** and of Apollon, who was left behind on the third floor; all three were once part of a single three-headed nodling that died and split long ago. | **Arief** | Level 80 Semeru dwarf; **War Leader** and commander of the Larracos Defense Force Infantry (Book 7) | Team Retribution's senior soldier and Carl's guide to Larracos — he is the one who walks the party down through the city's rings and explains its layout, its defences and the explosives rigged on every other down-ramp. Personally wants revenge on **[Architect Houston](architect-houston.md)**. On NPC mortality: "**We believe that for most of us, if one dies, they are dead forever. We are not immortal like the outworlders.**" Guards the door at Louis and Juice Box's wedding |
| **Big Tina** | Allosaurus; daughter of **Kiwi**, raised in the FUPA (Book 5–7) | The "ballerina dinosaur," still carrying her magical self-repairing feather boa and her dead magic wand. Later single-handedly takes out one of Larracos's fire lance towers and shields dozens of crawlers from being burned alive. Inseparable from Simoom the albino rhino, who she treats as a pet |
| **Bobby, Low Thi, and Morris Sp** | The Tomb Raiders — a subterranean-quadrant crawler team (Book 4) | Bobby D.J. All three die completing the quadrant's final task — disconnecting/reconnecting the necropolis's lightning-conduction wiring under fire from the ghost boss Quetzalcoatlus — earning the quadrant's liberation posthumously; teammates Tyler Storm, Mike Barnes 3, and Janice die earlier in the same questline |
| **Bodi** | Level 60 Big Game Spear Hunter; veteran of **Land War**, a different game inflicted on his own home planet (Book 7) | Tall, tan-skinned, and almost silent — "He'd said maybe three words since I'd met him, making Chris seem downright chatty." Book 5: rescued a second time from Floor 6, using her ballista skills against attacking mantises during the *Twister*'s destruction. **Book 7:** crews the airship *Party Planner* alongside Louis and **Skarn**, having swapped her Dallas Cowboys jersey for an oversized 105th Scream Warriors shirt ("We bleed when the job's done"). | **Captain Fresh** | Syndicate security officer, handler for the assassin Minus (Book 8) | Preps Minus for his undercover mission posing as his own dead brother "Linus," supplying five hundred hours of injected memory footage and a cover story built on the real Linus's finances: "If the AI so much as *suspects* you're anything other than a simple tourist, it's going to pop you like a boil." | **Cascadia** | Kua-tin lead engineer and **executive producer** of *Dungeon Crawler World: Earth*; the "kill, kill, kill" announcer (Books 1–6) | The voice that opens every floor. Book 6 shows her in the flesh for the first time: at the Faction Wars preproduction meeting Carl needles her about Administrator Loita's death, she blinks out — and then reappears **physically in the room**, splashing water across the table, rebreather strapped over her gills. "**I'm really here now, Carl… You want to hurt me? Now's your chance… *I fucking dare you*. It would be the end of the both of us. I would godsdamned *welcome* it.**" She also warns she is "one crisis shy of pulling the failsafe, which would kill us all." The instant the other warlords arrive she snaps on a fake smile and her announcer voice — a change Carl finds "instantaneous and unnerving." Recently divorced. **Book 7:** she disintegrates in public. The news is not public, and Vinata will not say how she knows it. Cascadia's last word in the meeting is a laugh with "a hint of madness to it": "**Well, why not? Why the fuck not?**" |

Odette has set aside a trust for him. **Book 7:** drafted as the warlord council's field correspondent because Magnificent Troy refuses to come down to the surface, and spends the meeting narrating over people's shoulders with a microphone and a lint roller. Odette takes over the broadcast in his place: "my very old friend Chaco, who is feeling a little… punched in the face right now" |
