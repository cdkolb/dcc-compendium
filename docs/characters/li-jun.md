# Li Jun

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Crawler
    - **Class:** Street Monk (Book 2)
    - **Level:** Ranked 9th on the Floor 3 leaderboard (Book 2)
    - **Party:** Independent; travels with his best friend Zhang and his sister Li Na on Floor 4
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** Acrobatic melee combat — flips, tentacle grabs, and throws; a red-glowing unarmed buff; **Walk on Air** (lost permanently to Carl at the end of Book 6, later re-bought from a book)
    - **Notable items:** **The Engine of Tomorrow** (Book 7) — a celestial-tier robotic eye replacing the one Carl ate
    - **Status:** **Deceased** — killed by the god **Harpocrates** in Chapter 68 of Book 7, drawing the god away from Donut's tower

## Introduction

First introduced in Book 2 as a fellow guest on *Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem*, whom Carl
recognizes as someone he'd previously helped save from troglodytes and Brindle Grubs during that
same abusive broadcast appearance. He ranks 9th on the Floor 3 leaderboard as a Street Monk.

## Floor 4: Leading From the Front

By Book 3, Li Jun has become a key ally in the fight to hold station 36 against the Iron Tangle's
collapsing ghoul-generator network, personally volunteering (along with his best friend **Zhang**,
an Earth Mover mage-tank, and his sister **Li Na**) to help Carl retrieve a stranded interdiction
cart through monster-infested tunnels. His fighting style is acrobatic and improvisational —
flipping through the air, grabbing tentacles, and throwing full-grown Krakaren monsters — and his
quiet competence contrasts sharply with his sister's blunter, more intimidating presence.

Li Jun and Zhang are instrumental in the operation to bait and capture two mantaur neighborhood
bosses as "vessels" for Carl's involuntary deity-summon gambit against the floor's mimic city
boss (see [Carl](carl.md)); Zhang's one-charge shrinking wand is also what makes it physically
possible to move a full-size interdiction cart through the Tangle's narrow service tunnels.

## Family

- **Zhang** — Li Jun's best friend, an Earth Mover mage-tank in segmented black-and-gold armor,
  quietly in love with Li Na without her brother's knowledge
- **Li Na** — Li Jun's sister, a Level 30 Changbi Slave Driver whose disturbing, demon-like
  appearance belies a sharp tactical mind; she's the one who proposes using the Def Leppard
  interdiction cart to bait and portal away a province boss, and who dispassionately amputates and
  repeatedly heals a captured mantaur to make it easier to manage as a summoning vessel

## Book 5 Notes

Li Jun is secretly infected with vampirism on Floor 6, telling only his sister Li Na and Zhang, and
is cured floor-wide when Miriam Dom dies. He continues fighting alongside Katia's splinter team
through the hunter war and the Butcher's Masquerade, visibly shaken afterward by the scale of the
final battle's losses.

## Book 6 Notes

Li Jun spends most of Floor 8 in Beijing with his sister Li Na and Chris Andrews, and comes to Cuba
with the rescue party. He is also the one who brings Carl the news of the **Battle of Beijing** and
the five Tier-3 Doggie Door upgrades [Lucia Mar](lucia-mar.md) left behind — including her parting
threat against Donut and Mongo, which Li Jun passes on privately.

### The Eye

In the last fight, Carl is hijacked by his own totem, Shi Maria, who needs ink for a tattoo. Li Na
fires a chain down through a burning apartment and Li Jun slides down it to help his friend.

> "Carl, what can I do?" It was Li Jun, looking at me worriedly. All around him, the walls continued
> to burn.
>
> *I warned him,* Shi Maria said in my mind.
>
> *No*, I thought. *No. He's my friend.*
>
> I took the stick from the tattoo kit, and I jabbed it directly into Li Jun's eye. He cried and fell
> back. I pulled the stick to my mouth, and I licked. *Oh god, oh god. His eye*. His eye was at the
> end of the stick. I crunched down and ate it.

Shi Maria, still wearing Carl, then threatens Li Na to keep her from healing him: "Do not approach,
demon, or your brother will lose more than an eye, and Carl will lose his own heart… Do not attempt
to heal him."

The eye does not come back. Carl's own verdict — "I'd eaten his eye. I'd eaten his goddamn eye. It was
gone. It wouldn't come back." — and Louis's casualty report afterwards confirms it. Carl does not get
the chance to apologise before the floor collapses. "I hadn't had time yet to apologize for what I'd
done to Li Jun's eye. I hoped he was okay."

## Book 7: This Inevitable Ruin

Li Jun enters Floor 9 wearing an eyepatch. He and [Li Na](li-na.md) skip the officer ceremony in the
guild hall entirely because they are out "seeking out a healer who might be able to regrow Li Jun's
eye" — a search that never succeeds. Carl opens the book by trying to apologise:

> Carl: Hey, Li Jun. I didn't have a chance to talk to you at the end of the last floor. I wanted to
> apologize about what happened, and I was hoping we could talk.
>
> He didn't answer.

Carl is meanwhile stuck carrying the evidence. The *Walk on Air* skill that Shi Maria tore out of Li
Jun is now permanently Carl's, and he uses it repeatedly through the book, each time with the taste
of the eye in his mouth. Katia's verdict on why Li Na has gone silent on Carl: "She's not mad at you.
She's mad at herself for not protecting her brother." (Li Jun does get *Walk on Air* back, but only
because they bought the skill book.)

### Shanty Town and Club Vanquisher

He spends the ceasefire and the first war phase in **Shanty Town** with Li Na and the
**107th White Dragon Urban Defense battalion**, reporting tower construction progress into the war
chat — and he is the one who spots **[Lucia Mar](lucia-mar.md)** drinking at the Possum Envy and
passes it to [Florin](florin.md).

In the assault on **Club Vanquisher** he is one of the wire team. He and **Zhang** trail the
capacitor wire from Li Na's clock tower into the club so her **Dreads** will reach inside, with Li
Jun "hanging impossibly off the wall" to loop a wire around the mounted dragon's head over the
fireplace, then holding the interior door open so the aura stays live. He also sits out in the open
watching his sister's dread levels and telling her when to switch.

### The Engine of Tomorrow

The raid earns five crawlers a Celestial, Who-Let-The-Gods-Out Box. Li Jun's is a celestial-tier
robotic eye, discussed on the broadcast the day before Carl and Katia's own interview:

> "Yesterday we talked to Li Jun about his celestial tier robotic eye and Zhang's new cape with the
> target on the back."

Carl's closer look at it:

> His new eye, which at the moment was a swirling orb of black, unnerved me. When it was working, the
> black changed to look like a miniature, clockwork engine, all contained in the round eye. Even the
> name of the thing was a little freaky. The Engine of Tomorrow.

It lets him see muscles tensing and slows his own perception of time in short bursts — "Like Bullet
Time from the Matrix movies." He and Li Na train with it nonstop. It is also the last thing he
notices about Carl before his death: he corners him outside the brig, asks "How are you doing?",
refuses the deflection, and presses — "Yes, but what about you?"

### Death

In **Chapter 68**, Donut has the entire allied army rolled up inside her **Rolling Battle Formation**
ball and cannot leave the Nest tower until the ball reaches the corpse field. The god
**Harpocrates** — who she has just spent several minutes insulting over a public address system — has
marked her for death and is closing on the tower.

> Li Jun: He's going to get to you before the ball reaches the other field. I have an idea. Speaker
> number 11 is still on the back of a cart. Mute all the speakers except that one. Play the song I
> just sent you.
>
> Li Na: Jun, no. No!
>
> Li Jun: Sorry, little sister. I'll jump before he catches me.

Carl, elbow-deep in the Madness castle at the time: "Li Jun was distracting the angry god so Donut
could finish her plan, and it was all I could do to not scream."

His last message is to his sister:

> Li Jun: Sister. Tell Zhang I'm sorry I didn't get to say goodbye to his face. Tell him I won our
> bet. And you. Protect him. I want you to be kind to the others. This was my decision. I had to do
> it. I know you get angry. If you must, be angry with me. I love you with all I have. I love you
> with the moon and the sun and all the stars in the sky. I will see you soon, and I will tell mama
> you said hello.

The death itself happens off-page. What Carl gets instead is a vision, in the half-second he spends
non-corporeal inside Architect Houston's trap room — the thing surgically attached to Houston turns,
leans in as if to kiss him, and briefly wears Li Jun's face:

> "Thank you for giving me more time with my sister and my best friend," Li Jun said as roots wrapped
> around him and suddenly whisked him away.

Then, in the wreckage:

> Li Jun: Thank you for giving me more time with my sister and my best friend.
>
> This message is from a deceased crawler.
>
> "No, no, Li Jun. No," I whispered, my voice hoarse. I thought of that moment when we'd been face to
> face. Was that real? What did that mean?

!!! note "Unresolved"
    The book never explains the vision. Carl himself cannot tell whether it was real, and nobody else
    saw it. The creature was a dead changeling that Houston had been surgically operating on while
    raving about something he called **the Beautiful Place**; no connection between that and Li Jun's
    appearance is ever drawn.

### Afterwards

Li Jun's sacrifice buys Donut the time she needs, and the guilt lands on everybody. Li Na tells
Donut, to her face, that her brother's death is her fault; Donut repeats it to Carl and then adds,
"But she's not wrong, either." Carl has no time to grieve — he registers, mid-battle, that "Li Jun
had been in charge of these towers" and has to take manual control of them from a menu, then notes
flatly: "There wasn't time to grieve the loss of Li Jun."

Zhang, who never got his goodbye, is the one who lets Donut off the hook:

> Zhang: Donut. Jun was always afraid he wouldn't do anything important. He was afraid his sister
> would be lost without him. You helped her get strong enough to protect herself. That would've made
> him proud, and it makes me proud to have you as a friend.

His sister's response is the rest of the book. She gouges out her own eye and installs his celestial
eye in the socket, takes Carl's spare Ring of Divine Suffering to its logical end, and becomes the
first crawler ever to max out every base stat — see [Li Na](li-na.md).
