# Corcunda

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** NPC — a half-mantaur stripper and Dong Quixote's long-lost lover, magically split
      years ago into two separate half-bodies
    - **Race:** Mantaur, split by a spell called **Split Personality** — "right down the center,
      right between the eyes and all the way down through the groin." Each half stands on one leg
      and has one full arm plus one exposed half-arm
    - **Class/Subclass:** Not stated
    - **Level:** Not stated
    - **Religion:** A worshipper of the war god **Grull** before the split; the devotion lapsed
      while he was divided, which Grull later treats as apostasy
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** None of his own; his reunification is performed on him by the
      fleshmancer [Grigori the Placid](grigori.md)
    - **Notable items:** None described
    - **Status:** **Deceased** — smitten by Grull for "failing in faith" moments after being made
      whole, and dies in Heat 5 of Floor 10 charging the god alongside [Dong Quixote](supporting-cast.md),
      lance in hand

## Overview

Corcunda is Floor 10's emotional centerpiece: a captive split in half years before the book opens,
found in two pieces on the racetrack, reunited by a hired fleshmancer mid-race, and killed by his
own god within minutes of being whole again — taking Dong Quixote, the man who never stopped
looking for him, down with him.

## Corky, Porky, and the Gimp

He is first spotted in Chapter 13 (Heat 2) as "the gimp" — a full-body vinyl suit worn to keep
his organs sealed in, listed as the property of the womantaur team **Genesis and Rapture**
("Lady Dominators and the Gimp"). Razor fox **Chiyome** explains the mechanic on sight:

> "There is a spell called Split Personality. Cuts you right down the middle... They can come
> together and get reglued with a fleshmancer."

Donut is the one who puts the name to the face:

> "So, do you think that's Dong Quixote's friend? The half-mantaur he's always talking about? What
> was his name? Corcunda?"

The system opens a two-part quest on the spot: reunite Dong with the half he's found (**Corky**),
then locate the other half — nicknamed **Porky**, who is "also somewhere on this floor" — and find
a fleshmancer willing to put the two of them back together. Porky turns out to be hiding elsewhere
on Floor 10, running a hidden stage-performance guild. Carl decides not to tell Dong Quixote what
they've found until the reunion is actually possible, worried the reveal is a setup: "If it is
Dong's long-lost partner or whatever, they put him here to fuck with us and the team... If we get
involved, it's going to backfire."

## Grigori the Placid

The party hires the fleshmancer **[Grigori the Placid](grigori.md)** specifically for this quest.
He admits up front he has never actually completed a reunion like this before — "It is quite rare
for two halfsies to survive more than a few minutes... so I haven't had the opportunity" — and, in
the run-up, never thinks to ask whether either half still worships a god. That oversight is what
kills him.

## The Reunion

The two halves come together mid-race in Heat 5, inside a collapsing steel-mill/gasworks track,
while the truck is sliding sideways through molten metal. Grigori has Dong and Donut strip both
halves out of their containment suits and wrap the exposed half-brains in plastic wrap to keep
them sealed, then physically press the torsos together:

> "Okay, hold them together!" Grigori yelled... Grigori cast something, and the guts, dangling
> from Porky's exposed double torsos, floated by his side... I finally saw both of their faces and
> half-mullets. They looked just like any other mantaur, only just cut in half.

Dong holds them together bodily through the whole procedure:

> "I have you," Dong shouted... "I have you, and I will never let go again!"

The joining itself is rendered as a system effect — two health bars, each in the red, merging into
one:

> The two halves, Corky and Porky, still had health bars over each half, also in the red, but as I
> watched, the health bars joined, and they became one. Their names disappeared and a single name
> reappeared. **Corcunda.**

Whole, he has "a bright red jagged scar right down the middle of his face and chest," not
perfectly aligned, with little bulges along the seam. (The text uses male pronouns for Corcunda
throughout — for both halves and for the fused whole — and Dong Quixote's own address to him, "my
love," is unambiguous about the relationship. An earlier revision of this page used she/her; that
was an error.)

His first words as a single being are not relief. They're a warning:

> "Hail! Grull has come for me," Corcunda groaned as he helped Dong up. His voice remained the same
> as before, though his mannerisms were suddenly much more mantaur-like. "I thought I was free of
> the vile god when I split, but the moment I came back together, he sent me a message and told me
> I was to be punished for not being troo!"

## Apostasy

Grull's own words to him make the charge explicit — not heresy toward a rival god, but abandoned
loyalty during the years he spent split and unable to worship at all:

> "Corcunda, you have failed in your faith, and now you must be punished. You cannot hide. You
> cannot flee. Accept your punishment like the warrior you once were."

Grigori takes the blame on himself:

> "I should have asked if you worshipped a deity before you split. I apologize for my oversight."

## The Charge

Corcunda refuses Donut's offer to fight the god in his place, and turns to Dong instead:

> "You worried if I would no longer love you once I returned to this form. But how could I not?...
> You have been nothing but a shining light in my life, and I want to thank you for always holding
> true. But now I have to go to Grull. The only way he will leave is once I am properly smitten."

He strips off the only thing he's wearing — Dong's own scarf, passed to Porky's half years
earlier — and returns it to him. Dong's answer:

> "My love," he said, "do you really think I will ever leave your side again?"

Dong draws the massive lance he keeps in his inventory — a weapon Carl notes he hasn't seen since
the day he handed Dong the sentient sock **Jefferson** — and says, "It's been a long time since
I've tilted a giant." Corcunda kneels on his middle arms so Dong
can ride him one last time: "Hail, my love... It will be an honor to be ridden one last time."

**The magnet:** Donut casts her **Big-Ass Magnet** spell onto Dong's lance to punch through the
horde of slag elementals between them and the god — the room floor is littered with millions of
loose screws from a shattered crate, and the enchantment drags every screw within 200 metres of the
lance toward it as they close the distance, shredding the elementals in their path. Donut's
warning: "It has to be cast on an object!... It will activate right away, but it will increase in
intensity for about fifteen seconds after I place it, so make sure you're far away from us or else
the lance will get ripped from your hand!"

Dong's goodbye, given before the charge:

> "Goodbye, Carl. Goodbye, Princess Donut," Dong said. "Please tell the others that I will miss
> them, but I will see them again on the other side. Tell them it was my pleasure to serve with
> them. Vale to you all. Vale."

And then the charge itself:

> And with a terrifying, in-unison roar, Corcunda and Dong jumped from the back of the truck and
> galloped directly at Grull just as the magnet spell activated. Every screw within two hundred
> meters of the lance jumped into the air and rocketed toward the charging pair... And through it
> all, Dong and Corcunda charged. They charged, and they charged. Riding into battle one last time.
> Dong Quixote, now with his lance lowered, now getting peppered himself with dozens, then hundreds,
> then thousands of screws, roared in triumph as the weapon made contact with the god of war.

Grull, unkilled but visibly moved, withdraws rather than press further:

> "A good death," Grull rumbled before he vanished, going back to the twelfth floor. "A good death
> indeed."

Both Corcunda and Dong Quixote are confirmed dead in the same breath the race ends. Carl: "Dong
Quixote was dead. I took a breath, letting it sink in. What a way to go. What a goddamned hero."
Donut: "Dong is dead... He was my friend, and he's dead." The Heat 5 results board lists it plainly:
**Eliminated: Lady Dominators and the Gimp.**

The quest-completion text underlines the timing: "Not only did you get them back together, but you
did it before Dong died."

Grigori offers the only comfort given afterward: "Some of us believe that death is nothing but
movement on to another plane of existence. Dong did not think this was the end."
