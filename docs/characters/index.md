# Characters

Full pages exist for the series' central duo, their guide, and anyone else who's earned it.
Everyone else who appears with real substance in Book 1 is listed below in a table; pages will
be split out as they accumulate enough detail across later books.

!!! note "Promotion rule"
    A supporting-cast entry graduates from a table row to a full page once either:

    - they've appeared with real material across **2+ books**, or
    - they already have a **significant plot role**, even within a single book — a real
      turning point, a named recurring ability/relationship, not just a scene appearance.

!!! note "Quick Facts standard"
    Every full page's Quick Facts box should include **Type**, **Class**, and **Subclass**
    (even if the answer is "not yet assigned" or "not applicable"), plus **Notable
    spells/abilities** and **Notable items** — linking to spell/item pages where they exist.
    If the book genuinely shows none (e.g. a Non-Combatant NPC), say so explicitly rather than
    omitting the field. Any spell, item, or physical skill named here must itself have a full
    Reference page (see [Spells](../spells/index.md) / [Items & Tattoos](../items/index.md) /
    [Abilities & Skills](../abilities/index.md)) — not just a table row — and the Quick Facts
    entry must link to it. Magic spells go under Spells; physical combat skills (no mana cost,
    no tome/scroll) go under Abilities & Skills.
