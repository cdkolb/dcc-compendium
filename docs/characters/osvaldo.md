# Osvaldo

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Human Crawler
    - **Race:** **Curupira** (drawn from Brazilian folklore) — small-framed, red-haired (not his
      natural color), with backward-facing feet hidden under large boots
    - **Class:** Ranger
    - **Level:** 60 (Book 6) → **72** (Book 8)
    - **Religion:** Worships **Tupa**, alongside teammate Filipe L
    - **Team:** **Team Flamengo** — unaffiliated through Faction Wars, then racing solo on Floor 10
      with Filipe L driving
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** Not described in detail beyond ranger fundamentals
    - **Notable items:** Two separate, non-functional **memorial crystals** tied to a standing quest
      from Tupa (see below); previously owned the pet hawk **Gimli**, killed at the end of the
      Butcher's Masquerade
    - **Status:** **Deceased** — killed in Heat 7 of Floor 10 by [Britney Proskurina](britney.md),
      who claims his memorial crystal from Ysalte and vanishes toward Sheol

## Overview

Osvaldo is a Brazilian ranger who spends three books circling the edges of Carl's story — a
leaderboard name, then a looter, then a quiet holdout — before Book 8 finally gives him both a real
arc and a real death. He never joins Carl's guild and never really likes Carl, but the two of them
share more history than either particularly wants: two crossed memorial-crystal quests, a shared
loss to the same crawler's carelessness, and, in the end, the same god's mercenary as his killer.

## Books 6–7: The Crystal Nobody Can Use

Osvaldo first appears in Book 6 at **#8** on the leaderboard, Level 60, owner of the pet hawk
**Gimli** — killed at the end of the Butcher's Masquerade, likely by Prepotente or his familiar
Bianca fighting over Queen Imogen's corpse. Osvaldo is the one who loots **Queen Imogen's Memorial
Crystal** off her body in the chaos, which puts him directly in the path of both Carl's own standing
Emberus quest (find whoever killed the god's son, Geyrun) and Prepotente's parallel private quest.
Book 7 shows him teaming with a crawler named Jurgen and, off-page, losing the crystal to
Prepotente during the chaos at the end of Faction Wars — the theft that finally moves Carl's
long-stalled Emberus quest forward. Osvaldo and Team Flamengo sit out most of Faction Wars in the
woods, a choice neither Donut nor Elle forgives, and then decline the Outreach Guild exit deal and
continue on to Floor 10 anyway.

## Book 8: A Second Crystal, and a Shared God-Quest

Osvaldo reappears on Floor 10 riding the giant slaughter gnu **Bruna**, with teammate **Filipe L**
(a level-63 human Strongman) holding the reins. He's still a prickly, standoffish presence — "he
only talked to me when terrible stuff was happening" — but opens up to Carl in a rare direct message
about the crystal business, revealing it was never resolved:

> "I had a quest to collect a memorial crystal for Tupa. I didn't get it until right at the moment
> that Imogen died, and my entire party would have gotten smitten if I didn't collect it, so I did.
> But then after I got it, he said that one was corrupted, wrong. Then I had a lead on a second one
> in the forest during Faction Wars, and we hunted it down, and then the god said that one was
> wrong, too, and now I have a new quest to find out why the memorial crystals aren't working
> right."

He gave the first (Imogen's) crystal to Prepotente not out of generosity but exhaustion and spite —
he'd meant to smash it in front of the goat as payback for Gimli's death, then simply handed it over
instead, only for Prepotente to be ungrateful about the timing. Osvaldo's closing line on the whole
chain of crossed quests doubles as the book's bleakest joke about Carl himself:

> "Ysalte. The Vinegar Bitch. The one Paz killed. He was my friend, you know. Paz and Anton and even
> Sister Ines. It's like everyone who ever comes in contact with you dies. Now it's our turn, I
> suppose."

## Racing Floor 10

Osvaldo and Filipe race independently through every heat, occasionally overlapping with Carl's team
or Jasha and Radoslav's Team Free Love in the garage row, trading beer-and-gossip banter about the
other teams (Dwight's cheating win, the Corcunda situation) without ever joining Carl's alliance
outright. Carl repeatedly urges him not to take unnecessary risks and to consider an exit deal,
without success:

> **Carl:** Osvaldo, you're in the same heat as us. Don't do anything too rash. We have plans in
> motion to get as many people out as possible. Don't kill any of the other racers.
>
> **Osvaldo:** Fuck off, Carl. I'm not talking to you. I won't attack you during the race unless
> it's you or us, but I don't give a shit about any of the other teams.

## Death

Osvaldo and Filipe reach Heat 7 — the last heat of Floor 10 — still unaffiliated and still refusing
to commit to an exit deal, which puts them in the same heat as Carl's team and forces a
crawler-vs-crawler outcome. During the race, cornered and stunned against a portal wall while
fleeing, he is killed outright by **Britney Proskurina**, who has jumped into the same heat and
claims the kill for a bounty:

> **A champion has fallen. A bounty has been claimed.**
>
> **Crawler Osvaldo has been killed by Crawler Britney Proskurina.**
>
> **Team Three, Team Flamengo, has been eliminated due to the death of their last racer.**

Britney lands directly on his corpse as the kill lands, a golden player-killer skull forming over
her head. Carl's only reaction is a private, helpless one: "You stubborn idiot... Why did it have to
come to this?"

What Britney takes from the body is not the crystal Prepotente already has — it's the *other*
memorial crystal Osvaldo had been chasing all book, the one tied to the long-dead goddess
**Ysalte**:

> "I saw her," I said, watching Britney, my paws up on the dash. The barbarian crawler casually
> sauntered back toward the gecko and Louis. "She has the memorial crystal from Ysalte now."

Samantha immediately flags what that means: "She needs to get to the Basilica or the Vulgar Temple
and place the gem in the plinth, and she'll be back for good." Britney disappears through a portal
shortly after, taking the crystal with her; see [Britney Proskurina](britney.md) for where that
leads.
