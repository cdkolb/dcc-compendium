# Elle McGibbons

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Crawler
    - **Race/Class:** Frost Maiden Blizzardmancer → **Tundra Princess** (Floor 6 specialty) →
      **Hailstorm Queen** (Book 7, Floor 9 — she picked wind)
    - **Age (pre-collapse):** 99
    - **Level:** 13 by end of Book 2 (10th on the Floor 3 leaderboard); **72** after *Graupel* in
      Book 6. **Not stated anywhere in Book 7**
    - **Party:** Independent; closely allied with [Carl](carl.md)'s party and Imani C/Li Jun's group on Floor 4. **General** in the Princess Posse (Book 7)
    - **Role (Book 7):** Deep-penetration scout and **missile spotter** for the Posse's artillery
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** High-speed flight (well beyond her normal limit when pushed with a scroll); freeze-based attacks, effective at turning caustic Krakaren acid pools solid so they can be safely cleared; [*Graupel*](../spells/graupel.md) (Book 6); **Subterfuge** (Book 7), which hides a whole party from enemy detection
    - **Notable items:** Not described in detail beyond her Frost Maiden gear. She carried the
      [Gate of the Feral Gods](../items/gate-of-the-feral-gods.md) through the first half of Book 7
      and **no longer has it** — it was taken from her hands in chapter 61

## From Meadow Lark's Elder to Floor 4's Wisecracking Scout

Introduced in Book 1 as one of the elderly "Meadow Lark" evacuees under [Brandon An](brandon-an.md),
Elle McGibbons became an unlikely Book 2 leaderboard entry (10th place, Level 13, Frost Maiden
Blizzardmancer) after starting the season at Level 1. By Book 3 she's a fully fleshed-out,
foul-mouthed, and fiercely loyal member of Carl's extended circle — no longer a background
survivor but one of the floor's most active scouts and problem-solvers.

## Role in the Floor 4 Crisis

Elle serves as the group's fastest long-range scout and messenger throughout the Iron Tangle
disaster, repeatedly flying at speeds well past her normal limit (at one cost of a "precious
scroll") to relay intel, hunt down missing crawlers (including a fruitless search for
[Eva Sigrid](eva-sigrid.md) on Carl's behalf), and freeze pools of caustic acid blood so
defenders can safely clear chokepoints. She talks Carl down from confrontations as often as she
escalates them, and delivers some of the book's driest commentary on the chaos around her
("Sometimes I think you're cheating, Carl. How do you know all this stuff?").

## Personality and Background

Sharp-tongued, protective, and openly affectionate with Carl and Donut despite the constant
ribbing — she kisses Carl on the cheek after a risky flying maneuver and needles him relentlessly
about his tendency to cause (and then have to explain) enormous explosions. She mentions a
boyfriend before her current partner "Barry" in passing, and coined the nickname "ghoul coaster"
for the improvised, monster-infested escape route crawlers without portal hats are forced to use
at the abyss.

## Book 5 Notes

Elle picks a **Tundra Princess** floor specialty on Floor 6, blending her Frost Maiden ice magic
with an earth-based build; Mordecai notes she's quietly pursuing a rare "four seasons" build that
requires surviving to Floor 12 with tightly controlled stat allocation. She fights alongside
Katia's splinter team through the floor's hunter war, personally interrogates a grieving hunter
at the Butcher's Masquerade about the circumstances of Diwata's forced "birth" from his brother,
and survives the climactic battle.

## Book 6 Notes

### Six Levels in One Cast

During the assault on the FOCSA building, with a wave of demons closing on the alley where Donut is
rooted mid-cast, Elle uses the one spell she's been saving — ***Graupel***, a war spell with a
one-week cooldown that she has cast exactly once before in her life.

> "Oh shit, oh shit," Elle shouted as the blue glow around her increased in intensity. "**Guys, it
> says it's casting more powerful than usual. Holy shit, head's up!**"

Fist-sized hail comes down hard enough to make the asphalt explode and the ground shake like an
earthquake. Almost every red dot on Carl's map turns to an X. When the steam clears there is a
knee-high field of melting ice and mangled demon corpses as far as he can see.

> Elle remained in place as level notifications pinged over and over above her head, settling her
> onto **level 72**. She'd risen six levels at once.
>
> "What the hell? That spell is strong, but it's not usually *that* strong. **It's only level five.
> It said it cast at level 16.**"

!!! warning "Unresolved"
    Nothing explains the amplification. Samantha, who can enhance spells, says flatly "I didn't do
    that," and then breaks into an incoherent rant about Elle being "on the four seasons path," a
    tree, a river, and "maybe the prince's spell enhanced her." The book never resolves it. See
    [Samantha](samantha.md#the-elle-rant).

### Second Voice

Elle spends Book 6 as the person who says the thing everyone else is thinking. She is the one who
interrogates Carl's escape plan on the floor's behalf — "What the hell, Carl? Are you blowing smoke
up our ass? Is this a sure thing, or are you working off a Carl theory?" and "That's the whole
message? Don't fight? Isolate the stairwells? You're gonna have to give us more than that, cowboy.
People are terrified. They're gonna need a solid reason." — and then she and Katia go out and sell it
to the rest of the dungeon.

She is also the one who states the guild's position on Carl's self-sacrifice plans: "That's not how
this works, Carl. You were right, what you said in your little speech. **We're all in this together.
We're all screwed if we don't all make it to the next floor.**" And she is the only person Imani will
talk to after Imani's squad dies.

!!! note "Wording"
    The book never labels Elle second-in-command. The role is visible in what she does, not in a
    title.

## Book 7: This Inevitable Ruin

### Hailstorm Queen

Floor 9 forces another class selection, and Elle reports it in chat before Carl has even finished
reading his own notifications:

> Elle: Hey guys, what’d I miss? I just had to go through class selection again. There were only a
> few of us in the warehouse this time. I picked wind. I went from Tundra Princess to Hailstorm
> Queen.
>
> Donut: YOU’RE A QUEEN? THAT’S GREAT!
>
> Elle: Yeah. This is probably as far as it’s gonna get. I can add the final element if I ever make
> it to the 12th floor, but I’m betting this whole shitshow self-destructs before we even get close.

!!! note "No class description is printed"
    The system never prints a description card for Hailstorm Queen anywhere in Book 7. The chat lines
    above are the entire text on the subject. What the class does has to be read off what she does
    with it — ice spells, flight, and the four-seasons progression she has been chasing since Book 5,
    which she now confirms needs the 12th floor to complete.

Her manager this floor is **Mistress Tiatha**, a drunk half-elf who spends the book being useful
anyway: she trains Imani on battlefield enhancement spells, and it is Tiatha, not Carl, who invents
the detonation-wire exploit used in the Club Vanquisher raid.

### Scout and spotter

Elle is the Posse's eyes past the wire for the entire floor. She maps both enemy borders, counts the
Dream's artillery ("approximately 40 of the big trebuchets set up and dozens of the small ones"),
watches a hundred Madness soldiers go invisible, and reports it all in real time. Her running
commentary is unchanged from Book 3:

> Elle: Holy cannoli, guys. These bald elf pricks have all sorts of hardware out here… Why are they
> allowed to have so many of these things when we can’t?

The spotter role is the new one. When Louis's airship goes in, Elle is the one lasing the anti-air
batteries for Carl's missiles:

> Elle: I have the first anti-air gun targeted. I hope this missile launcher of yours works as
> advertised, Carl. Firing the moment Louis drops.

> Elle: The second anti-air battery is down! Louis, hurry your ass up! The first one is almost back
> up. I’m running. They have a pair of mages on my scent.

Later, outside the Madness castle, she calls in **cheese cutters** — the anti-personnel,
shrapnel-filled missiles — against a charging column of hundreds of carts. She also carries
**Subterfuge**, a concealment spell that covers everyone standing inside it:

> Your platoon member, General Elle McGib has cast a Subterfuge spell, and your presence is hidden
> from enemy eyes. Do not venture from the area of the spell, or the Madness will be automatically
> warned of your incursion.

### Losing the Gate

Elle is the Posse's gate operator. She carries the
[Gate of the Feral Gods](../items/gate-of-the-feral-gods.md) through the middle of the book and works
it like artillery — walking it from front to front and dropping a feral god on each enemy castle in
turn.

> General Elle: Gate one is open, and I’m stepping through. Let’s see what comes out of this one.
> Next stop, the Reavers. Don’t be late, Carl.

Gate one lands **Meatus** beside the Dream castle while the Dream's army is out in the field, which
Rosetta calls immediately: "Yes! Good job, Elle. The Dream have made a tactical error leaving their
castle unprotected." The Reavers are supposed to be next, and Carl is supposed to meet her there off
Louis's bombing run.

Chapter 61 goes wrong in stages. The volcano the Posse detonated erupts harder than the dwarves
predicted, knocks over her winding box, spills the watches, and fires the gate early, putting a
twenty-minute clock on it. Then a search spell strips her Subterfuge. Then **King Stalwart** — the
new leader of the Skull Empire, fleeing a losing battle with Donut's magic missiles in him —
teleports directly onto her position, bringing a squad of war mages with him.

> Elle: Carl! Stalwart is here!
>
> I ran toward the commotion, preparing to use my Wraith Phase. As I rushed forward, Elle flew past
> me, flying over my shoulder, having been tossed by a spell. I saw the Unconscious tag over her
> head. She hit a tree behind me with a sickening crack, and the tree toppled over.

She got one spell off first — Carl arrives to a clearing under quickly-melting ice and four dead war
mages, killed by Donut's amped-up Magic Missile on the way in. The surviving mages are not there for
Elle. They tear King Stalwart apart in front of Carl ("My sister was right," he says, before he
explodes), and their leader turns out to be holding her gate.

> He had taken the Gate of the Feral Gods from Elle.
>
> The war mage was staring at my bare feet.
>
> “Nice shoes, asshole,” he said to me before the four of them teleported away.

Elle's own account, once she comes round, is one line:

> Elle: Sorry guys. That war mage prick snatched the gate right out of my hands.

Her next words are operational — "Carl, collect the bodies. They said the heads explode" — and then
she is back on her feet inside ten minutes and re-casting Subterfuge. The loss wrecks the plan; when
the fallback comes up later she is blunt about it: "It’s not really Plan B if we don’t have the feral
god gate." See [Akuma](akuma.md) for the war mage who took it.

### Kept alive on purpose

Long after the theft, Akuma opens a warlord chat to ask Carl to kill "that old lady" for him. Donut
assumes he means Elle and objects. He does not:

> Warlord Akuma: Is Elle the one on the Four Seasons path? Not her, dipshit. **Keep her alive if you
> wish for us to face Scolopendra and survive.** Agatha. The other warlord we were forced to accept
> in order to gain system access.

It is the strongest statement anyone has made about Elle's build. The most powerful non-god caster on
the floor regards her four-seasons progression as load-bearing for surviving **Scolopendra**, and
says so unprompted to the enemy.

### Elsewhere on the floor

- She and Edgar penetrate naga territory ahead of **Operation Snake Pit** and find the barracks full
  of rotting corpses — the discovery that the Blood Sultanate's army is already dead, killed by
  [Prepotente](prepotente.md).
- She leads the four-cart infiltration team into the naga stronghold with Imani, sweeps it, and takes
  the throne room.
- She argues hard *for* giving Li Na the spare Ring of Divine Suffering, over Imani's objection.
- She is frozen with everyone else by the **Bear Witness** spell, and gives Donut the plainest
  account of what Donut's *War Crime* actually saved them from: "it was like we were paralyzed, and
  we could feel everything, but we couldn’t move. It was awful."
- She helps hold the extracted naiad organs during Louis's transplant, and leads a flight of fairies
  icing over the lava pooling in the upper levels of Larracos.
- She is in the tent for Katia's goodbye — "Damnit, kiddo. Don’t make me cry." — and is alive at the
  end of the book.

Her register has not moved an inch in six books. On being knocked unconscious into a tree: "Damn, I
haven’t been walloped like that since my bachelorette party."

## Book 8: A Parade of Horribles

### Cracking Akuma

In Chapter 37, Carl's group ambushes **[Akuma](akuma.md)** at a Floor 10 upgrade shop after he sends
Carl a cryptic note about the Pineapple Cabaret. Elle, still owed payback for having the Gate of the
Feral Gods torn out of her hands back in Book 7, does the interrogating personally — freezing one of
his testicles solid with an ice bolt the instant Donut mutes him, then leaning in for the reunion she
never got to have:

> "Hey, remember me, you barely sapient magical fart?" Elle said, getting close to Akuma, whose
> sneering expression had turned to a look of extreme, gasping pain. "You and your little asshole
> friends tried to kill me. You stole the gate from me when I wasn't done with it yet. When I was a
> kid, do you know what we used to do to people who stole? We would hit them with sticks, and we
> would throw their shoes into the sound. But that was only because we couldn't get away with more."

The torture works. Held in an anti-magic shield with his health slowly draining, Akuma gives up the
existence of **Herot** — the 16th-edition cookbook author, a skink former crawler still alive and
secretly running the Pineapple Cabaret from the inside — along with extensive exposition on the
dungeon's "Big Six" power factions (see [Akuma](akuma.md#book-8-a-parade-of-horribles) for the full
lore dump). Elle gets one direct concession out of him for her trouble: he warns Carl elsewhere in
the same conversation to keep her alive specifically, because "if you wish for us to face
Scolopendra and survive," her progression matters.

### Linus, Unmasked

Elle also spends much of the floor with a devoted, lecherous superfan attached to her circle —
**"Linus."** He is not who he claims to be. See [Minus](supporting-cast.md) in the Supporting Cast
for the reveal: a Syndicate assassin wearing the identity of Elle's dead real-life fan, sent to kill
Imani C and Louis Santiago and destabilize the group. Elle later throws his exposure back at her own
allies as evidence nobody listens to her instincts: "After all the stuff me and Carl said at the
fuck-Linus party?"
