# Bautista (Daniel Bautista 2)

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Tigran Crawler
    - **Class:** Swashbuckler
    - **Level:** 28 (Book 3) → rising through Books 4–5
    - **Party:** Katia's splinter team
    - **Relationship:** Romantically involved with [Katia](katia.md) as of Book 5; still together at
      the end of Book 7, when she leaves the dungeon pregnant by someone else
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** Swashbuckler swordplay, trained alongside teammate Tran for a
      team-combat bonus
    - **Notable items:** A collection of hundreds of summonable beanbag-toy "stuffed animal"
      figures, looted on Floor 3, several with undocumented rare variants; a refresher kit letting
      him reuse a given figure's summon tag twice more; an **electronic stuffed-animal guidebook**
      (Book 7) that identifies any plush from its label before it is used

## Overview

Bautista is a Tigran (tiger-headed) crawler and the sole survivor of his family after Grimaldi's
lemurs killed them on Floor 3. He forms a debt/chat-contact bond with Carl after being rescued
during Book 3's Iron Tangle evacuation, later joining Katia's splinter team at the end of Book 4.

## Role in the Story — Book 5

Ranked #23 among crawlers by the time of the Butcher's Masquerade, Bautista fights with Katia's
team through Floor 6, using his beanbag-toy summons — including an
uncommon-variant beanie fitted with sunglasses and a fake tiara — to fake a convincing "clockwork
Donut" that fools Lucia Mar during a saferoom teleport-trap ambush aimed at drawing her out. He
confirms to Carl, somewhat nervously, that his
relationship with Katia is real and public; Donut reacts with theatrical, delighted shock. He
narrowly avoids having his head blown off by a hunter's pulse rifle during one of the floor's
coordinated raids (Gwendolyn Duet, fighting nearby, isn't so lucky — a separate pulse-rifle hit
punches a grapefruit-sized hole clean through her midsection, though she survives that one too),
and helps hold off Eva Sigrid alongside Florin when Katia nearly breaks the peace seal confronting
her at the Butcher's Masquerade.

## Role in the Story — Book 6

Bautista's Book 6 is his relationship with [Katia](katia.md) coming apart over her addiction and her
secrets, in four clear stages.

1. **He covers for her.** When Katia goes dark for ten hours after finding a drug den in the
   Desperado Club, Bautista stonewalls Carl: "She's okay. She's asleep. She found some secret merchant
   in the Desperado Club, and she's out of sorts. But she'll be okay in a bit." Pressed: "**Just leave
   it, okay? She'll be fine.**" Imani, whose husband is an addict, hears the same thing from him
   independently and names the pattern at once: "He didn't want to tell me any of this. The way he was
   making excuses for her… Let's just say it's familiar."
2. **The blow-up.** Carl wants a barely-conscious Katia, carrying twelve debuffs, to get up and go
   anyway. Bautista pushes her back down — "Where do you think you're going?" — then turns on Carl:
   "You can do this later. We have time." Carl refuses. "'**Fine. Kill yourself. But I won't stand by
   and watch,**' Bautista said. He turned and stormed from the club."
3. **Louis says it out loud** at the Christmas party, in the middle of the speech where he lists
   everything wrong with everyone: "Katia is struggling every day, and **Bautista is having a nervous
   breakdown**." Donut, separately, to Carl: "We're already losing Katia no matter what happens, and
   nobody is talking about it. Doesn't anybody see what it's doing to Bautista?"
4. **Where it stands at the end.** Carl asks Katia about him in Chapter 63: "The tiger crawler walked
   near the back of the group along with Elle, who was scanning the sky for threats. **It was clear
   their relationship had been damaged by recent events.**" Katia's only comment: "**He doesn't want
   me to use the orchid.**" Carl, privately, agrees with him.

Elsewhere on the floor he is doing exactly what he always does: making the tea that Elle reckons is
"the only thing keeping Imani together," spamming his stuffed-animal summons through the Ysalte fight,
and finally getting some use out of his shape-changing ring, turning from tiger into "a large,
four-armed creature." He is one of the four-man team that goes in on Sister Ines. Carl is also
deliberately not telling him that Donut looted an oak bracelet off one of his dead family members.

## Book 7: This Inevitable Ruin

Book 6 left Bautista and Katia's relationship damaged. Book 7 spends the whole floor letting Carl —
and the reader — assume it is going to end badly, and then resolves it in the last two chapters with
an act of quiet craftsmanship.

### The Floor

He fights all book as part of Katia's pair rather than as a commander in his own right. Carl's first
close look at him is in the field hospital before the war starts: "Bautista remained in the back of
the room, pacing back and forth, chewing on one of his finger claws. I wasn't too clear on the state
of Bautista and Katia's relationship, but she'd asked for him to be here."

He and Katia are attached to whichever front needs a hammer. Florin has them "with what's left of the
105th" flanking Carl's position during the King Rust standoff, holding open a retreat corridor into
Shanty Town. Later they are in **Group Four** with Carl, Li Na and the 107th White Dragons for the
Club Vanquisher operation, and he is part of Carl's hand-picked teleport squad for the assault on the
Madness castle — where he turns up "irritated he hadn't been summoned as part of the first group."

His job at Club Vanquisher is to walk into the temple of the Dagda and keep a suspicious cleric
talking while Li Jun and Zhang string Li Na's capacitor wire behind his back, killing him only if
necessary. He then holds the door out to Shanty Town so the aura stays live. He gets clear of the
club before the boss fight seals, which is why his achievement box is only legendary while the others
get celestial ones.

### The Eclipse Pyxies

His stuffed-animal collection finally gets a proper showcase. Katia's breach of Club Vanquisher is
opened by a pair of his plushes:

> From over both of Katia's shoulders flew a pair of ridiculously cute stuffed animals. They were
> stuffed versions of a demon mob called Eclipse Pyxies. They looked like fat, fur-covered guinea
> pigs, but with bat wings and jagged teeth. Bautista had a dozen of these things. The stuffed animals
> switched in midair from toys to actual monsters.

His legendary box from the raid is an electronic guidebook for the collection — scan a plush's label
and an info box tells you what it does before you summon it. He also uses the toys as a mapping tool
during the Reaver castle assault, throwing them into the randomly-shifting honeycomb corridors,
tracking where they end up and calling the results to Katia while she redraws the map on the wall.

He is separately the one who identifies the **Kimaris** plush that Carl is carrying — "He's one of
the brothers of Sheol. He has a summoning time of 'the remainder of the floor.' Be careful with him."
That identification is what triggers Samantha's revelation about who Kimaris was to her; see
[Samantha](samantha.md).

### Katia

The tension runs under everything. Carl catches them arguing silently in Shanty Town with their eyes
flashing, until Katia says out loud, "I said no," and "a look of rage flashed across Bautista before
he lowered his head." Katia's explanation is not what Carl expects:

> Katia: I'm good. It's okay. He's just scared for me. You need to remember what happened with his
> family after he came in here. He doesn't want anyone else he cares about to die. He thinks this is
> too risky and wants me to eat the flower now.
>
> Katia: He's afraid that if this doesn't go well, you'll turn on me to get the flower to give to
> Donut.

Samantha, riding his shoulder, spends the whole floor coaching him toward a jealous rage: "You are a
mighty Tigran, but you act like a lamb." Donut and Katia agree to keep their real plan from him
because "he's going to be mad." Carl notices him "staring at us" every time Katia hugs him.

When the orchid ceremony finally happens, Bautista panics — "Wait, we are not prepared! I need... I
thought..." — and then disappears from the tent entirely. Carl assumes the worst and messages him
that he is running out of time. The reply:

> Bautista: Sorry. I'm doing something in the crafting room quickly. I'm coming.

### What He Was Doing

The goddess Eileithyia makes Katia human again, and human means the body she actually has. Her right
arm is gone to the shoulder, her left leg is gone, her right leg is gone to the knee, and she is
missing an ear. "Just a few things I've lost along the way."

> "Here, quick," Bautista said, quickly bringing the cybernetic parts to her. She'd made them herself,
> in anticipation of this. She'd left them in the crafting room, and Bautista had finished them for
> her. We all worked, clicking and fastening them into place just as Eileithyia, ignoring all this,
> placed her three-fingered hand on Katia's stomach.

Katia made the prosthetics. Bautista finished them — and had known for some time without being told.
His exchange with her in front of the whole room, moments earlier:

> "Oh, Daniel," Katia said, speaking for the first time. "I didn't think you knew."
>
> "Oh course I knew," he said. "I love you. I'm sorry I made you doubt me."

The last thing Katia does before she vanishes is raise the cybernetic arm and wave. Bautista's last
words to Carl, on his way to the stairwell:

> "I know you think I was going to do something horrible or jealous. I want you to know I almost did.
> I keep losing family, Carl. It makes me scared, and it makes me angry. And I know I am not considered
> human anymore, and that is my great shame, especially today. But I would never do anything to hurt a
> brother like you."
