# Damascus Steel

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** NPC — Desperado Club staff; functionally the doorkeeper of the
      [Guild of Suffering](../dungeon-factions/guild-of-suffering.md)
    - **Race:** **Ifrit** — "Where there is sin, there are Ifrit." Metal to the touch but skin to the
      eye, smoke leaking from his skin and from empty white eye sockets, small goat-like horn stubs,
      heavy old scarring "like he'd once been raked by giant talons"
    - **Level:** 75
    - **Role:** **Assistant lead dancer at the Penis Parade**, the Desperado Club male strip club
      that fronts the Guild of Suffering
    - **Family:** Son of **[Astrid](astrid.md)** and the Night Wyrm (real name **Hamed**); brother of
      **Anaconda**, a Level 75 Quarter Naga and one of the club's lead dancers. Neither brother was
      ever a crawler; both parents were
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** None shown in combat — the system's warning is simply "Try not to
      get into a fight with this guy because he'll fuck you up"
    - **Notable items:** The **spiderweb elbow tattoo** of the Guild of Suffering — the same mark Carl
      carries, and the credential that gets Carl through the door
    - **Status:** Alive, and gone. Leaves with Anaconda in Chapter 34 to kill their father; the
      outcome is not shown in Book 6

## Overview

Damascus is the older, stoic one: he drinks tea at the end of the bar with a book, facing away from
the crowd, in an Aladdin-style vest and pants, and Dong Quixote introduces him to Carl as "the
cranky one." Donut, who knows the club's dancers far better than Carl does, has been telling him
about Damascus for two books — she once informed him he was Mongo's new daddy.

## The Door

The whole strip club is a front. The entrance to the Guild of Suffering is a hidden portal in the
last privacy cubicle in the corner of the Penis Parade, and Damascus controls it.

Carl doesn't have a password; he leads with a social credential instead — "I'm not looking for a
dance. We have a few mutual friends, one of whom is Donut." Damascus reads the spiderweb tattoo on
Carl's elbow, the twin of his own, and answers: "Carl… I'm glad you've come to visit. Donut speaks
highly of you." A pause. "As does Katia." Then: **"Walk with me."**

He escorts Carl to the corner cubicle with a theatrical bow, holds the door, slaps his ass on the
way in, locks the club door behind them and opens the secret portal with his hand:

> **Entering the Guild of Suffering.**
>
> **Welcome, spider. Welcome home.**

His only impatience with Carl's hesitation is professional: "Why are you just standing there? Don't
be a coward. Go in, get it over with, and come out. **I have a reputation to maintain, so you must
hurry.**" He also requires Carl to limp on the way back out, on pain of a broken knee — to preserve
the cover story.

!!! note "Wording"
    "Gatekeeper" is an accurate description of the function, not a title the book uses for him.

## The Realization

Carl and Katia take a Guild of Suffering contract without knowing the target. The target is Astrid.
After she dies, Carl finds Damascus sitting on the floor of the club with his face in his hands,
sizzling tears peppering the floor around him, Anaconda weeping beside him. Astrid was their mother.
The Night Wyrm — the guild's own patron, who issued the contract — is their father, and is still
married to her.

> "I didn't know who you were assigned to kill," he said. "I don't blame you for what happened. But
> I do blame him. **We were promised freedom. He wanted to make sure she was dead. They're still
> married. My mother has—had—so much, and the guild, it's rarely found by crawlers. He gets it all.
> He'll have it all when we get out.**"

Carl's reading, later: the family's indenture contract was almost up. If Astrid died inside the
game, her husband would inherit everything on the outside. Katia's summary: "So you think he had us
murder her in the game for real-life money? That's diabolical."

Damascus also lets slip how big this really is, then immediately shuts it down: "We have no time…
And I've already gotten a warning to shut up about this. **There's more of us than you know.
Anaconda and I are just two of thousands.**"

## The Mercenaries

Before he goes, Damascus makes Carl take his people.

With Astrid dead the club will close for "remodeling," a new assistant manager will be assigned, and
the whole Penis Parade crew — the dancers Damascus personally recruited, and who only counted as
safe because Astrid quietly parked a Meat Shields office inside the club — will be reassigned to the
Larracos Meat Shields and thrown into Faction Wars on somebody else's side.

> He grabbed my arm and leaned in. His grip was like an iron clamp. "**You owe me**," he whispered.
> "**They will *not* survive this. You have to get them out of here. I convinced them to come, to
> work in this place… If you don't take them now, you'll end up fighting against them on the ninth
> floor.**"

The mechanism: they all still hold Meat Shields memberships, so Carl can hire them on his mercenary
coupon on a **two-floor contract**, approved on the spot by **Rosemarie**, the elderly dwarf secretly
managing the club's shadow Meat Shields office. Price: **100,000 gold**.

**Fifteen** transfer to Carl's personal-space barracks immediately — thirteen male strippers plus
**Doctor Bones** the skeleton DJ and **Bucket Boy** the crocodilian. Only the men of the Penis Parade
were eligible; the women of Bitches and the middle-floor clubs are left behind. Several of them —
Dong Quixote, Doctor Bones, Splash Zone, Steve the troll — become significant to the Cuba endgame,
and Doctor Bones's *Super Spreader* is what creates the sluggalo army.

## Leaving

> "Brother, what are you doing?" [Anaconda asked.]
>
> Damascus turned and put his hand on the other stripper's shoulder… "**I'm going to avenge our
> mother. He won't win.**"
>
> The snake-like dancer looked up at his brother… "If you're going to fight him, I'm going, too."
>
> "Me, too!" called Dong Quixote.
>
> "**No**," Anaconda said without turning his head. "**This is family business.**"

The two brothers walk into the back cubicle without another word to anyone. Neither is seen again in
Book 6, and the confrontation with the Night Wyrm is never shown. Donut's reaction to the
mercenaries arriving is genuine and slightly heartbroken: "OMG, CARL. THIS IS THE GREATEST THING
SINCE YOU HIRED SLEDGIE… I'M SAD ANACONDA AND STEELIE AREN'T HERE, THOUGH."
