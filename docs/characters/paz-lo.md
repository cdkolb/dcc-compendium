# Paz Lo

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Full designation:** Crawler #8,199,454, "Paz Lo"
    - **Type:** Human Crawler (Cuban); a prison guard, not an inmate
    - **Race:** Human
    - **Class:** **Santero** — per Sister Ines, "a necromancer cleric. He also has a lot of healing
      and protection spells." Carl reads him as a warrior cleric. His mere presence agitates the
      undead: *"Warning: The presence of a Santero has aggravated the already-unsettled dead."*
    - **Subclass:** None printed — his character sheet shows race, class and deity only
    - **Level:** **45** at introduction → **101** as a totem card after killing a goddess
    - **Religion:** Worshipper of the god **Ogun** (palm-frond symbol), until Ogun smites him
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** ***Turtle*** — a temporary, very strong shield that draws aggro
      but roots him in place, unable to move or attack, and cannot be cancelled once cast;
      ***Self-Destruct*** — a suicide blast he can aim magically at a single target, which Carl
      covets for its precision; plus healing, protection and necromancy
    - **Notable items:** Full glowing silver plate armor with platform boots, worn with a backwards
      baseball cap; a mace "twice as long as he was with a diamond-shaped head the size of a beer
      keg" that he summons into his hands
    - **Status:** **Deceased**, twice over — killed by Ogun's unhealable smite in Chapter 16, flagged
      into a T'Ghee totem card at the instant of death, then freed when Carl and Donut ripped the
      card in Chapter 72

## Overview

Paz is one of three Cuban crawlers Carl and Donut meet on Floor 8, with his cousin **Anton Lopez**
and [Sister Ines Quiteria](sister-ines-quiteria.md). Carl initially assumes the two heavily-tattooed
men are convicts; Paz corrects him flatly: "we weren't prisoners. We were guards. It was a women's
prison. We were outside, walking them to the med building when it happened."

He is enormous, armored head to toe, and disarmingly gentle. His self-description of his combat
role: "Mostly I just curl up on the ground and let the bad guys hit me." Mongo, on meeting him,
immediately shoves his face into Paz's mirror-polished breastplate to look at himself and knocks
Paz flat on his back "like an upset turtle." Paz howls with laughter.

He and Anton both took up Ogun on a priest's advice that the god would protect them from being
controlled — a precaution against Ines's **Toxoplasma** racial skill. It did not work, and the skill
made them forget why they had done it within minutes.

## The Smiting

The Cuba storyline requires the party to repair a set of god-shrines. Anton, charmed by the goddess
Oshun, hurls the Ogun shrine piece out the door, and the system warns that the two Ogun worshippers
will take a smite for it. Ogun — **God of Blacksmithery and Armor, Level 250** — turns up in person,
because "Ogun strongly believes all punishments should be handed out in person."

Carl casts *Black Nimbus* on Anton to try to strip him out of the god's targeting. Ogun's reply:
"That was a good attempt. But all you have done is removed this man from my protection. From my
mercy. Not from my wrath." He then takes a step backward, steps on Anton's head, and crushes it —
"Just like that, as if he was nothing."

Paz gets worse. Ogun heals him completely first, so that he can take something away:

> "Paz, you are no longer under my grace. For your failure, I am taking your ability to heal this
> condition you now suffer. Go in darkness, and when you pierce the veil, you will find yourself
> alone and without armor."

The condition is a permanent, unstoppable bleed. Ines can top his health up but cannot slow the
drain. The smite leaves a hand-shaped burn across his face that strips the skin away, showing red
muscle and the white of his teeth. Paz calls the affliction **the Inevitable debuff**:

> "I remember it all," Paz said. "Ogun, he cured me of everything. Everything except the Inevitable
> debuff. I don't worship him anymore." He let out a groan. "He promised he would fix it, and he
> did. But only at the end."

!!! note "Not in the system text"
    "The Inevitable" is Paz's own word for the condition. No system debuff block for it appears
    anywhere in the book. (Unrelatedly, a T'Ghee card called *The Inevitable* is mentioned in the
    cookbook as representing the goddess Ysalte — the text draws no link between the two.)

## Becoming a Card

Paz spends his last minutes saying goodbye. Sister Ines refuses to let him:

> "*No*," Sister Ines shouted, her voice suddenly angry. "No! You're not leaving."
>
> "No, no, no. Please. You're not leaving me. Everyone leaves me."

She produces a squad flag and jabs it into his neck. *Whoom.* Paz becomes a card — green, the common
tier. Even Ines seems stunned it worked. It is the first time in the series a crawler is turned into
a T'Ghee totem, and it establishes that dead crawlers can be pressed back into service as summons.

Carl carries the guilt for the rest of the book.

## "Please. No More."

Ines summons him in the eight-way FOCSA card battle in Chapter 56. He comes out of the card with
the burn still across his face:

> "Sister," Paz called up to the nun. "Please. No more. I can't take… gah!"
>
> "Paz," she called. "Accept your suffering! Use your self-destruct skill the moment this false
> god…"

She never finishes the order — Quan Ch plays a *Force Play* card that burns her *Flee*, ends her
fight and loses her the key. Paz dissipates into dust in the snake's mouth.

## The God Predator

After Louis kills Ines, her celestial patron **Ysalte** — Goddess of Hopelessness and Insanity,
Level 250, invulnerable on this floor — enters the realm and inherits an enhanced version of Ines's
deck. Which includes Paz.

Carl is head-first inside Samantha's neck when it happens, so the only eyewitness account is Donut's:

> **KATIA GAVE PAZ AN ARROW THING, AND HE STUCK IT INTO THE DWARF LADY'S EYE, AND THEN HE BLEW
> HIMSELF UP, BUT THE EXPLOSION WAS ALL CONCENTRATED ON THE GODDESS'S NECK. HER HEAD FELL OFF! WE
> ALL FELL DOWN SEVERAL STORIES. THEY GAVE HIM CREDIT FOR IT EVEN THOUGH HE WENT AWAY WHEN SHE DIED.**

The arrow is Katia's [Bolt of Ophiotaurus](../items/bolt-of-ophiotaurus.md), the anti-deity bolt
Princess Formidable sent her back on Floor 4 for use on Prince Maestro; Katia had already fired it
once and recovered it, because magical bolts can be reused as long as they don't break. Paz's
*Self-Destruct* — a suicide blast he can aim at a single target — does the rest, concentrated
entirely on the goddess's neck.

> **A deity has fallen. The heavens tremble with rage.**
>
> **System Message. Crawler Paz Lo has slain the goddess, Ysalte. He has been marked for death by
> all deities except sworn enemies of the late goddess.**

Ysalte's equipment is awarded to Paz, upgrading his armor. Carl's reaction on hearing it: "Paz had
killed the goddess. But how? How was it possible? He was her own totem."

His card description has changed:

> **Paz Lo. The God Predator. Level 101 Former Crawler.**

## Release

Katia hands the card to Donut as soon as the goddess falls. In the last chapter, with the option of
keeping a single card down to Floor 9, Donut suggests keeping Paz — "Maybe there's a way to set him
free." Katia has already decided: "We rip it. That's what he wanted." Imani and Elle agree.

Carl refuses to keep him, remembering the look on his face when Ines summoned him:

> *Please*, he'd said. *No more.*
>
> "We can't," I said… "We should never have let the Sister turn him into one in the first place.
> It was my fault. It's not right."

Donut asks whether she'll get another player-killer skull for it. Carl: "No. It calls him a former
crawler now. I don't know what that really means, and I don't want to find out."

> And then, without any additional fanfare, Donut put one corner of the card in her mouth. She held
> the other corner down against my shoulder, and she ripped. Just like that. The card disappeared in
> a puff of smoke. She did not get a player killer skull.
>
> "Goodbye, Paz," I said.
