# Penelope ("Penny")

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Transformed pet → fully sapient crawler ally
    - **Race:** Yorkshire pig
    - **Class/Subclass:** Not applicable — enters the party too late in the book to select one;
      appears with no assigned class
    - **Level:** **10** (as Crawler #12,953,454, "Penelope 3")
    - **Prior status:** Belonged to **Nico and Dario**'s "One Fine Pig" team (see
      [Supporting cast](supporting-cast.md)), and was already the target of a stray love-spell
      (**Touch of Maw-Loo** / the Snowdrop Curse) that made certain NPCs — including the god
      **Taranis** — fall for her on sight, with Taranis magically protecting her from harm
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** None of her own; communicates over chat rather than speech
    - **Notable items:** A movement/oxygen harness, a collar with a trap module, and Katia's old
      **Enchanted Spiked Kneepads of the Munificent Goddess Kina** (passed down through Donut),
      which also grant temporary deity-proximity effects like Carl's Scavenger's Daughter patch
    - **Status:** Alive — ends the book with Carl's party rather than with Taranis

## Overview

Penny is a literal farm pig, mixed up with a mythic engine-of-war centipede, and turned into a
person entirely by accident. Once sapient, she becomes central to the endgame's most dangerous
gambit — deliberately provoking two gods into a fight — and comes out the other side both wiser
and, apparently, single.

## Made Sapient

Penny was already caught up in the plot before her transformation: as a pet of Nico and Dario's
team, she carries a curse that makes certain NPCs — most consequentially the god **Taranis** —
compulsively infatuated with her, with Taranis personally protecting her from harm on pain of
death. Carl takes custody of her after Nico and Dario die and he uses the winner's key to open
their abandoned garage.

!!! warning "Penny's transformation is not the biscuit mix-up"
    An easy error, and one this page previously made: Penny is **not** the victim of the famous
    mixed-up biscuit. Carl feeds her deliberately, with the correct item, during Floor 11's
    ninety-minute float-building phase — before the parade, before the arena, and before Eris ever
    scrambles anyone's inventory. The mix-up belongs entirely to
    **[Scolopendra](../bosses/scolopendra.md#the-mix-up)**, later, mid-fight. The two events use the
    same *family* of item, which is what makes them easy to conflate.

The feeding itself happens off-page; the last the reader sees is Carl calling her over in the garage
("Penny, come here. I got a snack for you") as the parade timer runs out. Carl's own retrospective
account, in the arena, names both the item and the intent — and is characteristically uneasy about
what he has done to her:

> We'd fed her the enriched pet biscuit. The Nothing Special Party Companion one... it didn't give
> her the ability to talk out loud.

Penny communicates over party chat rather than speech from that point on, and because Carl is the
one who fed her the biscuit, she is automatically placed in a party with him. The system assigns her
a new identity outright:

> Crawler #12,953,454. Penelope 3. Yorkshire Pig. Level 10.

## Bait

Carl is explicit that Penny's transformation immediately becomes tactical: "Especially since I was
planning on using this poor woman as bait." The plan is to use her — and Taranis's obsession with
her — to lure both Taranis and the fire god **Emberus** into the same place at the same time and
provoke them into fighting each other, buying the party room to work.

The execution is blunt and uncomfortable even by the book's standards. Carl straps Penny into a
movement backpack, doses her repeatedly with "splooge" (a running gag drug/potion — Penny's
repeating in-scene line is "GIVE ME MORE SPLOOGE. I CRAVE SPLOOGE"), fits a magic ring through her
nipple for underwater oxygen (an item called Petey Motteux's Little Secret), and finally holds a gun
to her head to force the confrontation into the open:

> "Taranis, if you don't want me to shoot this fucking pig in the fucking head, show yourself!"

Both Taranis and Emberus answer the summons. Taranis's reaction on seeing her in danger is immediate
and genuine: "Penelope! My sweet love! Hold on! I will protect you." When she is nearly killed
during the fight that follows, he personally revives her.

## The Breakup

Book 8 does **not** end with Taranis taking Penny away with him — a natural assumption given how
hard he falls for her, but not what the text shows. After the confrontation, Taranis sets Penny down
safely beside Carl and Donut, and Penny herself ends things, over a much smaller betrayal than
anything else that happened that day:

> "HE LIED ABOUT THE SPLOOGE, SO WE BROKE UP. I HAD NO IDEA HE WAS MARRIED."

Taranis leaves the realm afterward, but not with Penny — he departs with **Samantha**, who slips
away from the party to hitch a ride with him and **Krakaren Prime** on her own private mission (see
[Samantha](samantha.md)). Penny stays behind with Carl's group, folded into the party alongside the
newly-sapient **"Sexy Scolopendra."**
