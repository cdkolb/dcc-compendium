# Britney Proskurina

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Human Crawler
    - **Class:** Pit Fighter → **Pit Champion** (Book 5, Floor 6 specialization — upgrade details
      not fully described)
    - **Level:** Reaches the top-50 leaderboard independently by Book 5
    - **Party:** Katia's splinter team; Princess Posse officer and Team Retribution liaison (Book 7)
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** A powerful, thirty-second Shield spell blocking both magic and
      physical attacks (four-hour cooldown); berserking skills (Book 7)
    - **Notable items:** A jacket made entirely of stuffed slate butterfly figures (Book 5, sourced
      from [Bautista](bautista.md)'s beanbag-toy collection) — capable of summoning a live
      [slate butterfly](../items/talisman-of-the-slate-butterfly.md#book-5-britneys-jacket)
      that seeks out and extinguishes the nearest light source; **enchanted underwear** featuring a
      pin-up of Elle and the caption "Stay frosty, boys," which gives +10% dexterity and enhances all
      berserking skills (Book 7 — she has not told Elle about them)
    - **Status:** Alive at the end of Book 7; enters the Floor 10 stairwell "contemplating a deal,"
      afraid, and expecting to be back

## Overview

Britney Proskurina, a Ukrainian former pit fighter, is one of two survivors of Floor 5's water
quadrant original crawler team. She joins Firas's group by the end of Book 4, having refused to go
back underwater ever again, and remains a quiet, perpetually irritated presence around the party
for most of Book 5.

## Role in the Story — Book 5

Britney unexpectedly earns her own top-50 leaderboard spot by the Butcher's Masquerade, entitling
her to attend independently rather than as anyone's plus-one; she attends anyway as Donut's guest
and guitarist for the talent competition, revealing hidden musical skill Carl and Donut hadn't
known about. During the masquerade, she uses one of her stuffed slate-butterfly summons to
covertly short out a warning light monitoring the ballroom's guards — a small, high-stakes
sabotage she describes afterward as leaving her "heart in her throat."

At the battle's climax, Carl shouts for Britney to trigger her entire jacket at once, sending the
whole swarm of slate butterflies airborne to help swamp a near-dead Queen Imogen — but Imogen's
own last, desperate lightning attack strikes the dense, light-seeking swarm by accident instead of
its intended target, and the resulting explosion permanently, visibly burns Britney's face even
after healing and kills her close friend Firas outright.

## Role in the Story — Book 6

Britney enters Floor 8 at **level 55**, having risen significantly up the ranks, and in the worst
state of anyone in the guild: the burn from the Butcher's Masquerade covers the left side of her
face, and at the start of the floor she is "just sitting there, clutching onto Louis," unable to stop
crying.

### The Extinction Sigil

She arrives at the Desperado Club already wearing a new tattoo under her eye — an **extinction
sigil**, awarded for wiping out an entire species. The book never says which species, or when she
earned it. (Carl has one too, from a lizard race; the sigil grants a charm effect that removes the
hostility of the extinct species' natural enemies, and can cut the other way — some creatures do
extra damage to a sigil-bearer. See [Game Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md).)

### Tserendolgor

The friendship that gets her through the floor is with
**[Tserendolgor](tserendolgor.md)**, who had a similar facial scar before the crawl. Donut is in the
middle of clumsily consoling her — "scars are beautiful. It goes well with the whole barbarian
aesthetic you're going for" — when Britney cuts her off:

> "I'm not worried about it anymore," Britney said. "**I have been talking to Tserendolgor, who once
> had a similar scar upon her face.**"

Carl physically restrains Donut from saying anything nasty about her.

### The Smile

Tserendolgor dies in Chapter 60, detonating herself rather than fight Donut for a key. Three chapters
later, when the rescue party finally reaches Cuba, Carl catches this:

> Tran, too, was smiling as he reached over and pet Donut. Britney was on the ground, on her back as
> Mongo snuffled against her. She was laughing as well. It was the first time I'd seen either of
> those two, Tran or Britney, so much as smile since the end of the Butcher's Masquerade.
>
> Actually, come to think of it, **it was the first I'd seen Britney smile. Ever.**

The trigger is Mongo, not Ren — but Carl's next thought in the same passage is of Ren, "and of the
hopelessness that had broken her," and of everyone forced to confront their own personal nightmares
on this floor. His conclusion: "It was loneliness. It had always been loneliness… It was isolation
that would break us."

She also dances with the stripper Author Steve Rowland at Louis's Christmas party, tells Louis to
turn the music back on, and is one of the last crawlers holding the stairwell during the Ysalte
fight.

## Book 7: This Inevitable Ruin

Britney spends Floor 9 in a job nobody assigned her and everybody comes to depend on: she becomes the
Princess Posse's diplomatic channel to **[Juice Box](juice-box.md)** and the NPC faction, Team
Retribution. Carl's own summary:

> I wasn't exactly sure when and how this happened, but Britney had pretty much become our main
> liaison with the NPCs.

And later, in the briefing for the four simultaneous offensives:

> Britney will coordinate all of our communication with Team Retribution. It seems she's become quite
> close with Juice Box. She's gotten very good at being a go-between, working multiple lines of
> communication with the skill of a seasoned police dispatcher.

### The First Third

She is at Carl's shoulder for most of the opening act. She ties his hair back for him in the guild
hall — "Keep it tucked in or monsters will grab it, believe me" — and the burn down the side of her
face has finally stopped registering to him as a wound: "It fit her hard visage."

She is on the **Party Planner** unveiling, on the recruiting tour into **Larracos**, and in the room
for the *Donut Holes* t-shirt argument, where she is both the person who warned Louis not to let
Donut see it and the person he immediately sells out ("No, she has panties with a picture of Elle on
them"). On the tour, while everyone else is gossiping about Louis's wedding, she is the one who asks
the NPC Arief the question that matters — "Does your uncle remember dying?" — and who tells him
flatly that "the 'outworlders' aren't immortal anymore."

At the **Juice Box briefing** she reads the paperwork while everyone else argues. She notices Juice
Box has quietly withheld some of the documents, studies the fortification maps, and is the one who
spots the item that ends up deciding the whole floor: "How big are these diggers?" She then stays
behind at the Desperado Club to be taught the Larracos city defence system in person. At Louis and
Juice Box's wedding she is physically present and completely checked out — "Britney clutched onto a
pile of automaton plans and hadn't been paying attention."

### The War

Through the fighting she runs the Special Operations Forces in Shanty Town and works the changeling
spy channel — the Naga infiltration goes Carl to Britney to Juice Box or Arief, because Carl cannot
talk to the NPC operatives directly.

> Carl: Britney. We're almost ready. Are your guys in place?
>
> Britney: They're ready. Waiting on you.

During the four-front offensive she is effectively the theatre's radio: relaying dwarven confirmation
that the lava tubes have been agitated, calling the abort when a digger is about to surface next to a
god, reporting the Dream castle's shields down, and — when the war mages seize the Gate of the Feral
Gods and Juice Box vanishes mid-battle — holding the line with nothing: "I don't know! Juice Box
teleported away!"

When the Peeling Phase begins she is deep inside Larracos and has to run for her life. She resurfaces
in a column of Team Retribution changelings led by a ten-foot armoured beetle, with the warlord cat
**[Ferdinand](ferdinand.md)** poofed out and screaming on top of her head, still wearing his bowler
hat. From then on she commands from the **college** with Jamal and the others, coordinates the raid on
the castle of Larracos, calls the acid drain and the *Bounce House* catch, and afterwards stays behind
with Florin and Rosetta to repair the top defensive ring. She is also in the room for Kiwi's labour,
rubbing the bear's head with a wet cloth while Florin holds her hand.

### Eris's Claim

Near the end of the book the chaos goddess **Eris**, freshly unsponsored and free, freezes time, tells
Carl several things she says are true, and finishes with one that isn't explained at all:

> "Also, Britney picked that axe off the ground and is now possessed."
>
> She disappeared with a crack.
>
> Already married? I thought as I downed a healing potion. Axe? What axe? Possessed? By who?

Carl has Imani and Juice Box check her without telling her, and they find nothing.

> Imani: Her health is 100%. I don't see any sign that there's anything wrong with her. She doesn't
> have an axe, Carl. Or if she does, it's in her inventory.
>
> Juice Box: If she has a presence about her, it's deep. It's not anything I can feel. And believe me,
> I can feel most everything.

Juice Box cannot feel anything on Carl either, who is definitely carrying a presence, and suggests the
goddess may simply have lied. Elle wants to just ask Britney; Carl refuses — "If it's a presence like
mine, we don't want it to hurt her if it suspects we know it's there."

!!! note "Unresolved"
    The book never confirms or disproves Eris's claim. No axe is ever found, no possession is ever
    demonstrated, Britney is never told, and the thread is not picked up again before the floor ends.
    The description box for Eris says she is All Knowing and that "If she gives you information, it's
    probably true" — while also noting she is able to lie. Nothing in Book 7 settles it either way.

### Exit

At the stairwell she does not commit either way:

> Britney said she was contemplating a deal, but was afraid and would probably be back. She went in
> with [Chris](chris-andrews.md).

## Book 8: A Parade of Horribles

Britney's Book 7 exit thread — "contemplating a deal, but afraid" — resolves into the book's clearest
statement yet that whatever is or isn't riding inside her, she is now actively pursuing an agenda of
her own.

### Killing Osvaldo

In Heat 7 of Floor 10, Britney jumps into the same heat as **[Osvaldo](osvaldo.md)** and kills him
outright as he flees, stunned, toward a portal:

> **A champion has fallen. A bounty has been claimed.**
>
> **Crawler Osvaldo has been killed by Crawler Britney Proskurina.**

She lands directly on his corpse as the kill lands, earning a golden player-killer skull. What she
takes from the body isn't the Apito crystal already in Prepotente's hands — it's Osvaldo's other,
long-useless memorial crystal, tied to the dead goddess **Ysalte**:

> "I saw her," I said, watching Britney, my paws up on the dash. "She has the memorial crystal from
> Ysalte now."

### The Vulgar Temple

Samantha immediately identifies what the crystal is for, and where Britney needs to take it:

> "She needs to get to the Basilica or the **Vulgar Temple** and place the gem in the plinth, and
> she'll be back for good. I'll be curious to see if that actually works."

Carl's own gloss adds the stakes: "All I'm saying is Britney needs to get to either the Halls of the
Ascendency or Sheol if she wants to get that crazy Ysalte bitch out of her" — which reads the goal
as freeing/restoring Ysalte specifically, not merely charging the crystal. Britney disappears through
a portal shortly afterward without confronting anyone about it; Elle's frustration afterward is the
book's clearest statement that the party never actually addressed the Book 7 possession question
before losing the chance to: "I said from the start we should've confronted Britney, and we never
did, and now she's gone where we can't help her."

### Sheol

By the book's epilogue, Britney has reached **Sheol** itself — the Louis Santiago interlude finds her
there already, armed with a pickaxe, alongside a Taurin named Pontiff and the demon
[Forkith](supporting-cast.md). Whatever she's doing with the Ysalte crystal there is not shown on the
page.

!!! warning "Still unconfirmed"
    Book 7 left open, via the goddess Eris's own unverified claim, whether Britney is possessed or
    influenced by Ysalte. Book 8 does not resolve this either way — it only confirms Britney is now
    pursuing Ysalte's revival on purpose, with a specific object and a specific destination. Whether
    that pursuit is her own will or something else's is not settled by anything in this book.
