# Miriam Dom

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Race:** Human → **Vampire** (turned off-page sometime before Book 5, after going looking
      for a panicked Prepotente while separated from him). Book 7's MILK interlude, a flashback to
      Floor 5, shows her already reading as race **Vampire** on an NPC's examine at that point
    - **Class:** Shepherd (a mage-class build, per the Floor 3 leaderboard)
    - **Level:** 27 (Book 2) — ranked **5th on the Floor 3 leaderboard**; **52** as of Book 5
    - **Status:** **Deceased** — dies by voluntary sunrise exposure at the start of Book 5's
      second act, to lift a vampirism curse she'd unknowingly spread and to free Prepotente
    - **Signature trait:** Entered the dungeon with 15 pet goats
    - **Notable pet:** A **Hellspawn familiar** ("Bianca Del Ciao"), transformed from one of her
      goats via an Enhanced Pet Biscuit — Level 32 as of Book 5, one of ten surviving goat
      children she keeps in inventory pet carriers

## Overview

Miriam Dom is a broadcast-only figure, nicknamed "the goat lady" by Carl and Donut — Mordecai
mentions her before they ever see footage of her firsthand. She entered the dungeon with 15 pet
goats and, remarkably, managed to keep most of them alive well into Floor 3.

## The Goat Menagerie

By the point a recap episode shows her, she's down to 5 of her original 15 goats. Three (Boer
goats — a meat breed Carl recognizes from a childhood farm job) remain physically unchanged.
The other two were altered by Enhanced Pet Biscuits:

- One transformed into a bipedal, armored goat warrior wielding a double-headed axe, seemingly
  granted a Donut-like intelligence upgrade
- The other — after what Mordecai calls a biscuit that "didn't go so well for her, or several of
  her goats" — became a **Hellspawn familiar**: a horse-sized, multi-breasted, jet-black goat
  monster with glowing red eyes and constant steam rising off its body

Mordecai identifies the Hellspawn as one of the most powerful pet types in the game: "if she can
keep that thing alive, she's gotten it early enough that it'll level pretty high. It'll
eventually grow wings and will be the size of a damn dragon." It's shown trampling a group of
hyena-faced monsters to death on-air.

## Leaderboard

Ranked 5th on the tentative Floor 3 leaderboard Odette reveals late in Book 2, at Level 27 with

## Book 3: Revealed as Prepotente's Keeper

Miriam Dom reappears on Floor 4 as the caretaker and apparent romantic partner of
**[Prepotente](prepotente.md)** — the sapient, aerialist Caprid crawler who turns out to be one of
her own transformed goats. She manages his theatrical, murder-threat-prone personality with
unbothered affection, using her shepherd's crook to smack him when he's rude to strangers (Carl
and Imani among them) and rewarding good behavior with a kiss on the head, addressing him as
"Pony." She and Prepotente depart together to hunt a different province boss after their first
target is accidentally teleported away by Carl's group.

## Book 5: Cursed, and a Final Kindness

Miriam Dom and Prepotente reappear early on Floor 6 already having survived a run-in with Big Tina
(an allosaurus boss) — and Miriam is already a vampire by this point, having turned off-page some
time before, after Prepotente panicked and ran off during a separation and she went looking for
him and "somehow ended up a vampire." That existing vampirism, and her position as the "Prime"
carrier of a floor-wide infection spreading through the local wildlife, is what draws the
attention of an elite vampire hunter, **Viscount Fog**, star of a rival broadcast called *Blood
Hunter* — his mount, Sierra, does not curse her; he hunts her *because* she's already a vampire.
It's actually Miriam and Prepotente who come to Carl and Donut's aid first, not the other way
around: they find and heal a badly-injured Carl (hit by crossbow bolts during his own opening raid
on Zockau) and drive him to safety in their cart, and it's during that ride that Miriam confides
her full backstory to him: a farm outside Parma, Italy, twenty years spent caring for aging parents
and her goats rather than pursuing her own life, and a bone-deep devotion to Prepotente ("my
stubborn little boy") that predates his transformation.

Fog attacks Miriam and Prepotente directly on two separate occasions — the first while they're
fighting the allosaurus Big Tina, which they survive. The second time, Prepotente attempts to cure
Miriam by transferring her curse to Fog using a scroll of *Community Pool* — but Fog's countering
ring bounces the spell back and forth between them several times, leaving Prepotente Stunned, then
Fragile (one hit point, unable to heal). In the chaos, Prepotente's own newly-acquired
**Ring of Divine Suffering** activates on its own and marks Miriam — the only eligible target in
range — meaning Prepotente himself now carries **Left to Fester** and cannot heal until his own
mark, his mother, dies. Fog eventually dies of his own reflected curse at sunset; Miriam spends the
night fighting off infected wildlife to protect Prepotente's frozen body, then summons Carl and
Donut at dawn — not to save her, but to keep watch and, implicitly, to end her life before
Prepotente can wake and try to stop her himself.

Carl secretly marks her with his own Ring of Divine Suffering just before sunrise and lets her
burn to death in the light, curing every crawler and creature infected with her strain of
vampirism floor-wide and lifting the Left to Fester debuff so Prepotente can finally be woken.
She dies asking Carl to explain her choice to Prepotente "one day" and leaves her entire inventory
behind for him, save two scrolls Carl quietly keeps for himself. Her death triggers a world quest
(**The Creeping Apocalypse**) forcing every crawler and hunter on the floor to hunt down the
newly-created, uncontrolled undead "Odious Creepers" her infected wildlife left behind before
sunset, or face unspecified consequences.

## Book 7: This Inevitable Ruin

Miriam has been dead since Book 5, and she does not appear in Book 7's present day. Her only real
scene is a flashback, and the rest of her presence in the book is other people remembering her.

### The MILK Interlude: Thrown Out of Club Vanquisher

The interlude before chapter 26 is told from the point of view of **Milk**, the xenopus guildmaster
of the calligraphy and cartography guilds in [Club Vanquisher](../locations/club-vanquisher.md). It
is set on **the fifth floor** — during Book 4, well before Miriam's death — not in Book 7's present.

Milk wakes to sirens and screaming, and finds Miriam and **[Prepotente](prepotente.md)** fighting
their way through the club's mantaur guards with the hellspawn familiar **Bianca**. Prepotente is
the one shouting:

> "They can't do this, mother. I won't stand for it!" came the screech. "It wasn't your fault!
> Bianca, melt the guards!"

> "Prepotente Lorenzo De La Rosa! Stop that this instant!" came the woman's voice again, stern.
> "Stop. More guards are coming. They will kill us. We must flee."

When the pair burst into her hidden guild, Milk examines them and works out what the fight is
about:

> Milk examined the pair of crawlers plus their giant pet standing there, and she instantly knew
> what the problem was. The older, female crawler was originally a human, but her race was Vampire,
> which wasn't a race someone could voluntarily choose. That meant it was likely a recent thing,
> and upon noticing this, they had likely just told her that her access to Club Vanquisher was
> being revoked. Her child, who'd apparently taken a caprid race, was upset about this.

Two things worth being precise about. First, this is the passage that confirms Miriam's vampirism
on the page — the system's own race field reads **Vampire** — and it dates it to Floor 5 at the
latest, earlier than Book 5's account established. Second, the club expulsion is **Milk's
inference**, not a stated ruling: the narration says "they had likely just told her," and the word
*banned* is never used of Miriam anywhere in the book. (In Book 7 that word belongs to Prepotente,
who carries a Holy Crusade Bounty.)

What the scene is really about is Miriam's kindness. Bianca sets the guild on fire and tries to
incinerate Milk; Miriam's response is to rush over and treat the burn with her own salve.

> Milk just looked at the woman, open-mouthed. In all of her cycles in the dungeon, she'd never
> known of a crawler giving up supplies to heal an NPC.

She then apologises for her son — "he still hasn't learned how to politely wield it" — and, when
Milk hints that she can only give information if she is threatened, plays along by drawing a knife
so Milk can hand over the map of the club's secret exits without being punished for it. Miriam's
sad smile, "revealing sharp teeth," and a hand on Milk's shoulder are the thanks. It is the
encounter that eventually pulls Milk out of fifty seasons of hiding.

  word he doesn't know.
