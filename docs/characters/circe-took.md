# Circe Took

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Elite / Hive queen (mantis) — also functions as an avatar of the deity **Diwata**
    - **Race:** Mantis (Dark Hive)
    - **Role:** Businesswoman/hive queen; owns an amusement park; moderates a CrawlCon panel
    - **Family:** Mother of **[Vrah](vrah.md)** and Xindy (killed by Carl), plus several other

    - **Status:** **Deceased** — killed by her own children during the Butcher's Masquerade's

    - **Notable spells/abilities:** Sponsors and briefly possesses the shapeshifting fertility

      the possession
    - **Notable items:** None named

## Overview

Circe Took is a mantis hive queen — not part of the Dark Hive's military or the aggressive
"Burrower" clan, but a "businesswoman" who owns an amusement park and moderates panels at
CrawlCon. She's introduced moderating a live convention panel on the history of the crawl,
lecturing at length until Carl derails it by pointing out her own daughter is one of the hunters
gunning for him. She reacts with theatrical, controlled fury throughout the panel, at one point
slicing a table in half with her natural bladed forearms — a demonstration of the same weapon her
daughter Vrah uses to decapitate crawlers.

## Becoming Diwata

After Carl infects Vrah with the [Arrow of Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea](../items/arrow-of-enthusiastic-double-gonorrhea.md),
Circe sponsors the shapeshifting fertility deity **Diwata**, a minor deity of nature and fertility
capable of changing sex and species at will. Diwata first manifests in the realm by being born,
unbeknownst to onlookers, through a hunter tricked into acting as her birthing vessel during a
temple explosion in a small Ursine settlement; she then shapeshifts into a male mantis to mate with
and cure Vrah off-page, before turning female and exploiting a loophole in her own summoning spell
— because the possession only ends once the birthing stops, she stays manifest for days by
compulsively, continuously giving birth to an unending stream of mantis nymphs, eventually flooding
the High Elf castle with them (Vrah's own siblings-slash-children) during the Butcher's
Masquerade's climax, to the horror of everyone watching. Circe is effectively trapped in the form
for the rest of the battle, unable to access any other divine power while doing so.

## Death

Samantha, tasked with keeping the hunters' magic-blocking soul crystals from being reactivated by
the approaching Diwata, ends up using the crystals themselves as an improvised weapon when Diwata
breaks into the security room — the resulting explosion destroys all the crystals and knocks
Diwata unconscious, but doesn't end the possession or stop the births. Moments later, Donut's
[Laundry Day](../spells/laundry-day.md) spell — trained to 9, boosted to 12 with a Pawna's Cries
potion, then overshot to 17 with the more powerful Pawna's Tears potion (well past the level 15
actually required) — targets the mortal underneath the divine "armor," banishing Diwata back to
the Halls of the Ascendency and leaving Circe stripped of her stolen protection, back in mortal,
birthing-mantis form. Before Carl can strike the killing blow, a dozen of Circe's own mantis
children turn on her and tear her apart, apparently enraged rather than grateful. She dies
screaming Carl's name.
