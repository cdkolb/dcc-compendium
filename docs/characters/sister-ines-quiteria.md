# Sister Ines Quiteria

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Full designation:** Crawler #8,199,462, "Sister Ines Quiteria"
    - **Type:** Human Crawler (Cuban) — the secondary antagonist of Book 6
    - **Race:** **Cat Girl** — a bipedal humanoid cat with the long, thin face of a Siamese, coffee-dark
      fur and rounded ears; Donut identifies the look as a **Havana brown** and loathes her on sight
      for it. Ines did not pick the coloring ("I didn't get to choose… Maybe it's because I'm Cuban")
    - **Class:** **Poet Laureate** — a bard class that requires the crawler take a patron. She calls
      herself "a bard healer": she buffs allies, hits mobs with afflictions, and fights in melee with
      a glowing trident
    - **Subclass:** None printed — her character sheet shows race and class only
    - **Level:** **47** at introduction; the book never restates it
    - **Nickname:** ***El Segador de La Habana*** — "The Reaper of Havana," the name the Cuban papers
      gave her after she poisoned every nun in her own convent
    - **Order:** A **Discalced Carmelite** nun. She refuses to worship any dungeon deity — "I cannot
      bring myself to worship a false god"
    - **Sponsor and patron:** the **Nebulars**, a religious faction that preaches shutting down the
      crawl and eradicating every world that can host one. Her celestial Patron by the end is the
      goddess **Ysalte**, herself sponsored by Pontifex Shine of the Nebular Balance
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** *Word Weaver* (her charisma-scaling charm spell, raised to level
      15 by the Nebulars); the **Toxoplasma** racial skill (see below); a **level-15 Book of Rhymes**
      bard skill granted by her patron, used to amplify *Word Weaver*
    - **Notable items:** A glowing trident; a cloak and arm bracers
    - **Status:** **Deceased** — stabbed through the chest by [Louis Santiago](louis-santiago.md)
      while unconscious, Chapter 65

## Overview

Ines enters the book as one of three Cuban crawlers Carl and Donut meet on Floor 8, alongside
[Paz Lo](paz-lo.md) and his cousin Anton Lopez. All three worked at the same women's prison in
Havana — the two men as guards, Ines as an inmate. She was serving time for murdering every sister
in her convent by poison.

She is soft-spoken, funny, devout, and completely insane. Paz's summary: "She's insane. She's
*obsessed* with the idea of sin. But compared to how she was before? She is so much better." She had
been medicated before the collapse; the party assumed the pills were unnecessary inside the dungeon.

She picked Cat Girl deliberately: "it was the only race that gave me a charisma boost for my *Word
Weaver* spell, amongst others, and our game guide insisted it would allow me to choose the Poet
Laureate class, which it did. Plus, I always liked cats. We had several at the prison."

Carl likes her. After she stands between her party and a level-250 god with nothing but a trident
and a "You're not a real god," he concludes: "She'd been willing to die for her party members, and
that said more about her than anything that had happened in the before times. She was one of us…
I *liked* her." Donut wants her dead from the first minute ("Mongo, eat the nun").

## Toxoplasma

Her race carries a passive charm skill that Paz can only half-remember the name of, because the
skill itself keeps eating the memory:

> "It's her race. It comes with a skill called Toxo… Toxo plasma something. It makes people like
> her, want to protect her. You don't even realize it's happening. The effects are mild at first,
> but they get stronger and stronger as time goes on. And then eventually you'll do anything to
> protect her. My cousin and I, we didn't learn about it until it was too late. You don't even know
> it's happening. When you find out, you forget a minute later. That's how it works."

Paz and Anton took up the god Ogun specifically because a priest said he'd keep them from being
controlled — and then forgot why they had done it, minutes later. It didn't work anyway. Carl feels
the skill land on him in real time, catches himself thinking *We can party with her. It wouldn't be
so bad*, and gets a chill. He chooses to separate from her and warn the other crawlers rather than
kill her.

!!! note "Not in the system text"
    The word "Toxoplasma" is never printed in full and the skill has no system description block
    anywhere in the book — Paz's stumbling paraphrase above is the only source for it. See
    [Abilities & Skills](../abilities/index.md).

## The Card

Paz is smitten by his own god, Ogun, for failing a shrine quest — given an unhealable, permanently
draining condition Paz calls "the Inevitable debuff." As he finishes saying goodbye, Ines breaks:

> "*No*," Sister Ines shouted, her voice suddenly angry. "No! You're not leaving."
>
> "No, no, no. Please. You're not leaving me. **Everyone leaves me.**"

She produces a squad flag and jabs it into his neck. *Whoom.* Paz turns into a card — a common
green one. Even Ines looks stunned that it worked. It is the first time in the book a crawler is
turned into a T'Ghee totem, and Carl never stops feeling responsible for allowing it.

She then loots Anton's corpse and walks out without a word.

## The Chowder War

Floor 8's Cuba region carries a quest called **The Chowder War** — a conflict between the Monk Seals
and the Red *Maníseros* Land Crabs, which the crabs intended to end with a single sneak attack.
Ines negotiates with the Monk Seal Abbot instead and warns him of the crabs' treachery.

The system's verdict is that she has turned a quick, survivable rout into a genocide:

> **Instead of a quick attack and surrender, it has become a war of attrition, with both sides
> losing devastating numbers in only a matter of days… the only hope of survival from either side is
> for them to completely wipe out their enemy.**

The Chowder War quest fails. The mass death it generates is what floods the underworld and triggers
the floor's Demon Eviction event — the chain of consequences that produces "Hell Comes to Crawler
Town," Amayon, and the burning of Cuba.

## Madre de Aguas and Amayon

With *Word Weaver* boosted to 15 by the Nebulars and further amplified by her patron-granted,
level-15 **Book of Rhymes** skill, Ines glamours the **Madre de Aguas** — a **level 125 city boss**,
a whale-sized sea serpent she had earlier failed to flag and kill. Because the boss can command every
creature in its region, charming it hands her the region: the zombies, the beasts, even the fish
start dragging themselves out of the ocean to get closer to the poem.

> **Controlling the snake allowed her to in turn control pretty much every creature within shouting
> distance. It was a brilliant plan until the spell got broken prematurely.**

Samantha bites her mid-recitation and breaks it. When the Demon Eviction arrives, the demon prince
**Amayon** — one of the four princes of Sheol, **level 250** — is forced into the realm inside the
zombified snake's body. Ines enslaves him too. Demons across the city are tagged in Carl's interface
as "minions of Sister Ines," and the snake is visibly forced to speak Carl's name against its will.
Amayon screams at her constantly. Her grip on him is measurably slipping by the endgame.

## Her Endgame

Ines holds the church that contains the last door Carl needs, and she will not open it. Her chat
messages to him are lucid and completely deranged:

> "I am filled with power, Carl. With each demon who appears, I grow stronger. They come, they keep
> coming, and I am the last rampart protecting us all. It is because of my faith. I can save you.
> I must free you from this place before your soul is dragged into hell. I can't save the cat, of
> course, as she is nothing but a rank beast."
>
> "But I can save you, Carl. Come to me, and I will show you the way. **I showed my sisters, and I
> can show you.** It's not too late."

Carl's reading is that her sponsors deliberately pushed her back over the edge: "Whatever imbalance
she had, it's been deliberately kicked back into place." Mordecai's is colder — the Nebulars "likely
sponsored her because she's religious, and they either want to convert her or make an example of
her."

## Death

Carl's assault team climbs thirty-two floors of the FOCSA building to reach her. She has to die
*before* Amayon is freed, or the full-strength demon will torch the building in revenge for her.
Carl opens the door to find her already sprinting down the hall at him, glowing, screaming
"Sinner!" He drops her with a *Nighty-Night* wand. She is unconscious on the floor at his feet.

> "I'll do it," Louis said, pushing me aside. "I already have five player skulls and none of you
> guys do. Trust me. You want to put that off as long as possible." Before I could object, he pulled
> a dagger from his inventory. "Sorry cat lady. I never met you, but you need to go if we want to
> save everybody else." He took the knife and plunged it directly into her chest.
>
> Sister Ines Quiteria, The Reaper of Havana, finally died.

A god's marked-for-death indicator appears over Louis's head:

> **The death of the bard has angered her celestial Patron. She is coming to seek revenge.**
>
> **The Goddess Ysalte has entered the realm.**

Ysalte — Goddess of Hopelessness and Insanity, level 250, "The Downward Spiral, The Vinegar Bitch" —
materializes out of Ines's dissolving corpse, inherits a slightly enhanced version of her deck, and
tells Louis: "She was asleep, and you killed her. Her mind is finally at ease, which is a blessing,
but I was not done with her yet, and for that you must suffer."

The goddess is then killed by Paz Lo, the card Ines made.
