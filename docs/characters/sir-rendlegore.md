# Sir Rendlegore ("Rend")

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Pet — "This is a pet-class mob." Bonded to [Carl](carl.md), and inseparable from
      [Mongo](mongo.md), who is bonded to Donut
    - **Race:** **Tummy Acher**, "also known as Belly Achers, also known as Colon Worms" — armless,
      two-legged, round, with a single upward-pointing tooth and a permanent giggle
    - **Class:** Not applicable — pets do not take a class
    - **Subclass:** Not applicable
    - **Level:** **1** at bonding (Chapter 5) → **8** after his first training run (Chapter 15) →
      **10** by the base defense (Chapter 30). No level is printed for him after that
    - **Role:** Tank. "They have multiple tank-based special abilities and are generally resistant
      to most types of damage."
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** **Wrecking Ball** — "the only skill he had," a mass-and-speed
      special attack
    - **Notable items:** The **Enchanted Tooth Cap of Hi-Yo Silver**, a mount-grade tooth cap that
      lets Carl summon him from up to five kilometers away
    - **Status:** **Alive** at the end of Book 7
    - **Previously:** **Garret**, pet of [Tserendolgor](tserendolgor.md) ("Ren") in Book 6

## Overview

Rend is the same creature as Garret, and legally he is not. Tummy achers regenerate on death, and on
every fifth death they revert to level 1, lose their memories, drop their bond and must be renamed.
Ren killed Garret deliberately with the Knife of Frozen Tears in Book 6 so that exactly this would
happen and Carl could take him — "You’re going to go live with Carl. He’s going to take care of you
because I can’t do it anymore."

Carl arrives on Floor 9 with a level-1 meatball in his inventory and a promise attached to it. The
first thing [Donut](princess-donut.md) does is accuse him of an affair.

> “In all of my life, I have never felt a sense of betrayal this profound,” Donut said. “Are Mongo
> and I being replaced? Is that what’s happening here? You’re replacing us?”

Carl zaps him out onto the table. He is half the size of Carl's fist, with "a spattering of black
hairs atop his not-quite-perfectly-round head, reminding me of Linus from the Peanuts comic," and
Garret's old pronounced underbite with "his single, round tooth sticking straight up." Mongo swallows
him within a minute, and then throws him back up; Rend's response is to try to climb back in. The
two are inseparable for the rest of the book.

## The Card

The only status card the book ever prints for him is the pre-bond examination in Chapter 5:

> **Male Tummy Acher – Level 1**
>
> **This is a pet-class mob.**
>
> **This pet is not bonded with a crawler.**
>
> **This pet has died five times. As such, this pet’s stats are all buffed by 125%.**
>
> **This pet has five weaknesses. Weaknesses may only be examined if you are bonded with this pet or
> if you have him contained within a carrier.**
>
> **Tummy Achers, also known as Belly Achers, also known as Colon Worms, are named such because they
> were originally parasites, living in the intestinal tracts of Jotan-class Titans. They are
> generally good-natured, though infants can be a little... difficult to control. They are voracious
> eaters, and when they reach full size can swallow just about anything. They have multiple
> tank-based special abilities and are generally resistant to most types of damage. They can survive
> in almost any environment. In addition, while not necessarily known for their offensive skills,
> their Wrecking Ball attack is considered a terror on any battlefield.**
>
> **They may be dumb, they may smell a little like expired chorizo, but holy shit are they
> adorable.**
>
> **At its current regeneration level, this Tummy Acher will reach full size upon ascending to level
> 25.**

The regeneration rules follow in the same block, and they are what make him unusual:

> **This particular pet is unique in that it will regenerate upon death as long as their body has
> not been destroyed. After each regeneration, they will be stronger than before, their overall
> stats getting buffed by an additional 25%. They will retain their size and strength upon
> regeneration except upon every five deaths, wherein they will revert to level 1 and lose their
> memories. Bonded pets will have to be re-bonded at this time. You must change their name as well.
> After 10 deaths, their full potential size doubles.**
>
> **Warning: This pet will not regenerate if it dies before it hits level 5.**
>
> **Warning: Upon death, they will receive a new, random weakness. This weakness generally results
> in insta-death. The more deaths, the higher the chance the weakness will be something common, like
> contact with water. It’s considered quite rare for a Tummy Acher to survive past 10 deaths, thus
> it’s not advisable to deliberately allow your pet to die in order to increase its strength.**

Donut’s summary — "Five weaknesses?… So it’s stupid and defective?" Only one of the five is ever
named in the text: eating undead flesh (Chapter 15), which is why the party has to confirm a mob is
not undead before letting him into a fight.

## Bonding and Naming

Ren told Carl the bond would take several feedings. It takes one bowl of accidental chowder.

> “Too late,” I said. “Man, that was a lot easier than getting Mongo to bond. Ren said we had to
> feed him a few times, but it only took one bowl.”
>
> **Congratulations. You have bonded with Tummy Acher, level-one. A new tab is available in your
> interface. Bonded Pets. Please see this tab for more information.**
>
> **Your guild has a pet stable installed. New options are available in your Bonded Pets tab.**
>
> **Your Tummy Acher requires a name. Please choose a name now.**

Donut's ruling on the name is that it cannot be "Garret two" — "being known as a second edition, like
a photocopy? Can you imagine?" — and that it must "honor the past but make it clear they're not a
copy of someone." Rejected suggestions include Cousin Oliver, Scrappy, Meatball, Balzac, Sherman,
Tugboat and Kimmy the Second. The naming happens with the *Name Your New Pet* box still open and
[Katia](katia.md) about to pour hair tonic on Carl's head, and it is
[Louis](louis-santiago.md) who lands it:

> He held the meatball up with two hands, like that monkey in The Lion King. “Sir Rendlegore. You
> know, Sir, S-I-R like a knight. And then Rendlegore. You can call him Rend. Sir Rendlegore. It
> fits with the royal court theme, and it still honors the past. Plus, Ren would’ve loved it. Nobody
> could ever pronounce her name. She pretended it made her mad, but I think she kinda liked it.”
>
> “Okay, that is a good name,” Samantha agreed.
>
> “Done,” I said before anybody else could respond. “It’s a good name.” I typed it into the box and
> hit enter.
>
> **Your pet has been named Sir Rendlegore!**

## Growing Up

Pet training is accelerated on this floor, and Carl grinds him on a colony of oversized skunk mobs
in Chapter 15, initially by holding him in one hand and using him as a weapon.

> “Go!” I cried as I retracted my scoop. I moved Rend into my left hand and thrust him toward the
> dazed creature.
>
> “Chomp, Rend! Chomp!”

> It was like I was holding the business end of a food processor in my hand. Blood sprayed from the
> point of contact as Rend gurgled with glee.

He goes from meatball-sized to softball-sized on the first kill and finishes the session at level 8
and "about the size of a large beach ball" — "Level eight was a decent start because it meant he'd
regenerate if he died, but it still wasn't good enough." By Chapter 30 he is a level-10 tank who
takes two direct pulse-rifle hits and only falls over and squeals. By Chapter 56 he is "about ¾'s
the size of his former self," measured against Garret.

The old high elf **Kibben**, minding him and Mongo during the King Rust parley, recognizes him: "I
got a bit of experience with belly achers." Asked what separates a tummy acher from a belly acher,
his answer also settles Rend's pedigree:

> “No real difference,” he said. “They tend to call ‘em bellies when they get extra big, but it’s
> not written down anywhere or anything like that. I remember this fellow from his last incarnation,
> and even though he was on his first run, he was still pretty big. He’s a special one.”

## The Tooth Cap and Wrecking Ball

Before the general offensive (Chapter 65), Rend is fitted with a donated item.

> Someone had donated a tooth cap, similar to the ones Mongo had for his sharp teeth, but this was
> designed for mounts. It was called The Enchanted Tooth Cap of Hi-Yo Silver. We installed it over
> his single, round tooth, making the tooth gleam silver. It allowed me to summon Rend to my
> position. It wasn’t a teleport spell, but it had about a five kilometer range. The cooldown was
> only three minutes. When I summoned him, he’d get inexorably pulled toward me at great speed. If
> there was a wall in the way, he’d—possibly—smash through the wall.

Donut refuses to put one on Mongo because the skill is dangerous. On Rend it is a feature: whatever
is between him and Carl when the summon fires gets hit, and it pairs with **Wrecking Ball**, the
special attack that "increased his mass and speed, and it did exactly what the name implied."

Both the item and the ability are covered by other pages.

## Saving Mongo

The payoff comes at the end of Chapter 72, under the guns of Larracos.
[Lucia Mar](lucia-mar.md)'s rottweiler **Gustavo 3** has already killed Raul the crab and two
clockwork duplicates. It fires **Porcupine Defense** — steel quills that erupt through Mongo's mouth
from the inside — electrifies them, then takes the unconscious dinosaur by the throat. Carl is folded
in half by a gale tower, his arm shattered, his sleep wand exploded in his hand.

> No, I thought as I struggled to my feet. No. Not Mongo.

> She’s never going to forgive me, I thought. This is what will break her.
>
> A terrible, deep giggling filled the night.
>
> The dark, round silhouette appeared above the ridge. Rend.
>
> He’d gotten bigger. Much bigger.
>
> The dog and the dinosaur stood directly between Rend and myself.
>
> “Wrecking Ball!” I wheezed as I cast Hi-Yo Silver a second time. Another gale hit me, and I spun
> head over heels landing on my stomach.
>
> I watched, as if in slow motion, as Rend started rolling toward me. And as he was magically pulled
> in my direction, he activated the only skill he had. Wrecking Ball. The special attack increased
> his mass and speed, and it did exactly what the name implied.
>
> The back half of Gustavo 3, the second of Lucia Mar’s two rottweilers, terror of the dungeon,
> exploded in a red mist as Rend rushed right up to me, stopping inches from my face.
>
> “Good boy,” I gasped at Rend. I downed a healing potion.

A gale tower bolt hits him immediately afterward. "He barely moved, but he did giggle."

Lucia, still in her hag form and sobbing, pours a pet healing potion on Mongo, whose health "held
just the barest sliver of red." Carl's report to the chat is four sentences: "It’s okay. He’s okay.
Gustavo is dead. Lucia is conscripted. Rend saved him."

Donut, who has spent the entire book calling him a replacement and an imbecile, settles it in the
next chapter:

> She jumped from my shoulder, to Mongo’s back, to the ground next to Rend.
>
> “Thank you, Rend. I still think you’re disgusting, but you saved my boy’s life, and I will never
> forget that.”
>
> Rend belched loudly in her face.

!!! note "Chapter numbering"
    The kill itself falls in the closing pages of **Chapter 72**; Donut's thanks and the aftermath
    are in **Chapter 73**.
