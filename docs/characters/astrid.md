# Astrid

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** NPC — **Assistant Manager of the Desperado Club** (and its head of security);
      secretly a **former crawler** working off an indenture contract
    - **Race:** **Bloodlust Sprite** — a small fae race; see
      [Species](../species/index.md) and the [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md)
    - **Level:** 125
    - **Also:** Owner/head of the **Naughty Boys Employment Agency**, a 500,000-gold social upgrade
      sold as a saferoom addition; patron of the bard Doctor Bones; quietly ran a shadow Meat Shields
      office inside the Penis Parade so her sons' crew would count as protected mercenaries
    - **Family:** Wife of the **Night Wyrm**, head of the
      [Guild of Suffering](../dungeon-factions/guild-of-suffering.md), whose real name is **Hamed**;
      mother of **[Damascus Steel](damascus-steel.md)** and **Anaconda**
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** **Cardiovascular magic** — she can boil a target's blood with a
      snap of her fingers, and does; **Cosmic Sense**, a precognitive alert skill; flight. She
      once knocked Carl unconscious through a closed door
    - **Notable items:** None named — her magic is stored in her wings, which is what kills her
    - **Status:** **Deceased** — assassinated in the Desperado Club kitchens, Chapter 32/33

## Overview

Astrid has been part of the furniture since Book 3: the tiny, extremely dangerous fairy who has to
come down and clean up whatever Carl and Donut have just done to the Desperado Club. She was
summoned when a crawler tried to kill Donut; she was summoned again when the pair got caught on an
off-limits floor. Carl's assessment before the job: "She's a level 125 bloodlust sprite. She knocked
my ass out through a closed door last time. She has something called cardiovascular magic, and she
can make my blood boil at the snap of a finger. Plus she's always surrounded by a bunch of guards.
And she's a fairy. Because of my stupid goblin tattoo, she does even more damage to me."

She works and sleeps in a hollowed-out hole inside a tree, reached through a small vent near the
ceiling at the end of the top-floor employees-only hallway.

Book 6 promotes her from a name on the mob roster to a person, and then kills her.

## The Contract

The Night Wyrm gives Carl and Katia a Guild of Suffering job. Guild contracts are cover-blind —
saying the target's name out loud voids the quest and revokes guild access — so neither of them can
discuss it with anyone, and neither knows who they are really working for.

> "**We wish to remove the competition. She serves dual roles. She keeps our family in the ground.
> She keeps our blood dry. You must kill her. The owner of the Naughty Boys Employment Agency. The
> assistant manager of the Desperado Club. She is one. Astrid. Kill her. Bring her lifeless body to
> the family.**"

The job is Katia's, and it is the last of the three seals on her **Engaged Lock Box of the Night
Wyrm** — the only thing standing between her and the Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace, which is the only
exit she can see from the Crown of the Sepsis Whore. If the job isn't completed, the succession rules
will eventually force Katia and Donut to fight each other.

## The Ambush, and How It Fell Apart

Carl's plan: catch her in the narrow hidden hallway at the bottom of the secret passage she always
uses to come down from the top floor. A motion-triggered smoke trap over the doorway, directional
anti-NPC claymores packed with **orcish iron BBs** (deadly to fairies) aimed into the closet,
teleport traps carpeting the hall to dump her guards into a locked upstairs closet, and Katia herself
standing by invisible as the redundancy. Samantha, briefed on only half of it, was the lure — make a
scene in the badger bar, refuse to leave, force the crocodilian guards to summon Astrid.

Three things went wrong at once:

1. **Astrid took the main stairs.** "The door to the employee exit opened, and Astrid flew out. There
   was no warning… She went straight for the regular stairs. She hadn't used the secret stairwell at
   all." Every trap was in the wrong hallway.
2. **Samantha caused a containment breach in the casino.** A player lost the knife game and was
   pulled into the Nothing; the tear let out **Minge**, a **Level 225 Minor Feral Demon** and the
   little sister of Samantha's old enemy Slit, who began hunting Samantha through the club by smell.
3. **Astrid's *Cosmic Sense* had already warned her.** She took the fast route to the casino, and her
   response to the breach was to unleash the vorpals — the monsters sealed in the very back room the
   ambush had been built around.

Carl called an abort. Katia refused: "Carl, no. I have to do this… If I don't finish this now, we
won't get a chance. **The fight will be our distraction.**"

## Death

Cornered in the club kitchens amid the chaos, Astrid does not attack Carl. She warns him off — "Carl,
is that you? You need to get out of here. It is not safe." — tells him where the vorpals will and
won't go, and, unprompted, gives him her husband's real name as a kindness:

> "You're in the Guild of Suffering. **My husband is named Hamed, but he's known as the Night Wyrm.
> Don't tell him I told you his real name.** His lair is hidden up there on the top floor."

When Katia's eight-foot troll totem crashes in, **Astrid puts herself between the monster and Carl**.
She boils the troll's blood and kills it.

Katia — invisible, shapeshifted into "a metallic, spiked-covered, human-faced, canine monstrosity" —
grabs Astrid out of the air and raises a spike over her head, and then freezes solid, millimetres
short. Carl breaks the stalemate himself:

> Astrid was right there, hovering in front of me, still clutched in Katia's hand. **I reached
> forward, grasped onto her wings, and I ripped.** They pulled off of her easily.

A fairy's magic lives in her wings. Katia comes unstuck; Katia's skull-headed crow totem takes both
of Astrid's eyes. Blind, disarmed and dropped, she gets out most of a sentence:

> "No, please! **Save my children. I was almost done. I would be free soon. Tell them…**"

A spike shoots from the wolf's neck and skewers her through the head mid-word. Katia inventories the
corpse and shifts into an ogre on the way out so the NPC witnesses will blame a monster rather than a
crawler.

Astrid dies without knowing that her own husband ordered the hit, without knowing Carl was involved,
and having spent her last conscious act shielding him. "Astrid hadn't liked me, but she wasn't
expecting me to attack her, either. In the end, she'd moved to shield me, to protect me from the
monster. I'd ripped the wings right off of her."

## "I Was Almost Done"

The line haunts Carl for the rest of the book, and the answer arrives an hour later from her sons.

Astrid and Hamed were former crawlers serving out an indenture — Carl compares them to the bopcas —
with the family's contract nearly expired. Damascus: "**We were promised freedom.** He wanted to make
sure she was dead. They're still married. My mother has—had—so much… **He gets it all. He'll have it
all when we get out.**" She was killed in the game for money on the outside.

"Save my children" meant Damascus Steel and Anaconda, whose jobs, safety and cover all depended
entirely on her position. Carl unknowingly honours it within the hour by buying the entire Penis
Parade crew out of the club on a two-floor mercenary contract.
