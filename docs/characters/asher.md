# Asher

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Human, non-crawler — a **memory ghost** on Floor 8, and one of the nine heads of a
      city boss
    - **Race:** Human
    - **Class / Subclass:** Not applicable — Asher was never a crawler and was about six years old
      when the Earth was harvested
    - **Family:** Son of **[Carl](carl.md)**'s father and **Tami-Lynn**; Carl's **half-brother**.
      Carl never knew he existed
    - **Age:** "Maybe six years old," with a birthday party scheduled for the following day. The
      book never gives a firm number
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** As a hydra head, a lightning attack and lightning resistance —
      pointedly assigned to the boy whose bedroom wall is covered in crayon drawings of storm clouds
      destroying cities, because he is afraid of lightning
    - **Notable items:** A heart-shaped candy box holding a shiny rock, two dollars and a plastic
      cowboy. Carl leaves it where it is
    - **Status:** **Deceased**, killed in the initial collapse along with his mother — a fact Carl
      only works out afterwards, from the rules of what a Reminiscence Hydra is made of

## The Reveal

Floor 8 — "The Ghosts of Earth" — replays memories of the crawlers' own lives. Carl's takes him into
his father's trailer in Iowa, around June or July of the year before the collapse, to watch a man he
hasn't seen in over twelve years die of liver failure at 140 pounds with a Mariners game on.

A boy's head appears around the hallway from the back bedrooms. His father shouts after him:

> "Asher! Get back here you little shit!"
>
> *Asher,* I thought. *His name is Asher.*

Carl had been holding out hope that his father had simply met a woman with kids. The face ends that:
"But no. That nose. Those eyes. That hair. My mom always complained my dad's genes were so dominant,
anyone remotely related to him was doomed to look just like him." He finds a wedding photo with the
woman visibly pregnant, and a photo of his father with a one-year-old Asher on his lap.

> Brown hair that looked like it was enough for two people. Thick and haphazard and untamable. Blue
> eyes. That nose.
>
> Asher.
>
> Asher.
>
> My brother.
>
> I had a brother.
>
> *Goddamn you*, I thought.

Carl watches Tami-Lynn administer the lethal dose of morphine she's been hiding in the flowerpot
outside, and doesn't watch the end. Her reason, delivered much later: she saw what the man had done
to Carl and would not let it happen to Ash.

Before leaving the boy's bedroom, Carl says the thing he can say to someone who can't hear him:

> "I'm so sorry I didn't know about you," I said. "If I had, I would've been here. I would've made
> sure you were okay. In a few months, something is going to happen, something really terrible.
> Hopefully you'll be asleep in your bed when it happens. It's too late for me to do anything about
> that, but I want you to know something. **You won't be forgotten. Not ever.**"
>
> I reached forward, and I put my hand on his head, but my hand went all the way through.

He notes there was no picture of himself anywhere in the trailer. No sign the boy knew he had a
brother at all.

Carl does not tell Donut. "I wasn't certain why, especially after I promised him he wouldn't be
forgotten. There would be time for that."

## The Reminiscence Hydra of Malicious Compliance

The trailer burns, and out of it comes the floor's city boss: a stitched flesh-ball the size of a
small elephant on four stubby legs, with **nine screaming heads** on long stalks, each one the face
of somebody Carl knows. It is billed as a rebranded "Scolopendra Nymph," a monster from the third and
fourth seasons.

The nine: **Asher**; **Tami-Lynn**; Carl's friend **Monobrow Sam**; **Bea's mother**; **Sugar Cube**,
one of Donut's relatives; Carl's supervisor **Dick**; his landlord **Mr. Roth**; **Sally** from
billing; and **Judge Lucian**, a cat-show judge who acts as the hydra's deckmaster and leader.

The system's own announcer describes the cruelty of it out loud — "a naughty man with beautiful feet
sitting there in his underwear, facing down a nine-headed monster that's probably going to kill him
and his cat, all the while he wonders what any of this exposition has to do with the fact that one of
those nine heads is that of his recently-discovered little brother." The AI also notes that it
*swapped the boss out at the last minute*: the original was going to be Carl's father.

The Asher head taunts him — "Carl, why didn't you ever visit me?" — and Carl keeps lobbing bombs at
it specifically to get it off the field, while the other heads swoop in to shield it. His coping
mechanism is to read the writing: "The heads were clearly saying things the real versions of these
people would never say… It was like the dungeon was trying too hard to be edgy, which in effect
turned the whole thing into a parody. While making me watch the actual memory was a perfect way to
try to break me, this was just pissing me off."

## "The Echo of Your Kin Has Been Muffled"

Half-dead and on fire, Carl silently asks his own totem, the spider Shi Maria, for a mercy:

> *Asher,* I thought. *Get him out of here. Kill him fast. He's suffered enough.*
>
> Shi Maria turned her head to regard me.
>
> "Asher?" she asked, her head corkscrewing. "Which one of these little morsels of confusion is
> Asher?"

Her third eye — the one that is supposed to blind and madden anyone who looks into it — opens, and
this is the moment she gets inside Carl's head:

> *Ah, yes. I see now. Don't worry, Carl. I will not blind you. Not in the way you're thinking.*
>
> "Carl, I wish I'd known you, too," the Asher head called out to me just before he was swallowed
> and then bitten off.
>
> **Three Heads Remaining.**
>
> ***There, Carl. The echo of your kin has been muffled. He rests. I have done something for you.
> Soon, soon you will do something for me.***

That debt is the whole back half of the book. It is collected in Chapter 71, when Shi Maria takes
control of Carl's body and tattoos the Eye of the Bedlam Bride into his chest.

Tami-Lynn is the last head standing. Her dying words are the confession:

> "I don't regret it," she said. "I saw what happened to you, and I didn't want Ash to go through
> the same thing. So I made sure. I made sure it was done right."

Earlier in the fight she had screamed the line that detonates in the next chapter: "**I wrote you,
and you never answered! I needed help. Look what you made me do!**"

## What Beatrice Knew

Donut lets it slip by accident, recognizing the boy's face from before the collapse:

> "I've seen him a bunch of times, but I still don't really know who he is. **Miss Beatrice had his
> picture on her phone, and she was always talking about him with her mother. His name is Asher. I
> think he was her nephew or something. I missed that whole drama, but Miss Beatrice's mom said to
> just ignore it.**"
>
> …"And Miss Beatrice knew you had a brother, and she was hiding it from you? That's… that's
> something Georgina would do!"

Tami-Lynn had written to Carl, probably on Facebook. Beatrice saw it. Carl didn't.

> *Goddamn you, Beatrice*. Up until that moment, I realized I'd never been mad at her. Even after
> all the cheating, all the lying, I hadn't been angry. I was just pissed at myself for being so
> stupid and naïve. Not until now. Now that it was too late.

His own read on why: "She didn't want me leaving. That was the only explanation. I would've come
here, to Iowa, and she knew it." He tries once to extend her the benefit of the doubt and then shuts
the whole thing down: "This is done. It's in the past, and it's blinding you. It's making you weak.
It's what they want. They're trying to break you."

## Dead

Carl works out what membership in a Reminiscence Hydra actually means:

> A wave of sadness washed over me. I finally realized what it meant for someone to be in that
> hydra. **They were all dead. These were all people who'd been collected in the initial collapse.**

He had hoped Asher and Tami-Lynn were asleep in their beds when it happened, and had been terrified
the showrunners would use his brother as the boss outright — "They weren't allowed to do that
anymore, not with children, but it was clear they cared little about the rules at this point."

## The Reason for Everything After

The last words of Carl's story in Book 6, as Floor 8 is scoured clean and he is pulled into the sky
over Cuba:

> *Don't worry*, I thought as I looked down upon the quickly receding landscape. I knew none of these
> people, but I was overwhelmed with the urge to make certain they would be proud of me. **I thought
> of Asher, the brother I never knew. I needed to make him proud. I couldn't disappoint him. I would
> literally pull myself through hell on my hands and knees if I had to. I wasn't there for him while
> he lived, but I would be damned if I allowed his death to be for nothing.**
>
> *You will not break me.*
>
> *You will be avenged.*
>
> *I swear it.*
>
> *I swear it to you all*.

    Donut asks directly. Carl refuses: "What was your dad's name, anyway? I don't think you ever
