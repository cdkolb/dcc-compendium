# Brandon An

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Role:** Leader of the "Meadow Lark" evacuee group (Book 1); maintenance worker at an
      eldercare facility before the collapse
    - **Status:** **Killed in action, end of Floor 3** — dies holding a line against Shade
      Gremlins so the rest of his group can reach the stairwell in time
    - **Notable weapon:** A giant hammer
    - **Family:** Older brother of [Chris Andrews](chris-andrews.md)

## Book 1: Leading Meadow Lark

Brandon leads a group of eldercare-facility residents and staff (the "Meadow Lark" evacuees)
that Carl and Donut encounter and help protect on Floors 1–2, wielding a giant hammer in their
defense and making the hard calls to keep the mostly-elderly group moving toward each floor's
exit. He and Carl form a real, if understated, bond over the course of the evacuation.

## Book 3: The Last Message

Brandon and his brother [Chris](chris-andrews.md) have a falling-out on Floor 3, sparked by a
disagreement over strategy — Chris wants to leave the stairwell area to hunt and reconnect with
Carl and Donut, while Brandon insists on staying close to the exit and training safely. The
argument escalates into Chris leaving the party with several other men, all of whom are later
confirmed dead except Chris himself.

As Floor 3 collapses, Brandon and a resident named **Henry** hold the line against advancing
Shade Gremlins so Imani, Elle McGibbons, and the rest of the group can reach the stairwell in
time. Knowing he won't make it, Brandon sends a final message — to Carl, Imani, and Elle
simultaneously — that only reaches Carl after a delay, arriving in a new "Messages from deceased
Crawlers" folder just as Floor 4 opens:

> "Look, man. I got into a fight with my brother Chris, and he left the party... I said something
> stupid, and he got mad. He left, and now it's too late to tell him I love him. I never said it.
> I'm about to die, and it's all I can think about... tell him I said I was sorry, and that he
> was right. He'll know about what... And tell him I love him. That's the most important part."

Carl never manages to pass the message along directly — Chris disappears from contact shortly
after, resurfacing only through secondhand reports (see [Chris Andrews](chris-andrews.md)).
