# Princess D'Nadia

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** NPC royalty / broadcast panelist, later a Carl and Donut sponsor
    - **Race:** Saccathian ("Sac") — tentacle-faced
    - **Title:** Princess of the Prism Kingdom (Book 2–4) → **Empress D'Nadia** (Book 5, after her
      father's retirement) → **Warlord Empress D'Nadia of the Prism**, holding Spot 5 on Floor 9
      (Book 7)
    - **Class / Subclass:** Not stated anywhere — she is an NPC head of state and is never statted
      out as a crawler, even as a Faction Wars warlord
    - **Status:** **Deceased** — killed in her own throne room in Larracos at the end of chapter 26
      of Book 7; the system credits the kill to Mongo, Donut's pet, on Donut's order
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** None described through Book 5 — a diplomat/broadcast figure, not
      a combatant. Book 7: Donut's pre-raid briefing lists "A BUNCH OF ICE AND WATER SPELLS" plus a
      crown carrying water spells, but she casts none of them on-page
    - **Notable items:** None described through Book 5. Book 7: three separate tech shields built
      into the fabric of her gown, a teleport-to-safety ring (useless inside Faction Wars), a
      magical crossbow, a small concealed pulse gun, and a knife she uses on herself to stay
      injured and so block Carl's Ring of Divine Suffering

## Overview

Princess D'Nadia is a recurring, friendly saccathian panelist on the talk-show circuit since Book
2, gifting Donut the [Prism Industries goggles](../items/prism-industries-capacitating-and-focusing-goggles.md)
that expose Hekla's plot in Book 3. Her father's retirement makes her Empress of the Prism Kingdom
by Book 5.

## Role in the Story — Book 5

D'Nadia serves as one of three celebrity judges for the Butcher's Masquerade's pet show and talent
competition, alongside an unnamed, tall, thin soother and an unnamed jello-mold blob alien — not
the gleener and camel-alien pair (Hurk and G'valt) who separately judge Carl's earlier CrawlCon art
contest; the two judging panels are distinct. She reveals she's become one of Donut's sponsors —
via a corporate buy-in bid Carl's new legal entity nearly derailed without meaning to — and needles
Signet during the party, dismissively telling her that Signet's dead mother and fallen
tattoo-warriors were "never real," a comment that directly motivates Signet's decision to
sacrifice herself and prove otherwise by resummoning them all one final time. She survives the
ensuing battle, having been present only as a remote broadcast avatar throughout.

## Book 7: This Inevitable Ruin

The Faction Wars seat she spent Books 2 through 5 buying is the thing that kills her. D'Nadia
enters Floor 9 as Warlord Empress of the Prism, holding Spot 5 — the territory directly south of
the Naga Blood Sultanate and adjacent to the Princess Posse's southern front. She is the second
faction leader eliminated in the war, and the first killed by Carl and Donut personally.

### Before the Assault

The Prism signs the opening ceasefire along with everyone else. D'Nadia's position deteriorates
from there. Her broadcast-era relationship with Donut has already curdled: she has sold Donut's
sponsorship on, and Donut is "still mad at Empress D'Nadia for trying to get us deleted," to the
point that Carl has to stop her sending a message into the Prism chat about "her betrayal."

Her people fight dirty and badly. A Prism scout — not a mercenary, but "a Saccathian prince of some
kind, one of D'Nadia's actual relatives" — infiltrates the Princess Posse's southern trenches and
unleashes an Instant River scroll that drowns soldiers in an escape tunnel; he is blown apart by a
landmine while fleeing. Almost the entire enemy first trench line on that front turns out to be
flying Prism banners, which Carl reads correctly as her committing nearly everything she has
between his army and her headquarters. After the trench battle, most of it is dead.

### The Throne Room

In chapter 26 Carl walks into the Prism headquarters — a castle Carl later notes was a fraction of
the size of his own — announcing himself: "Oh, Empress. You have visitors." He puts the
[Ring of Divine Suffering](../items/enchanted-night-wyrms-ring-of-divine-suffering.md) on his finger
before he goes in.

Her guards are already dead or unconscious. She fights alone, and briefly: a crossbow bolt off
Carl's shield, a concealed pulse shot at Donut that goes wide. Carl works her over with banger
spheres while Donut casts *Clockwork Triplicate* on [Mongo](mongo.md) and sets the two duplicates on
her to strip her three layered tech shields, her gown, and the knife she has been using to keep
herself at less than full health — because the Ring of Divine Suffering can only mark a target at
100%. Carl heals her with a scroll and marks her anyway.

> You have successfully marked Warlord Empress D'Nadia of the Prism. Her highest stat is
> Intelligence.

What follows is less an interrogation than a sentencing. Carl brings up Tsarina Signet, and the
thing D'Nadia said to her at the Butcher's Masquerade:

> "You told her that she wasn't real. That her family wasn't real."

> "I'm sorry none of the golems are here right now. That's just how it works out sometimes. But
> that's okay. I think, in the end, she considered me as part of her family as well."

She pleads her old friendship and her old sponsorship — "Donut, I was your sponsor. I got you your
glasses. Don't you like your glasses?" — and then her motive: "I did it for my people." Carl:
"No, D'Nadia. You did it for you. And now your people are going to suffer because of it."

### Carl at the Edge

This is the scene where the Ring of Divine Suffering nearly takes Carl. He remembers meeting her on
Ripper Wonton's show, when she held his hand and cried over Manassa's assassination, and thinking
"perhaps she was one of the good ones" — before learning she had been trying for years to buy her
way into the dungeon to hunt people like him.

> The anger that washed over me was so brilliantly white, all three of my eyes burned. I'd never
> felt anything like this. Not with Maggie My. Not with Quan Ch.
>
> Not as I sat there, in that gas station with the button that would end it all.
>
> I hated her.

It is [Donut](princess-donut.md) who stops him:

> Carl: Donut, I changed my mind. I want to do it. Step aside.
>
> Donut: No, Carl. Please, no. I need you to step back. I need you to calm down right now. I got
> this. It's okay.

> And there it was.
>
> The edge.
>
> I saw it, clear as can be. Right there. I stood upon it, one foot stretched out over open air.
>
> You can do that, sometimes. You can find clarity when you need it most.
>
> I took a step back.

Minutes later, with ten captured gnoll mercenaries standing in front of him and the ring telling
him he could mark all of them at once, Carl pulls the ring off his finger in front of the whole
room. Donut asks him to throw it away — "It's doing something to you. You're scaring me." Carl
recognises the words as ones his mother used to say to his father, and still refuses: "Not yet. I
need it for just a little longer." See [Carl](carl.md).

### The Kill and What It Cost the Galaxy

Donut takes the kill, and takes it on Mongo's behalf, closing out the Book 6 pet-show grudge:

> "Empress, Darling," Donut said. "Mongo would like to express his utmost disappointment at how you
> judged him during the pet show."

> System Message: Warlord D'Nadia of the Prism has fallen. Credit for the kill has been given to
> pet Mongo of the Princess Posse.
>
> System Message: The Prism has been defeated.
>
> System Message: All assets of the Prism have been awarded to the Princess Posse.

Donut loots the body and then takes the corpse itself. The system AI's achievement — **Gavrilo
Princip** — treats her death as the only politically consequential one of the day:

> Empress D'Nadia is a whole different cookie, my friends. You just killed—and, by the way,
> bankrupted—the last living heir of a small but powerful trading dynasty. The Syndicate probate
> courts are already girding themselves for the endless court battles that are about to ensue. Even
> if this is the only system leader you manage to slaughter during the next few weeks, you have
> made an impact, my boy. A big one.

The reward is a **Legendary 1914 box** — the box that later produces Donut's *War Crime* spell and
Carl's *Run, Little Günter, Run*.

Carl's own reaction is not triumph. "All the anger I'd just been feeling was gone, drained out of me
like all my rage had been ejected out of an airlock... An unexpected sense of mourning hit me, all
at once, and it was confusing and strange and completely bewildering." He later reflects, "I'd seen
my father in myself at that moment."

Her castle cannot be held, since it does not adjoin Posse land. Carl leaves a lone Madness
mercenary sitting in it deliberately, to make the surviving warlords fight over the scrap; it
changes hands three times and eventually goes to the Dream.
