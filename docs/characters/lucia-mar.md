# Lucia Mar

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Age:** About 13
    - **Likely origin:** South America (broadcast context, unconfirmed)
    - **Signature gear:** A mace and a full set of glowing silver armor, worn under her original
      yellow-and-blue soccer jersey as a tabard
    - **Pets:** Two rottweilers, **Cici** and **Gustavo 3** — **both dead** by the end of Book 7
      (Cici decapitated by Donut's *Hole* in Book 5; Gustavo 3 killed by Carl's pet
      [Sir Rendlegore](sir-rendlegore.md) in Book 7)
    - **Notable stat:** Very high Dexterity (wall-running, flips, acrobatic combat); over **200
      player-killer skulls** plus 20 dead hunters by Book 7
    - **Status (Book 8):** **Alive.** Captured and conscripted by Carl on Floor 9, then released when
      the floor ends; she descends to Floor 10 partied with [Florin](florin.md). She is also a
      dead-man's switch for roughly **120,000** children held on the Earth surface, and by her own
      account an ally of **"the Eulogist"**. She leaves Floor 11 through the portal with an
      unexplained five-year-old girl. See [Who Is "Alexandro"?](#who-is-alexandro)
    - **Type/Class/Subclass:** Not shown in Book 1 → **Lajabless** race / **Black Inquisitor General** class (cleric/mage/warrior triple class), revealed Book 2
    - **Level:** 29 — **1st on the Floor 3 leaderboard**, the highest-ranked crawler shown
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** No named spells shown; a personal magical shield noticed late in Book 1; her Lajabless race halves her strength but doubles spell power in human form, and the reverse in her skeletal "goat-leg" form
    - **Notable items:** A mace; a full set of glowing silver armor; a personal magical shield

## Overview

Lucia Mar is never met directly by Carl — she exists entirely as a recurring figure on the
in-universe broadcast, one of the season's breakout stars and a running benchmark Carl measures
his own progress against. She's first shown in an early recap tearing through a cow-sized spider
with her two rottweilers before graduating to a mace and full magical armor, still worn over her
original soccer jersey.

## Cici & Gustavo 3

Her two rottweilers are dungeon "familiars" rather than standard pet-class mobs, which is why she
can have two of them at once (Mordecai later explains this exception applies to certain classes,
races, or unusually high-Charisma crawlers). Cici wears a chainmail sweater; Gustavo 3 develops
the ability to shoot lightning when he barks. Per Carl's naming-convention guess, "3" indicates
he's the third dog named Gustavo (without a surname) to enter the dungeon.

## Recurring Broadcast Presence

Lucia Mar reappears across multiple recap episodes throughout Book 1:

- Shown obliterating a nest of monsters before descending a stairwell, then whisked away for a
  guest appearance on *Dungeon Crawler After Hours with Odette* immediately before Carl and
  Donut's own appearance — one of her dogs attacks a producer backstage.
- By roughly the book's midpoint, she has **four player-killer skulls** by her name, with the
  circumstances never explained on-air.
- After Carl's "Pork Boy" incident goes viral, his appearance fee is reported to have doubled to
  the point of equaling hers — used as an explicit measure of how famous he's become.
- Later shown smashing a Brindled Vespa with a personal magical shield Carl hadn't previously
  noticed her have.

## Role in the Story

Lucia Mar never interacts with Carl directly, but functions as his constant point of comparison
for how a "successful" crawler is supposed to look — decked out in flashy magical gear, high
Dexterity, camera-ready — which Carl explicitly contrasts with his own scrappy, unarmed,
improvised approach.

## Book 2: Race/Class Reveal

On Floor 3, the recap show reveals Lucia Mar's choices: race **Lajabless**, one of the most
extreme options shown — she spends half the day as a beautiful adult woman (double spell power,
half strength) and the other half with a skull face and a transformed goat leg (double strength,
half spell power, and a permanent limp that ends her wall-running). Her class, a triple
cleric/mage/warrior hybrid called **Black Inquisitor General**, is described by Mordecai as
making her likely "the most powerful crawler in this game." By the time Floor 3 collapses, she's
ranked **1st on the leaderboard** at Level 29, ahead of every other named crawler including
Hekla and Carl. Donut, jealous, calls her "awful" and "ugly" on-air.

## Book 2: Desperado Club Incident & the Feud

Lucia Mar's dogs go berserk inside the [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md)'s
pet playroom, killing two attendants and another pet before breaking loose onto the dance floor;
while the room is frozen by one of her spells, she steals liquor and kills an elite gnoll with
her mace, getting her club membership permanently revoked. The incident (and Donut's own
drunken, overheard insult of the dogs — "bitch-ass rottweilers") is spun by the broadcast into a
manufactured rivalry, **"Princess Donut Versus Cici and Gustavo 3."** Confronted about it on the
arena show *Knuckle Cracking*, Lucia Mar — in her skull-faced Lajabless form — threatens to kill
Donut and "rip Carl to pieces" for his gear; Mordecai confirms the two parties will eventually be
forced together for a televised confrontation, but not before Floor 6 at the earliest.

## Book 5: The Fight with Donut, and the Voices in Her Head

Lucia Mar finally collides with Carl and Donut directly on Floor 6, seizing an entire village
(already named "Alucarda" — much to Donut's annoyance that she didn't get to name it herself) and
setting a network of saferoom teleport traps as an ambush. While Carl is pulled away to a
mandatory intergalactic fan convention, Donut faces her — and her rottweilers, Cici and Gustavo
3 — alone. Caught by a snare trap in Lucia's own treehouse pub, she survives Cici's lunge behind a
stacked *Entourage* illusion (with an invisibility potion layered onto her own "copy"), then casts
a single *Hole* beneath her own feet to drop straight through the floor to the ground below and
break the snare. When Cici sticks her head down through that same hole hunting for her, Donut
simply snaps the spell off from below — decapitating the dog with the closing hole rather than a
second, separate cast. The recap footage of the fight becomes one of the season's biggest
broadcast moments.

The fight reveals something new and unsettling: Lucia's personality visibly flips mid-conversation
between a genuinely scared, homesick child (in one aside, speaking with a Dutch accent and naming
herself "Jill," a name Florin later confirms belongs to a real, ten-year-old crawler) and her
usual violent, "Lajabless kid warlord" persona — implying multiple people, possibly children, are
somehow sharing or fighting over control of her body via her dogs. Florin, confronting her at the
Butcher's Masquerade, concludes the dog Gustavo is the one truly "in charge" and that the real
Lucia/Jill can only surface in short bursts. She escapes the masquerade's final battle using a
pre-set teleport trap before the boss fight even begins, avoiding the "Cage Fight Extreme" that
kills several other crawlers.

### What she says to the voice behind her

The Floor 6 recap is also the best source for how the "game" is framed to her. Everything below is
from Lucia's own mouth, and the first two are spoken to someone who is not there.

- **Stairwell station, end of Floor 4 (opening of Book 4):** she snarls "What did you say about my papa?" at a boy who said nothing about
  him, then says over her shoulder, "Don't take shit from anyone. It's just a game. It's not real."
- **Floor 6, to an empty spot:** "The fight is over. It is a draw." Then, after a pause, "No points?"
  And: "He's mad. He says I should finish you off, but you fought good."
- **To Donut, same scene:** "I had friends, on the last floor. Bini and Abraham and Siti. It was all ice,
  and we finished and popped the bubble. But I fell asleep, and I didn't tell Gus to sleep. And… He's the
  bad one. Cici does it, too, but Gus is worse." She then slaps the side of her own head in a steady
  rhythm. (Earlier recap: Gustavo "accidentally" killed a group of crawlers in her bubble, and she was
  shown sobbing alone, the first real emotion the broadcast had ever shown her.)
- **Her goal, to Donut:** "Soon, you will be tossed through the veil, and you will feast with the other
  losers. And I will have enough points to get across the bridge and back home with my papa." Donut's
  retort when she kills Cici ("The only bridge you'll be crossing today is made out of rainbows") is a
  pet-afterlife joke of Donut's own; the bridge in Lucia's line is hers, and the book never explains it.
- **When Cici dies:** "She killed the wrong one… What's going to happen now?"

Donut points out that the broadcast cut a line from this recap: "She said his name was Alexandro,
and he was her youth assistant." Mordecai's reaction is the key context. "Youth assistants are new. But
they're not a dungeon thing. Children under a certain age aren't allowed to enter the dungeon. If they
descend into the dungeon, they are taken away until after the crawl. The youth assistants are the ones
who take care of them… Kids shouldn't be in here. Youth assistants shouldn't be here. It's really
strange." Nobody follows this up.

Later, at the Masquerade, Florin reports what Jill told him: "Something to do with the dog and a guy named
Alexandro. But there are kids in there. In her head. Multiple kids. We have to get that dog. Not her. The
dog. It can still control her from afar, but only in short bursts." Carl asks Zev how a child crawler is
allowed. Zev: "It's complicated, Carl. You don't have to be involved in everything. We're not going to
discuss it."

## Book 6: The Battle of Beijing, and a Threat

Lucia Mar spends Book 6 entirely offstage and is one of its most consequential figures anyway. She
sits at **#4** on the leaderboard and, per Florin, buys up every teleport trap and stairwell-chamber
item that appears on the market: "Teleport traps and alcohol are the only things she purchases."
Florin's read on her, before the phases split: "She has good days and bad days, and it's been more
bad than good lately."

### The Battle of Beijing

It happens off-page. The only direct glimpse is an announcer's aside — "**We are interrupting that
one-sided Lucia Mar slaughter in Beijing to bring you whatever this is**" — and Li Jun's account of
the aftermath:

> "It was Lucia Mar." He swallowed. "**They're calling it the Battle of Beijing.** Lucia, she never
> really collected any cards. She refused to play it. She got the six totems they automatically gave
> her, but they were all low-level monsters. And whenever a battle started, she discarded the first
> one, and she played the rest until her hand was empty **which allowed her to use her spells and
> magic and inventory. She and her dog went in and killed everything.**"

> "**She killed so many people.** She set up a teleport trap, and Fang's team got caught up in it. He
> had a key. She killed him and his wife and took it. After that, we thought she'd already gone down
> the stairs, but when we got there, she was waiting for us. **She can hide her presence now.**"

### The Doggie Doors

Before going down the stairs she drops **five Tier-3 Doggie Door personal-space upgrades** on the
ground and leaves them for Carl's guild. Li Jun: "She said Florin had messaged her before he'd gone
down the stairs and told her to help us."

Nobody can work out where she got them. Katia: "She shouldn't have had one, let alone five of them.
It's a tier-3. Mordecai says these shouldn't be available until the ninth floor. He thinks Lucia stole
them when she went on a show or something." Imani prices a guild-wide rollout at **twenty-five
million** gold; they install one in Imani's room and hold the other four. Elle, on what it means:
"It would've changed everything… We've been chasing a fast travel system since the whole guild system
opened."

### The Threat

Li Jun holds one detail back for Carl alone:

> **Li Jun: This is just for you, Carl. There's more. Lucia dropped the upgrades, and she said she
> was helping us because Florin had asked, but before she went down the stairs, she went from really
> friendly to mean again, and she said she was saving something special for herself because she had a
> score to settle with Donut and that she would be seeing her on the next floor. She said she was
> going to kill both Donut and her pet dinosaur. That's why my sister wanted to fight her.**

!!! note "Reported, not quoted"
    The threat reaches Carl — and the reader — only through Li Jun's paraphrase. Lucia herself never
    appears on the page in Book 6.

## Book 7: This Inevitable Ruin

Book 7 finally pays off the threat Li Jun carried down from Floor 8. Lucia comes for Donut and Mongo, she
gets close, and the encounter ends with her losing her last dog, losing her autonomy, and handing Carl the
worst piece of information in the book.

She spends the ceasefire drinking. Li Jun’s early sighting: “She’s here in Shanty Town right now with her
dog, drinking at the Possum Envy right on the edge of the border with the robot team. The Reavers. One of the
new guys tried to recruit her, and she almost ripped his head off.” She refuses every recruiter, hides her
position from the Find Crawler skill, and joins no army. Zev will not even try to warn her: “Lucia doesn’t
respond to messages anymore. She was never my client, thank goodness.”

### The Nest Tower

Chapter 70. Donut is alone at the top of the **Nest**, the Princess Posse’s tallest synergy tower in Shanty
Town, sniping the Larracos defence towers while two gods fight below. Lucia walks in through the front door.
The garrison dies in the time it takes to send three messages:

> Toyotomi: No. It is a crawler. A woman with a dog. She’s coming in.
>
> This message is from a deceased mercenary.
>
> Sergeant Bret S: We need backup! Backup!
>
> Bomo: Will stop her now.
>
> This message is from a deceased mercenary.

**Bomo** — one of the four cretin mercenaries Quasar negotiated out of the Syndicate back in Book 5 — and the
Sai sergeant **Toyotomi** are both killed, along with a squad of mages. Carl finds them on his way up: “I
spared a quick, sad glance for Toyotomi, Bomo, and the defending mages, all of whom were cleaved right in half
with a spell.” Toyotomi is dead for good; Bomo would regenerate on the 10th floor, but the guild no longer
holds his contract.

Lucia also drops a net over the tower so Donut cannot teleport out, then starts climbing. What she wants is
stated plainly: “Give me your dinosaur, and I will leave. It is justice I seek. Nothing more.” Donut answers
by activating **Donkey Kong** — a last-ditch defence that turns the tower’s stairs into a ramp and rolls
barrels down it — and holds her off for several minutes while Carl levitates, bombs a theatre, accidentally
triggers the entire Larracos city defence system, and fights his way in behind her.

### Conscripted

The fight ends by accident. Donut throws Gustavo 3 down the ramp, Lucia goes with him, and the two of them
bowl into Carl halfway up, along with **Raul** — the crab from Donut’s Floor 8 card deck, now mythic-rarity
and level 110, who pins Lucia in the entrance hall while announcing his own magnificence.

Carl uses the potion Li Na had just duplicated for him: a **Hell-Kissed Potion of Conscription, level 18**,
looted from a dead war mage and brewed in the Nothing by Krakaren Prime. The item text notes it “will work
against pretty much any non-affiliated entities, regardless of their protections, including most deities.”

> I smashed the screaming, Hell-kissed Conscription potion against Lucia’s head.

> The potion worked. A pink glow appeared around Lucia’s dot.

*Under Orders* appears over her head, her terrified act drops instantly, and she spends the next several
minutes trying and failing to attack him. He orders her protections down — a list the warlord menu shows as
astonishingly long — and sits with her on the floor. His observation, examining her: “Through the stench of
burned hair, I could smell sweat and alcohol. But there was something else, too. A faint perfume. A sweet one.
The kind a child would pick.”

### Diah

Carl is about to mark her for death when the thing Florin has been warning about for two books surfaces and
speaks:

> “Sir,” she said. Her eyes had changed. Her voice had taken on a raspy quality. “My name is Diah. Lucia is
> with Alexandro. And Alexandro is only here when the dogs are close. My name is Diah. We are in the dark. You
> need to wait until the dog is close, and Lucia will be back. Wait until she is back. Please, sir. Please.”

Under a direct order to tell the truth, she confirms it, and describes where “we” are:

> “I... I. Yes. Sir. Please. We are in the dark, and it’s so cold. Sometimes we can hear them talk. We can
> hear them whispering. It’s like there’s a river running through our room, and if we touch it, it hurts. They
> say such sad things. When the dog wanders off, Lucia fades, and one of us takes over, and we can see again.
> We take turns. Sometimes it’s day, and we can see the light.”

Carl cancels the death mark. He never gets to ask a follow-up question.

### Rend Kills the Last Dog

Gustavo 3 gets to Mongo. Carl, pinned by a gale-strike tower with a shattered arm, watching the dog and the
dinosaur between him and safety, thinks: *She’s never going to forgive me. This is what will break her.*

Then the meatball arrives.

> A terrible, deep giggling filled the night.
>
> The dark, round silhouette appeared above the ridge. Rend.

Carl casts *Hi-Yo Silver* to yank his pet toward him, and [Rend](sir-rendlegore.md) uses the only skill he
has on the way past:

> The back half of Gustavo 3, the second of Lucia Mar’s two rottweilers, terror of the dungeon, exploded in a
> red mist as Rend rushed right up to me, stopping inches from my face.
>
> “Good boy,” I gasped at Rend.

Mongo’s throat is torn open and his health is at a sliver. Lucia — still conscripted, and for a moment
genuinely herself — drops to her knees and pours a pet healing potion into him, sobbing “You did it. You did
it.” Donut’s thanks, later: “Thank you, Rend. I still think you’re disgusting, but you saved my boy’s life,
and I will never forget that.” Rend belches in her face.

### “Now You Can’t Kill Me”

Seconds after saving Mongo, her posture changes, and she delivers the book’s parting shot:

> “You should’ve killed us both when there was still a connection,” the girl said to me. “Now it’s too late.
> Now the Eulogist has another ally in the dungeon, and now you can’t kill me. Not unless you want all the
> children of Earth to die.” She shook her head and walked off.

Zev fills in the rest, badly and reluctantly. Lucia’s presence in the crawl was originally a glitch at the
very start of the crawl, which the Syndicate side wanted to correct "and kick her back out, but once she
started earning, they.... We gave her a few loot boxes to compensate and keep her safe." The sentence breaks
off there and never says who "they" are. The AI now says it is “unable to extract her.” What nobody
understood until Gustavo 3 died is what is tied to her:

> Zev: It’s complicated, Carl. It’s not all the children. Just the ones that tried to get into the dungeon.
> And it’s only half of those. All the ones who were in a specific wing. They’re on the surface in the kinder
> facility.

> Carl: So what happens if she dies?
>
> Zev: As of about five minutes ago, we thought the glitch would self-correct and release everyone. That’s
> what’s supposed to happen. Now? We don’t know for certain. The Eulogist is another name for the AI in the
> center system. We don’t know how it’s all connected for certain. But now we think... we think if she goes,
> they all go.
>
> Carl: How many is it?
>
> Zev: I shouldn’t be telling you any of this.
>
> Carl: How many fucking children is it?
>
> Zev: About 120,000. Give or take a few.

Carl’s response: “I almost killed her, Zev. You almost let me kill her.” And then: “She’s a goddamn dead man’s
switch. A psychotic one. And she just lost her bodyguard. Don’t let her leave. Keep her safe.” Zev’s answer:
“It’s out of my control, Carl.”

Zev adds only: "We learned something new the moment the dog died. There's so much more, but now is not
the time."

“The Eulogist” is the name for the AI of the centre system, and it runs through the whole book
attached to the residual **Agatha**, the Nebular cult and the Open Intellect — see
[Game Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md). The book never states what Lucia’s alliance with it consists of,
who "Diah" is, or how children came to be inside her head. Books 5 and 8 do supply pieces about
"Alexandro" and the dogs; they are gathered in [Who Is "Alexandro"?](#who-is-alexandro) below.

### Afterwards

The conscription only runs to the end of the floor. Lucia spends the rest of the book in an interview room
with the liaisons — Florin, who has appointed himself her keeper, hopes “they talk her into taking a deal at
the beginning of the 10th. If not, I will volunteer to keep her safe.” Jurgen the barbarian, whose pregnant
wife Heidi is in the same kinder facility, attaches himself to the same job: “if Lucia’s fate is tied to that
kinder facility where they’re keeping all the kids and the pregnant women, he’ll die protecting the girl.”

The last sight of her on the ninth floor is in the rubble of Larracos:

> Lucia Mar was here, sitting on the ground, sobbing. Florin, Jurgen, and Imani surrounded her, talking.
>
> They were trying to convince her to take a deal. I hoped it would work.

It does not, quite:

> Florin had talked Lucia Mar into partying with him. He said she’d agreed to take a deal, but she’d been
> sketchy.
>
> None of us believed she actually would. And as a result, he would not take one, either.

## Book 8: Floors 10 and 11

With both dogs dead, Lucia is still partied with [Florin](florin.md) and still refuses every deal. Several
personalities now surface without the dogs: Carl notes "Lucia Prime's psycho personality had wandered back
into the body," and on Floor 11 that "Lucia Prime was not currently in her body, and the girl who was in it
had drawn a picture of her grandfather and her cat. She was from the country of Andorra." The Spanish-speaking
Lucia, the scared child with the different accent, Jill (Dutch, ten), Diah, Azra and Yaz are all separate
voices. Nothing in Book 8 mentions Alexandro.

### Chalchiuhtlicue speaks through her

On Floor 10, Lucia whispers in Carl's ear:

> "Chalchiuhtlicue is here. She's new to this place, but she is here. She doesn't need Cici or Gus like the
> Residual and the Primal did. She keeps telling us that she is kind and that she will protect us. But she
> says the kindest thing may be letting us die. She says we are vessels in the river, just as you are, Carl.
> We are drowning together, you and me and all the others. But more are coming. The veins are open, she
> says. All the trapped are coming, Carl. All seek the prize on the twelfth floor. Not just her kind. The
> real, the imagined, and the enslaved. All gather, focused on the Ascendency. It's not a game like they told
> me. But it's not a game like they told you, either. Not anymore."

Her demeanour then changes. "The presence is gone. The Scavenger's Daughter shudders in fear." The new voice
says: "My name is Azra. Yaz is here, too. She's crying. That goddess. She's talking to her now, telling
her that you used a spell that our father gave you to win your races." Azra's father is **Mehmet Or**,
crawler #3,756,330, the Istanbul bowling-alley owner whose note in *Carl's Book of Boom* gave Carl the
*Bumper* spell. His note says he took a deal and believes his daughters are in "that place where they're
keeping the children." Carl tells her he is safe and will see her soon. Azra, crying: "The angel lady says it might be better if we all die. She keeps talking
about people coming and taking our bodies." Asked who, she sees Florin and bolts.

[Chalchiuhtlicue](#who-is-chalchiuhtlicue) is the only named entity in Lucia's group besides the children,
and Samantha confirms her backstory in the next scene.

### The Floor 10 apartments

During the Floor 10 race, Florin reports: "Just went through our assigned spot on the sixth floor, and it
was Lucia's dad's house. Lucia is here, too, just sitting on the ground watching TV, laughing. The real
Lucia completely shut down there for a second." The AI explains in the same moment that the building's
apartments are what-if scenarios built from "atoms from the original" people. Lucia's father's house is
one of them.

### The child at the Floor 12 portal

On Floor 11, as the stairwells open, Lucia stands in front of one holding the unconscious Florin:

> But then, peeking out from behind her legs, was another person. It . . . it was a child. A girl, maybe
> five years old. She was too far away to examine. She held on to the side of Lucia's leg.

Carl shouts and nobody listens. Imani did not see it and guesses a summon spell, and Elle did not see it
either. The three step through. See [Book 9: What We Know](../chapter-summaries/book-09-what-we-know.md#the-mystery-child-who-is-the-girl-with-lucia)
for the competing readings. The AI's closing montage on Floor 11 includes Lucia by name, and separately,
among the figures even Prime Minister Victory cannot identify, "a human child."

## Who Is "Alexandro"?

Everything the books say about Alexandro, in order. The first line is Agatha's friend's own words; the rest
is what the wiki can piece together and is labelled where it is inference.

1. **Book 6 epilogue.** Agent 21, Agatha's friend of the Residual faction that serves the Eulogist, tells
   Agatha she "killed one and infiltrated the other, abandoning my previous shell" of two
   **Borant-employed humans** who landed on the **Mumbai site** with a forward team. "She didn't split, which
   meant the new host would soon be fully under her control." Her final message: "My name is no longer
   Parvati. I am now Alexandro." The epilogue flash message tells assets near Mumbai they "will receive
   separate orders," unlike everyone else.
2. **Mumbai is where the kinder facility is.** Book 6 elsewhere says crawlers disqualified for pregnancy
   are "exported to the Kinder Facility located in the former city of Mumbai, India." The Book 6
   epilogue's Syndicate Council scene shows humans "constantly battling the security forces, attempting to
   break into the kinder facility."
3. **Book 5.** Lucia tells an invisible "he" that "he says I should finish you off," and (per Donut) names him
   as "Alexandro... her youth assistant." Mordecai explains youth assistants look after children who
   are taken out of the dungeon. Florin reports that the dog and Alexandro are what let something "control
   her from afar." Zev refuses to explain.
4. **Book 7.** Diah says "Lucia is with Alexandro. And Alexandro is only here when the dogs are close." When the
   dogs wander off, "Lucia fades, and one of us takes over."
5. **Book 8.** Lucia says the goddess "doesn't need Cici or Gus like the Residual and the Primal did." The
   past tense and Alexandro's absence from Book 8 suggest that the dog-linked connection ended with Gustavo
   3, which would also fit Zev's "we learned something new the moment the dog died."

*Inference, not stated in the text:* Alexandro's host looks like a Borant youth assistant from the Mumbai
site, and the "specific wing" of children Zev describes is the wing that host worked in. If so, the
"glitch" that put Lucia in the crawl, and the 120,000 children's lives that now make her unkillable, are
tied to a Residual of Agatha's faction placing itself next to the children at the start. The book never
states this. The best case for it is that it explains why a child crawler plus a youth assistant would be
in the dungeon at all (Mordecai: "Kids shouldn't be in here. Youth assistants shouldn't be here"), why
Zev will not discuss it, why the AI is "unable to extract her," and how an agent whose kind "rarely made it
past the fourth or fifth floor" could last as long as Lucia has. Agatha's own epilogue plan depends on one
of her people lasting "long enough."

The unanswered parts are who "the Primal" in Lucia's Floor 10 line is, what the "bridge" she wants to cross is,
and what "people coming and taking our bodies" refers to.

## Who Is Chalchiuhtlicue?

A minor Aztec-analogue goddess who first appears in Book 8, on Floor 10, speaking through Lucia. What the
text says about her:

- **Prepotente:** the Aztec goddess of the same name. "She was considered benevolent, but the Aztecs also
  practiced human sacrifice. She is the originator of an Aztec flood myth because she cried for many days."
- **Rosetta:** "Chachi, as Samantha calls her, isn't a well-known one. She's not ever sponsored as far as I
  can remember." A goddess of childbirth and women, but also of water, fire and harvests. Such catchall
  deities "never do well in the Ascendency game because their powers are comparatively weak."
- **Samantha:** green skirt, talks funny, cries a lot. She claims to protect children, "but the second
  something bad or inconvenient happens, she's like 'We need to sacrifice a seven-year-old to make things
  right.'"
- **Carl:** concludes she "appeared to be one of the ones who'd escaped the dungeon. And she was in there
  with the kids stuck inside of Lucia Mars's head."
