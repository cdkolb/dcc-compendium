# Dmitri and Maxim Popov

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Two crawlers sharing one body
    - **Race:** Nodling — a two-to-six-headed race; splitting apart on death spawns a new
      toddler-then-adult creature per head
    - **Class:** Dmitri: Illusionist → **Visionary** (Book 5 specialization). Maxim: Bogatyr
      (melee)
    - **Level:** Ranked 5th on the Floor 6 leaderboard as a combined entity
    - **Status:** **Ineligible** — both are pre-collapse children, rescued from the dungeon as
      newborn nodling yearlings after their shared adult body dies in Book 5
    - **Notable spells/abilities:** A shared, flaming meteor-hammer melee weapon
    - **Notable items:** None named individually

## Overview

Dmitri and Maxim Popov are fraternal twin brothers whose game guide steered them into the Nodling
race specifically because it grants an "extra life" on death — not warning them that doing so
would fuse the two into a single, permanently shared two-headed body. Dmitri is the gentler,
rounder-faced half (an Illusionist/Visionary mage); Maxim is the more chiseled, blunt melee half
(a Bogatyr). Both are minors: Dmitri had just adopted a kitten, Kapitan Whiskers, the day before
the collapse.

## Role in the Story — Book 5

The Popov brothers rank 5th on the Floor 6 leaderboard and share an autograph table with Carl at
CrawlCon, where Dmitri repeatedly expresses a wish to meet Donut in person. They fight alongside
Carl and the rest of the top-50 at the Butcher's Masquerade, and are killed instantly during the
climactic battle when a flaming, magically hurled elf mount skewers their shared body.

Per Nodling racial mechanics, their death splits them apart into two separate infant bodies —
newborn **nodling yearlings**. Carl and Katia dig the pair out of the battlefield's wreckage
before anyone can loot them; their status flashes to **Ineligible** and they're automatically
teleported to a kinder facility on Earth's surface, revealed by Zev to have been engineered
deliberately: their original game guide chose the race specifically to guarantee this escape
route, using the dungeon's ban on pregnant or child crawlers as a loophole to free them.
