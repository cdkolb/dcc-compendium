# Level 3

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Entrances down to Floor 4:** 18,750 (halved again from Floor 3's own 37,500 entrances in)
    - **Time limit to find the next level down:** 8 days
    - **Crawlers entering the floor:** "Just over 700,000"
    - **Floor type:** The first **Over City** floor — an urban, village-and-ruins setting themed
      as a destroyed volcano-kingdom (see [Over City Floors](../mechanics/over-city-floors.md));
      much smaller and denser than Floors 1-2, roughly the size of Arizona
    - **Milestone:** The "training levels" officially end here — crawlers choose a permanent
      [race and class](../mechanics/race-and-class-selection.md) for the first time, quests and
      Elites are introduced, and the in-game leaderboard populates for the first time

## Arrival

Floor 3 opens with a system broadcast: "Welcome Crawlers to the third floor! We have just over
700,000 crawlers remaining. You will have eight full days to complete this floor." Mordecai's own
greeting signals the real shift: "The training levels have concluded. Now the games may truly
begin." Almost immediately, Carl, Donut, and Mongo are taken into a private guildhall session for
[race and class selection](../mechanics/race-and-class-selection.md) — Carl becomes a **Primal
Compensated Anarchist**; Donut deliberately picks **Former Child Actor** for its hidden
[Manager](../mechanics/managers.md) benefit, letting [Mordecai](../characters/mordecai.md)
accompany them outside guildhalls for the rest of the series.

## Layout & Notable Areas

- **Grimaldi's Traveling Circus** — a cataclysm-warped circus troupe run by [Ringmaster
  Grimaldi](../bosses/ringmaster-grimaldi.md); site of the [Heather the Bear](../bosses/heather-the-bear.md)
  fight and the "Show Must Go On" quest
- **The Medium Skyfowl Settlement** — a skyfowl-run town secretly controlled by [Miss
  Quill](../bosses/miss-quill.md) from behind a dead figurehead magistrate; includes a
  skyfowl-only shopping district and the settlement's guard depot
- **The [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md)** — the cross-instance
  nightclub/bazaar first teased in Book 1, fully explored here, including its rival **Club
  Vanquisher** and its own casino (not yet open on this floor)
- **The Apito Education Center** — home base of the **[201st Security
  Group](../dungeon-factions/index.md)**, a city-elf militia secretly directed by Miss Quill

## Stairwells

### General mechanics

- Floor 3 continues the halving pattern: 37,500 entrances in, 18,750 entrances down to Floor 4.
- Multiple stairwells exist across the floor's villages and ruins; crawlers share their locations
  informally, leaving written notes in bars.
- Some stairwells are quest-gated and stay invisible on the map until unlocked — Mordecai
  confirms "many of the stairwells from now on would only become visible to us after we completed
  quests," unlike the always-visible (if fogged) stairwells of Floors 1-2.

### The stairwell Carl, Donut & Katia used

Two separate stairwells come up in Book 2. The first appears in a park at Grimaldi's circus
immediately after Carl resolves the [Ringmaster Grimaldi](../bosses/ringmaster-grimaldi.md)
confrontation — it had been hidden underneath the circus the whole time, only revealed once that
quest completed. This is **not** the one the party actually uses.

The real descent happens later, during the climactic escape from the wild-magic explosion tied to
[Carl's Doomsday Scenario](../items/carls-doomsday-scenario.md). A location scouted in advance by
fellow crawler Daniel Bautista — a stairwell near a small, decrepit building elsewhere in the
settlement — becomes the evacuation point once escalating "precursor bursts" start stripping the
fleeing crowd of magical gear. With the Desperado Club (a safer alternate escape) closed off, Carl
funnels everyone without club access to this stairwell instead. Carl, Donut, Katia, and a mixed
group of other crawlers descend together with roughly three minutes to spare before the main
detonation.

## Quests

Floor 3 is the first floor to introduce a formal Quests tab (see
[Quests](../mechanics/quests.md) for the full mechanic). Both of Book 2's quests are worth
their exact wording — completion is judged on the literal text, not on doing what seems
intuitively "right."

### The Show Must Go On

> "You've discovered the remnants of an ancient circus. Hundreds of years after the toxic cloud
> that devastated the Over City, this traveling circus endures. But instead of joy and laughter
> and cotton candy, it now delivers devastation and pain. Their cotton candy is probably really
> gross by now, too. Find out why this circus still exists and put an end to its reign of
> terror."

- **Trigger:** Walking into Grimaldi's transformed circus grounds
- **Pass condition:** Ambiguous by design — Carl assumes it requires killing [Ringmaster
  Grimaldi](../bosses/ringmaster-grimaldi.md), but the quest completes the moment he resolves
  the confrontation through negotiation instead, suggesting "put an end to its reign of terror"
  didn't require a kill after all
- **Reward:** A Gold Quest Box, later upgraded to **Platinum** via the Hadji achievement
  (awarded for solving it in an unusually creative way) — contents: 5,000 gold, a skill potion,
  and a pair of [Fang Caps of the Expectorating Tizheruk](../items/fang-caps-of-the-expectorating-tizheruk.md)
- **Fail state:** Never tested in the text — Carl completes it on the first attempt

### The Sex Workers Who Fell from the Heavens

> "Prostitutes. Escorts. Street Walkers. Ladies of the Night. It doesn't matter how you describe
> them because these chicks are dead. Very dead. And every morning, several more of them appear
> scattered throughout the village. Nobody knows who they are or where they come from. Find out
> why."

- **Trigger:** [GumGum](../characters/supporting-cast.md) personally asks Carl and Donut for help
- **Pass condition:** The literal text only requires discovering the *cause* — not stopping it,
  saving anyone, or punishing whoever's responsible. This becomes a major plot point: after
  killing [Miss Quill](../bosses/miss-quill.md), the quest doesn't complete, because Carl and
  Donut haven't yet learned *why* the killings are happening — only after extracting a full
  confession from a dying [Krasue](../mobs/krasue.md) does the quest actually resolve
- **Reward:** A Silver Quest Box (notably lower-tier than "The Show Must Go On," since it starts
  with no known Elites or City Bosses attached) — its resolution also directly triggers the
  event-quest chain that produces [Carl's Doomsday Scenario](../items/carls-doomsday-scenario.md)
- **Side effect:** Killing Miss Quill turns out to have accidentally sabotaged a *different*
  crawler's separate, unrelated quest that also required her alive — the system awards Carl that
  other crawler's promised reward instead (another Silver Quest Box) via a one-time-only
  exploit-achievement, with an explicit warning that doing it a second time gets him reported

### The Fools Who Broke the Glass (Group Event Quest)

> "THIS IS A GROUP QUEST. All Crawlers currently within the 45 square kilometer blast radius
> will receive this quest... A while back a certain NPC started casting a very powerful spell,
> a spell so potent, that it had to be completed by a future generation... There's going to be
> an explosion. The epicenter of the blast is marked on your map. Every crawler within the
> designated blast area is fucked. The object of this quest is simple. Unfuck yourself. Don't
> die."

- **Trigger:** [Remex](../mobs/remex.md)'s death destabilizes a generations-old spell tied to
  every skyfowl/chickadee NPC in the area, threatening a 45-square-kilometer magical explosion
  in 7 minutes
- **Host:** Carl, Donut, and Katia — automatically designated and locked in with **no option to
  opt out**, even though Katia has nowhere safe to go (she lacks Desperado Club access, the
  only nearby escape route outside the blast radius)
- **Pass condition:** Survive. Every crawler in the blast radius receives the same quest and the
  same binary win condition
- **Fail state:** Death, or (implicitly) remaining in the blast zone after the 60-second window
  to reach safety — all safe rooms in the quest zone lock down once that window closes
- **Promised reward:** A Platinum Quest Box for every survivor
- **What actually happened:** See [Quests: Case Study](../mechanics/quests.md#case-study-the-financial-near-catastrophe)
  for the full story — a random other survivor's Bandit achievement pushed 83 crawlers' boxes to
  Celestial tier, and Borant's emergency veto voided every prize from the quest, including
  Carl, Donut, and Katia's own, despite them doing the actual work as Hosts

## Notable Events

- Carl and Donut choose a permanent [race and class](../mechanics/race-and-class-selection.md)
  for the first time; Mordecai becomes their [Manager](../mechanics/managers.md).
- Carl solo-kills [Heather the Bear](../bosses/heather-the-bear.md) after Tsarina Signet forces
  the fight by cursing Donut asleep.
- Carl resolves the [Ringmaster Grimaldi](../bosses/ringmaster-grimaldi.md) confrontation through
  negotiation rather than combat, securing a recurring guest-star deal and a hidden stairwell.
- Carl and Donut investigate a string of krasue killings, culminating in the death of [Miss
  Quill](../bosses/miss-quill.md) and the exposure of her generations-old genocide plot.
- Miss Quill's husband [Remex](../mobs/remex.md) survives as an unstable power source, triggering
  a mass event-quest explosion; Carl creates [Carl's Doomsday
  Scenario](../items/carls-doomsday-scenario.md) while containing it.
- [Katia](../characters/katia.md), a Doppelganger sent by Hekla's group, joins the party
  permanently.
- Carl publicly calls for the Skull Empire's citizens to rise up; the resulting retaliation chain
  kills the singer Manasa, then Prince Maestro and Queen Consort Ugloo.
- Carl, Donut, Katia, and a mixed group of crawlers flee the event-quest explosion and descend
  early to Floor 4 (see Stairwells above).
- Carl and Donut land on the leaderboard for the first time (see Leaderboard below).

## Leaderboard

Revealed by Odette near the end of Book 2 as a "tentative draft" — not yet official, but the
first time the leaderboard mechanic actually appears (it doesn't populate until a floor's
collapse, per the [Boss Tier Ladder](../mechanics/boss-tier-ladder.md) page's note on the
mechanic's absence in Floors 1-2). The overall ranking formula is never fully explained: per
Odette, "a mix of views, favorites, levels, and money earned." The numeric column itself,
though, has a specific, confirmed meaning — when Carl asks about it directly, Odette clarifies:
"That's just the bounty. It's how much other crawlers get if they kill you."

| 3 | Prepotente | Caprid | Forsaken Aerialist | 27 | 400,000 |
| 4 | Florin | Crocodilian | Shotgun Messenger | 24 | 300,000 |
| 5 | Miriam Dom | Human | Shepherd | 27 | 200,000 |
| 6 | [Carl](../characters/carl.md) | Primal | Compensated Anarchist | 27 | 100,000 |
| 7 | [Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md) | Cat | Former Child Actor | 26 | 100,000 |
| 8 | Ifechi | Human | Physicker | 18 | 100,000 |
| 9 | Li Jun | Human | Street Monk | 25 | 100,000 |
| 10 | Mrs. Elle McGibbons | Frost Maiden | Blizzardmancer | 13 | 100,000 |

A few notes worth calling out:

- **#10 is a real gut-punch reveal** — Mrs. Elle McGibbons, the dementia-affected, wheelchair-bound
  elderly resident from Book 1's Meadow Lark evacuee group, went from Level 1 to Level 13 and a
  top-10 spot entirely off-page, since Carl hasn't spoken to Brandon An in days by this point.
- **#9, Li Jun**, is a crawler Carl recognizes and had personally helped save from troglodytes and
  Brindle Grubs during a prior guest appearance on *Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem*.
- Bounties 6 through 10 are tied at exactly 100,000, suggesting the bottom half of the top 10 is a
  rougher/less precise cut than the top 5 — and that the leaderboard rank and bounty amount track
  each other closely even though the text never states they're formally the same number.
- Odette privately tells Carl she thinks the list itself is "bunk," and that she and Donut should
  rank 2nd and 3rd — a reminder that even in-universe, nobody fully trusts the leaderboard.
- Odette teases two unnamed "twin" crawlers as rising stars for Floor 4, not yet on the list.
