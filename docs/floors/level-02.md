# Level 2

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Entrances down to Floor 3:** 37,500 (halved again from Floor 2's own 75,000 entrances in)
    - **Time limit to find the next level down:** 6 days — the system explicitly speeds this up
      to "the minimum legally allowed by the rules" after crawler numbers came in lower than
      projected
    - **Live broadcast:** The first floor where crawler stats go fully public — Views, Followers,
      and Favorites all switch on the moment the "Welcome to the second floor" announcement ends
    - **Crawlers entering the floor:** ~1,300,000 ("just under 1.3 million")
    - **Crawlers remaining as of Book 1's end:** 1,033,992 (a mid-floor checkpoint — Book 1 ends
      before Floor 2 collapses, so no final count/total-lost figure exists yet)
    - **Leaderboard:** Not available — the in-game leaderboard explicitly doesn't populate until
      Floor 3 collapses

## Arrival

Floor 2 opens with a system-wide broadcast right after Carl and Donut clear [Level 1](level-01.md):

> "Welcome to the second floor. Congratulations to all of you. We have just under 1.3 million
> crawlers still in the game. That number is slightly lower than our projections, so we are
> speeding up the second floor timer to the minimum legally allowed by the rules. You have six
> days to find a staircase down. Once again, we urge you all to wait until the last possible
> moment to descend."

The same announcement flips on public viewership: "As soon as this announcement ends, you will
find that follows and favorites will be turned on for our viewers." Structurally, Floor 2 is
described as similar to Floor 1 but more reinforced and visibly cracked/battle-damaged.

## Layout & Notable Areas

- **Storm-shelter Safe Room** — their first Floor 2 base; includes a mailbox that automatically
  enrolls Donut in a spellbook-of-the-month club, netting her the [Second Chance](../spells/second-chance.md) tome.
- **[Rev-Up Moonshine Company](../dungeon-factions/rev-up-moonshine.md) distillery** — a
  pyramid-scheme moonshine operation staffed by Clurichauns and Laminak fairies, run by the
  [Krakaren Clone](../bosses/krakaren-clone.md).
- **Kobold fighting-pit arena** — an underground animal-fighting ring holding caged Danger
  Dingoes and the Frenzied Gerbil boss [Ralph](../bosses/ralph.md); clearing it unlocks an
  adjoining **pet reward room**, where Donut chooses the Mongoliensis that becomes
  [Mongo](../characters/mongo.md).
- **The stairwell chamber** — a large round room built around the hole down to Floor 3; used
  first by [Team Meadow Lark](../characters/supporting-cast.md)'s evacuees, later the site of the
  [Rage Elemental](../mobs/rage-elemental.md) fight, and finally where Carl and Donut themselves
  descend (see Stairwells below).

## Stairwells

### General mechanics

- Floor 2 continues the halving pattern: 75,000 entrances in, 37,500 entrances down to Floor 3.
- The timer is explicitly rushed to its legal minimum (6 days) because the season's crawler count
  came in under projection — a rare case of the system stating its own manipulation outright.
- A later patch note announces: "any non-sapient mobs who happen upon a stairwell will not be
  disintegrated" — a direct response to Carl and Donut exploiting the stairwell's disintegration
  effect to destroy the Rage Elemental without granting it a credited kill.

### The stairwell Carl & Donut used

Unlike Floor 1, no boss guards this stairwell. Carl and Donut find "the quadrant with the
stairwell" early in their time on Floor 2 and immediately begin escorting Team Meadow Lark toward
it; the evacuees descend to Floor 3 well before Carl and Donut do. The same stairwell chamber
later becomes the site of the Rage Elemental fight — the two lure the wounded elemental into the
stairwell's hole with a final bomb, dissolving it entirely (denying it a clean kill credit, and
prompting the anti-exploit patch note above).

Carl, Donut, and their newly-bonded pet [Mongo](../characters/mongo.md) camp in this same
stairwell chamber for the last seven hours of the floor's timer (deliberately waiting until the
recommended six-hour mark). Just before they descend, the mysterious **Agatha** reappears —
having already used the same stairs herself — long enough for Carl to realize the untraceable
Valtay Corporation tech in his inventory's History tab must have come from her looted shopping
cart. Carl, Donut, and Mongo then descend to Floor 3 together.

## Notable Events

- Carl and Donut's stats go fully public for the first time (see Arrival above).
- They adopt a strict "always destroy the corpse" policy after learning how the Brindle Grub
  escalation chain works.
- They survive the [Krakaren Clone](../bosses/krakaren-clone.md) by explosion rather than direct
  combat, inside the Rev-Up distillery.
- They defeat [Ralph](../bosses/ralph.md) and adopt [Mongo](../characters/mongo.md) from the
  resulting pet reward room.
- Carl publicly humiliates Prince Maestro of the Skull Empire ("Pork Boy") during a hostile
  interview on *Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem*, sparking a viral incident.
- A [Rage Elemental](../mobs/rage-elemental.md) — summoned as a bathroom-etiquette punishment —
  kills nurse Yolanda Martinez during the Meadow Lark evacuation; Carl and Donut destroy it via
  the stairwell-disintegration exploit.
- Carl discovers unexplained Valtay Corporation tech in his inventory's History tab, tied to
  Agatha's shopping cart.
- Carl, Donut, and Mongo descend to Floor 3, where they'll choose a race and class for the first
  time.
