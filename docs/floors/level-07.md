# Level 7

!!! warning "This is a stub, and deliberately so"
    Floor 7 lasted roughly **ten minutes** of in-world time. [Prepotente](../characters/prepotente.md)
    shattered the floor's starting chamber before the rules announcement even finished, dumping
    every crawler straight through to Floor 8. Almost none of the floor's actual content — the
    maze, its monsters, its bosses, its loot — was ever seen by anybody. Most of the Quick Facts
    fields below are therefore minimal or not applicable, not missing.

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Crawlers entering the floor:** not stated. The floor's welcome broadcast skipped the
      remaining-crawler line entirely. The next confirmed count is **38,532** at the opening of
      [Level 8](level-08.md)
    - **Time limit:** **20 Days** to level collapse, per the welcome broadcast. The floor survived
      about **ten minutes** of it
    - **Structure:** A single, enormous sealed maze — "several hundred kilometers of twisting and
      turning tubes." Crawlers were held in individual glass **Pole Position Chambers** stacked in
      a corkscrew around a vertical shaft of doorless, round stairwell portals leading to Floor 8
    - **Deaths:** **Zero.** Confirmed in Book 6 — "Without anyone actually dying on the seventh,
      the dungeon had some catching up to do"
    - **Floor spellbook:** None delivered. Donut's saferoom mailbox never produced the usual
      book-of-the-floor prize, and Mordecai's verdict was "You snooze you lose"
    - **Carl's standing:** Enters ranked **#3** with a bounty of **1,600,000 gold**; receives a
      Platinum Venison Box on arrival
    - **Theme:** "The Great Race" — built to mirror a well-known in-universe fairytale of the same
      name, exactly and fatally

!!! quote "AI Commentary (in-voice, written for this wiki — not a book excerpt)"
    "Twenty days of maze. Hundreds of kilometers of tube. Years of engineering. One goat with a
    hammer. We are not going to be talking about this floor again."

## Arrival

Carl, Donut, and Prepotente descend from [Level 6](level-06.md) — see that page's
[Stairwells](level-06.md#stairwells) section for the Floor 6 side of this transition — and land
directly inside a **Pole Position Chamber**:

> "Welcome, Crawler to the Seventh floor. 'The Great Race.' ... Time to Level Collapse: 20 Days.
> Leaderboard rank: 3. Bounty: 1,600,000 gold ... Entering Pole Position Chamber. The race will
> commence shortly. Stay tuned for the rules announcement in ten minutes."

The chamber is a featureless glass box roughly the size of an apartment living room — Donut's
immediate reaction is "Carl! We're in a fish tank!" Three walls are solid; the fourth is thick
vertical glass bars opening onto a tunnel that curves away downward like a waterslide. A gentle
tug pulls everything inside toward the bars.

The system immediately tried to lock the occupants down — Peace-Bonded, Frozen, Paralyzed, Slowed,
teleported, placed under an **Administrative Hold**, and finally four armed gnolls materialised in
the chamber. Every single attempt was negated, one after another, by AI resolution; the gnolls
popped out of existence within seconds. Borant knew what Prepotente intended and was structurally
unable to stop him.

## Layout & Notable Areas

- **The Pole Position Chambers** — one glass capsule per crawler (or party), arranged in a
  corkscrewing spiral stack. Carl, Donut and Prepotente were at the very bottom step of the spiral.
  Occupants could see neighbouring capsules above and to the side; Donut believed she spotted
  [Agatha](../species/gondii-residuals.md) and her shopping cart in one of them
- **The stairwell column** — the glowing shaft at the centre of the spiral, which turned out to be
  a vertical string of Floor 8 stairwell portals. No stairs, no doors; round portals enterable from
  any direction. They sat directly behind the back wall of every capsule
- **The maze** — never entered. Per the aborted rules announcement, each capsule was to slide to a
  starting chute and release its crawler into a system of branching tubes. Nodes offered multiple
  onward paths (sometimes hundreds), each labelled with a danger level and distance; pitstop
  saferooms sat in the nodes; backtracking was forbidden; some paths required solving maths
  problems or puzzles. A path closed once ten crawlers or one full party had used it. Each major
  node held one **zero tube** — a danger-free route, usable exactly once, ever

## Monsters (Level 7 area)

None recorded. No crawler reached the maze, so no Floor 7 mob or boss was ever fought, described,
or catalogued. Carl's achievement list from the floor includes **"Pacifist!" — "You survived an
entire floor without hurting a single, poor mob."**

## Quests

None issued. The floor collapsed before the rules announcement finished, and no floor quest,
world quest, or regional quest was ever handed out.

## Stairwells

### The fairytale, and the flaw

Prepotente worked the floor out in advance. The Floor 7 boon **Seventh Floor Pole Position** had
been on sale at the Butcher's Masquerade prize counter for thirty hands, and he bought it. He then
identified what the floor actually was:

> "This level is called the Great Race which also happens to be the name of a well-known fairytale
> popular in the universe. Based on some additional clues, I determined the seventh floor is indeed
> based on this tale."

His sources were a muted interview appearance in which the host and Cascadia discussed the floor
in front of him, and — decisively — the description text on his late mother [Miriam
Dom's](../characters/miriam-dom.md) **Boots of Kiznet's Final Flight**, an item description 503%
longer than average and therefore, to Prepotente, an obvious signal. The boots told the whole tale.

The fairytale itself, in his summary: a boy trapped in a maze where every tunnel seals behind him
once taken, presented with thousands of paths, forever choosing the wrong one, dying, being
resurrected by a god and starting again. Prepotente describes it as a mash-up of the Achilles,
Sisyphus and Wu Gang stories. In the tale the boy spent *years* trying to crack the walls of the
impossible maze, finally succeeding at the resurrection chamber at the very entrance.

Borant's engineers built the floor to mirror that story exactly — including the part where the
maze can be broken from the starting chamber. Structurally, the entire level behaved like a
**Prince Rupert's drop** at enormous scale: enormously tough almost everywhere, and catastrophically
self-destructing if nicked at the single correct point.

### The shattering

With about five minutes on the clock and the rules announcement still playing, Prepotente splashed
a potion onto the back wall of the capsule, took a thermometer-like instrument to find the spot,
pushed **The Enchanted Stick of Cascadia's Screams** halfway into the softened glass, and gave it
one very small tap with a ball-peen hammer.

Cascadia broke into the announcement to beg him personally not to: *"Godsdamnit, Prepotente. Don't
do it. Please. I worked so hard to make this floor happen."*

A lightning pattern of fissures spread. Then the wall shattered — and not just that wall. **Every**
wall on the floor detonated into fine dust simultaneously, and every crawler still waiting in a
capsule was sucked into the Floor 8 stairwells at once. The last thing Carl heard was Cascadia
screaming over the dungeon PA.

Carl and Donut landed in the same style of inter-floor globe chamber they had used between Floors
4 and 5, where Donut — now squad leader — chose the Floor 8 starting region. She was aiming for the
Bahamas, and picked Cuba instead.

## Notable Events

- **Donut briefly becomes a Viper Queen.** Given a class choice inside the capsule with no chat
  access and no time, she picked without reading it. She kept the class for only a few minutes, but
  carried its **Venom Spitter** skill (level 3) forward into Floor 8 as a permanent ability — see
  the [Spells overview](../spells/index.md).
- **Mordecai spends the floor as a slime.** His between-floors form reroll gave him no arms at all:
  *"It's better than the form I'd taken for the ten minutes the seventh floor was open. I was a
  slime. I had no goddamned arms."*
- **No floor prizes.** Donut's book-of-the-floor spellbook never arrived, and she spent part of
  Floor 8 demanding Carl's attorney sue over it.
- **Prepotente leaves the party** in the capsule, seconds before breaking the floor, handing party
  leadership to Donut.
- **The engineers take the blame.** Relaying what **Mistress Tiatha** (Elle McGibbons' manager) had
  just told him, [Mordecai](../characters/mordecai.md) explains on Floor 8 that Prepotente is
  unlikely to be punished:

    > "This one is on the engineers. They fashioned that level so it was just like the fairytale.
    > The kid in the story did the exact same thing the goat did, but it took him thousands of
    > years to figure it out. They should never have built it that way."

    He notes this is not the first doomed-from-the-start floor — there was a bacterial-infection
    incident in a previous Borant season, and a floor where crawlers had to hand-build bridges out
    of materials that made it impossible — but it is the first he knows of that failed *this* fast.
    The Apothecary and the Plenty, who supplied Prepotente the means, land on the Valtay's blacklist;
    Mordecai reads the whole affair as a political manoeuvre to make money for those two sponsors at
    the [Borant Corporation's](../factions/borant-corporation.md) and the Syndicate's expense. The
    Syndicate pays for tunnel bandwidth in advance and gets no refund for a skipped floor.
- **Cascadia's floor.** Zev later confirms the seventh floor "was Cascadia's baby," pushed through
  by the Valtay; its destruction triggered a serious loss of nerve among the new showrunners, who
  stepped back from day-to-day production afterwards.
- **Skipped-floor compensation.** Loot quality across the board spikes at the start of Floor 8,
  which Mordecai attributes partly to the AI compensating for the missing floor. Carl's own haul
  includes a "Fuck This Floor" achievement — *"You went down the stairs less than an hour after you
  arrived upon the level"* — and a "Collective Bargaining!" achievement for more than 1,000 crawlers
  receiving the identical box at the identical moment.

## Leaderboard

No Floor 7 leaderboard was ever published. The only ranking data attached to the floor are the
personal readouts in the arrival broadcast — Carl at **#3** with a **1,600,000 gold** bounty — and
Donut's delighted discovery, inside the capsule, that she had reached **#1** for the first time.
Both figures were already stale by the time the next recap aired on Floor 8.
