# Level 6

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Crawlers entering the floor:** 85,223, per the welcome broadcast
    - **Hunters:** 360 registered bounty hunters held under a **30-hour Grace Period** in the
      city of **Zockau** before release — the floor's central twist. Registration stayed open
      through the Grace Period, and the count had swelled past **1,000** by the time they were
      actually released (peaking near 1,013 shortly before release, dropping to roughly 290 and
      then far fewer as crawlers hunted them down). Carl enters as reigning **#1 crawler**, bounty
      **3,000,000 gold**, making him the hunters' single biggest target
    - **Time limit:** **17 Days** to level collapse, stated at the very start of the floor
    - **Floor type:** A **"Scolopendra level"** — see [Zerzura](../spells/zerzura.md), which is
      relevant to how Carl's group eventually gets a group of endangered NPCs off the floor
    - **Theme:** "The Hunting Grounds" — the floor's entire premise is crawler-vs-crawler: once
      the Grace Period ends, the 360 hunters are released to hunt marked crawlers for bounty,
      layered on top of the usual monsters and bosses

## Arrival

The floor opens with Carl's stats broadcast front and center — 512 sextillion views, 142
quadrillion followers, and his standing as the #1-ranked crawler with a 3,000,000-gold bounty —
before the system confirms the floor's premise directly:

> "Welcome, Crawler, to the sixth floor. 'The Hunting Grounds' ... Remaining Crawlers: 85,223 ...
> Remaining Hunters: 360. Grace Period begins now. All hunters have been transferred to the city
> of Zockau. They will be released in 30 hours. Run."

The 360 hunters are professional bounty-killers rather than ordinary crawlers, released
specifically to hunt the floor's highest-value targets — with Carl, as the top-bountied crawler
on the leaderboard, marked as their most valuable kill from the opening bell.

## Layout & Notable Areas

- **Zockau** — the city where all the hunters are held during the Grace Period; also the site of
  Carl's early Desperado Club scenes and, via Orren's liaison office, his negotiations over the
  mercenary-bodyguard deal
- **[Point Mongo](../locations/point-mongo.md)** — a large Ursine settlement Carl and Donut
  personally conquer (killing mayor Elmer) right as the Grace Period ends; Donut renames it,
  rules it as its "leadership charm" holder, and has Mordecai train its funeral-bell guards to
  fight off hunter raids before the party moves on
- **[Fort Freedom](../locations/fort-freedom.md)** — the former Naiad royal palace, occupied by
  the corrupt Naiad Confederacy; the target of Part One of Signet's "Vengeance of the Daughter"
  quest, taken in an amphibious night assault (and a fight with the boss Claude Sludgington) —
  not itself part of the hunter-versus-crawler conflict
- **The Butcher's Masquerade** — the floor's signature Special Event, a mass costume ball
  weaponized against attending crawlers; see [Game Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md#the-butchers-masquerade-book-5)
- **[The High Elf Castle](../locations/high-elf-castle.md)** — Queen Imogen's stronghold, the
  last standing town in the party's area as the floor collapse approaches, and the eventual
  target of the [Zerzura](../spells/zerzura.md) cast (see Stairwells below)
- **The Dark Hive** — the [Dark Hive](../factions/dark-hive.md) faction's territory, home to
  [Vrah](../characters/vrah.md) and the mantis hive queen [Circe Took](../characters/circe-took.md)

## Monsters (Level 6 area)

See the [Mobs overview](../mobs/index.md#book-5-mob-catalog) for the full Floor 6 catalog. Notable
mechanics layered onto the floor's usual monsters include the **Odious Creepers** (mutated,
self-reassembling corpse-fragment monsters spawned by Big Tina and the "Creeping Apocalypse" world
quest below), the **Gehenna Bramble** — an unstoppable wall of thorned, undulating vines that
swallowed the entire map after that quest's deadline was missed — and a floor-wide **vampirism
curse** originating from Miriam Dom — see
[Game Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md#the-creeping-apocalypse-odious-creepers-book-5) for all three.

## Quests

Floor 6's structural "quest" is the **Settlement Conquest & Collar Charm** system rather than a
single world quest — see [Game Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md#settlement-conquest-the-collar-charm-book-5)
for the trigger/reward mechanics governing town takeovers on this floor. On top of that baseline
system, three major, book-specific quest threads run through the floor:

- **The Recital** — a personal quest to stop (or redeem) [Big Tina](../bosses/big-tina.md), a
  rampaging Allosaurus who was once Tina, an Ursine child whose father cut short her dance
  recital just before Scolopendra's attack transformed the village's women into dinosaurs.
  Resolved not by killing her but by reuniting her with her mother, Kiwi, and staging the recital
  she never got to finish during the Butcher's Masquerade talent show.
- **The Creeping Apocalypse** — a floor-wide world quest triggered by the death of the vampire
  Miriam Dom: every crawler and hunter must hunt down and kill 50 boss-tier Odious Creepers before
  sundown. Failing it (as happened here) triggers the punishing **Gehenna Bramble** effect above.
- **The Vengeance of the Daughter** — the elite Tsarina Signet's two-part quest chain: Part One is
  the assault on Fort Freedom against the Naiad Confederacy; Part Two is killing Signet's
  half-sister, Queen Imogen. Carl and Donut are contractually bound to help via Signet's licensed
  secondary show, and the questline's climax is deliberately folded into the Butcher's Masquerade
  itself (see Notable Events).

## Stairwells

### General mechanics

Floor 6 does not reveal a single, floor-wide stairwell rule the way earlier floors did (e.g. the
halving-entrance pattern, or Floor 5's four-castle bubble gating). Individual settlements each
gain a stairwell once their local boss is defeated or the settlement is otherwise conquered —
consistent with the Settlement Conquest mechanic above.

### The exit Carl, Donut & the High Elf castle group used

Two separate exits happen at the close of Floor 6, and they should not be conflated:

- **The High Elf castle group:** faced with losing a group of rescued changeling children to the
  collapsing floor, Carl's allies smuggle them into the castle during the Butcher's Masquerade and
  have **The Sledge** cast [Zerzura](../spells/zerzura.md) on the High Elf castle itself once he
  fights his way inside. The cast completes moments after Queen Imogen's death, during the
  final boss fight, and confirmed passengers include Sledge, the changeling children (including
  Bonnie), Kiwi (transformed back into an Ursine), Big Tina, and the reanimated "tattoo" companions
  of the late Tsarina Signet. Per Zerzura's own mechanics, Floor 6 being a Scolopendra level means
  this cast **skips floors entirely** — the vanished castle is confirmed to land on **Floor 9**,
  not Floor 7 or 8. Whether the castle group is struck by the spell's revenant curse on landing is
  not confirmed by anything read for this page — see [Zerzura](../spells/zerzura.md) for what is
  and isn't established.
- **Carl and Donut's own exit:** entirely separate from the castle. In the final chapter, they
  (with Prepotente) are pulled into a **Pole Position Chamber** — an individual glass "fish tank"
  capsule tethered to one of a corkscrewing stack of round, doorless portal-stairwells. Prepotente,
  having pieced together the next floor's theme from clues in his mother's item description,
  deliberately shatters the chamber's wall early, collapsing the whole holding structure and
  sucking every waiting crawler into the stairwells at once. Carl and Donut land in the transition
  chamber between floors and cross into **Floor 7, "The Great Race,"** with Carl re-ranked to
  **#3** on the leaderboard and a bounty of 1,600,000 gold.

## Notable Events

- Carl negotiates a mercenary-bodyguard deal with a kua-tin Borant admin, brokered by his attorney
  [Quasar](../characters/quasar.md), surrendering the
  [Gate of the Feral Gods](../items/gate-of-the-feral-gods.md) as collateral in exchange for
  protection — including "The Sledge," the mercenary who carries the
  [Zerzura](../spells/zerzura.md) spell.
- Carl infects [Vrah](../characters/vrah.md), a [Dark Hive](../factions/dark-hive.md) heir hunter,
  with an incurable venereal disease during a confrontation; Vrah is later killed at the Butcher's
  Masquerade, finished off by Mongo, Kiwi, and Samantha after Carl reanimates two of her own
  trophy-heads to buy the opening.
- The vampire [Miriam Dom](../characters/miriam-dom.md) dies by Carl's own hand (at her request, to
  save Prepotente and cure the floor's spreading vampirism curse), triggering the world quest "The
  Creeping Apocalypse" and, after its deadline is missed, the Gehenna Bramble that consumes the map.
- The floor-wide **Butcher's Masquerade** Special Event — nominally a sacred, no-violence peace
  ritual thrown by Queen Imogen — turns into a trap once Vrah's mother sabotages it from within,
  effectively becoming a weaponized ambush against the attending crawlers.
- [Circe Took](../characters/circe-took.md), the mantis hive queen, sponsors the manifestation of
  the forest goddess Diwata, who takes on Circe's own form to cure Vrah's disease by giving birth
  to a swarm of mantis nymphs; during the masquerade's final battle, Donut's maxed-out *Laundry Day*
  spell strips away Diwata's divine "armor," and the newly-exposed, mortal Circe is killed
  moments later — swarmed by her own children.
- [Queen Imogen](../characters/queen-imogen.md), the Floor 6 country boss and Tsarina Signet's
  half-sister, is killed (the finishing blow struck by Prepotente), unlocking the High Elf
  castle's stairwell.
- [Tsarina Signet](../characters/tsarina-signet.md) sacrifices herself mid-party — cutting her own
  throat to power an *Ink Marauder* spell — deliberately breaking the masquerade's peace seal so
  Carl's group can fight back; her mother's memory-golem and her fallen companions' "tattoo"
  warriors briefly manifest at full size to join the battle.
- [Big Tina](../bosses/big-tina.md) (Level 80 City Boss) is not killed outright; the Recital quest
  is resolved by reuniting her with her mother Kiwi (restored to Ursine form via a potion), and
  both are then swept away to Floor 9 along with the vanishing High Elf castle.
- With the floor collapsing, Carl's allies cast [Zerzura](../spells/zerzura.md) on the High Elf
  castle to save a group of rescued changeling children, including Bonnie, rather than let them be
  lost to the collapse.
- In the epilogue, Prepotente shatters his and Carl's shared Pole Position holding chamber ahead
  of schedule, collapsing the structure and launching every waiting crawler into the Floor 7
  stairwells simultaneously; Carl ends the floor ranked **#3** with a bounty of 1,600,000 gold.
