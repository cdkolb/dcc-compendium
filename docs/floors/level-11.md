# Level 11

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Crawlers entering the floor:** **23**, all that remain of the 5,501 who started Floor 10. See
      [Level 10](level-10.md) for the full heat-by-heat attrition
    - **Time limit:** **3 hours, 15 minutes**, per the arrival broadcast — the shortest "floor" in the
      series to date, by design: "this 11th floor is designed to go quick thanks to a few... loopholes"
    - **Carl's standing at arrival:** **Final Ranking: 2** (Donut: 3). The leaderboard itself is
      already suspended — "the leaderboard is suspended once less than 30 crawlers remain on the
      battlefield" — and the bounty system is described as "no longer active." Views/followers are
      dismissed outright: "Do you really care about this anymore? It's everybody. Literally
      everybody"
    - **Structure:** a **90-minute mandatory float-building phase** (5 teams, 5 themes — Resolution,
      Decision, Betrayal, Destruction, Vengeance), a slow parade past thousands of silent, masked
      **[Horribles](../mobs/the-horribles.md)**, and a single arena finale against the dungeon's
      final boss
    - **Setting:** a featureless yellow-brick road across misty grassland with no visible sun, ending
      at a stadium-sized arena centered on a massive white tree
    - **Exits:** a line of stairwell portals at the edge of the arena, opening only once the floor
      boss is defeated (or, per the AI, effectively defeated)

!!! quote "AI Commentary (in-voice, written for this wiki — not a book excerpt)"
    "Twenty-three of you made it. I built you a parade. I built you floats. I even let a fleshmancer
    borrow my voice to tell you where I came from. And you people still tried to one-up my finale.
    Rude. Absolutely rude. Ten out of ten, would traumatize again."

## Arrival

Floor 11 opens with a compressed system message rather than a scene:

> **Welcome, Crawler. Welcome to the Eleventh floor. Time to Level Collapse: 3 hours, 15 minutes...
> Remaining Crawlers: 23... Entering Parade Float Staging Area.**

!!! note "An unreconciled number in the Quasar interlude"
    The interlude immediately preceding this arrival has the lawyer Quasar watching the last
    crawlers through and reporting "*That makes 203 people who've made it to the eleventh floor...
    So far, 172 have taken deals.*" Neither figure reconciles with the system's own **23**, or with
    the 274 crawlers who started Heat 7. The book never explains the gap and never repeats the 203
    figure; the in-dungeon system message is the number every other page on this wiki uses. Treat
    Quasar's count as an unexplained discrepancy in an unedited draft, not as a competing fact.

The garage doubles as the staging area, and the announcement that follows — delivered by a visibly
terrified **Chaco**, "voluntold by the AI to be the Grand Master of the parade" — lays out the whole
floor in one breath: 90 minutes to build a parade float (or float procession), a parade route lined
with spectators, a judging stand for each theme's presentation, and an arena at the end populated
with "monsters you previously killed on the tenth level or their stand-ins," plus a floor boss. Chaco
loses both his wings to the AI mid-announcement for trying to editorialize.

### The floor's central premise

Twenty-three crawlers are split into **five float groups**, each merged from one or two garages and
assigned a theme: **Resolution** (Florin, Lucia Mar, Ajib's crew), **Decision** (a mixed group who
build a giant menu as a joke), **Betrayal** (Imani, Elle, Chris Andrews, and three Chinese crawlers),
**Destruction** (Makana, Sarah Hayse, and a second group of Chinese crawlers, riding an Abrams tank
and an RV), and **Vengeance** (Carl, Donut, Prepotente, and Jurgen). The parade itself is a vehicle
for the single biggest lore reveal in the series to date: the AI possesses the fleshmancer
**[Grigori the Placid](../characters/grigori.md)** to narrate, float by float, the true origin of the
Primals, the Eulogist, and the Apothecary — see [Game Mechanics: The True Origin of the Primals, the
Eulogist, and the
Apothecary](../mechanics/index.md#the-true-origin-of-the-primals-the-eulogist-and-the-apothecary-book-8)
for the full reveal, which this page only summarizes.

## Layout & Notable Areas

- **The Parade Float Staging Area** — the combined garages of the five float groups, given 90 minutes
  and full access to garage, inventory, and safe-room contents to build with. Carl and Donut's
  procession absorbs three looted NPC vehicles (an ice-cream truck, an airport stair-truck, a Toyota
  Tundra) plus the tapir Sweety, the bear Onikuma, and the dog Old Shuck, all decked out in flowers
  and Gurkha-class regalia
- **The parade route** — a straight, roughly three-kilometer yellow-brick road through featureless,
  fog-shrouded grassland with no visible light source, lined ten-deep on both sides by thousands of
  silent, masked **[Horribles](../mobs/the-horribles.md)** — each one a fragment of the AI/Eulogist's
  own fragmented memory, not a resurrected person or ordinary mob
- **The judging stands** — bleachers partway down the route where each float group performs; the
  audience seated here turns out to be masked, bound **stand-ins**, not Horribles — see [Game
  Mechanics: The "Stand-In" System](../mechanics/index.md#the-stand-in-system-book-8)
- **The arena** — a stadium-sized enclosure centered on a massive white tree, tall enough that
  **Krakaren Prime** perches on top of it unseen until the fight begins; the ground is packed dirt
  that turns to mud with blood within minutes
- **Club Scolopendra** — reached mid-fight via *Gloom Wraith Phase* straight through the physical body
  of Scolopendra herself, where Carl confronts Emberus in his human-sized form; see [Club
  Scolopendra](../locations/club-scolopendra.md)

## Quests

### The Nine-Tier Attack — paused, not resolved

Continued from [Floor 10](level-10.md#the-nine-tier-attack-part-one-opened-not-resolved-on-this-floor):
Attack 2 (Sin of Consumption) fires mid-arena-fight, and the standing warning that killing or
abandoning Scolopendra triggers every remaining attack at once holds right up through her "defeat."
The party sidesteps the trigger entirely by transforming her into a Level 1 party member rather than
killing or abandoning her — see [Game Mechanics: Scolopendra's Nine-Tier
Attack](../mechanics/index.md#scolopendras-nine-tier-attack-book-8) for the full mechanic and the
biscuit resolution.

### Defeat the floor boss — Complete (ambiguously)

No named quest text is given beyond Chaco's opening announcement ("You defeat the floor boss, and the
stairwell opens"). The AI's own closing judgment on whether this was actually satisfied is blunt:
"Well, sort of. Neither of the bosses are dead. That's a little disappointing. But you won. Congrats.
This is a little iffy considering what just happened, but I'm gonna call it because I can."

## Notable Events

- **Chaco is forced into the Grand Master role** and loses both wings mid-broadcast for trying to
  editorialize about his own fear.
- **Grigori, possessed by the AI, narrates the parade's four completed "parts"** (Resolution,
  Decision, Betrayal, Destruction) across the first four floats — the Primal/Eulogist/Apothecary
  cosmology reveal. See the [mechanics page](../mechanics/index.md#the-true-origin-of-the-primals-the-eulogist-and-the-apothecary-book-8)
  for the full text; this wiki does not duplicate it here.
- **Prepotente sings "In the Air Tonight"** at the judging stand — the performance he never got to
  give at the Lollipop bar — earning the nickname "Pony" from Carl afterward.
- **Carl's vengeance speech triggers a sluggalo massacre of the VIP audience.** As Carl delivers his
  "we are coming" speech in front of the giant Unwashed effigy, the airship above drops Bigs and the
  surviving sluggalos directly into the judging-stand crowd of masked, bound stand-ins — described on
  the page as "bankers, politicians, elites" — who are slaughtered where they sit.
- **The arena finale.** Carl summons **Scolopendra** as the crawlers' joint pick; the AI unilaterally
  adds **Krakaren Prime**. Using the newly sapient farm pig **[Penelope](../characters/penelope.md)**
  as bait — deliberately made sapient with a correctly-chosen enriched pet biscuit during the
  float-building phase, expressly so she could be used this way — Carl lures both **Taranis**
  and **Emberus** into the open, then publicly renounces his worship of Emberus and falsely confesses
  to killing Emberus's son Geyrun — a lie told purely to enrage him; Carl admits in narration he
  still has no idea who really did it. The resulting smite burns his Emberus tattoos into the unexplained
  [Mark of the Damned](../items/mark-of-the-damned.md). Pinned and vulnerable, **Emberus is killed by
  his own brother Taranis** — permanence unconfirmed.
- **Eris's inventory-swap chaos effect** briefly scrambles every nearby crawler's inventory into
  random junk, nearly costing Carl the killing blow on Scolopendra.
- **The biscuit mix-up.** Rather than kill or abandon Scolopendra, the party feeds her what they
  believe is the mindless-pet "Nothing Special" enriched biscuit — Donut, searching Carl's swapped
  inventory under fire, grabs the wrong one, and Scolopendra instead becomes "Sexy Scolopendra," a
  sapient, flirtatious Level 1 party member. The book never names which biscuit she actually
  grabbed; see [Enriched Pet Biscuits](../items/enriched-pet-biscuits.md#the-mix-up).
- **Taranis departs the realm**, taking both the badly wounded Krakaren Prime and a stowaway
  **[Samantha](../characters/samantha.md)**, who reveals she means to hunt down "the demon mother lady
  and her jerk son" over having been "tricked into giving birth to a monster."
- **The Tracking Tag (the Kyryap)** turns up on Carl's leg in the arena's aftermath, tied to a
  dreaded, trap-setting presence already established inside Club Scolopendra. See [Tracking Tag (The
  Kyryap)](../items/tracking-tag-kyryap.md).
- **Tipid loses both legs** in the crash of the One Fine Pig APV during the arena fight; Prepotente and
  Jurgen are both injured but recover. The Abrams tank crew (Sarah Hayse and her Chinese-crawler
  teammates) are killed outright by a Krakaren tentacle strike, leaving Makana the sole survivor of
  the Destruction float.
- **At the stairwell down, Lucia Mar is glimpsed with an unexplained five-year-old child** clinging to
  her leg moments before both step through the portal — left unexplained on the page. See
  [Timeline: Left Unresolved Going Into Floor
  12](../timeline/index.md#left-unresolved-going-into-floor-12).

## Stairwells

A line of portals appears against one side of the arena the moment the AI calls the fight a win. There
is no lingering victory lap: with the level-collapse clock at 30 minutes and falling, the party sends
survivors, mercenaries, and salvageable mounts through as fast as they can be gathered, abandoning
anything that can't fit or can't move under its own power (the wrecked ice-cream truck and Tundra are
left behind; Hedy the mechanic is lost in the crash). Carl and Donut wait until last to make sure
everyone else is through before following.

## Leaderboard

No new leaderboard is published on Floor 11 itself — the ranking data already given at the floor's
arrival (Carl #2, Donut #3, board suspended below 30 crawlers, #1 never named) is carried over from
the end of [Floor 10](level-10.md#leaderboard) and not revisited again on the page.

## The Move to Floor 12

**19 crawlers** ("Twenty if you counted Penny, which I did not," per Carl) proceed to Floor 12,
alongside the newly sapient Penelope and Sexy Scolopendra. A system message delivered as the floor
ends reframes everything that follows:

> "From now on, all level collapse timers past the 11th floor are suspended until a winner is
> declared in the Ascendency battles. When this happens, dungeon-closure procedures will commence."

The Ascendency Battles begin on a short countdown as the book closes. See
[Timeline: The Move to Floor 12](../timeline/index.md#the-move-to-floor-12) for the AI's own
declaration of intent (refusing to ever "sleep," introducing **OI units**) that accompanies this
transition.
