    - **Time limit to find the next level down:** 5 rotations of the planet (5 days)

## The Announcement

Level 1 opens as part of the Syndicate's initial harvest announcement. Per the in-universe
broadcast:

> "The first level of this dungeon will open approximately 18 seconds after the end of this
> announcement. The first-level entrances will be open for exactly one human hour and one hour
> only. Once the entrances are closed, you may no longer enter."

Entrance counts halve at each subsequent level (75,000 to Level 2, 37,500 to Level 3, and so on),
rounding up, until Level 18 — which has only two entrances and a single exit.

## Layout & Notable Areas

- **Safe Room** — the entry checkpoint; crawlers are greeted with "Welcome to the Safe Room. You
  are on the First Level."
- **Rot sticker neighborhood** — home to small, round, black creatures that climb walls/ceilings
  and detonate on contact ("suicide bombed themselves against you"); a blast reportedly feels like
  being hit with a sledgehammer.
- **Elderly encampment** — a hallway of very elderly, wheelchair- and walker-bound NPCs/crawlers,
  most still Level 1, gathered together under blankets.

## Monsters (Level 1 area)

- **Cockroaches** — described as level one and two, appearing in large numbers
- **Rot stickers** — level one/two exploding creatures (see above)
- **Bad Llama** — at least Level 3, killed via a joint effort by Carl and Princess Donut

## Stairwells

### General mechanics

- Level 1 has **75,000 entrances down to Floor 2**, but Earth's surface area makes them sparse —
  Carl estimates they could be "hard to find" purely from the numbers, and it takes a full day of
  motorcycle scouting to locate even one.
- A stairwell (like a Safe Room) appears on the minimap automatically once in range, even in
  unexplored fog-of-war areas — you don't have to physically find it first, just get close enough.
- About 30 hours into the floor, the system announces that staircases down are now "populating"
  and explicitly recommends **not** descending early: doing so before the last 6 hours of the
  floor's timer holds a crawler in stasis until the floor actually collapses.
- Per Mordecai, bosses don't guard stairwells as standard practice until **Floor 4** — making a
  boss-guarded stairwell on Floor 1 a genuine anomaly, not the norm.

### The stairwell Carl & Donut used

After a full day driving a looted Goblin Copper Chopper down a highway corridor (roughly
following I-5, past Lake Washington, toward the Cascades), Carl and Donut spot a map marker
reading **"Stairwell to Floor Two"** inside a circular "spiral maze" area. Graffiti at the
entrance warns other crawlers: "DO NOT go deeper into the spiral or you will activate the borough
boss." It's guarded by the [Ball of Swine](../bosses/ball-of-swine.md) (Level 15 Borough Boss) —
despite Mordecai's advice to skip it and search for an unguarded stairwell elsewhere, Carl
decides they can't risk not finding a second one and fights it head-on, in a mixed 7-crawler
party alongside Brandon An's "Team Meadow Lark" evacuees and Agatha. Killing it drops the
red forcefield blocking the stairs (by this point more of a ramp than actual stairs), and the
whole group descends to Floor 2 immediately afterward.

## Notable Events

- Because Level 1 isn't broadcast to viewers, everything that happens here is comparatively

  Carl and Donut's stats go public for the first time.
