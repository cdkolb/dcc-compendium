## Arrival

Carl and Donut step directly out of the Floor 7 globe chamber — see
[Level 7](level-07.md#stairwells) for how that transition was forced — into the marble rotunda of
El Capitolio Nacional de Cuba, in the small hours of a warm December morning. They are the only two
crawlers at their stairwell.

> "Welcome, Crawler to the eighth floor. 'The Ghosts of Earth.' Time to Level Collapse: 21 Days ...
> Leaderboard rank: 3. Bounty: 2,000,000 gold ... Remaining Crawlers: 38,532. It is December 10th.
> Your squad has six open slots. Entering El Capitolio Nacional de Cuba."

Donut, as squad leader, had picked Cuba herself in the globe chamber — reasoning that the Bahamas
(where Miss Beatrice had gone on holiday) was too obvious a trap. Carl's view is that she simply
missed. The exit stairwell is right there in the building, and locked: it needs a key nobody has yet.

### The floor's central conceit

Cascadia's welcome broadcast lays out the rules of the replica:

- The starting area is **an exact replica of Earth's surface as captured on the indicated date** —
  geography, buildings, vehicles, and people.
- **Mobile biological entities are intangible memory ghosts.** They do not appear on maps, cannot
  see crawlers, and cannot be touched or hurt. Walking through one rips the clothing off it; the
  clothes become real objects on the ground and the exposed parts of the ghost render as a wavy
  translucent outline, while the ghost carries on with its day as though nothing happened.
- **Everything non-biological is solid and keeps its real mass and momentum** until a crawler, a
  mob, or an environmental factor interferes. Crawlers can enter buildings, break windows, drive
  vehicles, take an item out of a ghost's hand — and be run over and killed by a bus.
- **Anything looted from the Earth memory turns to dust when the floor collapses.** Carl's first
  instinct, arming the party out of a military base, is explicitly pre-empted.
- **Mobs are seeded from each region's own folklore** — and the research is bad. Carl recognises the
  work from the *Earth Beautiful* production crew, who admitted to sourcing everything from an
  incomplete copy of the internet. The result is real legends flattened into Saturday-morning
  cartoons and freely blended with pop culture, an offence [Sister Ines
  Quiteria](../characters/sister-ines-quiteria.md) takes personally and furiously.

Within hours the traffic system collapses worldwide, as crawlers and mobs cause pile-ups that
cascade until the roads are wholly blocked and the streets fill with drifting, naked, oblivious
ghosts.

## The floor's three phases

### Phase One — 14 days (Dec 10 to Dec 24)

Every crawler is locked into a **squad of no more than five**. Parties of six or more are split into
equal squads; leaving a party does not leave a squad. Each squad has **six slots above its crawler
count** (a solo crawler's squad has seven slots total). Squads must be filled before Phase Two or
they will be filled at random.

Slots are filled with monsters. Each squad leader receives **twenty flags** bearing an
auto-generated squad logo — Donut's is a pair of heart-covered boxer shorts with a bomb patch over
the crotch, reading "The Royal Court of Princess Donut." Driving any living entity below **5% health**
and physically planting a flag in it converts it into a **T'Ghee totem card** in the squad leader's
inventory. Lost flags are not replaced. Totems can be traded and stolen; surplus totems dissipate at
the start of Phase Two. Each surviving squad may keep **one** card past the floor's collapse.

The deck game layered on top — hands of four, ten-second draws, totems/utility/snare/mystic/special
card types, and the rule that a squad leader loses access to spells and inventory during deck
combat — is covered in full on the [T'Ghee Cards](../mechanics/tghee-cards.md) mechanics page.

Carl and Donut's region is Cuba, and it contains exactly **one other squad**: Sister Ines Quiteria,
[Paz Lo](../characters/paz-lo.md), and Anton Lopez, three Cubans who came into the dungeon together
from a women's prison where the two men were guards.

### Phase Two — the assault (Dec 24 to Dec 26)

At 12:01 AM on December 24 every crawler is teleported to a choosing chamber. **Only the squad
leader** enters; the rest of the squad is held in stasis while the leader picks a new region from a
shortlist and fights a first, deliberately easy opponent alone. The squad then joins them.

In the new region the squad must collect **nine tokens** from **nine card-wielding deckmasters**,
each five levels stronger than the last, standing frozen at intervals along the route. The tokens go
into a vending machine that unlocks the **keymaster** — the tenth deckmaster — who holds the region's
exit key. The key location is 10–15 km away and the squad has **48 hours**. Totems not in the active
deck are lost; from this point a squad may hold exactly six totems, no more and no fewer, and any
totem taken from a defeated opponent must replace one of your own.

Carl and Donut draw **Monroe County, Florida** — the Keys. Donut had chosen Miami; a placement glitch
first reported them as being in Iowa, then resolved itself by physically tearing a chunk of **Dallas
County, Iowa** out of the ground and dropping it on top of the Keys as a roughly **700-foot** mountain
of snowmelt mud, with the key location on its summit. The mountain visibly erodes into the ocean for
the whole two days they are on it. Zev's department confirms this was done on purpose, as "the only
way to do it without breaking anything further."

### Phase Three — the door (Dec 26 onward)

At midnight on December 26:

- **Keyholders** and their squads teleport back to a random point inside their *original* region and
  must make their way to their own stairwell.
- **Keyless squads** teleport to the doorway of a stairwell belonging to someone who *does* hold a
  key — that is, directly into the keyholder's path. Multiple keyless squads can be dropped at the
  same door. The design intent is stated outright: "the potential of crawler on crawler violence is
  quite high. Doesn't that sound just great?"
- A keyholder who plays a **Flee** card now **loses a key to their opponent**, chosen at random if
  they hold more than one. Keys cannot be voluntarily dropped or handed over; the only way to give
  one away is to start a fight and flee it.
- The **doorway** to a stairwell chamber can be destroyed — and destroying it destroys its key. The
  stairwell itself, and the sealed chamber around it, remain indestructible and unreachable. Several
  squads lost their only route out this way, including Carl and Donut's own Capitolio exit.

By Carl's estimate, roughly one key existed for every three or four squads.

## Layout & Notable Areas

- **Havana, Cuba** — Carl and Donut's Phase One region. El Capitolio Nacional de Cuba (start point
  and one of two stairwells), Old Havana, the coast road, the suburbs to the south, the southern
  marshlands, and the **Iglesia de Jesús de Miramar** (the second stairwell, and the one they
  eventually leave through)
- **The Colón Cemetery** — walled off and fog-filled, revealed to be a **Medium Ghommid Settlement**
  corrupted by the orisha Asojano. It contains a saferoom pub ("The Yellow Zone") and, at its
  centre, a [Club Vanquisher](../locations/club-vanquisher.md) entrance temple
- **The Bay of Pigs marshlands** — the land crab migration route and the web-choked swamp domain of
  Shi Maria, the Bedlam Bride
- **A roadside [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md)** south of Havana — the site of the
  Astrid assassination and of the club's near-total destruction
- **Monroe County, Florida (the Keys)** and the transplanted chunk of **Dallas County, Iowa** —
  Phase Two. The Iowa slab holds a commercial turkey barn, a trailer park, and Carl's father's trailer
- **The FOCSA building, Havana** — a 32-storey Y-shaped tower, the site of the floor's final battle
- **Elsewhere:** Iceland (Katia's squad), the Pacific Northwest and Belgium (Elle), Detroit (Imani),
  Beijing (Li Na and Li Jun), Ecuador and later Nigeria (Florin), the Bahamas and later northern
  Italy (Prepotente), Vietnam and Ukraine, Tokyo, Brazil, Turkey

## Monsters (Level 8 area)

The floor's regular mobs are drawn from local folklore, badly researched and freely mixed with pop
culture — see the [Mobs overview](../mobs/index.md) for the Book 6 regional catalog.
Representative Cuban examples include **the Experience** (a Taíno guardian of the dead rendered as a
dog with Che Guevara's head), **duendes**, **Monk Seal Picket Sentries** (masters of "Caribbean Kung
Fu"), **Red *Maníseros* Land Crabs** (masters of *Juego de maní*), and the **Unsettled Ghommids** of
the Colón Cemetery. Elsewhere crawlers report a Bermuda-shorts-wearing sasquatch, an undead Easter
Bunny, sentient umbrellas in Tokyo, Icelandic Nykur, and a Chinese dragon.

## Quests

### The Chowder War — **Failed**

An automatic quest received on examining a Monk Seal. The seals hold the ocean sacred; the Red
*Maníseros* Land Crabs must spawn in it; the AI frames the resulting conflict in the crudest possible
terms and demands that crawlers pick a side. Sister Ines warned the Monk Seal Abbot of the crabs'
sneak attack, which turned what would have been a quick rout into a war of attrition that gutted both
species. The quest is then formally marked **Quest Failed** and replaced with **Hell Comes to Crawler
Town** — it is not a win, and should not be recorded as one.

### Hell Comes to Crawler Town — Complete

The Chowder War's replacement, and the floor's pivot into its endgame. The mass death from the
crab–seal war, layered on top of a global zombie outbreak that Carl personally started by punting
zombie turkeys off the Iowa cliff into the ocean, attracts the attention of hell. The **Demon
Eviction** event begins (see [Game Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md)). Win condition: get down the
stairs without being dragged into Sheol.

### Pueblo de los Olvidados — Complete

A compulsory quest triggered by entering the Colón Cemetery, which seals behind the party. The
ghommid village has been driven mad by the trapped orisha **Asojano**; the fix is to repair his shrine
so he can leave, or to kill him. Resolving it instead opens the **Celestial Thorn Room** (below).
Reward: a Silver Quest Box, the settlement restored, and a working Club Vanquisher entrance.

### Spiders — Complete

A deity quest from **Eileithyia**: kill five spiders a day in her name. It is transparently a cover —
its real function is to give the goddess (and through her, her sponsor Huanxin Jinx) a private
messaging channel to Carl that the system is not permitted to monitor, plus a pretext for awarding his
party a boon. Donut enjoys it enormously.

### The Astrid assassination — Complete

Katia's third and final seal on the **Engaged Lock Box of the Night Wyrm** requires an assassination
contract from the [Guild of Suffering](../dungeon-factions/guild-of-suffering.md), whose entrance is
hidden behind the Penis Parade in the Desperado Club. The target: **Astrid**, Level 125 Bloodlust
Sprite, assistant manager of the Desperado Club — who turns out to be the Night Wyrm's own wife and
the mother of the dancers Damascus Steel and Anaconda. The job is completed, and the plan to do it
cleanly falls apart completely (see Notable Events).

### The Bedlam Bride — ongoing

An escort quest: get **Shi Maria** to the city of Larracos on Floor 9. Donut rips the spider's card at
the end of the floor; the quest does *not* go away, because Shi Maria has by then tattooed herself into
Carl's chest.

### Where the Sidewalk Ends → The Missing Piece — Complete

The regional quest hosted by Carl's squad once enough crawlers commit to his escape plan, and its
successor world quest. Part One, "The Big O," requires refilling the world with the right *kind* of
souls and then powering Amayon up enough that his portal covers the entire planet. Part Two, "Falling
Up," is the escape itself.

### Carried over from earlier books

- **Vengeance of the Daughter** — [Tsarina Signet's](../characters/tsarina-signet.md) questline pays
  out at the start of the floor. Carl's Gold Quest Box contains Edgar the tortoise's stick-and-poke
  tattoo kit; the promised right to loot the High Elf castle is worthless, the castle having been
  teleported to Floor 9. The kit becomes the instrument of the floor's final horror.
- **Find Out Who Killed My Son** — Emberus's quest regarding the murder of the god **Geyrun**
  advances substantially. Pater Coal, high cleric of Emberus in Club Vanquisher (and, Carl suspects,
  the AI wearing a face), names two threads: the origin of Katia's crossbow and the origin of Donut's
  oak bracelet, neither explorable without Osvaldo's memorial crystal. Later, Amayon states outright
  that he witnessed the deal being signed and will name the culprit only if Emberus helps him kill his
  three brothers. Hellik remains the nominal suspect; the existence of a memorial crystal for the
  still-living goddess Apito remains unexplained.

- **The *Shadow Boxer* broadcast.** Carl and Donut appear on a documentary programme run by
  [Rosetta Thagra](../characters/rosetta-thagra.md) — a former crawler, ninth-edition author of the
  [Cookbook](../items/the-dungeon-anarchists-cookbook.md), and employee of Carl's sponsor the Open
  Intellect Pacifist Network. She forces Carl through his mother's suicide on his fifteenth birthday,
  his father's abandonment, the police interrogation, and the Anacortes Boys' Ranch, on air. Buried in
  it are two coded messages: "potatoes and milk" (pointing him back to Crawler Milk's cookbook entries
  and the toraline root vegetable) and a run of Miami-set TV screencaps telling Donut where to go in
  Phase Two. The liaison **Harbinger** kills the interview mid-sentence, bans Rosetta from crawler
  programming, and tells Carl to his face that he is working to have him "disposed."
- **The Celestial Thorn Room.** Seven ruined Orisha shrines, one Sun of Reawakening, one repair. On a
  last-second warning from [Katia](../characters/katia.md) — who has been wearing the
  [Crown of the Sepsis Whore](../items/crown-of-the-sepsis-whore.md) since Eva Sigrid threw it at her —
  Carl has Bomo rebuild **Yemaya's** shrine instead of **Ogun's**, resurrecting Yemaya and buying
  Katia a path out of the crown. The cost is immediate and permanent: Ogun appears in person, strips
  Paz of his grace and of any ability to heal his death-debuff, and crushes **Anton's** head underfoot.
  Bomo is teleported to Floor 9 as a reward for his work and is lost to the party. As Paz dies, Sister
  Ines flags him with her squad banner and turns him into a card rather than let him go.
- **Katia's addiction is revealed.** Sent by Carl's own directions to a hidden drug den in the
  Desperado Club, Katia is found comatose for ten hours. She is addicted to four substances — Travis
  Priest, Creamsicle Charm, blitz sticks, and a single dose of Glory Bound — and the blitz habit turns
  out to predate the floor by a long way. Her stats are permanently scrambled and her Constitution
  gutted. Mordecai privately tells Carl the *smart* move is to let her self-destruct, because it is
  Katia or Donut; Carl refuses to discuss it.
- **The Astrid assassination disaster.** Carl's carefully trapped ambush corridor is never used.
  Samantha, sent to cause a diversion, blunders into the casino and tears open a portal to the
  Nothing, releasing **Minge**, a Level 225 feral demon. Astrid unleashes the **vorpals** — celestial
  suicide-bomber guards — and dies knowing she was about to be freed. Carl rips her wings off; Katia's
  totems finish her. The Desperado Club is destroyed and closed for reconstruction, which incidentally
  ejects thirty-five trapped Faction Wars officials into the shark-infested flood of Larracos. Carl
  uses a mercenary coupon to hire the entire Penis Parade — thirteen dancers plus Doctor Bones and
  Bucket Boy — into the guild.
- **Odette's confession and sponsorship switch.** Rather than become the Princess Posse's adjutant as
  Huanxin Jinx had arranged, [Odette](../characters/odette.md) announces on air that she is entering
  the dungeon herself as the sponsor of the goddess **Nekhebit**. Off the record afterwards she tells
  Carl the whole story: Pieter, Armita, the rigged tenth-floor summoning that killed Mordecai's brother
  Uzzi, her confession, Armita's murder, and her conviction that the crawl must be shut down without
  collapsing the inner system's fuel supply. The Princess Posse's adjutant ends up being **Baroness
  Victory**, an orc judge and half-sister of the late Queen Consort Ugloo.
- **The AI seizes control of the crawl.** After building the Reminiscence Hydra with no input
  parameters at all — "It was created by me with *no* contribution whatsoever" — the system AI takes
  the crawl away from Borant. The liaison **Orren** negotiates it back on air, the earliest a crawl
  has ever gone primal. Six of the nine Faction Wars teams immediately try to abdicate and are refused
  under the quarantine rules.
- **Carl discovers his half-brother.** The Phase Two keymaster's lair is his father's trailer, spliced
  in from a different date, and Carl is locked in with it for 42 minutes. He watches his father die of
  a morphine overdose administered by a stepmother he never knew existed, and meets **Asher**, the
  six-year-old half-brother he never knew existed either. Donut later reveals Beatrice knew about Asher

- **The Reminiscence Hydra.** The keymaster fight: a nine-headed flesh boss whose heads are Asher, his

- **The Residual and the failed trap.** A benefactor box from the Open Intellect Pacifist Network sends
  Carl alone to a homeless shelter in Homestead, Florida on Christmas morning to hear a

  mid-sentence and explains it as a suicide gambit by his own sponsors, who are now fugitives with
  their assets frozen. Carl trades the Residual's stash away rather than be killed for it.
- **The Christmas party power struggle.** Louis, furious, forces a Christmas celebration on a guild
  that has just been told most of them will not get a key. Carl's objection — "This isn't Christmas" —

- **Quan Ch, Tserendolgor, and the Capitolio.** Quan Ch out-plays Donut's deck twice with **Force Play**
  and takes both Cuban keys. Carl teleports across the city via a trap anchor lodged inside Samantha,

  it. **Tserendolgor** — Ren, the dog-soldier crawler and Donut's nemesis — will
  not fight them for the last one. She confesses on the record that she did cheat the pet show and that

- **Sister Ines's collapse and Ysalte's death.** Sponsored and patronised by the
  [Nebulars](../factions/nebular-sin-patrol.md), Sister Ines is pushed back over the edge her medication
  had held her behind. Her level-15 *Word Weaver* charm takes the Madre de Aguas — and through it every
  creature within earshot — and eventually **Amayon** himself. Louis kills her. Her patron, the goddess
  **Ysalte**, the Vinegar Bitch, arrives for revenge and is decapitated by her own Paz totem, whom
  Katia armed with the Bolt of Ophiotaurus.
- **Shi Maria's tattoo.** A Golden Combo card intended for Alpha Male Carl retargets onto Carl himself
  when the spider kills its intended partner. For 180 seconds Shi Maria wears Carl's body, rifles his
  inventory, burns a hole through the floor with *Self-Immolation*, gouges out and eats
  [Li Jun's](../characters/li-jun.md) eye for the blood on the needle, and uses the Vengeance of the
  Daughter tattoo kit to ink **the Eye of the Bedlam Bride** into his chest — anchoring herself to him
  permanently so that ripping her card can no longer be rid of her.

## Stairwells

Floor 8's official exits are the two stairwells per region, each behind a locked door that only a key
opens, and each usable only by the keyholder's own squad. Most of the survivors left that way.

Carl, Donut and [Imani](../characters/imani-c.md) did not, and neither did thousands of others,
because the design guaranteed there were nowhere near enough keys — and because destroyed doorways
destroyed their keys outright. The chamber around a stairwell is sealed and indestructible; *Hole*,
*Phase Through Walls*, and digging all fail against it.

Carl's solution, worked out with Prepotente over chat, exploits the **Demon Eviction** event itself:

1. Kill Sister Ines, freeing **Amayon** from her charm.
2. Teleport Amayon out of the Madre de Aguas's body so he takes his true, full-power form.
3. Refill the world with the exact type of souls that broke Sheol in the first place — crab spawn —
   which required talking **Raul the crab** into it.
4. Buff Amayon with Sheol fire, using Emberus's **Martyr's Path** boon to ignite every footstep Carl
   had taken outdoors, including the ones directly beneath the demon.

Powered that far, Amayon's homecoming portal expands to cover **the entire planet**. When it opens,
everything solid on Earth is sucked upward into the sky. The indestructible stairwell chambers are
torn out of the ground with everything else — and the moment a chamber strikes the hell portal, its
walls teleport away and the stairwell inside is exposed. Crawlers who dug trenches *underneath* their
chamber and stood directly beneath it were carried up alongside it and hit the stairwell in mid-air.

The floor therefore has no conventional final descent. Carl, Donut and Imani stand in a tunnel under
the Iglesia de Jesús de Miramar with a neon X painted on the ceiling above them, the heavens open over
Cuba, the world is stripped bare, and they are swept up into the sky and onto **Floor 9**. The floor's
last words:

> **Welcome, Crawler.**
>
> **Welcome to Faction Wars.**

Immediately before leaving, Carl spends the first of the Princess Posse's three **Emergency Action
Items**, arguing that the other Faction Wars teams' refusal to vote endangers the integrity of the game
and asking the AI to put the pending procedural votes to the viewing audience. It is granted. Among the
results:

- **Remove Safety Protections For Faction Sponsors** — passed, **98%** audience vote.
- **A tenth team populated with NPCs, afforded the same protections as the rest** — passed, **52%**.
  Designated the Home Team, based in Larracos, co-warlords [Juice Box](../characters/juice-box.md) and
  [Sir Ferdinand](../characters/ferdinand.md).
- Long-range artillery limits raised, accelerated pet leveling, and an enlarged Faction Wars playing
  field.
