# Level 5

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Crawlers entering the floor:** 178,887 (confirmed exact figure; the welcome broadcast
      rounds it to "just over 178,000")
    - **Structure:** Not a single open floor — **1,172 sealed "bubbles"** floating in a dark
      in-between space called the **Lacuna**, ~150 crawlers per bubble, no two alike. Carl's
      party lands in **Bubble #543**, Air Quadrant
    - **Entrances/stairwells:** Breaks the halving pattern entirely — over **4,000 castles**
      floor-wide, one per quadrant per bubble, each containing its bubble's stairwell. A bubble's
      own stairwell won't open until **all four** of its quadrant castles (Land, Sea, Air,
      Subterranean) are taken
    - **Time limit:** No single stated day-count in the welcome broadcast; checkpoints in-text
      put it at roughly **10 days** total (confirmed "five days left" at the five-day mark, and
      "four and a half days left" shortly after)
    - **Milestone:** The **bounty-doubling leaderboard** mechanic debuts here — surviving a floor
      while ranked in the top 10 permanently doubles all of a crawler's future bounties, via the
      achievement "The Quarry Sees Another Spring"

## Arrival

The party steps directly from a Floor 4 evacuation warehouse into Floor 5's desert wind, "just a
few minutes after the fourth level collapsed." The system confirms the numbers immediately:

> "Welcome, Crawler, to the fifth floor. 'The Bubbles.' ... Remaining Crawlers: 178,887.
> Entering Bubble #543 out of 1172. Air Quadrant."

The same notification wave delivers the bounty-doubling achievement. Donut, given only minutes to
pick a new Former Child Actor specialty, chooses **Glass Cannon** — trading away all future
Constitution training for a flat +15 base Constitution, faster spell training, lower mana costs,
and a massively raised cap on resurrection-type spells like *Second Chance* (see
[Game Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md#glass-cannon-class-book-4)).

## Layout & Notable Areas

- **[Hump Town](../locations/hump-town.md)** — the party's home base, a Dromedarian brothel
  settlement built around a roughly circular "bowl" about three miles across, secretly infiltrated
  by [The Changeling Principals](../dungeon-factions/the-changeling-principals.md)
- **The Air Quadrant castle** — guarded by [Ruckus](../bosses/ruckus.md), a Level 55 spring-loaded
  Chicken Hawk Sentinel
- **The Land Quadrant's Sandcastle** — held by the mage Ghazi and [Mrs.
  Ghazi](../bosses/mrs-ghazi.md); its collapse floods survivors down into the Water Quadrant
  rather than resolving cleanly
- **The wrecked submarine *Akula*** (Water Quadrant) — swallowed whole, along with Carl's boarding
  party, by the City Boss [Lusca](../bosses/lusca.md) in a live broadcast Special Event
- **The [Necropolis of Anser](../locations/necropolis-of-anser.md)** (Subterranean Quadrant) — an
  ancient flooded tomb guarded by [Quetzalcoatlus](../bosses/quetzalcoatlus.md), possessed by the
  banished lesser deity Psamathe
- **The dirigible dreadnaught *the Wasteland*** — [The Wasteland](../dungeon-factions/the-wasteland.md)
  faction's airship, destroyed mid-air after a fake-resurrection negotiation trick
- **Pandinus** — the Land Quadrant's pazuzu-run town, home to a Club Vanquisher branch
  (temporarily closed for "cleansing" after an incident involving Prepotente's hellspawn familiar)

## Quests

### Get Orthrus (World Quest)

> "New Quest! Get Orthrus. This is a world quest! All living crawlers on the fifth floor will
> receive this message! ... Orthrus, the two-headed puppy is bounding his way happily through
> the Lacuna, the world that houses the bubbles. This very adorable pup is running back to his
> former master's father. ... Let's kill it."

- **Trigger:** Automatically sent to every living crawler on Floor 5 once Orthrus — a
  perfectly innocent puppy warped by prolonged exposure to **the Nothing** — starts rampaging
  through the Lacuna trying to reach his father, the deity Emberus
- **Pass condition:** Kill Orthrus before he reunites with Emberus
- **Reward:** 1,000,000 gold, five level-up potions, and a pet monkey named Jimbo, for whoever
  lands the kill
- **What actually happened:** Carl's group actively works *against* the quest's own goal, flying
  an improvised aircraft to help Orthrus safely reunite with Emberus instead of killing him — Carl
  publicly threatens to kill anyone who harms the dog, and physically intervenes when the rival
  crawler **Quan Ch** tries to snipe it for the reward. Nobody collects the prize; Orthrus is
  saved.

## Stairwells

### General mechanics

- Floor 5 abandons the halving-entrance pattern used on Floors 1-4 entirely. Instead: over 4,000
  castles floor-wide, one per quadrant per bubble, each housing that quadrant's throne room *and*
  the bubble's single stairwell.
- A bubble's stairwell stays closed until **all four** of its quadrant castles are conquered
  (throne room occupied or quadrant boss killed) — crawlers who finish their own quadrant early
  may cross into other quadrants within their bubble to help finish the rest.
- Once all four castles fall, the bubble "pops" and its crawlers may leave it — but they still
  cannot enter any other bubble until that one has also been popped.
- Each bubble typically hides one environmental "easy mode" shortcut per quadrant that bypasses
  its throne-room boss fight entirely.

### The stairwell Carl, Donut & Katia used

With all four of Bubble #543's quadrant castles eventually cleared (Ruckus in the Air, the
Sandcastle in the Land, Lusca's wreck in the Water, and Quetzalcoatlus in the Subterranean), the
bubble's own stairwell — located back near Hump Town — opens normally. Carl's group spends their
remaining time using the newly-assembled [Gate of the Feral Gods](../items/gate-of-the-feral-gods.md)
to rescue crawlers trapped in *other*, still-sealed bubbles rather than leaving immediately, before
finally descending together with about five minutes left on the clock — Chris Andrews enters
first, with Carl, Donut, and Mongo right behind.

## Notable Events

- Carl kills [Ruckus](../bosses/ruckus.md) (Level 55 Borough Boss) while field-testing homemade
  missiles, then uncovers and thwarts a Skull-Empire-backed [Changeling Principals](../dungeon-factions/the-changeling-principals.md)
  infiltration of Hump Town's guard ranks.
- After hostage gnome Wynne is killed by a rival crawler group, Carl, Donut, and Katia trick the
  dirigible dreadnaught [The Wasteland](../dungeon-factions/the-wasteland.md) into a face-to-face
  negotiation and destroy it mid-air, rescuing Commandant Kane's daughter Bonnie from
  [Denise the Feral Goose Mother](../bosses/denise-the-feral-goose-mother.md).
- Carl accidentally assassinates dungeon admin Loita with a rigged toy-robot device during a
  sponsor infomercial taping, then talks his way out of a Syndicate liaison investigation.
- Katia's separate team kills the mage Ghazi and [Mrs. Ghazi](../bosses/mrs-ghazi.md) at the Land
  Quadrant's Sandcastle, though its collapse washes survivors into the Water Quadrant instead.
- Carl, Katia, and two survivors are swallowed by [Lusca](../bosses/lusca.md) in a live-broadcast
  Special Event and kill her from inside using a berserking potion and a salvaged buzzsaw.
- **Maggie My** — revealed to be an Infiltrator (Scree) who has fully puppeted
  [Chris Andrews](../characters/chris-andrews.md) since Floor 4 — is extracted and killed; Chris
  rejoins the party.
- [Quetzalcoatlus](../bosses/quetzalcoatlus.md), possessed by the banished deity Psamathe, is
  destroyed via an environmental lightning-tower trap at the cost of every crawler remaining in
  the Subterranean Quadrant.
- With all four castles cleared, Carl assembles the [Gate of the Feral Gods](../items/gate-of-the-feral-gods.md)
  and uses it to evacuate other sealed bubbles, releasing a feral god or demon into each one —
  including the puppy-god Orthrus, safely reunited with his father Emberus, God of Sun and Ash,
  whom Carl accidentally acquires as his own patron deity via a Celestial Grenade.
- In the finale, Carl sends a final feral god through a portal directly into the **Ninth Floor's
  Larracos marketplace** instead of releasing it onto the open floor, crippling every Faction War
  army's ability to resupply ahead of schedule.
- Carl ends the book as the dungeon's **#1-ranked crawler** (Level 47); Katia splits off from the
  main party to protect Eva Sigrid's scattered followers; Chris Andrews joins the core party.
