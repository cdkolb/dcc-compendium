# Level 4

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Entrances down to Floor 5:** 9,375 (halved again from Floor 4's own 18,750 entrances in)
    - **Time limit to find the next level down:** 10 days
    - **Crawlers remaining (mid-floor checkpoint):** 389,441 — no clean starting count is given;
      the floor's own preface warns it's "a massive, deliberately-confusing puzzle"
    - **Floor type:** "The Iron Tangle" — an enormous subway/rail network (150,000+ stations) in
      place of open terrain; see [The Iron Tangle Railway System](../mechanics/the-iron-tangle-railway.md)
      for the full transit mechanics
    - **Milestone:** The leaderboard's bounty mechanic goes fully live — killing a top-10 crawler
      now pays out their listed bounty, and survivors collect 10% of their own bounty on floor
      collapse

## Arrival

Carl, Donut, Katia, and Mongo arrive already in motion — teleported directly onto a moving,
graffiti-covered Moscow-style subway car (Red Line, Car 20), mid-tunnel, with no orientation
period:

> "Welcome, Crawler to the fourth floor. 'The Iron Tangle' ... Your title has reverted to Royal
> Bodyguard. Sponsorship bidding initiated on Crawler #4,122. Bidding ends in 45 hours."

Donut, whose Former Child Actor class forces a new specialty pick every floor, has six minutes to
choose between **Football Hooligan** and **Firecracker** with Mordecai's remote help over chat;
she picks Football Hooligan. The party immediately faces a navigation problem the book's own
preface warns readers not to stress over memorizing: "the fourth floor of the dungeon is set up
as a massive, deliberately-confusing puzzle."

## Layout & Notable Areas

- **Colored-line trains and Named trains** — see [The Iron Tangle Railway
  System](../mechanics/the-iron-tangle-railway.md) for the full mechanic; colored lines only
  travel forward, Named trains (the *Nightmare Express*, *Dismemberment Limited*, and others)
  loop and are the only way to travel backward down a line
- **The Abyss** — a mile-wide central pit where wrecked train cars and monsters are ultimately
  dumped; ringed by lettered trainyards (E, H, M, Q, and more)
- **Transfer stations** (numbers ending in a prime) — full settlements with a restaurant,
  general store, and often a [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md) entrance; the
  Iron Tangle's Station 131 branch is a noticeably smaller, skeleton-crew version of the Over
  City flagship
- **Ghoul Generator stations** (12, 24, 36, 48, 72, and parallel instances) — soul-crystal-powered
  disposal machines at the center of the floor's central crisis; see [Iron Tangle
  Management](../dungeon-factions/iron-tangle-management.md)
- **Station 75's "Downward Dog" tavern** — home of [Growler Gary](../dungeon-factions/iron-tangle-management.md#leadership-staff),
  the indestructible bartender whose severed hand keys the rapid-response interdiction carts
- **Station 433/Terminus** — lair of [Mimic Rex](../bosses/mimic-rex.md), one of six floor-wide
  Station Mimics, and (once she's gone) the site of a hidden level stairwell

## Quests

No individually-named discovery quest is called out for Floor 4 the way "The Show Must Go On" or
"The Sex Workers Who Fell from the Heavens" were on Floor 3 — the floor's central crisis (the
ghoul-generator overload) plays out as an open, floor-wide survival effort rather than a
personal quest chain, coordinated informally between Carl's group and allies like Imani C, Elle
McGibbons, Li Jun, and Daniel Bautista rather than triggered by a system quest card.

## Stairwells

### General mechanics

- Floor 4 continues the halving pattern: 18,750 entrances in, 9,375 entrances down to Floor 5.
- Stairwells are tied to specific stations (12, 24, 36, 48, 72, and the hidden Station 433) rather
  than scattered freely — a sharp contrast to the open, find-anywhere stairwells of Floors 1-2.
- A boss occupying a stairwell station hides it from the map entirely until the boss is gone —
  confirmed twice now, first with Ringmaster Grimaldi on Floor 3 and again with Mimic Rex at
  Station 433.
- During the floor's climax, Iron Tangle management's self-destruct announcement reveals the
  stairwells are centrally controlled: "these stairwell portals are powering up now and will open
  in exactly 18 hours. They will only be open for six hours before the self-destruction charges
  will blow."

### The stairwell Carl, Donut & Katia used

After [destroying the ghoul generator](../dungeon-factions/iron-tangle-management.md) threatening
Station 36 — the culmination of the rescue-and-demolition operation built around hijacked Named
trains and stolen interdiction carts — Carl's group returns to Station 36 to find the stairwell
already open, powered up as part of the self-destruct sequence, with the assembled survivor group
waiting for them rather than descending without them. Agatha, still alive and pushing her
shopping cart, descends first; Carl and the rest of the party follow shortly after.

## Notable Events

- Carl fights and kills [Gore-Gore the Mantaur](../bosses/gore-gore-the-mantaur.md), narrowly
  avoiding an accidental full summon of Grull via his own
  [War Gauntlet](../items/war-gauntlet-of-the-exalted-grull.md); gains a new sponsor, the Valtay
  Corporation.
- Carl learns the full story behind Book 2's dynamite trap from [Frank Q](../characters/frank-q.md)
  at the Desperado Club, shortly before [Chris Andrews](../characters/chris-andrews.md) kills him.
- [Hekla](../characters/hekla.md) is killed by [Katia](../characters/katia.md)'s off-target
  **Rush** attack while secretly orchestrating a plot against Katia and her own healer — ending
  the Book 1-2 rival/antagonist arc and disbanding Brynhild's Daughters.
- [Brandon An](../characters/brandon-an.md) dies holding a defensive line during the floor's crisis.
- Carl accidentally teleports [Mimic Rex](../bosses/mimic-rex.md) into a trainyard, triggering a
  broadcast **Death Challenge** against the involuntarily-summoned war god Grull — secretly
  piloted by a very-much-alive Prince Maestro.
- With 11 hours left on the timer, Iron Tangle management announces a system-wide self-destruct,
  blaming "the Krakaren," and floods every stairwell station with its accumulated monster
  population.
- Carl, Katia, and allies mount an improvised rescue operation using hijacked Named trains and
  interdiction carts to evacuate stranded crawlers and funnel monster hordes into the Abyss.
- Fire Brandy and the dwarf Tizquick ride the *Nightmare* train into the Abyss and detonate it to
  help collapse the ghoul-generator network.
- Carl ends the book at Level 41 (from 27), Donut at 33; the floor's ghoul crisis is contained,
  though not fully resolved, as the timer runs out.

## Leaderboard

Unlike Floor 3's single tentative reveal, Floor 4's leaderboard is live and changes multiple
times over the course of the book — and per an early-floor system announcement, killing a top-10
crawler now pays out their listed bounty directly: "hunting season is open." A late-book reveal,
right after Hekla's death airs, shows the list reshuffled:

Notes worth calling out:

- Carl himself starts the floor already ranked **6th** (a holdover from Floor 3's list) before
  this reshuffle bumps him to 3rd.
- Quan Ch — the accidental beneficiary of Book 2's "Fools Who Broke the Glass" disaster — is now
  the dungeon's single highest-leveled crawler (38), a fact Donut refuses to accept gracefully,
  repeatedly calling him "a cheater."
- **Dmitri and Maxim Popov** are the "twin" up-and-comers Odette teased back on Floor 3.
- Donut is indignant that she and Carl don't get separate individual ranks ("Carl and I should
  both be number three!") and that Prepotente — "that goat thing" — barely gets any screen time
  despite ranking 2nd.
- Mrs. Elle McGibbons, 10th on the Floor 3 list, falls off the top 10 entirely by this point in
  Book 3.
