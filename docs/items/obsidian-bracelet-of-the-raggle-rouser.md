# Enchanted Obsidian Bracelet of the Raggle Rouser

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut
    - **Slot:** Leg bracelet (Donut wears one on each of her four legs)
    - **Obtained:** Looted from a Dream elf hunter (Akland), killed by Carl
    - **Rarity note:** Already upgraded three times before Carl finds it; a 50% chance to
      permanently break if upgraded a fourth time

## Effect

- **+10 Constitution**
- **+5 Dexterity**
- **+15% Fire Resistance**
- Grants the wearer a castable **level-10 Fireball** spell, usable once every twenty minutes

## Scaling

Not described beyond its listed upgrade history (three prior upgrades, unspecified original
stats) — its current bonuses are presented as fixed at this upgrade tier.

## Role in the Story

Carl gives the bracelet to Donut immediately after looting it, over her initial complaints about
its color ("It's black, and it looks like it's made from glass"). It becomes her primary offensive
upgrade for the rest of Floor 6 — she uses its granted [Fireball](../spells/fireball.md) spell to
finish off the Eryops boss Claude Sludgington the Fourth, blasting him mid-air after Carl's
Protective Shell launches the rooted monster skyward, and again during her solo fight against
[Lucia Mar](../characters/lucia-mar.md), slamming a Fireball into her chest and setting the
treehouse pub around them ablaze (prompting Mordecai's after-the-fact reminder that it's an
outdoor spell).

Note: an earlier draft of this page claimed Donut also fireballs someone during the Butcher's
Masquerade's climactic battle against Queen Imogen — this is confirmed **false**. Katia's own
scouted stat readout on Imogen states outright that "she's immune to poison and curses and has an
ability to return magical fire. Nobody hit her with fireballs or magic missiles or magic bolts,"
which is exactly why the crawlers switch to blunt-force weapons (Florin's shotgun, thrown rocks)
for that fight instead. Donut's notable spell during the masquerade is *Laundry Day*, stripping
Diwata's divine armor off Circe Took, not Fireball. Separately, Donut also earns an off-page
"Did-You-Really-Just-Cast-Fireball-in-a-Room-This-Small" achievement at some unspecified point —
Mordecai's irritated aside is the only trace of that particular incident, and it isn't the
masquerade fight either.
