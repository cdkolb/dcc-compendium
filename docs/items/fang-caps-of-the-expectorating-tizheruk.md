# Enchanted Fang Caps of the Expectorating Tizheruk

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Mongo (transferred from Donut, who couldn't use them)
    - **Slot:** A pair of teeth (Mongo can wear up to six pairs — unique to his creature type)
    - **Obtained:** Platinum Quest Box, from the "Johnny Quest" + double "Hadji" achievement upgrade
    - **Type:** Fleeting item (disappears permanently once removed)

## Effect

Per the item's description, the caps "turn two of your incisors into ripping, tearing, deadly
chompers." Effects:

- **+50% melee bite damage**
- **25% chance per bite to inflict a level-5 Poisoned status**
- **10% chance per bite to inflict a level-5 Paralyzed status**

Once installed, Mongo's bite crackles with small bursts of electricity — startling enough that he
accidentally urinated on himself the first time it happened, before realizing the sparks don't
hurt him.

## Scaling

The percentages and damage bonus are flat, stated values with no described level curve — the
book gives no indication they improve with Mongo's own level or a skill.

## Role in the Story

Donut receives the caps as loot but can't use them (they don't suit her fighting style), so
they're transferred to Mongo instead — one of the few gear types the book notes can be swapped
directly between a crawler and their bonded pet. They become a small but real combat upgrade for
him, used against everything from Street Urchins to the krasue haunting Miss Quill's settlement.
