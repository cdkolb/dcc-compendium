# Talisman of the Slate Butterfly

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut
    - **Slot:** Collar charm
    - **Obtained:** A loot box opened during the Floor 1 tutorial
    - **Effect at time of receipt:** Raised Donut's Light on Your Feet skill from 3 to 7

## Effect

**+4 to the Light on Your Feet skill. +1 Intelligence. Winged fairies are no longer automatically
hostile toward the wearer.** Per its description: "Those who remember and commemorate the sad
tale of the Slate Butterfly are given a boon by the fairies of the world" — the actual "tale" is
never explained in Book 1.

## Scaling

The talisman's own bonus is a flat +4 to the skill, but that skill's effect scales with its total
level: Donut's jump height roughly doubled going from a Light on Your Feet of 3 to 7, letting her
clear obstacles (like the top of a play structure) she couldn't reach before.

## Role in the Story

A minor but useful item — the fairy-hostility immunity becomes relevant once the party starts
dealing with Rev-Up's Laminak and Clurichaun workers on Floor 2.

## Book 5: Retired

Donut retires the talisman from her collar-charm slot on Floor 6, swapping it out for the
settlement-tracking [Enchanted Collar Charm of the Effete Bourgeoisie](enchanted-collar-charm-of-the-effete-bourgeoisie.md)
after conquering the town of Point Mongo. The talisman itself is not destroyed, merely unequipped.

## Book 5: Britney's Jacket

Separately, fellow crawler [Britney Proskurina](../characters/britney.md) builds an entire jacket
out of dozens of common-rarity **Stuffed Slate Butterfly** beanbag figures (sourced from
[Bautista](../characters/bautista.md)'s collection), each summonable as a live, hand-sized
butterfly that instinctively flies to and extinguishes the nearest light source before reverting
to a stuffed toy. She uses one to covertly disable a warning light monitoring the Butcher's
Masquerade's guards; later, the entire jacket discharges at once when Queen Imogen's own offensive
lightning spell — meant for the crawlers assaulting her — gets drawn to the butterfly swarm
instead, saving several crawlers' lives at the cost of Britney's face being permanently burned.
