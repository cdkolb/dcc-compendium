# Goblin Pass (Tattoo)

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owners:** Carl (left inner forearm) and Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** A starting loot box, opened during the Floor 1 tutorial
    - **Type:** Faction tattoo — branded directly onto the body, not stored in inventory

## Effect

**Removes automatic goblin hostility. Allows free passage through goblin-controlled territory.**
Carries a stated drawback: holding a Goblin Pass causes fairy-class creatures to deal **20% more
damage** against the wearer. Per its own description, "it looks kinda badass" regardless.

The tattoo brands itself onto the skin the moment the box is opened — Carl describes it burning
like an actual brand, with the mark appearing both on his skin and on the exterior of his jacket.

## Scaling

None described — this is a binary, always-on faction pass rather than something with levels.

## Role in the Story

This pass is what allows Carl and Donut to walk safely into goblin territory and strike the trade
deals (vehicles, supplies) that drive much of the Floor 1 goblin storyline, including the events
leading to the [Goblin War Chieftain](../bosses/goblin-war-chieftain.md) fight.
