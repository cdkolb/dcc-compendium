# Enchanted Xistera Extension (Head-Throwing Attachment)

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Slot:** Xistera attachment (see [Xistera](xistera.md))
    - **Obtained:** Emergency Platinum Benefactor Box, from Carl's mysterious second sponsor, The
      Open Intellect Pacifist Action Network, Intergalactic NFC
    - **Unique item:** One-of-a-kind, custom-built specifically for Carl

## Effect

A wicker attachment that slots onto the end of Carl's [xistera](xistera.md), purpose-built to
throw decapitated withering-spirit heads:

> "Attaches to the end of a xistera. Allows for the tossing of a head. This special edition head
> tosser is custom made to fit decapitated love doll heads. When tossing a head of a withering
> spirit, the distance traveled will be greatly enhanced. (500 meters x Strength.)"

The tossed head is magically returned to Carl's inventory once the extension is removed from the
xistera.

## Scaling

Throw distance scales directly with Carl's Strength stat, multiplied by 500 meters — at his
Book-4 buffed Strength (over 100), this is enough to hurl [Samantha](../characters/samantha.md)
more than fifty kilometers in a single throw.

## Role in the Story

The item arrives moments before a feral demon (**Slit**, hunting Samantha specifically) reaches
Carl's location — the sponsor's emergency, extravagantly expensive box turns out to have been
built for this exact purpose. Carl uses it to hurl Samantha's head away from the fight as a
distraction/weapon, luring the demon off; a summoned god intercepts and kills the demon moments
later. Carl concludes the item was originally intended as a future-floor sponsor gift, rushed out
early because protecting him and Donut mattered more to the sponsor than the original timeline.
