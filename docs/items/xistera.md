# Xistera

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Slot:** Right wrist/forearm (retracts into a bracer, compatible with the War Gauntlet)
    - **Obtained:** A Gold Fan Box (voted on by Carl's own viewers) after passing 1 quadrillion views
    - **Type:** Non-magical, mechanical throwing tool

## Effect

A xistera is the curved wicker scoop used in the real-world sport of jai alai (Cesta Punta) —
Carl only recognizes what it is and how to use it because of the random skill granted by his
**Earth Hobby Potion**. Strapped on with two buckles, it lets him catch an item from his
inventory directly into the scoop and hurl it far harder and farther than a bare throw, using a
full-arm swinging motion. A pull-ring on the strap retracts the entire scoop into a metal bracer
that his [War Gauntlet](war-gauntlet-of-the-exalted-grull.md) fits over cleanly, so he can switch
between throwing and punching without removing either item.

## Scaling

Not an enchanted item, so it has no spell-style level curve — but its usefulness scales directly
with Carl's own physical stats (Strength, and later Dexterity for accuracy) and with the
**Explosives Handling**/**Dangerous Explosives Handling** skills he trains alongside it. By his
own account, accuracy improves the more he practices, and small, dense projectiles (a hob-lobber
bomb) work far better than irregular ammo like slingshot pellets, which the scoop can't properly
grip.

## Role in the Story

The xistera immediately replaces Carl's clurichaun slingshot as his primary ranged option,
letting him hurl hobgoblin "hob-lobber" bombs at roughly 250 mph — turning him into a genuine
ranged threat for the first time, not just a bare-knuckle brawler. Zev confirms on Carl's behalf
that "no more slingshot" is now official.
