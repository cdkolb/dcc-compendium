# Mysterious Bone Key

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Type:** Granted benefit of the
      [Scavenger's Daughter](the-scavengers-daughter.md) back patch
    - **Uses:** **One. Ever.**
    - **Effect:** Creates a one-time key that opens *any* lock in the dungeon
    - **Cost:** Destroys the patch **and** the jacket it's sewn to, and permanently consumes a bone
      from the user's body
    - **Status at end of Book 6:** Never used

## The Description

> **This benefit may only be used once for obvious reasons.**
>
> **Creates a one-time-use key to open a lock. Any lock. Any lock in the dungeon.**
>
> **WARNING!: (Notice how I used ALL CAPS for the warning?) Using this benefit will destroy both
> the patch *and* the item it is attached to. That means the jacket and all those ugly little
> patches inexpertly sewn to it.**
>
> **Uh, it also uses a bone from the user's body to create the key. The more difficult the lock,
> the bigger the bone it uses. It might just be a finger. Or your clavicle. Or your femur. No
> biggie.**
>
> **This bone will not grow back. So make sure it's a lock you really, really want to open.**

Mordecai doesn't know what the benefit is and has to have it read to him. Carl's response is
immediate: "*We're gonna mark that as a last resort item.*" He also half-remembers a note about a
similar item somewhere in
[The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook](the-dungeon-anarchists-cookbook.md) and means to go looking for
it — which he never gets around to.

## Scaling

The only scaling is inverse and unpleasant: **the harder the lock, the bigger the bone**. There is
no level, no training, and no way to improve the odds. The cost side is fixed and total — the patch
and the entire [Anarchist's Battle Rattle](enchanted-anarchists-battle-rattle.md) with every other
patch on it are destroyed regardless of which lock is opened.

## Role in the Story

The Bone Key hangs over the entire back half of Book 6 as the thing Carl refuses to use. Floor 8's
endgame turns on stairwell keys: there are fewer keys than squads, Carl gives his to Imani, and the
obvious way out for himself and Donut is the one benefit that opens any lock in the dungeon.

His friends find the idea intolerable. Imani's refusal to take his key comes with it attached:
"*Donut told me about your dumbass idea to use one of your own bones or whatever to get out. People
have bones for a reason, Carl. No. Like Elle said. It's all or nothing.*"

In the end the party solves the stairwell problem a different way — Carl's *Build Trench*
demolition plan to isolate and tunnel under the stairwell chambers — and the Bone Key is never
spent. It carries into Book 7 unused, along with the jacket it would destroy.

## Book 7: Absent

!!! success "Flag closed — but as a non-event"
    This page previously asked whether the Bone Key gets spent in Book 7. **It is not mentioned at
    all.** A case-insensitive search of the full text of *This Inevitable Ruin* returns **zero
    hits** for "bone key" (and zero for "battle rattle," the jacket it would destroy).

    The key is therefore **not spent**, and it carries forward into Book 8 still unused, along with
    the jacket and every patch on it. Do not read anything further into the silence: the book does
    not retire the benefit, re-describe it, rule it out, or have anyone consider using it. It simply
    never comes up.

    Floor 9's problem is not a locked door. Faction Wars is fought over territory, towers and
    trenches, and the one time the party needs to move a city they use
    *[Zerzura](../spells/zerzura.md)* rather than a key.

The jacket itself does appear in Book 7, though only obliquely and never by name — see
[Enchanted Anarchist's Battle Rattle](enchanted-anarchists-battle-rattle.md) for the two patches
Carl gains and refuses on Floor 9.

## Book 8: Still Absent

!!! success "Two books running, now — and still a non-event"
    Floors 10 and 11 give Carl every reason to want an any-lock key — a seven-heat vehicle race
    followed by a mandatory parade and a Dungeon Boss fight — and it still never comes up. A
    case-insensitive search of the full text of *A Parade of Horribles* returns **zero hits** for
    both "bone key" and "battle rattle."

    The Bone Key has therefore gone completely unused and unmentioned for **two books running**
    (Book 7 and Book 8), on top of the back half of Book 6 where it was first introduced and
    refused. Do not infer a resolution, a retirement, or a reason from this. The book simply never
    brings it up again — no locked doors force the question this time either, and neither Carl nor
    anyone else references the jacket, the patch, or the missing bone it would cost.

    This remains an open thread. Whoever processes Book 9 should keep checking.
