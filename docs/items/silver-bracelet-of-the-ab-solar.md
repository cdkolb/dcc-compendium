# Enchanted Silver Bracelet of the Ab-solar

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut
    - **Slot:** Leg bracelet
    - **Obtained:** Gold Benefactor Box from the Apothecary, delivered mid-Floor 6

## Effect

- **Protection against Vampirism**
- **Protection against Lycanthropy**
- **+25% Magical damage against all sapient undead**

## Scaling

Not described — presented as a flat, one-time set of protections.

## Role in the Story

Donut receives this bracelet from a Gold Benefactor Box sent by the Apothecary; in the very same
box-opening session, Carl separately receives an identical set of bonuses via a jacket patch (the
"Midnight Epicure" patch — see
[Enchanted Anarchist's Battle Rattle](enchanted-anarchists-battle-rattle.md)) from a *different*
sponsor's box, a Gold Benefactor Box from the Plenty. Mordecai reads the two sponsors handing out
matching anti-vampire/anti-lycanthropy protections to Carl and Donut at the same moment as clear
evidence "the Apothecary and the Plenty really are working together" — a coordinated warning about
something coming. That warning pays off almost
immediately: [Miriam Dom](../characters/miriam-dom.md) — already a vampire since an earlier floor —
accidentally infects the boss dinosaur Big Tina with her own vampirism while fighting alongside
Prepotente, and the curse spreads floor-wide through the local wildlife from there. Separately, an
elite vampire hunter named Viscount Fog (with his flying mount Sierra) specifically hunts Miriam
down as the outbreak's source; Sierra herself is injured and infected during one of these clashes,
later becoming the undead boss "Sierra – Northropi Vampire" that Carl and Donut kill. The bracelet
is never shown activating directly in combat, but its protections likely spare Donut from infection
during the vampire-plague sequence that follows, and the matching pair (Werecastors like Clint and
Holger are also encountered this floor) suggests both anti-vampirism and anti-lycanthropy
protection were deliberately bundled together.
