# Arrow of Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl — loots at least three separate copies over the course of the floor, from
      three different Dream elf hunters
    - **Slot:** Not equippable — a single-use thrown/melee weapon
    - **Obtained:** Looted from the corpses of hunters Arwick, an unnamed Dream artillery elf
      killed by one of Carl's homing missiles, and Akland — all from The Dream faction

## Effect

A regular arrow whose tip is dipped in a poison that inflicts **Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea**.
Per its own description:

> "This is a regular arrow, but the tip is dipped in a poison that will inflict you with
> Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea. Trust me on this. You don't want Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea.
> It doesn't kill you, but you'll want it to. It sets your genitals aflame. Literally. And then it
> heals that area of your body over and over. The only way to remove the disease is to, uh, geld
> yourself. Or pass it on to someone else."

## Scaling

No skill-level scaling described — the debuff is a flat, permanent affliction until cured by one
of its two listed methods.

## Role in the Story

All three copies were originally intended by Dream hunters as an insult weapon meant for the
crawler Louis, whose flirtation with a Dream leader's mother made him the faction's sole hunting
target on the floor. Carl loots the first from Arwick, a Dream hunter accidentally killed by the
elite ogre Areson while trying to take him alive on Carl's orders; the second from an unnamed Dream
artillery elf killed by one of Carl's own homing missiles during an attack on Point Mongo; and the
third from that elf's captured partner, Akland, whom Carl personally marks with the Ring of Divine
Suffering and interrogates before killing.

At the climax of a running duel with the mantis hunter **[Vrah](../characters/vrah.md)**, Carl
jams the arrow into her exposed neck moments before she can escape — a deliberate choice over an
outright kill, reasoning that as heir to a hive queen, Vrah cannot geld herself without
disqualifying her succession claim, and mantis mating customs make finding a consenting partner to
pass the disease to both socially and legally fraught. The gambit cripples Vrah for most of the
rest of Floor 6 and indirectly draws the goddess Diwata into the plot when her mother, Circe Took,
intervenes to cure her.
