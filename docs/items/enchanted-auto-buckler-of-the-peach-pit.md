# Enchanted Auto Buckler of the Peach Pit

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Katia, then Carl (Book 3)
    - **Slot:** Wrist bracer that forms a shield on command
    - **Obtained:** Not described; already in Katia's possession before Book 3, transferred to Carl once her new [Riot Shield](enchanted-shade-gnoll-riot-forces-crowd-control-shield.md) makes it redundant

## Effect

Forms a metallic buckler on command — via hotlist or by shaking the wrist. Enchanted with
**Shatter**: any attack landing on the shield has a 1.5% chance to disarm the opponent. If a
disarm triggers, there's a 90% chance the dropped weapon falls to the ground, a 5% chance it
breaks outright, and a 5% chance it teleports directly into the shield-wearer's own inventory.

## Scaling

No stated scaling — the 1.5% proc chance and its sub-outcome odds are flat values.

## Role in the Story

Katia mostly used the buckler passively, to add bulk to her mass rather than for its disarm
ability. She hands it off to Carl once her Book 3 fan-box shield renders it redundant (both
occupy the same arm slot); Carl equips it immediately and starts experimenting with the wrist-flick
activation.

## Book 6: Replaced

Carl never gets the Shatter proc to fire — "*it supposedly disarmed opponents 1.5% of the time, but
I'd never gotten it to work*." At the start of Floor 8 a Legendary Scourge Box delivers a strictly
better bracer for the same left-arm slot, the **Enchanted Right Back Atcha Personal Shield**, which
catches physical attacks by Dexterity and magic bolts by Intelligence, reflects 75% of what it
catches, and doubles the power of a reflected attack 25% of the time.

Mordecai's verdict is immediate: "*That's much better than your existing shield, and the bracer
matches your other one better.*" Carl pulls the Peach Pit off on the spot and adds it to the growing
pile of gear being stockpiled for the Faction Wars team. See the
[Book 6 item catalog](index.md#book-6-itemloot-catalog) for the replacement's full stat line.
