# Party Planner ("Casket in the Sky")

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** **Army Air General Louis Santiago**
    - **Type:** Tilt-rotor stealth light bomber. Counts as **tier-3 Armor** against the Princess
      Posse's Faction Wars vehicle limit
    - **Obtained:** Chapter 6, Louis's Celestial box
    - **Size:** ~**50 feet**, crew of **5**
    - **Signature stat:** **+50% yield** on every bomb it drops
    - **Fate:** **Destroyed in chapter 66**, ripped in half in mid-air by a gravity-magic dragon

Named for Firas's rejected suggestion for the Twister — "*I'd told him it was stupid*" — and powered,
like the Twister, by a silver-encased soul crystal. Elle's reaction on first sight: "*This makes
Carl's backpatch look like something he picked up at the dollar store.*"

"*If you happen to know anybody who can build you even better, custom-made bombs*" is the AI pointing
directly at Carl. See [The Waffle Maker and No Disassemble](the-waffle-maker.md).

## Specifications

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Length** | ~50 feet, "*about the length of a medium-sized private jet*" |
| **Fuselage** | Squished oval, ~8 feet tall; belly sits ~18 inches off the ground on chopper-style skids |
| **Interior** | "*just a hair too small for me to stand fully upright within*" |
| **Finish** | Silver metallic; heavily tinted glass nose reminiscent of a B-29; the entire underside appears to be glass |
| **Propulsion** | **Four propellers** that pop out of the top and sides in flight, stowed as rectangular protuberances. No visible wings, tail or rudder |
| **Crew** | **Five** — pilot, co-pilot/navigator, tail gunner, ball-turret gunner, bombardier. One person can fly it alone |
| **Defences** | Louis's *Protect Aircraft* shield, an anti-flak shield, upgradeable stealth. Door guns are a paid upgrade |
| **Support** | Requires a hangar upgrade and a hired mechanic gremlin |

Descriptions vary by who is looking at it. Donut: "*It looks like a submarine!*" Carl: a smaller-sized
bullet train car. Florin: "*It's like a half-sized, half-crew B-17 had a baby with one of those
next-gen stealth bombers.*" Carl's first reaction: "*Jesus, Louis. This thing looks like a death
trap.*"

Other models exist to buy or build; one is apparently called the **Dumbassmobile**.

It occupies one of the Posse's ten **Armor** slots. The other nine are Florin's flame spider tanks.

## Service History

- **Chapter 11** — inaugural flight to the base, carrying Katia, Bautista and a stowaway Samantha
- **Chapter 23** — southern-front bombing runs. Louis deliberately craters the ground *in front of*
  enemy trenches rather than hitting them directly, showering defenders with dirt and stripping traps
  and enhancements off the terrain
- **Chapter 25** — ferries Carl, Donut and Mongo
- **Chapter 50** — poison-cloud loitering over the naga stronghold
- **Chapter 59** — the first Reaver bomb run, with Carl and Samantha aboard. Survives a corkscrewing
  missile barrage despite the stealth failing. Bonnie and Skarn stow away on the door guns in direct
  violation of an explicit ban
- **Chapter 60** — the bombs-away moment: "*several spots on the ship's floor slid open at the same
  time. Tendrils of freezing mist curled up into the cabin as the airship bucked. Ching, ching, ching,
  ching. The square, shield and tech-busting **No Disassembles** clinked as they zipped out of the
  holders, pulled down by gravity, followed by the round high-yield **Waffle Makers**, which were
  shaped like tubes*"

## Destruction — Chapter 66

Killed by the gravity-magic dragon circling the Reaver honeycomb castle. Louis's chat message is the
whole of it, and it is a hard read:

> **Louis: A little busy now, Carl. Feather falling... Party Planner is dead. Ripped in half... Hit
> the dragon with missiles, and it just pissed it off... It cast a spell, and I tried to dodge. It
> ripped Party Planner down the middle. Dropped the bombs before they were armed. I'm sorry... They
> didn't go off. I'm falling through the air right now. Gonna land right on the Reaver castle... I
> think my door gunners are dead... Glad Bonnie and Skarn weren't here... He was right... That cop's
> husband. I'm sorry... The feral god is still there a mile west of the castle. I see him just sitting
> there. Dragon still flying above... I'm going to hit the castle by myself. Tell Katia when she wakes
> up that I'm sorry to be such a fuckup...**

Colonel Boomer had overridden Carl's instinct to call the run off. The unarmed bombs Louis dropped on
the way down are recovered by him on the castle roof and become, twenty chapters later, the remotely
detonated EMP that kills the Reaver robot force — see
[The Waffle Maker and No Disassemble](the-waffle-maker.md).

Louis's last word on it, chapter 87: "*I really wish I could've kept Party Planner.*"

**Florin later steals a Reaver replacement aircraft**, which is covered elsewhere.

## Scaling

No level. The two stated upgrade paths are **bomb-bay armament** (the +50% yield multiplies whatever
you load, so better bombs scale the whole aircraft) and **purchasable modules** — door guns, improved
stealth. The warning clause is the real ceiling: future floors may ban airships entirely.
