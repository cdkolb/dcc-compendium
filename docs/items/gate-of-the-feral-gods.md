# The Gate of the Feral Gods

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl (kept rather than destroyed)
    - **Tier:** Celestial-equivalent artifact — appearing three floors earlier than artifacts
      normally do (Mordecai says they usually don't show up until Floor 8 or 9)
    - **Composition:** Three separate pieces — two enchanted pocket watches and a wooden
      **winding box** — that must be reunited to function
    - **Origin:** Built by the ancient emperor Anser's court, on Floor 5's Necropolis of Anser

## Effect

The gate is a portal-opening device built around the mirrored pair of watches and the winding
box:

1. Dialing a time on one watch mirrors the same time onto the second, wherever it is
2. Placing both watches in the winding box and activating it "winds" the second watch's hands
   until they sync with the dialed destination time — the process can take moments or a full turn
   of the watch depending on distance
3. Removing the watches opens a **one-way portal** to the dialed destination, lasting roughly 20
   minutes or until the box and both watches are carried through it
4. The origin side of the portal remains open afterward as a **two-way gate into "the Nothing"**
   until a creature — usually a **feral god** — comes through from the other side; anyone still
   near the open gate when this happens risks "feral madness"

Per notes recovered from the Mad Dune Mage Ghazi, the winding box itself is *also* a standalone
conduit to the Nothing: winding it and feeding it charged energy opens a portal to the Nothing
directly, without needing a destination at all — this was how Ghazi accidentally summoned the
minor deity Psamathe (later hired as [Samantha](../characters/samantha.md)) instead of the god he
intended.

## Scaling

The gate has no combat stats — its power scales entirely with *what* comes through the far-side
portal. Per Ghazi's notes, nearly every use-combination results in something emerging on the
opening side; only one specific configuration (one watch on each side of the portal, with the
winding box itself passed through) avoids releasing anything at all, at the cost of destroying the
artifact.

## Role in the Story

The gate's three pieces are scattered between three of Floor 5's four factions: the changeling
principal Henrik carries one watch, gnomish Commandant Kane the other, and the Mad Dune Mage
Ghazi the winding box. Seven of the nine Floor 9 factions sue (unsuccessfully) to keep Carl from
assembling it, correctly guessing what he intends to do with it.

Once complete, Carl uses the gate twice:

- **Rescue operation:** Opening repeated short-range portals from stranded, doomed bubbles to Hump
  Town's Desperado Club, evacuating roughly 2,300 crawlers who would otherwise have died when
  their floor timer expired — at the cost of releasing a feral god into each abandoned bubble as
  the portal closes
- **The Larracos strike:** Rather than release a god onto the Floor 9 battlefield as everyone
  expects, Carl sends changeling ally [Juice Box](../characters/juice-box.md) safely through to
  Larracos's underground faction market while leaving the *second* watch behind — flooding the
  market with seawater, timed bombs, and sharks instead of summoning anything, crippling seven
  factions' ability to resupply their armies ahead of the Floor 9 wars

Mordecai repeatedly urges Carl to destroy the gate (using the "safe" configuration) rather than
keep it, warning that carrying a working portal to the Nothing is an active liability. Carl
refuses and keeps all three pieces in inventory as Floor 5 ends.
