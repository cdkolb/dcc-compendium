# Wand of Nighty-Night

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Type:** Wand, 10 charges
    - **Obtained:** Chapter 49, from an unnamed "wand box" opened alongside a batch of other Floor 8
      loot boxes
    - **Effect:** Casts a level-10 *Sleep* on a single **non-boss** target for five minutes

## Effect

A gnarled wooden wand. Its description is short and specific about its one trick:

> **Wand of Nighty-Night. 10 Charges.**
>
> **Knocks an opponent out onto their ass. Casts a level-10 *Sleep* on a non-boss, single target for
> a period of five minutes. Opponent will reawaken if they sustain physical damage. Multiple casts
> compounds the effect, which is why this wand is so fancy. If you don't know what that means, ask a
> math nerd.**

The target wakes up early if it takes physical damage, and stacking multiple casts on the same
target compounds the sleep effect rather than simply refreshing it — the wand doesn't explain how,
leaving it as one more "ask a math nerd" joke at the crawlers' expense.

## Scaling

No level-up path is given; it is a fixed level-10 effect with a flat 10-charge pool, not a
skill Carl trains.

## Role in the Story

Carl draws it, unused, at the climax of the hunt for Sister Ines Quiteria in chapter 65. She comes
around the corner already glowing and charging, screaming "Sinner!" and closing too fast for any
other plan:

> I pulled my new *Nighty-Night* wand. I opened the door to reveal a glowing Sister Ines, running
> full tilt down the hallway directly at us, screaming "Sinner!" at the top of her lungs. She was
> already too close.
>
> *"Gah!"* I zapped my wand, and she instantly went unconscious. She dropped and slid, coming to a
> stop at my feet.

The wand ends the fight before it can start — Sister Ines is executed unconscious a moment later by
Louis, which is what triggers the goddess Ysalte's entrance into the realm.
