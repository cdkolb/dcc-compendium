# Enchanted Mongoliensis Saddle

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut (rider); affixed to [Mongo](../characters/mongo.md) (mount)
    - **Obtained:** A fan-sponsored box, Book 3, adjusted to fit Donut's cat species
    - **Rider bonus:** +15% offensive spell damage, Anti-Piercing Resistance
    - **Mount bonus:** +20% Constitution, +20% melee damage while occupied

## Overview

A garish, tassel-covered saddle gifted by fans, magically self-affixing to Mongo and turning him
into a mount:

> "Magically affixes itself to the dreaded Mongoliensis, turning everyone's favorite murder
> chicken into a mount."

It has no straps or reins — just a seat and pommel — leaving Carl and Mordecai genuinely unsure
how Donut is meant to stay on or steer. Both Carl and Mordecai flag an obvious tactical problem:
the melee/Constitution bonus to Mongo is only active while occupied, but that means Donut has to
be sitting on him mid-fight to grant it, making her a much easier target. Mordecai's proposed
fix is training Donut to jump out of the saddle the instant Mongo attacks, to get the buff
window without staying exposed through the whole engagement.

## Scaling

No level-based scaling described — all four bonuses (rider and mount) appear to be flat,
static percentages tied to whether the saddle is occupied, not to any skill or item level.
