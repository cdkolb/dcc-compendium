# Quadruped Weapon Mount, "The Scorpion's Bite"

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut
    - **Type:** Non-magical bandolier accessory
    - **Obtained:** Chapter 86, from a Gold Benefactor Box sent by her sponsor, the Apothecary
    - **Effect:** Allows the mounting of multiple ranged weapons on her back; may fire and load via
      inventory action

## Effect

A simple mechanical harness rather than an enchanted item, delivered without fanfare mid-confrontation
with the goddess Huanxin:

> A strange device appeared on Donut's back. This was from the gold benefactor box she'd received
> from the Apothecary. A simple gift, but the implication was clear.
>
> **Quadruped weapon mount.**
>
> **"The scorpion's bite."**
>
> **Non-magical bandolier accessory. Allows for the mounting of multiple ranged weapons. May fire
> and load via inventory action.**

It immediately forms Katia's [Enchanted Repeating Crossbow of the Scavenger Mother of
Mothers](enchanted-repeating-crossbow-of-the-scavenger-mother-of-mothers.md) on Donut's back, "bigger
than Donut," attached with "an ominous click." Donut carries the weight without difficulty and keeps
moving.

## Scaling

No level or stat values are given; it is a fixed mechanical accessory, not a magical item that scales
with a skill or stat. Its only stated limitation is indirect: the mounted crossbow itself is weaker
without women in Donut's party, a property of the crossbow rather than the mount.

## Role in the Story

The mount lets Donut fire Katia's crossbow off her own back hands-free while she circles and
menaces the captured goddess Huanxin, stalking her "like liquid across the floor" while showing off
her new knee pads, her Claw skill upgrade (via her "champion shawl"), and her Monologue benefit in
the same scene. It is a purely practical, non-magical answer to a very magical fight — a strap that
lets a housecat carry and fire a crossbow bigger than she is.
