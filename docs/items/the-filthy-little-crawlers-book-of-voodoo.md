# The Filthy Little Crawler's Book of Voodoo

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owners:** Essentially **every crawler in the dungeon** — Carl, Donut, and the entire
      surviving field receive one
    - **Obtained:** A **Gold Prepotente Box** — a box type named after
      [Prepotente](../characters/prepotente.md) as a joke, because breaking Floor 7 upset the
      announcer Cascadia so badly
    - **Type:** Messaging artifact
    - **Restrictions:** Cannot be traded, cannot be destroyed, cannot be dropped (it returns to
      your inventory automatically)

## What It Is

A heavy, diary-like book of three or four hundred blank pages in a hard black leather cover with no
title. When Carl's box opens, it **screams**.

The name is a joke about pre-cellphone convention message boards: attendees' names were listed on a
big board, you stuck a pin next to someone's name to flag that you'd left them a note, and they
went to an alphabetical file to read it. "*Privacy be damned!*"

## How It Works

> **When you meet a crawler who owns this same item, their name will permanently appear in this
> enchanted book. You may use the book to exchange messages. You may write your message under their
> name, and only they will receive it. If you write under your own name, everyone who has your entry
> will see the message.**
>
> **This book is not retroactively filled with the entries of people you have already met. You must
> be within close-ish vicinity of them for their name to be added. Works only with crawlers who
> also own this book.**

So: **write under someone else's name for a private message, write under your own for a broadcast.**
Names only populate through in-person proximity, never retroactively. The book buzzes like a phone
when a message arrives, and opens itself to the relevant page. You can write manually or through
the messaging interface, and copy text out of it.

Carl's first message to Donut is "*You have bedhead, Donut.*" Her reply, in full caps, is a refusal
to ever use the thing: "*THIS IS A STEP BACKWARD, CARL. THIS IS THE EQUIVALENT OF CHATTING BY
LEAVING POST-ITS ON ONE'S LOCKER IN HIGH SCHOOL.*"

## Scaling

None. It has no level, no charges, and no upgrade path. Its only variable is how many names are in
it, which grows purely through meeting people face to face — and the book's real utility across
Book 6 turns out to be exactly that. It quietly builds a verified, in-person-only roster of
surviving crawlers, updating with entries for **Sister Ines Quiteria** and **Quan Ch** at
dramatically inconvenient moments, and it is what lets Carl broadcast the *Build Trench* stairwell
plan to the entire remaining field at the climax.

## The Obvious Setup

!!! warning "Flagged — unresolved, almost certainly deliberate"
    The item description goes out of its way to deny something nobody asked about:

    > **It was called the Voodoo board because of the pins. This book is the same thing. It's
    > called a book of Voodoo because it's named after the message board. Not because it can
    > secretly be used for any sort of nefarious purpose which will become clear later, so just get
    > that out of your head this instant, mister.**

    Carl notices. His own reaction: "*why would they give this to most everybody in the dungeon?
    The description was a little alarming. You never really knew when the AI was being serious or
    not.*"

    An undroppable, indestructible object that every crawler in the dungeon is carrying, with their
    true names written in it, distributed for free, by an AI that is openly telling you it has
    another purpose. **Book 6 does not reveal what that purpose is.** Do not guess.

## Book 7: The Other Purpose

The book's other purpose turns out to be **skill and spell distribution**. Written in the right
ink, a page of the Book of Voodoo can carry an ability from the person who wrote it to whoever eats
the page. Because the book broadcasts to every owner, that makes it the only mass-distribution
channel for abilities in the dungeon — the opposite of nefarious, in the end. What it enables in
chapter 85 is thousands of departing crawlers permanently giving up their own skills to arm the
people staying behind.

### The AI shouts about it for twenty chapters

Chapter 57, after Carl kills someone with an ability borrowed via
[tattoo](toraline-tattoo-transfer.md), the reward box is named:

> **Platinum Have You Goddamned Figured Out How To Use This In Conjunction With the Fucking Voodoo
> Book Yet? Jesus Christ I Can Only Help You So Much box.**

Carl does not figure it out. Edgar does, in chapter 74:

> "Rosetta was just telling Milk and myself about that book of Voodoo you have, and **I believe you
> may be able to share skills and spells that way as well, using the same ink. It will be more
> temporary.**"

And Milk states the distribution rule plainly in chapter 75, while handing Carl
[825 ml of Stayfast Ink and a fountain pen](stayfast-ink-and-adepts-fountain-pen.md):

> "He also has another book. One that all of you have. **The Book of Voodoo, I believe it is called.
> Anything he writes in it will be given to everybody who already has the book.** Best of luck to
> you."

### The book splits in two

Chapter 85, at the mass departure ceremony:

> **Your Book of Voodoo has been updated.**
>
> **Your Book of Voodoo has been updated with a metric fuckton of entries.**
>
> **For fucks sake. Finally.**
>
> **Your Book of Voodoo has been segmented into two volumes. The second volume is entitled Carl's
> Book of Boom.**

Volume two has its own page: **[Carl's Book of Boom](carls-book-of-boom.md)** — roughly 30,000 pages,
one per departing crawler, each granting that crawler's skill or spell temporarily when eaten. Donut
receives her own equivalent volume, **A Banquet Fit for a Princess**.

Crucially, each donor also wrote a **third** page onto their own entry in the *original* Book of
Voodoo, which Milk describes as "*a first come, first served sort of deal in the original book of
Voodoo that anyone can use.*" So volume one is no longer only a message board. It is now a shared,
open armoury for every crawler who owns a copy.

### Its ordinary use continues

The book remains the party's day-to-day comms channel throughout Floor 9 — Donut approves tattoo
designs through it, Katia sketches artwork in it, and war chat runs across it. Donut's Book 6 position
that it is "*A STEP BACKWARD, CARL*" does not survive her discovering she can use it to art-direct
other people's tattoos in all caps from several kilometres away.
