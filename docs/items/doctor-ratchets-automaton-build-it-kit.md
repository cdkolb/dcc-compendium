# Doctor Ratchet's Automaton Build-It Kit

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Obtained:** A **Legendary Scourge Box** — awarded for killing the most hunters on Floor 6 —
      which contained 20,000 gold, two crafting-table upgrade coupons, and this
    - **Form:** A large, ridiculously heavy steampunk chest that hisses with clockwork gears
    - **Contents:** Parts for **over fifteen automatons**

## Description

> **This kit contains enough items to create over fifteen automatons! Fun for the whole family!
> From personal servants to flame-throwing sentinel droids, this kit has a little of everything.
> The only limit is your imagination. And your insurance policy.**

It then lists off dozens of individual parts, several of which Carl recognises as exactly the
components he'd been missing for recipes in the level-5 Automaton crafting table and instruction
manual he stole from **Dr. Ratchet** at the Borant production facility in Book 5. His reaction is
one word: "*Excellent.*"

## Scaling

The kit itself doesn't level. What it unlocks is bounded by two other things: Carl's **automaton
crafting table** (level 5, stolen in Book 5, upgradeable with the coupons from the same box) and the
**trap skill** of whoever is affixing something to the finished robot. Carl's trap master skill lets
him put traps on non-static items; Donut's trap skill (level 3) does not, though her
**Automaton Pilot** skill (level 5) lets her affix a smaller set of traps to the robots' backs. The
parts are finite — fifteen-plus builds — and Carl spends down to his last automaton by the end of
the floor.

## Why It Matters: the Teleport-Trap Delivery System

This kit becomes the backbone of Carl's Part 3 tactics. The core insight is a chat message of his:

> **I can teleport if we use the automatons. We're doing it now. I can set the anchor on the back of
> the automaton and give it to someone else, and I can go wherever I want.**

Teleport traps are two-part devices. Bolting the **anchor** half to a small, mobile, disposable
spider automaton turns a static trap into a delivery vehicle, and from there into a personal
fast-travel and battlefield-insertion system. Confirmed uses in Book 6:

- **Door-opening bot** — a programmable automaton is sent unaccompanied down a hidden hallway inside
  Club Vanquisher on a sixty-second delay to dismantle Carl's wall bracers and release the vorpals,
  starting the Astrid assassination while he's on another floor
- **Suicide bomber** — a spider automaton with a snare on its back attaches itself to the demon
  **Minge** and detonates, splashing her with blood water from the Emberus shrine
- **Carried inside Samantha** — one spider, anchor-side up, is shoved into Samantha's severed limb
  so she can carry it somewhere Carl can't go
- **Emergency Loop-De-Loop** — a red-painted automaton carrying Donut's trap, which swaps her
  position with anyone who touches it. Donut's trap skill of 3 means she can only target crawlers,
  and only non-party ones; anyone else touching it makes it misfire
- **Breaking the demon out of the boss** — the last automaton in Carl's inventory carries **both**
  halves of a teleport trap, so that bumping into the *Madre de Aguas* triggers a teleport that goes
  nowhere. The snake stays put but still technically teleports, which is exactly enough to break
  Amayon out of the shell and force him into his true form
