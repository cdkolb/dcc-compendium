# Belt of Buoyancy

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut
    - **Slot:** Waist (a simple belt wrapped around her stomach)
    - **Obtained:** Chapter 23, from a silver "I'm Wet" box — a reward for the achievement **I was
      in the pool!** (surviving more than 60 seconds fully submerged)
    - **Effect:** Keeps the wearer from sinking; **only works in water**

## Effect

A plain, unenchanted-sounding belt with exactly one function:

> The I'm Wet box contained an additional ten scrolls of water breathing for each of us. Donut's
> box, for some unfathomable reason, was silver instead of bronze, and she received an additional
> item: a **Belt of Buoyancy**. It was a simple belt that wrapped around her stomach that'd keep her
> from sinking. It only worked in the water, and she spent a good three minutes bitching about it,
> about how she was never going back there again.

## Scaling

No level or stat-scaling is given; it is a flat, always-on buoyancy effect while worn, restricted to
water.

## Role in the Story

Donut's silver box is an upgrade over Carl's and Katia's matching bronze "I'm Wet" boxes, awarded
after the underwater necropolis fight against the sand-slime boss — a fight the party survives
without a proper boss box because the ooze itself was washed away rather than killed. The belt is
Donut's only individual prize from an otherwise disappointing haul, and she takes it as one more
grievance after being "buried alive, shot out of a water cannon, drowned, and then almost eaten by a
shark" for it.
