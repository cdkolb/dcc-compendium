# Enchanted Repeating Crossbow of the Scavenger Mother of Mothers

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Katia — looted from [Hekla](../characters/hekla.md)'s remains
    - **Slot:** Two-handed ranged weapon, **female-only**
    - **Obtained:** Found intact on the far side of a wall from Hekla's body after her death

## Effect

Per the item's own card:

> "This is a repeating, ranged weapon. It has the buffet enchantment, meaning it will not run
> out of basic ammunition. You may load and fire additional ammunition types to use with this
> weapon, though any special bonuses will only apply to the stock ammunition... This item may
> only be wielded by a female. For every female in your party, up to thirty, this item's damage
> and firing speed is increased by 25 percent. Your strength+level increases base damage 1.5X
> more than a standard crossbow."

- **+15 Dexterity** and **+10 Strength** while wielded
- Casts **Birth Defect** on monster types that generate or birth additional monsters (relevant
  against the Krakaren-spawned juveniles later on Floor 4)
- In practice, fires bolts at roughly two per second, auto-loading and auto-cocking between shots

## Scaling

The 25%-per-female party-member bonus scales linearly up to 30 women in the party. Separately,
effectiveness in Katia's hands is gated by her own **Crossbow** skill, still only level 4 by the
time she's using it against the Krakaren juveniles — enough to be useful, but not enough to
reliably punch through tougher, armor-like monster hide without many repeated hits.

## Role in the Story

The crossbow was Hekla's signature weapon throughout Books 1 and 2. Notably, Hekla herself never
personally kills crawlers — Carl observes no player-killer skulls over her at all; that grim work
is delegated to a lieutenant in her group, a woman marked with 13 skulls of her own. After Katia
accidentally kills Hekla with a mistimed Rush attack (see [Hekla](../characters/hekla.md)) and
her body is torn apart in the fight, Carl and Katia recover the crossbow intact from the far side
of a wall. It becomes Katia's primary ranged weapon for the rest of the floor — mounted on a
spider-leg tripod formed from her own Doppelganger mass in her "sentinel gun" combat
configuration — though Carl notes it won't perform as well for Katia as it did for Hekla, since
Katia lacks Hekla's stats and the female-party-bonus depends on a party composition Hekla had
deliberately built around herself.
