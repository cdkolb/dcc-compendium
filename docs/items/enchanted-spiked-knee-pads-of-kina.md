# Enchanted Spiked Knee Pads of the Munificent Goddess Kina

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owners:** **Katia** (chapter 56), then **Princess Donut** (by chapter 86)
    - **Rarity:** **Celestial** and **Divine**, unique
    - **Obtained:** Katia's Celestial **Who-Let-The-Gods-Out Box** from the Club Vanquisher assault
    - **Made of:** The eyelashes of the sea goddess **Kina**
    - **Grants:** 25% melee damage reflect, Breathe Underwater, level-15 Swim, level-10
      [Iron Stomach](../abilities/index.md#book-7-skill-catalog), level-10 Eater of the Dead
    - **As a divine item:** Gains varying extra benefits near deities

Everyone assumed these would end up on Carl. They end up on Donut, and they are what lets her shred
a CEO's tech shields in the last chapter.

## The Description

Note that the system's own title drops the "of":

> **The Enchanted Spiked Knee pads the Munificent Goddess Kina.**
>
> **This is a Unique item.**
>
> **This is a Divine Item.**
>
> **These are knee pads. They are literally made of the eyelashes of the sea goddess Kina, who is
> really just a weird, horny—and when I say horny, I am using both versions of that term—sea-urchin-
> like, bottom feeder of a goddess who is into some really weird stuff that makes even me blush.**
>
> **This item imbues the following effects:**
>
> **Plus 25% Damage Reflect for melee attacks.**
>
> **The Breathe Underwater benefit.**
>
> **Level 15 Swim skill.**
>
> **Level 10 Iron Stomach skill.**
>
> **Level 10 Eater of the Dead skill.**
>
> **As a divine item, These knee pads will imbue additional, varying benefits when they are in the
> presence of divine entities.**

The reveal happens on air, in a Kevin/Baroness Victory/Drick interview, and Katia does not even have
them on: "*I left them in the guild… I didn't know we were getting interviewed.*" An image is spun in
midair instead.

Drick's on-air read is that the award is a hint:

> "Katia's knee pads are a bit of a waste, considering that she's planning on leaving the
> dungeon—one way or another—pretty soon. This was clearly awarded to her in hopes that she'll give
> them to Carl to replace his existing knee pads. They even look similar."

He is half right. She gives them away. She gives them to Donut.

## Iron Stomach

The headline skill, and it is new to the setting. Mordecai had never heard of it; Prepotente already
has it.

**Iron Stomach lets you eat magical items.** The item is destroyed, and enough consumption
permanently raises Intelligence by one point. **At level 10 the ratio is one to one** — one item, one
point — and it becomes safe to eat **cursed items with no ill effects**.

Katia, who can shapeshift her mouth to any size and therefore swallow anything whole, immediately
exploits this:

> before she'd unequipped and given away the knee pads, [she] **had managed to gain 20 levels to her
> intelligence by eating random, throwaway items** before it became too much

Twenty Intelligence, from junk loot, in the time she had the pads on.

## Eater of the Dead

The same mechanic applied to **corpses**. Bodies must exceed a mass threshold, which decreases as the
skill levels. It requires considerably more consumption per stat point than Iron Stomach does, and
the stat raised is **random** between Constitution, Strength and Dexterity.

## Divine Item Stacking

Baroness Victory uses the pads to explain a rule the series had not previously stated. Divine-item
benefits are generally **identical between crawlers**, but:

- **Katia's benefits would be significantly more powerful in Kina's own presence** — and likewise
  amplified against Kina's enemies
- If a crawler holds **two divine items**, they do not stack: "*The more powerful of the two benefits
  will occur*"
- Matched divine armour **sets** exist

## Katia → Donut

The transfer is never dramatised, but it is confirmed in chapter 86, standing in front of the goddess
Eileithyia, in what is functionally a villain monologue:

> "And do you see my new knee pads? Aren't they lovely? **They're a celestial item I got from Katia,
> but they fit me.** I don't really need this with you, if we're being perfectly honest. But the
> Eileithyia boon is a 500% bonus against enemies. And because I'm partied with Carl, his bonus gives
> me another 500%. I don't have the same debuff he has because I'm a girl."

The divine-proximity clause is doing exactly what the description promised, and Donut has done the
arithmetic in advance. With the Eileithyia buff stacked twice — once as a female combatant, once via
Carl's party bonus — she crosses the circle faster than Carl can see and strips every tech shield off
Huanxin Jinx with [Claw](../abilities/claw.md). Her own verdict: "*that wasn't necessary. I'm just
showing off a little.*"

See [Crown of the Sepsis Whore](crown-of-the-sepsis-whore.md) for the full sequence.
