# Enchanted Anklet of the Fallen Oak

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut
    - **Slot:** Ankle (worn on a rear leg to keep it hidden in fur)
    - **Obtained:** Looted from a dead crawler (Nica, one of three sister crawlers killed by
      circus lemurs) on Floor 3

## Effect

**+1 Dexterity, +1 Constitution, +3 to the Double Tap skill** (a chance for a second consecutive
hit with the same attack to land as a critical).

## Scaling

No skill-level scaling described — the item's bonuses are flat.

## Role in the Story

Donut initially refuses it on aesthetic grounds ("Anklets are for the foot model, not me") but
accepts it once Carl points out her Constitution is dangerously low. It's a minor but early
answer to the party's running problem of Donut's fragility.
