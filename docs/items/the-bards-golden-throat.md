# The Bard's Golden Throat

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut
    - **Slot:** **Face accessory** — explicitly *not* a hat (Mordecai: "*It's a face accessory.
      Like your bandana*"), so it stacks with whatever tiara she's wearing
    - **Obtained:** A **fan box** — the item was voted for by her fanbase, the **Princess Posse**
    - **Appearance:** A golden, amethyst-encrusted headset microphone
    - **Effects:** Auto-tune within 1.5 steps, assistance with all bard songs and spells, up to 5×
      amplification, +5% Charisma

## Why It Exists

Donut's Legendary Diva kit from Book 5 has always had one crippling flaw: she cannot hold a pitch,
so her songs frequently fail to cast. The Posse fixes it by voting her a microphone.

Her reaction on opening the box is unrestrained — "*Oh my god. Oh my god! Thank you! Thank you!...
Carl, look! Look what my Princess Posse voted on for me!*" — and she is delighted that it has a
purple jewel: "*I'm going to look just like Britney! The real Britney, not the crawler.*" Mordecai's
reaction is relief: "*Oh, thank the gods... It's a good one. Sort of.*"

## Effects

The description is a history of Antares Audio Technologies and the 1997 release of AutoTune,
credited by the AI as the greatest invention of the twentieth century for curing "the disability"
of tone deafness. Then:

> **The Golden Throat enhancement. Automatically tunes songs within one and a half steps of correct
> note. Assists in the proper formation of all bard-based songs and spells.**
>
> **Amplification. While equipped, all spoken words and songs are amplified at twice the normal
> volume. This can be increased to up to five times of top volume.**
>
> **Charisma plus 5% when equipped.**

The moment she equips it her voice modulates and doubles in volume; her own assessment is "*it
sounds like I'm talking through a fan.*"

## Scaling

No level scaling. The two numeric limits are fixed: **1.5 steps** of pitch correction and **5×**
maximum amplification. The Charisma bonus is a flat percentage, so it does grow in absolute terms
as Donut's already-enormous Charisma climbs — relevant because Charisma governs the casting time
and area of *[Minion Army](../spells/minion-army.md)* and the reach of her charm effects.

Mordecai's advice is to keep it in her inventory and equip it only when singing. Carl's is blunter:
"*let's keep that as a secret weapon.*" Donut compromises by keeping it in her hotlist "*when I
need a little extra oomph. Or when you're not paying attention to me properly.*"

## Role in the Story

Beyond fixing her songs, the microphone turns Donut into the party's battlefield PA system. With
amplification cranked she can issue orders across an entire street fight, address massed NPC and
mercenary forces, and be heard over a hailstorm — a role that matters increasingly as the guild's
Floor 8 engagements scale from six-person skirmishes to coordinated assaults with dozens of
conscripted troops.
