# Enchanted Wrestling Belt of the Great Gorgo

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Katia
    - **Slot:** Waist
    - **Obtained:** Platinum Slam Master fan box, awarded for killing a set number of mobs using momentum-based attacks

## Effect

- **+5% Strength**
- **+5% Constitution**
- **The Avalanche benefit**

**Avalanche:** if the wearer hits a living creature with their own body while moving, the force
exerted is as if they'd been hit with twice their actual mass. Requires the wearer's entire body
to be in motion; the bonus does not transfer to weapons.

## Scaling

The stat bonuses are flat percentages of Katia's own (very high) Constitution and Strength, so
their effective value scales directly with her stat growth. The Avalanche benefit doesn't have a
stated numeric scaling, but synergizes directly with her momentum-based **Rush**/[Crowd Blast](../abilities/rush.md)
ability — a fact Carl privately notes without saying out loud, given Katia's discomfort with the
skill after using it to kill Hekla.

## Role in the Story

Awarded via fan vote alongside the [Riot Shield](enchanted-shade-gnoll-riot-forces-crowd-control-shield.md)
and [Riot Baton](enchanted-shade-gnoll-riot-forces-telescoping-crowd-control-baton.md), all three
clearly a comedic-but-effective nod to Katia's train-cowcatcher moment against
[Hekla](../characters/hekla.md). Its Avalanche effect doubles down on exactly the kind of
body-as-weapon combat Katia has already improvised on her own.
