# Spunky Jefferson the Enchanted Nickel Sock of the Elderly Miser

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** One-handed flail-equivalent — a white tube sock with red and blue stripes, weighted
      with several dollars' worth of nickels. Old, crusty, and blood-splattered
    - **Traits:** **Sapient** (it talks) and **incremental-damage** (it grows stronger with every
      kill). Already upgraded twice
    - **Origin:** Originally awarded to a crawler in a *Legendary You-Know-Everybody-Can-See-You-
      Right? Box* on Floor 5
    - **Obtained by Carl:** Looted from **Quan Ch**'s body in Havana (ch. 60; Quan dies at the end
      of ch. 58, and Carl strips the corpse after Tserendolgor's last stand)
    - **Current wielder:** **Dong Quixote**, the elderly Club Vanquisher dancer turned mercenary,
      to whom Carl gives it outright

## Lore

Jefferson is the life force of a miser who angered the spirit sent to show him the error of his
ways, and was cursed to inhabit a sock once owned by a teenage boy named Tanner. The AI's
description makes the sock's provenance clear and then moves briskly on: "*It's unusually
effective, more so than you might think.*"

## Effects

> **This weapon's damage increases by 1.5% for every mob it kills that is no more than 10 levels
> below the current wielder's level. Current upgrade: 238% damage increase.**
>
> **Increases wielder's dexterity by 10 points plus 10%**
>
> **Has a 5% chance to inflict *Bonked* on any mob struck with this weapon.**
>
> **All mobs killed with this weapon have a 20% chance to burst into a pile of gold coins upon
> death. The number of coins is equal to the (value of the items in their inventory)+(level x 10).**

## Scaling

This is the interesting part. Damage is **permanent and cumulative**: +1.5% per qualifying kill,
with no stated ceiling. The qualifier is the throttle — the kill must be within 10 levels of the
*current wielder's* level, so grinding trash mobs gives nothing and the weapon only grows when it's
being used on real threats by someone whose own level is keeping pace.

At the point Carl reads it, the sock is sitting at **+238% damage increase** — accumulated across
at least two previous owners and two upgrades before it ever reached him. Every subsequent
qualifying kill Dong Quixote makes pushes it higher.

The gold clause scales with the victim rather than the weapon: **(value of items in inventory) +
(level × 10)** coins, 20% of the time.

## Sapience

Carl's chat exchange on discovering the tag:

> **Carl:** Is a sapient weapon what I think it is?
>
> **Mordecai:** Oh gods. Yes. They can talk, but it's usually only in the mind of the wielder.
> Though some do talk out loud. They tend to be quite annoying. Samantha-level annoying, but
> luckily they're treated as objects, and you can keep them in your inventory if they won't stop
> yammering. I suspect Florin's shotgun might be sapient, but he hasn't said one way or another.

Dong Quixote, who had asked Carl for a one-handed flail and gets this instead, receives it
reverently — "*My lord, you honor me... This is a mythical weapon*" — and immediately falls into
conversation with it, one side of which the party can hear:

> "Yes. Yes. My name is Dong Quixote... Yes. Jefferson? It's nice to meet you... Yes. We are going
> to. I am a former adventurer turned entertainer turned mercenary... Yes. About five gold coins
> per dance, but more if we moved it to the cum closet... I do not know what a mutual fund is.
> Why?"

Katia's only question is why it smells.
