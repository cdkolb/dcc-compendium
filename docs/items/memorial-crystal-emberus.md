# Memorial Crystal: Emberus

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Type:** Memorial crystal — see
      [Soul Crystals & Memorial Crystals](../mechanics/index.md#soul-crystals-memorial-crystals-book-5)
      for the general mechanic
    - **Formed from:** The death of the god [Emberus](../bosses/emberus.md), Floor 11 arena climax
    - **Looted:** Moments after the kill, half-buried in the arena dirt
    - **Description:** *"Memorial Crystal. Emberus."* — nothing further given

## What It Is

Memorial crystals form from the deaths of gods and demigods (as distinct from ordinary **soul
crystals**, mined rather than dropped). Per the mechanic established in Book 5, a memorial crystal
stores its source's god-tier spells and knowledge, extractable by charging it through continued use
in combat, or worn as jewelry, or installed on a weapon or armor.

## How Carl Gets It

Immediately after [Taranis](../bosses/taranis.md#killing-emberus) kills his own brother Emberus at
the Floor 11 arena climax — see [Emberus](../bosses/emberus.md#pinned-and-killed-by-his-own-brother)
for the kill itself, which this page does not repeat — Carl steps on something half-buried in the
dirt:

> As I shifted, I stepped on something in the dirt. Just sitting there half-buried was an amber-hued
> glowing crystal. I reached down to pick it up.
>
> **Memorial Crystal. Emberus.**
>
> I took it into my inventory.

Nothing else is examined or extracted from it on the page. Carl pockets it and moves on almost
immediately — in the same breath, he discovers the [Tracking Tag ("The Kyryap has claimed
you")](tracking-tag-kyryap.md) planted on his own leg, and the scene shifts before either item gets
a second look.

## The Apito-Family Memorial Crystal Subplot

This is not the only memorial crystal connected to Carl's own family of gods. Emberus is Apito's
son, which makes this the **second** Apito-family memorial crystal in play by the end of Book 8 —
already covered in detail on the Characters pass and cross-linked here rather than repeated:

- [Prepotente](../characters/prepotente.md#the-crystal) holds **Queen Imogen's memorial crystal**
  — Apito's own — passed to him by Osvaldo out of exhaustion and spite rather than generosity, and
  central to Carl's own long-running Emberus-quest thread.
- [Osvaldo](../characters/osvaldo.md) is the crossed thread underneath that handoff: two separate,
  non-functional memorial crystals, a stolen one and a chased one, and a standing quest asking why
  memorial crystals in general "aren't working right."
- [Taranis](../bosses/taranis.md#the-crystal-the-rejection-and-leaving-with-samantha) loses his own
  memorial crystal to an unnamed thief mid-book — a third crystal in the same family orbit, stolen
  rather than looted.
- [Britney Proskurina](../characters/britney.md) ends up holding **Ysalte's** memorial crystal, the
  one Osvaldo had been chasing all book — a fourth data point in the same subplot.

Book 8 never draws these four crystals together into a single explanation of what's wrong with the
Apito pantheon's memorial crystals specifically, or why so many are changing hands at once. Treat
that as ongoing Characters-pass territory rather than something to resolve here.

## Status at Book's End

Sitting unopened and unexamined in Carl's inventory. No spells or knowledge have been extracted from
it on the page. Whether Emberus's death (see
[Emberus's own page](../bosses/emberus.md#permanence-not-confirmed) for the "is he actually dead"
question) is even permanent enough for the crystal's contents to matter is itself an open question.
