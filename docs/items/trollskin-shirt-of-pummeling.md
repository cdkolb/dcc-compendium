# Enchanted Trollskin Shirt of Pummeling

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Slot:** Under-armor shirt
    - **Obtained:** Gold Apparel Box, opened during the Floor 1 tutorial
    - **Rarity note:** Mordecai calls it something that would normally come from a Platinum or Legendary box — an early sign the loot system is quietly favoring Carl

## Effect

**+7 to the Regeneration skill.** All melee-based damage debuffs — Stun, Knockback, Disarm, and
Out-of-Breath — are automatically negated.

The in-game description notes it will not provoke a negative reaction from trolls despite being
made from troll skin — the opposite, in fact ("lady trolls might just want to haul you away").

## Scaling

The shirt grants a flat +7 to Regeneration rather than scaling with anything Carl does — the book
doesn't describe a level-by-level curve for the Regeneration skill itself, only the effect at the
level this item happens to grant: per Mordecai, a level-7 Regeneration means Carl is back to full
health in as little as two minutes after taking damage, making it one of the best under-armor
tanking pieces available this early in the game.

## Role in the Story

This shirt is one of the two pieces of gear (alongside the Nightgaunt Cloak) that immediately mark
Carl as having gotten unusually lucky with his very first loot boxes. Its regeneration bonus is
what allows him to survive repeated brutal hits throughout Book 1 — including broken bones from
punching bosses barehanded — without needing to burn through healing potions as fast as he
otherwise would.

## Future Enhancements

!!! note "Confirmed — Book 3"
    In Book 3 (*The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*), Carl applies a **Scroll of Upgrade** looted
    from a boss box directly to this shirt, permanently adding **+3 Dexterity** on top of its
    existing Regeneration and status-negation effects.
