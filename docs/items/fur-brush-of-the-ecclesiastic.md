# Enchanted Fur Brush of the Ecclesiastic

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut
    - **Type:** Grooming tool (used by Carl on Donut, not worn/equipped by her directly)
    - **Obtained:** A boss box, late in Book 1
    - **Functional analog:** Works like Carl's Enchanted Pedicure Kit of the Sylph (see [items catalog](index.md))

## Effect

**+2 Constitution for 30 hours**, granted as long as Donut is brushed for **10 minutes every
night**. Even with the buff active, her Constitution only reaches 4 — reflecting how low her base
Constitution is under the Enhanced Growth buff (see [Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md)).

## Scaling

None described — a flat, time-limited buff with a fixed duration and fixed bonus, not something
that scales with a skill level.

## Role in the Story

A small but meaningful item given how fragile Donut's Constitution otherwise is — it's one of the
few ways the pair have found to meaningfully improve her survivability rather than just her
damage output.

    Later books haven't been checked yet for whether this item gets upgraded or replaced.
