# Zhang's Cape of Odds

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** **Zhang**
    - **Rarity:** **Celestial**, unique
    - **Obtained:** Zhang's Celestial box from the Club Vanquisher assault
    - **Passive:** **+15% to all stats** and a **level-10 Dodge skill**
    - **Active:** **Roll the Dice** — once an hour, one of **333** outcomes, **66 of which are bad**
    - **The catch that makes it Celestial:** After **19 spins**, you may pick the outcome instead
    - **Never voluntarily used**

!!! note "Exact-verification note"
    **No system description block exists for this item in Book 7.** Everything below is Carl's
    narration on seeing the cape, quoted at length because it is the only account there is.

## Appearance

White, with the standard black, blue, red and yellow concentric circles of an **archery target** on
the back. Zhang wears it proudly. It was first mentioned a chapter earlier, on a broadcast, simply as
"*Zhang's new cape with the target on the back.*"

## What It Does

> It was a great, but dangerous, item. It was a unique item called Zhang's Cape of Odds. **When it
> wasn't activated, it gave him plus 15% across the board for all his stats plus a level 10 Dodge
> skill. His skill in that was already 11, so it brought it to 14.**
>
> But it also came with **a once-an-hour skill called Roll the Dice**, which reminded me of the Wheel
> of Fortune game at the casino. If he activated the skill, **one of 333 different outcomes could
> appear.** Most of the outcomes were great, such as doubled strength for ten minutes. The ability to
> fly for an hour, etc. There were a few really epic outcomes, as well, such as **Death Ray** and
> several various summons.
>
> But there were **66 outcomes that were bad**. None of them killed him outright, and all were
> temporary, but there was **paralysis**, **all stats are now 5**, and something called "**It's been
> four hours and I think it's time to call the doctor**" which sounded ominous as fuck. **The worst
> was "Summon the God Who Hates You Most."**
>
> But, and here was where the cape was especially insidious—and a true Celestial item—was that
> **after 19 spins, you could manually pick one of the choices.**
>
> Zhang wore the cape proudly, but **he said he wasn't going to actually use it unless absolutely
> necessary.**

## Why Nobody Uses It

The maths is the trap. **66 of 333 is just under one in five.** Reaching the 19-spin threshold that
unlocks manual selection means accepting, on average, roughly **four bad outcomes** along the way —
and one of the possible bad outcomes is *Summon the God Who Hates You Most*, on a floor where gods are
already walking around in the open killing people.

So the cape's best feature is gated behind a gauntlet nobody in a war zone can afford to run, and its
passive alone (+15% all stats, +3 effective Dodge) is already excellent. Zhang wears it and does not
touch it.

Note also the **stacking rule** the Dodge grant demonstrates: Zhang's own Dodge was 11, and a level-10
item skill takes him to **14**, not 21. Item-granted skill levels are added to the base as a partial
bonus, not substituted — and consistent with the series-wide behaviour, the total stalls just short of
15.

## Scaling

No level. The only progression is the **spin counter**, which is cumulative and one-way:

| Spins | State |
|---|---|
| 1–18 | Random. Roughly 80% good, 20% bad |
| **19+** | The wearer may **choose** the outcome |
