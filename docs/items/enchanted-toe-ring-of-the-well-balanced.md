# Enchanted Toe Ring of the Well-Balanced

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Slot:** **Left pinky toe.** The description is emphatic about this
    - **Obtained:** Chapter 4, from Carl's post-Floor-8 unboxing
    - **Grants:** Three linked sub-skills — **Heart Balance**, **Mind Balance**, **Physical
      Balance**, all at level 5
    - **Why it matters:** **Mind Balance** is what has been silently stopping Shi Maria from seizing
      Carl's body, and it becomes the first skill in the series trained past level 15 by battlefield
      conditions

The AI's framing in the description is unusually direct about the ring's purpose, and it is not a
compliment.

## The Description

> **Enchanted Toe Ring of the Well-Balanced.**
>
> **This special, dainty, perfectly delightful toe ring (which, by the way, must be equipped on your
> left pinky toe. Don't test me on this) represents balance. Balance in heart, mind, and your
> physical core. Yeah, I know that sounds like some yoga bullshit, but this has been given to you to
> save your ass because you're too dumb to realize how much danger you're in.**
>
> **When properly equipped, (again, left pinky) this toe ring imbues the following effects:**
>
> **Heart Balance, Level 5. Allows you to store one auto-trigger potion item if health reaches a
> certain criteria.**
>
> **Mind Balance, Level 5. Allows protection from Enslavement and Conscription.**
>
> **Physical Balance, Level 5. At level five, this skill imbues both the Extra Steady Balance skill
> and the Walk on Air Skill (already obtained.)**

"*Because you're too dumb to realize how much danger you're in*" is not a joke. Carl is at that
moment carrying a sealed demigod in his chest who has been trying to drive his body for weeks.

## Heart Balance

A stored auto-potion. On equipping, Carl is prompted to select a potion and a trigger condition; he
picks **a gold-standard healing potion, set to fire at 2% health.**

That is the whole of it — one potion, one threshold, fired automatically. It is the least discussed
of the three and the only one that never surprises anyone.

## Mind Balance

> **Mind Balance, Level 5. Allows protection from Enslavement and Conscription.**

Followed, immediately after equipping, by:

> **Mind Balance is currently triggered.**

No further information. Carl does not find out what that means for another ten chapters.

See **[Mind Balance](../abilities/mind-balance.md)** for the full account — the hundreds of blocked
Shi Maria casts, the silent arms race, and the level-16 break. The short version:

- It had been **triggered from the moment he put the ring on**, because Shi Maria never stopped trying
- It blocks *[Conscript](../spells/conscript.md)*, the defining threat of Floor 9
- It levels through use, and use is involuntary — every attempt Shi Maria makes trains it
- **The skill outgrows the ring.** By chapter 14 Carl notes "*now it was trained up so much, I'd still
  have the skill, minus five levels, even if I took the ring off. I didn't dare.*"
- At **level 16** it negates *[Bear Witness](../spells/bear-witness.md)*, a level-20 divine ceremony

## Physical Balance

Two granted abilities, and the second one detonates a Book 6 plot thread in Carl's face:

> **Physical Balance, Level 5. At level five, this skill imbues both the Extra Steady Balance skill
> and the Walk on Air Skill (already obtained.)**

**Extra Steady Balance** is never separately described. **Walk on Air** is the problem:

> "Already obtained?" I asked out loud. That didn't make sense. I moved into my menu. I knew what
> *Walk on Air* did, but I didn't have that skill. **It was what Li Jun…**
>
> I swallowed, suddenly feeling very, very dirty.
>
> It was what Li Jun used when he fought.

What follows is the memory of the taste of Li Jun's eye, and Shi Maria's line from Book 6 — "*you may
gain some of your friend's powers, too, with the blood on the needle.*" Carl has had **Walk on Air**
since that night, taken from a friend by violence, and had never once checked. He messages Li Jun to
apologise and gets no answer.

This is also the first hard evidence of the mechanic that
[the toraline ink tattoo system](toraline-tattoo-transfer.md) is built on: blood taken by force
transfers abilities permanently. The party works the rule out backward from this one line of item
text.

See [Walk on Air](../abilities/walk-on-air.md) for the skill itself.

## Scaling

| Sub-skill | Starting level | Observed progression |
|---|---|---|
| Heart Balance | 5 | No change shown |
| **Mind Balance** | 5 | → **9** (ch. 14) → **16** (ch. 65) |
| Physical Balance | 5 | No change shown |

Only **Mind Balance** is shown to level, and it does so because it is under constant involuntary
attack. Once trained above the ring's own level-5 grant, the skill is partially retained even without
the ring, at **minus five levels**.

!!! note "Exact-verification note"
    **Only three Balance sub-skills exist in Book 7** — Heart, Mind and Physical. "Extra Steady
    Balance" is granted *by* Physical Balance and is not a fourth sub-skill. No others are named.
