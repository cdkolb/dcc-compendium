# Enchanted Tooth Cap of Hi-Yo Silver

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Wearer:** **Rend**, Carl's Tummy Acher pet
    - **Type:** Tooth cap, designed for **mounts** — similar to Mongo's fang caps
    - **Obtained:** Chapter 65. "*Someone had donated a tooth cap*"
    - **Effect:** Summons the pet to its owner's position across up to **5 kilometres**, at great
      speed, **through walls**
    - **Cooldown:** **Three minutes**
    - **Not a teleport.** The pet physically travels the distance
    - **Donut refuses to put one on Mongo**

!!! note "Exact-verification note"
    **No system description block exists.** The single narrated paragraph below is the entire
    description the book gives. The ability is cast by name as **"Hi-Yo Silver."**

## The Description

> Someone had donated a tooth cap, similar to the ones Mongo had for his sharp teeth, but this was
> designed for mounts. It was called **The Enchanted Tooth Cap of Hi-Yo Silver**. We installed it over
> his single, round tooth, making the tooth gleam silver. **It allowed me to summon Rend to my
> position. It wasn't a teleport spell, but it had about a five kilometer range. The cooldown was only
> three minutes. When I summoned him, he'd get inexorably pulled toward me at great speed. If there
> was a wall in the way, he'd—possibly—smash through the wall. The skill was somewhat dangerous which
> was why Donut absolutely refused to put it on Mongo. But for Rend, it would work well, especially
> when combined with his new Wrecking Ball special ability.**

The distinction between "summon" and "teleport" is the whole item. The pet does not vanish and
reappear — it is **dragged bodily across up to five kilometres of terrain**, and anything between the
pet and the owner is a problem for that thing rather than for the pet. "*Possibly* smash through the
wall" is the part Donut objected to.

## Why It Works on Rend and Not Mongo

**Rend** is a **Tummy Acher**, also called a Belly Acher or Colon Worm. The species description from
chapter 5:

> Tummy Achers… are named such because they were originally parasites, living in the intestinal tracts
> of Jotan-class Titans. They are generally good-natured, though infants can be a little... difficult
> to control. They are voracious eaters, and when they reach full size can swallow just about
> anything. **They have multiple tank-based special abilities and are generally resistant to most
> types of damage. They can survive in almost any environment.** In addition, **while not necessarily
> known for their offensive skills, their Wrecking Ball attack is considered a terror on any
> battlefield.**

So the cap's one genuine hazard — that the pet may be flung through solid walls to reach you — is
irrelevant to a creature that is a rolling armoured tank and delighted about it. On a velociraptor it
would be a way to kill the velociraptor.

## The Combination

The tooth cap alone is a recall tool. Paired with **[Wrecking Ball](../abilities/wrecking-ball.md)**
it becomes a weapon, because *Wrecking Ball* scales with **mass and speed** — and the cap supplies the
speed for free.

Carl uses it in chapter 72, at almost point-blank range, while being blown off his feet by a gale:

> "**Wrecking Ball!**" I wheezed as I cast *Hi-Yo Silver* a second time. Another gale hit me, and I
> spun head over heels landing on my stomach.
>
> I watched, as if in slow motion, as Rend started rolling toward me. And as he was magically pulled
> in my direction, **he activated the only skill he had. Wrecking Ball. The special attack increased
> his mass and speed, and it did exactly what the name implied. The back half of Gustavo 3, the second
> of Lucia Mar's two rottweilers, terror of the dungeon, exploded in a red mist as Rend rushed right
> up to me, stopping inches from my face.**

Carl aimed a pet at himself, from behind an enemy, and let the item do the aiming. The summon is
targeted at *the owner*, so the way to fire Rend at something is to put yourself on the far side of it.
