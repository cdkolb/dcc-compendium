# Prism Industries Capacitating and Focusing Goggles

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut
    - **Slot:** Eyewear
    - **Obtained:** Gift from Princess D'Nadia of the Prism Kingdom

## Effect

A one-of-a-kind gift, personally signed by Princess D'Nadia. Per its own card:

> "Protects the eyes from a variety of environmental hazards, including magic-based attacks that
> render the user blind. Enhances all dark vision effects. Allows for heat vision and other
> visible-light viewing options. In addition, any energy-based spell that originates from the
> ocular region is caught and focused by these goggles, which allows for multiple combat and
> targeting options."

In practice this gives Donut three confirmed uses:

- **Enhanced vision** — lets her spot [Hekla](../characters/hekla.md)'s invisible "Dirty Little
  Phased Bolt" ammunition being fired into Katia, which is what exposes the plot in time to save her
- **Multi-shot casting** — turns her [Magic Missile](../spells/magic-missile.md) into an eye-fired
  laser capable of firing multiple bolts at once (a "Double Shot" headshots two Jikininki ghouls
  simultaneously in one fight)
- **Charge storage** — the goggles can separately hold the energy of one full-power spell cast in
  reserve and release it later as its own "double shot," demonstrated against an awake province
  boss during a cart-lift ambush

## Scaling

Not described in detail beyond the two confirmed effects above.

## Role in the Story

The goggles directly expose Hekla's plot against Katia — without them, the attack would have gone
completely unnoticed, since the bolts are specifically designed to be untraceable. Donut treats
them as a fashion statement first and a tool second, repeatedly drawing attention to how good they
look on her before mentioning what they actually do.
