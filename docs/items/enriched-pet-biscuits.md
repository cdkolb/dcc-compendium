# Enriched Pet Biscuits

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **What it is:** A refined, **guaranteed-result** subtype of the long-running Enhanced Pet
      Biscuit item, introduced in Book 8
    - **Source:** Celestial boxes — Prepotente receives three at once, each different
    - **Contrast with the base item:** Standard enhanced pet biscuits roll from "over a hundred
      different possibilities," good and bad, entirely by chance. Enriched ones name their exact
      result up front
    - **Named subtree paths shown on-page:** **The Paramour**, **Nothing Special**, **Nothing
      Special Party Companion**
    - **Central to:** The deliberate transformation of the farm pig
      [Penelope](../characters/penelope.md) into a sapient party member, and — separately, and by
      mistake — the transformation of the dungeon boss [Scolopendra](../bosses/scolopendra.md)

This page covers the item and its taxonomy. Scolopendra's own transformation is covered from the
boss's side on [her page](../bosses/scolopendra.md#the-mix-up); this page does not repeat that
narrative and instead explains the mechanic that made it possible.

## Background: the Base Item

The plain **Enhanced Pet Biscuit** has been in the series since Book 1 — the item Carl force-fed
Donut to make her sapient. Per Mordecai, feeding one to a pet or animal-class mob rolls from "over a
hundred different possibilities," and the result can be very good (Donut, Prepotente, the demon
familiar Bianca) or very bad (Miriam Dom's herd of goats, one of whom turned on and killed several
others after a bad roll). It is, by design, a gamble every time.

## The Enriched Variant

Book 8 introduces a refined tier of the same item family: the **Enriched Pet Biscuit**. Where the
base biscuit gambles, the enriched version names its exact outcome on the label before it's ever
fed to anything:

> **Enriched Pet Biscuit. "The Paramour."**
>
> **This is a unique item.**
>
> **Okay, so we all know by now what enhanced pet biscuits do.**
>
> **While extremely valuable, enhanced pet biscuits are items that leave the result up to chance,
> and when you feed your pet one, there's a very real risk you'll be saying goodbye to your furry
> friend forever. Or they'll be saying goodbye to you.**
>
> ...
>
> **This is an Enriched Pet Biscuit. This leaves nothing to chance. These are highly refined pet
> biscuits with a known result.**

Prepotente receives three enriched biscuits at once from a celestial box, each a different named
result: **"Nothing Special Party Companion,"** **"Nothing Special,"** and **"The Paramour."**

## The Taxonomy

The base biscuit's "over a hundred possibilities" sort into at least two named subtrees, both
referenced by the in-game description:

### The Party Companion subtree

Results under this subtree keep the pet's original form (or a variant of it) and grant sapience,
party membership, and the ability to level, use skills, spells, and equipment upgrades like any
other crawler.

| Tree path | Known result | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Path one** | **Enhanced Growth** (Donut's own result, Book 1) | Sapience granted; pet keeps its original anatomy — no speech ability, no "subtree two-B" physical transformation |
| **Path one** | **"Nothing Special Party Companion"** | The same base outcome as Donut's, minus the Enhanced Growth stat boost. Sapient, stays in existing form, party-eligible — but **does not gain the ability to speak out loud** (communicates via party chat instead) |
| **Path two** | **Smarty-Pants** (Prepotente's own result, prior book) | Sapience granted plus a **subtree two-B physical transformation**: reshapes the creature into an oxygen-breathing, upright, 1–2.5 meter humanoid form — "basically a cat girl. A mascot. A furry depending on what their original form was." Winged creatures keep wings but lose natural flight |
| **Path two** | **"The Paramour"** | Everything path two normally grants, *plus* a permanent, incurable\* charm: the transformed creature falls hopelessly in love with whoever fed it the biscuit |

Regardless of path, any Party Companion result also does the following: raises a non-sapient pet's
Intelligence and Wisdom\* to at least 3 (scaled to their original form), resets their level to 1,
assigns dungeon-born pets a crawler number and crawler status, and — if consumed after the third
floor has begun — requires the newly-sapient creature to reach a safe room and choose a class before
it can do anything else.

### The Pet subtree

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| **"Nothing Special"** | The most basic possible result. Transforms a creature into an ordinary **dungeon pet** — able to gain levels, skills, spells, and equipment upgrades like Mongo or Rend — but grants **no sapience whatsoever** and reverts the creature to juvenile form |
| **Hellspawn Familiar** (Bianca's own result, prior book) | A Pet-subtree outcome distinct from "Nothing Special," details not given here |

The plain "Nothing Special" biscuit was the one the party actually wanted for Scolopendra: a
harmless, mindless, juvenile pet, safely carriable in inventory as a fail-safe against the Dungeon
Boss's own remaining nine-tier attacks (see [Scolopendra](../bosses/scolopendra.md#the-biscuit) for
why a caged pet, rather than a kill or an abandonment, was the plan).

## The Mix-Up

Book 8 feeds enriched biscuits to two creatures. Only the second is an accident, and it is worth
separating them cleanly, because the wiki previously ran them together:

1. **Penelope — deliberate, and it worked exactly as labelled.** Carl needed a way to make a farm
   pig sapient on short notice, to use as bait against Taranis and Emberus. He feeds her himself
   ("Penny, come here. I got a snack for you") during Floor 11's ninety-minute float-building phase,
   well before the arena fight and well before Eris ever scrambles anyone's inventory. Carl's own
   retrospective confirms both the item and the intent: "We'd fed her the enriched pet biscuit. The
   Nothing Special Party Companion one. This was the one that turned her into a sapient party
   member, but it didn't give her the ability to talk out loud." Penny becomes Crawler #12,953,454,
   "Penelope 3," Level 10, and communicates by party chat. Because Carl fed her, she is
   automatically partied with him. See [Penelope](../characters/penelope.md) for her side of it.
2. **Scolopendra — the actual mix-up.** Mid-fight, Eris's chaos effect swaps every nearby crawler's
   inventory at random; Donut ends up holding Carl's. Working from his hotlist under fire, Carl
   tells her to combine "the ball in slot ten with the biscuit in slot twelve" — a banger sphere and
   what should have been the plain **"Nothing Special"** taming biscuit. Donut's hotlist has ten
   slots, not twenty-five; she has to search Carl's inventory blind, and grabs the wrong biscuit.
   The Level 500 Dungeon Boss collapses as intended, but resurfaces as a fully sapient, flirtatious
   ally rather than a mindless caged pet. See
   [Scolopendra](../bosses/scolopendra.md#the-mix-up) for the full scene, including her chapter-98
   introduction as "Sexy Scolopendra."

!!! warning "The book never says which biscuit Donut actually grabbed"
    Donut confesses to "a really, really bad" mistake, but neither she nor the narration ever names
    the item. The result rules out the plain **"Nothing Special"** (no sapience, juvenile form) and
    argues strongly against the **"Nothing Special Party Companion"** already spent on Penny, since
    that result is path one — existing form retained, "the anatomy won't change at all, meaning they
    won't gain the ability to talk" — and Scolopendra emerges human-sized, upright, with a humanlike
    face, and speaking aloud. Those are **subtree two-B** traits, i.e. path two, and the only
    enriched path-two result named in the book is **"The Paramour,"** whose signature extra is a
    permanent charm toward whoever fed the creature — which is exactly how she behaves toward Carl,
    who threw the biscuit. The text never draws that conclusion, never shows Prepotente's unique
    Paramour biscuit changing hands, and never says the three biscuits Prepotente received are the
    only ones in existence. Record this as **unresolved**, not as a Paramour confirmation.

The system's own reaction to the Scolopendra half of it is the closest thing to editorial comment
either page gets:

> **New Achievement. What the actual fuck, Carl? Okay, so there are power moves. And then there's
> this... Reward: ... Yeah, I got nothing for this.**

## What's Still Open

- **"The Paramour" is never used on-page — explicitly.** Prepotente considers using it on his
  ex-girlfriend, a goat named Ragazzaccia, and Donut talks him out of it entirely — see
  [Prepotente](../characters/prepotente.md#the-paramour-biscuit). Its permanent-charm effect is
  documented from its description only. The one place it may have been used is the Scolopendra
  mix-up above, where the *outcome* matches a Paramour result but the item is never named; that
  remains an inference, not something the book states.
- **The full "over a hundred possibilities" list** of the base (non-enriched) biscuit's outcomes is
  never given. Only the specific named results above are shown on-page across Books 1–8.
- Whether more enriched biscuits exist beyond the three Prepotente received is not stated.
