# Drakea's Enchanted Kerchief of Disorder

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Slot:** Head/face/neck (versatile — worn as a bandana around Carl's head)
    - **Obtained:** Boss box, awarded for killing the borough boss Ruckus
    - **Namesake:** **Drakea**, author of the *Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*'s 22nd edition — a
      bune crawler who died on (per Mordecai's uncertain memory) the 11th or 12th floor, but not
      before helping bankrupt the naga-controlled Sultanate via a legal settlement after one of
      his elaborate traps "unnaturally" backfired on him

## Effect

A black, white-paisley bandana that can be worn on the head, face, or limbs. It grants:

- **+5 to the Detect Traps skill**
- Access to a level-15 **[Tripper](#tripper)** spell, once every five hours
- The **Remote Detonator** benefit

### Tripper

> "This spell automatically triggers all passage, motion, heat, and weight-based traps in a
> certain radius."

- **Target:** A 10-meter-radius sphere centered on the caster's right hand, plus 10 meters per
  point of Intelligence
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Cost:** No mana — it's an item-based spell tied to the kerchief; unequipping the item removes
  access without resetting the cooldown

Tripper sets off every qualifying trap in range at once — including any that summon or release
monsters — without disarming them, making it a double-edged tool best used from a safe distance or
with backup ready.

### Remote Detonator

> "This benefit allows you to magically remote-detonate any explosive that has been designated by
> you."

Works in combination with a [sapper's table](../mechanics/index.md), letting Carl trigger
pre-placed bombs on demand rather than relying purely on fuses or impact detonation.

## Scaling

Not otherwise detailed; Tripper's radius scales with Intelligence as noted above.

## Role in the Story

Carl receives the kerchief as a boss-box reward for killing Ruckus and immediately ties it around
his head, over Donut's fashion objections ("a cross between someone wearing a racist Halloween
costume and an old dude desperately trying to be a rock star"). The Remote Detonator benefit
becomes immediately useful, letting him trigger a hidden bomb hours later without needing to be
physically present.
