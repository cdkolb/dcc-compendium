# Enchanted Shade Gnoll Riot Forces Crowd Control Shield

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Katia
    - **Slot:** Arm-mounted shield (requires the [Rush](../abilities/rush.md) ability to equip)
    - **Obtained:** Fan box, awarded shortly after Hekla's death

## Effect

- **+5% Constitution**
- **+5 to the Rooted in Place skill** — makes the wearer much harder to knock over or back,
  though the item's own flavor text warns this isn't unambiguously good ("physics being what
  they are")
- **Upgrades [Rush](../abilities/rush.md) to Crowd Blast** — see that page for the full effect

A tall, curved riot shield about six feet high, light enough to wield one-handed despite its size,
with a small viewing window and "GET BACK" stenciled across the front in Syndicate Standard.

## Scaling

Its flat Constitution bonus scales with Katia's already-massive Constitution stat. Rooted in
Place is granted at a flat +5 skill level with no stated further scaling curve yet.

## Role in the Story

The shield's requirement that the wearer already have Rush is a deliberate (and, per Katia, likely
trolling) callback to the ability she used — completely by accident — to kill
[Hekla](../characters/hekla.md). It renders Katia's older
[Enchanted Auto Buckler of the Peach Pit](enchanted-auto-buckler-of-the-peach-pit.md) redundant,
since she can't equip both; she gives that item to Carl.
