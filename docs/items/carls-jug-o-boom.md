# Carl's Jug O' Boom

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Inventor:** Carl
    - **Type:** Incendiary tossable
    - **Base ingredients:** A Rev-Up moonshine jug, goblin oil, and a standard torch as an ignition cork
    - **In-game recognition:** Added to the "Dungeon Codex" as a player-invented, stackable item — a rare achievement

## Recipe & Effect

Carl builds the device by pouring off about a quarter of a clay jug of Rev-Up moonshine, adding a
couple ounces of goblin oil, and sealing the jug's mouth with a lit torch, which fits it almost
perfectly. When thrown and shattered, it produces a modest initial explosion followed by
wide-area, long-burning splash flames.

Compared to standard goblin dynamite, it's considerably safer to carry and use, at the cost of
consuming a full jug of moonshine — itself a valuable, rare trade good — per bomb.

## Scaling

Burn duration scales directly with the thrower's skill level in **Incendiary Device Handling** —
roughly 15 seconds of burn per level. The book doesn't give a full level-by-level table, just this
per-level rate, so a higher-level thrower gets proportionally longer-burning splash flames from
the same jug.

## The Royalty Mechanic

Because it's the first crawler-invented **stackable** item of its kind, the system automatically
opens an **Accounts** tab in Carl's inventory tracking a **Creator's Fee**: 1 gold coin for every
kill made anywhere in the dungeon using a Jug O' Boom, payable to Carl for the rest of his natural
life (and, per the fine print, into future seasons if he survives). Cashing out charges a punishing
flat 2-gold-coin fee plus 25%, rounded up — meaning small balances are barely worth withdrawing.
In practice, the item's core ingredient (Rev-Up moonshine) is rare enough in Book 1 that no other
crawler is shown using the recipe, so the royalty stream stays negligible.

## Role in the Story

The Jug O' Boom becomes Carl's default tool for handling group encounters throughout the back half
of Book 1 — used to clear clurichaun dens, executed en masse against the second Krakaren
encounter, and stockpiled into the "babies" launched from the MOAB ("Bomb Chicken") cart during
the Rage Elemental chase sequence.

## Future Enhancements

!!! note "Checked — no enhancement found (Books 2-8)"
    Carl continues carrying and using boom jugs at least through Book 4, and other crawlers are
    shown having independently reverse-engineered the recipe by Book 2 (cutting into Carl's
    royalty stream). No book describes the item itself being upgraded or modified. In Book 4, Carl
    develops a separate "burn gel" recipe as a complementary tool for a fire-resistant enemy —
    a related invention, not a change to the Jug O' Boom itself.
