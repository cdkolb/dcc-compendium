# Stayfast Ink and Adept's Fountain Pen

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Obtained:** Chapter 75, a parting gift from the crawler **Milk**, handed over as what looks
      like a wineskin
    - **Quantity:** **825 ml** — "*Enough ink for several chonky books*"
    - **Property:** It never fades. "*Nothing. Not even distance or time or the death of a star*"
    - **Purpose:** Explicitly given so Carl can write skills and spells into
      [The Filthy Little Crawler's Book of Voodoo](the-filthy-little-crawlers-book-of-voodoo.md)

This is the successor to the toraline ink, and the moment the Book of Voodoo's long-flagged "nefarious
purpose" finally becomes legible.

## Why Milk Gives It

Milk's explanation is a confession before it is a gift. She had once written her recipes in a book,
switched to a different ink partway through, and lost everything she wrote afterward:

> "There's more than you can imagine. Even with just ink and calligraphy and maps, there are dozens of
> different methods. It is dangerous, though, attempting to learn too much. **There's a lot the right
> kind of ink can do.** But, over the turns, I've learned the danger of using recipes I don't quite
> understand. **Inks and letters that shine the brightest burn out the fastest.**"

> "**I had a book once, and I wrote several of my recipes in it. But later, I switched to a different
> type of ink. It was an attempt to share my knowledge with a wider group, and it wasn't until
> recently did I learn all that I wrote after I changed methods faded and disappeared.**… Some of my
> best recipes were lost, and it wasn't until they were needed the most did I learn they were gone.
> **It is my fault that I didn't write them properly.**"

This closes a loose end from chapter 47. Carl had gone looking in the cookbook for Milk's entries on
the tattoo mechanic and found nothing: "*There was nothing about this in her entries. I'd looked three
times.*" They had been written. They had simply evaporated.

Her people's saying, offered with the wineskin: "***No single recipe is a feast in itself.***" Meaning
there is always another way.

## The Descriptions

> **Stayfast Ink, 825 ml.**
>
> **Enough ink for several chonky books.**
>
> **Warning: this shit stains, so be careful. Nothing will ever make it fade. Nothing. Not even
> distance or time or the death of a star.**

> **Adept's fountain pen.**
>
> **The most basic of all the calligrapher's implements. How much grief, how much suffering has been
> caused by one of these things? Yet, how much joy? That whole saying, the pen is mightier than the
> sword, is one of the few human world metaphors that I believe to be universally true, especially
> since swords are terrible, barbaric weapons.**
>
> **This is the most versatile item anyone can own. What will you do with it? Write a book that
> changes everything? Come up with a new, titillating take on hucow fiction? (Don't look that up.) Or
> will you literally stab someone in the neck with it again?**

The "*again*" is the AI remembering that Carl has, in fact, previously stabbed someone in the neck
with a pen.

## What It Is For

Milk says it outright, and she says it looking Carl in the eye:

> "He also has another book. One that all of you have. **The Book of Voodoo, I believe it is called.
> Anything he writes in it will be given to everybody who already has the book.** Best of luck to
> you."

Edgar had reached the same conclusion a chapter earlier, working from the other direction:

> "Rosetta was just telling Milk and myself about that book of Voodoo you have, and **I believe you
> may be able to share skills and spells that way as well, using the same ink. It will be more
> temporary.**"

And the AI had been screaming it at Carl since chapter 57, in the form of an achievement reward box
named:

> **Platinum Have You Goddamned Figured Out How To Use This In Conjunction With the Fucking Voodoo
> Book Yet? Jesus Christ I Can Only Help You So Much box.**

So the chain is complete: the
[toraline tattoo system](toraline-tattoo-transfer.md) proved abilities can be written into skin;
Milk's calligraphy proved writing can amplify them; permanent ink plus a book that broadcasts to every
crawler alive means abilities can be **written and distributed at scale**.

The immediate consequence is [Carl's Book of Boom](carls-book-of-boom.md) in chapter 85 — 30,000 pages
of donated skills and spells, written by departing crawlers, distributed to everyone still fighting.

## Scaling

No level. The quantity — **825 ml**, "several chonky books" — is the only stated limit, and unlike the
toraline it is not consumed by a ration that runs out mid-plan. The ink's defining property is
**permanence**, which is precisely the property Milk lacked when she needed it.

Donut's contribution to the scene, for the record: "**CARL, WHAT THE HECK IS SHE TALKING ABOUT? IS SHE
HITTING ON YOU?**"
