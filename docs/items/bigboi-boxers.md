# Enchanted BigBoi Boxers

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Slot:** Underwear
    - **Obtained:** A boss box, late in Book 1 (a "really good prize" per Mordecai)
    - **Appearance:** White with little red hearts

## Effect

Original Book 1 benefits:

- **+2 Constitution**
- Grants the item-based spell **[Protective Shell](../spells/protective-shell.md)**, castable
  once every 30 hours at a fixed **level 15**, with no mana cost

Per its in-game description, size-altering/were-creatures like the "BigBoi" require fully
enchanted, self-sizing clothing to avoid destroying their outfits when they transform — "everything
they wear requires an enchantment... including their naughty little undies."

**Upgraded in Book 2** via a Scroll of Upgrade (see Future Enhancements below) to also grant:

- **+5 Dexterity**
- **+5 Intelligence**
- **+3 more Constitution** (total +5)
- **The Freeballing benefit** — +100% damage to all attacks originating below the waist, and 20%
  faster training for skills that originate below the waist, but only a single layer of clothing
  may cover the crotch while worn (i.e. no pants over them)

## Scaling

The item's own bonus (+2 Constitution) is flat. The **Protective Shell** cast it grants is fixed
at level 15 regardless of Carl's own stats — see the [Protective Shell](../spells/protective-shell.md)
page for how that spell's duration/radius scale from that fixed level.

## Role in the Story

This is a comedic prize Carl is embarrassed to receive, but it turns out to be one of the most
important items he owns — the Protective Shell it grants is what saves his life during the
Rage Elemental's charge later in the book.

## Future Enhancements

!!! note "Confirmed — Book 2"
    A Scroll of Upgrade, received from a Platinum Quest Box on Floor 3, applies a random upgrade
    to a single currently-equipped item — Carl's BigBoi Boxers are chosen (Mordecai calls it the
    system "playing a joke" on him, since he'd been openly wishing for actual pants). Full effect
    listed above. Books 3-8 not yet checked for further upgrades.
