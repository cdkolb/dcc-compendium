# Desperado Pass (Tattoo)

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owners:** Carl (a dagger design, on his neck) and Princess Donut (near her right shoulder blade)
    - **Obtained:** "Asshole Boxes" — a paired reward from the same neighborhood clear that yielded a Gold Asshole's Box
    - **Type:** Faction tattoo — branded directly onto the body, not stored in inventory

## Effect

Per its in-game description: **"This pass allows access to the Desperado Club."** A warning
attached to it states that holding a Desperado Pass **negates the ability to obtain a
"Vanquisher Pass"** — a trade-off never explained further in Book 1.

Donut is furious about receiving hers involuntarily, calling it "a disqualifying mark" and
comparing it to being "defiled." Both tattoos brand themselves on regardless of consent, the same
way the [Goblin Pass](goblin-pass.md) did.

## Scaling

None described — a binary faction pass, not something with levels.

## Role in the Story

Mordecai flatly refuses to explain the Desperado Club when Carl asks directly, saying only that
it's "off limits" and that Carl will "learn about the third floors soon enough" — one of several
plot threads Book 1 deliberately leaves dangling.
