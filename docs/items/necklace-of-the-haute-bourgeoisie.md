# Enchanted Necklace of the Haute Bourgeoisie

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Slot:** Necklace
    - **Obtained:** A Platinum Tyrant's Box, awarded for becoming magistrate of the Medium
      Skyfowl Settlement after [Miss Quill](../bosses/miss-quill.md)'s death
    - **Type:** A "chain of leadership" — the second-smallest tier — that tracks settlements a
      crawler personally conquers/controls
    - **Grants:** [Talon Strike](../abilities/talon-strike.md) (Level 5), among other per-gem bonuses

## Overview

A simple silver chain with a charm that gains one jewel per settlement the wearer controls. Its
official description lays out the mechanic directly:

> "Each jewel encrusted upon this charm represents a settlement owned and controlled by the
> bearer. If one still maintains a settlement's jewel upon the collapse of the level, the holder
> of this necklace will permanently receive a tax stipend every ten days from that settlement
> based on size and population. In addition, each gem will impart additional benefits based on
> the town."

Carl receives it already carrying one gem — a **Poor Sapphire** for the Medium Skyfowl
Settlement (Floor 3), which he inherited leadership of after killing Miss Quill, the settlement's
secret ruler.

## Current Gem: Poor Sapphire (Medium Skyfowl Settlement)

- **+5 Dexterity**
- **+Talon Strike (Level 5)** — see [Talon Strike](../abilities/talon-strike.md)
- **Taxes received:** 432 gold every 10 days

## Scaling

Per its own description: "In order to upgrade this necklace, one must first conquer a
Large-sized Settlement. Upgraded necklaces will also upgrade all existing gems" — meaning both
the tax income and the per-gem bonuses scale with settlement size, and a single conquest upgrades
every gem already on the chain at once, not just the new one.
