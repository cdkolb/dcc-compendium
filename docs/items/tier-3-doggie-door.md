# Tier 3 Doggie Door

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Personal space upgrade module
    - **Tier:** 3 — per Mordecai, these "shouldn't be available until the ninth floor"
    - **Obtained:** **[Lucia Mar](../characters/lucia-mar.md)** dropped **five** of them on the
      ground and walked down the stairs (ch. 63)
    - **Effect:** Turns your personal-space door into a selectable exit to **any door you have
      previously used on that floor** — a fast-travel waypoint network, shareable with guests and
      party members
    - **Cost to extend guild-wide:** **25 million gold**

## Description

> **Personal Space Upgrade. Tier 3 Doggie Door.**
>
> **So, you're out adventuring, and you suddenly have a hankerin' for those sweet pancakes you had
> two weeks back at some dive that's now over two hundred kilometers away. What are you gonna do?
> Travel for several hours in the wrong direction just to get another taste? Psshh. Okay, loser.**
>
> **The cool kids use the doggie door.**
>
> **Upgrades the door in your personal space to allow a selectable exit via any door you have
> previously utilized on this same floor, effectively creating a fast travel waypoint system.
> Allows shared gated access amongst guests and party members.**

A module from a box arrives as a physical object that must be placed in inventory and activated
through the personal-space tab; a bopca-purchased one would simply be installed into the room for
you.

## How the Guild Got Them

Li Jun hands the first one to Carl and can't explain it. Lucia Mar — fresh from what the broadcast
is calling the **Battle of Beijing**, where she killed her way through a building full of keyless
crawlers — was waiting for Li Jun's group at the stairwell. She had messaged with Florin before he
descended, and he'd told her to help them. She dropped five Tier 3 modules on the ground, said
nothing useful, and left.

> "She was really friendly, too, which was unsettling. My sister wanted to fight her."

Katia's assessment: "*She shouldn't have had one, let alone five of them. It's a tier-3. Mordecai
says these shouldn't be available until the ninth floor... He thinks Lucia stole them when she went
on a show or something.*"

## Scaling and Limits

"Tier 3" is the scaling. The tier is what makes it a **floor-wide waypoint network** rather than a
single fixed exit, and it is what makes it expensive and out of season. Three limits apply:

1. **Same floor only** — the door network resets when the floor does
2. **Previously used doors only** — you can only travel to a door you have physically walked
   through already
3. **One module, one room** — access is shared with that room's party and guests, so one installed
   module covers a whole party but not the whole guild

The guild installs the first in Imani's room, which immediately gives the entire Meadowlark party
door access to the church, Washington, Detroit, and Belgium. Four modules remain unspent at book's
end. Upgrading the whole guild so that every member's own door joins the network costs **25 million
gold**, which they cannot afford — they had just pooled everything they had to buy the five-million
barracks upgrade.

## Why It Matters

This is a capability the guild has been chasing for several books: a way to move a distributed,
multi-squad organisation around a floor faster than the floor can kill them. Arriving mid-Floor-8,
two floors early, via the dungeon's most feared player killer, it changes the shape of the endgame —
and quietly raises the question of how Lucia Mar came to be handing out ninth-floor infrastructure
for free.
