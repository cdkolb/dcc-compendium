# Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Superior Fire Demon's Hand Maiden

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut
    - **Slot:** Nipple ring — must be manually affixed the first time it is equipped; quadruped and
      female only
    - **Obtained:** A **Legendary Beguiler's Box**, awarded for charming
      [Ferdinand](../characters/ferdinand.md) on Floor 6
    - **Headline effect:** **All spells with a fire attack now deal (Intelligence)% more damage**
    - **Also grants:** Removal of cursed items, immunity to natural fire and lava, resistance to
      fire spells, no health loss inside Sheol, and one daily cast of *Bitch, What?*

## Lore

The description is a long, self-aware rant that Donut calls "the most sexist description I've ever
read." Superior fire demons, per the AI, are a matriarchal hierarchy on the fifteenth level who
are so dangerously unstable that the demon lords require all of them to be chaperoned by hellspawn
familiars — "Sheol's version of a Xanax." The ring is one of the protective items those familiars
use to survive their charges. Mordecai's advice: "*Just ignore it and look at the stats.*"

## Effects

> **Allows the removal of cursed items.**
>
> **User is impervious to natural fire.**
>
> **User is impervious to lava attacks.**
>
> **User is resistant to fire-based spells.**
>
> **User will not lose health while inside Sheol.**
>
> **All spells with a fire attack now deal (Intelligence)% more damage.**
>
> **User may cast *Bitch, What?* Once a day.**

Carl's verdict is instant: "*We're putting this on you right away.*"

## Scaling

The damage line is the scaling: the bonus is **equal to Donut's Intelligence, expressed as a
percentage**. There is no cap given, and Intelligence is the stat Donut stacks hardest (her
[Tiara of the Inebriated Dragonfly](enchanted-tiara-of-the-inebriated-dragonfly.md) alone adds +10, the
burning-book patch another +15%), so the multiplier climbs steadily across the book. The effect is
visible in play — the first *Wall of Fire* she casts on Floor 8 "crackled bigger than ever thanks
to her new nipple ring."

## Bitch, What? (Fire Berserker)

The once-daily granted spell is the one thing Mordecai wants to talk about before she equips it:

> ***Bitch, What?***
>
> **Type: Berserking Spell + partial invulnerability.**
>
> **Target: Self only. Cost: Item based.**
>
> **Duration: twenty seconds. Cooldown: Once every 30 hours.**
>
> **Effect: You ever seen an angry, on-fire weasel tossed into a room full of chickens?**

Mordecai has seen the state before and so has Carl — it is what a **Rage Elemental** is
permanently in, and the system's internal name for it is ***Fire Berserker***. He notes the AI has
been renaming things far more than usual this season. His one instruction is that Donut may only
cast it when she is by herself.

## Empowering Donut's Existing Fire Spells

This ring is what Mordecai is referring to immediately after its description is read aloud (ch. 2):

> **"Donut, only cast it when you're by yourself. You have three fire spells now. *Fireball,
> Magic Missile,* and *Wall of Fire*. All three of them are going to be much more intense. So,
> be very careful."**

"You have three fire spells **now**" is inventory, not acquisition. Donut already knew all
three — *Fireball* since Book 5 (from the
[Obsidian Bracelet of the Raggle Rouser](obsidian-bracelet-of-the-raggle-rouser.md)),
*Wall of Fire* since before Book 4, and *Magic Missile* since Book 1. The ring **empowers**
them via the (Intelligence)% fire-damage line; it teaches nothing. The genuinely new
information in the line is that **Magic Missile counts as a fire spell**, which had not been
established before.
