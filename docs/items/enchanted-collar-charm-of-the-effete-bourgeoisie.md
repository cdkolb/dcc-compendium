# Enchanted Collar Charm of the Effete Bourgeoisie

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut
    - **Slot:** Collar charm (replaces the [Talisman of the Slate Butterfly](talisman-of-the-slate-butterfly.md))
    - **Obtained:** Awarded automatically upon conquering the Ursine settlement of Point Mongo,
      Floor 6

## Effect

Functions as the settlement-tracking counterpart to [Carl's Necklace of the Haute Bourgeoisie](necklace-of-the-haute-bourgeoisie.md):
each conquered settlement adds a gemstone to the charm, and each gem grants its own bonus plus a
gold tax stipend paid out every ten days. The charm must be worn (not merely owned) for its wearer
to give orders to a settlement's guards.

Per its own description:

> "The middle size of the chains of leadership, this pendant signifies you as a member of the
> elite. Each jewel encrusted upon this charm represents a settlement owned and controlled by the
> bearer... In order to upgrade this necklace, one must first conquer an Extra-Large-sized
> Settlement. Upgraded necklaces will also upgrade all existing gems."

Point Mongo's plain turquoise gem grants **+8 Dexterity**, **+4 Intelligence**, and the skill
**Snitch** already at level 10 (the skill summons town guards to the wearer's location — 1 guard at
skill level 5, 5 at level 10, all of them at level 15), plus a tax stipend of exactly 3,592 gold
every ten days.

## Scaling

Upgrading the necklace's overall tier (by conquering a larger settlement) upgrades every existing
gem simultaneously, though this hasn't yet been demonstrated in the text.

## Role in the Story

Donut takes control of the settlement (renaming it from "Large Ursine Settlement" to "Point
Mongo") by casting a spell that shoves its elderly bear mayor off the edge of town, killing him in
the fall. She then switches her collar-charm slot over from the Talisman of the Slate Butterfly to
make use of the newly-awarded charm, complaining loudly about the turquoise gem's color the entire
time.
