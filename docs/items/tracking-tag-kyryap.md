# Tracking Tag ("The Kyryap has claimed you")

!!! danger "Open Book 9 setup — do not speculate about what the Kyryap is"
    This page documents only what Book 8 states on the page. The Kyryap's true nature is never
    explained, and this entry should not guess at it.

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Bearer:** Carl
    - **Type:** Enchanted dart / tracking tag
    - **Planted:** Somewhere during the Floor 11 arena climax, unnoticed until the very end of the
      book
    - **In-game description:** *"Tracking Tag. The Kyryap has claimed you. Fucked = you."*
    - **Status:** Unresolved — carries into Book 9

## The Discovery

In the arena's aftermath, moments after looting
[Emberus's Memorial Crystal](memorial-crystal-emberus.md), Carl notices something odd:

> As I shifted, I stepped on something in the dirt... I had a dart sticking out of my leg. I hadn't
> felt it, and I couldn't feel it now. I didn't know when I'd received it or how. I gently pulled it
> out.
>
> **Tracking Tag.**
>
> **The Kyryap has claimed you.**
>
> **Fucked = you.**
>
> "The shit?" I asked.

Carl checks his debuffs, finds nothing else new tied to it, and — distracted by an unrelated new
effect involving his [Emberus ring](enchanted-paupers-ring-of-the-steadfast-emberus.md#the-debt-the-curse-and-the-asterisk-still-explicitly-unresolved)
— shelves the question entirely:

> Hopefully, this Kyryap thing would be dead in the floor collapse.

Nothing further happens with the tag on the page. Book 8 ends without revisiting it.

## Earlier References to "the Kyryap" in Book 8

The name isn't new by the time the tag appears — Book 8 itself plants it twice earlier, both times
tied to the same location: the deep interior of **Club Scolopendra**, reached via
[Oozy Form](../spells/oozy-form.md) during the Floor 11 climax.

**On the caprid interview show *Plenty of Plenty*** (ch. 28-ish), a goat judge explains what
Justice Light's trap unleashed onto three separate floors when it broke the in-game Nothing:

> "We have no vision of what's happening on the fifteenth, and we only saw brief snippets of the
> ongoing slaughter and torture within Club Scolopendra. **The dread Kyryap is there.** As is
> Krakaren Prime."

**Inside Club Scolopendra itself**, while Carl and Penny move toward Emberus:

> A level 13 snare trap sat in the middle of the hallway ahead. Something set by **"the Kyryap."**
> I deactivated the trap and hesitantly moved to the stairs.

So by the time the tracking tag turns up on Carl's own leg, "the Kyryap" has already been
established within Book 8 as a named, dreaded, trap-setting presence loose in Club Scolopendra
— grouped in the same breath as Krakaren Prime, another Dungeon-Boss-tier threat. The tag suggests
whatever it is took a direct, personal interest in Carl specifically, sometime during the arena
fight.

!!! note "The brief's earlier guess was wrong — corrected here"
    An earlier draft of this catalog's brief guessed the Kyryap might trace back to Book 7's Floor 9
    or an earlier caprid conversation. **It does not.** A full-text search of *This Inevitable Ruin*
    and all earlier books turns up zero hits for "Kyryap." The name originates entirely within Book
    8 itself, first via the *Plenty of Plenty* goat panel and the Club Scolopendra snare trap, both
    earlier in the same book as the tracking tag payoff.

## What Is Not Known

- What the Kyryap actually is (species, individual, faction, or something else) — never stated
- Why it tagged Carl specifically, or when during the arena fight it happened
- Whether "Fucked = you" is a literal mechanical effect or purely flavor text — no debuff, stat
  change, or further notification is shown resulting from the tag
- Whether the Kyryap survived the Floor 11 collapse

Treat all of the above as deliberate, unresolved Book 9 setup.
