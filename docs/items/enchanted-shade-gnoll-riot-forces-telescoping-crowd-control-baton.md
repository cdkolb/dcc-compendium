# Enchanted Shade Gnoll Riot Forces Telescoping Crowd Control Baton

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Katia
    - **Slot:** One-handed melee weapon
    - **Obtained:** Fan box, awarded alongside the matching [Riot Shield](enchanted-shade-gnoll-riot-forces-crowd-control-shield.md)

## Effect

A telescoping baton adjustable in length from 25 centimeters to 3 meters. Automatically casts a
**Level-5 Cone of Knockback** if swung into a group of ten or more similar mobs, on a five-minute
cooldown.

## Scaling

The Cone of Knockback is fixed at level 5 with no stated scaling; the baton's main flexibility is
its adjustable reach rather than any growth curve.

## Role in the Story

Paired with the [Riot Shield](enchanted-shade-gnoll-riot-forces-crowd-control-shield.md) as a
matching set from the same fan box, both clearly styled after Katia's cowcatcher-and-crowd-control
role during the [Hekla](../characters/hekla.md) confrontation. Gives Katia a reliable, low-cooldown
crowd-clear option distinct from her once-a-day (now five-minute) Crowd Blast.
