# Hell-Kissed Potion of Conscription (Level 18)

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Level:** **18** — three levels above what the series had treated as the ceiling
    - **Brewed by:** **Krakaren Prime**, inside **the Nothing**
    - **Effect:** Conscripts "*pretty much any non-affiliated entities, regardless of their
      protections, **including most deities***"
    - **Risk:** "*Using the potion is likely suicide for the caster if the level disparity is too
      high*"
    - **Restricted to:** Floors **9, 12, 15, 17 and 18**
    - **Confirmed kills of the concept:** Three gods conscripted in Book 7 — Yarilo, Harpocrates,
      Lucia Mar

The bottle is shaped like a demonic head with no eyes and multiple tentacles, and when Carl takes it
"*the eyeless face started to scream.*"

## The Description

> **Hell-Kissed Potion of Conscription, level 18.**
>
> **This works like a regular potion of Conscription, but it has been hell-kissed. At level 18, it
> will work against pretty much any non-affiliated entities, regardless of their protections,
> including most deities. Most. It was brewed in the dark recesses of the Nothing by Krakaren Prime,
> who is said to yearn for domination, which was why she'd been banished there in the first place.
> She prepares for a war she considers inevitable. There's an irony that the in-dungeon version of
> Krakaren has supplied these potions to the War Mages. Then again, now that this potion has found
> its way to your hands, maybe it's not so ironic after all.**
>
> **Conscription potions only work on the 9th, 12th, 15th, 17th, and 18th floors.**

Three things in that block are worth separating out:

1. **"Most."** The description deliberately declines to say the potion works on *all* deities, and
   never elaborates on which ones it would fail against.
2. **Krakaren Prime is a real, named, extant entity** — banished into the Nothing for ambition,
   arming a rebellion from exile, and "*prepar[ing] for a war she considers inevitable.*" The
   in-dungeon Krakaren the party fought on Floor 1 is a copy of her.
3. **The floor list** — 9, 12, 15, 17, 18 — is a near-match for the Scolopendra levels, and confirms
   conscription is a designed feature of specific floors rather than an ambient rule.

Krakaren Prime surfaces again in chapter 76: the War Mages trade the **Gate of the Feral Gods** to a
Midnight Market merchant named **Paca**, "*an agent for Krakaren Prime, who is stuck in the Nothing.
Paca has the gate in her stomach.*"

## The Risk

Baroness Victory, chapter 69, on why this is not a free win:

> "They're using potions, not the spell. They'll have to be hell-kissed versions. Those will be level
> 18. But, it also means **using the potion is likely suicide for the caster if the level disparity
> is too high.**"

Confirmed within minutes. The first war mage to conscript a god is dead before the god finishes
turning around:

> **Samantha: A WAR MAGE JUST CAME OUT OF NOWHERE AND TOSSED A CONSCRIPTION POTION ON YARILO, AND IT
> WORKED! BUT THE MOMENT HE CAST THE SPELL, THE WAR MAGE GUY JUST BLEW UP!**

Carl's reaction: "*Holy fucking shit. The war mages were conscripting gods into their army.*"

## How the Party Got One

Li Na loots the potion from the war mage who attacked **Harpocrates** and **duplicates it** using Li
Jun's scroll of potion duplication. She uses the original on Harpocrates herself and gives the copy to
Carl.

## The Three Uses

| Target | Chapter | Caster | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Yarilo** | 69 | Unnamed war mage | Succeeded. The caster exploded |
| **Lucia Mar** | 71 | **Carl** — smashed it against her head | Succeeded. **Under Orders** appears over her; she is physically stopped by an invisible wall when she tries to attack him |
| **Harpocrates**, level 250 | 72 | **Li Na** | Succeeded, and she survived. *"Warlord Message: Harpocrates has been successfully conscripted. Harpocrates, level 250 is now a member of the Princess Posse."* |

Carl's read on the durability of the effect, immediately after taking Lucia:

> **There were ways to break conscription, as the war mages figured out, but it was all but impossible
> during the first few days. Especially with this version of the Conscription spell.**

And the hard limit: "*The conscription only lasts until the end of this floor.*"

Holding a god is not the same as controlling one. Chapter 74 shows Li Na "*literally riding
Harpocrates like he was a mount… the god was clearly enraged by this, but so far the conscription
held*," with the practical constraint that "*he's harder to control when I'm not near him.*"

## Scaling

The potion's level is fixed at 18. What varies is the **gap between the potion's level and the
target's**, which governs whether the caster survives the casting. No formula is given — only
Victory's warning and the immediate demonstration of what happens when the gap is too wide.
