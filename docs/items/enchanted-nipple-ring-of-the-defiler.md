# Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Defiler

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Slot:** Nipple ring — must be manually affixed the first time it is equipped
    - **Appearance:** A silver ring with an eyeball hanging from it
    - **Obtained:** A **Platinum Apostate Box**, from the achievement **"Marked for Life"** — for
      surviving being marked for death by the goddess **Diwata** and living through her actually
      entering the realm (a Book 5 event)
    - **Restriction:** "**This may only be equipped on a crawler who has been Marked for Death by a
      god.** Hey, that's you!"

## Effects

> **Instantly know the religious affiliation of all nearby mobs and crawlers.**
>
> **Allows the destruction of church shrines without further ill effects from deities.**
>
> **+25% damage against all clerics.**
>
> **The ability to cast *[Black Nimbus](../spells/black-nimbus.md)* once an hour.**

Carl receives it the same evening as a stack of other divinity-flavoured loot, prompting Mordecai's
flat assessment: "*Lots of divinity-themed items. That's never good.*" He also notes with some
irritation that **Li Na** could already do all of these things.

Donut is delighted, on the grounds that Carl had made fun of her own nipple ring first.

## Why It Matters

The religion-detection line is the one that actually shapes the floor. Floor 8's central hazard is
the **orisha shrine** storyline — seven broken shrines, competing gods, and a rule that repairing
the wrong one earns a smite — and Carl spends much of the early floor able to read at a glance
which crawlers and mobs are on which side. The first thing it tells him is that the three crawlers
he's just met (Paz, Anton, Sister Ines) include two worshippers of **Ogun**, which is what sends
him to Mordecai for a briefing on the god in the first place.

The shrine-destruction clause is the practical counterpart: it lets Carl break shrines without
repeating the Book 5 accident that got him marked by Diwata to begin with.

## Scaling

No level-based scaling. All four effects are flat. The one scaling component belongs to the granted
spell rather than the ring — *Black Nimbus*'s duration is a formula of caster and target stats, and
its chance of forcing a smite rises with spell level.
