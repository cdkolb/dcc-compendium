# Rockard's Ring of Sniping

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl — looted from Quan Ch's severed arm
    - **Slot:** Ring
    - **Namesake:** **Rockard**, an orcish warrior and one of the dungeon's most infamous
      crawlers, said to have led his season's top-10 list until his own mother knifed him in his
      sleep

## Effect

An amber-stoned ring granting two passive benefits:

- **Ripe:** All creatures with less than 50% health are shown on the map. Doesn't extend the
  map's normal view range, but combines with skills like Pathfinder to make experience-sniping
  (swooping in late to steal a kill) trivially easy.
- **Loaded:** All non-hidden creatures wearing magical gear are shown on the map — useful for
  finding well-equipped targets to rob or avoid.

## Scaling

Both benefits are binary detection effects with no stated scaling.

## Role in the Story

Carl rips this ring (along with two others) off Quan Ch's arm after tearing it from his body
during the fight over the celestial-tier puppy Orthrus. Carl notes the ring explains Quan's
reputation as a "glory-stealing" opportunist who repeatedly shows up late to steal kills and
loot from other crawlers' fights.
