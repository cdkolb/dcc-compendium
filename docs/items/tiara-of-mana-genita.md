# Enchanted Tiara of Mana Genita

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut
    - **Obtained:** A Book 3 loot box, replacing her lost [Crown of the Sepsis
      Whore](crown-of-the-sepsis-whore.md)
    - **Effect:** +3 Intelligence, increased mob-detection range, removes automatic hostility
      from worshippers of the goddess Mana Genita

## Overview

A white-jeweled tiara that replaces Donut's lost Crown, described with characteristically dark
in-universe humor:

> "A crystal tiara fit for royalty. Made in honor of all the lost children of the world. Because
> nothing helps sooth the tears of grieving parents like watching someone else dress up all
> fancy."

Mordecai admits he barely knows anything about Mana Genita herself — "a pretty obscure" goddess,
apparently associated with spells, one of thousands of deities the dungeon can reference, most of
which "never leave the 12th floor." Her identity and relevance remain unexplained as of the end
of Book 3.

## Scaling

No level-based scaling is described — the tiara's three effects (Intelligence bonus, detection
range, and the worshipper-hostility removal) all appear to be flat, static bonuses.

## Book 6: Benched, Not Replaced

The Mana Genita tiara is confirmed as Donut's standard everyday headwear right up to the middle of
Floor 8 — "*That's what she usually wore, and that was what she was wearing right now*" — sitting
alongside a collection of literally hundreds of unenchanted hats in her inventory.

Midway through the floor she builds the
[Enchanted Tiara of the Inebriated Dragonfly](enchanted-tiara-of-the-inebriated-dragonfly.md) from a blank
tiara and a Flawless Jeweler's Gem, and the dragonfly becomes her new daily wear. Importantly,
**the Mana Genita is deliberately kept rather than traded in** — Carl notes it "might end up useful
one day" — and Donut continues to swap tiaras situationally, explicitly "switching into her
dragonfly tiara" when she wants the Hover skill.

## Book 7: A Third Tiara Arrives, and It Is Neither of These

A third tiara arrives in chapter 3:
**[The Tiara of a Thousand Lights and Accompanying Charge Pillow](tiara-of-a-thousand-lights.md)**,
a Celestial benefactor box from Donut's sponsor Long Haul Biological Waste Management Solutions.

It is neither a replacement nor an upgrade of Mana Genita or the Dragonfly tiara in the ordinary
sense. On its own it grants **+10 Charisma and nothing else**; its value is **99 Absorption
Jewels** that can be permanently charged by draining other enchanted hats on an accompanying
pillow. The tiara's own "advanced targeting" doesn't come from the tiara itself — it's absorbed
into it from a **boonie hat** that ships with Donut's new Deathbed Hellcat class. See
[Targeting](../abilities/targeting.md) and [Magic Missile](../spells/magic-missile.md).

**The Mana Genita tiara is not drained and not mentioned in Book 7.** The sacrifice goes the other
way: it is the
[Enchanted Tiara of the Inebriated Dragonfly](enchanted-tiara-of-the-inebriated-dragonfly.md) that
Donut deliberately places on the purple universal charge pillow in chapter 3, permanently stripping
it of its magic so the buffs can be re-housed in the new tiara.

!!! note "Exact-verification note"
    Book 7 gives no on-page statement of where the Mana Genita ends up. Carl's Book 6 reasoning for
    keeping it — it "might end up useful one day" — is not revisited, and it is not among the hats
    shown being drained. Treat its status as **unchanged and unaddressed**, not as resolved.
