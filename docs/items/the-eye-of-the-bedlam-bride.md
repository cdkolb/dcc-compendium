# The Eye of the Bedlam Bride

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Bearer:** Carl
    - **Type:** Tattoo — centre of the chest, just above the sternum
    - **Applied by:** **Shi Maria**, the Bedlam Bride, while controlling Carl's body as a summoned
      totem (chapter 71)
    - **Applied with:** The [Enchanted Prison Tattoo Kit](enchanted-prison-tattoo-kit.md) and ink
      brewed on the spot
    - **In-game description:** *"Tattoo. The Eye of the Bedlam Bride."* — and nothing else
    - **This is the book's title item**

## How It Was Made

Shi Maria — "The Screamer. Shi Maria. The Widow Endless. The Bedlam Bride" — is summoned as one of
Donut's totems during the Ysalte/Amayon climax and seizes control of Carl's body. She is working to
a deadline: when the totem times out she returns to the deck, and Donut has every intention of
ripping her card. So she uses the time to make herself permanent.

The ink is brewed from scratch, in Carl's hands, out of three things:

1. **Extreme heat** — she makes him cast *[Self-Immolation](../spells/self-immolation.md)* with the
   party standing around him, burning Katia and Mongo badly and melting a hole through the floor
2. **A sliver of the toraline root vegetable** Carl's sponsors, the Open Intellect Pacifist
   Network, had given him, dropped into a beaker he'd looted on the first floor
3. **Li Jun's eye.** Li Jun slides down a chain to help — "*Carl, what can I do?*" — and Carl,
   screaming inside his own head, drives the tattoo stick into his friend's eye, pulls it out on the
   end of the stick, and eats it.

Then he tattoos his own chest with it, his hand "moving faster and faster, faster than possible."
It is a small image, right above the sternum.

## What She Said

> *"You have so much power in your hands, Carl, and you don't even know what you have. That's okay.
> Today, you live. If you won't bring this card to the next floor, I will have to attach myself to
> something you can't discard so easily."*
>
> *"There. There. Almost done. You may gain some of your friend's powers, too, with the blood on the
> needle. Not much. But I am with you now, Carl, until you decide to free me. I will no longer be a
> card you can abandon, one you can rip away. **I am a part of you, and you will have to decide.
> Keep me with you and gain my power, or set me free. Either way, I win.**"*

A second voice, male, interjects throughout — Shi Maria insists it isn't real and that she didn't
put it there; it answers "*You did. You killed me, and now I am here.*" Its parting advice to Carl:
"*Don't trust her, boy. Get her out of you as soon as you can. Don't ever trust her.*"

## What It Does

Nothing stated. When Carl regains control and looks down at his bloody chest, **the eye blinks once,
then closes and completely disappears against his skin.** Pulling it up in his interface returns
only the item's name:

> **Tattoo. The Eye of the Bedlam Bride.**
>
> *That's all it said. It gave no other description.*

What he can feel is "a hole inside of me. A bottomless hole." He touches it, hesitantly, and passes
out.

## Scaling

No mechanics of any kind are given — no level, no stats, no activation. The only thing the text
establishes is the **binary choice** Shi Maria framed: keep her and gain her power, or set her free.
Neither option's terms are explained.

## Status at Book's End

!!! warning "Open thread"
    **Donut rips the Shi Maria card** the moment the battle ends — "*She's not going to hurt Mongo
    ever again*" — which removes the spider from her deck permanently. The tattoo is unaffected.
    That was the entire point of making it: Shi Maria explicitly moved herself onto "something you
    can't discard so easily" *because* she expected the card to be destroyed.

    Carl's own summary, two pages later: *"I still felt it there, right on my chest. The tattoo. I
    had no idea what it really was or what it meant."*

    The associated escort quest — **"The Bedlam Bride,"** a quest to get her "crazy ass to the city
    of Larracos" — is never formally closed, failed, or updated on-page after the card is ripped.
    Book 6 does not say what the tattoo does, what "her power" would be, how one would "set her
    free," or what happens to the quest. Do not resolve any of this from inference.

## Book 7: The Terms, Finally Stated

!!! success "Book 6's open thread, answered"
    Book 6 established a binary choice and refused to explain either half of it. Book 7 explains
    both, and adds a third party nobody knew about: **Hapanzi**, Shi Maria's husband, a spider-god.
    Shi Maria is not merely attached to Carl. She is **imprisoned** in him, by her husband, and Carl
    is the lock.

### What she is

Panford, chapter 19: "*The entity contained within Carl is basically a special Reaper Spider Minion
merchant. **Part demon and part demi-god**.*" Baroness Victory adds the complication that explains
why Carl half-failed a teleport: "*it could also be the duality of the Shi Maria presence. **She, in
turn, has a god within herself.***"

### The quest fails first

Book 7 opens with the escort quest still live, and the AI needling him about it:

> **Quest Update! The Bedlam Bride.**
>
> **The quest is to get the creepy spider to the city. You're in the city, you have the spider with
> you, sort of, but you haven't won the quest yet? How is that so?**
>
> **Did you know that in some cultures, fares for travel aren't paid until the traveler safely
> arrives at their destination?**
>
> **The spider is still sitting on the bus, so to speak. You just gotta open the doors and let her
> out, and you'll win the simple prize. What's the worst that could happen?**

Carl declines to open the doors. In chapter 17 the quest reads **Quest Failed. The Bedlam Bride.**

### The procedure

Before that, the eye had been active and hostile. Chapter 16: "*The eye was open and blinking,
angrily looking back and forth, desperately attempting to cast this or that.*" Every one of those
attempts was being blocked by [Mind Balance](../abilities/mind-balance.md), silently, hundreds of
times, logged in a notification folder Carl had suppressed.

Mordecai, Rosetta and Katia construct a shared dream in which Carl must witness Shi Maria's worst
memory. Mordecai adds a drug — **Removal Order** — that gives Carl the option to **lock her in the
memory permanently** at the end.

Inside the dream, Shi Maria's own defence is a scream she repeats in two separate scenes: "*I didn't
kill you, Hapanzi! I would never kill you!*" and later "*I didn't kill him! I didn't kill him! I did
not, I did not, I did not!*"

### Hapanzi's terms

At the end of the dream, Shi Maria disappears and a second spider takes her place.

> I have her now, son, he'd said. **She is good and trapped. You can always set her free, if you
> must. But do not unless all is lost.**

And the canonical statement of the mechanic, chapter 19:

> He told me that she was gone, **trapped in my own body**. He told me that **I would now have control
> over the eye and the containment she was within. I could summon her again at will, if I wanted. But
> I could only do it once, and if I did so, she could choose. She could choose to take over my body or
> expel herself back into her own form.**
>
> In other words, **Shi Maria had become yet another bomb in my inventory. An especially dangerous
> bomb.**
>
> I had an uneasy feeling as I watched the replay. **She didn't look nearly as trapped as Hapanzi
> said.** Still, I now had control of the eye on my chest. I could feel it there, clenched closed.

So the terms are precise, and they are terrible:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Uses** | **One. Ever.** |
| **Who decides the outcome** | **She does.** Not Carl |
| **Outcome A** | She takes over his body |
| **Outcome B** | She expels herself into her own form |
| **Carl's input** | The decision to open the door. Nothing after that |

### The eye as a weapon

Carl gains conscious control of the eye and can see through it, at a cost:

> **I could now see from the eye on my chest. It was still a tattoo, but I could feel it when it was
> open. I had to make a conscious effort to keep the eye closed.** Another wave of nausea flooded me.

> I tried opening the third eye, but I clenched it shut a moment later. I had to close it. **There was
> power there. So much power. I had to keep it closed.**

It is also passively dangerous to anyone who looks into it. In chapter 54, with Vinata crushing him
and his shirt torn open, "*the eye on my chest was open, and Vinata was staring directly into it.*
**She had been driven mad by the Eye of the Bedlam Bride.**" The same thing had happened to Warlord
Stockade, which Carl only works out on review: "*I remembered that moment from the video, when he
looked right into my eye. I shook my head. Not my eye. Shi Maria's eye.*"

The dungeon knows about this. Rishi's cell in chapter 58 has a prismatic containment shield that
"*would supposedly protect from charm spells and things like the Eye of the Bedlam Bride.*"

### The comparison Carl makes himself

> Shi Maria wasn't truly gone, but she was trapped. I remembered now. **She was like an egg inside of
> me. One I could break at any time, but I had to do it myself.**
>
> **Just like Carl's Doomsday Scenario. I was gathering quite the collection of weapons of mass
> destruction.**
>
> That should've been an enormous relief. It was not.

See [Carl's Doomsday Scenario](carls-doomsday-scenario.md) for the other one.

### She keeps asking

Shi Maria spends the book negotiating from inside him, and gets more articulate as she goes:

> "**I'm still here, Carl. All you need to do is set me free. Use me while you still can. I can save
> you. Even you can't survive this chaos. It's the end of days.**" (ch. 54)

> "**You're always on rails… You're a puppet, dancing on their stage. I'm still here. You just need to
> untie me.**" (ch. 62)

!!! warning "Still open at the end of Book 7"
    **Shi Maria is never released. The one-shot is unspent.** Carl carries her, and the option,
    into Book 8.

    Also note the friendly voice in chapter 65 that pushes Carl's Mind Balance to 16 — "*You're family
    now, after what you did… She would be proud of you*" — is **explicitly neither Shi Maria nor
    Hapanzi.** It is never identified.

## Book 8: Used at Last — Without Releasing Her

!!! success "The one-shot is still unspent — but the eye itself gets used"
    Book 7 closed with the one-time release option unspent and the friendly-voice question from
    chapter 65 unidentified. Book 8 does not resolve either of those. What it does, for the first
    time since the tattoo was made, is show Carl **consciously opening the eye and drawing on its
    power** — without triggering the one-shot release clause at all. Shi Maria is not consulted,
    not freed, and not heard from on the page.

### Freezing Mitch, as a dog

During the Floor 10 Desperado Club ambush (see [Mitch](../bosses/mitch.md) for the encounter from
the mob's side), Carl — transformed into a dachshund by one of Scolopendra's nine-tier-attack
curses, with the tattoo relocated to his forehead in canine form — is cornered by the reforming
Shadow Mimic. He opens the eye on reflex, and it works as a freezing weapon:

> I'd reflexively opened the eye in the center of my forehead, and he froze in place.
>
> "No," Mitch said. His entire form started to sparkle like someone had poured glitter into him.
> "No. Don't do this. It's too cold... It's too cold!"

Donut finishes Mitch off with a full-strength *Magic Missile* while he's frozen solid. This is the
first time in three books that the eye is used **on purpose, as a weapon, by Carl** — every prior
mention was either Shi Maria trying to seize control from inside it, or the eye's passive
madness-inducing gaze (Vinata, Warlord Stockade, both in Book 7).

Afterward, Carl confirms privately that he stayed in control the entire time and deliberately shut
the eye again before Floor 11 opened:

> "No. You stay where you are. You are trapped, and you will stay trapped. I am in control."

See [Carl's own page](../characters/carl.md#freezing-mitch-as-a-dog) for the rest of that scene
(the Shadow Mimic taxonomy reveal, the Big Six factions) — it is not repeated here.

### What this does and doesn't change

- **Shi Maria herself is not released.** The one-time choice from Book 7 — summon her, and let
  *her* decide whether to take over Carl's body or expel herself — is never invoked. She does not
  speak in Book 8 at all.
- **The mechanism behind the freezing effect is never explained.** The text does not say whether
  this is "her power" in the sense Shi Maria offered back in Book 6 ("*keep me with you and gain my
  power*"), a property of the eye itself independent of her, or something else. Do not guess at
  which.
- This is the clearest on-page evidence yet that Carl has some form of **partial, controllable
  access** to the eye that stops short of the all-or-nothing release clause — a capability neither
  Book 6 nor Book 7 demonstrated.

### The Book 7 chapter-65 voice: still unidentified

!!! warning "Still open"
    Book 8 never revisits or explains the mysterious voice from Book 7, chapter 65 — the one that
    told Carl "*You're family now... she would be proud of you*" while pushing his
    [Mind Balance](../abilities/mind-balance.md) to level 16, and which the text explicitly said was
    **neither Shi Maria nor Hapanzi**. It is not named, referenced, or followed up on anywhere in
    *A Parade of Horribles*. This thread remains fully open for a future book.
