# Mark of the Damned

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Bearer:** Carl
    - **Type:** Permanent scar-tattoo, backs of both hands
    - **Received:** Floor 11 arena climax, the instant Carl renounces his worship of
      [Emberus](../bosses/emberus.md#renounced-and-confessed)
    - **In-game description:** *"Mark of the Damned"* — and nothing else
    - **Mechanical effect:** None stated anywhere in Book 8

## What Happened

Carl already carried sun-shaped tattoos on the backs of both hands from his time as a Devotee of
Emberus (Book 6). At the Floor 11 climax, he pulls off the
[Enchanted Pauper's Ring of the Steadfast Emberus](enchanted-paupers-ring-of-the-steadfast-emberus.md)
and formally renounces his faith as part of a trap to draw Emberus into a confrontation over the
death of the god's son, Geyrun (see
[Carl's own page](../characters/carl.md#renouncing-emberus) for the full confrontation and
confession). The smite that follows the renunciation doesn't strike him down — it burns the
existing tattoos into something new:

> The tattoos on the back of my hands no longer glowed. The sun shapes remained, but they now were
> raised, angry scars. When I examined them, they both had the same description with no further
> explanation. **Mark of the Damned.**

## What It Does

Nothing stated. No level, no benefit, no debuff, no further examination text. The name is the whole
of it.

!!! warning "Open thread for Book 9"
    Book 8 never revisits the Mark of the Damned after this scene. There is no indication of what,
    if anything, it does mechanically, whether it marks Carl to other gods or NPCs, or whether it
    connects to the unresolved [ring debt/curse question](enchanted-paupers-ring-of-the-steadfast-emberus.md#the-debt-the-curse-and-the-asterisk-still-explicitly-unresolved).
    Do not invent an effect for it. Treat it as deliberate, unresolved setup.
