# Enchanted Tiara of the Inebriated Dragonfly

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut
    - **Slot:** Head
    - **Built from:** A blank silver tiara + the **Flawless Jeweler's Gem** (a citrine), which came
      from a Legendary boss box for killing Queen Imogen
    - **Made at:** The gnome jeweler inside the [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md),
      for **25,000 gold**
    - **Effects:** 50% potion cooldown reduction, +10 Intelligence, poison protection, negates the
      inebriation Dexterity debuff, and a level-10 **Hover** skill

## Construction

The Flawless Jeweler's Gem sat unidentified in Donut's inventory from the very start of the floor —
its effects can't be read until it's actually set into something. Buying the setting takes Donut
twenty minutes of agonising over blank tiaras; she only picks a plain silver one when Carl threatens
to leave. She dislikes the citrine's colour right up until she reads what it does.

Crucially, they **keep** the [Enchanted Tiara of Mana Genita](tiara-of-mana-genita.md) rather than
trading it in — Carl notes it "might end up useful one day." The dragonfly tiara replaces it as her
day-to-day wear, but the Mana Genita stays in inventory, and Donut swaps tiaras situationally
throughout the book (she "switches into her dragonfly tiara" specifically in order to show off
*Hover*).

## Effects

> **50% Reduction to potion cooldown.**
>
> **Plus 10 to intelligence.**
>
> **Protection from poisoning (already obtained.)**
>
> **Negates the dexterity debuff when inebriated.**
>
> **A level 10 *Hover* Skill.**

The lore is a children's story about a dragonfly who gets too drunk to be let back into the nest
and hovers outside all night waiting for his wife to fall asleep — which is where both the hover
and the inebriation clause come from.

The potion cooldown halving is the effect Carl cares about (it is what makes chain-drinking
situationally viable) and the Intelligence is what feeds Donut's
[fire-damage nipple ring](enchanted-nipple-ring-of-the-superior-fire-demons-hand-maiden.md). Donut
cares about none of that. She cares about *Hover*.

## Hover

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Skill level | 10 |
| Uses per day | 5 |
| Duration | ~90 seconds |
| Height | 2–3 feet |
| Cooldown | 5 minutes |

Unlike *Levitate*, it is **not** negated by taking damage, and if activated mid-fall it sets the
user gently on the ground — meaning both Carl and Donut now have fall protection. Carl describes
it as "a dollar store version of what Elle had."

Donut's response — "*I can fly now. Carl, I can fly! I'm like Wonder Woman!*" — sets up a running
problem for the rest of the book: she burns hovers on practice, on showing off to other crawlers,
and on simply not walking, and Carl spends the floor telling her to stop wasting them.

## Scaling

The tiara's own bonuses are flat. The only levelled component is the granted **Hover** skill, fixed
at level 10 by the item; there is no indication it can be trained higher. The +10 Intelligence does
scale indirectly, since Donut's fire-spell damage bonus is equal to her Intelligence as a
percentage.

There is one stated cost, raised when Donut considers a different hat: wearing anything else means
losing both the *Hover* skill and the 10 Intelligence points.
