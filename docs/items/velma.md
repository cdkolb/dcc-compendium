# Velma (Tserendolgor's Enchanted Flamethrower)

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Original owner:** **Tserendolgor** ("Ren"), the Mongolian crawler and CrawlCon pet-show
      entrant
    - **Current owner:** Carl, by bequest
    - **Type:** Enchanted flamethrower — "part breastplate, part weapon," worn as a snap-on
      attachment rather than carried
    - **Obtained:** Chapter 59, in the collapsing stairwell chamber at El Capitolio Nacional in
      Havana, as Ren's dying gift

## The Weapon

Velma is not a hand weapon. It is an apparatus Ren wears: a breastplate with a flamethrower built
into it, unsnapped and dropped as a single unit. Carl only registers what it actually is when he
loots the rest of her gear afterward — "*the enchanted flamethrower, which was really part
breastplate, part weapon.*"

It is her entire fighting style. Her tank **Chuluuna** could turn his skin to stone specifically so
he could withstand it; her teammate **Khulan** paralysed targets so Ren could torch them; and she
can use it even inside a card battle, where Carl hears "the whoosh of Tserendolgor's flame thrower
[go] off, followed by an unholy screeching and a wave of heat."

Ren did not name it. Her only comment on handing it over is: "*My flamethrower is named 'Velma,' by
the way. I didn't name it that.*"

## Scaling

No stats, levels, or mechanics are given on-page. Carl loots it in the middle of a building
collapse and never gets to a saferoom to read the description before the book ends — his own
inventory note lists "Velma the Flame Thrower" among the best prizes taken from that fight,
alongside the *Eye in the Sky* scrolls, the magical tomes,
[Spunky Jefferson](spunky-jefferson.md), and Quan's celestial cloak, all of which he still needs to
examine properly.

## The Bequest

The scene is one of the book's bleakest. Ren, out of keys and out of options, refuses to fight Carl
for his, tells him "*You are an idiot... You're a symbol. You're the embodiment of our hope,*" and
then starts emptying her inventory onto the floor one item at a time: rings, clothing, potions,
scrolls, and a stack of the very rare *Eye in the Sky* scrolls.

> "I'm donating to the war effort," she said.

Carl recognises the gesture immediately and it lands hard — he flashes back to
[Miriam Dom](../characters/miriam-dom.md) doing exactly the same thing in a forest clearing, and to
his own mother: *This is my birthday present to you. I am giving you a chance at life.*

The last item out of her inventory is the **Knife of Frozen Tears**, a sylvan-crystal blade that is
the only thing capable of permanently killing her regenerating pet, Garret. She breaks while
explaining it.

Velma is later put to an unexpected use: during Shi Maria's possession of Carl's body in chapter 71,
the spider examines "Ren's flamethrower" as a possible heat source for brewing tattoo ink, before
rejecting it in favour of forcing Carl to cast *[Self-Immolation](../spells/self-immolation.md)*
instead.
