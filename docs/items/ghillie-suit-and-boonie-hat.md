# Ghillie Suit and Boonie Hat

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut
    - **Slots:** Ghillie suit — jacket (magically covers her whole body despite the slot); boonie
      hat — head
    - **Obtained:** Floor 9 opening (Book 7, chapter 1), bundled in as the accessory kit for her new
      **Deathbed Hellcat** class; collected from the saferoom mailbox in chapter 2
    - **Effects:** Suit — **Blend into the Background**, **Cone of Silence**; Hat — **Targeting**,
      increased spell range
    - **Combined effect:** Worn together, all of the above benefits are further amplified

## The Class Kit

Donut's new class, **Deathbed Hellcat** — a long-range sniper/assassin class — quadruples the range
of all her existing spells, raises her Dexterity, improves her instakill odds, and grants at-will
invisibility. It also ships with an accessory kit: a cat-sized ghillie suit and a matching boonie
hat, neither of which Donut recognises by name:

> Donut: IT COMES WITH A HAT! OMG! AND SOMETHING ELSE! WHAT'S A GHILLIE SUIT?

Carl has to explain both terms to her once they're laid out on the saferoom floor: "*And it's ghillie
suit. Not ghoulie.*" and "*We call those boonie hats. It's not the same thing as a bucket hat. It's
what snipers wear.*"

## The Ghillie Suit

> I returned my attention to the small, cat-sized ghillie suit. It came with a few interesting
> benefits, though she would indeed look absolutely ridiculous in it. The thing looked to be made of
> long, shredded bits of color-changing fabric that blended into the background. It took up a
> "jacket" slot on in her inventory even though it magically covered her entire body once she wore
> it. The suit added a skill called [Blend into the Background](../abilities/blend-into-the-background.md),
> which I hadn't yet had the time to examine, and a Cone of Silence benefit. Combined with
> invisibility, it would be great.

Donut's own verdict, before she's talked into wearing it, is considerably less enthusiastic: "*I am
absolutely not wearing this. It's like one of Signet's tattoo people fell through a paper shredder.
If I put that on, I'll look like a tassel.*"

## The Boonie Hat

> The floppy hat added a [Targeting](../abilities/targeting.md) skill, which allowed her to zoom in
> and examine mobs from afar. It was similar to the gnomish farseerer, but a lot less awkward. It
> also increased the range of all her spells, even more than they were already buffed.

Donut is more forgiving of the hat, mainly on aesthetic grounds: "*At least the hat is nice... I'm
not a huge fan of beige-colored items as a rule, but it is extra floppy, and that's always a nice
bonus.*"

## Worn Together

> If she wore both the suit and hat at the same time, all the benefits were buffed even further.
> She'd be able to snipe line-of-sight enemies from well over a mile away.

Carl's own reaction is unease rather than triumph — he immediately notes that enemy factions have
access to equivalent gear.

Mordecai later confirms both items can be worn simultaneously with Donut's other accessories: the
crupper (waist), the [Cloak of the Benevolent Champion](cloak-of-the-benevolent-champion.md)
(shoulder), and the ghillie suit (jacket) all occupy different slots.

## Scaling

No numeric level or stat values are given for either item; the effects are described only
qualitatively (increased range, added skills, mutual amplification when worn as a set).

## Role in the Story

The kit becomes a visual signature for Donut through the rest of Floor 9 — by chapter 85 she is
seen atop the Nest tower "dressed in her ghillie suit," a camouflage suit undercut by the fact that
her [Tiara of a Thousand Lights](tiara-of-a-thousand-lights.md) "glowed like a lighthouse beacon" on
her head, marking her position for every enemy gunner on the field. Both items vanish from her
person (along with the crupper) in the Book 7 epilogue when her old gear is retired.
