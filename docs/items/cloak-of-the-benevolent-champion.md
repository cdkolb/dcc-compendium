# The Cloak of the Benevolent Champion

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Princess Donut (chapter 3 onward)
    - **Slot:** Shoulder accessory — wearable alongside her crupper (waist) and ghillie suit (jacket)
    - **Rarity:** **Celestial**, **unique**
    - **Origin:** Awarded in a **Celestial Quest Box on the third floor** to **Quan Ch**; looted from
      his corpse by Carl in Book 6
    - **Headline effect:** The wearer's **lowest base stat rises to match their highest**, over 66
      hours
    - **Also:** Level-5 **Find Weakened**; **four random spells to level 15**; **one random skill to
      level 15**
    - **Distinction:** The **first Celestial item awarded during Dungeon Crawler World: Earth**,
      destined for the museum

!!! success "This is 'Quan's celestial cloak' from Book 6, finally read"
    Book 6 listed this only as an unidentified looted cloak whose "*effects change depending on who
    wears it*," which Carl planned to give to Donut but never finished reading before the floor
    ended. Book 7 chapter 3 is where the description is finally read out in full — and Carl's Book 6
    guess turns out to be right for an unexpected reason. The effects do not vary by *wearer type*;
    they vary because they are **indexed to the wearer's own stats and arsenal**.

## Physical Description

Silky fabric, black on one side and blue on the other, cut something like a magician's cape but with
short arms, so more robe than cape, and no vest. It glows soft white. It sizes itself to the wearer —
Mordecai notes that cloak-class items fit any quadruped — though on a cat it ends up "*like a shawl…
ridiculously short*," with a cerulean lining.

Donut refers to it as her shawl for the rest of the book, and it is the item she credits in chapter
86 for pushing her [Claw](../abilities/claw.md) skill from level 4 toward 15.

## The Description

> **The Cloak of the Benevolent Champion.**
>
> **This is a unique item.**
>
> **This item was originally awarded in a Celestial Quest Box during the third floor.**
>
> **Man, I hate that word. "Champion." It has lost its punch over the years, don't you think? It used
> to mean the best of the best of the best. And now? That term is handed out like candy and venereal
> diseases…**
>
> **Anyway, if you wear this magical, armed cloak, you are a champion. This is an early-dungeon
> Celestial item, and its sole purpose is to help you help your fellow crawlers survive as long as
> possible. Upon equipping this, you will slowly but surely rise to your full potential.**
>
> **This magical cloak offers the following benefits:**
>
> **Your lowest base stat will, over the course of the next 66 hours, become equal with your highest
> base stat at the time of first equip.**
>
> **A level-five Find Weakened skill, so you'll always know who to protect first.**
>
> **Four random spells from your current arsenal will increase to level 15. This benefit is immediate
> for one spell and will slowly occur over a period of one week for the others.**
>
> **One random skill from your list will increase to level 15 over the course of the next 504
> hours.**
>
> **Note: these two benefits include your full spell and skill arsenal, so it's possible an
> item-based spell or skill will be enhanced. In most cases, you will be able to retain the spell or
> skill, minus the level from the item, if you remove said item. But not always…**
>
> **Note: All of these benefits require you to wear this item continuously. You may unequip for short
> periods, but if another crawler equips this, all countdowns will immediately reset. You will not
> gain new benefits. In other words, no sharing! There can only be one champion.**
>
> **Note: This item has the distinction of being the first Celestial Item awarded during Dungeon
> Crawler World: Earth and will automatically appear in the Dungeon Crawler World: Earth museum
> display upon completion of the crawl. Legacy stipend rules apply should you survive.**

Donut's observation on the copy itself is one of the book's better small ideas: the text reads as if
written for a superhero, and she wonders whether Quan — who was Vietnamese — got a different, kinder
version. Carl confirms item descriptions vary by crawler; Katia's differ from his, and Li Jun's
reference Chinese pop culture. Mordecai waves off the museum line: "*Don't worry aboot that. It means
nothing right now.*"

## The Charisma Trick

The first benefit is a one-shot snapshot: the wearer's lowest base stat rises to the highest stat
**as measured at the moment of first equip**. Mordecai therefore refuses to let Donut put the cloak
on until she has been artificially inflated.

His potion, formerly called **Charisma Cheat** and now system-named **MySpace Photo**, "*trick[s] the
system into thinking your Charisma is 30 points higher than it really is*" — **193 instead of 163**.
It lasts **two minutes**. Donut drinks it, equips the cloak inside the window, and her Constitution
therefore climbs toward 193 rather than 163.

She receives a "very rude **Catfish**" achievement for the trouble. She also reads the *Shield*
spellbook immediately beforehand, deliberately widening her arsenal before the random-spell roll.

## The Four Spells

This is the part the party agonised over, because the roll is random and some outcomes were actively
feared: *Twinkle Toes* at 15 "*would probably just end up killing her*"; an item-based *Fireball* at
15 "*would burn the whole city down*"; *Laundry Day* was already 15 and would waste the slot; *Torch*
was already 14.

What actually came up:

| Spell | Timing |
|---|---|
| **[Bad Attitude](../spells/bad-attitude.md)** | Immediate |
| **[Wall of Fire](../spells/wall-of-fire.md)** | Over the following week |
| **[Heal Critter](../spells/heal-critter.md)** | Over the following week |
| **[Puddle Jumper](../spells/puddle-jumper.md)** | Over the following week |

Mordecai on the results: level-15 *Bad Attitude* "*can cast it on large groups all at once*";
level-15 *Wall of Fire* combined with her class and new bulk makes her "*a terror on the
battlefield*"; and level-15 *Puddle Jumper* drops the cooldown to a minute or two and lets her jump
**through walls**.

Donut had wanted *Fireball*, *Why Are You Hitting Yourself?* and *Hole*, and got none of them.

!!! note "Exact-verification note"
    **[Twinkle Toes](../spells/twinkle-toes.md) was NOT boosted by the cloak.** It appears only in the
    pre-equip list of outcomes the party was afraid of. The four boosted spells are the ones tabled
    above.

## The Skill

Unidentifiable at equip time, and it takes **504 hours — three weeks — to complete**, which is longer
than the floor. Mordecai: "*It'll probably be obvious in a week or so.*" Donut speculates hopefully
about her level-6 litterbox navigation skill.

The book never conclusively names which skill won the roll. **Claw** is the obvious candidate — Donut
credits "*my lovely shawl*" for taking it from 4 to nearly 15 in chapter 86 — but the text does not
state outright that Claw is the cloak's randomly selected skill.

## Find Weakened

> **A level-five Find Weakened skill, so you'll always know who to protect first.**

In groups of three or more like creatures, it flags which one is closest to death. Carl notes the
grim inversion: it is a healer's skill, and Quan used it "*for the exact opposite of its intended
purpose. He'd used it to find and pick off the weakest crawlers in a group.*"

## Scaling

The cloak itself has no level. Everything about it is a **timer**, and every timer is
non-transferable:

| Benefit | Duration | Restarts if another crawler equips it |
|---|---|---|
| Lowest base stat → highest | 66 hours | Yes |
| One spell → level 15 | Immediate | — |
| Three more spells → level 15 | ~1 week | Yes |
| One skill → level 15 | 504 hours | Yes |

Brief unequipping is permitted. Letting anyone else put it on resets everything and grants them
nothing. "*There can only be one champion.*"
