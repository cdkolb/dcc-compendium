# Enchanted Nightgaunt Cloak of Stoutness

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Slot:** Cloak
    - **Obtained:** Gold Apparel Box, opened during the Floor 1 tutorial (the same box that produced the Trollskin Shirt)
    - **Rarity note:** Received alongside the Trollskin Shirt in the same box — Mordecai visibly gasps at both appearing together

## Effect

**+4 Constitution.** Resistant to poison- and ice-based attacks. Adds Anti-Piercing resistance to
all worn armor. Per its in-game description, it also "makes you look like a dollar store Batman,"
with a warning that an actual Nightgaunt spying the wearer "probably won't be too happy with you."

## Scaling

The cloak grants flat bonuses only — the book describes no level-by-level curve for its
Constitution boost or resistances.

## Role in the Story

Along with the Trollskin Shirt, this is one of the two pieces of gear that immediately mark Carl
as having gotten unusually lucky with his very first loot boxes — Mordecai's reaction (a gasp) is
the earliest signal in the book that Carl is being favored by the loot system.
