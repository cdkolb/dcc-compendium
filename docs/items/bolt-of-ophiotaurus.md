# The Bolt of Ophiotaurus

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Katia
    - **Slot:** Crossbow ammunition
    - **Obtained:** Benefactor box, sponsored by Princess Formidable of the Skull Empire
    - **Rarity:** 1 of 100 ever generated

## Effect

Functions as an ordinary crossbow bolt unless fired directly into the eye of a deity. On a
successful strike, it pauses the target god's invulnerability for **fifteen seconds**.

## Scaling

Not applicable — a single-use, flatly-defined effect rather than something that scales with a
stat or skill.

## Role in the Story

Katia receives exactly one, gifted by **Princess Formidable** — youngest sibling of the Maestro
and Crown Prince Stalwart. Carl reads the gift less as genuine support for Katia and more as
Formidable sending a message to her surviving brothers. It goes unused during the Floor 4 fight
against the resurrected Maestro (piloting the god Grull), since the party's plan doesn't call for
directly damaging the invulnerable deity — Carl notes it would have been wasted there regardless.
