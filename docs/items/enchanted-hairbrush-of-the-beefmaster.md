# The Enchanted Hairbrush of the Beefmaster

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Owner:** Carl
    - **Rarity:** **Celestial** — his third, after the
      [Scavenger's Daughter](the-scavengers-daughter.md) and the Gloom Wraith Phase potion
    - **Obtained:** Chapter 56, from a **Celestial Who-Let-The-Gods-Out Box** awarded for the attack
      on Club Vanquisher
    - **Requirement:** Male, with an average hair length of **25 centimetres or longer**
    - **Use:** Brush for **five minutes straight**, **once per day**, on **one person**
    - **Duration:** **30 hours**
    - **Grants:** Shining Charisma, [Speedster](../abilities/speedster.md), **+25% Constitution**,
      and a level-10 *[Entourage](../spells/entourage.md)*
    - **It screams**

The hair requirement is the joke that stopped being funny. Katia's hair-tonic mishap in chapter 5
left Carl with waist-length hair he had every intention of cutting off. This item makes cutting it a
25%-Constitution decision.

## The Description

> **The Enchanted Hairbrush of the Beefmaster.**
>
> **This item may only be used on a male with an average hair length of 25 centimeters or longer.**
>
> **This item may only be used on a single person, once per day.**
>
> **Samson. Fabio. Michael Bolton. Jim Morrison. The entire band Stryper. Yanni.**
>
> **Your history is filled with luscious men whose power resided in their lovely locks. Now, it's
> your turn.**
>
> **If you use this magical hairbrush upon your head for five minutes straight, you will receive the
> following benefits for the next thirty hours:**
>
> **The Shining Charisma benefit.**
>
> **The Speedster benefit.**
>
> **Plus 25% to your constitution.**
>
> **Level 10 Entourage.**
>
> **Plus your hair will be shiny and healthy, and it will goddamn glisten.**

!!! note "Exact-verification note"
    The item is usable **once per day** and the benefits last **thirty hours**. Those are two
    different numbers in the same block, not a single "once per 30 hours" limit.

## The Screaming

Donut owns an ordinary-looking magical hairbrush. This one is shining gold with a jewelled handle,
and it does not brush quietly:

> every time I pulled it through my hair, it screamed. **It screamed like a group of women squealing
> and cheering at a rock concert.** And when I stopped brushing my hair, the brush would shout
> "**Encore! Encore!**" for two minutes straight until the voices finally faded away.

Five minutes of continuous brushing, followed by two minutes of a phantom stadium demanding an
encore, once every single day, for the rest of the floor.

## What the Benefits Are Actually Worth

Carl's own assessment, benefit by benefit, is refreshingly unsentimental:

**Shining Charisma** — gives everyone in his party an additional 20% of his Charisma. Donut's
Charisma is already godlike, so she gains six points. Marginal.

***Entourage*** at level 10 — an illusion spell that mirrors party members. Donut already has it as a
bard skill. Redundant.

**+25% Constitution** — "*huge… That alone made the brush frustratingly invaluable. It was just like
with my boxers. **It was now too good to give up.***" This is the one that traps him. The hair stays.

**[Speedster](../abilities/speedster.md)** — the real prize:

> The benefit was similar to Donut's *Twinkle Toes* spell, though instead of casting it on Mongo, it
> worked on myself. I could already run super-fast thanks to my strength and dexterity, but Speedster
> basically allowed me to **move short distances very, very quickly**. I wouldn't be quite like the
> Flash superhero, but I was still impressively quick. And while I couldn't do it long distance, **I
> could flash across the battlefield in seconds. And best of all, I could combine it with my
> [Gloom Wraith Phase](../spells/gloom-wraith-phase.md), allowing the distance I could travel while
> phased to be much further.**

Mordecai had never seen **Speedster** awarded by an item. It is normally a rare natural unlock at
Dexterity 100, and even then uncommon — the crawler **Osvaldo**, not a member of the Princess Posse,
is the only other person known to have it.

## Scaling

The hairbrush has no level. Its granted *Entourage* is fixed at level 10 and the Constitution bonus
is a flat percentage. The variables are entirely logistical:

- **Five uninterrupted minutes** of brushing to activate, in a war
- **Once per day**, for **thirty hours** of benefit — so the effect can be kept permanently active
  with six hours of overlap, provided nothing interrupts the daily ritual
- **25 cm of hair minimum**, which is now a permanent equipment constraint rather than a cosmetic
  choice
