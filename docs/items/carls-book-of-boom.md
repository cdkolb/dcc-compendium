# Carl's Book of Boom (and A Banquet Fit for a Princess)

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **What it is:** **Volume two** of
      [The Filthy Little Crawler's Book of Voodoo](the-filthy-little-crawlers-book-of-voodoo.md),
      created in chapter 85 when the original book splits itself in half
    - **Contents:** Roughly **30,000 pages**, each donated voluntarily by a departing crawler
    - **Mechanic:** **Eating a page** temporarily grants that crawler's skill or spell
    - **Donut's copy:** A separate equivalent volume titled **A Banquet Fit for a Princess**
    - **Cost to the donor:** **Permanent.** They lose the ability they wrote down
    - **Organised by:** **Milk**, using
      [Stayfast Ink](stayfast-ink-and-adepts-fountain-pen.md)

This is the Book of Voodoo's "nefarious purpose" — flagged since Book 6 as obvious setup — arriving as
a going-away present from thousands of people who are leaving the dungeon alive.

## The Ceremony

Chapter 85. Standing in the rings above Larracos are thousands of crawlers who have taken a deal on
the next floor, plus the former crawlers going home. Milk addresses them with her voice magically
amplified by a word etched on her own throat:

> "We have a present for you, and for all the brave souls who have decided to stay and fight on."

Then the notifications:

> **Your Book of Voodoo has been updated.**
>
> **Your Book of Voodoo has been updated with a metric fuckton of entries.**
>
> **For fucks sake. Finally.**
>
> **Your Book of Voodoo has been segmented into two volumes. The second volume is entitled Carl's
> Book of Boom.**

"*For fucks sake. Finally.*" is the AI's editorial. It had been telling Carl to work this out since
chapter 57.

## The Book

> Somewhere along the way, the book of Voodoo had magically changed since it now should have literally
> **almost 30,000 pages**. It still had about 500 pages, and when I flipped through it, **it sort of
> zeroed in on the entry I was supposed to find.** This second one was the same.

A sample page:

> **Crawler Number 1,982,122 Asi Hamami Level 59 Fox Warrior.**
>
> **Carl, thank you for helping us. I have lost so much, but I still have not lost my hope.**

Beneath the note, a crude red-ink drawing of a lock and a key, and:

> **Consuming this page will give you a level 10 Lockpick skill for a period of five minutes.**

Donut gets her own volume, and her own reaction:

> **CARL, CARL I GOT A BOOK CALLED A BANQUET FIT FOR A PRINCESS! IT'S FILLED WITH PICTURES AND NICE
> THINGS ABOUT ME! AND SPELLS!**

## The Mechanic

Milk's explanation is the load-bearing paragraph, and the cost clause is the part worth reading twice:

> "They're leaving the dungeon, so **I taught them how to remove a skill or spell and paint it onto a
> page. They each did three.** One for you, Carl. One for you, Donut, and one on their own entry page,
> which is now a **first come, first served** sort of deal in the original book of Voodoo that anyone
> can use. The one for you and Donut are personal for you. **They permanently lose the skills when
> they transfer it to the book, and when you eat the page, you only gain it for a very short period of
> time, depending on the quality of the drawing.** But each one should have a personal note
> attached…"

| | Donor | Recipient |
|---|---|---|
| **Cost / gain** | **Permanently loses** the skill or spell | Gains it for "*a very short period of time*" |
| **Quantity** | Three pages each: one for Carl, one for Donut, one public | 30,000 pages, single-use each |
| **Duration** | — | Scales with **the quality of the drawing** |

The third page each donor wrote goes into the **original** Book of Voodoo on their own entry page, as
a **first-come first-served** resource any crawler who owns the book can claim. So the gift is not
only to Carl and Donut — it is a shared armoury for everyone still fighting.

Thousands of people about to walk out of the dungeon alive chose to permanently cripple their own
abilities, three times over, to hand them to the two crawlers staying behind. That is the whole
sentiment of the scene and the book does not belabour it.

Donated **gear** is also left waiting in the guild for distribution.

## The Chain That Got Here

The mechanic did not appear from nowhere. It is the end of a thread running through the whole book:

1. **[The toraline ink tattoo system](toraline-tattoo-transfer.md)** proves an ability can be moved
   between people via blood and ink
2. **Milk's Words of Power calligraphy** proves written script can amplify an inked ability
3. **Edgar's new ink** proves an ability can be extracted from someone who is no longer alive to give
   consent
4. **[Stayfast Ink](stayfast-ink-and-adepts-fountain-pen.md)**, which never fades, plus a book that
   broadcasts to every crawler who owns one
5. **This**

## Scaling

No level. The two variables are **quality of the drawing**, which sets how long the eaten page's
ability lasts, and **page count** — 30,000 single-use pages, depleted one at a time by eating them.
