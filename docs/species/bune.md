# Bune

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **First described:** Book 4, *The Gate of the Feral Gods* (by Mordecai)
    - **Type:** Small, winged, dragon-like lizard people — "the elves of the lizard world"
    - **Book 7 role:** A very common former-crawler race among the 50,000 who return for Faction
      Wars; **Opiee**, the Madness's adjutant, is one

## Mordecai's write-up (Book 4)

> "He was a bune," Mordecai said. "They are a slight, dragon-like people. Naturally peaceful, but
> they can be some of the most clever fighters. That was his real race. I've seen a few crawlers
> this season who've picked it. Good rogues and magic casters, but they have low constitution. They
> grow wings and get a dexterity bonus when they hit level 50. If crocodilians are the barbarians of
> the lizard world, think of the bune as the elves."

## Book 7

Bune are everywhere in Book 7, largely because so many of them are former crawlers who came back.
The first three through the door with Tipid and Rosetta are bune: "All three were small, winged,
lizard-like creatures called bune." A crowd of them mobs Donut on her arrival at the FUPA — "a swarm
of bune dragon people came rushing up to surround Donut" — and they're listed among the assembled
races cheering her: "humans, skyfowl, crocodilians, bune, yenk, and so many others pumped their
fists or wings in the air, chanting."

The book also clarifies the flight limitation Mordecai skipped over:

> Opiee rose in the air, his little wings fluttering. **These bune things usually walked on the
> ground, but they could fly short distances.**

### Opiee

**Opiee** is the adjutant for **the Madness** — "a tiny, floating Bune. The little dragon creature's
name was Opiee." He is one of the three adjutants on the minor quorum that adjudicates Commander
Stockade's cheating, and he is the one who lays out the case against **Architect Houston** before
declining to charge him:

> "Houston's presence here in the castle and the possible collusion by a family member, who is not
> bound by any such rules, is circumstantial evidence. Quite damning circumstantial evidence at
> that..." He paused for dramatic effect. "...but I don't feel it's enough to officially charge him.
> Unless you want to confess."

A bune mage named **Huey** fights in the defence of the FUPA.
