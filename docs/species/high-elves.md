# High Elves

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Near-immortal ruling elf caste, first mentioned in Book 2
    - **Homeland (Floor 6):** A single fortified castle in the "Liana district," south of the
      river dividing the Hunting Grounds
    - **Patron deity:** [Apito](../mechanics/index.md#the-apito-pantheon-book-5), the Blessed Oak Mother
    - **Ruler (Book 5):** [Queen Imogen](../characters/queen-imogen.md), a Floor 6 country boss

## Overview

High Elves first appear in Book 2 as the source race for the banished
[City Elves](city-elves.md) — a near-immortal caste that practices mass exile of anything it
judges "defective," up to and including entire offshoot bloodlines. Book 5's Floor 6 finally shows
their society directly: a single, heavily isolated, xenophobic nation occupying the thickest part
of the jungle south of the river, powerful enough that even the floor's professional hunters give
their territory a wide berth.

Racially, High Elves are near-identical to Bush Elves and [City Elves](city-elves.md) by any
biological measure — Mordecai notes a DNA test would find no real difference — but consider
themselves a separate, superior bloodline anyway, reserving wealth, magic education, and comfort
for themselves while treating every other elf-adjacent people as disposable labor. Bush Elves are
the ordinary rural Floor 6 elf population — poorer, more numerous, and treated with the same
casual contempt as every other non-High-Elf race on the floor; several fight alongside Signet's
resistance.

## The Genocide of the Chee

Long before Scolopendra's nine-tier attack, then-Princess (now Queen) Imogen — acting as her
father King Finian's "Sorcerer General" — led a campaign to seize the land of a peaceful, carpenter
people called the **Chee**, using a mass transfiguration spell to permanently turn every villager
into an animal before sending in "cleansers" to finish the job. A handful of Chee children escaped
as **were-castors** — see [Were-castor](were-castor.md) — and spent the following centuries as
a guerrilla resistance, eventually allying with [Tsarina Signet](../characters/tsarina-signet.md)
for Book 5's endgame.

This same pattern — seize land, erase the people who lived there, absorb their magic — is a
recurring thread in every piece of Floor 6 lore about the High Elves, including the belief (per
the were-castor Clint) that the elves knew Scolopendra's attack was coming and had already looted
critical protective magic from her lair beforehand, which is why they alone came through the
disaster essentially unscathed while every neighboring people was devastated or transmuted.

## Society and Religion

High Elf society is built entirely around worship of **Apito**, the Blessed Oak Mother — see the
[Apito pantheon](../mechanics/index.md#the-apito-pantheon-book-5) for the deity's full mythology
and the **Butcher's Masquerade** ritual that structures the book's climax. Per Queen Imogen, Apito
"seeks harmony above all," and to the High Elves this doctrine justifies exactly the kind of
ethnic cleansing practiced against the Chee: *"To grow the perfect tree, one must first cut off the
rot."*

The nation is a strict, layered aristocracy. Ordinary High Elf soldiery ranges from unarmored
**Footmen** and **Castle Guards** (gold-liveried, relatively friendly working staff) up to
black-and-gold **royal guards** and white-clad, silver-lanced **soldiers** who escort the queen and
her familiar's ceremonial processions. Pets and familiars — including
[Sir Ferdinand](../characters/ferdinand.md) — are kept as long as a reigning monarch is personally
fond of them, but High Elf history is described as a repeating cycle of tiring of a pet (or an
entire allied species) once it's judged too intelligent, too cunning, or too eager, and ordering
its execution alongside the rest of its kind.
