# Gargoyle

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **First appearance:** Book 7, *This Inevitable Ruin*
    - **Type:** A selectable **crawler race** — stone-skinned, durable, unloved
    - **Known individual:** **[Volteeg](../characters/volteeg.md)**, Tank Buttress, author of the seventh edition of the
      *Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*; birth race [Sturnus](sturnus.md)
    - **Signature cost:** Gargoyles **cannot sing**

## Overview

Gargoyle is one of the race choices available on the third floor, and Book 7 uses it as a case
study in the permanence of race changes. **Volteeg** — who entered the dungeon as a pet songbird
and was given intelligence by a pet biscuit — chose gargoyle for the mind it gave him:

> "The pet biscuit had been a curse, but changing his race had been the worst decision of all. The
> biscuit had given him some intelligence and the ability to speak, but not much else. It wasn't
> until the third floor, when he chose to turn into a gargoyle, did his brain finally start to
> churn. Did the images start to make sense in his mind. Did the strategies coalesce, and his sense
> of self finally take form."

And the price:

> "He'd chosen a race that could not sing. Not like before, and that was such a terrible, terrible
> mistake."

## Traits

| Trait | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Cannot sing** | Stated outright. Volteeg's post-change voice is "like rocks being dragged against one another" |
| **Nobody wants to fight one** | "One of the few good things about being a gargoyle was that nobody actually wanted to fight you" — enough that Volteeg survived 26 seasons as an unprotected NPC guard in a combat guild without killing a single crawler |
| **Sleeps for very long stretches** | "This was another good thing about his race. He could sleep for long periods, and nobody would ever know" |
| **Stone skin / stillness** | Can "turn his skin to stone and just disappear" |
| **Gravity intolerance** | Outside the dungeon, "if he spent too much time in a place with gravity heavier than half of his original planet, he would start to collapse in on himself. In heavy gravity, he needed a brace just to stand properly" |

!!! warning "The magical traits do not survive the dungeon"
    This is the part Book 7 stresses. On exit, "the magical properties of his adopted race didn't
    work anymore. Not outside the dungeon. That was something they didn't tell you when you changed
    your body. They changed you, and they couldn't change you back." Volteeg loses the long sleep
    and the stone skin, and is left with only the gravity problem — which forces him into a career
    "where he never had to go down a gravity well ever again," working security on the Auditor
    General's compliance barge.

    The commonly cited "sleeps for entire seasons" framing is a paraphrase; the text says "long
    periods."

## Volteeg's end

Volteeg fights in Faction Wars as **Weeper Commander Volteeg for the Operatic Collective**, having
been quietly reclassified by the Open Intellect Pacifist Network as a former indentured rather than
a former crawler so he wouldn't be dragged into the Bone Clan's conscript pool. He ends his own
story by filling a Weeper-class tank with explosives and detonating it inside the Bloc's formation,
killing over 10,000 of his own side including Warlord Hortense Leadership Unit — which eliminates
the Operatic Collective from Faction Wars. See
[The Faction Wars Campaign](../factions/faction-wars-campaign.md).
