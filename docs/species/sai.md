# Sai

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Bipedal rhinoceros people; "steampunk samurai" in presentation
    - **Size:** Roughly seven feet tall and heavily built
    - **Former profession:** Firefighters, until war-zone firefighting nearly wiped them out
    - **Current role:** **The Armored Sai** — the standing guards of the **Larracos level**, the
      bottom floor of the [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md)

## Overview

Carl hears about the Sai before he ever sees one. Reading up on the Desperado Club's Larracos
level, he notes that its psionic management keeps "rock creatures, lizard creatures, and 'The
Armored Sai'" in line, and that "the sai were the regular guards of the Larracos level, though I'd
never seen one. Apparently they were like samurai rhinoceroses."

They turn up in person during the club's destruction, when a demon breaks loose in the restaurant:

> "a whole stampede of bipedal, rhinoceros samurai dudes dashed from the room, screaming as they
> rushed the demon in the restaurant. They were decked out like steampunk samurais, complete with
> swords and blades at the end of sticks. Each stood about seven feet tall and were huge. The
> ground shook as they charged."

## The AI's write-up

> "The Sai are quite serious about their guard duties, mostly because it's the only gig they can
> really get. They used to be firefighters, but firefighting in a war zone is a good way to make
> yourself almost extinct. So, they migrated into the Desperado Club and now are known as the
> ever-present guards of the bottom floor of the Desperado. Focusing on their duties distracts them
> from all those urban legends about their armor being too itchy. These creatures aren't really
> known for their intelligence, but they're generally good guys, loyal until the end. That's what
> makes them good guards. They don't care about rules or laws or morals. They just do what they're
> told."

Carl explicitly distinguishes them from the ordinary rhinoceros mounts elsewhere in the series —
Sir Ferdinand's rhino mount is "more like the real deal than these guys, who walked on two feet."

## Book 7: what happened to the guards

The destruction of the Desperado Club has a direct cost to the Sai as a people. When the club
reorganizes under its new management, the survivors lose the only job their species reliably gets.
Carl meets one of them as a prisoner — a duct-taped mercenary sergeant named **[Toyotomi](../characters/toyotomi.md)**, level 70,
"Sai Outpost Guard Mercenary Leader," working castle security for the Prism:

> "Toyotomi was once an honorable, experienced leader of Sai. He has since lost his way. His entire
> squad was killed during an unfortunate incident at the Desperado Club that you may or may not have
> been partially responsible for. **During the club's reorganization, the few Sai survivors were
> relegated to the mercenary markets, where he was quickly snatched up.**"

Carl draws the line himself: "I remembered what Clarabelle had told me earlier. All the sai and
cretin guards had been fired and replaced." Other Sai turn up in the Bone Clan's army — "There were
quite a few of the Sai rhino guards in their midst, along with several of the Bactrian and
Dromedarian camels."

## Edifice Shield

Toyotomi's introduction also gives the Sai a signature ability. It is a fortification buff, and
notably it is **scale-limited** — it works on outposts, not palaces:

> "I was hired to help protect this castle, but my magic is for outposts, not primary palaces. If
> your castle is bigger than this place, I won't be able to cast my **Edifice Shield** spell.
> Therefore, I am useless to you."

Carl finds a use for it anyway. Toyotomi joins the Princess Posse and becomes part of the defence of
**the Nest**, Justice Light's tower:

> "And as soon as the men up there place the last block and the tower is complete, the bonus will
> activate and the strength will increase to 200%. And after that, I will cast Edifice Shield which
> **doubles that**. With Princess Donut's Sentry and Flak spells, most incoming fire should get
> deflected."

Toyotomi is killed defending it — "cleaved right in half with a spell... Toyotomi was dead-dead."
Book 6's read on the species holds up: they are not clever, and they are loyal until the end.
