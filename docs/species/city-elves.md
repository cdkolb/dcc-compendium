# City Elves

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Origin:** Banished offshoot of the High Elves
    - **Religion:** Worship of Apito, the Oak Mother, and her "angels" (the skyfowl)
    - **Notable group:** The **201st Security Group** — a militia/cult active on Floor 3
    - **Danger:** Individually weak magic-users; genuinely dangerous in organized groups

## Overview

Per Mordecai, all elves originate from the **High Elves**, a species built around near-eternal
life and a culture of "banishing and wholesale genocide" against anything they consider
defective. City elves are High Elves' rejects — individuals whose beliefs are considered such an
embarrassing genetic/social defect that they're expelled to live among the "earthbound" races in
cities. [Tsarina Signet](../characters/tsarina-signet.md), a half-Naiad, half-High-Elf, was
similarly rejected by her father's people for being a "mongrel," though she's not herself a city
elf.

City elves gather in urban settlements and frequently form small, poorly-organized militias.
Mordecai compares them directly to real-world conspiracy-theorist subcultures — "tin-foil-hat-
wearing, silver-drinking nutjobs" — except their specific obsession is theological: they believe
the **skyfowl** are literal angels, and that Apito (the elvish mother-of-all-gods) commanded that
any violence against them must be stopped, on pain of universal destruction. Skyfowl themselves
treat city elves as a running joke — non-religious, and openly contemptuous of "flightless"
races, they find city elves' obeisance more amusing than useful.

## The 201st Security Group

The specific city elf militia encountered in Book 2, based out of a skyfowl settlement's "Apito
Education Center." Its members wear a homemade uniform patch (**201st Security Group – Magical
Ops**) styled after real-world military insignia, and genuinely believe themselves a legitimate
armed force. In practice they're a small cell of low-level (~Level 16) mages — Root Druids, Wind
Mages, Fire Mages, Summoners, Light Healers — who'd already lost several members ("champions")
in earlier, failed confrontations with Carl and Donut before the group's final destruction.

The group had been secretly glamoured and directed by [Miss Quill](../bosses/miss-quill.md),
posing as a divine messenger of Apito, into serving as her enforcers: corpse disposal, corralling
her manufactured krasue, and recruiting sex workers into her scheme under the pretense of temple
outreach.

### A Known Weakness

City elves have an extreme, almost hysterical aversion to items made from oakwood — a nod to
their goddess's name. Princess Donut's [Enchanted Anklet of the Fallen Oak](../items/anklet-of-the-fallen-oak.md)
(a minor item with no combat-relevant properties otherwise) is enough to send an entire armed
cell into a full panic, convinced she is "the Oak Fell," their prophesied antichrist figure.
