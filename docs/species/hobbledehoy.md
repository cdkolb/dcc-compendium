# Hobbledehoy

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Proportionally small humans — a generic category rather than a nation or culture
    - **Distinguishing feature:** *Not* halflings and *not* elves — no hairy oversized feet, no
      pointed ears, just a human scaled down
    - **Reputation:** Short-tempered, ego-driven, and prone to picking fights they lose
    - **Book 6 individual:** **Pater Coal**, Hobbledehoy High Cleric Supreme of Emberus
      (level 101) — see [Characters](../characters/index.md)

## Overview

The AI introduces the race in a single block when Carl meets Pater Coal at
[Club Vanquisher](../locations/club-vanquisher.md):

> "First off, this guy is a Hobbledehoy. In case you haven't figured out what that means, it's a
> generic term for a small human. They don't have the hairy, oversized feet of halflings, or the
> pointed ears of elves. Everything about them is proportionally smaller to Earth humans, except
> their egos and their tempers. They tend not to survive long in integrated cities because they're
> always starting fights and getting themselves wiped out."

Carl's own first impression matches: "a human-like creature, older, wearing long, red robes... only
about four and a half feet tall, but he didn't have the wide body and face of a dwarf. He looked
like a regular person that had been shrunk," with long white hair — "a weird mix between Gandalf
and a hobbit."

## Pater Coal

Pater Coal is explicitly flagged as an exception to the race's reputation: "the even-tempered high
cleric is in charge of the Emberus shrine(s) here in Club Vanquisher. He is one of the few mortals
who is in constant contact with the deity."

He intercepts Carl at the Club Vanquisher door, talks past the Hellik-worshipping Ares ram guards,
suppresses Carl's slugpox, and performs the foot-washing sacrament in the secret **Emberus High
Temple of the Ending Sun** — a rite whose real purpose is that it blocks other gods from
eavesdropping, letting him brief Carl on the murder of Geyrun without being overheard. He also
quietly arranges for Carl to "buy" a debuff-resisting ring disguised as a strength ring.
