# Were-castor

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Were-creature race (beaver-based transformation), formerly the **Chee**
    - **Floor:** 6 ("The Hunting Grounds")
    - **Named individuals:** Clint Smashgrab, Holger, and four unnamed brothers; their teacher
      Nadine (a transformed Bearded Sage Caterpillar); their village leader, the child golem "young Clint"
    - **Allied with:** [Tsarina Signet](../characters/tsarina-signet.md)

## The Chee

Before High Elf Queen Imogen — then a princess and her father's Sorcerer General — led a campaign
to seize their land, the **Chee** were a peaceful people of carpenters and teachers on Floor 6.
Imogen's forces cast a mass transfiguration spell on the entire village and sent in "cleansers" to
finish the job; the only survivors were a handful of children, transformed into new forms that
stuck permanently. Six boys became **were-castors** — human-sized were-beavers, capable of
shifting between a short, stocky "Chee" humanoid form and a fully furred, buck-toothed beaver form
with a lisp — while their teacher, a woman named Nadine, became a **Bearded Sage
Caterpillar** (see the description on her page in-book as a giant, intelligent, tree-dwelling
insect who raised the six boys to adulthood in hiding).

## In the Story

Two of the six, **Clint Smashgrab** (bald, missing a leg below the knee, replaced with a metal
bar) and **Holger** (recognizable by his mullet), are Carl and Donut's primary contacts among
Signet's forces — bickering, foul-mouthed, and quick to violence with each other but fanatically
loyal fighters, immune to the paralysis effect of [Night Weasels](../mobs/night-weasel.md) and
strong enough in constitution to tank sustained mob damage without much visible injury. Both
fight alongside Carl and Donut at [Point Mongo](../locations/point-mongo.md) and during the
assault on [Fort Freedom](../locations/fort-freedom.md).

Clint and Nadine are both killed during the Fort Freedom assault — Nadine to a stray lightning
bolt from [Claude Sludgington the Fourth](../bosses/claude-sludgington.md), Clint on the opposite
shore to [Big Tina](../bosses/big-tina.md). Signet has Edgar the tortoise tattoo both of their
memories onto her own skin that same night, using a needle dipped in Nadine's remains — the
resulting "blood and ink elemental" recreates not just Nadine but a small, tattooed version of
Clint as he appeared as a child, since Nadine's memory of him was strong enough to bring his
likeness along without a body to draw from.

Holger survives through the book's end, present at the Butcher's Masquerade fighting alongside
Carl's group.
