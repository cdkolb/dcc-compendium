# Primal

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Race choice available to crawlers (also a species in its own right, per lore)
    - **Stat effect:** -1 to all base stats, but unlocks all higher-tier skills up to level 20
    - **Notable crawler:** [Carl](../characters/carl.md), Book 2 onward
    - **In-universe status:** Ancient, vanished precursor species — near-mythical

## The Race Choice

"Primal" is a selectable race during a crawler's Floor 3 race/class selection. Per its in-game
description:

> "For the first several seasons of *Dungeon Crawler World*, all contestants started off as
> Primals. Primals are blank slates. You will look the same as before. You will obtain all
> skills associated with your current race, with the following exceptions: -1 to all base stats.
> All higher-tier skills are unlocked and are able to train to 20."

Choosing it costs a crawler roughly five stat points relative to picking Human (which grants +2
to all base stats), but removes the normal training cap most skills hit around level 10–11 —
according to Mordecai, a level-15 spell is dramatically more powerful than its base version
("four times as potent"), and a level 20 skill can be effectively unstoppable. It's a high-risk,
high-ceiling choice most crawlers avoid; Mordecai notes only a rare few who pick it ever
successfully train a skill past 15.

## The Lore

Per Mordecai, Primals are the setting's deep-lore precursor race — "the progenitors, the first
known species to conquer the universe... the boogeymen of the cosmos." Nobody knows what they
actually looked like; they vanished after a galaxy-spanning war of unknown origin, leaving only
ruins. When someone discovers an abandoned Primal site, the site's resident AI reportedly takes
on the appearance of whoever found it — which is offered as the in-universe explanation for why
a crawler who picks Primal "looks the same as before" rather than transforming. Some cultures
worship Primals as gods; mothers invoke them to frighten children; it's said they will one day
return.

## Notable Crawlers

Carl chooses Primal as his race on Floor 3, largely because it pairs with his intended
Compensated Anarchist build and its emphasis on training a small set of skills as high as
possible, rather than because of the lore attached to it.
