# Naiad

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** River-dwelling, aquatic humanoid species
    - **Floor:** 6 ("The Hunting Grounds") — the river dividing the city and elf districts
    - **Notable individuals:** [Tsarina Signet](../characters/tsarina-signet.md) (half-Naiad,
      half-High-Elf), [Samantha/Psamathe](../characters/samantha.md) (half-Naiad, a minor deity)
    - **Government:** The **Naiad Confederacy**, holed up in the ruined river palace
      [Fort Freedom](../locations/fort-freedom.md)

## Overview

Naiads are a hostile, aquatic, blue-tinged species native to the river that bisects Floor 6,
splitting the human-controlled city district from the [High Elf](high-elves.md) territory to the
south. Full-blooded Naiads are fanged, horned, and demon-like in appearance — a look that carries
over into their half-blooded offspring; Signet is described as favoring "the more demon-like,
monstrous visage of her naiad heritage" over her human-passing half-sister Imogen. Wild Naiads
attack anything that lingers too near the water, and are fast and vicious enough to snatch a
freshly-killed corpse off a riverbank in under a second.

Water does not touch a Naiad's skin directly — tears and rain both hover a millimeter off Signet's
body before flying away — which she describes not as a magical convenience but as a curse, cast
against her by a High Elf collaborator the night her mother was assassinated.

## The Confederacy

Before Scolopendra's attack, the Naiad kingdom was ruled by a royal line; Signet's mother was a
princess in that line, secretly involved with the High Elf King Finian (Signet's father), which
made Signet an unrecognized bastard rejected by both royal households. A revolution — quietly
assisted by the High Elves in exchange for peace between the two peoples — assassinated the
tsarina-to-be before she could take the throne peacefully, installing an anarchic government
euphemistically called **the Naiad Confederacy**. In the centuries since, the Confederacy has done
essentially nothing but occupy the old river palace, letting the wider Naiad population regress
into starving, feral anarchy while continuing to pay tribute to the High Elves.

By the time Carl and Donut arrive, the Confederacy's own throne room has been quietly squatted by
an entirely separate menace — see [Claude Sludgington the Fourth](../bosses/claude-sludgington.md) —
without anyone in the government seemingly able (or willing) to do anything about it.

## Vengeance of the Daughter

Signet's Book 5 storyline (see her [character page](../characters/tsarina-signet.md) and the
[Timeline](../timeline/index.md#floor-6-the-hunting-grounds-book-5)) is built around avenging her
mother and reclaiming the Naiad throne, in exchange for eventual help against the High Elves. Part
One of her *Vengeance of the Daughter* quest — a coordinated amphibious assault on
[Fort Freedom](../locations/fort-freedom.md) with Carl and Donut's assistance — successfully
topples the Confederacy, though Signet declines to rule in her mother's place herself, citing her
curse. Part Two redirects the quest entirely toward Queen Imogen and the High Elves; Signet never
gets the chance to rebuild Naiad society before the book's climax.
