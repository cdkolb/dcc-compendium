# Shadow Mimics

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Sapient mob species; one of the "Big Six" power groups vying for control of the
      dungeon, per the system AI's own taxonomy (see [Factions & Organizations](../factions/index.md#the-big-six))
    - **Growth mechanic:** Unbounded — "one of the few entities in the dungeon who grow
      significantly in intelligence and strength and power as they age with no limit"
    - **Detection:** Functionally undetectable to most spells; described by Mordecai as "like one
      of Carl's level 15 traps. All but invisible"
    - **Declared archenemy:** The [Changeling Principals](../dungeon-factions/the-changeling-principals.md) —
      per the system AI, Shadow Mimics are "the counter to their archenemies, the changeling
      principals"
    - **First confirmed individual:** **[Mitch](../bosses/mitch.md)** ("Mitch Fiorelli"), an
      Adolescent Shadow Mimic, level 120 — killed on Floor 10 (Book 8)
    - **Confirmed infestation sites:** The [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md) casino
      (Floor 10, Book 8) and the [Pineapple Cabaret](../dungeon-factions/pineapple-cabaret.md)
      (a Book 8-revealed pocket dimension)

## Overview

Shadow Mimics are Book 8's biggest species reveal: a mimic subtype the system AI singles out,
mid-fight, as one of the true power players of the dungeon's endgame rather than just another
monster type. They are introduced through **Mitch**, an ambush boss disguised as the Desperado
Club's Wheel of Fortune croupier, and confirmed independently as an infestation problem inside the
**Pineapple Cabaret**, the hidden NPC/mob sanctuary the party spends the back half of the book
trying to access. Between the two locations, Shadow Mimics touch nearly every major thread of the
book's second half.

## What They Are, Per the System AI

The AI delivers its lore dump on Shadow Mimics mid-ambush, treating the reveal as a scheduled
checkpoint in Carl's education about "the true bad guys of the dungeon":

!!! quote "Book 8, Chapter 86"
    "There are a few dozen types of mimics in the dungeon, from your basic treasure-chest-impersonating
    mimic to the prostitute-imitating, vagina-as-mouth demon mimics, to the behemoth-sized mimic rex.
    Shadow Mimics are a whole other Popsicle stand. They are the counter to their archenemies, the
    changeling principals. They are one of the few entities in the dungeon who grow significantly
    in intelligence and strength and power as they age with no limit."

Three features distinguish them from every other mimic variety on the ladder — including the
treasure-chest mimics, the "vagina-as-mouth demon mimics," and the behemoth **[Mimic
Rex](../bosses/mimic-rex.md)** — none of which share these traits:

- **No growth ceiling.** Unlike a normal boss or mob, which levels within a fixed band, Shadow
  Mimics simply keep growing "in intelligence and strength and power" for as long as they survive.
  The text never specifies the underlying mechanism (feeding, aging, or something else) beyond "as
  they age" — this is stated as fact, not explained further.
- **Prone to "ideation breaking."** The AI's own gloss: "Because of the nature of how they're
  designed, they're especially prone to ideation breaking. That's a technical term some of you
  crawlers call 'becoming self-aware.'" A floor designer who understands what a Shadow Mimic is
  "knows not to mess with them" — implying their self-awareness is an accepted occupational hazard
  of the species, not a rare glitch.
- **Near-total stealth.** "What's even more insidious is that they're almost impossible to detect."
  Mordecai independently confirms this after Mitch's death: "They have a special ability to avoid
  most detection spells. They're like one of Carl's level 15 traps. All but invisible... They can
  mimic crawlers, and the system will label them as such until you discover them."

Mitch himself is offered as a data point on the growth curve, not the ceiling: "Mitch here, the
strongest of the mimics *in this room right now*—hint, hint—is still a baby. He's level 120, and
he's pretty damn strong." The implication, left unstated but pointed, is that older, stronger
Shadow Mimics exist elsewhere in the dungeon.

### Counter to the Changeling Principals

The AI names Shadow Mimics as the direct opposite number to the [Changeling
Principals](../dungeon-factions/the-changeling-principals.md) — the Floor 5 cult of disguised
[Changelings](changeling.md) — without elaborating on what "counter" means mechanically or
historically. No further explanation is given in Book 8; the parallel (both are near-undetectable
species built around impersonation, at opposite philosophical poles) is left for the reader to draw
and for a future book to potentially confirm.

## Confirmed Sightings

### Mitch and the Desperado Club Infestation

The species' first on-page confirmed member is **[Mitch](../bosses/mitch.md)**, who spends most of
Floor 10 disguised as the Desperado Club casino's new Wheel of Fortune croupier before revealing
himself — along with the entire casino floor's furniture — as a coordinated ambush. See Mitch's own
page for the full fight, his death by the **[Eye of the Bedlam Bride](../items/the-eye-of-the-bedlam-bride.md)**
and a point-blank *Magic Missile*, and what became of the real Mitch (a "Wall Rat" — an NPC who
figured out how to escape the standard dungeon-wipe-and-rewrite cycle — eaten by his impersonator).

Mitch, while dying, claims the Shadow Mimics "needed" crawlers "for the Cabaret" — that the war
mages, "the ogres," and a third faction he calls **"Cock-A-Doodle-Do"** are locked in a three-way
struggle to control "the dungeon back door," and that possessing captured crawlers would have given
the mimics leverage in that fight. He also states he was "sent up here by \<redacted\> to fuck
Hamed's shit up," implying the Desperado Club infestation was a deliberate operation against
[Hamed](../characters/supporting-cast.md), the club's current owner, rather than incidental
squatting — though the employer behind the redaction is never named.

The aftermath is a permanent status change for the club itself:

!!! quote "Mordecai, Book 8"
    "If the Desperado Club is infested, you guys can't go back. Those things are almost impossible
    to root out. The whole place will need to be fumigated. ... You should all devise safe words and
    quiz each other every day from now on."

See [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md#book-8-shadow-mimic-infestation) for the
location's own updated status.

### The Pineapple Cabaret Infestation

Independently of Mitch, Akuma reveals that the **[Pineapple Cabaret](../dungeon-factions/pineapple-cabaret.md)**
— the hidden pocket-dimension sanctuary built by the former crawlers Herot and Menerva — has also
been overrun:

!!! quote "Book 8"
    "That Cabaret place was currently overrun. Akuma had been a little... fuzzy... on who the bad
    guys were. He mentioned something called the Ogre Imperium, which was a long-forgotten plotline
    that had gotten binned because the ogres were made too intelligent and they had immediately
    figured out that they were trapped in a dungeon. And there was a shadow mimic infestation, which
    was just as bad."

This makes the Cabaret a second, separate Shadow Mimic presence from the Desperado Club one — the
text never confirms whether the two infestations are coordinated, though Mitch's dying claim about
crawlers being wanted "for the Cabaret" suggests at least some overlap in planning. See [The
Pineapple Cabaret](../dungeon-factions/pineapple-cabaret.md) for the full three-way contest over the
space.

## As One of the "Big Six"

The system AI explicitly places Shadow Mimics inside **Group Four: the sapient mobs** of its own
"Big Six" taxonomy of dungeon power factions, delivered in the same breath as the Mitch reveal —
alongside the **Ogre Imperium**, non-deity boss monsters like **[Krakaren
Prime](../bosses/krakaren-prime.md)**, and the **[War Mage Rebellion](../factions/war-mage-rebellion.md)**:

!!! quote "Book 8"
    "The sapient mobs. This is the group our friends the Shadow Mimics are a part of. But we also
    have the Ogre Imperium and a few other non-deity boss monsters in here, like Krakaren Prime.
    The War Mages, of course, are also part of this group... within this group, the Shadow Mimics
    are one of the strongest."

See [Factions & Organizations: The Big Six](../factions/index.md#the-big-six) for the taxonomy in
full — treated there as a stub pointer to a future Mechanics-pass page rather than duplicated here.

## Open Threads

- **Who sent Mitch.** The system's own examine text calls Mitch "an Enforcer for the \<Redacted\>,"
  and the AI itself declines to name the employer beyond confirming they're "still in their early
  stages of planning." Whether this is the same faction contesting the Pineapple Cabaret, a
  different Shadow Mimic leadership structure entirely, or something else is not resolved in Book 8.
- **"Cock-A-Doodle-Do."** Mitch's name for the third faction fighting the war mages and the Ogre
  Imperium over "the dungeon back door" is never explained or repeated elsewhere in the book. Treated
  here as an unresolved, possibly jokey, dying declaration rather than a confirmed faction identity.
- **The growth mechanic's actual trigger.** "As they age" is the only stated condition for a Shadow
  Mimic's growth; whether feeding, successful impersonation, or something else accelerates it is
  never addressed on-page.
