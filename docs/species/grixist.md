# Grixist

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Hairy, six-eyed alien species; their polity is called the **Grixist Swarm**
    - **Book 6 individual:** **Huanxin Jinx**, heiress, CEO of **Icon Industries**, and a
      long-running Celestial Ascendency player — see
      [Supporting Cast](../characters/supporting-cast.md)
    - **Legal footprint:** *Grixist Swarm v. Syndicate Council* is settled caselaw on sponsor
      boxes and looted rewards, cited by Quasar to keep Carl's Residual loot

## Overview

The Grixist are established almost entirely through one extremely wealthy individual. Odette's
Floor 11 prologue supplies the physical description, in the form of a fantasy about strangling
"the thin-necked, hairy bitch until all six eyes popped out of her head." Donut later shortens it
to "this six-eyed Huanxin lady."

Beyond six eyes, hair, and a thin neck, Book 6 gives no further physiology.

!!! warning "Unresolved"
    Book 6 never describes the Grixist limb count, homeworld, or the nature of the "Grixist Swarm"
    as a political entity — the name appears only in Huanxin's sponsor tag and in the case citation
    *Grixist Swarm v. Syndicate Council*. Mordecai's briefing is entirely about Huanxin personally,
    not her species.

## Huanxin Jinx

Mordecai's summary of her is the closest the book comes to a Grixist profile:

> "She's rich, obviously. The type of rich where governments come to *her* when they need a loan.
> She is very, very intelligent. Was known as a brat when she was younger, but she's grown into her
> own. She's the CEO of a company called Icon Industries. Something to do with habitat structures.
> She's a type of alien called a Grixist. She's been playing the Ascendency game for a very long
> time. Ever since she was a kid, actually, but she's never won. She got a multi-season suspension
> and banned from using her old goddess a long time ago because of illegal bribery."

Her Floor 8 role is played through sponsored deities — she drives the storm goddess **Dodola** in
the Floor 11 flashback and sponsors **Eileithyia** on Carl's floor, using the goddess as a private
message channel to Katia. Odette, who loathes her, warns Carl that Huanxin is "out for revenge.
That's it. Nothing more... She's the most evil person I know, and that's saying a lot. She's also
smart, so we must be... delicate in how we deal with her."
