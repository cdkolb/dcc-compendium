# Changeling

A shapeshifting species stranded on Floor 5's bubble world generations ago, living in secret
among the [Dromedarians](index.md#other-book-4-species) of Hump Town after their own settlements
were destroyed. Changelings can take almost any humanoid or animal form at will, but are
contractually forbidden from imitating the dromedarians who took them in except for paid
entertainment purposes at bars — a rule most of them quietly break anyway. [Juice
Box](../characters/juice-box.md), the Hump Town sex worker who befriends Carl's party, and
**Henrik**, the changeling secretly posing as the town's elder statesman, are both members of the
species.

## Taxonomy, per Mordecai

Mordecai delivers an extended lecture distinguishing Changelings from the dungeon's other
body-snatching creature types, all frequently confused for one another:

- **Doppelgangers** (like [Katia](../characters/katia.md)) must consciously "sculpt" a new
  appearance rather than transforming instantly.
- **Mimics** and **Illusionists** are unrelated creature/class types that merely resemble other
  things.
- **The Gondii/Valtay** and **Intellect Hunters** are brain-parasite lineages that take over
  bodies (living or dead) from the inside — see [Species: Intellect
  Hunters](index.md#other-book-2-species).
- **Infiltrators (Scree)** are a related-but-distinct parasite race that specifically hijacks
  *living* bodies over a period of days, locking the original mind out entirely rather than
  merging with it — see [Species: Infiltrator](index.md#other-book-4-species) below.

Changelings, by contrast, are a true species in their own right — no parasite, no host, just
innate shapeshifting.

## Compression Sickness and the Changeling Principals

Because Changeling bodies aren't fully adapted to this world, birth defects are increasing with
each generation — roughly one in four births now results in **Compression Sickness**, a condition
causing missing limbs and a sunken skull (seen firsthand in the child Ruby); affected individuals
are sterile. A cultish sect within the species, the
**[Changeling Principals](../dungeon-factions/the-changeling-principals.md)**, believes the cure
lies in a plant-based spell known only to the ghost Quetzalcoatlus, and has spent years
infiltrating Hump Town's guard ranks in pursuit of it.

## The Hunting Grounds

Changelings consider Floor 6 — "the Hunting Grounds" — their ancestral homeland, and believe there
was once a way back that was lost along with their original town hall. Per Juice Box, this
awareness of being trapped somewhere they don't belong marks them as a "situationally aware" NPC
type — the same category Mordecai's cookbook warns is far more psychologically volatile than
fully-immersed NPCs once the truth surfaces.

## Book 8: A Declared Archenemy

Book 8 gives the Changeling Principals cult a counterpart nobody saw coming. Introducing the newly
revealed **[Shadow Mimics](shadow-mimics.md)** species, the system AI names the Principals directly
as their opposite number: "They are the counter to their archenemies, the changeling principals."
No further mechanical or historical explanation is given — the wiki records the claim as stated,
without speculating on what "counter" means beyond the parallel that both are shapeshifting,
near-undetectable groups built around impersonation.
