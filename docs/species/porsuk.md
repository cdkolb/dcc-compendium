# Porsuk

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Badger-headed humanoid race
    - **Standing role:** The bartenders throughout the [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md)
      are Porsuk — the club's badger bar staff on every level
    - **Book 6 crawlers:** **Burcu** (level 59 Swashbuckler, #10 on the leaderboard) and
      **Charles Ellis** (level 25, a Desperado Club bartender) — see
      [Supporting Cast](../characters/supporting-cast.md)

## Overview

The Porsuk are one of those races Carl has been looking at for four books without ever learning
the name. When he inspects a Desperado Club bartender on Floor 8, the label finally resolves:

> "I gave the badger man a cursory inspection. He was a level-25 **Porsuk**, which was the name of
> the badger race in this game. The man's name was Charles."

The name matters because of who else turns out to be one. When Carl reviews the top-ten
leaderboard, the newcomer at number ten is:

> "a level-59, badger-headed **Porsuk**, which was the same race as all the bartenders at the
> Desperado Club. Her class was a **Swashbuckler**, the same as Tran and Bautista."

So the race sits on both sides of the glass at once: as the anonymous service staff of the
dungeon's biggest club, and as one of Earth's highest-ranked crawlers.

## Burcu

Burcu is a Turkish crawler Carl met only briefly during the Butcher's Masquerade on Floor 6 — she
was one of the three who fought over the memorial crystal at the end of that boss fight. He has her
in his chat but has never really spoken to her. His read: "very serious, reminding me of Hekla."
Carl suspects she and the other Turkish crawlers around her are all from the same family.

## Charles Ellis

Charles Ellis is a Desperado Club bartender who has the extraordinary misfortune of being briefly
courted by Samantha. He is torn in half during the club's destruction, "spraying the kitchen with
blood" — "All that was left hanging was the shoulder, neck, and head of the very-dead, very unlucky
bartender."
