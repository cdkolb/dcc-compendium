# Sturnus

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **First appearance:** Book 7, *This Inevitable Ruin*
    - **Type:** Small songbird people — **Volteeg's birth race**
    - **Status:** Named exactly once in the series, on Volteeg's character sheet

## What the text actually says

"Sturnus" appears in Book 7 in a single place — the closing sheet of Volteeg's POV chapter:

> **Class:** Tank Buttress — **Race:** Gargoyle — **Birth Race:** Sturnus — **Top Level:** 71

Everything else about the race is inferred from Volteeg's own history, told in the same chapter
before he became a [Gargoyle](gargoyle.md).

## The bird Volteeg was

Volteeg entered the dungeon as a **pet**, belonging to Mistress Henspar. His pre-transformation life
is described in small-bird terms throughout: he perches on her shoulder at the market, she
"scritched under his wing," he bites a shopkeeper's finger over a berry, and he dreams afterward
"only of berries and fire and the warmth of the hearth."

Above all, he could sing:

> "When he felt that love, he would sing. He knew not to sing in the market, because that was a
> secret, and he was protecting her just as much as she was protecting him. But later, when they
> were back outside and his belly was full, he would sing for Mistress Henspar. He would sit on her
> shoulder and sing, and she would have salty-tasting tears spring down her cheeks."

A **pet biscuit** gave him intelligence and speech. On the third floor he chose to become a
gargoyle, which gave him a working mind — and cost him his voice:

> "He'd chosen a race that could not sing. Not like before, and that was such a terrible, terrible
> mistake."

!!! warning "'Songbird' is an inference"
    The word *songbird* never appears in the text. The identification rests on the real-world genus
    *Sturnus* (starlings), on Volteeg's perching-and-singing pet life, and on his later inability to
    whistle — "his voice was like rocks being dragged against one another." The wiki treats it as a
    reasonable reading, not a stated fact.
