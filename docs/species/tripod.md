# Tripod

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **First appearance:** Book 7, *This Inevitable Ruin*
    - **Type:** Round, basketball-bodied aliens balanced on three long jointed legs, covered in eyes
    - **Reputation:** Famously, completely emotionless
    - **Known individual:** **Fopsy** — adjutant for **the Dream**, and a *former Syndicate Prime
      Minister*

## Appearance

Carl meets his first one when a second adjutant teleports into an adjudication:

> "The thing was a round, basketball-like creature standing upon three, long, multi-jointed legs.
> The round ball was covered in black dots that might be eyeballs. I was pretty sure this was a type
> of alien called a 'tripod.' I'd heard of them, but I'd never seen one before. They were supposedly
> completely without emotion, **like extra asshole versions of Spock from Star Trek**."

The eyes are functional and expressive in their own way: "All the eyes on the adjutant's round body
twinkled as he moved through his menus, glittering like a disco ball." Donut's field identification
is blunter: "IT'S THAT WEIRD ALIEN WHO LOOKS LIKE A BASKETBALL ON STILTS. FOPSY."

Prepotente supplies the only other data point in the book, complaining about a harem: "One of the
harem girls was one of those tripods, like the adjutant for the Dream. It would be like having
sexual congress with the Sputnik satellite!"

## Fopsy

Fopsy is the only named tripod in the series so far, and he is considerably more than a bit part.
He is the Dream's Faction Wars adjutant **and** a former Prime Minister of the Syndicate — a
combination that becomes politically explosive when Prime Minister Glory falls. Victory, on the
no-confidence vote: "if the vote goes a certain way, this guy here might actually be the Prime
Minister again, which is doubly interesting because he's the Dream's adjutant. You'd think he'd know
if his client was a robot." Fopsy: "I had no idea. No inkling whatsoever, but it does make sense now
considering some clues."

He is ultimately passed over — "as people weren't certain of former Prime Minister Fopsy's
relationship to the Dream, he was passed over for the newly-vacated position" — and
[Baroness Victory](../characters/baroness-victory.md) becomes acting Prime Minister instead. See
[The Syndicate Council](../factions/syndicate-council.md).

Fopsy also supplies two of the book's key pieces of exposition: he traces the war mages' trade of
the Gate of the Feral Gods to the Midnight Market, and he theorises about Agatha's real motive for
seizing the [War Mage Rebellion](../factions/war-mage-rebellion.md).

!!! note "Emotionless, but not useless"
    The "completely without emotion" line is the *reputation* Carl has heard, and Fopsy's dialogue
    is consistent with it — his stock response to catastrophe is "Interesting." The book never tests
    the stereotype directly.
