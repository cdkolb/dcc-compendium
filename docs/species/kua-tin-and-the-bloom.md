# The Kua-Tin & The Bloom

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Species:** Kua-tin — aquatic, roughly 2 feet tall, wear water-suits or rebreathers on land
    - **Political party:** The Bloom — ultra-nationalist party currently controlling the kua-tin government
    - **Role:** Own the Borant Corporation; the in-universe "real enemy" per Carl
    - **Notable individual:** Zev, Carl and Donut's assigned PR/Outreach Admin

## The Species

Kua-tin are the dominant species of the Borant System and principal owners of the Borant
Corporation. Carved reliefs of them (large, fish-demon-like) guard the entrance to Floor 1, but
the real kua-tin Carl eventually meets — Zev — stands only about two feet tall, "barely tall
enough to reach my knee," and wears an old-fashioned water-suit rather than the more common
rebreather. Calling a kua-tin "mudskipper" is treated as a serious slur — the term was nearly
classified as hate speech by the Syndicate.

Cameras are said to always turn off when kua-tin are physically present in the dungeon —
suggesting even Borant doesn't want its owners' direct involvement caught on-air.

## The Bloom

The Bloom is described by Mordecai as the kua-tin's current ruling political party: "ultra-
nationalism... the closest thing you have in your history is maybe Axis Japan with a good splash
of Nazi Germany thrown in, but even that's not quite right." When Mordecai signed his own
indentureship contract, the Bloom held under 15% of the vote; they now control the entire kua-tin
system government and have run it into bankruptcy.

Per Mordecai, the Bloom's financial desperation is what forced this season's early start — they
need cash from the show's success as fast as possible, even though Borant itself doesn't see
patron or advertising revenue until the season ends. Party membership is now mandatory to
indicate via badge.

## "The Real Enemy"

Carl repeatedly frames the kua-tin/Borant/Syndicate axis as the true antagonist of the story, as
distinct from the individual dungeon monsters (many of which are transformed, sympathetic
humans or "real" alien species just doing their jobs): "The Syndicate. Borant. The kua-tin... the
real enemy." Mordecai warns Carl to control his temper around kua-tin regardless of rank — "She's
about as low-level as you can get and still be an Admin, but... All kua-tin are dangerous, party
members or not."
