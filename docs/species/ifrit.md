# Ifrit

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Demon race, drawn up from **Sheol** on the 15th floor
    - **Appearance:** Humanoid, blurry at the edges, skin and hair visibly smoking; completely
      white eyes with no pupils, smoke rising from the sockets; tiny stub horns like a baby goat's
    - **Attuned to:** The *aura* of lust, sin, envy and greed leaking out of the underworld —
      as distinct from succubi/incubi, who are fixated on sex itself
    - **Book 6 individual:** **[Damascus Steel](../characters/damascus-steel.md)**, level 75, assistant lead dancer at the Penis
      Parade and a member of the [Guild of Suffering](../dungeon-factions/guild-of-suffering.md) —
      see [Characters](../characters/index.md)

## Overview

Ifrit are introduced when Carl finally goes to meet Damascus Steel at the
[Penis Parade](../dungeon-factions/penis-parade-crew.md). The AI's description sets out both the
cosmology and the attitude:

> "The closer one gets to the world of Sheol on the 15th floor, the more demons one might find
> leaking out into the regular world, especially in places that haven't been cleansed in a while.
> And by 'cleansed,' I mean in a biblical sense."

> "An ifrit is like a cockroach or a rat, though usually a lot sexier and more entertaining than
> either of those. And smart. And cunning. And they usually smell good, too, which is weird
> considering how they look. Where there is debauchery, there are usually demons. Where there is
> sin, there are Ifrit. Unlike a succubus or incubus, who are inordinately obsessed with sex, an
> Ifrit is more attuned to the *aura* of lust and sin and envy and greed and all those other nasty
> iniquities that spill over the world. ... Try not to get into a fight with this guy because he'll
> fuck you up."

Ifrit are a **demon class**, which matters mechanically as well as thematically: during the
Desperado Club disaster, Carl notes that Damascus's mere presence is dangerous while vorpals are
loose in the building, because vorpals are drawn to demons.

## Physical description

Damascus reads as "mostly humanoid with Arabic features, but his body seemed a little... fuzzy
around the edges, like he was constantly going in and out of focus." His skin feels like solid
metal to the touch while looking like ordinary skin. He steams constantly, weeps sizzling tears
that pit the floor where they land, and has pointed ears despite being much too large to be an
elf. The stub horns are "tiny, little stubs, like something one would see on a baby goat" — not
the sawn-off trunks of larger horns.
