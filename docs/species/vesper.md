# Vesper

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **First appearance:** Book 7, *This Inevitable Ruin*
    - **Type:** **Milk's birth race** — bat-like, winged
    - **Status:** Named three times in the whole series, always in contrast to
      [Xenopus](xenopus.md), the race Milk chose as a crawler

## What the text actually says

Vesper exists in the books entirely as the "before" half of Milk's race change. Every mention:

> "Yes, I am a xenopus, but before I came into the dungeon, I was a **vesper**."

> **Race:** Xenopus — **Birth Race:** Vesper

> "...she'd written extensively about the differences between the wings of her chosen race, the
> Xenopus, and those of her birth race, the Vesper."

Carl describes Milk as a "**half frog, half bat** creature," and the bat half is the Vesper
inheritance — but the book never independently describes what an unchanged Vesper looks like, where
they come from, or anything else about them.

!!! warning "Deliberately thin"
    There is no standalone description of the Vesper as a people anywhere in Books 1–7. The bat-like
    reading is an inference from Carl's "half frog, half bat" phrasing plus the wing comparison; the
    wiki does not extend it further.

## Why the race change matters

Milk's birth race / chosen race split is one of a matched pair in Book 7. The other is
[Volteeg](gargoyle.md), whose birth race was [Sturnus](sturnus.md) and who chose **Gargoyle** — and
whose entire tragedy is that gargoyles cannot sing. Both characters are cookbook authors, both chose
a race for utility, and both carry the consequence permanently: "That was something they didn't tell
you when you changed your body. They changed you, and they couldn't change you back."
