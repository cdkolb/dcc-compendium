# Xenopus

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **First appearance:** Book 7, *This Inevitable Ruin*
    - **Type:** Winged, clawed, frog-like people
    - **Known individual:** **[Milk](../characters/milk.md)**, guildmaster of the Cartography and Calligrapher's guilds and
      author of the sixth edition of the *Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook* — a **chosen crawler race**,
      not her birth race (see [Vesper](vesper.md))

## Overview

Xenopus is a crawler race, and the book has fun with the fact that its name is borrowed straight
from Earth taxonomy. When the vampire crawler Miriam asks Milk what she is —

> "A xenopus is the genus name of a type of clawed frog," the caprid said as he handed the woman a
> magical salve. "Common throughout the world. Disgusting creatures. **The earth versions do not
> have wings.**"

— the correction is Prepotente's, and it's the only physiological detail the text gives outright:
the dungeon version *does* have wings.

Milk answers for herself:

> "Yes, I am a xenopus, but before I came into the dungeon, I was a vesper."

## Appearance

Carl's first sight of her, when he discovers she is his sister:

> "But I barely registered this as I stared at the **half frog, half bat** creature. The guildmaster
> met my gaze... My eyes moved to the wings of the creature. She'd written extensively about the
> differences between the wings of her chosen race, the Xenopus, and those of her birth race, the
> Vesper."

The "half bat" half of that composite is Milk's Vesper inheritance showing through; the wings of the
two races are different enough that she wrote about the contrast at length in the cookbook.
