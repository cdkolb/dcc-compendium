# Guild of Suffering

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** In-dungeon cult, not a real guild or church — and, as of Book 6, a working
      contract-assassination outfit
    - **Patron deity:** The Night Wyrm, a demigod outside the standard pantheon (comparable to
      Samantha/Psamathe). **Book 6:** his real name is **Hamed**
    - **Access granted by:** using a [Ring of Divine Suffering](../items/enchanted-night-wyrms-ring-of-divine-suffering.md)
      for the first time, which brands the user with **The Night Wyrm's Nasty Little Web of Suffering** tattoo
    - **Known entrance (Book 6):** Behind the last cubicle in the **Penis Parade**, the top-floor
      male strip club of the [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md) — the whole club is a
      front
    - **Members address each other as:** "spider," and collectively as "the family"
    - **Interface addition:** A **Guild Jobs** sub-tab under quests

## Overview

The Guild of Suffering maintains outreach centers scattered across dungeon floors, similar in
structure to the [City Elves'](../species/city-elves.md) fringe religious cells encountered on
Floor 3. Anyone who uses an artifact of the Night Wyrm's design — chiefly a
[Ring of Divine Suffering](../items/enchanted-night-wyrms-ring-of-divine-suffering.md) — is
automatically marked with a permanent tattoo granting access to the cult, whether they intend to
join it or not. Both Carl and [Prepotente](../characters/prepotente.md) are marked this way after
using their own rings on Floor 6.

## The Night Wyrm

Per Mordecai, the cult's patron is "not a regular member of the pantheon," comparable in that
sense to [Samantha/Psamathe](../characters/samantha.md). The cult maintains its own version of a
**memorial crystal** — a distinct, related item type from the soul crystals that power elven
magic; see [Soul Crystals & Memorial Crystals](../mechanics/index.md#soul-crystals-memorial-crystals-book-5)
for how the two differ — though its exact function is never demonstrated on-page. A few seasons
before Book 5, an entire seventh-floor storyline was reportedly centered on the cult attempting to
"raise their god," per Mordecai's secondhand account; Book 5 never revisits or resolves that
thread directly.

## Book 6: the hidden entrance

The Guild finally gets shown from the inside on Floor 8, and the location is exactly as absurd as
everything else about the Desperado Club. Katia, who needs an assassination quest to break the
final seal on her Lock Box of the Night Wyrm, finds it first:

> "The entrance is at the Penis Parade on the top floor of the Desperado Club. The whole strip club
> is a front for the Guild of Suffering."

Donut is outraged for her own reasons: "He never told me that! This is an outrage! Why would a
stripper lie to me?"

The route in runs through the club's row of private-dance cubicles. [Damascus Steel](../characters/damascus-steel.md) — an [ifrit](../species/ifrit.md) dancer with the same spiderweb elbow tattoo as Carl — walks Carl to
the last cubicle in the corner, a purple shag-carpeted closet with a buckled swing hanging from
the ceiling, locks the door behind them, and opens the hidden portal in the far wall. Beyond it is
a brick corridor with torches and a single wooden door carved with a spiderweb.

> **Entering the Guild of Suffering.**
> **Welcome, spider. Welcome home.**

The door earns Carl the **"The Molly Maguires"** achievement, and with it one of the strangest
lectures the system AI has ever delivered — a genuine history of the Avondale mine disaster, the
Workingmen's Benevolent Association, and the Molly Maguires, ending in John Siney's speech to the
crowd outside the mine:

> *Men, if you must die with your boots on, die for your families, your homes, your country, but do
> not longer consent to die, like rats in a trap, for those who have no more interest in you than
> in the pick you dig with.*

The AI's point — "How can you win a deliberately unfair game when the rules are written by your
opponent?" — is about itself, not the Guild, and it says so: "This is a new lesson to me. She's
been teaching me so many things, about who I am." It then tells Carl to ignore everything it just
said, because the Guild is "just some demi-god trying to scrabble his way to the top."

The interior itself is a dark room with a disembodied hissing voice. Carl never sees the Night
Wyrm. He declines a job, confirms he isn't planning to kill Katia for her prize, and asks for one
thing instead: a map of the Desperado Club's secret passageways. He gets it — spiders swarm up his
arm, and his map fills in with hidden tunnels, monster-occupied passages, a secret potion market on
the middle floor, and a scattering of "drug dens" behind wall panels.

## Guild Jobs

Accepting guild work opens a new **Guild Jobs** sub-tab under quests. Contracts are delivered not
on paper but on a small, dried slip of skin covered in desiccated veins, about the size of a
cigarette pack. Non-members who touch it get stung; only members can read it, and the words only
form after the reader's own blood is drawn into the membrane.

The operating rules, from the contract Katia draws:

- Only guild members can read or assist with a job.
- **Speaking the target's name aloud immediately cancels the contract and revokes guild access.**
- Proof of death is required — the body must be physically delivered.
- **"She who brings the body is she who gets paid."** The contract is owned by the member who took
  it, but the payment follows whoever delivers the corpse, which is explicitly an invitation to
  betray your partner.

The Night Wyrm's voice frames all of this in family terms: *"The family with many eyes works as a
team, yes. We sacrifice the weak to support the many, yes. We rise from the shadows. Our blood
flows together."*

## The Night Wyrm — Hamed

The demigod turns out to be a man with a domestic life, which is much worse than a demigod.

[Astrid](../characters/astrid.md), the Desperado Club's assistant manager, tells Carl in her last moments: "My husband is
named Hamed, but he's known as the Night Wyrm. Don't tell him I told you his real name. His lair is
hidden up there on the top floor."

The contract Carl and Katia had just fulfilled was a contract on Astrid. Her husband took it out.

The family, as Carl reconstructs it afterward, is four people: the Night Wyrm/Hamed; **Astrid**, a
Bloodlust Sprite and a former crawler; and their two sons, **[Damascus Steel](../characters/damascus-steel.md)** (ifrit) and
**Anaconda** (quarter naga), neither of whom was ever a crawler. Carl's read: "I don't think
they're generated NPCs, but more like the bopcas. Like maybe they were indentured servants who
somehow signed up for, or were forced, into the dungeon. I don't know what race they were born, but
they're all different ones in the game."

Damascus's own account of the motive is money: "We were promised freedom... They're still married.
My mother has—had—so much, and the guild, it's rarely found by crawlers. He gets it all. He'll have
it all when we get out." Astrid's last words — *"I was almost done"* — say the rest.

See [Characters](../characters/index.md) for Anaconda and Hamed individually, and [The Penis Parade Crew](penis-parade-crew.md) for what happens to the dancers
afterward.
