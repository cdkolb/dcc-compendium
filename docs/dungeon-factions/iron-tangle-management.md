# Iron Tangle Management

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** In-dungeon corporate transit authority — runs Floor 4's rail network as a company, not a monster faction
    - **Floor:** 4 (Book 3)
    - **Leadership:** "Rod" (CFO, never seen — possibly not real), Madison (HR Associate, Substation E), Doris (HR, Substation B)
    - **Enforcement:** The Kravyad (six-armed naga-like HR staff who hypnotize/detain workers), gnoll Transit Security, hobgoblin Interdiction Teams
    - **Downfall:** Self-destructs and blames "the Krakaren" once its ghoul-disposal pipeline overloads

## Overview

The Iron Tangle presents itself, in-universe, as a corporate employer — dwarves, grapples,
mantaurs, gnolls, and hobgoblins all "work" the rail network as conductors, porters, engineers,
and security, subject to mandatory overtime, productivity quotas, and human-resources doublespeak
straight out of a cubicle farm. It's Book 3's version of the recurring "in-dungeon business"
pattern also seen with [Rev-Up Moonshine](rev-up-moonshine.md), but darker: the workforce is made
of NPCs seeded with false memories of families, homes, and coworkers that were never real,
and the company's cost-cutting decisions are what's actively killing them and the crawlers alike.

## Leadership & Staff

- **Madison** — Human Resources Associate at Substation E; a Level 10 non-combatant NPC and a
  played-straight parody of corporate HR ("Do you know what overtime is? Mandatory."). Runs the
  **Kravyad program**: workers who ask for time off are given a gold armband, which secretly tells
  the substation's Kravyad they're safe to eat instead of teleport home.
- **The Kravyad** — six-armed, blue-skinned, naga-like beings who hypnotize dwarves and grapples
  getting off shift, holding them in a stupor for 8–12 hours before sending them back through a
  station-60 portal for their next run. Explicitly *not* boss monsters — "part of the human
  resources department." A Kravyad's portal is life-bound to it: kill one, and the only way
  through that station's employee portal closes with it. Bautista's group accidentally kills the
  Substation-435 Kravyad while trying to disable her, sealing off 150+ crawlers who'd sold their
  souvenir hats.
- **Doris** — Madison's opposite number at Substation B, in charge of "excess citizen disposal"
  (i.e. the ghoul-generator pipeline) and keeping the Krakaren "happy." Her decision to route
  ghoul disposal through an existing train line instead of a dedicated conveyor — to save money —
  is the root cause of the entire floor's ghoul-generator overload. Substation B falls to the
  ghouls before Substation E does.
- **Rod** — the unseen CFO Madison repeatedly insists will "come" and fix everything. Never
  appears; likely fictional, part of the same false-memory scaffolding as the rest of the NPC cast.
- **Growler Gary** — a Level-25 Shade Gnoll bartender at Station 75's "Downward Dog" tavern, and
  the only known Iron Tangle NPC who is genuinely indestructible: killed, he simply resurrects a
  few minutes later, sober and unaware he died. Per the cookbook, an indestructible NPC always
  means they hold something plot-critical — in his case, his own left hand, the only key that can
  start the yard's rapid-response interdiction carts. Carl's party has to kill and re-harvest his
  hand fourteen times to get enough carts running; Gary, once he understands why, stops resisting
  and asks only for alcohol afterward.

## The Ghoul-Disposal Pipeline

Every non-undead mob that dies on Floor 4 spawns a single ghoul from the nearest of several
**Ghoul Generators**, soul-crystal-powered machines stationed at specific stops (12, 24, 36, 48,
72, and their many parallel instances). Robots collect ghouls into rolling cages and send them
down a hidden conveyor ("roller coaster") back toward the trainyards, where they're meant to be
shipped to [the Abyss](../mechanics/the-iron-tangle-railway.md#portals-trainyards-and-the-abyss)
for disposal. The system
is deliberately cheap and fragile — designed, per the gremlin mechanic Widget, to break down
rather than work reliably — and once ghoul volume exceeds capacity, generators back up, cages
spill, and ghouls flood back into the stations they were meant to be removed from.

## Downfall

With eleven hours left on the floor's timer, the company issues a system-wide announcement
blaming "the Krakaren" for betraying them, declares the transit system a lost cause, and
initiates a self-destruct sequence — opening escape tunnels floor-wide and flooding every
stairwell station with the accumulated Krakaren monster population before the charges detonate.
Whether the Krakaren "betrayal" is real or just corporate scapegoating for Doris's cost-cutting is
never confirmed. In the aftermath, word spreads among the surviving workforce that Madison's false
family and life were never real; the dwarves and gremlins she'd bullied for weeks build a stand
and a noose and hang her. Fire Brandy, the demon fire-stoker who'd finally realized her own
children and marriages weren't real either, chooses to ride the **Nightmare Express** into the
Abyss and detonate it rather than be reused this way again.
