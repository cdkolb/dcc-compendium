# Rev-Up Moonshine Company

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** In-dungeon pyramid-scheme moonshine business
    - **Floor:** 2
    - **Leadership:** Krakaren (see the [Krakaren Clone](../bosses/krakaren-clone.md) boss page) and her "downline consultants"
    - **Workforce:** Clurichauns (rank-and-file) and Laminak fairies (Elite Consultants)

## Overview

Rev-Up presents itself as a multi-level-marketing opportunity ("Rev-Up. Make Money. Be Your Own
Boss. Move to the next floor down") staffed almost entirely by exploited fae workers. A recovered
recruitment pamphlet is transparently a pyramid scheme — literally illustrated with a pyramid
captioned "It's not a pyramid!" — and advertises a discontinued product line, "Rev-Up Smoothies,"
crossed out and marked "Discontinued."

## Product & Distillery

The company's flagship product is **Rev-Up Toilet-Grade Moonshine**, 180-proof liquor distilled
using Brindle Grub slime trails and an undisclosed "proprietary source." It's sought after by
drinking establishments on Floors 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, and 18. Drinking a 1.5oz swig inflicts the
Shit-Faced debuff, has a 50% chance of 30-hour blindness, a 5% chance of instant death, and a 45%
chance of permanently raising a random stat by two points.

## Labor Structure

- **Clurichauns** — rank-and-file distillery workers
- **Laminak Rev-Up Consultant Elites** — the management tier; per their monster card, underlings
  who fall behind quota are punished by being forced to drink the product, with survivors
  "repurposed as workers for the filling room, or worse, as still engineers, working directly
  under Krakaren herself"

## Role in the Story

Carl improvises a "Rev-Up Immunity Smoothie" (moonshine blended with a dead laminak fairy) using
a recipe found in the distillery, reviving the discontinued smoothie line's namesake to cure/
prevent **the Taint**, a mucus-borne infection inflicted by the Krakaren Clone boss.
