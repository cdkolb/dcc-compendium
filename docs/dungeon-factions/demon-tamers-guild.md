# The Demon Tamer's Guild

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Defunct in-dungeon guild. Never shown operating; known only by what it left behind
    - **What it left behind:** A sealed **practice arena** behind the middle floor of the
      [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md)
    - **Contents:** Fifteen red-tagged mobs, later identified as **vorpals**
    - **Containment:** A hidden corridor, a handful of traps, sandbags and wall bracers. That's it
    - **Book 6 role:** The club's emergency weapon, and the instrument of its destruction

## Overview

The Demon Tamer's Guild exists in Book 6 as a locked door on a map. Carl finds it while working
through the secret-passage map he extracted from the Night Wyrm:

> "At the end of this corridor was another room with a closed door. Behind that door was a group of
> fifteen mobs. We didn't know what the red-tagged monsters were, but according to the map, the
> room was an abandoned practice arena of the now-defunct 'Demon Tamer's Guild.'"

Per the Night Wyrm, nobody knows what the monsters are — only that they are outrageously dangerous.
Every party that has ever tried to clear the room has died. Carl finds it absurd that the club
simply left them there behind a few traps, then reminds himself: "this wasn't real life, but game
setup."

## What's actually in there

The answer comes from [Astrid](../characters/astrid.md), in the middle of the disaster: "I had to unleash the vorpals."

**Vorpal is a condition, not a species** — Mordecai's full explanation of what that means, and why
they are so dangerous, belongs under [Game Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md). In brief: vorpals are
celestial foot soldiers deputized by the gods, of any race, who grow stronger and angrier the
closer they are to a demon, draw demon aggro, kill anyone near them, and detonate rather than lose
a fight.

The Demon Tamer's Guild connection makes retroactive sense of the arena: a guild that trained
people to handle demons would want captive demon-hunters on hand, and a guild that went out of
business would leave them sealed in place.

## How the club dies

Carl's plan against Astrid depends on the vorpals staying put. Samantha accidentally breaches "the
Nothing" in the casino and releases a minor feral demon, which forces Astrid's hand — she pulls the
trigger and unleashes the arena herself. Carl, meanwhile, has already sent a modified door-opening
automaton down the hidden hallway on a sixty-second delay to dismantle the wall bracers and do
exactly the same thing.

The vorpals tear through the lower levels of the Desperado Club, kill Astrid herself, and eventually
kill the demon Minge. Astrid's last instruction to Carl: "If they ask what happened to me, tell them
it was a vorpal."

They are supposed to dissipate and return to the celestial realm once they have bathed in demon
blood. Nobody in the book is confident about how long that takes, and Damascus Steel — an
[ifrit](../species/ifrit.md), and therefore a demon class — is a standing hazard to everyone around
him while they remain loose.

[Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md) for the aftermath.
