# The Stage Performance Guild

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Hidden, task-based guild — teaches performance/showmanship skills
    - **Location:** Behind a secret door inside **the Lollipop**, a karaoke bar in Floor 10's Over
      City hub (Book 8)
    - **Entrance requirement:** Complete one of a handful of secretly-marked karaoke songs from
      start to finish with the performance gauge ("the needle") staying in the green the entire time
    - **Guildmaster:** **Porky**, the left half of the split mantaur **[Corcunda](../characters/corcunda.md)**
    - **Discovered/entered by:** [Carl](../characters/carl.md) and [Princess
      Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md), singing a-ha's "Take On Me" mid-bar-brawl
    - **Structurally parallel to:** The Cartography and Calligraphy Guilds inside Club Vanquisher
      (Book 7), both secretly run by [Milk](../characters/milk.md)

## Overview

The Stage Performance Guild is Book 8's version of a pattern the wiki has already seen once: a
guild hidden in plain sight inside a members' club, gatekept by an obscure trigger nobody thinks to
try, and secretly staffed by an NPC whose real identity ties into the book's emotional core. Where
Book 7's Cartography and Calligraphy Guilds turned out to be run by Carl's own sister, Book 8's
Stage Performance Guild turns out to be run by **Porky** — the other half of **Corcunda**, the
half-mantaur reunion-and-tragedy storyline running in parallel across the same floor.

## The Entrance Exam

The trigger is a secret mark on select songs in the karaoke bar's binder-bound song list, explained
to Carl by the bartender **Tipid**:

!!! quote "Tipid, Book 8"
    "There's a secret mark on a handful of songs. If you pick the right song, it'll engage the
    entrance sequence for the guild... Only those who get the green needle the whole time will be
    able to enter. So if you want in, you gotta sing."

Carl and Donut's attempt happens under the worst possible conditions: mid-song, the bar's DJ is
stabbed by a drunk unicorn NPC named Dwight, and the karaoke performance becomes the backdrop to a
full brawl breaking out across the room — glass, fireballs, and a dinosaur (Mongo) all crossing the
stage while Carl, wearing a *Gelded Choirboy* scroll effect that spikes his voice an octave, sings
a-ha's "Take On Me" opposite Donut. Both performance gauges stay pinned in the green through the
entire song despite the chaos, and Carl's chimes with an extra flourish ("little flames coming out
of the top") that Donut's does not — a discrepancy she refuses to let go of.

!!! quote "Book 8"
    "Congratulations, Carl. You have been granted permanent access to the Stage Performance Guild!
    New Achievement! Sing for your supper!"

The reward text is characteristically snide about the whole endeavor ("The next thing you know,
you'll be earning a third of a cent per stream on Spotify..."), but the mechanical payoff is real:
discovering the guild this way permanently reveals other hidden, entrance-gated guilds on Carl's map
whenever he's near one. Donut, singing her own verses correctly, still FAILS the exam outright — an
outcome she immediately declares off-limits for future discussion ("We are never going to mention
this again... I do love that song, though").

## Porky, the Guildmaster

Behind the secret door is a full theater — "a whole damn theater with red velvet chairs that looked
as if it could fit five hundred people" — dusty and unused, with a single spotlight trained on the
guild's sole occupant: **Porky**, lying on the stage floor with a stuck zipper on his containment
suit.

Porky is the left half of **Corcunda**, split from his other half (**Corky**) years earlier by the
spell *Split Personality* and never reunited until this floor's central quest resolves it. He has
been running the guild alone and unnoticed for an unstated length of time, offering services and
skill-ups to anyone who manages to pass the entrance test:

!!! quote "Porky, Book 8"
    "Welcome to the stage performance guild. I can see you have some rudimentary levels in
    performance skills, but the fact you were able to gain entrance suggests you have a lot of raw
    talent. And for helping me today, I will throw a few free skill levels onto your Razzmatazz
    skill."

Porky is fluent in showmanship theory even in total isolation — his answer to Donut's questions about
"bringing the house down" ("Any performance is 10% talent, 90% presence... You want them to limp out
of the theater after you're done with them, gasping for breath as if you've just made love to their
very soul") reads as a man who has had nothing to do for a very long time but think about his craft.

Carl uses his discovery of Porky to drive the book's other major Floor 10 storyline: reuniting him
with Corky before the race that would otherwise kill his other half. See
[Corcunda](../characters/corcunda.md) for the reunion, Grull's smiting of the newly-whole Corcunda
for religious apostasy accrued during the years apart, and her and Dong Quixote's joint death —
covered there in full rather than duplicated on this page.

## Structurally Parallel to the Cartography and Calligraphy Guilds

The Stage Performance Guild is a direct structural echo of Book 7's **Cartography and Calligraphy
Guilds**, hidden inside Club Vanquisher and secretly run by the same guildmaster, **Milk** — Carl's
own biological sister. Both pairs of guilds share the same shape: an obscure, easy-to-miss entrance
condition, a near-total absence of patrons ("She never bothered to hide the papers in the back where
crawlers wouldn't see them... What was the point?"), and a lonely NPC guildmaster whose personal
story turns out to matter more than the guild's nominal function. Carl draws the comparison himself,
internally, while talking to Porky in his spartan backstage quarters: "I wondered how long Milk had
spent alone in that lonely guild. This was an NPC, but still..."

No dedicated page exists yet for the Cartography and Calligraphy Guilds; see [Dungeon Factions:
Other Book 7 dungeon groups](index.md#other-book-7-dungeon-groups) for their existing entry.

## Aftermath: An Open Question

Once reunited into a single **Corcunda**, Porky ceases to exist as a separate identity — and
Corcunda dies minutes later, smitten by Grull for religious apostasy accrued during the split. Book
8 never states what becomes of the Stage Performance Guild, its "Razzmatazz" skill line, or its
hidden-guild-detection reward after its sole guildmaster is gone. Whether the guild remains
accessible, closes, or transfers to new management is left unresolved.
