# The Princess Posse Army

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Book:** 7, *This Inevitable Ruin* — the Floor 9 Faction Wars army
    - **Co-warlords:** Carl and Princess Donut. **Adjutant:** [Baroness Victory](../characters/baroness-victory.md)
    - **Headquarters:** the **FUPA** — the *Fortress of Unyielding Power and Authority*
    - **Starting strength:** 58 soldiers. **Reinforcement:** just over **50,000** former crawlers
      shipped in aboard the *Homecoming Queen*
    - **Structure:** seven numbered battalions (101st–107th) plus Special Operations Forces (SOFs)
    - **Survivors at the end:** about **28,000** of the 50,000 — "They had survived the dungeon,
      left, and they came back to die"

## Origins

The army begins as a farce. Carl and Donut arrive on Floor 9 with the system warning them their
"army is at suboptimal force levels. Current army size: **58 soldiers**," and "You have no officers
in your army!" Officers can only be created by **physically touching** the recruit — a rule Donut
discovers immediately and complains about: "It says we have to physically touch someone to make
them an officer! That's not fair! I was going to make Katia a general!"

The force that actually fights the war arrives from outside: **Tipid** and **Rosetta** bring in just
over **50,000 former crawlers** recruited by Doctor Hu's call and carried down on the *Homecoming
Queen* — see [The Crawler Project](../factions/crawler-project.md) and
[The Open Intellect Pacifist Network](../factions/open-intellect-pacifist-network.md). Each of the
eight opposing sponsored teams simultaneously receives about 20,000 hired mercenaries.

## Ranks

Donut goes through the room touching everyone in turn to make officers. The hierarchy is built by
hand, mostly by **[Florin](../characters/florin.md)**:

| Rank | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Warlord** | Carl and Donut, co-warlords |
| **Adjutant** | Baroness Victory — a system role, not an army rank |
| **General** | Florin's own rank after he tinkers with the naming (it began as "War Leader III," then "Colonel," then simply "General"). Generals may recruit and grant any officer rank below their own, and may command large detached formations. Most of the core group hold this rank |
| **Colonel** | Battalion command (e.g. Colonel Boomer, Colonel Tipid). Carl gives Prepotente this rank on joining — "My first instinct was to be a dick and give Pony the rank of Legionnaire, but I ended up making him a Colonel" |
| **Sergeant First Class** | The Penis Parade strippers' rank; allows recruiting and commanding base troops |
| **Chief / Corporal** | Construction crews and crawler specialists get sergeant/chief so they can alter the camp; corporals lead individual teams |
| **Legionnaire** | The rank-and-file — notably the **sluggalos**, who are formally redesignated *Legionnaires* |

Florin initially named the sergeant tier "Adjutant" and had to change it, because it collided with
the existing Adjutant class. Officer slots are capped: "You only get a limited number of officers."

## The seven battalions

| Unit | Designation | Commander | Role and details |
|---|---|---|---|
| **101st** | Crocodiles | **[Florin](../characters/florin.md)** | The main maneuver force; holds the northern front against the Operatic and later the orc border. Leads the charge that stops an armoured column and captures two Weeper tanks |
| **102nd** | Bulwark | **Tran** | Defensive mages and fortification engineers, with a thin layer of hand-to-hand specialists. Builds the forward positions and trench lines; holds the southern naga border |
| **103rd** | Recon Legionnaires | **Bodi** | Scouts and recon. Later used for corpse-delivery and as the diversionary force against the Madness castle |
| **104th** | Naughty Little Piggies | **Tipid** | Base defence: scout towers, early-warning posts, and the castle perimeter |
| **105th** | Scream Warriors | **Jurgen** | Shock infantry. Split into two half-battalions trained as a **rapid-deploy** force — five mages chanting the same spell for thirty straight hours can mass-teleport an entire battalion, something Rosetta and Mordecai say has only happened a handful of times in Faction Wars history |
| **106th** | Bloody Leeches | **[Colonel Boomer](../characters/colonel-boomer.md)** | Warriors, trap mechanics and thieves; the reserve/counterattack battalion |
| **107th** | White Dragons (Urban Defense) | **Li Na** | Holds **Shanty Town**, the donut-shaped ring settlement on the lip of Larracos, and bankrupts the treasury building its defences |

Colonel **Boomer** is a grizzled elf survivor of one of the earliest seasons of Dungeon Crawler
World — "someone had taken the world's fattest bulldog, pickled it for about 5,000 years, gave him
elf ears, and then shoved a cigar in his mouth." He is put in charge of the 106th by Tipid, and
"the soldiers all loved him."

## Special Operations Forces (SOFs)

| Squad | Role |
|---|---|
| **The Frothing Tunnel Moles** | Sappers and tunnellers. Their gnome-sized members go down holes cut at regular intervals into the trench walls; one wears "a massive, poorly-drawn mole tattoo on his chest" reading *The Frothing Tunnel Moles*. Justice Light later directs volunteer sappers to "go find Colonel Tipid and tell him you're trained sappers, and maybe you can join up with the Frothing Tunnel Moles" |
| **The Beefy Boys** | Base-defence mages whose sole job is keeping every wall buff and base shield topped off. Named as a joke by their leader **Lonnie** after someone told him to "beef up the wall defenses." They are half-dead when the Madness raid the FUPA, and Lonnie is mind-controlled into switching off the interior protection stacks before he is killed |
| **The Tenner Wraiths** | A squad within the 103rd Recon Legionnaires, named on the elves' neck tattoos |

## Patches, tattoos and mottos

The army develops its own iconography almost overnight.

**The 103rd Recon Legionnaires' neck tattoo:** "a large, flaming skull that was some sort of
human/cat hybrid thing that looked like it was straight from a 1980's skateboard deck. Surrounding
the skull in large letters in Syndicate Standard read the words **'Princess Posse or Death.'**" The
battalion name is added at the bottom, and below that the squad name, *The Tenner Wraiths*. Griffin
volunteers the other side of his neck for a second one if he's transferred, and the front for a
third.

**The 105th Scream Warriors' logo:** "a drawing of a screaming girl who looked suspiciously like
the mascot for the Wendy's restaurant chain wearing a Viking helmet with only one horn." Their
merchandise also carries "a crudely drawn, bloody child berserker character holding an axe... a
little girl that had to be eight years old," and **the unit's motto**:

> **"We bleed when the job's done."**

**The 104th Naughty Little Piggies** fly a battalion flag; Dong Quixote carries one on the tip of a
war lance when reassigned to them.

!!! note "'Fuck the cheese sticks' is army slang, not a unit motto"
    **"Cheese stick"** is what the whole Princess Posse army calls enemy soldiers. Carl: "I had no
    idea where the term 'Cheese Stick' had originated, but it had spread through the army like
    wildfire. It's what everyone called the enemy soldiers. I'd seen a few **'Fuck the Cheese
    Sticks'** tattoos already." It is shouted as a battle cry going over the top ("'Fuck the cheese
    sticks' someone else yelled as they, too, propelled themselves onto No Man's Land"), used
    casually by Boomer and Florin in dispatches, and is the subject of a running argument with
    Samantha, who insists it should be "cheese dicks." The only quoted **unit** motto in the book is
    the 105th's.

## Attached and irregular forces

- **The sluggalos ("Legionnaires")** — Carl's Book 6 mercenary infantry, carried straight into
  Faction Wars as the army's mass rank-and-file. Their tactical doctrine is unchanged: "they'd swarm
  the castle face-first, inexpertly killing and getting themselves splattered like a bunch of
  idiots." They are fiercely protective of Imani, and chase Prepotente across naga territory on foot
  after he insults her, nearly wrecking Operation: Snake Pit. **Sneaky Tyler C** is the lead
  sluggalo; **Kandy "Bigs" Newton** gets credit for killing the Sepsis Whore. The system counts them
  as a distinct class of NPC, which makes them immune to several area effects that hit everyone
  else. See [The Mercenary Arcade](mercenary-arcade.md)
- **The Penis Parade crew** — now officers. The strippers are all made **Sergeant First Class**, and
  Dong Quixote is reassigned into the 104th for the Madness assault. Splash Zone, Doctor Bones and
  Gluteus Maxx fight on Carl's personal assault team. See [The Penis Parade Crew](penis-parade-crew.md)
- **[Prepotente](../characters/prepotente.md)** — joins late as a **Colonel** after an extended, furious refusal
- **Team Retribution** — the allied NPCs, coordinated through **[Britney](../characters/britney.md)**, who "has become quite
  close with Juice Box" and works "multiple lines of communication with the skill of a seasoned
  police dispatcher." See [Team Retribution](team-retribution.md)
- **[Justice Light](../characters/justice-light.md)** — works semi-independently beneath the FUPA on a trans-dimensional trap and a
  buried "antenna," using sluggalo labour because they "didn't seem to mind the idea of accidentally
  tripping traps and getting themselves skewered"
