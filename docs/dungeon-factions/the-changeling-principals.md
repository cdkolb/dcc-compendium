# The Changeling Principals

A secretive cult sect of [Changelings](../species/changeling.md), disguised as the guard ranks of
Hump Town's Dromedarian population on Floor 5. Their public role — negotiating and maintaining
the town's "collateral" arrangement with the Dirigible Gnomes to prevent bombing — masks their
real, generations-long agenda: obtaining a plant-based cure for **Compression Sickness**, the
increasingly common birth defect afflicting their species.

## Leadership and Method

**Henrik**, posing as Hump Town's elder statesman, leads the sect. Believing the ghost
[Quetzalcoatlus](../bosses/quetzalcoatlus.md) is the only living knowledge-holder of the needed
spell, the Principals maintain the gnome hostage **Wynne** — a Flesh Mechanic capable of
temporarily giving a ghost physical form — specifically to force him to give Quetzalcoatlus flesh
so a Principal could touch her and learn the cure. Wynne cooperated under drug coercion (via a
psychoactive mushroom) for years before resisting, at which point the Principals began forcibly
overdosing him.

The hostage arrangement itself runs through a pair of magically-linked pocket watches — one of the
three pieces of the [Gate of the Feral Gods](../items/gate-of-the-feral-gods.md) — exchanged daily
with Commandant Kane of [the Wasteland](the-wasteland.md) as proof Wynne remains alive.

## Collapse

The plan unravels when a rival crawler group (led by the hostile "D-Bag Geek" Low Thi) kills Wynne
before the Principals can force the resurrection spell out of him, triggering an in-universe
"Cockblock" achievement for the killers and starting a countdown to the gnomes' retaliatory
bombing of the town. Henrik and the surviving Principals — including a Level 49 lieutenant named
Svern, killed by Carl in a single hit via [Talon Strike](../abilities/talon-strike.md) — abandon
the hostage plot afterward and retreat into the Necropolis of Anser itself, attempting a
"Plan B or C" to reach Quetzalcoatlus directly by way of the drowned *Akula* submarine wreck.
Henrik is ultimately killed there by Quetzalcoatlus herself, and every remaining Principal dies in
the necropolis alongside the crawlers sent to clear it.

## Aftermath

- Carl and Katia impersonate Henrik and a resurrected, decoy version of Wynne to trick the gnomes
  into a face-to-face negotiation, using the ruse to ambush and destroy the airship *Vahana* and
  its crew mid-flight — see [The Wasteland](the-wasteland.md)
- Juice Box, Henrik's sister and the only Principal who openly sides with Carl's party, survives
  the floor and agrees to carry a hidden piece of the Gate through to Floor 6 in exchange for
  Carl's promise to try to bring surviving changelings down with him on future floors
