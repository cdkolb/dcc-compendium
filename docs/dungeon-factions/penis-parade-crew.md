# The Penis Parade Crew

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Male strip club on the top floor of the [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md)
      — and, from Book 6, Carl's standing infantry company
    - **Doubles as:** The front door of the [Guild of Suffering](guild-of-suffering.md), and an
      unlisted [Meat Shields](meat-shields.md) branch run from Rosemarie's table
    - **Hired:** 13 male dancers plus **Doctor Bones** and **Bucket Boy**, on a two-floor mercenary
      contract for **100,000 gold** — the exact value of Carl's coupon
    - **Why:** Because the alternative was the crew being reassigned to Larracos and fielded
      against Carl in Faction Wars
    - **Sister clubs:** **Bitches** (women, top floor); **Penis Palace** and **Girls of Ill Refute**
      (middle floor)

## Overview

The Penis Parade is the seedier of the Desperado Club's two male strip clubs, and Donut's favourite
place in the dungeon. Book 6 turns it into something else entirely: the crew walks out of a burning
nightclub and becomes the nucleus of Carl's Faction Wars infantry.

The club as Carl first sees it: a naked, mohawked forest troll gyrating on stage twirling a war
club "with the enthusiasm of a majorette tripping on ecstasy," drunk elf and dwarf NPCs pelting him
with gold coins, a skinny crocodilian kid scrambling after the coins with a bucket, and a skeleton
in a golden crown DJing from a booth in the shadows. There is no pole.

Per [Astrid](../characters/astrid.md)'s arrangement, the dancers here were never supposed to be this good. Carl's read:

> "I think the men in the Penis Parade on this floor are supposed to be like that. Like super, uh,
> unqualified. But they're not. Well, some are. When Astrid ended up with the job as the assistant
> manager of the club, she made certain her two sons were safe and got them a gig at the Penis
> Parade where she could watch over them."

## The hire

After Astrid's death and the vorpal rampage, Damascus refuses to leave but demands Carl take
everyone else:

> "The club is going to be closed for remodeling after this. It's happened before. With my mother
> dead, they'll assign a new assistant manager. There'll be a restructuring. They'll kick us out...
> The new management is going to reassign them to the Larracos level. Or worse. If you don't take
> them now, you'll end up fighting against them on the ninth floor."

Rosemarie the dwarf — secretly the manager of the club's unlisted Meat Shields branch — approves
the contract on the spot: the whole crew, two floors, 100,000 gold.

> "The 13 male strippers plus that skeleton DJ guy and the crocodilian Bucket Boy kid from the
> Penis Parade were already in the barracks. They'd transferred outside and to the front door of
> our personal space the moment we hired them."

Only the men of the Penis Parade were eligible. The women from **Bitches**, and both the male and
female clubs on the middle floor — the **Penis Palace** and **Girls of Ill Refute** ("Not repute
with a P") — are left behind. Damascus had already noted that "the pricks at the Palace won't have
us."

## As a fighting unit

Housed in the guild barracks and trained in the [Mercenary Arcade](mercenary-arcade.md), the crew
turns into a real infantry company over the back half of the floor. They are also the delivery
mechanism for Carl's **sluggalo** scheme: Carl infects Doctor Bones with *Super Spreader*, Bones
infects the rest of the crew simultaneously and casts a spell to keep the resulting slugs
non-hostile, and the locked training room fills with roughly forty new bodies.

One hard limitation Carl notes: mercenaries born in the arcade regenerate there, but the hired crew
do not have that protection outside it. "I'm pretty sure if any of the stripper guys die outside of
the training arcade, they die for real. Only some special types of mercenaries can regenerate." The
death of Author Steve Rowland proves it.

By the end of the book they are simply part of the team — moving off toward their barracks with
everyone else, Dong Quixote quiet and grieving for his friend, while Carl notes he'll have to talk
to him.
