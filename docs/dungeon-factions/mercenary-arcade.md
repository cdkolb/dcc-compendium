# The Mercenary Arcade

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Purchasable guildhall upgrade — a training room for hired mercenaries
    - **What makes it different:** Mercenaries inside it **fight, die, and regenerate a minute
      later**, over and over, gaining levels as they go
    - **Eligibility:** Only mercenaries housed in the **communal guild barracks** can train here.
      Ones billeted in individual party safe spaces cannot — see [Meat Shields](meat-shields.md)
    - **Crawler access:** Carl can spar, but is warned he would not be safe in there himself
    - **Also includes:** An AI drill sergeant (the same instructor as the regular training room,
      instanced many times over) and a magical healer who works on mercenaries but not on crawlers
    - **Not to be confused with:** the saferoom **practice arena** for totem cards, which the
      Mercenary Arcade explicitly does not replace

## Overview

Mordecai flags it as a priority the moment mercenary coupons start appearing: "you need to purchase
a mercenary training arcade for the guildhall. I'll talk to Imani about it." Donut has Imani buy
both the barracks upgrade and the arena while Carl is off assassinating Astrid.

The system takes care to distinguish it from the new saferoom card arenas: "The mercenary training
arcade some of you may have purchased for your guildhall will not work for this."

## What's inside

> "The room was significantly larger and could do a lot more than I had anticipated. The AI drill
> sergeant was the same guy from the regular training room, and multiple instances of him appeared
> across the room. The difference was, the AI guy was literally killing them sometimes, only for
> them to regenerate a minute later, just like it had been with Growler Gary. They were also
> practicing fighting each other to the death, which was ridiculously fucked up, but I imagined it
> was a great way to train yourself up if you ignored the trauma of dying over and over."

This is the entire point. Outside the arcade, a dead mercenary is dead — "Only some special types
of mercenaries can regenerate." Inside it, death is a training cost. An NPC hired at level 10 can
be ground up into something dangerous in the time it takes a floor to collapse.

It is also where Carl discovers what he actually bought with his 100,000-gold coupon: Splash Zone
is a powerful water mage, Bucket Boy is a healer, Doctor Bones is a level-90 Skin Skellie Bard with
crowd-control spells that turn enemies into minions. See
[The Penis Parade Crew](penis-parade-crew.md).

## The sluggalo scheme

The arcade is the engine behind the book's most dubious piece of army-building. Carl locks the
Penis Parade crew inside it with Doctor Bones, who carries an improved version of Carl's *Super
Spreader* spell:

> "They're not punk rock. They're Sluggalos. And I only infected one of them. Doctor Bones. He has
> the same *Super Spreader* spell as I do, but his is better. I can only do it on one person once
> per hour. He infected the others all at the same time, and then he cast a spell on the slugs so
> they're not so hostile after they come out."

Because the slugs are **born inside the arcade**, they regenerate there like any other arcade-born
mercenary, and they can be levelled the same way. Roughly forty of them come out — mostly levels
20–30, with a handful of 50s — and the strongest are housed in the newly upgraded 100-slot
barracks, "which they were apparently trashing." The weakest are sent out the door in waves to
soak up demons.

Carl's *Conscript* spell is the other half of the trick, and he is honest about its limits: "It
makes them fight on our side, sort of. It doesn't really change their attitude, and they can be
rebellious. It's kinda like turning them into totems."

Elle's assessment of the whole operation, once she works out why Carl wanted the barracks upgrade:
"Of all the fucked up shit you've done to NPCs, I'm pretty sure this is the worst."

Between hires and sluggalos, the arcade is a mercenary multiplier: the reason Carl's instruction to
every party is "Make sure every individual team has as much mercenary space as they can. We're
gonna fill every spot."
