# Meat Shields

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Mercenary market chain — the in-dungeon business where crawlers hire NPC fighters
    - **Known locations (Floor 8):** Inside the [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md),
      inside [Club Vanquisher](../locations/club-vanquisher.md), and formerly one in Larracos.
      Plus a **hidden fourth**, run out of a dwarf named **Rosemarie**'s table in the Penis Parade
    - **Payment:** Gold, including **mercenary coupons** from loot boxes — Carl's is worth
      100,000 gold of services at any participating location
    - **Guild capacity:** Barracks hold **20** mercenaries by default; upgradeable to **100** for
      roughly **five million gold** (with Donut's discount)

## Overview

Meat Shields is the name over the door wherever mercenaries are sold. Carl first encounters it as a
line of text in a loot box:

> **Mercenary coupon! The bearer of this instrument is entitled to 100,000 gold worth of mercenary
> services at any participating Meat Shields location.**

Mordecai's explanation: "Probably the name of the mercenary markets on this floor. There's one in
both the Desperado and Club Vanquisher. And there probably was one in Larracos, too."

The Desperado Club's branch sits in a side hallway off the badger bar, alongside the gnome jeweler
and a few other shops. Carl and Donut both hold coupons and spend most of the book saving them.

## Rosemarie's hidden branch

The Book 6 twist is that there was a second Desperado Club location the whole time, and it isn't on
the map. **Rosemarie**, an elderly dwarf with a mole named Bernie on her shoulder, spends her
evenings at the Penis Parade heckling the dancers and throwing coins at their faces. She is also
the manager of the top floor's Meat Shields location:

> "I didn't know there was one on this floor," I said, instinctively pulling up the map. There
> wasn't a location listed.
>
> "I work out of my home office," she said, waving vaguely over where her table used to be.

This was [Astrid](../characters/astrid.md)'s arrangement. Carl works it out afterward: Astrid installed her sons at the Penis
Parade where she could watch over them, they brought their friends, and she parked Rosemarie in the
club so that the whole crew were "technically still mercenaries, but since nobody knew the club was
also a Meat Shields location, they were safe."

Rosemarie approves the entire Penis Parade crew's transfer on the spot when Damascus insists —
100,000 gold, a two-floor contract, the exact value of Carl's coupon. She is not happy about it:
"If you guys leave, they'll fill this place up with the dregs again. I'll never get no business."

## Memberships, contracts, and the ninth floor

The mechanic Damascus exploits is that **Meat Shields membership persists**. He, his brother and
every dancer in the club still hold theirs, which makes them hirable even though they are club
staff:

> "We all still have our memberships to Meat Shields. Donut said you have a coupon. You can hire
> us. Do it for a two-floor contract, and they'll be able to leave with you."

The alternative was being reassigned to the Larracos-level Meat Shields in the post-Astrid
restructuring — which, on Floor 9, means being sold to somebody else's Faction Wars army. Damascus
puts the stakes plainly: "If you don't take them now, you'll end up fighting against them on the
ninth floor."

## Housing and capacity

Hired mercenaries have to be housed. The relevant numbers, from Elle:

- The guild's communal **barracks** hold **20**.
- The upgrade takes it to **100** and costs "almost five million gold, and that's with Donut's
  discount" — "you gotta sell a kidney to get it."
- Individual parties can hire additional mercenaries beyond that and house them in their own
  personal safe spaces.
- **But only mercenaries housed in the communal barracks may train and gain levels in the
  [Mercenary Arcade](mercenary-arcade.md).** That restriction is the whole reason Carl scrapes
  together five million gold before the floor collapses.
