# Team Retribution

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Who they are:** the NPCs of Larracos — the tenth Faction Wars team, created by Carl's Book 6
      action item and named by the NPCs themselves
    - **Book 6 name:** the **Home Team**. "Team Retribution" is their own choice
    - **Co-warlords:** **[Juice Box](../characters/juice-box.md)** (a changeling) and **[Sir Ferdinand](../characters/ferdinand.md)** (a cat)
    - **Adjutant:** **Drick**, an "elf outworlder" who is not really an elf
    - **Role in the game:** the *defending* team. They hold Larracos; every other team must take it
    - **Alliance:** **The Good Guys**, with the Princess Posse — the only alliance on the board that
      never breaks
    - **Fate:** voluntarily forfeit at the very end, handing the Princess Posse the win

## Origin and standing

Cascadia's opening broadcast introduces them dismissively — "those ungrateful NPC upstarts" — and
then sets out their unique position:

> "Anyway, there are ten teams total. Nine attacking teams and one defending team, the NPCs, who
> have decided to call themselves Team Retribution. They are headquartered in Larracos. Everyone
> else is spread out like a pie with Larracos in the center."

Their soldiers are marked on the map as **white dots with a green outline**. Crucially, Team
Retribution's *allies* also get to stay inside the city when the ceasefire expires — which is why
Donut's snap acceptance of their truce offer, minutes into the floor, matters so much.

Ferdinand's warlord access exists only as a workaround: "Ferdinand was added because the system
didn't allow Juice Box access to the warlord menu."

## Component forces

### The Larracos Defence Force

The city's standing military, commanded by **War Leader Arief**, a level 80
[Semeru dwarf](../species/semeru-dwarves.md):

> **War Leader Arief. Level 80 Semeru Dwarf. Commander of the Larracos Defense Force Infantry.**
> This is an ally. As you are both officers, attacking him will automatically negate your peace
> treaty if there are witnesses.

Arief greets Carl and Donut at the Larracos College of Magic with a deep bow — "Honored Warlords
Carl and Princess Donut. It is an honor to have you within our borders" — apparently unaware, or
unbothered, that they are the two who flooded his city. Every Semeru in the force carries the
permanent **Despondent** debuff from Ysalte's death: a 15% hit to all stats and 10% extra damage
taken from worshippers of other deities.

### The Jatismara

Semeru who remember previous crawl seasons. Arief, on his uncle:

> "I don't remember this, but there are some with the memory quickened. I believe I am new, as are
> most of us. But some have persisted. My uncle remembers dozens of seasons now. Not as many as
> Juice Box, but he's one of the **Jatismara**, and he sits upon her war council."

That single sentence is the entirety of what the series says about them — see
[Semeru Dwarves](../species/semeru-dwarves.md#the-jatismara).

### The changeling network

Juice Box is a changeling herself, and changelings form the team's intelligence apparatus *and* a
chunk of its officer corps. When reinforcements crash the courtyard late in the book:

> "It was a horror show of monstrosities of all shapes and sizes, and for a terrifying moment, I
> thought we were about to get overrun. But then I saw the indicators over them. **Team Retribution.
> And not just that, but officers. Changelings.**"

Book 7 also introduces a formal classification — "class A and B NPC changelings" — which matters
because Justice Light miscalculates by assuming procedurally generated NPCs (his sluggalos) can't be
copied by them.

### Dwarven diggers

The Semeru's giant excavation machines: "basically a boxy, god-sized robot covered in all directions
with spiral digging arms and goblin-murder-dozer-like spinning wheels," crewed by a pair of Semeru
in a shoulder control panel. Tunnels criss-cross the whole floor to move them.

### The general citizenry

The college campus is staffed by "Elves, draconians, and fairies, along with a smattering of several
other races," all flagged as Team Retribution soldiers. When Juice Box finally commits ground troops
to Shanty Town, they are "mostly Semeru dwarves and the regular citizens of Larracos."

## The alliance in practice

Relations with the Princess Posse start cordial and stay guarded. Juice Box marries the crawler
**[Louis Santiago](../characters/louis-santiago.md)** in a small ceremony that formalises the crawler/NPC alliance. **[Britney](../characters/britney.md)** becomes
the standing liaison — "Britney will coordinate all of our communication with Team Retribution. It
seems she's become quite close with Juice Box. She's gotten very good at being a go-between, working
multiple lines of communication with the skill of a seasoned police dispatcher."

But the NPCs never entirely trust the crawlers, and [Justice Light](../characters/justice-light.md) says so to Juice Box's face:

> "You are distrustful of us former crawlers. I understand that. You fear we crawlers will sacrifice
> you to save ourselves, and you have positioned yourselves to protect against that."

### Their action items

Ferdinand's two emergency action items are among the most consequential in the book, and both are
delivered in his own register. The first shuts down the live feeds:

> "Because them alien cunts on other floors are being little bitches and cheating, we propose that
> nobody in the dungeon, including those pricks partying it up in that centipede's asshole, be
> allowed to watch any outside feeds for the remainder of this little game."

It is approved immediately. The loophole that survives it — every off-world team buys the galactic
news media centre upgrade and communicates by repeatedly renaming its own channels — is Odette's
scoop at the warlord council.

The second, late in the book, keeps the summoned gods on the board:

> "Gods that appear during Faction Wars don't gotta go home right away. Uh, Juice Box told me to say
> a bunch of other stuff too but now I can't remember it all."

### Their military record

Team Retribution take both the **Bone Clan's** and **the Dream's** throne rooms during the endgame —
neither of which Carl planned — and inherit all of the Skull Empire's assets when the War Mage
Rebellion kills Stalwart, because the war mages were not an active team. Juice Box also possesses
Louis to drop the bomb that kills Warlord Fang of the Reavers.

## The forfeit

Agatha's third action item makes the endgame zero-sum: every soldier and warlord of a defeated team
now drops dead where they stand. With both teams unable to coexist, Juice Box and Ferdinand simply
walk off the board:

> **System Message: Both Warlords Ferdinand and Juice Box have left the realm. Under the current
> ruleset, they have abandoned their posts and have forfeited their position.**
>
> **System Message: Team Retribution has been defeated.**
>
> **System Message: The Princess Posse has won Faction Wars.**
