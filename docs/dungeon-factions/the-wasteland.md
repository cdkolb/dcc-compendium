# The Wasteland

The military crew of the dirigible-gnome dreadnaught ***the Wasteland***, the airborne
antagonist faction of Floor 5's Air Quadrant. Once peaceful "sky garden" builders who tried to
stay neutral above the world's conflicts, the [Dirigible Gnomes](../species/index.md#other-book-4-species)
militarized generations ago after hostile crawler-like "winged predators" arrived through a
portal and refused to share the skies — repurposing their old ceremonial peace balloons
(**Legate Balloons**, still recognizable by their black-and-gold stripes) into the war fleet Carl
encounters.

## Leadership

- **Commandant Kane** — commands the *Wasteland* and personally orders the bombing of the
  Bactrian settlement after their hostage collateral (a pet pig) is killed; found dead in his own
  house at the hands of his own pet, [Denise the Feral Goose Mother](../bosses/denise-the-feral-goose-mother.md),
  shortly before Carl's party arrives.
- **Leon the Commissar** — Kane's chief political officer and negotiator; a "master politician"
  who inspects hostage collateral in person and personally casts changeling-detection spells.
  Killed mid-air during Carl's decoy-negotiation ambush.
- **Crixus** — Kane's sniper captain, armed with a wide-bore rifle firing **Live Ammo
  Balls** (clamshell munitions each containing a stasis-trapped [Frenzied
  Gerbil](../mobs/frenzied-gerbil.md)). Killed by Katia mid-fight.
- **Bonnie** — Kane's young daughter and the *Wasteland*'s true final resident; recovered alive by
  Carl's party after the dreadnaught is destroyed.

## The Collateral System

To keep towns from being bombed outright, the *Wasteland* maintains a hostage arrangement with
each Dromedarian/Bactrian settlement, confirmed daily via a pair of magically-linked pocket
watches — one of the three pieces of the [Gate of the Feral Gods](../items/gate-of-the-feral-gods.md).
Hostages are periodically inspected in person by Leon, who casts a spell to confirm they haven't
been replaced by a [Changeling](../species/changeling.md).

## Downfall

After the [Changeling Principals](the-changeling-principals.md)' gnome hostage, Wynne, is killed
by a rival crawler group, Carl exploits the collateral-verification ritual itself: Donut resurrects
Wynne's corpse into a temporary zombie state, Katia disguises herself as both Wynne's undead body
and the (already-dead) Henrik's corpse, and the trick convinces Leon to fly the group aboard the
*Vahana* legate balloon under a false truce. Mid-flight, Carl's allied archers destroy the escort
fleet with missiles while Donut's *Hole* spell drops Leon, Crixus, and the pilot out of the basket
to their deaths; Carl then pilots the balloon directly at the *Wasteland* itself and fires the
last of his missiles into its exposed underside before the party parachutes clear. The dreadnaught
is destroyed in the resulting explosion, killing the bulk of its remaining crew — Carl gains two
character levels from the kill count alone.

## Aftermath

- Bonnie survives the crash in the wreckage of the family "castle" (an ordinary suburban house
  kept aloft by a soul-crystal-powered balloon), guarded by the boss
  [Denise](../bosses/denise-the-feral-goose-mother.md); Carl and Donut adopt her into Hump Town's
  care afterward
- Surviving gnomes who bail out or crash-land are hunted down individually over the following day,
  including a second, unexpected wave of gerbil-swarm retaliation — see [Frenzied
  Gerbil](../mobs/frenzied-gerbil.md)
- The house/castle's soul crystal and the second piece of the Gate of the Feral Gods (looted from
  Kane's corpse) both pass into Carl's possession afterward
