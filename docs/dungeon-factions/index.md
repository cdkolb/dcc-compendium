# Dungeon Factions

Businesses and local organizations encountered *inside* the dungeon itself — as distinct from
the interstellar governing/broadcast powers running the show (see
[Factions & Organizations](../factions/index.md)) and from the peoples/species themselves (see
[Species](../species/index.md)).

## Full pages

- [Rev-Up Moonshine Company](rev-up-moonshine.md) — Floor 2 pyramid-scheme distillery run by Krakaren
- [Iron Tangle Management](iron-tangle-management.md) — Floor 4's corporate transit authority, its exploited NPC workforce, and its self-destruct
- [The Changeling Principals](the-changeling-principals.md) — Floor 5's cult of disguised Changelings, seeking a cure for Compression Sickness
- [The Wasteland](the-wasteland.md) — Floor 5's militarized dirigible-gnome dreadnaught crew and bombing campaign
- [Guild of Suffering](guild-of-suffering.md) — a cult built around any Ring of Divine Suffering's forced tattoo-branding; Book 6 reveals its hidden entrance, its "Guild Jobs" contract assassinations, and the family running it
- [Meat Shields](meat-shields.md) — the mercenary market chain, its hidden Penis Parade branch, and the barracks capacity rules
- [The Penis Parade Crew](penis-parade-crew.md) — 13 dancers plus Doctor Bones and Bucket Boy, hired en masse and turned into Carl's standing infantry
- [The Mercenary Arcade](mercenary-arcade.md) — the guildhall upgrade where mercenaries die and regenerate to level up; the engine behind the sluggalo army
- [The Demon Tamer's Guild](demon-tamers-guild.md) — defunct, and the sealed arena of fifteen vorpals it left behind the Desperado Club
- [The Princess Posse Army](princess-posse-army.md) — Book 7: the seven battalions, the SOFs, the
  ranks, and the tattoos
- [Team Retribution](team-retribution.md) — Book 7: the NPCs of Larracos, their component forces,
  and the forfeit that ends Faction Wars
- [The Pineapple Cabaret](pineapple-cabaret.md) — Book 8: the hidden pocket-dimension sanctuary
  built by former crawlers Herot and Menerva, the book's central escape hatch for NPCs, mobs, and
  Li Na alike
- [The Stage Performance Guild](stage-performance-guild.md) — Book 8: a hidden karaoke-gated guild
  inside the Lollipop bar, secretly run by Porky, half of the split character Corcunda

## Other Book 3 dungeon groups

| Name | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Krakaren Drug Cartel (Floor 4) | Franchised narcotics operation | The same Krakaren "brand" from [Rev-Up Moonshine Company](rev-up-moonshine.md) recurs on the Iron Tangle — a separate Borough Boss-tier Krakaren runs each race-specific drug den, distributing the addictive Rev-Up Vitamin Immunity Shot to mobs via Pooka neighborhood bosses (who transform into giant goats if attacked); not personally engaged by Carl. Per Mordecai, the whole Krakaren/Pooka drug-den setup is Borant's in-universe political cartoon aimed at "the Plenty" (see [Factions](../factions/index.md)), not a literal depiction — withdrawal from the shot is also what generates the floor's entire ghoul-disposal crisis, see [The Iron Tangle Railway System](../mechanics/the-iron-tangle-railway.md) |

## Other Book 6 dungeon groups

| Name | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Chowder War factions | Two mob factions | The **Monk Seals** (masters of "Caribbean Kung Fu," who hold the ocean sacred) and the **Red *Maníseros* Land Crabs** (masters of *Juego de maní*, who can only breed in the sea). Their annual collision is a scripted Floor 8 quest. [Sister Ines Quiteria](../characters/sister-ines-quiteria.md) tips the seals off to the crabs' sneak attack, and instead of a quick rout it becomes a war of attrition in which "the only hope of survival from either side is for them to completely wipe out their enemy." **The Chowder War quest fails outright** and is replaced by *Hell Comes to Crawler Town*: the sheer volume of death attracts the attention of gods and demons, and Ines — having charmed the level-125 city boss [Madre de Aguas](../bosses/madre-de-aguas.md) with a Nebular-boosted *Word Weaver* — turns both armies into zombies that Sheol demons then possess and ride into the world. Carl's totem cards **Geraldo** (Monk Seal) and **Raul** (Red Maníseros crab) are flagged from these two factions, and loathe each other. Treated here as a short entry rather than a full page: the detail belongs to the quest chain and the [Timeline](../timeline/index.md) |
| Club Vanquisher | Members-only club | **A location, not an organization** — see [Club Vanquisher](../locations/club-vanquisher.md), which the Locations pass owns. Book 6 gives the series' first real interior look: a ski-lodge lobby with trophy heads on the walls and a black dragon's head over the fireplace, Ares warrior-clerics of **Hellik** (ram-headed, twenty brothers who compete for door duty) guarding the entrance, **mantaur** guards in bowties, a **Comforting Supplies** market, the **Blessed Sun Enchantments** jeweler, an **All Healer**, and the secret **Emberus High Temple of the Ending Sun**. Non-members with only deity access get one pass a day, may visit **one location per visit**, must state where in advance on a request form, must be escorted by a cleric of their god (or pay a fee for a guard escort), and may not use the refreshments or entertainment |
| Desperado Club | Members-only club | **A location, not an organization** — see [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md). Book 6 developments: the Night Wyrm sells Carl a full **secret-passage map** (inter-floor stairwells, monster-occupied tunnels, a hidden potion market on the middle floor, and drug dens behind wall panels — self-serve narcotics counters "set up like one of those frozen yogurt places"); the club hosts the [Guild of Suffering](guild-of-suffering.md), an unlisted [Meat Shields](meat-shields.md) branch, and the sealed [Demon Tamer's Guild](demon-tamers-guild.md) arena; and the whole building is **wrecked and closed for remodeling** after the [Astrid](../characters/astrid.md) assassination in chapters 32–33 unleashes the vorpals. Orren moves out; the rebuild is done by NPCs with hammers because "in certain cases, we find continuity the best choice" |

## Other Book 7 dungeon groups

| Name | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Larracos College of Magic | Magic university / Team Retribution garrison | Occupies about a quarter of a ring roughly three-quarters of the way up the funnel of Larracos, on the **50th level** of the city. Dorms, an observatory, a stained-glass lecture hall and an alabaster tower whose top floor is a cafeteria attached to a saferoom — which makes it Carl and Donut's in-city base. Staffed and garrisoned by [Team Retribution](team-retribution.md) NPCs: "Elves, draconians, and fairies, along with a smattering of several other races." Its front gate holds through the zarogoth siege — "A group of Semeru dwarves, all with their ornate parade armor, stood at the front gate to the college, pushing back a rush of the shadowy mobs." The AI notes the college was refounded after Scolopendra's attack by "new scholars... finding the old research intact" |
| The Midnight Market | Hidden bazaar | A special bazaar on the bottom regular floor of the [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md). Access comes from a small **Upgrade Patch** depicting "a traveling Reaper Spider Minion merchant," which grants "**Access to the Midnight Market. Safety or Sanity not guaranteed**," merchant proximity alerts, and +5% Dexterity. Its known operator is **Paca**, "an agent for **Krakaren Prime**, who is stuck in the Nothing" — Justice Light calls her "an ambassador for the in-game's version of the Apothecary." The [War Mage Rebellion](../factions/war-mage-rebellion.md) trades her the **Gate of the Feral Gods** for five hell-kissed Conscription potions, and she swallows it. **Carl never actually visits it on-page** — Justice Light retrieves the Gate with a crew of sluggalos ("There have been seasons when my shop was within the market. I know it well") |
| The Gray Market | Hidden market | Mentioned exactly once in the whole series, and never visited: "There was another hidden market on the middle floor I'd never managed to get into called the Gray Market, which was also on my list to visit." **An explicit dangling thread** |
| The Cartography and Calligrapher's Guilds | Hidden guilds inside Club Vanquisher | Both run by the same guildmaster, **Milk** — a [Xenopus](../species/xenopus.md) and, as Book 7 reveals, Carl's biological sister. Both guilds' doors are hidden and effectively nobody uses them: "She never bothered to hide the papers in the back where crawlers wouldn't see them. She hadn't had a one-on-one with a floor supervisor in over 50 seasons. What was the point?" Her indenture terms are brutal — "Will not be freed until her services are used at least 333 times in the dungeon. **Times used so far: 6.**" The calligraphy guild teaches an ink-and-lettering magic style "that I haven't seen used often in the dungeon," used in Book 7 to etch temporary buffs onto Donut and, at the climax, to let departing crawlers transfer skills onto paper as gifts. Milk's warnings about it: "Inks and letters that shine the brightest burn out the fastest." Rosetta advises the crew to kidnap her, on the grounds that "kidnapping and 'torturing' guildmasters for their information was not only allowed, but encouraged" |
| The Trap Master Guild | Crawler/indentured training guild | The guild **Justice Light** — skyfowl, Class Trap Master, eighth-edition cookbook author — instructed in as a Game Guide and Guildhall Instructor. His friend **Dante** "immediately applied to move from the lockpicking guild to the trap master guild" to be near him, and was later executed by administrative action for refusing an order to attack crawlers. Justice Light's own indenture was as a **shopkeeper**, replacing the NPC he killed during his season — a punishment he requested and extended his sentence for |
| Meat Shields | Mercenary market chain | Referenced by its function rather than its name in Book 7 — the chain itself is not named on-page. See [Meat Shields](meat-shields.md) |
| The Penis Parade crew | Dancers turned soldiers | Now formally inside Carl's army. All the strippers are made **Sergeant First Class**, "which would allow them to recruit and command the base troops, such as the Sluggalos." Splash Zone and Dong Quixote serve in Carl's "Personal Guard" platoon; Doctor Bones, Gluteus Maxx and Splash Zone fight in his personal assault team at the Madness castle; Dong is reassigned to the 104th and carries their flag on a war lance. The club itself "has re-opened... All new dancers" — none of the originals. See [The Penis Parade Crew](penis-parade-crew.md) |
| The sluggalos / "Legionnaires" | Mercenary infantry | Carl's Book 6 arcade-grown slug army, formally redesignated **Legionnaires** and forming the Princess Posse's mass rank-and-file. See [The Princess Posse Army](princess-posse-army.md) and [The Mercenary Arcade](mercenary-arcade.md) |
| Club Vanquisher | Members-only club | **A location** — see [Club Vanquisher](../locations/club-vanquisher.md). Book 7 wrecks it. The Posse breaks in through the Temple of Dagda to reach the Temple of Khepri and kill Princess Vinata, and the system's verdict is a permanent ban: *"Breaking into and defiling a church is one thing, but breaking into Club Vanquisher, hub of all religion? ... **Reward: You are permanently banned from Club Vanquisher. You are permanently banned from all temples except your own religion (Emberus) and any temples where you've been named friend of the church (Apito. Eileithyia. Eris. Grull. Hellik. Issitoq. Nekhebit. Theia. Yemaya.)**"* Carl: "it wasn't a surprise I'd been black-listed from all temples after that clusterfuck at Club Vanquisher." The mechanics of the ban belong to the Items/Mechanics passes |
| Desperado Club | Members-only club | **A location** — see [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md). Reopened under new management: **Hamed**, the Night Wyrm, leader of the [Guild of Suffering](guild-of-suffering.md), husband of the late Astrid and father of Damascus Steel and Anaconda. **All crawlers are banned.** Clarabelle, still on the door: "It's not just you, if that makes you feel better. It's because of you, but it's not just you. New management wants his first few days to go smoothly, so he's banned all crawlers. If he ever un-bans the crawlers, then we'll talk." The purge extends to staff — "All the sai and cretin guards had been fired and replaced" — and Hamed keeps bodyguards who are "Not exactly NPCs. Not exactly former crawlers" |

!!! warning "Unresolved: the missing Desperado Club staff"
    Book 7 raises several disappearances at the Desperado Club and resolves **none** of them.

    - **Damascus Steel** and **Anaconda**, Hamed's sons, went into the club to hunt and kill their
      father. Donut glimpses Damascus walking past a doorway once — "Do you think you can just sneak
      by and I wouldn't see you?" — and nobody else sees him. Dong searches the club "looking for
      some sign of the two missing strippers"; the new concierge and Clarabelle both say they haven't
      seen them. Carl: "That was a mystery for another time."
    - **The entire Bitches dancer crew**, including Splash Zone's wife **Snail Trail** (early shift)
      and her mother, his mother-in-law, **Grandma Sticky** (later shift). Splash Zone: "Same with
      Bitches, and we don't know where the old ones went. When this is over, I need to go back in
      there and keep looking for them. The whole crew at Bitches, including Snail Trail and Grandma
      Sticky are missing."
    - A dancer named **Tart**, who moved to "High Class Bitches" after breaking her jaw and became a
      mercenary — "nobody has seen her since."

    No fate is shown for any of them. The wiki records the disappearances and does not speculate.

## Other Book 8 dungeon groups

| Name | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Coblyn Corporation | Knocker (gnome-like) corporate drug cartel | A Floor 10 racetrack-level satirical corporation, in the same vein as [Rev-Up Moonshine Company](rev-up-moonshine.md): knockers, displaced from their mineral-rich caves by "the Corporatocracy running the mines," bootstrap themselves into legitimate toy and household-goods manufacturing on the back of a drug empire built selling to addicted miners. Having gone legitimate but still short of real corporate wealth, they diversify into **Toddler Cocaine** ("Daddy's Little Snow Drops") — low-dose, highly addictive product deliberately marketed to children via a line of collectible mascot dolls (Sally Speedball, Toot-Toot, Belushi, and their in-universe girl-band the Nose Candy Star Dusters). A botched defensive-elemental spell meant to protect the factory instead animates the dolls themselves into hostile, regenerating **Cocaine Elemental** mobs, and further protection spells produce wildly aggressive, "pass-through"-capable **Slag Elemental** bosses that are "pretty much impossible to kill" without a boss-box-qualifying kill. Encountered as a Heat 3/4 racetrack hazard (the "Gasworks Screw Factory, Steel Mill, and Doll Workshop") rather than a questline; the knockers themselves are described as "pretty harmless" if left alone |
| The Desperado Club | Members-only club | **A location** — see [Desperado Club](../locations/desperado-club.md#book-8-shadow-mimic-infestation). Secretly infested by [Shadow Mimics](../species/shadow-mimics.md), revealed via the boss [Mitch](../bosses/mitch.md); the club is left needing full fumigation and is effectively off-limits going forward, on top of Hamed's pre-existing ban on all crawlers |
