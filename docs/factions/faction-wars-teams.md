# The Faction Wars Teams

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Count:** Nine at the start of Book 6, **ten** by the last page
    - **Structure:** Eight sponsored (non-crawler) teams, plus the crawlers' **Princess Posse**,
      plus the NPC **Home Team** added in the closing chapter
    - **Setting:** The ninth floor, based on the city and castle of **Larracos** — see
      [Semeru Dwarves](../species/semeru-dwarves.md)
    - **Key Book 6 event:** Six of the eight sponsored teams try to quit. All six are refused

## The preproduction meeting

The fifth preproduction meeting, chaired by Cascadia with the mexx robot U56KL ("Urkel") running
the action-item queue, is where Carl and Donut meet the other warlords for the first time. Two
things happen there that shape everything after:

**Epitome Noflex crashes it.** The viceroy's head falls off mid-meeting because
[Juice Box](../characters/juice-box.md), wearing the face of Epitome Tagg's mother, has just killed
him. She takes his chair and puts her feet on the table. Tagg — who had told the others he'd
"taken care of this" — demands Cascadia intervene and is told she can't: "The rules haven't
changed. She's an NPC. The AI won't let us touch this."

Tagg's parting complaint is the thesis of the entire back half of the book:

> "They're becoming self-aware. All of them... It's going to be impossible for anybody to win."

**Carl proposes the tenth team.** In the same meeting: "I propose a new action item. A tenth team
populated with NPCs, afforded the same protections as the rest."

## The mass abdication

Late in Book 6, six of the eight sponsored teams give up at once:

> **The Operatic Collective has abdicated their spot and indicated their intention to abandon
> Faction Wars.**
> **The Blood Sultanate has abdicated their spot...**
> **The Dream has abdicated their spot...**
> **The Lemig Sortion has abdicated their spot...**
> **The Madness has abdicated their spot...**
> **The Reavers have abdicated their spot...**
>
> ***Please Wait...***
>
> **Offboarding request has been denied. The teams will remain as designated. All off-boarding
> requests are currently suspended while the Earth system remains in quarantine per Syndicate rule
> 470.B through 495 in the System AI Stability Addendum. And as you all know, the rules must be
> followed.**

Donut, arriving covered in tinsel: "Carl, Carl, did you see that? They tried to run away!"

The two that don't try to quit are the **Princess Posse** and the **Prism Kingdom** — the crawlers
who want the fight, and the Empress who spent everything she had to buy her way in.

The Burrowers and the Dreadnought Clan are absent from the roster entirely; both had already
withdrawn at the end of Book 5, and it is those two vacated slots the Prism Kingdom and the
Princess Posse bought.

## The final action items

With both warlords forced into the stairwell early, Carl spends the Princess Posse's **first
Emergency Action Item** on a procedural request: that all remaining pending action-item votes be
handed to the viewers, or failing that decided by the AI. It is granted immediately.

> "Zev said the AI is a lot friendlier if you ask nicely and if you give it options. Have you read
> all the previous emergency action items? Everyone was being complete dicks about it. Of course
> the AI is going to reject it. They still treat it like a computer, and it's not. They call it an
> AI, but there's nothing artificial about it."

The audience then decides the queue in thirty-second increments:

| Proposed by | Item | Result |
|---|---|---|
| The Madness | Limit Celestial Summoning events to five per team | **Rejected, 37%** — existing limit of fifteen stands |
| The Dream | Relax physics limits on long-range artillery; raise the playing-field ceiling | **Passed, 72%** |
| The Reavers | Accelerate pet leveling during Faction Wars | **Passed, 83%** |
| Princess Posse | **Remove safety protections for faction sponsors** | **Passed, 98%** |
| Princess Posse | **A tenth team populated with NPCs, afforded the same protections as the rest** | **Passed, 52%** |

The tenth-team result carries the detail that matters:

> **This team will be designated as Home Team with the city of Larracos as their base. Effective
> immediately, peace rules apply until the game starts. This team's assigned co-warlords are the
> NPCs Juice Box and Sir Ferdinand.**
>
> **An adjutant will be assigned before the battles commence.**

Sir Ferdinand already has a full page from Book 5 — see [Ferdinand](../characters/ferdinand.md).

The 98% vote to strip sponsor safety protections is the one Carl reacts to out loud, jabbing a
finger at the ceiling: *"Your people voted for this. Your own citizens said, yes, we're okay with
the crawlers killing you one by one."*

## The other side of the board

What none of the teams know at the end of Book 6 is that the AI has also agreed to let the
[Valtay](valtay-corporation.md) distribute **over 150,000** mercenaries and private soldiers evenly
across the **eight sponsored teams**, with orders to kill every crawler — and that the
[Open Intellect Pacifist Network](open-intellect-pacifist-network.md) has simultaneously boarded
**50,000 former crawlers** onto the Princess Posse's roster aboard the *Homecoming Queen*. Rosetta
asks the obvious follow-up in the book's last exchange:

> "I am worried about that tenth team... If the AI is going to allow the eight sponsors plus the
> crawler team to bolster their numbers so much, what's it doing for the NPCs?"

!!! warning "Common misattribution"
    **Commander Stockade belongs to the Lemig Sortion, not the Operatic Collective.** Odette names
    him as "Commander Stockade of the Lemig Sortion... the last of the 35 to be devoured," and
    Quasar had earlier noted that the Lemigs' commander was stuck in the Desperado Club. Separately,
    Odette and Carl watch "a submerged viceroy from team Madness get devoured by a shark" — two
    different deaths, two different teams.

## What happens next

Book 7 renames several of these teams and then destroys all of them. The **Home Team** becomes
**[Team Retribution](../dungeon-factions/team-retribution.md)**; the Skull Empire competes as **the
Bone Clan**, the Lemig Sortion as **the Democratic Sortion**, and the Prism Kingdom Clan as **the
Prism**. Eight of the nine sponsored teams immediately ally as **The Bloc** against the crawlers and
the NPCs, and the Princess Posse wins. For the alliances, the elimination order and the betrayals,
see [The Faction Wars Campaign](faction-wars-campaign.md).
