# The War Mage Rebellion

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Book:** 7, *This Inevitable Ruin*
    - **Type:** A **"non-team entity"** — a category the system invents on the spot and then,
      through a procedural accident, promotes to full warlord standing
    - **Leader:** **[Akuma](../characters/akuma.md)**, War Mage, Level 100 — "arguably the most powerful non-god magic caster
      on this floor"
    - **Unwanted co-warlord:** **Agatha**, whom Akuma openly asks Carl to kill
    - **Adjutant:** **Deng Bao**, the grixist who had been the orcs' adjutant — "he's the only one
      who would do it"
    - **Stated goal:** obtain Krakaren Prime's hell-kissed Conscription potions and recover
      **[the Scavenger's Daughter](../items/the-scavengers-daughter.md)** from inside the Larracos castle
    - **Fate:** Defeated when Akuma simply leaves the realm, having got what he came for

## What war mages are

War mages are one of the few genuinely **dungeon-born** entities — nobody designed them. Per the
AI, they are the accidental end product of too many combatants having access to *You're Not Done
Yet*: a flesher (skinned corpse) consumes a living being and re-sleeves it, casts *Boned* to
reanimate the skeleton, and the two become a symbiote. Symbiotes are prime flesher targets, so
they get re-sleeved again and again. After roughly twelve to sixteen iterations the magic itself
can become **sapient**, fusing flesh, skeleton and sapient magic into one being.

> "They're also all complete dicks. If they ever made action figures of these things, their
> accessories would include keys to a BMW, a few bumps of cocaine, and a membership card to some
> secret club where you can hunt homeless people for fun."

Each war mage's final form depends on its first two forms. Akuma's: "originally a high-elf cobbler
with a human skin, resleeved 12 times before the ambient magic gave him his final form." They come
with knowledge of hundreds of spells, and **their heads explode into a "Sapient Moment of
Destruction" roughly ten minutes after they die**.

The AI flags their existence as a standing liability the showrunners have never taken seriously:
because each war mage was created naturally and is rarely recycled, "most war mages find their
minds wiped and themselves sitting in quests or mercenary guilds each time a new dungeon opens" —
and the AI pointedly compares them to "that pissed-off changeling army that's in the middle of
wrecking your shit."

## How they gained standing

The rebellion announces itself by killing **War Chief Stalwart of the Bone Clan**. They had been
conscripted into his army; per Baroness Victory they "broke their own allegiance against the Skull
Empire, which they shouldn't be able to do," using "a clever combination of Pull spells." Their
dots turn white with a brownish-orange glow belonging to no army on the board.

> **System Message: The War Mage Rebellion is not an active team.**

Victory: *"We just received notification that there's a new, non-team entity called the War Mage
Rebellion. I still don't know what that even means. 'Non-team entity' is not a thing. At least it
wasn't until just now."*

**Drick** calls a foul on the grounds of illegal interference, alleging the war mages have been
working with Juice Box. A full quorum is convened — and the ruling backfires spectacularly:

> **Warlord Message: After a careful deliberation, it has been determined that the Non-Team Entity
> known as the War Mage Rebellion will not be allowed access to Larracos until the Peeling phase.**
>
> **Warlord Message: Since we are now in the Peeling Phase, this ruling is rendered moot. However,
> the system has determined that the Quorum's ruling has given the War Mage Rebellion standing.**

By ruling on them at all, the quorum makes them real.

## Agatha's three action items

Within moments of gaining standing, **Warlord Agatha** — named co-warlord over Akuma's objection,
the same way Ferdinand was bolted onto Juice Box — spends three emergency action items:

| # | Item | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Full resources and access to the warlord system; permission to claim thrones | Granted — "And frankly, you already had this, so this was pretty much a waste of an action item" |
| 2 | Full access to the **Conscription** spell | Granted — unaffiliated players can now be conscripted into the War Mage Rebellion |
| 3 | During the Peeling Phase, revert to original Faction Wars rules: **warlords and all soldiers of a defeated team automatically die** | Granted, with the caveat that it applies from that point forward — "I mean. Why not? You want drama. Now we got drama." |

The third item is the one that turns the endgame into a genuine zero-sum fight between the Princess
Posse and Team Retribution — "exactly what we'd been trying to avoid."

Agatha is later removed by the system for abandoning her post:

> **Warlord Message: Warlord Agatha of the War Mage Rebellion has been deemed to have abandoned her
> post. As such, she has been removed as Warlord from the War Mage Rebellion. Warlord Akuma has
> been given full system access and named sole Warlord.**

## They are not allies of Team Retribution

Despite Drick's accusation, the war mages are not working with the NPCs — they storm Team
Retribution's own throne room and occupy it. Juice Box, mid-assault: *"We need help! They're
everywhere! The War Mages aren't our friends. We need help!"*

Victory's read on the internal split: *"They clearly did not anticipate Agatha would be named as a
co-warlord, and the other leader, Akuma, does not like her... Based on that Akuma guy's reaction
when they assigned her as co-warlord, I don't think they are assisting each other at all."*

**Fopsy** the tripod adjutant offers a theory about Agatha's motive — that as a residual, she isn't
just trying to win Faction Wars and zero out the crawlers but to end the Celestial Ascendency as
fast as possible, because "once the ascendency is considered finished, assuming there are no
crawlers left, it automatically triggers the system's shutdown and hibernation sequence... a
possible alternative to the failsafe." He adds: "What I do not understand is how she convinced the
war mages to assist her."

## Akuma's stated motive

Akuma messages Carl directly:

> **Warlord Akuma:** Hey, underwear boy. Do me a favor. Kill that old lady for me, and I'll forgive
> you and your pet for killing off so many of my brothers.

And, asked who he means:

> **Warlord Akuma:** ...Agatha. The other warlord we were forced to accept in order to gain system
> access. She was forced upon us, and she is an enemy to you. She is an enemy to us all. We had no
> hand in those so-called "action items" other than the Conscription one. She slipped away before I
> was able to kill her. Do this for me, and we will be allies.

His explanation of the whole enterprise:

> **Warlord Akuma:** You won't be seeing me again on this floor. We never wished to keep that
> falling-down castle. We sought to test the potions we gathered. We wished to find the Scavenger's
> daughter, who was hidden within the castle, and we found her. She is with us now. Once we had her
> in our possession, we left. That was all we ever wanted with this place, and we are no enemies to
> you. But Agatha's actions continue to place everybody and everything in jeopardy, and she must be
> dealt with. She has a method that allows her to jump to the Waiting Room and reappear outside an
> open stairwell. You won't likely see her on this floor again. You will have to find her on the
> 10th. Do this for us if you want to survive. She seeks to kill us all. Not just you and not just
> me. Everybody and everything.

He abandons the throne room the moment he has her, and the rebellion is dissolved:

> **Warlord Message: Warlord Akuma has left the realm. As per the current season's ruleset, the War
> Mage rebellion has been defeated. They have no assets to award.**

## The potions

The "potions we gathered" are **hell-kissed Potions of Conscription, level 18**, brewed "in the
dark recesses of the Nothing by **Krakaren Prime**, who is said to yearn for domination, which was
why she'd been banished there in the first place. She prepares for a war she considers inevitable."
At level 18 the potion "will work against pretty much any non-affiliated entities, regardless of
their protections, including most deities."

Fopsy traces the transaction: the war mages **traded the [Gate of the Feral Gods](../items/gate-of-the-feral-gods.md)** — taken from
Elle's hands at Stalwart's death — "with a minion at the Midnight Market in exchange for five of
the potions." That minion is **Paca**, an agent for Krakaren Prime, who swallows the Gate.

!!! warning "Unresolved: who or what is the Scavenger's Daughter?"
    This is left explicitly open at the end of Book 7. Carl asks directly —

    > **Carl:** Mordecai, who is the Scavenger? And who is the Scavenger's Daughter? I know it's the
    > name of my patch, but I didn't know there was more to it than that.
    >
    > **Mordecai:** Are you talking about the in-dungeon one or the fairy tale? Either way, that's a
    > conversation that's not going to matter if you don't figure out how to find and kill those two
    > warlords...

    — and never gets an answer. Carl's own speculation later is that the Scavenger's Daughter the
    war mages took might be **Samantha's** daughter, which would make Samantha the Scavenger: "I
    still didn't know who that was. I really needed to sit down and figure all this out." This is a
    hunch, not a reveal. The wiki does not identify the Scavenger.

## Book 8: A Parade of Horribles

Akuma resurfaces on Floor 10 leading what remains of the Rebellion, this time seeking Carl's help
rather than avoiding him — he needs a fighting force to clear the **[Pineapple
Cabaret](../dungeon-factions/pineapple-cabaret.md)** of a Shadow Mimic and Ogre Imperium infestation
so the war mages can use it as their own exit from the dungeon. The full account of that alliance,
Elle McGibbons's interrogation of Akuma, and the "Big Six" taxonomy he helps introduce all belong on
his own page rather than duplicated here — see
[Akuma: Book 8](../characters/akuma.md#book-8-a-parade-of-horribles). The system AI later places the
War Mage Rebellion inside **Group Four (the sapient mobs)** of its own "Big Six" list, alongside
Shadow Mimics, the Ogre Imperium, and non-deity boss monsters like Krakaren Prime — see [Factions:
The Big Six](index.md#the-big-six).

Akuma states his intent to leave the floor before the book's end but is not shown captured, killed,
or freed on-page afterward; the Rebellion's fate past that point is left open.
