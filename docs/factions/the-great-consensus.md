# The Great Consensus

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** A political philosophy, not an organization — anti-Syndicate isolationism spreading
      across multiple species and systems
    - **Core claim:** "The Syndicate was a mistake, and we are all better off on our own"
    - **First heard by Carl:** from **Loita**, the kua-tin he assassinated on the fifth floor
    - **Explained in full:** Book 7, by **Rishi** of the Blood Sultanate, shortly before his death
    - **Its biggest known operation:** Princess Vinata's plan to steal the naga shipyard fleet and
      leave the galaxy

## The philosophy

Rishi lays it out when Carl asks why he hid instead of fighting:

> "Have you heard the term 'The Great Consensus' before?"

Carl has — from Loita, on Floor 6 — and his first guess is "It's some sort of nazi bullshit, isn't
it?" Rishi's answer is more careful than that:

> "I don't know that term. But it is a philosophy that is gaining momentum amongst many systems. It
> basically teaches the Syndicate was a mistake, and we are all better off on our own. **There are
> many aspects of it, and some have taken the idea and turned it into something xenophobic and evil,
> which is ironic considering the name of the philosophy.** The Bloom party of the kua-tin, for
> example, believe in racial purity and dislike the cultural influence of so-called 'dry' races. The
> mantids disdain what they believe are weaker and inferior species. Amongst the naga, some want
> what you want. Some naga believe while we can learn from each other, alien communities should have
> no say in how we live our lives."

## The three strands named

| Strand | Species | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Racial purity** | Kua-tin — specifically [the Bloom](../species/kua-tin-and-the-bloom.md) party | Belief in racial purity and hostility to the cultural influence of "dry" races |
| **Supremacy** | Mantids | "Disdain what they believe are weaker and inferior species" |
| **Departure** | Naga | Non-interference, taken to its logical end: leave the Syndicate's reach entirely |

Rishi is careful to distance himself from the philosophy even while explaining it: "I said she was a
proponent of the philosophy. **I am not.**"

## Princess Vinata's plan

The naga strand is the one that nearly comes off. Vinata — the Blood Sultanate's Faction Wars
warlord, playing the historical **Sepsis Whore** — was reportedly using the philosophy as the
justification for a fleet theft:

> "Vinata was a firm believer in this philosophy. She'd seen war on alien soil, and she firmly
> believed it was all for nothing. Her father insisted she participate in Faction Wars. We needed
> the tourist money. We needed to keep the shipyards staffed and running. **We were going to steal
> the entire fleet and run. We were going to travel, as a whole, for an entire generation, off in a
> direction that none of the tunnel threads point. Off to a place where it would be a very, very
> long time before we were found. That was our Great Consensus, engineered by Princess Vinata. To
> leave.**"

The naga system's grand in-orbit shipyards still exist, but "the factories were now mostly owned by
other entities, and the nagas themselves were a source of cheap labor." The fleet they proposed to
steal was, in effect, someone else's.

**The leak ends it.** As Rishi tells it: "Just before the meeting of the warlords, news broke that
laid out our plans regarding our intention to renege on our shipyard contracts, which caused
immediate chaos in our system. Because of the Emergency Action Item you enacted, we were no longer
able to see what else was happening at home. That is when I decided to flee and hide." The leak is
one of the three shocks that breaks the Bloc apart — see
[The Faction Wars Campaign](faction-wars-campaign.md).

## Rishi's counter-offer

Having hidden through the destruction of his wife's army, Rishi's actual purpose was to reach Carl
with a signed, AI-witnessed document naming Carl as **de facto leader of Earth** — a claim the naga
petitioned for on his behalf and which the court granted, on the grounds that "the fact you are a
warlord and the number one crawler on the leaderboard makes your claim legitimate." The document
offers Earth natives citizenship rights across all naga territories plus **five Naphil-class
generation ships**, in exchange for the right to terraform and settle the rest of Earth's solar
system after the crawl.

Vinata never signed it. "She would not agree to something like this." Rishi drafted and signed it
himself — the opposite of the Great Consensus, from a naga who had watched the philosophy's biggest
gamble collapse.

The system subsequently coins a new name for the polity: the **Naga Protectorate of the New
Reformer**.
