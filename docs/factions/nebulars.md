# The Nebulars / Nebular Balance

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Nomadic, machine-augmented religious civilization drawn from many species at once —
      not a single race
    - **Object of worship:** The ancient, stable **macro AI at the centre of the galaxy**, which
      runs the inner system and predates Syndicate society
    - **Core doctrine:** Replicating that AI is blasphemy
    - **Stated political goal:** Shut down every crawl in existence — by eradicating every planet
      capable of hosting one, and every other religion along with them
    - **Ethical position:** Pacifists **outside** the dungeon. Not pacifists inside it
    - **Formal name used for the sponsoring body (Book 6):** **the Nebular Balance**
    - **Hunter wing:** [Nebular Sin Patrol](nebular-sin-patrol.md) — the priests-in-training who
      hunt crawlers each season
    - **Distant relatives:** The [Reavers](faction-wars-teams.md), once related to the Nebulars,
      who took the same augmentation path further and kept only their brains

## Overview

Through Books 5 and 6 the Nebulars have shown up as robed, tall-hatted weirdos at the edge of every
scene, and Book 6 is where Mordecai finally explains what they actually want. The picture that
emerges is coherent, internally consistent, and completely deranged.

> "Near the center of the galaxy is a very large AI that extends a benign enhancement zone over
> several solar systems, and it's where a whole lot of people live. The 'inner system.' This AI is
> the same sort of thing that runs the crawl, but it predates Syndicate society. It is stable and
> is without personality. It's the only one like it. The Nebs worship it like it's a god, and they
> think it's blasphemy to try to replicate it."

Every macro AI built in the mantis facility and installed into a Primal Engine — see
[Game Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md) — is, by that doctrine, a forgery of God. Every crawl is
therefore a blasphemy factory, and every planet that *could* host one is a future blasphemy.

## What they want

Mordecai lays it out, and Carl immediately spots the contradiction:

> **Mordecai:** "...they really, really want to run a crawl themselves sometime, but nobody is ever
> going to let them because their stated goal is to shut all of this down. Hence they try to keep
> winning the Celestial Ascendency since one of the prizes of that is a seat at the table of the
> Syndicate crawl council. Outside the crawl, they're pacifists. You could literally walk up to one
> and punch him in the face, and he'll let you do it. Not so much inside the dungeon."
>
> **Carl:** "They want to shut the crawl down?"
>
> **Mordecai:** "Yeah, but don't get too excited. They preach the eradication of all the places
> that could host crawls, such as this planet. And all the people on it. And all other religions,
> too. They're psychotic."
>
> **Carl:** "Preaching for genocide doesn't exactly go along with pacifism."
>
> **Mordecai:** "Yeah, it doesn't make much sense to anybody else, either."

Mordecai's summary of the organization as a whole: "What they want is easy to explain, but what
they do is not because their actions never make sense."

So the strategy is not revolt but infiltration by the rules — win the Celestial Ascendency, take
the seat it grants on the Syndicate crawl council, and dismantle the institution from inside. They
field a hunter team every season, and they campaign endlessly for the right to run a season
themselves, which no one will ever grant them.

## Sponsorships in Book 6

The Nebulars buy influence at two levels on Floor 8, and both purchases go badly.

**Sister Ines Quiteria.** The Nebulars are both her **sponsor and her patron** — Mordecai notes
those amount to the same thing here. The announcers make the connection explicit during the
Chowder War coverage: because "her sponsor *and* patron, the Nebulars, helped her raise that *Word
Weaver* spell to level 15," she is able to charm a level-125 city boss *and* the spells that boss
can cast, which is what turns a mob war into a zombie apocalypse.

Carl reads the sponsorship as deliberate sabotage of a fragile person: "Whatever imbalance she had,
it's been deliberately kicked back into place. I think it's one of her sponsors... I think they
gave her something that sent her back over the edge." Mordecai's own guess is blunter: "They likely
sponsored her because she's religious, and they either want to convert her or make an example of
her." See [Sister Ines Quiteria](../characters/sister-ines-quiteria.md) and the
[Chowder War](../dungeon-factions/index.md#other-book-6-dungeon-groups).

**The goddess Ysalte.** Separately, the Floor 9 goddess Ysalte enters the realm on Floor 8 carrying
the tag: **"This goddess is sponsored by Pontifex Shine of the Nebular Balance."** This is the
book's only use of the name *Nebular Balance* and the only appearance of Pontifex Shine, who is
never seen. Ysalte is killed on-page by [Paz Lo](../characters/paz-lo.md) — see [Ysalte](../bosses/ysalte.md) —
which makes it two Nebular investments destroyed in the same floor.

!!! warning "Unresolved"
    Book 6 does not say whether "the Nebular Balance" is the Nebulars' formal name, a specific
    organ or sect within them, or simply the sponsoring entity's registered title. Pontifex Shine
    is named once and never appears.

## Relationship to the Reavers

When Carl meets Warlord Fangs at the Faction Wars preproduction meeting, Mordecai's briefing
reveals a shared origin: the Reavers "supposedly were once related to the Nebulars," and "like the
nebs, they were originally several different biological races who enhanced themselves with
machines, though these guys took it a step further and kept only their brains."

The two have ended up as opposites in every way that matters — the Reavers are a megacorporation
and system government that plays Faction Wars to win the trophy, the Nebulars a religion that plays
to end the game.

## Public standing

Nobody with money takes them seriously, and everybody with money keeps having to deal with them.
Quasar's reaction to the Residual trap is representative: "I bet those Nebular fucks were all
jerking each other off while all this went down." The Residuals are an object of Nebular religious
fascination precisely because the galactic-centre AI the Nebulars worship is the same entity the
Residuals call **the Eulogist** — see [Gondii / "Residuals"](../species/gondii-residuals.md).

Carl also sees them as an audience: the Floor 11 arena crowd watching Odette's team is "thousands
and thousands of robe-clad people wearing large hats."
