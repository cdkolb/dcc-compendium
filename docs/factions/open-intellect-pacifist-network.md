# The Open Intellect Pacifist Network

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Full registered name:** Open Intellect Pacifist Network, Intergalactic NFC (a non-profit)
    - **Type:** Media/advocacy non-profit and crawler sponsor — publicly. In fact a conspiracy of
      **former crawlers who want the entire crawl system to fail**
    - **Sponsors:** Carl, from Book 5 onward
    - **Known members:** [Rosetta Thagra](../characters/rosetta-thagra.md) (a Crest, former crawler,
      cookbook contributor, host of *Shadow Boxer*); **Tipid**, a Crest and former crawler;
      **"Milk"** and **Porthus**, both cookbook contributors; **Doctor Hu**, the strategist who
      recruited them
    - **Owns:** *Shadow Boxer*, a weekly interview programme about Earth
    - **Book 6 outcome:** The Residual trap fails. Assets frozen, members declared fugitives,
      network scattered — and in the epilogue, aboard the freighter *Homecoming Queen*

## Overview

The Open Intellect Pacifist Network reaches Book 6 as the least interesting of Carl's sponsors: a
non-profit that funds documentaries and owns a talk show. It leaves Book 6 as the organization that
tried to get the galaxy's failsafe pulled with Carl standing in the blast radius.

Carl's first real suspicion comes from the cookbook. *Shadow Boxer*'s host, Rosetta Thagra, is a
former crawler whose cookbook notes are full of explosives recipes. Digging further, Carl finds
that at least one and possibly three of the network's people are former owners of
[The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook](../items/the-dungeon-anarchists-cookbook.md) — Rosetta, "Milk,"
and probably a third:

> "now that I had definitive proof that my sponsors, the Open Intellect Pacifist Network, employed
> at least one, likely two, possibly *three* former cookbook owners, then whatever it was they were
> attempting to get me to do was of crucial importance."

The clue itself is a cookbook in-joke. During Carl's *Shadow Boxer* interview, Rosetta steers the
conversation to the stew he used to cook at the boys' home — **milk and potatoes** — which points
Carl at a passage in the cookbook where Milk had written about root vegetables and Rosetta had
replied. That is how he works out what to do with the toraline root he'd been sent.

## What they actually are

Liaison Orren states it plainly after the trap collapses:

> "The Open Intellect Pacifist Network is comprised of former crawlers who want to see the crawl
> fail."

Their sponsorship of Carl was never charity or fandom. It was the delivery mechanism for a plan
they had been building toward for a long time.

## The Residual trap

The scheme: use a sponsor box to send Carl a flyer that would put him, in person, within earshot of
a [Residual](../species/gondii-residuals.md) reciting its daily speech during a memory-based floor.
The network gambled that whatever the Residual said would be catastrophic enough to force one of
two outcomes — and either one ends the crawl:

1. The showrunners yank Carl out of the memory mid-speech. That would enrage the system AI badly
   enough, in the network's estimation, that it would kill everyone.
2. The showrunners *don't* yank him, the information gets out, and the **Syndicate Council**
   immediately pulls the **failsafe** — detonating this solar system's star.

Orren, in Carl's own words:

> "In other words, they were willing to sacrifice you and the entire planet. They were using you,
> and they've been using you this whole time."

It failed on the first move. Orren had anticipated a yank and pre-negotiated the scenario with the
AI, which allowed the feed to keep running to head off conspiracy theories. And what the Residual
was actually saying, per Orren, was "long-debunked folklore about the collapse of the inner system."

> "I'm quite certain your former sponsors didn't actually know what he was going to say, and they'd
> gambled everything on it being something profound. They gambled, and they lost."

The collateral damage lands on the Residuals rather than the network: the AI, annoyed at having
been misled too, strips its protections from every remaining Residual on the floor.

See [Gondii / "Residuals"](../species/gondii-residuals.md) for the speech itself, and
[Game Mechanics](../mechanics/index.md) for the Failsafe and the Residual mechanic.

## Collapse

Carl notices the network's people vanishing before he understands why — "I couldn't stop thinking
about the disappearance of the members of the Open Intellect Pacifist Network." After the trap:

> "Now they are fugitives, their assets frozen, and their lives ruined."

Carl's sponsorship spot is frozen, and the Syndicate is left undecided about the network's
representative still participating in the Ascendency game. The network manages one last act first:
an **Emergency Gold Benefactor Box**, delivered and seized by liaison action, then forcibly
un-seized by the AI under "Syndicate rule 855.C. AKA the 'Don't be annoying little bitches' rule."

## Epilogue: the *Homecoming Queen*

The final scene of the book puts two of them in the cockpit of a converted garbage freighter in
Earth orbit — **Tipid**, a Crest and former crawler, and **Rosetta**, wearing a scrambler to hide
from security sweepers. Tipid paid to convert the ship (or rather, as Rosetta points out, he
didn't — someone else did) and also paid for the Celestial box they just sent Princess Donut.

Doctor Hu recruited them both, and made them one promise: *"I can only promise two things. Eventual
death. And eventual justice."* Hu also predicted, correctly, that Carl would get the Faction Wars
safety protections turned off, and predicted the Syndicate Council's response to it.

The ship is cleared for on-boarding in the book's last page. Its cargo is fifty thousand former
crawlers, now troopers for the Princess Posse. See
[Long Haul Biological Waste Management Solutions](long-haul-biological-waste-management.md).

!!! warning "Unresolved"
    Book 6 names **Doctor Hu** only in the epilogue and **Porthus** only as a cookbook contributor
    ("Note added by Crawler Porthus, Second Edition"). The text of this book never states that they
    are the same person, and Carl never counts his "possibly three" cookbook owners by name. Treat
    any Porthus = Dr. P. Hu identification as unconfirmed by Book 6 itself.

    **Resolved in Book 7.** Carl's narration states it outright: "Dr Hu. **Dr Porthus Hu.** Author of
    the second edition of the cookbook. The first crawler to receive the item."

## Book 7: Dr. Porthus Hu and the two organizations

Book 7 gives the network a documented history. Porthus founded the larger, legitimate
[Crawler Project](crawler-project.md) first, and then "In addition to the Crawler Project, Dr. Hu
formed the Open Intellect Pacifist Network, which was a smaller, more focused group whose purpose
was to end the crawl all together. **This organization did not have nearly as much support.**"

Carl's Floor 8 meeting with Paulie the residual was "just one of several actions that had caused
authorities to finally start to crack down on the non-profit." By Book 7 both organizations have
scattered under a terrorism accusation, and even Tipid doesn't know where Porthus is.

### Reach inside the Syndicate

The network's real asset is bureaucratic infiltration, not weapons. Two examples:

- **Volteeg's reclassification.** Volteeg entered the dungeon as a pet, not a crawler. "Someone
  working for the Pacifist Network, someone imbedded deep, deep in the bureaucratic quagmire of the
  Syndicate had changed the code on that designation to make it appear as if he was something else.
  They thought he was a former indentured, like the bopcas and so many others. Not a crawler. Just
  another refugee" — which is why he was never dragged into the Bone Clan's conscript pool.
- **An agent driving a goddess.** "The Open Intellect's agent, **Rectrix**, was driving Theia" in
  the Celestial Ascendency — Samantha's own mother in dungeon lore, which the Posse exploits during
  Operation: Snake Pit.
- **Baroness Victory.** Rosetta notes the arrangement with the Posse's adjutant "had been brokered
  between Porthus and Victory long before Victory was even chosen as an adjutant," and that Victory's
  motivations in helping the network remain a mystery even to her. "Once again, he seemed to be a few
  steps ahead of everybody else."

### The *Homecoming Queen* is armed

The refurbished Crest garbage freighter carrying the 50,000 former crawlers turns out to be the most
dangerous ship in Earth orbit, on Porthus's orders:

> "Homecoming Queen did have armament, however. A lot of it. Though that was a secret, and York had
> worked hard to keep it that way."
>
> "And not only was the ship well-armed, but it had **a full complement of enhancement zone guns and
> beams**. Highly regulated, high-energy weapons that would normally only work in the center system.
> **Porthus had ordered them installed.** These weapons were now green across the board, making
> Homecoming Queen **likely the strongest ship in orbit**. Even the Syndicate security corvettes
> didn't bother with the enhancement zone weapons."

She also carries **Cronus-class power armor**, breaching suits and enhancement-zone suits. Boomer,
docking at the end of the book: "Set aside four of the Cronus suits and a landing skiff. We have a
pregnant veteran on the surface who needs backup. And for the rest of us, **it's time to bring the
war out of the dungeon.** Better warm up the lasers."

It is also the ship that destroyed the orc royal yacht. **York** fired an NPB point-defence weapon
at "the small yacht... the one filled with children, including the new king of the orcs," on a side
mission Rosetta had commissioned, then let the blame fall on general crossfire. The deaths of those
children are what makes Baroness Victory eligible for political office.

### Renamed: the Open Intellect Action Network

In the epilogue, a pre-recorded broadcast that Rosetta directed goes out on the news feeds:

> "On the screen, Porthus was announcing the formation of a new militant group. He was warning that
> they would now be moving against **all Syndicate governmental properties and systems across the
> known universe.** The government had until the end of the current crawl to agree to their demands,
> which basically boiled down to, **'stop the crawl or else.'**"

York's own end-card confirms the new name:

> **Current status:** Captain in the newly coined resistance organization, **The Open Intellect
> Action Network**.

## Book 8: Total System Domination in a Day

Book 8 confirms the militant turn promised at the end of Book 7 actually happens, and faster and
more completely than Porthus's own broadcast threat implied. Production assistant **Lexis** briefs
Carl on it mid-floor:

!!! quote "Lexis, Book 8"
    "The Open Intellect Action Network... You know, those people you won Faction Wars with? Those
    insurgents. They sent a boarding crew to pretty much every major ship in orbit and took all of
    them. It happened very fast. That *Homecoming Queen* is decked out in enhancement-zone weaponry
    that actually works now that the AI has escaped containment. It's the equivalent of a fully
    armored ogre fighting against blind toddlers. They achieved total system domination in a day."

The seizure is enabled directly by the escaped system AI: enhancement-zone weapons, normally
regulated to near-uselessness outside the dungeon's own zones, "actually work" now that containment
has failed, turning the *Homecoming Queen*'s already-substantial armament (see [Book 7](#the-homecoming-queen-is-armed)
above) into a decisive, one-sided advantage against every other ship in Earth orbit — including, per
Lexis, the Syndicate's own security corvettes. The result leaves Borant itself short-staffed and
scrambling: "Zev is now juggling pretty much everything on her own," production duties are picked up
by loaned staff like Lexis, and the Syndicate's own personnel, including Prime Minister Victory, are
displaced to a temporary headquarters on Earth's surface rather than operating from orbit.

This is the same broadcast-adjacent chaos backdrop against which Carl makes his own galaxy-shaking
disclosure live on *Plenty of Plenty* — see [Carl](../characters/carl.md#live-on-plenty-of-plenty)
for that scene, which is a separate incident from the ship seizure but occurs in the same period of
general systemic collapse.
