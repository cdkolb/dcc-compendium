# The Dark Hive

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Private, for-profit mantis-run hunting/entertainment corporation
    - **Distinct from:** The Burrowers, the actual mantis system government
    - **Owner:** [Circe Took](../characters/circe-took.md), a minor hive queen who also owns an
      amusement park
    - **Notable hunters:** [Vrah](../characters/vrah.md) (heir), Xindy (her sister, killed on Floor 6's
      first day), a male merchant/accountant named Edict

## Overview

The Dark Hive is not the mantis species' government — that's the separate, much more
conservative **Burrowers** — but a private corporation, run by Circe Took's family, that fields
professional hunter teams for the *Dungeon Crawler World* hunt and also operates an interstellar
amusement park. Circe's daughters (all warriors) and at least one son (a non-combatant merchant
who handles trading and logistics) make up its Floor 6 hunting roster: seven warrior-daughters
plus Vrah as heir-apparent, alongside a male representative, Edict, who stays behind in Zockau to
buy and resell looted gear.

Dark Hive hunters answer only to their own chain of command and are widely feared by the floor's
other hunter groups — a few seasons before Book 5, Vrah legally killed a rival hunter in a dispute,
and "since then, people just do what the Dark Hive says." They also poach kills from other hunting
groups by swooping in from above, a habit resented across the hunter population but tolerated out
of fear.

## Mantis Mating and Honor Culture

Mantis coupling customs carry real legal and social weight: all pairings must be registered ahead
of time with a trial and psychological counseling for the male, and unauthorized coupling between
rival hives (such as the government-employed Burrowers and the for-profit Dark Hive) is treated as
a serious act of dishonor. During mating, the female decapitates the male, who survives briefly on
a secondary abdominal brain. This custom becomes a direct plot weapon in Book 5: Carl deliberately
infects Vrah with a venereal disease (the **Arrow of Enthusiastic Double
Gonorrhea** — see [Items](../items/arrow-of-enthusiastic-double-gonorrhea.md)) specifically
because the only cure is either self-sterilization (disqualifying her from becoming hive queen) or
passing it on through mating — and no eligible, honor-compliant partner exists for her on the
floor.

Edict, the Dark Hive's own merchant, is caught between this rule and Vrah's demand that he cure
her: coupling with her would dishonor his own hive (contracted to the Burrowers), refusing would
bring the Dark Hive's wrath down on his family, and simply waiting her out risks his own death by
association. He resolves it the only way his culture allows — provoking his own honor-death by
attacking Carl three times inside a saferoom, triggering an automatic ejection-and-death penalty —
dumping all his collected loot on the ground as he goes.

## Book 5's Climax

Vrah's mother, Circe Took, ultimately breaks mantis reproductive taboo herself, using her
sponsorship of the goddess [Diwata](../mechanics/index.md#the-apito-pantheon-book-5) to
temporarily assume male mantis form, mate with (and cure) her own daughter, then return to female
form and begin giving birth to a swarm of mantis nymphs — simultaneously Vrah's children and
siblings. This floods the [High Elf Castle](../locations/high-elf-castle.md) with hostile nymphs
during the [Butcher's Masquerade](../mechanics/index.md#the-butchers-masquerade-book-5), in a
last-ditch attempt to force the peace seal to break.

By the book's climax, every one of Circe's warrior-daughters is dead, including Vrah (killed by
Mongo and Kiwi after Carl marks her with the Ring of Divine Suffering) and Circe herself (torn
apart by her own newborn nymphs once Donut strips Diwata's divine protection with *Laundry Day*).
