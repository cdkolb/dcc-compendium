# Nebular Sin Patrol

!!! tip "Parent organization"
    The Sin Patrol is the hunter wing of a much larger religious civilization. Book 6 finally
    explains what that civilization wants — see [The Nebulars / Nebular Balance](nebulars.md).

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Nomadic, mechanically/bio-augmented religious cult (not a single species)
    - **Floor 6 role:** One of several hunting groups; claimed [Prepotente](../characters/prepotente.md)
      and [Miriam Dom](../characters/miriam-dom.md) as targets
    - **Doctrine:** "The Ballad of Survival" — nonviolence and anti-scarcity preaching, contradicted
      by its own hunting practices

## Overview

The Nebular Sin Patrol are not a distinct alien species but a religious order drawn from many
species at once — soothers, humans, elves, and others — identifiable mainly by their full-body
robes, tubes, and tall, distinctive hats. They worship a "space oddity" deity said to sit at the
center of the inner system, and live nomadically, settling only empty or barely-habitable worlds
just long enough to raise a generation of bioengineered children before half the community moves
on to a new world.

Their central religious tenets, quoted from their own hymn, the **Ballad of Survival**, sound
pacifistic: *"Violence breeds chaos. From chaos we were born and into chaos we will succumb."*
In practice, their young men — priests-in-training — are sent to hunt crawlers as a literal
crusade, believing anyone born on a "seeded" world (i.e., every crawler) is an abomination. The
Sin Patrol is one of the few hunter organizations that reliably fields a team on every crawl,
spending enormous sums (including deity sponsorships) to do so, and is actively campaigning for
the right to run an entire season of *Dungeon Crawler World* itself one day.

## The Burrower Feud

The Nebular Sin Patrol has a long-standing grudge against the mantis system, stemming from an
incident where nebular settlers squatted uninvited in territory belonging to the **Burrowers**
(the mantis government, distinct from the private [Dark Hive](dark-hive.md)) — a dispute that
several hunters conflate with the unrelated Dark Hive when discussing it. This old feud resurfaces
on Floor 6: once [Vrah](../characters/vrah.md) is publicly humiliated and the Dark Hive's grip on
"who's allowed to hunt whom" starts slipping, the Sin Patrol's priests openly move against targets
the Dark Hive had claimed for itself, including attacking [Lucia Mar](../characters/lucia-mar.md)
despite her being a Dark Hive-claimed target.

## Wiped Out Before the Masquerade

The Sin Patrol's feud with the Dark Hive comes to a head earlier than the Butcher's Masquerade
itself: when Carl teleports back into the ambushed village of Alucarda (mid-book, well before the
party), roughly ten robed Sin Patrol priests and a handful of Dark Hive mantis warriors are already
there together, having moved in on Lucia Mar at the same time. Carl's trap-triggered "Ballroom
Blitz" song inflicts an *Unsettled* debuff on the mantises, and in the resulting chaos — compounded
by Carl's own hob-lobber and marked kills — the two hunter factions turn on each other outright. By
the time the dust settles, every one of the Sin Patrol priests present is dead, along with all but
one of the mantis warriors (who then hunts Carl and Donut down personally). No Sin Patrol priests
are shown at the Butcher's Masquerade itself; the hunter side of that final battle is instead
overrun by Vrah's Dark Hive kin and a flood of Circe Took/Diwata's newborn mantis nymphs.

## Book 6: the parent body resurfaces

The Sin Patrol itself doesn't field a Floor 8 team, but the Nebulars do not go away. They sponsor
and patron [Sister Ines Quiteria](../characters/sister-ines-quiteria.md) through her descent, and
separately sponsor the goddess **Ysalte** via a cleric called **Pontifex Shine of the Nebular
Balance**. Mordecai also reveals that the Faction Wars team **the Reavers** were once related to
the Nebulars — the same "augment yourself with machines" tradition, taken to the point of keeping
only the brain. Full detail on all of this is on the parent page,
[The Nebulars / Nebular Balance](nebulars.md).
