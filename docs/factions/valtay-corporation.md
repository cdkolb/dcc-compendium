# The Valtay Corporation

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Corporate system government; brain-parasite Intellect Hunters inhabiting host
      bodies. Slogan: "Keeping the Best of You Alive"
    - **Position entering Book 6:** Holds 51% of the Borant Corporation (won in Book 5) and is
      running the next crawl as well
    - **Book 6 posture:** Publicly **steps back from day-to-day production** after the Floor 7
      disaster, to protect investor confidence
    - **Their "solution" to the quarantine (epilogue):** An engineered **extinction event** —
      **over 150,000** mercenaries and private armies distributed across the eight sponsored
      Faction Wars teams, with the explicit goal of killing every crawler
    - **Priority targets:** "the two warlords, Carl and the cat"

## Stepping back

Zev explains the Valtay's Floor 8 stance off the record:

> "the Valtay have taken a step back after the Prepotente incident and are distancing themselves
> from the production's day-to-day. They need investor confidence for the next crawl, which they're
> also running. That seventh floor was Cascadia's baby, but they pushed to make it happen, and when
> it got ruined, they had themselves what you humans call a come to Jesus moment. A big one,
> especially after how the sixth floor ended."

The logic is purely financial. The Borant Corporation "is going to be liquified after the crawl no
matter what." If the Valtay step back now, they can point to the bottom line at the end and say
*we made this profit despite how terribly the mudskippers ran this season.* Carl and Donut are
earning more than usual — but at the expense of several sponsors rather than the production, which
is what makes the numbers unusual.

Princess Formidable's read from orbit is that Borant is already a shell: "The vestiges of the
Borant Corporation that continued to pretend to run the day-to-day operations were nothing but
theater, remaining in their ocean headquarters because like everyone else, they too were trapped
until the crawl concluded."

## The Valtay solution

At the emergency Syndicate Council session in the epilogue, the Valtay representative — "the
assistant to the Vice President of Operations," a worm inhabiting a Gleener body that was once
mother to Prime Minister Glory — argues against pulling the failsafe on the grounds that everything
is fine:

> "The crawl council and the liaison guild is in constant contact with the AI. It is being showy
> and bombastic, yes, but they assure me that the communication lines remain open and that nothing
> out of the ordinary is happening. This happens every season."

What the Valtay are actually proposing is stated a few pages later, from Princess Formidable's
point of view:

> "The Valtay solution was a dangerous one. They believed the best resolution to the problem was to
> create an extinction event as quickly as possible. Once all the crawlers were dead, the quarantine
> would be lifted."

Before the system went primal this would have been simple. Now it cannot be done wholesale — the
AI won't allow it — so it has to be laundered through the game itself:

> "The AI had agreed to allow tens of thousands of the fully-armed mercenaries to enter the Faction
> Wars battle, as long as they were spread equally around the eight sponsored teams. Them plus the
> private armies and security details of all the participants. People they wouldn't normally allow
> anywhere near the dungeon. Formidable didn't know what the total number of mercenaries was, but
> it was well over 150,000. More than enough to completely wipe out both the crawlers and the NPC
> rebels.
>
> Their goal was singular. Kill all the crawlers. Kill them as soon as possible, especially the two
> warlords, Carl and the cat."

This is the same mercenary pool the Valtay had been using on Earth's surface to guard their assets
against the humans attacking the kinder facility — "not just the gnolls, but the other, less savory
mercenaries as well." All of them are recalled, and the planet's surface is abandoned.

## Formidable's suspicion

Princess Formidable does not believe the AI is being outmanoeuvred:

> "With all its weird emphasis on fairness, there was no way the AI would just let this happen. It
> *had* to be a trap of some sort."

She notes the AI is actively pushing back against attempts to get the crawlers to kill each other,
that it "wanted the game to continue" and "was enjoying itself," and that it permitted the NPC
escape storyline to run. Her conclusion is that letting the Valtay proposal through is a stall or a
setup — which is why she demands the failsafe be pulled *now*, and is muted for it. See
[The Syndicate Council](syndicate-council.md).
