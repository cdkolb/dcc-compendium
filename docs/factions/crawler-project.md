# The Crawler Project

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Non-profit advocacy organization for released former crawlers
    - **Founder:** **Doctor Porthus Hu** — author of the second edition of the *Dungeon Anarchist's
      Cookbook* and "the first crawler to receive the item"
    - **Relationship to the OIPN:** The Crawler Project came first and is the larger, more
      legitimate body; Hu founded
      [the Open Intellect Pacifist Network](open-intellect-pacifist-network.md) later as a smaller,
      more radical offshoot
    - **Signal achievement:** automatic Syndicate citizenship, free healthcare and guaranteed
      housing for released crawlers
    - **Status at the start of Book 7:** scattered, after the authorities declared it a terrorist
      organization

## The reveal

Book 7 finally connects two names the series had been carrying separately. Tipid, telling the story
of his life after release, mentions "Doctor Hu of the Crawler Project, an NFC organization dedicated
to helping former crawlers navigate the universe after release" — and Carl's narration lands the
point in four words:

> Dr Hu. **Dr Porthus Hu.** Author of the second edition of the cookbook. The first crawler to
> receive the item.

Porthus, Carl's own sponsor's frontman, is the founder of both organizations.

## What it won

The Crawler Project is the *respectable* arm, and it worked:

> "Dr. Hu's work had been instrumental in helping to create multiple laws that would protect
> crawlers upon release. **Because of the Crawler Project's campaigning, all released crawlers were
> now automatically citizens of the Syndicate and given access to the inner system primary zone,
> which came with free healthcare and guaranteed housing.** When Tipid was released, he wasn't
> afforded any of that."

Tipid is the counter-example that motivated the whole thing: released with no citizenship, no
healthcare, and crushing debt from the surgery that kept him alive.

**Mistress Tiatha**, drunk as usual, provides the sceptical footnote:

> "That citizenship perk is only for new crawlers... And they'll still bleed you dry on taxes. Us
> old guard are gonna get extra fucked when we're released. They tell us we'll be 'full citizens,'
> but I don't believe it. The rich folk sure like getting their faces up on the screen when they're
> donating to some charity, but the moment a scruffy, former crawler shows up in their home system
> looking to settle down, they'll fight like hell to prevent it. Mark my words."

## The split, and the crackdown

> "In addition to the Crawler Project, Dr. Hu formed the Open Intellect Pacifist Network, which was
> a smaller, more focused group whose purpose was to end the crawl all together. **This organization
> did not have nearly as much support.**"

The OIPN's methods eventually poison both. Carl's own Floor 8 meeting with **Paulie the residual**
in the Florida shelter "had been just one of several actions that had caused authorities to finally
start to crack down on the non-profit," and by Book 7 the members have scattered under a terrorism
accusation. Tipid, asked where Hu is: "We all had to scatter when they accused us of being a
terrorist organization. I don't know where he is."

Rosetta is unbothered: "Oh, I hope they try, warlord. But now that we're in the game, there is
nothing they can do. They'll wait until we get out to try to arrest us."

## The *Homecoming Queen*

The link from advocacy to armed intervention runs through one practical suggestion. After Tipid
settled in a Crest colony working for a waste-management company, "it was Doctor Hu who had
suggested the idea of **refurbishing one of the garbage freighters into a passenger liner**. He put
out a call to former crawlers who wanted to get back into the game to help us with Faction Wars, and
he'd been overwhelmed by the response."

That freighter is the *Homecoming Queen*, and the response is the **50,000 former crawlers** who
enlist in the [Princess Posse army](../dungeon-factions/princess-posse-army.md). Carl:

> "They'd come to help fight their former captors. Even when I managed to get the protections turned
> off, they still came."

!!! note "'NFC'"
    The text reads "an NFC organization." Elsewhere in the same passage it is called "the
    non-profit," so this is almost certainly a typo for NFP. Quoted here as printed.
