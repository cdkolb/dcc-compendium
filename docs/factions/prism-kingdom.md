# The Prism Kingdom

!!! info "Quick facts"
    - **Type:** Small but influential trade-focused kingdom; ruling race Saccathian
    - **Ruler:** **Empress D'Nadia** (Princess D'Nadia until her father's retirement) — see
      [Princess D'Nadia](../characters/princess-dnadia.md)
    - **Faction Wars:** Fields the **Prism Kingdom Clan** from seat five, one of the two spots
      vacated at the end of Book 5
    - **Book 6 turn:** Sells Donut's sponsorship; burns all three of her Emergency Action Items
      trying to delete the Larracos NPCs, bar crawlers from Faction Wars, and delete Carl and Donut
      outright. **All three are rejected by the system.**

## Before Book 6

The Prism Kingdom enters the series as one of the good ones. D'Nadia publicly condemns the Skull
Empire's yacht strike after it kills her friend Manasa; she sponsors Donut from Book 3 on; she
judges the Butcher's Masquerade pet and talent contests in person and is genuinely, visibly fond of
Donut. When the Dreadnought Clan and the Burrowers both withdraw from Faction Wars at the end of
Book 5, she claims one of the two open spots.

Buying that spot is what breaks the relationship. Carl works it out immediately: "she'd likely
overextended herself getting that Faction Wars spot."

## Book 6: the heel turn

**The reunion.** At the fifth Faction Wars preproduction meeting, D'Nadia is still all warmth —
waving at Donut across the table, complimenting her Masquerade performance, inviting the others out
to eat, laughing at Donut's jokes at Prince Stalwart's expense. "Hi Donut!"

**The sponsorship sale.** Mid-battle, Donut receives a notification that D'Nadia has sold her
sponsorship to [Long Haul Biological Waste Management Solutions](long-haul-biological-waste-management.md).
Donut takes it hard — "I'm not a trading card to be collected and discarded, Carl" — and Carl,
who'd been expecting it for a while, gives her the diplomatic version: "she does like you, but it's
not really appropriate for her to sponsor you when we'll be fighting her." Privately he notes it
must have made her a lot of money.

**The three Emergency Action Items.** The real turn surfaces during Odette's interview, when she
pulls up three items Carl and Donut had never been shown:

> **Emergency Action item proposed by Warlord Empress D'Nadia of the Prism: delete all existing
> NPCs on Larracos level to maintain game integrity for Faction Wars.**
> ***Item rejected by system.***
>
> **Emergency Action item proposed by Warlord Empress D'Nadia of the Prism: Disallow crawlers from
> participating in Faction Wars in any capacity.**
> ***Item rejected by system.***
>
> **Emergency Action item proposed by Warlord Empress D'Nadia of the Prism: Remove the Princess
> Posse from Faction Wars and/or delete Crawlers Carl and Princess Donut.**
> ***Item rejected by system.***

Donut's reaction, live on camera: "That bitch! I thought she was my friend! She can't do that! We
didn't even see these!... She tried to delete us!" Followed, characteristically, by: "I'm starting
to think maybe she really did vote against Mongo in the pet show!"

## Why it matters mechanically

Odette uses the moment to teach Carl the rule that shapes the rest of the book. Emergency Action
Items are **not** put to a vote — the system decides them — and **each team only gets three.**
D'Nadia has now spent all three of hers and got nothing. Every other team had already burned theirs
on the Larracos flood, on purchasing a team, or on the elf castle landing in the Lemig Sortion's
battlefield.

Carl and Donut go into Faction Wars with all three of theirs unspent, and Carl spends one in the
book's closing pages to hand the remaining action-item votes to the audience — which is how the
[Home Team](faction-wars-teams.md) and the removal of sponsor safety protections both pass. In
other words, D'Nadia's heel turn is directly responsible for Carl learning the mechanic that
guts her side of the game.

## Faction Wars standing

The Prism Kingdom is notably the **only** non-crawler team that does *not* try to quit. When the
quarantine rules bite, the Operatic Collective, the Blood Sultanate, the Dream, the Lemig Sortion,
the Madness and the Reavers all abdicate and are all refused. The Prism Kingdom stays in. See
[The Faction Wars Teams](faction-wars-teams.md).
