    Princess Donut¶

    Quick facts

    • Full title: GC, BWR, NW Princess Donut the Queen Anne Chonk (self-insists on "Princess Donut," or "Princess" in polite company)
    • Crawler title (Book 1): Grand Champion Best in Dungeon
    • Type: Cat Crawler
    • Race: Cat (tortoiseshell/Persian mix)
    • Class: Not yet assigned (Book 1) → Former Child Actor (Book 2) — a rare Earth Class requiring the "Cut!" achievement and 1 trillion+ views — plus, from Book 7, a second permanent class, The Oak Fell, awarded for casting three Atrocity-tier spells on one floor and stacked on top of Former Child Actor rather than replacing it; Mordecai and Rosetta have never seen a class granted that way
    • Subclass: Not applicable — instead has the Character Actor skill, letting her re-pick a temporary "floor specialty" class each floor (picked Artist Alley Mogul on Floor 3; a Hooligan specialty on Floor 4; Glass Cannon on Floor 5; Legendary Diva — a Bard floor-specialty — on Floor 6, unlocking a whole singing-based spell kit; Master Telephone Psychic — a psionic specialty — on Floor 8, granting greatly improved mapping, +5 Intelligence, and three charm/debuff spells; Deathbed Hellcat — a long-range sniper/assassin specialty — on Floor 9, quadrupling the range of every spell she has, raising her Dexterity and instakill odds, granting invisibility at will, and coming with a ghillie suit and boonie hat)
    • Title (Book 7): Champion of Nekhebit — awarded alongside The Oak Fell by the goddess Nekhebit, currently driven by Odette. She need not worship Nekhebit but may worship no deity outside her court, enemies of Nekhebit collect a bounty for killing her, and per Rosetta it is "not too unlike Conscription, but with a lot more autonomy" — the goddess can order her to act or smite her
    • Level: 26 (end of Book 2) → 33 (end of Book 3) → 39 (end of Book 4) → 55 (end of Book 5) → 63 (end of Book 6) → 135 (Book 7, last level stated in the text) — enters Book 6 ranked #2, having just lost the top spot to Prepotente. In Book 7 she is level 65 entering the Nest tower, 74 after hours of sniping, and jumps to 135 the moment her third Atrocity lands, making her the highest-levelled crawler in the dungeon and, per Mordecai, "the most powerful crawler in the history of this game"
    • Stats (Book 7): base Charisma over 200 (well over 300 with gear, though not at base); base Intelligence and Strength both over 100. Natural benefits taken: Brain Trust (Intelligence), Muscle Mommy (Strength — also adds 25% of her Strength to Mongo's), Wholesale Discount (Charisma), and Monologue, a fear-based charisma skill that pays experience in proportion to how terrified the enemy is, received when her boosted stats topped 300
    • Party: The Royal Court of Princess Donut (party leader — highest combined stats) — briefly designated Squad Leader as Floor 7 begins; loses the leader slot to Carl in Book 7 ch. 27 and reclaims it in ch. 77
    • Buff: Enhanced Growth — gains +1 Strength, +1 Intelligence, +2 Charisma automatically every level, with no free stat points to distribute; Charisma passes 100 during Book 3, and reaches base 138 (temporarily doubled to 276+ by a benefactor tiara) by Book 5
    • Faction Wars: Co-Warlord (with Carl) of The Princess Posse, sponsored by The Society for the Eradication of Cocker Spaniels — the fan-funded advocacy group Donut has been quietly building since Book 4 buys a Floor 9 Faction Wars seat at the end of Book 5. The Princess Posse wins Faction Wars at the end of Book 7
    • Succession status: Formerly enrolled in the Blood Sultanate's line of succession via the (now-destroyed) Crown of the Sepsis Whore — Book 5 confirms losing the physical crown never lifted the obligation; the crown itself resurfaces and is forced onto Katia's head at the very end of the book
    • Notable spells/abilities: Magic Missile, Fireball (Book 5, item-granted), Torch (Book 5: reaches level 14+, strong enough to ignite vampires), Second Chance, Puddle Jumper, Heal Critter, Clockwork Triplicate, Minion Army (uncastable), Hole (Book 5: used to decapitate the Rottweiler Cici; also cast through the ceiling connecting the attendant ballroom to the main hunters' stage during Mongo's pet-show number, letting extra "girlfriend" raptors briefly appear on stage from the room below — confirmed by Donut's own after-the-fact explanation to Tserendolgor), Wall of Fire, Twinkle Toes, Standing Ovation, Entourage, All Eyes on Me (all Book 5, Legendary Diva songs), Laundry Day (Book 5, a separate floor-spellbook gift, not part of the Diva kit), Slice Attack/Back Claw (melee), Night Vision, Cat-like Reflexes, 9 Lives (cat racial benefits), Love Vampire (Book 5: finally works at scale, charming multiple enemy elves at once during the final battle), Deflection, Brain Freeze, Bad Attitude and Why Are You Hitting Yourself? (all Book 6, Master Telephone Psychic), Legionnaires of the Damned and Mute (Book 6, tomes), Bitch, What? (Book 6, item-granted berserking spell), and a level-10 Hover skill (Book 6, tiara-granted); Book 7 adds the three Atrocity-tier spells Rolling Battle Formation, You're Not Done Yet and War Crime (the last castable only three times, ever, and only on Scolopendra levels), plus Flak, Shield and Sentry from floor and quest boxes, Bijanbi (tattoo-granted, from the god Wepwawet), Astral Paw, the druidic Oak Fell deity spells Raise Forest, Gather Ye, Vermin and a water-to-salt-water spell, and a Claw racial skill trained from level 4 toward 15 by her champion shawl — note: the Wisp Armor tome, once considered for her, ends up read and cast by Carl instead
    • Notable items: a Belt of Buoyancy; a cat-sized ghillie suit and boonie hat (Book 7, Deathbed Hellcat kit — Blend into the Background, Cone of Silence, Targeting); celestial knee pads passed on by Katia (Book 7, a 500% Eileithyia damage bonus against enemies); Enchanted Anklet of the Fallen Oak; Enchanted Collar Charm of the Effete Bourgeoisie (Book 5, tracks conquered settlements, matching Carl's necklace); ~~Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore~~ (destroyed in Book 2, obligation persists); Enchanted Fae Scale Quadruped Crupper of the Fleet; Enchanted Fur Brush of the Ecclesiastic; Enchanted Mongoliensis Saddle; Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Superior Fire Demon's Hand Maiden (Book 6, fire immunity and Bitch, What?); Enchanted Obsidian Bracelet of the Raggle Rouser (Book 5, grants Fireball); Enchanted Silver Bracelet of the Ab-solar (Book 5, anti-vampirism); Enchanted Tiara of Mana Genita; Enchanted Tiara of the Inebriated Dragonfly (Book 6, grants Hover); her champion shawl (Book 7, trains Claw); Prism Industries Capacitating and Focusing Goggles; a Quadruped weapon mount — "The scorpion's bite" (Book 7, a non-magical bandolier from the Apothecary, used to fire Katia's crossbow off her own back); ~~Talisman of the Slate Butterfly~~ (Book 5: retired, replaced as her collar-charm slot by the leadership charm below); The Tiara of a Thousand Lights and Accompanying Charge Pillow (Book 7, unique — 99 absorption jewels that permanently drain any enchanted hat's buffs into the tiara; +10 Charisma on its own)
    • Sponsors: Princess D'Nadia (Books 2–6, until she sells the contract mid-Book 6) → Long Haul Biological Waste Management Solutions (Book 6 — revealed in the epilogue to be a front for the freighter Homecoming Queen)

    Background¶

    Before the collapse, Donut was a champion show cat owned by Carl's ex-girlfriend Beatrice, with a room in their apartment devoted to her competition trophies and ribbons. She was with Carl (not Beatrice) when the dungeon opened because Beatrice was traveling and Carl had been asked to cat-sit. Donut initially enters the dungeon classified as a pet, not a full crawler — a status that changes when Carl force-feeds her an Enhanced Pet Biscuit from a Legendary Pet Box.

    The Transformation¶

    The enhanced biscuit dissolves Donut into a puddle of goo for several minutes before she reforms — physically almost unchanged, but now able to speak and reason at a human level. Her stats before the transformation were all 1 (except Dexterity at 8, Charisma at 5); the biscuit boosted four random stats and applied the permanent Enhanced Growth buff. Post-transformation stats climb fast — by the tuskling "Ball of Swine" fight she's already at Strength 18, Intelligence in the 20s, and a Charisma so high (37–43 through most of Book 1) that she can casually charm sapient mobs (goblins, fairies) into compliance, provided their aggro is already neutral.

    Once she can speak, Donut immediately renames the party The Royal Court of Princess Donut, appoints herself Grand Champion Best in Dungeon, and demotes Carl from "manservant" to Royal Bodyguard.

    Personality¶

    Vain, status-obsessed, and initially dismissive of Carl, Donut nonetheless refuses to abandon him when he tries to split up early on, admitting she doesn't want to be alone and that she'd noticed him quietly looking at apartments that allowed cats (i.e., planning to keep her) before the world ended. Over Book 1 she becomes a genuinely loyal partner — dragging Carl to safety twice after near-fatal injuries — while never losing the diva affect, especially once cameras get involved.

    She develops a visible addiction to audience attention after her first talk-show appearance (on Dungeon Crawler After Hours with Odette), obsessively tracking her Views/Followers/Favorites counts, to the point Carl has to repeatedly talk her down from playing for the camera during actual combat.

    Combat Role¶

    Donut is the party's ranged/magic damage dealer, though she's reluctant to use her claws (calling melee "inelegant") until circumstances force her to (see Quick Facts above for her spell/ability list). Her physical stats eventually let her one-shot low-level mobs with her claws, but her Constitution stays crippingly low (as low as 2, raised to 4 with gear) because Enhanced Growth never lets her allocate points there — making her fast and hard-hitting but fragile.

    The Crown of the Sepsis Whore (see Quick Facts) is worth calling out specifically: a Legendary, cursed tiara she puts on without reading the full warning. It's a Fleeting + Unique item that permanently enrolls her in the royal line of succession for the Blood Sultanate on Floor 9, meaning she (and by extension Carl) will eventually be required to kill the Sultan and the entire royal family to descend past that floor.

    Relationships¶

    • Carl — bodyguard, "manservant," and, increasingly, genuine found family
    • Mordecai — mutual affection; he's forbidden from telling her much but clearly likes her
    • Zev — the pair bond quickly over shared taste in Earth television (Gossip Girl, Riverdale)
    • Mongo — adopts a baby Mongoliensis (a velociraptor, per Odette) from a Floor 2 pet reward room and spends the rest of the book getting bitten on the nose while training him
    • "Ferdinand" — repeatedly mentioned by Donut as someone she loves, described dramatically as a forbidden lover; revealed live on air in Book 2 to actually be Gravy Boat, a neighborhood tomcat belonging to a woman named Marjory back on Earth
    • Katia — a new party member recruited in Book 2 (see Katia)

    Book 2: Race and Class¶

    On Floor 3, Donut refuses every race option and stays Cat — but confirming the choice (rather than leaving it a non-choice) unlocks new racial benefits: Night Vision, Slash Attack, Cat-like Reflexes, and 9 Lives (halves damage from her first nine physical hits per day).

    For class, she's shown only 34 options (the fewest Mordecai has ever seen, due to her Desperado Pass and her Crown) and picks Former Child Actor — a rare Charisma/Chance Earth Class built around the Character Actor skill: each floor, she re-rolls a temporary "specialty" class for that floor only, with the AI randomly determining how many of that specialty's skills/spells she actually receives. Her hidden Manager benefit (see Carl for how it was obtained) makes Mordecai personally accompany the party instead of being guildhall-bound.

    Book 2 Notable Events¶

    • Kidnapped and placed under a magical sleep (Water Lily) by the Elite Tsarina Signet as leverage to force Carl into a fight
    • Learns Clockwork Triplicate, a spell that creates two temporary clockwork duplicates of a bonded pet/minion
    • Insists on pursuing NPC GumGum's "Sex Workers" quest after GumGum is killed, reasoning "she died because of us" — pushes Carl to keep going with the investigation multiple times when he's ready to drop it
    • Loses the Crown of the Sepsis Whore — forced to remove it during the Doomsday Scenario wild-magic event to avoid a worse stat penalty; being Fleeting, it disintegrates the instant it comes off (the succession curse it created remains regardless, per that item's own page)
    • Appears alongside Carl and Hekla on Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton; publicly reveals, live on air, that her long-mentioned love "Ferdinand" was actually a neighborhood tomcat named Gravy Boat
    • Welcomes Katia into the party as a new permanent member, insisting on protecting her during the Doomsday Scenario's evacuation even at cost to their own safety
    • Ends Floor 3 ranked 7th on the leaderboard (Level 26), just behind Carl

    Book 3 Notable Events¶

    • Receives the Prism Industries Capacitating and Focusing Goggles from Princess D'Nadia — their hidden vision mode lets her spot the two invisible "Dirty Little Phased Bolts" Hekla fires at Katia, exposing the plot that gets Hekla killed
    • Reaches 100 Charisma and unlocks Love Vampire, a passive that reflects damage back onto lower-level attackers; kept carefully unused for most of the floor since the party avoids letting her get hit
    • Casts Second Chance on Hekla's corpse mid-battle, then Clockwork Triplicates the reanimated result into three bodies to hold off the rest of Brynhild's Daughters while Carl and Katia loot the remains — reuses the same combo later on exploded Krakaren clone corpses
    • Picks up a Hooligan floor specialty via Character Actor, gaining minor Guinness and Mascot skills for the duration of Floor 4
    • Champions collecting and freely redistributing roughly 700 escape-portal "train hats" to stranded crawlers rather than selling them, over her own vocal objections about the lost gold — ends up gifted dozens back in gratitude by the survivors she helped
    • Publicly reveals, on Odette's show, that she was present for — and inadvertently starred in — a new viral "snick" mocking the resurrected Maestro, continuing Carl's Book 1 humiliation arc
    • Grows close to Katia as a full-time party member, repeatedly insisting on protecting her (and covering for her) through the trauma of killing Hekla
    • Ends Floor 4 at Level 33, having spun the wheel that sends the party into the Air Quadrant of Floor 5, against the Dirigible Gnome Wasteland Fortress

    Book 4 Notable Events¶

    • Picks Glass Cannon as her Floor 5 specialty on Mordecai's advice — the buff proves genuine, visibly strengthening every spell she casts for the floor, though she never receives the specialty's usual Constitution bonus
    • Cast as the "healer" in Carl's gnome-hostage con, using a fake-corpse Second Chance resurrection of a dead dirigible gnome to bluff a Wasteland ambassador into ferrying the party aboard his balloon
    • Becomes the unwilling face of Veriluxx RealPet Companions, a sponsor selling a cheap, repeatedly-exploding "Princess Donut the Queen Anne Chonk" toy alongside a bootlegged Garfield personality; goes on the toy's infomercial anyway, where Carl's needling about the sponsor's slavery-linked parent company (Veritan Linkage) triggers the toy's rigged detonation and kills admin Loita
    • Loses her PR contact Zev for a second time when Loita takes over, then celebrates her return after Loita's death; develops a visible, Carl-noted addiction to her newly-purchased social media board
    • Portals Chris Andrews to safety rather than let Carl mercy-kill him while possessed, overriding Carl's judgment in the moment ("You're not a killer, Carl")
    • Casts Meat Hooks to lure the celestial-tier puppy Orthrus out of bubble 543 and to safety, then helps heal him mid-air-battle against Quan Ch and the god Emberus
    • Ends the book newly obsessed with hiring "staff" for the personal space, having personally championed keeping the possessed doll head Samantha as a hired trainer

    Book 5 Notable Events¶

    • Picks Legendary Diva (Bard) as her Floor 6 specialty, gaining a starting kit of five songs — the party-support Standing Ovation, Entourage, and Encore (a heal-party/cleanse spell), plus two offensive numbers she never gets much use out of — and separately picks up Laundry Day as a bonus regular spell from her floor's spellbook club (not part of the Diva kit itself). She's blocked from casting any bard song reliably until she trains her singing enough to hold pitch "in key," a running joke and a real mechanical bottleneck for most of the floor. All Eyes on Me is the name given to one of her Diva songs only when she finally performs it at the Masquerade
    • Tames the pack-leader velociraptor Kiwi using a sponsor-supplied Charm Mongoliensis potion brewed from a full liter of Mongo's own blood, gaining Kiwi's entire raptor pack as loyal (if unpredictable) minions for the back half of the floor, and later magically charms Queen Imogen's cat familiar Ferdinand into betraying his mistress at the Butcher's Masquerade using a second charm potion that the group's planning notes say requires "a liter of cat blood" — with Donut, as the only cat on hand, the implied "ready source" (she jokingly balks at being drained a second time, "I don't have a liter of blood in me. I am very petite," and the book never shows the actual collection on-page, so treat the exact blood source as strongly implied rather than explicitly confirmed)
    • Wins Point Mongo's conquest outright by pushing its bear mayor into a naiad-infested river, then renames and redecorates the town over Carl's objections; separately conquers a second and third settlement (Ferdinand Peak, Nipton)
    • Fights and nearly loses to child crawler Lucia Mar solo while Carl is pulled away to a mandatory intergalactic fan convention — decapitates Cici, one of Lucia's two rottweilers, using Hole, and out-maneuvers Lucia with an Entourage illusion stack before escaping (her Cockroach passive doesn't trigger here; it does trigger twice later in the book — once when she throws herself in front of a lightning bolt meant for Mongo during a roadside encounter with Queen Imogen's familiar Ferdinand, and again during the Butcher's Masquerade's final battle when a mass debuff wave zeroes out her health)
    • Publicly, coldly rejects her original owner Beatrice, who reappears alive on Odette's talk show — delivering the emotional gut-punch of the book ("You're not my person anymore. Carl is. He's always been.") before privately admitting to Carl she isn't sure why she doesn't feel the grief she expects to over the friends they lose at the Butcher's Masquerade
    • Headlines the Masquerade's talent competition, singing her mother's (Beatrice's) old drunken lullaby, "All Eyes on Me," to unknowingly charm the entire ballroom — including Queen Imogen — moments before Signet breaks the peace seal
    • Cures Kiwi of Scolopendra's centuries-old Transmutation curse using a sponsor-gifted "I Take It All Back" potion mid-battle (a Prepotente benefactor-box item passed to Donut), turning the velociraptor back into her original Ursine form on the spot; Kiwi was never a vampire — the floor's vampirism plague had already been cured earlier when Miriam Dom died
    • Ends the book as Co-Warlord of the Princess Posse in Floor 9's Faction Wars, alongside Carl

    Book 6 Notable Events¶

    Floor 8 is Donut's most competent book. She starts it at level 56/57 and ends it at 63, and she spends the whole of it as squad leader — which on this floor means deckmaster, the only person who can hold, draw, play or flee with T'Ghee cards. Carl is legally a minion for the duration of every card battle.

    New class: Master Telephone Psychic¶

    Her Character Actor floor specialty. Mordecai offers her a choice between Agony Urchin (safer) and Master Telephone Psychic (better mapping, offensive charm spells); she refuses the Urchin on sight because it would change how she looks — "I HAVE AN IMPORTANT IMAGE TO MAINTAIN."

    What the psionic kit gives her:

    • Enormously improved mapping — "IT'S ALMOST LIKE WE ALREADY HAVE THE MAP FROM A NEIGHBORHOOD BOSS… IT'S EVEN BETTER THAN THAT PATHFINDER SKILL"
    • +5 Intelligence
    • Brain Freeze — single-target, drains health and lowers resistances, and blinds at level 10 or above. It starts at level 8.
    • Bad Attitude — per Mordecai, "similar to Carl's Fear spell, but it'll be a lot more potent in your hands… Fear tends to affect dexterity while Bad Attitude drains intelligence and lowers resistances"
    • Why Are You Hitting Yourself? — "IT MAKES PEOPLE STAB THEMSELVES IN THE FACE!" She eventually lands it on a level-250 demon prince: I CAST WHY ARE YOU HITTING YOURSELF ON SATAN!

    The class grants no fire spells — her fire magic (Fireball, Magic Missile, Wall of Fire) is all gear-granted and is separately amplified by her new nipple ring. She also keeps her Viper Queen venom spit (level 3) and her Bard songs, minus a couple she's forgotten.

    No system text

    Master Telephone Psychic has no printed class-description block, and none of the three spells has a printed spell block. Everything known about them comes from dialogue.

    Deckmaster¶

    Donut builds an eighteen-card deck off crowdsourced chat math (roughly an 80% chance of pulling a totem in the opening spread) and learns to run it well. Her final active deck is six totems — Shi Maria, Jola the Yule Cat (a gift from Katia), Lazarus A-Bang-Bang, Skylar Spinach, Geraldo the monk seal and Raul the crab — plus utility, snare and mystic cards.

    The real skill turns out to be personnel management: refereeing Raul against Geraldo, shouting the orisha Asojano into line, coaching Raul out of grovelling, and negotiating with Shi Maria, who demands to see her hand and offers tactical advice. Carl deliberately stays out of it — "I was trying not to be a backseat deckmaster. When the real fights started, I wouldn't be able to sit there and help her."

    Her best plays include stealing a summon out of an enemy deckmaster's hand mid-cast with a Thief card, and permanently fusing Lazarus with Skylar Spinach using a Golden Combo followed by the Glow-Up consumable Tserendolgor gave them — building the best possible single card, since only one may be carried down to Floor 9.

    She does not enjoy it. "I don't like being in charge of the deck. You're usually in charge of the important stuff."

    The Golden Skull¶

    Carl brings Quan Ch down, but Quan dies a beat before the finishing blow lands and the system credits the kill — plus the bounty and both exit keys — to Donut. She gets her first golden player-killer skull for it.

    Donut sported a new, golden player killer skull. She hadn't commented on it, and neither did I. That felt important, the nonchalance of it all. We were losing more of ourselves every day.

    Losing D'Nadia, Gaining the Garbage Truck People¶

    Mid-card-battle, Donut gets a notification that Empress D'Nadia — now warlord of the Prism Kingdom Clan, and therefore an opposing Faction Wars faction — has sold her sponsorship contract to someone else.

    "I'm not a trading card to be collected and discarded, Carl." … "Carl, I thought she liked me."

    Carl's read is that it was partly conflict of interest and mostly money: "Selling Donut's sponsorship had to have made her a lot of money. I'd actually been expecting this for a while now." D'Nadia subsequently files emergency action items to delete the Princess Posse from Faction Wars, and to delete Carl and Donut.

    The replacement sponsor is Long Haul Biological Waste Management Solutions.

    MY NEW SPONSOR IS NAMED LONG HAUL BIOLOGICAL WASTE MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS, AND I JUST CAN'T WITH THAT. I KNOW I'M SUPPOSED TO APPEAR GRATEFUL, BUT THAT SOUNDS ABSOLUTELY DISGUSTING. CARL, ARE THEY GOING TO SHOW COMMERCIALS FOR GARBAGE TRUCKS ON MY FEED? THIS IS NOT ACCEPTABLE.

    Long Haul are the galaxy's corpse-disposal contractors — they normally don't arrive until after the last crawler has died. The epilogue reveals what the sponsorship actually is: their freighter Homecoming Queen, parked in Earth orbit, is crewed by Rosetta Thagra and Tipid and loaded with fifty thousand former crawlers who have just been cleared to enter the dungeon as troopers for the Princess Posse.

    In the last minute before the floor collapses, she gets her first celestial box from them. It is still unopened when the book ends.

    "It's a celestial box! My first celestial box, and it's from the garbage truck company? The posse is going to be scandalized."

    Ripping Shi Maria's Card¶

    After Shi Maria hijacks Carl's body, burns Mongo's wing off and tattoos herself into Carl's chest, Donut ends the argument the moment the battle does:

    "I ripped her card," Donut said after a moment. "Shi Maria. As soon as the battle ended, I ripped it. She's not going to hurt Mongo ever again."

    It doesn't work the way she hoped. The escort quest to deliver Shi Maria to Larracos survives the rip, and so does Shi Maria — in Carl. Donut also holds, and at Carl's insistence rips, Paz Lo's card. She does not get a skull for that one.

    Other Book 6 Notes¶

    • She picks Cuba as their Floor 8 region, against advice, on the grounds that it's near the Bahamas and Miss Beatrice. The entire floor follows from that choice.
    • New tiara: the Enchanted Tiara of the Inebriated Dragonfly — a blank silver tiara plus a 25,000-gold flawless citrine — granting a 50% potion cooldown reduction, +10 Intelligence, poison protection, negation of the inebriation dexterity penalty, and a level-10 Hover skill. "I can fly now. Carl, I can fly! I'm like Wonder Woman!" / "Wonder Woman can't fly." / "She has an invisible jet, Carl. Don't ruin this for me." She never gets to use Hover in a real fight.
    • New nipple ring: the Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Superior Fire Demon's Hand Maiden — cursed-item removal, immunity to natural fire and lava, no health loss inside Sheol, fire spells scaling with Intelligence, and a once-daily berserking spell, Bitch, What?, which Mordecai tells her to only ever cast alone.
    • New spells: Legionnaires of the Damned and Mute, in addition to the psychic kit.
    • Mongo reaches level 40, is tripled by Clockwork Triplicate in almost every fight, and has his right wing burned off by Carl's body under Shi Maria's control. A potion Carl upgraded will regrow it.
    • She hears Carl's whole childhood for the first time on Shadow Boxer, and the most striking thing she does with it is decide not to push.

    Book 7 Notable Events¶

    Floor 9 — Faction Wars, fought over the funnel city of Larracos — is Donut's book. She enters it as Co-Warlord of the Princess Posse and the party's ranged damage dealer, and leaves it as the highest-levelled crawler in the dungeon, holding two classes, a goddess's title, and the only spell in the game the system files under Atrocity. It costs her more than any previous floor, and the book is unusually direct about the cost.

    Level endpoints

    She finished Book 6 at 63. The text's first Book 7 figure is 65, the level at which she climbs into the Nest tower around Chapter 70. Hours of sniping take her to 74 by Chapter 74. Then her third Atrocity, in Chapter 76, jumps her straight to 135 — "Her level had grown to 135." That is the last level stated for her in the book, and Mordecai's read is blunt: "I think you're now the most powerful crawler in the history of this game."

    New class: Deathbed Hellcat¶

    Her Character Actor floor specialty for Floor 9, picked off-page while Carl is distracted. It turns her from an artillery piece into a sniper:

    "Great, isn't it? It's a long-range sniper and assassin class. It doesn't come with many new spells, but the range on all my existing spells is quadrupled, and my dexterity is much higher. The odds of one of my spells inflicting an instakill is higher, too! Oh, oh, I can go invisible at will, and to top it off, I kept most of my spells from the last floor! But best of all, it comes with a hat! And a ghoulie suit! I don't know what that means, but I see a saferoom around the corner, and I must insist we get there straight away so I can collect my accessories. But isn't the name of the class absolute perfection? It's downright terrifying, and I love it."

    Carl has to break the news that it is a ghillie suit, which she refuses to wear on sight — "It's like one of Signet's tattoo people fell through a paper shredder. If I put that on, I'll look like a tassel. You know how I feel about tassels, Carl." The kit is a cat-sized colour-changing ghillie suit (jacket slot; Blend into the Background, Cone of Silence) and a boonie hat (Targeting, plus further range). Worn together the bonuses compound, and Carl notes she could "snipe line-of-sight enemies from well over a mile away."

    No system class block

    Like Master Telephone Psychic before it, Deathbed Hellcat never gets a printed class description. Everything above is Donut's own dialogue and Carl's examination of the gear.

    The Tiara of a Thousand Lights¶

    Her first celestial benefactor box from Long Haul Biological Waste Management Solutions — opened in Chapter 4, carried over unopened from the end of Book 6 — delivers a spaceship that folds into a mechanical hand holding a pillow and a tiara.

    The Tiara of a Thousand Lights and Accompanying Charge Pillow.

    This particular tiara is imbued with 99 Absorption Jewels.

    The tiara itself has no inherent magical properties, and in its base state it gives no additional benefits or buffs except plus 10 to Charisma. However, it has the ability to be permanently charged with up to 99 different buff items.

    Any enchanted hat left on the charge pillow for ten hours is permanently drained into the tiara — the hat survives, stripped, for her collection. The system's own assessment: "With some careful planning, one can turn this tiara into one of the most powerful items in the history of Dungeon Crawler World." The first thing Mordecai has her drain into it is the Deathbed Hellcat boonie hat, which is what makes the rest of the floor possible: with the tiara's targeting she no longer needs a sniper tower at all.

    The Nest, and sniping a city¶

    Because the tiara covers the targeting, the Posse's great tower — the Nest, built on the lip of Shanty Town — is instead tuned to synergise with Atrocity-class spells, amplifying their area of effect across the whole battlefield. Donut works out of the top of it for the back half of the book, invisible in the ghillie suit, dropping war mages and defence towers with Magic Missile from across the funnel.

    It is also where the book stops treating her competence as a joke. Carl finds her mid-shift:

    Only then did Donut look up, and I could see her face. I tried not to react. I could see the hollowness to her eyes, as if they were trying to focus on something so far away, she couldn't quite see it. It'd only been a few hours, but the change was stark.

    She'd been level 65 when she'd gone up into this tower. She was now level 74.

    We gain things, and we lose them.

    Three Atrocities¶

    Over the course of Faction Wars Donut casts three spells the system classifies as Atrocities. None of them are hers by choice; each one is the least-bad option on the table at the time. The mechanics belong on the individual spell pages — what follows is only when and why.

    Rolling Battle Formation (Chapter 65) — with Louis shot down, a feral god loose and thousands of allied and enemy bodies fused into a single writhing pile outside the Madness castle, Donut casts it to roll the entire mass away from the god fight and onto a field of corpses. It sweeps up more than she intended, including the feral god Meatus and the goddess Eris, and holds every creature inside immobile and invulnerable until it breaks. Carl later tells her flatly that it was the right call: "You saved them by casting the Battle Formation spell. If you hadn't done it, half of everyone would be dead by now."

    System Message: Warlord Princess Donut has committed an atrocity.

    Donut: WELL, I NEVER. THAT WAS VERY RUDE.

    You're Not Done Yet (Chapter 69) — a Legendary quest-box spellbook from early in the floor, which Mordecai recognises with genuine fear: "It's similar to Donut's Second Chance, but it's a lot more insidious." Tower-enhanced and cast over a field of thousands of donated corpses, it flays them into fleshers that sail across the battlefield at the Madness army. The AI's commentary elsewhere notes that war mages exist at all as "a result of too many combatants having access to You're Not Done Yet."

    System Message: Warlord Princess Donut has committed another Atrocity. Wow. What a naughty kitty. I sure hope she isn't forced to cast a third.

    War Crime (Chapter 76) — from the Legendary 1914 box awarded for killing Empress D'Nadia. It can only be cast three times ever, only on Scolopendra levels, and it combines two of the caster's own spells into something "most governments would consider unconscionable"; it also drops the caster to 1% health and cannot itself kill, so a finisher is mandatory. Carl reads the full description early and is horrified, mostly at what casting it might do to her. The plan is to use it to separate out the Reaver and Dream armies as the battle-formation ball breaks. Rosetta's case is that without it "we will lose hundreds, if not thousands," and Carl agrees. The combination she fires is Bijanbi and Astral Paw — a kilometre-wide ethereal cat's claw that catches thousands of soldiers and a god and crushes them into a flesher swarm over Shanty Town, to a world-wide soundtrack of Limp Bizkit's "Break Stuff."

    She casts it under protest and mid-panic, with Carl shouting revised instructions at her:

    "Okay, okay," I said. "Cast it, but don't pick fire! Pick something else! We need the Nest to stay intact!"

    "What? What should I pick!?"

    Afterwards she goes rigid, her health draining, and asks: "Did it work... Carl, I don't feel good. I have a new class. I have a bunch of new spells. What's happening?"

    The Oak Fell, and Champion of Nekhebit¶

    The third Atrocity triggers the rest of it, all at once:

    System Message: Warlord Princess Donut has committed a third Atrocity.

    System Message: Warlord Princess Donut has received a new class.

    System Message: Warlord Princess Donut will now be referred to as The Oak Fell.

    System Message: The Oak Fell has been named Champion of Nekhebit.

    The Oak Fell stacks on top of Former Child Actor rather than replacing it, and Mordecai and Rosetta both say they have never seen a class awarded that way. Its spells are deity spells and read as druidic — Raise Forest (trees and plants over a square kilometre per spell level), one that turns water to salt water, and Gather Ye, Vermin, which draws every small mammal within ten kilometres to her. Donut is scandalised by the last one and blames the AI.

    Champion of Nekhebit is the goddess Odette is currently driving. It is presented as a reward and functions as a leash. Donut's own summary:

    "This new Oak Fell class and this champion thing are in addition to my original class. I don't even worship Nekhebit, and it says I don't have to if I don't want to, but I'm not allowed to worship anyone else who's not in her 'court.' Both the class and the title come with a bunch of weird spells. ..."

    Rosetta gives Carl the version she takes care Donut does not hear:

    "It's similar to worshiping the deity. Enemies of Nekhebit will receive a bounty if they kill her." She'd lowered her voice, making certain Donut wasn't in earshot. "Honestly, it's not too unlike Conscription, but with a lot more autonomy for Donut. Should the goddess want her to do something, she can order her to do it or face a smite."

    Milk's theory is that the class is "a side effect of some posturing that's currently happening on the 12th," and that the Champion title in particular "was something that was set in motion by Nekhebit." Rosetta guesses at the mechanism: "Probably something along the lines, 'the first crawler to cast three atrocities on the next floor gets the title and class.'" Carl's reaction is two words: Fucking Odette. Donut's objection is administrative: she spends the next several chapters in warlord chat as The Champion of Nekhebit, unable to change her display name back — "ALSO HOW CAN I CHANGE MY NAME BACK TO WARLORD DONUT? IT DOESN'T DO THIS IN THE REGULAR CHAT."

    Breaking the stat ceiling¶

    The same moment hands her four benefit choices at once, and she takes them all in a single burst of chat while Mordecai tries to slow her down:

    Donut: GOODNESS, I'M NOT GOING TO PICK THE SWOLE BENEFIT. DO YOU REMEMBER WHAT IT DID TO CARL? CAN YOU IMAGINE? I'M GOING TO PICK MUSCLE MOMMY FOR STRENGTH SO MONGO IS STRONGER, AND FOR INTELLIGENCE I PICK BRAIN TRUST SO I CAN MAKE MAGIC MISSILE EVEN MORE POWERFUL. I ALSO HAVE ONE TO PICK FOR MY CHARISMA TOPPING 200. I'M GOING TO PICK ONE CALLED WHOLESALE DISCOUNT.

    Mordecai: Uh, are you sure about that Muscle Mommy benefit?

    Donut: TOO LATE. I PICKED IT. WOW! HAVE YOU SEEN MY CHARISMA?

    • Base Intelligence and Strength both cross 100 — Brain Trust and Muscle Mommy, the latter adding 25% of Donut's strength to Mongo's.
    • Base Charisma crosses 200 — Wholesale Discount, everything at cost in the store.
    • Mordecai on the result: "I think the Bopcas will be worshiping you again. Your charm and Love Vampire skills are stronger than that of most gods."

    A fourth natural benefit, Monologue, arrives separately and on different terms — Carl describes it as "one of several benefits crawlers received when their boosted stats topped 300." It is a charisma-based skill that terrifies the enemy and pays her experience in proportion to how frightened they are. She debuts it while circling Huanxin Jinx in Chapter 86.

    Her own verdict on the whole package, delivered while insisting Mongo and Rend be kept caged away from the gore: "As the strongest crawler in the dungeon I have to maintain a sense of decorum."

    The dog on her flank¶

    To make War Crime work, Rosetta needs Donut to carry a second spell at level 8 or higher within minutes, which means a tattoo. Donut refuses, loudly and repeatedly — "DONUT: ROSETTA AND EDGAR ARE TRYING TO DEFILE MY ASS" — and Carl calls it off rather than force her. Then she tells him the real objection:

    "Carl," Donut said, looking up at me. "It's okay... You're right. It'll work. It's just... I hoped none of them would be on the outside."

    "What do you mean?"

    "The scars."

    Carl's answer is that a chosen mark is not a scar, "it will be a badge of honor," and that she can still say no. She agrees on one condition — "I'll only do it if you also get a matching one. A matching ass tattoo" — and Carl accepts.

    Edgar the tortoise inks it on her shaved right flank, holding the poker in his mouth, using an ink he has never tried before, refusing to say what he is drawing until the last second ("A dog."). Milk then writes Words of Power over it in two colours of calligraphic ink to force the spell temporarily up to level 15. The result:

    It was a simple, rather adorable line art tattoo of a dog. As I watched, the tattoo did, indeed, move. It lifted its leg and started to lick itself.

    The spell is Bijanbi — a wide-area variant of Dinner Bell, extracted from a fallen cleric, which drags enemies toward a designated point. It comes from a god called Wepwawet, which delights her for entirely the wrong reason: "Carl, Carl, it says he's in the 'Court of Nekhebit!' and I can worship Odette now, too!" She also picks up two achievements for it, one calling her a traitor and one awarding her a stolen catalytic converter, and immediately announces she is getting it lasered off on the next floor.

    Killing Empress D'Nadia¶

    Her former sponsor, now warlord of the Prism, dies in Chapter 26 at Donut's direction. Carl has marked her and is on the edge of doing it himself in a state he has never been in before; Donut talks him back over chat ("No, Carl. Please, no. I need you to step back."), then takes the kill herself, in character, out loud:

    "Empress, Darling," Donut said. "Mongo would like to express his utmost disappointment at how you judged him during the pet show."

    The system credits it to the raptor:

    System Message: Warlord D'Nadia of the Prism has fallen. Credit for the kill has been given to pet Mongo of the Princess Posse.

    System Message: The Prism has been defeated.

    It also earns Carl the Gavrilo Princip achievement and the Legendary 1914 box that War Crime comes out of — so D'Nadia's death is, indirectly, the origin of the spell that reshapes the rest of the floor.

    Killing Huanxin Jinx¶

    Chapter 86, with eight minutes left before Emberus destroys the floor. Huanxin Jinx, CEO of Icon Industries, has arrived driving the goddess Eileithyia to collect Katia. Donut has arranged for her to be standing on a floor inscribed with words of power, and the moment Huanxin is distracted she is ripped out of the goddess's soul armour and dumped, shielded and furious, into the circle. Eileithyia herself is frozen until Katia makes her choice.

    Donut circles her, weaving Monologue, strips her shields with a Claw skill she has trained from level 4 toward 15 ("All cats have it, naturally"), and then introduces the last guest.

    The chain of custody on the kill is precise, and every link matters:

    1. The mount is a Quadruped weapon mount — "The scorpion's bite," a non-magical bandolier from a gold benefactor box sent by the Apothecary. It allows ranged weapons to be mounted on her back and fired and loaded by inventory action.
    2. The weapon is the Enchanted Repeating Crossbow of the Scavenger Mother of Mothers — Katia's crossbow, formed onto the mount, "bigger than Donut" and unloaded. Donut notes it is underpowered for her because "I don't have any women in my party."
    3. The bolt is Mordecai's. Plain, unenchanted, and tipped with his mother's ashes:

    "No, I can't. Not directly," Mordecai said. He held something up. It was a simple crossbow bolt. There was nothing magical about it. "You see this?" He flicked the tip. "My brother died because of you and because of Odette and because of Chaco. But mostly because of you. Some of my mother's ashes are built into the tip of this bolt. It's a funeral rite in my culture. To make a weapon with the ashes of those you lost. My mother died before the crawl came to my world, and I'm grateful for the opportunity to allow her to avenge her son."

    Mordecai — a non-combatant NPC who cannot touch her — loads the bolt into the crossbow on Donut's back and walks out of the tent. Donut finishes her sentence and fires:

    "We don't really need to go through all this. But we want to. We want to show those of you on the 12th floor what's coming. You're strong, yes. But we are getting stronger every day. And you know what? We don't need to kill the gods and goddesses to win. No, honey..."

    The crossbow on her back fired, and the bolt slammed right between the multiple sets of eyes of the alien.

    Huaxin Jinx, CEO of Icon industries rocketed backwards, slid across the floor, and came to a rest at Victory's feet, dead.

    "...We just need to kill you."

    She undercuts the whole thing herself a chapter later, which is the most Donut thing in the book:

    "I feel really bad about pooping in Mordecai's mother's ashes now. Do you think any of it got into the tip thing? I'm never going to not think of that now when I think of Huanxin. It was supposed to be this big, grand gesture, but she was really killed by a cat poop arrow."

    "They are not going to break me."¶

    Donut ends Book 7 having lost Katia to the twelfth floor, lost the sponsor who sold her, and spent hours doing things she will not be able to un-know. Carl watches her cover it — "I could tell she was struggling, but the fact she was actively covering it was such a relief" — and at the very last moment, walking to the stairwell as Emberus burns Larracos off the floor, asks her directly:

    "You doing okay?" I asked Donut as we moved to the stairwell.

    "I'm going to miss her," she said. "But you know what, Carl?"

    "What's that?"

    "They are not going to break me. No matter how hard they try."

    I reached up, and I gave her a pat.

    Together, we walked into the stairwell, leaving the now-empty battlefield of Larracos behind.

    It is deliberately the same sentence Carl has been saying to himself since Floor 1, turned outward and made plural.

    • Party leader: Carl takes the role from her in Chapter 27 on base stats and she pretends to be furious about it; she takes it straight back in Chapter 77 — "At least I'm back in my rightful spot as leader of the party."
    • New gear: the Tiara of a Thousand Lights and its charge pillow; the Deathbed Hellcat ghillie suit and boonie hat; a cloak she insists on calling her "champion shawl," which has been quietly training her Claw skill; celestial knee pads passed to her by Katia, carrying an Eileithyia 500% damage bonus against enemies; and the Apothecary's quadruped weapon mount.

    Book 8 Notable Events¶

    Claw Breaks the Level-15 Cap¶

    Early on Floor 10, Rosetta fits Donut for claw caps to keep her from accidentally shredding furniture, and casually suggests she check her Claw skill again. It has passed the previous level-15 ceiling entirely:

    "My goodness," Donut said a moment later. "It's at level 17! 17! I thought it topped out at 15!"

    Mordecai's read is that a divine-tier item is behind it: "It's from the gods, and when it comes to gods, all bets are off... you've gone from a ranged powerhouse to one of the most powerful melee fighters in the game's history. Or you can use your Astral Paw spell to cut through almost anything." (The full mechanics of the upgrade belong on an Abilities-pass page once one exists for Claw; this page notes only that the jump happens and when.)

    Astral Paw, Twice More¶

    The upgraded claw gets two standout demonstrations later in the book. In Heat 7, three Russian crawlers (the "Tracksuit Troika") try to double-cross an escape pact and make a run for a portal in a shielded Cadillac; once the shield drops, Donut ends it in one line:

    Donut cast Astral Paw. She smashed the car where it stood, crushing it like a tin can. The Cadillac simply ceased to exist.

    Moments later in the same heat, the drunk, grieving unicorn Dwight — armed with a missile launcher and screaming threats — loses his head "in a single swipe of Donut's claw" the instant the race begins, before he can fire a shot. See Supporting cast for Dwight and Lucienne.

    Killing Mitch¶

    During the Heat 7 Desperado Club ambush, the shadow mimic posing as the casino's croupier ("Mitch") is frozen solid by Carl's Eye of the Bedlam Bride while Carl is transformed into a dog; Donut lands the actual kill with repeated Magic Missile casts while he's immobile. See Carl for the full scene.

    Facing Down Chandra¶

    When Princess Chandra, Esq. — Donut's newly assigned attorney — presents her with a conservatorship claim arguing Donut's species "was not yet recognized as a legally competent racial entity," Donut dismantles the argument without raising her voice, turns a matching set of Carl's father's own court documents back on her, and drives her out of the guild's safe room in a panic:

    "They paint a very distinct picture of who Carl's father was as a person. Much the same way these papers paint a picture of who you are, Princess Chandra... What a sad little snake you are. I pity you."

    See Chandra's own page for the full confrontation and its aftermath.

    The Wrong Biscuit¶

    At the Floor 11 finale, mid-battle, an Eris-triggered inventory scramble scatters every party member's items into everyone else's hotlists. Carl needs Donut to find and combine a specific banger sphere with an enriched pet biscuit meant to turn the boss Scolopendra into a harmless, mindless pet the party can carry in inventory as a fail-safe. In the chaos, she hands him the wrong one — the same kind of "Nothing Special" sapience-granting biscuit already used on Penelope (see Penelope) — and only admits it after the fact:

    "So, with the inventory. It was happening so fast, I made a mistake. Like, a really, really bad one."

    Instead of a mindless pet, the transformed Scolopendra emerges as a fully sapient, flirtatious crawler — "Sexy Scolopendra," Level 1, Crawler #12,953,456 — who promptly wraps herself around Carl: "Hi, Carl." Carl's own reaction makes the stakes plain: "Donut had given me the wrong pet biscuit." The intended fail-safe — carrying a mindless Scolopendra as insurance against the party's own deaths — is gone; what they get instead is a new, willing ally with no obligation to obey them.
