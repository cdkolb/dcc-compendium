    Supporting Cast¶

    Characters who appear with real substance but haven't yet earned a full page. See the Characters overview for the promotion rule that moves someone from here to a full page.

    Name Role Notes
    150-Soldier AK-47 Group Broadcast-only crawler group Never given individual names/leader; shown moonwalking through the dungeon well ahead of schedule, then reduced to melee and improvised weapons after running out of ammunition
    Admiral Corinne Whimpleshin Dromedarian camel; adjutant for the Blood Sultanate (Book 7) A single-humped camel in a sundress holding "an honest-to-goodness martini," and a little heavyset for a camel. Teleports into the middle of Carl's Operation Snake Pit raid on the naga castle mid-slaughter, spraying her drink, shouting "I am calling foul! I am calling for an end to this immediately!" Like Baroness Victory, she is not frozen by the effect that has stopped everyone else in the room
    Agatha Homeless woman; revealed in Book 6 to be Residual Agent number 22 Survives events she shouldn't; carries unexplained high-tech items (Valtay Corporation weapon/shield/comms) later found in her cart. Still alive and pushing her cart on Floor 4 (Book 3); briefly glimpsed on Floor 5 (Book 4), now Level 12. Book 6's epilogue is her POV and explains everything: she is a member of a rare, ancient species that "peeled away from the collective" ten years before the collapse, wearing a human shell she took by hunting down and killing one of the Apothecary's own residuals — which is how she has access to both feeds. She spent those ten years in Wenatchee, Washington, and liked it. At the emergency collapse order she is in the alley behind the Meadowlark Elder Care facility, planning to light a barrel fire at exactly the right moment to draw staff and police outside so a dungeon entrance will generate near them — i.e. she is why the Meadowlark crawlers got in at all. She was fond of Brandon, who shared his lunch with her. Her faction's goal is recruiting the system AI; her private view is that "this entire universe would be swept clean of biological life". Book 7: Zev relays a direct order from Prime Minister Glory that Carl is not to interact with her. She is then named co-warlord of the War Mage Rebellion alongside Akuma, who plainly does not want her there. Fopsy the Dream's adjutant theorises her aim is not to win Faction Wars at all but to end the Celestial Ascendency early — because once the ascendency is finished with no crawlers left, the system's hard-coded shutdown-and-hibernation sequence triggers, an alternative to the failsafe — and that she now believes the local AI is "a threat to the Eulogist." Akuma's own warning to Carl is that she "seeks to kill us all"
    Allister Cookbook author, 13th edition; T'Ghee practitioner (Books 3, 5–7) Contributes recurring marginal notes to The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook on explosives stability, vampire lore, and protection spells, all filtered through his home culture's T'Ghee card-and-meditation tradition — his own T'Ghee deck holds cards like the Plague Bearer and the Open Wound. For him, T'Ghee is a genuine religion, not the card-battle totem game Ren later runs, and Carl leans on that distinction when he first encounters totem combat in Book 6. Confirms Batbilge's death was likely his final entry
    Anaconda Level 75 Quarter Naga, lead dancer at the Penis Parade (Book 6) Brother of Damascus Steel and second son of Astrid and the Night Wyrm, though the brothers "weren't even remotely the same species" — evidence that the family's in-game races aren't their birth races. One of Donut's two favourite dancers. Found weeping beside Damascus when word of their mother's assassination reaches the club, and insists on going with him to confront their father: "If you're going to fight him, I'm going, too… This is family business." Does not join Carl's mercenaries; neither brother is seen again in Book 6
    Anton Lopez Level 47 human crawler, class Fugitive (Book 6) Cousin of Paz Lo, fellow Havana prison guard, and the third member of Sister Ines's Cuban squad. Bald, caped, covered in real prison tattoos including his face; carries a crossbow with a monkey's open mouth on the front; two player-killer skulls, earned killing two of his own party on an early floor for trying to kill Sister Ines. Worships Ogun. Charmed by the goddess Oshun into throwing the Ogun shrine piece out the door, which triggers the smiting of both men; when Carl casts Black Nimbus to pull him out of the god's targeting, Ogun simply steps backward onto his head and crushes it — "Just like that, as if he was nothing"
    Areson the Wise Ogre strongman, one of the three brothers from Grimaldi's circus (Book 6–7) Brother of Herman the Fleet and of Apollon, who was left behind on the third floor; all three were once part of a single three-headed nodling that died and split long ago. Fights on Carl's side through Book 7 and is one of the soldiers teleported in for the assault on the Madness castle
    Arief Level 80 Semeru dwarf; War Leader and commander of the Larracos Defense Force Infantry (Book 7) Team Retribution's senior soldier and Carl's guide to Larracos — he is the one who walks the party down through the city's rings and explains its layout, its defences and the explosives rigged on every other down-ramp. Carries a Despondent warning because his goddess is dead: he cannot worship another deity, all his stats take a 15% hit, and worshippers of other gods do 10% more damage to him. Personally wants revenge on Architect Houston. On NPC mortality: "We believe that for most of us, if one dies, they are dead forever. We are not immortal like the outworlders." Guards the door at Louis and Juice Box's wedding
    Big Tina Allosaurus; daughter of Kiwi, raised in the FUPA (Book 5–7) The "ballerina dinosaur," still carrying her magical self-repairing feather boa and her dead magic wand. Kiwi refuses to let Carl use her in the castle defence — "She's just a child" — and Carl nearly overrules her before deciding it is Kiwi's call; Donut overrules them both, and Tina comes out roaring. Later single-handedly takes out one of Larracos's fire lance towers and shields dozens of crawlers from being burned alive. Inseparable from Simoom the albino rhino, who she treats as a pet
    Bobby, Low Thi, and Morris Sp The Tomb Raiders — a subterranean-quadrant crawler team (Book 4) Bobby D.J. (Spy, trap disarmer), Low Thi (D-Bag Geek), and Morris Sp (Arachnid Freelance Psychiatrist) survive a chaotic, trap-filled slog through the Necropolis of Anser's subterranean quadrant, aided by Katia's hand-drawn maps. All three die completing the quadrant's final task — disconnecting/reconnecting the necropolis's lightning-conduction wiring under fire from the ghost boss Quetzalcoatlus — earning the quadrant's liberation posthumously; teammates Tyler Storm, Mike Barnes 3, and Janice die earlier in the same questline
    Bodi Level 60 Big Game Spear Hunter; veteran of Land War, a different game inflicted on his own home planet (Book 7) Tall, tan-skinned, and almost silent — "He'd said maybe three words since I'd met him, making Chris seem downright chatty." Elected a battalion commander on experience alone, and everyone treats him as someone famous without ever saying why. Carries a boomerang-enhanced spear that returns to his hand. Takes over the 103rd Recon Legionnaires and runs the feint that pulls the Madness defenders off Carl's infiltration route
    Bomo and the Sledge Two of the four cretins (rock monsters) freed on Floor 6; members of Carl and Donut's 15-strong Personal Guard platoon (Book 6–7) In Book 7 they run the Princess Posse's entire missile battery from Shanty Town, firing over 1,500 missiles at the Madness and Reaver castles through Trollish Portable Control Rooms modified to look like the oversized game controllers they already knew how to use. Sledge's contribution to the war effort: "Robots go boom." Bomo is killed in Chapter 70 defending Donut in the lower levels of the Nest tower against Lucia Mar, alongside Toyotomi and the defending mages — his last message, "Will stop her now," is followed by This message is from a deceased mercenary
    Bonnie Young dirigible-gnome NPC, daughter of Commandant Kane (Book 4) About ten years old; witnesses her pet pig Sausage taken hostage and killed, then her father's death (mauled by the boss Denise, whom he'd raised as a replacement pet). Kept calm afterwards only by a mind-altering potion her father gave her before dying. Rescued by Carl and Donut, gifted the family's soul-crystal power source, and adopted into Hump Town's dromedarian/changeling community under Juice Box's care. Book 5: rescued a second time from Floor 6, using her ballista skills against attacking mantises during the Twister's destruction. Book 7: crews the airship Party Planner alongside Louis and Skarn, having swapped her Dallas Cowboys jersey for an oversized 105th Scream Warriors shirt ("We bleed when the job's done"). Carl repeatedly orders both children off the ship and they repeatedly stow away; she is calm and chewing gum while manning the door gun through the dragon attack. Transfers to Floor 12 with Kiwi and the other children
    Brad Bea's affair partner (Book 1, 4) Present at the original Seattle collapse; survives on the surface with Bea for months in Book 4's epilogue, quietly resentful and fantasizing about having entered the dungeon instead. Shot (non-fatally, left to bleed) by Odette's operative Lexis after reaching for a dropped weapon during Bea's capture
    Brother Widespread Tempest Level 125 Ogre Basilica Cleric Guard, Club Vanquisher (Book 7) A worshipper of Taranis standing guard over the temple district during Operation Snake Pit. One line in the system text and nothing more
    Bucket Boy Young crocodilian NPC, former Penis Parade attendant and low-tier healer (Book 6–7) Hired with the rest of the club crew as one of Carl's mercenaries. In Book 7 he is Imani's assistant and runs the Princess Posse's field hospital, and later a clinic by the castle — where Kiwi gives birth. Scales notwithstanding, he visibly pales when Mordecai proposes a Removal Order on Carl: "Like, won't his head explode if it doesn't work right?"
    Burcu Level 59 Porsuk (badger-headed) Swashbuckler, Turkish crawler (Book 6–7) A newcomer at #10 on the leaderboard, same class as Tran and Bautista. One of the three who brawled over Queen Imogen's memorial crystal at the end of Floor 7. Carl knows her only as "very serious, reminding me of Hekla." Her drunk teammate Emir threatens Carl with "My Burcu is going to kill you." Killed in Book 7 on a table in Architect Houston's surgical theatre. Houston cut out her tongue and voice box and tortured her mother to death in front of her; she gets a message to Carl through his Book of Voodoo with a map of the room and its fireball traps. Her last words to him, and the reason she is one of the book's cruellest loose ends: "He tortured my mother. He killed her right in front of me. Free me so I can kill him… You owe me, Carl. You killed my father. Free me now." Carl never learns what she meant about her father. His attempt to carry her out tips the table and sets off the traps; she dies, and he barely escapes
    Calliope Level 130 Cybernetic Bleached Drake; pet of Warlord Fang (Book 7) The gravity-magic dragon that shoots down the Party Planner and then circles the abandoned Reaver castle, ignoring the battle underneath it. Fang is hiding inside her. When Carl detonates the No Disassemble device she spirals out of the sky screaming and explodes, showering the party in blood — and taking her owner with her
    Captain Fresh Syndicate security officer, handler for the assassin Minus (Book 8) Preps Minus for his undercover mission posing as his own dead brother "Linus," supplying five hundred hours of injected memory footage and a cover story built on the real Linus's finances: "If the AI so much as suspects you're anything other than a simple tourist, it's going to pop you like a boil." Never appears again after the briefing
    Cascadia Kua-tin lead engineer and executive producer of Dungeon Crawler World: Earth; the "kill, kill, kill" announcer (Books 1–6) The voice that opens every floor. Book 6 shows her in the flesh for the first time: at the Faction Wars preproduction meeting Carl needles her about Administrator Loita's death, she blinks out — and then reappears physically in the room, splashing water across the table, rebreather strapped over her gills. "I'm really here now, Carl… You want to hurt me? Now's your chance… I fucking dare you. It would be the end of the both of us. I would godsdamned welcome it." She also warns she is "one crisis shy of pulling the failsafe, which would kill us all." The instant the other warlords arrive she snaps on a fake smile and her announcer voice — a change Carl finds "instantaneous and unnerving." Recently divorced. Book 7: she disintegrates in public. Her floor-opening announcement is slurred and "a little too chipper"; by the warlord council her scales are falling out and the ones left have "a dull, sickly sheen," and she drinks tequila out of a softball-sized potion ball through her rebreather while Odette tries to interview her. Princess Vinata supplies the reason, whispering it to Carl: the Borant civil war has killed Cascadia's former husband and most of her family — rebels hired the Valtay against the Bloom, and a derelict warship hit the liquid habitation ring around Borant Three, killing millions. The news is not public, and Vinata will not say how she knows it. Cascadia's last word in the meeting is a laugh with "a hint of madness to it": "Well, why not? Why the fuck not?"
    Chaco Wolf-headed skyfowl/pterolykos Bard, prize-counter attendant (Book 3, 5–6) Mordecai's former party-mate. Book 6's prologue finally explains the Book 3 on-air attack: on the tenth floor, Odette — their manager — coerced Chaco, a worshipper of Dodola, into throwing the Dart of Ophiotaurus into Mordecai's brother Uzzi, force-summoning Dodola into the corpse inside a containment circle so the goddess could be killed and the stairwell opened. He refused at first — "You want me to kill Uzzi? It's not going to happen" — and she bought him with a promise of protection and a lie about the Outreach Guild. He jumped ten levels and took the godkill credit. In Book 5 he runs the Butcher's Masquerade's prize counter and apologizes to Carl again without explaining why. Odette has set aside a trust for him. Book 7: drafted as the warlord council's field correspondent because Magnificent Troy refuses to come down to the surface, and spends the meeting narrating over people's shoulders with a microphone and a lint roller. He asks the room to vote on whether the newly-crowned King Stalwart looks stupid in his crown, and Stalwart — who is physically in the same room — punches him unconscious across the floor. Odette takes over the broadcast in his place: "my very old friend Chaco, who is feeling a little… punched in the face right now"
    Chiyome Razor fox, "Mistress of Nunchaku," Team Wild Hunt (Book 8) Introduced performing a group Size Up scouting spell on Carl and Donut with two silent, flipping ninja-style teammates; relaxes into a genuine tactical ally once alone, drinking beer and explaining the "Split Personality" spell that created Corcunda's two halves. Loses a teammate, Ito, to the Heat 2 kangaroo-demon boss
    Chuluuna and Khulan The last two members of Tserendolgor's squad (Book 6) Human, Mongolian, married, from the same village as Ren. Chuluuna is her tank — a strongman who turns his skin to stone and casts Chain Lightning, and the only one who could stand inside her flamethrower. He is killed by Ren's own Mongolian Death Worm totem after Quan Ch flips totem allegiance mid-card-battle, then eaten and vomited back out by it. Khulan, tiny and weathered, paralyses targets so Ren can burn them; she was teleport-trapped away and arrives after the fight, and will not leave her husband's body. She had also lost a little girl in the collapse. Ren states she will kill herself as soon as she stops crying, dumps her own gear on Carl, and is inside the dome when Ren detonates it — but Khulan's death is never confirmed on the page; only Tserendolgor's name enters the deceased-crawlers folder
    Clarabelle Crocodilian Desperado Club door bouncer (Book 4–5) Runs security at the Desperado Club's entrance; still on good terms with Carl and Donut in Book 5 despite past incidents, and reluctantly reveals the club's secret bathroom stairwell down to the Hunting Grounds level after Donut extorts her over a decade-old refund discrepancy
    Clint, Randy, and Todd Three child NPCs living in the Princess Posse's guild hall (Book 7) Clint was a chee kindergartner on Floor 6 until Scolopendra's nine-tier attack turned his whole class into were-castors; he grew up, fought the high elves for centuries, died, was turned into a tattoo on Tsarina Signet by Edgar, reverted to his pre-attack child form, and became a flesh golem when Signet died — so he is a kindergartner again, permanently. "Edgar says I might never grow up because I'm a flesh golem now." Randy and Todd are orphaned ursine bear cubs whose father was killed by Kiwi before she changed back from a velociraptor; Todd is disconcertingly pious ("We're going to bathe in apostate blood"). All three turn up in metal skull caps with training spears demanding to fight, and are sent back inside. Transfer to Floor 12 at the end
    Commander Stockade Level 76 Phase Sorcerer; warlord of the Lemig / Democratic Sortion (Book 6–7) A seven-foot upright caterpillar who talks like an aged Southern California surfer — the leader whose proxy Luke stood in for him in Book 6. His one gambit is the book's first faction-ending disaster: he uses a level-15 Summon Ally invitation, precisely timed by an illegal outside observer, to teleport Carl to his throne room while Carl is unconscious and infested, intending to strand the Shi Maria spider there to massacre the room. It half-works, the spider gets loose on his side anyway, and it kills half his family and Luke.
