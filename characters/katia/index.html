    Katia¶

    Quick facts

    • Full designation: Crawler #9,077,265, "Katia Grim" — ranked General of the Princess Posse in Book 7; the epilogue of her arc gives her full name as Katia Grimmsdottir
    • Type: Human Crawler (in appearance only — see Race below). No longer a crawler as of Book 7 — reverted to human and ejected from the dungeon (see Status)
    • Race: Doppelganger — can reshape her own body/gear (of equal mass) at will, but must consciously "sculpt" the result herself; her true, resting appearance is black-haired, pale, mid-30s, with light, wide-set eyes
    • Class: Monster Truck Driver, a combined Earth/Staunch Barrier + Jouster "Juggernaut" tank class — massive Constitution that scales further the faster she's moving
    • Subclass: Not yet revealed
    • Level: 9 when introduced, jumping to 21 during the Doomsday Scenario event, then 24 and, after killing Hekla, a single-event jump to 37 — briefly the highest-level member of her own party and 8th on the Floor 4 top-10 leaderboard → 44 by the end of Book 4 → 60 by the end of Book 5 → never stated again in Books 6 or 7
    • Party: Joins The Royal Court of Princess Donut at the end of Book 2, at Hekla's request; remains a party member on paper but operates as leader of her own splinter team (Bautista, Gwen, Tran, Louis, Firas, Florin, Li Jun, Li Na, Zhang) through Books 4–5
    • Relationship: Romantically involved with Bautista as of Book 5; still together, if badly strained, when she leaves in Book 7
    • Succession status: Inherits the reforming Crown of the Sepsis Whore at the very end of Book 5, placed on her head by Eva Sigrid's dying hand — putting her in direct succession conflict with Donut over which of them may descend from Floor 9 to Floor 10. Resolved in Book 7 by the death of Princess Vinata and by Katia removing herself from the succession entirely
    • Status (Book 7): Alive, and out of the dungeon. Eats the Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace in Chapter 84 and chooses the pregnancy boon over the Celestial Attendant post — made human again, fitted with her own cybernetic prosthetics, impregnated with Louis Santiago's son, flagged Ineligible and exported to the Earth-surface "kinder facility" holding roughly 120,000 children and pregnant women. No longer a crawler; Donut is freed to descend
    • Notable spells/abilities: Rush (once-daily active skill; propels her body forward like a battering ram — upgraded in Book 3 to Crowd Blast, a five-minute-cooldown cone attack), Second Fuel Tank (random damage-absorb-and-reflect proc, once per fight), Pathfinder (helps locate stairwells; also a factor in Book 4's submarine expedition), immunity to wind-spell damage, plus her shapeshifting/"sculpting" ability — refined in Book 3 into a full combat form (a tripod "sentinel gun" mount for her looted crossbow) and near-perfect gnoll mimicry, and in Book 4 into a full-sized diving-bell/elevator shape and a working camouflage-skinned lookout form. Book 7 adds Hamzo (stuns and summons a single enemy to her location) and, on the non-combat side, the automaton-table engineering that produces Tran's exo-suit, Jamal's spider legs and her own prosthetics, plus the transfer-tattoo work she does on Carl and Imani
    • Notable items: A ring granting +15 Constitution (her main stat-stacking piece); Enchanted Repeating Crossbow of the Scavenger Mother of Mothers (Book 3, looted from Hekla; Book 4: fires Petrify Rock Class bolts — briefly petrifies rock-type creatures rather than killing them, and a fan-box bolt adding armor-piercing plus level-scaled damage in place of her unlimited free ammo); Enchanted Wrestling Belt of the Great Gorgo (Book 3); Enchanted Shade Gnoll Riot Forces Crowd Control Shield and matching Telescoping Crowd Control Baton (Book 3); The Bolt of Ophiotaurus (Book 3, sponsor gift, 1-of-100 anti-deity ammunition); an adjustable mass-storage backpack with retractable stilts (Book 4, a boss-box upgrade of her Book 3 "Ugly Ass Backpack" exploit); The Left Fang of the Green Sultan (looted from Eva in Book 3; returned to Carl in Book 4, then used to kill Eva outright in Book 5); a knit Apothecary beanie (Book 5, +3% to all stats and the party-wide knockback spell I Need My Personal Space — given away to Carl mid-battle at the Butcher's Masquerade); the Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace (carried uneaten out of Book 6, consumed in Book 7); the Enchanted Spiked Knee pads the Munificent Goddess Kina (Book 7, a divine item from the Club Vanquisher raid — used to eat twenty levels' worth of Intelligence via its Iron Stomach skill, then unequipped and given to Donut); a set of cybernetic prosthetics she built for herself and Bautista finished (Book 7)

    Introduction¶

    Katia is a member of Hekla's group, Brynhild's Daughters, who gets separated from the team after they scatter entering Floor 3. Too far away to share in the group's experience gains, Hekla personally asks Carl and Donut (as a favor) to take Katia in and help her level up, introducing her by chat before the two parties meet in person.

    What She Actually Is¶

    Katia is a Doppelganger — a notoriously difficult race that her own game guide, Bannon, talked her into picking without properly warning her what it entailed. Unlike Mordecai's Changeling race (which grants the actual non-physical abilities of whatever it copies), a Doppelganger only looks like whatever shape it sculpts — she has to physically "chisel" her own appearance by will alone, and doing so hurts. Before Mordecai helped her practice, her race literally displayed as "What the f-word?" in her info box. She can only see her true, unsculpted face when asleep or in the bathroom (and can't reshape herself in either state), reduced early on to using the reflection off a health potion as a mirror — until Carl gives her a shard of broken glass looted back on Floor 1. Equipped gear disappears into her body mass once worn; only her weapon stays visibly separate.

    Before the collapse, Katia was an art teacher — a fact she finds ironic given how poorly she can initially sculpt her own face ("Those who can't do, teach").

    Class & Combat Role¶

    Her class, Monster Truck Driver, is Mordecai's best guess at a combined "Juggernaut" build — a Staunch Barrier/Jouster hybrid that grants an escalating Constitution bonus the faster she moves. Her Book 2 stats: Strength 11, Intelligence 8, Constitution 51, Dexterity 30, Charisma 8. Mordecai notes she's an extremely tanky wall that's still vulnerable if an enemy can simply pick her up and remove her from a fight, rather than trying to out-damage her directly.

    The Doomsday Scenario¶

    Katia fights alongside Carl and Donut during the climax of the Miss Quill/Remex quest chain (see Carl's Doomsday Scenario), using her Rush skill to save herself from one of the event's escalating "precursor bursts" — the impact visibly breaks her nose, which she doesn't seem to notice. As a designated co-host of the resulting emergency group quest, she can't retreat to a safe room and has to help evacuate NPCs instead while Carl and Donut gamble on containing the blast. She survives, jumping from Level 9 to 21 in the process — the largest single-event jump of anyone involved.

    Joining the Party¶

    At the end of the Floor 3 event, Katia formally joins The Royal Court of Princess Donut as a new permanent member, appearing alongside Carl, Donut, and Mongo on Odette's talk show as "the newest member" of the group. Her placement in the party makes her subject to the same Manager arrangement with Mordecai, who "really likes her" per Carl's own observation.

    Odette privately warns Carl that Katia is likely not a willing participant in Hekla's separate scheme to recruit Donut away from the party — implying Katia may be more entangled in outside plots than she currently lets on.

    Book 3: The Hekla Betrayal¶

    Katia becomes the emotional center of Floor 4. After the party discovers Hekla is manipulating events to have Carl killed and Donut isolated into joining Brynhild's Daughters (see Hekla for the full plot), a multi-way brawl breaks out aboard a moving train. Hekla shoots Katia with two invisible, untraceable bolts meant to kill her slowly; Eva Sigrid, Katia's oldest friend and the actual architect of the scheme, cruelly reveals the full plot to Katia's face as a final cut. Katia's Rush — canonically unreliable in both distance and aim — goes off while she's aiming for Eva and instead slams Hekla into the train wall, killing her outright. Katia becomes the first crawler to claim a top-10 bounty (500,000 gold, Hekla having been ranked 2nd), jumping from Level 24 to 37 in a single event and earning a permanent player-killer skull and Club Vanquisher access.

    She spends most of the rest of the floor visibly suppressing the trauma of both the kill and Eva's betrayal, throwing herself into engineering problems instead — most notably discovering that the Iron Tangle's rail network is a physical, "double-sided noodle" recreation of the Syndicate's own corporate logo (see Mordecai), a breakthrough that directly enables the party's endgame plan to blow the floor's ghoul-generator network.

    The Backpack and the Blood Exploit¶

    Katia develops a signature Book 3 technique: mass-storing liquid (blood, mostly, from acting as a literal human cowcatcher on the front of a train — her own idea) directly in her inventory to keep her "scoop" form clean, later re-manifesting it in an "Ugly Ass Backpack" of mass she carries in humanoid form. A Book 3 patch that bans storing unContained liquids in inventory catastrophically and comedically backfires this exploit, causing her to violently discharge weeks of stored gore across the party's personal space the moment Mordecai returns from his ban.

    Other Book 3 Notes¶

    • Reaches 98% accuracy sculpting a convincing gnoll disguise, closing in on being able to pass Iron Tangle security checks in gnoll form
    • Picks up the Find Crawler and Gear Head skill/knowledge potions, the latter proving essential to salvaging and rebuilding hobgoblin cart engines
    • Loots The Left Fang of the Green Sultan — Eva's dropped saber, non-magical without its matched twin — denying Eva her main weapon
    • Gives Carl her Enchanted Auto Buckler of the Peach Pit after a fan box renders it redundant with her new riot shield

    Book 4: Growth, Command, and Departure¶

    On Floor 5, Katia spends five days effectively running the party alone after Carl and Donut are detained off-page by a Syndicate liaison following Admin Loita's death — coordinating with a new ally, Gwendolyn Duet, to breach the land quadrant's castle walls, storing an entire flying house (balloon included) in her own inventory, and generally proving to herself (and to Carl) that she doesn't need his presence to function. She engineers the elaborate hostage-swap con against the Wasteland's gnomish ambassador almost entirely with her own body — simultaneously playing a fake corpse, a chair, and the "body" of a resurrected zombie gnome — and separately builds a working facsimile of the Gate's winding box from memory, swapping it for the real one during a goodbye hug so Carl can use the genuine artifact.

    She also personally forms a "diving bell" elevator shape to ferry Carl and two other crawlers 500 meters down to the drowned submarine Akula, fighting off a city boss (Lusca the octo-shark) at point-blank range using a repurposed giant buzzsaw as an improvised melee weapon.

    Leaving the Core Party¶

    Having spent the floor talking with survivors of Hekla's old group, Brynhild's Daughters, Katia learns that Eva Sigrid — Katia's former friend and Hekla's actual co-conspirator — has been picking off the group's scattered "daughters" one by one, including healer Silfa and her two children. Katia decides she can't keep letting this happen and, at the end of the book, splits off from Carl and Donut's day-to-day party (though not formally out of the group) to lead a new team — Gwen, Tran, Louis and Firas, Bautista, and Florin among them — dedicated to gathering and protecting the surviving daughters before Eva (or someone else) gets to them. Carl gives her back The Left Fang of the Green Sultan to return to Eva as a parting gesture.

    Book 5: Killing Eva¶

    Katia spends Floor 6 running her own splinter team's manhunt for Eva Sigrid, who has been running solo, killing repeatedly, and wielding a poisoned rapier prosthetic on the wrist where she lost a hand back in Book 3. During the book she also begins a relationship with Bautista, confirmed publicly to Donut's shock. In a private, Katia-only point-of-view passage at the very end of the book — not something she confides to Carl, who remains unaware of it — she's revealed to be secretly addicted to Blitz Sticks, using them to relive a false memory of stopping Eva sooner and of the daughter, Annie, she never got to raise back on Earth.

    At the Butcher's Masquerade, Katia is nearly goaded into breaking the party's peace seal confronting Eva directly, and is only barely held back by Bautista and Florin. After the seal is broken by Signet's sacrifice, Katia hands off her Apothecary-gifted beanie to Carl instead so he can use its knockback spell to clear a path to Donut — leaving her own head bare. The text is explicit that it's the bare head, not the missing spell, that costs her: with any headgear occupying that equipment slot, the reforming Crown of the Sepsis Whore would not have had anywhere to land.

    In the battle's aftermath, Katia and Li Na dig out a buried, unconscious Eva; Li Na casts a spell to stop her from healing, and Katia hacks off Eva's remaining hand-with-a-ring (her means of escape) and the poisoned rapier prosthetic, leaving her only her two lower hands. Once Eva wakes and begs to be spared, Katia executes her with The Left Fang of the Green Sultan, finally closing the loop from Book 3's betrayal. As she dies — but before she's fully dead — Eva uses one of her two remaining hands to pull a hidden item from her own armor: the reforming Crown of the Sepsis Whore. It flies to Katia's bare head, and by the time it finishes equipping, Eva has already died. The book ends with Katia realizing this puts her in direct succession conflict with Donut — only one of them will be permitted to descend from Floor 9 to Floor 10.

    Book 6: The Crown, the Orchid, and the Drugs¶

    Book 6 is Katia's heaviest arc. She spends it working a single problem — the Crown of the Sepsis Whore, and the fact that only one of her or Donut will be permitted to descend from Floor 9 — and comes apart doing it.

    The Crown, Confessed¶

    She tells Carl mid-floor by chat, then tells Donut properly at the top of Chapter 19. The Crown came off Eva Sigrid's dying hand at the very end of Book 5, and she blames herself for it:

    "It was Eva. She did it at the last minute, right before she died. It was my fault. I'd talked when I should've just killed her."

    "You were monologuing? You know we don't monologue, Katia."

    Donut goes straight to the arithmetic — "But what are we going to do? Only one of us can leave the next floor." Katia's answer:

    "They want us to fight. That's not going to happen. I love you, Donut. And I will never fight you. I will die before I let that happen."

    The Lock Box and the Orchid¶

    She has been carrying a second secret since the start of Floor 6: an Engaged Lock Box of the Night Wyrm, won in a celestial prize box for opening the Gate of the Feral Gods and flooding Larracos. Dark wood carved with spiderweb patterns, three purple jewels (one lit, two burnt out), and slats showing a pink orchid bud inside.

    The box is attuned to her personally; if she dies, it destroys the prize. Three seals:

    • ✅ Kill someone you are currently or formerly partied with — Eva Sigrid
    • ✅ Kill a country or level boss — Queen Imogen
    • ⬜ Find a Guild of Suffering and complete an assassination quest

    Inside is the Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace. Eating it knocks the user unconscious for an hour at 1% health with no healing possible; survive, and Eileithyia grants one of three permanent boons, at the goddess's discretion:

    1. Base Constitution tripled — "pretty much indestructible to physical damage," but it lapses if Eileithyia dies or the user passes Floor 12.
    2. Celestial Attendant — immediate transfer to the twelfth floor as a temporary non-combatant NPC, out of every party, progress frozen.
    3. Reversion to her birth race and immediate pregnancy, father of her choice — which disqualifies her from the game entirely and exports her to the Kinder Facility in former Mumbai.

    Two of the three solve the Crown problem by removing Katia from the succession. Only the first leaves it live.

    She worships Eileithyia off a scroll of worship that arrived in an emergency sponsor box from Princess Formidable of the Skull Empire. She read it and worshipped on the spot, and only afterwards discovered that Eileithyia's sponsor is Huanxin Jinx — who promptly messaged her with terms. Get Carl to repair Yemaya's shrine, and Huanxin will steer the boon so that both Katia and Donut survive.

    The Drugs¶

    Katia finds a drug den in the Desperado Club — "set up like one of those frozen yogurt places, where you get a bowl, and you self-serve stuff into it and add toppings" — because Carl told her where it was. She goes under for ten hours. Bautista covers for her: "She's asleep… Just leave it, okay? She'll be fine." Imani, whose husband is an addict, recognises the pattern immediately.

    Four drugs, per Mordecai:

    • Travis Priest — a very strong, very addictive painkiller (cured first; the source of the vomiting)
    • Creamsicle Charm — ground-up fairy wings, "a bit like shrooms, but it also makes you forget your worries"; not very addictive
    • Blitz sticks — "like weed plus a hallucinogen that shows you non-existent memories, which is why they're so damn addictive." She has been on these for a while
    • Glory Bound — taken once. "That's the bad one and is the most addictive. By far. It puts her in a coma and allows her to relive a memory… She basically would've just gone back in time and fully re-experienced something"

    The Glory Bound cure is itself a dose of Glory Bound: "she'll have to re-live one of her worst memories. It's pretty fucked up." She never completes that final treatment in Book 6.

    The damage is permanent. Mordecai's summary:

    "The problem is she's already jacked up her stats. It's not good. Everything is scrambled. Her intelligence took a hit, though it's actually still higher than I thought it was from before thanks to the blitz… Her highest stat, constitution, has taken a big hit. Her strength is higher. Her Charisma took a hit. She'll be permanently susceptible to any sort of addictive spells from now on. She's probably forgotten a spell or two. And she's probably lowered a few skill points."

    No system block

    The stat damage is reported only through Mordecai's chat message. No numbers are given anywhere, and the system never prints a debuff block for it.

    Killing Astrid¶

    The third seal on the lock box is a Guild of Suffering contract. Guild jobs are cover-blind — saying the target's name aloud voids the quest — so neither Katia nor Carl knows they have been hired by the target's own husband.

    The mark is Astrid, level 125 Bloodlust Sprite, assistant manager of the Desperado Club. The ambush fails on every axis: Astrid's Cosmic Sense warns her, she takes the wrong staircase, and Samantha simultaneously tears open the Nothing in the casino and releases a level-225 feral demon. Carl calls an abort. Katia refuses:

    "Carl, no. I have to do this… If I don't finish this now, we won't get a chance. The fight will be our distraction." … "Carl, this is all my fault. I have to fix it."

    In the kitchens, Katia — invisible and shapeshifted into "a metallic, spiked-covered, human-faced, canine monstrosity" — grabs Astrid out of the air and then freezes solid mid-strike, spike millimetres short. Carl breaks the paralysis by tearing Astrid's wings off with his own hands. Her crow totem takes Astrid's eyes. A spike from the wolf's neck finishes her, cutting off her last words.

    Katia stores the body and shifts into an ogre on the way out so the witnesses will blame a monster rather than a crawler: "We don't want them knowing a crawler did this." Then: "Carl, we did it. I have the orchid."

    Bautista¶

    The relationship does not survive the floor intact. He covers for her, then blocks her — physically — from going on the Astrid job while she has twelve active debuffs:

    "Where do you think you're going?" … "Don't touch me like that. Don't ever touch me like that."

    "Fine. Kill yourself. But I won't stand by and watch," Bautista said. He turned and stormed from the club.

    Louis names it out loud at the Christmas party — "Katia is struggling every day, and Bautista is having a nervous breakdown" — and Donut names it to Carl: "We're already losing Katia no matter what happens, and nobody is talking about it. Doesn't anybody see what it's doing to Bautista?" By the book's end they are walking at opposite ends of the group, and Katia's summary is one line: "He doesn't want me to use the orchid."

    Unresolved¶

    Katia ends Book 6 holding the Orchid, uneaten. Carl doesn't want her to use it either — "The more I think about it, the more scared I get it's a death sentence" — and her answer is that not using it is also a death sentence. The calculus got worse once Odette announced she was personally entering the Ascendency as a goddess, because the only version of the plan that saves both Katia and Donut depends on trusting Huanxin Jinx, whose entire motive was revenge on Odette.

    Carl's closing thought on it: "The call was ultimately Katia's to make, but there had to be another way. There had to be."

    She also, at the climax, hands Paz Lo the reused Bolt of Ophiotaurus that kills the goddess Ysalte, loses an arm to Carl's hijacked Self-Immolation and simply regrows it, and is the first to say what should be done with Paz's card: "We rip it. That's what he wanted."

    Not stated in the text

    Katia's level is never given anywhere in Book 6. Early in the book she sits around #6 on the top-ten list; no end-of-floor level or rank appears.

    Book 7: This Inevitable Ruin¶

    Book 7 finishes everything Book 6 left open. Katia gets clean, spends the floor building machines for other people, and then eats the Orchid — and the choice she makes with it takes her out of the dungeon and out of the story.

    Getting Clean¶

    The Glory Bound cure Mordecai couldn't finish on Floor 8 happens in Chapter 16, in a field hospital outside the base rather than in a saferoom. The dose is a jar of fizzing green slime: "basically two hits of Glory Bound, but compounded with a subdermal gel and mixed with a potion called 'Tough Love.'" Mordecai puts Katia under for eight to ten hours. Carl takes the same drug at the same time, his dose cut with Removal Order so he'll walk Shi Maria's memory instead of his own.

    Edgar's warning is that once Katia is under she is alone, and waking her early could kill her. She goes under anyway, and tells Carl one thing first:

    "My favorite memory and my worst memory are the same thing. I'm going to the same place I was last time. I hope that doesn't mess this up."

    They reach for each other's hands as the potion takes hold. Imani separates them physically, but the connection through Carl's river holds, and Carl ends up inside Katia's dream with her — the only reason the reader ever sees it.

    The memory is Reykjavík, about a year before the crawl. Katia is trying to adopt a nine-month-old girl named Annie, hospitalised with brain cancer. Fannar — a fellow professor, the man she was originally going to adopt with, whom Katia reported after he slept with a student — walks into the adoption office ahead of her to tell them about her decade-old addiction history. "I can take a test," the memory ghost pleads. "I haven't touched anything in ten years. It was so long ago." The application is denied. Katia locks herself in a bathroom stall, puts her fist in her mouth, and screams silently. Then she goes upstairs and holds the baby for the last time.

    "And here is the worst moment of my life because I know it's the last time I will ever see her," Katia said. A moment passed. "It's also my favorite memory, and it is where I keep coming back to."

    Annie died alone weeks later; Katia only found out by running into a nurse at a park. The crow-totem card she has carried since Floor 8 — a bird with the skull of an infant — is named Annie for her.

    What Carl also sees is what she thinks of herself: that she is a burden, that she is ashamed everyone is doing this for her, and that she is angry — including, briefly, at Carl. Her explanation:

    "It's not that I'm angry with you," she said… "I'm just mad that you let people hurt you. You meet someone, you start to care for them, and then it becomes…"

    She does not overstate the result afterward:

    Katia: I think so. It's not like a switch that one flips on and off. We won't really know right away. But, yes. I think it worked.

    Not stated in the text

    Book 7 never prints a system block confirming the addiction cleared, and never revisits the permanent stat damage Mordecai described in Book 6. It also never gives Katia's level anywhere in the book.

    The Engineer¶

    Katia spends most of the floor at Carl's automaton table rather than in the line. Three builds matter.

    Tran's conveyance. Tran lost his magical wheelchair between floors. Katia repurposes a recipe from Carl's automaton manual into a two-part rig: a mechanical crab body he can climb walls and sprint on, plus a battle mode where "a secondary exo-skeleton covered the crab legs, crawling up his body, encasing him in full-plate armor and a helmet." Carl's summary — "as if Odette and Iron Man had a baby." So much of Tran is metal that he can't carry ranged weapons without the system reclassifying him as Armor.

    Jamal's legs. Jamal the hammerhead shark flesh golem breathes air, can't swim, and can only flop. Carl builds three voice-controlled prototypes and Jamal breaks all three by hopping; Katia's version is the real one — eight double-jointed legs and a harness, thought-driven rather than voice-driven, and strong enough that he outruns most of the party. Rosetta's crafting team bolts Tserendolgor's flamethrower Velma to the underside of the harness. A No Disassemble blast later fries the cybernetics, and Katia simply hands over the spare set she'd already built.

    Her own prosthetics. She builds these quietly, in the crafting room, and tells nobody what they're for. Bautista finishes them for her while she's unconscious under the Orchid.

    The Transfer Tattoos¶

    The toraline-yam ink that Shi Maria brewed lets a willing crawler lend a spell or skill to another in the form of a permanent tattoo. Edgar is the party's actual tattoo artist, but he's off scouting naga territory, so the work falls to Katia, who admits she has never done a real tattoo before. (The mechanic itself belongs with the toraline ink and the Enchanted Prison Tattoo Kit; this is her part in it.)

    She does two:

    • Carl's chain dragon, a large piece on his right arm, carrying Li Na's level-13 Daughter's Kiss. Li Na gave her blood freely, so she loses five levels of the skill temporarily rather than permanently — the opposite of what happened to her brother Li Jun when Shi Maria took Walk on Air by force.
    • Imani's barbed wire, a small band around her ankle, carrying Carl's Tripper. Donut objects loudly to the style on aesthetic grounds; Katia's answer: "Donut, didn't we agree you were going to stop complaining about the tattoos? It's not a disfigurement."

    She also did Samantha's neck lettering, which she declines to count.

    General Katia Grim¶

    She holds the rank of General in the Princess Posse and runs the northern front alongside Florin for most of the fighting. For Operation: Snake Pit — the raid on Club Vanquisher to wipe out the Blood Sultanate's line of succession — she shapeshifts into a mantaur, covers herself in fake gore (cherry cobbler from Carl's broken food box), and talks her way through the temple door with an improvised battle cry: "Fight to the death!… Kill all the heathens! The demons come! Uh, steel and heavy metal!"

    The raid kills Princess Vinata and settles the succession on the crown's side of the ledger; see Crown of the Sepsis Whore for the mechanics. The system names Katia in the payout:

    System Message: All assets of the Blood Sultanate, as per prior arrangement, have been split evenly with the surviving blood relatives of the Sepsis Whore, Warlord Princess Donut and General Katia Grim, both of the Princess Posse.

    Her celestial box from the raid is the Enchanted Spiked Knee pads the Munificent Goddess Kina, a divine item. Drick calls it on air as a gift intended to be handed to Carl, since she's leaving either way. She eats twenty levels' worth of throwaway magic items with its Iron Stomach skill to pump her Intelligence, then unequips the knee pads and gives them to Donut — who uses their Eileithyia boon to kill Huanxin Jinx with them on.

    Privately, she tells Carl something she never tells Bautista: if the plan collapses, the Orchid goes to Donut.

    Katia: I know. But I want you to know something. I haven't told Daniel this, but I'm telling you now. If it comes to that, I will give you the flower. That Huanxin bitch will rage, but I'm certain she'll take Donut to the 12th floor.

    Eating the Orchid¶

    Chapter 84. Samantha's confession summons Emberus, who gives the floor 66 minutes before he burns it. The Orchid takes exactly 60 minutes to work, so the goodbye everyone had planned for days later happens in about ninety seconds.

    "Katia!" I called… "Eat the orchid. You have to do it now. Now, now, now!"

    "But…" she began.

    "Carl, we haven't had the chance to say goodbye to her!" Donut cried. "I thought we had days left!"

    She hasn't finished transferring her inventory, and there's gear she needs still sitting in the saferoom. She eats it anyway.

    "I love you," Katia said. She raised her voice. "I love all of you."

    Donut leaps for the table and lands too late. Katia is at 1% health with Waiting for the Goddess over her head before the cat touches down.

    The Choice¶

    She wakes in Chapter 86 with eight minutes left on the world. Eileithyia arrives in her true form — and wearing Huanxin Jinx, who immediately starts hurrying her along. The book never prints a system block for the three boons here; the narration is all the reader gets:

    Right now Katia had three choices floating in her vision. The first would cause her constitution to get extremely powerful. The second would allow her to become a celestial attendant. The third would cause her to get pregnant. Ultimately the choice was up to the goddess, but Katia had to pick one before Eileithyia could whisk her away.

    Huanxin's own framing of it: "Damnit, Katia. I can't take you until you make a choice. It doesn't matter what you pick. Just pick one." Donut then casts Laundry Day, rips the heiress out of the goddess's soul armor, and kills her with Mordecai's ashes-tipped bolt fired from Katia's own crossbow — the deal with Huanxin dies with her, and Katia chooses free.

    She hugs everyone, kisses Donut on the forehead — "You keep him safe. And now I have to press the button" — and picks the third option.

    "Please, goddess. Please." She took a breath.

    "I wish to be made pregnant."

    Eileithyia offers her the safe option one more time, explicitly:

    "Are you certain? This is a hard, cruel world you have found yourself in, and as a woman with gift, your challenges will be even greater. If you come to assist me and Yemaya in my court, I promise you will be kept safe. I sense the strength in you, and you would be welcome."

    Katia turns down the Celestial Attendant post, and the reason is not her own safety:

    "The rules of this world are strange," Katia said. "If I am pregnant, I get sent somewhere else. There are no children or pregnant women allowed here in this place. At least not of my kind. But there are several children, up there," Katia said, pointing upward. "And they are mostly alone. I think they're in trouble. They all need help, and this is the only way I can get to them."

    She means the kinder facility on the surface of Earth, where Zev has admitted roughly 120,000 children and pregnant women are being held — the ones who tried to enter the dungeon and were routed somewhere else instead. Eileithyia, who is a goddess of childbirth, can suddenly sense them and cannot reach them. Donut closes it: "You can't get there. But Katia can, and she can protect them for you."

    What She'd Been Hiding¶

    The goddess makes her human again first, and the shapeshifting stops covering for her.

    Her entire right arm was gone, all the way to the shoulder. Her left leg was gone. Her right leg was gone up to the knee. She was missing an ear as well.

    Katia smiled sadly from the bed. "Just a few things I've lost along the way."

    I remembered when she'd lost the ends of her fingers on her hand, all the way back on the Iron Tangle.

    "I had no idea," I said.

    "How could you? We've all become experts at hiding what we've really lost."

    Bautista bursts in with the cybernetic limbs, which she had built herself in advance and left in the crafting room for him to finish. The party clicks them into place while the goddess works.

    Not stated in the text

    The text does not say when each limb was lost, beyond Carl's memory of her fingertips on the Iron Tangle (Book 3). The wording — "accumulated over seven floors" — is Carl's inference from the scene, not a system statement, and nothing identifies which floor cost her the arm, the legs or the ear. Nor does the text call the replacements Bautista's work: "She'd made them herself, in anticipation of this. She'd left them in the crafting room, and Bautista had finished them for her."

    Leaving¶

    Eileithyia rules that the father must be human, and that it cannot be Carl — "I see who you wish for it to be, but it cannot be so," with Bautista standing right there. Katia asks Louis, fresh out of surgery and worried he may not count as human any more. Eileithyia takes his hand: "Of course you are. And a good soul at that."

    "It is done. It will be a boy."

    "Spoilers," Louis muttered.

    Ineligible appears over her head.

    And then, just like that, Katia Grimmsdottir… Art Professor. Crawler. Hero. Human. Friend.

    Mother.

    …disappeared in a puff of smoke.

    She leaves two things on the table: the Annie card, which Donut takes, and a joke bra with Carl's face on one cup and "Time to pay the daddy tax!" on the other.

    With Katia out of the dungeon, Donut is free to descend. The succession crisis that had run since the end of Book 5 ends not in a fight but in Katia removing herself from it.
