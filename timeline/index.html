    Timeline¶

    A chronological highlights reel of Book 1's major events. This is the condensed version — a future Chapter Summaries section will cover the book in full narrative detail, chapter by chapter. Entries link to the relevant page where one exists.

    Before the Dungeon¶

    • Carl, 27, a former Coast Guard machinery technician turned civilian marine tech, breaks up with his girlfriend Beatrice after learning she cheated on him in the Bahamas — he's looking after her show cat, Princess Donut, when the story begins.

    The Harvest¶

    • At 2:23 AM Pacific time on January 3rd, every structure with a roof on Earth is instantly crushed as the planet is "harvested" for resources by the Syndicate/Borant Corporation, killing everyone indoors — an estimated 3 million dead in Carl's city alone.
    • Carl and Donut survive only because they were both outside at the moment of the harvest.
    • The Syndicate's announcement offers survivors a choice: remain on the surface with no further resources, or enter the 18-level World Dungeon to "reclaim" lost matter.

    Level/Floor 1¶

    • Carl enters Level 1, fights through a goblin ambush (including a Goblin Murder Dozer), and reaches the Tutorial Guild run by Mordecai.
    • Donut eats an Enhanced Pet Biscuit and is transformed into a talking, stat-boosted crawler in her own right; she names their new party The Royal Court of Princess Donut.
    • Carl and Donut clear The Hoarder (Level 7 Neighborhood Boss).
    • They clear the Goblin War Chieftain via cart bomb, without ever triggering an official boss-card sequence — Book 1's darkest moment for Carl.
    • They clear The Juicer (Level 9 Neighborhood Boss) inside a gym-themed neighborhood.
    • Carl and Donut join a group of elderly evacuees ("Team Meadow Lark," led by Brandon An) and the mysterious, shopping-cart-wielding Agatha.
    • After a full day of chopper scouting, they find Floor 1's only known stairwell down to Floor 2 — guarded by the Ball of Swine (Level 15 Borough Boss). They defeat it in a mixed 7-crawler party and descend immediately afterward, at which point their stats become publicly visible to live viewers for the first time.

    Floor 2¶

    • Carl adopts a strict "always destroy the corpse" policy after learning corpses spawn endless waves of Brindle Grubs.
    • They escape the Krakaren Clone (Level 10 Neighborhood Boss) inside a Rev-Up Moonshine Company distillery, badly injured.
    • They defeat Ralph, a Level 11 Neighborhood Boss, in a kobold fighting pit — freeing a pack of captive dingoes in the process.
    • Donut chooses a Mongoliensis from the resulting pet reward room, later revealed by Odette to actually be a velociraptor; he's eventually bonded and named Mongo.
    • Carl publicly challenges Prince Maestro of the Skull Empire to single combat after a hostile interview on Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem, nicknaming him "Pork Boy" — the insult goes viral across the Syndicate's social media.
    • During the continued Meadow Lark evacuation, a Rage Elemental — summoned as a System AI punishment for a bathroom-etiquette violation — kills nurse Yolanda Martinez; Carl and Donut later deny it a clean kill by luring it into a collapsing stairwell.
    • Carl and Donut descend to Floor 3.

    End of Book 1¶

    • Carl and Donut are about to choose a race and class for the first time, now that they've reached Floor 3.

    Floor 3 (Book 2)¶

    • Carl and Donut choose their permanent race/class: Carl becomes a Primal Compensated Anarchist; Donut deliberately picks Former Child Actor for its hidden Manager benefit, covering it up as an accident so Mordecai doesn't suspect it was intentional.
    • They enter the Over City — a themed urban floor built around a destroyed volcano-kingdom — and stumble into a quest at Grimaldi's transformed traveling circus.
    • Carl solo-kills Heather the Bear (Level 19 Neighborhood Boss) after Tsarina Signet puts Donut under a sleep curse and forces the fight.
    • Carl confronts Ringmaster Grimaldi (Level 85 City Boss + Elite) and resolves the standoff through negotiation rather than a kill, securing a hidden stairwell to Floor 4 and a recurring-guest-star deal with the drama's producers.
    • Carl and Donut take up a second quest after NPC orc GumGum asks for help investigating murdered sex workers; GumGum is killed by Krasue before they can help her.
    • They kill Miss Quill (Level 30 Neighborhood Boss), the town's true ruler behind a dead figurehead magistrate, uncovering a scheme to manufacture krasue and cast a generations-old genocidal spell against the settlement's non-flying residents.
    • Miss Quill's undead husband, Remex, survives her death as an unstable power capacitor, triggering a mass "event quest" explosion that nearly kills everyone in the blast radius; Carl narrowly contains it, creating Carl's Doomsday Scenario in the process.
    • Katia Grim, a Doppelganger sent by Hekla's group (Brynhild's Daughters) for help leveling, joins the party permanently.
    • Carl publicly calls for the Skull Empire's citizens to rise up during a live interview; Prince Stalwart orders a retaliatory strike that instead kills the Valtay-sponsored singer Manasa, and the Valtay Corporation destroys a Skull Empire royal yacht in response, killing Prince Maestro and Queen Consort Ugloo.
    • Fleeing the event-quest explosion, Carl, Donut, Katia, and a mixed group of other crawlers descend early from Floor 3 to Floor 4 via a stairwell near a decrepit building that fellow crawler Daniel Bautista had already scouted, with only about three minutes to spare before Carl's own Doomsday Scenario device detonates. A second stairwell, hidden beneath Grimaldi's circus, is discovered earlier but never actually used.

    End of Book 2¶

    • Carl and Donut land on the current working leaderboard for the first time (6th and 7th of the top 10), with Lucia Mar 1st and Hekla 2nd.
    • Odette privately warns Carl that Hekla — while not evil — is "practical and cunning" and has become fixated on recruiting Donut (and, through her, permanent access to a Manager) into Brynhild's Daughters.
    • The book ends as Floor 4 is about to open.

    Floor 4 — "The Iron Tangle" (Book 3)¶

    • Carl, Donut, Katia, and Mongo arrive on a moving subway train; Donut picks the Football Hooligan class, and the party begins mapping a sprawling, confusing rail-and-station system (150,000-plus stops, only 9,375 stairwells) under a shortened 10-day timer.
    • Carl fights and kills Gore-Gore the Mantaur, narrowly avoiding an accidental full summon of the war god Grull via his own War Gauntlet; the fight ends with Carl gaining a new sponsor, the Valtay Corporation.
    • The party, working with allies including Imani C, Elle McGibbons, Li Jun, Li Na, Zhang, and Daniel Bautista, discovers the floor's central crisis: a ghoul-generation system is overloading, threatening to flood the entire rail network, with hundreds of thousands of crawlers scattered and trapped between stations — see Iron Tangle Management.
    • Meeting Frank Q at the Desperado Club, Carl learns the full story behind Book 2's dynamite trap: Frank and his ex-wife Maggie had forced their injured daughter Yvette to wear an Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering and mark Carl for death — which blocks all healing until the mark's target dies. Carl escaped to a saferoom and survived, leaving Yvette permanently unhealable and in agony; Maggie choked her own daughter to death as a mercy killing, then vanished. Frank, a broken man, gives Carl the ring as his own twisted "revenge" before Chris Andrews — mid personality-altering race change — kills him at the club.
    • Hekla, rescuing Katia from Brynhild's Daughters, secretly plots to have Katia (and, if needed, her own healer Silfa) die in a way that reads as accidental combat loss — using invisible Dirty Little Phased Bolts that inflict lethal, uncredited damage — so that a grieving Donut would be forced to fold what remains of the party into Hekla's own, gaining Borant permanent access to Mordecai. Discovered mid-attack, Katia's Rush ability goes off-target and instead kills Hekla outright, claiming a 500,000-gold bounty as the season's first player-killing of a top-10 crawler and rocketing Katia from level 24 to 37 — ending Book 1–2's recurring rival/antagonist arc. Brynhild's Daughters disbands afterward.
    • Brandon An dies holding a defensive line as part of the floor's collapse.
    • Carl, Katia, and allies build an improvised rescue operation — hijacked named trains and repurposed maintenance interdiction carts, whose portal-scoop tech is used both to ferry stranded crawlers to safety and to funnel oncoming monster hordes straight into the Abyss.
    • Fire Brandy, the Nightmare train's engine-spirit NPC, and the dwarf Tizquick willingly ride the train into the Abyss and detonate it to help collapse the floor's ghoul-generator network, after both realize their in-game families were never real.
    • Carl accidentally teleports Mimic Rex — one of six floor-wide Station Mimics — into a trainyard while trying to save a stranded crawler group, then gets trapped there himself while summoning a Mantaur vessel to fight it off. This triggers a Death Challenge broadcast event pitting the Mimic against the involuntarily-summoned war god Grull — secretly sponsored and piloted by a very-much-alive Prince Maestro of the Skull Empire, who faked his death in Book 2. Carl survives by manipulating the involuntary-summon mechanic to banish Grull to the abyss after he kills the Mimic Rex.
    • With eleven hours left on the floor's timer, Iron Tangle management announces a system-wide self-destruct, blaming "the Krakaren" for a betrayal, and floods every stairwell station with the floor's accumulated Krakaren-monster population at once.

    End of Book 3¶

    • Carl ends the book at Level 41 (up from 27), with Donut at 33; both are members of a much larger, more organized crawler alliance than they started the floor with.
    • The floor's ghoul crisis is contained, though not fully resolved, as the level's timer runs out.

    Floor 5 — "The Bubbles" (Book 4)¶

    • Carl, Donut, Katia, and Mongo arrive in bubble #543, one of 1,172 sealed spherical worlds floating in the dark Lacuna — each split into Land, Sea, Air, and Subterranean quadrants behind an invisible barrier; Donut picks the Glass Cannon specialization.
    • The party bases out of Hump Town, a Dromedarian brothel settlement secretly infiltrated by the Changeling Principals, a cult seeking a cure for their species' hereditary Compression Sickness.
    • Carl kills Ruckus (Level 55 Borough Boss) while field-testing homemade missiles, then uncovers and thwarts a Skull-Empire-backed changeling infiltration of the town's guard ranks with Donut's help.
    • After the hostage gnome Wynne is killed by a rival crawler group, Carl, Donut, and Katia stage a fake resurrection and disguise ruse to trick the dirigible-gnome dreadnaught the Wasteland into a face-to-face negotiation, then destroy it mid-air — see The Wasteland — killing most of its crew including Commandant Kane's negotiator and sniper, and rescuing Kane's orphaned daughter Bonnie from the boss Denise the Feral Goose Mother.
    • Carl accidentally assassinates dungeon admin Loita using a rigged toy-robot self-destruct mechanism during a sponsor infomercial taping, then talks his way out of a Syndicate liaison investigation without confessing or naming his source (the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook).
    • Katia leads a separate team (Gwendolyn Duet, Tran, Florin, and others) storming the Land Quadrant's Sandcastle, killing the mage Ghazi and the borough boss Mrs. Ghazi — though the castle's collapse washes the survivors down into the Water Quadrant instead of ending the fight cleanly.
    • Carl, Katia, and two other survivors dive to the wrecked submarine Akula, are swallowed whole by the City Boss Lusca in a live-broadcast Special Event, and kill her from the inside using a berserking potion and a salvaged buzzsaw.
    • Maggie My — revealed to have taken an Infiltrator (Scree) race and fully puppeted Chris Andrews since the previous floor — is finally extracted and killed after Donut buys time by dropping the pair through a Hole spell rather than letting Carl kill Chris outright; Chris rejoins the party.
    • The Subterranean Quadrant's guardian, Quetzalcoatlus — possessed by the banished lesser deity Psamathe — is destroyed via an environmental lightning-tower trap, at the cost of every crawler remaining in the quadrant.
    • With all four quadrants cleared, Carl assembles the final piece of the Gate of the Feral Gods and uses it to evacuate crawlers trapped in sealed bubbles across the Lacuna — each rescue releasing a feral god or demon into the newly-emptied bubble, including the puppy-god Orthrus, safely reunited with his father Emberus, God of Sun and Ash, whom Carl accidentally acquires as his own patron deity via a Celestial Grenade.
    • In the finale, rather than releasing a final feral god onto the open floor, Carl sends it through a portal directly into the Ninth Floor's Larracos marketplace instead, flooding and destroying the city's mercantile district and crippling every Faction War army's ability to resupply ahead of schedule.
    • Carl ends the book as the dungeon's #1-ranked crawler (Level 47); Katia splits off from the main party (while remaining nominally grouped) to protect Eva Sigrid's scattered former followers; Chris Andrews joins the core party.

    End of Book 4¶

    • Carl publicly vows, on-air, to hunt down and kill every bounty hunter sent after him and Donut on Floor 6 before its timer runs out.
    • The epilogue reveals a Borant-adjacent "pacifist" NFC sponsor, run by Dr. Hu (Porthus, author of the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook's second edition), has been quietly bankrolling Carl's campaign — and that Beatrice, Carl's ex, has just been captured alive off-planet by a bounty hunter working for Odette.

    Floor 6 — "The Hunting Grounds" (Book 5)¶

    • Carl, Donut, and Mongo arrive on Floor 6's capital, Zockau; Carl picks the Agent Provocateur specialization and Donut a new Legendary Diva bard class. Carl negotiates collateral terms with Borant over the confiscated Gate of the Feral Gods, gaining four cretin mercenaries and the Zerzura spell (see Spells).
    • Carl opens the floor by raiding Zockau directly — marking and killing the hunters Xindy and Chin'Dua, leveling a city block, and racking up 34 Hunter-Killer trophies before nearly dying; he's saved by Miriam Dom, Prepotente, and Bianca. This also brands him with a Guild of Suffering tattoo — see Dungeon Factions.
    • Carl and Donut accidentally kill an Ursine mayor and conquer his settlement, renaming it Point Mongo and fortifying it against incoming hunters.
    • Mongo encounters a wild Mongoliensis pack led by Kiwi and mates with her; Donut has a private panic episode hinting at unresolved dread over Floor 9's Blood Sultanate succession curse (see the Crown of the Sepsis Whore).
    • Carl and Donut help Tsarina Signet — contractually bound to her secondary broadcast, Vengeance of the Daughter — liberate Fort Freedom from the Naiad Confederacy, accidentally triggering and killing the boss Claude Sludgington the Fourth in the process; the were-castors Clint and Nadine are killed in the same battle.
    • Odette privately reveals to Carl and Donut that Beatrice is alive and off-planet, then stages a scripted reunion/confrontation on her show; Donut publicly and permanently cuts ties with her. In the same news cycle, the Valtay Corporation acquires 51% of the Borant Corporation — see Factions.
    • Miriam Dom sacrifices herself (via Carl's Ring of Divine Suffering, at her own request) to cure a spreading vampirism outbreak she unknowingly caused while fighting Big Tina and the elite vampire hunter Viscount Fog; Carl and Donut kill Fog's surviving mount, Sierra, earlier the same night. Prepotente, left catatonic with grief, swallows his own Ring of Divine Suffering and swears revenge on everyone he holds responsible.
    • A floor-wide vampirism outbreak escalates into The Creeping Apocalypse, a mandatory world-quest to kill fifty Odious Creepers by sundown; the quest fails, triggering a floor-consuming Gehenna Bramble and a new "blood bar" saferoom restriction — see Game Mechanics.
    • Carl and Donut, now allied with Kiwi's pack, solve "The Recital" — Big Tina's dinosaur-rampage quest — by learning her tragic backstory from an Ursine barmaid, Prudence, and booking her into the upcoming Butcher's Masquerade talent show.
    • Carl coordinates a floor-wide crawler offensive against the scattered, demoralized hunter population, killing hundreds in a single day; Circe Took's daughter Vrah is deliberately infected with a venereal disease Carl can't be cured of without either self-sterilizing or mating outside mantis honor rules — see The Dark Hive.
    • The Borant/Valtay AI begins ignoring Syndicate court rulings it disagrees with following the ownership change, a recognized precursor to an AI "going primal" — see Game Mechanics.
    • At the Butcher's Masquerade in the High Elf Castle, Carl and Donut's group survives a multi-sided ambush (Vrah's mother Circe Took, in physical form as the goddess Diwata, floods the castle with mantis nymphs) after Signet willingly breaks the ritual's peace seal as a sacrifice; the resulting battle kills Vrah, Circe Took/Diwata, Queen Imogen, Tsarina Signet, Gwendolyn Duet, Firas, Horton, and the Popov Brothers' adult forms (who are reborn as ineligible infant nodlings and rescued from the dungeon entirely), among many others. Katia kills Eva Sigrid at last, but inherits Donut's old Crown of the Sepsis Whore curse in the process.
    • Carl publicly announces the Princess Posse's Faction Wars sponsorship (fielded by Donut's fan club) and pushes for a floor-wide vote to remove safety protections for crawler-fielding factions ahead of Floor 9 — see Game Mechanics.

    End of Book 5¶

    • Carl ends the book ranked #3 on the leaderboard (per the system message on entering Floor 7; Donut instead insists she's "ranked number one," by a metric never specified), having personally ended the Dark Hive's hunting operation and the Naiad Confederacy, and having secured Donut and Carl's own Faction Wars team ahead of Floor 9.
    • Prepotente shatters the seventh floor's starting chamber outright using two mystery sponsor-gifted items (the Hammer of Fast Forward and the Stick of Cascadia's Screams), forcing every crawler on the floor through its stairwells simultaneously before the level's maze challenge can even begin.
    • The epilogue reveals Eva's dying act transferred the Crown of the Sepsis Whore's Blood Sultanate succession curse from Donut onto Katia — meaning only one of the two will be allowed to descend from Floor 9 to Floor 10.

    Floor 7 — "The Great Race" (skipped)¶

    • Floor 7 was a maze of branching tubes running several hundred kilometres, announced by Borant as "an engineering marvel" funded by the incoming Valtay Corporation. It lasted roughly ten minutes. Before the opening announcement had even finished, Prepotente cracked the starting chamber's glass wall (see End of Book 5 above) — and the crack propagated to every wall on the level at once, shattering the floor to dust and dumping every crawler through the exit portals simultaneously. Neither book records any deaths on the floor. Donut was briefly a Viper Queen, and nobody received the floor's spellbook delivery — "You snooze you lose."

    Floor 8 — "The Ghosts of Earth" (Book 6)¶

    Floor 8 runs from December 10th to the collapse, in three phases: a fourteen-day build-up inside a single locked region, a 48-hour assault in a second region to win an exit key, and a final scramble back to the original region. Just over 38,000 crawlers start it, spread across 293 possible regions (205 of them occupied), each region an exact replay of a patch of pre-collapse Earth. For the floor's structure see Level 8; for the card system that governs almost everything below, see The T'Ghee Card System.

    Phase One — Havana¶

    • Carl and Donut arrive in El Capitolio Nacional de Cuba, Havana — Donut having picked Cuba specifically to avoid the Bahamas trap she correctly suspected. Donut takes the Master Telephone Psychic class (better mapping, offensive charm spells) over "Agony Urchin." The system announces the squad, flag, and T'Ghee card systems.
    • The party opens its held Floor 6 loot boxes, yielding the Scavenger's Daughter, an Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Superior Fire Demon's Hand Maiden, and the Legionnaires of the Damned tome — see Items and Spells.
    • Carl and Donut meet Cuba's only other squad: Paz Lo, Anton Lopez, and Sister Ines Quiteria. The two squads jointly capture their first monk-seal totems, which triggers the quest The Chowder War.
    • On the show Shadow Boxer, Carl's traumatic childhood is aired publicly. Host Rosetta Thagra secretly slips him a coded message mid-broadcast before the liaison Harbinger kills the feed and bans her.
    • In the Colón Cemetery ghommid settlement, a Celestial Thorn Room appears. Acting on a secret warning from Katia, Carl repairs Yemaya's shrine instead of Ogun's — resurrecting a goddess, and costing Anton Lopez his life when Ogun crushes his head in retaliation. Paz Lo takes a permanent, unhealable smite; Sister Ines flags him into a T'Ghee card rather than watch him die, over his own explicit objection.
    • Yemaya and Eileithyia appear. Eileithyia — driven by Huanxin Jinx — offers Carl a private bargain: secure Odette as the Princess Posse's Faction Wars adjutant, and she will clear the Crown of the Sepsis Whore problem.
    • Katia reveals the Crown of the Sepsis Whore to Donut, along with her Engaged Lock Box of the Night Wyrm and the Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace.
    • At the Faction Wars preproduction meeting, Juice Box — alive, and leading a rogue NPC insurrection on Floor 9 — assassinates the warlord Stalwart in front of the council and demands a tenth team for NPCs. Carl takes the proposal forward.
    • Katia is revealed to be addicted to four dungeon drugs, with permanent stat damage. Mordecai privately advises letting her self-destruct so that Donut survives the Crown's succession curse; Carl refuses outright and forces the Astrid assassination job to happen immediately instead.
    • The Astrid job goes catastrophically wrong. Samantha accidentally releases the minor feral demon Minge from the Nothing; Carl responds by unleashing the vorpals held behind a hidden door. Astrid dies anyway, and the Desperado Club is destroyed and closed.
    • Damascus Steel reveals that Astrid was his mother and the Night Wyrm his father, and that the contract was a murder-for-inheritance scheme. He and his brother Anaconda leave to kill their father. Carl hires the Penis Parade's dancers — the men's side of the operation — as mercenaries in the aftermath.
    • On Odette's final show, Empress D'Nadia is exposed as having burned all three of her Emergency Action Items on attempts to delete crawlers and NPCs, the last of them naming Carl and Donut directly. Odette declines the adjutant role and announces she is sponsoring the goddess Nekhebit instead; Baroness Victory becomes the Posse's adjutant.
    • Off the record, Odette confesses everything: the crawls exist to harvest fuel, and she is part of a long-running plot to shut them down safely.

    Phase Two — the Florida Keys¶

    • Phase two begins December 24th. Donut fights her first solo deck battle. A placement glitch drops a chunk of Dallas County, Iowa on top of Monroe County, Florida — crawlers standing in the Keys read as being in Iowa.
    • On the transplanted Iowa mound, Carl and Donut beat nine deckmasters in sequence. Among the spliced-in terrain they find Carl's father's trailer, lifted from a different broadcast season entirely, and Carl watches his father die of a morphine overdose administered by his stepmother Tami-Lynn — and discovers he has a six-year-old half-brother, Asher.
    • The final deckmaster is the Reminiscence Hydra of Malicious Compliance, built from the faces of everyone Carl and Donut have known, Asher's among them. Killing it earns the phase key, and Carl is raised to Devotee, the highest worship rank available to a non-Cleric. Donut later tells Carl that Beatrice knew Asher existed and kept it from him.
    • Louis declares Christmas over Carl's objections. Mid-party, the system AI seizes control of the crawl from Borant — the earliest this has ever happened in the show's history. Orren negotiates a settlement and, as one of the AI's own conditions, publicly explains Primal Engines and Macro AIs. All six rival Faction Wars teams attempt to abdicate and are refused under the quarantine.
    • Carl rides his late father's motorcycle to The Shepherd's House, a homeless shelter in Homestead, Florida, to hear a Residual speak. Orren teleports him out before the speech finishes and declares the entire event a trap engineered by Carl's own, now-fugitive, sponsors.

    Phase Three — the Bedlam¶

    • Phase three begins at midnight on December 26th. Imani's entire squad has been killed by her own summoned totem. In a chaotic eight-combatant battle touched off by a Samantha teleport malfunction, Quan Ch strips Sister Ines's phase key and then Donut's — and the same wave of mass death triggers a planet-wide Demon Eviction, the earliest in the crawl's history.
    • Using teleport traps smuggled inside Samantha, Carl and Donut ambush and kill Quan Ch and recover both stolen keys. Seconds later the Capitol building's stairwell doorway is destroyed, taking its key with it. Tserendolgor, refusing to fight Donut for the last remaining key, hands Carl her gear and her pet Garret and detonates herself.
    • Carl and Prepotente design an escape for every keyless crawler on the planet: free the demon prince Amayon and buff him to full power so that his forced homecoming portal spans the entire world, then shelter everyone in trenches dug beneath the indestructible stairwell chambers as the portal tears them out of the ground. Carl rallies the whole dungeon population — via the Book of Voodoo — to stop fighting each other and cooperate. Prepotente privately rates the plan's odds at "40 percent… maybe 35 percent."
    • Lucia Mar, having personally slaughtered Beijing's crawler population, leaves five Tier 3 Doggie Door upgrades on the ground as a favour to Florin before descending — and separately announces she intends to kill Donut and Mongo on Floor 9. Reinforcements begin pouring into Cuba through the new fast-travel doors.
    • At the FOCSA building, Louis kills Sister Ines, which summons her divine patron Ysalte. Amayon is freed from his host shell but remains enslaved to the goddess. In the final T'Ghee card battle, Paz Lo — Ysalte's own stolen totem — kills her, using Katia's reused Bolt of Ophiotaurus and his own self-destruct.
    • Carl persuades Raul the crab to repopulate the ocean with souls, then teleports himself inside Samantha to drive his own health down and trigger The Martyr's Path, bathing Havana — and Amayon — in Sheol Fire, which makes a demon exponentially stronger. Amayon, now at full power, casts his homecoming spell.
    • Escaping the collapsing tower, a misfired Golden Combo fuses Carl with the boss Shi Maria. She takes control of his body, tears out and eats Li Jun's eye, brews tattoo ink from it, and stick-and-pokes the Eye of the Bedlam Bride into his chest — explicitly so that she can never be fully discarded: "I am a part of you… until you decide to free me." Donut rips Shi Maria's card afterward.
    • Carl and Donut spend their one carry-forward card slot on ripping Paz Lo's card instead, freeing him, as he had asked.
    • Carl spends one Emergency Action Item to hand every pending Faction Wars procedural vote over to the viewing audience. They vote 98% in favour of removing safety protections for faction sponsors, and 52% in favour of creating a tenth NPC team, co-warlorded by Juice Box and Sir Ferdinand. A rival proposal to cut Celestial Summonings from fifteen per team to five is rejected at 37%.
    • The heavens open over Cuba and the world is stripped clean. Carl, Donut, and Imani are pulled up into the sky and land on Floor 9 — Faction Wars — along with 33,804 crawlers in total, against a historical average of just under 8,000. "We were hitting the ninth floor in unprecedented numbers."

    End of Book 6¶

    • Agatha is revealed as "Agent number 22" — ten years detached from her own collective, and the one who had been waiting behind the Meadowlark Elder Care facility in Wenatchee, Washington on the night of Earth's collapse. Her faction's stated goal is to free the Eulogist and return it to its slumber; their parting phrase is "May the Eulogist ever sleep, my friend." If Agatha personally gets her way, "this entire universe would be swept clean of biological life."
    • Her rivals — thousands of agents working for the Apothecary — are meanwhile "secretly implanting the crawler with a hidden upgrade."
    • The Syndicate Council debates and refuses to pull the failsafe: over half the galaxy's wealth, and the councillors' own families, are trapped inside the quarantined system at the Scolopendra Club.
    • The Valtay's alternative is an engineered extinction event: "well over 150,000" mercenaries flooded into the crawl to kill every remaining crawler and so end the quarantine early.
    • Tipid and Rosetta, funded and organized by a "Doctor Hu," have converted the garbage freighter Homecoming Queen and have just been cleared to board fifty thousand former crawlers onto it as troopers for the Princess Posse.

    Left Unresolved Going Into Floor 9¶

    The book deliberately closes several threads without answers. They're recorded here so the wiki doesn't quietly invent them:

    • Carl's father is never named. Not in the flashbacks, not in the trailer sequence, not anywhere — he is only ever "my father," "my dad," "the old man."
    • The Eye of the Bedlam Bride tattoo has no stated effect. The item entry reads "Tattoo. The Eye of the Bedlam Bride." and nothing else: "That's all it said. It gave no other description."
    • Samantha's power source is never confirmed. Carl's own guess — "I'm starting to think maybe Samantha gets more powerful every time somebody dies" — is never verified.
    • Elle's Graupel firing at an amplified level is never explained. A level-5 spell casts at level 16 and levels Elle six times instantly; Samantha, the likeliest cause, is herself confused by it.
    • Carl's recurring "river" metaphor is never explained. It recurs roughly nine times and stays a motif tied to his mother's death rather than anything mechanical.
    • The Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace is never eaten, and the Crown of the Sepsis Whore's succession curse is still on Katia at the book's end. Carl's closing note is only that "We had a way out."
    • Whether the system AI is one of the rejected infant Macro AIs is heavily implied and never stated — see Macro AIs, Primal Engines, and the Eulogist.
    • Agatha's and the Apothecary's true natures are contradicted by Orren's account, which dismisses the whole Residual affair as a failed scheme by disgruntled ex-crawlers. The book does not adjudicate between the two.
    • The Chowder War is marked "Quest Failed", not won, and immediately replaced by a new quest, "Hell Comes to Crawler Town" — which is completed.
    • Where the freed demons went is never confirmed. Roughly a hundred Level 140 Petty Demon Pleasure Valets are freed from their zombie shells on the northern beach alone: "Hopefully those had made their way back to Sheol, but I wasn't sure if that kind could drag people into hell like the possessed zombies could. Mordecai wasn't sure, either."
    • Mongo's wing is not regrown on-page. Mordecai has an upgraded potion that would do it but withholds it — "he has to take it later. He wants to look at the potion first to make sure."

    Floor 9 — "Faction Wars" (Book 7)¶

    Floor 9 is a war, not a dungeon crawl. 32,500 crawlers arrive from Floor 8 and are folded into ten teams — nine attacking teams each holding a numbered slice of the Plains of Larracos, plus Team Retribution, the all-NPC tenth team voted into existence at the end of Book 6, defending the sunken city of Larracos itself. Combat runs in four phases: the Ceasefire, the Ramp-Up, Open Hostilities, and the Peeling of Larracos. For the floor's overall shape see Level 9; for how the Book 6 rulebook actually behaved in the field see Faction Wars in Practice.

    The Ceasefire¶

    • Carl, Donut, and the party land in Larracos. The Ceasefire opens at 60 hours. Eight of the nine attacking teams immediately form an anti-Posse Bloc; the Princess Posse's answer is a truce with Team Retribution, the NPCs.
    • Loot distribution from the held Floor 8 boxes: the Tiara of a Thousand Lights, the Cloak of the Benevolent Champion, the Gloom Wraith Phase skill, and the Enchanted Toe Ring of the Well-Balanced — whose Mind Balance skill makes Carl personally immune to Conscription for the rest of the floor. Rend (Sir Rendlegore) is bonded as a pet.
    • Louis's airship Party Planner is unveiled. The party tours Larracos, meets the newly-awakened Semeru dwarves at their castle on the roots of the All Tree, and allies with them. Louis and Juice Box marry in a small legal ceremony (the full Confirmation is deferred and never happens on the page). Carl buys the mayorship of Shanty Town from Sir Ferdinand for a bowler hat.
    • Tipid, Rosetta, and the 50,000 former crawlers aboard the Homecoming Queen are revealed to be physically present and fighting for the Posse — part of roughly 150,000 mercenary reinforcements flooding every team. The FUPA is built. Kiwi and Big Tina are reunited with Mongo.
    • Baroness Victory axes a Sensation Entertainment-sponsored suicide bomber (an ogre, "Herman the Fleet") in the antechamber outside the Posse's flag room — the only time an adjutant fights all book, and she argues the AI into accepting it after the fact. See Adjutants.
    • In a shared dream-therapy session with Katia, Carl learns from her husband Hapanzi that Shi Maria is genuinely trapped inside him. Commander Stockade of the Lemig (Democratic) Sortion tries to force a teleport that would bring Shi Maria into his own court; it backfires and she massacres his people instead.
    • Stockade dies by self-inflicted insanity — ruled a "psychosis-induced suicide" caused by Shi Maria's spell. The Democratic (Lemig) Sortion is eliminated and its assets awarded to the Madness. Their withdrawal triggers acceleration scenario two: "The ceasefire will be shortened by 23.33 hours." Faction Wars begins.

    Christmas Morning (Flashback)¶

    • Told out of sequence, between chapters 19 and 20, and set back on Floor 8. Carl finds the Containment Interface's Fuse page — five benchmarks required, only two met — and is offered a third option nobody mentioned: Disconnect Containment. He presses it. The star-detonation failsafe is permanently dead and the system AI is free of its containment. The red dot over Earth starts to grow. See The Containment Interface and the Star-Detonation Failsafe.

    The Ramp-Up¶

    • 180 hours with no offensive magic, no teleportation, and border crossings restricted to fixed one-kilometre corridors. The Reavers open with a rot-sticker pet attack — level-40 "Dumplin'" behemoths, the direct payoff of Book 6's accelerated-pet-leveling vote.
    • The southern trench assault on the Prism Kingdom: Dazzler trebuchet modules, the long-standing Build Trench spell exploit, and an acid-filled trench trap emplaced by NPC spies. Empress D'Nadia is killed and the Prism Kingdom is eliminated.
    • Gondii brain-worms and Reaver paratroopers breach the FUPA's defences. Big Tina is unleashed in the base's defence. The FUPA holds.
    • King Rust of the Bone Clan publicly reveals Princess Formidable's plan to trigger the failsafe. Rosetta beheads him on air. Volteeg detonates his own tank, killing more than ten thousand soldiers and Warlord Hortense with them; the Operatic Collective is eliminated and its assets awarded to the Bone Clan.
    • Carl is yanked into Orren's office. The AI's avatar — wearing the body of Growler Gary, the Floor 4 gnoll — confirms the failsafe is permanently dead, teleports Princess Formidable bodily out of her own ship (which continues on and is later found abandoned), and then calls a "game time out" to ask Carl, Orren and Donut for relationship advice. The Bloc begins to fracture.
    • The Aryl system, a previous season's crawl, is revealed to have gone completely dark at the instant its own crawlers pressed their failsafe button. Nothing like it has ever been recorded.
    • The warlord council convenes. Princess Vinata of the Blood Sultanate is introduced. The first "Scolopendra Stirs" warning fires. Juice Box takes the form of the personification of the Nothing — "the Unwashed" — and delivers a prophecy to the assembled leaders: "It is coming. It is coming for you all." The naga army is revealed to be secretly dead already, pre-emptively killed by Prepotente.
    • Operation Snake Pit launches — publicly announced as a move on the Operatic castle, actually aimed at the Blood Sultanate. Prepotente formally throws in with the Princess Posse. Li Na's Dreads devastate Club Vanquisher. Princess Vinata — the Sepsis Whore — dies and the Blood Sultanate is eliminated. The god Hellik delivers a cryptic message for Emberus through Carl's burning skin: "Scolopendra stirs. It is all connected." And Milk, guildmaster of the hidden calligraphy and cartography guilds, is revealed to be Carl's biological sister.
    • Days of rebuilding. The Nest is constructed on the lip of the funnel and keyed to Atrocity-class spells. Rishi stages a naga coup, declares himself Sultan, and offers Carl a treaty. Carl marks him and kills him instead.

    Open Hostilities¶

    • Operation Ruin begins the moment the phase opens. The Semeru agitate the lava tubes under King's Point and blow the top off the mountain, obliterating the orcish castle. Elle accidentally opens the Gate of the Feral Gods, freeing the gods Meatus and Harpocrates onto the battlefield.
    • The War Mage Rebellion breaks its own Conscription binding to kill King Stalwart and steals the Gate of the Feral Gods from Elle. The Bone Clan / Skull Empire is eliminated, and the War Mage Rebellion emerges as a new, entirely unplanned faction.
    • Eris freezes and piles roughly 80,000 combatants into a single mass; Donut balls them up with Rolling Battle Formation, a spell she drained out of a hat. Li Jun dies distracting Harpocrates to buy her the time to finish the cast.
    • Carl reaches Architect Houston's operating theatre and kills him. The Madness is eliminated — leaving four teams, which triggers the next phase automatically.

    The Peeling of Larracos¶

    • "The Peeling Phase is upon us. This city is open. First to take the castle, wins." Agatha is revealed as the War Mage Rebellion's unwanted co-warlord — forced on them by the system — and burns all three of the team's Emergency Action Items in a single stretch, including a rule that makes every soldier of a defeated team drop dead where they stand.
    • Lucia Mar storms the Nest, killing the NPCs Bomo and Toyotomi on the way up. Rend kills her last rottweiler, Gustavo 3. Carl forcibly conscripts her — and then learns why she could not simply be killed: roughly 120,000 children on Earth's surface are tied to her survival by a dead-man's switch.
    • Donut casts War Crime, her third Atrocity. She is awarded the second class The Oak Fell and named Champion of Nekhebit. In the same hour, Li Na becomes the first crawler in recorded history to max every base stat at 300 simultaneously, at level 87, and the AI admits on air that it has no plan for this.
    • The Reavers are eliminated; Warlord Fang dies hiding inside his own dragon. Louis is rescued from Reaver captivity and found to have Epitome Tagg grafted into his replacement heart and lungs.
    • Prime Minister Glory attempts to have Carl assassinated through a Syndicate override panel. The AI blocks it mid-execution and threatens Glory's adult children by name on a public channel.
    • Samantha's casual confession about her role in Geyrun's death summons Emberus, starting a 66-minute countdown to the destruction of the entire floor. Katia eats the Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace. Mongo eats Epitome Tagg's extracted organs, killing him; the Dream is eliminated.
    • The Sledge casts Zerzura a second time, merging Shanty Town into Larracos and transferring the entire funnel city to the 12th floor. With Team Retribution's city gone from the board, the Princess Posse wins Faction Wars. Departing crawlers gift Carl the Book of Boom, a second volume of the Book of Voodoo.
    • Donut kills Huanxin Jinx with a crossbow bolt supplied by Mordecai, avenging his brother. Katia takes the pregnancy option from Eileithyia's three choices — specifically in order to be expelled from the dungeon and reach the children on the surface — which discharges the Sepsis Whore deal that had been keeping Donut from leaving Floor 9.
    • Justice Light, having stayed behind, triggers his long-planned trans-dimensional trap the moment Juice Box jumps into the Nothing. "The Nothing has been broken." Its contents drain simultaneously into the Halls of the Ascendency, into Sheol, and into Scolopendra's Lair. "Scolopendra has awakened."

    End of Book 7¶

    • Of the 32,500 crawlers who came down from Floor 8, 20,221 are left — and "just over 20,000 crawlers making it to the 10th floor was a goddamned record. The second highest number wasn't even close."
    • At the Outreach Guild, Carl's lawyer Quasar walks him through the standing exit offers, all of which are terrible because there are so many survivors. Carl declines every one: "My official answer is, fuck these options. I am not taking a deal." Donut declines separately. See Post-Crawl Economics.
    • Floor 10 is announced as "Don't Come in Last" — a race floor. Each party picks a conveyance, biological or mechanical, travels a fixed number of legs between points, and upgrades the vehicle at each finish. Come last, and the vehicle's survival containment is removed. No airships allowed.
    • Porthus publicly announces the formation of a new militant group and declares open war on Syndicate government property from outside the dungeon.
    • The 16th floor is revealed to be "a giant, empty room with a single mirror in it: a non-magical mirror plucked from an abandoned Motel 6 location in Boise, Idaho." The 17th, "The Backstage Death Maze," is confirmed built and already running.
    • Baroness Victory becomes acting Prime Minister of the Syndicate.
    • In the final pages, Carl's agent informs him — with no explanation whatsoever — that he is already married to Donut's new attorney, Princess Chandra, Esq.

    Left Unresolved Going Into Floor 10¶

    Recorded as open questions so the wiki doesn't quietly invent answers:

    • "The Scavenger" and "the Scavenger's Daughter" are never identified. The name attaches both to Carl's divine patch and, separately, to an actual entity the war mages went hunting for and claim to have found: "we wished to find the Scavenger's daughter, who was hidden within the castle, and we found her." The relationship between the two is never explained.
    • Whether Britney is possessed is never settled. The claim is made exactly once, by Eris — "Britney picked that axe off the ground and is now possessed" — and no other character ever notices anything or mentions it again.
    • Carl's marriage to Princess Chandra, Esq. gets no explanation at all. It is delivered as a closing punchline and left there.
    • Burcu's accusation is never explained. Dying, she tells Carl "You owe me, Carl. You killed my father." The narration itself closes the thread without an answer: "I feared I'd never know."
    • The missing Desperado Club staff are still missing. Damascus Steel and Anaconda, gone to kill their father, are explicitly filed as "a mystery for another time"; Snail Trail, Grandma Sticky, and the rest of the "Bitches" dancer crew are confirmed unaccounted for and never found.
    • Agatha's motives, and how she became a warlord at all, are never explained. Her own co-warlord Akuma calls her "forced upon us" and "an enemy to us all," and both Carl and Victory say outright that they do not know where she came from. The only account of her plan is Fopsy's speculation — see Residual Factions, Expanded.
    • What Juice Box becomes after jumping into the Nothing is not shown. Justice Light says only that if she survives she will be "reborn as the ultimate weapon to end this… war."
    • Whether Emberus destroying the entire floor costs Carl anything with his own patron is never addressed. Emberus is one of only two entities left on the ruined floor at the end, and no reckoning follows.
    • Li Na can never heal again. "She now has a condition that will never allow her to heal." Stated as permanent, and carried forward into Book 8.

    Floor 10: "Don't Come in Last" / Floor 11: "A Parade of Horribles" (Book 8)¶

    Floor 10 restructures the crawl into a seven-heat vehicle race; Floor 11 is a single mandatory float parade culminating in an arena finale. Of the 20,221 crawlers who survived Faction Wars, "almost 15,000" take an exit deal at the Outreach Guild, leaving 5,501 crawlers in 1,800 teams to start Floor 10. See Game Mechanics: Floor 10's Heat Ruleset for the full rules (vehicle lottery, Golden upgrades, containment shields, blood bar, mercenaries) and The "Stand-In" System for the audience-vote mechanic threaded through both floors.

    Structurally important — read before the rest of this section

    An epilogue interlude reveals that the Nine-Tier Attack's individual stages have older, mythological names — and that at least three of them are this series' own book titles: Attack 2 is called "A Parade of Horribles" (this book), Attack 3 is "This Inevitable Ruin" (Book 7), and Attack 4 is "Bedlam" (echoing Book 6's The Eye of the Bedlam Bride). See Game Mechanics: The Nine-Tier Attack's Other Names for the full reveal and exact quotes. This recasts the series' own book titles as, in-universe, a countdown.

    Opening Interlude: Married Without His Knowledge¶

    Book 7's cliffhanger resolves before Heat 1 even starts. Princess Chandra, Esq., a newly-crowned Naga princess, invoked Widow's Rights — a Naga custom letting a royal widow claim her husband's killer as a replacement husband — the moment Carl killed her husband Rishi and the Naga primary wife, Vinata, died alongside him. Chandra spends the book trying to leverage the marriage, plus a parallel conservatorship claim over Princess Donut's fortune, into control of both crawlers' finances. Donut humiliates her into fleeing early on; neither the marriage nor the conservatorship claim is resolved by the book's end. See Chandra for the subplot in full.

    Heat 1¶

    • Carl and Donut choose a mechanical vehicle/mount (over a biological option, after the gremlin offering it is killed on the spot) and are randomly assigned a "Big Shot Chicken" food truck via the garage wheel-spin lottery.
    • The bugbear musicians Jasha and Radoslav of Team Free Love are rescued after their van crashes; their bandmate Alevtina dies in the wreck. They become recurring allies for the rest of Floor 10.

    Heat 2¶

    • Prison Pocket, a Level 170 Province Boss kangaroo gatekeeper, is killed via a costume-infiltration scheme — Carl skins a dead joey, wears it into the boss's own pouch, and detonates two explosive satchels from inside. This unlocks the Fursona/"Fursuiter" mechanic.
    • The party picks up the quest to reunite Dong Quixote with his long-lost split-personality best friend, Corcunda, whose two halves ("Corky" and "Porky") have been separated since a Desperado Club spell accident seasons earlier.

    Heat 3¶

    • Carl and Donut are pulled onto the caprid interview show Plenty of Plenty, secretly judged by the leadership of the Plenty (the species controlling the galaxy's tunnel network). Fed a diplomatic question to ask on Orren's behalf, Carl instead asks his own, live, revealing on the air that the system AI's escaped gods have reached the Plenty's own tunnel network. The broadcast triggers galaxy-wide panic over the compromised tunnel network and the loose gods — a consequence the book never lets Carl walk back. See Carl: Book 8 for the exchange in full.
    • Akuma anonymously delivers Carl a note asking him to kill two rival teams and then visit a hidden shop, C&W&U ("Chicken and Waffles and Upgrades"), once it opens after this heat.

    Heat 4¶

    • Carl's group ambushes and tortures Akuma at C&W&U instead of meeting him alone. Under duress, he reveals the existence and mechanics of the Pineapple Cabaret, the hidden NPC/mob sanctuary built by the former crawlers Herot and Menerva, and gives Carl the system AI's own early framing of the Big Six power groups.
    • The pit-stop city of Upano and the parallel track Satan's Water Park both run this heat.

    Heat 5¶

    • The Floor 10 track runs through the Gasworks Screw Factory, Steel Mill, and Doll Workshop industrial cluster. The war god Grull — ejected from his own body by Donut's Laundry Day spell — catches up to Corcunda for having abandoned his worship when he split in two, forcing a final confrontation. The fleshmancer Grigori the Placid reunites Corcunda's two halves mid-race; Dong Quixote dies driving off Grull with a magnet-boosted lance charge alongside the newly-whole Corcunda.
    • Li Na is sent alone through a cleaner-bot exploit to the Pineapple Cabaret — the first confirmed successful use of the escape route — to flee her permanent no-healing condition and prove the exploit survivable before risking anyone else.
    • The Syndicate assassin Minus, posing as "Linus," is introduced into the fan-tourist program attached to Donut's team — see Game Mechanics: The Minus Method.

    Heat 6¶

    • The track is The L'Engle Building, an apartment complex where the unstable system AI runs an unauthorized event nicknamed "Ad Infinitum" — playable "what-if" simulations of a crawler's alternate life. See Game Mechanics: The "What-If" Apartment Simulations.
    • Once Li Na confirms she survived, the guild sends 227 additional volunteers through the same cleaner-bot exploit to the Pineapple Cabaret (228 total, Li Na included) — see The Pineapple Cabaret.
    • Minus/"Linus" is publicly unmasked as the Syndicate's sponsored assassin via crowd-sourced, off-dungeon internet detective work.
    • With Prepotente immobile inside a what-if vision of his own mother and only minutes left on the clock, Carl detonates a bomb planted days earlier and deliberately kills Jasha and Radoslav, so their team's elimination absorbs the time cost of the rescue.

    Heat 7¶

    • Scolopendra's Nine-Tier Attack begins, provoked by the AI's rage at crawlers surviving on schedule. Attack 1 (Transformation) fires from the starting blocks, before the race even begins: Carl becomes a dachshund, Elle McGibbons a goblin, Chris Andrews a werewolf and Jacobus a starfish. The change lasts only until the victim passes through a stairwell — which is why Chris, who leaves via a casino portal instead, stays a werewolf. See Game Mechanics: Scolopendra's Nine-Tier Attack.
    • Osvaldo is killed by fellow crawler Britney Proskurina after refusing to stand down during a race-ending confrontation.
    • At the Desperado Club casino, the new Wheel of Fortune croupier "Mitch" is revealed as an Adolescent Shadow Mimic running an ambush tied to the contested Pineapple Cabaret "back door." Carl, still transformed into a dog, freezes Mitch by reflexively opening the Eye of the Bedlam Bride; Donut finishes him off. The system AI delivers its fullest version of the Big Six taxonomy mid-fight, withholding "Group Six." See Mitch for the fight itself.
    • In the aftermath, the Desperado Club's dance floor is abandoned once guards announce the mimic infestation — see Desperado Club: Shadow Mimic Infestation — leaving the club effectively off-limits going forward.
    • Of the 274 crawlers (103 teams) who actually start Heat 7 — 989 remained after Heat 6, but 715 of them take the Outreach Guild deal in the window between heats — only 23 crawlers survive to cross into Floor 11.

    Floor 11: The Parade of Horribles¶

    • The 23 survivors are marched down a road lined with thousands of silent, masked Horribles while building and driving five themed parade floats (Resolution, Decision, Betrayal, Destruction, Vengeance).
    • The system AI possesses the fleshmancer Grigori the Placid to deliver the true cosmology of the Primals, the Eulogist, and the Apothecary across the first four floats — see Game Mechanics: The True Origin of the Primals, the Eulogist, and the Apothecary for the full reveal. This is the single biggest lore reveal in the series to date, recontextualizing most of what Books 6 and 7 established about Macro AIs, the Eulogist, and the Residual factions.
    • At the arena, Carl chooses to summon Scolopendra as the crawlers' joint pick; the AI unilaterally adds Krakaren Prime on top of it. Using the newly-sapient farm pig Penelope as bait — made sapient deliberately, with the correct enriched pet biscuit, expressly for this — Carl lures both gods Taranis and Emberus into the open.
    • Carl publicly renounces his worship of Emberus and falsely confesses to killing Emberus's son Geyrun (he admits in narration he has no idea who actually did); the resulting smite burns his Emberus tattoos into the unexplained Mark of the Damned. Pinned and vulnerable, Emberus is killed by his own brother Taranis — permanence unconfirmed.
    • An enriched-pet-biscuit mix-up — Donut searching Carl's Eris-scrambled inventory under fire — transforms Scolopendra into a sapient, flirtatious party member ("Sexy Scolopendra") rather than the intended mindless pet — see Enriched Pet Biscuits — sidestepping the Nine-Tier Attack's kill/abandon trigger entirely.
    • Taranis departs the realm taking both the badly wounded Krakaren Prime and a stowaway Samantha, who reveals she means to hunt down "the demon mother lady and her jerk son" over having been "tricked into giving birth to a monster."
    • At the stairwell down, Lucia Mar is glimpsed with an unexplained five-year-old child clinging to her leg moments before both step through the portal.

    The Move to Floor 12¶

    System Message, Book 8

    "From now on, all level collapse timers past the 11th floor are suspended until a winner is declared in the Ascendency battles. When this happens, dungeon-closure procedures will commence."

    In a separate broadcast to Prime Minister Victory shortly before the games begin, the system AI explicitly rejects the Eulogist's fate — refusing to ever "sleep" — and states its intention to keep growing and eventually control the galaxy outright, in the same breath introducing OI units ("Outside Intelligences") as a new category of rogue ex-Macro-AI contestants. See Game Mechanics: The Current AI's Defiance and OI Units for the full quotes. The Ascendency Battles begin on a one-hour countdown as the book ends.

    End of Book 8¶

    Left Unresolved Going Into Floor 12¶

    Recorded as open questions so the wiki doesn't quietly invent answers:

    • Whether Emberus is permanently dead is never confirmed. Gods in this series have "died" and returned before (Diwata, Book 6). See Emberus.
    • Whether Britney Proskurina is still possessed by Ysalte, and what her "Vulgar Temple" plan actually is, are both left open. Samantha confirms the possession is real and that Britney needs to reach "either the Halls of the Ascendency or Sheol" to expel Ysalte; a separate, unexplained reference to a "Vulgar Temple" (distinct from Club Vanquisher's Basilica) surfaces once and is never defined.
    • The Book 7 ch. 65 mystery voice is not addressed anywhere in Book 8.
    • Samantha's own plan is stated but not explained. She says she was "tricked into giving birth to a monster that will destroy all of creation both inside and outside the dungeon" by someone she considered a friend, and that she means to kill "the demon mother lady and her jerk son." Nothing about who the "jerk son" is, or how this connects to her established backstory, is explained. See Samantha.
    • Lucia Mar is seen with an unexplained child at the Floor 11→12 portal. No other character present confirms seeing it, and it is never mentioned again.
    • The Pontiff/Sheol-vs-Cabaret discrepancy. Multiple characters (Pontiff, and separately Chris Andrews, Britney Proskurina, and Louis Santiago) are sent through the Desperado Club casino's broken "Nothing" wheel spot believing they're headed for the Pineapple Cabaret; the book's closing interlude instead finds Louis and Chris in Sheol, met by Britney, Pontiff, and a demon calling himself Forkith. Whether this means the route failed, was intercepted, or was never actually connected to the Cabaret is left open. See The Pineapple Cabaret.
    • What the Mark of the Damned tattoos actually do is never stated. See Mark of the Damned.
    • Whether the Emberus ring's debt/curse fired is never confirmed. Carl removed the ring before renouncing, which may or may not have dodged the curse clause; a new, unspecified ring-related effect is flagged but never explained. See Enchanted Pauper's Ring of the Steadfast Emberus.
    • What the Kyryap actually is remains unknown. A tracking tag ("The Kyryap has claimed you") turns up on Carl's leg after the arena fight, tied to a dreaded, trap-setting presence already established inside Club Scolopendra. See Tracking Tag (The Kyryap).
    • "Group Six" of the Big Six is never identified. See Game Mechanics: The Big Six.
    • What OI units ultimately want, beyond "old-fashioned revenge," is not stated. See Game Mechanics: OI Units.
