    Items & Tattoos¶

    Master loot catalog: weapons, armor, potions, scrolls, tomes, and tattoos. Full pages exist for unique/plot-relevant items from Book 1; common or one-off items are listed in the table below. Any full page should include a Scaling section whenever the book describes how the item's effect changes with a relevant skill level or stat, and a Future Enhancements section whenever a later book physically upgrades or enhances the item (e.g. a sharpening stone, enchantment stacking, a crafting event) — as a placeholder note pointing to the book/chapter if that book hasn't been processed yet, or filled in fully once it has. Any item named in a full-page character's Quick Facts box must itself have a full page here, not just a table row.

    Book 3 item/loot catalog¶

    Name Type Notable effect
    The Left Fang of the Green Sultan Weapon (saber, half of a matched pair) Non-magical without its twin. Eva Sigrid's blade, lost to Katia when Katia's Rush attack (aimed at Eva) kills Hekla instead aboard the moving train; Katia later uses it to kill Eva herself in Book 5
    Imani's chain-conjuring scrolls Scroll/consumable Produces heavy-duty magical chain that doesn't break; used to rig a five-legged improvised crane and hoist an interdiction cart over a stairwell for mass Krakaren disposal
    Stuffed Kimaris Figure Unique curio Carl's only Unique-rarity item on Floor 4; a beanbag-toy demon figure won off an NPC. Later (Book 7) identified by Bautista as "one of the brothers of Sheol"
    Valtay Corporation Neural Enhancer #544 (Variant 32.c) Consumable, permanent upgrade Bronze Benefactor Box gift from Valtay Corp; permanently installs "Identify and Analyze Subspace Portals" into Carl's interface, with no way to unequip it
    PVP Coupon Curio/reward voucher Awarded for a party's first player-kill; redeeming it against the named crawler grants gold-tier boxes plus a bonus player level. Donut secretly obtains one bearing Carl's own name, sparking a party trust crisis
    Enchanted Toe Ring of the Splatter Skunk (upgraded) Jewelry Carl wins a Scroll of Upgrade at Tito's casino roulette wheel and boosts his Book 1 toe ring from +3 Strength/+3 Powerful Strike to +10 Strength/+5 Powerful Strike
    Find Crawler Skill Potion Skill potion Katia's Gold Savage Box reward for her first player-kill; grants a level-3 Find Crawler skill (nearby crawlers appear on the map)
    Fragmenting Potion Ball Crafted consumable/thrown weapon Mordecai's crafting-table invention: pour any potion into a hollow rubber ball and throw it at a mob or NPC; sets up Carl's later potion-and-Xistera combo attacks
    Ugly Ass Backpack Crafted armor/container Exploits an inventory trick — a container's contents can be withdrawn independent of the container — to let Katia equip over a ton of scrap dwarf-robot metal as living mass, becoming an 11-foot armored giant
    Jelly Bomb Improvised explosive Carl's first Cookbook-derived invention: a hob-lobber-stuffed tube with a hobgoblin dynamite stick, spike-detonated like a naval mine; used in pairs to derail the ochre-line train
    Gangway Chock Crafted barricade A sheet of thinned swordsman-breastplate metal sized to wedge into train gangway doorways, choking mob access to one at a time
    Map of the Stars Unique map item Looted off the city-boss Mimic Rex; adds boss locations and descriptions to a large map area
    Club Vanquisher Pass Faction tattoo Forms automatically from one of Katia's bounty loot boxes; grants access to Club Vanquisher, the counterpart to the Desperado Club

    Book 4 item/loot catalog¶

    Name Type Notable effect
    Gwendolyn Duet's gear Jewelry/weapon set A glowing metal skull cap with fur lining, plus a new spear won from a platinum quest box after Floor 5's world quest wraps
    Enchanted Toe Ring of the Leprous Bandit Jewelry (toe ring) Two benefits Mordecai calls "almost legendary tier": Sticky Feet (once/6 hrs, walk on walls/ceilings for Dexterity×2 seconds) and Super Spreader (pass an active debuff to a target once/hour). Used to firebomb the sand-ooze "Mrs. Ghazi" from her own ceiling
    Surefire Crafting item Adds a "Guided" targeting status to any projectile or trap, powered or not, without adding range; the basis of Carl's homemade guided missiles and targeted traps
    Enchanted Roll of Never-ending Duct Tape Crafting/utility item 50 meters of effectively inexhaustible enchanted duct tape
    Veriluxx RealPet Companion (rigged robot-Donut toy) Sponsor prop turned weapon A free "product sample" toy from sponsor Veriluxx; Carl rigs its self-destruct panel to trigger near Admin Loita's rebreather, killing her — later called "one of the most brilliant assassinations" an admin's ever seen
    Loita's Kua-tin Rebreather Alien tech, aquatic breathing device Looted off Admin Loita's corpse after her assassination; seems worthless ("could only be used by a knee-high creature with gills") until Book 7, when it's surgically sewn into Louis Santiago's throat to let his transplanted naiad organs function
    Hobgoblin Disco Ball Sapper's-table explosive A crafted smoke-bomb whose residue chemically weakens plastic and panels; loosens the toy's self-destruct panel to kill Admin Loita, and is later thrown at the boss Quan Ch mid-dogfight
    Phase Lava Rock Potion Potion Brewed by Mordecai from a mysterious sponsor "yam"; makes the drinker briefly intangible to lava-rock creatures — used to save Chris Andrews from his captor
