    Locations & Places to Go¶

    A directory of recurring place types a crawler encounters on every floor, plus full pages for specific named venues that are places to go rather than organizations with their own agenda (an organization that runs a location, like Rev-Up Moonshine Company, still gets filed under Dungeon Factions instead — the distinction is whether the entry is about the place or the people/group running it).

    Clubs¶

    Members-only social venues, gated by a pass/tattoo rather than run by any single named organization — see Dungeon Factions instead for locations that are actually run by a group with its own agenda.

    • Desperado Club — Floor 3's cross-instance nightclub/bazaar
    • Club Vanquisher — its upscale, clergy-heavy rival

    Both clubs recur on Floor 5 with branches in Hump Town (Desperado Club) and the pazuzu town of Pandinus (a Club Vanquisher, temporarily closed for "cleansing" after Prepotente's hellspawn familiar attacks a group of clerics inside).

    On Floor 8 both clubs change permanently. The Desperado Club is destroyed and closed after the Astrid assassination goes wrong and Carl releases a holding pen of vorpals inside it. Club Vanquisher, meanwhile, is finally shown from the inside — including its Emberus High Temple of the Ending Sun — reached through temple entrances seeded into the floor's own geography rather than through a pass.

    On Floor 9 both clubs are back. The Desperado Club reopens on ring 4 of Larracos, with the Midnight Market hidden on its bottom regular floor, a Gray Market on its middle floor, and a Cosmic Lounge in the basement that lets non-warlords teleport straight to the 18th. Club Vanquisher gets two entrances per floor as usual — one inside the city, which "would almost always get trashed," and one outside the city limits, which this season is the Temple of the Dagda.

    On Floor 6, the Desperado Club's Zockau branch introduces a new rule: each dungeon floor a crawler has visited maps to a corresponding physical floor of the club itself, and — after Carl's opening-day raid strands a group of hunters inside — crawlers and hunters are barred from mixing between those floors, reached via ordinary doors on the crawler side and a secret stairwell hidden in a bathroom toilet tank for anyone who needs to reach a floor they're not supposed to access directly. See Desperado Club for detail.

    Recurring Place Types¶

    Safe Rooms / Safe Zones¶

    Combat cannot occur inside a Safe Zone, and they're the only places loot boxes can be opened. They always appear on the minimap automatically, even under fog of war. Size and amenities vary widely — Carl and Donut's first real base is built from an intact Polish fast-food restaurant with a playground; their first Floor 2 base is a storm shelter. Larger Safe Rooms may include restrooms, sleeping cubicles, food, and healing-potion fountains. Restrooms are notably the one place camera drones can't follow a crawler.

    Tutorial Guilds¶

    Seeded on Floors 1–3 specifically, run by a Non-Combatant NPC guide (like Mordecai). A Tutorial Guild is where a brand-new crawler first unlocks inventory/menu access and gets walked through core mechanics — it's a one-time onboarding experience, distinct from the many ordinary Training Guilds scattered elsewhere.

    Training Guilds¶

    Ordinary guildhalls found scattered across every floor (not just 1–3) — Mordecai's own Tutorial Guild is itself a Training Guild, just the first one a crawler visits. Once a crawler has been "trained" (assigned a registered guide), entering any other Training Guild on that floor automatically redirects them back to their own guide's room instead: "if you enter any additional training guilds, you'll be transported back to this room." They're common enough that groups scouting a floor track which quadrants still have accessible ones nearby.

    Reward Rooms¶

    A rarer room type offering items not obtainable from ordinary loot boxes. Discovering one triggers a distinct achievement:

    "You have discovered a reward room! Scattered throughout the dungeon, reward rooms offer crawlers items generally not available within loot boxes. Most reward rooms only allow one choice."

    Pet reward rooms are a named subtype — caged, selectable pet-class creatures (this is where Mongo is found):

    "You have discovered a pet reward room. From caterpillars who secrete vodka to basilisks who can turn mobs to stone, a good pet can make the difference between survival and the end of the road."

    Other Named Locations¶

    Organization-run — see their own pages instead of duplicating detail here.

    • Floor 2: Rev-Up Moonshine Company — the pyramid-scheme distillery run by the Krakaren Clone
    • Floor 3: Grimaldi's Traveling Circus and the Medium Skyfowl Settlement — see Level 3: Layout & Notable Areas for both, and Dungeon Factions for the organizations that run them
    • Floor 5: Hump Town, a Dromedarian brothel settlement, and the Necropolis of Anser, the ancient tomb at the heart of the bubble — both "places to go" in their own right rather than organization-run venues
    • Floor 6: Point Mongo, a conquered Ursine settlement serving as Carl and Donut's base of operations; Fort Freedom, the ruined Naiad river palace liberated during the Vengeance of the Daughter quest; and the High Elf Castle, site of the Butcher's Masquerade and the book's climax
    • Floor 8: El Capitolio Nacional de Cuba, Colón Cemetery / the Temple of Yemaya, the FOCSA building, and the transplanted Dallas County, Iowa — plus the shorter entries below
    • Floor 9: Larracos, the City of Dreams, the funnel city the whole of Faction Wars is fought over; Shanty Town, the new ring around its lip; The Plains of Larracos, the nine-slice battlefield outside it; The Nest, the Princess Posse's Atrocity-keyed synergy tower; and Area 52, their forest airbase

    For a floor-by-floor breakdown of layout (as opposed to organized by place type, as above), see the Dungeon Floors section — each floor's page has its own "Layout & Notable Areas" section.

    Book 6 (Floor 8): Cuba¶

    Floor 8's phase-one and phase-three region for Carl and Donut is a replay of Havana and its surroundings, exact down to the Christmas decorations. See Level 8 for the floor's overall shape.

    Location What it is
    El Capitolio Nacional de Cuba The floor's starting chamber and one of the region's two stairwells. Destroyed in phase three; its doorway's destruction takes every key cut for it. Full page.
    Colón Cemetery A corrupted Medium Ghommid Settlement, liberated and renamed the Temple of Yemaya. Holds a Club Vanquisher entrance and the Yellow Zone saferoom pub. Full page.
    The FOCSA building A green-and-tan, Y-shaped, 32-storey residential tower; the site of the book's final battle. Full page.
    Old Havana The downtown zone immediately outside the Capitolio ("Entering Old Havana"), adjoining Miramar. A named starting area rather than a destination.
    Cuba Libro "A small coffee shop name 'Cuba Libro'," with a mob-filled park a street over — a named saferoom, never described inside.
    Iglesia San Hilarion A phase-three drop point: the interior of an old, empty Catholic church, moonlight through stained glass, roughly 50 km southwest of the exit stairwell.
    Iglesia de Jesús de Miramar The region's other stairwell, and the only surviving exit once the Capitolio falls. Its grounds hold a temple of Ibeji and a temple of the ocean goddess Kuraokami — the latter doubling as a Club Vanquisher entrance. Both gods are named in a single sentence and nowhere else. The church is deliberately demolished so a channel can be dug beneath it for the escape plan.
    Guanajay A small town north-west of Havana, reached past midnight, with a huge pileup of cars and a population of duendes. A waypoint on the way to intercept Quan Ch.
    The northern beach A rocky, empty stretch of coast where Carl and Donut capture monk-seal totems — and, later, where roughly a hundred Petty Demon Pleasure Valets are freed from their zombie shells during the Demon Eviction.
    The Bay of Pigs marshlands The land-crab migration route and the web-choked swamp realm of the boss Shi Maria — webs strung over roads and trees "like a cotton candy machine had exploded," broken crab husks, a moped cocooned in silk. See Bosses.

    Book 6 (Floor 8): Phase Two — Florida and Iowa¶

    Location What it is
    Monroe County, Florida (the Florida Keys) Carl and Donut's phase-two region. Arrival is inside a dark Victorian mini-mansion; outside is a narrow beach town on a two-lane road with ocean close on both sides. Donut: "we're in the Miami area, but we're at the bottom of the map. We're really in the Florida Keys!"
    Dallas County, Iowa A 700-foot mesa of Iowa farmland dropped bodily onto the Keys by a placement glitch, complete with snow, a commercial farm, a turkey barn, a trailer park, and Carl's father's trailer spliced in from another season. Full page.
    Homestead, Florida — "The Shepherd's House" A homeless shelter just south of Miami, found via a flyer: "Don't spend Christmas alone… Food, a warm bed, and fellowship. Intake open 24 hours." A graffiti-covered building in an industrial fruit-packing district, with a wreath on the door and a memory-ghost desk clerk watching anime. Hidden in the graffiti, in Syndicate Standard, is the real message: "Look for me at breakfast every morning. I wear the red hat with the flaps. Coffee then speech." Inside, rows of cots full of sleeping ghosts. This is where Carl hears the Residual Paulie speak — and where Orren pulls him out before the end. See The Residuals as a Plot Mechanic.

    Book 6: Off-Floor and Recurring Locations¶

    Location What it is
    Club Vanquisher and the High Temple of the Ending Sun The rival club, shown from the inside for the first time, along with the Emberus temple that is Carl's de facto church. Full page.
    The Hunting Grounds Casino The Desperado Club's middle-floor casino. Carl has "never actually gone into this casino before" and still doesn't — it is only ever seen from the corridor outside, during a security alarm.
    The Mock Battle Arena A practice arena attached to every saferoom on Floor 8. The base room is a featureless gray hangar; a menu lets a squad pick an environment and adjust gravity, terrain, and crowd favourability by slider. Confirmed presets: the Arena of the Eulogist (a colosseum with a hostile Nebular crowd whose friendliness is the only adjustable slider), Verdant Hills (grassy rises that break sightlines), and a full 1980s shopping mall (arcade, Kay-Bee Toys, Waldenbooks, Orange Julius, a Santa line). See The T'Ghee Card System for pricing and rules. No "abandoned arena" preset appears under that name in the text.
    The Mercenary Arcade Not an independent venue but a purchased training room inside Carl's own guild space, named by Katia. Larger than expected, staffed by an AI drill sergeant who appears in multiple instances at once and can genuinely kill trainees (who "regenerate a minute later"); mercenaries also spar to the death for practice. Carl is warned he "wouldn't be safe in here," and later locks the Penis Parade dancers inside it as a containment measure.
    The underwater production facility A complex on the ocean floor beneath a space station parked over the Indian Ocean, reached by transferring through the station. It houses the production trailer where Odette's show is taped (dressed to look like Larracos) and the sponsor meeting room Carl knows from CrawlCon — a round ten-seat table with holographic remote attendees, run by a robot called Urkel under the executive Cascadia.
    Larracos Still flooded from Book 5's Gate of the Feral Gods; its restaurants reopen but the attendants' boardroom remains unavailable, and the master pulpmancer who could have freed Shi Maria is presumed drowned. New this book: the goddess Ysalte, "The Downward Spiral," is native to Larracos and worshipped by its earliest citizens; and the battlefields outside it hold the Semeru Dwarves' castle, built along the roots of the All Tree. In the epilogue vote, Larracos is designated the home base of the new all-NPC tenth Faction Wars team. "Larracos level" is also Desperado Club slang for a punitive club tier guarded by Armored Sai rhinoceros samurai.
    Sheol, the 15th floor Named and described but never visited — "There's no tourist activity at all on that floor… we never get to see Sheol itself." The closest the book gets is a remote viewing of a lava-rock hellscape crawling with tiny flaming centipedes. Portals to it read "Entrance to Sheol, Floor 15" and pull like a black hole. See Sheol and the Fifteenth Floor.
    Wenatchee, Washington — the Meadowlark Elder Care facility An epilogue location, and the same facility the Meadowlark crawler group is named for. Agatha spent almost ten Earth years disguised as a homeless woman in the alley behind it, sitting by a barrel near a lit kitchen window, shown small kindnesses by two staff brothers named Brandon and Chris. She was there on the night of the collapse.
    The Kinder Facility, Mumbai Where disqualified crawlers are exported: "You will be disqualified and exported to the Kinder Facility located in the former city of Mumbai, India on the surface of the planet." Glimpsed once more in the epilogue, with humans "constantly battling the security forces, attempting to break into the kinder facility." No interior is ever shown.
    The Scolopendra Club Mentioned exactly once, and only as leverage: "do you know who is currently trapped in the Scolopendra Club? Over half of the entire galaxy's wealth is already gathered, and that includes my own children." It is the reason the Syndicate Council will not pull the failsafe. No physical description exists.
    The Penis Parade and the Guild of Suffering lair The Desperado Club's first-floor strip club — dark, bass-heavy, no pole, with a tusked mohawked troll dancer on the main stage and a young crocodilian "Bucket Boy" working tips — and a front for the Guild of Suffering, whose entrance sits inside it and requires an elbow tattoo. The Night Wyrm's own lair is "hidden up there on the top floor. The vorpals won't go up there," and is never toured. See Dungeon Factions for the organization.

    Recorded for continuity. Note that phase one, phase two, and phase three are three different places for most squads, and the phase-two assignments were widely corrupted by the same placement glitch that moved Iowa.

    Crawler Phase one Phase two Phase three
    Katia Reykjavík, Iceland Chose Ukraine, landed in a Ukraine/Vietnam hybrid; key at Da Nang Back in Iceland
    Imani C Western US (the Great Wolf Lodge) Detroit — given only one choice, the city she grew up in Detroit, at her family home
    Elle McGibbons Pacific Northwest, near Portland Belgium Olympia, Washington
    Li Jun, Li Na, Chris Andrews The Galaxy Soho building, Beijing "Still in China, but further into the mainland" — never named more precisely Back at Galaxy Soho, for the Battle of Beijing
    Florin Ecuador Eastern Nigeria —
    Prepotente The Bahamas Northern Italy Back in the Bahamas
    Lucia Mar — Never stated; she travels alone as "the most isolated crawler" Beijing, where she slaughters the crawler population in the Battle of Beijing

    Other regions named in passing: Turkey (Hekla's daughters' squad in phase three), Tokyo and Brazil on recap footage, and one unnamed squad leader who was offered the entire globe and "dropped herself into the middle of the ocean for some reason."

    Book 7 (Floor 9): Larracos and the Plains¶

    Floor 9's geography is unusually simple to state and unusually consequential: one sunken funnel city, one new ring of slums around its lip, and nine pie-slices of open plain radiating outward, one per attacking team. See Level 9 for the floor overview.

    Location What it is
    Larracos, the City of Dreams A funnel-shaped city sunk into a volcanic pit, built as 66 rings numbered from the bottom up. Held by the all-NPC Team Retribution; still flooded to ring 3 from Book 4. Transferred bodily to the 12th floor at the end of the book, leaving an empty pit. Full page.
    Shanty Town A settlement that generated itself around the top lip after the flooding. Carl buys the mayorship from Ferdinand for a bowler hat. Has no throne room — it can only be taken by killing the mayor. Full page.
    The Plains of Larracos Nine numbered territories plus the city. Mostly flat except the rocky, lava-prone north. Border crossings are restricted to fixed one-kilometre corridors during the Ramp-Up. Full page.
    The Nest The Princess Posse's master-quality granite synergy tower, keyed to Atrocity-class spells and built at the edge of the hole into Larracos. Donut's perch. Full page.
    Area 52 The Posse's forest airbase, missile battery, and rear saferoom. Full page.
    The FUPA The Princess Posse's main castle in spot 3 — the Fortress of Unyielding Power and Authority, named for the acronym. Site of Baroness Victory's one kill, a Gondii brain-worm infiltration, and a Reaver paratrooper drop. See The Plains of Larracos.
    King's Point The orcs' pre-built mountain castle in spot 1 — the only territory that came with a castle. Destroyed by a deliberately engineered volcanic eruption, "leaving nothing but a throne room just sitting there in open air."
    Architect Houston's operating theatre The Madness warlord's lair, beside his throne room, which nobody had ever breached. Carl does, kills him there, and finds it rigged to a dead-man's switch.
    The Reaver honeycomb The Reavers' castle: dozens of honeycomb cells stacked three or four storeys high that constantly and randomly re-arrange themselves, with the throne room behind the centre cell.
    The Sugar Cube The Floor 6 High Elf Castle, Zerzura'd down to Floor 9 at the end of Book 5. It lands in Lemig Sortion territory and becomes their HQ, and is thereafter referred to as the Dream castle in spot 6 — a chain of custody the book never reconciles. Demolished by fighting gods.
    The Temple of the Dagda The season's out-of-city Club Vanquisher entrance temple. Dagda is "a weather and 'masculinity' god" whose only membership requirement is a beard.
    The Temple of Khepri An endless buffet: "one long table covered with a never-ending feast. And that feast is covered with 433 different kinds of bugs." Reached by a ritual in which female adherents swallow the god's beetles.
    The Midnight Market The hidden bazaar on the Desperado Club's bottom regular floor — "Access to the Midnight Market. Safety or Sanity not guaranteed." Run by the merchant Paca, an agent for Krakaren Prime, and the source of the hell-kissed Conscription potions.
    The calligraphy and cartography guilds Two adjoining hidden guildhalls with the same guildmaster, Milk, who hasn't had a floor-supervisor meeting in over fifty seasons. The calligraphy guild teaches Words of Power. Their physical location is never stated — only that both doors are hidden.

    Book 7: Off-Floor and Other Locations¶

    Location What it is
    The Outreach Guild Where a departing crawler negotiates their exit deal, with an assigned attorney. Physically a bare, doorless "zero zone" cubicle. Carl's lawyer is Quasar; Carl refuses every offer — "My official answer is, fuck these options. I am not taking a deal." See Post-Crawl Economics.
    The production facility The Borant/Valtay broadcast site where the season's shows are taped, including Odette's — the same complex introduced in Book 6 beneath a space station over the Indian Ocean. "Entering Production Facility."
    Orren's office The liaison's office Carl keeps getting yanked into without warning — multicoloured wood panelling and a framed painting of Astrid. This is where Carl is introduced to the AI's avatar, Growler Gary.
    The Squirties waystation "Squirties waystation and refueling platform. Located just outside the main tunnel access route entrance to the Earth system." The setting of the book's closing scene: its saccathian owner Zander is going bankrupt under the Earth-system embargo and facing indenture, and then Eris walks in.
    The kinder facility (Earth's surface) Where children and pregnant women — ineligible to crawl — are held. Zev puts the count at "About 120,000. Give or take a few." It is tied to a dead-man's switch on Lucia Mar's survival, and it is what Katia deliberately makes herself ineligible in order to reach: "There are no children or pregnant women allowed here … But there are several children, up there … I think they're in trouble … this is the only way I can get to them." (She never names the facility at her departure; the connection is strongly implied rather than stated outright.)
    The Aryl system A previous season's crawl — a battle-royale rather than a dungeon — revealed to have gone completely dark at the exact instant its own crawlers triggered their failsafe: "They tried triggering it, and nothing happened here. But the moment they pressed the button, the Aryl system went dark." Nothing has been heard from it since. This is the single fact that collapses the Syndicate's failsafe debate. See The Containment Interface and the Star-Detonation Failsafe.
    The 16th floor — "Agony of Mirrors" Revealed, in a deliberately anticlimactic aside, to be nothing at all: "the 16th Floor, supposedly entitled 'Agony of Mirrors' is actually a giant, empty room with a single mirror in it: a non-magical mirror plucked from an abandoned Motel 6 location in Boise, Idaho."
    The 17th floor — "The Backstage Death Maze" Confirmed fully built and already running, in the same breath: "Unfortunately, the 17th floor, called 'The Backstage Death Maze' is currently built and running. You will have to proceed through this level should you want to get to the 16th."
    The Scolopendra Club (18th floor) Finally described rather than merely cited: a members-only VIP viewing facility holding over half the galaxy's wealth, with food menus, an activity schedule, personal-trainer feeds and gossip-trained massage NPCs — all "programmed from orbit" and therefore usable as a covert channel into the dungeon. Also used as a sentence ("Death. Scolopendra Club. The Cabaret backstage. Or Earth's surface. Pick."), and evacuated at the end of the book when Scolopendra wakes.

    Book 8 (Floor 10-11): Don't Come in Last / A Parade of Horribles¶

    Floor 10 replaces the usual open dungeon with a seven-Heat vehicle race, funneled through a single hub town and a rotating set of themed tracks; Floor 11 is a single mandatory float parade ending in an arena. See Level 10 and Level 11 for the floor overviews once those pages exist.

    Location What it is
    Hungry Eyes Village The floor's hub town — a "1980s neon-infused, cyberpunk fever dream" holding the Team Roundabout garages, the vendor village, the Hairpin and Lollipop bars, and the mercenary market. Full page.
    Chicken and Waffles and Upgrades (C&W&U) A hidden upgrade shop behind an ordinary diner front, tucked into Hungry Eyes Village's vendor strip — its jukebox is the real door, and its back room is the war mage Akuma's hideout. Full page.
    Upano A jungle pit-stop city shared by four parallel Heat 4 tracks at once, ringed by unexplained black tubes and cliffs. Full page.
    Satan's Water Park A Heat 4 track owned by the invulnerable, comic-relief country boss Satan (see Bosses) — waterslide tubes, a River of Sloth, and a mountain-sized British hedgehog narrating the whole thing. Full page.
    Gasworks Screw Factory, Steel Mill, and Doll Workshop Heat 5's single combined industrial track — one dilapidated factory building, not several separate sites — and the site of Dong Quixote and Corcunda's reunion and double death fighting the war god Grull. Full page.
    The L'Engle Building Heat 6 and 7's shrunk-scale apartment-complex track, home to the AI's unauthorized "Ad Infinitum" what-if simulations — see The "What-If" Apartment Simulations. Full page.
    Club Scolopendra A luxury resort built literally inside Scolopendra's own body, reached via Oozy Form during the Floor 11 arena climax — this wiki treats it as the same venue as "the Scolopendra Club" documented above under Book 7's 18th floor, with Book 8 finally revealing the mechanism (see the location page for the reasoning). Also where "the Kyryap" is first established as a real presence. Full page.
    The Pineapple Cabaret's "back door" Referenced repeatedly but never a physical set — see Dungeon Factions: The Pineapple Cabaret for the hidden pocket-dimension sanctuary itself.
    Floor 11's parade route and arena A road lined with thousands of masked Horribles leading to a colosseum-style arena; no independent location page exists since the route and arena are one continuous set piece covered by the mechanics and boss pages above rather than duplicated here.
