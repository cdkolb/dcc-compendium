    Mobs¶

    Regular (non-boss) monster types encountered across the series. Full pages exist for mobs with real recurrence or plot weight in Book 1; everything else is listed in the table below.

    Full pages¶

    • Brindle Grub — Floor 2's corpse-eating "janitor" mob, matures into Brindled Vespas
    • Tuskling — Orcish Supremacy ruling class; forms the Ball of Swine
    • Rage Elemental — Level 93 elite, bathroom-violation punishment mob
    • Street Urchin — Floor 3's corpse-cleanup "janitor" mob
    • Krasue — undead vampire-women manufactured by Miss Quill
    • Shambling Berserker — undead body-part pile, quadrupled power in darkness
    • Remex — Miss Quill's undead husband, a Soul Leech Capacitor
    • Drek — Floor 4's swarming, demonic-baby horde mob
    • Jikininki — Floor 4's ghoul "janitor" mob, first tier of the Iron Tangle's corpse-disposal system
    • Frenzied Gerbil — Level 11 swarm mob; the System AI's recurring in-joke, from Ralph through Floor 5's gnomish "Live Ammo Balls"
    • Funeral Bell — Floor 6's mushroom-headed town guard, trap-deactivating and faction-agnostic
    • Night Weasel — Floor 6's invisible, paralysis-biting pack hunter
    • Flesher — animated corpse-skin, stage one of the war mage cycle; weaponised at scale on Floor 9
    • War Mage — Level 100, a true dungeon-born entity nobody designed; the Floor 9 wild card
    • Zarogoth — Level 50 soul-generated "sludge ghoul," the swarm mob of the Peeling Phase
    • Hunter Wraith — Floor 10–11 possession monster carrying the Inevitable Ruin curse, the book's title
    • Bijou — tiny fairies that physically carry worship-tendrils between gods and their adherents
    • The Horribles — Floor 11's masked, silent parade avatars; revealed as literal fragments of the system AI's own lost memory

    Book 6 mob catalog¶

    Floor 8's mobs are grouped by region rather than by area, because the floor is a patchwork of recreated Earth locations and crawlers are scattered across all of them. Most of the entries below also double as T'Ghee totem cards — Floor 8 runs on card combat, so a mob's real significance is often what it does once it has been flagged rather than how it fights.

    Cuba / Havana region (Phase One)¶

    Name Level(s) Notable trait
    The Experience 55 Taino folklore — a dog-bodied creature with an oversized human head in a red beret, guardian of the world of the dead. Drops the magical red beret Donut wears on and off through the floor (a small boost to all stats)
    Duende 20 Foot-tall rag-men who tackle through the memory ghosts' clothing and hoard the shoes; physically weak and uncoordinated, and the cause of most of the region's car pileups. One is named Mordecai
    Monk Seal Picket Sentry 70 Caribbean-kung-fu-fighting monk seals sworn to hold the coast against the land crabs. Named individuals: Yago (Sister Ines's totem), Geraldo (Donut's), and Chuy
    Red Maníseros Land Crab 70 "Cultivators" — peanut-farming crabs, masters of Juego de maní, at war with the seals over their ocean breeding grounds. Named individual: Raul, a "Sun-Kissed Cloud Rank Warrior" and one of Donut's six starting totems
    Unsettled Ghommid 30 Non-corporeal visitors from the land of the dead, in endlessly varied one-of-a-kind forms — toddler minotaurs, six-legged foxes, bug heads, a naked obese man walking on his hands with flaming Dumbo ears. Their touch inflicts a powerful freezing effect. Driven mad en masse by Asojano's presence
    Ghommid Splitter 66 A donkey-head-and-snake hybrid ghommid. Named individual: Skylar Spinach, one of Donut's six starting totems. Has the Splitter ability — it breaks into two pieces on a fatal blow and must be killed three times total, and unusually, each iteration is more powerful than the last
    Spirit Box — A trap, not a creature. Roots the target for sixty seconds, lowers spirit resistance, and summons a random non-corporeal entity from the area to possess them — permanently, if it succeeds
    La Ciguapa Legendary A hairy woman with backward feet who lures opponents with a charm attack. Per the Bahamas book, she is hard to kill by any means except familiars and pets — never by a crawler directly. Mongo kills the one they find before it fully wakes
    Pox Slug / Sluggalo 1, then escalating Juggalo-themed slugs that pop out of the boils of anyone infected with SlugPox. Each successive slug is one level higher than the last. Later weaponised: crawlers deliberately infect themselves and rebrand as "Sluggalos," with their leader, Doctor Bones, carrying a superior Super Spreader spell
    Zombie Turkey 10 Undead farm stock; the Havana region's zombie outbreak also produces zombie cows, pigs, fish and seagulls, all of which spread the infection by bite

    Florida & Iowa region (Phase Two)¶

    Name Level(s) Notable trait
    Babababoon 45 Human-faced baboon mob, returning from Floor 4 at a much higher level; one runs a deck as a Florida-region deckmaster
    Reef Explorer 65 A ghost-type class in a glowing deep-dive suit; encountered as a deckmaster
    Swordfish Interloper 50 Bipedal, armless swordfish-headed creatures whose heads stick straight up; attack by leaping through the air like a missile. Reef Explorer's bodyguards and totems
    Skunk Ape 80, rare A rare Florida-region totem card found in the Babababoon's deck; never summoned on the page
    Quench Moth 30 Dog-sized moths; gain a speed and constitution bonus while touching a light source. The Visitor's minions
    Squonk 35 "Eastern United States Region Legendary Creature." Too ugly and miserable to fight — it has no attacks at all, cries constantly, and eventually turns to water and dissipates on its own. Encountered in the mock battle arena
    Wakinyan Temple Guard 80 Turkey-headed temple guards from Tom's deck
    Unk Tehi Temple Guard 85 Lizard-headed equivalents from Sharp-Elbows's deck; the two types scream at each other about which one Mother loved more
    Turkey 5 Thousands of non-hostile white turkeys in the Iowa barn; Carl's Extinction Sigil normally keeps them friendly, but the boss event negates the charm
    Zombie Turkey 10 Raised by Sharp-Elbows from the turkeys Carl kills; they turn on the living birds the moment the survivors revert to neutral, and go on to infect the region's pigs

    Book 8 mob catalog (Floors 10-11)¶

    Floor 10's races run through several recycled and satirical Earth-adjacent regions before the Floor 11 finale. The Horribles, the finale's central masked mob, has its own full page above rather than a table row here.

    Name Level(s) Notable trait
    Screeching Death Manatee 90 A theme-park-satire mob populating Heat 1's track: real manatees relocated to a former Land War terraforming world whose bankrupt orcish owner named everything after its worst trait — "the raccoons are Doom Crier Beasts. The cockatiels are called Fuck-Off Cockatiels." Individually named victims (Miss Talulah, Miss Brianna Kim) are killed by the racers' mounts rather than fought directly
    Sugar Hermit 11 Migratory, skull-wearing swarm creatures — "these horrific little terrors feed off of two things. Brains and bones" — that wear painted Day-of-the-Dead-style skulls "like a snail wears a shell" and migrate toward a kaiju skull blocking the Heat 3 finish line. Not the same mob as any Book 6–7 mock-battle-arena creature; nothing by this name appears before Book 8
    Coblyn Corporation "Toddler Cocaine" doll-elementals 12 A deliberately, self-consciously satirical corporate mob: the knockers (small gnome-like miners displaced from their own caves) turn to drug manufacturing for survival, then legitimize into the Coblyn Corporation, then — having run out of adult customers — begin selling low-dose cocaine to children through toy-doll mascots (Sally Speedball, Toot-Toot, and Belushi, together "the Nose Candy Star Dusters"). The dolls themselves become infused Cocaine Elementals with a contagious "pass-through" ability once elemental magic leaks in their factory. The text's own framing is unusually blunt about the satire: "There's not really a lesson here or a point to any of this, just a sad observation"
    Knocker Unspecified The small, gnome-like miners behind the Coblyn Corporation above; driven from their mineral-rich caves by "larger, stronger, and more powerful creatures," they turn to drug manufacturing and eventually corporate ownership as their only path to survival
    Great Rusty / Ginger cannibal tribes 99 (Great Rusty, boss) Two warring cannibal settlements (north and south) of pale, mask-wearing "Gingers" living in the Heat 3 jungle track, led by chieftains Great Rusty (south, one-shot killed by Donut, triggering a mandatory kill-quest against his rival) and Maurice (north). The book explicitly, self-consciously flags this content as satire rather than playing it straight: Carl calls the AI's own description "both socially conscious and outrageously racist at the same time," and the description itself frames the joke as being about colonial exploitation cycles rather than the Gingers themselves
    Zipper Shrew 10 Small, drill-headed rodents infesting Satan's Water Park (Heat 6); swarm the racers' shielded vehicles in the thousands and are weaponized by Donut's Summon Ye, Vermin spell to finish off the Maurice variant hiding among the water park's ginger "season pass holders"
    Slag Elemental 75 (Neighborhood Boss) Regenerating, molten-metal gorilla-shaped elementals infesting the inverted steel-mill ceiling maze of the Coblyn Corporation factory (Heat 5); a cut-rate security measure that went wrong — "wildly contagious, regenerating, infused, and very pissed-off elementals all over the place." Immortal for practical purposes: "it's pretty much impossible to kill these things" unless the corpse is kept from falling back into the molten metal below
