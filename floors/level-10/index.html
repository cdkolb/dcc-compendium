    Level 10¶

    Quick facts

    • Crawlers entering the floor: 5,501, organized into 1,800 teams of up to four, after almost 15,000 of the 20,221 survivors of Faction Wars took an exit deal at the Outreach Guild. See Timeline: Floor 10 for the full Floor 9-to-10 handoff math
    • Structure: Seven Heats (vehicle/mount races), run out of a home-base cul-de-sac and the neon-lit hub town of Hungry Eyes Village. There is no single floor-wide countdown — each heat has its own time limit, and the floor simply ends the moment Heat 7 does. See Game Mechanics: Floor 10's Heat Ruleset for the full rules (vehicle lottery, Golden upgrades, containment shields, mercenary pricing)
    • Theme: "Don't Come in Last" — Zev's own gloss: "I guess you can figure out what that means." Coming in dead last is normally instant death for the whole team; several heats soften that with a survival containment rule instead
    • Carl and Donut's vehicle: a "Big Shot Chicken" food truck, assigned at random by the garage wheel-spin lottery after they chose mechanical over biological
    • Carl's arrival stats: not shown on the page. Unlike Floor 9, the text never displays a formal "Welcome to Floor 10" system block with Carl's rank, bounty, or view count — the floor opens directly into Heat 1's garage-lottery scene. The only hard numbers given are at the other end: Carl finishes the floor ranked #2 on a leaderboard that is suspended the moment fewer than 30 crawlers remain (see Leaderboard, below)
    • Casualties: the seven heats grind 5,501 crawlers down to 989 crawlers (353 teams) by the end of Heat 6; 715 of those take a deal in the Outreach Guild window that follows, so 274 crawlers in 103 teams actually start Heat 7, and only 23 crawlers survive to cross into Floor 11
    • Exit: no traditional stairwell chambers — the floor ends when explosive charges blast a vertical path through The L'Engle Building up to a cluster of portals on the roof, all of which lead to Floor 11

    AI Commentary (in-voice, written for this wiki — not a book excerpt)

    "You wanted a war, so I gave you Faction Wars. You survived that, so now I'm giving you a demolition derby with a body count. Buckle up, bitches. Literally. There's a seat belt feature now."

    Arrival¶

    Floor 10 opens with no formal welcome broadcast shown on the page — the text picks up mid-scene, in the garage lottery of Heat 1, with Carl and Donut being handed a "Big Shot Chicken" food truck after a gremlin offering a biological mount is killed on the spot. The floor's actual introduction comes from Zev, reading a script over the loudspeaker before the first race:

    "This is the tenth floor. The floor is called 'Don't come in last.' I guess you can figure out what that means. The floor will consist of seven heats, or races, starting with nine teams each. You've been split into teams no bigger than four, and if you're solo, you've been teamed up with one or two other crawlers."

    Within Heat 1, the party rescues the bugbear musicians Jasha and Radoslav of Team Free Love after their van crashes (their bandmate Alevtina dies in the wreck), towing them to the finish line rather than letting a second team fail to finish. They become recurring allies — and, much later, a deliberate casualty of Carl's own bomb.

    Married Without His Knowledge¶

    Book 7's cliffhanger resolves in the safe-room downtime just after Heat 1: a legendary "fan box," shaped like a cobra head, dumps the entire wrecked contents of Rishi's household into Carl's safe room, confirming what Princess Chandra, Esq. had already claimed — that her invocation of Widow's Rights made Carl her husband the instant he killed Rishi. The marriage is never formally undone anywhere in this book; see Chandra's own page for how she tries (and fails) to leverage it into control of Carl and Donut's finances.

    The floor's central premise¶

    Each heat sends every remaining team's vehicle or mount down a track with a fixed time limit, hazards specific to that heat, and (from Heat 4 on) a survival containment bubble that softens — but does not eliminate — the old "come in last, die instantly" rule. Winning a heat earns three regular upgrades or one Golden upgrade; a bonus Golden upgrade is handed out to everyone after Heat 4 regardless of placement. Losing outright (second-to-last, or dead last where survivable) hands the next upgrade choice to an audience vote, which trends punitive — Carl spends the better part of Heat 3 stuck in a disintegrating kangaroo costume as the direct result of one such vote. Full rules are on the mechanics page; this page covers only what actually happens heat by heat.

    The seven heats¶

    Heat 1 — the garage lottery¶

    Vehicle/mount assignment via the giant wheel-spin lottery in the home cul-de-sac ("Team Roundabout"). Carl and Donut get the food truck; Jasha and Radoslav's van crashes and is towed in. Results: 1st One Fine Pig, 2nd The Jugglers, 3rd Lady Dominators and the Gimp, 4th The Wild Hunt, 5th Girth the Trouble, 6th Team Sparkles, 7th The Royal Court of Princess Donut, 8th Team Free Love. Eliminated: The Trauco Triplets.

    Heat 2 — Prison Pocket¶

    Prison Pocket, a Level 170 Province Boss kangaroo gatekeeper, blocks the road with a twin, "Party Foul," on the parallel path. Carl skins a dead joey to make a wearable Enchanted Juvenile Macropus Dominus Skin, hops into the boss's own pouch disguised as a joey, and detonates two explosive satchels from inside before escaping with Gloom Wraith Phase — unlocking the Fursona/"Fursuiter" temporary-class mechanic in the process. Bucket Boy spots Corcunda ("Corky") riding unconscious with the Lady Dominators, opening the two-part quest to reunite him with Dong Quixote (see Quests, below). Results: 1st Team Sparkles, 2nd The Jugglers, 3rd Team Free Love, 4th The Wild Hunt, 5th Lady Dominators and the Gimp, 6th One Fine Pig, 7th Royal Court. Eliminated: Girth the Trouble.

    Heat 3 — live on Plenty of Plenty¶

    Carl and Donut are pulled into a live interlocution on the caprid interview show Plenty of Plenty, secretly the leadership of the Plenty (the species controlling the galaxy's tunnel network). Fed a diplomatic question to ask on Orren's behalf, Carl asks his own instead — live, on-air — revealing that the system AI's escaped gods have reached the Plenty's own tunnel network. The broadcast triggers galaxy-wide panic and gets the liaison Harbinger exploded by the AI mid- threat. See Carl for the full exchange. Back on the track, the race itself is a steep, straight downhill run through a migration of Sugar Hermits (skull-wearing swarm creatures) toward a giant kaiju skull blocking the finish line. Akuma anonymously delivers a note asking Carl to kill two rival teams and then visit Chicken and Waffles and Upgrades once it opens. Results: 1st Royal Court, 2nd Team Sparkles, 3rd Lady Dominators, 4th Team Free Love, 5th One Fine Pig, 6th empty (replacement pending). Eliminated: The Wild Hunt, The Jugglers.

    Heat 4 — the Pineapple Cabaret reveal¶

    Carl's group ambushes and tortures Akuma at C&W&U instead of meeting him alone (Elle shatters one of his testicles with an ice bolt). Under duress he reveals the existence and mechanics of the Pineapple Cabaret — the hidden NPC/mob sanctuary built by the former crawlers Herot and Menerva — and gives Carl the AI's own early framing of the Big Six power groups. The race itself runs through the jungle trail to the pit-stop city of Upano, through territory held by two warring cannibal "Ginger" tribes; Donut one-shots the southern chieftain Great Rusty, triggering a mandatory kill-quest against his northern rival, Maurice. The goddess Eris makes her first appearance here, permanently summoning Imani's reverse tooth fairy card and handing Carl a five-sided coin tied to the eventual fate of himself, Donut, and their friends. One Fine Pig's APV runs over and kills Carl's three hired mercenaries (Olga, Finley, and the panic-stricken fire fairy Quemada) in the pit stop; Carl retaliates with a hidden trap that kills both Tigran racers, Nico and Dario, sparing their pig, Penelope, who is protected by Taranis. Results: 1st Royal Court, 2nd Lady Dominators, 3rd Team Flamengo (Osvaldo and Filipe, introduced this heat), 4th Team Free Love, 5th Team Sparkles. Eliminated: One Fine Pig.

    Heat 5 — the reunion and the double death¶

    The track runs through the Gasworks Screw Factory, Steel Mill, and Doll Workshop. Mid-race, the fleshmancer Grigori the Placid reunites Corcunda's two halves while the truck slides sideways through molten metal — and Grull, the war god she abandoned while split, appears within minutes to smite her for apostasy. Dong Quixote dies charging the god alongside her, lance in hand, magnet-boosted through a field of slag elementals; full account on Corcunda's own page. Li Na is sent alone through the cleaner-bot exploit to the Pineapple Cabaret — the first confirmed successful use of the escape route — to flee her permanent no-healing condition. The Syndicate assassin Minus, posing as the tourist "Linus," is introduced into Elle's fan-meet program around this point; see Game Mechanics: The Minus Method. Results: 1st Team Sparkles, 2nd Team Free Love, 3rd Team Flamengo, 4th Royal Court. Eliminated: Lady Dominators and the Gimp.

    Heat 6 — Ad Infinitum¶

    The track is The L'Engle Building, where the unstable AI runs an unauthorized event nicknamed "Ad Infinitum" — playable "what-if" simulations of a crawler's alternate life, including Prepotente's own mother as a concert pianist who never raised him. Once Li Na confirms she survived (a single flower blooms on the tracking cactus), the guild sends 227 additional volunteers through the same exploit to the Cabaret. Minus/"Linus" is publicly unmasked as the Syndicate's sponsored assassin via crowd-sourced, off-dungeon internet detective work at a farewell party held just before this heat. With minutes left and Prepotente frozen inside his apartment's what-if vision, Carl detonates a bomb planted days earlier and deliberately kills Jasha and Radoslav, so their team's elimination absorbs the time cost of the rescue. Results: 1st Team Sparkles, 2nd Royal Court, 3rd Team Flamengo. Eliminated: Team Free Love.

    Heat 7 — the culling¶

    Just before the start, the reshuffled heats put Imani, Elle, and Chris in the same race, and Louis and Britney against Florin and Lucia Mar — the crawler-on-crawler pairings the party had spent the whole floor trying to avoid. Seconds later, and still in the starting blocks, Scolopendra's Nine-Tier Attack begins: Attack 1 (Transformation) turns Carl into a dachshund, Elle into a goblin, Chris into a werewolf and Jacobus into a starfish, until each next passes through a stairwell (see Game Mechanics: Scolopendra's Nine-Tier Attack). Osvaldo (Team Flamengo) is killed by fellow crawler Britney Proskurina moments after his own last-second stunt-jump for the gate; Team Sparkles and a trio of Russian crawlers nicknamed "the Tracksuit Troika" are both killed by the party rather than let through a possible false exit. Believing they've found a working escape route, the survivors storm the Desperado Club casino, where the new Wheel of Fortune croupier "Mitch" is revealed as an Adolescent Shadow Mimic running an ambush tied to the contested Pineapple Cabaret "back door." The AI delivers its fullest version of the Big Six taxonomy mid-fight (withholding "Group Six") before Donut kills Mitch outright; see Mitch for the fight. In the aftermath, guards announce the mimic infestation and the club's dance floor is abandoned — see Desperado Club: Shadow Mimic Infestation. Of the 274 crawlers (103 teams) who actually start Heat 7 — 989 remained after Heat 6, but 715 of them take a deal first — only 23 crawlers survive to cross into Floor 11.

    Layout & Notable Areas¶

    • The home cul-de-sac ("Team Roundabout") — a circle of numbered garage doors (one per team) that travels with the surviving teams from heat to heat, physically relocating to a new pit-stop location for multi-track heats. A team's door gets an "X" painted on it once they're eliminated
    • Hungry Eyes Village — the 1980s-neon-cyberpunk hub town attached to the cul-de-sac: the Vendor Village market street, the bar strip (the Hairpin, entrance to the Desperado Club; the Lollipop, karaoke bar and hidden entrance to the Stage Performance Guild), and the mercenary market
    • Chicken and Waffles and Upgrades (C&W&U) — the blank-fronted diner-and-black-market shop where Akuma is cornered
    • Upano — the lost jungle pit-stop city reached through cannibal "Ginger" territory (Heat 4)
    • Satan's Water Park — a waterslide track owned by a mountain-sized, invulnerable, comic-relief hedgehog named Satan, run in parallel with Upano (Heat 4/5)
    • Gasworks Screw Factory, Steel Mill, and Doll Workshop — an industrial track riddled with a toddler-cocaine drug cartel's cast-off elemental magic (Heat 5)
    • The L'Engle Building — the seven-floor apartment complex hosting "Ad Infinitum" and, at the very top, the starting blocks for the final heat (Heat 6/7)
    • The Desperado Club's top-floor casino — remodeled since Book 7, and, as of Heat 7, infested with Shadow Mimics

    Quests¶

    I have dreamed thee too long / Half a prayer, half a song — Complete¶

    The two-part quest to reunite Dong Quixote and Corcunda, opened in Heat 2 and completed mid-race in Heat 5. Reward: two stand-ins of the party's choosing may be sent to the Floor 11 arena.

    Pasty Inferno — Complete¶

    Mandatory quest opened the instant Donut kills Great Rusty in Heat 4: find and kill his northern rival, Maurice, before the power vacuum lets the northern tribe overrun Upano. The heat cannot be completed until the track's own Maurice is dead.

    Ad Infinitum — Complete¶

    A mandatory, floor-wide "quest" that is really the AI's own confession, delivered mid-Heat-6 once the "what-if" apartment simulations start upsetting crawlers faster than intended. No real reward beyond finishing the heat; see Game Mechanics: The "What-If" Apartment Simulations.

    The Nine-Tier Attack, Part One — opened, not resolved on this floor¶

    Triggered mid-Heat-7 by Scolopendra's rage at crawlers surviving on schedule; Attack 1 (Transformation) hits immediately. The quest itself, and its resolution, carries over onto Floor 11 — see Game Mechanics: Scolopendra's Nine-Tier Attack and Level 11.

    Notable Events¶

    • The marriage certificate arrives. Chandra's inheritance fan box confirms the Widow's Rights marriage in the safe room right after Heat 1. See Chandra.
    • Prison Pocket falls to a costume infiltration. See Heat 2, above, and Fursona/"Fursuiter".

    Stairwells¶

    Floor 10 has no scattered stairwell chambers of the kind seen on earlier floors. Instead, the entire floor funnels into a single mandatory final heat at the top of The L'Engle Building, and the exit is manufactured on the spot: Carl detonates a line of "poleaxe" charges — bombs designed by him specifically to breach floors vertically without leveling the building — blasting a jagged path from the ground-floor lobby all the way to the roof, where a cluster of portals to Floor 11 waits. Only 23 of the 274 crawlers (103 teams) who started Heat 7 survive long enough to reach them.

    Leaderboard¶

    No top-ten table is published anywhere on Floor 10. The book never reads out a ranked list during the floor itself — the only leaderboard data given arrives retroactively, in the "Welcome to the Eleventh floor" system message at the very start of Level 11, once fewer than 30 crawlers remain and the leaderboard is formally suspended:

    • Carl finishes Floor 10 ranked #2.
    • Donut finishes ranked #3 ("Number three? I ended as number three on the leaderboard?").
    • Who holds #1 is never stated. The system message omits the name outright, and Donut's own question — "It doesn't even say who number one is! Carl, was it you?" — goes unanswered on the page. This wiki does not speculate past what's shown.
