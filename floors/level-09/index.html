    Level 9¶

    Quick facts

    • Crawlers entering the floor: 32,429, per the arrival broadcast. Over 1,300 had died in the four days between the Floor 8 collapse and materialising here. 20,221 were still alive when the floor ended — described in-text as a record, with the second-highest total "not even close"
    • Time limit: 30 Days, or 90 hours after a winner is declared, whichever is sooner. Neither clock ran out: the floor was destroyed early by the god Emberus on a 66-minute doomsday countdown
    • Carl's standing at arrival: rank #1, bounty 6,000,000 gold, 231 septillion views, 887 quadrillion followers, 420 quadrillion favorites. He also receives a Platinum Venison Box on arrival
    • Setting: Larracos, City of Dreams — a funnel-shaped city sunk into an enormous pit, with 66 numbered rings counted from the bottom up, and the Semeru dwarves' castle at the very bottom, built on the roots of the All Tree. The surrounding Plains of Larracos are cut into nine pie-slice territories, one per attacking team; a tenth NPC team defends the city itself instead of holding a slice
    • Theme: "Faction Wars" — the dungeon's long-running inter-government war game, played for the first time with a crawler team on the board, an NPC team on the board, and every participant's safety protections switched off
    • Structure: four combat phases — the Ceasefire, the Ramp-Up, Open Hostilities, and the Peeling of Larracos. The book's four Parts map onto them exactly
    • Exits: 293 stairwell chambers seeded across the floor, sealed until Faction Wars is won or 30 hours remain to collapse, whichever comes first

    AI Commentary (in-voice, written for this wiki — not a book excerpt)

    "Nine of the richest creatures in the galaxy paid a fortune to come down here and play soldier with people who couldn't say no. Then I took their safety off. Then I took their showrunners' crawl. Now get out there and kill, kill, kill."

    Arrival¶

    Floor 9 opens directly out of Floor 8's ending, with no gap and no globe chamber. Carl and Donut materialise in a wet, stinking alley between two crumbling stone buildings on a recently-drained lower ring, the mud still reeking of the Floor 5 ocean and the corpse of a Concierge Shark ("Killed by suffocation when the Semeru Warden finally decided to open the drain") lying deeper in the alley. Imani lands elsewhere in the city and reaches them by chat within seconds.

    "Welcome, Crawler. Welcome to Faction Wars. You are on the ninth floor. Time to Level Collapse: 30 Days or 90 hours after a winner is declared, whichever is sooner ... Leaderboard rank: 1. Bounty: 6,000,000 gold ... Remaining Crawlers: 32,429 ... 10 teams remain. Entering Larracos, City of Dreams."

    The arrival warnings do most of the work of setting the floor up: everything looted from the Floor 8 memory simulation is stripped out of inventories; the Ceasefire is announced at 60 hours; and, tucked in among the routine army/officer notifications, the system delivers the floor's actual thesis statement — the reason Faction Wars deaths are real deaths for the first time in the series:

    Warning: Protection enhancements have now been removed. While some protections exist during a ceasefire, those of you who were getting regenerated can now die. So don't trip and break your neck. At least wait until the ceasefire ends.

    It's delivered as a throwaway line-item warning, sandwiched between "you have no officers in your army" and a truce announcement — no fanfare, no dramatic buildup. That flatness is the point: after eight floors of narrative safety nets, "dead is dead" finally applies to Faction Wars combat exactly as bluntly as it applied to mobs back on Floor 1.

    Within minutes of landing the board reshuffles itself. Eight of the nine off-world teams form a truce named The Bloc and declare war on both Team Retribution and the Princess Posse. Donut accepts Team Retribution's counter-offer of a truce before Carl can finish a sentence, which turns out to be the single most important decision of the floor: allied with the defenders, the Posse is never expelled from Larracos.

    The floor's central premise¶

    Cascadia's welcome broadcast (delivered in a slurring, clearly drunk voice) lays out the shape of the thing:

    • Ten teams. Nine attacking teams spread around Larracos "like a pie," and one defending NPC team headquartered in the city.
    • The goal is the castle at the centre. Attacking teams are eliminated if their warlord is killed or if their own throne room is held for six hours. Soldiers of an eliminated team are "traditionally given the option to join the new team or be slaughtered."
    • Crawlers are, in theory, bystanders. They may enlist with any faction during the Ceasefire, or flee into the woods — but every team can hunt down strays and conscript them. This is why recruiting every surviving crawler into the Princess Posse becomes Carl and Donut's first job.
    • The city is closed until four teams remain. Only then does the final phase, the Peeling of Larracos, begin.
    • This season the NPCs fight back from the start, which the game has never seen before. The Semeru dwarves are drilling, mining explosives into the down-ramps, and sabotaging enemy camps before the shooting starts.

    Two developments turn the floor from a spectator sport into a massacre. First, around 50,000 former crawlers — organised by Doctor Porthus Hu's Open Intellect Pacifist Network, shipped in aboard a refitted garbage freighter named Homecoming Queen — volunteer into the Princess Posse at level 60 with no gear. Second, each of the other eight teams is simultaneously handed roughly 20,000 armed mercenaries apiece, about 150,000 in total, who keep their off-world weapons and power armour.

    The four combat phases¶

    Phase One — The Ceasefire (nominally 60 hours)¶

    Saferoom rules apply everywhere inside Larracos; crawlers can cast and build but cannot harm anyone living within the city. Teams recruit, fortify, and lock their thrones in place. The phase ends the moment the last warlord marks themselves ready, or by unanimous vote, or — the loophole that actually fires — if a warlord voluntarily withdraws, which shortens the clock by a preset amount.

    It does not last 60 hours. Commander Stockade of the Lemig ("Democratic") Sortion, driven insane by a dose of Shi Maria's madness during a botched attempt to weaponise her against Carl, smashes his own skull open against the floor of Queen Imogen's kitchen. The system rules it a psychosis-induced suicide, counts it as a voluntary withdrawal, and cuts the ceasefire by 23.33 hours. Total elapsed Ceasefire: roughly 37 hours.

    Phase Two — The Ramp-Up (180 hours)¶

    The rules that define the floor's first week of fighting:

    • No offensive magic. A suppression field covers the whole floor. Spells that heal, buff, shield, obfuscate, dig, or build still work; anything designed to directly harm does not.
    • No teleport-based magic or skills. Puddle Jumper, the Gate of the Feral Gods, and teleport traps are all dead. Carl's Gloom Wraith Phase survives on a technicality — it is a phase, not a teleport.
    • No celestial summonings.
    • Entry into enemy territory only through designated corridors roughly one kilometre wide, at fixed points. Only crawlers with the Scout endorsement (18 per team) may cross elsewhere.

    The intent is stated outright by Baroness Victory: "It is designed to force bloody, hand-to-hand combat. And non-magical artillery." Against opponents with pulse rifles and trebuchets, it is designed to kill the Princess Posse in the first week.

    Phase Three — Open Hostilities¶

    Triggers when the Ramp-Up expires. No corridors, no magic ban, no restrictions on summoning — borders stop meaning anything and every team can move anywhere. This is the phase the Posse opens with Operation Ruin, a simultaneous four-front offensive.

    Phase Four — The Peeling of Larracos¶

    Triggers automatically once only four teams remain. The city opens to everyone. Any attacking team that holds the central throne room for six continuous hours wins Faction Wars; the defending team must hold it for a full week to win, and re-taking the room restarts that week. Only single teams, never alliances, may claim the throne. Whoever holds it controls Larracos's network of 100-plus automated defence towers, with a half-hour grace period after the room changes hands.

    Layout & Notable Areas¶

    • Larracos, City of Dreams — the funnel city, 66 numbered rings counted from the bottom up (not all of them complete circles), each ring a wide terrace with a yawning hole at its centre. Gothic stone architecture, vine-covered arches, a single waterfall falling from the upper rings and cascading down the roofs like a staircase. Ring 50 holds the Larracos College of Magic and the upper cultural district; ring 49 is housing; ring 45 is clothing shops; ring 9 holds the Piccadilly Theatre, directly under the waterfall; ring 4 holds the Desperado Club. The lower rings are still flooded on arrival from Carl and Katia's Floor 5 water dump, drained down to ring 3 by the time the floor opens
    • The Semeru castle — at the very bottom, built along the roots of the All Tree as "a monument. An apology." Still submerged at the start of the floor. Below it, once the city is gone, nothing but a bed of dead roots as thick as elephant trunks over a hole
    • Shanty Town — a ring of hovels and markets that sprang up out of nowhere around the top lip of the funnel after the flooding, far larger than the shanty town that normally forms. It encircles the entire entrance, counts as a settlement in its own right, and is the only neutral ground bordering every territory. Carl buys the mayorship off Ferdinand for a bowler hat
    • The Plains of Larracos — nine slices of mostly flat grassland, the exception being the rocky, occasionally lava-wreathed mountain in the north where the orcs' pre-built castle, The King's Point, sits. The region's only major river runs off that mountain and into Larracos as the waterfall
    • The FUPA — the Princess Posse stronghold, the Fortress of Unyielding Power and Authority, so named after a tug-of-war between the changelings and the Sledge left the AI to break the tie. Built around a bait-shop saferoom on a hill in spot 3
    • The high elf castle (the "Sugar Cube") from Floor 6, transferred down and used as the Lemig Sortion's headquarters — see High Elf Castle
    • Area 52 — a forest saferoom and forward base used for missile batteries and for reloading Louis's bomber
    • The Nest — a purpose-built granite synergy tower on the lip of Shanty Town, the tallest structure on the floor, keyed to Atrocity-class spells. Donut fights the last third of the war from its parapet
    • Club Vanquisher — reached this season through two entrance temples, one in Larracos and one in Shanty Town (the Temple of the Dagda). Site of Operation Snake Pit and of the floor's worst single atrocity

    Monsters (Level 9 area)¶

    Faction Wars is a war, not a dungeon crawl, and most of what the crawlers kill are soldiers rather than mobs. The floor's regular wildlife lives in the expanded outer forests that the AI bolted on at the last minute, and consists of recycled mobs from earlier floors levelled up to suit — chilly goats from Floor 1, danger-dingo-riding kobolds from Floor 2, and a Rolling Battle Formation Ball (Level 70) that is a straight riff on the Ball of Swine, stuffed with Skank Skunk Warriors (Level 45) under the command of a Fancy Boy Battle Wizard (Level 55). See the Mobs overview for the wider catalog.

    Quests¶

    Operation Snake Pit — Complete¶

    Not a system quest but the Posse's own name for the assassination of the entire Blood Sultanate line of succession — four nagas: Vinata herself, her two cousins ("Ikbals," in real life a graphic designer and a PR agent), and the head of her castle guard, who was also her secret husband. Killing all four is the precondition for lifting the Crown of the Sepsis Whore curse that binds Donut and Katia to the floor.

    The operation hinges on Rosetta's reconstruction of how the sultana has vanished from every Faction Wars for decades: she worships the obscure light god Khepri, uses his Travel the Path and Welcome Home abilities to follow a bijou fairy's tendril straight into his temple inside Club Vanquisher, and swallows her guards as beetles to bring them along. The Posse storms the Dagda temple, breaks into Club Vanquisher, and forces the doors with Samantha — who counts as Damned for having survived the Nothing. Vinata's counter-trap is to summon Khepri himself into his own temple. Carl drops a Waffle Maker thermobaric bomb at his own feet while briefly immortal, and survives by about forty seconds.

    Operation Ruin — Complete¶

    The four-front offensive that opens the Open Hostilities phase: Florin against the orcs, Boomer against the Reavers, Donut against the Dream, and Carl against the Madness, with Louis flying bomb runs in support and Team Retribution's diggers and spies attached. The northern prong deliberately agitates the lava tubes beneath the King's Point mountain, blowing the top off it — the eruption is far larger than the dwarves predicted, nearly knocks Party Planner out of the sky, and pours a river of lava that eventually reaches Larracos.

    This is How the Gristle Grinds — Complete¶

    The floor's last quest, issued to every crawler and combatant in the Princess Posse when Emberus enters the realm. No host, no opting out. The terms: everything on the floor will burn in 66 minutes, this cannot be stopped, so win Faction Wars and get out. Reward: one year off any indentureship deal negotiated on the next floor, and an "extra toasty" option on all food boxes.

    Emberus's questline — advancing, and getting worse¶

    Find Out Who Killed My Son continues from Floor 8 and gets steadily more dangerous. Hellik, sitting beside a dying Carl in Club Vanquisher, denies killing Geyrun, says their mother and sister Apito has been corrupted, that the All Tree is beginning to rot, that Nekhebit has returned and is behind it, and that Scolopendra stirs — "It is all connected." He then brokers Emberus's access to Issitoq himself. Emberus's reaction is to issue a new quest, kill 100 worshippers of Hellik before the 11th floor collapses, followed by a message that puts Carl in an impossible position: "If you survive killing Hellik, like he surely deserves, I fear I may turn my rage and grief on you for daring to hurt my own kin, and for this, I love you." A side quest, Kill the Blasphemer, pays out a Celestial boon for killing a ram cleric of Hellik.

    The Bedlam Bride — Failed¶

    The Floor 8 escort quest to deliver Shi Maria to Larracos is formally marked Quest Failed when Carl instead traps her inside himself. Mordecai's compound of Glory Bound, Removal Order and a Shock Wake salve, cast while Carl was unconscious and simultaneously half-teleported into the Lemig throne room, ends with the spider devouring her own husband Hapanzi in a dream and being sealed inside the tattoo. She is not gone; she is a bomb Carl can detonate exactly once.

    Where the Sidewalk Ends

    The Floor 8 quest Where the Sidewalk Ends and its successor The Missing Piece do not appear anywhere in This Inevitable Ruin. They were completed on Floor 8 and are not carried forward.

    Notable Events¶

    • The Ceasefire collapses early. Stockade and Architect Houston use an off-world observer to time a Summon Ally request to the exact instant Carl went under for his Shi Maria extraction. The plan half-works, splitting Carl between two locations; Mordecai counters with a salve that expels the spider into the Lemig throne room, where she slaughters about fifty goblins. Stockade beats his own brains out against the floor, the Sortion is eliminated, and the ceasefire is cut by 23.33 hours with nobody's defences finished.
    • Carl frees the AI. In the Christmas Morning flashback, Carl sits in a Florida gas station beside a memory-ghost dog named Lightning Lou with the Residuals' containment interface in his head and two buttons available: trip the fuse and nova the system, or Disconnect Containment. He presses Disconnect. The AI's sphere of influence begins expanding across the solar system. When Princess Formidable's crew later triggers the failsafe manually, nothing happens here — and the Aryl system, home of the previous crawl, goes dark at that exact nanosecond.
    • The Prism falls. Carl and Donut walk into Empress D'Nadia's under-built castle during the first hours of the Ramp-Up and strip her shields off with clockwork Mongos. Carl, in a white rage over what she said to Tsarina Signet, nearly kills her with the Ring of Divine Suffering; Donut talks him back off the edge, and Mongo makes the kill.
    • Rosetta beheads King Rust. Rust comes to parlay, tells Carl his own daughter is about to blow the system failsafe, and offers a withdrawal in exchange for Carl asking her to stop. Rosetta erupts out of a Semeru tunnel and takes his head with a whip, then loots his ring and retina to empty the Skull Empire's accounts — the money that buys the Open Intellect Pacifist Network its generation ships.
    • Volteeg's tank. The gargoyle mercenary and cookbook author, secretly a former crawler, fills a Weeper tank with unstable energy balls and detonates it in the middle of the Bloc's own army, killing over 10,000 soldiers and Warlord Hortense with a single blast and eliminating the Operatic Collective.
    • Operation Snake Pit and the death of the Sepsis Whore. See Quests above. The raid defiles Club Vanquisher, gets Carl permanently banned from every temple but his own, and releases a dozen-plus gods into the club's hallways. Vinata, driven mad by the Eye of the Bedlam Bride, spends her last seconds beating Carl's head against the floor while both are immortal, and is killed by a level-29 sluggalo. Carl meets Milk, the sixth-edition cookbook author, in her hidden calligraphy guild and breaks down in her arms.
    • The volcano and the death of King Stalwart. Operation Ruin blows the top off the King's Point mountain. Stalwart, pinned in a walking scorpion tank, tries to teleport out and lands among his own war mages, who use a chain of Pull spells to break their conscription and tear him apart. His last words: "My sister was right." His death creates the War Mage Rebellion as a recognised entity.
    • Eris's Ceremony of Chaos. Panicking mid-fight, the goddess of chaos casts Bear Witness and Bring Forth the Witnesses across the entire floor, freezing and then hauling roughly 80,000 combatants — both armies, several gods, and most of the NPCs — into one enormous living pile beside the Dream castle. Only those indoors, plus pets, sluggalos, former tattoos and, inexplicably, Carl and Dong Quixote, are spared.
    • Donut's Rolling Battle Formation. With the pile about to lose its invulnerability and be crushed, Donut casts the skunk wizard's Rolling Battle Formation on all 80,000 of them at once, making a living ball the size of a hill with the god Meatus flopping out the back, and steers it like a remote control across the battlefield. She then insults Harpocrates over a floor-wide PA to keep him off it. Li Jun dies drawing the god away so she can finish.
    • Donut casts War Crime. To separate friend from enemy without losing thousands of her own, Donut chains Bijanbi (tattooed onto her flank by Edgar in about a minute) with Astral Paw through the Nest's Atrocity synergy. The enemy soldiers are hauled into the air under a giant spectral cat's paw, and she slams them, repeatedly, through a lattice of Li Na's chains until the screaming stops. It is her third Atrocity of the day. The system responds by giving her a new class — The Oak Fell — naming her Champion of Nekhebit, and taking her from level 65 to 135.
    • Architect Houston's death starts the Peeling. Carl finds the Viceroy warlord on his own operating table, surgically fusing himself to a dead changeling in pursuit of what he calls the Beautiful Place. Carl heals him to keep him alive, fails to rescue the crawler Burcu, and phases straight through him as the Sheol-fire containment traps fire. The Madness is eliminated, four teams remain, and the Peeling Phase begins.
    • The War Mages take the castle. The rebellion seizes the Larracos throne room, floods the city with zarogoths from a generator in the Piccadilly, and starts a six-hour clock on winning the whole season. Carl levitates out of the funnel and dumps an entire inventory folder of bombs onto the theatre, which kills the generator and also triggers the city's defence towers on himself.
    • Lucia Mar attacks the Nest. Lucia kills Bomo, Toyotomi and the tower mages and climbs toward Donut demanding Mongo, leaving layered magical nets that trap Donut in place. Rend's Wrecking Ball kills Gustavo 3, her last rottweiler, and Carl conscripts her with a hell-kissed potion. Her personalities fracture — a voice calling itself Diah says children are trapped in the dark with her — and Zev later confirms roughly 120,000 of Earth's children are tied to her life.
    • The rescue of Louis and the Tagg reveal. Louis is shot down, captured, and cut open; his heart and lungs are replaced with biomechanical organs grown from Epitome Tagg himself, the last step before a brain swap. Juice Box, in hunter wraith form, possesses him to smuggle him out. Samantha proposes the fix — transplant the heart and lungs of Princess Lunette's flesh golem — and Mordecai, Edgar, Imani and Juice Box perform the surgery in thirty seconds. Louis survives with naiad gills and a kua-tin rebreather sewn into his bronchial tube; Mongo eats Tagg.
    • Katia's departure. With Emberus's clock running, Katia eats the Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace. Donut's Laundry Day rips Huanxin Jinx out of the goddess's soul armour into a circle of Milk's words of power, and Donut kills her with Katia's crossbow and a bolt tipped with Mordecai's mother's ashes. Katia then refuses the celestial-attendant offer and chooses pregnancy — the boon that ejects her from the dungeon — specifically so she can reach the kinder facility on Earth's surface where the children and pregnant women are being held. Louis is the father.
    • Samantha's confession summons Emberus. Mid-conversation, apropos of nothing, Samantha mentions that she "may have had an indirect, possibly direct hand in the death of Geyrun." Emberus enters the realm within seconds with a 66-minute countdown to burn the floor. Carl throws her to him with the xistera head-tosser to buy time, and retrieves her at the last moment.
    • Zerzura. The Sledge casts the spell that transfers the entirety of Larracos and Shanty Town to the 12th floor, carrying the Semeru, the changelings, the children, Kiwi and her twins, Big Tina, Ferdinand and Juice Box with it. Team Retribution's warlords thereby abandon their posts, the team forfeits, and the Princess Posse wins Faction Wars.
    • Justice Light breaks the Nothing. Justice Light stays behind, hidden, after lying to Juice Box about leaving with her. The "antenna" he built under the FUPA was never a defence: it is a legendary trans-dimensional trap that drops a bijou fairy trap and a demon trap into the permanent portal made from the broken winding box of the Gate of the Feral Gods. He snaps the twine as the fire reaches him.

    Stairwells¶

    There are 293 stairwell chambers seeded across the floor, and they do not open at all until Faction Wars is won or 30 hours remain on the collapse clock. The Posse's victory generates them in a ring around the bare pit where the Semeru castle used to be — and the moment it does, a 72-hour countdown to level collapse appears.

    Nobody gets 72 hours. Emberus's arrival puts a hard 66-minute ceiling on the floor, so the descent to Floor 10 is a sprint rather than the usual lingering victory lap. Crawlers stream into the stairwells in the pit while the volcano erupts again overhead and red ash falls on everything. Carl deliberately empties the floor first — sending Prepotente, Louis, Imani, Elle, Bautista, Zhang, Li Na, Lucia and the rest ahead — and stays behind with Donut for Katia's ceremony until there are minutes left. By the time he walks in, the only two things left alive on the floor are Emberus and Justice Light.

    The floor's closing system messages are the most consequential in the series to date:

    System Message: The Nothing has been broken.

    System Message: The Nothing is currently draining into The Halls of the Ascendency.

    System Message: The Nothing is currently draining into Sheol.

    System Message: The Nothing is currently draining into Scolopendra's Lair.

    System Message: Scolopendra stirs.

    Planetwide Message: It is advised all current guests at the Scolopendra Club evacuate immediately.

    System Message: Scolopendra has awakened.

    20,221 crawlers reach Floor 10, from the 32,500 who came down from Floor 8 — a record by a wide margin, with the second-highest total "not even close." Of the roughly 50,000 former crawlers who volunteered, about 28,000 survive; they are shipped back up to Homecoming Queen, where Boomer and York immediately begin the next phase of Porthus's war. Most of the surviving crawlers intend to take an indentureship deal at the start of the next floor. Carl and Donut both decline theirs.

    Floor 10 is named "Don't Come in Last" — a vehicle-race floor where each party chooses a conveyance, biological or mechanical, makes a fixed number of journeys from point A to point B with an upgrade at each finish, and whoever comes last has the survival containment around their vehicle removed. It is outside this page's scope; it is simply where the book leaves off.

    Leaderboard¶

    No leaderboard table is published anywhere on Floor 9. Unlike Floor 8, the book never reads out a top ten, and there is no recap episode with rankings. The only ranking data given are narrative:

    • Carl enters the floor at #1 with a 6,000,000 gold bounty — the first time he holds the top spot outright, and the basis on which the naga later petition the Syndicate courts to have him recognised as the de facto leader of Earth.
    • Midway through the floor, announcer Kevin states that Li Na "shot up to level 84 and number two on the top ten list, passing Prepotente and landing just behind Carl."
    • Burcu, the porsuk swashbuckler, is described as "off and on the top ten." She dies in Architect Houston's surgical theatre.
    • Prepotente reaches level 99, briefly the strongest crawler in the dungeon, after single-handedly destroying most of the Blood Sultanate's standing army.
    • Li Na becomes the first crawler in the history of the game to max out every base stat at 300, using a second Ring of Divine Suffering on tens of thousands of marked enemies at once. Three of her marks escape to the 18th floor, so she can never heal again.
    • Donut ends the floor at level 135, with the class The Oak Fell and the title Champion of Nekhebit — by Mordecai's assessment, "the most powerful crawler in the history of this game."
    • Carl ends the floor at level 81.
