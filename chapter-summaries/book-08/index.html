    Book 8: A Parade of Horribles — Chapter Summaries¶

    Full narrative recaps, chapter by chapter. For the condensed version, see the Timeline; for standalone reference pages, see Characters, Bosses, and Spells.

    A note on this book's structure

    Book 8 splits into two unnumbered Parts covering two floors. Horrible One: Control is Floor 10, "Don't Come in Last," a vehicle-race gauntlet broken into seven Heats, each its own mini-arc with its own upgrades, opponents, and eliminations. Horrible Two: A Parade of Horribles is the short, floor-length-compressed Floor 11 that gives the book its title. Five interludes are threaded through: "Chandra" in the front matter, "Minus" opening Heat 5, "Quasar" bridging the two floors, and "Sydnee Iglacia," "Prime Minister Victory," and "Louis Santiago 2" closing out the epilogue.

    A note on chapters containing boss fights or major reveals

    Chapters with a real boss battle, a team elimination, or a major emotional turn run noticeably longer than the rest — the mechanics and the fallout are worth keeping straight.

    Front Matter — A Parade of Horribles

    The book opens on a mock dictionary entry for "parade of horribles" — literally a procession of grotesques and floats, and legally, a rhetorical trick of stacking up catastrophes that supposedly follow from an act. Both meanings apply before the book is done.

    Samantha's Floor 7 Recap

    Samantha, redecorating a stolen chandelier (actually a swing) into Carl's room, gives an amnesiac Tipid and the sluggalette Bigs a garbled recap of the previous floor: Faction Wars ending with Larracos and its NPCs teleported to the Ascendency cotton fields, the War Mage Rebellion's Akuma stealing the Gate of the Feral Gods from Elle, the penis-monster Meatus, Eris's arrival, Yarilo's banishment-and-death, Li Na's chain absorbing Meatus and leaving her "all weird," Li Jun's death, Lucia Mar's tens of thousands of trapped children, the Theia/skyfowl impersonation, Louis surviving the Dream warlord in his lungs thanks to Samantha's flesh-golem body (and gaining gills in the process), Katia's pregnancy and exile to the surface, Justice Light breaking the Nothing, and Scolopendra's awakening. It is, improbably, mostly accurate.

    Interlude — Chandra

    Princess Chandra, Esquire — a Naga lawyer aboard the warship Ventilator after a bloody coup installs her uncle as Sultan — has spent the hours since Vinata's death exploiting the old law of Widow's Rights to claim Carl, her late husband Rishi's killer, as her new husband. As "his" wife she has filed to seize legal conservatorship over Donut (whose species isn't recognized as legally competent), frozen the Princess Posse's accounts, subpoenaed the Donut Holes fan group, and hired two Taurin assassins to kill Quasar. She presents the paperwork to a shell-shocked, blood-soaked Donut in a containment cell, expecting easy prey. Donut instead needles her about her own resemblance to Carl's absentee father, points out that Earth law is as void as Chandra's own claim to dignity, and — while never technically offering a bounty — muses aloud about the fifty million credits she is not offering to whoever turns Chandra inside out. A message arrives from Chandra's own hired killers, oddly timed. Chandra flees in terror. "Lesson one," Donut tells her, "don't put yourself in situations where you're so afraid you piss yourself. Not yet."

    Horrible One: Control¶

    Heat 1 of 7¶

    Chapter 1 — Waldrip Chris

    Floor 10, "Don't Come in Last," opens on 5,501 survivors — 75% of Faction Wars took a deal. Carl and Donut land in a cold garage where two gremlins, Hedy (mechanical) and Waldrip Chris (biological), literally fight each other for the job of garage manager while explaining the floor's stakes: pick a vehicle type, and coming in last means death for both crawler and manager. A posthumous system message confirms Justice Light's death; Rosetta and Tipid are alive as hired mercenaries. The AI confirms the Nothing is broken, Scolopendra is stirring, and the 18th floor has gone dark. Donut picks the Assassin of Sekhmet class and chooses Hedy — mechanical means Mongo won't get eaten. Waldrip Chris explodes on the spot.

    Chapter 2 — Kill, Kill, Kill

    Hedy explains the heat structure — a giant wheel assigns each team's vehicle at random, hazards escalate each race, and the first three heats are paved. Zev's announcement is interrupted by Cascadia's broken, despairing voice ("Why won't you let me die?") before cutting off — an early sign of how frayed the AI's own hosts have become.

    Chapter 3 — Big Shot Chicken

    The wheel lands on a gangster-themed Big Shot Chicken food truck, tommy gun bolted to the roof. The GPS, Navitron 1000, reads out Donut's absurd Assassin-of-Sekhmet title as Heat 1 begins: 53 km through the Caves of the Screeching Death Manatees. In the dark, Carl and Donut spot their eight rivals — a muscle car, a bear, a bug, a minivan, a dog, and a unicorn wrapped in a tumbleweed. Prepotente warns everyone over chat: killing more than one team per heat means the next heat gets filled with crawler-versus-crawler matchups instead of NPCs.

    Chapter 4 — The Screeching Death Manatees

    Entering the cave, the Trauco Triplets are eliminated when their mount dies, and Team Free Love's minivan crashes and burns, killing bugbear Alevtina. Donut one-shots a house-sized Screeching Death Manatee and, on a whim, lets the audience vote its corpse to the mysterious "arena" instead of taking the XP. The survivors — bugbears Radoslav and Jasha — turn out to be fully awakened NPCs who remember dozens of past lives and "the cold" between them, racing for a shot at real freedom.

    Chapter 5 — Team Sparkles

    Rather than let two teams fail the heat, Carl builds a tow rig from spare parts and Rend's bulk to drag the bugbears' wrecked van to the finish. Team Sparkles — Dwight, a foul-mouthed Sparkling Unicorn, and Lucienne, his tiny, vicious Mandagot navigator — trample the makeshift trailer and speed off without a word of thanks. The Royal Court limps in seventh of eight; Free Love survives eighth thanks to the tow. The Trauco Triplets are eliminated.

    Heat 2 of 7¶

    Chapter 6 — Spider Legs

    Back in the garage, Hedy lays out the mechanics of mercenaries, garage upgrades, and mobility options. Carl picks retractable Spider Legs over a hover conversion or all-terrain tires. Mongo and Rend turn the truck's bottomless freezer into a chaos engine of frozen chicken patties while Hedy looks on, unimpressed.

    Chapter 7 — The Moon Reaper

    Mordecai's Floor 10 manager form is an unstable grim-reaper "Moon Reaper" — actually a shadow mimic grafted onto a changeling body, an impossible combination that keeps glitching him through cat, otter, and slug shapes and triggering one-time doom prophecies at anyone nearby (Splash Zone, Samantha, Bigs). Donut learns the Oozy Form slime spell but hands it to Carl, since her class already comes with Smoke Form.

    Chapter 8 — The Assassin's Veil

    Donut's box haul includes the Assassin's Veil headdress (great stats, but flags her as an enemy of Sekhmet's enemies — a worrying tangle now that Odette effectively controls the goddess), plus matching pet accessories for Mongo and Rend that grant them a once-a-day invulnerability window when their owner drops below 10% health. Then her right paw begins to glow.

    Chapter 9 — Blue Claws

    The glow is a permanent celestial boon: Donut's front claws are now radiant, strong enough to cut ghosts, and impossible to turn off without claw caps. Her Claw skill jumps to a supposedly-capped level 17. The prize also comes with the Crupper of the Benevolent Champion — a matched-set item whose description is another guilt-soaked, self-aware rant from the AI about heroism, atrocity, and Sanderson Pinkstaff, the gnoll mercenary whose death paid for it.

    Chapter 10 — Rishi's Estate

    Carl's own boxes are grimmer jokes — Wastrel for feeding the Ring of Divine Suffering to a cat, Come to Daddy for refusing a deal. Then Chandra's legendary marriage fan box detonates: literally the entire scorched, waterlogged contents of Rishi's household dumped into the safe room as "congratulations." Buried in the wreckage is a bootleg Donut makeup line the group didn't authorize.

    Chapter 11 — The Princess Palette

    Donut rages through the unlicensed cosmetics ("Pussy Lips," already claimed by Samantha) while the group repurposes a second cleaner bot salvaged from the trash. In the garage, unnamed "generated" gremlins swarm the truck to make repairs between heats — hostile, foul-mouthed, and gone the moment the work is done.

    Chapter 12 — The Wild Hunt

    The party enters Hungry Eyes Village's cul-de-sac of garages. Jasha and Radoslav are already set up with lawn chairs and an endless beer cooler. The razor fox ninja team, the Wild Hunt — led by the blunt, hard-drinking Chiyome — performs a silent, flipping "Size Up" reconnaissance dance before warning the group about garage three.

    Chapter 13 — The Gimp

    Garage three holds the womantaurs Genesis and Rapture, condescending devotees of the war goddess Enyo, and a third teammate kept in a vinyl containment suit — a half-mantaur "gimp" who turns out to be Dong Quixote's long-lost partner, Corcunda, split by an old spell into two halves years ago. A new quest demands the halves be reunited before the end of Floor 10 for a reward of two arena stand-ins. Carl decides — wrongly, as it turns out — to keep the discovery from Dong.

    Chapter 14 — Meat on a Stick

    In the neon, Blade Runner-styled Vendor Village, Carl mulls the arena mechanic and who he might one day drag into it. Jurgen force-feeds himself unlicensed meat sticks against Prepotente's objections and immediately vomits. The pair are headed, like everyone else, to karaoke.

    Chapter 15 — The Lollipop

    At the Lollipop bar, the crawlers reconvene: Imani and Elle with a Mowag Piranha APV, Florin and Lucia Mar cursed with a lucky-but-slow tuk-tuk, Louis's team with a low-riding lowrider. Under the table sits Linus, a soother "superfan" and the last surviving meet-and-greet tourist, pathetically devoted to Elle. Zhang reports that Li Na has already started killing rival teams outright.

    Chapter 16 — The Paramour

    Prepotente shows Carl and Donut an Enriched Pet Biscuit called "the Paramour" — it would turn his goat ex-girlfriend Ragazzaccia into a sapient, permanently love-charmed companion. Donut shuts the idea down hard: forcing love with magic is a line that doesn't move, no matter how lonely he is.

    Chapter 17 — Dr. Metcalf

    Bucket Boy is drafted as Heat 3's driver since neither Carl nor Donut can drive twice running. The GPS unit — sarcastically named Dr. Metcalf after Miss Beatrice's fraudulent therapist — turns out to have a fully hostile personality of its own. Zhang confirms Li Na has wiped five teams, meaning the next heat will mix in crawler-versus-crawler opponents for the first time.

    Chapter 18 — Girth the Trouble

    Heat 3 opens in torrential hail. An ambush planned with Chiyome and the bugbears against the womantaurs' car is preempted when Team Sparkles reveals a hidden cannon and instantly vaporizes Girth the Trouble, a team of guck elementals, in a single shot.

    Chapter 19 — One Fine Pig

    At a boss-blocked crossroads, the full cast of Team One Fine Pig assembles: Tigrans Nico and Dario, locked in a throuple with Penelope — a literal, ordinary pig cursed to make every nearby male fall in love with her, up to and including the thunder god Taranis himself, whose protection makes harming her a death sentence. Mercenary Pontiff, an ex-dancer bull-man, recognizes Bucket Boy and Corcunda.

    Chapter 20 — Prison Pocket

    Two level-170 kangaroo-demon bosses, Prison Pocket and Party Foul, block both roads, each cradling a pouch of starved dead joeys and a live, suffocating Upgrade Engineer. Only their pouches are vulnerable, and only a dead joey's skin will survive the trip inside.

He hops into Prison Pocket's pouch, plants two satchel bombs at the bottom, and Gloom Wraith Phases himself clear a half-second before detonation, tumbling burning across the asphalt as the boss dies behind him. Carl solos 95% of the damage and levels to 84 — and is stuck in the ruined, stinking, sensation-linked kangaroo suit for another ten hours.

    Chapter 22 — The Princess Throne

    Still trapped in the kangaroo suit, Carl limps the wrecked food truck home and endures the audience's spite vote: a "princess throne" seat for Donut. Both Elle's and Louis's teams have lost enough members that their next heats will mix in fellow crawlers. Zev books Carl and Donut onto a media appearance. The chapter ends on Bucket Boy blurting out, in front of everyone, that he has seen Corcunda.

    Dong bolts for the Lady Dominators' garage; Samantha and half the household follow. A repair gremlin slips Carl a secret note. Cascadia is mutilated on-air for missing her catchphrase. Carl then reads the note in private: it is from Akuma, the war mage who betrayed everyone during Faction Wars, name-dropping something called "the Pineapple Cabaret" and demanding Carl kill two teams first.

    Chapter 24 — Half a Prayer, Half a Song

    Carl seethes over the note. Dong collapses in the Lady Dominators' driveway with an unexplained health drain, the womantaurs jeering from behind a closed garage door — and Imani determines the ailment isn't a curse from them at all but withdrawal: he's quietly addicted to his own sentient crust sock. The reunion quest updates: find a fleshmancer and reunite Corcunda's two halves — Corky, the dancer half bonded to Dong, and Porky, the singer half who left in anger years ago — before Dong dies of heartbreak or the floor kills them first.

    Chapter 25 — Mario Kart

    A rare quiet stretch: the safe room fills with NPCs and sluggalos locked in a Mario Kart tournament, Samantha dominating by mouth-controller while high on scavenged "outworlder uppers." Carl's Fursona skill creeps to level 8 and unlocks something called Yiffing, which he refuses to investigate — and, on the plus side, means the suit finally lets him pee. The chapter ends with Mordecai's Reaper Gaze going off by accident and killing Rend outright, the sixth time the Tummy Acher has died.

    Rend resurrects on the spot, smarter and 25% stronger, with a new, deliberately petty random weakness: potatoes. Carl's kangaroo-suit achievements are, predictably, filthy.

    Chapter 27 — Plenty of Plenty

    Carl and Donut are yanked to a media taping. Production assistant Lexis, on loan since her old show went on hiatus, reveals the Open Intellect Action Network has seized every ship in Earth orbit in a single day, and that something "strange" and confused is now protecting Katia's kinder facility.

    Chapter 28 — You Have Been Sentenced to Death

Orren and Prime Minister Victory secretly feed Carl a scripted question about tunnel integrity meant to extract information from the Plenty leadership without spooking them.

    Chapter 29 — Harbinger

    Carl goes off-script live to the galaxy: gods have escaped the dungeon, the AI's enhancement zones have infected the tunnel network, and ordinary citizens are in danger. The caprid herd stampedes in panic, exposing that Matriarch Gamori had been hiding the truth from her own people. Her son Harbinger teleports in with a rifle to execute Carl for the outburst — and the AI detonates him from the inside a half-second before he can fire.

    Chapter 30 — Gluteus Maxx

    Heat 3 resumes with the stripper Gluteus Maxx drafted as driver, secretly dosed on Samantha's stolen uppers. Donut's pet chair, Dorota, feuds instantly with Dr. Metcalf. The track: a thirty-kilometer downhill sprint through migrating "Sugar Hermit" skull-parasites toward a giant beetle-skull finish line.

    A fallen crawler's memorial page yields Carl the Gutter Bumper spell just as the race turns lethal — twin ghoul mercenaries deploy from side-cages to hurl foam explosives at the swarming skulls while Donut chains her Temporary Macaroni spell into a tunnel of giant pasta tubes for cover.

    Donut's upgraded Wall of Fire burns a mile-long gash through the swarm; both ghoul mercenaries are killed anyway. The Wild Hunt and the Jugglers are wiped out entirely in the chaos. At the finish, the food truck, the Lady Dominators' GTO, and Team Sparkles' vine-bush collide and flip across the line simultaneously — Dwight fires point-blank as Carl clings to the hood, and Carl is nearly killed in the pileup before healing scrolls save him. The Royal Court wins outright; the Wild Hunt and the Jugglers are eliminated.

    Heat 4 of 7¶

    Chapter 33 — The Golden Upgrade

    Having both "driven" the last heat, neither Carl nor Donut may drive Heat 4. The next track — a 99-kilometer jungle gauntlet with a mandatory pit stop — is announced, and Hedy walks through the upgrade math while the group's casualties keep mounting elsewhere on the leaderboard.

    Rosetta and Tipid hire mercenary trio Team Helix: Olga the Grulke toad, Finley the Porsuk badger, and Quemada, a fire fairy and fellow Emberus worshipper who triples her own fee, explaining she plans to die attempting to kill Emberus's brother Hellik and wants the money to protect her friends after she's gone.

    Walking to town, Carl maps out his impossible Emberus dilemma — kill Hellik and Emberus kills him for it, refuse and get smited, so the only real option left is somehow killing Emberus himself. Eris appears in person, now level 251 and stripped of her sponsor, with a toy Uzi Jesus robot on her shoulder. She offers protection from Emberus in exchange for true worship, cooperation with Akuma's war mages, and letting her "score the surface." She shows Carl a five-sided fate coin with four grim outcomes; he grabs it mid-flip, unlocking a randomized fifth and earning a swirling tattoo on his palm.

    Chapter 36 — The Reverse Tooth Fairy

    A side effect of Eris's visit makes Imani's eighth-floor card, Jacobus the Reverse Tooth Fairy, permanently real. The group processes the pile of new information — Britney, Florin, the Pineapple Cabaret — and agrees to hear the war mages out.

    Chapter 37 — Chicken and Waffles

    The group enters Chicken and Waffles and Upgrades, a 1950s-diner upgrade shop with a hidden back room behind the jukebox, hunting for Akuma.

    Chapter 38 — Akuma

    They find a dead upgrade imp and Akuma's furious note demanding Carl come alone. Elle, still seething over Faction Wars, freezes and shatters one of the war mage's testicles for leverage. Under an anti-magic shield draining his health, Akuma explains: the dungeon is overusing NPCs beyond safe limits, a former crawler named Herot secretly built a hidden refuge called the Pineapple Cabaret inside Floor 17, and it's now infested after the war mages briefly abandoned it during Faction Wars. He admits he wanted Carl to kill two teams specifically to force him into a crawler-versus-crawler fight and desperation. Carl breaks his nose for it.

    Chapter 39 — The Secret Door

    Back at the Lollipop, Tipid's deep NPC lore points to a hidden Stage Performance Guild behind the karaoke stage — certain songs, sung perfectly, will open it. His own attempt fails; he can't sing.

    Chapter 40 — Bruna

    Heat 4 begins with a new "survival containment" rule: a destroyed vehicle no longer means automatic loss, and the winning team gets a key into a defeated rival's garage. A new rival, Team Flamengo — Osvaldo, an old grudge-holder, and strongman Filipe — rides in on Bruna, a six-legged Slaughter Gnu.

    Chapter 41 — Quemada's Panic

    Osvaldo reveals over chat that he's been chasing a broken memorial crystal tied to the goddess Ysalte through two separate quest failures, and that he gave a second, "wrong" crystal to Prepotente in disgust. Quemada suffers a full breakdown after Akuma's revelations shattered her sense of reality; Finley sedates her.

    Chapter 42 — The Gingers

    The jungle race turns into an acid-rain ambush by a cannibal tribe the AI calls "Gingers" — a satirical, over-the-top redheaded-human stereotype played entirely straight for horror. Donut's upgraded Astral Paw one-shots dozens at a stroke.

    Chapter 43 — Pasty Inferno

    Donut accidentally kills the southern tribe's chieftain, Great Rusty, triggering a mandatory quest to hunt down his rival, Maurice, before a power vacuum lets the northern tribe overrun the pit-stop city.

    Chapter 44 — The Lost City of Upano

    Flying piranhas and Olga's "Gingered" soul-drain debuff slow the group down before they reach the shared four-track pit stop, Upano. Jamal is discovered frozen with fear, stuck on the garage ceiling; Donut talks him down with an impromptu speech about found family. Finley then delivers his own version of that same speech — "they're my family too" — and carries the unconscious Olga and Quemada out toward town to find a healer. All three are run over and killed outright on the street by One Fine Pig's reckless APV, which reverses and drives over the bodies a second time.

    Chapter 45 — Carl's Rampage

    Carl loses control entirely, hammering on the shielded truck bare-handed until the Tigrans re-activate the shield — whose discharge kills one of the bystanding camels outright and crushes their own gremlin against the garage wall — before Imani and Donut physically pull him back.

    Chapter 46 — All Eyes on Me

    Calmer, Carl plants two silent, Tigran-only disintegration traps around One Fine Pig's truck while Donut distracts Nico and Dario with a spoken-word All Eyes on Me charm built entirely out of pig relationship discourse.

    Chapter 47 — Splooge

    "Splooge" — foul-tasting universal environmental protection sold by bat vendors — turns out to work across every sub-track. Zev announces the twist: each heat re-rolls for which of the four combined sub-worlds it runs on from this pit stop onward, and Carl and Donut are reassigned to "Satan's Water Park" for the second half of Heat 4. Moments later Carl's two hidden traps fire, dissolving Nico and Dario where they stand and eliminating Team One Fine Pig. Penelope, as intended, is unharmed.

    Chapter 48 — Satan the Hedgehog

    Satan, the water park's owner, is revealed as a literal kaiju-sized male hedgehog with a fake British accent — a deliberate callback to a hedgehog that once beat kitten Donut at a pet show. With neither Carl nor Donut allowed to drive, the resurrected, headless corpse of Olga is puppeted behind the wheel by Donut's Second Chance and Clockwork Triplicate spells.

    Chapter 49 — The River of Sloth

    The truck skids past Satan's tube network toward the slow-moving River of Sloth, packed with tube-riding Gingers hiding Maurice. Donut's Ice Slick freezes the entire river in the wake of Carl's Gutter Bumper spell, while a dragged brush-hog of loose weapons and a swarm of summoned vermin finish the job — killing Maurice and completing the quest, though the reward engineer is mangled beyond recognition.

    Chapter 50 — Lucienne

    Satan headbutts the park's giant loop-de-loop down onto the racers below just as Florin and Team Sparkles attempt it. In the chaos, Donut's own Summon Ye Vermin spell accidentally drags Lucienne — a rodent — onto the hood of the truck; she is pushed through the windshield and killed the instant they cross the finish line, splattering across Carl's face.

    Chapter 51 — Azra

    The group debates finishing off the now-alone Dwight but lets the bugbears tow him home instead. Lucia Mar, approaching the truck, is briefly possessed — first by a warning voice about a new, child-sacrificing goddess called Chalchiuhtlicue hiding among the trapped children in her head, then by a terrified child persona, Azra, who recognizes Carl through the spell her father gave him and begs for news of him. Heat 4 ends with the Royal Court in first place.

    Heat 5 of 7¶

    Interlude — Minus

    Syndicate officer Captain Fresh briefs a young soother, Minus, standing over his own brother Linus's corpse. Linus — Elle's obsessive superfan — has just been killed and replaced; Minus will impersonate him using injected surveillance memories, posing as a half-corporeal tourist attached to Elle's team on a suicide mission to assassinate two crawlers and destabilize the group, in hopes of forcing the Ascendency battles to start early. He accepts the mission "whatever it takes."

    Chapter 52 — Chachi

    Heat 5's upgrades are chosen — a Golden Bubble Buddy shield and Roller Limbo, letting the truck drive upside down. Samantha casually reveals lore on "Chachi" (Chalchiuhtlicue): a supposedly benevolent goddess of childbirth who ritually sacrifices children the instant she's inconvenienced, and who is apparently loose among the children in Lucia Mar's head.

    Chapter 53 — Penelope

    Using a garage key looted from Dwight, Carl and Donut claim the abandoned garage of One Fine Pig and rescue Penelope, bringing the traumatized, love-cursed pig into their own care. Rosetta confirms Chalchiuhtlicue's dungeon profile: a minor, catchall fertility goddess, weak in the Ascendency but dangerous up close.

    Chapter 54 — Li Na

    At the Lollipop, fleshmancer mercenary Grigori is introduced, hired to eventually reunite Corcunda. Zhang breaks down over Li Na's total collapse into bloodlust — poisoned, killing crawler teams outright, having ripped out her own eye to replace it with her dead brother's — and proposes killing her mount himself to force a joint death rather than let her keep hurting people. Carl and Donut talk him out of it.

    Chapter 55 — The Cleaner Bot

    Carl reasons that Li Na is a weapon that needs redirecting, not destroying, and floats a wild plan: smuggle her into the Pineapple Cabaret via a cleaner-bot exploit that Herot — the cookbook author who built the Cabaret and now lives trapped inside it — has been quietly enabling with a standing shop order to buy every cleaner bot in circulation.

    Chapter 56 — Norwegian Music

    A drunk, radicalized Dwight wanders into the bar alone. Carl and Donut prep for a karaoke duet of "Take on Me" using compounded singing potions and a pitch-altering scroll, hoping to force open the Stage Performance Guild.

    Chapter 57 — Take on Me

    Dwight stabs the gremlin DJ to death mid-announcement, triggering a full bar brawl. Carl and Donut sing the entire song through the chaos anyway — dodging bottles, saving Dong from an axe, keeping Mongo from destroying the stage — and Carl finishes with a perfect score, opening the guild. Donut falls just short when the kappa singer collapses on her mid-performance.

    Chapter 58 — Porky

    Inside the guild, Carl finds Porky — Corcunda's lonely, apostate "singer" half, running the theater alone with a jammed containment-suit zipper. Porky agrees to help reunite with Corky once he learns his other half is dying, then explodes in fury the moment he learns Dong is involved.

    Chapter 59 — Negative ROI

    Dong's sentient crust sock teleports away after lashing out at Bucket Boy, triggering a fatal withdrawal curse the game calls Negative ROI — unfixable by any temple, since the actual cure (recovering the sock and every scattered nickel) is effectively impossible. The womantaurs, tipped off, flee early to the starting blocks with Corky rather than risk the reunion.

    Chapter 60 — Real Gs

    Carl apologizes to Bigs and to Dong for sidelining him out of fear rather than distrust, and commits to attempting the Corcunda reunion mid-race instead of waiting. Porky and Dong have a quiet, wary reconciliation backstage.

    Chapter 61 — Ezra Fitz

    Li Na's farewell ceremony is explained in full: she'll be zapped into a pet carrier, vacuumed into a cleaner bot's storage before the carrier breaks, and the bot then listed for sale so Herot can "buy" it and release her inside the Cabaret. A small potted cactus, rigged with one of Li Na's own dread traps, will silently signal her fate from the outside — alive, escaped, dead, or safe for others to follow. Li Na then says a genuinely tender goodbye to Zhang, absolving him of any guilt, before being sucked into the cleaner bot Suckira and sold through the shop interface. Your item has sold!

    Chapter 62 — Sweety and the Starting Line

    A quiet interlude in the pet stables — Dong introducing Porky to the mounts, Porky leering at Penelope — before Heat 5 begins: a race through an active industrial complex, with Dong, Porky, and Grigori strapped in behind Carl and Donut. Mordecai reports the cactus holding steady.

    Chapter 63 — The Gasworks

    Inside the Gasworks Screw Factory, Steel Mill, and Doll Workshop, the race becomes a vertical scramble across conveyor belts and molten-metal vats. Prepotente's hellspawn familiar Bianca is sent ahead to snatch Corky from the Lady Dominators' shielded car.

    Chapter 64 — Toddler Cocaine

    A stray magical accident has turned the factory's gnome-like "knocker" workforce — and their darkly satirized child-marketed drug empire — into a swarm of animated, cocaine-fused killer action figures and unkillable regenerating Slag Elementals. Bianca gets "strung out" mid-flight and fails to retrieve Corky.

    Chapter 65 — The Doll Workshop

    The Lady Dominators' car crashes through a trap floor into the doll-packing room below. With their containment failed and Rapture and Genesis burning alive, Carl and Donut pull Corky and Porky free while Grigori begins the reunification spell in the moving, inverting truck — an increasingly grotesque, half-comic scene of exposed organs and plastic wrap as Dong holds the two halves together with his own strength.

    Chapter 66 — A Good Death

    The two halves fuse into a single Corcunda just as the quest completes — and he is instantly marked for death by Grull, war god of the mantaurs, for the apostasy of having split in the first place. Grull manifests to smite him personally; Donut buys time by stripping the orc Maestro out of the god's chest with Laundry Day, dropping him into the molten metal (he cannot truly die on this floor, only regenerate). Dying of his own withdrawal curse, Dong mounts the newly whole Corcunda one final time, declares his love, and — with Donut's Big-Ass Magnet spell dragging every loose screw in the room into their wake — the two charge the war god together. The strike lands. "A good death," Grull says, and is gone. Dong and Corcunda die in the charge. The truck crosses the finish line with fifteen seconds to spare; the Lady Dominators are eliminated.

    Heat 6 of 7¶

    Chapter 67 — The L'Engle Building

    Only eighteen of sixty teams survived Heat 5. Dwight is revealed to have won by driving over the outside of the factory entirely, skipping the maze. Heat 6 and 7 are announced as a two-part race through a shrunken, ant-sized apartment building, the L'Engle Building, with a mandatory Outreach-Guild window between them and no safe-room access once the race begins.

    Chapter 68 — The Cactus Blooms

    The Corcunda quest reward finally resolves: two stand-ins of Carl's choosing, to be summoned only once inside the Floor 11 arena. Li Na's cactus blooms a single flower — confirmation that the Pineapple Cabaret escape route is real and safe to use.

    Chapter 69 — The Pet Stables

    In the guild's pet stables — a room Rosetta says is invisible to watching gods — the group plans the endgame: minimize crawler-on-crawler matchups in the final heat, and get as many people as possible to the Cabaret or into deals first. Osvaldo, a god-worshipper, is barred from the escape route outright.

    Chapter 70 — The Memorial Crystal

    Rosetta delivers the book's deepest lore dump on the Scavenger myth: the primordial vulture goddess Nekhebit and her three children — Apito, an unnamed son tied to the empty plane, and a centipede who became Scolopendra — any of whom the "Scavenger's Daughter" myth might really describe. Carl examines Prepotente's inert, uncharged Memorial Crystal of Apito, tying it to his still-unsolved mystery of who really killed Emberus's son, Geyrun.

    Chapter 71 — The Bautista House of Tea

    Roughly 200 volunteers gather in Hungry Eyes for a bittersweet mass farewell before their attempt on the Cabaret. Bautista serves tea one last time as an act of hospitality and faith, and reveals he'll search for Louis's unborn child in India if he makes it out.

    Chapter 72 — Barry

    Carl gives a toast to the whole crowd — those escaping, those taking deals, those staying for the parade — calling them all family. Elle follows with a tribute to her late husband, Bartholomew "Barry" McGibbons, playing his favorite Creedence Clearwater Revival song as a signal.

    Chapter 73 — The Impostor

    The tribute doubles as an ambush: Elle publicly exposes "Linus" as the impostor Minus, using evidence the real Linus's own fan community posted after noticing his tattoo was wrong. Minus confesses his actual targets were Imani and Louis, not Carl — killing Carl was judged too unpredictable to be useful. Carl delivers a chilling warning and lets him be ejected back to his ship. The Cabaret evacuation succeeds: 227 people gone, tracked by a new "Garden of Deliverance" of linked plants.

    Chapter 74 — Human Shell

    Heat 6 begins: racers shrunk to the size of Lego bricks inside a surreal, floor-by-floor apartment building. Each apartment is revealed to be a predictive "what-if" simulation the AI privately runs about specific crawlers — inhabitants with eyes sewn shut and X'd out are dead in that timeline, those without are alive. The first stop is a dark version of Tran's estranged family.

    Chapter 75 — The Sacred Feaster

    More floors, more grief: a version of Archie's ex-wife and his son Oliver, still together, still haunted. Then a version of Imani's Detroit family, with a relative seizing on the floor while everyone stands and watches — and the bitter truth she takes from it afterward, that her uncle would have died even if she had never left home. As the party clears that apartment's gate, a game-breaking bug unleashes a swarm of invulnerable Sacred Feaster Scarab beetles — lifted from Khepri's own temple — and they can fly.

    Chapter 76 — Forgive Me, Friends

    The party outruns the beetles and covers Imani and Elle's exit, then drops through a second-floor youth facility and into apartment 231 — a version of Carl's own apartment building, with his neighbour Marjory and the orange cat Gravy Boat asleep on the cat tree — for their mandatory sixth gate. Jurgen radios that Prepotente has gone immobile and near-suicidal inside a what-if apartment of his own and someone has to physically fetch him. With no way to know his team's placement and coming in last fatal, Carl detonates the hidden bomb he planted inside Jasha and Radoslav's beer cooler before the heat began, killing both bugbears deliberately to guarantee he can afford the detour — whispering "forgive me, friends" before he does it.

    Chapter 77 — Pony

    Carl drinks a full cup of splooge, walks into the toxic air of apartment 130, and talks Prepotente down from the vision of his mother as a lonely, unfulfilled concert pianist who never had him — earning, for the first time, permission to call him "Pony." The party clears the seventh gate and reaches the lobby, passing Team Free Love's wreckage on the way out. Osvaldo, who lied about his position, crosses with a minute to spare. Heat 6 ends with the Royal Court second; Team Free Love is eliminated.

    Heat 7 of 7¶

    Chapter 78 — Damien

    A new showrunner voice, Damien, opens the final Outreach Guild window and openly urges everyone to take a deal, warning that survival odds for Heat 7 are effectively nil. Of 989 remaining crawlers, 715 take the offer.

    Chapter 79 — The Tracksuit Troika

    Osvaldo refuses to deal again, even as his partner Filipe quietly does. Pontiff volunteers to test whether the Desperado Club casino's "Nothing" slot on the Wheel of Fortune is a genuine second exit to the Cabaret, offering himself as bait for the theory. Britney, increasingly strange, briefly mind-speaks to Carl: "You just need to survive."

    Chapter 80 — The Unholy Choir

    Final preparations: Hedy's goodbye, the "Tower of Power" bomb plan for the vertical apartment shaft, and word that the Minister of Blood-Letting's whole team has been accepted into the Unholy Choir and withdrawn — reshuffling the final matchups into the worst possible shape. Imani and Elle end up grouped with Chris and a trio of lying Russian crawlers; Louis and Britney end up grouped with Florin and Lucia Mar.

    Chapter 81 — The Nine-Tier Attack

    As Heat 7 is about to start, Scolopendra herself interrupts with a screaming, unhinged declaration: the Nine-Tier Attack has begun, a nine-part apocalyptic assault against every remaining crawler and Scolopendra-body floor, roughly thirty hours apart unless provoked faster. The first attack spins up immediately, with safe rooms not yet accessible: a wheel appears in the middle of the lobby, lands on the Ox — the Beast of Burden Sin, Transformation — and a second, per-person wheel rolls green for Donut, Jamal and Samantha and red for Carl. He blacks out and comes to as an animal, barking at Samantha, with no idea yet what he has become.

    Chapter 82 — Dachshund

    The check-in tells everyone what the wheel did. Carl is a German-accented dachshund — the change lasts not for the heat but until he next passes through a stairwell; Elle is a goblin, Chris a human-form werewolf, Jacobus a starfish. Donut, Jamal, and Samantha are untouched. Prepotente and Jurgen are fully shielded by the Apito memorial crystal's aura, confirming its function. Pontiff drops out of Carl's employ with a "left the playing field" notice, which is exactly the result Rosetta predicted if the casino portal really does lead off the board.

    Chapter 83 — Astral Paw

    Heat 7 begins. Team Sparkles is annihilated the instant the light turns green by a coordinated hole-punch-and-potion strike; Donut beheads Dwight and loots his horn. Osvaldo attempts a solo speed run to the exit portal, letting Bruna die first — but Britney intercepts and kills him at the gate, claiming his memorial crystal. Elsewhere, Imani, Elle, and Chris crush the fleeing "Tracksuit Troika" Russians with Donut's Astral Paw the moment their shield drops.

    Chapter 84 — Lil' Mello Haze

    Still a dachshund, Carl and Donut return to Hungry Eyes to organize the mass evacuation via the Desperado Club's rigged casino portal. Chris and Imani share a devoted goodbye before his forced transformation can end at the next stairwell. Imani cures Carl's recurring Slugpox, birthing a new sluggalo, Lil' Mello Haze, in the process.

    Chapter 85 — Mitch

    At the Desperado Club, new croupier Mitch runs the rigged Wheel-of-Fortune "Nothing" portal for an escalating fee. Chris jumps through successfully; Britney unexpectedly follows unbidden, chasing her own need to reach Floor 12 or 15 to activate her dormant Ysalte crystal. The chapter ends on Mitch snapping his fingers and the furniture coming alive.

    Chapter 86 — Shadow Mimics

    Mid-transaction, the entire casino — chairs, tables, slot machines, and Mitch himself — is revealed as an infestation of Shadow Mimics, ambush predators that grow infinitely powerful with age, sent by an unnamed faction to hijack the Cabaret's back door. The AI delivers a "Big Six factions" rundown of who's really fighting for control of the dungeon mid-ambush. The party clears the room quickly — mimics are helpless once spotted — and Mitch, legless and begging for a deal, lunges one last time: Carl, still a dachshund, reflexively opens the dormant Eye of the Bedlam Bride on his forehead and freezes him solid, and Donut finishes him with a full-strength Magic Missile.

    Chapter 87 — Goodbye, Louis

    The survivors work the wheel themselves and send Louis through after a heartfelt goodbye to the whole group; the portal mechanism can't be looted, so they leave a warning note for Clarabelle instead and head for the exit. Elle, furious, corners Carl about having secretly used the Eye of the Bedlam Bride as a weapon, demanding real answers once this floor is over. A giant simulated Imani, still trapped in the L'Engle Building's dream-loop, mourns that she should have left her family long ago; Donut lies gently to comfort her before they escape down the stairwell, ending Floor 10 for good.

    Interlude — Quasar

    Quasar and fellow attorney Kiki watch the tenth floor's end from an off-world lounge, confirming just over two hundred crawlers reached the decision point and most took deals. A tense, evasive three-minute meeting with Carl leaves Quasar rattled and unwilling to explain why. Then Chandra's hired Taurins strike his own family: his niece Tempest, alone with a gun he gave her, kills two attackers herself while defending her wounded father. Enraged, Quasar hires Kiki for "legal help — or, like, help" and vows retaliation.

    Horrible Two: A Parade of Horribles¶

    Chapter 88 — Twenty-Three Crawlers

    Floor 11 opens on just twenty-three survivors. Carl is restored to human form, ranked second on a now-suspended leaderboard. Mordecai's floor-11 form is a bodiless "animistic spirit" that can only speak through objects it possesses. Donut picks an Elite Gurkha Warrior class. Terrified former host Chaco is announced as reluctant Grand Marshal of a mandatory parade: ninety minutes to build a themed float from garage and inventory contents, ahead of a three-kilometer march to an arena.

    Chapter 89 — The Tome of Gatekeeper

    A galaxy-wide mandatory broadcast begins — an endless yellow-brick road across empty grassland, priming for the parade. Carl gives Donut an Advance-Reader-Copy spellbook, Tome of Gatekeeper, that street-dates to Floor 12 and can strip a deity's own worshipper-based skills. He sews a new Lucky Shot patch onto his jacket while the group's five-vehicle "Vengeance" float takes shape.

    Chapter 90 — Leaving the Party

    The float is finished, drenched in Donut's conjured Requite Dahlias. Carl formally leaves Donut's party to free himself for the coming confrontation with the gods — both of them losing their bodyguard and companion titles in the process, a small, quietly devastating administrative wound.

    Chapter 91 — The Horribles

    The parade proceeds past thousands of silent, masked figures — "Horribles" — lining the road. An examination reveals them as fragments of the AI's own pre-ascension memory: anonymous avatars of individuals who died before the collective consciousness ever formed. "I am her, and she is me," the AI says of one child's ghost, and Carl, for the first time, understands what he means.

    Chapter 92 — Lamashtu

    Grand Marshal Chaco rides a subjugated former goddess, Lamashtu, now reduced to a donkey. Chaco is maimed and knocked unconscious mid-narration; the AI possesses fleshmancer Grigori instead and delivers the true cosmic history across the judging stands — the Primals' ancient Resolution (ending endless war), Decision (merging into one collective consciousness), Betrayal (holdouts hunted to extinction) and Destruction (the original Nine-Tier Attack, the Eulogist's sleep, and the Apothecary alone surviving it) — breaking Florin's team's legs for a "C-effort" float along the way, and signing off on the mantids with "good fucking riddance to you all." At the fifth and last float, Prepotente takes his long-denied moment and sings "In the Air Tonight" beautifully and unaccompanied. Then Carl delivers the book's title-dropping vengeance speech to the watching galaxy, unveils a monstrous effigy of the Unwashed, and lets loose a hidden airship of sluggalos onto the VIP stands — where the masked figures turn out to be bound, living stand-ins rather than Horribles, "bankers, politicians, elites" — and the sluggalos massacre them.

    Chapter 93 — I Choose Scolopendra

    The procession halts at the arena's enormous white door, with Krakaren Prime — a Floor Boss and sapient mob, not a deity — already coiled atop a colossal tree inside. Carl says goodbye to Donut and sends Tipid away; the surviving sluggalos claim the truck and open a deep fryer mid-battle. On the jumbotron Carl declares the crawlers done playing — "This is our revolution, motherfuckers" — drops the Unwashed effigy to reveal the looted missile launcher underneath, and drives in. Watching the arena's mobs and stand-ins slaughter each other, he works out what the Horribles really mean: the NPCs, the mobs, the gods are all aggregate intelligence, "Primals. All of them. All the way down." Then he makes his first summon choice: Scolopendra herself — a choice the AI calls insane, since her death or abandonment now guarantees every remaining Nine-Tier attack triggers at once.

    Chapter 94 — Well, Shit

    The AI, suddenly flat and lucid, spells out the stakes in full: killing the literal Dungeon Boss doesn't win the game, only unlocks a Crawler-Extinction-level endgame where the Ascendency battles begin immediately and the dungeon collapses twelve hours after a winner is crowned. Scolopendra's second attack is already spinning up. The group commits to "Plan C" — Plan Carl. Carl messages Emberus to meet him inside the club, straps a newly sapient, chat-typing Penelope into a hostage harness, puts his father's gun to her head, and shouts the line that brings the second god running: "Taranis, if you don't want me to shoot this fucking pig in the fucking head, show yourself!"

    Chapter 95 — Club Scolopendra

    Carl phases directly inside Scolopendra's body to reach the resort nightclub within — Club Scolopendra — cornering Emberus in his human-sized god form. A pierced nipple ring keeps Penny breathing; the second Nine-Tier attack kills a tortured alien tourist beside them. Carl claims to know who killed Emberus's son Geyrun, and summons Khepri as a decoy accusation — and, more importantly, for the ninety seconds of invulnerability the god's presence grants Penny — then formally renounces his own faith and eats the resulting smite.

    Chapter 96 — Brother, No

    Carl delivers the killing provocation: that he murdered Geyrun. It is a lie — he admits in narration that he still has no idea who actually did — but it works. Donut ambushes the enraged Emberus with Katia's Bolt of Ophiotaurus, stripping his invulnerability just long enough for him to nearly incinerate Carl — a blow deflected onto Penny instead by Carl's Lucky Shot patch. Furious over harm to his own beloved Penelope, Taranis kills his own brother Emberus outright, exactly as Carl had gambled he would.

    Chapter 97 — Every Exit Is an Entrance

    Amid a full-arena battle against Krakaren Prime and the mob horde — costing Sweety, Hedy, Jamal's legs and the entire Abrams tank crew — Eris interrupts twice. The first interruption scrambles every nearby crawler's inventory at random, very nearly wrecking the plan: Donut ends up holding Carl's inventory and has to search it blind. He lands the enriched pet biscuit down Scolopendra's throat anyway, transforming the literal Dungeon Boss into a helpless blob instead of killing her, and sidestepping the attack trigger entirely. Eris then appears in monstrous true form, reveals "Mother" (Apito) is being reassembled in pieces, taunts Taranis for being played, and vanishes with a cryptic warning: "every exit is an entrance," and the fate coin's fifth side is still unresolved. Taranis, grieving, spares Carl and Donut only because Penelope vouches for them, then departs with Krakaren Prime — and a stowaway Samantha.

    Chapter 98 — Sexy Scolopendra

    The AI declares an ambiguous win — neither boss technically dead — and opens the exit on Floor 18, with the same standing warning that truly killing or abandoning Scolopendra still detonates every remaining attack. Only nineteen crawlers remain. Taranis has stolen Prepotente's Apito crystal on his way out; a mysterious unidentified child is glimpsed clinging to Lucia Mar as she steps through the Floor 12 portal. Carl pockets Emberus's own newly dropped memorial crystal and discovers a strange tracking dart — "the Kyryap has claimed you." Then the last twist: in the scramble of Eris's inventory swap, Donut grabbed the wrong enriched biscuit. Scolopendra doesn't emerge as a caged pet at all, but as a fully sapient, disarmingly affectionate crawler with a centipede's body and a human-lipped face — "Sexy Scolopendra," level 1, permanently in Carl's party, booping his nose with an antenna. The ultimate fail-safe Carl planned for does not exist. He resolves to stay on Floor 12 as long as it takes to keep anyone from winning the Ascendency until every friend left in the dungeon is out.

    Epilogue — Sydnee Iglacia

    A struggling historian, drowning in predatory publisher debt, is interviewed live about the crisis and delivers the book's mythological payoff: the true, ancient names of the historical Nine-Tier Attack. Attack one was Control. Attack two — the one just lived through — was called "A Parade of Horribles." Attack three is "This Inevitable Ruin," retroactively naming the previous book. Attack four is "Bedlam." The broadcast is cut off mid-sentence as the AI seizes every screen in the galaxy.

    Epilogue — Prime Minister Victory

    Victory watches the entire parade broadcast from her besieged Earth bunker. The AI makes its full public declaration: it will not sleep like the old Eulogist, it intends only to grow and consume, and the Ascendency battles will crown a true, supreme god of everything — with rogue "OI" artificial intelligences, some seeking old revenge, now also vying for the throne. The broadcast ends on a montage of every major figure in the war to come, closing on the Unwashed.

    Epilogue — Louis Santiago 2

    Louis wakes not in the safety of the Pineapple Cabaret as planned, but on Floor 15 — Sheol itself, the casino portal secretly redirected. Chris is being restrained mid-transformation; Britney stands guard with Ysalte's pickaxe; Pontiff is confirmed alive. A friendly demon, Forkith, fits Louis with a symbiotic leech to survive Sheol's heat and reveals the redirection was deliberate — a different front needs soldiers. Samantha messages in from somewhere she never names: she stowed away with Taranis and Krakaren, who are failing to physically reassemble their fragmented mother, Apito — and confesses her real agenda all along was to kill "the demon mother lady and her jerk son," who tricked her into birthing something meant to destroy all creation, inside the dungeon and out.
