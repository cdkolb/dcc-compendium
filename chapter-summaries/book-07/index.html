    Book 7: This Inevitable Ruin — Chapter Summaries¶

    Full narrative recaps, chapter by chapter. For the condensed version, see the Timeline; for standalone reference pages, see Characters, Bosses, and Spells.

    A note on this book's structure

    Book 7 is built differently from the rest of the series. After the front matter and prologue it splits into four numbered Parts containing 87 chapters, with ten unnumbered interludes threaded between them. Most of those interludes are narrated by a different author of the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook, presented in edition order — Porthus (2nd), Dante (3rd), Tipid (4th), Everly (5th), Milk (6th), Volteeg (7th), Justice Light (8th), Rosetta (9th), and finally York (10th) in the epilogue. Two further interludes — "Christmas Morning" and "Several Days Earlier" — are Carl's own. They appear below in reading order, not grouped at the end.

    A note on chapters containing boss fights or major reveals

    Chapters with a real boss battle, a team elimination, or a major emotional turn run noticeably longer than the rest — the mechanics and the fallout are worth keeping straight.

    Front Matter — Tempest's Floor 8 Recap School Report

    The book opens with Quasar, Carl's attorney, being interviewed by his teenage nullian niece Tempest, who needs a Floor 8 recap for a school report and has been made to fix his (purely decorative, non-functional) sink as payment — his lesson being never give anything up for free. His recap is crude, foul-mouthed, and mostly accurate: the card-collecting gimmick, Cuba, Yago and Raul and Alpha Carl, Quan stealing Carl's key and dying for it, Tserendolgor's death, Sister Ines the serial-killer nun, Louis putting her down and getting marked by Ysalte, Paz killing the goddess, and the something-only-viewers-saw involving one of the women in the party. He lays out the stakes for Floor 9: neither Donut nor Katia can leave until every naga is dead, and even then only one of them goes, which is why Katia has the Huanxin Jinx deal and the flower. Ten teams, one of them the NPCs under Juice Box. His closing advice is that everyone rich on this floor — Prince Stalwart, the Operatic banker, Epitome Tagg — can now actually die. Then Tempest turns the recorder off and asks whether he's in real danger for representing Carl. He tells her the truth: they all are, and for the first time in his life he's glad they live in the middle of nowhere.

    Prologue — Christmas Morning

    The real message. In the Florida homeless shelter, Paulie — the Residual also called Goff — is mid-ramble when the world freezes: the AI has granted a genuine, time-stopped conversation. Everything Paulie says aloud is cover; this is the truth. The crawl is unnecessary. Every living thing born on a seeded world carries a grain-of-sand primal engine in its brain, and the Syndicate harvests those engines from the dungeon's dead to feed the bloated, comatose center system, which expands with every feeding. Stop the crawl and the zone would shrink over generations — nobody alive would suffer. It exists only because the spectacle makes money. Paulie tells Carl to drink the frozen coffee, which installs an illegal Valtay Containment Interface in his natural neural interface. That interface can pop "the fuse" — the mantid failsafe that turns this system's star nova, killing the AI and everyone else. Then Paulie drops the pitch and speaks as the human half of himself: cross-contamination has taught him compassion, he hates the idea of spending millions of innocent lives, and he begs Carl to be absolutely certain before ever pressing it. The interface begins installing, and Carl is dumped into Orren's office thinking holy fucking shit.

    Interlude — Porthus (2nd Edition)

    Porthus sits bloody and shaking in an outreach guild zero zone at the start of the eleventh floor, being read deal options by a soother attorney who brought snacks and won't meet his eyes (his race/class is never stated in the text). He cannot stop thinking about Menerva — the retiring schoolteacher who screamed Go. Run. with her foot trapped on the tenth floor while he jumped into the exit instead of climbing back for her. The tenth had been a world where every manufactured object was sapient and psychotic and grew by eating other objects. Of his twenty-strong party, he believes he is the only survivor. Offered low-floor undead, city attendant, or game guide, he thinks of the cookbook — of the pride of adding to it, and the guilt of not writing more — and takes the game-guide deal, a hundred seasons, knowing it means never learning whether she lived. Top level 89. Survived his indentureship. Author of the second edition. Current status: Missing. Wanted by Syndicate Security.

    Part I — The Ceasefire¶

    Chapter 1 — Welcome to Faction Wars

    Carl and Donut land face-first in a drained, stinking alley in Larracos, City of Dreams. 32,429 crawlers remain; 30 days to collapse; a 60-hour ceasefire, after which only Team Retribution and its allies may stay in the city. Carl's Scavenger's Daughter is dangerously full of souls — Soul Poisoning, blocked partway by his Emberus ring — so he vaporizes a dead concierge shark with Daughter's Kiss. Eight teams immediately form the Bloc and declare war on both Team Retribution and the Princess Posse; Donut accepts the NPC truce before Carl can finish a sentence, keeping them in the city, and Ferdinand promptly renames the alliance "Ferdinand and his Bird." Cascadia — audibly drunk or worse — lays out the rules: ten teams arranged as a pie around Larracos, the city sealed until four teams remain, then the Peeling of Larracos, where attackers need the throne room for six hours and defenders need to hold a week. A team dies when its warlord dies or its throne falls. Donut picks her new class: Deathbed Hellcat, a long-range sniper-assassin that quadruples her spell range, grants at-will invisibility — and comes with a hat and a ghillie suit she does not yet understand.

    Chapter 2 — The Ghillie Suit

    In the guild saferoom Donut refuses to wear the ghillie suit ("I'll look like a tassel") but approves of the boonie hat, which grants a Targeting skill and stacks with the suit to let her snipe line-of-sight enemies from over a mile out. Mordecai's Floor 9 form is a bespectacled Canadian in a snowsuit and raccoon hat who says "aboot" and gets furious when it's pointed out; his upgraded alchemy table now lets him reverse-engineer recipes, and he is giddy. Mongo's burnt wing will be regrown by a Revitalize Pet potion. Carl works through fifty-plus backlogged achievements — Lord of the Flies for trapping a fellow crawler, Chickenshit Exploit for breaking the card system, Touched by a Demon (only one ever awarded), a Crab Chowder achievement that permanently adds a "chowder style" option to every food box, and an ominous, groaning Unbirthing for teleporting into Samantha's neck hole that ends with the AI telling him he is in fucking trouble.

    Chapter 3 — The Tiara of a Thousand Lights

    Donut's boxes: a Shield spellbook, a Sentry war-protection spellbook, and a smoking, screeching black tome called You're Not Done Yet that makes Mordecai step backward in fear. Her celestial benefactor box — from the baffling sponsor Long Haul Biological Waste Management Solutions — delivers the Tiara of a Thousand Lights and its charge pillow: any enchanted hat left on the pillow for ten hours is permanently drained and its buffs transferred into one of the tiara's 99 absorption jewels. A second, purple pillow arrives from the Apothecary, doubling the charging rate. Then Quan's Cloak of the Benevolent Champion, the first celestial item ever awarded on Earth: it raises the wearer's lowest stat to match their highest over 66 hours, grants Find Weakened, and pushes four spells and one skill to level 15. Mordecai's trick is a MySpace Photo potion that fakes Donut's Charisma 30 points high, so her Constitution climbs from 4 to 193. The cloak buffs Bad Attitude, Wall of Fire, Heal Critter, and Puddle Jumper.

    Chapter 4 — Gloom Wraith Phase

    Carl's turn: a fan box of ammunition and a leather shoulder holster for his father's Glock (he has no intention of ever using it), automaton fabricator parts, a Spider Reaper Minion patch that opens the Midnight Market, a town-upgrade coupon, bagpipes, and the Enchanted Toe Ring of the Well-Balanced — Heart Balance (an auto-trigger healing potion at 2%), Mind Balance (protection from enslavement and conscription), and Physical Balance, which lists Walk on Air as "already obtained," a gift Carl realizes with revulsion came from eating Li Jun's eye. The ring immediately reports Mind Balance is currently triggered. His Celestial Heavy Metal box — delivered by a demon drum kit — is a talking, hell-kissed skill potion that grants Gloom Wraith Phase, level 13: non-corporeal lunges that unravel unarmored flesh. Mordecai, shaken, tells them they are now the two most powerful crawlers he has ever worked with. "Good," Carl says. "Because we have eight whole armies to kill."

    Interlude — Dante (3rd Edition)

    A crocodilian game guide and trap-master instructor reads a newsletter announcing that the guild staff are being reassigned as ice castle guards for the coming eighth floor. Beside him is his skyfowl neighbour Justice Light, ten seasons into an indentureship he specifically negotiated: he demanded to be made the shopkeeper of the very NPC he murdered during his own crawl, and he spends his days rearranging shelves and sewing strange three-dimensional fabric sculptures. Dante recognized him instantly as a fellow cookbook owner and transferred guilds just to be near him, but never told him — guilt, he thinks, at having written only a single entry. Justice Light says flatly that he will refuse to fight crawlers. Nothing good will happen if he does, Dante tells him, and privately admits he has plenty of violence in his heart, just not for the innocent. Top level 89. Killed by administrative action for refusing to follow orders, 14 seasons short of freedom. Author of the third edition. Current status: Dead.

    Chapter 5 — Sir Rendlegore

    Carl finally confesses to owning Garret, Ren's tummy acher — who died a fifth time and reverted to a nameless, memoryless level-1 meatball. Donut treats it as an affair. The creature immediately climbs into Mongo's mouth, is swallowed, and is yakked back up giggling. Bonded over a bowl of chowder, he needs a name, and after a long argument Louis lands on Sir Rendlegore — "Rend" — which honours Ren without making him a copy. Meanwhile Samantha has escaped to hunt Tish, Ghazi's ex-girlfriend at the Larracos college, and Katia has to chase her down. Katia then attempts Carl's hair restoration; Splash Zone's power-wash fails because his spell counts as offensive in a saferoom, and Carl emerges from the shower with waist-length romance-novel hair that regrows every two hours for the next fortnight. Donut goes around the room physically touching people to make them officers; Florin becomes General, Louis becomes Army Air General.

    Chapter 6 — Party Planner

    Louis's celestial box: an Airship Portal guild upgrade (a dimensional hangar and elevator, with a vulnerable trapdoor on the saferoom roof), and an aircraft. Waiting for the elevator, Carl notices a strand of Christmas tinsel that survived the memory collapse and has a small vertigo of realization — they are living in a simulation of a simulation, where make-believe consequences are often deadlier than real ones — and remembers Lightning Lou, the old dog at the Florida gas station, and Paulie's warning. The eye on his chest blinks. Then the elevator opens on Party Planner: a fifty-foot silver "Casket in the Sky" tilt-rotor stealth light bomber, with room for five crew, a bomb bay that boosts all yields by 50%, and almost no defensive armament. Florin calls it a half-sized B-17 crossed with a stealth bomber. Carl calls it a death trap.

    Chapter 7 — War Leader Arief

    Leaving through the college saferoom, Donut discovers Louis's enchanted "Donut Holes" bootleg fan-club t-shirt — a cartoon of her backside, complete with an implied butthole — and demands royalties from Zev, who informs her the licensing threshold has never been reached by any crawler. Outside, Larracos is staggering: a Tolkien college of alabaster towers, flowering vines, and NPCs who are visibly preparing for war rather than going about their lives. They are met by War Leader Arief, a young Semeru dwarf in parade armour and a feathered heirloom helmet, commander of the Larracos Defense Force Infantry and carrying a Despondent debuff because his goddess Ysalte is dead. His people's history is the floor's real tragedy: millions of Semeru dug toward the gods, found the roots of the All Tree, built their castle as an apology — and were annihilated by Scolopendra's nine-tier attack, leaving fewer than ten thousand stewards of an empty city. Arief then bows to Louis: Warlord Juice Box wishes to solidify the truce in the traditional manner. Through marriage. Immediately.

    Chapter 8 — Down Through the Rings

    Epilogue — York (10th Edition)

    Three movements. York, a tenodera poet and the tenth-edition author, is the sole living soul aboard the orbiting Homecoming Queen, writing bad verse and turning off the gravity to pretend he is a ghost. He is the one who vaporized the orc yacht full of children — including the new king — and he volunteered for it not out of duty but because he hoped it would make him feel something. It didn't. He mourns his own absence of emotion, a numbness bred into his warrior ancestors for exactly this work. Then the news: the newest mantis colony system has simply gone nova. Then Boomer calls in to dock: Porthus's pre-recorded announcement is going out declaring a militant resistance that will move against Syndicate property everywhere unless the crawl ends, the ship's hidden enhancement-zone weapons and Cronus power armour are all online, and Boomer wants four suits and a skiff set aside for a pregnant veteran on the surface. It's time to bring the war out of the dungeon. And at that, at last, York feels something. Top level 71. Author of the tenth edition. Current status: Captain in the newly coined resistance organization, The Open Intellect Action Network. Second: Carl's exit meeting with Quasar in a freezing, doorless zero zone, where the silence is so complete he could sleep in it. "She's your new wife."
