    Chapter 1 — The Iron Tangle

    Carl, Donut, Katia, and Mongo arrive on Floor 4 aboard a moving subway car — "the Iron Tangle," a sprawling train-and-tunnel maze with only 9,375 stairwells hidden among hundreds of thousands of stops. Donut picks the Football Hooligan class (gaining the party-buffing Mascot skill tied to Mongo's kills). Investigating a mysterious sealed car (#15), the party is swarmed by baby-like Drek at Sirin Station and ducks into a "Janitor's Lair" car, killing two Jikininki ghouls via a Magic Missile Double Shot and a Smush stomp. Trapped between hordes front and back, Carl sets an Alarm Module (accidentally blasting "Fergalicious") and a Spike Module as delays, then casts Protective Shell as an improvised plow, bulldozing the mobs the length of the train while the party sprints to the rear car.

    Chapter 2 — Station 83

    At transfer station 83, the party meets Non-Combatant shopkeeper Limp Richard (a Mole Man) and settles into a fast-food-themed safe room. Mordecai arrives in a new Grulke (toad) form, having lost daily-update access as a Manager. The crawler count has already dropped to 389,441. Opening Book 2's delayed quest loot, Carl gets the Necklace of the Haute Bourgeoisie (granting Talon Strike) and the Bang Bro spell tome; Donut gets the Tiara of Mana Genita and the Hole spell; Katia gets a shapeshiftable arm-shield. Using AI-granted "screw the sponsors" coupons, the party combines three level-3 personal spaces into a level-5 communal base with a 2-hour-sleep Ultra-Stabilized Bed and a Training Room, finally giving Mordecai his own room.

    Chapter 3 — Brandon's Letter

    After a full night of base-building, the party sleeps just two hours thanks to the new bed's Good Rest bonus. The recap show reveals the tentative Floor 4 leaderboard in full for the first time — Lucia Mar, Hekla, and a mysterious goat-person named Prepotente round out the top 3, with Carl and Donut at 6th and 7th. A system announcement declares bounty hunting officially open on the top 10. Then Carl finds a message in a new "Messages from Deceased Crawlers" folder: Brandon An died holding a line against Shade Gremlins as Floor 3 collapsed, and reveals he fought with his brother Chris just before dying, leaving an "I love you" he never got to say. Carl trains alone in grief, repeating his private mantra.

    Chapter 4 — Formation One

    The party settles into a routine: buffs, an hour of skill training, crafting, then grinding the rails. Carl crafts with new tools (the Goo-inator, earning the "Martha Stewart" achievement) and trades westerns for sci-fi novels with Limp Richard. Scouting the figure-eight Nightmare Express and the Yellow Line, they discover platform-sniping is safe against Cave Mudge Bonkers and Shock Chompers but reject it as an inefficient "time trap." Boarding a train, they drill Formation One — a V-shaped kill zone — against a wave of Bonkers, with Katia shapeshifting her arm into a weapon-catching limb.

    Chapter 5 — Vernon

    Fortified with crafted "Gangway Chock" barricades, the party survives a Pollyslog boarding attempt (severed by the closing doors) and reaches the Yellow Line conductor's break room, meeting Vernon, a dwarf trapped in an unexplained time loop — he blacks out at the end of the line and wakes at the yard, gold and mementos intact but wounds and hunger persisting. He reveals engineers are only ever seen after a derailment. The party finds the Porter reward room and picks numbered "prize" suitcases — Carl's holds an actual invisibility potion, but Donut's is trapped, unleashing Literal Fire Ants that burn the car and kill Vernon before the party barely escapes onto the platform.

    Chapter 6 — The Control Sigil

    At Banshee Station, Carl grapples with the horror of NPCs like Vernon believing in fabricated lives and memories. Investigating a side room, the party finds an inert pair of dwarven combat automatons guarded by a mysterious "control sigil" only Carl can see (via Escape Plan) — Mordecai explains it as sloppy, showrunner-controlled scripting, not standard AI mechanics. Meanwhile a PR call from Zev pressures Carl and Donut to make Katia "more interesting" for viewers, prompting an uncomfortable makeover conversation. The party demolishes the robots for fifteen tons of scrap metal. Daniel Bautista relays word that Imani C urgently needs to talk about Brandon and Chris, and warns of bounty hunters circling the leaderboard, before the party heads toward transfer station 131, home to a Desperado Club.

    Chapter 7 — The Prize Carousel

    Mongo hits level 15, maxing out his growth (now pony-sized) and gaining the Earthquake jump attack, as the party arrives at station 131. Mordecai crafts potions (including a two-use "immortality" brew and stat-buff potions) and Fragmenting Potion Balls. Fan boxes bring Donut the Enchanted Mongoliensis Saddle for riding Mongo, and Carl a live "Prize Carousel" segment hosted by an NPC named Chaco — whose mere presence enrages Mordecai into attacking him, triggering a 7-day manager suspension. Rattled, Carl picks a mysterious anarchy-symbol book as his prize: The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook, revealed to be a secret, AI-invisible, edition-spanning guide to explosives, potions, and dungeon secrets passed between crawlers for generations.

    Chapter 8 — Dirty Shirley

    Carl skims the Cookbook's rules (contribute knowledge or lose access; secrecy is mandatory) and a few explosive recipes before forcing himself to stop. Without Mordecai, Carl and Donut visit the Desperado Club, hiring rock-monster bodyguards Bomo and The Sledge. They reunite with Elle McGibbons (now a foul-mouthed, floating Frost Maiden) and Imani C (an Obsidian Butterfly fire-support healer), who explain that Brandon's brother Chris Andrews underwent a personality-altering Igneous race change, split from the group after a fight, and is now hunting for Carl — around the same time a resident named Henry accidentally spawned an army of Shade Gremlins. The reunion is interrupted when an assassin knifes Donut in the back; her Cockroach skill barely saves her, and she kills him in self-defense, earning her first player-kill skull.

    Chapter 9 — The Wheel of Fortune

    Elle kills the assassin's female accomplice with an icicle before she can strike again. Club assistant manager Astrid, a Level 125 Bloodlust Sprite, smooths over the incident and grants Carl and Donut free security. Comparing notes with Imani and Elle, the group realizes all known transfer stations are prime numbers. At the casino, Carl learns the Hunting Grounds and a war-torn "Larracos" both lie below, then gambles his old comp chip on the Wheel of Fortune — narrowly dodging a "get stabbed" and "Nothing!" (a literal trapdoor to oblivion) before landing a Scroll of Upgrade that boosts his Toe Ring's Powerful Strike bonus significantly.

    Chapter 10 — Testing the Rails

    Back at base, Katia demonstrates a near-perfect Doppelganger disguise of Hekla using a new Makeup Table (paid for with her own upgrade coupons, to Carl's private dismay). After training and gear distribution, the party tests whether the third rail is truly electrified (confirmed, safely) and builds Jelly Bombs — improvised landmines adapted from the Cookbook — to attempt a train derailment. Elle's group reports discovering a Borough Boss version of Krakaren on another line.

    Chapter 11 — Gore-Gore

    The derailment succeeds spectacularly, earning the "Locomotive Breath" achievement. Amid the wreckage, the ochre line's engineer is finally forced out: Gore-Gore, a Level 40 Mantaur — a bizarre two-torsoed, four-armed creature bred for the grueling engineer role — emerges from the ruined engine car to confront the party. (See Gore-Gore the Mantaur for the full encounter.)

    Chapter 12 — Sponsors

    Looting Gore-Gore's corpse yields 498 gold, an Ochre Line Engineer's Key, a Rev-Up Magic Hair Restoration item, and a full neighborhood map of the entire Ochre line from station 11 to 435. Sponsor reveals finally land: Carl gets the Valtay Corporation, Donut gets Princess D'nadia of the Prism Kingdom, and Katia gets a mysterious "Princess Formidable of the Skull Empire." Katia realizes several of her fingers were permanently severed in the fight, a grim "losing myself" moment about her doppelganger flesh-as-clay nature. Studying the map, the party learns monsters walk the open track between stations to reach assigned stops, and that station spacing varies wildly — mere blocks apart early on, 40-50 miles apart further down the line. Katia plots a cross-line route using the Dismemberment Limited and the Nightmare Express to skip ahead quickly, while Imani and Elle report finding a Krakaren-run "drug den" system where Pooka hand out addictive, race-specific sedatives to keep mobs docile and cycling through the trains — with a warning that interrupting the supply triggers dangerous mob transformations.

    Chapter 13 — The Ugly Ass Backpack

    Zev books Carl and Donut for an upcoming "Planet Beautiful" pageant-and-pet-show narration segment. Riding the Dismemberment Limited, the party meets the tragic hybrid creature Levi the 7th and fights off Dismember the War Mage and his "Bereft Minions" Fleshers by choking a doorway and using a "Surprise Three" — reaching through a Hole spell puncture to rip off Dismember's head. Carl reads further Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook entries from crawlers Rosetta, Allister, Forkith, and the emotionally raw Drakea. Back at base, Carl engineers Katia an "Ugly Ass Backpack" using a discovered inventory trick — a container's contents can be selectively withdrawn independent of the container — letting her equip over a ton of dwarf-robot scrap as living mass, transforming into an eleven-foot armored giant with a strength bonus at the cost of dexterity. That night Carl receives a Bronze Benefactor Box from the sinister Valtay Corporation: a Neural Enhancer pill that grants him the ability to see and analyze subspace portals through doorways.

    Chapter 14 — Stealing a Train

    Waiting for the Nightmare Express at station 283, Carl builds an informal crawler information network by fist-bumping chat contacts at the Desperado Club. When the boarding plan (climbing the exposed, monster-filled car roofs) proves suicidal, Carl instead uses Protective Shell to smash through the train from the tunnel while Donut's mistimed Puddle Jumper leaves him exposed — he's clipped by the runaway train's cowcatcher and forced to climb into the engine car itself. There he meets Fire Brandy, a Lesser Demon MILF firewoman who births and burns her own stillborn young as boiler fuel ("Sheol Bricks"), and who talks him through driving the train after both human engineers are found dead. Carl's shield spell has killed every monster aboard, including three neighborhood bosses, netting him three levels (to 32), a Steam Engineer's Key, and the achievements "Kept a Rollin'" and "Three Cheers for Slaughter" plus a Platinum Big Daddy Box.

    Chapter 15 — The Abyss

    At the Ochre line's switching station, Carl discovers the mechanism behind the trains: colored-line cars drive through one-way portals and are dumped into a mile-wide, mile-deep pit called the Abyss, while only the engine cars teleport back to be rebuilt into new trains — explaining why NPC "loops" are really fresh builds, not time travel. Analyzing the portals reveals the key: any of three "marked" items (the Ochre Key, Steam Train Key, or the free souvenir conductor hat every crawler received) grants passage to the far-side trainyard. Carl broadcasts this to his growing contact network as one of several possible routes to the sealed-off early stations. Meanwhile Donut receives her first benefactor box from Princess D'nadia — the Prism Industries Capacitating and Focusing Goggles, which turn her Magic Missile into a multi-shot laser — and levels up to 30, unlocking the new Love Vampire ability.

    Chapter 16 — Babababoons

    Elle and Imani find the secret ghoul-cart tunnel out of the robot rooms. Donut discovers she finally has enough spell points to read her long-dormant Minion Army tome (a slow, situational mass-charm spell with a five-minute cast time). The party is then swarmed at the transit station by Babababoons — feral, drug-addicted baboon-men suffering "stage two DTs" from withdrawal on the Rev-Up Amazing Cure-All shot — and slaughters dozens with Powerful Strike, Banger Spheres, and Katia's new bone-spike grappling trick. Back at the switching station, Carl examines the "Pass-Through" portal gating trains into Station and Repair Hub E and watches Fire Brandy bond with Donut and Katia over motherhood before the party commits to driving the Nightmare through.

    Chapter 17 — Widget

    Trainyard E is being overrun by a ghoul infestation from a broken "excess citizen disposal" system. Grease Gremlin mechanic Widget offers to guide the party out via chain and turntable, but a "dead" ghoul reanimates and rips his head off mid-rescue, forcing Donut to fumble the turntable controls herself via chat. The party escapes with rescued NPC Madison (an Iron Tangle HR associate) aboard, passing station after station down the employee line. Interrogating Madison at station 24, Carl learns of six ravenous Station Mimics disguised as terminus stops and fires off a last-second warning to Bautista not to get off at one.

    Chapter 18 — Human Resources

    Bautista reports his crawler group trapped at a Station Mimic disguised as a boss-free stairwell chamber. Carl learns the Kravyad guarding station 435's employee portal are non-combatant NPCs who hypnotize dwarven workers into endless labor loops, and that the portal snaps shut the instant its Kravyad dies. Investigating the identical five-stairwell chamber atop station 24, Katia privately confesses she's grown a working third eye — and reveals she secretly recovered a PVP Coupon bearing Carl's name that Donut had buried and hidden on the tracks; Carl confirms with Imani that every party member who receives a first player-kill "Savage Box" gets one of these betrayal coupons, unsettling him about what Donut may still be hiding. Riding the line down through stations 36 through 60, the party finds every "employee town" hollow and unfurnished — proof the NPCs' entire home life is fabricated backstory with no real substance behind it. Madison, confronted with her nonexistent apartment and family, refuses to leave and is abandoned on the platform to await her ex-husband "Rod," while Donut connects Madison's cruelty to the memory of Miss Beatrice's callous mother.

    Chapter 19 — Frank Q

    Back at station 41, Carl gets updates: Imani's group survives a ghoul-swarmed Trainyard D conveyor landing, and Bautista's group neutralizes the Kravyad, only for her to kill herself and seal the portal, stranding over 150 hatless crawlers. At the Desperado Club, Carl finds Frank Q — Brandon's killer's ex-husband — a broken, drunk night elf assassin who reveals the horrifying truth of Book 2's ambush: he and ex-wife Maggie forced their teenage daughter Yvette to wear a cursed ring and mark Carl for death, and Maggie choked Yvette to keep the mark's pain from being healed once the attack failed. As an act of self-destructive "revenge," Frank gifts Carl the Ring of Divine Suffering itself, and its dangerous Marked for Death skill; Carl accepts the stat bonus but resolves never to use the skill.

    Chapter 20 — Planet Beautiful

    Zev teleports Carl and Donut to a luxury underwater production trailer piloted by the sardonic robot Mexx-6000 for a Planet Beautiful culture segment. Carl refuses to read a script full of degrading, AI-hallucinated "facts" about human beauty pageants, and producer Bin — venting about network pressure to make the show "more interesting" — swaps it for a videogame-history segment instead, while Donut happily narrates off-script about cocker spaniels. Word arrives that Chris Andrews murdered Frank Q at the Desperado Club before vanishing, and Carl finally clears the air with Donut over the PVP coupons, which she'd disposed of out of guilt. Returning home after looting the trailer's fridge shelves, makeup table, and couch, Carl finds their personal space suddenly full of unfamiliar women — Hekla's entire crew has arrived uninvited.

    Chapter 21 — Brynhild's Daughters

    Hekla and roughly thirty members of Brynhild's Daughters have used Carl's station-60 intel to reach the party's base directly, thanking Carl for training Katia and revealing they crossed via the employee portal after Sadie's Glass Prison spell neutralized their local Kravyad. Carl meets Eva Sigrid, Katia's half-nagini, half-orc friend — now carrying thirteen skulls, evidently serving as Hekla's dedicated enforcer — and immediately senses an uneasy hierarchy within the otherwise-welcoming all-women crew.

    Chapter 22 — The Plow

    To rescue a thousand crawlers trapped at station 101, Hekla's team commandeers a Vermillion-line subway car while Katia builds herself into a living cowcatcher — a spiked, rubber-insulated scoop bolted to the front windows, able to pull dead mobs into her inventory. As the train plows through a ghoul horde at stations 72 and 75, Katia's health mysteriously plummets despite the healer Silfa's inaction, and Donut — watching through her magic-catching goggles — spots Hekla firing invisible cursed bolts into Katia's scoop on purpose, blasting Hekla in the face with a Magic Missile the instant she confirms it.

    Chapter 23 — Rush

    Hekla flies back into the cab wall as chaos erupts, Eva and two mage "daughters" joining the fight. Cornered and accused, Hekla and Eva reveal nothing outright, but Eva's cruel taunting about Katia's failures at last triggers Katia's unpredictable Rush ability — and instead of hitting Eva, Katia's body veers off-target and kills Hekla outright, claiming the game's first top-10 bounty (500,000 gold) and rocketing from level 24 to 37 in one blow.

    Chapter 24 — The Scavenger Mother of Mothers

    Donut resurrects Hekla's corpse with Second Chance and Clockwork Triplicate to buy time while Katia loots the body — securing the Enchanted Repeating Crossbow of the Scavenger Mother of Mothers, a unique weapon that scales with the number of women in the party — before the trio seals themselves in the engine and reverses the train to safety. Carl pieces together Hekla's real plan: sacrifice Katia and healer Silfa to bait Carl into attacking a "daughter," giving Hekla cover to kill him and absorb Donut (and Mordecai) into her depleted, healer-heavy team. With Brynhild's Daughters shattered and Eva missing, Carl hands the rescued survivors and the second engine off to Li Jun, directing them to make a stand at station 36 alongside Imani and Elle's group.

    Chapter 25 — Station 75

    Carl and company collect over 700 souvenir hats and keys from grateful survivors to distribute to crawlers stranded at the Abyss, then explore the ruined workshop station 75 — a gnoll-and-hobgoblin depot wiped out in a ghoul war. Katia's bounty haul nets her a Club Vanquisher tattoo, the Wrestling Belt of the Great Gorgo, and a Find Crawler skill potion; the recap show frames Hekla's death as a tribute special and reveals Eva abandoned Katia on the third floor to join Hekla first. The new leaderboard puts Lucia Mar at #1 and Carl, Donut, and Katia all in the top eight. Zev visits with bad news: a Bloom-party rival named Loita is angling to take over as their representative, and something unspecified but serious has happened back at Borant headquarters.

    Chapter 26 — Growler Gary

    At the ruined station 75 depot, Carl finds an indestructible gnoll bartender, Growler Gary, who regenerates minutes after dying — a sign, per Cookbook lore, that an NPC holds something vital. Investigating the yard, the party discovers Interdiction Rapid-Response Railway Repair Carts: gnoll-only, dual-handed vehicles that project a mobile portal ahead of them, capable of scooping an entire track's worth of wreckage and monsters into either the Abyss or a trainyard. Devising a scheme to rescue three separated groups of crawlers trapped at the Abyss, Carl realizes the only way to activate the carts (which require two separated left-hand prints) is to repeatedly kill and loot Gary's regenerating hands — a grim, consent-eventually-given process that nets 14 severed hands and Carl's lasting guilt.

    Chapter 27 — Physical

    The rescue plan launches: paired carts (one tuned to the Abyss, one to a trainyard, each with a randomized Top-40 song as an audible warning) blast down three colored lines, funneling wreckage, ghouls, and stranded crawlers to safety. Carl also detonates a hobgoblin-dynamite train bomb to destroy a monster horde threatening a soul-crystal-generator team, netting a level and closing that line for good. Testing combat against a mutated, tentacled "stage-three DTs" monster, the party learns its true nature when it explodes into thousands of tiny Krakaren Crotch Dumplings — the Krakaren race's disturbing cloning method — forcing a scramble to burn them all before they mature. Word arrives that station 24 is now overrun with the things.

    Chapter 28 — Yard H

    A disguised railroad-crossing barrier turns out to be a tentacle of the station-433 Mimic Rex in disguise, and Carl's cart accidentally teleports the entire city-boss-tier mimic to trainyard E. Warned by Elle's manager that the mimic can only be killed by stripping over half its mass at once — and that the separated pieces then attack independently until it dies — Carl scraps the fight entirely and instead routes Bautista's roughly 1,400 stranded survivors straight through the main Abyss portal to a mimic-free yard, using the party's stockpiled souvenir hats. The rescued crowd showers Donut with tribute hats in gratitude.

    Chapter 29 — The Bolt of Ophiotaurus

    Bautista reveals his secret weapon: stuffed-animal figures looted from the dead Miss Quill's apartment that transform into true, temporary monster summons when their tags are pulled. Back at a safehouse, Katia's loot haul includes a riot shield and baton that upgrade her Rush into the area-effect Crowd Blast (usable every five minutes instead of once a day), an Auto Buckler she passes to Carl, and — from her mysterious sponsor Princess Formidable — a single, precious Bolt of Ophiotaurus, capable of pausing a god's invulnerability for fifteen seconds. The recap show reveals a hidden stairwell at the now-mimic-vacated station 433, and a jarring system announcement: private crawler chats are now sellable to viewers, and inventory can no longer hold loose liquids. Mordecai returns from his suspension just as Katia's stored gallons of ghoul blood — no longer inventory-legal — explode out of her across the room.

    Chapter 30 — The Syndicate Logo

    Mordecai forces Carl to remove the dangerous Ring of Divine Suffering, explaining that Marked for Death rings make their wearers priority targets for faction-war hunters on later floors. Studying Katia's map, Mordecai recognizes the Iron Tangle's overall shape as the Syndicate's own wormhole-network logo, and identifies the drug-den Krakaren/Pooka storyline as an in-universe political cartoon smearing a rival species, the Plenty, over their instant-communication technology. Katia, obsessing over the map, works out that each rail line is mirrored by an inverted twin line on the far side of a hidden chamber — and realizes the sealed five-stairwell rooms sit directly above those hidden spaces, meaning the province-boss ghoul masses gathering at station 36 and 48 may be positioned to flip up into the stairwell chambers themselves.

    Chapter 31 — The Crane

    Acting on Katia's mirrored-track theory, Carl's team rigs a captured interdiction cart into a makeshift crane above station 36's stairwell circle, aiming its scoop-portal straight down at the hidden chamber below. When the Iron Tangle's system "self-destructs" and the floor's center collapses, waves of now-human-sized Krakaren Juvenile Clonelings pour upward exactly as predicted — and the dangling portal swallows them by the thousands into the Abyss, saving the station. Amid the chaos, Carl learns dwarven and gremlin NPCs have lynched Madison in retaliation for the disaster, and a deliberately triggered soul-crystal chain reaction begins wiping out nearby ghoul generators — confirming Carl's suspicion that killing a province boss detonates every crystal in its blast radius.

    Chapter 32 — Grull's Encore

    Reinforced by Li Jun's terrifyingly competent sister Li Na (a chain-wielding Slave Driver) and Zhang, Carl's crew lures the mirrored-chamber province boss into the raised cart's portal and banishes it to the Abyss, then runs into the deranged, screaming goat-man Prepotente and his handler Miriam Dom mid-hunt for another boss. Retrieving their last interdiction cart from the trainyard, the group finds the employee-line entrance blocked by two mantaur neighborhood bosses — so Carl deliberately triggers an involuntary summon of the war god Grull through one mantaur's body, planning to hurl him at the rampaging Mimic Rex and then banish him to the Abyss before he can do more damage. The plan goes sideways instantly: as the first mantaur is thrown into the trainyard portal, its clawed hand locks onto Carl's numbed, invulnerable foot, dragging Carl through right along with it.

    Chapter 33 — Death Challenge

    Carl tumbles into trainyard E face-to-face with the Mimic Rex, and the broadcast reframes the moment as a live "Death Challenge" gambling event, revealing Grull's secret sponsor: Prince Maestro, previously presumed dead, now piloting the war god for his own on-air comeback. Carl survives by hiding, an invisibility potion, and Mordecai's near-invulnerability brew while Grull annihilates the mimic; Katia disguises herself as Carl to lure Grull away, and Li Na horrifically dismembers the second captive mantaur, keeping him alive with heals so the team can trigger a second involuntary summon and banish Grull to the Abyss with only a second left on the clock. (See Mimic Rex for the full Death Challenge and Grull encounter.)

    Chapter 34 — Air Quadrant

    With five hours left on the floor, Carl loots the Mimic Rex's Map of the Stars, and Fire Brandy and dwarf Tizquick — having each learned their "families" were never real — choose to ride the Nightmare into the Abyss as one final, willing bomb, triggering enough deaths to collapse the region's remaining ghoul generators and boosting Carl to level 41. The survivors finally descend the station 36 stairwell as the floor winds down, and a new admin, Loita of the Bloom party, announces she's taking over as primary handler while Zev is abruptly sent to a "reeducation retreat" for an unspecified personal tragedy.

    Epilogue — Choose Your Opponent

    At the post-floor interview, Loita imposes new restrictions (no inventory items, no snacks, tighter interview censorship) while Odette needles Carl about a viral "snick" video, reveals the Death Challenge had absurd 50-to-1 survival odds that cost Borant heavily, and gets abruptly muted by Loita whenever the topics of Eva or "something about Chris" come up — confirming both threads remain live dangers. Sent to the fifth floor, Donut spins the entry wheels for the party: they'll enter in the Air Quadrant, facing the Dirigible Gnome Wasteland Fortress.
